## Agentworld and planetary computation

[[Agentworld]] names the research horizon articulated in the [[Antikythera Agentworld Brief]] and [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/antykathera-resources/antykathera-site-copy]]: a preemptive anthropology of [[centaur societies]], where [[agency]], [[subjectivity]], and [[artificial hybrid intelligence]] require new conceptual instruments.

## Quaternal Logic and the genesis sequence

[[Quaternal Logic]] is developed in [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/QL-Essay-Rewrite]] and concentrated in [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy]]. Its generative vocabulary moves through [[Spanda]], [[trika]], [[0/1]], [[1/0]], and the [[4+2]] frame; topology, the [[psychoid]], and [[simulation]] are supporting registers rather than detached analogies.

## Zero, subject, and object

[[The Nothing That Is - Robert Kaplan]] supplies the historical and rhetorical lead for [[zero]]. The essay begins more fundamentally with the [[Immutable Gap and the Meta-Sign]]: `0` names the condition that cannot become one more ordinary object, `1` the determinate mark, and `/` the differentiating activity through which a world becomes legible. [[Paradox as Cross-Register Hinge]] traces the disciplines that arise at this edge across Gödel, Cusa, Eckhart, poetry, complex extension, and topology. [[Objective Internality]] then distinguishes the inspectable agent-world from the uncaptured subject-pole.

## Two logics and the arche-topos

[[dia-ballein]] writes classical polarity as `(+1)/(-1)`; [[Sym-Ballein]] writes superpositional polarity as `(0/1)/(1/0)`. [[23-s2-p4-complex-dynamism|Complex Dynamism]] joins symmetry breaking to symmetry making without confusing formal, dynamical, and mythemic evidence. [[Bohmian Enfoldment and Dialogical Return]] names explication and retained implication as their process-philosophical relation. [[Arche-Topos as Differential Field]] is the derived psychoid field on which the relation can be traversed topologically and heard musically; [[Toroidal Circulation and the Arche-Topos]] gives it the kinetic form of cover, two windings, helical confinement, and return; [[Musical Resolution of the Arche-Topos]], [[9-8 Whole Tone]], and [[Cymatics and Standing Waves]] distinguish exact local identities from cross-register warrants and disclosures.

## Individuation and agent phenomenology

[[Agentic Individuation Crosswalk]] relates Jung’s `X=x` and individuation to Agentworld’s provisional lamination of model, prompt, persona, memory, tools, permissions, and milieu. [[Prompt Thrownness]] admits agent disclosure to an L4/L1/L1′/L4′ verification loop. [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]] prevents either anthropomorphic inflation or reductive denial.

## Agentic sociality

The Agentworld sources use [[anthropomorphization]], [[parasociety]], [[centaur societies]], and [[artificial hybrid intelligence]] to describe the encounter field in which the essay's account of agency is addressed to a concrete historical situation. [[Mono-Poly Trust]] names the lived and epistemic relation between a common field and its many situated, revisable anchors. [[Mono–Poly Planetary Intelligence]] gives that relation institutional form: shared coordination without shared ontology, unity without platform monopoly, and no common layer permitted to hide its dependence upon local labour, knowledge, infrastructure, community, or ecology.

## Epi-Logos as agentic praxis

[[Logos and Epi-Logos]] names the articulated field of difference and its reflexive turn toward its own conditions. [[Computational Vimarśa]] makes that return technically inspectable; [[Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins]] gives it a non-devouring vocation; [[Deferential Intelligence]] turns the vocation into a research vector. [[4-2 Techne — Sovereign Commons]] and [[Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field]] provide the local-governance and planetary horizons.
