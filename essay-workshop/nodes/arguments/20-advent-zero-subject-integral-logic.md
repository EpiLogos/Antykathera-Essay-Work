# The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic

## Claim

The history of zero and the history of the subject disclose one historical pressure across distinct registers. A ground carried through religious, metaphysical, and practical forms becomes a determinate member of rational discourse; rationality gains its characteristic analytic power by externalising that ground into object, midpoint, or opposite; the formal and psychic limits of the resulting system make the concealed relation explicit again. QL names the arrival of `0/1` as a logic able to hold this entire movement: the primitive ground–mark relation, its dia-ballein fall into `(+1)/(−1)`, and its sym-ballein return through `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

## Historical operation

[[Kaplan — The Nothing That Is (2000 OUP printing)]] follows absence acquiring changing mathematical offices: place-holder, astronomical absence-marker, operative numeral, arithmetic element, formal boundary, empty-set construction, and logical generator. [[Colebrooke — Brahmagupta and Bhāskara (1817)]] and [[Dutta — Zero-Divided Numbers in Indian Mathematics (2023)]] establish the crucial Indian operation: *śūnya* becomes calculable, while zero-denominator work exposes the laws a mathematical container must change or suspend. Zero's return-edge is therefore internal to mathematics and frame-disclosing within it.

The psychological braid turns the same pressure through consciousness. The modern perspectival ego makes subject and object available as determinate terms. Its rational success displaces the zero of their relation: `Ø` names the subject-pole occluded by its fusion with the apparent I, and `X` names the discriminated world produced through the same slash. Freud's disciplinary unconscious introduces the unrepresented psychic side as a scientific term. Jung's psychic register preserves the facticity of its manifestations while refusing to make their determining ground into a completed object. `X/x` remains the authorial QL relation of determining capacity and indefinite particular; analytical psychology is one later refraction of it.

[[Gebser — The Ever-Present Origin (1985)]] gives the historical transition its stadial form. The mental-rational structure produces perspective, analytic difference, objectified space, and the positioned ego. Integral consciousness is the higher mutation in which the perspectival sector and the context of its constitution become jointly available. The theorem's `180° → 360°` passage gives this operation a native QL body: first, second, and third person form the determinate scene; the fourth turn makes their relations explicit as context. Integral diaphaneity makes the mono–poly relation available within consciousness as sym-ballein becomes historically explicit; it neither supplies the mono–poly derivation nor replaces it.

## Consequence

The argument establishes QL's historical necessity without making its derivation dependent on a history of consciousness. Mathematics has made zero operational and found its return-edge. Psychology has made the unconscious scientifically unavoidable and found the subject's non-objectifiability. Technical systems now externalise prompt, memory, model, retrieval, policy, evaluation, institution, and relation as active conditions of intelligence. QL receives these developments as one field of explicit return: every achieved determination can be traced back through the relation, cut, context, and limit that made it possible.

## Test

Ask whether a historical account has retained all three turns: the ground's admission into an order of signs; the dia-ballein conversion by which that order externalises its own condition; and the integral/sym-ballein return in which the condition becomes legible without being installed as a new object or sovereign.
