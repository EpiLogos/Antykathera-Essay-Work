# Sym-Ballein

## Claim

The two logics are the grammar beneath every polarity the essay handles. Their prior condition is [[The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme|the two ones]]: `0/1` makes mono–poly legible before dia/sym become two operations of accounting.

- `(+1)/(−1)` is the classical, signed reading: opposed determinations become externally measurable through symmetry breaking. This is **dia-ballein**—difference projected as counter-position.
- `(0/1)/(1/0)` is the superpositional reading: each orientation retains the zero and the slash through which it is related to its inverse. This is **sym-ballein**—difference holding the provenance of its distinction inside itself.

They are conjugate views of differentiating activity. Dia-ballein makes a determinate world possible; sym-ballein keeps the determination answerable to the field it cannot exhaust. Moral judgment belongs to the consequences of their operation: dia-ballein without return hardens distinction into severance, while sym-ballein without distinction decays into fusion.

Superposition is native to the dual-non-dual ontology before it becomes a comparison with any particular physical formalism. Ground and manifestation are distinguishable orientations of one appearing-act, each given through the relation that prevents either from becoming self-sufficient; separately complete terms would already presuppose the bridge the derivation is meant to explain. `0/1` and `1/0` write that co-presence in inverse directions; their outer relation makes the inversion itself available. Quantum state space, complementarity, and non-classical logics can therefore become rigorous neighbours without lending the ontology a meaning it did not already possess.

Dia-ballein is the symmetry-broken, determinate projection of this field into signed counter-position. Sym-ballein is the knowledge retained within the cut that both poles arose through one differentiating activity. Restoring zero to `−1/+1` reopens the mediation concealed by the duality, rather than adding a neutral midpoint to the signed line. The pair can then be read as Trika and carried toward the quaternary context in which knower, knowing, and known become simultaneously available.

The historical form of this conversion is carried by [[The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic]]. Mental-rational consciousness gains its exact powers through the signed line; integral consciousness is the higher `180°→360°` turn in which the line's constituting relation becomes contextually available. The theorem derives the operation; the history shows why its advent now becomes explicit.

This is why mono–poly, subject/object, internal/external, one/many, self/other, order/chaos, Apollo/Dionysus, and every further conceptual polarity can be read together without being declared synonymous. Mono–poly names the ontological whole/many relation; dia/sym name the operations by which a polarity holds or loses that relation. Their contents differ; their relational grammar passes through the same two logics. [[Core Theorem Bridge|*pros hen* analogia]] names the method: each polarity speaks in its own register while pointing through structural proportion toward one focal relation.

QL supplies the usable attractor-space for this analogia. Its positions operate as transcendental attractors around which distinct mathematical, psychological, metaphysical, linguistic, and technical expressions constellate. A taxonomic box would require identity of content; an attractor constrains the movement while preserving differences of register. The core theorem-units show how the attractors arise from `0/1`; MEF records how each refraction reaches them, what it preserves, and where it diverges.

The two logics are the primitive operations of [[Computational Process Ontology|a computational process ontology]], with their diagrams and philosophical descriptions displaying the operations in different registers. Dia-ballein distinguishes, excludes, compares, ranks, routes, and selects. Sym-ballein preserves provenance, inverts a determination, recomposes differentiated results, and returns the achieved form to its field. Their pulse is the runtime by which a mind computes a world.

“Divide and conquer” gives the dia operation its native computational formulation. Recursive decomposition makes a problem tractable by treating subproblems as locally independent. Yet the algorithm succeeds only because the results are subsequently combined. Atomisation begins when the conquest is mistaken for the whole method—when division loses recomposition, provenance, and responsibility to the field it divided. The complete computational movement is **divide / relate / recombine / return**; “division versus unity” obscures the sequence by turning conjugate operations into static camps.

The same distinction governs knowledge's concealed relation to trust. Dia-ballein denies the provisionality of its local anchor and projects unheld uncertainty onto an enemy, scapegoat, or false ground. Sym-ballein offers determinate anchors while retaining their dependence upon the relations, sources, practices, and worlds that make them possible. In mono-poly language: many real `1`s articulate one relational field; monopoly begins when a local `1` claims ownership of the slash that relates them.

The historical-political expression is not exhausted by left and right. Those are one signed projection of a deeper field in which empire meets subject, institution meets citizen, and accumulated wealth meets the labour and conditions on which it depends. Dia-ballein makes these relations legible as isolated camps and lets a local `1` occupy the absent ground. Mass formation is the psychic circulation of that capture: atomisation, free-floating affect, narrative fixation, excluded enemies, and delegated responsibility can make the capture feel like collective purpose. The QL reading is **Argued** from, not extracted from, Le Bon, Jung, and Desmet; Iakovou's institutional qualification remains constitutive of the claim.

“Divine and concur” gives the complete counter-operation its name. Sym-ballein divines the third—not a centrist term on the spectrum, but the `0/1 ↔ 1/0` relation whose capture generates the spectrum as managed antagonism. Differentiated positions concur through that ground without fusion. The praxis is directed against the dia-ballein operation that makes relation unthinkable, unaccountable, and ownable.

## Matheme

`0/1` is the ground–mark relation; `1/0` is its counter-reading; `(0/1)/(1/0)` is the relation relating to itself. Its self-relation yields the full unit: `2 + 2² = 4 + 2 = 6`. The theorem precedes the analogies.

## Shadow

Sym-ballein's failure is fusion: forced coincidence, ontology collapse, counterfeit belonging, or enclosure presented as wholeness. Dia-ballein's failure is severance: the cut forgets its field and treats the other as eliminable.

## Test

Ask where the seam went. If it is externalised as enemy or silently erased through fusion, the relation has lost its ground.
