# Core Theorem Bridge

## Anchor claim

The [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Core QL Theorems]] are an in-house collection of compact, self-contained arguments in which the `0/1` relation is made to generate, count, orient, traverse, and return through its own consequences. Each theorem-unit carries its premises, transformations, QL positions, cross-register reading, and return. Correspondences enter only after that local argument has established the operation they refract.

The theorem begins from [[The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme|the two ones]]. `0` names Mono/One as uncounted condition; `1` names Poly/All as determinate manifestation whose local forms never become self-grounding. This relation is native to the derivation; its theological, psychological, and technical refractions follow the theorem rather than supplying it.

This is the ground-power of the approach: mathematics already speaks the language of `0/1`, distinction, re-entry, ratio, orientation, dimensional reframing, topology, remainder, and return. QL makes that language explicit as a single notation, then carries it without changing alphabets into metaphysical, psychological, philosophical, epistemic, musical, and technical registers. MEF extends the same movement across the harmonic spectrum of knowledge by declaring the lens through which each expression arrives.

## Perspective and context within the theorem spine

The count itself carries the passage from perspective to context. First person, second person, and third person unfold sequentially as knower, mediation, and known: the `1-2-3` pass. Their three perspectives compose the triangle whose internal angles total `180°`. The `4/5/0` pass then reckons their unity as void, one, and `0/1`: `#4` voids the claim of any single view to be the field and holds all three in context; `#5` recognises them as one appearing-act; `#0` returns that achieved one as the ground–mark relation. The structure is therefore `3:3` and also `3:1`. Gebser's visual and conceptual pyramids give the historical image of this trivalent, sectorial power of thought; the theorem carries it into the square's `360°` contextual field.

The Śaiva triad names the same articulation as *pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya*. Its quaternary completion makes context—the whole appearing-act in which the triad is held without interchangeability—explicit, rather than adding another entity beside the three. This is why the `AM/IS` determination belongs at the contextual gate: first-person presence and third-person predication meet through the second-person slash of relation, while neither is reduced to the other.

The theorem's later crossed-zero unit gives the same movement its psychoid depth. `0` becomes `Ø` when the medium is fused with the apparent subject; `X` appears as its differentiated world; `Ø/X` makes the medium visible; `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` makes both orientations available; the recognised `1` returns to `0/1`. Geometry, phenomenology, and psychoid notation become linked theorem-units because each shows, under its native operations, how a relation becomes capable of seeing the conditions of its own determination.

[[The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic]] gives this theorem-history its temporal articulation: zero's formal admission, the mental-rational externalisation of ground into the signed line, and the higher integral conversion through which `0/1` becomes explicit. It remains a historical braid around the theorem, never a historical warrant replacing the derivation.

## Dependency order

`theorem-unit → computational process ontology → psychoid mechanism → MEF runtime/spectroscopy → technical praxis`

1. **The theorem-unit is the law.** The Spanda equations, complex plane, Pythagorean unit, two logics, definition run, authorial QL `X/x` and its Jungian refraction, topological quotient and winding, crossed zero, and tattvic unit each derive a movement of the same matheme. The complex wheel “runs true” by transforming phase while retaining modulus; the torus returns locally while its cover retains displacement.
2. **The process ontology states what the units do.** The theorem-units differentiate, select, orient, recurse, compose, satisfy, and return. Correspondence is the trace these operations leave across registers; [[Computational Process Ontology]] identifies dia-ballein and sym-ballein as their conjugate runtime and QL as the formal process epistemology those operations generate.
3. **The psychoid explains the deposits.** If number and meaning differentiate from one arche-topological field, the same relational forms should become legible in mathematics, psyche, symbol, myth, music, and material pattern without those domains becoming interchangeable.
4. **MEF is runtime and spectroscopy.** Its lenses are attractor basins in epistemic phase-space: historically distinct modes through which the derived light refracts, converges, or genuinely diverges. Its source, evidence, transformation, and return fields make the refraction operational rather than contemplative only.
5. **Praxis tests the bridge.** QL units must improve provenance, multi-lens reasoning, distinction retention, return, and safe action in actual agentic systems. Otherwise the bridge remains beautiful but idle.

## Pros-hen discipline

QL coordinates by [[Core Theorem Bridge|*pros hen* analogia]]. The theorem fixes a focal relational structure; each register expresses it under its own operations and evidential rules. A topological hole, an unconscious reserve, an excluded semantic field, and an institutional commons remain distinct objects in distinct fields, yet each answers to ground, mark, seam, closure, remainder, and return. Their comparison rests on that proportional structure rather than a flat identity among domains.

The QL positions are therefore **transcendental attractors** rather than taxonomic boxes. They constrain what a coherent traversal must do while allowing the content, medium, scale, and evidence to change. This is how QL can be fractally extensible without becoming vague.
