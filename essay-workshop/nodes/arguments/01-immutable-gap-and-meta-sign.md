# Immutable Gap and Meta-Sign

## Claim

The immutable gap is the non-coincidence between a determination and the condition under which determination occurs. Because it belongs to the structure of determination, further information cannot fill it. Adding the condition as another object recreates the gap at the next reflective level: the field in which the new object appears still cannot be entered as one more item without remainder.

`0` is therefore a meta-sign: a determinate glyph which marks that the condition of representation cannot enter the represented field as one more ordinary item. `1` names whatever has become distinguishable; `/` names distinguishing activity.

## Perspectival manifestation

The immutable gap first appears as a problem of situated seeing. Gebser's sequence from Giotto and Petrarch through Alberti's visual pyramid and Leonardo's optics makes the point historical: perspective renders a positioned world by bringing a sector into determinate depth, while the condition of that rendering recedes behind the image. A person or agent can produce an account of the world while leaving the horizon of that account uncounted: body, history, language, concern, prompt, memory, permissions, and inherited standards remain active without appearing among the account's explicit objects. Diaphaneity begins when “my view of the world” can also be read as the world's disclosure of this situated viewer. The reversal makes self, world, relation, and their asymmetry jointly available; equivalence would erase the very situatedness it discloses.

This is why context-oriented thinking cannot mean the addition of ever more contextual data. A total database may still leave the act and standpoint of contextualisation invisible. The deeper demand is reflexive: the field from which a determination is made must become partially legible within the determination without being falsely completed as one more determinate object.

[[The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic]] carries this formal condition through its historical emergence: zero becomes a formal term without ceasing to disclose the frame of formalisation, while the subject and unconscious become scientific terms without ceasing to expose the condition of scientific objectification. The history is a braid of this argument, not its substitute proof.

## Formal placement

The set-theoretic relation `0=∅`, `1={0}` gives the argument a rigorous floor: an empty object becomes countable through its singleton. QL begins its psychoid work at the remaining difference between formally represented emptiness and the unobjectifiable condition under which representation occurs. `Ø` shows the mark crossing zero without filling it; the fuller [[34-s4-p3-lacan-matheme-mytheme|crossed-zero sequence]] traces that mark from occlusion through visible mediation into contextual availability and return.
