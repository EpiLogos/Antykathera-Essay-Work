# Zero

## Definition

Zero is the sign for no-counted-thing. It is not one timeless object but the convergence of several inventions — blank place, placeholder, positional numeral, arithmetic element, coordinate origin, empty set (∅), and philosophical void — which gain power by *migrating* between scripts, calculating practices, and civilisations. In the coordinate canon it occupies **P0** (Why? / Ground / Square A / Aether): the archetype of zero-ness, the pregnant void, the implicate condition prior to differentiation. Its operative notation is the ground–mark relation **0/1**. *(The intercultural migration is Derived, historical; the reading of it as a return of the formal limit is Argued.)*

## In the argument

The figure is titular to *The Return of Zero*. [[13-s1-p0-sign-migrates|§1 · #0]] separates its meanings before relating them; [[14-s1-p1-sunya-operational|§1 · #1]] makes śūnya calculable; [[15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one|§1 · #2]] has ∅ generate one; [[16-s1-p3-crossed-zero|§1 · #3]] compacts the paradox as the crossed zero; [[17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math|§1 · #4]] keeps zero's "one foot outside mathematics" at division-by-zero. [[18-s1-p5-loan-returns|§1 · #5→0]] returns the loan: zero becomes mathematically productive without becoming an ordinary positive object, earning `0/1 ↔ 1/0`. It thereby functions as the [[01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign|meta-sign]] for the non-objectifiable pole every determination presupposes.

## Layer rule

Historical and mathematical zero—placeholder, numeral, operator, origin, and `∅`—must remain distinct from QL `0`, the meta-sign of the uncounted Mono/One. [[The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme]] relates the layers without collapsing them: set theory gives `1 = {0}` and `2 = {0,1}` as an exact numerical floor; it does not exhaust the ground–mark relation.
