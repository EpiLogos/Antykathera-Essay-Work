# Concept Map

This is the concept surface of the vault — a map of the operative vocabulary of *The Return of Zero*, not a store of it. Each entry routes by **analogia** to the **one canonical home node** where the concept is actually worked; the gloss here is a pointer, not a copy, and the coordinates locate the term on the base bimba (`context/epi_logos_coordinate_system.md`). No concept is defined twice: if two terms share a home, they are two faces refracted from one node. Concepts are grouped by their **transverse braid** — the argumentative thread that carries them across the spine — with the unbraided Śaiva–Buddhist–Jungian foundations gathered last as the shared ground-vocabulary the braids draw on.
