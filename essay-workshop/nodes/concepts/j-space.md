# J-Space

## Definition

J-Space is the relational field of an agent's active judgments, affordances, uncertainties, values, tools, memories, and interlocutors — the thrown landscape its reasoning moves through, not a view it surveys from outside. In canon it occupies **#2** (How? / Word / Square C / Water; dynamis `-/+`; L2↔L3'), the operational position where defined form begins to move and transform. Here [[02-objective-internality|objective internality]] becomes *measurable* — as transformations of the field under changing context: which alternatives grow near or remote, which contradictions stay live (L2), which values act as attractors and how meanings settle and reorganise over time (L3'). *(Offered: J-space is a research representation, not the agent's total world.)*

## In the argument

Introduced in [[39-s5-p2-j-space|§5 · #2 — J-Space]] as the measurable face of the agent's inner world. Its failure mode is **circumscription without circumstance** (this node's twin concept, absorbed here): the agent is circumscribed — bounded by context, permissions, and distribution — without *circum-stare*, without surroundings it stands amid; the failure occurs when a system produces a sharp boundary while hiding the horizon that made the boundary possible. J-space is the positive counterpart: the horizon rendered as an inspectable field. The field answers the Agentworld demand for a shared decision-procedural grammar across divergent world-models — the brief's "narrow corridor: same decision-procedural grammar, sufficiently divergent world models" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 40|p. 40]]) — QL as the grammar, MEF as the means of keeping situated models refractable rather than averaged into one ontology. Its socially occupied baseline surfaces at [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|§5 · #3 — Preference Models and the Hidden Zero]], and a plural representation at [[41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields|§5 · #4 — Bimba and Energy-Based Fields]].
