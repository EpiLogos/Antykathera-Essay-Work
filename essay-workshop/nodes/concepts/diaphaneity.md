# Diaphaneity

## Definition

Diaphaneity — from *dia + phainein*, "shining-through" — is Jean Gebser's name for the mode of presence proper to integral consciousness. Gebser reads the history of consciousness as a sequence of structures — archaic, magic, mythic, mental-rational, integral — that are **mutations, not upgrades**: earlier structures remain co-present rather than superseded, and integration means their relations becoming transparent, not the rational stage accumulating enough parts to simulate a whole. A perspective becomes diaphanous when it can reveal its own placement without pretending to stand outside that placement; origin, limit, and the co-present other structures shine through the determinate form without dissolving it. Transparency is a *form*, not one more manifested content.

## The method it gives the essay

Diaphaneity supplies the governing rule for cross-register argument. Mathematics, phenomenology, psychology, myth, and technical design remain non-identical apertures with their own vocabularies, objects, and warrants; each must become transparent to the origin, limits, and exclusions that condition it. MEF instantiates the demand by attaching every claim to its lens and keeping divergence visible. The reversal seeded at the essay's opening is diaphaneity operationalised: "self viewing world" and "world disclosing this situated self" are reciprocal orientations of one event — asymmetric, hence never interchangeable; one event, hence never separable.

Internet and AI signal a contemporary contextual mutation in this precise sense. Perspectival optics organised a world through a positioned viewer; networked and agentic systems make prompt, memory, retrieval, permissions, policy, provenance, feedback, and relation operational conditions of intelligence. The historical claim and the methodological one are inseparable: context has become technically active, so its origins, exclusions, and powers must become readable within the intelligence it conditions.

The eastern sequence provides the parallel from inside determination itself. Apoha's double negation shows a mark as empty of isolated self-subsistence and full of its constitutive exclusions; *pratītya-samutpāda* shows the arising of any determination through conditions; *saṃkalpa* names the world-building gathering of those conditions. Śūnyatā is pūrṇatā: the empty determination opens as the full relational field. Gebser's diaphaneity gives this one operation its historical and optical articulation—context shines through a perspective without abolishing the perspective—and Internet/AI make that contextual field technically operative.
