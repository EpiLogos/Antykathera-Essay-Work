# §5 · #5→0 — Six Research Vectors

## Claim
The arche-topos becomes researchable through six discriminating programmes: seam retention, gauge disclosure, lens refraction, topological continuity, QL recursion, and deferential return.

## Warrant
E1 compares provenance-rich and output-only agents; E2 exposes preference baselines; E3 compares declared lenses; E4 tests attractors and phase changes; E5 tests fractal nesting; E6 measures [[Deferential Intelligence]] and [[06-computational-vimarsa-ahi|Computational Vimarśa]].

## Tension / limit
Each vector needs a baseline, metric, failure condition, and counter-hypothesis. None may manufacture a claim of machine subjectivity.

## Anchor and transition
**QL anchor:** every experiment performs the `1/0` return by declaring origin, exclusion, lens, and limit. Sustaining this practice requires an institution in [[43-s50-p0-theory-vocation-compassion|§5→0 · #0 — From Theory to Vocation]].
