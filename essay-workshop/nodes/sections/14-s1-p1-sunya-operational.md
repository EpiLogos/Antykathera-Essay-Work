# §1 · #1 — Śūnya Becomes Operational

## Movement thesis

The decisive turn occurs when śūnya is no longer only the empty place but something through which arithmetic can pass. Brahmagupta gives rules for operations involving zero; Bhāskara later presses into khahara, the division of a number by zero. Absence acquires operational force. It changes calculations while remaining unlike the positive magnitudes among which it operates.

The history distinguishes causal claims about mathematical practice from semantic claims about *śūnya*. The warranted claim is that the term could carry philosophical density and mathematical precision together, and that this conjunction formed part of the history transmitted into the modern number system.

The operationalisation is a decisive historical event in the braid: zero becomes eligible for formal procedure while remaining unlike an ordinary positive magnitude. The later subject-history refracts the same problem in another field, where a formally admitted term names the condition a science cannot simply hold as an object.

## Formal payload

- As additive identity, \(a+0=a\), zero leaves a determination unchanged.
- As multiplicative absorber, \(a\cdot0=0\), it returns any finite determination to zero.
- As divisor, \(a/0\), it exposes the limit or transformation of the containing algebra.

The first two operations are stable within ordinary arithmetic. The third is the pressure-point that §1 · #4 reopens. Zero is simultaneously inside the system as a lawful element and at its edge as the term that forces the system to state what it means by completion.
