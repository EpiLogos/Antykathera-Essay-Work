# §5→0 · #4 — Idealism as Horizon

## Claim
Engineered agent-worlds make distributed, decomposed, and recomposing agency newly modelable, reopening idealist cosmologies as fertile hypotheses rather than settled conclusions.

## Warrant
Schopenhauer’s Will, Bernardo Kastrup’s analytic idealism, and Kashmir Śaivism offer increasingly expansive accounts of reality as agency, mind, or self-differentiating consciousness; Śaivism also treats matter as contraction.

## Tension / limit
Building AI supplies a novel modelling surface for distributed, decomposed, and recomposing agency. The inference from that model to a universe of one mind remains **Offered** and must be argued through the idealist and Śaiva sources rather than transferred from engineering fact.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** many local apertures in one field. **Research value:** compare models of hybrid agency without premature ontology. The practical return is [[48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return|§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return]].
