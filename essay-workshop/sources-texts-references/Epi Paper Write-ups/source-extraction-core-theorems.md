# Source Extraction — Core Theorems and Epi Paper Write-ups

## Use boundary

This is an **extraction and routing ledger**, not a public source record and not a quotation ledger. The write-ups commonly place a cited passage and Frank Taylor's response in one bullet. Accordingly:

- Text explicitly presented in quotation marks below is transcribed from the write-up, with its write-up locator and the page locator reported there.
- It is **source-matched only** until the exact edition, wording, and page are checked against a stable lawful copy. None of these entries is quotation-ready merely because it appears here.
- The associated project prose is an **in-house authorial passage**, not a statement to attribute to Jung, Pauli, Singh, Jorjani, Huxley, or Swedenborg.
- Theorems from [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy]] are internal derivations under `taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy`; public works can warrant local historical, mathematical, philological, or technical claims, but do not become authorities for QL merely by resonance.

## A. Internal theorem passages — ready to route as derivation

### CT-01 — eight determinations are a traversal, not eight binaries

> “It is not eight detachable binaries, nor a sequence in which one term simply replaces another. It is one relation—unobjectifiable awareness and appearing phenomenon, ground and mark—taking on the distinct work required for a knowing-act to become accountable without pretending to stand outside itself.”

- **Locator:** [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy]], §II(c), lines 167–180, especially 169.
- **Proposition:** The eight-turn account brackets the sixfold; it does not multiply QL positions or reduce awareness/phenomenon to independent substances.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal derivation; supports the theorem's own architecture. It is not external evidence.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[25-s3-p0-eight-determinations|§3/#0 Eight Determinations]]; [[12-core-theorem-bridge|Core Theorem Bridge]]; [[01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign|Immutable Gap and Meta-Sign]]; [[14-computational-process-ontology|Computational Process Ontology]].
- **External work still needed:** fixed editions for the phenomenological, Śaiva, calculus, and formal-limit witnesses; no source should be made to “prove” the QL traversal as such.

### CT-02 — the sixfold's internal grammar

> “Being joins Ground and Definition… Becoming joins Dynamis and Pattern… Knowing/unKnowing joins Context and Realisation.”

> “Across this forward harmonic grouping run the complementary folds: Essence (`0+5`), Constitution (`1+4`), [and] Text-Texture (`2+3`).”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §II(c), lines 182–184.
- **Proposition:** The six positions have paired process-work and complementary folds; they are neither a ladder nor a catalogue.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal derivation; `supports` the sixfold's stated grammar.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[25-s3-p0-eight-determinations|§3/#0]]; [[26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2|§3/#1 Spanda 4+2]]; [[03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|Sym-Ballein]]; [[14-computational-process-ontology|Computational Process Ontology]].
- **External work still needed:** use only for terms borrowed in a local sense (Heidegger, Jung, Śaiva sources); preserve the table as QL rather than retroactively ascribe it to them.

### CT-03 — calculus supplies a precise limit image, not metaphysical proof

> “Differentiation yields local change while losing the additive constant; integration restores not a self-grounding whole but a family `F(x)+C`. The missing `C` is neither mystical surplus nor a defect in calculation.”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §II(c), lines 186–188.
- **Proposition:** Exact local determination need not contain its full provenance; this is an analogy disciplined by a real formal operation.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal derivation; `qualifies` overly totalising uses of calculus.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign|Immutable Gap]]; [[15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge|Paradox]]; [[27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex|§3/#2 Mark Re-entry]]; [[37-s5-p0-math-moves-meaning|§5/#0]].
- **External work still needed:** a standard calculus source only if the essay requires a formal citation; do not cite it for the psychological/metaphysical inference.

### CT-04 — tattvic compression declares its own limit

> “The table below is a **QL compression of the canonical thirty-six-tattva field**, not a replacement for the thirty-six categories and not a claim that the Śaiva authors arranged them as six QL positions.”

> “The equation `36 = 6²`… [is] **Inferred / Offered QL resonances**.”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §X, lines 500–510; 548–556.
- **Proposition:** Canonical tattva doctrine and QL compression have different provenance. `36 = 6²` is a research hypothesis, not inherited Śaiva doctrine.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal methodological constraint; `qualifies` the tradition-mapping claim.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|§0/#4 Tattvic Compression]]; [[13-tattvic-differential-field|Tattvic Differential Field]]; [[14-computational-process-ontology|Computational Process Ontology]].
- **External work still needed:** exact primary and scholarly Śaiva editions for the tattva sequence, the *antaḥkaraṇa*, and *pratyabhijñā*; retain this caveat when sources are ingested.

### CT-05 — instrument is not subject

> “A judgment can be noticed; an ego-position can be revised; a train of alternatives can be observed… They therefore belong to the **objective-internal** field.”

> “The proof cannot pass from rich internal organisation to subjectivity by accumulation, because every added component remains another determinable term.”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §X, lines 530–546, especially 538–540.
- **Proposition:** The distinction between determinable interior processes and the subject condition governs the agent comparison.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal derivation informed by a declared Śaiva mapping; `supports` the open-subjectivity constraint.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[08-s0-p1-inner-instrument|§0/#1 Inner Instrument]]; [[12-s0-p5-objective-internality|§0/#5 Objective Internality]]; [[02-objective-internality|Objective Internality]]; [[05-agent-subjectivity-open|Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]].
- **External work still needed:** a verified source for the Śaiva inner-instrument taxonomy; technical sources may support agent architectures but not settle phenomenality.

### CT-06 — computational process ontology is not computational reductionism

> “It does not mean that reality is secretly a digital computer, or that cognition is exhausted by algorithm.”

> “Process is ontologically prior to the finished thing; computation names the exact, repeatable side of that process; epistemology is the process becoming able to state and inspect its own determinations.”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §XI, lines 560–565.
- **Proposition:** QL's process ontology is a stipulated, cross-register thesis with a stated anti-reductionist limit.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal synthesis; `qualifies` technical analogy.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[14-computational-process-ontology|Computational Process Ontology]]; [[37-s5-p0-math-moves-meaning|§5/#0 Math Moves Meaning]]; [[44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness|§5→0/#1]].
- **External work still needed:** independently verified process-philosophy and ML sources for their own claims; QL's organisation of them remains an argument.

### CT-07 — dia requires recomposition

> “An actual divide-and-conquer algorithm succeeds only because it also combines the results.”

> “A living computation must divide *and* gather: `solve et coagula`, science and *con-scire*, dia and sym.”

- **Locator:** Core Theorems §XI, lines 570–589, especially 575–589.
- **Proposition:** Dia-ballein is a necessary local operation; atomisation is decomposition without provenance-preserving recomposition.
- **Provenance / relation:** `extracted` internal derivation; `supports` the runtime distinction and `qualifies` a moralised reading of division.
- **Recommended consumers:** [[20-s2-p1-dia-ballein|§2/#1 Dia-Ballein]]; [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|§2/#2 Sym-Ballein]]; [[03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|Sym-Ballein]]; [[14-computational-process-ontology|Computational Process Ontology]].
- **External work still needed:** a computer-science source for divide-and-conquer as a method; Greek etymological/lexical sources for the semantic claims about *dia-ballein* and *sym-ballein*.
