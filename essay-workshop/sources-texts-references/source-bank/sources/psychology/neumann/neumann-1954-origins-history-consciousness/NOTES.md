## Resonance map (candidates — the harmonisation pass weighs them, not this file)

### 1. Uroboros -> the torus made imaginal
The self-containing round, both parents in one, the whole before the cut. This is the torus's mythic face: the whole that stays whole by keeping its opening open. The uroboros bites its own tail -> the torus's hole is not another patch of surface -> the round that contains its own return. Our *hole/*whole* false cognate gets its exact topological force from this image.
- Locus: Part I ch. I (The Uroboros); ch. II (uroboros with phallus — the round carrying the father's mark).

### 2. World parents -> 0/1; the rotundum and the phallus; 1/0 as the Son's relation back
The separation of the world parents is the first differentiation: one round splits into Father Heaven / Mother Earth. Read in QL: the maternal round is the uncounted `0` (rotundum — the alchemical round, the undifferentiated whole), the father's upright manifestation is the `1` (phallus — the determinate one), and the slash IS the separation of the parents — a cut that still relates. `1/0` is the Son's relation back: the hero's return through the maternal ground he left (slaying of the mother -> descent -> the captive released). `0/1 = 1/0` as one relation read both ways: the parents are a relation, not a fixed hierarchy — the world parents ARE the parent relation.
- Locus: Part I ch. III (Separation of the World Parents); ch. IV–VI (birth/slayings); ch. VIII (liberation — the return).
- Essay-side: [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|Two Logics and Sym-Ballein]], quilt §2 · #19–20, [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/19-two-ones-mono-poly-matheme|Two Ones / Mono–Poly Matheme]].

### 3. Good/evil twins -> diaballein: "/" vs "-/+"
The archetype splits into good and evil figures — the two mothers, the two fathers, the divine and the demonic twin. This is the psychological form of our dia-ballein grammar: the pure `/` keeps the cut as relation (the twins still share one ground); the collapse into `(-1)+/-(+1)` turns the twins into enemy-gods — annihilation or arrogation, the satanic thread we treat as real (see chang notes p. 84). Neumann gives the mythematic body to the difference between polarity and duality.
- Locus: Part I ch. II (good/bad mother); ch. VI (the two fathers); the brothers/twins motifs throughout the hero cycle.
- Essay-side: quilt §2 · #20 (Dia-Ballein), [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|Two Logics]], the evil/satanic register per [[Chang Chung-yuan — Tao: A New Way of Thinking (2014)|Chang notes]] p. 84.

### 4. Stadial consciousness -> Gebser's series (phylogenetic recapitulation)
Neumann's mythic stages (uroboros -> Great Mother -> world parents -> hero -> transformation -> liberation) are the history of consciousness recapitulated in every ego. Gebser's structures (archaic -> magical -> mythical -> mental -> integral) are the parallel series. Read through and alongside: Neumann gives the psychic morphology, Gebser the structural diaphaneity; the hero's mental-rational achievement opens into the transpersonal exactly where Gebser's mental opens into the integral. Operational homology, not identity — and Gebser still needs his own house before any quotation.
- Locus: Part I whole; Part II (the historical stages of ego-consciousness).
- Essay-side: quilt §4 · #35 (Apollo Through Dionysus), §5→0 (Gebserian diaphaneity/mutation), [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/20-advent-zero-subject-integral-logic|Advent — Zero, Subject, Integral Logic]]; P5 – Gebser write-up in Epi Paper Write-ups.

### 5. The Great Mother -> the Tao as Mother
Neumann's maternal uroboros is the psyche's figure of the source that gives birth to all forms and never stops holding them: Tao Te Ching's Mother (ch. 1, "the mother of all things"; ch. 6, the valley spirit/mysterious female; ch. 25, "I do not know its name, I call it Tao... Mother of all things"; ch. 52, "having known the mother, know the child"). The Great Mother's two faces — nourishing and terrible — are the Tao's own doubleness: giving birth and devouring return. And our terminal Motherhood (candidate 08, the planetary return given a body) has its archetypal depth here.
- Locus: Part I ch. II (The Great Mother).
- Essay-side: [[Chang Chung-yuan — Tao: A New Way of Thinking (2014)]], quilt Tao layer §7, quilt §5→0 · #48 (Motherhood/AHI/Planetary Return).

### 6. Myth on a topological substrate -> Lacan + QL's core imputation
Neumann's whole method is that myth is the psyche's self-projection — the collective unconscious objectified as mythic image. That is the psychological half of our claim: what the psyche expresses as mythological content stands on a formal substrate. Lacan gives the other half in his topological mathemes (the torus, the Möbius, the cross-cap, the Borromean knot — the unconscious structured like a language, but also like a surface). QL's core imputation: the substrate is a topological formalism that ensures right relation — harmonia — and Neumann's mythic stages are its mythemic record. Neumann is the depth-psychological witness to the claim that Lacan formalised and QL derives.
- Locus: the work's governing method (Introduction; the stage-chapters as myth-morphology).
- Essay-side: [[Lacan — Talking to Brick Walls (Price, 2017)]] (topological mathemes), quilt §4 · #34 (Lacan/Matheme/Mytheme), [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/04-arche-topos-topology-music|Arche-Topos / Topology / Music]], [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/symbolon/matheme/topology/README|symbolon · matheme · topology]], [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/symbolon/mytheme/myth/README|symbolon · mytheme · myth]].
