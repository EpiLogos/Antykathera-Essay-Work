# Vikalpa

> [!summary]
> Differentiating conceptual articulation: the mind forms a determinate alternative by qualifying and distinguishing within an inherited field.

**Reference kind:** tradition. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0 · #2 — Vikalpa and saṃkalpa as world-making]] and [[Computational Process Ontology]].

## Operative meaning

The Sanskrit root `√kḷp` concerns ordering, fitting, preparing, and arranging. The prefix `vi-` carries differentiation, separation, or distribution. Etymology alone does not settle a technical doctrine, but it supports the use made here: *vikalpa* is the articulation by which a field is rendered as qualified alternatives, conceptual constructions, or this-rather-than-that determinations.

In the essay, vikalpa is not simply “false thought.” It is a necessary power of manifestation. Without differentiating articulation there is no object, proposition, plan, or context-world. Its pathology appears when the produced distinction forgets that it was produced and claims self-subsistence.

## QL and computational role

Vikalpa is the cognitive face of dia-ballein's necessary operation: distinguish, qualify, exclude, and select. It leaves a trace in the object-field and changes the alternatives available to later acts. A computational system exhibits the objective operation whenever latent relations are differentiated into candidates and one candidate becomes locally actionable. This does not settle whether the system possesses conscious conceptual experience.
