# Wittgenstein — Limit, Silence, and Forms of Life

> [!summary]
> Early Wittgenstein draws the limit of propositional saying and reaches silence; later Wittgenstein returns meaning to rule-governed practices and forms of life.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0/1 · #3 — The formal-limit genealogy: paradox, incompleteness, silence, creativity]].

## Early movement

The *Tractatus* seeks a perspicuous account of what propositions can picture. Its own propositions become a ladder to be discarded once the limit is seen. Ethics, value, and the mystical are not additional facts that the system forgot to list; they appear at the limit of factual saying.

## Later movement

The *Philosophical Investigations* relocates meaning in use, rule-following, language-games, and forms of life. No formal rule determines its own application independently of a living practice. The move does not provide QL's subject-pole, but it prevents language from being treated as a closed autonomous calculus.

## Argumentative use

- **Extracted:** conditions of meaningful saying cannot all be said as objects at the same level.
- **Argued:** Wittgenstein's two periods trace a Western trajectory from the formal boundary to the lived practice that makes formal sense possible.
- **Not licensed:** that silence proves mystical doctrine, or that later Wittgenstein simply repudiates rigour.

## Relations

Russell marks paradox with hierarchy; Gödel marks undecidability; Wittgenstein marks the unsayable and later the practice-ground of saying; Whitehead marks Creativity. QL treats these as non-equivalent views of a recurring limit.
