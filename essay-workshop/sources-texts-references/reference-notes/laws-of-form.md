# Laws of Form

> [!summary]
> Spencer-Brown begins from the injunction to draw a distinction and later permits the form to re-enter its own indicational space. QL uses the calculus as a formal neighbour of seam-retaining distinction, not as retrospective proof.

**Reference kind:** primary-text. **Evidence status:** source-synthesized from working materials; edition-specific verification pending.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0/1 · #3 — The formal-limit genealogy: paradox, incompleteness, silence, creativity]] and [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§3 · #2 — Mark, re-entry, iterant, and complex orientation]].

## Permitted claims

- A distinction and an indication arise together through the mark.
- The primary arithmetic distinguishes marked and unmarked states asymmetrically.
- Re-entry makes the value of a form depend upon itself; later interpretations resolve the pressure through oscillation/time or imaginary logical value.

## QL reading

The unmarked state is the field from which the mark is drawn, not a signed rival to the mark. This is structurally closer to `0/1` than to `(+1)/(-1)`. The reading is **Argued**. Spencer-Brown does not formulate QL, sym-ballein, or the tattvic relation.

## Verification warning

Chapter and appendix wording varies by edition. The working chat's claims concerning British Rail circuits, exact clock language, oscillator/memory examples, and metaphysical gestures must be checked before quotation.
