# Dimensional Reframing at Zero and Infinity

> [!summary]
> Mathematical obstructions are often made legible by enlarging, quotienting, compactifying, or otherwise changing their representational space. Zero and infinity repeatedly mark the pressure-point; they do not all perform one universal operation.

## Case ledger

- Signed line → [[Complex Plane]]: orthogonal rotation makes `i²=-1` representable.
- Affine line/plane → [[Projective Line]]: parallel directions acquire ideal meeting-points.
- Complex plane → [[Riemann Sphere]]: infinity becomes the compactifying point of `CP¹`.
- Covering plane → [[Surface Classification 4g+2g]]: quotient identifications generate periodic surfaces such as the torus.
- Infinite continuum → `∞/dx`: differential and limit procedures relate inexhaustible field to local change.
- Division by zero → [[Division Pluralisms]]: alternative containers can treat poles or points at infinity, but ordinary field arithmetic is changed rather than silently repaired.

## Status boundary

Each mathematical case is **Derived** only within its own definitions. The cross-case proposition—mathematics often preserves an obstructed relation by changing its space—is **Argued**. “Zero generates dimensionality” is too broad and should not appear without a named construction.
