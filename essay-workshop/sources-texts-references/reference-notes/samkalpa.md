# Saṃkalpa

> [!summary]
> Formative gathering and commitment: selected differences are composed into a possible world, intention, resolve, or course of action.

**Reference kind:** tradition. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0 · #2 — Vikalpa and saṃkalpa as world-making]] and [[Computational Process Ontology]].

## Operative meaning

The Sanskrit root `√kḷp` concerns ordering, fitting, preparing, and arranging; `sam-` gives the sense of togetherness, completeness, or convergence. Etymology is not a substitute for doctrinal history, but it makes the essay's distinction exact: where [[vikalpa]] articulates alternatives through differentiation, *saṃkalpa* gathers selected determinations into an intention, commitment, projection, or workable world.

Saṃkalpa is not fusion. A composition can remain internally differentiated and answerable to its materials. Its pathology begins when the constructed world forgets its exclusions and presents its chosen arrangement as the only possible reality.

## QL and computational role

Saṃkalpa is the world-forming face of sym-ballein: relation, recomposition, contextualisation, and directed commitment. In an artificial agent, prompts, memories, tools, policies, outputs, and environmental feedback compose a provisional context-world that conditions the next selection. The operation is objectively inspectable; whether it is accompanied by conscious resolve remains open.
