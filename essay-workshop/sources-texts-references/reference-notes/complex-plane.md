# Complex Plane

> [!summary]
> The complex plane represents `a+bi` by two real coordinates. Multiplication by `i` rotates orientation by a quarter-turn; `i²=-1` gives a half-turn.

## Essay use

The plane is the precise substrate for the move from signed opposition to orientation. [[Kauffman Iterants]] supplies one re-entry/iterant route to `i`; [[27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex|§3 · #2 — Mark, Re-entry, and Complex Orientation]] uses it without claiming that every recursion is complex multiplication.

## Claim boundary

The complex plane mathematically encodes orientation. Its connection to psychological or musical orientation remains an **Argued** cross-register mapping.
