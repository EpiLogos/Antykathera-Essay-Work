# Pros Hen Analogia

> [!summary]
> Aristotle's focal meaning: being is said in many ways (*pollachōs legomenon*) while its senses remain ordered toward one (*pros hen*) rather than collapsing into one univocal definition.

## Argumentative use

*Pros hen* analogia is the discipline by which QL coordinates polarities and knowledge registers. A polarity in psychology, topology, music, metaphysics, or agent architecture retains its local content and evidential rules while its relational form is oriented toward the focal `0/1` structure. This is neither mere resemblance nor identity: it is a relation of relations.

The method is therefore mono–poly. The poly is genuinely many; the mono is the focal relation through which the many can be read together. Dia-ballein appears when the registers are severed into unrelated territories. Fusion appears when analogy is inflated into identity. Sym-ballein holds proportional participation: faithful refraction without collapse.

## QL/MEF placement

L0.5 / P5 carries analogia as the return-question: after a determination has been made, why-for and in relation to which focal meaning does it hold? The QL positions function as transcendental attractors; MEF records the lens-specific route by which each register reaches, misses, or transforms those attractors.
