# Logos and Epi-Logos

> [!summary]
> Logos is the articulated field of distinctions made transmissible as word, number, image, model, method, institution, and code. Epi-Logos is that field bending back upon the conditions of its own articulation.

## QL placement

- `0`: non-objectifiable condition of articulation.
- `/`: differentiating and gathering activity.
- `1`: articulated determination.
- Logos: the relational field of determinations.
- Epi-Logos: `1→0` recognition of source, exclusion, limit, and provisionality, followed by renewed articulation.

Wittgenstein's limit remains active: the `1` cannot speak `0` without producing another `1`. Epi-Logos therefore does not capture the unsayable; it makes speaking answerable to the fact that it cannot.
