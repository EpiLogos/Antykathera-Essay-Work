# Re-entry

> [!summary]
> Re-entry is the point at which a distinction operates within the space it distinguished. Contradiction-pressure becomes oscillation, temporality, retention, or imaginary orientation rather than being removed through a hierarchy of types.

**Reference kind:** concept. **Evidence status:** source-synthesized; exact formal presentation and edition wording pending.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§3 · #2 — Mark, re-entry, iterant, and complex orientation]].

## Argument node

- **C1 — extracted:** a re-entering form cannot receive a stable ordinary Boolean value without additional structure.
- **C2 — extracted/edition-check:** time-dependent oscillation is one resolution of the re-entering form.
- **C3 — argued:** the chain `distinction → re-entry → oscillation → frequency → retention` provides a formal bridge from a seam-retaining mark to the temporality of a constructed world.
- **C4 — offered:** dia-ballein's `(+1)/(-1)` can be interpreted as the re-entrant `0/1` seam unrolled through the time it generated.

## Tension

Russell's type hierarchy and Spencer-Brown's re-entry are not simply wrong and right solutions. The former prevents paradox to preserve stable logical typing; the latter changes the descriptive resources so reflexivity can become dynamical. QL needs both: boundaries capable of decision and returns capable of revising the boundary.
