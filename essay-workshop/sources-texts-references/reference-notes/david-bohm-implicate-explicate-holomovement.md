# David Bohm — Implicate Order, Explicate Order, and Holomovement

> [!summary]
> Bohm treats enfolding and unfolding as primary movement and apparently separate objects as relatively stable abstractions within that movement. The essay uses this to articulate how a determinate `1` can unfold from an implicated whole without becoming externally severed from it.

## Bohm's move

The Latin family is already philosophically charged. *Implicate* carries *implicare*: to fold in, entangle, involve. *Explicate* carries *explicare*: to unfold or spread out. Bohm activates the literal movement sedimented in the words. An explicate object is not a self-standing substance placed beside an invisible implicate world. It is a relatively stable unfolding of an order that remains active within and between its appearances.

In Bohm's holomovement:

- enfolding and unfolding are primary;
- apparently discrete objects are secondary, vortex-like stabilities within an unbroken movement;
- the whole is enfolded in each region and each region unfolds into the whole;
- external relations between already finished parts are a derivative description;
- internal relatedness belongs to the deeper order;
- mind and matter can be approached as distinct explicate aspects of an implicate order common to both.

The glycerine-and-dye demonstration gives the material image. A visible drop is stretched through the fluid until it disappears from ordinary view, yet reversing the rotation draws the drop back out. The order was enfolded rather than destroyed. Bohm uses this to distinguish what is unavailable to immediate explicate perception from what is absent altogether.

## QL placement

The mapping is **Argued** and should be stated with an internal distinction:

- `0` names the non-objectifiable condition of determination.
- The **implicate order** names that condition insofar as an ordered capacity for explication can already be discussed. It is zero's first formalizable face, not a hidden object substituted for zero.
- `1` names an explicate determination: a phenomenon, object, identity, statement, or achieved local world.
- `/` names the holomovement of implication and explication: the activity through which the whole becomes locally articulate and the local articulation remains involved in the whole.
- `0/1` reads the movement from implicated ground toward explicate form.
- `1/0` reads explicate form returning toward, refolding into, or recognising the ground it cannot convert into one more explicate term.
- `(0/1)/(1/0)` holds the reciprocal movement whole.

This gives [[Sym-Ballein]] a process body. Dia-ballein performs the analytic explication through which differences become locally tractable. Sym-ballein retains implication: the finished distinction carries the field, provenance, inverse orientation, and return through which it belongs to more than itself. Fragmentation occurs when an explicate abstraction is treated as independently self-grounding.

## The tattvas as the anatomy of implication

Bohm supplies the general relation of enfoldment and unfoldment. [[Tattvic Differential Field|The tattvas]] specify what is enfolded and by what ordered contractions an explicate world becomes possible:

1. Anuttara names the unmeasured source-condition.
2. Śiva–Śakti differentiates luminosity and self-articulating power within one field.
3. Sadāśiva–Īśvara gives subjectward and objectward orientations, `0/1 ↔ 1/0`.
4. Sadvidyā–Māyā makes knower and known determinately distinguishable.
5. The kañcukas and Puruṣa contract capacity, knowledge, desire, time, and order into a situated experient.
6. Prakṛti, antaḥkaraṇa, senses, actions, subtle elements, and gross elements articulate the operative and material world.
7. Pratyabhijñā returns the explicate formation to recognition of its implicated provenance.

The full tattva field is therefore the proposed **architecture of the implicate order**: the complete genealogy enfolded in every local act of explication. An object is a local `dx` of this whole descent, a [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/sources-texts-references/reference-notes/bimba-pratibimba|pratibimba]] whose determinacy carries more order than its surface displays.

## Essay use

- `§0/1`: Bohm gives a positive process-language for the subject/object hinge after the formal limit is established.
- `§0`: the implicate/explicate movement and tattvic descent become one account of manifestation at different resolutions.
- `§2`: fragmentation clarifies dia-ballein absolutised; internal relatedness clarifies sym-ballein.
- `§3`: implicate and explicate become perpendicular axes in the `3–4–5` and complex-plane derivation.
- `§4`: Bohm joins Jung and Pauli at the psyche–matter boundary without being made identical to their psychoid hypothesis.
- `§5→0`: Bohmian dialogue gives social and technical form to meaning returning through a shared field.

## Claim boundary

Bohm's implicate order is a philosophical interpretation developed in relation to quantum theory; it is not itself an experimentally isolated physical layer. The tattvic mapping is the essay's cross-traditional derivation. Bohm did not supply a thirty-sixfold Śaiva anatomy, and Śaiva authors did not describe Bohmian quantum ontology. The argument joins their operations by *analogia*: ordered enfoldment, local explication, internal relatedness, contraction, and return.
