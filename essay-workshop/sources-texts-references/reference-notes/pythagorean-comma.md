# Pythagorean Comma

> [!summary]
> The non-closure between powers of 3/2 and octaves.

## Exact relation

Twelve pure fifths and seven octaves do not coincide:

`(3/2)^12 / 2^7 = 531441/524288 ≈ 1.013643`

This residual interval is the Pythagorean comma. It is a standard mathematical fact; a suitable musicological source is still required before historical quotation.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan]].

**Use rule:** employ the comma as an example of tuning non-closure, not as the primary derivation of QL. The cleaner central kernel remains [[9-8 Whole Tone]].
