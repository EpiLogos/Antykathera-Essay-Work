> [!summary]
> Mono–poly is the essay’s own language for wholeness differentiating through real plurality. Mono names the shared relational ground; poly names its many locally determinate expressions. ## The mono-poly relation

Reality is whole as relational condition before it is decomposed and recomposed by its processes. “Before” is structural rather than temporal: the whole is not a completed super-object preceding its parts. It is the field within which anything can become a part.

The poly is therefore not an aggregate of independently grounded pieces. Apoha reveals that each determination is empty of self-subsistence because its content depends upon exclusions, conditions, relations, and a field it did not create. The many are real precisely as differentiations of the one field. Mono without poly would have no expression; poly without mono would have no relation.

In QL terms, the local \(1\) remains answerable to \(0\) and to the slash through which it became determinate. The enemy of plurality is not unity. It is a local \(1\) claiming ownership of the shared slash: **monopoly**.

## Trust as the lived mono-poly

Knowledge is provisional form within this relation. It can function sym-ballein-wise as a distributed trust-anchor: a revisable mark supported by embodied competence, relationship, lineage, land, ritual, family, source, and institution. Or it can deny its provisionality and function dia-ballein-wise: the distrust it cannot hold is projected onto an enemy, scapegoat, false ground, or excluded object.

Trust is therefore not an optional moral addition to knowledge. It is the lived mono–poly relation which knowledge either acknowledges or conceals. Sever distributed anchors and trust does not disappear; it becomes available for capture by a single totalising object.

## Infrastructure and power

Every infrastructure distributes trust and dependence. Authority can accumulate upward while its material and epistemic conditions run downward through labour, communities, maintainers, data, energy, hardware, ecological systems, and inherited knowledge. The upper layer becomes monopolistic when it presents this dependence as self-grounding sovereignty.

QL supplies shared procedure; MEF keeps lens and context differences visible; Bimba records provisional local determinations; federation moves artifacts without claiming a view from nowhere. Mono becomes common grammar and commons. Poly remains local sovereignty, plurality of world-models, and the right of each context to contest and revise the shared image.
