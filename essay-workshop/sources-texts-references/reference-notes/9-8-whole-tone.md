# 9-8 Whole Tone

> [!summary]
> The whole-tone interval is the exact quotient between the perfect fifth and perfect fourth: `(3/2)/(4/3)=9/8`.

## Exact identities

`16/9=(4/3)²`

`(3/2)/(4/3)=9/8`

`(16/9)(9/8)=2/1`

Equivalently, `(4/3)(9/8)(4/3)=2/1`. These are ordinary rational identities and are **Derived**. QL’s reading of the whole tone as carried remainder is **Argued**.
