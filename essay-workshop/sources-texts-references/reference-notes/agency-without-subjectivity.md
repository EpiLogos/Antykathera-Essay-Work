# Agency Without Subjectivity

> [!summary]
> Agentworld separates functional agency and performed subjectivity from any settled theory of phenomenal subjecthood.

## Source anchor

[[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 13|Pages 13–15]] traces agency’s historical association with intentional subjectivity and argues that agentic AI destabilises that linkage. [[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 14|Page 14]] describes provisional individuation as a decomposable lamination.

## Essay use

The essay distinguishes consequential agency, [[Objective Internality]], operational self-modelling, and the unobjectifiable subject-pole. [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]] states the boundary; [[Prompt Thrownness]] gives disclosures a verification route.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan]].

**Use rule:** do not infer either consciousness or its absence from functional decomposition alone.
