# Apoha

> [!summary]
> Dignāga–Dharmakīrti exclusion theory: conceptual determination does not grasp a self-subsisting universal but functions through exclusion of what the term is not.

**Reference kind:** tradition. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0 · #3 — Apoha: determination through exclusion]] and [[Computational Process Ontology]].

## Operative meaning

*Apoha* means exclusion or removal. In Buddhist philosophy of language, its technical force is that a concept's applicability is constituted through exclusion rather than participation in an independently existing universal. The historical theories and later refinements are more intricate than the slogan “meaning by difference”; the essay uses only the load-bearing point that determination depends upon a field of non-application.

This makes apoha the epistemic law of the dia-operation. A term, class, or selected token becomes locally determinate through the alternatives it excludes. The excluded field does not become irrelevant: it remains the contrastive condition under which the selection means what it means.

## Computational bridge

Logits, softmax, sampling, and argmax provide a technical comparison. A token's selection depends on its relation to alternatives; changing the comparison field changes the local meaning and probability of the mark. The comparison is **Argued**, not an identity between Buddhist semantics and machine learning. Its value is operational: an inspectable agent should retain the chosen mark, the relevant excluded field, and the contextual conditions under which the contrast was formed.
