# Mathematical-Artistic Image Register

> [!summary]
> Each image carries one argumentative operation. No image is promoted because it looks profound; it enters only where its mathematical, historical, poetic, or technical movement performs work the prose needs.

## Rule of use

An image earns main-text space when the reader can say what changes in the argument by seeing it. The source's own level remains visible:

- a theorem or construction can carry a **Derived** mathematical claim;
- an artwork, mytheme, mystical formula, or engineered device can carry an **Argued** or **Offered** disclosure;
- no artistic resemblance inherits the authority of the mathematics it sits beside;
- no mathematical diagram is treated as exhaustive of the lived or metaphysical field it renders.

## The governing artistic relation

Mathematics and art meet here because both can preserve an invariant through transformation. Mathematics proves which relation survives a change of coordinates, scale, orientation, quotient, or phase. Art makes the survival available to perception and memory. Poetry presses a statement until more than one determination remains alive within the line. Mystical paradox brings predication to its apophatic edge. QL requires all four while retaining their different burdens of proof.

The test is Bohm's phrase: does the image **run true**? A mathematical-artistic figure belongs when its transformations remain faithful to the relation the essay is deriving. Visual excitement without such fidelity is decoration.
