## Section wager

Differentiation structurally retains a zero, and mathematics supplies exact forms through which absence becomes operative: placeholder, numeral, arithmetic element, empty set, origin, and exceptional boundary. Once zero enters a positional system, the whole field of number changes; once `0` is taken as `∅`, one and the natural numbers can be generated from its reiterated marking.

**Present burden:** establish the history precisely enough to carry the philosophical argument, then derive `0/1` as the minimal generative relation before presenting the two ways that relation can be accounted.

### #4 · Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics

**Incoming pressure:** [§1 #3 · The Crossed Zero](../../nodes/sections/16-s1-p3-crossed-zero.md)

**Earned position (Argued):** Division by zero is the point at which a formal system must disclose itself. In an ordinary field, \(1/0\) is undefined because no element multiplied by zero returns one.

**Why this move:** This is why zero keeps one foot “outside mathematics.” Zero is entirely mathematical, but the return of a finite determination to zero reaches the edge at which the current mathematical world must refuse the operation or transform its own space.

**Carry-forward:** “Outside” means frame-disclosing, not supernatural.
