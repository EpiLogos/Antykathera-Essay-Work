# §1 — Reading Route

Kaplan supplies the narrative spine of this section because he lets zero be learned as a sequence of changed permissions. The source house retains the full historical route, page map, mathematical workbenches, exercises and provenance. This file gives the order in which that material becomes the six-movement argument.

## 1. Learn the book’s route before extracting from it

Open [Kaplan’s source house](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/SOURCE.md#why-this-book-is-load-bearing), then read its [historical spine](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/SOURCE.md#historical-reading-spine) from the Babylonian place-holder through Greek astronomical notation, Indian arithmetic, European reception and the late mathematical return. Keep the printed/PDF locator map beside the text. The point is not that one invariant “nothing” travels intact. A blank, place-holder, numeral, arithmetic element, empty set and boundary operation acquire different offices in different practices.

That sequence carries [#0 — A Sign Migrates Between Worlds](../../nodes/sections/13-s1-p0-sign-migrates.md). Use [Rotman’s source house](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/rotman/rotman-1987-signifying-nothing/SOURCE.md) when the paragraph turns from a history of marks to the semiotic change made by placing absence inside inscription.

## 2. Work the Indian arithmetic rather than summarising it

At [#1 — Śūnya Becomes Operational](../../nodes/sections/14-s1-p1-sunya-operational.md), move from Kaplan’s Indian chapters into [Colebrooke’s Brahmagupta and Bhāskara carrier](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/SOURCE.md#passages). Put the additive identity, multiplicative absorber and zero-denominator rule on paper. Then open [Dutta’s reconstruction](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/dutta/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india/SOURCE.md) for the changed cancellation regime and the specialist correction to Kaplan’s cultural compression. Kaplan provides the scene; the primary carrier and later scholarship decide what historical and mathematical claims the essay can attribute.

## 3. Build one from zero

Read [#2 — The Empty Set Generates One](../../nodes/sections/15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one.md) as a workbench. Write the first four von Neumann ordinals and distinguish the empty set from its singleton. The result used here is exact and local: `∅` and recursive enclosure generate the natural-number sequence. The movement does not identify formal emptiness with every philosophical or experiential zero; it gives the essay a rigorous floor on which `0/1` can be read as ground, mark and retained relation.

## 4. Test the boundary operations

Return to Kaplan at the source house’s [mathematical workbench](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/SOURCE.md#mathematical-workbench). Work the ordinary-field cancellation argument for division by zero, then the mediant construction joining `0/1` and `1/0`, and finally the NOR/NAND distinction. Each example exposes a different rule-space. This is the material field for [#3 — The Crossed Zero](../../nodes/sections/16-s1-p3-crossed-zero.md) and [#4 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics](../../nodes/sections/17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math.md): a boundary operation forces the containing system either to refuse the expression or to declare a transformed space in which it can be handled.

## 5. Return the loan

Finish with Kaplan’s [passage and provenance ledger](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/SOURCE.md#passage-and-provenance-ledger) and [return-to-the-essay map](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/SOURCE.md#return-to-the-essay). No Kaplan passage is yet quotation-ready; the book is presently usable for private learning, page-located paraphrase under its citation state, and exact acquisition planning. [#5→0 — The Loan Returns](../../nodes/sections/18-s1-p5-loan-returns.md) then brings the historical and formal zero back to the opening question with more determinate force.

Return to [§1 in the master manuscript](../../THE-RETURN-OF-ZERO.md#section-s1-return-of-zero) at the movement you have just worked. The question for a paragraph is no longer “what does Kaplan say about zero?” but which historical scene, operation, locator and qualification make this particular transition possible.
