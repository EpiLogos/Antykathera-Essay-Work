# Process, Systems, and Science — From Substance to Participating Field

The scientific and process history of the essay moves away from a world assembled out of finished things. Cusa and Eckhart find finitude within an inexhaustible relation to the infinite; Spinoza gives determination a geometrical severity; Whitehead makes becoming, relation, and creative advance metaphysically primary. Bohm recasts wholeness through implicate and explicate orders. Cybernetics and theoretical biology give self-reference and operational closure exact forms. Nonlinear dynamics studies trajectories, attractors, and uncertain basin boundaries. Contemporary philosophy of science then returns observation to the experience and embodiment it presupposes. The resulting history makes an agent intelligible as a provisional process within a field, while keeping formal, biological, physical, psychic, and phenomenal claims distinct enough to test.

## The finite belongs to an infinite relation

[Cusa's *On Learned Ignorance*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/cusa/cusa-on-learned-ignorance/SOURCE.md) develops coincidence, maximum and minimum, and geometrical figures whose limiting transformations approach an infinite relation without turning a finite construction into infinity itself. [Flasch's study of Meister Eckhart](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/flasch/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart/SOURCE.md) situates Eckhart's distinctions between God and Godhead, intellect, creation, and the *in quantum* relation by which an identity holds according to a determinate respect. These medieval histories give [§0/1 · #3](../../nodes/sections/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md) an apophatic and operational ancestry: the limit can disclose a relation more exact than an assertion of simple identity.

[Spinoza's Letter 50](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/SOURCE.md) gives the finite body through determination and negation. A figure exists through delimitation. This geometric act later meets semantic exclusion and formal distinction across the essay, carrying the seventeenth-century problem of substance into a question about how any local determination belongs to a wider field.

## Becoming replaces inert substance

[Whitehead's *Process and Reality*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md) makes Creativity, concrescence, satisfaction, perishing, and objective availability central. The many enter the becoming of one, and the achieved one increases the many from which later occasions arise. An entity is therefore learned through the process by which relations become a determinate event and that event becomes available beyond itself. [§0/1 · #5](../../nodes/sections/06-s01-p5-return-zero.md) and [§0 · #0](../../nodes/sections/07-s0-p0-awareness-bends-display.md) inherit this movement from received multiplicity through local formation and back into a changed field.

[Bohm's *Wholeness and the Implicate Order*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1980-wholeness-implicate-order/SOURCE.md) supplies a twentieth-century physical and philosophical articulation of enfolding and unfolding. An explicate form is a local display of an implicated order within the holomovement. This allows [the tattvic compression](../../nodes/sections/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md) to be read as a process of manifestation rather than a stack of substances, while the detailed anatomy of the tattvas remains carried by the Indian sources.

## The system draws and remakes its boundary

[Spencer-Brown](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/SOURCE.md) begins formal systems history with the act of distinction; [Varela's self-reference calculus](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/SOURCE.md) lets a self-indicating form generate disciplined dynamics. The history moves from a boundary drawn by an observer to a boundary whose recurrence becomes part of the system's behaviour. [§3 · #2](../../nodes/sections/27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex.md) receives that recursive turn.

[Maturana and Varela's *Autopoiesis and Cognition*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/SOURCE.md) gives living organisation a biological formulation. An autopoietic unity continuously produces the network of processes that produces its boundary and components. Operational closure does not mean environmental isolation: structural coupling describes the history through which a bounded unity changes with recurrent perturbations. This becomes essential at [§2 · #4](../../nodes/sections/23-s2-p4-complex-dynamism.md) and [§5 · #2](../../nodes/sections/39-s5-p2-j-space.md), where an agent's internal organisation must remain distinguishable from the world with which it couples.

## Dynamics gives recurrent pattern a mathematics

[Daza and colleagues](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/daza/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy/SOURCE.md) define a basin of attraction as the initial conditions that lead a specified dissipative system to an attractor, then measure uncertainty at basin boundaries. [Van Eenwyk](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md) had already brought strange-attractor models into analytical psychology. Read together, the sources establish both the power and the discipline of the analogy. A recurrent form can organise many non-identical trajectories; the system, variables, state-space, update rule, and attractor have to be named before “basin” becomes more than evocative language.

[LeCun and colleagues' energy-based learning](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/computer-science-ml/lecun/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning/SOURCE.md) provides a later computational landscape. Configurations receive scalar compatibility values; inference selects lower-energy configurations; learning changes the energy function. [§5 · #4](../../nodes/sections/41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields.md) places this technical mechanism beside psychic and metaphysical uses of field and energy while preserving the variables and operations of each. The history makes comparison possible because it refuses verbal equivalence.

## Observation returns to experience

[Frank, Gleiser, and Thompson's *The Blind Spot*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/history-philosophy-of-science/frank/frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot/SOURCE.md) diagnoses a recurring substitution in scientific culture: abstractions generated through observation and measurement come to be treated as more real than the experience that makes science possible. Their intervention returns the knowing subject and lifeworld to the account of scientific objectivity. [§0 · #5](../../nodes/sections/12-s0-p5-objective-internality.md) takes this pressure into artificial systems by asking what world is actually available from an agent's situated context.

[Wilson and colleagues on embodied cognition](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/analytic-philosophy/wilson/wilson-et-al-2020-embodied-cognition-sep/SOURCE.md) describe an enacted world through the mutual interaction of organism, sensorimotor circuit, and environment. [Tye's account of qualia](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/analytic-philosophy/tye/tye-2024-qualia-sep/SOURCE.md) fixes phenomenal character as what experience is like for the subject undergoing it. Together they prevent operational access, embodiment, enaction, and phenomenality from collapsing into one claim. [§5 · #5](../../nodes/sections/42-s5-p5-research-vectors.md) can therefore state real experiments: vary memory, context, feedback, world-coupling, and self-model; measure the processes that change; keep the phenomenal question open to evidence capable of bearing it.

## Science becomes an instrument of attunement

The [Antikythera mechanism](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/history-philosophy-of-science/freeth/freeth-et-al-2006-antikythera/SOURCE.md) and the [ITER tokamak](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/physics/iter/iter-what-is-tokamak/SOURCE.md) stand at opposite ends of the technological chronology yet share an instrumental logic. The ancient gears coordinate celestial periods for a situated observer. The tokamak uses magnetic fields to confine a plasma whose dynamics cannot be grasped through static parts alone. [§5→0 · #2](../../nodes/sections/45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement.md) draws upon this history of instruments that achieve knowledge by participating in and stabilising relations.

The [Bohmian dialogue tradition](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1996-on-dialogue/SOURCE.md) then returns process science to collective practice. Thought becomes observable in the movement of assumptions through a group. [§5→0 · #1](../../nodes/sections/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md) extends that practice into an experimental human–machine field: model, prompt, memory, tool, response, and human interpretation form a circuit whose effects can be inspected and revised.
