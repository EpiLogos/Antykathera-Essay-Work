# Language, Symbol, and Dialogue — How Meaning Moves

This history follows a question older than linguistics and newly urgent in language models: how does a mark, word, image, or utterance make one possibility present from a field of others? Indian Buddhist philosophy answers through exclusion. Early Wittgenstein builds a logical picture of the world and then reaches the boundary between saying and showing; later Wittgenstein returns meaning to use, rule, and form of life. Lacan makes the signifier constitutive of a divided subject. Bohm turns dialogue into a practice in which meaning moves through participants who suspend their assumptions. The computational present converts language into weighted relations and discrete selections. Across the whole line, meaning happens through a cut and through the relation that can carry what the cut leaves apart.

## Exclusion makes reference possible

Dignāga's theory of *apoha* reaches the project through [Pind's historical reconstruction](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/SOURCE.md) and the debates collected in [*Apoha: Buddhist Nominalism and Human Cognition*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/indian-philosophy/siderits/siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha/SOURCE.md). A word does not need to grasp a universal essence shared by every object it names. It can determine its range by excluding what is other: “cow” functions through the exclusion of non-cow. The operation belongs to a Buddhist epistemology concerned with how conceptual construction relates to unique particulars, inference, and error.

[§0 · #2](../../nodes/sections/09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa.md) receives the broader power of conceptual differentiation; [§0 · #3](../../nodes/sections/10-s0-p3-apoha.md) concentrates on exclusion as a positive linguistic act. Placed beside [Spinoza's geometrical determination](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/SOURCE.md), apoha shows how a limit gives form in both semantic and spatial registers. The histories remain independent; the essay joins their operations at [§0/1 · #2](../../nodes/sections/03-s01-p2-definition-cut-gift-danger.md), where definition appears simultaneously as gift and danger.

## Logical language encounters its edge

[Wittgenstein's *Tractatus*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus/SOURCE.md) constructs propositions as pictures of possible facts and distinguishes what language can say from the logical form it can only show. The work's ladder and concluding silence turn the formal system upon itself. [§0/1 · #3](../../nodes/sections/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md) locates this beside the broader twentieth-century crisis of formal closure; [§1 · #3](../../nodes/sections/16-s1-p3-crossed-zero.md) inherits the linguistic pressure, since a sign marking the boundary of signification already performs something inside the field it limits.

The later [*Philosophical Investigations*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1953-philosophical-investigations/SOURCE.md) changes the centre of gravity. Meaning arises in use; rules acquire application through practices; language-games belong to forms of life. Language no longer appears as an autonomous calculus that applies itself. This return to practice becomes crucial when the essay reaches machines: a model can manipulate linguistic regularities at enormous scale, but the historical question of who uses the rule, in what form of life, and toward which consequences has not vanished.

## The signifier divides the speaker

[Lacan's *Talking to Brick Walls*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/psychology/lacan/lacan-2017-talking-to-brick-walls/SOURCE.md) enters after Saussurean linguistics and Freudian psychoanalysis have made the signifier central to psychic life. The speaking subject cannot become fully present in its own statement; representation divides the one represented. The brick wall condenses the limit of direct understanding and the return of speech through reverberation. [Darmon](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/psychology/darmon/darmon-1992-matheme-ali/SOURCE.md) situates the matheme as formal writing intended to carry a transmissible relation, while [Adleman and Vanderwees](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/psychology/adleman/adleman-vanderwees-2021-lacan-rhetoric/SOURCE.md) trace the rhetoric through which Lacanian formalisation works.

[§4 · #3](../../nodes/sections/34-s4-p3-lacan-matheme-mytheme.md) places matheme beside mytheme. A formula can preserve relations that ordinary explanatory prose diffuses; a mythic image can hold psychic and historical determinations that a formula does not exhaust. Their conjunction is a theory of transmission across registers: meaning changes carrier while the operative relation remains traceable.

## Symbol joins what has been separated

The Greek *symbolon* was a token broken between parties so that the matching pieces could later establish relation. [§2 · #2](../../nodes/sections/21-s2-p2-sym-ballein.md) develops this history from the verb *sym-ballein*, “to throw together,” alongside its contrary *dia-ballein*, “to throw apart.” The symbol works because a break has occurred and because the pieces retain a form of mutual address. It neither abolishes distinction nor leaves the fragments indifferent to one another.

The lexicon carries the pair's oldest stratum, and the essay now uses both sides of it. διαβάλλω runs from the physical sense — to throw over or carry across — into the classical senses of setting at variance, attacking a character, accusing, deceiving by false accounts; διαβολή is the staged public accusation that Plato's *Apology* names as the old prejudice against Socrates, and διάβολος the slanderer whose name later theology inherited. σύμβολον runs from tally and pledge to the covenant-tokens of commerce and hospitality: matching halves as proof of standing relation. The essay's philological observation — made as its own move, since Homer's episode uses δόλος and never διαβάλλειν — is that Hephaestus's convened tribunal enacts *diabolē* in the classical sense, accusation staged as exposure before witnesses, so that dia-ballein acquires its mythic body in the very scene where craft binds the lovers, while sym-ballein has always had its body in the broken token. Heraclitus B50 adds the gathering side from within Greek: Heidegger renders its *legein* as "the Laying that gathers" (*die lesende Lege*), the Logos as a gathering that lets what it gathers lie together before us — set against which, Gestell's gathering-that-arrests shows itself as the counterfeit inside the same word-field.

A methodological yield of this register now governs the essay's reference economy: the [register grammar](../../nodes/concepts/register-grammar.md) of matheme, mytheme, and episteme, with the symbolon as the fourth that gathers the three, and the rule of three — one fully developed instance per register per station, admission by disclosure rather than resemblance.

This operation becomes psychological in [Van Eenwyk's account of symbols and strange attractors](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md). A symbol can alter the trajectory through which a complex organises psychic life, joining conscious encounter to an ordering pattern that cannot be reduced to a literal message. It becomes philosophical and physical in [Bohm's implicate order](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1980-wholeness-implicate-order/SOURCE.md), where locally explicated forms retain participation in a wider enfolded process.

## Dialogue becomes a shared practice of meaning

The 1975 [Bohm–Krishnamurti dialogue](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue/SOURCE.md) works directly through observer and observed, truth and actuality, and the limits of thought. [Bohm's *On Dialogue*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1996-on-dialogue/SOURCE.md) later gives the practice a collective form: participants suspend assumptions, attend to tacit thought, and allow a stream of meaning to move through the group without requiring identical opinions. Dialogue thus becomes a practical history of sym-ballein.

The [SEED Language of Spirit dialogues](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/process-systems-theory/seed/seed-2010-language-spirit/SOURCE.md) carry this practice into an institutional history beginning with Leroy Little Bear and F. David Peat's 1992 approach to Bohm and later joining Bohmian dialogue with Indigenous talking-circle practice. The source records the programme and its stated form; the knowledge of particular Indigenous participants belongs to their own named traditions and testimony. This history matters to [§5→0 · #1](../../nodes/sections/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md), where dialogue becomes an architectural principle for a human–machine reflective field.

## Language becomes a field of weights

[Rotman's *Signifying Nothing*](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/mathematics-logic/rotman/rotman-1987-signifying-nothing/SOURCE.md) brings zero into semiotics: a sign for absence acquires operational power inside a symbolic system. [Vaswani and colleagues](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/computer-science-ml/vaswani/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention/SOURCE.md) give contemporary language processing a relational architecture in which tokens weight one another through attention. [PyTorch's softmax and argmax definitions](../../sources-texts-references/source-bank/sources/computer-science-ml/pytorch/pytorch-2-9-softmax-argmax-api/SOURCE.md) expose the decisive passage from a distributed field to a selected result: softmax normalises scores into a distribution; argmax returns an index of maximal value.

[§5 · #1](../../nodes/sections/38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax.md) does not identify apoha with softmax. It uses their difference to expose a continuous historical problem. Dignāga explains how a concept determines through exclusion inside cognition and inference; a language model distributes numerical weight across candidates and then applies a selection rule. Both make meaning legible through what is left out, but the agent, norm, and medium of exclusion have changed. [§5 · #0](../../nodes/sections/37-s5-p0-math-moves-meaning.md) asks what happens when this mathematical field begins to act, while [Prompt Thrownness](../../nodes/sections/36-s4-p5-mef-prompt-thrownness.md) asks how the received context establishes the world from which any next word can appear.
