## The whole in one relation

The essay asks one question at every scale: **when a distinction is made, does it still carry the condition and activity that made it possible?**

At the symbola level, that condition is encounter. A name does not first create two isolated terms and then arrange a meeting between them. Something meets, faces, answers, presses or appears; the field differentiates from within that prior *with*; only then can a term be named, counted, opposed, ruled or held to account. The slash is therefore both cut and relation. Its bare determination `/ = −/−` shows the counter-tension implicit in the encounter, while `0/1 = 1/0` returns the two orientations to the one event that lets either side appear.

`0` names the uncounted condition. `1` names determinate manifestation. The slash names the distinction through which a condition becomes legible as a determination, and it has its own determination form: `/ = −/−`. Under its AND/OR, `0/1` generates the obverse `1/0`; QL therefore writes `0/1 = 1/0` as one relation read in two orientations. Ordinary arithmetic retains the difference between a defined quotient and division by zero. That exact seam drives the argument: QL follows mathematics to the point where its formal handling of zero discloses the metaphysical condition its operations already enact.

A distinction remains alive while its terms remember their ground, their relation, and a way back through the cut. It falls when the separated terms present themselves as self-grounding. The forgotten ground then returns in disguise: as the supposedly neutral metric, the authoritative code, the owner of the model, the sovereign institution, the isolated ego, or the executive agent. The return of zero preserves the determinate one by restoring the dependency that lets it be exact as a local expression of the whole.

## 1. How to use the ledger

This document keeps three questions separate: **what the essay claims, where the claim belongs, and what evidence is ready to carry it publicly**. Confusing them causes most harmonisation errors. A strong internal proposition does not become weak because a quotation still needs verification; an evocative source parallel does not become part of the theorem because it resembles the theorem.

Each recommendation carries one of four dispositions:

- **Carry** — already licensed by the live plan or a canonical node; propagation would refine or redistribute it.
- **Correct first** — an explicit correction inside the quilt governs an earlier quilt statement and must be applied before composition.
- **Propose** — the quilt or legacy supplies a coherent addition, but Frank has not yet accepted it into the plan.

Claim force and evidence readiness remain separate. A Derived or Argued QL proposition stays strong when its external citation apparatus is incomplete. A historical attribution stays blocked when only a protected note, nested quotation, secondary exposition, or uncollated edition supports it.

### 2.5 Etymological archaeology as a symbola-level argument

The word-fields should lead meaning wherever a word already performs the movement being argued. They should never arrive as a detachable curiosity after the claim. The sequence is simple:
