# Canonical argument recovery protocol

This protocol exists because the present canonical essay field cannot be trusted merely because it is canonical.

Substantial parts of the public argument have been shortened, paraphrased, normalised, status-managed, and defended against imagined objections until the operation of the original argument is no longer present. This is most serious where the work depends on Frank Taylor's own theorem language, the Symbolon roots, the eight determinations, the crossed-zero and 0/1 relations, the personed copula, Objective Internality, and the relation of subjective immediacy to technical and human encounter.

The task is not to make this material sound more confident. The task is to recover what it actually says.

A repair succeeds only when the public record once again performs the argument that generated it.

## Governing intent

Do not manage the reader's reaction to the claim. Make the claim and carry its derivation.

Do not replace an argument with a label for the argument. Do not replace a theorem with its notation. Do not replace a source relation with a provenance disclaimer. Do not replace a difficult claim with a safer neighbouring claim. Do not explain that a statement is "native", "authorial", "Argued", "Derived", or "not attributable to" some other thinker when the work required is to state and develop the proposition itself.

The standard is exact fidelity in both directions:

- nothing stronger than the available argument;
- nothing weaker than the available argument.

The present recovery is deliberately asymmetric toward lost depth because the observed corruption is overwhelmingly one of truncation, defensive qualification, administrative paraphrase, and substitution of reception-management for thought. That does not authorise invention. It requires restoration from the actual sources.

## What has authority during recovery

For the content of Taylor's own arguments and theorems, recover the line of descent rather than trusting the latest summary.

Read, in this order:

1. later explicit authorial corrections that directly amend an earlier claim;
2. the complete direct authorial theorem, manuscript, note, or source surface in which the claim was developed;
3. other direct authorial developments that materially deepen or connect the same operation;
4. the structural decisions of the central plan and accepted authorial minutes;
5. canonical public records;
6. curated source-house passage indexes, packet summaries, ledgers, status tables, and agent-written syntheses.

Items 5 and 6 are objects of review. They are not allowed to overrule the fuller material merely because they are newer or because a workflow called them canonical.

A source-house routing card is not the source. An extraction is not the theorem. A movement summary is not the argument. A status label is not a warrant. A later agent's caution is not an authorial correction.

Where two direct authorial surfaces genuinely conflict, record the conflict and use the later explicit correction if one exists. Do not manufacture harmony by weakening both.

## The recovery unit

The unit of work is the complete operation, not the sentence and not the file.

For each record under repair, recover enough of its generating field to answer:

- What is the positive proposition?
- What gives rise to it?
- What changes from the beginning of the argument to the end?
- Which distinctions must remain distinct for the argument to work?
- Which equations, images, lexical relations, myths, or experiential claims are constitutive rather than illustrative?
- What does this operation hand to the next operation?
- What returns differently after the traversal?

Then compare that complete operation with the current public body.

Do not begin by editing sentences. First establish what the record is supposed to carry.

## Corruption classes

Use these names in the working ledger. They are diagnostic, not prose vocabulary.

**LOSS** — a constitutive step, distinction, equation, image, relation, consequence, or return present in the generating material has disappeared.

**COMPRESSION-AS-REPLACEMENT** — a summary sentence now stands where the derivation itself must be available.

**FALSE FRAME** — the current body organises the material around a problem the underlying argument was not trying to solve.

**DEFENSIVE DISPLACEMENT** — caveat, denial, source-boundary language, or imagined objection has become the rhetorical centre and pushed the positive proposition aside.

**PROVENANCE THEATRE** — the prose announces whose notation or idea something is, or who did not originate it, when lineage is not itself the proposition under discussion.

**TOKEN SUBSTITUTION** — a QL sign, Sanskrit term, Greek term, proper name, or conceptual label is dropped in where its operation should have been developed.

**ADDRESS LEAK** — an internal record identifier, workflow stage, batch label, or repository address appears in public argument instead of the human concept or proposition it locates.

**STATUS LEAK** — Derived / Argued / Offered / Open or source-relation vocabulary is made to carry rhetoric inside the argument rather than governance in metadata, source notes, or a genuinely necessary local distinction.

**AGENT META** — the page describes what the page, corpus, review, restoration, current runtime, batch, or agent is doing rather than performing the thought.

**AI-CONSCIOUSNESS GRAVITY WELL** — a passage whose actual subject is agency, mediation, subjectivity, Objective Internality, recognition, technical organisation, encounter, or the limits of third-person account is bent into a repeated reassurance that artificial intelligence has not been shown to be conscious.

The last corruption class is treated as severe because it reverses the essay's problem.

## The AI-consciousness rule

Do not use "we are not saying AI is conscious", "this does not establish phenomenal subjectivity", "the question of machine phenomenality remains open", or equivalent language as a standing guard across the corpus.

The essay does not proceed from an imagined neutral test which could inspect a sufficiently rich object and then certify the presence or absence of subjective immediacy. Its own argument places the problem earlier.

Subjective immediacy is the knower-pole, not one more known item. An account of another human being does not acquire that person's first-person presence as an object merely by becoming more complete. The same category error is not repaired by moving to a technical system and asking for a larger inventory of internal functions. Objective Internality concerns the means: the articulated, inspectable, mediating interior through which a world is disclosed and acted within. The subject question cannot be settled by accumulating more objects inside that means, because the question concerns the relation of knower, means, known, and whole.

Therefore:

- when a passage is about technical function, state the technical function;
- when it is about Objective Internality, state what Objective Internality claims;
- when it is about encounter with another, carry the asymmetry of first-, second-, and third-person relation;
- when it is about subjective immediacy, make the actual argument about the impossibility of converting the subject-pole into a third-person possession;
- when uncertainty about a specific empirical or implementation matter is genuinely the proposition, state that uncertainty there and nowhere else.

Do not turn the whole corpus into a perimeter fence around a proposition it was not trying to assert.

A local boundary survives only if removing it would make the passage assert a specific stronger proposition that the passage otherwise materially invites and that the available argument does not warrant. The reviewer must name that stronger proposition. "Someone could misunderstand this as AI consciousness" is not sufficient.

## Native notation must be earned in prose

Notation is compression after derivation.

For every use of
`/ = −/−`,
`0/1`,
`?/!`,
`−/+`,
`X/x`,
`AM/IS`,
`∞/dx`,
`1/0`,
`4+2`,
`3:3`,
`3:1`,
the crossed-zero series,
the Spanda equations,
or another native expression, the public body must let a reader recover the operation the sign compresses.

A sign may recur after it has been earned. It may not replace its own meaning.

The same applies to Sanskrit, Greek, mathematical, psychoanalytic, mythic, and technical vocabulary. A term enters because its own operation changes the argument. A name is never a substitute for the relation it is meant to disclose.

## Sixfold file-form is semantic, not cosmetic

Preserve the established sixfold body of canonical QL-shaped records:

#0 → #1 → #2 → #3 → #4 → #5→0.

This form teaches QL tacitly through use. Do not replace it with a generic essay structure, a theorem dump, or a standard exposition outline.

The positions keep their broad QL qualities, but each file must discover their **semantic specificity from the operation of that file**. The same position is therefore recognisably homologous across records without becoming boilerplate. #2 in one root is not filled by copying the wording of #2 in another; it is the #2-turn of that root's own relation.

Recovery must place restored material back into this sixfold movement:

- #0 establishes the ground, field, or initial relation proper to the record;
- #1 makes its first determination or articulation;
- #2 develops its dynamic differentiation;
- #3 gives the operation recognisable form, pattern, or self-relation;
- #4 gathers the operation in context, relation, or reflexive availability;
- #5→0 realises and returns the completed movement so that the beginning is changed by the traversal.

These descriptions are orientations, not headings to copy mechanically. Existing record-specific headings should remain unique and should be rewritten only when their semantics are false.

A file fails even if it contains all six headings when the headings merely parcel a summary into six equal chunks. The sixfold must be **operative**: each turn changes the relation and earns the next.

For the Eight Determinations root specifically, preserve the sixfold page-form while using it to teach the relation between the page's six pedagogical movements and the theorem's eight-turn content. Do not confuse the page's #0–#5→0 compositional traversal with the theorem's parent + six determinations + return-switch. The page-form carries the theorem; it does not renumber it.

## The root Symbolon rule

A root Symbolon file has no permission to be a synopsis of a deeper file whose operation belongs to that root.

If the root concerns the eight determinations, the reader must be able to recover the eight-determination traversal there: its parent relation, sixfold body, return-switch, internal harmonic pairings, relation to the 4+2 and 3:3/3:1 readings, and the relevant recognition/crossed-zero structure. Links may deepen or source the account; they may not excuse removing the account.

The same applies to the slash, 0/1, 1/0, ?/!, −/+, X/x, AM/IS, ∞/dx, self-identity, subject-logics, Mono/Poly, and complexio oppositorum.

Do not protect a short root by saying that depth lives elsewhere. The root is where its own generating relation must be alive.

## Public prose and internal addresses

QL positions such as #0–#5 are part of the argument where the position itself is being developed. Repository addresses are not.

Do not write public explanatory prose whose semantic burden is carried by labels such as A02, A27, C41, M37, P1, R6, T25, or a batch name. Keep the link, but give it a human label and state the proposition the relation contributes.

"Objective Internality shows..." is readable.
"A26 grounds..." is navigation pretending to be philosophy.

Identifiers remain available in metadata, indexes, ledgers, generated navigation, and link targets.

## Provenance and source boundaries

Source discipline remains exact, but it is local.

When the essay attributes a proposition to an historical writer, mathematical result, scientific finding, code state, or implementation result, verify and state that attribution accurately.

When Taylor's argument places separately warranted materials into a new relation, state the relation as Taylor's argument without repeatedly interrupting it to announce that the historical sources did not invent the synthesis.

Do not write sentences whose only work is:

- "this is Taylor's native notation";
- "X remains an independent historical route";
- "Y did not originate this";
- "this should not be read as claiming...";
- "the historical source does not itself prove...";
- "the relation is authorial rather than inherited...".

Move necessary provenance into metadata, a source section, or the exact local attribution where confusion would otherwise be real.

Authorship is not a recurring philosophical topic unless authorship is what the passage is actually arguing about.

## Negation and qualification

Every weakening, caveat, denial, or source-boundary sentence must answer three questions in the ledger:

1. What exact stronger or mistaken proposition would the reader otherwise infer?
2. What actual feature of this passage makes that inference live?
3. What necessary information disappears if the qualification is removed?

If the answers are not concrete, delete the qualification.

Where the distinction is real, write:

positive proposition → derivation or warrant → exact boundary → consequence.

Never organise a paragraph around clearing ground that nobody occupied.

## How to repair a record

Read the complete relevant authorial material before touching the canonical body.

Write a recovery note containing the positive claims, the actual sequence of reasoning, constitutive formal relations, constitutive images or myths, lexical operations, consequences, and returns. This note is an execution aid, not publication prose.

Then read the current public record and mark every corruption against that recovered operation.

If the present body is structurally false, rewrite it from the recovered operation. Do not preserve bad paragraphs merely to minimise diff size.

If the body is substantially sound, repair it surgically.

Preserve stable links and record identity where they remain correct. Preserve genuine source boundaries. Preserve unresolved questions that are actually unresolved. Preserve the difference between historical fact, formal derivation, philosophical argument, lived report, image, and technical observation.

Do not preserve truncation, caution, or workflow residue merely because another agent previously ratified it.

## Review

The reviewer receives:

- the direct generating sources;
- later explicit authorial corrections;
- the repaired record;
- the recovery ledger.

The reviewer does not receive the repairer's self-defence.

A rejection must identify an exact mismatch:

- source claim altered;
- theorem step lost;
- distinction collapsed;
- consequence invented;
- historical attribution overstated;
- formal entailment overstated;
- implementation state misstated;
- or a real unresolved proposition falsely closed.

"Too strong", "could be controversial", "sounds metaphysical", "needs more caution", "AI consciousness", "scholars may disagree", or "this should be framed as Taylor's view" are not findings unless tied to a specific false proposition in the text.

The reviewer must also reject underclaim, omission, euphemisation, reception-management, provenance theatre, and token substitution.

## The execution ledger

For every changed record, use:

`record → generating sources read → recovered positive operation → corruption found → repair made → necessary boundary retained, if any → downstream consumers reopened`

For a caveat retained, append:

`mistaken stronger proposition prevented → textual reason the inference is live`

For an unchanged record, state why the complete generating operation is already present. "No obvious problem" is not a review.

## Run order

Start where corruption has the greatest multiplier.

First recover the theorem-bearing Symbolon root: the complete eight determinations and their direct relations, then the slash, 0/1, 1/0, ?/!, −/+, X/x, AM/IS, ∞/dx, self-identity, subject-logics, Mono/Poly, complexio oppositorum, and the arguments that presently mediate those roots.

Then recover A/C and the complete A/A′/C/S field wherever those records depend on the theorem or have inherited defensive framing.

Then recover the generative Matheme, whole-Mytheme, Etymology, history, dossier, and map records whose current public bodies have lost their developed operations or have been turned into source-management prose.

Then pass through all eight rooms and forty-eight movements. At this stage a movement must carry the actual proposition and operation it needs; it may not gesture toward a record identifier and assume the reader received the argument.

Only after the substrate is repaired should final manuscript quilting and enrichment resume.

The final writing pass inherits the repaired claims. Its job is composition: connection, interstitial movement, rhythm, scale, image, tone, compression, expansion, and the full return of the essay. It must not spend its intelligence excavating the argument from defensive canonical summaries.

## Completion condition

This work is complete when a cold reader can traverse from the direct theorem and authorial corpus into the root Symbolon, through arguments and concepts, through the register-depth field, through the forty-eight movements, and into the manuscript without encountering a second agent-voice whose job is to apologise for, quarantine, attribute away, or pre-emptively domesticate the argument.

The question at every layer is simple:

**Does this text perform the thought, or does it manage somebody's imagined reaction to the thought?**

Keep the former. Remove the latter.
