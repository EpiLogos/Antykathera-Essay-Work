# Apportionment / Economy — Historical Branches

## #0 — Distribution acquires a public history

A portion becomes effective through an arrangement: someone allocates it, someone can use it, and use changes what remains available. [Apportionment within its sustaining economy](WHOLE-FIELD-apportionment-and-economy.md) follows a share through its name, measure, office and household place. These encounters constitute different practices, not one uninterrupted history from Greek vocabulary to computation.

Taylor’s [earlier lexical inquiry](HISTORY-apportionment-and-economy.md) opens the historical relations of share, name and measure. Particular witnesses distinguish lexical descent, attested usage and the further institutional comparison; phonic resemblance supplies no historical proof. The selected lexical, Aristotelian and Ephesians witnesses retain their editions and collation limits in the source references below.

## #1 — Allotment, possession and naming

<a id="e6-names"></a>
### The name and the numbered share

[LSJ’s νέμω entry](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q009) attests distributing portions, assigning honour or privilege and, in the middle voice, holding something as one’s portion. Meat, property and land supply different examples. Giving and possessing a share are related acts in this semantic field; the selected uses establish no compulsory chronology from pastoral life to every later law.

[Νόμος is related to νέμω in LSJ](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q003), with customary-practice and law senses. Differently accented νομός remains a separate entry. Lexical connection does not turn pasture into a required universal origin of the state.

A portion of meat is consumed; a privilege affects what one can do; a plot can be held and worked. Each example has a distributor, recipient and continuing field, but the terms of return differ. Apportionment alone does not settle whether a share is a gift, entrusted use, sale, inheritance or domination. Those relations require the practice's own account.

Latin *nomen* and *numerus* remain different. The earlier name-family reconstruction and proposed number/apportionment connection have no newly admitted comparative dictionary witnesses; no Indo-European genealogy follows from the public operation. Their meeting in denomination has an independently intelligible operation: naming identifies the circulating unit; numbering specifies the amount claimed under it; an issuing and receiving practice makes that claim usable. A shared spelling or a sound cannot perform the work of the issuer and bearer.

A [public mark](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A15-Ratio-Rationality-Measure-Reckoning-Harmony-Account.md) joins naming and counting without exhausting the particular addressed. An [office’s authority](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C29-Mediating-Office-Derivative-Sovereignty.md) derives from the source relation it serves. What the mark authorises remains a further institutional question, distinct from the word’s lexical history.

## #2 — Exchange brings unlike work under a measure

<a id="e6-ratio"></a>
### Reckoning, proportion and the criterion

[Lewis and Short’s ratio entry](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q006) connects the noun with *reor/ratus* and gives reckoning, account, calculation and computation among its senses. Receipts, expenditure and rendering an account specify its uses. The selected Latin range does not verify a common ancestry with *arithmos* or harmony.

In [Nicomachean Ethics V.3–4, W. D. Ross’s translation](../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/aristotle/aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross/aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross.md#aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross-q001), Aristotle distinguishes proportional distribution and rectification of injury. [His account of reciprocal exchange in V.5](../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/aristotle/aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross/aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross.md#aristotle-nicomachean-ethics-ross-q002) brings unlike products under a measure. The builder’s house and shoemaker’s shoes must become comparable for exchange; money mediates demand by convention. He connects *nomisma* with *nomos* and says users can change it or make it useless. Goods that are radically different become sufficiently comparable relative to demand. These are philosophical distinctions and claims, not an archaeology of the first coins.

A [result under a criterion](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A15-Ratio-Rationality-Measure-Reckoning-Harmony-Account.md) owes its exactness to that denominator; the criterion’s [authority to govern an encounter](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md) is a further judgment. Exchangeable price is not adequate to every question about the goods. A model’s score likewise retains criterion, comparison class and selecting office so disagreement can reach the condition actually exposed. In this technical comparison, the [local judging agent](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) is a relative functional knower, its sources, model and comparison rule are means, and the alternatives it judges are known. The whole apparatus serves as means within a person’s containing inquiry; another inspection can make the judging centre known without exhausting the present act of knowing. Source, answer, task or model can be corrected under a fitting retained criterion; a failed evaluator or permission must reach capable authority; an adequate condition can be retained with reasons. The later use inherits the finding and consequence. Aristotle’s exchange examples and this contemporary return remain separately warranted.

Finite-ratio calculation and QL cross-comparison retain their native derivations. Latin usage neither derives those operations nor licenses an arithmetic identity they do not claim. In [preference evaluation](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero.md), an affected answer can correct a score or source under an adequate measure, challenge a failed measure or its authority, or confirm a fitting criterion with reasons. A claim that processing actually changed requires the identified change and the later act that inherited it; general source-answerability does not require measure revision on every return.

## #3 — A household's provision and the accumulation of money

<a id="e6-house"></a>
### Arrangement, use and dependence

In [Politics I.8–10, Jowett’s translation](../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/aristotle/aristotle-politics-jowett/aristotle-politics-jowett.md#aristotle-politics-jowett-q002), Aristotle distinguishes acquisition of provisions from household use. A shoe may be worn or exchanged; his origin narrative has wider exchange lead to money and a stamp replacing repeated weighing. Accumulation then becomes an end apart from provision. This narrative is not independently verified coinage chronology. The household includes master and enslaved person, husband and wife, parent and child; its hierarchical distribution cannot be redescribed as a modern equal commons.

[Lewis–Short’s credo senses](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q002) distinguish lending, committing something to another’s protection, confidence and belief. Entrusting has a particular Latin semantic range; it does not make every delegated capacity a loan. The cited Cato passage remains a dictionary-mediated context, not a newly collated primary text.

[Commissioned work](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) separates office from the ancient hierarchy. An output depends on work and capacities its recipient did not originate. Measuring only the acquired product can omit labour, constraints and depleted conditions. Those consequences must reach the appropriate source, evaluator, permission or commission; warranted correction and justified retention then condition the next act. Actual costs require their own evidence.

The ancient household and modern commons meet a problem of distribution, with different participation and authority. In [Ostrom’s selected institutional conditions](../../sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture.md#ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture-q001), most affected participants may make and modify resource rules, their local rights are recognised and governance may be nested. Her next passage distinguishes social and ecological conditions and warns against rigid transplantation. These are particular institutions and conditions, not a universal successor to *oikonomia*.

[Shared ordering](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly.md) preserves consequential local authority and refusal. A [contribution to another’s world](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) can persist as a condition that participant receives and judges. A federation’s local Bimba is an actual downstream reference while remaining answerable to wider sources. Nested relation requires neither one completed account nor surrender of every community’s ground.

[Training a comparison, delegating work and proposing a commons](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#commons-and-responsible-return) distribute different powers. Who performs, benefits and bears cost remains identifiable through the allocation; report claims and observed execution remain different evidence.

## #4 — Administration, fullness and differentiated restoration

In [Ephesians 1:9–10, the selected SBL Greek text](../../sources/biblical-studies/pauline-corpus/ephesians-sbl-reverse-interlinear/ephesians-sbl-reverse-interlinear.md#ephesians-sbl-reverse-interlinear-q001), *οἰκονομίαν τοῦ πληρώματος τῶν καιρῶν* belongs to disclosure of divine purpose. Administration concerns the fullness of times and gathering things in heaven and earth in Christ. Arrangement receives an explicitly temporal and Christological fulfilment, not a theorem that every economy returns to plenitude. The selected Greek phrase does not supply a complete translation or reception history.

In [Against Heresies I.10.3, Roberts–Rambaut’s ANF translation](../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/irenaeus/irenaeus-1885-against-heresies-book-1/irenaeus-1885-against-heresies-book-1.md), Irenaeus describes exposition of God’s dispensation in salvation through temporal and eternal things, successive covenants and the Son’s advent. His hostile report in I.1–8 presents a different Valentinian distribution, which he opposes. In I.7, Achamoth’s union with the Soter, the Demiurge’s intermediate destination and matter’s destruction remain unequal conclusions. Reported adversaries and his own rule of faith retain their different speakers and doctrines.

Fullness can organise different accounts of creation, mediation and completion. In the [Valentinian telling](../../../mytheme/worlds/late-antique-gnostic/valentinian-sophia-horos-achamoth/WHOLE.md), the jointly contributed Soter and unequal destinations have distinct offices. Shared vocabulary establishes neither one doctrine for all participants nor an identical return for everything.

An [answerable commons](WHOLE-FIELD-apportionment-and-economy.md#commons-preserves-the-wider-economy) concerns arrangement and differentiated consequence in Taylor’s institutional argument. Ephesians and Irenaeus do not certify a QL institution; a contemporary claim of local authority does not retrospectively alter either theological witness.

## #5→0 — Name and power return through vocation

Recovering an institution’s purpose can leave its affected lives abstract. [Loving sensitivity to origins](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md) instead attends to the particular other whose answer can correct the treatment. Taylor’s [double devotion to whole and aperture](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md#taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3-q023) holds what exceeds a frame together with the specific frame of arrival. His terminal vocation releases completed work into other lives. Ancient theological and Egyptian attributions retain their separate sources.

[Name and Power](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/conjugate/A18-prime-Traversal-Run-in-Code.md#a18p-name-power) connect committed expenditure, an authorised course, an affected other and returned work with its costs and correction rights. [Delivery of a product](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) differs from changing the commission that governed it; [material and institutional dependencies](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C62-Planetary-Computation.md) extend beyond the visible interface. These consequences carry the five operations from finite capacity through measure, labour, commons and planetary economy into another use.

The selected witnesses leave precise historical limits: *nomen/numerus*, the proposed *nem-* reconstruction, nomenclature/denomination and the *arithmos/harmony* braid remain without independently collated comparative entries. Selected Aristotle, Ephesians, νέμω, ratio, nomos and credo have bounded textual or lexical witnesses, not complete print or reception histories. The selected ANF and Ostrom passages retain their own scope. Coinage origins, theological reception, current AI labour and planetary supply chains require their particular empirical or historical evidence; those absent expansions do not replace the separately argued institutional relations.

Through [source, projection, contribution and acceptance](../../dossiers/oi-technical-responsibility.md#oi-commons-and-return), a shared artifact can condition another local field without transferring source ownership. Refusal and correction rights remain effective in the distribution. The historical six-constructor checks establish bounded record behaviour; the wider architectural undertaking still requires its own execution evidence.
