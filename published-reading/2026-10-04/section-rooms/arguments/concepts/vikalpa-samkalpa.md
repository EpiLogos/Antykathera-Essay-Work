# Vikalpa / Saṃkalpa

## Definition

Both terms grow from `√kḷp`, whose lexical range includes fitness, suitable relation, ordered preparation and formation. The causative also includes arranging, making and, in particular uses, trimming or cutting. The root therefore does not guarantee that every arrangement is revisable or that cutting is absent; the actual use decides the operation. Monier-Williams distinguishes *vi-* as separation, differentiation or distribution and *sam-* as conjunction and togetherness. His entry presents the relation of *vi-* to *dvi* as probable, not established descent. ***Vikalpa*** includes alternatives, variation, distinction and mental construction; ***saṃkalpa*** includes conception, intention, resolve and decision. Taylor reads differentiating articulation and constructive gathering through this shared formative range. Dividing and uniting become two operations of world-making, rather than two unrelated powers. The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/chat-logs/taylor-claude-2026-derivational-chat/taylor-claude-2026-derivational-chat|derivational chat record]] retains the origin of this comparison; the selected lexical witnesses below supply its separate public word evidence.

Two boundary terms complete the family. ***Nirvikalpa*** names cognition or awareness without constructed alternatives; in Dyczkowski's selected account, inward self-awareness and outward discursive representation remain phases of the same conscious activity. ***Śuddha-vikalpa*** ("purified thought-construct") names the refinement invoked here: a formation directed against an entrenched conviction of limitation until its work can be released. Thought as medicine for thought: a vikalpa aimed at its own obsolescence. Taylor's requirement that a determination retain its revision conditions develops a corresponding operation; correspondence does not establish a historical line of descent from that refinement. Passage-specific primary exegesis of śuddha-vikalpa remains a separate doctrinal task.

## In the argument

[[09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa|Constructive cognition]] inherits its actual conditions: each act inherits a field, makes a cut, and alters the field available to the next act. Artificial agency becomes philosophically relevant here through an operative sequence: a retained field conditions differentiation, selected outputs change context, and the changed context conditions another act. The return becomes consequential when an encountered source, alternative or correction can change the means producing that act. Objective Internality is this mediating office within the whole relation of knower, means and known. In the [computational-process cartography](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), vikalpa is the cognitive face of dia-ballein (distinguish, qualify, exclude, select) and saṃkalpa the world-forming face of sym-ballein (relate, recompose, contextualise, commit). Through [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/apoha|constitutive exclusion]], a determination acquires content by distinguishing what does not apply: the excluded field participates in what the selected alternative means. Differentiating, excluding and gathering occur within [[13-tattvic-differential-field|manifestation through a contracted horizon]]. Finite capacity, knowledge, desire, time and order make a local world workable, while the inner instrument judges, appropriates, coordinates, remembers and forms alternatives within that world. These are formative operations of appearing, rather than commentary added after a finished world. The pathologies are symmetrical: a vikalpa that forgets it was produced claims self-subsistence; a saṃkalpa that forgets its exclusions presents its arrangement as the only possible world. [[38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax|Differential selection]] gives these operations a technical body when the source, candidate set, rule and resulting choice remain available to further inquiry. The local comparing agent occupies the relative functional knower office, the sources and comparison rule are means, and the alternatives are known. The comparison serves as means within the person's containing living inquiry; further inspection can make its comparing centre known without objectifying the immediate condition of knowing. A returned correction can change the answer, task or model under a fitting rule, reach a failed governing condition and capable authority, or retain a fitting condition with reasons. The next act inherits that judgment and its source relation.
