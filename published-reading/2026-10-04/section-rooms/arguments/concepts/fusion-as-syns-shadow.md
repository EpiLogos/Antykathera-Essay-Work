# Fusion as Syn's Shadow

## Definition

Sym-ballein writes a polarity as `(0/1)/(1/0)`: ground becoming legible through a mark, and the mark returning toward a ground it cannot convert into content, held together as conjugate orientations of one differentiating event. The outer slash does the holding, and it operates as AND/OR — the orientations conjoined because neither is the present relation without the other, disjoined because ground is not mark. Fusion enters when that outer slash stops relating and starts merging: the retained ground is taken to have abolished the orientations it grounds, so the conjunction survives and the disjunction goes.

"sym-ballein without distinction decays into fusion": [[03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|the relation requires differentiated terms]]. "forced coincidence, ontology collapse, counterfeit belonging, or enclosure presented as wholeness" name failures where those terms lose their actual offices. "sym-ballein remains healthy only when the seam stays visible": [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|separated halves of a symbolon]] authenticate one another across separation, distance and time. Their fitting completes a whole by retaining the fracture as relation. Erasing the difference would remove the condition of that authentication.

A determination [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|defines towards wholeness]] by retaining its ground, inverse and self-relations through `0/1 → 4+2` and release. Completion can achieve a differentiated whole: the finite form carries the source it does not own and the seams through which it can receive another act. A local `1` occupies zero when it claims possession of that originating condition or makes its own arrangement immune to return. Achieving a whole and appropriating its origin are therefore different operations. Fusion belongs to the second failure when the shared ground is used to abolish the determinations that it grounds; the native whole retains them.

A cut becomes severance when its account loses the common operation and field that produced its poles and treats the excluded term as eliminable. "their respective failures are severance and fusion": [[19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped|difference retains both risks]]. Conjunction without fusion and disjunction without severance keep the two necessary operations distinct. The following comparison concerns two failures of gathering rather than equating differentiation itself with arrest.

## Distinguished from counterfeit gathering

[[counterfeit-gathering|Counterfeit gathering]] retains distinguishable terms and the outward form of composition while ordering them for further use and withholding their return upon the arrangement. Fusion erases the differences themselves, leaving no distinct source, recipient or seam through which relation and return could proceed. Neither thereby completes sym-ballein. The diagnosis concerns the operation: a visible plurality can be captured, and a declared unity can lose the plurality it would have to relate.

One test covers both, and the two logics give it in a line: ask where the seam went. Externalised as an enemy, and the relation has been severed; silently erased, and it has been fused.

## In the argument

[[19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped|A shared condition does not remove conflict]]: "must not sell superposition as a softer moral replacement for conflict" preserves the actual positions that must meet. Concurrence becomes consequential through differentiated answers and consequences rather than through an asserted harmony.

[[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|Shared ground]] can be used to deny distinct positions or dissolve power from view. **Divine and concur** instead lets differentiated positions move together through a ground none possesses. The resulting relation retains the answer, refusal, correction and responsibility of each position; agreement or a numerical midpoint alone does not perform it.

[[23-s2-p4-complex-dynamism|Apollo]] makes measure, boundary and form legible; Dionysus returns fixed form to participatory life. Dissolution loses its relation when it abolishes every form through which life could answer. Diaphaneity holds form transparent to origin and participation capable of returning as form. Through [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|the integral rereading]], "the Apollo–Daphne and Dionysian cycles display form’s dangers of objectification and dissolution’s dangers of fusion." The distinct dangers and their bodily consequences remain in the complete tellings.

A [[14-computational-process-ontology|computational arrangement]] suppresses relation when "pathology is fusion or enclosure: a totality erases the seam and calls its capture wholeness." Retaining a source, rule, commission and returned consequence gives a local judgment a route through the means that produced it. In Bohm's distinct process register, analytic divisions become fragmentation when they are taken for the whole's final structure; their relative autonomy remains available within the containing process. "fragmentation is dia without that memory; fusion is sym without the seam." Here the local comparing centre occupies the relative functional knower office, rules and sources are means, and the determinate field is known. Within a person's containing living inquiry the apparatus serves as means; inspection can make the local centre known while the immediate knowing condition remains irreducible to the inspected contents. An actual correction or reasoned fitting retention reaches the relevant office and is inherited by the next act, preserving the differences a return requires.
