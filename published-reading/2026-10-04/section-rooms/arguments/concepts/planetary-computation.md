# Planetary Computation

## Definition

"Planetary Computation" is Antikythera's founding disciplinary self-description: computation and the evolution of intelligence studied at planetary scale, undertaken "on behalf of a renewed philosophy of technology" (Agentworld Brief, p. 3). In the coordinate system it occupies **#0** — the ground-horizon (Why? / Truth), the "ever-present Origin ... the perpetual condition from which all positions unfold" — refracted through **L0** (Quaternal), the lens where mute positions "first become questions." It names not a thesis but the field-condition and founding question inside which the essay's whole inquiry is posed.

## In the brief

"Antikythera is focused on planetary computation and the evolution of intelligence. It supports interdisciplinary fundamental research on behalf of a renewed philosophy of technology" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief#Source PDF page 3|p. 3]]). The Agentworld special issue is framed as a contribution "to its development as a reorientation of planetary computation" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief#Source PDF page 58|p. 58]]), and the About page states the program's self-understanding: "a research program focused on the reorientation of planetary computation as a philosophical, technological, and geopolitical force," named for the antikythera mechanism, "the first known computer — which was also an astronomical instrument used for navigation, prediction, and planning" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief#Source PDF page 59|p. 59]]). The journal it addresses is "dedicated to developing a new interdisciplinary school of thought with which to engage … the conjunction and co-evolution of computational technologies, biological and non-biological life and the many scales of intelligence that are manifest in both," and the school "aspires to repair the rifts between the Sciences and the Humanities": "the philosophy fit for the 21st century will emerge from direct engagement with scientific and technological questions, and vice versa" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief#Source PDF page 59|p. 59]]). Agentworld inserts itself into this field at planetary register: "agentic machine intelligence is now a co-pilot of the Anthropocene and whatever comes next" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief#Source PDF page 4|p. 4]]).

## In the argument

The [formal limit](../A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) opens a question within this planetary field: a system can make its means of determination available without attaining a final position outside every condition of knowing. Computation participates in a world through materials, institutions, histories and persons; scaling its reach does not remove those constituting relations. [Agentworld’s hybrid sociality](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md) gives that dependence an organised research scenario rather than an accomplished universal society.

Within a local inquiry, an interpreting agent can function as knower, its models, retained sources and tools as means, and the addressed planetary situation as known. Within the containing person’s inquiry that apparatus itself serves as means. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) preserves these nested offices within Life / Mind; a larger model does not turn its represented world into the whole from which representation becomes possible.

The [planetary return](../A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) develops a contribution to this field through QL’s distinctions and MEF’s differing apertures: judgments expose the frame and means through which their objects became intelligible. [Trust](../../../symbolon/episteme/maps/trust-faith-formal-limit.md) makes a finite account answerable to the sources and lives which can exceed it. A returned consequence can correct an application under a fitting criterion or expose a faulty permission, measure or commission to an office able to change it. Later action inherits what was corrected or reaffirmed.

[Mono-Poly planetary intelligence](../A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) names the relational whole and its genuinely many expressions. It does not name Antikythera’s discipline or erase the differing histories and powers within its field. A commons requires an actual arrangement through which participants can contest a determination and the conditions of deciding; no planetary viewpoint owns that containing ground. This is a philosophical and proposed organisational contribution whose implementation must earn the corresponding powers of answer, correction and renewed entrustment.
