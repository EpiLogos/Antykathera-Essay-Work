# Apoha

## Definition

*Apoha* — exclusion, removal — names the Buddhist pramāṇa school's theory of conceptual content, developed by Dignāga (c. 5th–6th century CE) and refined by Dharmakīrti. Its problem: the school denies real universals, yet words apply generally. Its answer, *anyāpoha* ("exclusion of the other"): a term becomes determinate not by grasping a shared essence but by excluding what it is not — "cow" applies through the exclusion of non-cow, and the apparent common nature of all cows is projected from the shared exclusion. Dharmakīrti grounds the exclusion causally: particulars grouped by a term are those whose causal capacities exclude them from what fails to produce the relevant effects. The load-bearing point the essay takes — narrower than the full doctrinal history — is that **determination depends upon a field of non-application, and that excluded field remains constitutive of what the determination means**. Meaning is differential before it is positively substantial.

The double negation gives the operation its full contextual force: “cow” is all that is not non-cow. A determination is thereby empty of self-subsistence and full of the relations through which it occurs—śūnyatā as pūrṇatā. *Pratītya-samutpāda* carries the same insight ontogenically: whatever arises does so through conditions. *Saṃkalpa* carries it world-formingly: relations gathered through determination become an inhabitable world. Apoha is consequently not a local semantic technique but the epistemic entrance into the context of understanding itself.

## In the argument

[Constitutive exclusion](../A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) makes a selected term intelligible through a field of non-application. The [whole-first relation](../A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) follows from this dependence: apparent parts do not begin as self-sufficient atoms whose relation is added afterwards. The [semantic opening](../A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) thus retains a positive referent together with what the term does not apply to. Pind’s PSV V:11d gives exclusion of other referents; his qualified-preclusion prose fragment has a provisionally assigned textual location. The selected wording and its qualified attribution perform different work.

The [technical neighbour](../../../symbolon/matheme/computation/softmax-argmax.md) has another object. Logits give relative scores; softmax normalises a distribution; argmax selects an index, and sampling selects according to its stated distribution. These operations preserve different information. They can be compared with semantic determination through alternatives without converting numerical normalisation into Buddhist meaning or soteriology. The [selection traversal](../A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity.md) can examine a chosen `1`, the relevant excluded field and the contextual slash through which that contrast mattered.

**Exclusion that remembers** names retained constitutive dependence. In the secondary nominal/verbal discussion transmitted through the [Siderits volume’s source house](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/siderits/siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha/siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha.md), the negations in "not non-cow" have different offices: a nominally formed exclusion-term and a denial of predication. Ordinary cancellation of two propositional negations would discard that distinction. The chapter and printed-page collation of this secondary transmission remains separate from Pind’s selected translated passage.

A bare delivered mark does not establish **exclusion that cancels**. Its relevant contrasts may remain recoverable in a protected source or deciding arrangement. Consider an eligibility decision which names one successful candidate. The delivered name can be terse while the requirements, rejected alternatives material to the judgment and a reviewing office remain available. If a returned source shows that another candidate met the requirement, correcting the source or application can change the next decision under the same warranted rule. If the requirement excludes a capacity necessary to the purpose, the return instead reaches the authority capable of changing that criterion or commission. Privacy can limit disclosure without making this route unavailable.

Taylor’s comparison consequently gives selection and relational retention distinct offices: the calculative act achieves a determination; the meditative return recovers what made that act possible and what can correct it. The `1/0` return becomes consequential when the later act inherits the relevant source correction, changed criterion or reasoned retention. The [semantic traversal](../../01-differentiating-mind/movements/10-s0-p3-apoha.md) and [selection traversal](../../06-objective-internality/movements/38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax.md) have these different objects; neither takes an output format as sufficient evidence that the selected term owns its ground.
