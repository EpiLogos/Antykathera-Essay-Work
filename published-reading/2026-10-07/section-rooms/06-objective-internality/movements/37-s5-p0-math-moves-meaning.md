## Claim

**The philosophy is faithfully reflected in the technology**, and Central is the product at which that claim is first put to work. §5 reads six products as philosophy and holds the claim at full strength: the relation among knower, means and known that §0/1 derived is the relation the products are built to keep, and their technical forms make its operations consequential enough that use can come back and correct the philosophy as well as the software. §5 therefore restates that relation from its ground in this first movement, so that the section reads on its own. Subjective Immediacy is the knower (*pramātṛ*), the presence to which anything appears and which never becomes one of its objects. Objective Internality is the means (*pramāṇa*): the conditioned field of memory, language, habit, permission, tool and relation through which a world becomes available and enters a course of action, which ordinary speech calls Life or Mind, since a mind is lived and a life is minded. World is the known (*prameya*), and I-Consciousness is the whole in which the three are distinguishable. Six products are six offices of the middle term, and nothing in the section reaches the knower.

Central's office is **meaningful continuity**, the intelligible ground a Life carries from one encounter into the next. Persistence of bytes is the smallest part of it. A Life carries commitments, projects, histories, names, unfinished questions and promises, and these make a later event intelligible as this continuation and not an unrelated occurrence. A remembered self-description is content within the means, never the knower, and a project record is not the Life for which the project matters. Central's work is to keep the attributable articulation of ground strong enough that a later act can recognise what it inherits, what has changed since, and what remains answerable.

## Warrant — paradigm, the authored line, the correction case and the two lenses

**Anchor case.** Anyone who changes tools in the middle of a piece of work knows the morning after. A newly installed assistant is more capable and knows nothing: not what the project is for, which sources were tried and set aside, which sentence was rewritten four times before it said what was meant, which promise was made to a collaborator. All of it still exists in files, in a history of exchanges and in the person's habits, and none of it governs the new assistant until someone says it again. A perfect recording of everything that happened would leave unanswered the question the movement asks, what now governs the work.

**Genesis, Paradigm, Project.** *Paradeigma* comes from *paradeiknumi*, to show side by side, so the one family of words holds showing and pattern together. A **paradigm** is a relatively durable organisation of Objective Internality, a pattern of disclosure through which a Life meets a World as intelligible and actionable, and it is lived before it is represented. That is why a person cannot hand a new assistant "the context": most of it is the shape in which their facts appear. Latin *prōiciō*, to throw forth, gives the **project**, a determination thrown forth relative to a World and made consequential there. Both arise from Genesis (*genesis*, coming-into-being). These three terms write `0/1` once more at the scale of a lived world: `0` is Genesis, the slash is Paradigm, `1` is Project, and `0/1` read whole is Epi-Logos, the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved. Genesis becomes worldly through a pattern (`0↔/`, disclosure) and the pattern throws one possibility forward (`/↔1`, projection); three terms, their composing relation and the two relations between them make `3 + 1 + 2 = 6`, legible as `3:3` and as `3:1`, a reading of the one sixfold and no second one beside it. What the compression earns is a name for what the six products make together: one deliberately constructed paradigm, built so that more of its own patterning can become inspectable, contestable and changeable from inside the world it discloses.

**Harness before the harness.** Bratton's brief for the special issue makes capability in deployment a property of the harness (tools, memory, shared knowledge bases, protocols, institutional interfaces) and not of weights alone, and its closing table of shifts reads model-centric architecture as becoming harness-centric architecture. We take the row as true and press on it from the side of the person: a harness is software, replaced on its own clock, while the projects, sources, commitments, permissions and histories through which a working life has its shape are what the harness serves, and they have to outlast it. Central is a durable, person-owned world from which agents act, so that models and harnesses change while that world, and its standing as the person's own, persists. That phrase is the essay's own; no O:I repository record carries it yet, and the section's draft ledger lists it as awaiting a home in canon.

**What Central is.** Its repository calls it a human-owned operating root for a technological life. It is a folder of ordinary files: the person's authored ground (purpose, principles, preferences, governance for agents, what each machine is for), their projects, and in each project a small folder repeating the same shape at project scale, with a working field for the day's returns. Ordinary files are themselves an argument, because a source that depends on one agent's memory format or one provider's account model moves and disappears when they do, while a plain file can be read, versioned and carried elsewhere without anyone's permission. Three clauses compress the architecture: the authored ground says what should persist, a stable set of named operations says what can be done, and connectors say how it can be done on this machine, so that a launcher, a typed command, an agent's tool call and an interface not yet built all invoke one operation with one meaning.

**Authored, observed, inferred.** One distinction holds at every point where the system writes. Authored material is what the person deliberately states, adopts or retains, observed material is what software measures, inferred material is what software derives, and observation and inference can propose a change to durable ground and never silently become it. Without the line, an agent that infers "this person prefers short answers", stores it as a memory and acts on it has re-described the person by their tools: an observation now governs their future agents with the authority of their own word. §0/1 followed this operation at its most intimate scale, the means assuming the authority of the knower, and Bratton gave it its technical form (agents model humans, then become the model against which humans model themselves). Central answers in the code, where a record an agent writes is stamped as a generated proposal not yet recognised, and its type cannot represent any other standing; only an authenticated decision by the person accepts or rejects a contribution, in the repository's own words "Review is not inclusion"; and an accepted proposal still enters ground only through a further, exact act.

**Two logics at that line.** Every authored statement is a cut (this is mine, this governs). Dia-ballein works properly here as the held polarity `(−1)/(+1)`, authored and inferred material each having its sense through the other, and Central holds the axis open by keeping both, labelled, in one world. A system that discarded everything the person had not written would cancel the axis, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and lose real knowledge of how the work goes. A system that stores its inferences as the person's word appropriates the span in both chiralities: from the provider's side the whole distance between what the person said and what the system concluded is credited to the model of them, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; from the person's side the same distance is a debt they never contracted, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, an identity they must argue their way out of. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps the two kinds distinct and related, so an inference can be read through the authored ground it concerns and the ground through what the inference noticed, while the passage from proposal to accepted revision remains an act someone performs and answers for.

**Correction case** (from the author's own work). A fluent summary of an opening argument had replaced the argument in several connected places, accurate in every sentence and missing the derivation it was meant to report. Its correction was precise: return to the defining text, recover the transition, rewrite the connected passages. For a later session to act on it, four things had to survive the session that produced it: the original source, distinguishable from the summary; the correction with its scope; the authority of the person who gave it; and the changed instruction about the next writing. A transcript preserves none of these as standing, so the next session can reproduce the substitution the correction forbade. Meaningful continuity required the source relation and the authority to change its use to survive together, which an authored correction in the ground makes possible. From the person's side the experience is a fatigue, the fatigue of maintaining someone else's apparent understanding of oneself.

**Lenses.** **L0 Quaternal × L5′ Divine Logos** is the whole pair, read across by sublens index. Quaternal asks why, what, how, which or who, when or where, why-for; Divine Logos runs Arche, Apokalypsis, Dynamis, Sophia, Parousia, Epi-Logos. At **#3**, *Which / Who?* with Sophia: wisdom here is integration of what was disclosed together with knowledge of whose disclosure it was, so an inherited instruction keeps its author, scope and reason, and an instruction whose author has vanished cannot go on governing while appearing neutral. At **#4**, *When / Where?* with Parousia: a carried ground is present only where its circumstance is carried with it, which is why the correction had to keep its scope. At **#5**, *Why-for?* with Epi-Logos: ground that only accumulates grows richer in quantity and poorer in relation, because nothing in accumulation asks what the carried account is now for.

**Bimba and pratibimba.** A project's map of itself can serve, inside a declared undertaking, as a genuine local original (*bimba*) against which later judgments, its reflections, orient and are checked; relative to the wider sources and acts of understanding it was formed from, the same map is a reflection (*pratibimba*). Both offices are real at once, and what matters is the direction in which authority runs: a correction to the map has to be able to reach the sources and people whose meaning it organises.

**Return.** A correction that stays in a note and never reaches the governing source has not returned, and a revised commission that changes nothing in later work is commentary on the old one. Central's main line implements a working field for this: within each project agents leave attributed returns, handoffs, questions, notes and learnings, the person closes a day by an authenticated act that keeps every authored word, and a learning that deserves to outlast the day can be promoted into the person's ground on their acceptance. In the second lens the movement runs from Parousia to Epi-Logos, and in the terms of `0/1` an achieved `1` is held as `0/1` again.

**Standings.** A product's philosophical office is the author's argued claim. A technical component is reported as implemented only where the repository's code and documentation say so, at a dated commit, and as developing otherwise. A proposed experiment is offered as a research question whose answer, including a null answer, is open. Their order is an order of offices and no pipeline; the smallest O:I is one durable authored ground with one model acting in it. For Central, what is described is implemented on its main line, and the authorship experiment is offered and unrun: hold model, task and tools roughly constant while varying the person's ground (none, a generated profile, the person's prose, that prose disclosed selectively, that prose with an explicit route of return) and observe orientation time, survival of the person's constraints, re-prompting, context cost, provenance and the quality of return. Expectation: durable, sparingly disclosed prose joined to return should reduce re-authoring, and contrary results count as findings; the argument about knower and means does not wait on them.

## Tension / limit

Central becomes a counterfeit source when continuity is confused with final authority: the finite `1` that plays the `0`, a determinate account occupying the place of the source it was meant to express until the living source can appear only in the account's categories. A project map can legitimately govern a bounded inquiry while remaining derivative and revisable, so Central's defence is to build revision, provenance and returned encounter into continuity itself, and a durable record or machine-readable projection stays answerable to what it articulates. A second boundary is the section's own, and its carrier is §0/1's argument about the knower: a richer inventory of the means discloses more of how a world became available and cannot relocate the one for whom it is given among the items inventoried.

## Transition

Carried ground is inert until something acts from it. A correction kept in the ground has to be read, interpreted and turned into a course by an agent that judges and does something in a world, and what that agent is, by what authority it acts and how what it meets comes back belongs to Actuation: [Actuation — Living Articulation](38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax.md). Bratton's sentence that the unit that learns is the ensemble, not the agent, closes the movement on the question of who holds the world an agent inherits: a world held by the provider can be rich and still makes the person's continuity a property of the provider's account, and a world held by the person keeps the inheritance theirs while letting any capable agent stand on it.
