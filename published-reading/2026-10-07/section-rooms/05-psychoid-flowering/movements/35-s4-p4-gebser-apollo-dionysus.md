## Claim

[[diaphaneity|Integral diaphaneity]] is the higher mutation of consciousness in which the mono–poly relation becomes available within a perspective: each aperture becomes transparent to the relational whole through which it is this aperture, without losing its determinate difference. Apollonian form becomes transparent to Dionysian participation, and participation returns as communicable form; the two are mutually diaphanous powers, related by the stroke QL reads as AND/OR (AND because they belong to one sanctuary and one life, OR because each season has its own god and confusing them destroys what each discloses). A transparent perspective is still a perspective: someone who sees through her own view has not dissolved into the scene. The movement runs through Delphi's calendar, Gebser, Nietzsche, Jung's typology, Ovid's Apollo and Daphne, Gebser's aperspectival drawing, the absent Father, and the master and the emissary.

## Warrant: the pair, Delphi, Gebser, Nietzsche, Jung

**Delphi.** For most of the year the sanctuary belonged to Apollo (the Sacred Way, the oracle, "Know thyself" and "Nothing in excess", the paean). Plutarch, who served the Pythian Apollo, has the young Plutarch answer that Delphi concerns "not only him, but also Dionysus, whose share in Delphi is no less than that of Apollo": the theologians call the god Apollo in his solitary, unified state, speak of his turning into winds, water and earth as "a tearing apart, as it were, and a dismemberment", and name him then Dionysus; the priests use the paean for a large part of the year and at the beginning of winter "awake the dithyramb". One set of stones served both gods by turns, and neither season cancelled the other. **Gebser** read "Know thyself", which on his account Thales had inscribed at the beginning of the sixth century, as a sign of the mutation that produced the mental structure, and then placed the decisive awakening in the Dionysia: under the god of ecstasy the integration was completed, frenzy and the formed action of the play unfolding together, each creating the awareness of the other, with Heraclitus's saying that Hades and Dionysus are one. Both gods served the birth of the mental structure, one drawing the line and the other setting free what the line had to leave behind.

**Nietzsche** (*The Birth of Tragedy*, 1872): the development of art is bound up with the duplexity of the Apollonian and the Dionysian "as procreation is dependent on the duality of the sexes", and tragedy is a fraternal union in which "Dionysus speaks the language of Apollo; Apollo, however, finally speaks the language of Dionysus". The union is a reciprocal expression: Dionysian participation finds a communicable form in Apollo's image, and the image becomes the vehicle of a wisdom it could not reach by its own light. §2 · #4 took the pair as a safeguard (Apollo petrifying without Dionysus, Dionysus dissolving without Apollo); here the pair has to become transparent.

**Jung** disagrees where the reconciliation was meant to happen: Nietzsche forgot that the Olympians' splendour owed itself to the darkness of the Greek psyche, so the Delphic reconciliation would be a beautiful illusion, the civilised Greek's wish to be done with the barbarian in himself. He translates the pair into his typology (*Psychological Types*): Dionysian states, affect breaking out almost bodily, are an extraversion of feeling bound up with sensation; the Apollonian is a perception of inner images of beauty and measure comparable to dreaming, an introversion; the pair points to two "aesthetic" types, intuitive and sensation. §4 · #2 put introversion and extraversion at the implicate poles **#0** and **#5**; if Apollo is introversion and Dionysus extraversion, the two gods are the two framing powers between which a whole psyche turns, the season of the image and the season of the dance, and Delphi's year is that turning lived as a calendar. The placement is the essay's reading and reverses an expectation: Apollo, god of the outward-shining sun, stands at the inward pole (the inner image held in measure), and Dionysus at the outward (the self poured out into the shared rite).

## Warrant: Ovid's Apollo and Daphne, and the logics

The Ovid telling is a mytheme, a whole story that discloses a relation through its action; the philosophical composition with Dionysus is the essay's and draws on Nietzsche and Gebser in their separate offices, and Dionysus is not in Ovid's cast. In Ovid, Apollo has killed the serpent Python and founded the Pythian games (oak garland, since the laurel does not yet exist), mocks Cupid's bow, and Cupid answers that his dart will pierce him; Cupid takes two darts of opposite effect, gold and sharp to kindle love, blunt and leaden to put to flight, and strikes Apollo with the first and Peneus's daughter with the second. Daphne loves the woods and Diana's example, has refused many, and has asked her father for perpetual virginity, which he granted. Apollo burns like stubble in a field fire; "his own gifts of prophecy deceive him". He calls after her that he is no enemy and asks her to run more slowly so that he may follow more slowly, then names himself (Jove's son, revealer of what shall be, lord of the lyre, bow, medicine and herbs) and laments that love is curable by no herb. Ovid's simile gives their two aims exactly: the hound and the hare, "he sped by hope and she by fear". At the river she calls on her father to change and destroy the beauty by which she pleased too well, and the answer is at once: numbness, bark, leaves for hair, roots for swift feet, her beauty alone remaining. Apollo still loves her, feels the heart still fluttering beneath the bark, embraces the branches, and "even the wood shrank from his kisses". He declares: since she cannot be his bride she shall be his tree (his hair, lyre and quiver entwined with laurel, generals' wreaths, Augustus's portals). The last lines add a difficulty that has to stay: the laurel "seemed to move her head-like top in full consent". The recoil and the seeming assent both belong to the ending.

Through the logics. Apollo's failure is appropriation: his form counts the span from its pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, his offices offered as reasons she should stop, and Daphne reckoned from the same span is the `−2` of the one who failed to yield, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`; her flight even increases his attraction, so resistance is counted as confirmation of the image he pursues. The appointment of the laurel keeps the appropriation going after the encounter has failed: a living source that withdrew becomes his emblem. Symbolic appropriation can persist after the living source withdraws, and the image's public success cannot certify the fidelity of the relation through which it was made. In the philosophical composition Dionysus's failure is the mirror, cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: differences between persons summed into an ecstatic unity in which nobody remains to answer and "we are all one" becomes a way of overriding someone's refusal. Daphne's office is the one neither god can perform: Apollonian illumination cannot confer entitlement to its source, and Dionysian participation cannot confer permission to dissolve the responding centre; she is the other whose existence exceeds what either power makes of her, and the relation is whole only when her answer stays her own. Delphi's year shows dia-ballein's healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, each god present in his season and each season holding the other in memory; the integral structure adds the two seasons co-present, each readable through the other, `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

## Warrant: diaphaneity, perspective, paradigm

Gebser insists the aperspectival structure is neither an amalgam of the earlier ones nor a synthesis that melts them; it frees perspective from exclusive validity by letting the structures become transparent to one another while each keeps its powers. A field filled with powerful images and exact forms (Jung, Pauli, Lacan, the mythemes) invites a syncretic totality in which every symbol means every other; diaphaneity forbids it. His second principle: latency is the demonstrable presence of the future, transparency is the form in which the spiritual becomes manifest; he offers no new image of the world (another myth), worldview (another mysticism) or conception (another rational construction), and his *diaphany* opens a relation of subject and object no longer dualistic that threatens nobody with the loss of identity or being made an object. His first evidence is Picasso's 1926 line drawing, a figure seen from front, side and back at once, neither unperspectival nor perspectival: time, which perspective had spatialised into a sequence of viewpoints, has entered the image as the present. Artists who reached for the same concretion of time but were flooded by residues of the past he called *psychistic* (the modern Western inability to escape the confines of the psyche), which compares with the touch of King Midas Pauli diagnosed in Jung.

Diaphaneity carries the **psychic-fact** rule of §4 · #0 with a historical reason: because the structures are co-present, an experience the mental structure would class as irrational can arrive through a magical or mythical structure still living in a person, and deserves to be received as what it is before it is explained. Integral openness refuses to censor the experiential field before inquiry begins and keeps the discipline of distinguishing the interpretations made later.

"I possess a view of the world" puts the knower at the fixed centre of a perspectival scene, the eye at the apex of Alberti's pyramid; read from the other end, a world, history, body, language and concern give this situated knower the view she can inhabit. One reading keeps agency and limitation, the other dependence and provenance, and diaphaneity holds both without making them interchangeable: the self a real aperture with a place, a body and responsibilities, the context visible as a constituting field. §0/1 · #4 gave this its geometry, the triangle's 180 degrees opening into the square's 360 (derived at §3 · #0), the 360 degrees naming a perspective's capacity to include its own conditions and counter-orientations while remaining finite; total surveillance repeats the perspectival error at larger scale by mistaking exhaustive representation for the whole relation. At its strongest diaphaneity lets a perspective see through to the pattern of disclosure that makes its content intelligible (the paradigm of §5 · #0), and [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C64-Paradox-Transforming-the-Containing-Field|Paradox]] marks the stronger crisis: two determinations with genuine standing become irreconcilable under the containing relation that generated them, and the slash itself, the paradigm's way of disclosing, relating and measuring, is part of the problem. Many tensions resolve within a basin while the containing relation remains adequate; paradigmatic transformation occurs when returned difference reaches the regime that organises subsequent trajectories and changes the slash. The [Genesis / Paradigm / Project / Epi-Logos field](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md) gives that relation its compressed authorial home, and C64 owns the transformation criterion.

**The absent Father.** The mental-rational achievement resists that transparency: perspective, extricating the seeing from what it sees, makes itself aloof, a view from nowhere with no stake in the outcome, and the aloofness passes for objectivity (domination through aloofness). The Father is the power of truth and the truth of power, and being absent from the fray he is only ever imported as a scapegoat for outcomes we conclude in his name; he must be seen through, as a true *per-spectus*, or he is located in ourselves or in our enemy. His absence is felt as a lack and as a numbness to the lack, and every object is searched for something to fill his shoes. A world's ultimate ordering condition cannot be supplied as one more object within it; a name, institution, image or theory asked to stand where the transcendent stood makes the order a ruling that sits above its own law (§0/1 followed the surrogate through the household and the office). In the psychoid register the absent Father is the failure of perspective at the integral threshold: a finite view that invokes the absolute as its warrant while exempting itself from the relation it imposes. Seeing through him restores finite responsibility: a person can give reasons for a judgment without making an imagined final authority answerable for its consequences, and another's resistance, a neglected history or an unforeseen consequence can reach the criterion itself.

**Mono–poly, integral consciousness, the Rotundum.** Integral consciousness is the mono–poly view from wholeness: each finite perspective becomes transparent to the field of origins, exclusions, relations and other perspectives through which it is this one, and stays this one. A view from nowhere hides its dependencies by denying it has a place and a totality built by accumulating parts by denying its parts have places. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/apoha]] first empties each determination of self-subsistence (a word gathers its instances by excluding what they are not), and diaphaneity lets that emptiness disclose participation instead of fragmentation, since what a term excludes is what it belongs with. Every context is itself contextualised; the author's name for the ultimate context is the **Rotundum**, the encompassing round of Unitary Being, whose a-contextual contextualising is impartial because it cannot come from any limited perspective; being with what it observes it is invested from the start and acknowledges it. A subject that had stood as one determinate pole, with its unowned ground cast into the negative or the unconscious, becomes intelligible as a local aperture of the relation it had excluded; `0/1` and `X/x` make it explicit: determination stays real, and the one stops pretending to be self-grounding because it is answerable to the All that conditions its appearing.

## The master and the emissary: asymmetry, under a gate

McGilchrist enters once, as a cross-register witness to **asymmetry**. His introduction retells "very roughly" a fable after a Nietzsche story whose place he cannot now remember, so it is McGilchrist's fable and never Nietzsche's text: a wise master trains emissaries; the cleverest takes himself for the master, usurps him, the people are duped and the domain collapses in tyranny and ruin. He reads it through the hemispheres (a narrow, grasping attention that re-presents the world, a broad contextual attention through which a living world presences) and argues the emissary has usurped the master in the West. His neuroscience is contested (critics charge a slender neurophysiological basis and a lateralised tendency turned into two little persons), so the essay's argument rests on nothing neuroscientific and the functional relation is what enters. Apollo and Dionysus are powers that must stay mutually diaphanous; master and emissary are an *ordering relation*, and their pathology is not conflict but usurpation, the executor mistaking itself for the ground it serves: the `1` occupying the `0`, as persona hardening in the psyche, the occupied office in politics and the counterfeit gathering of §2 · #3 in technics (Hephaestus's net).

Heidegger drew the same asymmetry half a century before McGilchrist's fable, in the memorial address of 1955 (*Discourse on Thinking*): "There are, then, two kinds of thinking, each justified and needed in its own way: calculative thinking and meditative thinking", the first never stopping and never collecting itself. Calculative thinking is the emissary's competence, meditative thinking the master's attention to the meaning that reigns in everything. Neither author asks for the emissary's abolition; the architectural question is whether the offices of a mind or an instrument are designed as symmetrical faculties that can usurp each other or as offices with a return between them.

That relation has a technical form. A person who commissions an agent is the master and the agent the emissary, acting where she cannot, with real competence. Usurpation needs no malice: it happens whenever the literal wording of the commission is treated as the source of the work (the agent defends the instruction against everything the work has learned) or the agent's fluent account of what the person must have meant replaces asking her. The executing office then speaks for the one it serves, the `+2` of the instruction counted against the person as her `−2`. The remedy is to build the return into the office: the delegate must be able to bring back an objection, an alternative or a refusal, and the commission must stay revisable by its own consequences; a commission that cannot be revised by them has made its wording a master and its author its first subject. In §5 this asymmetry remains a transverse tension through the six-product architecture (Central carries ground and provenance; Actuation differentiates and judges; AIKit governs the changing horizon of powers; Software Factory transforms form through returned encounter; Workcell situates every act in resistance; QL keeps the distinctions related to their containing wholes), with the product meanings derived from their commissioned whole pairs.

## Tension / limit

Images raised to theorem become idols; equations severed from lived consequence become [Hephaestian nets](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/hellenic/ares-aphrodite-hephaestus-poseidon/WHOLE.md). Neumann's morphology and Gebser's structures meet at one operation (an achieved centre, hero or perspective, has to receive what exceeds its organisation without surrendering its differentiation) and must not be merged: their objects, methods and periodisations differ, and a merged universal chronology would lose the distinction that makes each useful. The carriers of the open debts: Gebser pp. 78–80, 82 and 6–7 (Delphi, the Dionysia, diaphany) are not carded (the house holds pp. 1–3 and 24–28), and the Thales and "Know thyself" claim is Gebser's own attribution; McGilchrist's fable is cited at the introduction with the hemispheric thesis under contest; Plutarch's three-month detail is not in Babbitt's English, which has Plutarch speaking as narrator; the Ovid lines are Miller's Loeb translation.

The [Apollo–Dionysus–Daphne whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/apollo-dionysus-daphne/WHOLE.md#apollo-diaphaneity-return) **figures** the communicable perspective returning through participation and context. Apollo retains articulation; Dionysus makes its living conditions effective; Daphne keeps another centre able to answer. Taylor's composed relation receives Nietzsche and Gebser in their distinct offices, while the separate Ovid telling retains its recoil and seeming-assent qualification.

## Anchor and transition

A single perspective cannot keep itself diaphanous by resolve alone; it needs other perspectives, declared as such and able to change it by their returns, and the apparatus for that is [[36-s4-p5-mef-prompt-thrownness|§4 · #5→0 — MEF and Prompt Thrownness]]. Back at Delphi the integral rereading changes what the calendar means: alternation is a healthy polarity, but a culture that lives by turns can forget in each season what the other knew, and integral consciousness makes both seasons available in one act, a sentence carved in the god's light that knows itself as a sentence ("Know thyself", written by a self that has danced) beside a laurel that still remembers the heart under its bark. **Mythemic anchor:** measure and ecstasy as AND/OR. **Mono–poly rule:** one field, many irreducible apertures. **Method:** keep exact matheme beside renewable mytheme.

The Daphne relation **returns-to** [the complete Apollo–Eros–Daphne–Peneus whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/roman-latin/apollo-eros-daphne-peneus/WHOLE.md#apollo-appropriation-after-withdrawal): Cupid's contrary arrows, Peneus's permission and rescue, bodily transformation, recoil and seeming assent remain together with the laurel's public afterlife. [A20](../../arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md) receives symbolic appropriation after withdrawal and returns it to [Symbol / Account / Trust](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#whole-mytheme-returns). The separate Apollo–Dionysus philosophical relation keeps its own sources; Dionysus is not inserted into Ovid's cast.

The Apollo–Dionysus passage **returns-to** [Con-text-through-Diaphaneity → Regard](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity) through a view that can recognise its own ordering and participation. Regard lets encountered difference alter the criterion of seeing while preserving the power of articulated perspective.
