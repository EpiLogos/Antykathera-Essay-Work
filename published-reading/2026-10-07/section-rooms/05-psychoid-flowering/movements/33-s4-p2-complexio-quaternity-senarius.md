# §4 · #2 — Complexio, Quaternity, and Senarius

## Claim

The *complexio oppositorum* holds opposites without cancelling their energy: it is the indivisible relation within which opposed determinations become distinct and consequential. Jung's quaternity gives that wholeness a fourfold explicit form, and QL's `4+2` adds the two implicate poles, the ground from which the four open and the recognition through which they return, and with them motion. The **senarius** is therefore a dynamic quaternity, four held between two, and no competing sacred count; six is what a two amounts to once its distinction includes its own relating (`2+2²`, §0/1 · #1). The manuscript's route to this is the problem Jung and Pauli met in the figure itself: how one gets from three to four.

## Warrant: Pauli's and Jung's quaternios

In a letter of 27 May 1953 (62P) Pauli drew his position as a quaternio: Einstein opposite Bohr, Jung opposite Pauli, four people for four mental attitudes, with physics, fated to strive for completeness, coming up against the psychology of the unconscious it lacks, and the archetype of the *coniunctio* constellated. Einstein had been the shadow in his life, and the Self was contained in the shadow too; the unconscious had built a correspondence of physics and psychology by sacrificing the requirement of objectivity and choosing completeness. In October (64J) Jung's footnote turned the figure on itself: placing Jung as the fourth to three physicists repeats the dilemma of Plato's demiurge, who had to force the fourth element into the mixture; the transition from three to four is awkward because the three deny the fourth, or the fourth neutralises the three into a whole, as in the axiom of Maria the Prophetess (out of the third comes the one as the fourth). He closed on the same problem, the two-thousand-year-old question of how one gets from three to four, and had sent Pauli a quaternio of his own, transcendental reality as the whole above the material, spiritual and psychic, "as usual three plus one", the fourth determining the unity. The figure had a history in Pauli's Kepler essay: Kepler's trinitarian sphere against Fludd's fourfold admitting the dark, material and feeling side, so in 1953 the old quarrel came back with Pauli holding the pen.

Both figures say more than either man said of them. Pauli's has an empty centre; its coherence comes from two crossing oppositions, a pair and its reflection, which is how his egg dream produced four. Jung's footnote names two ways the figure fails. If the three deny the fourth, the psychology of the unconscious stays outside the circle and the figure is a triangle pretending to be complete: an exclusion that forgets its own cut. If the fourth neutralises the three, the psyche absorbs the physicists, the King Midas wholeness Pauli complained of, and in the arithmetic the fourth counts the whole span from its corner, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, while the three, reckoned from their side, appear as `−2`, a deficit the fourth was needed to make up. Jung's own quaternio shows a third possibility: its fourth does not sit beside the three as another aspect but is their unity, the whole within which material, spiritual and psychic are distinguished, and so determines the unity without becoming a part. His argument for it: Pauli had sent three readings of a dream (physical, theological-metaphysical, psychological) and said that even the third was not the ultimate truth; Jung agreed that the psychological reading holds only what can be conceived in psychic terms and is incomplete compared with the whole truth as the ego is compared with the Self, though it is the conception of truth we have. We are virtually sealed off in the psyche (Leibniz's windowless monads) but the psyche has windows through which broader realities are seen, so the psychic aspect of reality is the most important one. Psyche is the medium through which everything is known, and the psyche is not the whole: the fourth determines the unity because it is the one term no picture contains.

§0/1 had already followed the quaternity some way (the quaternity "forms the logical basis for any whole judgment"; the four functions beside Aristotle's four causes and the tetralemma's four corners; the connected quaternios at the close of *Aion* as passages in which a whole enters, four determinations are articulated and a whole returns; the alchemists' "One born of the Four" at **#5**) and answered the 3+1 problem with a sequence: at **#4** the three perspectives are voided as independently complete, at **#5** their relation is recognised as one appearing-act, at **#0** the achieved unity returns through its originating condition, so `3:3` is also `3:1`. What it could not do is say what holds the quaternity together while it moves, and why its two framing places are not further members of the four.

## Warrant: the concept, the typology, the inferior function

**Complexio oppositorum** is Jung's name for the Self's whole, a totality holding conscious and unconscious, good and evil, light and dark in one relation without cancelling their energy. The concept has two moments. Original *complexio* is `0/1` before any opposition is lived: One and All, source-unity and manifest-unity, are different aspects of one whole and their inseparability precedes every later reconciling. Achieved *complexio* is an encountered opposition brought back into a containing relation that has changed by receiving it, which individuation accomplishes repeatedly. Two ways of losing the relation lie between: severance eliminates the relation and leaves two terms facing each other across nothing; fusion eliminates the terms and leaves a relation that relates nothing. A *complexio* refuses both, and that is what lets opposites keep their force.

**The typology shows the count.** In the introduction to *Psychologische Typen* (1921) Jung distinguishes two attitudes (introversion, extraversion) from four functions (thinking, feeling, sensation, intuition), insists both attitudes occur in everyone and each function can be oriented either way, and retracts an earlier classification identifying thinking with introversion and feeling with extraversion. The correction makes the attitudes orientations of the functions and not two further functions: four functions oriented in two directions are not six, and QL's senarius is not the discovery of six faculties. It is the `4+2` derived from a distinction's relation to itself, two implicate offices and four explicate operations, and the comparison has content because the relation holds, not because the numbers match. A function cannot operate without an orientation, an orientation cannot do what a function does, and the attitudes are the directions through which the field of functions opens and returns: introversion at **#0** (the inward turn from which a function draws its material) and extraversion at **#5** (the outward completion into a shared world), as in §0/1's phenomenal lens.

**The inferior function is the fourth's drama.** Commenting on Schiller, Jung describes a culture that prizes one function and drives the rest into inferiority, so that modern man identifies with one function and denies the relevance of the others. The inferior function is the least developed and most unconscious, the fourth that arrives last because it was neglected first, and what unites opposites on the path of nature is its acknowledgement, a coming to terms, not its redemption by the superior one. In the arithmetic: a culture privileging one function counts the span from its pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the inferior function becomes the deficit `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, so the identified and the despised are the two ends of one appropriation. Acknowledging the fourth is the syn-ballein move: it does not reverse the hierarchy, which would shift the appropriation to the other pole, but lets the fourth determine the unity and so changes what the first three were. In QL the fourth arrives at **#4**, Context, where each perspective is voided of its claim to stand alone: the inferior function is the context the superior function excluded in order to be superior. The axiom of Maria (one becomes two, two becomes three, out of the third comes the one as the fourth) runs, Jung reads, beneath Christian dogma, interpolating the even numbers between the odd; the unconscious wavers likewise, and the least differentiated function is so contaminated with the collective unconscious that, when conscious, it brings up the archetype of the Self, the one as the fourth. The inferior function is the door through which the whole enters, because it is the part least able to pretend to be the whole.

**Number's four properties.** In May 1953 (61J) Jung wrote that inasmuch as number is an archetype it can be assumed to have substance, an individual form, meaning and relationship connections to other archetypes, and asked Pauli whether there was no systematic compilation of the numbers' mathematical properties from one to nine. §0/1 claimed QL is that inventory, begun where the letter could not begin, at zero. His four are explicit properties (the first two nearer the outward face, the last two the inward) held between a ground no property exhausts and a statement the number makes of its own accord (67J: number, by its dynamis, can make mythical statements). **I read** the list as a quaternity of number's properties held between number's zero and number's speech, a `4+2` of number itself; Jung counted from one.

**Static and moving quaternity; the grammar of six.** A quaternity drawn as a square around a centre is a still figure, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/jung/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2|Jungian Quaternity]] **sources** the moving form: the *Aion* diagram is a ring of quaternios that turn, circulating from an unconscious totality to a conscious one. The two places that make the ring turn are the places a static quaternity leaves implicit, a whole entering and a whole returned. The senarius derives its force from that relation; its perfect-number and harmonic offices belong to §3. Six positions have an internal grammar (§3 · #0): three harmonics across adjacent pairs, Being (**#0**, **#1**), Becoming (**#2**, **#3**), Knowing (**#4**, **#5**), and three folds across pairs summing to five, Essence (**#0**, **#5**), Constitution (**#1**, **#4**), Text and Texture (**#2**, **#3**), with **#2** and **#3** in both. Jung's quaternity inside this grammar is the four explicit positions, Definition, Dynamis, Pattern and Context; Essence folds the two implicate poles, and the *complexio* is three kinds of pairing working at once, kept in play by the Self.

**The shadow.** In *Aion*'s closing chapter Jung reconstructs four quaternios from Gnostic and alchemical material (Anthropos, Shadow, Physis, Lapis, in the names §0/1 gave them) and says the Shadow Quaternio is his own modern insertion, because an exclusively upward-turning religious orientation could not contain the psychic situation of his time. Circulation therefore passes downward as well as upward. A shadow contains capacities as well as what is morally condemned, so incorporating it is no endorsement of evil, and a more inclusive totality leaves the person fully responsible for judgment: wholeness is harder than goodness, keeping the difference between good and evil consequential while refusing to put evil outside the whole.

**Paradox.** The pressure through which a containing relation becomes available to transformation: two determinations with genuine standing cannot be held together by the relation under which they acquired it, and another answer of the same kind will not settle it, because the means of answering has entered the problem. Russell's paradox was such a pressure on collection-forming, Gödel's sentence on truth and proof, Pauli's dream of a two-dimensional world on physics; in a life it arrives when she can neither go on being what she was nor become its opposite (the daughter of §4 · #1 can neither repeat her father's sentence nor become a mother who never says no). Resolution changes the slash, the containing relation through which the poles were produced, and a quaternity is the form in which the change can be seen: two opposites as two corners of a square whose other two corners are their relation read from each side, between a ground and a return neither corner owns. The third position is no compromise; it is the relation become visible as a position.

## Anchor: Harmonia and Psyche

**Image:** Ares and Aphrodite bearing Harmonia without merger, opposition bearing concord because the relation survives. §2 followed the difference between Harmonia's birth and the apparatus-marriage of Hephaestus's net, which held the same lovers in a perfect enclosure and bore nothing; Heraclitus's harmony of bow and lyre is the instrument, the string's tension against the frame the condition of the note. Beside Jung's quaternity the myth adds what the diagram cannot show: concord is born when the relation between the opposed survives the opposition, and its child is a new term with its own life. **QL anchor:** quaternity-in-motion.

The manuscript carries Eros and Psyche in compact prose. Apuleius's Psyche, parted from Eros by her forbidden look, performs four labours for Venus (sorting the seeds, the golden fleece, water from the Styx, the descent for Proserpina's box of beauty), the explicate middle; the descent and the waking from the deathlike sleep the opened box pours over her are the implicate thresholds; and the marriage in which the two remain two is crowned by their daughter Voluptas. Read through QL, the tale is a `4+2` lived from the inside: wholeness that can move is earned through four differentiated works held between a descent and an awakening. The optional self-contained QL plate remains available from this movement (four labours as the explicate middle, descent and waking as implicate thresholds, marriage without merger), outside the prose so no second long narrative sits inside it.

## Tension / limit

The psychological `4+2` of functions and attitudes is a comparison with its own source task (the Jung dossier), and the native eightfold derivation of §3 · #0 is wider than it; the numerical agreement licenses the comparison and infers no identity. The carriers of the open debts (DEBTS, §4 draft): the *Aion* passages at ¶¶408–13 and ¶¶418–27 (the house holds ¶¶402–13 only), the 61J p. 115 transcendental-reality quaternio and the 64J p. 129 windowless monads (no card), and the introduction to *Psychologische Typen* pp. 7–13 (not matched to a card). The Aion quaternios are named as in §0/1 (Anthropos, Shadow, Physis, Lapis); Jung's own name for the third is usually the Paradise quaternio, and the §0/1 wording should be checked against *Aion* ¶¶390–403.

## Transition

A form of this kind has to be transmitted, and every transmission risks closing what it carries; Lacan made that risk his life's problem and wrote a notation to meet it: [[34-s4-p3-lacan-matheme-mytheme|§4 · #3 — Lacan, Matheme, and Mytheme]]. It passes from Jung to Pauli, from a dream to the dreamer, from this essay to its reader, and a closed transmission turns the living relation into a figure that stands where the relation stood.
