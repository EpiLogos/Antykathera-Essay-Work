What returns at the end of a derivation is the person who made it, and §4 asks what happens when the form changes the one who recognises it: §4 · #0 — The Psychoid Problem.

## Section wager

The mathematical substrate becomes psychologically consequential when the opposed terms are psyche and matter, subject and signifier, conscious and unconscious, form and participation. Jung, Pauli, Lacan, and Gebser furnish different, non-interchangeable approaches to the surface QL calls psychoid.
