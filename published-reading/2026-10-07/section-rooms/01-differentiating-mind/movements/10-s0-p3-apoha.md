## Movement thesis

A word becomes determinate by excluding what it is not: "cow" is articulated as *not non-cow*, and the selected `1` is intelligible against the field it excludes. §0/1 opened the cut through apoha and Spinoza together, and here [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/apoha]] receives its own semantic and ontological force. A determination is semantically dependent, because the contrast through which it applies stays constitutive of its meaning, and the movement carries that dependence from reference to the occurrence of anything: each determination, understood through what it excludes, opens onto the field that constitutes it. Our case is a child of eighteen months learning "dog". Nobody gives her a definition; she receives a series of exclusions (not the cat, not the cow, not the sheep) from someone she trusts, and the word acquires its reach from them, so that the cow stays active inside "dog" when she says it of a spaniel and none of them is in sight. Her question, "Is a wolf a dog?", is asked at the edge her exclusions have drawn.

Double negation keeps the point exact. Scholarship on apoha distinguishes two negations in *not non-cow*: a verbally bound negation denies a predication, and two of them cancel; a nominally bound negation forms a term whose content is the exclusion itself, and negating that term does not collapse into a bare positive, because it carries the structure of what it was formed against. This is exclusion that remembers (§5 · #1 develops it). Dignāga's tradition keeps the positive side as firmly: the word reaches a real thing qualified by exclusion and not a mere exclusion, so the spaniel is reached and no self-sufficient essence of dog stands behind the animal.

## Warrant and comparison boundary

Dignāga's problem is every theory of meaning's: a general word applies to many unlike things and the universal it seems to name is no separate thing. In his own formulation, as Tillemans gives it, "A word talks about entities only as they are qualified by the negation of other things", so the word reaches every cow by excluding the non-cows and no shared essence has to be posited. Dharmakīrti turned the account toward causes: only particulars, unique and momentary, are fully real because only they act, and exclusion classes are grounded in what particulars do for those who use the word. Tuske's review puts the two founders as top-down (how we refer to kind terms) and bottom-up (how general concepts are formed from the perception of particulars), and the child's word grew in both directions. Dyczkowski uses Dharmakīrti's *arthakriyā*, efficacy, for the outer face of every phenomenon, and up to here the Buddhist and Śaiva accounts agree: concepts carve a world by what things do, through exclusion.

English's one small "no" covers operations that apoha keeps apart. When the parent says the cow is not a dog, a word is refused an application (semantic non-application, apoha's exclusion); when the spaniel is not in the room, something is absent, and the word works in its absence; setting dog against wolf draws a contrast between determinations that already have their reach; the bitten child's refusal of the word is the psychic exclusion called repression, with its own mechanism. A fifth is **severance**: a difference is achieved and then loses the relation that made it, so the excluded field no longer returns to judgment. Successful exclusion does not perform it. A word that has excluded the cow knows the cow is there to be excluded, and a word that has severed it has forgotten it was cut; Horizon is the institutional case, a figure that went on meaning *the branch is short* because the alternative, *the software is wrong*, had been severed from it.

Apoha has a metaphysical counterpart. *Pratītya-samutpāda*, dependent arising, holds that whatever comes to be does so through conditions, and Nāgārjuna's verse in Garfield's translation draws the consequence: what is dependently co-arisen "is explained to be emptiness", being a dependent designation, the middle way. Our spaniel is empty of existence apart from the conditions that make it this dog and the exclusions through which it is grasped as a dog. Each doctrine keeps its own object, one concerning reference and the other occurrence, and what they share is the refusal of self-subsistence. We state the positive side as an equation of our own, argued from the operation and attributed to no school: ***śūnyatā* is *pūrṇatā***, emptiness is fullness. An appearance emptied of being its own source becomes available as the expression of the relations it concealed, so the spaniel loses an imaginary autonomy and gains a world, and "dog" loses an essence and gains a field. Dyczkowski's *pūrṇatā* names the fullness of consciousness, and the symbolon of §0/1 · #1 already wrote it: the determinate half is empty of exhausting the whole and full of the whole's trace. This is the essay's argued synthesis and no translation of one tradition into another.

Chinese Huayan gave the fullness its image, and the telling does the reasoning. In the contemplation attributed to Dushun, a net hangs through the palace of Indra with a jewel at every knot, each reflecting all the others. Taking the southwest jewel, all the others appear in it and each reflects the whole, including the southwest jewel; to the objection that one cannot enter all the jewels while staying in this one, the text replies in the essay's rendering that only because one does not go out of this jewel can one enter all, since apart from it there is no other. In the text's words, the one is "the total aspect, made complete by the many". Its proof is an experiment: put an ink dot on the southwest jewel and ink dots appear everywhere in the ten directions. Then the text turns on its own image: "The dharmas are not like this", since jewels take one another's images while their substances stay distinct and the dharmas interpenetrate in their whole substance, so the image teaches where its likeness ends. Van Norden and Jones give the doctrine in a sentence (each thing depends for its existence and identity on every other and keeps its distinctness), and the child's word hangs in such a net, an ink dot at one edge shifting the whole field. Apoha concerns how a term means and the net how anything is, and the contemplation adds what neither states, recursive mutual inclusion. Read through the notation the southwest jewel is a `1` and the net is the All `1` writes, the total aspect is `0/1` from its manifest side, and an ink dot is the return written as an act. §2 · #3 sets this net beside the bonds Hephaestus forged.

Buddhist and Śaiva readings divide on what apoha requires of the one who performs it. Buddhist epistemologists treat experience as a stream of momentary events and the persisting knower as one more construction, which is the critique of the self-thing §0 · #1 kept in force. Śaiva philosophers granted momentariness for outer objectivity and refused it for the knower: Kṣemarāja's reason is that the Lord creates and destroys only the objective aspect of the perceiver, since were the subject to waver there would be no one to whom emanation and absorption could appear. To exclude the cow from *dog* she must hold both in one act of knowing, the boundary grasped as a boundary, and a series of perishing cognitions could not do that, so the Pratyabhijñā took exclusion (*apohana*) from the Buddhist account of concepts and gave it back to consciousness as one of its powers. Five pressures place the knower where the critique of objects cannot reach: recollection returns as *I saw*, comparison holds the remembered tone with the present one, exclusion holds the cow beside the dog, action keeps its intention through its steps, and arising and perishing appear only to a knowing that does not arise and perish in the same way. None restores a self-thing. Our comparative judgment is argued: by the criterion of wholeness §0/1 set, which asks whether an account keeps both the differentiation and the unity in which differences are known, the Śaiva articulation is the more complete for this task, since it keeps the Buddhist destruction of every self-subsistent determination and gives positive places to the knowing in which exclusion is performed, the power by which constructions are made and the recognition by which a contracted life can know its source.

Apoha also opens Mono and Poly onto knowledge. Apoha empties every part of self-subsistence; poly names the many real differentiations of a relational whole, each constituted through relations it did not create, and mono names the whole's structural priority, the field through which anything becomes a part, and no supreme object behind its expressions. Whole and many are given together in every act of naming, and the slash carries the AND/OR readings §0/1 · #1 drew from the copula: every word is singled out (OR: this, not that) and held among the others (AND: this, with all that bounds it). Apoha also shows the dependence at the root of meaning, which is trust: each exclusion came from someone she relied on before she could check any of them, and §0/1 · #2 defined trust as already-enacted dependence. Phenomenal existence trusts relational context in this way, a gesture made because the world is assumed coherent enough to answer it and a word spoken because a field of shared difference is trusted enough to carry it. Analysis decomposes and recomposes that prior coherence and never manufactures the whole from nothing; this is the essay's phenomenological extension and no doctrine attributed to Dignāga.

## Consequence

Every selection leaves a remainder, and every generated `1` depends on a field of unselected possibilities. Exclusion itself is healthy dia-ballein: dog and not-dog on one axis through the zero of the field of animals, `(−1)/(+1)`. Cancellation drops the field, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, *not non-cow* summed to a bare *cow* with its formation erased, which is how a fluent passage loses its source. Appropriation lets the term claim the field, and Schmitt's friend and enemy gives the purest case on the essay's reading: a friend defined by nothing but the enemy it excludes counts the whole span from its own pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the enemy receives the same span as its debt, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, each side's `+2` the other's `−2`. Bratton's "humanity of the gaps" is the same appropriation turned on a species, and apoha explains why the defence is lost before it begins: a term determined only by what it excludes moves when the field moves, and the human term shrinks with every capacity a machine acquires. Syn-ballein holds the two as a symbolon, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, human and machine each read through the other, the excluded remaining constitutive of the term. An adequate account of determination keeps enough of the excluded field, the source conditions and the comparative frame for the selected mark to stay answerable. Artificial systems make the selection operational (scores normalised into a distribution and one alternative chosen), and whether the field remains available afterwards is a matter of design, which §5 · #1 follows with the semantic and soteriological differences kept in view. That computational payoff is withheld here.

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dushun/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra|Huayan jewel-net passage]] gives a distinct relational image: reflections pass through every jewel and recur within the reflections themselves. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/van-norden/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep|Huayan scholarship]] states the native force more carefully, since each member depends upon the others for existence and identity while remaining distinct. We relate that operation to apoha's constitutive exclusion and to the prior movement's world-building pulse, with the traditions and claims kept separate. [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]] later sets this living web against Hephaestus's bonds after the Homeric scene has supplied the opposing mytheme. This eastern sequence is the inner epistemic and ontogenic parallel to Gebser's diaphaneity, where a perspective becomes transparent to the context that makes it possible, and the essay's mono–poly reading of apoha is its own wider relational claim: no Buddhist doctrine is credited with it ([[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|mono–poly]]).

The [Indra's jewel-net whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-whole-return) **compares** the selected jewel's constitutive field with this movement's account of determination. Its recursive inclusion joins the authored braid, with apoha's semantic exclusion, conditioned occurrence and saṃkalpa's world-building each kept distinct, and its complete contemplation supplies the positive image before the later two-net encounter.

## Anchor and transition

A determinate term **returns-to** [Name-through-Count](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count) with the contrast through which it applies. Recovering the materially constitutive non-selection makes the chosen name answerable and leaves its positive reach intact. Apoha's semantic operation and the native count have different objects, so a shared word for exclusion does not establish their relation, and neither does an inventory of every absent possibility. Last comes the child's question about the wolf: her word has a boundary, she has found where it is unsure, and she asks, which is exclusion working as it should, the selected term returning with the alternatives that made it answerable. Asked of Horizon's figures by anyone with authority to hear it, the same question would have been the ink dot that showed the whole net.

**Conceptual anchor:** a positive determination whose excluded contrast remains constitutive. **Ethical image:** every mark carries a remainder. Full contraction is compacted in [[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|§0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression]].

Pind's two passages and the secondary account of nominal and verbal negation keep distinct evidential offices, and the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-exclusion) **qualifies** this historical attribution. Dushun renderings are the essay's own, and the Dignāga formulation reaches the essay through Tillemans and Pind and not from a primary text.
