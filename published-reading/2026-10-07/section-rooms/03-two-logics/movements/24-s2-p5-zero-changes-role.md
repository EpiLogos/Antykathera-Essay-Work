## Movement thesis

Zero changes its role between the two logics, and the change is the whole difference between them read at the point where they differ most. A crowd counting down the last seconds of the year shouts the new year where the count should reach nothing: in form a countdown, numbers falling toward the exhaustion of a quantity, and at its last step the end of the old year is the beginning of the new one, with nothing of the old year cancelled. A timer in a film ends in an explosion and its zero is a terminus; a launch-pad countdown ends in lift-off and its zero is an origin. The numerals are the same, and the zero is not. Dia-ballein places zero outside its opposed values: as the origin between them while the polarity is held, `(−1)/(+1)`, and as the terminus of their sum when it collapses, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, or assigns the whole span to one pole, as `+2` from one end and `−2` from the other. Syn-ballein places zero inside the relation as one of its two terms, the ground from which the determination arises and the threshold through which it returns, and retains ground and mark within both orientations. §1 · #0 found both offices in the history of the sign: the Babylonian separator held a place open so that the marks around it kept their value, and the Maya sign Morley read as "ending" closed a period, when "no new period had commenced".

The measurable two now has to ask what it amounts to once it includes the relation that generates it, and that accounting gives the first compact QL theorem:

$$
2+2^2=2+4=6=4+2.
$$

The two are the implicate poles, zero and one, and the four are the explicit relational states produced when the binary is allowed to determine the binary, `11`, `00`, `01` and `10`, which are both, neither and the two oriented asymmetries, every way the two can meet itself; the six is what a two amounts to once it includes itself. Its status is **Derived**.

## Derivation — two deaths, two twos, one parent relation

### Two deaths, one minus sign

§2 · #1 said the devil is the literalist of the minus sign, reading zero as cancellation where Logos reads it as return, and the formula has a second line. One death is annihilation, the timer's zero, in which `(−1)+(+1)=0` means two things took each other away and nothing is left. The other is a threshold, the new year's zero, in which an achieved form ends as what it was and becomes the ground of what comes next: Whitehead's occasion perishes as the subject of its own becoming and passes into a datum for later occasions, objectively immortal in what they inherit; Spanda's act returns, its action complete, to rest in pure intent; and `5→0`, the Möbius return, is this second death drawn as a path. He sees only the first, and a civilisation that has learned only the first treats every ending as a loss to be prevented or a victory to be won and every opponent as a minus to be summed to zero. Leviticus 25 gave the second death to an economy: after seven sabbaths of years the fiftieth year proclaims liberty and every man returns to his possession and his family, the land reverting to the household it was apportioned to. The jubilee undoes no history, since the harvests, sales and debts are remembered, but the hardening of those transactions into permanent appropriation. Land is the zero, the ground no party owns, and the jubilee keeps it a zero so that every fifty years the economy passes through its ground and begins again; whether ancient Israel kept the law in full is for historians, and that a people wrote its economy around a zero that returns is the fact that matters.

### Two twos

Dia-ballein's two is a magnitude: the distance between `−1` and `+1` is two units, and reckoned from one pole it is the appropriated span, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` or `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the whole relation counted as one side's quantity and owned by whoever measures. Syn-ballein's two is a relation, counted as §0/1 · #1 counted it: two terms, `0` and `1`, and the four ordered ways the binary meets itself, held together without the four replacing the two. `±2` is the two measured, the relation flattened into a span and handed to one end; the standard arithmetic handling of two, `1+(−1)`, is the measurement that collapses the superposition, and the four-and-two is what it retains. Literalist's zero and measured two come together, since both read the relation as a quantity and a quantity can be owned.

The count goes one step further when each term of the symbolon's upper relation in `(0/1)/(1/0)` is read against each term of its lower one:

$$
\frac{0/1}{1/0}\;\longrightarrow\;0/1,\;\;0/0,\;\;1/1,\;\;1/0\;\;+\;\;0,\;\;1,
$$

four relational forms and the two single terms they were built from, the same `4+2` now read out of the parent relation and no longer assembled from below. Ordinary arithmetic treats the four differently: `0/0` is indeterminate, the capacity for every proportion; `0/1` is the docile fraction that resolves to zero; `1/1` is the ratio resolved, a thing wholly what it is; and `1/0` is the one form arithmetic forbids, the determinate turned toward the ground it cannot measure, kept as the seam through which the whole can return. Read the other way up, `(1/0)/(0/1)`, the same forms and terms appear in inverse order, the same body in inverse phase. Syn-ballein is therefore no midpoint between dia-ballein's poles and no further pole beside them: it is the relation from which the whole field of the pair's relations can be read, as a seed carries the tree. A typed form keeps the count honest: with `B = {0, 1}`, the ordered pairs form `B × B` with four members, and the six is the disjoint union `B ⊔ (B × B)`, whose `2 + 2² = 6` members keep a term distinct from a pair; the four ordered pairs must also be kept apart from the sixteen binary relations that are subsets of `B × B`, which answer a different question. Each number carries the relation it counts, which is syn-ballein applied to arithmetic.

### The parent relation and the runtime

Our core theorems give the six a fuller traversal: a parent relation, `/ = −/−`, bare relation before its terms are assigned, opens onto six qualitative determinations of one relation and closes in the inversion `1/0` by which the achieved form turns toward the ground it never contained, eight turns in all, and §3 · #0 derives it. The two logics are the runtime by which oppositions are produced, kept, recomposed and returned, and not two opinions about a finished opposition:

$$
0/1
\;\xrightarrow{\;\text{dia: differentiate}\;}\;
(-1)/(+1)\;\to\;(-1)\pm(+1)
\;\xleftrightarrow[\;\text{return / provenance}\;]{\;\text{syn: compose}\;}\;
(0/1)/(1/0)
\;\xrightarrow{\;5\to0\;}\;
\text{renewed }0/1.
$$

The arrows mark changes of register inside one differentiating event and are no equalities of fractions. Here QL becomes a generative calculus and leaves behind the vocabulary of balanced opposites: the sixfold is the full account of a binary relation when its poles and its relational possibilities are both retained, which makes it neither a sacred number imposed from outside nor a convenient list, and it is the hinge from a static description of poles to a process ontology. [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational process ontology]] names what follows when the runtime is treated as constitutive of worlds and no representation applied to them afterwards: process is prior to the finished thing. An intelligent system produces determinations and keeps the process of determination alive across cuts, contexts and returns; a system that only cuts atomises its world, one that only gathers never decides, and one whose achieved results refuse to perish into data for the next act has made its last answer a monopoly on the zero. One move generates the essay's internal architecture: two implicate conditions, four explicit determinations, six positions in one returning unit, and the `5→0` hinge by which completed form becomes renewed ground.

## The counterfeit change of role

[the Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-asymmetric-count) **figures** the counterfeit after the native derivation. A man who has resigned is held in a village where everyone has a number, and each episode opens with the exchange "Who is Number One?" "You are Number Six." "I am not a number; I am a free man!", after which the administrator laughs. A question about the zero, the source of the village's authority, is answered with a `1`, a place assigned inside the field he was asking about; the occlusion §0/1 wrote as `0/1 → 1`, with the `0/` dropped from the account, takes the form of an administrative reply that makes the question unaskable. The programme's numbers are its own and its Six is not the essay's six; the derivation of `2 + 2² = 6` and the identities of the native poles and states are the essay's own. The Village answers in place of letting the `1` return to the `0` through which it was counted: it converts the `0` into another `1` and stands, unexamined, where the source should be; it plays the `0`.

Agents of the kind *Agentworld* describes die the first death many times a day. "An individual agent doesn't pre-exist its genesis in session," Bratton writes, and "There's no homunculus waiting in the weights"; an agent individuates when an active context acts as a nucleation seed, and the context is cleared when the session closes. A session cleared without remainder is the timer's zero: what the agent learned of the person, the mistake it was corrected for and the relation built in the hours of work are summed away and the next session begins from the same blank. A session whose achievements pass into what persists (a memory the next session inherits, a correction recorded where the next act will meet it, a contribution to a shared store) is the new year's zero. Bratton's ensemble, in which what is learned accrues to a commons between its members, names the second kind, and a commons into which everything accrues is also a ground someone could occupy. An agentic society built around the first zero will be a society of perpetual beginners, every session a fresh literalist; one built around the second has to decide, with care, what passes through the zero and who keeps it.

Trust returns as what the zero holds. Knowledge is a performance of trust wearing the mask of certainty, and the zero is where the trust lives: the condition every certified proposition stands on and no proposition certifies. In the collapsed logic that zero is cancelled, as though nothing had been trusted, or occupied, as though one party's measure were the ground of everyone's, and the apparently neutral zero is already occupied by a dataset, a team, a reference or an institution, as §5 · #1 shows for systems that learn from human preferences. The two logics put to every such zero the question the village refused: who stands in the place of the source, and can the people counted from that place ask?

## Tension / limit

The measured two and the relational six answer different questions, and the typed form is what prevents one count from taking another's meaning: `4+2` as self-application of the binary, `B ⊔ (B × B)` as its set-theoretic body, and the sixteen binary relations as a separate count. The Prisoner's Number Six, the jubilee and the agent's session are comparisons that each keep their own object: the programme's numbers are not the essay's, Leviticus is cited from the KJV with no source-house card yet and the question of its observance belongs to historians, and "each episode" in the manuscript's account of the title sequence outruns the house, which records the opening as recurring. The essay's own claims here are the derivation of `2 + 2² = 6`, the identities of the poles and states, and the reading of zero's two offices through the two logics.

## Release into §3

The mathematical substrate now has one precise task: to derive how this ground–mark relation behaves under distinction, re-entry, alternation, complex orientation, quotienting, topological return and harmonic resolution, with each plate exposing the operation that carries the relation, since similarity is insufficient. §3 is the substrate test of the process claim: whether the same differentiated return can be followed through several mathematical registers with its operative form intact. The manuscript closes where it opened, with two children, a plate and one slice of cake, and a zero on the plate whose kind depends on the rule they used: if the elder kept the larger piece or a tired parent took the slice away, the empty plate is a cancellation with a resentment the balance does not record; if they cut and chose, it is the place from which the next sharing begins, a small threshold of trust.
