# Zero

## Definition

Zero is the sign for no-counted-thing. Its several written and operative forms have different tasks: a blank leaves a place unmarked; a placeholder distinguishes positions; a numeral enters a script of numbers; addition and multiplication give zero distinct arithmetic offices; an origin locates a coordinate system; the empty set has no members. Their transmission through scripts, calculating practices and civilisations changes which written distinctions and operations become available; these changes have distinct histories. Philosophical void and QL's uncounted One address a further register. In the coordinate canon **P0** (Why? / Ground / Square A / Aether) names the implicate condition prior to differentiation, and **0/1** retains that ground through its mark. A written zero can refract this relation without becoming identical to all its other uses.

## In the argument

A sign [[13-s1-p0-sign-migrates|migrates between worlds]] when a receiving practice gives it a new permission while retaining its written inheritance. Making [[14-s1-p1-sunya-operational|śūnya calculable]] gives zero operations without granting every quotient. [[15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one|The empty-set successor]] produces one as the singleton containing the empty set; [[16-s1-p3-crossed-zero|the crossed zero]] retains the present knowing act within inscription. [[17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math|A zero denominator]] exposes an operation's restriction, and a changed construction must state which laws it preserves. The [[18-s1-p5-loan-returns|returned loan]] carries these differences back to the ground–mark relation, `0/1 ↔ 1/0`. As [[A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit|meta-sign]], zero makes the non-objectifiable pole legible through the determination that presupposes it; the pole itself has not become one more counted object.

## Layer rule

Historical and mathematical zero—placeholder, numeral, operator, origin, and `∅`—must remain distinct from QL `0`, the meta-sign of the uncounted Mono/One. [The uncounted One and polyvalent All](../A11-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md) retain different offices within one relation. In the von Neumann representation, `1 = {0}` and `2 = {0,1}` give exact singleton and successor constructions; their memberships do not exhaust the native ground–mark relation.
