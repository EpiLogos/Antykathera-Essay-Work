# C28 — Covenant / Primary Arbitration

## #0

A parent who says "because I am your father" invokes more than a preference. The title calls on an order of kinship, obligation and authority through which the instruction is supposed to have standing, and the child receives both the instruction and a place in that order. Lacan's Name-of-the-Father names this symbolic function: the paternal office takes its force from the relation between a person and the law they represent, so that a parent administers an order through which they, too, became a parent, and claiming its authority binds them to obligations toward the child.

**Covenant** is the explicit relation through which participants acknowledge a shared source-condition and bind finite authority to fidelity, scope and return. **Primary Arbitration** names the source-level relation from which a mediating office receives measure. It grants no finite actor possession of absolute source authority.

## #1

Action requires an authorised criterion. Latin *arbiter* named first a person present to see or hear, a witness, before the word named an appointed office that decides, and Latin could say *fidem alicui arbitrari*, to judge that credit is owed to someone, so that withness stands at the root of judgment. Three operations hide inside every authoritative account: a criterion distinguishes, deciding who and what may count, a delineation gives that difference a workable boundary, and an arbitration decides through the boundary and acts on the result. [The prior source relation](../../../quilt/final-argument-quilt-2026-08-23/COVENANT-ARBITRATION-AND-MEDIATING-OFFICES-SEAM.md) binds participants through obligation, promise, law and belonging, and interpretation, administration and transmission exercise the authority received through that binding. The mediating office receives an already arbitrated field, and its execution does not originate that field because participants repeatedly meet it through the office.

## #2

Priest, king and politician mediate received order through different forms of interpretation and power. A priest transmits or interprets an obligation, a king can personify the authority of a received order, and a political office decides through a constitution of participating powers. Repeated mediation can make the office appear generative, as the administrator of the relation comes to claim its source. [Mediating arbitration](C29-Mediating-Office-Derivative-Sovereignty.md) retains the office's real competence, [the protected account](C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim.md) insulates it, and [monoisation](C26-Monoisation-Counter-Generation.md) reverses its dependence.

## #3

Received authority takes different historical forms. Jewish, Christian and Islamic covenantal relations give priesthood, law and belonging different offices, and those particular arrangements are not derivable from a shared name of covenant. Political arbitration can be distributed across law, electorate, courts, parties and institutions. The operation here is this plurality's shared source/office question: which prior relation gives an actor standing, what decision does that standing permit, and where can its consequences return? Priest, king and politician name differentiated mediations within that question, and their arrangement supplies no universal chronological succession.

## #4

Delegation makes fidelity practical. The authorised agent needs a named role, bounded powers and a route for the consequences of work to change the commission, and return includes refusal when obedience would destroy the relation the office serves. [Prior reliance](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust.md) **grounds** this fidelity before the office can certify its own action, and [labour and cost](C53-Power-Delegated-Labour.md) make the undertaking consequential. Ostrom's [recognised local rule-making and nested governance](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture.md#ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture-q001) **compare** as a distinct institutional neighbour, qualified by her [warning against transplanting rigid principles](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture.md#ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture-q002).

## #5→0

An appeal's effective reach depends on the jurisdiction of the office that receives it. Prior standing empowers a received office, and different offices can alter different parts of the arrangement, so a hearing before an authority unable to alter the rule or commission does different work from an appeal that reaches rule-making power. The comparison requires jurisdiction and consequence to be identified, and it supplies no universal priest–king–politician chronology.

[Fides before arbitration](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#fides-before-arbitration) **historicises** the order of the terms: prior reliance comes before the commissioned office of Nomos, and renewed Credere comes after its exercise. The covenant can consequently receive a law without treating law as the origin of entrustment. Historical lexical witnesses and this sixfold relation stay distinct, and the traversal requires the deciding office to return its consequences to those whose reliance made it possible.

[Arbitration in crisis](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#arbitration-in-crisis) locates the covenant's finite deciding office within a field it receives. A decision can bind participants without becoming the source of their participation, so its return must reach the commission and criterion of decision when consequences disclose that either has failed the primary relation.

Covenant renews authority through answerability to the shared condition. It exceeds contract between supposedly self-sufficient parties, because their relation helps constitute the parties and the field in which they act, and their shared condition therefore enters what can be promised, delegated and held to account. Renewed authorisation remains finite: the office can decide, receive dissent and consequence, revise its scope and return its power without claiming to be the source it serves.
