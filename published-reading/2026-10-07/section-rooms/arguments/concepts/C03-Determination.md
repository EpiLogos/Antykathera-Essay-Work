# C03 — Determination

## #0

Draw a circle on a plane. The line gives a *here* by also giving a *there*: inside and outside arise through one stroke, and each lets us locate the other, so we can point to the edge, measure the interior and cross from one side to the other. Spinoza, in his letter to Jarig Jelles of 2 June 1674, named this work of finite determination negation. Matter considered without limit has no figure, he argued, and a body has its shape through the limits of its extension, so that figure, being nothing but determination, is made by marking where the shape ends. What looks like a shape's positive possession was achieved through its relation to what it excludes.

Determination, in this record's sense, is that act in general: the act through which a field becomes **this**, so that something can be distinguished, addressed, compared, remembered or acted upon. A distinction makes its terms available together, in a relation that lets one count as this and another as its alternative, and so gives difference actual consequence. The result is positive. A mark need not falsify what it discloses merely because the mark is finite, and a [faithful definition](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) therefore has to say something definite, while its defining act belongs to what it attempts to account for and stays available for further use and return.

## #1

The first distinction has two faces, since it identifies something and makes the difference by which it can be identified. Saying "this" selects from a field while holding enough continuity for the selected instance to be recognised again, as two parents hold one child distinct through a change of hands before anyone has settled on a name. The [copula](../A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md) **grounds** the point, because it relates a name to this instance in this circumstance and keeps identity through their difference. Repeating a name does not count its different bearers, and determination includes the individuation through which a name can reach anyone or anything at all.

The marked/unmarked distinction in [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form|Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form]] **compares** with this first distinction at one point: both make a contrast available, his within a calculus of indications, ours for the act to which a marked distinction becomes available. The unmarked state belongs to the rules of the calculus, and the subject-condition concerns the act for which a marking occurs, so a comparison has to keep those two operations apart. The exact passages on opening and re-entry have not yet been located in the selected 1969 edition, and until they are the comparison rests on the book's general account of drawing a distinction.

## #2

To determine positively is also to exclude. What falls outside the selected term helps give it a boundary, even when the account never names what it excluded, and "not selected" cannot mean "irrelevant to how the selection was made".

Three operations are easily run together here, and two Indian carriers separate them. [Apoha](C18-Apoha.md), the doctrine of exclusion associated with Dignāga, makes the excluded alternatives consequential to a term's meaning: to apprehend something as a cow is to exclude what is other than a cow, and what the word gathers depends on which differences it treats as relevant. [Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa](C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) differentiates alternatives and then gathers them into formative commitment. Negation denies, distinction lets terms differ, and selection makes an alternative operative, so reading all three as rejection loses the positive world-making the cut accomplishes.

The `−/+` determination gives this work its force. Withdrawal and extension stay co-present as directions of one differentiating movement, which is why a cut can become effective without its poles becoming independent substances. Our own word for knowing carries the point: Latin *scire*, to know, is traced on the standard account to a root meaning to cut or split, and *scientia*, science, descends from it. Knowing cuts, and the cut grows more exact as more of the relation between the thing cut and the cutter becomes intelligible.

## #3

A determination can recur as **another one of those**. In `X/x`, determining capacity becomes legible through a local instance, and the instance stays available to recurrence, comparison and change. A recurring dream, a rhythm or a thought discloses the capacity through another particular occurrence, so the occurrence changes while the capacity remains available for further differentiation.

Taylor's [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|core derivation]] **derives** the sequence: parent `/ = −/−`; conscious circumstance `0/1`; question and assertion `?/!`; force `−/+`; recurrence `X/x`; personed context `AM/IS`; differential horizon `∞/dx`; return `1/0`. The [complete order and its inversions](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) relate an achieved determination to the activity through which it becomes available. Each of the eight is a distinct joint of determining activity, and none is a synonym for another.

Measurement makes a further commitment. A unit, a comparison or a ratio renders a difference reckonable, and a [ratio](../A15-Ratio-Rationality-Measure-Reckoning-Harmony-Account.md) **defines** how determinate quantities are compared through a stated relation. The precision belongs to that relation. It cannot promote the chosen measure into the source of everything it measures.

## #4

In `AM/IS`, what can be predicated meets the one for whom it matters through a relation of address. Body, memory, task, inherited language and concern take part in what becomes this object or this decision. [Context](C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) makes that address effective within its horizon, and [operative measure](C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) apportions what the situated actor can do, know, desire, time and order.

An account of a selection is itself another selection. The [achieved form cannot absorb the act now accounting for it](C04-Formal-Limit.md), and this further selection reproduces the determining relation: a successful distinction stands, while its determining condition has not become its possession.

## #5→0

Three small cases show determination returning to its conditions. [Referential exclusion](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-exclusion) makes absent alternatives consequential to a term's meaning. A finite figure gives a distinction visible form. The act of determining selects and relates, and makes the result available for further inquiry.

The [zero-reception history](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/zero-reception.md#2--a-reception-story-also-requires-a-source) **sources** a fourth: a correction to the source of a claim changes the next assertion made from it. Nothaft's official abstract, which is what has been consulted, challenges the familiar story of a satanic zero and a ban on it. That abstract gives the direction of the article's argument and offers no evidence for any individual medieval case, so the next historical assertion has to change its warrant and its scope while the distinct mathematical operations stay available. A claim stays determinate by naming what its available evidence can test.

A completed determination enters the inherited field of the next act, and return makes its source, criterion, exclusions and consequences available again. For [operational parity](C45-Operational-Parity.md), a distinction made explicit has to change some actual operation if it claims efficacy, and a label alone has not performed that return.

Within a [[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD|region of encounter]], name and count make a participant discriminable, and an account becomes answerable when the region and the participant can return to alter it. A [faithful definition](C02-Faithful-Definition.md) is the one definite enough to use and related enough to answer for its use.
