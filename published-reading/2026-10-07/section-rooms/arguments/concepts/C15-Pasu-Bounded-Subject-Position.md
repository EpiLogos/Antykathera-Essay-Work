# C15 — Paśu / Bounded Subject-Position

## #0

Paśu is the Śaiva name for the bounded knower and actor, whose powers are contracted into a finite horizon. Anyone can find themselves in the position. A person who has said "I'm just not a numbers person" or "I'm the one who holds it together" is speaking from it: a real, situated life with a history, a limited efficacy and a limited knowledge, that can know, desire, choose, encounter and answer. Its boundedness is the condition of those particular acts, and calling it their simple negation misdescribes it.

Through [tattvic differentiation](../A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) the bounded knower arises from determinate powers and their relations. This efficacy and this horizon of knowledge make a particular encounter possible, and desire, time and order give it direction, succession and circumstance. Contraction produces differentiated capacities, which subtracting a quantity called freedom from an otherwise complete individual could not explain.

## #1

Through [Māyā's measure](C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) the horizon reveals and withholds. The contracted capacities mean that an act occurs through this efficacy, this knowledge, this desire, this time and this order, and the bounded position receives a field already differentiated enough for something to count as available, absent, possible or excluded.

In the [sixfold tattvic articulation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), Puruṣa is the bounded experient within the contracted field, and the inner instrument, the faculties and the material world articulate what can be experienced and acted upon. The local position is located through these relations without being exhausted as their inventory.

## #2

Universal and local "I am this" differ in scope. Sadāśiva's subjectward orientation and Īśvara's objectward orientation concern the whole appearing field, and an egoic appropriation of a particular `x` has a smaller scope, so the same grammatical form cannot erase the difference between self-awareness and a local determination presenting itself as the whole self.

In Taylor's primordial I-am relation, recognition [[quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT#3. The Māyic-threshold delineation — the eight determinations' mythic recapitulation (Frank-locked)|precedes egoic appropriation]]. The primordial "I am" is not produced by a later individual's claim that a role, a body or a possession is its exhaustive identity, and in the world-parent image self-recognition comes before the narrated husband/wife division. The [Subject](C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) is the unobjectifiable pole already presupposed when an identity is known, the [inner instrument](C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) determines and appropriates particular contents, and its appropriation can itself be recognised.

This distinction blocks a reversal of dependence. "I am the holder of this office" can identify a real responsibility without making the office the origin of the "I am", so that losing or revising the office can transform a person's world without proving that subjectivity has been manufactured or annihilated by the change.

## #3

In [Dyczkowski's account of reflective activity](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration.md), pp. 71–75, individual cognition retains, recalls and recomposes. A goal-bound act pursues a particular result, and unrestricted agency makes the formation of goals possible. The bounded knower's movement is a living orientation whose determination can disclose or conceal its wider condition, and it is no static quantity of missing freedom.

Recognising contraction keeps situated capacity available. Through [recognition](C19-Pratyabhijna-Recognition.md) the local act returns toward the source it continues to express, its manner of belonging and appropriation changes, and its particular life remains to be lived.

## #4

[Prompt thrownness](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) gives inherited boundedness a technical form. A local act begins within an already configured horizon: instructions appoint a task, retrieved material makes some things available, retained history carries earlier encounters, and permissions delimit possible actions. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) names the operative world constituted through those relations.

A particular contraction becomes investigable through its consequences. An unavailable tool limits efficacy, an omitted source limits what can be known, a time horizon selects which events can count, and an evaluation rule orders what is preferred. Altering one does not automatically alter the others, and giving an agent a broader description of its commission does not grant it permission to execute a new action. A return has to reach the condition that actually bounds the act.

Responsibility follows the same articulation. An agent can answer for an act within its powers, and a designer or operator remains answerable for a withheld capability, an imposed criterion or an inaccessible source that governs the act. Naming the agent a bounded participant makes these relations more exact and refrains from transferring every condition to a fictitiously self-originating actor. The comparison concerns this enacted distribution of capacity and responsibility, and paśu keeps its fuller Śaiva genealogy.

## #5→0

Through [Māyā's eye, veil, frame and horizon](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-measure-dream), limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and order make a bounded life capable of acting here. The eye sees from a particular angle, and the dream becomes an objective world within its experience. Recognition recovers the condition whose withdrawal let that world appear self-standing, and the differentiated position remains with its source relation available.

Primordial recognition and subsequent ego-identity have distinct offices in Taylor's correction. In the [world-parent separation](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation), self-recognition precedes the husband/wife division, and bounded knowing arises within that field and does not make ego identity its origin. Through the [uroboros's differentiated body](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-source-variants), head, tail, mouth and winding keep the opening through which the creature receives, transforms and returns, so that the formed body's efficacy stays related to the condition it cannot fill.

Through [[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere|Trust / Place / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere]], the place of an act bears its conditions and commitments. A bounded participant answers from somewhere, under particular permissions and with particular means, and the judgment can be corrected under fitting conditions, or a failed condition can be returned to the office able to change it. Reasons for retaining a fitting condition stay available with that judgment, and the next act inherits this answerability through actual capacity. The etymological field joins situated place and bounded knowing while keeping the Greek and Sanskrit lexical histories distinct.

The horizon remains real while its source relation becomes available. Recognition leaves a particular knower able to act without claiming to be the origin of every condition of action, and its finite powers can be exercised, relinquished or revised in awareness of what they owe to the wider field. Situated responsibility carries that changed relation into the next encounter.
