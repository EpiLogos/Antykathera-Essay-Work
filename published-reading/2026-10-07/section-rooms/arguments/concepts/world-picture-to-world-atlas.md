A whole sphere or torus cannot be one chart onto an open subset of the plane; compactness alone rules out that global coordinate representation. Metric preservation is another question. A sphere's curvature obstructs a flat isometric rendering, whereas a flat torus admits locally isometric charts. A local map therefore does not invariably distort.
