# C08 — Context / Context Frame

## #0

Latin *textus* meant texture, tissue or an interwoven structure, and *contexere* meant to weave or join together, so that *contextus* named the resulting connection or coherence: fabric and connected speech already shared one family of words. The "text" of a life is one's account of it, necessarily authored and held from a given perspective, and a context is the weave of many such views. Context in this record's sense is the active relation through which a determination has meaning and possibility, and a **Context Frame** is a bounded holding of those conditions through which a particular inquiry can proceed. The boundary lets an operation become intelligible to itself, and it leaves that operation inside its world.

Take a task handed to an agent: *summarise this contract.* The frame selects what is currently held together, which document counts as the contract, which sources and rules apply, what the agent may do and who authorised it. [Worldhood includes conditions that this selection has not captured](C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md), so the frame is neither an indefinitely receding excuse nor a finished container waiting to be filled. It holds enough to proceed and stays open to what it left outside.

## #1

In [operational self-holding](../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/QL-Essay-Rewrite.md), Part III §3.1, a process gives itself enough form to continue processing. A temporary boundary stabilises a relation without requiring its movement to cease, and the frame is itself an operation within what it frames.

A local relation can articulate its own ground, distinctions, means and return, and so become a participant within another relation. The same sixfold therefore recurs at a different grain. Taylor calls this [recursive architecture](../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/epi-logos-plugin-resources-copy-10-07/resources/updated-ql-mef/non-dual-binary/the-immutable-subject-and-the-matheme-of-its-recognition.md) "The context-frame architecture", with each local operation determining what the positions do there, so recurrence leaves every frame free to name its six positions in its own terms.

Within the native [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|parent and inverse return]], six determinations are bracketed while each parent can hold the complete body in its own orientation. A nested frame recurs inside this field and adds no primary determination to it.

## #2

A working frame makes its inclusions consequential. Sources supply material and standing, rules constrain permissible transformations, tools make particular operations available, permissions establish authority to act, and histories retain how the current state arose. Selecting them does not confer finality on the selection.

[Notation, nomological rules and consequential exclusions](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **ground** the field within which a technical judgment can be made. The frame has to distinguish a source from a world-object, a lens from an evaluator, and an authority from an instruction, and reducing all six to "context" would hide the relations the frame exists to expose.

A local judging agent occupies the functional knower's office, its held sources, rules and tools serve as means, and the claims or alternatives addressed are known. In the containing person's inquiry the same apparatus serves as means, and a further inspection can make the local centre known in another act, without making the first-person condition one more item in its inventory. These relative offices stay within the whole of Life and Mind in which determining, means and determined belong together.

## #3

A frame can contain a **local original/reference office**. A selected source-field can serve as the basis against which downstream projections are compared, though the source-field is itself a constructed determination relative to the wider world. It can occupy Bimba's office locally and remain Pratibimba in the encompassing relation.

[A map can govern further comparison within its stated frame](../A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) while depending on wider originating sources, and it does not become the ultimate Original because another map derives from it. Two frames can differ over source, object, lens, gauge, reference or permission, and an atlas has to preserve which relation changed, so that their disagreement is not flattened into two opinions about one finished object.

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics|present configuration and its actual history]] condition one another, so that a returned projection can change the reference field for the next inquiry.

## #4

A commission gives this locality a practical test. Human intention can ground an agent's task, and the written prompt is already a determination of that intention. Literal compliance with the first wording can fail the purpose when the task meets an unforeseen constraint. The human occupies the determining source office relative to this commissioned agent, and that agent can in turn determine a bounded task for another agent while its own task stays answerable to the containing commission. Human0/agent1 names these positional, recursive offices, and the prompt remains a determinate representation of a purpose it does not exhaust.

[Delegation preserves who authorised what, which means are available, and where a discovered contradiction must return](../A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md). The arriving agent [inherits conditions it did not wholly author](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md), and a failure concerning a source, instruction or permission has to reach the office able to correct that condition, since local compliance alone cannot settle a contradiction in the wider purpose.

## #5→0

The [changing window](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-regard-refraction) **figures** how a bounded view acquires effective authority. Its pane, angle, source and exclusions determine what can be compared here, and an achieved projection can become a genuine local Bimba for further readings, while returned experience can revise that reference, which stays pratibimba toward wider sources. A frame's ability to govern an inquiry is therefore different from a claim to exhaust its horizon.

In [Encounter-in-Region](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#encounter-in-region), a chosen source, rule or permission changes the inquiry through the conditions its selection makes operative. Returning the result can expose a faulty selection or local reference and make it answerable for correction, and where the condition is fitting, a corrected result or a reasoned retention is inherited by the next act without any change to the condition.

[Situated return](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#topos-situated-return) **defines** how the bounded holding of sources, rules and permissions receives an addressable account through Logos. Articulating those conditions does not place the framing act outside them, and returned evidence revises the frame's next use while its local reference office stays answerable within a wider relation.

The [travelling jigsaw's prior rule of fit](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-native-and-archetypal-field) **embodies** the same organising office: it governs the next placement. Encounter makes sources, projection and omitted continuations available for judgment, and an exposed failure can alter a placement, the reference against which it was judged, or the rule itself when that rule has failed. An adequate rule stays, with the corrected placement and its reasons available for the next decision, and the frame keeps its bounded organising office within a world it cannot exhaust.

[Diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md) makes the shaping conditions readable through their effects. Contextualisation succeeds when the account can show how its means, exclusions and position shaped what it says, and when returned resistance can reach those terms for correction or warranted retention.

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis|Con-text-through-Diaphaneity]] makes the held relations available to Regard. Con-text-through-Diaphaneity is the generated relation, and Regard is the operation that becomes available there: it can turn toward the exposed relation and answer what the frame's inclusions and exclusions have done.

[Another participant can externalise enough of its ground to meet this one](../A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) while retaining its own source-relations. What each frame has disclosed can change their encounter without absorbing either into the other's account. A bounded frame stays useful through its corrigibility within [the world that bears it](C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md).
