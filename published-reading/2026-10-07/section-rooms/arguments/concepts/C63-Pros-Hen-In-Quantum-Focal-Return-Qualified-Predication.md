# C63 — Pros Hen / In Quantum — Focal Return and Qualified Predication

## #0

A body has health, a regimen preserves it, and a complexion indicates it. All three are called healthy, and none of them has health in the same way. Aristotle's example shows how one word can run true across different things without turning into one definition: the uses are ordered by their relation to one focus. **Focal return** and **qualified predication** let a claim be fully true in its stated relation without turning that relation into exhaustive sameness. Two source-distinct grammars meet here: *pros hen*, "toward one", directs different senses toward one focal term, and *in quantum*, "insofar as", states the respect under which a predicate holds. Their conjunction is the essay's authorial operation, and it does not identify Aristotle's focal account with Eckhart's theological predication.

## #1

The health example has to be walked through its relations. A person has health, a diet is called healthy insofar as it contributes to that health, and a complexion is called healthy insofar as it indicates it. Producing and indicating are different operations, and the three uses are intelligibly ordered without saying that the diet, the complexion and the person possess health in an identical way.

[Considering a person insofar as just](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/formal-limit.md#eckhart-qualified-predication) predicates justice under that respect. Kurt Flasch's *Meister Eckhart: Philosopher of Christianity* restores the weight of Eckhart's primary determinations, Being, Oneness, Truth and Goodness, as constitutive, so that their reality is active in whatever is, is one, is true or is good. *In quantum* says how that reality is present through its determination: the just person, insofar as just, is Justice itself, and Justice is actual in this deed, in these circumstances, with these consequences, the particularity being the mode in which justice becomes manifest. The qualification does not certify every act of the person, and it differs from several uses referring to one focus, as producing health and indicating health do.

In [Taylor's worked health relation](<../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/definition-of-god-working/The Definition of God — Draft 3.md>), a person bears the condition, a diet contributes to it, and a complexion gives a sign of it. Changing the diet can alter the condition, and reading the complexion can disclose it without causing it, so their ordered dependence makes the shared word intelligible while keeping contribution and indication distinct.

## #2

The qualifying respect prevents the relation from spreading without warrant. Taylor's just person **insofar as just** is predicated through justice, and the qualification concerns that respect. Likewise, an image can truly disclose its source through a determinate aperture without exhausting the source.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/flasch/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart|Flasch's reading of Eckhart]] concerns primary determinations, analogy, predicate and reversal, and qualified predication. Taylor takes the stated respect into a broader relation between source and aperture, in which an image can be a true disclosure without exhausting what it discloses. That native use remains distinct from verification of the wording of a particular Eckhart sermon or Parisian question.

## #3

The optical image gives the qualification a visible operation: one source appears through differentiated colour and aperture, and each disclosure remains indexed to its conditions. The image's truth does not require it to become the unrefracted source, and recognising its conditions need not drain it of truth. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C06-Copula-Identification-with-Difference|Copula]] keeps identification through difference active, and [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C09-Diaphaneity|Diaphaneity]] lets the mediating conditions show through what appears.

*Insofar as* also qualifies an epistemic assertion. A historical assertion, a formal derivation, a symbolic image and a technical proposal state how they run true, and the respect determines the reach of the predicate, so that it is no generic "perhaps" attached to an otherwise established claim.

## #4

In [[quilt/2026-08-02-PARALLEL-HARMONISED-QUILT#2.3.6 Aristotle, Gebser, and Epi-Logos|a coordinated inquiry across registers]], each received relation changes what can be understood while preserving its object and warrant. A mathematical quotient, a psychic transformation and a technical return retain different objects and tests while their specified operations meet within one inquiry. Proportional analogy compares relations across distinct wholes, focal relation orders distinct senses towards one office, and qualification limits a predicate's respect, and one cannot silently stand in for another.

A [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C60-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence|reflection can lead knowledge while depending ontologically on its source]], and causal efficacy, epistemic precedence and originating authority then have different respects. An [[section-rooms/arguments/A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority|appointed office can bind within delegated scope]] while its power remains derived from the source relation and open to a returned commission. These applications keep their full local force by retaining the relation that licenses them.

## #5→0

In [Aristotle's focal account](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/ancient-philosophy/DEVELOPMENT-ancient-philosophy.md#ancient-focal-causes), healthy things relate to health through preserving, producing, indicating or being capable of it, and medical uses likewise refer differently to an art. In the selected IV.2 argument, different senses of being have a primary reference, and substance is the primary object of inquiry. The relation orders different senses and gives each no identical definition.

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy|Comparing operations]] requires the transformed relation and retained difference to remain available. Through [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C61-Symbolon-Disclosure-Architecture|formal, imaginal and epistemic disclosure]], a reader can follow the precise respect into another application and receive what changes there. The [[section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero|returned determination]] remains a real source for subsequent comparison while depending on what its aperture cannot exhaust, and a warranted correction can revise the claim or its use, while a fitting relation can be retained for reasons that the next act inherits.
