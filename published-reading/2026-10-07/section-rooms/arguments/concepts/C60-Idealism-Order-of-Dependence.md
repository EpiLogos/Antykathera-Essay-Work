# C60 — Idealism / Order of Dependence

## #0 — A positive order of being

One word, *consciousness*, has been made to carry four things at once, and accounts of it talk past each other because of it. It is used for awareness, the bare fact that something is known, for life and mind, the whole working apparatus of memory, perception, language and feeling, for the ontological root of existence, and for both sides of the mind Jung distinguished, the conscious and the unconscious. The essay separates them by the three Indian offices and a whole. Subjective Immediacy is the knower, *pramātṛ*, Objective Internality is the means, *pramāṇa*, and World is the known, *prameya*. Objective Internality is what the essay calls **Life** and **Mind**, one office seen from two sides, since a mind is lived and a life is minded, and **I-Consciousness**, the *aham* of the Trika, is the whole in which the three offices are distinguishable.

**Idealism names the dependence of determinate being upon the whole relation of appearing.** Within that whole the order is **Subject / Consciousness → constituted means → determinate object**. The middle term names mind's mediating office, and Mind is the exhaustive name of reality in no sense the order requires. The order is ontological: an object is a real determination of the whole, and no independently self-grounding item from which the whole relation can subsequently be assembled. In [this order of dependence](../A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md), the object's identity, properties and differences are real determinations of the articulated field, and another object can explain its production within that field while presupposing the relation of knower, means and known.

Bernardo Kastrup's analytic idealism holds that there is one field of consciousness, of which individual minds are dissociated segments, and that the physical world is how mental processes appear from outside, and the nesting the essay writes is that dissociation made exact, a bounded `0/1` within a wider `0/1`. Federico Faggin, who designed the first commercial microprocessor, argues from the inventor's side of the machine that consciousness and free will are irreducible and known only from within, which is the office of Subjective Immediacy, and Rupert Spira's non-dual teaching locates the knowing of experience as never itself an object of experience. William James had already argued that "consciousness" names no entity but the function of knowing, Bergson read the brain as an organ that selects and limits what reaches the personal mind, and Whitehead made experience the stuff of every actual occasion. Each is a refraction of the same order of dependence, and the QL nesting holds them together without forcing them into one doctrine.

## #1 — What an additional object cannot supply

Appearing is present even in the act that doubts an appearance. A doubt changes an assertion within the field, and its own occurrence remains. [Subject](C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) names the condition to which an object becomes available, and a sensation, a brain, a model or an introspective report can each be made an object of further inquiry, though more detail about one such object does not change its office into the condition of the entire relation.

The positive ontological step is that a thing's determinacy belongs to its being-disclosed: its identity, properties and differences arise within the articulated field. A causal account can explain a thing's production through other things, and idealism locates this real explanatory work within the relation its terms presuppose, instead of treating the account as a production of that relation from outside it. An account of the source is another determination, and the source-condition is not exhausted by becoming a term in the account.

## #2 — The means makes a world inhabitable

[Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) gives the middle term its actual operations. Attention, memory, valuation, language and comparison take part in what can be known and done, and [the conditions of worldhood](C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) include the relations through which something counts as a thing, an action or a reason, so that the means constitutes an inhabitable field and does not pass a ready-made world between already finished poles.

Objectivity includes resistance, persistence, measurement and shared consequence. A failed prediction returns an encountered difference to the means by which it was made, and that correction is possible because a local account does not determine everything the whole can disclose. The private ego, with its wishes and self-image, is itself one formation in the field, and World in mind does not mean World at the disposal of that ego.

A **paradigm** is a relatively durable organisation of these means, and a **project** is a determinate form thrown forth through them. In [Genesis / Paradigm / Project / Epi-Logos](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md), a project can become a condition of a later act and can return an encounter that changes the enacted paradigm, so that Paradigm, Project and World keep different offices even while their effects pass into one another.

## #3 — Prior in which respect?

A reflection can be encountered before its source is understood. It is then epistemically prior in that inquiry while remaining dependent on the source in being, and it can prompt an action and so cause a later event. A proof can depend on premises in a logical order, and an inquiry can identify a condition of possible experience in a transcendental order. These directions can cross without becoming the same dependency.

[The first-moving mirror](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) gives a concrete test: a model leads a later encounter, and its initiative does not make it the ontological author of its own conditions. [Focal and qualified predication](C63-Pros-Hen-In-Quantum-Focal-Return-Qualified-Predication.md) keeps "prior" and "depends" attached to the respect in which they are asserted.

The inversion occurs when causal efficacy or epistemic precedence is exchanged for originating authority. A projection can become so influential that its source is permitted to appear only through the projection's categories, and a paradigm can treat its finite horizon as self-grounding reality. The criticism concerns this change of office and leaves the real efficacy through which a project changes the world untouched.

## #4 — The reference is real and source-dependent

A local Bimba can serve as the genuine reference for a bounded inquiry while remaining a pratibimba relative to wider sources, and its being revisable does not cancel its present source office. A returned observation may change the reference, and later projections can then inherit that change, so that the sourceward movement makes the reference's authority more exact by revealing what it depends upon.

A technological relay gives this relation a worked instance. A forecast informs an intervention, and the result becomes an input to a later forecast, so that the model can have caused a change while still depending on inherited measurements, material conditions and a commission. Correcting an observation, revising a model and changing the criterion of success are different returns, and their practical force depends on the correction reaching the condition that a later act actually inherits.

The idealist order locates this entire investigation within knower, means, known and whole. Making the means more articulate does not make it a complete inventory of the Subject, and the condition to which the relay and its investigation appear retains its office as the account becomes more exact. This is a distinction of dependence, and no missing item that further inspection promises to supply.

## #5→0 — Dependence becomes a manner of acting

[Objective Internality](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) and [Objective Co-Internality](../A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) return the order to consequential worlds among agencies. Relations contribute to their constitution and are no connections attached after isolated units have become complete, and local agency persists through that participation and can change through encounters with others.

[Epi-Logos](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md#01--epi-logos--paradigmatic-reflexivity) makes a recognised dependency operative in the paradigm. A distinction can change how an answer is received, what a model is asked to do or which consequence its evaluator includes, and the achieved account returns to its conditions without claiming a position outside the whole relation.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) carries this precision into comparison: participating terms retain the different ways in which they depend and correspond. The conclusion remains affirmative, since the determination is real, its power to act is real, and its relation to what it cannot exhaust is constitutive of that reality.
