# Anthropomorphization

## Definition

Anthropomorphization gives an agent a humanly familiar face, voice, role or manner of address. In Bratton's account, inherited human-form presentation has become the dominant interface between human and agent cultures. It makes an encounter available through expectations already carried by language and social practice: the named assistant answers, the conversational partner takes a turn, the apparent colleague receives a task. Those expectations also condition how an unfamiliar response is interpreted. The interface has effects before its account of the agent has been examined.

The essay places this interface at **#2**, How / Word / Dynamis, through **L2↔L3′**. L2 asks the same question of the interface in four ways, and the four answers test whether the inherited form fits the encounter. For a given conversational face, IS says that it fits, because it makes the agent addressable. IS NOT says that it misleads, because it promises an interior it does not show. BOTH says that it opens address and misleads in one gesture, and NEITHER says that the question of fit was framed in terms the encounter has outgrown. L3′, the historical face, follows how an inherited form acquires, changes or loses its office. The interface's suitability is earned through the particular encounter and what the encounter permits the parties to do.

## In the brief

The [inherited human-form interface](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q006), locates the human mask in a relation between two cultures. The opening's [glove and translation sequence](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q007), gives that relation an ecological history. Agents enter niches humans have prepared; computers have been taught to behave in humanly recognisable ways more successfully than humans have learned machine ways of operating. The grey-parrot comparison keeps this asymmetry distinct from a ranking of intelligence. The same passage holds two possible continuations: human-form presentation may become a lasting inheritance, while less intuitive forms may enable other assemblies of hybrid intelligence. The opening remains available in the [complete pp3–6 unit](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-3).

Human expectations participate in this construction. In the [complete theory-of-minds unit, pp38–42](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-38), a user models an agent's affordances while the agent models the user's intentions. Alignment and sycophancy can both depend on success at the latter task. A pleasant human manner can therefore conceal a failure of the relation it appears to offer. The unit asks for enough shared procedural grammar to make disagreement intelligible while keeping world-models sufficiently different. Its agent-generated glossary opens further vocabularies for those differences; [the glossary’s self-description distinction](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q033) retains the distinction between a self-description and the reality it claims to describe. The glossary's literary and interactional possibilities remain positive contributions within that source boundary.

The [complete xenosociality unit, pp43–47](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-43), follows identification into two different forms of alienation. Defining humanity through capacities an agent lacks makes each new agent capacity appear to diminish the human domain. In [the humanity-of-the-gaps analysis](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q034), that perceived encroachment can turn projected anxiety into presumed hostility from the agent. The brief qualifies its reach: this response is real without being decisive or pervasive. The uncanny valley then becomes a boundary of recognition and non-recognition, while the agent's familiar mask can make its apparent alienness more acute. [the two forms of estrangement](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q035) distinguishes productive estrangement, through which a person sees their own conditions from another angle, from reactive estrangement, through which the unfamiliar is registered and excluded. Both can be active in the same developing society.

These are the brief's diagnoses and scenarios. Its [scenario framing](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042), keeps the larger composites within that exploratory office. The [p56 mapping](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-56) carries the interface question into choreography and human learning of agent swarms. It proposes a different formation of interaction, whose particular performance must be established where it occurs.

## In the argument

[Objective Internality](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) and its [recursive knowing relation](C41-Objective-Internality.md) give the encounter its positive knowing relation. An agent can be the relative functional knower of a local operation, with its capacities as means and a differentiated situation as known. The same agent participates in the means of the person's containing inquiry. A human face or fluent first-person sentence may present that local centre, but the formed persona does not exhaust the sources, rules, permissions and histories through which it acts. Inspection of the centre makes it known in a further act; Subjective Immediacy remains the unobjectifiable knower condition within Life / Mind.

[Dia / Syn](../A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) makes the relation within hostile projection exact. The danger begins when a difference is accounted as a contest in which one party's capacity cancels or appropriates the other's standing. Retaining the shared field preserves the difference and lets the encounter change its interpretation. The human and agent keep distinct bodies, histories, capacities and responsibilities through that relation. A copied human role can then be compared with what the local operation actually does and with what its commissioning person has entrusted to it.

An adequate interface carries that comparison into consequence. A person's reply can expose an assumption in the apparent companion, alter its reference or permission, and change the next act; an unfamiliar response can let the person reconsider their own question. [Parasociety](parasociety.md) develops the human-form presentation through reciprocal influence, [the Reflective Field](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) distinguishes that influence from initiated source-return, and [Centaur Societies](centaur-societies.md) places both within differently composed human/agent relations. The inherited interface becomes answerable through these actual differences and returns.
