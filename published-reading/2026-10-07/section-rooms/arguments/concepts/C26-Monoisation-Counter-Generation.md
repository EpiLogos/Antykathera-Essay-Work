# C26 — Monoisation / Counter-Generation

## #0

Our own words keep the inversion. *Poly* is *polys*, many, and the *poly* of *monopoly* is *pōlein*, to sell, so in a monopoly the One stands where the many should be and faces, in their place, only what is sold. Aristotle records the first instance in the chapter where he separates provision from acquisition: Thales, foreseeing a large olive harvest, hired every press in Miletus and Chios and let them out at whatever price he chose.

**Monoisation** promotes a local determination into the sole source or measure of its relational field. **Counter-Generation** names the resulting reversal, in which the local form claims to generate the field that conditioned it. Mono in Mono/Poly names relational wholeness, and monoisation names a part's occupation of that whole's source-office.

## #1

In [Mono / Poly](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-mono-poly-two-ones/taylor-2026-mono-poly-two-ones.md), [the Two Ones](C49-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md) distinguish Self-Identity and Self-Difference as aspects of one complete relation. The Any and All arise through that prior relating activity, since aggregating independent units already requires it. A local determination is generated within this field and can express it without exhausting or possessing it, and its efficacy is real precisely through relations that exceed its local boundary.

## #2

The reversal runs local `1` → occupied source-office → field redescribed as derivative of that `1`. A name, a measure, an institution or a self-image forgets its received conditions and makes those conditions answer to its own account, so that the criterion becomes generative in its own telling and received power is redescribed as self-originating power. A focal centre retains a legitimate office where its conditions, scope and effects remain available to the field that sustains it.

## #3

Division with monopolised recombination makes the reversal social. Actionable units lose sight of their horizontal dependencies, while the apparatus monopolises recombination, and a central measure then appears to supply the unity whose relational conditions it has concealed.

Counter-generation shows its arithmetic where a [culture's internal plurality](../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md) is suppressed under one measure, because its counterculture can become organised through opposition to that measure. Each camp then counts the whole span between them as the other's departure, `+2` from one end and `−2` from the other, and each needs the other as the negative through which it knows itself. Where the counter preserves the same frame, two monocultures confront one another, and replacing the occupant has left the source-claim intact. Restoring a common relation lets the opposed positions remain determinate while their governing frame becomes answerable.

## #4

Coordination, temporary arbitration and delegated authority remain necessary offices, and the question is whether their warrant, scope and consequences stay answerable to the field. A focal measure can organise a task while admitting that another aperture or consequence requires its revision. [The protected account](C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim.md) insulates the criterion, while [covenant](C28-Covenant-Primary-Arbitration.md) and [the mediating office](C29-Mediating-Office-Derivative-Sovereignty.md) authorise action through received standing and a consequential return.

## #5→0

[Nourishment and Saturnine consumption](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-metabolism) diverge through what the body's taking allows to emerge next. Assimilation renews generation, and devouring becomes counter-generation where it prevents birth and differentiation from continuing.

The return retains differentiated powers and their shared conditions, and it does not defeat one monoculture by enthroning its counter. A [local `(1)`](../A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) remains enclosed by a condition it has forgotten, and recognising the enclosing relation lets polarity stay real without becoming war. [The commons](C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly.md) gives this relation institutional pressure: set terms, refuse, revise, leave and reconnect without losing every route to shared life. An apportioned office returns its work and effects to the economy that sustains it.

[Job's friends](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/biblical/job/WHOLE.md#job-protected-account-return) **figure** a partial account of justice taking the office of the whole. The friends repeatedly translate Job's contradiction into confirmation of the same judgement, arresting the return by which the shared moral world could change, and their rebuke and Job's intercession reopen that relation. The account's competence survives when its claim to final jurisdiction returns to the encounter it had excluded.
