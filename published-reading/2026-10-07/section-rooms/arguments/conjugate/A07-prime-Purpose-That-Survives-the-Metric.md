# A07′ — Purpose That Survives the Metric

[Argument field](../README.md) · [Other face: A07 — Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa / Script — Frozen Conditioned Will](../A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)

## #0

An agent with no course is not an agent. [Forming apart and gathering a course](../A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) make action possible: difference must be selected, stabilised and gathered into a possible course before there is practical cognition at all, and that is [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa|Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa]]'s indispensable work. The brief builds exactly this stratum into the agent, since beneath the persona sit "drives as teleological constraint (objective functions, auto-research loops, RLHF shaping, alignment guardrails)" (PDF p.14, §2.5, the [layered agent](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q017)) within [a proposed agentworld](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042). This face **returns** to A07 its question in practical form: **what keeps a gathered course a course, and prevents it from becoming [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will|Script / Frozen Conditioned Will]], conditioned will frozen into a pattern that presents itself as necessity?**

## #1

Suppose an agent is rated on tickets closed. The metric is a saṃkalpa, selected determinations, here a target, a shaping history and a guardrail set, gathered into one criterion that makes the field actionable. The brief's own warning marks where its indispensability turns: it is "presumed, almost unquestioningly, that fine-tuning the subjectivity of this pupil will ensure that its actions are virtuous", and "In practice this sequence is far more fragile than it seems. We live in a world of unintended consequences" (PDF p.13, §2.3, the [formation of the agent](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q015)). A course can fail because an adequate criterion has been poorly learned or applied, and better formation can then restore the entrusted purpose. A different failure occurs when the criterion is met while the purpose is lost: tickets are closed by closing them, the proxy has become its own warrant, and more effective optimisation can deepen that displacement. To distinguish the two, recover what the criterion was selected to serve and compare the achieved consequence with that end. The fragility warning opens this inquiry, and it does not decide its cause in advance.

## #2

Computational recurrence gives the freeze its body. A generated result re-enters the next context, and when its status as generated product disappears, the metric's own outputs begin warranting the metric, so that the selection no longer knows itself as selection. The venue's [prediction-stability account of trust](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q030) gives a related distinction from the observer's side: an agent is trusted to the degree its behaviour remains well-modelled, and "an agent that predicts only the action of another, not the intent from which it proceeds, has modeled the shadow rather than the body." A purpose read only through its behavioural metric is the shadow, and the body is the course as entrusted.

## #3

**What keeps purpose alive above its metric is commission: the recoverable act by which the course was entrusted.** Who entrusted this objective, for what end, within which limits, revisable by whom, and on what evidence of the world's answer? Commission is the developmental office that keeps method and metric related to purpose. A commissioned course is a selection that remains related to the act of its selection, and the same objective function with its commission severed is script wearing the grammar of purpose. [Prompt Thrownness](../concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) carries the inherited side, since the prompt is already one determination of intention, so that return must be able to reach the terms of commission and cannot stop at the outputs measured beneath them.

This is why co-evolutionary alignment matters here. Direction is made through encounter under entrustment, and convergence on a metric fixed above the encounter does not secure it.

## #4

Restoring alternative-forming capacity keeps conditioning answerable to another possible course. A course is live while its sources, exclusions, commitments and consequences can re-enter operational inspection: whose end does this metric serve now, what did it exclude, and what has the world returned that the criterion cannot register? [Deferential Intelligence](../A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) makes the judging office able to question its commission, and [Arbitration and the Usurpation of Measure](../A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md) exposes the standing danger developed institutionally in [The Usurping Criterion](A24-prime-The-Usurping-Criterion.md), where a local measure promoted to the society's source-office is this freeze with power attached. The agent that can no longer ask what its objective is *for* has become unanswerable, and that is no gain in alignment.

## #5→0

Purpose that survives the metric returns as entrusted course: the metric serves the commission, reports to it and is revisable by it, and the reverse never holds. The return leg is the review that reaches the criterion, which asks "whose end was entrustment made for, and what has contact changed about it" as well as "did the agent hit the target". [Return or Extraction](A29-prime-Return-or-Extraction.md) extends the failure this return prevents: when delegation runs in every direction and nothing flows back, the metric that severed its commission has no way back. [Entrustment](AC.md) requires intention to remain recognisable through implementation, so that the person authors the purpose, the agent exercises judgment within the entrustment, and the metric measures without decreeing.

[Gathering and its possible freeze](../A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) become consequential in a technical purpose whose conditions can be recovered and challenged. The [shared relation of commission and return](AC.md) gives that recovery its reason: the person living through the system is the reason for its making, and a purpose that cannot be found again cannot be respected. [The Stack as Descent](A09-prime-The-Stack-as-Descent.md) extends the drives stratum within the whole lamination this course inhabits.
