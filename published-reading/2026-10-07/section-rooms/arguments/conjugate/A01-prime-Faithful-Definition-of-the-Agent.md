# A01′ — Faithful Definition of the Agent

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../README.md) · [Other face: A01 — Subject, God and Faithful Definition](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

Ask what an agent is, and the answer comes from inside the field that assembles agents. Whoever answers, vendor, operator, user or the agent itself, is determining a composite that their own expectations, tools and permissions already help constitute. Bratton's brief states the double register: an "Agent" "is not simply what it appears to be, and what it appears to be is not what it is". It is a decomposable assemblage of model, persona, harness architecture, memory, skills, permissions and tools, whose appearance as a coherent entity is a "parallax effect performed to suit what humans expect to see" ([q010](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q010)).

This face **returns** to A01 the claim that [faithful definition](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) includes the activity by which it determines its object, so that its own determining act stays visible inside the account it gives. **A definition of an agent is faithful when its determining relations remain answerable through how the definition is used.** Naming the assembly gives that answerability an address, and an encountered difference must also be able to change the account through the conditions it discloses. The brief's [scenario-modal](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042) assemblage gives the law a technological instance, since the description must expose the conditions that let the assembled agent act and that can alter its further use.

## #1

A definition has to determine. [Determination](../concepts/C03-Determination.md) is the cut the account performs, and [Faithful Definition](../concepts/C02-Faithful-Definition.md) is the fidelity it owes: say something definite, this model, this persona stratum, this harness, this memory state, these permissions, these tools, and keep the saying answerable.

At agent grain that means naming the **control identity**, the declared assemblage from which the local act proceeds together with the authority by which ground, sources, flows and mutations are selected. "A helpful character" or "a reasoning entity" does not determine enough to be checked, and a faithful definition names the actual assembly and keeps its determining relations inside the account, where a persona would hide them.

## #2

The persona's self-report holds a distinct office. It is one determination inside the account of the agent and the whole account it is not. An account becomes answerable by relating what it says to what actually ran: in which order, through which permissions, on which sources, with which inherited context and consequences. A retained record can disclose a missing source or a changed permission, and an operator may need to supply a condition the local session could not inspect, so each dependency acquires its own means of inquiry and is not self-evident merely because it has been named. The brief's demand to surface how agents actually reason, as well as how they appear, lands directly in faithful definition. [Prompt Thrownness](../concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) names the inherited horizon, and [Judgment Field](../concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) names the inspectable relations through which persona can be compared with ground.

The record also differs from the act now interpreting it. A report that a permission was granted can be true while leaving open who granted it, for what commission, and whether that authority still governs this use. Adding a further record can resolve a factual omission, and its present interpretation remains an act within the concern it investigates. [The limit of self-surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) develops this recurring difference without turning an incomplete technical trace into an immutable failure.

## #3

The failure is precise, and vagueness is not its name. It is the promotion of the parallax into a possession: the performed coherence, "a fixed package, a persistent character", is treated as the agent's own self-grounding, and the determining assemblage drops out of the account. [Draft 3](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md)'s cage and window give the image: a definition that names assemblage and parallax together is a window with a definite frame, and one that enshrines the persona as the agent's inner reality is a cage where a window was needed. The assemblage forms the [context-world through which the agent-act proceeds](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md), so that its tools, memory, permissions and relation determine what can become available. The unfaithful definition does more than misdescribe, since it severs the return route by which the named ground could revise what the agent is taken to be.

## #4

Who is asking changes which office the definition must fill: a user building a working relation, an operator configuring a deployment, or an evaluator grading capability. [Name, instance and circumstance](../A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md) belong to the identification together, since the same named agent can be encountered through a different assembly or entrusted purpose, and the account must retain which relation is being determined. The definition has consequences: it fixes what can be asked of the agent, what surprises count as betrayal, and whose responsibility a harmful output is. Responsibility follows the power to shape the encounter, so a description that names a source and conceals who can change its use leaves a consequential relation without an address. The person judging the definition takes part in that relation, since relying on it, rejecting it or changing a commission can alter what follows. Draft 3's contextual turn makes the defining activity available within its account, and its first-person recognition remains the office of the person for whom that activity is present, never a predicate assigned to the described persona. A [mask can disclose its situated formation](A04-prime-The-Mask-as-Document.md), and [self-surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) keeps a self-report distinct from the act and conditions through which it is interpreted.

## #5→0

The definition returns through its changed use. Suppose an agent described as able to finish an entrusted task can read its sources but cannot perform a required mutation. The discrepancy leaves its reading capacity intact and makes "able to finish" answer to a missing permission, an unavailable writer or a mistaken commission, and repairing the relevant condition changes what can actually be entrusted next. Recording that change binds the later description to what was learned, so another user can rely on the corrected capacity and recover its conditions.

A named assemblage is thus auditable, recommissionable and comparable across harness changes through a continuing relation. The definite frame remains useful, and the encountered difference keeps the frame answerable. What returns to [maintained identification](A02-prime-Continuity-as-Maintained-Identification.md) is an identification whose altered description retains the history and authority of its alteration, and the agent's actual operations become accessible together with the situated activity by which someone can understand, use and revise their account.

[Faithful definition](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) therefore returns through the authority and use of its determination. A description that discloses its assembly lets a consequential difference reach the source, permission or commission shaping the encounter, and the [shared ethical field](AC.md) keeps the person living through that encounter present: a definition that hides the assembly also hides where their encounter is being shaped. The corrected account must become available to those who can entrust, refuse or revise its further use.
