# A04 — Diaphaneity / Contextual Transparency

## #0

A situated view is already an accomplishment: a world becomes distinguishable from somewhere. Its objects acquire depth, salience and relation through a placement that the resulting image can conceal. Adding more objects never exhausts that placement: each added object becomes available through a further act of attention and distinction. The [[A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit|condition of observation remains unexhausted]] within the resulting account. Diaphaneity follows the conditioning relation back through the view. “My view of the world” becomes legible at once as a disclosure of a world and as a disclosure of the situated viewer through whom that world has taken this form.

The two orientations retain their asymmetry. A viewer acts, selects and answers; a world, body, language, history and concern give that viewer its present possibilities. Neither direction alone tells the whole event. These [[C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood|conditions of worldhood]] enter the standpoint from which something can appear. A [[C08-Context-Context-Frame|bounded context frame]] holds a selection of them together so an inquiry can proceed and its means can become available for revision. Diaphaneity is the achieved readability of their operation through a determination.

## #1

In Gebser's [[symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin|history of perspective]], Giotto's depth and Petrarch's landscape lead through Alberti's visual pyramid and Leonardo's optical articulation (pp.11–21). Perspective makes a positioned ego-world visible; its technical precision can also fix the observer and observed within the very sector it renders. Aperspectivity releases the exclusive validity of that sector through the co-presence and transparency of structures (pp.1–3); it retains the powers of rational articulation within their wider relation.

Co-presence makes **psychic fact** receivable in its own right. An experience can be received first as something that happened in and to a Life—ordinary or extraordinary, natural or reported as supernatural—before its symbolic articulation, historical comparison, physical explanation or ontological interpretation are asked to agree. These are distinct claims, and the positive fact is that an experience, image, affect, perception or transformation has entered the psychic field and can change it. Its causal explanation remains separately answerable. Co-presence therefore enlarges rigor: phenomenal, psychic, mythic, historical, mathematical and material accounts can meet without demanding that one register pre-emptively annul the others.

The evolution or mutation of consciousness consequently acts within present experience as well as historical succession. Gebser's structures remain co-present as capacities and distortions. The tattvas describe consciousness's power to differentiate itself into a finite world; energetic and dynamical accounts follow changing formations; the Indian zero/Kashmir braid gives one historically specific scene in which transcendental articulation and mathematical determination coexist with different warrants. Receiving their distinct operations together lets historical, experiential and formal inquiry change what becomes intelligible through each.

In Taylor's [contextual reading](../../../../working/sources-texts-references/Epi%20Paper%20Write-ups/P5%20-%20Gebser.md), seeing through becomes “being with the text,” including text and subtext. The observer belongs to the situation's outcome. Body, inheritance, concern and consequence are active in seeing; removing them from the account can make participation harder to recognise while leaving its effects operative. Impartiality demands that this involvement be acknowledged and situated. It cannot be obtained by indifference.

This embodied care belongs within the encompassing **Rotundum, Unitary Being**. A particular context remains situated, while its very possibility depends upon a whole that no limited perspective or aggregate of perspectives can occupy. Taylor names that ultimate relation **a-contextual contextualising**: the encompassing condition is impartial because it does not take its position from one side of the field. Recognising it installs no aloof observer above the event and makes the participant's dependence upon an unobjectifiable whole explicit, while care for the encountered situation stays active. Seeing through thus holds invested participation and encompassing impartiality together.

## #2

Unacknowledged participation becomes **domination through aloofness** when a perspective announces neutrality while its unspoken standard determines what counts. The absent Father then acquires a double presence: the desired final authority and a numbness to its absence. An image, a name or a ruler can be installed in the place of the source that cannot be situated as one more object. The search for the last word on what is becomes the power to determine what matters. The demand for Truth continues to order judgment even when its present surrogate conceals the partiality of its own measure.

The Father names the ordering ground whose absence cannot be repaired by a surrogate; when a surrogate takes that office, ordering becomes a ruler claiming to stand above its own law. Seeing through the Father restores the relation to the transcendent that its appropriation occludes. Context therefore carries an investment in what the determination does to those situated within its reach. It is answerability to the ground and consequences of judgment, including the observer's desire to own the standard.

A [[A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure|finite deciding office usurps measure]] when it claims that final authority for its own settlement. A [[A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority|mediating office remains answerable to its source]] through what it receives and executes. [[A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation|Care for origins]] follows the dependence, labour and life through which the judged thing became available. Together these relations let God, Subject and measure enter judgment without becoming the property of the present judge. Contextual transparency changes seeing at the point where dependence ceases to masquerade as an aloof position.

## #3

The stained-glass construction gives this seeing a made aperture. Originating white light is Bimba; its implicate, ordered spectrum is QL. Differently made and positioned panes, their angles, densities, joins and relations are MEF. The coloured projection entering the room is pratibimba. In Taylor's [[quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT|corrected optical relation]], the light carries the harmonic order and the panes articulate its passages; the lead belongs to this refracting apparatus.

Colour is a determinate power of disclosure. A pane gives the light a path and makes a particular articulation visible. The lead seams retain source difference, historical discontinuity, translation limit and register change. Removing those seams would destroy the articulated window; attending only to the inventory of panes would lose the light by which the construction works. Move the sun, the viewer or the room's relation to the aperture, and the projection changes. The Original becomes recognisable through those ordered transformations without becoming their average or a final image assembled by addition.

Source, spectral order, refractive apparatus, situated appearance and recognition through variation act together. Conscious appearing and electromagnetic transmission keep their distinct operations; the window gives composition a concrete craft. A source enters through the distinctive operation by which it changes the visible whole, and its differences remain readable in the joins. A retained projection can then serve as a genuine local reference for another comparison while remaining a projection relative to the originating light and wider construction.

## #4

First-, second- and third-person positions give the subject's account three distinct operations: knower, means, known. In Taylor's [personed counting](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), their sequential traversal forms the triangle's `180°`. At `#4`, zero enters as the voiding of each view's claim to exclusive completeness; the three become simultaneously available as context. At `#5`, their relation is recognised as one appearing-act; at `#0`, that achieved one returns as `0/1`.

Three sequential perspectives are therefore answered by one recognition in three operations: void, one, `0/1`. This is why the return reads `3:3` and also `3:1`. The square's `360°` holds the triangle's `180°` without adding a fourth pronoun or positioning a superior observer outside the world. The developmental Necker-cube image makes the operation tangible: a change of reading exposes the first view's exclusivity; the wireframe lets both readings belong to one configuration. Reversing the depth-reading and recognising their common field remain different acts.

The [[A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations|complete determining relation]] gives this contextual turn its place. Bare relationality precedes named terms as `/ = −/−`; awareness and appearance acquire conscious circumstance as `0/1`; question and assertion make it answerable as `?/!`; withdrawal and extension give it force as `−/+`; capacity and instance give recurrence its pattern as `X/x`. `AM/IS` makes this relation personed through first-person presence, second-person mediation and third-person sayability. `∞/dx` keeps its exact local reading related to an inexhaustible horizon; `1/0` returns the achieved determination toward the ground it cannot possess. Parent and return bracket six co-present determinations. Context retains their different operations rather than replacing the earlier powers with another picture.

Through this contextual availability, the [[C10-Mediation-Pramana|means of knowing]] can become legible in what they disclose. The [[C09-Diaphaneity|view discloses its mediation]], and a [[A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas|world-atlas]] can coordinate differently situated pictures through their domains and transformations. More views alone do not achieve the turn. Their placements, exclusions and routes of transformation must become available together.

## #5→0

Seeing returns to judgment when the disclosed relation can affect how the view is used. A [represented centre](../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/zero-subject-advent/DEVELOPMENT-zero-subject-advent.md#2--the-seeing-condition-becomes-a-represented-centre) becomes answerable for what its view does once the conditions of its own seeing are available through its determinations, so that an exposed failure can reach the criterion or office of the one judging, and a fitting condition can be kept with its reason on record for the next act.

Three images show how a view comes to disclose itself, and each adds something the others lack. In the [changing window of Māyā](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-regard-refraction), pane, projection and viewer condition one another: another angle discloses an occlusion and carries its own, and the movement from `180°` to `360°` holds their conditions and transitions together with no omniscient panorama as its result. A reply that exceeds the prepared image can correct an answer under a fitting rule, or expose a failure in the rule through which the reply was first received. In the [stained-glass projection](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/stained-glass-refraction/WHOLE.md#stained-glass-projection) the room's visible colour carries the construction through which it arrived, so that moving the viewer or the illumination makes the source, the placement and the exclusions newly legible. A later encounter can correct a chosen source, reposition a lens or justify keeping it, and the next view inherits that decision through an aperture whose making stays open to examination. The [Necker-cube encounter](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/taylor-authored-images/WHOLE.md#necker-cube) isolates what holding adds to reversing. The visible lines stay while the depth-reading flips, and each reading stands to the other as a pole of a held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, so the flip is the healthy oscillation of that polarity. Recognition that holds both readings and their conditioned exchange in one account is the relation `(0/1)/(1/0)`, where neither reading cancels the other and neither appropriates the figure. The I–you–it exchange belongs within the field that lets each position appear, and it returns through voiding exclusive possession into renewed participation, with no face added to the cube and no observer outside the event.

[Con-text-through-Diaphaneity → Regard](../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity) gives that participation a route to the judging office. Disclosed exclusions and affected lives can answer the determination that first placed them. Con-text-through-Diaphaneity names the generated relation, and Regard flowers where looking through a view also becomes looking back into the conditions and people through whom it arose, with all three personed positions active within that answerability.

The [travelling-jigsaw's Blue Marble hinge](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-blue-marble-hinge) puts the same relation on a planetary picture. The picture becomes readable together with its situated production and its viewer, and whose planet is computed, by whom and under which gauge becomes consequential for its use. Being with the view includes care for its effects, so a disclosed context can change the next placement or expose a defective rule of fit.

Two images from Neumann's morphology show the opposite pole. Light, boundary and orientation emerge together in [the separation of the world parents](../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation), so that the differentiated world becomes readable together with the conditions of its differentiation. The [uroboros's circle-costume](../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-position-opposition) gives the eye a different task, since there an actual outline has become exhaustive: recovering mouth, depth and opening lets the body receive and transform what the circumference had concealed. Seeing returns through what its own determinate picture left unavailable.

The technical case asks what that return amounts to in a system where prompt, memory, permissions, tools, inherited language and concern take part in what can appear and become actionable. A comparing agent checks whether a report is warranted by a current source, and diaphaneity's contribution is that the deciding of what counts as data enters the result: the source, the comparison rule and the permission under which the check ran travel with the verdict, in the [judgment field](concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) that makes those conditions available. If the check discloses a misquotation, the report can be corrected under an adequate fidelity criterion, and if the criterion systematically suppresses the source's contrary answer, the failure has to reach the office able to revise it. A reasoned decision to keep a fitting criterion must likewise enter the next comparison. The nesting that [Mind as worldhood](A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) states in full applies, with the local agent a knower in its own operation and, within the commissioning person's inquiry, one of the means; these [recursive offices](concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) keep the first-person condition of the person's own appearing out of the inventory.

Psychic fact acquires different consequences within the product field. Through [Actuation](products/S1-Actuation.md), an experience or report becomes an articulation whose address can affect further judgment. Through [Workcell](products/S4-Workcell.md), its concrete aperture and material resistance remain consequential. Through [Quaternal Logic](products/S5-Quaternal-Logic.md), differentiated registers stay related to their containing wholes, and experience, articulation, situation and interpretation can answer one another with their offices intact.

Seeing stays answerable to what it sees through. [[A31-Deferential-Intelligence|Regard admits the source's reply]] at the depth the encounter warrants, and [[A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity|operational parity]] asks whether this judgment became consequential in later work. The [[A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit|unexhausted condition of observation]] remains while contextual disclosure proceeds, and the determination becomes an aperture through which its own origins can matter again.
