## Movement thesis

When a relation fails under the present rules, mathematics preserves it by transforming the space in which it is written, and the earlier truths survive on their own domains. The movement carries this through a set of distinct constructions with their own hypotheses (the projective line and plane, the Riemann sphere, the cross-ratio, the Bloch sphere, atlases of charts, four-valued and five-valued logics, division in a field, a totalised operation, a collapsed ring and a wheel) and argues that the recurrence of zero and infinity as the pressure points at which a space discloses its limits makes obstruction and reframing a method and more than a list of cases. The manuscript's opening case is the rails that meet at the horizon: both descriptions (no meeting anywhere; a meeting at a point not on the ground) are exact, and the question is what space lets both be true.

## Projective and quantum payload

**The projective line.** A point is a pair `[x : y]`, not both zero, up to a nonzero multiple. Where `y ≠ 0` the coordinate is `u = x/y`; the one point `[1 : 0]` escapes it. In `v = y/x` it has the ordinary value `v = 0`, and where both apply `v = 1/u`. The arithmetic of the reals is untouched and zero acquires no inverse; the space is completed by one point. The swap `[a : b] ↦ [b : a]` is defined everywhere, exchanges zero and infinity, and is an involution (§3 · #1's parents). `0/0`, as `[0 : 0]`, names no point. So `0/1` and `1/0` are two readings of one relation, exchanged by a total involution, which gives §3 · #1's table (the expression that computes, its inverse that does not, the indeterminate that fails by excess) a geometry in which the first two belong to one space and the third has none. The geometry is standard and reading it as the space of the matheme's two orientations is ours.

**The plane and the sphere.** Triples `[X : Y : Z]` give the parallel lines `y = 0` and `y = 1` as `Y = 0` and `Y − Z = 0`, both met by `[1 : 0 : 0]`, a point with `Z = 0`; every family of parallels meets on `Z = 0`, the line at infinity, which is the painter's horizon (§0/1 · #4's vanishing point as an honest coordinate). Adjoining one point to the complex plane gives the Riemann sphere (stereographic projection; in `w = 1/z` the north pole is the ordinary `w = 0`): the completion changes the global space without adding a local dimension, and a reframing sometimes adds one (the complex numbers added an axis) and sometimes only closes or folds.

**What survives.** Bilinear maps `w(z) = (az+b)/(cz+d)`, `ad − bc ≠ 0`, move every point and preserve one relation among four, the cross-ratio `[z₁,z₂;z₃,z₄] = ((z₃−z₁)(z₄−z₂))/((z₃−z₂)(z₄−z₁))` in one standard convention. The reason is visible in one line: `w(u) − w(v) = (ad−bc)(u−v)/((cu+d)(cv+d))`, so every difference acquires the same determinant factor and two denominator factors, and all cancel in the ratio. The points `0, 1, 2, 3` have cross-ratio `4/3`; through `w(z) = (2z+1)/(z+1)` they become `1, 3/2, 5/3, 7/4`, every coordinate altered, cross-ratio `4/3` still. A relation among relations survives a change of description: the shape of the parent `(0/1)/(1/0)`, a ratio of ratios. The cross-ratio is a specified geometric invariant and the parent a recognitive question (how two complete orientations belong together); they meet in form and neither substitutes for the other.

**Qubit.** A pure state of a two-level system, modulo overall phase, is `cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + e^{iφ} sin(θ/2)|1⟩`, a point of the Bloch sphere, which is the complex projective line, the Riemann sphere again. Two features matter: the overall phase is quotiented away because it changes nothing observable and the relative phase `φ` retained because it changes everything (the phase §3 · #2 found in the turn of `i`); and the two poles are joined by a continuous sphere of states, a disciplined neighbour for superposition. QL's `0` and `1` do not become basis states by sharing digits with them; the neighbour lies in the operation.

**The atlas.** On the circle `x² + y² = 1`, the chart from the north pole gives `u = x/(1−y)` and from the south pole `v = x/(1+y)`; where both apply `uv = 1`. At `(3/5, 4/5)`, `u = 3` and `v = 1/3`, and either gives the height `y = (u²−1)/(u²+1) = (1−v²)/(1+v²) = 4/5`. If `du/dt = 2` then `dv/dt = −u⁻² du/dt = −2/9`, and both give `dy/dt = 6/25`. The charts translate a motion they did not choose, so the law of the motion is supplied separately and carried faithfully from chart to chart.

Three facts make a plurality of charts an atlas. A compact curve or surface (circle, sphere, torus) cannot be covered by one chart onto flat coordinates, since a compact image cannot be an open flat region; that is the lid's limit in §3 · #5→0's jigsaw. Charts relate by declared transition functions that compose on triple overlaps, `(φₖ∘φⱼ⁻¹)∘(φⱼ∘φᵢ⁻¹) = φₖ∘φᵢ⁻¹`. And compatibility can fail: on the line, `x` and `t = x³` are each a chart, but the transition `x = t^{1/3}` has derivative `1/(3t^{2/3})`, undefined at `t = 0`, so the two accurate descriptions are not translatable as one smooth atlas at that point. An atlas therefore keeps adequacy discriminating: some descriptions fail their own tests, some address different objects, some genuinely conflict, some are each exact and mutually untranslatable at a definite point.

## Logical plurality and comparison boundary

Logic has its own enlargements of the value space, and the movement takes four formal neighbours of the tetralemma.

**Priest's FDE.** The four corners become the values of First Degree Entailment, the four subsets of `{T, F}`: `{T}`, `{F}`, `{T, F}`, `{}`; Priest states: "Negation maps t to f, vice versa, n to itself, and b to itself. Conjunction is greatest lower bound, and disjunction is least upper bound." A statement is designated when its value contains `T`. A contradiction does not explode: with `p` valued `{T, F}` and `q` valued `{}`, `p` and its negation are designated and `q` is not. Four subsets of two elements is `2²`, an independent arrival at QL's four pairings, and the difference is stated: QL keeps the two singles beside their four pairings and writes `2 + 2²`, where FDE has no place for them. The count of six and the count of four differ because QL keeps among what it counts the terms that do the pairing.

**Belnap's store.** Belnap proposed the same four values in 1977 for a question-answering machine fed by several sources, each of which may report a statement true, false, both or nothing; a machine treating conflicting reports as an inconsistent theory could derive anything, and Belnap's values record instead what the store has been told. The corners the tetralemma walked and the states of an information store are one lattice, and the second reading is the one an agent's memory needs. (The motivation is taken from the chapter's abstract and its reception; the chapter itself has not been read, and the page range is the standard citation.)

**Priest's fifth value.** Nāgārjuna rejected all four corners, and Priest's answer is a value outside the four-valued order, "(e): none of the above", infectious in compounds. To speak of the ineffable without contradiction he lets evaluation be a relation, and at the level of ultimate truth the formalism falls silent. The gate is at this joint. Kapsner finds the fifth value open to concerns grave enough to reject it, Siderits reads the rejection of the four corners as presupposition failure with no non-classical logic, Westerhoff presses parsimony and non-uniform use of the tetralemma across Madhyamaka texts. Each objection targets `e` as a value, and none touches QL's **#0**, which was never offered as a value: the ground is the generative condition of the four, the position from which positions are taken, and a truth-value is a position taken.

**Jizang and Dōgen.** Jizang iterated the two truths: the distinction between conventional and ultimate is itself drawn at one level and so merely conventional at the next, and Priest's chapter on it is titled "And So On". The hierarchy has no top and no stage of it is a statement that could stay; Priest presents an iteration, and reading it as a practised return that never lands on a final articulation is the essay's comparison, offered. Priest's narrative converges on Dōgen, where ineffability and the practice of enlightenment meet; Priest describes the book as reconstruction and not authoritative history, and the strategies keep their doctrinal differences from one another and from QL. What the comparison takes is that the last move is not a further proposition and that a completion that is an operation can be kept only as practice. The manuscript does not use Vimalakīrti's silence, which the plan names at §3 · #3, because the Priest house carries no passage card for it; the earlier version of this note used it, and it is retired here until a card exists. The Priest 2018 book is not collated; Jizang and Dōgen are summarised from reviews.

## Division pluralisms and the immutable gap

[[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|Division pluralisms]] make the point algebraically: a written `1/0` means what its container makes it mean, and each container pays with a law. In a field `a/0` has no value. Set `ι(0) = 0` and `a ÷ b = a·ι(b)`: now `6 ÷ 0 = 0`, defined everywhere, but `0·(6 ÷ 0) = 0` and not 6, so the inverse law has been given up. Give zero a true inverse in a ring and `1 = 0·0⁻¹ = 0`, whence every `x = x·1 = x·0 = 0` and the ring collapses to one element. Carlström's wheel makes division by any element, including 0, possible, at the price his abstract states: `0x = 0` does not hold in general. Field, projective line, totalised division, collapsed ring and wheel are five meanings, each disclosing which laws it keeps and surrenders; an answer to `1/0` tells us which container we are in and never licenses free movement among them. §1 · #4 follows the plurality into machines.

Gödel's theorem (§0/1 · #3) lets us place these: the gap between what a theory can express and what it can establish relocates with every enlargement and returns through it. Every reframing here is progress of the kind Gödel's stronger theory makes, with earlier truths surviving on their domains, and none reaches a space beyond spaces: the projective line has its excluded `[0 : 0]`, the ring its collapse, the wheel its lost `0x = 0`, the four-valued logic its fifth question. Jizang's ladder with no top is the same law kept as a discipline.

## Argumentative consequence

Zero and infinity function as stress points because forcing them into an existing representation reveals which laws and distinctions the representation is using. The responses (complex extension, compactification, projective completion, changed logic, changed algebra) are separate operations with separate hypotheses, and their shared lesson is methodological: when a relation fails under a frame, inspect both the relation and the frame before declaring the relation impossible or the frame absolute. Kinds of obstruction must be told apart: Russell's paradox has two repairs that do different work, the theory of types restricting what can be expressed (a collection takes members only from the level below) and separation restricting what can be collected (`R_S = {x ∈ S : x ∉ x}` is provably not a member of `S`); a projective point at infinity answers no contradiction at all. Some obstructions are errors inside a frame and call for correction there; others are the frame's own assumptions showing through. A frame changes when the constituting relation changes, when what counts as a point, a value, a collection or a valid move is altered, and the change succeeds when the earlier results remain available with the conditions under which they were true.

For an agent the test is practical. When two sources contradict, sometimes one is wrong and the answer is corrected inside the frame given; sometimes the question as posed, the criterion of relevance or the permission that excluded a third source was the trouble, and the correction must reach the frame so that the next act inherits the changed frame and not merely a changed answer. A four-valued store keeps the agent from exploding meanwhile; an atlas tells it whether the sources are two charts of one thing, descriptions of different things, or exact and untranslatable at a definite point; the immutable gap tells it that any frame it reaches has a limit of its own.

This is the exact formal neighbour of [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C64-Paradox-Transforming-the-Containing-Field|Paradox / Transforming the Containing Field]]. A paradox can sometimes be dissolved by correcting a mistake inside the current scheme; in other cases it exposes the scheme's own assumptions as part of the problem. Mathematics supplies disciplined examples of the second possibility, and each carries its own conditions.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#e5-whole-returns) **qualifies** the formal comparison by keeping its generated objects countable. FDE's subsets of `{T,F}` give four values; QL's `2+2²` retains the two initial terms and gives six. Adjoining a point, moving from affine to projective geometry, compactifying a plane and changing an algebra have distinct hypotheses and preserved relations.
