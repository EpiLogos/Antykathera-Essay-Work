## Movement thesis

Eight determinations are the successive limits that one act of knowing sets within one relation, from the parent stroke `/ = −/−` to the return-switch `1/0`. Each keeps what the one before made available and adds its own operation, so none replaces another, and pulling any turn out of the sequence leaves a list of events where there had been one encounter. We follow the sequence through a single encounter. A shape moves on a lane at dusk and we stop; it becomes a darker form against the hedge, a thing that gathers and goes still, a deer with a notch torn from one ear, a gaze that reads us as we read it, an ear that turns by a fraction, and finally an anecdote in which the one who saw appears nowhere. Eight turns pass through that act of knowing, and none adds a thing to the lane. That each turn counts as a determination rests on §0/1 · #2, where Spinoza's letter to Jelles supplied the principle that a figure has its shape from the limits marking where it ends, so that determination is negation.

We write the traversal as the core theorems do, `/ = −/− → 0/1 → ?/! → −/+ → X/x → AM/IS → ∞/dx → 1/0`; [the core theorems' table of eight determinations](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **sources** it, and its order fixes what each pair means. Eight counts the turns of one passage; six remains the number of positions the passage moves through. `/ = −/−` and `1/0` bracket the six instead of enlarging it: the first stands before any count, the second is the turn after the count is complete, and the six between are the qualitative body of the `4+2` that §0/1 · #1 counted as `2+2²`.

| Turn | Expression | Operation | At the lane | What stays invariant |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Parent relation** | `/ = −/−` | two unassigned marks held in one stroke: relation before the terms carry values | the halt in the walking, before watcher and watched divide | the relation has a condition that cannot appear as one more product of the relation |
| **#0 Ground** | `0/1` | awareness and phenomenon, conscious circumstance: "I am here; something appears; the appearing is known" | we are here and something has appeared | the ground is immanent to every mark and is neither another mark nor a hidden object behind them |
| **#1 Definition** | `?/!` | questioning and asserting held as one act | "What is that?" and the loosely held answers: sack, dog, stump | every assertion carries the question that makes it accountable, every question already takes a provisional mark |
| **#2 Dynamis** | `−/+` | withdrawal and extension: difference acquires force | the shape gathers and goes still, our attention tightens and slackens with it | the two poles remain one differentiating movement |
| **#3 Pattern** | `X/x` | determining capacity and its indefinite particular: force becomes recognisable as kind | *deer* is the capacity, this notched ear the instance | the instance makes the capacity legible and does not exhaust it |
| **#4 Context** | `AM/IS` | first-person presence and third-person sayability, met through the second person | the deer reads us, and our account has to make room for its account of us | knower, means and known remain one appearing-act without becoming interchangeable |
| **#5 Quintessence** | `∞/dx` | inexhaustible horizon and exact local difference | the turning ear carries the whole wood behind it | exactness never becomes possession |
| **Return-switch** | `1/0` | the achieved determination turns toward the ground it presupposes | the story is complete and its teller is absent from it | the limit is generative: `5→0` renews the cycle without cancelling what was determined |

The core theorems label the **#5** turn Realisation; the manuscript and PROSE-STANDARD name the position Quintessence, and this note follows the manuscript.

## Warrant: the turns, as the manuscript derives them

**Parent stroke and Sheffer.** Logic has a close neighbour for a single cut that generates a whole field of relation. In 1913 Henry Sheffer founded Boolean algebra on one binary operation and read his stroke as rejection, "neither p nor q", adding in a closing footnote that the results hold equally for the dual reading, "not both" (the house `sheffer-1913-five-postulates` holds both passages). Writing `p↓q` for rejection, `p↓p` negates, `(p↓q)↓(p↓q) = p∨q` and `(p↓p)↓(q↓q) = p∧q`, so every truth-table can be written with the one stroke. Sheffer's neighbour is exact at one point: one relating cut, repeated and arranged, generates a complete field of determinate relations without a second primitive, and the duality of readings is the formal shadow of the slash's own AND/OR. It differs at another: Sheffer's stroke operates on values already given, true and false, whereas the parent stroke's two marks are the same unassigned sign twice, so QL claims at the level of the act what Boolean logic shows at the level of connectives. Every determinate relation of the traversal presupposes the stroke, and the stroke never appears among them as one more.

**`0/1` and `?/!`.** §0/1 earned `0/1`; here it is the bare circumstance before any question. `?/!` is double accountability: to ask what something is already asserts it has appeared stably enough to call for an account, and to assert what it is keeps open whether the account runs true. What the etymology of symbol, account and trust **grounds** is that accountability of the mark to what called it forth ([Symbol answers to source](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source)). §0/1's six questions unfold from this determination. Its glyph returns at §3 · #1 with another office, the undecidability of `1/0` at **#3**, and the two mappings are kept apart: one is a judgment's accountability, the other the arithmetic's refusal.

**`−/+` through both logics.** The pulse is the Spanda "subtle movement" of §0/1 · #3. Held as dia-ballein's healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, the animal's stillness and movement and our approach and halt are opposed positions on one line of tension. Cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, dismisses the encounter ("it was nothing"). Appropriation reckoned from our side is `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, the whole relation as the animal's threat to us, and from the animal's side `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, we are the danger it bolts from: our `+2` is the deer's `−2`, one span charged from opposite ends. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps what both collapses lose, each orientation readable through the other.

**`X/x`.** The notation is the author's own and works in the algebraic, mythematical, musical, narrative and psychic registers; the capital is the presiding capacity, the lower case its local temporal face and also the sign of multiplication, so an instance can recur and compound ("another one of those"). Recognition carries continuity and correction together: the capacity lets a new instance be recognised and lets an apparent instance fail, as the stump failed and the deer passed. §4 · #1 follows `X/x` into individuation.

**`AM/IS`.** The second person sits at the slash. §0/1 · #5→0 placed the grammatical persons in the Trika's offices, *I* in the knower, *it* in the known, *you* in the mediating relation; the slash is where Objective Internality works, the means a mind lives through (habits of looking, stories about deer and dusk, the fear that tightened attention). Context here is the lived *where* of an encounter, total because nothing appears outside a horizon and incidental because the horizon does not stand forth as one more object. *Pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa* and *prameya* remain one appearing-act and stay distinct within it.

**`∞/dx`, calculus and the constant.** `dx` is the infinitesimal difference of the calculus, not the instance `x`. Every appearance is a true local ratio of what exceeds it, precise as relation and partial as expression. The calculus makes this exact: `x²` and `x²+7` both have the derivative `2x`, which is right about each at every point, and integrating `2x` returns the whole family `x²+C`. Only a further condition, such as the value at zero, selects the member from which the derivative was taken; the theorem on the general form of an antiderivative states this for functions on an interval. The missing `C` is the formally required provenance that local change cannot reconstruct, and the core theorems read it so: a determination can be exact without containing the whole condition from which its exactness was taken.

**The gauge, planted once.** Much of what machines learn has the same structure. In Bradley and Terry's 1952 model of paired comparison, the probability that one item is preferred to another depends only on the ratio of their strengths, so every common rescaling of the strengths leaves every prediction unchanged. That common level is invisible to the data, as the constant of integration is invisible to the derivative, and whatever fixes it is supplied from outside the comparison. §5 · #1 follows the invisible level into the training of agents.

**`1/0` and the return.** The achieved determination turns toward the condition it presupposes and meets the incomputable where it had hoped for an object called "the ground", since no number multiplied by zero gives one. §0/1 · #3 watched Gödel's second theorem give this orientation arithmetical precision. At dusk the turn is quieter: the story of the deer is complete and the seeing was the condition of the story, not an episode in it. The Śaiva sources call the turn *pratyabhijñā*, recognition, the opposite of acquisition, and recognition is the return `5→0`.

**Eckhart's *in quantum* and the eight.** §0/1 · #5→0 promised that the eight would honour Eckhart's constitutive approach through QL's own construction and deconstruction of `0/1`. Eckhart's *in quantum* ("insofar as"), which §0/1 took from Flasch's reading of the primary determinations, is itself a `0/1`: the determination is the `0` present through the deed, the deed is the `1`, *insofar as* is the slash. Each of the eight is the whole relation *in quantum*, and read from `/ = −/−` through `∞/dx` the traversal constructs `0/1`, building determinacy turn by turn. `1/0` deconstructs it in the strict sense, taking apart what was built together without destroying any of it: each achieved determination is loosened from its claim to stand alone and returned to the relation it expressed. Eckhart's insofar-as keeps the just person from claiming to be Justice outright; `1/0` keeps the finished account from claiming to be the ground.

## The internal grammar of the six

Adjacent pairs form three harmonic families: **Being** joins Ground and Definition (`0/1` with `?/!`: a field is present and can be called to account); **Becoming** joins Dynamis and Pattern (force becomes intelligible as recurrence, and recurrence stays alive as force capable of a further instance); **Knowing / unKnowing** joins Context and Quintessence (lived predication becomes honest when its horizon exceeds every situated claim). Across the forward pairs run three folds of positions whose numbers sum to five: **Essence** (**#0**, **#5**), **Constitution** (**#1**, **#4**) and **Text–Texture** (**#2**, **#3**, the felt weave of force and its readable pattern, the *textus* and *contexere* of §0/1 · #4). Only (2,3) among the adjacent pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5) also sums to five, so Dynamis and Pattern are the hinge at which Becoming and Text–Texture coincide, and, as the compass figure below shows, where the count crosses its own diameter. So the six form a structured body, neither a ladder nor a catalogue.

## Perspective becomes context

§0/1 · #4 gave the demand for diaphaneity its first geometry: the triangle's 180 degrees doubled into the square's 360, the square as two triangles in diametric co-opposition, context therefore needing at least two chiralities. Mathematics supplies why these geometries fall where they do, beginning in an oddity of the count. Formal QL starts at **#1** and returns at **#0**, and the numbers do not match the positions:

| Position | **#1** | **#2** | **#3** | **#4** | **#5** | **#0** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Number | 1 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 4 | 5 |
| Office | Definition | Dynamis | Pattern | Context | Quintessence | Ground |

The explicate triad sits where its face value puts it; zero goes to **#4**, four to **#5**, five to **#0**, each at the position that names its function. Consequences: (i) `1+2+3 = 1×2×3 = 6`, but with the zero of **#4**, `0+1+2+3 = 6` while `0×1×2×3 = 0`; zero is the power of the name, since adding a name changes nothing and multiplying by the Nameless voids all names. Context therefore de-fines by addition and con-fines by multiplication, and §0/1 · #4's voiding at **#4** is multiplication by the zero that addition let pass. (ii) The first three numbers count the three persons in sequence, the line, the angle and the triangle of 180 degrees; at **#4** the zero voids each view's exclusive claim and the triangle opens into the square's 360; at **#5** the three are one appearing-act (`1/1`, §3 · #1); at **#0** the achieved one returns to ground and mark. So `3:3` is also `3:1`. (iii) **#4** is the last of the four explicate positions in the `4:2` reading and the first of the recognition triad in the `3:3` reading, so the gate bears an implicate number on an explicate seat.

The core theorems set the four pairings on the compass in the order the count acquires them: `00` North, `01` East, `10` West, `11` South. East to West leaps 180 degrees across the diameter, the inversion of `01` into `10`, `0/1` into `1/0`, already running in the first pass. With North `(0,1)`, East `(1,0)`, West `(−1,0)` and South `(0,−1)`, the first three bound a triangle of area one; adding South and joining North, East, South, West makes a square of area two, and the East–West edge becomes a diagonal shared by the reflected triangles North–East–West and South–East–West. What was the limit of a perspective has become a seam inside a context, which is the formal content of "context needs at least two chiralities": the first triangle's claim to be the whole is voided while its edge is kept. The seam of the context turns out to be the inversion of the matheme.

Geometry (order, coordinates, areas, angle sums) is derived. The essay argues the reading that the triangle is three persons and the square their contextual holding, which is what makes the Trika quaternary: knower, means and known become jointly available as one appearing-act on a surface where situated viewer, world viewed and mediation can be mutually legible, with no view from nowhere installed. ## Spinoza reversed

The *Ethics* (1677) demonstrates metaphysics in the manner of Euclid from a substance whose attributes are given at the outset, so the demonstrator stands outside the order demonstrated. QL derives from an act of knowing that has to count itself among what it derives, as the definition of the Subject in §0/1 · #1 had to include the one defining. Self-inclusion needs these eight at least: take away the parent relation and the act has nothing to begin from; the question, and it cannot be answerable; the kind, and it cannot recur; the return, and it cannot come back to the one who knows.

## Held in the note, not carried in the prose

- The Jungian `4+2` of four functions and two attitudes was a refraction offered here in the earlier draft; the manuscript now places it at §4 · #2, which argues that the attitudes orient the functions and are not two further functions.
- The zero–subject braid: the mental-rational subject stands within the `#1` line as an apparently self-grounding term and lets its unowned condition fall into the external `−1`; `0/1` keeps the ground–mark relation explicit, `X/x` stops the particular severing itself from its capacity, `AM/IS` opens the 180-degree triangle into context. M25 does not make this claim; its pieces are carried in §4 (the crossed zero at #3, the integral turn at #4).

## Tension / limit

Sheffer's stroke acts on given values and the parent stroke precedes them (source: Sheffer 1913, with the dual reading in his footnote). Reading the missing `C` as provenance is the core theorems' move and is argued; the identities and the antiderivative theorem are derived, on an interval (source: Strang and Herman §4.10). The glyph `?/!` has two real offices that must not be conflated (the theorem spine writes `1/0` at **#3** with the same glyph). Two debts are uncollated: the Flasch page for the primary determinations and the Bradley–Terry page stating the model's form (see the §3 draft's DEBTS).

## Transition

A traversal this complete needs a generator that produces the sixfold body from one relation and reads it back, and the Spanda equations are that generator: [[26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2|§3 · #1 — The Spanda Equations and 4+2]]. Three joints carry the traversal onward. `0/1` holds the seam of ground and manifestation, read from both ends by the Spanda equations. The turn from **#3** to **#4**, the triangle opening into the square across the diameter of an inversion, makes perspective into context, and §3 · #3's projective constructions rebuild that turn in larger spaces. `∞/dx` relates exact local difference to its horizon, and §3 · #4 meets that horizon as `∞/0`, a neighbouring expression, on the torus and its cover.

The language-field comparison, which the manuscript prose does not carry, **returns-to** [E1's native-field return](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#native-field-return) within the full traversal `/ = −/− → 0/1 → ?/! → −/+ → X/x → AM/IS → ∞/dx → 1/0`: encounter, naming, counting and personed address each refract several of these operations, and `X/x` remains the essay's own notation with no lexical derivation behind it.
