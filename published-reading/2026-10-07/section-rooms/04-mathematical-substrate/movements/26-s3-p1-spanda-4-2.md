## Movement thesis

[The Spanda equations](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **define** QL's generative kernel: two native readings of one sixfold from its two ends. The First is horizontal: a reading by proportion that follows the relation from ground through distinction to integrated return and balances as `3:3`. The Second is vertical: a reading by count that takes the achieved whole and accounts for it as `4+2`. Everything later in §3 comes from holding them against each other, because their difference generates the relation that choosing either alone would lose. The manuscript's case is an accordion's bellows: two movements in opposite directions make one breath, and the turn at the end of each stroke is where the instrument is most alive; the Self leaning into manifestation and the manifest leaning home, whole beneath the swing.

Both are read in the declared QL register. `0/0` and `1/0` are symbolic orientations, and the displayed `+` composes orientations; it is never the addition of two quotients, one of which has no value. The mediant (below) supplies the missing step, that the composition is a definite operation.

$$
0=(0/0)
\longrightarrow
\frac{(0/1)}{(1/0)}
\longrightarrow
[T_0]
\longrightarrow
[T_1]
\longrightarrow
\left(\frac{1}{0}+\frac{0}{1}\right)_{
\mathrm{QL}}
\longrightarrow
\frac{1}{1}=100\%.
$$

## The slash-forms under proportion

Each position holds a fraction built from the two marks, and the fractions behave very differently under ordinary arithmetic. In the table the middle column is the core theorems' and the right-hand column is arithmetic anyone can check.

| Position | Slash-form | Under proportion | Ordinary arithmetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | `(0/1)/(1/0)` | the relation of the two orientations; unfolds into `0/1, 0/0, 1/1, 1/0` with the singles `0` and `1`: the whole `4+2` | a term with no value; QL reads a relation |
| **#1** | `0/0` | the indeterminate, every ratio held at once | no unique value: every number times zero gives zero |
| **#2** | `0/1` | the expression, computable and docile | zero |
| **#3** | `1/0` | the inverse, incomputable | no value: no number times zero gives one |
| **#4** | `1/1` | the ratio resolved, one whole | one, or one hundred per cent |
| **#5** | `(1/0)/(0/1)` | the inverse relation of the two orientations; the whole in inverse phase, `4′+2′` | as at **#0**, read the other way |

In a field `(0+0)x = 0x+0x` forces `0x = 0`; so `0x = 1` has no solution (one divided by zero has no value) and `0x = 0` is solved by every number (zero divided by zero has no single value). These two failures differ and the table keeps them apart. `0/0` fails by excess and the theorem reads the excess as the indeterminate, the capacity for every proportion, marked `%` as the glyph of potency. `1/0` fails by absence, the determinate mark divided by the ground yielding nothing a number can name, and the theorem locates this at **#3**, Pattern, and nowhere else, writing it `?/!` because the system cannot decide whether it is a question or an assertion. Formal identity, the recognition of *what* a thing is, rests on a foundation that does not compute: the Gödelian point of §0/1 · #3 at the level of one position. This `?/!` is an undecidability and is the second of the two offices §3 · #0 told apart, distinct from the living question-and-assertion of definition at **#1**.

Two findings follow. **The parents contain the children.** For a ratio of ratios `(a/b)/(c/d)`, relate the first upper term to each lower term and then the second, giving `a/c, a/d, b/c, b/d`, and keep the two singles beside them. Applied to `(0/1)/(1/0)` this yields `0/1, 0/0, 1/1, 1/0` with `0` and `1`: the ground bears the whole `4+2` as its own relational interior, a seed carrying the full tree, and the relation at **#5** bears the same whole in inverse phase. Reading the parent into its inverse and back restores every term, so the inversion is an involution, and what the arrangement records is which of two phases is active, never how many reversals have been performed. Two parents thus carry one child in mirror image, *bimba* and *pratibimba*. **The relation leans.** `0/1` settles into a value and `1/0` stays permanently open, so the relation of ground and mark is handed. That is the structural reason consciousness can never wholly catch itself, since every catching conceals what it catches from, and the reason it keeps trying. Read through the Indian analysis of knowing, each slash-form is a complete act compressed: numerator the knower (*pramātṛ*), slash the means (*pramāṇa*), denominator the known (*prameya*); `0/1` is the void knowing the mark, `1/0` the mark attempting to know the void through ratio.

The crossed zero has a mathematical life here. `Ø` (§0/1 · #3, §1 · #3) lays the stroke across the zero so that the means of seeing passes for the one who sees; in `(0/1)/(1/0)` the stroke stands between the two orientations and is legible as a relation. Occlusion and recognition are the same two marks in two arrangements, and §4 · #3 follows the sequence by which the fused stroke comes undone.

## The First Spanda: sequence, bridge, mediant

Bare ground `0` reads itself and opens `0/0`; that differentiates into `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the first cut, *inter-legere*, reading-between, of nothing with nothing. `[T₀]`, the emanative arc from `0/(0/1)` toward `0/1`, takes expression as its denominator: figure generated from ground. `[T₁]`, the reversionary arc from `(1/0)/0` toward `1/0`, takes the ground as denominator: figure acknowledging what sustains it. Both arcs meet in the bridge `1/0 + 0/1`, find they were one circuit, and resolve into `1/1`, one hundred per cent: to be one of oneself is to be oneself wholly.

A bridge that asks this much has to name a definite operation, and arithmetic has one of the right shape. A mediant forms from two fractions a third by adding numerators and adding denominators, `(0+1)/(1+0) = 1/1`, and it is defined when a boundary is `1/0` because it divides nothing. **I argue** that the mediant is the operation of the First Spanda's bridge; §1 · #5→0 builds and proves the tree of all positive ratios the same operation generates between `0/1` and `1/0`, and the bridge needs only its first step. Two further constructions show the result holding its two readings. Two objects `A`, `B` with an arrow `f` and its inverse `g` (`g∘f = id_A`, `f∘g = id_B`) give two objects and four arrows in one connected structure, a `2+4` unity that does not identify `A` with `B`. Or take `b = (0,1)` and `p = (1,0)`: the sum `u = (1,1)` is unchanged by swapping coordinates and the difference `d = (1,−1)` changes sign, and `b = (u−d)/2`, `p = (u+d)/2`: the invariant alone cannot reconstruct what was distinguished, but invariant and sign-changing difference together recover both. Whole and phase are needed together, which M27 develops.

## The `3:3` and its six pairs of faces

| Emanative triad: source toward expression, *bimba*, *prakāśa* | Reversionary triad: return toward recognition, *pratibimba*, *vimarśa* |
|---|---|
| 1. `(0/0)`, the indeterminate | 4. `[T₁]`, the reversionary arc toward `1/0` |
| 2. `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the slash differentiating itself | 5. `1/0 + 0/1`, the bridge |
| 3. `[T₀]`, the emanative arc toward `0/1` | 6. `1/1`, achieved unity, one hundred per cent |

The partition is the `1-2-3 / 4-5-0` of §3 · #0, and its primary sense comes from the definition of the Subject: the first three unfold first, second and third person; the second three void the isolation of the three views, recognise their unity and return it as `0/1`. Horizontally the reading is `3:3` and, the second triad being one recognition in three operations, also `3:1`. Emanation and reversion, *bimba* and *pratibimba*, *prakāśa* and *vimarśa* are Śaiva readings of this subject-structure; none is the source of the arithmetic.

Each position carries the doubled reading as a pair of faces, the series of Name and the series of Power: at **#0** Truth and Play, **#1** Mind and Need, **#2** Word and Sacrifice, **#3** Logos and Decision, **#4** Son and Love, **#5** Image and Work. At every position the articulated determination as it appears from outside and the same determination felt as a demand from within are co-present, as the two arcs require: emanation that never returns would be Name without Power, a return that never went out would be Power with nothing to bear.

## The Second Spanda: where the operation changes

$$
100\%\;=\;2^6+6^2\;=\;64+36\;\longrightarrow\;\frac{64}{36}\;\xrightarrow{\;\div 4\;}\;\frac{16}{9}\;=\;\frac{2^4}{3^2}\;\longrightarrow\;4+2.
$$

On a scale of a hundred, the whole is the sum of two counts of different kinds: `2⁶ = 64` binary assignments across the six positions (the count whose history §1 · #5→0 tells) and `6² = 36` ordered pairs of positions. A state assignment and a relational address are different objects, so the sum is a sum of tagged collections, and calling it one hundred per cent normalises it to a whole without making the number one a count of a hundred things. Selecting these two counts as the whole's constituents belongs to the QL derivation; §0 · #4 offers the resonance of the thirty-six with the thirty-six tattvas as `6²`.

| Step | Mathematical standing | QL reading |
|---|---|---|
| `100 = 2⁶ + 6² = 64 + 36` | arithmetic identity | the achieved whole as binary-through-six plus six-squared |
| `64 : 36` | a chosen change of operation, not a consequence of the sum | the two constituents read in proportion |
| `64/36 = 16/9` | ordinary reduction | the shared quaternary removed, the difference exposed |
| `16/9 = 2⁴/3²` | prime factorisation | four binary factors and two ternary factors: the vertical `4:2` |

Nothing in the sum implies the comparison; QL changes from adding the counts to taking their ratio, and dividing the *sum* by four would give twenty-five and answer a different question. Counting factor instances gives four of the binary, read as the explicate positions, and two of the ternary, read as the implicate poles. This reading is selected by the sixfold already derived from the binary and its self-application, since many decompositions of a hundred exist; the QL frame finds a coherent arithmetic expression of its own `4:2` relation, and the further relations that follow can test the choice. Independently, `2+2²=2+4=6` grounds the sixfold itself.

A second reading holds in the numerator and fails in the denominator. `2⁴ = 4² = 16`, so `16/9 = (4/3)²`, two musical fourths; but `3² = 9` while `2³ = 8`, so the exchange of base and exponent that works above changes the value below, and the reading rests on the numerator's equality alone. Two relations carry lighter warrant and enter no later step: `16 + 9 = 25 = 5²`, the first Pythagorean triple `3² + 4² = 5²`, whose area is `6` and perimeter `12`, and `100 = 4 × 5²`. The identities hold, and **I offer** the readings attached to them (implicate three and explicate four at right angles, the five as hypotenuse and connector) as resonance.

## Warrant: the cross-reading and the harmonic family

Ordinary fraction arithmetic would collapse `(4:2)/(3:3)` to `2`, a single number that has lost both orientations. QL declares a comparison of corresponding entries, `ℋ_QL(4:2, 3:3) := (4/3, 2/3)`, and once it is declared its outputs are ordinary ratios with reciprocals: `4/3` the perfect fourth (manifestation), `3/4` recognition, `2/3` the descending fifth (grounding) and `3/2` the ascending fifth, read with Whitehead as the lure. `4/3 · 3/2 = 2/1`. Squared, the fourth `16/9` falls short of the octave by a definite interval reached by three routes that start from different pairs and converge exactly:

$$
\frac{2}{16/9}=\frac98,\qquad \frac{3/2}{4/3}=\frac98,\qquad \frac{(3/2)^2}{2}=\frac98 .
$$

The third is the Pythagorean keyboard's own construction; Scholtz writes, "Thus, 3/2 x 3/2 = 9/4. 9/4 is divided by 2, making D = 9/8." Hence `16/9 · 9/8 = 2/1` and `4/3 · 9/8 · 4/3 = 2/1`: the system almost doubles, falls short by one whole tone, and the whole tone carries it the rest of the way. The core theorems name `9/8` the measure of the matheme's non-closure, the live tick each cycle hands forward; §3 · #4 pays the reading out in sound. A totality equal to its octave would be a dead identity, and the arithmetic is derived while that reading of the remainder is argued.

`3:3` and `4:2` need each other as the bellows need folds and breath. Without the `4:2` the `3:3` is a vibe (proportion with nothing to proportion); without the `3:3` the `4:2` is a catalogue (positions with no inner relation); together they are a logic, countable and relational: the First Spanda is the return-reading of the Second's frame, and the ratios are born from holding the two together. Restated as a law, the bridge is the standing identity `0/1 + 1/0 = 1/1 = 100%`: as ordinary arithmetic it is impossible, and as QL composition, with the mediant as its definite operation, it says the instrument is whole at every point of its stroke including the turn. A language model's softmax does the same kind of normalising, the weights summing to one among the words a position attends to; the `1` of such a whole is a normalisation, as the hundred per cent is, and what the normalisation does to the excluded alternatives (they keep a weight, however small) is the apoha that §5 · #1 develops.

## Proof status and limits

Four standings are kept apart. **Derived, arithmetic:** `2+2²=6`, `100=64+36`, `64/36=16/9`, `16/9=2⁴/3²`, the interval identities involving `4/3`, `3/2`, `9/8`, `16/9`, `2/1`, the field facts about `0` and the mediant's definedness. **Derived within declared QL operations:** the ordered traversal, its `3:3` and `4:2` organisation, and the table of slash-forms. **Argued:** selecting `64` and `36` as the whole's constituents and the step from sum to ratio, the mediant as the bridge's operation, the operator `ℋ_QL` and the reading of the ratio family and of `9/8` as non-closure. **Offered:** the `3-4-5` and `100 = 4×5²` readings, the tetraktys amplification below, and the Śaiva, Whiteheadian and metaphysical glosses. Open debts, from the §3 draft's DEBTS: Concrete Mathematics §4.5 (the Stern–Brocot tree) has no house; Whitehead's "lure for feeling" and the mediant's pages in Kaplan are unlocated.

## Held in the note, not carried in the manuscript prose

The tetraktys `1+2+3+4=10` is a genuine Pythagorean relation when separately sourced, and the coincidences that `100=10×10` and that `2⁴/3²` contains the exponents 4 and 2 are numerical resonances kept as Offered amplification outside the proof spine. Pythagorean history can enter as a sourced neighbour of the exact musical content, never as a certificate for it.
