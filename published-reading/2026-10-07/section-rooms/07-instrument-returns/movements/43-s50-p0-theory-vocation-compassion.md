## Claim

Six products in §5 are six offices of one constructed paradigm, and a completed theory of that kind stands where a night nurse stands at a quarter to seven, with a handover to write. **Epi-Logos is the vocation of such a theory:** an achieved account returns to the relation it came from, so that what it meets can reach the terms in which it was made and the paradigm that produced it can change. A completed account can return in two directions. It can return the world to itself, reading everything it meets in its own terms so that it never has to change, which is counterfeit mediation; or it can return itself to the world and its sources, which is Epi-Logos. Both are returns by sophisticated accounts doing real work, and they differ in which way the dependence is allowed to run. The vocation is the second direction, and its manner is **Compassion**, loving sensitivity to origins.

## Warrant — the handover, the fence, the theōros, the two returns and compassion's three tenses

**Anchor.** A night nurse writes the handover for a day nurse who has slept: bed four's temperature, bed six's consultant, bed eleven not to be moved to the window. Whatever the note leaves out the reader will not know to ask about, whatever it states as settled will look settled, and an unrecorded mistake will be made again before lunch. Machines now run the practice: an agent working across sessions remembers only what was written for it and begins each session from that file (§0/1's bedside and the agents' own *prompt thrownness*; §4 · #5→0 follows what the word shows about continuity). Whoever writes the note decides in tired minutes what counts as the starting world for a reader who cannot check. "The relative in bed six is difficult" hands on a verdict and withholds the history; "the family has been promised three times by three people" hands on the same difficulty with its origin attached. Both are accounts, and they differ in whether the account keeps a route back to what it accounts for.

**Chesterton's fence and its limit.** The wiser reformer, in Chesterton's parable, will not let a gate across a road be cleared until its use is seen: somebody had a reason, and until the reason is known the reasonableness of the clearing cannot be judged. A constructed handover line for bed eleven is such a fence, five words compressing a woman's flooded childhood, her terror of rain on glass and the last time she was moved. Its direction is its limit: it faces the origin that is gone and says nothing about the person at the gate who needs to pass this morning or about the field beyond it. A handover can honour every reason of the night and still fail the patient in the morning, because what the patient will need today has no reason yet, only a beginning.

**Theory contained its return.** *Vocation* is from *vocare*, to call: an address that reaches someone from beyond, so the person called chooses the answer and not the voice. *Theōria* stands behind the *theōros*, first an envoy sent to consult an oracle or attend a festival on the city's behalf and only later a spectator. He was sent because the city needed to know something it could not see from where it stood, and his seeing finished when he came home and gave an account the city could act on. Modern theory keeps the seeing and loses the journey home, leaving what it returns to for a later stage called application. Nothing has to be added to a theory to make it vocational; something it always carried has to be allowed to act.

**Epi-Logos and the protected account.** §0/1 · #5→0 defined Epi-Logos as the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved, with the eros of Logos as the desire that bends an account back toward its making. At the end of a theory that desire meets a choice it could not meet earlier, because only now is there a finished account powerful enough to be tempted by its own finish. A ward has a name for the first direction: a patient who complains of pain relief is "anxious", one who refuses the night medication is "non-compliant", and once the description has hardened every new sign confirms it. §0/1 · #2 named the operation behind it: a finite `1` that **plays the `0`** borrows the place of the source while withdrawing its conduct from the encounter in which it could be judged. An account does so whenever its governing criterion is insulated from the encounters that could revise it (dissent heard as the deviance predicted, refusal as confirmation, counter-example as noise). A protected account is more dangerous than a false proposition, since a false proposition can be corrected while the means of correction live, whereas a protected account absorbs its corrections as proof of its authority. Mediation is not the fault: every working paradigm mediates, and the fault begins when a derived authority forgets its derivation and becomes immune to return.

**Logics in a handover.** Between the one who leaves and the one who arrives runs a held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`: the one who knows the history and the one who meets the situation fresh, each useful because of the other. Cancel it, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the successor starts "with a clean slate", an erasure that sounds like freedom, with whatever the old arrangement protected left unprotected by no one's decision. Appropriate it and the span gets a direction: the incoming manager counts each improvement as their own, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the same span enters every failure against the one who left, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the predecessor becoming the scapegoat of the first year and the successor the vandal of a careful system, while the shared zero, the work and the people it serves, drops out of both accounts. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, writes the note so the successor reads the predecessor's reasons through the present situation and the situation through those reasons.

**Planetary handover.** A model receives, before any conversation, a context distilled from the written record, the largest handover ever composed, with most reasons, labour and exclusions missing from the surviving lines; §0/1 · #0 called the agent a child of the collective and of the particular groups who decided what it would do. Shumailov and colleagues trained successive generations of generative models on the previous generation's output and found irreversible defects in which the tails of the original distribution disappear, with the remedy being access to real human-produced data. Read through the notation, each generation's account is taken as the world by the next and the tails are cancelled, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, without anyone choosing to cancel them; the finding concerns recursive training under the conditions set out and its lesson follows from them. Origins that a finished manner conceals are often people: Perrigo reported in January 2023 that the safety system making ChatGPT less likely to produce abusive text was built from examples labelled by workers in Kenya, employed through the outsourcing firm Sama at roughly $1.32 to $2 an hour, who read and classified descriptions of sexual abuse, murder, suicide and torture. A user's cleaner instrument, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, is the labeller's residue, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and the common world both live in has fallen out of the account. Heidegger's modern technology is a revealing that challenges, setting upon nature to supply what can be extracted and stored, and what is so ordered is *Bestand*, standing-reserve; a model trained on the record is that challenging turned on language and culture, and also a mirror in which the operation becomes visible. AI therefore enters this return first as a phenomenon that concentrates challenging-forth and by concentrating it makes hidden gauges, displaced labours and appropriated grounds available to inspection. Gebser's diaphaneity names the response, making the structures through which AI appears transparent to their origin, history, exclusions, powers and effects, with no prior verdict on whether anything in the machine is aware.

**Compassion.** §0/1 · #5→0 named the manner of the return: loving sensitivity to origins, approaching a doctrine, image or person through the conditions from which its form drew life, so the insight worth keeping can be told from the fixation that stops it disclosing anything further. The author reached the definition reading Chang Chung-yuan on the twentieth chapter of the *Tao Te Ching* (where Chang brings in Nishida on the union of subject and object), and in his reading notes it grew a clause: creative, loving sensitivity to origins gone, to come and ever present. Its three tenses carry the content. **Origins gone** are what Chesterton's fence protects, the languages, labours, injuries and gifts through which a present form became possible and which go on acting in it; bed eleven's instruction is such an origin, and sensitivity means unpacking it and asking before obeying blindly or overruling. **Origins to come** are beginnings that cannot yet show their value in the terms by which value is recognised, and care for them means protecting the conditions under which they could be tried while judgment waits; a culture that makes conformity to existing forms the price of beginning extinguishes them and experiences the stagnation as a shortage of ideas. Mathematics keeps the case of Hardy and Ramanujan in 1913: Hardy received a clerk's letter of unproved formulae from Madras, suspended the measure under which it failed (credentials, format, proof) long enough to read it on its own terms, and then judged it with full severity, separating the false from the known and the known from the new; a false statement proved fruitful for their joint work on partitions. That origin to come was protected and examined at once, and the story keeps its shadow in Ramanujan's hard years in England, since sensitivity does not end at first reception. **Origin ever present** is the capacity to begin, which Gebser's ever-present Origin names (§0/1 · #4): receiving the present without treating its existing form as the last thing it can become, so the morning reader is addressed as someone who can begin.

Compassion is neither pity nor indulgence, because knowledge of another's origins is power. A history of vulnerability predicts where pressure will work (the MKULTRA depatterning experiments of §0/1 · #4 are the extreme, intimate knowledge of how a self was organised used to strike it) and the same knowledge in another relation begins care. What separates the two is what the knowledge does to the knower's use of power: the other can withhold, correct, speak beyond the explanation offered for them, or leave, and their history gives reasons to understand them and no title to their future. Three powers work together in the return. Eros supplies the motive, **epistemic humility** (§0/1 · #5→0's standard at **#0**) keeps the returning `1` from claiming to have become `0`, and compassion supplies the orientation, a return that does not devour. Without eros the return never starts; without compassion it becomes an inventory compiled for use; without humility it ends on a new throne.

**Names and Powers.** To each QL position belongs a Name, the articulation proper to it, and a Power, the cost or force through which the Name becomes capable (the pairing is the author's), set beside L0's questions: **#0** Why? Truth, Play; **#1** What? Mind, Need; **#2** How? Word, Sacrifice; **#3** Which / Who? Logos, Decision; **#4** When / Where? Son, Love; **#5** Why-for? Image, Work. Across a row one position shows itself through all three: at **#2** every articulation spends something (the handover that names three problems has not named a fourth, and its writer chose which); at **#3** an account settles a course and someone settled it, so a note signed by nobody hides a decision behind a Name with no one at the slash; at **#4** what comes forth must be allowed its own relation, a successor or released software meeting circumstances its maker did not foresee, which is love at that position. A Name without its Power would certify itself at no cost; a Power without its Name would be force with no account of what it serves.

**Judgment, refusal and addresses.** Compassion includes judgment and refusal. A faithful delegate may return a commission as mistaken, a community may withhold material whose circulation would expose its members, a maker may stop an operation whose success destroys its purpose; the return can equally be affirmative, a commission changed so an excluded person can take part. Against a protected account stands an accountable mediation that can still judge, bind and refuse while remaining reachable by what its judgments do, and refusing to judge at all is cancellation by another route. A return has to arrive somewhere to count, and the six products give it addresses: Central's carried ground, Actuation's evaluator or commission, AIKit's capability and permission horizon, the Factory's developmental form, Workcell's material arrangement, Quaternal Logic's framing relation. Recording a difference does not return it (a complaint filed where nobody reads, a correction logged beside an answer still given), so the criterion is practical: did what was encountered reach the condition it implicated, and does the next act inherit the change?

**Trust, double devotion, culture, finish.** Trust is the dependence already enacted on language, memory, witness, method and other people before any account certifies it (§0/1 · #2); a handover is an act of trust in both directions, and compliance manufactured through opacity betrays it even when every statement is true. The author's unpublished draft, *The Definition of God*, gives the maker's double devotion, to the whole that exceeds every frame and to the specific frame through which it arrives now, without the first the frame becomes an idol and without the second the whole an abstraction; devotion to the whole alone clears every fence in the name of the road, devotion to the particular alone keeps the gate shut because it loves the gate. A culture meets the same test: it can promote a collective to the throne (State, Church, Humanity, Platform) or recognise an inherited image as an image, and a counterculture can inherit the throne it opposed, counting `(−1)−(+1)=−2` where the old order counted `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, the same zero lost between them. Jung's individuation as differentiation leading to broader collective relationships, which §0/1 · #3 followed, is the culture's form: it can meet its own internal relations and their consequences. Knowing when a work is finished belongs to the vocation: at seven the nurse signs the sheet, reads it aloud, answers two questions and goes home, and the note has done its work if it leaves the reader more able to begin. In the draft's words the essay's doctrine demands a vessel in the world of tools.

## Tension / limit

Compassion here is loving sensitivity to origins and carries no claim of machine feeling and no sentimental agreement; the carrier of the boundary is the definition itself, an orientation of the return with the other's power to withhold, correct and leave retained. A justified refusal can be more faithful than reconciliation under a protected criterion. Return also leaves room for stability: a standard can remain after challenge, a commission can be reaffirmed, an account can reject a counterclaim, provided the relevant office was addressable by the encounter and the judgment stays attributable. Mediation as such is unfaulted; the fault is derivative authority that forgets its derivation. Destruction, capture and counterfeit provenance are distinct failures, and the counterfeit-source field is held without any universal empirical accusation. Model collapse carries its own limit as the manuscript states it, recursive training under the experiment's conditions, and the labelling case rests on one reported workforce.
