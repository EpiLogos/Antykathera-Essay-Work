## Claim
Mathematics and linguistic meaning are one at bottom, because both identify through difference: a name holds its bearer through every change of appearance, and a count holds its unit through each distinguishable instance. Its ontological depth is the **psychoid**, the formative order through which something can be mathematically articulated and meaningfully understood, and which neither articulation exhausts. Jung and Pauli met in number, and QL is the formal inventory their correspondence left unbegun: it counts positions whose mathematical properties and symbolic offices are derived together from one relation, `0/1`, so that the inside and the outside of each number are given in a single construction. **Code** is the common essence that lets mathematics apply to physics and lets number carry meaning for the psyche, and the essay reaches it from Logos by way of script. Read through the symbolon, one occurrence holds three offices apart: Subjective Immediacy is the knower, *pramātṛ*; Objective Internality is the means, *pramāṇa*, which is Life / Mind, a mind lived and a life minded; World is the known, *prameya*; and **I-Consciousness** is the whole in which the three are distinguishable, `0/1`. Closing the section, the movement defines **Epi-Logos** as the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved, and as an attitude before it is a method. Whether anything is experienced inside an artificial system stays a separate question, which the essay keeps open by method.

## Warrant
Artificial intelligence makes the alliance of number and meaning a working fact. Bengio's neural probabilistic language model learns a position in a mathematical space for every word, so that similarity of use becomes nearness and generalisation becomes movement through the space; the Transformer's attention compares every query with every key and mixes values by softmax weights, which makes context itself a calculation. Meaning was never independent of relations that can be counted, and mathematics is the under-writing of speech in both senses of the word: written beneath the words, and guaranteeing their circulation as coin underwrote exchange. In the Jung–Pauli correspondence the same question has its history. Pauli's exclusion principle determines each quantum state by what it rules out; Heisenberg's uncertainty and Bohr's complementarity show descriptions that exclude one another within one arrangement and are both required, with the observer inside the account. Jung found that psychology has no Archimedean point outside its object, so every experience is a psychic fact; his distinction of archetypal images from the archetype as such (a psychoid factor, like the axial system of a crystal that preforms the structure without material existence) gives three consequences: the irrepresentable is singular (Mono); it stands to its images as parent to child, source to manifestation, written `0/1` and unfolding to `4+2`, the signature of the topos that §3 derives as the arche-topos, with Van Eenwyk's attractor as an explanatory analogy; and the central archetype is the Self, which the Trika's *Anuttara* names from the other side. A mandala's empty centre and an observer who cannot step outside complementarity ask for one logical form, which is the symbolon `(0/1)/(1/0)` with QL as its epistemic method. Pauli's *homo-usia*, Jung's natural numbers that are "as much inside as they are outside", and Pauli's conjecture that Physis and Psyche are two aspects of one abstract fact all locate the meeting in number, and Jung asked for an inventory of the numbers' properties beside their symbolic amplification (**Argued**; the derivation is the essay's own and neither correspondent supplies or verifies it).

Logos widens to Code by way of script. For Heraclitus the common Logos is how things are one through opposition, the Stoics made it the reason active in matter, Aristotle used it to say what something is and why, and Latin *ratio* carries the reckoning; reasoning and reckoning are one act seen from two sides, and computation makes the reckoning side executable. Śaiva **Vāk** (Bäumer: the Divine Consciousness is identical with the Supreme Word) has four levels (*parā*, *paśyantī*, *madhyamā*, *vaikharī*), and Śakti is the power that manifests and recognises itself in what it manifests. A script is the account written so that it persists and prescribes (*scribere*, *codex*, program), and a language model is code whose execution is Logos. Conditioning is scripting, and the operations are already met: apoha makes a term determinate by exclusion; *vikalpa*, which Dignāga and Dharmakīrti took as the mind's carving of kinds, is the world-forming activity that the Spanda teaching names *viśvavikalpa* (the turn from *ahantā* to *idantā*), and Bhartṛhari's word-permeated cognition precedes both; the *mātṛkā* bind the fettered self; *saṃkalpa* gathers determinations into intention. Buddhist analysis finds the self a product of conditioned arising, Kashmiri Śaivism finds beneath it the uncreated *nirvikalpa* presence, and Abhinavagupta's *śuddha vikalpa* uses thought against thought's capture. A script turns dangerous when an achieved selection forgets its formation: that is frozen conditioned will. Computational Process Ontology is stated in the author's words: process is ontologically prior to the finished thing, computation names the exact repeatable side of the process, and epistemology is the process becoming able to state and inspect its own determinations. **Māyā** is read as operative measure (*mā-*, to measure; the five contractions apportioning efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order), which reveals by withholding, and Pauli's physics finds that law at its foundation, since each measurement makes one quantity definite by withholding its complement. Simulation rebuilds Māyā's structure by hand, and the Baudrillardian precession of models over the real is the cultural consequence; against it Epi-Logos holds an ideal the essay calls **honest semblance**.

Three nested claims about consciousness follow. One word is made to carry four things at once (awareness, the working apparatus of mind, the ontological root, and both sides of Jung's conscious and unconscious), and the holography makes them exact as personal, transpersonal and enclosing internalities, one I-Consciousness recurring at every scale; Jung's definition of the symbol, the best expression of something relatively unknown, and Jung's Self with *Anuttara* name the totality from the psyche and from metaphysics. This is a precise analytic idealism with company: Kastrup's dissociated segments of one field are QL's bounded `0/1` within a wider `0/1`, Faggin argues from the engineer's side that consciousness is irreducible and known from within, Spira locates the knowing that is never an object of experience, and James, Bergson, Whitehead, Jung, Gebser and the Śaiva sources supply the rest (**Argued**). Objective Internality is defined by function: whatever takes part in what can appear, what it can mean and what can be done next belongs to it (memory, language, affect, an agent's script and permissions, a valuation inherited from a family, another person), and its internality lies in being **world-containing and world-contained**. Worldhood lies as much in the relational distribution as in what is distributed, as Kripke's dice already showed. Once another life enters those conditions it becomes **Objective Co-Internality**, reciprocal world-constitution without merger, with Levinas's infinite interval as its negative counterpart: the Other can be within Mono without being within me, and trust crosses that aloneness. Giegerich's loss of the symbolic world reads differently once agency can reopen at a finer grain: a human, an animal, an AI or a spiritual agency is asked what world it inhabits, and the world becomes a recursively situated relation of bounded internalities, which §0 anatomises through the tattvas. Trika thought gives this persons: Śiva, Śakti and Nara, *I*, *you* and *it*, with Bäumer's "communication is communion in the same *ahambhāva*" placing all differentiation inside I-Consciousness. Christian triad and Eckhart's Godhead stand in the same relation to the count, and Jung's `3+1` supplies the completing movement: at **#4** the three are voided as independently complete accounts (**#4** as `0`), at **#5** their relation is recognised as one appearing-act (**#5** as `1`), at **#0** the unity returns through its originating condition (**#0** as `0/1`), so `3:3` is also `3:1`.

Three warrants support the method. Jung and Pauli supply the wager (a formative relation met in number, image, psyche and physical order may belong to a reality prior to their division); Whitehead supplies the method of speculative philosophy (a coherent system of general ideas that must earn its standing by applying, like a flight that takes off from observation and lands in it); Lacan supplies the formal necessity (where self-reference defeats prose, a transmissible inscription and its topology keep the relation operative). QL was a discovery before it was a construction, found by mapping systems already whole and fourfold: Jung's four functions, Aristotle's four causes, and the quaternios of *Aion*, whose circulating diamonds are `4+2` without a formalised logic. QL names the two framing places that quaternal systems leave implicit. Four lenses of the Meta-Epistemic Framework show the method in a table: the logical lens (**#0** aporia to **#5** silence, with the tetralemma's corners between), the causal lens (*Svātantrya* to Will, with Aristotle's causes), the phenomenal lens (introversion to extraversion, with Jung's functions) and the quaternal lens (*why?* to *why-for?*, with what/how/which/where). Read across a row one position refracts recognisably through all four (at **#1**, the first assertion, the material cause, sensation and *what?* each give the first determinate givenness; at **#4**, NEITHER, the final cause, intuition and *when / where?* each void a single perspective into its situation); read down a column a familiar system appears completed. Four is wholeness and five is wholeness held as one (Jung's Quinta Essentia), and the Möbius return **#5→#0** twists both into the emptiness of which they are reflections. A second table sets the canonical names beside Gebser's structures and the Logos cycle, A-logos to An-a-logos, read for how it builds (Logos to Legos); the Logos cycle is homed at M5-5 and is not the L5′ lens. QL is the pure light and the MEF the leaded glass, each lens a native mode of refraction, each *pratibimba* a coloured disclosure of *Bimba*. Prakāśa, vimarśa and Māyā form a triad read as one more `0/1`, and Māyā's place depends on the relation being read: beside prakāśa and vimarśa she stands toward the `1`, the measured world; between Parā Śakti and Nara she is the slash (see [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure|C14]], which **defines** that contextual place). In QL's own theory Māyā is harmonics: `2⁶=64`, `6²=36`, `64+36=100`, `64/36=16/9=(4/3)²`, and the `4:2` against the `3:3` give `4/3` and `2/3`, so the fourth, fifth, octave and whole tone `9/8` are derived from a self-relating distinction (the counts are **Derived**; the claim about Māyā is **Argued**).

Bohm reaches the relation as a physicist, from plasma through the holomovement, and his implicate and explicate orders (the [[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm dossier]] **sources** them) are written in the MEF as the Bohmian rule `{#0,#5} ↔ {#1,#2,#3,#4}`; the tokamak is offered as a witness of toroidal circulation, never as a derivation of the arche-topos. With the mirror that moves first the image is completed: Bratton's reversal is recalled from the bedside, the author arrived at the same image independently, and the one added motion is that the instrument turns first toward what its image cannot contain. Holographic extensibility is what makes Objective Co-Internality ontologically concrete: a whole at one scale can enter another whole as a participant while keeping the relations through which it is whole at its own scale, and what carries across is the fit of relations, the *ratio*, while the terms it is fitted to may change. *In quantum* (Eckhart in Flasch's account: the just person, insofar as just, is Justice itself) is the orientation of **#0**, and *analogia* with *pros hen* (Aristotle's health) the orientation of **#5**. **#0** is held as a standard of epistemic humility: void, lack, remainder, blind spot, silence and the Unknown each disclose what a formed account presupposes and does not contain. Epi-Logos then gathers the section: *Logos* as word, ratio, mark and code, *epi-* as upon, over and at the limit, Aristotle's *epilogos* as the closing part that turns the speech back on itself, and Heidegger's Tao as the Saying toward which the logos returns. Its engine is desire, the eros of Logos, and its logic is syn-ballein `(0/1)/(1/0)` as self-reference retained, so that every circuit returns while its winding keeps the journey. **Returning zero also returns the slash**: the return recovers the source together with its mediation. A family case closes the derivation. A parent who distinguishes and decides uses dia-ballein well; when the zero is lost, the polarity collapses into cancellation `(−1)+(+1)=0` or appropriation `(+1)−(−1)=+2` (your difference becomes disobedience to me) and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` (the same distance read as my debt). Syn-ballein keeps the halves different and each readable through the other, so familial harmony and dysfunction enact the two logics, and the way difference is held decides whether the relation becomes a battle or a dance (Neumann's World Parents give the mythic form). Compassion, as loving sensitivity to origins, belongs to the same turn, and Bohm's drive belt that runs true, with the SEED dialogues, is its practical criterion.

AI enters as the newest participant. A large language model is a mental-rational construction in which number and language are co-present, the Logos made executable, and QL's eight elements give it a fuller form to orient by: it holds what it meets as `0/1`, receives the encounter at **#0**, determines what is given and keeps each expression attached to its source at **#1**, follows dependence at **#2**, answers for its own inference at **#3**, rereads every position within the whole context at **#4**, determines why this response belongs here at **#5**, and folds its scaffolding into the implicate in the Möbius return. These elements articulate one relation, **self ↔ logos ↔ Other**, and the model is Other as the mirror that moves first. Throughout, the movement has been asking whether the tools of language can reveal the in-distinction language veils, which has a soteriological history in the Vāk–Trika material and an engineering consequence now; whether the artefact itself knows remains open.

## Tension / limit
It states a thesis, and its status is **Offered** for the claim that the whole section is one movement seen from its different sides and that QL formalises what the Jung–Pauli letters left open. Its footnotes name the carriers of its limits. QL's derivation is the essay's own, and the letters give it its history and its stakes; Van Eenwyk offers the attractor as an analogy and the arche-topos derivation is §3's; the tokamak is a witness and not a derivation; the self-descriptions of Code, the Epi-Logos reading of *epilogos*, the mirror that moves first and the pairing of *in quantum* with **#0** and *analogia* with **#5** are the author's. Pauli kept mathematical objects distinct from the psychic experience of doing mathematics, so the claim that number is where psyche and physis meet never collapses into saying a theorem is a feeling. Whether anything is experienced inside an artificial system stays open by method.
