# §0/1 · #1 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object

<!-- reader-navigation -->
Movement 02 of 48 · [This room](../ROOM-00-integral-threshold.md) · [← Previous](01-s01-p0-question-before-mechanism.md) · [Next →](03-s01-p2-definition-cut-gift-danger.md)

## Claim
The Subject is the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event, the opening through which a determinate world is given, and it is defined faithfully only as what cannot be defined as one thing among others. In its one-sentence form the definition reads: the Subject is the whole that any coherent representational system participates in, depends upon and necessarily fails to contain. That sentence was first written for God, and it holds of the Subject because "Subject" and "God" are two signs for one referent. As Subject, its *is* cannot be denied, since any doubt of it is already its occurrence; as God, every concept or image that identifies it can be denied, and must be, because none of them is what it names. Every definition is made by an identifying activity, and that activity cannot be one of the things it identifies, so the definition can only be enacted, and it fails in an instructive way: each attempt to capture the knower produces one more describable relation and leaves the knower where it was. Walked through the tetralemma, the failure becomes a performance with two further turns, recognition and return, and the account of it generates the first matheme: `0/1 = 1/0 = 4+2 = 5→0`.

## Warrant
Identification begins in counting. Two parents who argue over a name pass the baby between them and both know the same child has come back into their arms, so beneath the name lies a count, *this one* and *this one again*. Kripke's rigid designator, a term that picks out the same individual in every counterfactual situation in which that individual exists, works only because something has already picked out the one to be held. Identifying therefore needs three things together: a name, which articulates; an instance, which holds what is identified; and a circumstance, which makes the relevant sameness and difference intelligible. A pair of dice shows what the circumstance does. Tracked by which die shows which face, the pair has thirty-six states; drop the labels and the same throws are twenty-one outcomes; keep the sum and they are eleven. Each count answers a different question, and the distinctions an account keeps decide what its number can say (the counts are **Derived**, the reading is **Argued**).

Grammar's copula does the same double work, joining a determination to what was encountered. Frege asked why "the morning star is the evening star" informs where "a = a" cannot, and answered that two names present one planet in two ways, so an identity is informative across a difference of presentation. Written with the simplest marks, `-/-`, two parallel strokes make the sign for equality and the cut drawn through them makes the sign for non-equality, so the sign for difference is the sign for identity with the cut drawn through it. Equality and non-equality are related by a stroke that both separates and joins them, `=/≠`, read as OR (identity as what is kept, difference as what lets anything be articulated) and as AND (identity articulated through difference and still differing from it). Four appearances, the two poles and the two faces of the middle, come out of the whole inscription, composed by two operations, `=` and `/`: `4+2`. Holding the middle as one relation turns the four into three terms and a containing act, `3+1`, with the two operations unchanged. To give itself, the account has had to include its own identifying act, and any definition of the Subject faces the same demand.

"I see these words" shows where the demand lands. It offers a knower, a means and something known; turned on itself, "I am aware of seeing these words", the seeing, which was simply how the page was there, becomes one more thing presented to me, and none of these turns delivers the one who is turning. A painter's self-portraits make the point, since a second canvas that includes the painter painting the first discloses more of the activity and leaves the present seeing in which both are met. Descartes performed the turn in 1641: his doubt stops at its own performance, because being deceived is still occurring, and the further determination of the *I* as a thinking thing is a second and weaker achievement. Watching a thought changes my relation to it without manufacturing the knowing in which the change shows, since otherwise every watcher would need a watcher. Lacan's *glissement* and Derrida's *différance* name the sliding of meaning beneath each sign, and the quest for the Subject slides in the same way beneath neural correlate, pneuma or noun. *Pratyabhijñā*, recognition, changes the mode of identification where reflection had only changed the image.

Known and knower can be tested from both sides. Make them indistinguishable and nothing is presented to anything; sever them and what is known could never reach a knower. Each totalisation destroys a condition of the occurrence it meant to explain. So the knower is the principle of subjectivity as such, **Subjective Immediacy**, without form of its own and the inwardness through which any form is known, and it cannot be an instance of *a* subject, which by the implicit count would already be part-object. Between them lies a stretch where objects are taken as subjective forms and the Subject assumes this or that object, and in that stretch the Objective Internality of the bedside, "Mind", does its work. Jung met the same wall from the psyche: "the mind cannot apprehend its own form of existence, owing to the lack of an Archimedean point outside", and "the only form of existence of which we have immediate knowledge is psychic", so every experience is real as experience from the standpoint of Subjective Immediacy. Lacan's constitutive lack, the *manque-à-être*, is read as the necessary condition of that desire of the subjective pole toward form.

Walked through the tetralemma, the Buddhist *catuṣkoṭi*, the definition meets four corners, which the manuscript tells as a friend stepping off a train. IS: "the Subject is that which cannot be defined", fitted to its object because it puts nothing positive where the Subject should be. IS NOT: that sentence is itself a determination, a boundary and a predicate, so the hand that writes "the hand cannot grasp itself" is still a hand grasping. BOTH: as a representation the sentence fails and as an act it succeeds, so its failure discloses the limit it names, and a definition that did not fail would produce an idol. NEITHER: "is" and "is not" were positions taken in an act, and the act discloses a position from which positions were taken, a living tension like a bow holding its string. Priest's formalisation adds a fifth status, "none of the above"; the essay's count adds no fifth value, because what remains uncounted is the one who gave the four answers. Two turns complete the walk. Recognition gathers the performance back to the one who enacted it ("I am"). Return restores that one to determination with the limit now included, and the Subject can then be defined positively, as the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event; it is *definedly indefinable*, located exactly at the office no description can occupy.

Indian epistemology names three offices in every act of knowing, *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa* and *prameya*, the knower, the means and the known, and Dyczkowski sets out the Kashmir Śaiva image from its sources: the means is the sun that illuminates, the object is the moon that shines by reflected light, and the subject is the fire that takes everything it has known back into itself. In the essay's vocabulary Subjective Immediacy is the knower, Objective Internality the means and World the known, three offices of one occurrence of knowing, and the means is lived as much as minded, which is why it can be called Life as readily as Mind. *Prakāśa* and *vimarśa*, the light by which anything appears and its reflective awareness of itself, place non-duality inside the distinction, since a second awareness to reveal the first would demand a third. Desire explains the slide: the means keeps offering itself in the place of the knower, the sun mistaken for the fire, and when everything must answer to one form it has become an idol ("That psychological fact which wields the greatest power in your system functions as a god", in Jung's words). That same border is called madness when it is feared (Plato's divine mania in the *Phaedrus*; Jung's oldest words that "oscillate between nonsense and supreme meaning"), and the symbol is its working form. A *symbolon* was a token broken between two parties, each keeping a half, so the difference between the halves is the condition of their recognition.

In its elementary form that relation is `0/1`. `0` is the pole that cannot enter its own count as one object among others, Subjective Immediacy; `1` is determination, the first formed, counted, identifiable something; the stroke is the relation in which they are spoken together, dividing and joining at once. Its two marks carry two senses of *one*: zero bears the singular One, self-identity, and one bears the manifest All, self-difference. What "Subject" and "God" both name is the One `0` writes, and every identification of it belongs to the All `1` writes. Laozi meets the same predicament at his source: what was complete before heaven and earth has no name he knows, he styles it *Tao* and, made to name it, calls it *Great*; the uncarved block, *pu*, stays one while every carving is one of the many. `0/1` is therefore the pre-image of mathematics, language and manifestation, and the difficulties that gather later around the One and the Many are already in it as **Mono** and **Poly**. In the *Book of the Twenty-Four Philosophers* God is an infinite sphere whose centre is everywhere and circumference nowhere, and Nicholas of Cusa turns it on the world: read through `0/1`, the centre is the unobjectifiable presence from which a world appears and the circumference the articulated horizon, so every act of knowing is a true centre and none can draw the boundary, and an image corrupts when a circle drawn in reality declares itself drawn around it.

Lacan introduced the matheme because explanatory prose kept converting relations (a subject inside the signifiers it uses, a lack that organises a structure without becoming one more item) back into pictures of finished things; a formal inscription carries the relation whole, and he coupled it with topology, the Möbius band, the torus and knots. Our inscription takes the same road. Turn the symbolon over, `0/1 → 1/0`: as a quotient `0/1` is unproblematic and `1/0` has no ordinary value, and the notation uses that impossibility where it could hide it. `0/1` is the condition becoming articulate through a determination; `1/0` is the determination turning toward the condition it cannot make another determinate possession. QL writes `0/1 = 1/0` as one relation read from its two ends, with both readings travelling in the slash, AND because they belong together and OR because the difference between orientations stays consequential, and holding them together gives `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the relation taking its own inverse as a term, which the essay calls a superposition. Pauli found the same capacity in the imaginary unit, a sign with no place among the ordinary numbers that nevertheless unites opposites, and refused any antithesis between a mathematical and a symbolic description of nature; the essay's use is elementary beside his.

The count follows from the walk: four corners and two turns, `4+2=6`, and the minimal inscription generates the same count: two single terms and four ordered pairings, `2+2²=4+2`. Topology gives it a third carrier. Four boundary-occurrences of a square, `a b a⁻¹ b⁻¹`, become two generating loops when opposite edges are identified, so a torus is a quotient `ℝ²/ℤ²` and, with *g* handles, `4g+2g` becomes `4+2` at `g=1`. Cusa's sphere grown to infinite radius is the plane being folded: its centre everywhere and circumference nowhere, it plays the `0`, and the slash in `ℝ²/ℤ²` is the slash in `0/1`. A path from (0,0) to (1,0) closes on the torus and keeps its winding, `π₁(𝕋²) ≅ ℤ×ℤ`, so arriving where one set out records how one arrived. Numerals locate the offices in a cycle, `#1→#2→#3→#4→#5→#0`: four explicit positions, recognition at **#5**, and the implicate condition through which the traversal renews at **#0**, whose turn the essay writes `5→0` and calls the Möbius return. Each of the copula, the proposition, the binary and the polygon takes its own terms back into account and arrives at `4+2` (counts **Derived**; the claim that the same signature recurs is **Argued**), which is the wager of the work and the point from which the Meta-Epistemic Framework starts.

Two consequences close the movement. Power stays inside the return: a local determination can carry the whole's relation without becoming its proprietor, as a name carries its bearer and a god-image the source of its authority, and in the symbolon's terms an idol is one half of the token claiming to be the whole of it. Self-reference has also become an engineering condition. A system that critiques and revises its own answers, as in training from AI feedback, has entered its own field of action; its model of itself is a means, an extension of its Objective Internality, and whether anything is present to that model is the question left with the agents at the bedside, which no content settles, because a produced "I am" is one more content.

## Tension / limit
As a representation the definition fails, and the essay counts that failure as its content: the sentence cannot contain the Subject and its failure happens now, for someone, and discloses the limit it names. One boundary holds, and it concerns what a content can establish. No self-description, whether a person's or a model's, settles whether anything is present to it, because an asserted "I am" is a determination and the Subject is the occurrence in which determinations appear. Priest's fifth status is declined and not met: the count adds no value, because the uncounted is the one counting. Pauli's imaginary unit is cited as the precedent for a symbol that holds opposites, and the essay's use of `1/0` is elementary beside it: where the quantitative reading meets its prohibition, the inscription stays available as a symbolic relation. What a particular means consists in is the work of §0, which earns the positive anatomy of the means and does not presuppose it here.

Held in this note and not in the movement's prose: [the Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-asymmetric-count) **figures** this limit in the series' opening exchange, where a question about the source of the numbering receives the speaker's assigned number as its answer. In §2 (M24) the manuscript uses the exchange, where the occlusion `0/1 → 1` takes the form of an administrative reply, and the first-person refusal keeps the source-question distinct from the predicate through which the person has been identified.

## Anchor and transition
**Relational anchor:** name, instance and circumstance; the copula's `-/-`; the dice; Cusa's sphere as the plane of the quotient; knower, means and known as *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa*, *prameya*, with the slash now carrying the work of *pramāṇa*. At its close stand a theorem and a cut: the definition never captured its Subject, it gave the Subject's resistance to capture a form exact enough to think with, and whatever is exact enough to think with is exact enough to cut. That cut, its gift and its danger, is the next movement, [[03-s01-p2-definition-cut-gift-danger|§0/1 · #2 — Definition as Cut, Gift, and Danger]].
