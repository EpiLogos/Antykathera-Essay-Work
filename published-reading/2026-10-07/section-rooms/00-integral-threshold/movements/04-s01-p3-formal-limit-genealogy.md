# §0/1 · #3 — The Formal-Limit Genealogy

## Claim
A sufficiently articulate system cannot convert every condition of its articulation into an ordinary internal term without remainder. That limit appears first in the foundations of mathematics, where the instruments of repair are implicated in what has failed, and it reappears in language, in process thought, in physics and in the sciences of mind, each time as a different relation between a form and the act that makes it. From it the movement derives two limits and two acts, which the essay holds in four lines: the approachable limit is the basis of investigation, and the absolute limit is the basis of faith. Russell asked investigation to supply the basis of faith, and Gödel made exact, for arithmetic under stated hypotheses, that a theory cannot certify its own ground from its own resources. Here the essay also defines the crossed zero `Ø`, names Deferential Intelligence, and carries trust, knowledge, belief and faith (defined at M03) under the formal limit.

## Warrant
Our case begins with Whitehead and Russell's *Principia*, which tried to make the grounds of mathematics as explicit as its proofs, and with Russell's wish for "certainty in the kind of way in which people want religious faith". Frege's principle that any condition we can state gathers a collection looked too obvious to defend, and Russell's paradox follows from it (told as a librarian's catalogue of the catalogues that do not list themselves): gather every collection that does not belong to itself, and `R∈R ⟺ R∉R`. Nothing in the reasoning was careless, and what failed was the principle that whatever condition we can state gathers something we can hold as one. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/russell/russell-1908-theory-types/russell-1908-theory-types|Russell]] restricted the formation of collections with the theory of types, which sorts them into levels and lets a collection take members only from below. Read through the notation, membership and non-membership are the two poles of one axis, `(−1)/(+1)`, and the swing of the paradox is the polarity still alive. Trouble lies in the quick exits from it: a decree that the collection belongs hands the whole span to membership, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, a decree that it does not hands the span to non-membership, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and dismissal as nonsense cancels, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, leaving Frege's principle free to produce the paradox again. Russell's theory of types is dia-ballein put to its proper use, a cut made and stated with its reason, and a reader who keeps both answers in view until the conflict shows their common source in the generating rule performs syn-ballein `(0/1)/(1/0)`. Russell's paradox also shows name and count meeting at the foundations: `R` has no bearer standing apart from its description, so "what has been identified as this one?" becomes the opening question asked of a collection.

Where the fault lay, the two authors decided differently. Russell placed it in the expressions and put his trust in a cleaner instrument; after some twenty years he had to separate the value of reasoning from the absolute reassurance he wanted from it. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/whitehead-1978-process-reality|Whitehead]] placed it in the picture of reality, the **fallacy of misplaced concreteness** that takes an abstraction for the concrete, and asked how anything becomes a definite actuality at all. His answer is **concrescence**, from *con-* and *crescere*, an occasion's becoming by taking up (**prehending**) the conditions available to it and integrating them into one experience, which perishes into a datum for later occasions ("The many become one, and are increased by one"). We read `0/1→4+2` as a logic of epistemic concrescence: the many grow while the One `0` writes is neither added to nor diminished. "In formal logic, a contradiction is the signal of a defeat: but in the evolution of real knowledge it marks the first step in progress towards a victory" is Whitehead's second judgment, and Jung reaches it from the psyche (paradox "one of our most valuable spiritual possessions", and Tertullian's faith in paradox as the highest religious certainty). From this the movement concludes that the religious relation is the honest relation to `0/1`, since the distinction between what can be determined and what cannot is the condition of rational certainty itself, and Russell's paradox was that condition showing through the system built on it. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form|Spencer-Brown]] approached the same question from the side types had closed: a distinction that re-enters its own space alternates between marked and unmarked, the clean oscillation of the held polarity given time, and his re-entering mark writes in a calculus of indications the turn the essay writes `5→0`. In Kashmir Śaivism the Spanda school gives the same pulse as three powers of one act (will, knowledge, action), which the essay reads as a `0/1`: will as the `0` turning toward determination, knowledge as the slash, action as the `1`, with the first and final moments coinciding in the `5→0` return. Inquiry also has an eros: the **eros of Logos** joins Jung's Logos (discrimination, judgment) with Eros (the capacity to relate), and *savoir*, from *sapere*, keeps the taste inside the knowing.

Gödel's paper of 1931 is a different kind of result. Let a theory be effectively specified and contain enough arithmetic to represent the checking of proofs; a sentence $G_T$ can be constructed that says no number codes a proof of it in $T$. If $T$ proves it, $T$ proves a statement and its negation, so a consistent $T$ cannot, and what the sentence says is then true; ω-consistency closes the other route, so $T$ proves neither $G_T$ nor its negation. Consistent still, the theory goes on establishing genuine truths, and paradox returns through the success of self-reference as a separation of truth from provability relative to a stated theory. Gödel's second theorem reaches the confidence with which the theory is used: for a theory strong enough to formalise the relevant reasoning, a proof of its own consistency would yield a proof of $G_T$, so a consistent theory cannot prove it. Here the orientation `1/0` takes arithmetical form (the determinate system asked to account for its ground can answer only with another sentence it cannot prove), and trust becomes examinable, since every use of the theory enacts a reliance that now appears inside the reasoning as a stated hypothesis. A stronger theory $T'$ can prove $G_T$ without contradicting it, because the sentence concerns the absence of a proof in $T$, and the same result applies to $T'$ in turn. Between what a theory can express and what it can establish lies the **immutable gap**: it relocates with every enlargement and returns through it, so it was never one withheld fact awaiting discovery. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/godel/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions|Gödel]] therefore proves scoped internal limits and leaves the desire for a complete, internally certified account of arithmetic untenable.

Cusa's polygon pictures the relation: the intellect is to truth as an inscribed polygon is to the circle, ever more similar as sides are added and never equal unless resolved into identity, a change in kind no further side achieves; the square became a torus by the same kind of act, identification of its edges. Out of the image comes the aphorism: the approachable limit is the basis of investigation, the absolute limit the basis of faith, the limit of investigation the limit of determination, the limit of faith never to be determined. Gödel's sentence is an approachable limit and the recurrence is the absolute one. Faith here is the lucid continuation through the limit every use of a theory already enacts, with its grounds, scope and conditions of revision retained, and it is no belief in a proposition the theorem failed to settle. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus|Wittgenstein]] met self-reference earlier: a proposition shows through its articulation what it cannot add to its contents as another fact, "the subject does not belong to the world but it is a limit of the world", and the book's own propositions are a ladder thrown away after climbing. A torus gives the void a definite relation to the form around it: on a sphere every closed loop shrinks to a point, and on a torus the two circuits cannot be shrunk, so the opening belongs to what makes the form a torus, and Lacan's two circuits (demand around the body, the empty circle around the hole) say the same of desire.

Religious and psychological material follows the same relation. An archetypal image presents a relationship in a form through which it can be felt, its force can be numinous, and it can organise a **complex**, which the theory of complex systems describes as a basin of attraction; a living symbol disturbs the categories of the gathering and lets another organisation develop. A quilting point settles an identity by binding it to law, the God-image gives the need for ultimate authority a form, and the movement writes the first occlusion as `Ø`, the crossed zero: the zero of the Subject with the mediating stroke fused with the apparent subject, so that the formed interpretation is experienced as the interpreter. This gives Nietzsche's self-overcoming an exact question: how can I exceed what I have become if every encounter must first confirm what I am? Derrida's openness and Wittgenstein's rule-following supply the practical requirement that a language for intelligence must let a received expression meet a circumstance it did not specify. Descartes's lived union (the sailor and the ship) shows the order of his inquiry reversed by the inheritance that keeps his mind/matter distinction and drops his certainty. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/history-philosophy-of-science/frank/frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot/frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot|The Blind Spot (Frank, Gleiser, Thompson)]] names what that reversal leaves unseen, the lived experience on which science depends, and the essay places the Subject at exactly that spot. If "physical" means what a future complete science will describe, its content is not yet supplied, and the expectation still governs what counts as explanation; investigation is asked to supply the basis of faith at the scale of a culture. Chalmers's hard problem states the consequence, Carter's anthropic principle meets the observer by another door, and neurophenomenology and Bache's twenty-year first-person experiment show inquiry changing the investigator. Kekulé, Tesla, Einstein, Newton, Swedenborg and Faggin show discovery arriving through image, felt configuration and transformation before it becomes demonstrable, and a culture that expels the event while inheriting the result conceals the powers through which new evidence arises. Epistemic humility therefore belongs in the first drawing of an inquiry: state what the method admits, what it brackets, and how an encounter could change those decisions, and let the commission, the criterion and the claimed authority stay examinable too. Jung's "I don't need to believe. I know" reports knowledge of an encounter, with faith as the continuation through what that knowing cannot determine, and **Deferential Intelligence** begins where what has been encountered can change the means by which the next judgment is made. Politically, a civilisation gives its ontology force through institutions, and the denial of the Subject becomes political when it deprives a life of standing to question the means by which it is administered; the movement closes on the meaning crisis, decadence and Gebser's mutation.

## Tension / limit
Each witness keeps its own office, and the convergence carries the architecture. Gödel performs a formal proof under stated hypotheses (effective axiomatisation, enough arithmetic, consistency, ω-consistency), and the essay's reading of the second theorem as `1/0` in arithmetical form is **Argued** with the boundary stated in the prose: Gödel does not prove the notation, and within his hypotheses he gives the incomputable return of the `1` upon its ground a theorem's precision. Cusa and Eckhart perform mathematical-apophatic and linguistic disciplines; Whitehead supplies process metaphysics; Russell's wish for certainty is his own report. One tension holds throughout: Russell asked investigation to do faith's work, and the two bases differ in kind, so a life of inquiry needs both. Because their objects and methods differ, the agreement of the witnesses is evidence of one pressure met in several registers, and no single theorem is asserted on their behalf.

## Anchor and transition
[E3’s fides before arbitration](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#fides-before-arbitration) carries the enquiry's prior reliance and its renewed entrustment afterwards: formal investigation already undertakes a practice before it can arbitrate its results. Russell's, Gödel's and Wittgenstein's limits keep their own scopes, and *fides* and *credere* carry the project's relational passage through them, as Latin lexical history that derives none of the formal results. [A03](../../arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) **derives** the immutable gap that this movement now names at Gödel's stronger theory, [A23](../../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) **grounds** trust and faith under the limit, and [A31](../../arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) **defines** Deferential Intelligence, first named here.

The [complete Investigation and Faith aphorism](../../../symbolon/episteme/aphorisms/investigation-and-faith.md#complete-aphorism) **returns-to** the authorial relation carried through A03 and A23: investigation makes determinations, and [faith consciously continues through their non-exhaustion of ground](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active). In the manuscript the four lines follow Cusa's polygon and anchor the movement.

[[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C64-Paradox-Transforming-the-Containing-Field|Paradox]] **defines** how this limit moves across registers (Gödel, Cusa, Eckhart, Whitehead, the sciences of mind) as a transformation of the containing field without becoming one homogenised theorem.

**Mathematical anchor:** undecidability under stated formal conditions, and `R∈R ⟺ R∉R`. **Images:** Cusa's polygon approaching the circle, the re-entering mark as `5→0`, the ladder, the torus against the sphere. Held in this note and not in the movement's prose: Varela's calculus of self-reference as the formal bridge for Spencer-Brown's re-entry ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference|Varela]]), and the Flasch reading of Eckhart's qualified predication, of which the manuscript keeps only Flasch's naming of Eckhart as the philosopher of Christianity ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/flasch/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart|Flasch]]). Its question goes forward: [[05-s01-p4-gebser-diaphaneity|§0/1 · #4 — Gebserian Diaphaneity]] receives the mutation Gebser names, in which people recognise the conditions of their own seeing.

The [Formal Limit dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/formal-limit.md) **historicises** the genealogy through distinct consequences: Russell restricts formation, Gödel bounds derivability within a stated theory, and the early Wittgenstein distinguishes saying and showing. Later practice inquiry (rule-following in the *Investigations*) keeps its separate work identity. Cusa's theological precision and Eckhart's qualified predication keep their own objects.

*Tractatus* 5.632 and the saying/showing distinction bear a source-specific limit, and later practice requires its separate work and edition; the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-practice) **qualifies** this historical attribution.
