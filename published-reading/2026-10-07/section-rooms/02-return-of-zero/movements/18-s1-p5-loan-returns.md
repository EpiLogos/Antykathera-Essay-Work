## Movement thesis

§0/1 borrowed zero, and the loan is returned with what passed through it. A book lent for a year comes back with a train ticket, a softened spine and pencil in the margins: the same book, carrying the reading it went through. Its pencil marks are interest in the oldest sense, since §0/1 · #4 traced *interest* to *inter-esse*, what lies between, and a book returned with its margins full brings back what lay between lender and borrower without pricing it. §0/1 wrote `0` for the pole of knowing that cannot enter its own count as one more object and `0/1` for that pole's relation to the determinate world, before showing where the sign came from or what it could bear, on the trust that readers knew zero from school. Zero has now been followed from a gap a Babylonian reader filled from context to the term at which every number system declares its laws, and it comes back carrying a history of offices it did not have when it was lent. We have now earned the notation as the essay's own relational notation:

$$
0/1 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad 1/0.
$$

`0/1` names the indefinite condition becoming available through definite form, and `1/0` names definite form returning toward a condition it cannot convert into ordinary content. The slash is the act of differentiation and not always ordinary division. It can be named the root *symbolon*: overdetermined, because `0`, `1` and `/` stay mathematical signs, logical roles, phenomenological poles and directions of return, each under its own distinguished reading; generative, because the two orientations can be inverted, related, and made to take their own relation as a further term. Its status is **Argued** (synthesis), standing on the mathematics the previous movements derived.

## Warrant — the pair at work in arithmetic

In the Stern–Brocot construction the two signs do exact mathematical work together. Treat `0/1` and `1/0` as pairs (top, bottom) and insert between neighbours $a/b$ and $c/d$ their mediant $(a+c)/(b+d)$: the row grows $\frac01,\frac12,\frac11,\frac21,\frac10$, then $\frac01,\frac13,\frac12,\frac23,\frac11,\frac32,\frac21,\frac31,\frac10$, and continued without end puts every positive fraction in the row exactly once, in lowest terms and in order. No division is performed and nothing is divided by zero. Two properties, checkable by hand, make it work: the mediant lies strictly between its parents, since $\frac{a+c}{b+d}-\frac ab=\frac{bc-ad}{b(b+d)}>0$, and neighbours always satisfy $bc-ad=1$, true of the starting pair and preserved by each insertion, which keeps every fraction in lowest terms. Moritz Stern described the tree in 1858 and the clockmaker Achille Brocot found it a few years later, looking for gear ratios that approximate awkward fractions. `0/1` is arithmetic's most docile fraction and `1/0` its one forbidden gesture, and set side by side as a boundary pair they generate every positive ratio without either being consumed: as a quotient the forbidden gesture produced "Error", and as a boundary it bounds a whole field. Stern and Brocot read nothing philosophical into this; it shows that the two orientations of the essay's relation are already in plain mathematics the two edges between which every ratio is generated, and the relation is generative because neither is reduced to the other.

Each term of the notation now has a history behind it. Zero kept a place open in a Babylonian column, and the `0` of the notation keeps open the place of the knower. It became the additive identity in Brahmagupta's verses, and the `0` is self-identity, the One that remains itself through every determination. Von Neumann gathered zero into the first one, and the `1` is that holding, a mark whose whole content is its relation to a ground it does not contain. The crossed `Ø` records the mistake of taking the stroke for what it crosses, and the `1/0` is the determinate turning toward its ground and finding there the frame in which all its values were defined. Warrant runs both ways: the notation makes the history legible as its own prehistory, and the history gives the notation the exactness of what mathematics learned to do with zero.

§0/1 assigned the two senses of *one* (zero the singular One of self-identity, one the manifest All of self-difference), and they are older than the notation. al-Khwārizmī's Latin says that "one is the root of every number, and is outside number", naming the one that counts and the One that is not counted in a single word. History has sorted them into two signs: Bhāskara's *khahara*, the One as arithmetic could approach it, unaltered by everything inserted or taken away; and the zero ring, the All as arithmetic is forced to refuse. Ordinary number lives by refusing both, and the double refusal is what protects the two senses. Number cannot hold the One and the All together without severing or fusing them, and its edges reach for another register in which they can be held.

When a determinate `1` turns back toward the zero it carries, it meets that zero as its own division, `1/0`, a quotient no number supplies. It can be refused, as a field refuses it: difference is kept and return is forbidden, dia-ballein's proper cut. It can be fused, as in the zero ring: cancellation `(−1)+(+1)=0` carried through every pair, so that return succeeds by abolishing every difference; the other collapse, appropriation, would hand the span to one end and leave the return in the appropriator's hands. Or it can be carried, and Bhāskara's *Līlāvatī* keeps a number multiplied by zero as "a multiple of cipher" and, when a later division by zero arrives, the number returns unchanged: the break becomes the moment at which what was carried is recovered. That is the shape of `0/1` completed as `(0/1)/(1/0)`, syn-ballein in plain arithmetic. Bhāskara's rule does not prove the notation; it shows a twelfth-century arithmetic that had learned to keep a zero in play through an operation that would otherwise have destroyed the record of what it held. A small exercise makes the test apply to the notation itself: write `1` on a sheet, write `0` beside it with the stroke between, and the figure `0/1` is now one more determinate mark relative to the paper; erase it, and the sheet and the hand remain, knowing what the inscription did. `5→0`, the Möbius return, has the same shape: the achieved figure gives itself back to the condition from which it was drawn, and what returns is a capacity that has learned from what it made.

## Śaiva register and the binary

In the Trika, Śiva, Śakti and Nara are the *I*, the *you* and the *it* of one consciousness, set in `0/1` as Subjective Immediacy at the zero, Objective Internality at the slash and World at the one. That power of differentiation, the slash, is the psyche and the whole working apparatus of knowing, and Western mathematics developed that power to its exactness in rules, constructions, completions and machines. Taken by itself the power presents its products as everything there is, and the zero of the knower becomes either one more product or a void; returned to its place, it is recognised as Śakti, the power of a Subject it does not itself constitute. Zero's return is the return of the West, with all its exactness, to the subject from which that exactness proceeds, and the return of zero to the metaphysical domain where it was first named, now carrying what the West made of it.

Leibniz read the binary in a particular way and the notation reads it the other way round. In May 1696 he sent Duke Rudolph August a piece on the "wonderful origin of all numbers from 1 and 0", an image of creation from God and otherwise from nothing; in a New Year's letter of 1697 he proposed a medal inscribed *Imago Creationis* with the motto *omnibus ex nihilo ducendis sufficit unum*; in 1703 he added the correspondence, learned from Bouvet, between the numbers zero to sixty-three and the sixty-four hexagrams of the *Yijing*, $2^6$, the number §0/1 reached through six positions. For Leibniz one was God or unity and zero was nothing, so that the binary imaged creation *ex nihilo*. In `0/1` zero is the One, the pole of self-identity that "Subject" and "God" both sign, and one is the All, the manifest field through which that pole becomes articulate. Leibniz externalises zero, set against the one as its opposite; computation took the same digits as two determinate states of a switch, so both digits belong to the `1`-side of the symbolon and nothing in the machine plays the part Leibniz gave to God or the part the notation gives the uncounted pole. Binary logic is ambiguous at its root, zero read as nothing or as ground, and that ambiguity poses the next question: once zero and one are retained together, why does binary reason so often externalise zero and set two signed ones against each other?

The manuscript closes on *ratio*, from *reri*, to reckon or think, which carried a calculation and the account a person gives of it. A tenants' meeting votes ten to nine to repair the lift and the ten is exact; the caretaker who lives in the basement and was never on the register objects, and the objection asks whether the register was the right unit. Every reckoning stands in a frame it did not reckon, and once the terms of a count are stated they can receive an answer, the decisive gain often being a changed criterion that lets something previously excluded count, as a zero once let an empty column count.

## Tension / limit

Mathematical exactness and philosophical return stay joined by relation and need no single provenance story: the loan's return completes §0/1's loan in the sense of *credere*, entrustment renewed after an undertaking has returned its consequences, and the use of the sign remains a commitment answerable to what the naming discloses. Carriers still open: Graham, Knuth and Patashnik (no page) for the construction and Stern 1858 and Brocot 1861 as remembered, with Kaplan, to whom the earlier note attributed the pair at printed pp. 204–207, no longer relied on for the tree (that attribution was not verified); the May 1696 piece and the 1697 letter were confirmed only from secondary web sources and are to be read in Strickland and Lewis; the Leibniz *Explication* pages are not given. Reading the history as the notation's prehistory is the essay's synthesis, and Rotman's semiotically produced observing role remains a distinct interlocutor: the [zero-reception dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/zero-reception.md#4--rotmans-meta-subject-is-a-distinct-interlocutor) **compares** this return with it, which sharpens the difference between a formal observer-position and the irreducible Subject and leaves zero's documentary reception open to revising the historical account.

Returning the loan **returns-to** [Credere — renewed entrustment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#credere-renewed-entrustment) through an undertaking with a bearer and a consequence: examining the account clarifies what is owed, and renewed entrustment lets the relation proceed beyond what the examination can guarantee.

## Transition

At the kitchen table the child writes 105 correctly the second time, with the circle holding the tens open so that the hundred keeps its place. The sign works without its history, which matters to those who ask what the circle is and why something that counts nothing should hold everything else in its place, and the answer it returns is that the circle marks the place of the one who counts, present in every count and never counted. Next the cut receives its logics: **polar dia-ballein** as `(−1)/(+1)`, where zero stays the axis of tension and connection, collapsing into `0` by cancellation or into `+2` or `−2` by appropriation, each in its own chirality, and **syn-ballein** relating `0/1` and `1/0` within one differentiated relation.
