# Dossiers

A dossier gathers sustained work on one pressure that no single source house or essay paragraph can teach. It may compare doctrines, scientific formalisms, political institutions, historical interpretations or witnesses across registers.

The opening states the live question and the essay claim the dossier serves. Each participant appears in its strongest sourced form, and the comparison then identifies agreement, difference, consequence and what remains unresolved. A dossier points to the canonical source houses for its evidence and to the exact essay blocks that use it.
