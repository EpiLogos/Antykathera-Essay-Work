# Investigation and Faith

## #0 — The complete form

### Complete aphorism

> **The approachable limit is the basis of investigation;  
> the absolute limit is the basis of faith;  
> the limit of investigation is the limit of determination;  
> the limit of faith is never to be determined.**

Frank G. Taylor's complete four-line formulation gives investigation and faith one [contemplation object](../../../quilt/final-argument-quilt-2026-08-23/OUGHT-BE-APHORISM-ARCHITECTURE.md). The four lines can be met together first and unfolded afterwards through the two acts they distinguish, determining and entrusting.

The first pair says what each activity stands on. Investigation stands on a limit that can be approached, so that more work can always move it a little. Faith stands on a limit that cannot be approached in that way, because it is the condition of there being any account at all. The second pair says what each activity does at its edge. Investigation ends where determination ends, at the point where what it was determining has been settled. Faith continues beyond every settlement and declines to make the absolute limit one more settled thing. One life does both, since it asks and it relies, and the aphorism keeps the two acts together while giving each its own work.

The manuscript's account of the formal limit shows the distinction in a case. Russell turned to mathematics because he wanted a certainty of the kind people seek in religious faith, and in that move he asked investigation to supply what faith supplies. Frege's foundation then failed on a paradox, Russell's types repaired it, and Gödel's theorems showed that each enlargement of an arithmetic system, however exact, leaves an act of accounting that the enlarged system has not yet included. Gödel's sentence is the approachable limit, since a stronger theory can settle it. The recurring relation between any such theory and the act of giving it is the absolute limit. Russell asked the first kind of limit to do the work of the second.

## #1 — An approachable limit gives inquiry work

Investigation works on something that can become more determinate. An omitted observation can be made, a claim examined, an instrument's conditions exposed, a frame revised, and each result changes what can next be asked and done. "Approachable" names this capacity to enter a question and make a difference within it.

An [enlarged account](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) can bring in the body, language, memory, method and institution that an earlier account left unexamined. Each addition is a real gain, because a condition that was omitted has become available to judgment. The addition is also an act, since someone includes the condition, and that inclusion is something the enlarged account has not yet described. Inquiry therefore has two things before it at every stage, its next approachable task and the act by which it undertakes that task, and calling both simply unknown would hide what investigation has accomplished.

The [operation-specific question](../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C04-Formal-Limit.md) asks what a given container of conditions permits and what its operation reproduces. A different formal container can permit a different determination, and the revised operation has laws under which its result holds. An investigation has a subject, conditions and an achieved result that it answers for, and the exactness of that result is what makes the next inquiry possible.

## #2 — Determining the condition renews the relation

An account can go on to investigate how its own result became available. It can represent the observer's position, the rule of selection and the act that produced the first account, and this second account then comes about through a present act of representation. Having more content does not make that act identical with the content it presents.

The [gap relocates as inquiry succeeds](../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C05-Immutable-Gap.md): capture the act that made the first account, and a further act performs the capture. What recurs is a non-coincidence between an account and its being given, and no single withheld fact waits to be discovered. Three offices stay apart through every gain in knowledge. A represented empty object can be exactly determined, a formal operation keeps its specified rules, and the native sign marks the unobjectifiable condition of determining. The aphorism's return to determination condenses this performed relation with the three offices intact.

The absolute limit names the fact that determining is never exhausted by another determination. It gives no description of a hidden entity, and whatever is said of a particular source, person or event needs its own warrant. The limit keeps inquiry answerable for those determinations, and it stops their completion from turning into possession of the whole condition of determining.

## #3 — Faith proceeds through the absolute limit

[Continuing through a finite account](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) already depends on memory, language, witness, instruments, embodied competence and other people. Investigation can disclose and test these dependencies, and acting on what it establishes is an undertaking within them: examining a witness and deciding to rely on the testimony are related acts with different work to do.

Trust is dependence already enacted. Knowledge is a finite articulation that claims fidelity, and belief stabilises an orientation. Faith is the conscious continuation through the formal limitation. Its basis is at work whenever a well-established account is used, because even a rigorously examined account is used in a lived relation that no certification can manufacture. Faith therefore proceeds through the absolute limit, and it does not occupy a shrinking residue of questions that investigation has yet to settle.

The whole movement from reliance through position, articulated account, instituted order and inherited belonging into [renewed entrustment](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#relational-offices) gives this continuation a temporal body. A person relies on language, witness and practice before any account can be examined. Examination makes the reliance explicit and settles a particular question, and another undertaking then becomes possible. The settlement did real work, and its future fulfilment remains to be lived by those who answer for it.

## #4 — A finite commitment remains answerable

A particular undertaking can have a named bearer, a stated obligation and conditions under which it is corrected, withdrawn or renewed, and the last line's non-determination leaves these particulars open to inquiry. What the line denies is that any determinate undertaking can fix the absolute circumference of faith by making its own terms final.

A [symbolic account remains answerable](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active) to the relation its form discloses. Articulation makes the relation inspectable and leaves the source unsubstituted. Trust lets the account guide action while source, reply and consequence stay able to alter what was undertaken, and the effects of use re-enter the conditions from which another account proceeds. Symbol, account, trust and return are four operations, and one finite commitment carries all four.

A ground can withdraw, harm can occur and refusal can be justified. A commitment's finitude does not make it disposable at every difficulty, and invoking faith does not exempt its exercise from judgment. Investigation goes on distinguishing what occurred, who bears the obligation and what the returned finding warrants. A mistaken result can be corrected under a fitting rule, an inadequate rule, source, permission or commission calls for return to the office able to revise it, and an examined condition can also be kept with reasons. The later act inherits that judgment, a justified refusal included, and faith claims no possession of the source through the account.

## #5→0 — Return to the inquiry and its source

### Source return

[Different formal-limit responses](../../../section-rooms/00-integral-threshold/movements/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md) make the inquiry more exact through their different objects, methods and warrants. Restricting permissible formation, establishing limits of derivability under stated hypotheses, and performing the act that determines a condition have different consequences, and the philosophical relation between investigation and conscious continuation stays present as each is examined under its own laws.

The reader can now return to the [complete block](#complete-aphorism) with both movements in hand: investigate the determination and its conditions, receive the limit met in that very activity, and carry an answerable commitment into what follows. [Determining more](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) renews the difference between the enlarged result and the act that determined it, and [continuing through that difference](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) carries the examined account into renewed entrustment with its return to source still active. The [different psychic, institutional and technical settings](../maps/trust-faith-formal-limit.md) give the relation specific bearers, conditions and consequences, and each undertaking stays answerable at the scale where it acts.

The four lines remain one authorial form through these returns. Each line can stand alone, which lets the form be met whole, and the developed inquiry waits for the moment the compact relation asks to be unfolded.

### Source standing

The complete wording and order are Taylor's ratified form. The surrounding explanation unfolds the existing relation of investigation, determination, absolute limit and faith, and it supplies no additional authorial lines. The [protected lexical history](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/HISTORY-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md) concerns distinct word-fields, and the relation among prior reliance, examined account and renewed entrustment is Taylor's operational construction, with no shared descent claimed for investigation, limit and faith. The selected formal witnesses keep their particular hypotheses and methods beside the independently argued relation of the determining act.
