# Psychology — Formation, Symbolic Transformation and the Return of Responsibility

## #0 — A person acts within formations they did not consciously choose

Psychological [formation and transformation](HISTORY-psychology.md) begin with a particular question: how do formations beyond conscious intention organise attention, affect and action, and how can their organisation become answerable? Collective psychology, analytical psychology, psychoanalysis, cultural histories of consciousness and contemporary agent design intersect with different objects and methods. Their sequence follows the changes in this question, and no school is made the culmination of another.

The distinction between formation and aggregate begins the route. A crowd can act differently from its members considered separately. A complex can orient a person's responses before deliberation. A signifying relation can exceed a speaker's intended message. An institution can preserve authority across changing incumbents. Each case requires its own account of what persists, what changes and who can answer. To call them all a psyche would erase the institutional and material differences that the history exists to disclose.

[Answerable arbitration](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md) gives the formation a consequential return. Continuity-in-Indeterminacy lets it have a history without exhausting its conditions; Criterion-through-Distinction and Delineation-through-Difference make its selecting measure and frame determinate; Arbitration-in-Crisis gives the selection consequence; Con-text-through-Diaphaneity lets excluded conditions bear on it; Resolution-in-Reconciliation returns its achievement with its provenance active. Anamnesis / Recognition / Return remembers how the formation arose so its authority can become answerable. Psychological and institutional witnesses meet these operations through their own histories rather than deriving them from Greek or Latin word histories.

## #1 — Collective formation acquires a political apparatus

In the selected 1895 English edition of [Le Bon's *The Crowd*](../../../sources/psychology/le-bon/le-bon-1895-crowd-popular-mind/le-bon-1895-crowd-popular-mind.md), book I, chapter 1 **historicises** a psychological crowd as a formation rather than a count. A common influence can organise people without their physical assembly, and their combination can produce characteristics absent from each person in isolation. That formulation makes distributed formation intelligible before the present technical media through which it travels. The relation taken from Le Bon is this narrow one, and his racial hierarchy stays outside it, as does any generalisation of his diagnosis to every collective undertaking.

The selected 2013 Hull translation of [Jung's *The Undiscovered Self*](../../../sources/psychology/jung/jung-2013-undiscovered-self-routledge/jung-2013-undiscovered-self-routledge.md) **extends** the question from emergent formation to the person's diminished standing within it. Chapter 1, pp. 9–11, passes from the statistical abstraction to the negligible individual, then to transferred responsibility and the personification of the State. The stages have different consequences. Averaging makes a comparison possible; substituting the average for the person makes the concrete case disappear. Delegated responsibility can then relieve participants of the judgment through which an institution remains their undertaking. The apparently animate State conceals people able to operate its apparatus. This is Jung's diagnosis, preserved through the selected edition rather than redated to 2013 as an originating event.

Desmet's author-published [2022 web essay](../../../sources/psychology/desmet/desmet-2022-psychology-totalitarianism-web-essay/desmet-2022-psychology-totalitarianism-web-essay.md) **compares** a more specific affective sequence: isolation and loss of meaning leave anxiety and frustration available for binding to a narrative. The narrative's force includes its capacity to make contrary material inaccessible. An account of the world thus participates in organising the conditions under which it will next be believed. The source states Desmet's diagnosis, including the mass–elite relation, and a general law or a diagnosis of a particular contemporary population would need further evidence. [A13](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) **grounds** a reading of the sequence in the signed arithmetic, which is Argued: the narrative fixes the population's unbound affect on an excluded figure, and the whole span of the grievance, reckoned from the public's pole as a `+2`, is that figure's `−2`.

[Iakovou's review](../../../sources/political-theory-institutions/iakovou/iakovou-2022-misuse-totalitarianism/iakovou-2022-misuse-totalitarianism.md) **qualifies** that account by asking for political specificity. Her directly consulted [journal text](https://existenz.us/volumes/Vol.15-2Iakovou.html), paragraphs beginning “The concept designates” and “Several related critical aspects,” requires institutions, political forces and differences between forms of government. Her Arendt-mediated discussion names offices, secret police and camps; these cannot be replaced by a diffuse social mood. The displayed Fall 2020 issue date conflicts with the reviewed 2022 book, so bibliographic dating remains unresolved. The substantive criticism is recoverable without pretending that conflict has disappeared.

A [local arbitration regime](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md) can protect its governing criterion from what it excludes. Psychic fixation and institutional capture do this through different mechanisms. Psychological recognition leaves an office's powers, its coercion and its means of appeal where they were, and changing a rule leaves untouched the affective investment that sustains its use. Affect, judgment, rule and material power each stay consequential within their circuit.

## #2 — Symbolic totality changes the interpreter's place

In [*Aion*](../../../sources/psychology/jung/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2.md), the conscious ego encounters shadow, projection and symbolic totality. The consulted paperback’s §§1–5 and 13–19 distinguish the conscious reference point from autonomous affect and the self-reinforcing isolation of projection. §§43–49 then qualify both directions of inflation: ego can appropriate the Self, while the Self’s demands can overwhelm the ego’s relative autonomy and worldly obligations. A larger image is therefore not automatically a better integration.

The [complete Aion whole](../../../../mytheme/worlds/analytical-psychology/jung-aion-fishes-christ-antichrist-alchemy/WHOLE.md) **historicises** the changing material through which Jung asks that question: Christ and Antichrist, fishes and astrological time, alchemical work, Gnostic comparisons, and the return of a transformed psychological account. Christian doctrine, ancient reported rites, medieval materials and modern reconstruction keep their separate tellings. Jung's insertion of a Shadow Quaternio in §§402–413 matters precisely as a modern act of interpretation. The diagram's circulation returns to consciousness with a changed relation; it cannot be treated as an unaltered ancient schema discovered intact. The house now has sixteen consulted passage maps and the actual local copy. The selected 1978 hardcover and consulted 1979 paperback remain distinct; quotation and selected-printing collation are separate tasks.

[Smythe's 2013 dialogical reading](../../../sources/psychology/smythe/smythe-2013-dialogical-jung/smythe-2013-dialogical-jung.md) **qualifies** the whole-forming relation through voices beyond ego control. His conclusion places the ego among autonomous positions and refuses to make the inner other an ordinary object fully available to truth-apt representation. This gives the history a precise change of method: a description of inner contents is insufficient if the describing position retains unquestioned sovereignty over what those contents can say. Smythe's account remains a scholarly interpretation of Jung.

[Neumann's source and protected encounter](../../../sources/psychology/neumann/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness.md) **compares** the formation of a differentiated centre through separation, parental powers, transformation and centroversion. The common structuration lets a local centre receive more than it contains. It leaves each myth's geography, historical mediation and temporal order with that telling. Neumann's developmental morphology is a morphology, and no empirically demonstrated timetable through which all peoples passed stands behind it. In the author's encounter, the marked paper and its enabling field give this relation a further native reading; Neumann does not originate QL's notation.

[Differentiated recognition](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) changes the relation among ego, complexes, archetypal capacities and the whole-forming office while preserving this person’s body, history and responsibility. Becoming undivided keeps difference, and no chief complex is promoted to the position of the whole. An achieved local organisation can receive what exceeds it and respond without possessing the source.

## #3 — A dream, a mathematical expression and a speaking place

The 1932–1958 [Jung–Pauli correspondence](../../../sources/psychology/jung/jung-pauli-meier-2001-atom-archetype/jung-pauli-meier-2001-atom-archetype.md) **historicises** an exchange across disciplinary practices. Pauli's “Modern Examples of ‘Background Physics’,” Appendix 3, distinguishes physical concepts used qualitatively in dreams from quantitatively formulated physics. Fierz's Appendix 2 identifies the recovered text as an undated typescript or outline. Its unfinished character belongs to the historical object, rather than being a defect to conceal with a completed theory.

The [Pauli egg whole](../../../../mytheme/worlds/analytical-psychology/pauli-egg-dream/WHOLE.md) **figures** the passage with its seven pictures intact. A woman arrives with a bird; its egg divides spontaneously; Pauli notices another egg in his own hand and divides it; four eggs become a trigonometric arrangement and quotient; his final spoken recognition is followed by a circle. The pair of dreams occurred four days apart in mid-March, without a year supplied in this passage. Göttingen belongs to the companion dream, not the egg scene. The difference between spontaneous production and conscious participation gives the symbolic movement its historical particularity. The primary telling is now recovered in the Princeton-imprint reading carrier; equivalence to the house's selected Routledge 2001 edition remains uncollated.

[Atmanspacher's 2020 formulation](../../../sources/physics/atmanspacher/atmanspacher-2020-pauli-jung-conjecture/atmanspacher-2020-pauli-jung-conjecture.md) **compares** the conjecture through a psychophysically neutral basis from which mental and physical descriptions are distinguished. His speculative extension of quantum terminology is explicit. This is a later conceptual reconstruction, not the date or wording of the correspondence, and not an observed correlation. [Courtney's 2019 salt-point discussion](../../../sources/psychology/courtney/courtney-2019-salt-point-kairos-chaos/courtney-2019-salt-point-kairos-chaos.md) **qualifies** the neighbouring quaternity vocabulary: symbolic dynamism and the meaning of an opportune moment cannot be reduced to a physical mechanism by mentioning time crystals. Her nested Jung, Jacobi and Edinger references remain mediated.

A different scene of address occurs in Paris at Sainte-Anne, on 6 January 1972. [Lacan's *Talking to Brick Walls*](../../../sources/psychology/lacan/lacan-2017-talking-to-brick-walls/lacan-2017-talking-to-brick-walls.md) **historicises** the move from amphitheatre to chapel, the address to its walls, an audience interruption and the tuning of voice to reverberation. The currently source-matched Price passages at pp. 80–81 and 86 retain that material and responsive setting. The walls return speech in a way the speaker must hear; the audience remains implicated. [Adleman and Vanderwees's 2021 account](../../../sources/psychology/adleman/adleman-vanderwees-2021-lacan-rhetoric/adleman-vanderwees-2021-lacan-rhetoric.md) **qualifies** this through the analytic work of hearing one's own discourse and the simultaneous dividing and connecting effected by symbolic mediation.

[Darmon’s 1992 matheme account](../../../sources/psychology/darmon/darmon-1992-matheme-ali/darmon-1992-matheme-ali.md) develops structural writing and transmission, including the stated transformation of Saussure’s algorithm. The wall scene and this formal ambition have different offices. Through [operational comparison](../../../etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md), Pauli’s mathematical dream, Lacan’s formal ambition and Taylor’s native `X/x` can meet through an explicitly stated relation while retaining different notation, methods and source claims. A shared bar or numeral supplies no historical identity.

## #4 — Dynamical and cultural accounts make context consequential

[Van Eenwyk’s 1991 article](../../../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1991-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1991-strange-attractors.md) and [1997 book](../../../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors.md) bring analytical psychology into encounter with nonlinear dynamics. In Frank’s protected pp. 65–73 reading, archetypal dynamics, complexes as affective organisation, images as particular appearances and symbols as agents of transformation retain distinct offices. The resulting known→unknown→known movement can alter the conscious categories through which another encounter becomes available. Exact book and nested-author attribution is located separately in the source standing below.

In [Taylor’s Symbolon Dynamics](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics.md), a symbol changes the organisation through which an interpreter receives an image; the changed interpreter then conditions the next encounter. Enough integrity must persist for the encounter to have a bearer, and enough openness must remain for its effect to change that bearing. Neither disintegration nor rigid restoration supplies the only measure of change. The native `x→X→x` retains a particular on return; real local Bimba originality remains effective while itself standing within a further source/appearance relation.

[Daza and colleagues’ 2016 basin definition](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/daza/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy.md) specifies how initial conditions lead a given dynamical system toward an attractor; open Hamiltonian escape basins require a different treatment from dissipative attraction. A psychological model claiming that exact technical identity must specify its dynamics, state space and observables. This sharpens the scientific claim while the symbolic transformation continues to ask how an encounter changes the organisation through which it is received. Explanatory analogy and measured dynamical result retain different operations.

[Gebser's *The Ever-Present Origin*](../../../sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin.md) **historicises** another way context becomes consequential. Its mapped pp. 11–21 place Giotto, Petrarch, Alberti and Leonardo in an account of depth, landscape and perspective; pp. 24–28 and 470–86 turn to the disclosure of time and multiple aspects in later painting. Its method reads changes in consciousness through the means by which a world becomes visible. Aperspectivity makes perspective's conditions available while retaining its capacities. The observer's achieved placement remains, and the artistic histories stay several, with no single psychological experiment standing for them.

[Symbol, account and trust](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md) keep four exact relations active. Symbol answers to source; Account does not replace source; Trust keeps the return route active; Account re-enters source-field. An interpretation can affect the world in which it will next appear convincing. An encountered discrepancy must therefore reach the consequential condition it exposes: answer, task, world-model, interpretation, evaluator, permission or commission. A fitting criterion can be retained with reasons; its successful use cannot exempt its scope from question. [Arbitration’s lexical history](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md), the symbol/account relation and [comparison’s history](../../../etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) retain their separate evidence.

## #5→0 — Provisional agency returns the institutional question

The June 2026 [Agentworld brief](../../../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md) develops agency through constituted and provisional selves. At pp. 13–14, it distinguishes performed subjectivity and functional agency without settling model intentionality. Active context individuates an agent from prior capacities; persona is one layer among skills, memory, teleological constraints and relational infrastructure. §2.5 adds the collective’s individuation through the agent, making the relation constitutive rather than a surrounding backdrop. The proposed milieu retains the brief’s p. 53 scenario modality.

A coherent persona gives no assurance that its criterion is answerable, and an effective collective gives none that its members retain responsibility. At pp. 35–36, institutional intelligence belongs to rules, roles and procedures, and cultural differences remain consequential. A local interpreting or judging agent has a functional knowing office: memory, tools, rules and permissions mediate its comparison, while the alternatives or modelled world are known. The complete apparatus is itself a means within the person’s containing inquiry, and its judgment can become known in a further comparison. An encountered discrepancy becomes consequential when it can reach the source, task, interpretation, evaluator or commission that actually warrants correction or reasoned retention. A later use must preserve what consequence was received and which producing condition was retained with reasons or revised. This specifies the proposed technological operation; demonstrating it requires the actual intervention and its next-use consequence.

[Embodied cognition](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/wilson/wilson-et-al-2020-embodied-cognition-sep/wilson-et-al-2020-embodied-cognition-sep.md) compares organism, sensorimotor activity and environment as conditions of disclosure. [Phenomenal character](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/tye/tye-2024-qualia-sep/tye-2024-qualia-sep.md) concerns how experience is present to the one living it. The person’s lived synthesis, an agent’s local interpreting office and the inspectable apparatus through which comparison proceeds retain distinct scopes within one containing Life. Making an apparatus’s consequences available can change how that life receives and entrusts its further operation.

[Recognition](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) retains an achieved history; [image and valuation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md) become answerable through the conditions under which a representation shapes its next encounter. The [crowd→complex→psychoid→signification→cultural-context→agent-world passage](HISTORY-psychology.md) returns this question across different bearers of responsibility: which local authority selected the account, what excluded condition can reach it, and who can act upon the corrected relation?
