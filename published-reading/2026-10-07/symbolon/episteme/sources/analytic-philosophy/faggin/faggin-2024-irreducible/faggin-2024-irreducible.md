## Source scholarship

Faggin, the physicist and engineer who led the design and development of the Intel 4004 (IEEE Computer Society pioneer profile: he “took over the project and, in 11 months, single-handedly developed the four-chip set that included the first single-chip microprocessor”), describes in his later books and interviews an experience at Lake Tahoe during a Christmas holiday (dated by secondary accounts to December 1990) as the origin of his turn to consciousness. The experience is reported in *Silicon* (2021) and *Irreducible* (2024); the passage cards below are drawn from a 2022 published interview in which Faggin tells it, because neither book was available.

## Passage card — `faggin-2024-irreducible-q001` — the Lake Tahoe experience (interview account)

**Paraphrase:** Faggin woke in the night during the family’s Christmas holiday at Lake Tahoe and experienced himself as an unbelievably strong love, an energy both scintillating white and self-knowing, coming from him; the energy exploded and he saw that everything is made of that same love; he experienced himself as the world made of that energy observing itself, and was both the observer and the observed; the experience was very short and set him on a path of wanting to understand consciousness.

- **Locator:** Beshara Magazine, issue 20 (Winter 2021/22), interview “Consciousness as the Ground of Being” (Richard Gault and Jane Clark with Federico Faggin), p. 2 of the online PDF; the corresponding passages in *Irreducible* and *Silicon* are not located.
