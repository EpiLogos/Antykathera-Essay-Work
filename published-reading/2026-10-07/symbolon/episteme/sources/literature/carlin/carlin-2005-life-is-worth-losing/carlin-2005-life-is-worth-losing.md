# Carlin — Life Is Worth Losing (HBO, 2005)

## Bibliographic identity

*George Carlin: Life Is Worth Losing*, stand-up special, HBO, performed at the Beacon Theatre, New York, and recorded with its live HBO broadcast on November 5, 2005 (his thirteenth HBO special; director Rocco Urbisci; released as a live album January 10, 2006, and on DVD later). The broadcast date and venue are from secondary listings (Wikipedia and TheTVDB, agreeing; no HBO primary page consulted). An unofficial online transcript (Scraps from the Loft, which dates the special "2006") was read 2026-10-04 to locate the routine; the special itself was not watched, so no time code is given. The essay's note cites 2005; that is the broadcast year.

## Source scholarship

The line "it's a big club and you ain't in it" belongs to the late routine in which Carlin speaks of "the real owners" of the country, who control the corporations and lobby for what they want; it falls after the passage on the owners' lobbying and before the "American dream" line that closes the routine. In the unofficial transcript it falls a little past the middle of the special. The wording of the clause as the essay prints it ("it's a big club and you ain't in it") matches that transcript, which is a fan transcription and not a certified text.
