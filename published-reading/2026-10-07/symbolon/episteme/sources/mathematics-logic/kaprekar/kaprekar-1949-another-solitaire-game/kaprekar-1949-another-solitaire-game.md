# D. R. Kaprekar — "Another Solitaire Game" (1949)

## Bibliographic identity

The first printed account of the digit-sorting routine that converges on 6174 (Kaprekar's routine). The article itself has not been consulted. Its identity is cross-attested by independent citations: a 1961 notice in *Scripta Mathematica* of Kaprekar's booklet *6174* (Devlali, 1959) describing it as an "amplification of work published in SCRIPTA MATHEMATICA, 1949, p. 244-245, and 1955, p. 304" (Google Books snippet, ID I_sqAQAAMAAJ, not opened as a page); Yutaka Nishiyama, "Mysterious Number 6174," *Plus Magazine* no. 38 (March 2006), https://plus.maths.org/content/mysterious-number-6174, which cites "Another Solitaire Game," 15 (1949): 244–245; and citations in *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (1979) and *Fibonacci Quarterly* (1981) seen as Google Books snippets. Verified by a delegated search, agent, 2026-10-05.

**Known error to avoid:** MathWorld ("Kaprekar Routine") and some books merge this paper with the 1955 note (the 1955 title with the 1949 volume and pages). See `kaprekar-1955-interesting-property-6174`.

## Source scholarship

Kaprekar's routine: sort the digits of a four-digit number (not all equal) into descending and ascending order and subtract; iterate. All such numbers reach 6174 within seven steps; repdigits fall to 0000. MacTutor (see `oconnor-robertson-mactutor-kaprekar`) reports that Kaprekar announced the result at the Madras Mathematical Conference in 1949. Whether this 1949 paper already names 6174 is not established without the text.
