## Source scholarship

Siderits admires the formal work and rejects the textual reading: Indian Buddhists did not accept true contradictions; the Buddha's rejection of all four lemmas is better explained by presupposition failure (a false presupposition shared by all four), which needs no non-classical logic; and Candrakīrti and Bhāviveka avoid the paradox of ineffability or turn it against opponents. He locates Priest's own admissions and moves by page: that he is no scholar (xvii), the methodology (149), presupposition failure (29), ineffability (60–61), the embraced contradiction (83), Candrakīrti (85).
