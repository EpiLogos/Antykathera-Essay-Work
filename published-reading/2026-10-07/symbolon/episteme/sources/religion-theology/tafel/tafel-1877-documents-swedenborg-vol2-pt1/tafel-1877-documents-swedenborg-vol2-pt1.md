## Passage card — `tafel-1877-documents-swedenborg-vol2-pt1-q001` — prepared through the natural sciences, 1710–1744

> I was introduced by the Lord into the natural sciences, and thus prepared, and indeed from the year 1710 to 1744, when heaven was opened to me.

- **Locator:** Doc. 208, p. 139 (Swedenborg to Oetinger, 1766, as quoted and translated by Tafel).
- **Status:** locator-verified.
- **Verification:** Wording read in the Project Runeberg OCR of p. 139 (unproofread), 2026-10-04; not checked against the Latin letter or a page image. agent G6b, 2026-10-04.
- **Source relation:** Extracted.
- **Evidential action:** supports.
- **Consumers:** essay §0/1, M04, marker `s01-swedenborg`.
- **Use boundary:** Warrants the prose’s “in his own account his earlier study of nature had prepared him for that vocation.” The English is Tafel’s translation of Swedenborg’s own statement, made two decades after the event.

## Passage card — `tafel-1877-documents-swedenborg-vol2-pt1-q002` — natural knowledge as preparation to open the Word

- **Status:** locator-verified.

- **Source relation:** Extracted.
