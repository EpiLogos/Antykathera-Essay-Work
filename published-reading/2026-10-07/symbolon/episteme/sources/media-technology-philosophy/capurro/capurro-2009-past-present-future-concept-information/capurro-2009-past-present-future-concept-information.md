### capurro-2009-past-present-future-concept-information-p001 — Informatio: giving form, and informis

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://doi.org/10.31269/triplec.v7i2.113; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** p. 128.

**Located operation:** Capurro says informatio has two fundamental meanings: giving a form to something material and communicating knowledge to another person; and that the prefix *in-* can accentuate an action or negate, as in *informis*, without form. The page also cites Vergil (*Aeneid* 8.426) and Tertullian. This supports the M05 sentences on Latin *informare* and *informis*.
