Ernst Haeckel, *Generelle Morphologie der Organismen: Allgemeine Grundzüge der organischen Formen-Wissenschaft, mechanisch begründet durch die von Charles Darwin reformirte Descendenz-Theorie*, 2 vols. (Berlin: Georg Reimer, 1866) [full title to confirm from the title page], vol. 2, 286. Scans: Internet Archive vol. 1 https://archive.org/details/generellemorphol01haec and vol. 2 https://archive.org/details/generellemorphol02haec; Darwin Online also holds both volumes. Chapter 11 of vol. 2, "Oecologie und Chorologie," runs pp. 286–89 (Frank N. Egerton, "History of Ecological Sciences, Part 47: Ernst Haeckel's Ecology," *Bulletin of the Ecological Society of America*, July 2013, 222–44, https://esapubs.org/bulletin/current/history_list/history47.pdf).

## Provenance and standing

Pages located by the research pass for `s01-sourcelock`, 2026-10-04: the OCR of vol. 2 shows the heading "XI. Oecologie und Chorologie" at p. 286 and Egerton gives pp. 286–89. The 1866 spelling is *Oecologie* (oe digraph); *Oekologie* is a later respelling. The German definition ("Unter Oecologie verstehen wir die gesammte Wissenschaft von den Beziehungen des Organismus zur umgebenden Aussenwelt ...") was seen only on de.wikipedia, cited there to vol. 2, p. 286; Robert C. Stauffer's translation is printed in Egerton from "Haeckel, Darwin, and Ecology," *Quarterly Review of Biology* 32 (1957): 140–41.

### haeckel-1866-generelle-morphologie-p001 — Coinage of Oecologie (vol. 2, p. 286)

**Provenance:** That the word is built from Greek *oikos* and *logos* is not read in Haeckel's own words in the sources examined; a footnote at vol.

**Locator:** Vol. 2, chap. 11, "Oecologie und Chorologie," p. 286.

**Located operation:** Haeckel defines *Oecologie* as the whole science of the relations of the organism to the surrounding outer world. That the word is built from Greek *oikos* and *logos* is not read in Haeckel's own words in the sources examined; a footnote at vol. 1, p. 8 (and pp. 237–38) is reported in a search snippet and not confirmed.

**Status:** locator-verified for the coinage page; derivation unverified

**Source relation:** Paraphrased.
