## Source scholarship

In the seminar of 1972–73 Lacan says of mathematical formalization that it is the aim of psychoanalytic transmission because it alone is a matheme, that is, capable of being transmitted integrally. Secondary summaries paraphrase the sentence as "formalization is our goal, our ideal ... because formalization as such is a mathème: it can be fully transmitted" (a translation that differs from Fink's). The seminar is the book in which Lacan couples the matheme most closely with topology (the Borromean knot among later developments); the essay's wider account of the matheme and topology goes beyond the seminar text and rests on Lacan's other seminars and writings as well.
