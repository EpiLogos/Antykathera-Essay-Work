## Source scholarship

“The Instance of the Letter” takes up the Saussurean sign, writes the relation as S over s with the signifier above the bar, and argues that signification is produced along the chain of signifiers, with the signified sliding beneath it. “On a Question Prior” works through Schreber and brings the Name-of-the-Father and foreclosure (*Verwerfung*) into the account of psychosis.
