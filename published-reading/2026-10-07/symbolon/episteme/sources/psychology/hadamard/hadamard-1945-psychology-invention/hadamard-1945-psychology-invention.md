# Hadamard — An Essay on the Psychology of Invention in the Mathematical Field (1945)

[Source index](../../../README.md)

## Bibliographic identity

Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1945; the consulted copy is the Dover reprint of 1954, which states that it is “an unaltered and unabridged reprint of the first edition” (archive.org scan, item eassayonthepsych006281mbp). The contents page lists Appendix I at 137, **Appendix II at 142** and Appendix III at 144; Appendix II is “A Testimonial from Professor Einstein,” Einstein’s letter answering Hadamard’s questionnaire (the questions correspond to the questionnaire of *L’Enseignement mathématique* reproduced in Appendix I). Section VI of the essay is “Discovery as a synthesis. The help of signs.” The Princeton 1945 pagination is assumed to be the same as the Dover reprint’s and was not separately checked (agent G6b, 2026-10-04).

## Source scholarship

Hadamard’s survey of mathematicians’ introspective reports asks what the “mental pictures or words” are that carry thought. Einstein’s letter answers (A)–(E) briefly; it is the one reply Hadamard singles out as more important than any other because of its author and its thoroughness.
