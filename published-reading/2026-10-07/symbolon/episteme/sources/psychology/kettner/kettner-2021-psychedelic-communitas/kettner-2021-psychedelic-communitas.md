## Source scholarship

Observational, web-based survey (N = 886) measured at five time points: two weeks before, hours before and the day after a psychedelic ceremony, and the day after and four weeks after leaving the ceremony location. The authors validate an adapted Communitas Scale (perceived togetherness and shared humanity; Cronbach’s alpha 0.92) and test, by correlation and path analysis, whether communitas experienced during group ceremonies predicts changes in psychological wellbeing and social connectedness.

## Passage card — `kettner-2021-psychedelic-communitas-q001` — communitas and social connectedness

**Paraphrase:** Communitas experienced during the ceremony correlated significantly with increases in psychological wellbeing (r = 0.22) and social connectedness (r = 0.25), the latter measured four weeks after the retreat; the study is observational.

## Passage card — `kettner-2021-psychedelic-communitas-q002` — qualification: mediation and design

**Paraphrase:** The effect of ceremony-communitas on long-term outcomes was fully mediated by communitas experienced in reference to the retreat as a whole, and the extent of personal sharing (self-disclosure) contributed to this process. Participants were self-selected attendees of ceremonies and retreats, and there is no control condition.
