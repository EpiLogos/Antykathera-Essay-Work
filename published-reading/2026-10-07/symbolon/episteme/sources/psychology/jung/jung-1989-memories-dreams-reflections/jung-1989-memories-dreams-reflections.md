## Passage card — `jung-1989-memories-dreams-reflections-q001` — the Siegfried dream

**Paraphrase:** Jung dreams that he is in high mountains with a youth before daybreak; Siegfried’s horn sounds; they recognise Siegfried as their mortal enemy, ambush him on a path, and both fire as he arrives on a chariot of bones; he falls dead. Jung is shaken and feels he must understand why he killed the hero; his interpretation (not checked here) concerns the sacrifice of an heroic attitude.

- **Locator:** Chap. 6, “Confrontation with the Unconscious.” Page not verified: a secondary web citation gives p. 173 for the opening of the dream in the 1963 edition; p. 180 is commonly cited for the Vintage printing. Neither was checked.
