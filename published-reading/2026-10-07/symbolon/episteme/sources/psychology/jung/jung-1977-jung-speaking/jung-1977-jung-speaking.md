# Jung — C. G. Jung Speaking: Interviews and Encounters (McGuire and Hull, 1977)

## Bibliographic identity

*C. G. Jung Speaking: Interviews and Encounters*, edited by William McGuire and R. F. C. Hull, Princeton University Press (Bollingen Series XCVII), 1977. Its section "The 'Face to Face' Interview" (pp. 424-439) prints the transcript of John Freeman's interview with Jung for BBC television, filmed at Küsnacht in March 1959 and broadcast in Great Britain on 22 October 1959, with an editorial headnote (p. 424) that names Hugh Burnett as producer and notes that part of the transcript had appeared in a different form in *Face to Face*, ed. Burnett (London, 1964).

### jung-1977-jung-speaking-p001 — "Do you now believe in God?" (p. 428)

**Locator:** "The 'Face to Face' Interview" (pp. 424-439), p. 428, the first exchange on the page, immediately before the question about why Jung became a doctor. The preceding exchange ("And did you believe in God? Oh, yes.") is at the foot of p. 427 (the index entry for Jung's belief in God gives 427-28).

**Located operation:** Freeman asks whether Jung now believes in God; the transcript has a pause, "Difficult to answer," and then the sentence the essay quotes. The printed transcript wording agrees with the owner's quotation ("I don't need to believe. I know.").
