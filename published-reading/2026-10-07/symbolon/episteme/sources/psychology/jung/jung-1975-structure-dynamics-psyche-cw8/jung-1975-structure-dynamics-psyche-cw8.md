### jung-1975-structure-dynamics-psyche-cw8-p004 — The scarab case

**Locator:** "Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle," ¶843, printed pp. 437–438 (PDF pp. 449–450); interpretation continued in ¶845, pp. 438–439 (PDF pp. 450–451).

**Located operation:** Jung dates his puzzlement over synchronicity to the mid-1920s, when he met coincidences so meaningfully connected that their chance concurrence would be astronomically improbable; as example he tells of a young woman recounting, at a critical moment in treatment, a dream of being given a golden scarab, while a rose-chafer (*Cetonia aurata*), the nearest local analogue, tapped at the window and was caught by him as it flew in; he says nothing like it happened before or since (¶843). In ¶845 he reports that the event pierced the patient's rationalism and animus possession so that transformation could begin, and reads the scarab as a classic rebirth symbol (Khepri).

**Status:** locator-verified; paraphrase-only (carrier rights provenance unregistered).

### jung-1975-structure-dynamics-psyche-cw8-p005 — Synchronicity defined; distinguished from synchronism

**Locator:** ¶849, printed p. 441 (PDF p. 453); ¶850, pp. 441–442 (PDF pp. 453–454).

**Located operation:** Jung explains that he chose the term because simultaneity of two meaningfully but not causally connected events seemed an essential criterion, and uses it in the special sense of a coincidence in time of two or more causally unrelated events with the same or similar meaning, in contrast to "synchronism," mere simultaneity (¶849). Synchronicity therefore means the simultaneous occurrence of a psychic state with one or more external events that appear as meaningful parallels to the momentary subjective state, and in some cases the reverse; the scarab shows the simultaneity directly, the birds-at-death case less so (¶850).

**Status:** locator-verified; paraphrase-only. "Meaningful coincidence" as a phrase recurs throughout the essay (e.g., the flock-of-birds and ESP discussions), not only here; for the definition cite ¶849, for the psychic-state formulation ¶850.

**Verification:** as p004 (margin numerals "849", "85°").

**Source relation:** Paraphrased; ¶850's "in certain cases, vice versa" and its admission that the second example's simultaneity is not obvious are the qualifying pair.
