### nhs-england-fdp-faqs-q001 — Contract awarded to a Palantir-led consortium, November 2023

**Locator:** Question "What has been procured and what’s the value of the contract?", second paragraph; value in the same answer.

**Exact text:** "A consortium led by Palantir was awarded the contract in November 2023 based on their ability to meet the specific needs defined by NHS England for the NHS Federated Data Platform."

Same answer: "The contract is valued at £330 million for over a seven-year period and this will provide funding for up to 240 NHS organisations (trusts and integrated care systems)."

**Context and use boundary:** The answer says the procurement followed "an open tender process where any supplier could respond", that "The full contract is available on Contracts Finder", and that the separate NHS Privacy Enhancing Technology contract "was awarded to IQVIA in November 2023". The later answer on lock-in states that only a three-year term is committed, extendable by two years and then two one-year periods.

### nhs-england-fdp-faqs-q002 — Data controllers and the supplier's limits

**Locator:** Question "What is the NHS Federated Data Platform?", sub-heading "Who controls the data?"; question "What can the supplier do with data in the NHS Federated Data Platform?".

**Exact text:** "NHS England is the data controller for the National Instance."

"Each NHS trust and ICB is the data controller for their instance, this means that they remain responsible for their own data and decide how to use it locally."

"The supplier will not control the data in the platform and will not be the data controller."

**Context and use boundary:** These are contractual and governance claims by NHS England, not independent findings about how the platform is used.

### nhs-england-fdp-faqs-q003 — Built on Foundry; Palantir keeps its product rights

**Locator:** Question "What is the software that underpins the NHS Federated Data Platform and where can I find out more?"; question "How is our intellectual property protected – can Palantir benefit commercially?".

**Exact text:** "The NHS Federated Data Platform is built on Palantir’s Foundry software. Palantir retains intellectual property rights in their existing Foundry product, however the NHS has protected its interests through the contract in several important ways."

**Context and use boundary:** The IP answer states that "Palantir has no intellectual property rights (IPRs) in NHS data as part of this contract" and that NHS England owns the commissioned "data ontology … known as the Canonical Data Model". The "Can Palantir profit from NHS Data?" answer says Palantir cannot "commercialise or market NHS data, even on an anonymised basis" or use it to train an AI model. Cite both sides together.
