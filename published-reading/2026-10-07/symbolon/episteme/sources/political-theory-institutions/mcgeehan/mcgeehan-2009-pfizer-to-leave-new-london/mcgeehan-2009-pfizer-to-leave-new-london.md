## Source scholarship

News report on the company's announcement that it would pull about 1,400 jobs out of New London within two years and move most to a campus in Groton, leaving the city's largest office complex and "an adjacent swath of barren land" cleared of dozens of homes for a hotel, stores and condominiums that were never built. Quotes Susette Kelo and neighbour Michael Cristofaro, the lawyer Scott Bullock, the councilman Robert Pero and a Pfizer spokeswoman, who said the company had no stake in the Kelo outcome or in the development of the land taken. Records the 5-to-4 decision. The article gives some figures (jobs, assessed value, tax arrangements); the essay claims none.
