## Source scholarship

CBS News counted fully redacted pages in the first tranche released December 19, 2025: at least 550 pages entirely redacted, among them a run of three consecutive documents totalling 255 pages and a 119-page document labelled as New York grand jury material (a largely unredacted version of which DOJ said was released the next day), plus at least 180 blacked-out pages inside files that were otherwise mostly redacted. DOJ is reported as saying the only redactions applied were those required by law; lawmakers of both parties, Ro Khanna among them, said the release did not meet the Act.
