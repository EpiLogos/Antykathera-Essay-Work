## Source scholarship

Reports that U.S. prosecutors filed a criminal complaint, made public on June 21, 2013, in the Eastern District of Virginia against Edward Snowden, charging espionage-related offences and theft/conversion of government property, and asked Hong Kong to detain him on a provisional arrest warrant. Other press reports of the same day (Washington Post, Jurist) give the complaint's date as June 14, 2013 and its three counts as theft of government property, unauthorized communication of national defense information and willful communication of classified communications intelligence.
