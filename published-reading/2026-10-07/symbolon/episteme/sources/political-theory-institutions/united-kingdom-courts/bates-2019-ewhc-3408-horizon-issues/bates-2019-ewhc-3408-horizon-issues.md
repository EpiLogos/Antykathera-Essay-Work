## Source scholarship

Fraser J's findings on the Horizon accounting system used in Post Office branches: bugs, errors and defects capable of causing, and actually causing, apparent shortfalls in branch accounts, in both Legacy Horizon and Horizon Online; and his criticism of the Post Office's refusal to consider alternatives to its view of the system.
