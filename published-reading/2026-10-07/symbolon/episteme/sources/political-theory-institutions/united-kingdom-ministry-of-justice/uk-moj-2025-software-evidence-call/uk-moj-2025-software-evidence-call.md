## Source scholarship

States the common-law presumption that a computer was operating correctly unless there is evidence to the contrary, its origin in the repeal of PACE 1984 s. 69 by the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 s. 60 following Law Commission Report No. 245 (1997), and its replacement in 2000 by a rebuttable presumption.

## Passage card — `uk-moj-2025-software-evidence-call-q001` — the computer is always right

> Current principles around the use of evidence generated by computer software in criminal proceedings were established over two decades ago, with the common law presumption that a computer was operating correctly unless there is evidence to the contrary. In simple terms, 'the computer is always right', unless someone can show it is not.

## Passage card — `uk-moj-2025-software-evidence-call-q002` — origin of the presumption
