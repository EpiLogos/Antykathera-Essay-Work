## Source scholarship

ICHP reports on implementing OPTICA in North West London (Chelsea and Westminster NHS Foundation Trust with the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea; The Hillingdon Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust with Hillingdon Council). It reports a 23% fall in unnecessary bed days and 990 staff hours freed in Hillingdon. It is a promotional summary by the implementing partner, not an independent evaluation. The article says OPTICA is "endorsed through" the FDP. It does not say OPTICA is built on the FDP or name Palantir.
