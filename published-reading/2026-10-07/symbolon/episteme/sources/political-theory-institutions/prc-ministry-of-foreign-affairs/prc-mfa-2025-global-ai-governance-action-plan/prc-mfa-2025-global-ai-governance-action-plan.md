# Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the PRC — Global AI Governance Action Plan (2025)

## Bibliographic identity

The official English text of the Global AI Governance Action Plan as carried on the website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China, under News › Top Stories, stamped "Updated: July 26, 2025 23:55." The page's lead sentence attributes publication to the meeting, not the Ministry: "The 2025 World artificial intelligence (AI) Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance published Global AI Governance Action Plan on July 26." The full text follows: a preamble of two paragraphs and thirteen numbered items. ### prc-mfa-2025-global-ai-governance-action-plan-p001 — Principles: respecting national sovereignty, fostering open cooperation

**Locator:** Preamble, second paragraph.

> To this end, we hereby put forward the Global AI Governance Action Plan, calling on all parties to take concrete and effective actions in advancing global AI development and governance based on the objectives and principles of promoting AI for good and in service of humanity, respecting national sovereignty, aligning with development goals, ensuring safety and controllability, upholding fairness and inclusiveness, and fostering open cooperation.

**Context and use boundary:** The first preamble paragraph calls AI "an international public good that benefits humanity" and ties the plan to the UN Pact for the Future and the Global Digital Compact.

### prc-mfa-2025-global-ai-governance-action-plan-p002 — Open-source cooperation

**Locator:** Item 5, "Creating a diverse, open, and innovative ecosystem," second and third sentences.

> We need to build cross-border open-source communities and secure, reliable open-source platforms, facilitate the open sharing of basic resources, lower the thresholds of technological innovation and application, avoid redundant investment and resource waste, and enhance the inclusiveness and accessibility of AI technology services. We need to promote the development of an open-source compliance system, clarify and implement the technical safety guidelines for open-source communities, and promote the open sharing of development resources such as technical documentation and API documentation.

**Context and use boundary:** The item closes with "enable the open flow of non-sensitive technology resources" — openness is bounded to non-sensitive resources and paired with compliance and safety guidelines. Item 2 also asks to "reduce and remove technology barriers."

### prc-mfa-2025-global-ai-governance-action-plan-p003 — The UN as main channel; respect for national sovereignty

**Locator:** Item 11, "Jointly implementing the Global Digital Compact," second and third sentences.

> We need to take the U.N. as the main channel, work for the goal of helping developing countries bridge the digital divide and achieve equitable and inclusive development, and promote the establishment of an inclusive and fair multilateral global digital governance system based on complying with international law and respecting for national sovereignty and developmental differences. We need to support the establishment and early operation of two mechanisms under the U.N. framework—the Independent International Scientific Panel on AI and the Global Dialogue on AI Governance—to facilitate meaningful discussions on global AI governance, particularly in advancing the safe, fair and inclusive development of AI.

**Context and use boundary:** "respecting for national sovereignty" is the page's wording, transcribed unaltered. Item 4 adds support for "countries, especially the Global South, in developing AI technologies and services in line with their national conditions."

### prc-mfa-2025-global-ai-governance-action-plan-p004 — International cooperation on capacity building

**Locator:** Item 12, "Strengthening international cooperation on AI capacity building," first sentence.

> We need to place international cooperation on AI capacity building high on the agenda of global AI governance, and encourage leading countries in AI to take concrete actions, such as collaborating on AI infrastructure development, establishing joint laboratories together, building mutual recognition platforms for safety assessment, organizing education and training programs for AI capacity building, facilitating supply-demand matchmaking events for the AI industry, and jointly developing high-quality AI datasets and corpora, to support developing countries in enhancing their comprehensive capacity building in AI innovation, application, and governance.

**Context and use boundary:** Item 13 extends this to "an inclusive multi-stakeholder governance model"; item 8 supports the ITU, ISO and IEC on standards.

## Essay uses

The plan published by the 2025 World AI Conference meeting and carried by the PRC Ministry of Foreign Affairs, three days after the US plan: open-source communities and platforms (item 5), the UN as "the main channel" with respect for national sovereignty (item 11 and the preamble), and capacity-building cooperation with developing countries (items 4 and 12). The essay's comparison with the US export-of-the-stack plan (`white-house-2025-americas-ai-action-plan`) is its own.
