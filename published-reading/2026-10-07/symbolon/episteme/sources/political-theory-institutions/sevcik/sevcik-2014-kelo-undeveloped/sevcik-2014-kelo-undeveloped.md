## Source scholarship

Wire report that the Fort Trumbull land taken in the Kelo case lay empty nine years after the decision; Pfizer had left; the mayor is reported to have called the vacant land a "black stain" on the town's reputation. The article's statement that Pfizer "abandoned the project" is loose (Pfizer left New London; the redevelopment plan was the city's); the essay does not rely on it.
