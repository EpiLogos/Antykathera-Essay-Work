# Francophone anticolonial encounter

Fanon writes from a situated position in a racial-colonial order, and the inquiry keeps that speaking position. The Antilles and the metropolitan French encounter are the historical conditions of language, gaze and recognition in it. The whole rests on the 1952 French work and its electronic carrier, and no English translation has been selected.

Each of the [geographical route](../../atlas/geography/README.md) and the [temporal route](../../atlas/temporality/README.md) **returns-to** these same wholes.
