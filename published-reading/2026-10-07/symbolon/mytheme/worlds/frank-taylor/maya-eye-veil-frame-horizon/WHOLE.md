# Māyā — Eye, Veil, Frame, Horizon

This record serves the essay's account of how a finite view discloses a world, forgets that it is finite, and is called back by what it had excluded. It works in the mytheme register, and its image is Taylor's own composition: an eye with a constitutive blindside, Śakti's manifesting veil, a frame that claims the horizon, and a recognition that returns through the world the claim had enclosed. Six turns carry the image through a dream, a living mirror, a nested relay, the answering Other and the refracted return. The Śaiva, psychological, optical and technical sources that enter along the way keep their own histories, and no inherited myth is being retold, so the page never presents Śaiva, Jungian and Gebserian material as one historical narrative. What discloses the relation here is the action of the whole image, and that is a different office from formal proof and from historical attribution.

<a id="maya-eye-condition"></a>
## #0

We begin with the eye as an image of situated disclosure rather than an organ. When an eye opens on a world, something stands forward and something recedes: a face can be approached, a path followed, an obstacle avoided. Seeing needs this orientation, a foreground against a background and a position from which things are seen, together with limits beyond which the present seeing does not reach. All of it is at work in the act of seeing, and none of it appears in the picture the way the face and the path do, so the eye uses its condition and does not meet it among the things it sees.

Suppose we turn toward that condition and try to see it. A mirror shows the eye, a diagram describes its structures, another observer supplies an angle that is unavailable from here, and each of these disclosures is real. Each also happens through a further situation of seeing, with an orientation and a limit of its own. We have therefore not caught an object that was overlooked by accident and whose recovery would finish the field. If every condition of a perspective had to appear as an object inside that perspective, a further perspective would already be presupposed, the one from which the enlarged field was given. The power of disclosure keeps exceeding the image it forms of itself, and this is why the blindside in the authored image is more than the anatomical blind spot: it is what lets a view have a foreground at all, and it belongs to finite seeing as finite seeing.

A frame gives this condition an edge, and the edge is productive. Without an aperture there is no particular photograph, and without an orientation no particular prospect. A landscape taken under a given aperture, frame and sensitivity is an actual appearance of that landscape, and its limits are what make the arrival possible. Taylor's [Draft 3 aperture](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md) grounds the point as a principle: finitude gives disclosure its positive capacity, and the loss of relation overtakes that capacity only after the finite view has made something available.

A horizon is a different thing from a frame. A frame bounds a presentation, while the horizon is the field within which anything can come forward, recede or be encountered at all. Walking changes what the horizon affords, and another person's reply changes what the situation asks, and neither requires anyone to possess the whole in advance. Seeing already belongs to more than its current account of itself.

<a id="maya-measure-dream"></a>
## #1

The Śaiva account traces this opening to its source. Anuttara or Paramaśiva names the unobjectifiable ground, and Śakti is its power of manifestation and self-articulation, so the appearing world is active disclosure. Prakāśa, luminosity, and vimarśa, reflexive awareness, belong together: the light is living because the power that displays also knows, distinguishes, joins and withdraws its displays. Māyā brings measure into this field, so that this appears and that is withheld and a subject and its object become distinguishable. Where we place her depends on the relation being read. Beside prakāśa and vimarśa she stands toward the `1`, the measured world, and the torus reading below takes her as the measure-field wound around the opening.

Through the kañcukas, the five contractions, the bounded knower acquires effective terms of life. Kalā apportions what can be done, vidyā what can be known, rāga the desire of an incomplete situation, kāla its temporal limit and niyati its determinate ordering, so that a person can act here, know this much, want something, wait, remember and stand in these relations. Paśu names that bounded position, and puruṣa together with the inner instrument articulates an inhabitable field in which something is mine, something can be judged and an intention can form. Within the instrument, buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates under *I* and *mine*, and manas coordinates presentations and alternatives, and their joint activity gives experience continuity. An encounter leaves a trace, recollection brings the earlier affection into the present as past, and imagination selects and recomposes that material into a possible course not previously experienced in that arrangement. Hearing a sentence shows the same thing on a small scale, since the earlier sounds remain active in the arrival of the later ones and the last sound alone cannot speak the whole. Memory is therefore not already a command, and the instrument can carry inheritance into a new act. Universal subjectward and objectward orientations also come before the ego's local appropriation in the Śaiva account, so that the words "I am this" have a different scope when *this* is the whole manifest field than when it is a particular role or possession.

[Dyczkowski's page-labelled account](../../../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration.md) sources the demand from which the differentiation begins: blue and yellow are known as different in one cognition. A wholly homogeneous light could not distinguish them, and disconnected awarenesses could not hold their difference together. The field is formless in that no single form confines it and omniform in its power to bear these distinct appearances, and creative separation yields an effective world's differences without importing that world from a second reality outside consciousness. The veil therefore belongs to manifestation's own power and is no alien substance thrown over an otherwise finished spectator: it is the positive power by which a finite aperture can exist, and the same manifestation that makes a world visible keeps the whole from appearing as one more thing inside that world, so that the source is absent as exhaustive content while it remains present as condition.

Two movements cross in every act: manifestation and withdrawal unfold diversity and gather it into unity, and approach and return in perception bring a particular object into an encounter. Will, knowledge and action give the act its direction, its disclosure and its consequence, and a finite event carries more than its visible result because it is still an expression of the power through which it occurs.

The dream carries the same pressure into the most intimate case. A sleeper does not first build a theory of sleeping and then decide to believe the dream, and while dreaming the fact of sleep can drop out of the experienced situation. Paths lead somewhere, encounters matter, and fear and desire have an immediate world. Its objects need not be externally material to be objective within that experience, and waking changes their relation to their condition without making the urgency they had something that never occurred. In Taylor's ontological account dissociation, tattvic contraction and the slash describe one event, a whole becoming locally inhabitable through a difference whose condition can be forgotten, and the dream makes that event intimate: the experienced world takes its apparent self-standing reality from a condition absent within its own horizon, and recognition returns that condition to the world whose urgency it sustained.

A crystal makes a second limit visible. A polished surface can display a landscape without knowing that it does, and Dyczkowski's contrast at p. 70 would leave luminous appearing unknown to anyone if reflexive awareness were absent. Adding another reflection to the crystal cannot supply that missing office. Two differences are at work: a displayed image differs from the self-apprehension through which an appearance is known, and and an ordinary mirror differs from the Śaiva mirror of consciousness in its original, since the ordinary mirror receives something exterior while the Śaiva mirror generates and hosts its appearances. Multiplying reflections or finding an object outside a particular optical frame explains away neither operation, and the living mirror bears a differentiated world and knows it in one act.

<a id="maya-frame-usurpation"></a>
## #2

A second forgetting begins when a finite measure loses its relation to being finite. The frame then calls its own boundary the horizon of reality, and whatever it cannot admit is redescribed as unreal, irrelevant or already explained, so that the operation that once made a situation intelligible becomes the authority by which every later situation must be judged. Taylor's compression of the step is that a necessary methodological blindness becomes an unnecessary metaphysical blindness: the cut earns its powers of comparison, objectification and exact measure by being disciplined, and then the success of the cut is used as evidence that what the cut cannot contain is unreal.

This changes the eye's encounter with another person. Before the person can answer, the frame supplies a role, a score, a diagnosis or an expected motive, and the person's words become further evidence of what the frame already knew. The account's success at arranging a world conceals the conditions under which it did the arranging, and a determination claims its determining power, so that `x` acts as `X`, the reflected image as its source, and the local `1` as the `0` on which it depends. Constitutive limitation has become usurped jurisdiction, and the premise at the centre of the rational account is then irrational and unadmitted: the jurisdiction of my measure is the jurisdiction of reality itself.

Psychically a complex makes this arrest durable. An image attracts investment, investment colours what can be perceived, and the changed perception confirms the image. That organisation is an achievement with a history, and the organised basin nevertheless treats its own criterion as necessity. The field it has forgotten includes rejected alternatives, bodily and affective conditions, other agencies and other lives, and individuation has to return those relations to the local centre while preserving the capacity that centre achieved.

A nested simulation relay gives the image an outward consequence. A model describes a situation, action taken on its result changes the situation, and another model then receives the changed world, so that the relay continues through simulations of simulations and counter-simulations. A description has entered the field it describes, and the membrane between model and consequence has become permeable. In [Bratton's Agentworld](../../../../episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md), p. 25, §§5.1–5.3 distinguish descriptive correspondence, predictive anticipation and speculative exploration as different temporal tasks. The Matryoshka image figures nested relays, collaborative and adversarial, and no Russian folktale is being retold; [Simulation's consequential relay](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/simulation.md) defines what the relay needs in order to stay answerable: the model's reference, gauge, exclusions and permissions have to remain available where its output becomes a condition of further action.

Recurrence alone does not make a relay captive. A result can become a new condition while it carries its provenance and stays revisable, and capture begins when the result erases its status as a result and circulates as self-originating authority, after which more iterations only repeat the closure. In the technical relay a representation enters an institution's causal operation and helps form what can next be encountered. In the sleeper the condition of an experienced world drops out of that world's horizon. Both cases follow one capacity of a produced world, its ability to conceal the relation through which it is produced.

<a id="maya-regard-refraction"></a>
## #3

The Other interrupts. A reply refuses the role prepared for it, and an encounter changes which question deserves to be asked. Where the frame had disregarded the person by looking through an already completed measure, regard begins when this excess is allowed to alter the measure of the encounter. Attention becomes answerable: the person still judges and acts, and the criterion can now be questioned by what it concerns. Regard is therefore no permanent suspension of judgment. Judgment stays intelligent because its criterion stays answerable to what it claims to judge.

[Watson's Buddhist–psychotherapeutic conversation](../../../../episteme/sources/psychology/watson/watson-1998-resonance-of-emptiness/watson-1998-resonance-of-emptiness.md#passages) sources the point through Gans's reception of Levinas: difference is made available to egoic need, while the face-to-face exceeds the image held of the Other. A changed economy of desire is involved, and ethical excess cannot be represented adequately as a stronger quantity on an unchanged scale of power. Another person can reveal that the scale itself has mistaken what matters. Listening then changes more than the answer selected under the old scale, because it can change the question, the purpose and the terms on which an answer will count.

Context weaves the newly available relations. One view meets a counterview, and their conditions become legible through their difference. Taylor's 180°→360° movement names the change from a perspective that encloses its world to perspectives and their transitions held in context. Another angle discloses an occlusion and carries an occlusion of its own, so the three-hundred-and-sixty degrees do not make one observer omniscient. They name the weaving of positions, transitions, conditions and counter-orientations in which no single perspective is permitted to impersonate the field. [Gebser's contextual transparency](../../../../episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin.md) sources the release of a perspective's exclusive validity with its achievement retained, and Taylor's contextual amplification makes the passage among the views part of what becomes visible. The notation 180°→360° has its own native derivation.

A stained window lets us see this change. Light passes through panes whose material, angle, density and colour alter what reaches the room, and lead seams hold the construction together while marking its divisions. As sun, pane, room and viewer change, the coloured projection changes, and a later view reveals what an earlier arrangement withheld. The seams preserve the difference between sources, translations, histories and modes of evidence, and belong to the apparatus that makes comparison possible.

In the window each relation has its own office. Bimba is the Original order, QL is the full ordered spectrum immanent in the light, MEF is the refractive operation of declared media, angles, transformations and exclusions, and pratibimba is the situated projection. The cast picture is not the originating light, and QL is not reduced to the lead frame; recognition proceeds through exact differences among the projections toward the order they disclose, so the Original is prior in being and later in recognition. Within a declared Context Frame an achieved image can also become a local original for subsequent readings. It occupies Bimba locally while remaining pratibimba relative to its wider sources, and this gives the reference enough authority for comparison and error to be determinate. Returned experience can revise the local reference itself. This distinction prevents two losses: the installation of a produced artifact as metaphysically Original and the denial that any finite reference can govern responsibly.

Holographic refraction lets one local unit carry the whole descent, because that unit retains its relations to condition, articulation, contraction, manifestation and return, and re-entering the unit opens those relations again, which accumulating disconnected miniatures would not do. That whole-bearing organisation is native to the authored field, while optical holography and Bohm's implicate order remains an independent source and technical question, and the image supplies neither an optics experiment nor a licence to treat every fragment as exhaustive information about a physical whole.

<a id="maya-native-archetypal-field"></a>
## #4

This whole's [native articulation](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) is `/ = −/− → 0/1 → ?/! → −/+ → X/x → AM/IS → ∞/dx → 1/0`, and its [eight determinations](../../../../matheme/ql/eight-determinations.md) hold the parent and returning turns around the six-position body. The six narrative turns unfold this one relation and leave the eight determinations, with their distinct qualitative and processual placements, intact.

Read through the eye, the parent relation precedes a determinate view. `0/1` carries the conscious circumstance and the inseparable identity and difference through which it appears, and `?/!` brings the question and its assertion into that circumstance. `−/+` makes reserve and issue effective, since something is withheld as something is put forward, and `X/x` keeps determining capacity distinct from its local determination. `AM/IS` makes the situation personed, so that I, and what can be said of me, meet an addressing You. `∞/dx` gives a local act exactness within an inexhaustible horizon, and `1/0` returns the achieved determination toward the condition that never became its possession. The finite act remains, and only the direction of its belonging changes.

Across the [Neumann image-field](../../../archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation), archetypal structuration gives this complete relation a mythic body. Taylor's eightfold reading carries En-Soph and tohu-bohu at `−/−`, world-parent separation at `0/1`, coming to light at `?/!`, hunger and its systole and diastole at `−/+`, ego against unconscious ground at `X/x`, the "I am" recognition at `AM/IS`, the great round's Mono/Poly fullness at `∞/dx`, and descent and return at `1/0`. These eight relations traverse the whole image-field: Neumann supplies the transmitted images and their psychological development, the native notation supplies the complete relation through which they are re-read, and the particular history of each image stays part of what it brings.

Recognition has priority in Taylor's reading of the Bṛhadāraṇyaka passage that Neumann transmits at p. 105: it precedes the separation into husband and wife, so its "I am" cannot be reduced to an ego's later appropriation. A bounded position arises within the field of recognition and cannot retrospectively originate that field. The [transmitted Neumann passage and Taylor's encounter](../../../../episteme/sources/psychology/neumann/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness.md) keep their distinct scopes, and in [the returning bond](../../../archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-return-bond) recognition preserves the differentiation through which an encountered life became possible.

The torus assigns the offices exactly. Anuttara or Paramaśiva keeps the unobjectifiable opening, the subject or kartṛ pole. Māyā is the measure-field wound around that opening, the differentiated pattern through which manifestation becomes inhabitable, and Śakti's activity and joy belong to this manifestation. To call Māyā the hole would remove her positive work and put measure in the place it cannot enclose. The authored uroboric correction preserves depth, opening and winding where a circle-picture would suppress them, and the topological witness gives those three to the mythic relation, holding the source and its measure-field together through their difference.

Place changes its office through these crossings. Kashmir Śaiva discourse has a historically situated doctrinal geography, the eye's *here* and the frame's orientation are phenomenological places, and a simulated environment has configured operative boundaries. Taylor's authored movement brings them into relation and keeps what each location makes possible. The selected Singh translation of 1988, Dyczkowski's 2000 reprint, Neumann's 1954 edition, Watson's thesis and book sequence and Bratton's 2026 brief belong to transmission time, while kāla contracts temporal capacity and remembering, anticipating and returning have lived temporal offices. None of these dates supplies a mythic location, and no location collapses the distinct temporal tasks.

<a id="maya-recognition-return"></a>
## #5→0

Recognition returns through the veil. The world is still differentiated, the knower still situated and the instrument still able to select and act, and what had seemed self-standing is now recognised through the power that makes it possible. Līlā's play has real positions and real consequences, and a world in which every finite centre possessed the whole as content would leave no local disclosure through which encounter and recognition could occur. The return keeps those positions and releases their claim to originate the whole. Taylor's governing compression says it exactly: "Līlā veils; hybris absolutises the veil; recognition sees the veil as Śakti." The fall therefore lies in the frame forgetting that it is framed, and the existence of the frame is no part of it.

Through [Arbitration, Hybris, Regard and Anamnesis](../../../../episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md), which grounds the return in its words, an achieved measure becomes answerable to its origin and to what it has excluded. Continuity-in-Indeterminacy generates the relation of origin to the unmeasurable, Criterion-through-Distinction gives measure, and Delineation-through-Difference gives frame. Arbitration-in-Crisis bears decision and the danger of hybris, Con-text-through-Diaphaneity enables Regard, and Resolution-in-Reconciliation enables Anamnesis. These are six generated relations with their semantic flowerings, and they are neither twelve merged nouns nor one common etymological root.

The return has to become actual in the relation it has reopened. A person hears an answer that their old account could only explain away. They can file it as one more exception, or they can let it show that the criterion of their judgment was mistaken, and in the second case the next encounter begins differently: another possibility can be noticed, another reply can count and another act can follow. The memory has become material for a renewed intention and has stopped serving as evidence for the old necessity.

The same difference acquires a technical body in the model relay. A comparing agent is the local functional knower, its sources, memory, rules and tools are the means, and the disputed output with the alternatives it discriminates is what is known. Within a person's containing inquiry this complete apparatus is itself among the means, so inspection can make the local judging centre known in a further act without enclosing the first-person condition through which the inquiry appears. Logging an objection leaves its consequence undecided, and the return has to reach whatever failed. A better source can correct the answer while a fitting fidelity criterion stays in force, a false premise can change the task, and an encountered resistance can change the world-model. A faulty evaluator, a withheld permission or a misrepresented purpose requires the return to reach that governing condition and its actual authority. Adequate reasons can instead preserve the condition, with the objection and the reasons staying available for the next act. A later judgment has to inherit the correction, or the justified retention, for the return to become consequential. The source or controller of an inaccessible condition remains part of the account, and naming a layer grants no power over it.

This is the story's force for [arbitration and usurped measure](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md), where necessary measuring and the claim to absolute jurisdiction call for different responses: the first is preserved and the second has to become answerable. A [complex's local regime](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md) can then recover its history, [contextual seeing](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) can change judgment, and [individuation with recognition](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) can preserve the differentiated life while it changes its relation to the whole. Anamnesis keeps the recovered relation active in the judgment that follows, with the reasons for retaining or revising the conditions.

The eye's inexhaustible condition enters the [faithful definition of the Subject](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) and [defined indefinability](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md), which ground the claim that its present seeing does not become one more object seen. The [immutable gap](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) and [formal limit](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C04-Formal-Limit.md) qualify the condition as something other than information hidden behind the eye. [Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) and its [inseparable pair](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C13-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) ground the living mirror's appearing and self-apprehension as one act. Through [tattvic genealogy](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md), [operative measure](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) defines how a [bounded knower](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) receives an effective world, articulated through its [inner instrument](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md), and the [local tattvic movement](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md) extends that anatomy by carrying the complete descent and return through one whole-bearing unit.

[Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) derive how alternatives are distinguished and a selected course gathered, and recognition restores that formative capacity. [Constitutive exclusion](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) and [Apoha](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C18-Apoha.md) ground the claim that a finite distinction preserves what it excludes. [Image, valuation and possession](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md), the [living symbol and idol](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md), and the [protected account](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim.md) define the change from a source-bearing image to a defended criterion, and [context](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) with [diaphaneity](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C09-Diaphaneity.md) reopen the arrangement through its relations.

The [world-picture becoming world-atlas](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) and its [conceptual relation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C37-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) extend that reopening into travel between views. [Bimba and pratibimba](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) define how the achieved local reference is preserved without becoming the ultimate Original. [Self and Other](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) grounds the answering person, and [deferential intelligence](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) and the [mirror that moves first](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) extend the reply into revision of the deciding field.

[Homology and analogy](../../../../episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) compares the dream, the crystal, the window and the simulation as crossings that disclose different dependencies, so that their power lies in carrying those differences into the relation and none reduces to another. Through [compassion as sensitivity to origins](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md), an origin can change how the next act meets what it depends upon. In [integral zero's return](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md), the completed view keeps its exactness while it returns to the zero it could never occupy.

The eye opens again and still sees from here. A face can now answer the account prepared for it, a memory can become material for another intention, and a frame can disclose the condition that its earlier certainty hid. The finite view stays precious because it is where the whole becomes encounterable, and recognition has to be lived again in the next seeing.
