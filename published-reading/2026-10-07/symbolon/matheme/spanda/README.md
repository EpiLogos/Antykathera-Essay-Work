# Spanda

Spanda carries the equations through which relation becomes generative pulse. Its records develop the two poles and the relations of their relation, namely `2 + 2² = 6 = 4 + 2`, the movement `5→0`, and the larger equation sequences that connect differentiation, inversion and return.

Each equation gets a full derivation, a definition of every term, the status of each equality, and its exact relation to QL. Doctrinal Spanda material and textual history belong in Episteme, and the lived or poetic vibration belongs in Mytheme. This domain holds the formal operation that those two refractions both meet.
