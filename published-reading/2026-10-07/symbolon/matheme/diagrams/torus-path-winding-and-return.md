# A Return That Keeps Its Winding

The path `γ(t)=[2t,−t]` runs straight in the covering plane from `(0,0)` to `(2,−1)` and closes on the torus `T²=R²/Z²`, where it appears as two segments leaving one corner point and returning to it. The endpoints differ in the plane and coincide on the torus, and the winding pair `(2,−1)` stays with the loop. A linear flow `[αt,βt]` returns exactly when some `T>0` makes both `Tα` and `Tβ` integers: the ratio 3:2 returns at `T=1`, and the ratio `1:√2` never does. **Status: Derived.** Source of record: the essay's display in `§3 · #4`; [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/hatcher/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology|Hatcher, Algebraic Topology]], chapter 1.

## Proposition

The path `γ(t)=[2t,−t]`, `0≤t≤1`, begins and ends at `[0,0]` on `T²=R²/Z²`, and its lift runs from `(0,0)` to `(2,−1)`. The endpoints differ in the plane and coincide on the torus, and the pair `(2,−1)` records two turns one way and one back. A deformation that keeps the loop based cannot erase that pair, which is why `π₁(T²)=Z×Z`. On the same torus the linear flow `[αt,βt]` returns exactly when some `T>0` makes both `Tα` and `Tβ` integers; two nonzero rates with irrational ratio never do.

## Inputs

The covering plane with its integer lattice; the quotient by `(x,y) ~ (x+m, y+n)`; the path `γ`; the flows `[3t, 2t]` and `[t, √2 t]`.

## Transformations

Lift `γ` to the plane. Wrap the straight lift into the unit square by subtracting the integer parts. Identify all four corners as one point. Compare two linear flows under the integrality condition.

## Invariant

The integer pair `(2,−1)`, the difference of the lifted endpoints, under any deformation that keeps the loop based.

## Proof boundary

- **Derived.** The figure is a locator and construction diagram. It does not replace the covering-space or fundamental-group argument (Hatcher, chapter 1).
- **Illustration only:** the flow `[t, √2 t]` is drawn for a finite time and cannot by drawing show that no return occurs; the integrality condition proves it.
- **Not drawn:** a Möbius or Klein identification, and the `4+2` count of sides and generators, which belongs to [the square quotient](torus-square-quotient-and-winding.md).
