# Projective Completion and Changed Coordinates

## #0 — Specify which space is completed

Completion changes the representational object under an explicit rule. The [projective line](projective-line.md) adds one point to an affine line over a chosen field, and the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) gives the complex case its compact surface. The [NIST house](../../episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/nist/nist-dlmf-2026-complex-variable/nist-dlmf-2026-complex-variable.md) **sources** the complex completion, and the other projective constructions here are given directly.

The [core's](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) dimensional-reframing operation **compares** with these as exact neighbours, and its native claim and the construction that carries it are two things.

## #1 — Homogenise an affine plane

Embed a real affine point `(x,y)` as `[x:y:1]` in `ℝP²`, whose points are nonzero triples modulo common nonzero scale. The affine line `y=mx+c` becomes `v=mu+cw` in homogeneous coordinates `[u:v:w]`.

For two distinct parallel lines with the same slope `m` and different intercepts, subtracting their homogeneous equations gives `w=0`. Their common projective point is then `[1:m:0]`. Different slopes give different points on the line at infinity.

## #2 — Work a pair of parallels

Take `y=2x+1` and `y=2x−3`. Their affine equations have no common solution. Homogenised, they are `v=2u+w` and `v=2u−3w`. Together they imply `4w=0`, hence `w=0` and `v=2u`. Since the triple is nonzero, the intersection is `[1:2:0]`.

This is an exact intersection in the enlarged projective plane. The original affine lines still meet at no finite coordinate, and no real scalar is assigned to ordinary division by zero.

## #3 — Distinguish homogeneous coordinates from added dimension

The triple has three components, but quotienting by nonzero scale leaves projective dimension 2. Homogenisation uses an extra coordinate; it does not simply raise the intrinsic dimension of the affine plane by one. Similarly `ℂP¹` has complex dimension 1 and real dimension 2.

One-point compactification of the complex plane and projective completion of the real affine plane also have different boundaries: the former adds one point, the latter an entire projective line. Saying merely “infinity becomes a point” would conceal which construction is operating.

## #4 — Preserve the original limit

A changed chart can make a previously unavailable coordinate finite, and not every operation becomes globally defined by it. Reciprocal extends over a projective line, and a general binary quotient still has indeterminate cases. A chosen completion has its own topology, its own transformations and its own exclusions.

The native interpretation reads this as an account learning to state the limits of its representation and to move through them without discarding the achieved determination. The native relation keeps the determination while changing the representation through which its limit can be crossed, and its local warrant is this explicit representational change. The proof of a projective intersection is a proof of that intersection, and an ontology of unobjectifiable ground needs the argument the essay gives for it elsewhere.

## #5→0 — Return with both descriptions available

The result holds the affine failure and projective success together: the lines do not meet in the original plane, and their completed representatives meet at the named ideal point. Neither statement cancels the other because their spaces differ.

[Dimensional reframing](../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/28-s3-p3-projective-dimensional-reframing.md) **returns-to** the parallel lines under both descriptions, so the enlarged relation leaves their affine nonintersection standing. [The eight determinations](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **ground** that changing representation in the native field, and [translations](../mono-poly/translations.md) carry an achieved determination with the laws of its source space and its receiving space in view. A later comparison can correct the chosen completion or translation, and the construction already earned stays.
