# Operational Parity: Six Discriminating Tests

## #0 — Make a promoted distinction do work

The [six research vectors](../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/42-s5-p5-research-vectors.md) **extend** the arche-topos into a concrete experimental programme, and this record specifies how each vector is tested. A technical distinction earns promotion when removing or varying it changes a declared operation under an adequate comparison. The [native relation](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) joins a determination to its means and to the conscious condition through which it appears, and each experiment varies one specified technical means and measures what follows. Its outcome then bears on the implementation claim actually being made.

The input is a proposed mechanism, a task family, a baseline and an intervention. The output includes failures and null results alongside any success, so that no example is chosen after the fact.

## #1 — Specify six interventions

| Vector | Intervention | Baseline | Observable difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 Seam retention | Preserve source, exclusions and transformations | Output alone, matched access/compute | Better correction, delegation and explanation grounded in recoverable inputs |
| E2 Gauge disclosure | Expose and vary comparison sets/reference policies | Same task with these conditions fixed or concealed | Judgement changes attributable to a stated condition |
| E3 Lens refraction | Change a declared lens over stable source objects | Same source and prior lens | Different salient/actionable relations remain mutually traceable |
| E4 Topological continuity | Perturb context and inspect trajectories | Matched non-topological representation | Robustly detected continuity or transition beyond a chosen plotting effect |
| E5 QL recursion | Let an achieved field serve as a scoped local reference | Flat context with equal information | Scope and return survive nested frames |
| E6 Deferential return | Introduce resistant evidence or affected dissent | Candidate revision inside a fixed evaluator | Justified revision of map, lens, gauge or frame when required |

The programme proposes these six interventions. Their execution and outcomes remain to be recorded.

## #2 — Work a gauge experiment

Use [paired comparison](preference-gauge.md) to separate two interventions. First add the same constant to every score: predicted probabilities must remain unchanged. This checks implementation of the declared invariance. Then change the reference policy while holding a candidate policy fixed: the log-ratio comparison can change, as the worked DPO example shows.

The two interventions have to be kept apart, because otherwise a faulty experiment goes unnoticed. A changed answer after a harmless score shift points to a numerical or pipeline fault, whereas a changed answer after replacing the reference may be the expected operational effect. Reading that effect as a question of governance then needs evidence about who selected the reference and with what consequences.

## #3 — Specify failure before inspecting outcomes

E1 fails if the retained seam cannot correct an introduced source error or merely lengthens the log. E3 fails if lens variation changes prose style without a traceable difference in judgement. E5 fails if a local reference is silently promoted to global authority. E6 fails if resistant evidence yields only a disclaimer while the invalid governing condition remains intact.

The metrics can include successful source correction, the rate of unauthorised action, scope violations, recovery of excluded alternatives, calibration and the effect on affected participants. Each is tied to a real task and a measurement procedure, and the results are reported separately so that no single aggregate score hides a trade-off among them.

## #4 — Keep counter-hypotheses and scope visible

Model, token budget, tool access, memory, retrieval and evaluator exposure are matched or accounted for, since extra information or compute can explain an improvement without establishing the proposed mechanism. Repeated trials and held-out cases are chosen to test the actual pressure the mechanism claims to relieve, and they cannot simply mirror the implementation.

The six O:I centres keep their distinct authored roles, so a result in one centre does not demonstrate the others. A [J-space](j-space.md), an [energy model](ebm-resonance.md) or a richer log can each fail to improve conduct. A successful comparison identifies the operation that changed and rules out the competing explanations of that change. It licenses the correspondence that was tested, under the stated conditions, and the untested mappings wait for their own inquiry.

## #5→0 — Return the result into the proposal

The result is a receipt that can revise the design. It records the conditions, the intervention, the actual outcome, the alternative explanations, the failures and the remaining scope. A null effect can narrow or reject a technical correspondence, and the native argument that motivated the test stands as it did. A success licenses the operation tested, with its conditions attached.

The [research programme](../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/42-s5-p5-research-vectors.md) **returns-to** this page for its outcomes and their actual conditions. [Syn-ballein](../dia-syn/syn.md) binds the tested differences into the achieved account, and [translation](../mono-poly/translations.md) **compares** which source operations and which receiving operations that account has actually set side by side. The proposal stays answerable through the conduct it changes and through the consequences that can return to revise its interpretation, design or commission. A condition that proves adequate stays, with the reasons for keeping it, and a result that exposes a fault calls for repair at the condition it implicates.
