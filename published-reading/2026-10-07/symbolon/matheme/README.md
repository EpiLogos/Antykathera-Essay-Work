# Matheme

Matheme carries the exact and transmissible operations of *The Return of Zero*, and its own subject is the full equation together with the expressions it takes in number, geometry, music and logic.

$$\frac{0}{1} = 4+2 = 5 \rightarrow 0 = \frac{1}{0} = 4'+2' = 5' \rightarrow 0' = \frac{0}{1}$$

The shorter chain `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 0/1` is the definitional statement. It is read from the `0` side, and **definition** and **quilt** carry it. The full chain, which passes through the inverse phase `1/0 = 4′+2′ = 5′→0′` before it returns, has to be earned by following the process step by step, and **process** and **music** carry it. Each chain is licensed by the vantage from which it is read, and the register keeps them apart for that reason. Beneath both runs the standing identity `0/1 + 1/0 = 1/1 ≡ 100%`: the manifesting lean and the returning lean always sum to the whole. In the Vāk layering of the registers this is **paśyantī**, the visionary operative logic. It is more primordial than the mytheme, which builds formed operations through and upon it.

## Record form

A Matheme record introduces its symbols before using them and derives each operation before compressing it into an identity. It keeps three claims apart:

1. what QL derives in its own language;
2. what an external formalism independently establishes;
3. what relation between them the essay argues or offers.

Each record names its inputs, operations, result, proof boundary, source dependencies and exact essay targets. Its worked steps stay on the page even when the main essay carries only the compression the argument has earned. The folder structure above is the locked skeleton, and pages are projected into it from the housed files with each file's own headings kept as the load-bearing names.

## Two locks

1. **Two chains, two vantages.** Definition and quilt carry the short chain, and process and music carry the full chain. Flattening one into the other would lose the vantage that licenses each.
2. **The prime glyph carries two meanings in the sources.** In File 2 it marks the inverse-phase positions (#0′–#5′), and in File 3 it marks the Night-pass sequence (#0′–#5′). The projection keeps the two meanings distinguished wherever the glyph appears.
