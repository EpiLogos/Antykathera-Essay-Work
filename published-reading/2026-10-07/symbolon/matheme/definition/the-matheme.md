# §0/1 — The Matheme

## #0 — How is it that I am present to my experience?

Experience is present before its terms receive mathematical signs, and “I see x” supplies the initial expression. It has three inputs: a knower, a determinate content, and the activity through which the content is known. The operation is to make that mediation explicit without adding a second experience beside the first.

[Taylor's Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) **sources** the start from this act, and his [core theorem](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) keeps knower, means and known within one whole. [0/1](../../0-1.md) **defines** the relation among the three. The notation can be used because it keeps the activity through which its terms first became distinguishable.

## #1 — The sentence exposes its mediation

Rewrite the expression as “I am aware of the seeing of x.” The seeing has become available as something of which there is awareness. The content *x* remains what is seen. The rewrite separates the act of seeing from the fact in which seeing appears.

Repeating the operation with a thought, a memory, an intention or a self-image yields one more determinate content each time. Each can enter an account, and the awareness to which the account is given is presupposed by the account's occurring at all. [Faithful definition](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) **grounds** the practice of keeping an account answerable to what it names, and the [immutable gap](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) **derives** why the available self-description never coincides with the condition in which it is given.

## #2 — Three minimal marks

Assign the signs only after that distinction is available:

| Sign | Input it marks | Office |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unobjectifiable fact of awareness | Capacity for appearing; not another appearing object |
| 1 | Determinate content | What can be named, counted, doubted, or measured |
| / | Seeing, thinking, attending, measuring | The mediation through which the content is given |

The result is `0/1`. Its signs distinguish offices within the event that made their inscription possible, and they place no two completed substances side by side.

## #3 — The sign returns to its elements

To inspect what makes the signs distinguishable, remove their assigned names while retaining their placement. Two bare marks and their relation remain: `−/−`. The operation descends from a determinate reading of the relation to the minimal activity needed for any such reading.

[−/− = AND/OR / X/x](minus-over-minus.md) **extends** this descent by asking how the same kind of mark can appear twice, how a slash makes their positions distinguishable, and how identity stays readable across their difference. That is why the copula follows the opening as a consequence of it, where a formal axiom would simply be laid down.

## #4 — The definitional chain

The relation can now be read through its short definitional chain:

$$
0/1=4+2=5\rightarrow0=0/1.
$$

The successive readings preserve the operations already performed: the relation becomes countable; its operative pair and four appearances give `4+2`; the count is held and recognised as one relation; the achieved articulation returns to conscious circumstance. [The copula](copula.md), [recount](harmonics-and-recount.md), and [six determinations](six-determinations.md) extend these steps.

Through these transformations the signs keep their structural offices. Ordinary arithmetic evaluates `0/1` as zero and leaves division by zero undefined, so the equals signs of the chain relate structural readings of one relation, and its expressions are not asserted to share one scalar value. Process and music earn the full inverse-phase chain from their own vantages, and this page stays with the short chain.

## #5→0 — The opening remains active

With this notation the original experience can be differentiated, inverted, counted and returned, and no self-description is asked to become its own source. The result is a transmissible relation whose applications stay answerable to the act they formalise.

The [eight determinations](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **extend** the relation into its qualitative body, and their [ordered traversal](../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md) keeps ground, question, force, pattern, personed context and horizon each in its place. The [witness](immutable-subject.md) can recognise the act, and the description it has made never exhausts it.
