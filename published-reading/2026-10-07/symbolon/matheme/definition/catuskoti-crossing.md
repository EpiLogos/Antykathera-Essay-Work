# §5→0 — The Catuṣkoṭi Crossing

## #0 — The fact being logicised

The input is the conscious circumstance: something appears, its appearing is known, and that knowing is not one further object beside the appearance. The symbols are already earned: `0` marks unobjectifiable awareness, `1` the appearing determination, `/` their mediating relation.

File 1 `§5→0` in the [Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) **sources** the crossing of **0 IS AND/OR IS-NOT EQUAL TO 1**. It is the author's own development of the four corners of the catuṣkoṭi in QL's notation and positions, so the Buddhist source supplies the corners and QL supplies what is written across them. The [?/! root](../../question-assertion.md) **grounds** the relation of inquiry and assertion on which the whole crossing works.

## #1 — IS and IS-NOT

**IS:** the phenomenon belongs to awareness in the act in which it appears. Its givenness cannot be removed while preserving it as something presently known. Taken as exhaustive identity, this reading would dissolve the distinction through which something stands forth.

**IS-NOT:** awareness is not consumed by the particular phenomenon now appearing. The determination has a limit; its condition continues to permit further determination. Taken as exhaustive separation, this reading would sever the knowing from what is given through it.

Each corner performs necessary work. Their limits arise when either claims the whole relation for its own operation.

## #2 — BOTH

**BOTH** holds the two operations together. The phenomenon belongs to awareness and does not exhaust it, and the slash marks the difference of respect between the two claims: belonging is affirmed while the offices stay distinct, and non-exhaustion is affirmed while the knowing stays joined to what is given.

This keeps what each corner achieved. But to say “both” is itself to give an account, and whoever gives it stands inside the relation described and not outside it. BOTH therefore still claims a completeness, the claim to have the two offices fully in hand, and the next corner tests exactly that claim.

## #3 — NEITHER

**NEITHER** turns the identifying apparatus upon its own condition. Identity, difference and their held conjunction have each become available for thought, and their complete description still occurs within the circumstance it tries to determine. Since every member of that set of predicates is used inside the circumstance, none of them can take the office of the whole condition of predication.

File 1 places `1/0` at this constitutive limit. QL's recognition that the circumstance cannot be enclosed is written with the one division that ordinary arithmetic leaves undefined, and the two keep different formal offices: arithmetic's undefinedness is a rule of a calculus, while QL's recognition is a claim about the act of predicating. [A03](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) **qualifies** the philosophical movement with the formal-limit argument. Its boundary is that an act of position-taking is not a formal system satisfying the hypotheses of an incompleteness theorem.

## #4 — SILENCE

**SILENCE** names the recognition through which the four corners become one lived movement. It is no fifth truth value appended to their list, as Priest's fifth corner is. Affirmation, denial, their co-presence and their suspension all stay available, and the person who uses them recognises the circumstance in which all four occur.

File 1 places this recognition at #5, where [`∞/dx`](../../infinity-dx.md) **embodies** its exactness: the field can be articulated locally, to any precision, without being enclosed by its articulation. Read from here, [the six determinations](six-determinations.md) are the ways the conscious circumstance becomes legible to itself, and they are no descriptions imposed on it from outside.

## #5→0 — Encounter again

The return reaches the opening situation with the full crossing retained, so the person who began by noticing that something appears now notices the four ways of speaking about it as ways of speaking from within it. The short chain `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 0/1` carries this definitional vantage. What changes is the person's relation to the determinations through which they speak, know and answer.
