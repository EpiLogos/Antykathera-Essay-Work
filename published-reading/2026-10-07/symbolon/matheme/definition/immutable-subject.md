# §0/1–§5→0 — The Immutable Subject

## #0 — The act of denial appears

The input is a claim that the subject has ceased or is absent, and the operation asks what makes that claim available as a claim. If the cessation is known, the knowing presupposes a witness-position from which it is known. If it is not known, nobody has reported a cessation at all.

The [Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md), in File 1's opening and return, **sources** this argument. Immutability here belongs to the subject-position that perception, doubt, denial and recognition each presuppose. Personality, biography and mental state all change, and nothing in the argument asks them to stay. [The subject-logics root](../../subject-logics.md) **defines** the complete relation.

## #1 — Fact and contents

A thought about awareness, a body-image, a feeling of ownership and the sense of being a witness can each become a content. When we examine such a content we find something appearing, and the fact of its appearing is still at work in the examining. Refining the content can make the account more accurate, and the fact and the content remain two offices however accurate the account becomes.

[C01](../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) **defines** the subject distinction on which this rests, and [A01](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) **embodies** it by performing the return in its own defining act, which walks the sixfold and succeeds because it fails. A self-report is a determinate content, and its production and its reception belong to the means through which it can be known. The first-person condition of their appearing is already involved in the inquiry that examines them. Each added content makes the mediation more legible, and knower, means and known stay distinct offices within Life / Mind throughout.

## #2 — The apparent subject has an instrument

The native assignments are:

| Sign | Office in this traversal |
|---|---|
| 0 | Unobjectifiable subject-position |
| Ø | Apparent subject in which the inner instrument is fused with the subject-office |
| X | Object-field articulated through the instrument |
| 1 | Integrated unity in the core §IX recognition sequence |
| Ø/X | Instrument recognised as medium between apparent subject and object-field |
| (0/Ø)/(1/X) | Subject-side and object-side recognition held in one field |

The *antaḥkaraṇa* names the inner instrument, whose operations are determination (*buddhi*), appropriation (*ahaṃkāra*) and coordination (*manas*). The source's point is that whatever can be noticed as an operation belongs to the field being known, so an operation cannot take the subject-office merely by being internal.

## #3 — The charitable cogito

File 1's opening reads Descartes as correctly encountering indubitable presence in thinking, doubting, or being deceived. Its objection concerns the identification that follows: the I can be gathered too quickly into thought's operation. “I am aware of thinking” opens the relation again. Thought becomes explicit as means; awareness remains the fact in which thinking is given.

This is the author's Argued reading. [A34](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md) **grounds** it in the order of dependence, which holds however the knowing positions are misfilled. The [core-theorems house](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), at CT-11 to CT-16, separates the native crossed-zero derivation from its companion reading of the cogito and from the historical source tasks.

## #4 — The field becomes available

At `(0/Ø)/(1/X)`, the upper relation distinguishes unoccluded subject-position from apparent subject, while the lower relates integrated unity to the cut object-field. The outer slash gives their shared field of availability. This is a local symbol assignment: `X` here names the object-field, distinct from the capacity-office it bears in the native `X/x` determination.

The full core §IX sequence continues through integrated `1` and returns as `0/1`. The [A/C conjugate root](../../../section-rooms/arguments/conjugate/AC.md) **extends** what this recognition changes in the relation between fact and account. No further internal component and no more complete description can stand in for the recognition, because each would only add one more object to the inventory.

## #5→0 — A defined instrument, an unobjectified subject

The instrument keeps its real functions, and what changes is that they are distinguished from the condition in which they are known. File 1's short-chain return reaches the conscious circumstance with that distinction active. The flagged coinage *isoid* names signs that work as entity and as operation at once within the native development, and it is a term of that development with no separate external theorem about subjectivity behind it.

This projection **returns-to** [A03](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md), [A18](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) and [Movement16, Crossed Zero](../../../section-rooms/02-return-of-zero/movements/16-s1-p3-crossed-zero.md). The subject-position stays unobjectified through the very operations that make its instrument increasingly legible.
