# The Slash — Selection and Co-presence

## #0 — Specify what the stroke does

The inputs are two distinguishable terms and a field in which either can be selected. `/` carries two native operations: **OR** makes a determinate selection, and **AND** keeps the relation in which the alternatives belong together. The [slash root](../../the-slash.md) **grounds** their whole-bearing office, and the [core theorem spine](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), §IV, **sources** the formal account.

## #1 — Selection requires alternatives

A computation selecting `0` or `1` makes one value actual in its specified operation. The two values remain distinguishable possibilities under the rule that selects them. Naming the selected value does not by itself record the field, criterion, or exclusions through which it was selected.

This is OR's exact office in the native account: a difference becomes available for determinate action. The complete account of the act of distinction needs AND beside it.

## #2 — Co-presence retains the field

AND holds the alternatives in one relation. They coexist as possibilities, and both need not be the selected output of a single ordinary Boolean computation. The native operation keeps the field through the distinction that makes an output possible.

`AND/OR` therefore names a paired relational demand, and it silently replaces conjunction and disjunction with no new truth table. An external logical calculus states its own propositions, values, connectives and entailment relation before any comparison is made.

## #3 — Work the two orientations

Applied to `0/1`, the stroke keeps the ground-through-expression reading together with the inverse `1/0`. Applied again, it gives `(0/1)/(1/0)`. The outer slash acts upon two complete first-order relations.

[The primordial-symbolon derivation](primordial-symbolon.md) **extends** the four slash-forms and the retained pair. The arithmetic seam stays exact, since ordinary `1/0` has no value, and QL's relational operation uses that seam to make the return toward the unobjectifiable ground visible.

## #4 — Distinguish neighbouring strokes

A division bar, a quotient by an equivalence relation, a Sheffer stroke, and the native AND/OR share graphical possibilities without sharing one universal operation. Division requires a specified algebraic domain; quotient construction requires an equivalence relation or action; a Sheffer stroke has its specified truth-functional definition.

[The formal-neighbour register](../formal-neighbours/README.md) **extends** those comparisons. The present record keeps the native primitive answerable by stating its own inputs and result, which are a selected difference together with the field of co-presence.

## #5→0 — A determination can return

A result that carries its selection and its relation can be reconsidered when its conditions change. The [Dia/Syn folder](../dia-syn/README.md) **extends** the productive and the collapsed accountings of this operation.
