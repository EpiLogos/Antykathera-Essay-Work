# §0/1 · #1 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object

## Claim
The subject is the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event: the opening through which a determinate world is given. Treating that condition as a hidden substance inside the world would turn it into another object and repeat the error under examination.

## Warrant
The ordinary sentence **“I see the world”** already contains more than the apparent pair *I / world*. There is a knower, there is what is known, and there is the seeing through which the two become available in relation. Reflection can objectify any self-description—body, character, memory, brain-state, profile or model—but cannot thereby exhaust the condition under which that description appears.

Śaiva epistemology names the three moments *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa*, and *prameya*. *Prameya* is the definitive pole of what can be made known; *pramātṛ* is the indefinitive pole present in knowing but never exhausted as another known; *pramāṇa* carries their asymmetric relation. The familiar subject/object pair is therefore already a compressed triad: it conceals the seeing, thinking, measuring, or valuing through which knower and known become available together. The compressed triad is the first clue that what appears as duality may have to be thought as Trika.

The later essay will name these offices more directly as **Subjective Immediacy — Objective Internality — World**, held within **Life / Mind** as their whole relation. That language is planted here but not yet unpacked. At this threshold the important point is simpler: the means is constitutive without being the Subject, and the known world is real without being self-disclosing apart from the relation through which it is known.

## Tension / limit
“Non-objectifiable” establishes a discipline of inquiry: relations and effects remain describable even though the pole cannot be captured as an occult object or exempted from scrutiny. Nor does naming the mediating office settle what a particular means consists in; §0 earns that positive anatomy rather than presupposing it here.

[the Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-asymmetric-count) **figures** this limit in the opening exchange: a question about the source of the numbering receives the speaker's assigned number as its answer. The first-person refusal keeps the source-question distinct from the predicate through which the person has been identified. The narrative return does not substitute a concealed Number One for the non-objectifiable Subject.
