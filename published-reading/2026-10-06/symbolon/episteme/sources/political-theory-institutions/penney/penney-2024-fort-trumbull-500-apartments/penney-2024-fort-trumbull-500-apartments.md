## Source scholarship

Local report that a city-owned property sale expected in autumn 2024 would set the stage for 500 apartments on "long-vacant" land, and that several parcels on the peninsula had been vacant almost twenty years, the result of the demolition and development push of the former New London Development Corporation that led to *Kelo*.
