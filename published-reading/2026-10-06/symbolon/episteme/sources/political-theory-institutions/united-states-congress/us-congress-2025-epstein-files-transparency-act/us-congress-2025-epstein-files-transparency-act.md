# Epstein Files Transparency Act (Pub. L. 119-38)

## Bibliographic identity

Official slip law (H.R. 4405, 119th Congress), approved November 19, 2025; passed the House November 18 and the Senate November 19, 2025. Four-page GPO slip law (139 Stat. 656-58) downloaded and read 2026-10-04.

## Source scholarship

Primary legal text. Section 2(a) orders the Attorney General to publish all unclassified records relating to Epstein, Maxwell, flight logs and related matters within 30 days. Section 2(b)(1) forbids withholding, delay or redaction on the ground of embarrassment, reputational harm or political sensitivity. Section 2(c) lists the permitted withholdings and redactions (victim identifying information, child sexual abuse material, material that would jeopardise an active federal investigation or prosecution, depictions of death or injury, properly classified national-defense or foreign-policy information), requires a written justification for every redaction (2(c)(2)), and section 3 requires a report to the Judiciary Committees with a summary of redactions and their legal basis.
