## Source scholarship

The agreement's credit-for-time-served clause records that the defendant was incarcerated pending extradition at His Majesty's Prison Belmarsh, entered Belmarsh on or about April 11, 2019, and had served approximately 62 months at the time of the agreement; the parties recommend a sentence of time served; the agreement provides for release on bail and travel to Australia. DOJ reports that Assange pleaded guilty and was sentenced on June 26, 2024 in Saipan.
