## Source scholarship

The *point de capiton* is introduced in this seminar to account for the fact that, despite the continual slippage of the signified under the signifier, there are points where meaning is temporarily held. The same seminar develops foreclosure and the Name-of-the-Father that “On a Question Prior” (in *Écrits*) restates.
