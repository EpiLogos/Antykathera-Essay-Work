### mohrhoff-2008-evolution-of-consciousness-gebser-p001 — Gebser and Aurobindo compared

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://antimatters2.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/2-3-05-gebser-origin.pdf; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** Article, passim; comment "Relation of space to time."

**Located operation:** Mohrhoff mainly summarises Gebser. He states that Gebser's integral structure corresponds to Aurobindo's supermind, and in a comment on the relation of space to time argues that although Gebser equates integral consciousness with the supermind, "there appear to be certain differences," citing *The Life Divine*, pp. 1090–92, and Gebser's 1970 lecture in which Gebser says his conception resembles Aurobindo's. This supports "Mohrhoff later developed the comparison" only in a limited sense; no other Mohrhoff article on the comparison was found.
