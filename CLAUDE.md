## Scholarly source architecture — governing rules

1. **A source is a recoverable object.** One book/edition, article, transcript, webpage, report, dataset, PDF, archival object, or internal manuscript gets one stable `source_id` and one canonical `SOURCE.md`. Never create a source for `Abhinavagupta`, `apoha`, `re-entry`, `softmax as apoha`, or another person/concept/argument. Select the actual work(s).
   An optional sibling `NOTES.md` belongs entirely to Frank. Agents read it to recover source encounter, intent, insight, and quotation leads, but never mutate it. Nothing in it acquires citation or quotation status without independent verification into `SOURCE.md`.
2. **Use Chicago 18 Notes and Bibliography.** Every public source carries a full note, shortened note, and bibliography form when its identity is complete. Cite the exact edition consulted. Prefer DOI URLs for articles. Chicago 18 does not require place of publication for books.
3. **Citation readiness and quotation readiness are separate.** `citation-ready` means bibliographic identity is complete. It never licenses a quotation. A quote requires exact transcription, edition/version, page/section/folio/timecode, context check, transcription method, verifier/date, and a named consuming claim.
4. **No evidence tiers.** Do not use `A`, `B`, `C`, `A/B`, “primary tier,” or a single ordinal as a substitute for analysis. Track `source_role`, `metadata_status`, `edition_status`, `citation_status`, `quote_status`, and exact consumption independently.
5. **Keep developed essay synthesis in its owning argument.** A source file may contain sustained scholarship, the source's contribution, essay use, and exact relation to consumers. The essay's developed inference belongs in `essay-workshop/nodes/arguments/` or a concept node.
6. **Name the evidential relation.** Distinguish **Extracted**, **Paraphrased**, **Argued from**, and **Resonant with**. Never make a source author endorse the essay's inference. Gödel does not prove QL; Bohm does not formulate the tattvas; a tokamak does not prove the arche-topos.
7. **Internal corpus is first-class provenance, not public warrant.** The core theorems, Draft 3, derivational chat, musical derivation, MEF reference, revision notes, 4:2 report, and visual artifacts have records. They establish the origin and content of in-house claims. Historical, textual, mathematical, and technical facts leaving that corpus require public sources.
8. **The derivational chat is never a public citation.** Treat it as a source-discovery and reasoning-memory object. Resolve every attribution, quotation, historical fact, theorem, etymology, and named theory to a source record; resolve every original inference to its argument/theorem node.
9. **Acquire passages in pairs.** For a load-bearing source, collect at least one supporting passage and one limiting/qualifying passage. This prevents the bank becoming an anthology of convenient lines.
10. **Intake before prose.** New leads enter `source-bank/source-intake-queue.md`; exact works and editions become canonical `SOURCE.md` houses; stable passage cards retain text, locator, provenance and quotation state in that house; only verified passages are wired into section prose. Frank may instead capture a private lead in that source's `NOTES.md`; agents leave the note untouched.
