## Mytheme: the locked directory approach

```text
mytheme/
  archetypal-ground/           # Neumann genesis/world-parent articulation and authored quilt relations
  worlds/
    <region-or-transregional-cultural-world>/
      <local-world-when-needed>/
        README.md
        <whole-id>/
          WHOLE.md
          variants/
          AMPLIFICATIONS.md
  constellations/
    figures/
    motifs/
    peoples-and-polities/
  atlas/
    geography/                # places, regions, peoples and overlapping cultural worlds
    temporality/              # periods, succession, transmission and reception
```

This is the target skeleton, not a statement that these folders already exist. Instantiate branches only where actual recovered material needs them. Do not manufacture six continents, six cultures, six variants or six mandatory root folders. Story, scene, poem, music, image and artwork remain whole carriers; medium becomes an atlas facet rather than the primary division of the world.

**World homes.** A home's identity follows the situated source tradition, community, intellectual milieu or authored setting being recovered. Geographical regions, cities, cultural formations and diasporic/transregional worlds may overlap. Record source/production setting, narrated setting and later reception setting separately; a story set in a place is not thereby sourced from that place. A modern nation is one historically situated formation, not a timeless filing boundary. Do not give every creation story a Greek, European or Neumannian address merely because it was encountered through a comparative author.

**Whole and variants.** `WHOLE.md` preserves sequence, materially active figures and relations, turning points and aftermath. A story family may hold substantial temporal, local and syncretic versions under `variants/`; each version must remain an independently readable telling with its own carrier. The family introduction explains the relationship and never fabricates a composite original. When an encounter joins independently grounded traditions, preserve both homes and add an explicit relation rather than choosing one as owner of the other.

**Amplifications.** Start with exact story-internal occurrences; only then compare across recovered wholes. `AMPLIFICATIONS.md` may separate a substantial relation ledger from an overgrown whole, but it is not a duplicate source/quotation system. Every interpretive relation keeps the existing `human-amplified: yes/no` law, provenance, local operation and reverse link to its whole occurrence. Today's approval ratifies this structure and the already authored Neumann development; it does not turn every scaffold suggestion or agent-generated parallel into `yes`.

**Constellations.** Figures and motifs are derivative, generative reading routes. A parent, serpent, sun, moon, tree, child or sky acquires meaning through its actual office in each whole. `peoples-and-polities/` receives historically developing symbolic self-images, foundational stories, forms of sovereignty, adversaries, internal disagreements and transformations. It links the historical evidence in Episteme and preserves the distinct telling communities. National archetypes can thus develop through particular histories and images; a constellation does not assign a fixed psychological essence to every member of a people.

**Archetypal ground and situated worlds.** Reconcile the existing `mytheme-neumann-images` and `mytheme-uroboros-trickster` identities into the top-level archetypal field without duplicate bodies. Neumann's source account remains recoverable in its own house; the authored QL/Mytheme articulation developed through it belongs to the register's shared ground. Stories keep their complete local and temporal variants and exact relations to that ground. Archetypal universality, geographical situation and temporal development have distinct offices and remain navigable together.

**Authored/transregional works.** A work whose proper setting is a modern or transregional intellectual/artistic world receives that evidenced home, with multiple geographical atlas relations. Personal authored work retains its authorial setting. Do not force a nation-of-origin classification onto every poem, interpretive work or project-composed image. The exact world labels are routine evidence-led migration decisions within the ratified approach.

**Existing medium directories.** `myth/`, `narrative/`, `poetry/`, `media/`, `art/`, `music/`, `plates/` remain current physical locations until the migration receipt exists. Move canonical bodies once; update inbound links and identities. Afterwards retain only useful navigation/provenance surfaces, without duplicate whole bodies or aliases that capture the new home. Plates are presentations of a whole/constellation with owned assets; they do not become a substitute for its source-bearing body.

Preserve source IDs and the domain/author/source house arrangement. Sources and etymologies are essential, not optional supplements. SOURCES own external evidence, edition, quotation, provenance and source-consumption history; protected NOTES remain Frank's untouched encounter. Etymologies keep their whole-field generation and distinguish philology, semantic history, operational homology and poetic/phonic use.

Histories follow changes through persons, places, events, institutions, texts and practices. Existing ten historical streams must all receive a preserve/move/split-with-provenance disposition; do not overwrite the protected HISTORY bodies with a generic taxonomy. Similarity is not evidence of transmission; record comparison, attested transmission and disputed contact as different relations.

Dossiers gather one sustained pressure, keeping disagreements and source differences available. Arguments and Concepts remain the project's declared propositions and distinctions, not a ceiling on the knowledge admitted. New material can enter through a genuine research question even before its exact essay consumption is known. Dialogue records stay provenance of thinking, never evidence of the works discussed.

## Two connected atlases, one set of records

Mytheme's atlas follows image-worlds, tellings, figures and reception. Episteme's follows evidence, history, language, places, periods, traditions and routes of transmission. They link the same situated world/period/tradition identities wherever shared context is intended; they do not maintain competing duplicate place histories. Canonical historical accounts belong to Episteme, whole stories to Mytheme. An atlas entry can expose both in one encounter.

Atlas links are explicit and typed: source-setting, narrated-setting, attested-in, told-by, variant-of, received-in, adapted-from, compared-with, transmitted-through, contested-by and returns-to are useful examples, not a mandatory universal vocabulary. Give dates/ranges and uncertainty where the claim requires them. Preserve the source or authorial basis of every relation. A similarity-only edge never masquerades as an attested migration.

The wiki supplies navigation over canonical identities; a new database is not a prerequisite. Generated maps and indexes remain disposable views over written relations. Existing `episteme/maps/` retains declared argumentative dependencies; atlas supplies geographical/historical entrances. An atlas page is not evidence merely because its links resolve.
