Every lane may already:

- resolve exact current identities/census;

- identify repeated-family burdens;

- locate prior visual/form occurrences already available;

- every lane enters one or more live sessions;
- Anima makes and performs the work;

- accepted artifacts and visual occurrences accrue in Point-Cloud;
- practice observations return to the corpus inventory.

# 3. E1 — sovereign essay / section rooms

**Primary subject:** sovereign essay reading path + eight rooms × six movements.

### Recover

- authored reading root;
- exact room/movement order and M48→M01 return;
- section/subsection relations;
- per-movement canonical alignment into the A/C field;
- room-generated vs canonical-node authority distinction.

### Material burden

Create a coherent whole reading Expression / family of addressable room states which lets a person move through the argument without losing exact source/room/node Return.

Test where text should remain readable as text versus become field relation; do not convert the entire manuscript into particle typography merely because the engine can.

### Likely Anima emphasis

`composition` + `temporal` lead; `material` supports; `integration` preserves room/node relations.

### Aletheia questions

- does the route remain intelligible?
- does movement feel like argument rather than a slideshow?
- does visual motion clarify section transition or merely decorate it?
- can source/room/argument be opened and returned from without identity loss?

# 4. E2 — A family

**Subject:** all current A records from the live census.

### Immediate work

- recover all A identities and their declared relations;
- derive shared family burden and local exceptions;
- resolve argument proposition/operation/source/return anatomy;

### Live work

Use one A-family Expression/profile grammar with independently addressable A states/scenes. Test variation where an argument's formal burden requires it.

### Do not

### Practice target

A family lane is a strong place to learn reusable argument-expression Methods because it provides many related but non-identical examples.

# 9. E7 — Concepts / other Episteme carriers

**Subject:** Episteme canonical pages beyond the primary A/A′/C/A/C/S family division: figures, dialogues, dossiers, histories, lenses, etymologies, aphorisms and other live types admitted by census.

# 12. E10 — Mytheme wholes

**Subject:** every canonical Whole Mytheme and its derivative occurrence field.

### Mandatory whole-first order

```text
recover whole source/story
→ identify active transformations/relations
→ design whole scene sequence
→ craft actual Expression
→ ensure ending can qualify beginning
→ bind derivative figures/motifs to exact moments
→ then compare across stories
```

### Anima team

This lane is a strong candidate for multi-office/subagent execution:

- composition holds narrative whole;
- material develops content-local figures/forms;
- temporal carries transformation and pacing;
- integration preserves whole/derivative/source refs.

### Aletheia

- did the sequence preserve transformation?
- did iconography reduce ambiguity too early?
- are motif recurrences grounded in exact occurrences?
- is human amplification standing retained?
- does the form bank help comparison without replacing the story?

### Never

Flatten a Whole Mytheme to one iconic summary image for production convenience.

# 14. Cross-lane form/asset practice

There is **no E12 procurement worker**.

Instead, every lane may create form candidates under normal source/rights law. Point-Cloud Aletheia curation admits them and supplies the shared occurrence index.

When a later lane needs a similar form, Hen should disclose prior occurrences and evidence so the worker can reuse, modify or reject with context.

This is how the corpus develops its visual language associatively through actual work.
