## Essay burden offices

The recovered whole must preserve depth while distinguishing what the linear essay itself must carry:

- `linear-essential` — core inference depends on the operation;
- `supporting-visible` — materially clarifies or figures the argument and should remain visible;
- `radial/depth` — wider Epi-Logos, specialist, speculative or deeply comparative material remains fully reachable without burdening the linear essay;
- `whole-form/Mytheme` — myths, poems, artworks and narratives retain their wholeness while the essay links the exact operation it needs.

Radial/depth is an editorial/argumentative office, not a judgment of truth or importance.
