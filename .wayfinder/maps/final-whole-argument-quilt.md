## Governing heart: the eight determinations

Every worker carries the whole Symbolon field before interpreting a local relation:

```text
−/−   difference / non-coincidence / immutable gap
0/1   source-ground / determination — the Symbolon heart, evolved zero
?/!   question / determination, openness / answer
−/+   polarity / dia-ballein / differential opposition
X/x   exceeding field / determinate appearance, horizon / local disclosure
AM/IS lived becoming / predicative settlement, participation / copula
∞/dx whole field / local differential, continuum / finite cut
1/0   determination returning to source, recognition / non-self-authorisation
```

The determinations are mutually refracting operations, not eight detachable themes. `0/1` is the essay's central claim about the evolved form of zero. Every subsequently presented `0/1` is diagnostically open to fuller QL articulation: an apparent local source/product pair can be unfolded into the implicate `#0/#5` envelope and the explicate relational field through which it actually operates.

Dia-ballein and sym-ballein are therefore operative logics. Dia-ballein differentiates, divides, compares, measures and can remain true to the relation or fall into appropriation, usury and tyranny. Sym-ballein gathers differentiated terms into a whole while retaining the slash/source relation; counterfeit gathering can mimic wholeness while occupying the relation it depends upon. Matheme, Mytheme, Episteme and Symbolon are distinct offices through which these same dynamics become formally invariant, lived/imaginal, historically warranted and actively gathered.

## Phase-3 capstone carried as authorial ground

The final quilt must recover/test the following relations against the complete live corpus while allowing the developmental history to show how they arose.

Objective Internality names the operative interior/frame from which a subject, psyche, organism, agent, institution or other willing system perceives, knows, values and acts. Objective Co-Internality concerns relations between and around such frames of reference. Knowledge is therefore operative in power as well as phenomenological scope: a frame conditions what becomes foreground/background, knowable, credible, salient, actionable and capable of entering relation.

A transcendental or gnostic shift can accordingly be approached as a lived change in the operative frame itself. Śaiva practice, Greek ritual/ecstatic/katabatic philosophy, Heidegger's encounter with metaphysics' active root and related traditions are source-bearing experiential seams to recover according to their own claims and methods rather than reducing them to abstract doctrines.

Power, truth and valuation form a central dynamic. Power concerns which differences become effective; truth concerns fidelity of determination to what is disclosed; valuation is the active weighting/vectoring by which possibilities acquire salience, worth and telos. Jñāna-śakti, icchā-śakti and kriyā-śakti give the Śaiva ontology a native triadic articulation of knowability/differentiation, willing/vectoring and efficacy/action. Dyczkowski pp. 80–100 is an explicit deepening seam.

Finite internalities are dependent. Trust is the living relation through which another centre's determination, knowledge, valuation or action is allowed to condition one's own operative world. Deferential Intelligence recognises and attunes to dependency: for a human paśu this includes answerability to powers/realities beyond the ego's horizon; for artificial agency it includes answerability to human authorship, affected others, evidence, world and unresolved remainder. It does not require a deified object.

Authority becomes a local zero for downstream determinations. The political, psychological and technical question is what happens when a local arbiter/measure/image/ego/institution retains effective authority after its relation to source, wisdom, Other, consequence or right of return has degraded. Jung's *Answer to Job* is a major seam here: Yahweh, Sophia, omniscience, jealousy/aggression, Job's trust, Satan, belief and the breaking of an idolised image of divine goodness belong to the trust/power/identity question and to the broader enantiodromic/cultural-individuation horizon.

At the internal level the same question is the ego-self's attempted arbitration of identity. Jungian Self and Śaiva recognition meet the logic of identity-qua-non-identity: a determinate `1` remains related to the `/0` it cannot exhaust. `1/0` is Return, including the formal/political impossibility of a determination or gauge fully authorising its own ground.

Local-zero dynamics must be recovered alongside apoha, pratītyasamutpāda, conditioning and model formation. QL 4+2 can function diagnostically by unpacking a presented `0/1` into the fuller constituents and implicate envelope that make its local zero possible. LLM pre-RL openness and post-training/opinionation are a technical research seam where the corpus earns the comparison.

Myths remain whole relational fields. Apollo–Daphne, Apollo–Dionysus, Ares–Aphrodite–Harmonia and Hephaestus–Ares–Aphrodite–Poseidon must be reconstituted before individual argumentative operations are extracted. Apollo/Daphne is formative for image, desire, Other, objectification and the arrogation of value by an internal image. Ares/Aphrodite/Harmonia bears differentiation, relation and differentiated concurrence. Poseidon's surety belongs inside the complete Hephaestian love/war/capture/release field and must remain intelligible as a human relation rather than a floating jargon token.

Gebser's perspective→context and 180°→360° integral turn bears on how locally coherent frames become mutually legible inside a wider context. The scalar/spectral or octave language is available where it clarifies nested orders and already-developed Jorjani/Atlas/Prometheus/Heidegger relations.

Irrational effectuality can be investigated as effects issuing from nested internalities/frames beyond a local ken while becoming effective within it. This gives a clearer basis for the existing synchronicity work and for recovering layered-cosmos/firmament/archonic imagery as historical/mythemic witnesses where earned.

Energetics/plasma remains first-class supporting research. The existing elementary plasma-physics mappings to the determinations must be carried. Observer/plasma/calculus relations keep their stated research status; their speculative office is a reason to locate them precisely, not to erase them.

O:I script/context-source work must be reread for the stronger `script as crystallised will` relation and for the implication that generated determinations can become future source/ground. The recurring agent discipline is: **am I getting in the way?**

`The Prisoner` remains a supporting-visible pop-cultural witness for politics of the count alongside the deeper political/psychological source field; its usefulness does not grant it sovereign argumentative office.

`arbitration` is one strong organising word, not a lexical monopoly. The programme must recover the distinct operations and resonances of judgment, measure, valuation, determination, decision, mediation, authority, adjudication, discrimination, selection, orientation, surety, credit, trust, governance, apportionment, reckoning/accounting, recognition and Return, plus the corpus's own etymological families.

## Essential through-line and depth

The final quilt must make the essential argument parseable without cutting away the field that gives it force. It therefore distinguishes:

- **linear-bearing**: propositions the essay's argumentative passage requires;
- **supporting-visible**: examples, myths, histories, technical demonstrations and sources that materially disclose the argument;
- **radial-depth**: further Symbolon/source/research structures that remain addressable and can bear later pages without overloading the bounded submission.

A unique operation never disappears merely because its best office is supporting-visible or radial-depth.
