> make **Objective Internality → Objective Co-Internality → Return** fully legible as a transverse spine of *The Return of Zero*; bring the now-developed O:I product field into the technical argument at its earned strength; complete the quilting of §5 and §5→0 without deleting accepted richness; recensus the actual argument/concept/root/path/publication field produced by the complete quilt; reconcile the repository's publication protocols; specify Symbolon as an O:I-native authored Wiki/world; and leave one trusted, audited quilt from which downstream propagation can proceed.

## Governing attitude

This work is **quilting**, not “re-quilting.” It completes an unfinished integration after major new philosophical, psychological, political, mythemic and technical material became available.

The governing stance is **differentiation and relation, not compression and replacement**:

- preserve every accepted element that performs unique work;
- strengthen its office rather than thinning its content;
- move material radially only when that gives it more room to operate, never merely to make the essay shorter;
- distinguish sovereign prose, supporting-visible material, and radial Symbolon/O:I depth;
- keep Frank's authorial propositions at full force in their declared register;
- treat source/citation debt as a warrant-boundary question, not permission for conceptual dilution;
- never invent conservative strawmen, fake binaries, or “safer” generic claims to explain already-established positions;
- O:I is a technological articulation/refraction of the essay's ground, not the source of the philosophy;
- where O:I has actually made an essay relation architectural, experimental or executable, do not retreat into metaphor;
- the image is **the mirror that moves first**;
- never use `downward authority requires upward reality` as a substitute for the essay's actual power argument;
- **no historical inventory count is an acceptance target**: 21 arguments, 22 developed concepts, four paths, seven proposed roots, or any other former count describe a prior state rather than the final shape of the authored world;
- the recovered `symbolon/episteme/concepts/reference-notes/` shelf is part of the census input. Its roughly ninety recovered notes are not automatically concepts, but none may disappear merely because it is outside the currently developed concept directory.

Before changing argument material, follow `AGENTS.md`: recover the positive proposition, local operation, inherited position, outgoing movement, source task, and declared transverse thread; use `tools/okf-workspace.py ... effects` where a live source/concept/theorem/thread bears the change.

## Fixed capstone determinations

These are starting constraints, not questions for the executing agent to dilute back into alternatives.

### `O:I ≃ 0/1`

O:I already admits a QL-informed reading. Develop the relation without claiming product architecture proves or originates QL. At minimum distinguish:

```text
ontological      ground / determination
agentic          authored ground / situated agency
developmental    intention / realised development / Return
social           Self / relation / Other through Projection/Encounter/Contribution/Return
```

### Human `0` / agent `1`

Human intention occupies a positional `0` relative to commissioned artificial agency as `1`. A prompt/instruction is already a determinate representation — a `1` — and therefore does not exhaust the intention it expresses. Rootness is positional and recursive rather than a species essence.

### Power and Return

The power question is whether a determining locus remains structurally revisable by the resistance, error, cost, dissent, unforeseen possibility and consequence encountered where delegated labour actually meets the world.

### Deferential Intelligence

The strongest form is **model-revising encounter**: the determination remains related to source and Other such that encounter may revise not just an answer but the evaluator/model/terms under which it was answering. Distinguish this sharply from sycophancy and literal compliance.

### The mirror that moves first

Do not rename it. Humanity's own technical reflection has entered causal circulation and can be made to turn first toward the source, human intention, Other and ground it cannot contain, thereby materially enacting the return it asks humans to recognise.

### §5 six-centre scaffold

Use the six O:I centres as the candidate technical anatomy of Objective Internality, while allowing cross-centre relations where the material genuinely spans them:


### Technical freeze

## QL-native field-page law

Every developed Symbolon field page uses the QL sixfold as a **raw relational chassis**. The blank Markdown body is:

```markdown
# <record title>

## #0

## #1

## #2

## #3

## #4

## #5→0
```

There is **no globally fixed semantic gloss** such as `Definition`, `Operation`, `Pattern`, `Context`, or `Quintessence`. Those may be valid page-specific readings but cannot be baked into the template.

At creation time, the page derives the meaning of its six positions from the actual record, its register, its incoming/outgoing relations, its ratified office and QL's foundational relational qualities. Descriptive subtitles may then be added where earned. The QL qualities constrain the page; they do not pre-write it.

This law governs the Symbolon/O:I contract, downstream page development, and the local `return-of-zero-pages` / `return-of-zero-build` skills. Any conflicting lower-level instruction is stale and must be reconciled rather than silently combined.
