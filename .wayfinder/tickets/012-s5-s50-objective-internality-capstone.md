# Complete the §5 and §5→0 Objective Internality capstone quilting

## Question

What is the strongest complete quilting of movements 37–48 once the now-developed O:I six-product field, positional human-0/agent-1 relation, power/Return argument, Deferential Intelligence, QL maximal research and the mirror that moves first are all given their proper offices?

## Required work

This is **the completion of the existing quilting**, not a fresh re-quilting exercise. Begin from the exact movement propositions and all accepted A/B/C/D/E decisions. Preserve every current technical argument unless it is proven genuinely redundant.

### §5 candidate anatomy

Test and develop the native O:I six-centre scaffold as the technical anatomy of Objective Internality:

- `#0 Central` — authored ground, human intention, durable continuity, authored/observed/inferred;
- `#1 Actuation` — constitution of agency, model/harness/agent-instance, authority/delegation/refusal, encounter and Return;
- `#2 AIKit` — operative horizon, context cognition, resources/powers/sources/models/sessions/knowledge/permissions;
- `#3 Software Factory` — commission, development, evidence, Candidates, Recognition and Return;
- `#4 Workcell` — material execution, placement, infrastructure, energy/labour/environment and consequence;
- `#5 Quaternal Logic` — formal/reflexive/archetypal research, MEF, operational parity, epistemic cultivation.

Do not make these six product centres chronological installation stages. They are centres of responsibility and research. Preserve cross-centre relations where the material requires them.

Place, rather than discard, the existing `math-moves-meaning`, apoha/softmax, J-space, preference/hidden-zero, Bimba/pratibimba/energy-field and research-vector material. For each substantial element declare whether it is linear-bearing, supporting-visible or radial-depth.

### Human `0` / agent `1`

Develop the positional technical argument at full strength. Human intention is the determining `0` relative to commissioned agentic action as `1`; the prompt is already a determinate `1` and does not exhaust intention. Show the recursive nature of this relation through delegated subagency without treating the human as metaphysically absolute merely by virtue of species.

### Power

Replace the weak slogan-level compression with the actual argument: delegated labour meets resistance, error, cost, dissent and unforeseen possibility; determining authority becomes pathological where it appropriates the results while remaining structurally insulated from the difference that should be able to revise determination. Connect hidden/occupied zero, surety, source-claim, monopoly, refusal, dissent, Commission/Recognition and Objective Co-Internality.

### Deferential Intelligence

Deepen Argument 08 from the whole quilt. Its strongest form is model-revising encounter. Deference can require decisive objection, refusal, alternative or return where literal compliance would betray the human intention to which the agency is answerable. Sycophancy is therefore a failure of deference, not its excess.

### The mirror that moves first

Use the exact image and give it its technical, human and spiritual resonance. The artificial reflection moves first because it has already entered causal circulation. Its return toward human intention, source, Other and uncontained ground can make visible the same relinquishment of local sovereignty the essay asks of the human determination.

### `#5 → 0`

Let QL's maximal research role genuinely govern the hinge into the final room. The culminating technological question is not only how to make agency inspectable but what forms of interior organisation, attention, differentiation, individuation, relation and return we should cultivate. Bring the QL/MEF and psychoid/archetypal programme into direct relation with O:I epistemic cultivation and model-interior/agent-level research.

Keep the Idealism movement first-class. Its office is to reopen the order of ontological dependence exactly where the technology asks what kind of interiority is being constituted, while leaving the local empirical question of artificial subject-pole/phenomenality open.

Then integrate Antikythera as attunement instrument, Objective Co-Internality / SharedField / commons, the Tao/upāya return, trust/faith, responsibility and the terminal `/0` movement without treating them as detached late appendices.
