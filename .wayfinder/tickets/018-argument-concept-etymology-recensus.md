# Derive the ought-be relational architecture

## Why this gate exists

Recovery is valuable because it lets the project determine the **right whole**. This gate converts the fully recovered and post-section-reviewed field into the newly minted relational architecture before any canonical rebuilding.

Inherited filenames, counts, scopes and boundaries are provenance, not authority. The architecture is derived from what the final argument requires.

## Ground consumed

Consume together:

- native 017 developmental provenance;
- native 024 complete six-surface quilt field;
- native 025 inherited active/defunct/orphaned corpus recovery;
- native 020 whole-mytheme/deep-seam recovery;
- native 021 primary Antikythera/Agentworld recovery;
- native 027 final authorial intake of etymologies, aphorisms, direct formulations, title candidates and root-web relations;
- native 026 post-section quilting review of MEF integration, section↔Context-Frame conformance, Agentworld breadth, burden offices and root-web health;
- Phase-3 capstone developments;
- native 019 source/evidence support continuously, without giving incomplete citation work veto power over internally developed theory.

## Architecture targets

### 1. Argument architecture

Determine the actual load-bearing argumentative distinctions, scopes, dependencies, conjunctions, returns, section consumers and transverse relations.

- merge inherited nodes where an accidental file boundary separated one operation;
- split nodes where distinct operations are compressed together;
- create missing arguments demanded by the recovered whole;
- retire or demote records whose work belongs in concept, etymology, Matheme, Mytheme or another Episteme office;
- preserve provenance for every changed identity;
- use the final direct-language formulations as clarity tests without deleting the depth that generated them.

An argument exists because a distinct inferential operation must be carried, not because an earlier agent made a file.

### 2. Concept architecture

Determine which terms require stable instituted definitions and recurrent reuse.

- distinguish concepts from aliases, facets and merely frequent words;
- rehome Symbolon-root, Matheme, Mytheme and other Episteme work to its proper register;
- disposition developed, recovered, reference-note, legacy and defunct concept-bearing material;
- create new concepts where the final argument depends on a distinction not currently instituted.

A concept exists because the field needs a controlled distinction that other records can rely upon.

### 3. Etymology architecture

Treat etymology as a first-class reasoning system rather than a source appendix. Recover the existing word-fields and the final native-027 authorial return, then derive the architecture the new argument actually needs.

- merge, divide, expand, retire or add clusters according to their distinct operations;
- preserve the four truth-conditions: philological descent, attested semantic field, operational homology, poetic/phonic re-entry;
- route every live word-operation into exact arguments, concepts, sections and other records;
- keep source debt separate from the authorial operation a word-field already performs;
- preserve the recursive relation in which developed argument can also reveal which lexical relation genuinely matters.

An etymology/word-field exists because linguistic memory or re-entry materially sharpens and carries a relation in the argument.

### 4. Aphorism architecture

Aphorisms are Episteme condensations, not proof units and not a new Symbolon register.

- admit only authorially supplied/ratified formulations;
- retain `0 = One, 1 = All` and its supplied pithy unfoldings as current ground;
- map each aphorism to the theorem, concept, argument, etymology, section and source routes it compresses;
- distinguish an aphorism from a slogan, summary sentence or decorative poetic phrase.

An aphorism exists because a substantial traversal has reached a maximally compressed, unfoldable formulation useful as a contemplation object.

### 5. Section and root-web architecture

Determine the healthy relation among:

`source ↔ etymology ↔ concept ↔ argument ↔ section ↔ quilt`

and the reciprocal returns that make this a living graph rather than a one-way dependency tree.

Produce exact section-to-section relations and retain the Context-Frame conformance findings from native 026 without mechanically assigning one CF to each section.

### 6. Essay-burden architecture

Every major carrier receives one of four offices:

- `linear-essential`;
- `supporting-visible`;
- `radial/depth`;
- `whole-form/Mytheme`.

This preserves the wider Epi-Logos paradigm and speculative/deep research without forcing the bounded essay to prove or narrate every developed field. Whole myths/poems/images retain their full integrity through depth pages while the linear argument links only the exact operation it needs.
