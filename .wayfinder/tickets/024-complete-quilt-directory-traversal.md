- unique derivation/argument/material it carries;
- what it says had already propagated versus what remained prospective;
- proposed movement/argument/concept/path destinations;
- eight-determination and dia-/sym-ballein relations where material;
- Symbolon / Matheme / Mytheme / Episteme office;
- source/evidence debts;
- corrections, supersessions, contradictions and still-open decisions;
- relations to every other quilt surface.
