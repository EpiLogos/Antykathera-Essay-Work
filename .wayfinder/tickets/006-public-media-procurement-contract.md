# Make public image and video procurement first-class

## Question

How should search, collection, licensing, temporal clip selection, acquisition, rights review, provenance, rejection, and content-addressed storage enter the action model for both images and videos? Decide whether the fixed 66-action registry gains a parallel `video.collect` or a media-general acquisition action, or whether the existing `source.ingest` contract can carry the work without hiding video-specific operations.
