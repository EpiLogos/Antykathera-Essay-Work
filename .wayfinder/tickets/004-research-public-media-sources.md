# Establish the production public-media source surface

## Question

Which current public image and video search/acquisition sources can a production Epi-Card runtime access under explicit rights and provenance, and what official APIs, authentication, quotas, clip selectors, attribution requirements, download constraints, and content metadata must conforming adapters preserve?

## Resolution

Resolved in [Production public-media source surface](../research/public-media-source-surface.md). The production surface is Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons, and NASA for image/video acquisition, with Openverse limited to image discovery followed by upstream rights verification. Whole renditions are acquired to controlled storage, probed and hashed before runtime-owned temporal clipping. Rights and attribution remain evidence-bearing records through final outputs.
