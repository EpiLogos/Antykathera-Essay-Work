# Maintain the parallel source, quotation and evidence debt support queue

## Question

What supporting work can independent agents complete so source acquisition strengthens the quilt without becoming a gate on thought?

## Work

Maintain one explicit index of missing quotation checks, edition/page locators, source-house acquisitions, historical attributions, empirical support seams and public citation boundaries.

Every debt records the proposition/operation supported; current claim status; source relation required (`Extracted`, `Paraphrased`, `Argued-from`, `Resonant-with`); source/edition/locator; local source state; intended office; and an isolated completion task.

Initial seams include Dyczkowski pp. 80–100; Jung *Answer to Job*; Jung/Self/non-dual binary material; Nietzsche/valuation; apoha/pratītyasamutpāda; Śaiva experiential method; Greek ritual/ecstatic/katabatic philosophy; Heidegger's active-root formulation; Gnostic archons/layered cosmos; plasma/observer/calculus/energetics; Jorjani/Atlas/Prometheus; politics-of-number sources; Van Eenwyk; Faggin/Kastrup/Spira; O:I `script as crystallised will`; and the primary venue brief.

## Governing law

An open evidence debt does not demote or postpone an internally Argued/Derived relation. It names the support task and source boundary precisely enough that another agent can close it independently.

## Resolution

Keep `SOURCE-EVIDENCE-DEBT-INDEX.md` live throughout T06D.

## Done when

Every material debt discovered by the final quilt has a named, independently actionable task and no theory ticket is waiting merely for citation completion.
