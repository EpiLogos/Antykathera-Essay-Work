# Cold-audit the capstone, independently backcheck the census and hand the completed quilt to T07

## Question

Does a genuinely fresh reader, given the governing sources but not the drafting agents' self-justifications, find the Objective Internality / O:I capstone **and the proposed whole-authored-world census** complete enough that the quilt can finally be trusted and put to Frank's T07 ratification docket?

## Auditor posture

The auditor is not the capstone author and does not defend its choices. It receives:

- `AGENTS.md` and local review/orientation guidance;
- live central-plan authority and orienting principles;
- the complete argument quilt and complete parallel harmonised quilt as independent source surfaces, not merely their derived summaries;
- the pre-capstone harmonisation state;
- the mandatory source-and-authority map;
- the completed 48-movement traversal;
- Pass-A and Pass-B states of the canonical field census;
- protocol reconciliation;
- §5/§5→0 capstone quilt;
- Symbolon/O:I Wiki contract;
- capstone decisions;
- the dated quilt registration and current proposed movement + whole-field map.

Do not give the auditor hidden reasoning or an explanatory brief about why choices were made. The test is what the recorded work itself supports.

## Independent census backcheck — Pass C

The auditor must **not** begin by asking whether the proposed census is internally consistent. First reconstruct the likely field from the quilts and governing contracts with enough independence to notice what the synthesis normalized away.

Specifically:

1. Sample/trace every argument-bearing contribution from the complete quilt into the proposed argument census. Any contribution with no surviving argument/supporting/radial carrier is a finding.
2. Trace the parallel harmonised quilt's argument/concept/path/legacy ledgers and all later dated supersessions into the proposed census. Earlier rows may be superseded, but their unique operations need carriers.
3. Compare every former 21 argument node against the proposed census without treating any filename as entitled to survival.
4. Verify that **all developed concept files plus every recovered `concepts/reference-notes/` file** have an auditable disposition. The recovered shelf is candidate material, not an optional backlog and not automatically a concept quota.
5. Backcheck proposed root, Matheme, Mytheme, Episteme, path/map, history, etymology, dossier, figure, dialogue, plate/media, technical/research and source-facing homes against the one-home/register grammar.
6. Look specifically for accidental duplication introduced by splitting the O:I capstone into Objective Internality / Co-Internality / positional delegation / power / Deferential Intelligence / mirror / epistemic cultivation / Idealism / trust / world-atlas offices.
7. Look specifically for accidental compression of late Jung/Job/counterculture/protected-account/Man-Humanity/cultural-individuation material into one broad argument that loses distinct reasoning work.
8. Confirm every merge/redundancy disposition names the surviving carrier for **each unique operation**, not just the destination filename.
9. Confirm no target count has silently reappeared as a completion test.

Material discrepancies return to ticket 014 for repair; this ticket does not invent a third competing census.

## Protocol audit

Independently test the reconciled protocol against actual instructions/tools. At minimum verify:

- raw QL field-page body is `#0/#1/#2/#3/#4/#5→0`;
- no universal `Definition/Operation/Pattern/Context/Quintessence` semantics survive in active executable page instructions;
- page-specific semantics are derived at creation from record/register/relations/QL foundational qualities;
- build intake is subordinate to ratified census and cannot mistake heading extraction for completeness;
- record identity, register, source authority, O:I Projection and return proposals remain distinct;
- old inventory counts in descriptive documents cannot override the ratified census.

A duplicated or contradictory instruction capable of producing a materially different publication body is at least a MEDIUM finding until reconciled.

## Audit questions

1. **Whole-argument continuity** — does Objective Internality genuinely recur through the essay, or has the capstone retrospectively painted O:I over unrelated material?
2. **Authorial sovereignty** — has any established philosophical/QL claim been weakened, replaced by generic AI language, or made to appear derived from O:I?
3. **Technical consequence** — where the essay has genuinely become architectural/experimental/executable in O:I, is that consequence stated strongly enough rather than demoted to metaphor?
4. **`O:I ≃ 0/1` discipline** — is the relation rich and exact without treating product-number correspondences as proof?
5. **Human `0` / agent `1`** — is the positional and recursive character clear, including the prompt-as-`1` distinction and the difference between deference and literal compliance?
6. **Power** — is the determining/labouring/encounter/Return argument present at full weight, without reliance on weak slogan compression?
7. **Deferential Intelligence** — does the capstone reach model-revising encounter, source/Other relation and the sycophancy distinction rather than mere uncertainty disclosure?
8. **Mirror** — is the image consistently **the mirror that moves first**, with its technical and spiritual operation preserved?
9. **Late Jung integration** — are Job's multiple offices, monoisation/counterculture, cultural individuation, Man/Humanity, protected account, trust/efficacy and Epi-Logos-as-culture treated with their actual distinctions and source boundaries?
10. **§5 integrity** — do all existing technical arguments survive in an earned office under/through the six-centre scaffold?
11. **§5→0 integrity** — are QL maximal research, psychoid/archetypal work and Idealism directly earned by the technological question rather than appended apologetically?
12. **Symbolon/O:I boundary** — does Symbolon remain canonical native ontology while O:I/AIKit own projection/navigation/operation/return seams?
13. **Canonical-field integrity** — does the proposed argument/concept/root/path/publication census actually follow unique operations in the completed quilt rather than old filenames or neat counts?
14. **Recovered concept field** — can every recovered reference note be located in the final census or a named surviving carrier?
15. **QL page form** — is the sixfold structural without globally imposed semantics?
16. **Preservation** — can every affected accepted element be located as linear-bearing, supporting-visible, radial-depth, or explicitly redundant-with-reason and surviving carrier?
17. **Claim/provenance discipline** — are authored positions, source claims, product vision, architecture, current implementation, experiments and interpretation kept distinct?
18. **Technical freeze truth** — does the capstone avoid pretending current packaging/manifests constitute final suite acceptance?
19. **Premature propagation** — have no proposed capstone/census changes been silently installed into canonical section-room/argument/concept publication nodes before Frank's ratification?

## Resolution

The auditor records findings directly into the cold-audit section of `CAPSTONE-ACCEPTANCE.md`, ranked by severity and tied to exact artifacts/claims. Material findings return to the relevant ticket and must be repaired before this ticket closes.

When clean, update the T07 handoff section in `CAPSTONE-ACCEPTANCE.md` with:

- exact commit/revision set;
- final deliverable list;
- final proposed argument/concept/root/path/publication census and remaining genuine alternatives;
- evidence of recovered-reference-note coverage;
- protocol-reconciliation result;
- decisions requiring Frank's explicit ratification;
- downstream propagation scope changes;
- unresolved source/software debts that do **not** prevent quilt ratification but remain named obligations.

## Done when

A fresh cold audit finds no HIGH or MEDIUM omission/dilution/provenance/structure/census/protocol failures; LOW findings are either repaired or explicitly accepted for T07; the auditor has independently backchecked the argument and concept/publication census rather than merely reading its tables; the capstone Wayfinder can be marked complete; and GitHub T07 has a coherent entry point for ratifying the **completed trusted quilt and the relational authored world it now entails**, rather than the pre-capstone 2026-08-12 state.
