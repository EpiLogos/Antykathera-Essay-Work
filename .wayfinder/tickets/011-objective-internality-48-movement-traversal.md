# Build the 48-movement Objective Internality traversal

## Question

How does Objective Internality already operate across the complete 48-movement essay, and where should its recurrence become more explicit without imposing a false topical superstructure?

## Required work

Read all eight stations and all 48 movements as a whole traversal, not from section summaries alone. Read declared transverse threads and run effect maps where the live theorem/concept/path actually bears a proposed relation.

For each movement record:

1. its exact current proposition and outgoing transformation;
2. the form of internality operative there, if any;
3. what makes that internality objective rather than merely private/interior;
4. the local `0/1`, `X/x`, source/determination or whole/part relation genuinely at work;
5. what the achieved determination depends upon but does not contain;
6. the relevant selfing / closure / hidden-zero / occupied-zero pathology, if earned;
7. the form of Return available at that register;
8. the relevant O:I/product relation only where it performs actual explanatory work;
9. claim/register status and source boundary;
10. publication office: linear-bearing, supporting-visible, radial-depth, or no new capstone action.

Do not force all six products or all Objective Internality motifs into every movement. The purpose is to discover recurrence and missing joins, not manufacture a correspondence table.

The traversal should pay particular attention to the change in office as the argument moves through phenomenology, logic, process, mathematics, psychoid/archetypal material, artificial agency and the final return.

## Done when

All 48 movements have a disposition; no movement is represented by a generic gloss that ignores its actual operation; the traversal identifies exact pressure points for tickets 012 and 013; and the result can function as a new transverse reading path for later Symbolon generation without becoming a second central plan.
