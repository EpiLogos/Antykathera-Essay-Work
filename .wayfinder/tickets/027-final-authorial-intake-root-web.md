# Final authorial intake — etymology, aphorisms, direct arguments, root web

## Why this gate exists

The completed section/corpus recovery gives the project enough contact to receive one final human-authored conditioning layer before the post-section review: the parallel etymological archaeology still being refined, concomitant aphoristic/koan formulations, simpler/direct formulations of core arguments, and title/nomenclature candidates.

This material is **pre-compositional ground**, not cosmetic polish. It must enter the same relational graph that will later generate arguments, concepts, etymologies, sections and depth pages.

Do not freeze material before the author supplies or ratifies it. The point of this ticket is to make the intake durable and relational once supplied.

## Governing distinctions

### Source work remains parallel

Incomplete citation, quotation, edition or source-house work does not block authorial/theoretical quilting. A supplied etymological or argumentative relation may be authorially live while its publication evidence remains debt in native 019.

### Etymology is pre-compositional intelligence

Word-history and semantic archaeology can carry compacted relations and histories of meaning into the concept/argument field. Etymology does not prove an argument merely by origin. Its job is to sharpen the operative relation and make its linguistic memory traversable.

### Aphorisms belong to Episteme

Aphoristic/koan formulations are an Episteme record family. Do not invent examples or a corpus. Current supplied ground includes:

`0 = One, 1 = All`

and the concomitant pithy lines the author supplies/ratifies.

An aphorism is a compressed contemplation object whose relations to theorem, concept, argument, section, etymology and sources are explicit and unfoldable.

### Burden classification

For every recovered or newly supplied relation, distinguish:

- `linear-essential` — the essay's core inference requires it;
- `supporting-visible` — materially clarifies/figures the argument and should remain visible;
- `radial/depth` — opens into the wider Epi-Logos paradigm, specialist research or speculative depth without burdening the linear essay;
- `whole-form/Mytheme` — preserves a myth, poem, artwork or narrative as a whole while the essay uses only the exact operation it needs.

Radial does not mean weak or disposable. It means the linear essay does not require traversal of the whole depth record.

## Root-web relation

Strengthen the actual intelligence graph:

```text
source / lived formulation / linguistic history
              ↕
etymological field
              ↕
concept
              ↕
argument
              ↕
section
              ↕
quilt / whole
```

Return relations matter as much as downward derivation: the developed argument can reveal which lexical relation is genuinely operative; the etymology can sharpen the concept; the concept can clarify the argument; the section can expose missing or overgrown nodes.

Every useful relation should be expressed through meaningful `[[wikilinks]]`, typed relation verbs and semantic backlinks rather than mere co-occurrence.
