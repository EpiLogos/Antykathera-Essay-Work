# Establish the current optional generative-video provider surface

## Question

What officially callable production APIs currently exist for Seedance 2.0/2.5 and viable provider-independent alternatives, and how should the capability registry represent their real inputs, duration, continuation, audio, provenance, policy, cost, and polling behaviour without making any one provider a release prerequisite?

## Resolution

Resolved in [Generative-video provider surface for Epi-Card v1](../research/generative-video-provider-surface.md). Seedance 2.0 is callable through BytePlus ModelArk or Runway; Seedance 2.5 is announced but lacks a verified production API. The registry must represent exact input/output profiles, availability lifecycle, authentication, asynchronous execution, billing, policy, provenance, and live health. Generative unavailability never blocks a card that can be rendered from approved public, imported, or commissioned plates.
