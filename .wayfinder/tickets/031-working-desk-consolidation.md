No paraphrase of his stipulations — quote, locate, organise by topic.
2. **Absorb.** Quilt-worthy ledgers and content (chiefly `final-argument-quilt-2026-08-23/`, plus anything else judged real content rather than review) move — never copy — into `submission-package/essay/quilt/` with a one-line intake note each. Authorial ratification files are signal: they feed the brief, not the quilt.
3. **Retire.** Agent-review pollution (`manuscript-fidelity-2026-09-19/` reviews, `pre-manuscript-refinement-2026-09-10/` R1–R6 returns and scaffolding, `writing-runs/` packets, `harmonisation-2026-08-12/`, `harmonisation-2026-08-18-*`, `recovery-checkpoints/`, `p2-enrichment/` non-salvaged mass, `publish-readiness-2026-09-24/` and `expressions-relation/` receipts, `conjugate-field/` after salvaging any proposal of value) moves to `working/_to_delete/2026-09-25-retire/` with a `MANIFEST.md` listing every file and a one-line reason. Nothing is deleted outright.
4. **Remaining top level:** the assembly manuscript (`manuscript-source-enrichment-2026-09-20/`), `manuscript-drafts/`, the brief, `process-ledger.md`, `sources-texts-references/`, `antykathera-resources/`, `legacy/`, `_to_delete/`, plus any root files of standing value.

Untouchable: `working/sources-texts-references/`, `working/legacy/`, `working/process-ledger.md` (append-only), the assembly manuscript's contents. When in doubt whether a file is signal: quote it into the brief and retire the file — the manifest makes everything recoverable. No commits; no builders; no suite runs.

Report → `.wayfinder/research/031-working-desk-report.md`: counts salvaged/absorbed/retired, the brief's table of contents, everything ambiguous.
