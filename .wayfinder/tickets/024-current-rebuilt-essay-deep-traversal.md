# Deep-traverse the rebuilt current essay and granular argument field

## Question

What is *The Return of Zero* **actually saying now**, at the level of every current authored movement, argument, concept and deep supporting surface, after the rebuilding work already completed?

## Why this gate exists

The developmental quilt establishes `how this became`. The rebuilt current corpus establishes `what this is now`. They are conjugate retrievals.

A final quilt that moves from developmental quilts directly to a census can cream the current argument off its own surface: it may know the themes without knowing what each current section now actually does. This ticket therefore forbids false completion by summary.

**No quilt traversal, file count, inherited synthesis, argument-name inventory or recensus substitutes for this deep read.**

3. Derived projections may be classified as such only after inspection.
4. All 21 current cross-sectional argument nodes in full.
5. All developed concept nodes in full, plus recovered reference-note/deep-concept surfaces that materially carry their meaning.
6. All transverse maps/paths.
7. Histories, etymologies, source houses, internal write-ups, theorem documents, response matrices and deep research files actually required by the current units or carrying unique developed argument. Discover these from the live tree rather than remembered filenames. Explicitly inspect `working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/`, core theorem/QL working sources, deep technical/political/psychological write-ups and orphaned argument-bearing resources such as `unit-social-power` where present.
8. After the eight-room/current-corpus traversal, read the non-parallel companion quilts against the recovered current body. They function as discrepancy/provenance pressure, never as substitutes for current state. The Parallel Harmonised Quilt remains late under native 022 / GitHub #43.

## Unit-of-contact protocol

For every current movement, argument, concept and argument-bearing deep surface record:

- **what it is here**;
- **why the distinction/unit exists** and what it enables;
- local argumentative burden and unique contribution;
- authored/developed provenance and upstream determinants;
- materially active positions in `−/− · 0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx · 1/0`;
- relevant dia-/sym-ballein operation;
- Symbolon / Matheme / Mytheme / Episteme office;
- upstream and downstream consumers;
- transverse threads it actually bears;
- later developmental correction/deepening or unresolved pressure;
- source/evidence debt without weakening the developed relation;
- duplication, conflict, degraded propagation or missing current carrier.

**Local integrity precedes transverse synthesis.** Recover the unit on its own terms before using it to prove a larger thread.
