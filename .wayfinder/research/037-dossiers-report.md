## The short answer to the fear

The fear was that the dossiers' content never reached the writing passes. Mostly it did — five of the seven are consumed deeply and often beautifully in the essay body. What actually happened is narrower and fixable: **the section-room movement files carry a handful of dossier-derived sentences that the sovereign essay never picked up.** The rooms are ahead of the essay at M11, M13, M18, M27, M31 and M44. Alongside those, a few developed dossier passages (the Nothaft correction, the formal-limit frequency/retention chain, the cross-entropy block, the Language-of-Spirit institutional return) exist in the dossier but in no surface at all.

## What needed incorporating — the pointers

Each pointer: dossier → where → the point it would carry → the manuscript sentence it would serve. Essay quotes are from `submission-package/essay/THE-RETURN-OF-ZERO.md`; movement paths are exact.

### zero-reception (the dossier with the most unrealised cargo)

1. **Nothaft correction → §1, M13** (`THE-RETURN-OF-ZERO.md`, M13 "A sign migrates between worlds"; parallel movement `section-rooms/02-return-of-zero/movements/13-s1-p0-sign-migrates.md`). The dossier's decisive corrective — the familiar story connecting medieval zero, the Devil and institutional bans is a modern myth (Nothaft 2020, *On the Genesis of a Modern Myth*) — appears nowhere in the manuscript, though the essay never makes the demonisation claim either. Adding it would let the essay perform in public the very source-discipline it practises. Serves: "The journey includes manuscripts, astronomical tables, teaching and commercial calculation. Each receiver has to acquire more than recognisable figures."
2. **Rotman's meta-Subject as distinct interlocutor → §1, M15 or M18** (parallel movement `18-s1-p5-loan-returns.md` carries it; the essay does not). The dossier's discipline: a semiotically instituted observing role is not the essay's irreducible subject-pole, and the comparison is productive precisely because both stay in view. Serves, in M15: "A represented condition and the condition in its operative presence have different offices."
3. **Gerbert and Salem as separate, still-open Latin routes → §1, M13.** Numeral form, use in a calculating apparatus, and positional use of an operational zero are different predicates; the Latin recovery tasks stay live without being filled by story. The movement file says this; the essay's Latin-reception paragraph does not. Serves: "Kaplan follows that life through Arabic mathematical writing and Latin reception."

### formal-limit

4. **Tractatus 6.4–6.522, the value boundary → §0/1, M04** (`section-rooms/00-integral-threshold/movements/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md` is the room counterpart; the dossier passage is developed, the essay's Tractatus paragraph stops at saying/showing). The dossier's positive Tractarian propositions — ethics cannot consist of further factual propositions; good or bad willing changes the world's limits, adding no fact; the complete scientific account leaves the problem of life untouched — would deepen M04 right where the aphorism about failed attempts at the Unknown follows. Serves: "A representation's ability to disclose something and its ability to state the condition of that disclosure have come apart."
5. **Frequency-retention chain and the signed dia seam → §2 M24 close and/or §3 M27** (`section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex.md` already links the dossier's `#frequency-retention-and-signed-dia-research` anchor and states the task; the essay's M27 has the iterants but not the task). What recurs, how a frequency is measured, what retains the prior state, and how that retention changes the next crossing — the exact open flank of the essay's own re-entry-with-phase construction. Serves, in M24: "How does the native formal body preserve that acquired history through its own transformations?" and in M27: "A temporal alternation, a third value and a new algebra are different responses to the difficulty."
6. **Varela's third state grants no exemption from Gödel's conditions → same M27 paragraph.** The dossier keeps the constructive gain and the incompleteness boundary together; the essay's Varela sentence invites exactly this one clause. Serves: "Each must show what it retains from the earlier setting and what it adds."

### bohm

7. **Common implicate order as the psychoid's process ground → §4, M31** (`section-rooms/05-psychoid-flowering/movements/31-s4-p0-psychoid-problem.md` line for line carries it; the essay's M31 opens straight from the Arche-Topos). Mind and matter as distinct explicate orders whose relations are internally carried by a deeper holomovement gives the psychoid's "common ordering field" a process body before the correspondence is introduced. Serves: "The psychoid relation established through the Arche-Topos now has to be encountered from within a life."
8. **Analysis versus fragmentation → §2, M19–M20.** Fragmentation occurs when the account treats the achieved difference as self-grounding and disowns its constituting relations. The essay argues this natively as monopoly and source-claim; Bohm's named distinction is the neighbouring witness and the word "fragmentation" never occurs in the essay. Serves: "A formed `1` is occupying the office of the `0` through which it became possible."
9. **Language of Spirit: the institutional return → §5→0, M46** (dossier §4; nowhere in either surface). A common procedure can sustain differences between knowledge-worlds; translation may require a retained local term or an explicit untranslatable remainder; no participant's vocabulary becomes the master ontology; and the practical test is whether participants can affect the question and the criteria of response. This is exactly the wisdom-feed's receiving office. Serves: "A result produced elsewhere can inform the next inquiry without being accepted merely because it arrived through the common network. The recipient must be able to decide what the artifact establishes, what it leaves open and how its conditions differ from those of the new use."

### indian-philosophy

10. **The gnomon: `36−25=6²−5²=11=5+1+5` → §0, M11 (or §3, M26).** The dossier separates exact arithmetic from the Offered/Argued cross-reading of the eleven (five pure powers, Māyā hinge, five kañcukas) without making Abhinavagupta the inventor of the schema. The essay loves this kind of exact-but-bounded arithmetic (M26's 137) and M11's six-turn compression never receives it. Serves: "The thirty-sixfold account is carried here through six turns of contraction and recognition. This is a compression of the traditional field, preserving the direction of its dependencies rather than replacing its enumeration."
11. **The five-step computational-Vimarśa acceptance cycle and the three-office proposal (anchored ground / generation / evaluation) → §5→0, M44** (`section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md` carries both; the essay's M44 has the four offices QL/MEF/Bimba/harness but neither the cycle nor the three offices). Preserve claim and excluded field; expose prompt, source, lens, evaluator, baseline; produce a counter-reading that can reach the conditions; state what the frame cannot settle; return the revision with provenance. Serves: "The return must be capable of altering the relevant operation. An explanation attached to an unchanged verdict leaves the governing standard protected."
12. **Chang's p. 99 appointment of AI to cross the threshold first → §5→0, M43/M44.** The dossier (and the O:I dossier's protected encounter) carry this authorial vocation for the instrument's initiative; the essay's third-motion instrument never names it. Serves: "Before asking the person to conform, the instrument turns towards the conditions through which its answer became authoritative."
13. Lesser: **karma-vimarśa versus kriyā-vimarśa (Dyczkowski p. 72) → §0, M10.** The source-specific articulation of agency — outward karma-vimarśa against unrestricted kriyā-vimarśa — would sharpen the action paragraph. Serves: "Action then carries these relations beyond contemplation."

### oi-technical-responsibility

14. **The cross-entropy block → §5, M38.** The essay has the Bradley–Terry shift, the temperature distinction and the DPO reference policy, but not the loss itself: `H(q,p) = −Σ qᵢ log pᵢ`, the one-hot reduction to `−log p_y`, the gradient `∂H/∂zⱼ = pⱼ − qⱼ`, and the point that the loss is not the softmax normalisation or the argmax chosen afterwards. The dossier insists a training loss has its own target and assumptions; M38's gauge paragraph is where that lands. Serves: "A small mathematical distinction makes the difficulty exact." and "One must identify which relation is being varied before claiming that a system has changed its judgment."
15. **The bounded SharedField evidence → §5, M42.** The dossier holds one bounded execution receipt (2026-09-08: six direct constructor checks, explicitly not a deployment claim). The essay's test paragraph says "not reported experimental successes" — true for the big tests, and it could honestly name the one small receipt that does exist, scope intact. Serves: "These are proposed research questions, not reported experimental successes."
16. **AIKit's explanation duty → §5, M39.** "Explanation must disclose absence or degradation where it occurs. A persuasive answer cannot substitute for a source which was never actually read." The essay's correction-path paragraph stops one step short of this formulation. Serves: "Merely retrieving every related document would conceal rather than resolve those differences."

### process (least outstanding)

17. **The autopoiesis boundary applied to agents → §5, M41.** A software agent editing a file or calling a tool does not establish that an operative unity regenerates its own production network; equally, dependence on external infrastructure does not make its actual agency unreal. The essay runs the discipline biologically in M23; the technical application belongs at Workcell. Serves: "Workcell names this concrete being-there, including the resistance through which a projected world discovers what can actually happen."
18. Lesser: **the harness-norm test → §5, M42.** Identify the norm, the boundary it sustains, the perturbation admitted, and the loop through which the resulting distinction changes conduct. Serves: "The test of such an architecture must therefore include cases in which the original alternatives are adequate and cases in which they are not."

The essay could name the horizon without any passage claim. Serves: "Jung's return to natural number gives the shared inquiry a definite object."

## Where the gains concentrate (priority order)

1. **§1 (M13–M18)** — zero-reception's corrective half (Nothaft, Rotman, the Latin predicates). The essay's historical line is strong; its reception-criticism half is missing entirely. Biggest single gain.
2. **§3 and the M24/M27 seam** — the formal-limit frequency/retention chain and the Varela-Gödel boundary; the movement file already models the sentences. Plus M04's Tractatus value boundary, which deepens the threshold's own climax.
3. **§4→§5 seam (M31, M43/M44)** — Bohm's common implicate order under the psychoid, Chang's threshold appointment, and the five-step cycle with the three offices. All three already exist in movement files; the essay lags.
4. **§5 (M38, M39, M42)** — cross-entropy completion, AIKit's explanation duty, and naming the one bounded constructor receipt. These make the technical register as exact as the dossier keeps it.
5. **§5→0 (M46) and §2 (M19–M20)** — the Language-of-Spirit institutional return at the commons, and Bohm's analysis/fragmentation at the two-logics opening. Smaller, but each is one sentence of real gain.

One pattern worth holding onto for the enrichment pass: in six of these pointers the room movement already says the thing and the essay is the laggard surface (M11, M13, M18, M27, M31, M44). Those are transcription recoveries, not new writing. The rest — Nothaft, Chang, Language of Spirit, cross-entropy, the named bounded receipt — are compositions the dossier has earned and no surface yet carries.
