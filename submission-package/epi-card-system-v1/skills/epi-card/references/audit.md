# Twelve-position QL audit

Every material decision is itself a full conjugate tick.

## Bimba audit

| Address | Role |
|---|---|
| P0 | Decision ground, permission scope, initiating question |
| P1 | Objective, target, or candidate field |
| P2 | Proposed operation/transformation/generation |
| P3 | Selection pattern, rule, test, or success criterion |
| P4 | Pasu, temporal, medium, production, and contextual constraints |
| P5 | Provisional integrated outcome |

## Pratibimba audit

| Address | Role |
|---|---|
| P0′ | Ungrounded assumptions, unknowns, and uncaptured freedom |
| P1′ | Traces, evidence, residual detail, and provenance |
| P2′ | Obstruction, artifact, failure, cost, or sacrifice |
| P3′ | Counter-pattern, alternative interpretation, or hidden bias |
| P4′ | Missed context, accessibility issue, Pasu mismatch, or wider horizon |
| P5′ | Verified work, accepted revision, approval, and deposited output |

The return stores selected outcome, rejected candidates, uncertainty, semantic/media delta, and next ground. Use externally inspectable evidence—not hidden model reasoning.

An action cannot succeed until all twelve audit positions exist. An absent audit position needs an occupancy reason. Read-only retrieval may opt out; ranking, selection, interpretation, or recommendation remains auditable.
