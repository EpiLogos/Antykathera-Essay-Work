# Symbolic media pipeline

## Twelve atoms, six pairs

Every film has one scene atom for each `P0…P5′`. Pair `Pn` and `Pn′` into six reciprocal scene pairs for pacing and card-edge presentation. The pair choreography is one of:

`phase_flip`, `crossfade`, `interleaved`, `split_field`, `masked_reveal`, `conjugate_cut`, `audio_visual_exchange`.

Base profile: 9:16, 6–12 seconds total, 6 reciprocal pairs, 12 atoms. Extended profile: 40–60 seconds with the same structure and longer internal development.

## Generated plates and exact finishing

1. Generate or collect candidate world plates.
2. Accept a plate against the scene atom's intent and QL audit.
3. Apply deterministic crop/time/colour/texture operations.
4. Add the approved SVG symbol as overlay, window, alpha/luma mask, rim, or transition matte.
5. Add outlined display lettering and QR deterministically.
6. Add/mix the Faust QL Resonator audio deterministically.
7. Render with the pinned Remotion and FFmpeg versions.
8. Validate seam, timing, alpha, colour, audio, and output profile.

The generator does not own canonical symbol geometry, readable typography, exact timing, QR, or canonical tuning.

## Symbol pipeline

Search bank → choose reuse/parameterise/transform/combine/generate-new → propose grammar → generate candidate → reconstruct/sanitise SVG → validate geometry → derive Bimba/Pratibimba and twelve states → approve → emit alpha/mask/print derivatives.

## Audio pipeline

Resolve ratio/tuning/resonator palette → render twelve coupled states through QL Resonator → analyse FFT/CQT/chroma and loudness → mix restrained drone/bell body → validate loop. Incidental generator audio is separate from canonical sound.
