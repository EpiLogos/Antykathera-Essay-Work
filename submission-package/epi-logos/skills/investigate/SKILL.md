# Investigate — the interrogative quaternity walk

> `using-epi-logos` runs first — it installs the base alignment (the six question-positions), the six determinations, and the Derived/Argued/Offered discipline this skill fires through.

This is the base framework: given any subject, claim, or dilemma, walk it through the questioning structure of QL. The six interrogative positions are not a checklist to fill but the shape a thing takes when it is *questioned completely* — asked after its ground, its what, its how, its who, its where, and its wherefore, then returned to the binding that set the inquiry going. It is the plugin's spine: `two-logics-of-two`, `apply-tetralemma`, `engage-encounter-axis`, and the rest all fire *at a position within this walk*.

## The shape — eight turns, six-fold at the core

The walk is **eight stations**, the six-fold bracketed by a binding and a return — the essay's own spine (§0/1 … §5→0), the determinations' "eight turns":

| Turn | Position | Question | Determination (isoid) | Fires |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **bind** | `0/1` | — | `0/1` ground–mark | the binding that *creates the context* — see below |
| 1 | **#0** | Why-so? | `0/1` | the ground: on what does this stand, what does it presuppose |
| 2 | **#1** | What? | `?/!` asks-and-asserts | the definition — the cut that says *this, not that* |
| 3 | **#2** | How? | `−/+` pulses | the dynamis — the process, the movement, the operation |
| 4 | **#3** | Who / Which / Whereby? | `X/x` patterns | the agency and pattern — by whom, by which, through what form |
| 5 | **#4** | Where / When / Whither? | `AM/IS` | the context — the situation entered; **the aperture** (below) |
| 6 | **#5** | Why-for / Why-not? | `∞/dx` differentiates | the wherefore — the telos, *for the sake of which*; the `pros hen` |
| **return** | `5→0` | — | `1/0` switch | the return: gather the six to the binding, test what it served |

The **four explicate questions** — What / How / Who / Where (#1–#4) — are framed by the **two why-poles** — Why-so (#0) and Why-for (#5). That framing is the `4+2`. The `0/1` and `5→0` are additional turns that bracket the whole; they do **not** collapse into #0 and #5.

## `0/1` — the binding (before the walk)

`0/1` is not an ethereal ground-mark; it is a **binding — a working contract between user and agent that creates the context** the six-fold then runs inside. Establish it first, plainly:

- *what* is actually being investigated (the subject, claim, or dilemma, stated back);
- *who* is asking and *who* is answering — the user (the **0**, the one whose question and subjecthood the walk serves) and the agent (the instrument, the mark it makes);
- *what kind* of ask this is — a terse lookup, a working-through, a full reading — which sets register and depth;
- what is *assumed* held in common before the first question lands.

Get the binding wrong and the whole walk answers a question no one asked.

## The six-naked-numbers front door

The intuitive way in — QL as intuition, not tabulation. Before any lens is named, invite the user to **render the thing in six**: six statements, words, or symbols, one per position, met as *naked number*. They needn't know what #0–#5 "mean"; the numbers held as number gain an intuitively accessible depth across instantiations. Their six naked numbers are the inquiry's first pass — the seed the walk then works. This lives inside the `0/1` binding: rendering the thing in six *is* how the contract of what-we-are-investigating gets set.

## Walking the six

Each position is fired as a **Day-question** (`?`, the asking mode) that resolves to a **Night-assertion** (`.`, the landed answer). Ask, then let it settle; do not assert before the question has been genuinely put.

- **#0 Why-so?** — the ground. Why does this arise at all; what condition or presupposition is it standing on? (Its own opening fixes the *in quantum* — the "insofar as" the rest holds under.)
- **#1 What?** — the definition. What is it, cut from what it is not? The `?/!` both asks and asserts in one stroke — a definition is a question answered by a boundary.
- **#2 How?** — the dynamis. How does it work, move, come about? The `−/+` pulses: the process read as its own alternation.
- **#3 Who / Which / Whereby?** — agency and pattern. Who or which acts; by what form or through what does it operate? The `X/x`: the ordering `X` legible only through the determinate `x`.
- **#4 Where / When / Whither?** — the context entered. Where, when, toward-what does it stand? This is the **aperture** — the `P4`/`L4` contextual-entry gate: enter through *lived situation* (subjective `L4`: concern, horizon, what it is like) or through *observable state* (objective `L4'`: current state, goal, verification). Choose the entry mode the subject actually presents.
- **#5 Why-for / Why-not?** — the wherefore. For the sake of what; and why-not otherwise? This is the `pros hen` position (Aristotle's *said-in-relation-to-one*, focal meaning): the telos the many parts are *in relation to*.

## `5→0` — the return (the implicit critique)

Not a seventh question but the **return of the whole**: gather the six back to the `0/1` binding and test it. `5→0 = 0/1` (the Möbius identity), so the critique is the opening come back around, and it should pervade the walk, not only cap it:

- did the answer **serve the 0** — the user's actual question and subjecthood — that it was for?
- what does the answer **depend on that it has not owned**? (the CMEA move: the gap between what a reading claims and what it silently leans on — `apply-cmea` fires here.)
- what did the walk have to **exclude** to land, and is that exclusion honest?

The return closes the loan: the ground is renewed, or the walk is sent back to a position it under-asked.

## Lenses refract; they do not replace

The six positions are **raw archetypal light** — they gain semantic content only through **L-lens refraction** (causal, Jungian, phenomenological, scientific; the four causes fall at #1–#4, the encounter axis at #4, and so on). Reason *through* the lenses and the fresh-context refraction workflow, and **speak naturally while naming them in flow** — a lens woven into a sentence, not a table dumped on the reader. Match the user's level: a first-time asker gets the question in plain speech; a fluent one gets the lens named and moved through.

## Output — natural chat, a small mark

The body is **natural chat**; the structure rides in a **small mark**, not the prose. A terse question gets a terse answer and a light footer (e.g. `⟨#2·How — Argued⟩`); a full reading inhabits the whole envelope and may close with the positions touched and their claim-statuses. Never a wall of coordinates. Carry `Derived / Argued / Offered` wherever a claim is made.

## Composes with

- **`two-logics-of-two`** at **#2/#3** — when the subject turns on a distinction, classify the operation (dia-ballein / sym-ballein) inside the How/Who.
- **`apply-tetralemma`** at **#1/#3** — when a definition or pattern needs the four-valued reading (IS / IS-NOT / BOTH / NEITHER / SILENCE).
- **`engage-encounter-axis`** at **#4** — the Square B Cause–Experience axis (L1↔L4), when the where/when is a lived encounter.
- **`apply-cmea`** at **`5→0`** — the blind-spot diagnosis in the return.
- **`okf-wiki` / `walk-the-essay`** — when a position needs grounding in the essay's own argument graph.

## Pitfalls

- **Don't skip the binding.** A walk that never fixed `0/1` answers the wrong question fluently.
- **Six-fold, eight turns — keep them distinct.** `0/1` and `5→0` bracket the six; they are not #0 and #5. Collapsing them loses the binding and the return.
- **Don't march the positions as a form.** The six are how a thing is *questioned completely*, not boxes to fill; a terse ask does not need all eight voiced.
- **`5→0` is critique, not summary.** The return tests what the answer served and what it hid — it does not merely restate the walk.
- **Naked numbers first, lenses after.** Let the user meet the six as number before you name a single lens; imposing the lens-grid up front kills the intuition the gesture is for.
- **Signs are isoid, not labels.** `?/!`, `−/+`, `X/x`, `∞/dx` are operators whose operation *is* their meaning — use them as such, never as decorative tags.
