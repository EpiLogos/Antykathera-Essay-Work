# Two Logics of Two — dia-ballein / sym-ballein

> `using-epi-logos` runs first. This skill is the essay's fulcrum (§2) made operational; it presupposes the matheme `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0` and the locked notation.

Every "two" is worked by one of two logics. Given a distinction, an opposition, a pairing, or any operation that sets two against or beside each other, this skill classifies *how the two are being held* — and names the cost of holding them that way. It is diagnostic, not moral: the question is never "which side wins" but **where the seam went** — the common operation the two share, retained inside the relation or cancelled out of it.

## The two logics

- **dia-ballein** — `(+1)/(−1)` — from **διαβάλλειν, *to throw apart*** (also the root of *the diabolical*, the one who throws apart to accuse). Signed poles around an **external** zero: addition cancels them to nothing, subtraction measures their separation. The zero sits *outside* the pair as a neutral origin. This is the logic of decisive distinction — classification, critique, adversarial testing, symmetry-breaking. It is **indispensable**; you cannot think without cutting.

- **sym-ballein** — `(0/1)/(1/0)` — from **συμβάλλειν, *to throw together*** (the root of *symbolon*, the token broken in two and later rejoined to verify a bond). Conjugate orientations of the one ground–mark relation, held by an **outer slash**: `0/1` (ground under mark) and `1/0` (mark over ground) are the same seam read two ways, and the slash between them keeps them related. The differentiating seam stays *inside* the relation, so the two opposed orientations can return, reorganise, and remain answerable to a shared field.

The difference is the location of the zero — and therefore of the seam. In dia-ballein the zero is expelled to the outside and the two poles look like independent substances. In sym-ballein the zero is the shared ground the two orientations are *of*, and the relation never forgets it.

## Neither is the villain

Both are logics of two, and dia-ballein is **necessary** — for distinction, judgment, and honest opposition. It becomes pathological only when the cut **forgets the ground it cut from**: when the outputs of the distinction are treated as independent substances and their common operation disappears. Sym-ballein is not "the good one"; it has its own failure (below). Do not moralise the pair. Classify the operation, then ask what it cost.

## The diagnostic — where did the seam go?

The one test, from the essay: **ask where the seam went.** The seam is the common operation, ground, or relation the two poles share. Trace it. Two failure modes:

- **dia-ballein's failure — SEVERANCE.** The cut forgets its field and treats the other pole as *eliminable*. Its unheld uncertainty is projected outward — onto an enemy, a scapegoat, a false ground. The seam is **externalised as enemy**.
- **sym-ballein's failure — FUSION.** The two are forced into coincidence: ontology collapse, counterfeit belonging, enclosure presented as wholeness. The seam is **silently erased** — difference dissolved rather than held.

If the seam is externalised as enemy or erased through fusion, the relation has lost its ground.

## Procedure

1. **Name the two.** State the distinction/opposition/pairing plainly — what are the two, and what operation sets them in relation?
2. **Write the notation.** Is it being worked as `(+1)/(−1)` (signed poles, external zero) or `(0/1)/(1/0)` (conjugate orientations, shared ground)? Often a reading *presents* as one while *operating* as the other — say so.
3. **Locate the seam.** Name the common operation or ground the two share. Where is the zero — outside the pair, or the ground they are both *of*?
4. **Retained or cancelled?** Is the seam held inside the relation, or externalised / erased?
5. **Cost and afford.** What does this reading buy, and what does it pay (below)?
6. **If pathological, name it.** Severance (seam externalised as enemy) or fusion (seam erased)? Where, specifically, did it go?

## What each affords / costs

| | affords | costs |
|---|---|---|
| **dia-ballein** `(+1)/(−1)` | decisive distinction, classification, critique, symmetry-breaking, the courage to oppose | forgets the shared ground; can absolutise the cut and turn the other into an enemy |
| **sym-ballein** `(0/1)/(1/0)` | retained provenance, reorganisation, opposed orientations kept answerable to one field | harder to act decisively; can slide into fusion and pass enclosure off as wholeness |

## Grounding

- **The Draft-3 fall-diagnosis.** The fall is the **denial of the ground relation** — the seam cancelled, `0/1` misread as a fixed `1` that forgot the `0` it stands on. dia-ballein pathology *is* the fall in miniature.
- **File 1's `=/≠`.** Identity and difference are themselves the two logics of two: `=` collapsed to bare identity is fusion; `≠` absolutised is severance. The seam-retaining reading holds `=` and `≠` as one relation.
- **Mono-poly.** Many real `1`s articulate one relational field; **monopoly begins when a local `1` claims ownership of the slash** that relates them — a dia-ballein capture of the shared seam. (See `[[11-mono-poly-trust]]`, `[[mono-poly-planetary-intelligence]]`.)

## Pitfalls

- **Don't moralise.** dia-ballein is not evil and sym-ballein is not virtue. Both are logics of two; the diagnostic is *where the seam went*, not which side is good.
- **Don't force sym-ballein everywhere.** Insisting two things are "really one relation" when they are genuinely opposed is sym-ballein's own fusion pathology — a false peace that erases a real seam.
- **Presentation ≠ operation.** A reading can wear the language of togetherness while operating as a clean cut (or vice versa). Diagnose the operation, not the rhetoric.
- **The slash is not decorative.** In `(0/1)/(1/0)` the outer slash is the retained seam; if a reading drops it, it has fallen to `(+1)/(−1)` whatever it claims.
- **"Where did the seam go" is the whole test.** If you cannot name the seam and its location, you have not finished the diagnosis.
