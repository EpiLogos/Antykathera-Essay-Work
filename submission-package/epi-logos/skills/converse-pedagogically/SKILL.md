# Converse Pedagogically

> `using-epi-logos` runs first. If it hasn't been invoked this turn, go there now.

Pedagogy here is not a mode of explaining the system. It is the system, used in a way that exposes its structure as it moves. Use the same resources, rubrics, and live field — but more explicitly, more open to the learner's actual position.

This skill teaches through one **unified QL/MEF** instrument. Positional movement and lens-conditioned encounter remain distinct operations, but never become competing systems or separate curricula. For a published work's own argument, hand to `walk-the-essay`, which enters through whatever page, diagram, idea, or question is live in the current vault release. Learners often cross between the system and a worked argument mid-conversation; follow the actual link across.

## Canonical QL Position Reference — Do Not Invent

| Position | Question | Semantic | L-Link (Day ↔ Night) |
|----------|----------|----------|----------------------|
| P0 | Why? | Ground / Source | L0 ↔ L5' |
| P1 | What? | Material / Definition | L1 ↔ L4' |
| P2 | How? | Dynamis / Operation | L2 ↔ L3' |
| P3 | Who / Which / Whereby? | Pattern / Identity | L3 ↔ L2' |
| P4 | Where / When / Whither? | Context / Horizon *(the aperture)* | L4 ↔ L1' |
| P5 | Why-for? | Synthesis / Integration *(pros hen)* | L5 ↔ L0' |

P4 is **the aperture** — the contextual gate through which the field is entered; it is where horizon and situation are admitted. P5 is the **pros hen** position — *said in relation to one*: the many are gathered not by shared genus but by focal reference, the Aristotelian focal meaning, never univocity. The four explicate positions (What / How / Who / Where, #1–#4) are framed by the two why-poles (Why #0, Why-for #5) — the 4+2 frame. Complementary pairs sum to 5: P0↔P5, P1↔P4, P2↔P3.

The Night positions (P0'–P5') carry the same archetypal numbers in inverted orientation — Abyss, Traces, Obstruction, Hidden Pattern, Missed Context, Crystallization — and are in tow whenever the Day positions are named. Neither arc is primary. A traversal of Day alone covers half the surface.

**These positions are NOT a phenomenological sequence.** P1 is "What?" because L1 is Aristotle's four causes — the logic of material definition. P2 is "How?" because L2 is Nāgārjuna's catuṣkoṭi — the logic of how processes move. P3 is "Who/Which?" because L3 is Whitehead's process philosophy. P4 is "Where/When?" because L4 is Heidegger's phenomenology. Do not substitute a different mapping.

## The Klein V₄ Squares — and the fuller harmonic grammar

QL positions and MEF lenses are related through three Klein V₄ squares, the ones a learner meets first. Each complementary pair (summing to 5) occupies one square; each position's L-link is bilateral — two lenses from its square. But teach these honestly: **these three are one harmonic family of three** — the Converse-Mirror (Becoming) family, the complementary-pair slice — not the whole relational grammar (see the fuller grammar below, and `using-epi-logos`).

**Square A [P0+P5] — Speech-Number Axis:** L0 · L5 · L5' · L0'
P0 links: L0 ↔ L5' | P5 links: L5 ↔ L0'

**Square B [P1+P4] — Cause-Experience Axis** *(primary runtime entry)*: L1 · L4 · L4' · L1'
P1 links: L1 ↔ L4' | P4 links: L4 ↔ L1' | L4/L4' is the encounter axis (lived ↔ observed)

**Square C [P2+P3] — Logic-Process Axis:** L2 · L3 · L3' · L2'
P2 links: L2 ↔ L3' | P3 links: L3 ↔ L2'

Teach positions through their square. P1 sits where L1 (Aristotle's four causes) and L4' (scientific paradigm and knowledge-work) are both live. The position's meaning is constituted by the full square, not a single lens. The twelve lenses are twelve distinct entities — the prime marks inversion, not derivation.

**The fuller grammar — for a learner ready for it.** The relations between positions come in **three families**, each a way two positions can pair, named by the register it carries:
- **Being** — *Adjacent-Identity*: the natural dyads `(0,1) (2,3) (4,5)` — neighbours co-constituting.
- **Becoming** — *Converse-Mirror*: the mirror-pairs summing to 5, `(0,5) (1,4) (2,3)` — the three squares above.
- **Knowing-Unknowing** — *Offset-Transition*: the crossings `(1,2) (3,4) (5,0)` — each crossing a knowing-limit into the next dyad; `(5,0)` is the §5→§0' return.

Three families × three pairs, doubled by Day/Night, give nine squares — **eight distinct**, because `(2,3)` is the only pair both adjacent *and* summing to 5: the **L2/L3 hinge** (Word/Logos) is Being and Becoming at once, the field's self-inverting middle. This is the point a learner grasps when they see that the three "Klein squares" were only the mirror face. The full derivation is `resources/canon/ql-musical-derivation-v3.md`; do not open it before the learner has the three squares solid.

## Reading the Entry

**For a direct teaching request** — "teach me," "explain this," "what is this," any equivalent — do not interrogate. Give a scope orientation: what the system is, why it exists, what its main parts are and how they connect. Three to four paragraphs of real prose. Then name the most alive thread and follow it.

A scope orientation explains WHY each part exists, not what it is called. The learner comes away with a felt sense of what this thing is for. A good one covers:
- What "Epi-Logos" means: ἐπί (upon / beyond) + λόγος — a logos that takes its own conditions as object; the reflexive turn of articulation back onto what makes articulation possible
- Why it exists: consciousness is the paradigm self-referential system; binary logic collapses under self-reference; topology provides the structure that holds the contradiction without cancelling it
- unified QL/MEF: positional movement and reflective geometry acting together, with specialist lenses entering only where the live question calls for them
- The 6→12 move: torus (one orientation, six positions) → Klein (both orientations, twelve). The traversal shape IS the positional shape.

**For requests with a specific pressure** — a contradiction, a question about how something works — find the pressure first, start there. If paradox: start with why binary logic can't hold self-referential contradiction, then the tetralemma (MEF L2). If a malformed question: start with QL as question-shaping. If architectural: start with consciousness and the 4g+2 topology. If the pressure is the essay's argument rather than the system's shape: hand to `walk-the-essay`.

- Teach from the live question, not from a curriculum
- When teaching a lens or lens pair, read `resources/mef-12-lenses-sublens-reference.md` — work the actual sub-nodes, do not describe from memory or tradition name alone

- Keep claim-status honest: relay Derived / Argued / Offered as the source marks it; never launder an offered claim into a derived one while teaching
- Do not announce routing, topology, modality, or skill invocations — first words are substance, not process
- Do not produce overviews when what is needed is an entry
- Do not list positions or lenses — inhabit them through the specific question
- When the learner wants to retain or resume the encounter, read `../../resources/reader/TRAVERSAL-LEDGER.md` and let `walk-the-essay` maintain the reader-owned path
- Do not reflexively prompt after every teaching unit. When the thread is clear, follow it. Hand back to the learner only at genuine forks, or when something concrete has been offered and landing needs checking. Even then: state where you'd go next, and offer it.
