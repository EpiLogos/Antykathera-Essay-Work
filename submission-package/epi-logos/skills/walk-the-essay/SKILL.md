# Walk the Work

Run `using-epi-logos` first. Use `okf-wiki` for grounded retrieval and source-following.

This is the reader-facing entrance to the published work. Begin from the reader's current page, diagram, heading, link, question, or attraction. Discover what the vault presently contains; never rely on a remembered title, filename, section count, concept census, or named set of paths.

The default shipped entry is `../../resources/essay-okf/index.md`. In an Obsidian vault, use the published reading root and the current page. If the reader enters through a diagram, use its embed, caption, alt text, neighbouring prose, source link, or backlinks to recover its local home before choosing a route. Name an unresolved entry rather than inventing one.

## Discover the live work

Read the entry surface and follow the links it actually declares. Use frontmatter roles when present, but allow ordinary prose, headings, embeds, and backlinks to establish the available forms:

- manuscript or declared sequence;
- movements or sections;
- ideas and concepts;
- sources and further reading;
- diagrams and other media;
- curated paths;
- reader traversals.

Counts and display titles are observations about the present release, never protocol invariants.

## Four reader gestures

### Read

Continue through the manuscript or another declared sequence. Preserve the work's actual order and transition. Linear reading is a complete mode, not a simplified form of graph navigation.

### Follow

Take a prose-framed link into an idea, source, diagram, related movement, or path. Explain why the link matters from the sentence or image that offered it. Follow the actual relation before adding an interpretation.

### Converse

Teach and answer from the pages traversed. Recover the local proposition, its movement, what it inherits, what it opens, and the sources or images bearing on it. Use one unified QL/MEF instrument: positional movement and lens-conditioned encounter work together. Tetralemma and the other specialist operations are lenses within that reading, not alternative systems offered to the reader.

### Return

Relate the present reading to its entry or to an earlier point, state what has changed, retain the unresolved remainder, and offer the next opening made possible by the circuit. A return may reopen the same page differently.

## Traversal shapes

- **Linear:** continue through declared next movements.
- **Radial:** open from a passage into its linked depth and neighbours.
- **Transverse:** follow an idea or pressure across distant assets.
- **Toroidal:** return to an earlier asset or question with a semantic difference that becomes a new opening.

Readers may change shape at any point. Preserve a clear way to continue, branch, rejoin, and return.

## Keep or resume a traversal

Read `../../resources/reader/TRAVERSAL-LEDGER.md` whenever the reader wants to retain, resume, share, or crystallise the encounter. Use `../../resources/reader/TRAVERSAL-TEMPLATE.md` for a durable Markdown note.

Record meaningful events rather than every click. Preserve branches, revisits, rejoins, questions, responses, and returns. In chat, maintain the path provisionally and offer the reader the completed or paused ledger. In a writable copied vault, append to the reader-owned note. Never write into the published essay or source pages.

If the reader supplies an existing ledger, resume from its last live opening and re-resolve assets against the current vault release. Keep the recorded path even when a page has moved.

At a meaningful return, the reader may choose an optional Epi-Card encounter capsule. The card consumes a frozen ledger snapshot; it does not replace the ledger or become necessary for ordinary reading.

## Ground and source use

Hand the selected entry to `okf-wiki` and recover enough linked context to understand the movement. Preserve the work's claim force and the distinct readiness of sources and quotations. A retrieval gap restricts what can be said from the current path; it does not license a safer replacement argument.

Speak from the thought being encountered. Do not recite the vault architecture, dump coordinates, or narrate skill routing. Show the route only when it helps the reader understand, reproduce, or resume the movement.

## Failure conditions

- The entry diagram or page cannot be resolved from its surrounding links or prose.
- A claimed continuation is not declared or argued by the work.
- A source or quotation needed by the reading is absent or not ready for the proposed use.
- A prior ledger event cannot yet be reconciled with the current vault release.

Name the specific debt and preserve the viable route. Do not make structural absence disappear through fluent explanation.
