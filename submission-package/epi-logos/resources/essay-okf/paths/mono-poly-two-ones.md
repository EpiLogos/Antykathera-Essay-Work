# Mono–Poly and the Two Ones — Transverse Thread

This path preserves the ontological kernel across the essay without turning it into a parallel topic. It begins only after zero and one have been earned, carries their formal differentiation through the two logics and the eight determinations, and lands politically and technically where shared relation must coordinate real plurality without claiming to have manufactured trust.

1. [§1 · #2 — The Empty Set Generates One](../sections/15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one.md) gives the numerical floor: `0 = ∅`, `1 = {0}`, and `2 = {0,1}`; whole-number and natural-number firstness divide the two ones without reducing QL `0` to a set-theoretic object.
2. [§1 · #4 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics](../sections/17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math.md) exposes the return-edge at which the marked one cannot make its condition into ordinary content.
3. [§1 · #5→0 — The Loan Returns](../sections/18-s1-p5-loan-returns.md) earns `0/1 ↔ 1/0` as the root symbolon and introduces Mono/One and Poly/All in their exact asymmetry.
4. [§2 · #0 — Difference Cannot Be Escaped](../sections/19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped.md), [§2 · #1 — Dia-Ballein](../sections/20-s2-p1-dia-ballein.md), and [§2 · #2 — Sym-Ballein](../sections/21-s2-p2-sym-ballein.md) distinguish the primordial relation from the two ways polarity is accounted: signed displacement and seam-retaining return.
5. [§2 · #3 — Ares, Aphrodite, Harmonia, Eros, and Hephaestus](../sections/22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia.md) gives the relation a mythemic body. Hephaestus remains inside this circuit: capture makes relation observable only after substituting its surface for encounter.
6. [§2 · #5→0 — Zero Changes Its Role](../sections/24-s2-p5-zero-changes-role.md) derives `2 + 2² = 4+2`; QL emerges from the two ones rather than receiving mono–poly as a later application.
7. [§3 · #0 — Eight Determinations](../sections/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md) and [§3 · #1 — The Spanda Equations and 4+2](../sections/26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2.md) carry the relation through `X/x`, `∞/dx`, and the sixfold body.
8. [§4 · #1 — Individuation through the QL `X/x`](../sections/32-s4-p1-jung-individuation.md) and [§4 · #2 — Complexio, Quaternity, and Senarius](../sections/33-s4-p2-complexio-quaternity-senarius.md) provide psychic refraction and recall the mythic relation without detaching it from its source.
9. [§5 · #3 — Preference Models and the Hidden Zero](../sections/40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero.md) gives the technical trial: benchmark observability and sycophantic compliance can leave trust unanswered while a metric or owner occupies the field.
10. [§5→0 · #1 — QL, MEF, Bimba, and Harness](../sections/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md), [§5→0 · #3 — 4:2 Technē and the Sovereign Commons](../sections/46-s50-p3-4-2-mono-poly.md), and [§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return](../sections/48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return.md) turn the relation into a shared grammar, a sovereign commons, and a planetary return without falsely installing a super-subject.

[Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit — Transverse Thread](trust-faith-formal-limit.md) runs alongside this path. Trust enters as the lived relation beneath knowledge and remains distinct from the whole/many topology that can protect or capture its conditions.
