# The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic — Transverse Thread

This path follows the historical emergence of the relation QL makes explicit. The theorem remains the derivation; the history carries the mathematical, psychological, Gebserian, and technical movements in their own registers while showing their shared sequence: `0` enters the modern order of signs, falls into the externalising logic of the mental-rational, and returns as `0/1` through an integral grammar of context, relation, and recognition.

1. [§0/1 · #4 — Gebserian Diaphaneity](../sections/05-s01-p4-gebser-diaphaneity.md) plants the historical frame: perspective produces an ego-world by locating the seer outside the scene, while diaphaneity makes the conditions of that seeing available as context.
2. [§1 · #0 — A Sign Migrates Between Worlds](../sections/13-s1-p0-sign-migrates.md) gives absence its material itinerary: placeholder, numeral, operator, origin, empty set, and meta-sign remain distinct historical offices.
3. [§1 · #1 — Śūnya Becomes Operational](../sections/14-s1-p1-sunya-operational.md) establishes the Indian mathematical hinge: zero enters arithmetic without becoming another positive magnitude.
4. [§1 · #4 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics](../sections/17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math.md) makes the return-edge exact: `1/0` reveals the law of its formal container.
5. [§1 · #5→0 — The Loan Returns](../sections/18-s1-p5-loan-returns.md) earns `0/1 ↔ 1/0` as the root symbolon, returning zero's formal history into the subject-pole announced at the opening.
6. [§2 · #1 — Dia-Ballein](../sections/20-s2-p1-dia-ballein.md) names the historical fall of the relation into `(+1)/(−1)`: an internal zero becomes external midpoint, blank absence, or cancellation while local terms present themselves as self-grounding.
7. [§3 · #0 — Eight Determinations](../sections/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md) carries the native QL body through `0/1`, `?/!`, `−/+`, `X/x`, `AM/IS`, `∞/dx`, and `1/0`; `X/x` remains authorial before the psychological refraction arrives.
8. [§4 · #0 — The Psychoid Problem](../sections/31-s4-p0-psychoid-problem.md) and [§4 · #1 — Individuation through QL X/x](../sections/32-s4-p1-jung-individuation.md) receive the psychological history: the unconscious becomes scientifically unavoidable, while its images and formations do not exhaust its determining capacity.
9. [§4 · #4 — Apollo Through Dionysus](../sections/35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus.md) gives the integral mutation its formal payoff: the `180°` perspectival triangle opens into the `360°` contextual field, retaining the mental-rational achievement inside a higher diaphanous relation.
10. [§5→0 · #1 — QL, MEF, Bimba, and Harness](../sections/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md) and [§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return](../sections/48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return.md) make the advent contemporary. Technical systems externalise the contextual conditions of intelligence; QL gives human and machine work a grammar of return without installing a technical system as the owner of its own ground.

[Mono–Poly and the Two Ones — Transverse Thread](mono-poly-two-ones.md) gives the ontological kernel. This path gives the historical morphology through which the kernel becomes explicit. [Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit — Transverse Thread](trust-faith-formal-limit.md) gives the lived relation at the point where the formal return meets a finite being's need to continue.
