# Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit — Transverse Thread

This path preserves one movement across the stations rather than making trust a detachable theme. The formal limit exposes the rational account's assumed ground; humility receives that exposure without abandoning reason; knowledge then becomes either a provisional anchor or an instrument of projected distrust; faith names the active continuation where no final ground can be possessed. The thread passes through psyche, politics, infrastructure, and the agentic field without allowing any one register to define the relation for the others.

1. [§0/1 · #3 — The Formal-Limit Genealogy](../sections/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md) establishes the limit intrinsic to a self-accounting rational system.
2. [§2 · #0 — Difference Cannot Be Escaped](../sections/19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped.md) names knowledge's dependence on a relation it cannot certify from inside itself.
3. [§2 · #1 — Dia-Ballein](../sections/20-s2-p1-dia-ballein.md) follows the unheld remainder into projection, scapegoating, atomisation, and false totality.
4. [§2 · #2 — Sym-Ballein](../sections/21-s2-p2-sym-ballein.md) gives determinate knowledge its proper task: a provisional anchor which does not claim to replace the relation beneath it.
5. [§2 · #3 — Ares, Aphrodite, Harmonia, Eros, and Hephaestus](../sections/22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia.md) embodies the difference between a risked, generative relation and capture that can only make it observable.
6. [§5 · #3 — Preference Models and the Hidden Zero](../sections/40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero.md) brings the issue into AI evaluation, where observability and benchmark performance must not be mistaken for trust.
7. [§5→0 · #1 — QL, MEF, Bimba, and Harness](../sections/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md) makes return structurally inspectable without claiming that an inspectable system has generated the trust it requires.
8. [§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return](../sections/48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return.md) releases the argument at the formal limit, where faith is neither closure nor a blind leap but lucid continuation.
