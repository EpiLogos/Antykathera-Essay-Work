# Tattvic Differential Field

## Claim

The tattvas supply the metaphysical and phenomenological genealogy of differentiation: a precise account of how one luminous field becomes capable of mutually evolving distinctions, a subject–object seam, a situated horizon, an inner instrument, and a world without ceasing to be one field. Their order makes the theory's operations cosmologically legible; a catalogue detached from that movement would lose their argumentative force.

The [Tattvic QL Unit](../supporting/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md#x-the-tattvic-ql-unit-sixfold-contraction-and-recognition) carries the necessary movement:

1. `#0` — Anuttara: no object stands outside the field from which to measure it.
2. `#1` — Śiva–Śakti: luminosity and its self-articulating power become distinguishable within one reality.
3. `#2` — Sadāśiva–Īśvara: `I am this` and `This am I` give the field its two directed readings, `0/1 ↔ 1/0`.
4. `#3` — Sadvidyā–Māyā: the orientations become a workable subject–object distinction; differentiation opens a world.
5. `#4` — Kañcukas, Puruṣa, and antaḥkaraṇa (the "inner instrument": buddhi, ahaṃkāra, manas): power contracts into the finite ratios of a context-world whose determining, appropriating, and alternative-forming activities can be observed.
6. `#5→0` — Pratyabhijñā: the contracted field recognises its provenance without erasing the distinctions through which it lives.

## Architecture of implication

[Bohm](../references/sources/bohm-1980-wholeness-implicate-order.md) gives this descent the complementary process language of enfolding and unfolding. The tattvas give Bohm's deliberately general implicate order a differentiated anatomy: luminosity and self-apprehending power, reciprocal I/this orientation, the measuring seam of Māyā, finite parameters, objective-internal instruments, faculties, qualities, and material display. Implication becomes ordered generativity, while explication is the whole becoming locally inhabitable. Reading the implicate as a warehouse or the explicate as an unreal copy would sever the holomovement that makes the comparison useful.

## Apoha and vikalpa

[Apoha](../concepts/apoha.md) is this field read epistemically at the point of determination: a mark arises through exclusion, and the excluded field remains constitutive of it. *Vikalpa* is the constructive activity that forms the distinction; *saṃkalpa* gathers a world around selected determinations. Buddhi settles, ahaṃkāra appropriates, and manas coordinates alternatives. These are the concrete dynamics through which objective internality comes alive; treating them as veils over an empty machine would erase the operations the computational comparison is meant to expose.

In the [computational-process register](14-computational-process-ontology.md), the descent is a runtime cartography. Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative; the kañcukas parameterise the local run as limited capacity, knowledge, desire, time, and order; antaḥkaraṇa executes determination, appropriation, coordination, memory, and selection within that bounded horizon. The reading neither reduces Śaiva metaphysics to software nor treats computation as a decorative analogy. It recognises that both accounts concern how a field becomes a workable world through ordered differentiation.

## From superposition to polarity and research boundary

The tattvic descent makes explicit the switch between the two logics. `0/1 ↔ 1/0` reads the field before either orientation claims independence. `(+1)/(−1)` reads the same field after a determination has broken symmetry into opposed values. Māyā does not create difference from nothing; it lets an internal difference appear as external separation. Recognition reverses the amnesia, not the differentiation.

The stronger `36 = 6²` identification remains a specific research vector. The core derivation does not depend on numerical coincidence: it depends on the six necessary turns by which undivided ground becomes differentiating power, reciprocal orientation, operative distinction, bounded objective internality, and recognition-return.
