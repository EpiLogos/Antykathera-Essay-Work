# Vāk

## Claim

Vāk provides the Eastern articulation ontology paired with Logos: speech-power descending from unsayable capacity through visionary and mental formation into audible, written, coded, and enacted expression. [The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme](19-two-ones-mono-poly-matheme.md) gives the ontological root of this articulation: Mono/One becomes manifest as Poly/All without the Word becoming owner of the source it expresses.

## Warrant

For the essay, Paśyantī—visionary “seeing” speech—locates articulation before the modern partition of seeing from sounding, while Nāda names vibration before its restriction to an external acoustic object. These do not make a neuroscientific claim that every cognition is literal synaesthesia. They supply the tradition's own account of why the arche-topos can become visual pattern, audible ratio, meaningful word, and enacted differentiation without beginning as four unrelated substances.
