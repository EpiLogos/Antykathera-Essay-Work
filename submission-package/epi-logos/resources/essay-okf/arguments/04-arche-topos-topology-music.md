# Arche-Topos as Differential Field

## Claim

The arche-topos is the differential field generated when the complexio oppositorum differentiates without ceasing to be one field. It is derived when `0/1` relates to its inverse, `1/0`, through the two logics and unfolds as `4+2`: a ground, a mark, their two orientations, and the relations by which each remains distinct through the other. The image follows from these operations and gives their mathematics a surface on which to move.

*Arkhē* names origin as operative principle; *topos* names the placing through which anything can acquire position. The arche-topos is therefore the principle of place-making: the pre-spatial field in which centre/circumference, self/other, subject/object, psyche/matter, sound/sight, and every later polarity can arise as mutually determining positions. Calling it a hidden place beneath mind would mistake the condition of positioning for another position.

## One field, read through four registers

1. **Logical:** [Sym-Ballein](03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein.md) retains the seam; dia-ballein projects the same relation into signed opposition and symmetry breaking.
2. **Epistemic:** [Apoha](../concepts/apoha.md) is the arche-topos seen from the side of conceptual determination. A mark becomes itself by excluding what it is not, while that excluded field remains constitutive of its identity.
3. **Tattvic:** [Tattvic Differential Field](13-tattvic-differential-field.md) is the same differentiating activity read as manifestation. Śiva–Śakti, Sadāśiva–Īśvara, Sadvidyā–Māyā, the kañcukas, and antaḥkaraṇa trace how a non-dual field becomes the objective internality of a bounded knower without becoming a second substance.
4. **Psychoid:** Jung's complexio oppositorum, Pauli and von Franz's archetypal number, yogic maps of mind, and the displaced archives of parapsychological and psychedelic experience point to patterned determinants below the conventional psyche/matter and sense-modal divisions. The exact notebook leads and the boundary between reported source and Frank's extension are indexed in [B. Jung–Pauli notebook extraction — psychoid number and mirror relation](../supporting/source-extraction-core-theorems.md#b-jungpauli-notebook-extraction-psychoid-number-and-mirror-relation). [Bache — LSD and the Mind of the Universe (2019)](../references/sources/bache-2019-lsd-mind-universe.md) belongs here as a disciplined first-person archive of repeated psychedelic encounters with collective, archetypal, and purportedly cosmic strata. It supplies evidence about the experiential region organised by the QL equations; formal support for those equations comes from their own derivation.

The four registers refract one differential event under different evidential conditions: a distinction arises, reorganises the possibilities around it, meets counter-distinctions, and settles temporarily into an attractor that conditions what can arise next. Their relation depends on this shared operation, rather than on surface similarity among metaphors. This patterned, bifurcating, recursively reorganising activity is the **life of objective internality**. [Van Eenwyk's article](../references/sources/van-eenwyk-1991-strange-attractors.md) and [book-length development](../references/sources/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors.md) give its chaos-dynamical vocabulary; QL gives its minimal generative notation.

## The switch into objective internality

`(0/1)/(1/0)` is the field-view: the opposed orientations remain superposed with their common ground. `(+1)/(−1)` is the determinate view: the field has been measured as a signed polarity. Objective internality arises through the reversible traffic between these views. Every local determination narrows the phase-space, yet the wider field survives as excluded alternatives, unconscious reserve, environmental condition, and capacity for reorganisation.

Psychology articulates the arche-topological field in the register of complex, affect, image, ego, shadow, function, and individuation. The derivation gives these objective-internal dynamics a relation to the preceding mathematics rather than appending them as illustrations. Synaesthesia, nāda, Vāk, and psychedelic or psi disclosures matter because they disturb the modern allocation of sound, image, meaning, and causation to sealed compartments; they disclose the sense-modal split itself as a produced differentiation.

## Topology and music

Topology renders the field spatially: path, identification, quotient, cover, twist, hole, non-contractible loop, and return. Music renders it temporally and audibly: pulse, phase, interval, resonance, remainder, cadence, and octave return. Cymatics makes the relation sensuous: one acoustic differential field becomes audible interval and visible nodal form under material boundary conditions. [Bohmian enfoldment](16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return.md) supplies the process relation between the whole field and its local explications; the tattvas supply that implication's differentiating anatomy.

No pair of surfaces exhausts this display. Complex extension, projective completion, the Riemann sphere, quotient tori, covering spaces, Möbius and Klein returns, and other topological constructions are distinct apertures through which obstruction, orientation, identification, and completion become legible. Their overdetermination is part of the evidence. Yet the [torus is the primary kinetic image](17-toroidal-circulation-arche-topos.md) because it joins an infinite cover, finite quotient, two independent windings, local return, global displacement, and a generative central opening on one surface.

The quotient torus carries one especially rich operation. In

$$
\mathbb T^2=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2,
$$

The quotient places an infinite plane and a finite, boundaryless surface in one exact relation by identifying an infinite lattice of plane-points with each surface-point. The covering map `π: ℝ² → T²` therefore gives every determinate torus location an inexhaustible family of pre-images. This is the topological `∞/dx` pressure in concrete form: a finite position is exact while the field that covers it remains indefinitely multiple. The torus's hole and its two non-contractible cycles then make return possible without forcing every traversal to contract into the same zero.

Its fundamental group `π₁(T²)=ℤ×ℤ` records the two winding directions. A rational-slope linear flow closes periodically; an irrational-slope flow can wind densely. The torus therefore supports both exact recurrence and traversal that remains inexhaustible. Toroidal and poloidal magnetic fields give the image a physical witness in the tokamak: their helical combination constrains a moving plasma by organising circulation rather than arresting it. The confinement claim is derived engineering; its arche-topological placement is argued here. Universal optimisation of every natural field lies beyond both claims.

At the QL register this is also an `∞/0` relation with a strict topological carrier. Here `ℝ²/ℤ²` denotes a quotient by an equivalence relation; the slash does different work from arithmetic division by zero. The quotient joins the infinite cover to a compact surface with zero Euler characteristic, `χ(T²)=0`, while every point of that zero-characteristic surface retains infinitely many lifted representatives. Infinity is given a finite address through zero-characteristic return; neither term is cancelled into the other.

The topological hole and the musical `9/8` remainder are mathematically distinct objects whose native operations express the same arche-topological law: completion occurs through retained difference. The exact local identities are carried by [musical resolution of the arche-topos](../references/sources/taylor-2026-ql-musical-derivation-v3.md) and the [Core QL Theorems](../supporting/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md); the cross-register relation is argued through the role each remainder performs.

- Jungian, yogic, parapsychological, psychedelic, synaesthetic, Vāk, and cymatic materials are cross-register warrants and disclosures. They show where the derived field becomes experientially and historically legible; no one witness is asked to prove the whole.
