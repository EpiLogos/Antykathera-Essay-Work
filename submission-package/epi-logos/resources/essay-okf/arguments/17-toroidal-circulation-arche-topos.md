# Toroidal Circulation and the Arche-Topos

## Claim

The torus is the kinetic image missing from the arche-topos. Its quotient construction, two independent winding directions, universal cover, and central opening show a bounded surface carrying circulation, return, and irreducible history at once. Tokamak and stellarator confinement then show toroidal circulation becoming an engineered energetic vessel rather than remaining an abstract diagram.

## Mathematical warrant

For `T²=ℝ²/ℤ²`, every surface point has an infinite lattice of lifts. Its fundamental group `π₁(T²)=ℤ×ℤ` records two winding numbers. A loop can close locally while its lift remains displaced globally. The surface therefore joins exact finite position to inexhaustible pre-image, and return to retained traversal.

`χ(T²)=0` is active closure: a closed orientable surface whose hole supports non-contractible circulation. Zero characterises the global balance of the surface rather than an absence of structure.

## Physical warrant and analogy boundary

Toroidal magnetic confinement closes field-line paths around a plasma so charged particles do not encounter simple linear end-loss. Toroidal and poloidal components compose a helical field around nested flux surfaces. The tokamak is therefore a physical image of sym-ballein: independent directions remain distinguishable while their composition forms the containing field through which energy can circulate.

The analogy has limits. Real magnetic confinement leaks, destabilises, and requires active control; not every torus trajectory closes; toroidal geometry is not the only fusion architecture. Its argumentative force comes from the exact problem it solves: how a high-energy process can remain dynamically moving while being held away from a terminating boundary.

## Arche-topological consequence

The arche-topos should be drawn as circulation rather than static taxonomy. A determination moves around the explicit world-cycle, through the implicate return-cycle, and across their interference. Winding number becomes a model of retained history; the universal cover becomes the inexhaustible field; the surface point becomes objective-internal determination; the hole becomes the unfilled subject-condition that permits circulation.

Music supplies the temporal partner. A toroidal path returns by winding; a harmonic phrase returns by cadence and octave. In both, the route matters. The QL plate should let the reader see and hear the retained traversal.
