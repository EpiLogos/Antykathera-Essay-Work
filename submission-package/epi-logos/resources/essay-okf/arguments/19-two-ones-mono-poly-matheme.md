# The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme

## Claim

`0/1` is the primordial QL relation from which mono–poly becomes legible. `0` names the uncounted condition of determination; `1` names a determinate mark which appears only through relations it does not own. In this operation, `0` bears Mono/One and `1` bears Poly/All. **Zero is One, and that One is All; Zero is All, and All are One** compresses that operation after it has been stated.

## Formal floor

[§1 · #2](../sections/15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one.md) carries the formal neighbour: whole-number counting begins at `0`, positive natural-number counting begins at `1`, and the von Neumann construction gives `1 = {0}` and `2 = {0,1}`. This mathematical floor does not identify the QL meta-sign `0` with the empty set. It establishes the limited formal neighbourhood in which a first mark contains the zero it cannot generate.

`0/1` and `1/0` are the two orientations of one relation. [Sym-Ballein](03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein.md) derives dia-ballein’s signed accounting, `(+1)/(−1)`, and sym-ballein’s seam-retaining accounting, `(0/1)/(1/0)`. Mono–poly names the whole/many relation to which these distinct operations answer; it does not replace either operation.

## Consequence

[§2 · #5→0](../sections/24-s2-p5-zero-changes-role.md) derives the hinge `2 + 2² = 4+2`; [§3 · #0](../sections/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md) carries the relation through `X/x`, `∞/dx`, and `1/0` return. `X/x` is authorial QL notation: `X` is determining capacity and `x` the indefinite particular. Jungian individuation is a later psychic refraction, not the notation’s owner.

## Boundaries

[Mono-Poly: Whole and Many](11-mono-poly-trust.md) carries the social, institutional, and planetary consequence. [Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit](18-trust-faith-formal-limit.md) carries the lived relation revealed where knowledge meets its own ground. The two meet but cannot be fused. [Sym-Ballein](03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein.md) derives the two operations; it does not replace the ontological kernel. [Prakāśa-Vimarśa](09-prakasa-vimarsa.md), [Vāk](10-vak.md), and [Bohmian Enfoldment and Dialogical Return](16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return.md) each give a distinct refractive operation with its own source boundary; *pros hen* and Eckhart’s *in quantum* govern proportional and aspectual predication rather than deriving the matheme.

## Test

Ask whether a local `1` has been allowed to appear as an articulation of the field, or has installed itself as the source of the field. The latter movement is monopoly in institutional life, closure in knowledge, and false self-grounding in the matheme.
