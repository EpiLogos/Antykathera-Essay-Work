# Prakāśa-Vimarśa

## Claim

Prakāśa names luminous appearing; Vimarśa names the reflexive power through which appearing is present to itself and can articulate a world. The pair gives the metaphysical reading of the `0/1` movement without positing two substances: the light and its self-apprehending differentiation are one complexio oppositorum. [The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme](19-two-ones-mono-poly-matheme.md) clarifies the placement: Śiva–Śakti refracts the Mono/One–Poly/All relation at manifestation’s first self-articulation; it does not derive the QL matheme.

## Cross-register warrant

At the arche-topological register this is the first bend of the field toward position. At the tattvic register it unfolds as the descent from undivided luminous freedom into the objective-internal apparatus of a bounded knower. At the epistemic register it appears as the slash through which a mark is selected and recognised.

The **Trika** — the grounding three — names Prakāśa, Vimarśa, and their inseparable unity (*Ahaṃ*): light, the turning-back of light upon itself, and the recognition that consciousness is inherently self-reflective. In the matheme this is the mediator term `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the freedom (*svātantrya*) to hold both readings, on which both directed tracks depend. The same doctrine supplies the essay's subject/object vocabulary: the **kartṛ-pole** is the un-objectifiable doer/experient that any signifying activity presupposes, and **kārya** is the articulated, perishable field its activity deposits — knower and made, held through the means that relates them.

The AI application is bounded: [computational vimarśa](06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md) names functional recursion over articulated products. Prakāśa and phenomenal awareness remain ontological and experiential questions requiring different warrants.
