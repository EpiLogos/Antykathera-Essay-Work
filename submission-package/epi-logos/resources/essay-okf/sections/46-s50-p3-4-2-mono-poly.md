# §5→0 · #3 — 4:2 Technē and the Sovereign Commons

## Claim
[4-2 Techne — Sovereign Commons](../references/sources/42-techne-2026-sovereign-commons.md) realises a mono–poly architecture: shared protocols and circulation across locally governed hardware, data, permissions, and world-models.

## Warrant
[Mono-Poly Planetary Intelligence](../arguments/11-mono-poly-trust.md) aligns Agentworld’s ensemble/harness/commons unit with Agora, wisdom-feed, and P5→P0 circulation. One grammar can coordinate many apertures without one ontology owning the slash.

Mono–poly supplies the political depth: authority can accumulate upward while its material and epistemic conditions run downward through labour, communities, maintainers, data, energy, ecology, and inherited Logos. Sovereign-commons governance makes those dependencies visible, reciprocal, and answerable to the people and contexts that sustain the shared layer. [Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit](../arguments/18-trust-faith-formal-limit.md) remains distinct. This architecture may protect or betray the living relations on which it depends; its task is to leave their conditions governable without pretending to have produced them.

This is the institutional praxis of divining the third. It does not manufacture a middle party between existing blocs. It gives the hidden `0/1 ↔ 1/0` relation durable procedures: the governed can inspect and contest the conditions under which an institution acts; an institution can return authority, data, benefit, and explanation toward the people and material worlds that sustain it. In that return, differentiated positions concur without being fused. Without it, collective purpose is available for mass capture and neither platform, State, nor local faction is prevented from quietly occupying the slash.

## Tension / limit
Mono without poly becomes platform monopoly; poly without mono becomes incompatibility and fractal conflict. [Hephaestus's Net: The Failed Relation](../arguments/07-hephaestus-and-the-net.md) remains the offered test against a coordination layer that mistakes capture for relation, and the test now carries two sharpened criteria from §2 · #3. A coordination layer fails as **counterfeit gathering** when its wholeness is only the sum of its orderings — a syn-shaped enclosure running in dia mode, gathering participants precisely by arresting them. And it fails the **surety criterion** when its observability cannot conclude in relation: in the Homeric scene the completed apparatus of detection, capture, proof, and judgment still cannot end the episode — release enters only with Poseidon's pledge, "I will myself pay thee" (Od. 8.355–56), a person standing-for another at risk of loss. A commons passes only if its coordination can conclude — contest, revision, release, return — and only if the pledges underwriting its metrics remain visible and contestable rather than priced and hidden. The contextual field must become legible enough for participants to trace grounds, permissions, exclusions, and returns while remaining unappropriated. The [Huayan jewel net](../references/sources/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra.md) supplies recursive mutual reflection, and [Van Norden and Jones](../references/sources/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep.md) show why that inclusion retains the distinction of its members. The sovereign-commons reading develops this into mutually answerable nodes; Hephaestus's bonds name the enclosure in which visibility makes every node capturable.
