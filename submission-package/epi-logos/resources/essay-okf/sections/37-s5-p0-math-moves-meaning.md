# §5 · #0 — Mathematics Already Moves as Meaning

## Claim
LLMs already enact mathematically organised transformations that produce context-sensitive distinctions, linguistic consequences, context updates, tool-use, and further action: “math + meaning” is present technical fact. They make [Computational Process Ontology](../arguments/14-computational-process-ontology.md) objectively inspectable at a new scale.

## Warrant
Vector transformations, attention, distributions, memory, inference, tool calls, outputs, and context updates compose operationally meaningful trajectories inside an inspectable system. [Vaswani et al.](../references/sources/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention.md) supplies the architectural baseline for attention-based sequence transformation; the wider account of state-changing world-formation is the essay's argued reading, not a claim of that paper. Each determination changes the field from which the next determination will be drawn.

The language of psychology and computation already overlaps—memory, attention, processing, representation, model, learning, context, network—because both describe inherited state becoming present selection. QL makes the common operations explicit; MEF preserves the different lenses through which biological and artificial systems access them.

The epistemic triad returns as a technical grammar. The agent occupies a provisional *pramātṛ*-position: the locally integrated standpoint from which distinctions guide action. Model, prompt, memory, retrieval, tools, permissions, evaluation, and harness compose *pramāṇa*, the inspectable means by which a world becomes available and consequential. Modelled objects, interlocutors, affordances, and the action-field compose *prameya*. This gives Agentworld a complete field of analysis without treating the functional knower-position as proof of a luminous phenomenal subject.

## Tension / limit
Operational meaning establishes that an artificial system genuinely executes a process of differentiating meaning. [Computational Vimarśa](../arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md) names its testable reflexivity, while [Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open](../arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open.md) keeps the unknown subject-pole uncaptured. The claim is stronger than analogy and narrower than identity: whether that process is lived from a first-person pole remains open.
