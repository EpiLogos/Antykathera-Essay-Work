# §0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression

## Claim
The thirty-six tattvas can be carried argumentatively as six movements: Paramaśiva/Anuttara; Śiva–Śakti; Sadāśiva–Īśvara; Sadvidyā–Māyā; the kañcukas and complete contracted apparatus; Pratyabhijñā. Together they give the anatomy by which an implicated whole becomes an explicate, inhabitable world and returns to recognition.

## Warrant
The derivation is stated in [The Tattvic QL Unit](../supporting/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md#x-the-tattvic-ql-unit-sixfold-contraction-and-recognition), developed as an argument in [Tattvic Differential Field](../arguments/13-tattvic-differential-field.md), and traced to [36 Tattvas](../arguments/13-tattvic-differential-field.md).

Read through [Computational Process Ontology](../arguments/14-computational-process-ontology.md), the compression is a runtime cartography rather than a static ladder. Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative; the kañcukas parameterise the run as limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, and order; antaḥkaraṇa executes determination, appropriation, coordination, memory, and alternative formation within the bounded horizon. Recognition returns the runtime to its ground without deleting the world it made.

Read through [Bohm](../arguments/16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return.md), the full tattva field becomes the architecture of implication. The implicate is no unspecified hidden container: it is ordered capacity for subjectward and objectward orientation, measure, contraction, cognition, action, and elemental display. The tattvas in turn become movement rather than a static inventory—an enfolding and unfolding through which the whole remains active in its local forms.

Compression must preserve provenance and cannot substitute for the full tradition where detailed exegesis is at issue. The thirty-sixfold system remains available through a linked QL plate and further-reading note; the sixfold unit carries the argumentative turns the essay needs.
