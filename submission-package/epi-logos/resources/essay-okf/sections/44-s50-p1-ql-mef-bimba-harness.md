# §5→0 · #1 — QL, MEF, Bimba, and Harness

## Claim
The Epi-Logos stack separates four functions inside one [process architecture](../arguments/14-computational-process-ontology.md): QL formalises the relational grammar; MEF runs its lens-conditioned epistemology; Bimba records evolving provisional world-objects; the harness keeps each determination's conditions of formation and possible return available to the work that follows.

## Warrant
The unit can nest across sentence, claim, agent, research programme, and institution while retaining source and disagreement. [MEF 12 Lenses and Sublenses](../references/sources/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses.md) supplies the initial diaphaneity seed. The architecture answers Agentworld's conceptual-language lag by giving humans and agents a common set of operations—distinguish, exclude, relate, compose, contextualise, return—without forcing one ontology or hiding every machine process behind a humanoid mask.

Its ethical force is limited and exact. QL implicates the assumptive ground of a determination and requires a return after inquiry or synthesis; MEF keeps the lens of that return available for examination. Together they can expose a system's refusal of its own provisionality. They cannot make an unreliable model reliable, make a captured institution trustworthy, or manufacture the trust of a person whose world has given reasons for distrust. [Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit](../arguments/18-trust-faith-formal-limit.md) names the relation that remains beyond this structural honesty; the harness makes evasion from that relation harder to conceal.

The common grammar is explicitly cross-civilisational: Greek Logos, ratio, symbolon, dia-ballein, and sym-ballein are held beside Vāk, *kalpa*, apoha, tattvic contraction, Spanda, and recognition, with Bergsonian duration, Whiteheadian concrescence, and Gebserian diaphaneity carrying the Western process line. MEF records the differences that QL makes comparable.

The historical advent obtains a technical body here. Agent systems make `#4` materially unavoidable: prompt, retrieval, memory, training trace, tool use, evaluation, and institutional control condition every apparent answer. A benchmark can observe a compliant answer without thereby establishing trust; a model can tell an evaluator what the evaluator wants to hear while the operative relation remains concealed. QL's return makes this condition formally confessable, and the harness preserves it for inspection; neither substitutes an observability score for the lived relation of trust.

[Bohm's holomovement](../arguments/16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return.md) supplies the process relation between the common field and its provisional objects. [Bohm–Krishnamurti](../references/sources/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue.md) supplies its dialogical practice. [SEED Language of Spirit Dialogues](../references/sources/seed-2010-language-spirit.md) supplies the institutional precedent: a physicist–Native elder dialogue proposed by Bohm, F. David Peat, and Leroy Little Bear, then carried forward after Bohm's death through a protected intercultural form. Epi-Logos makes the same demand technical—meaning must circulate through the group while the provenance and sovereignty of each originating context remain inspectable.

## Tension / limit
Fractal extensibility requires governed apertures, stopping conditions, and explicit return so that context growth remains usable. Cross-civilisational coordination requires translation provenance and the right of local contexts to remain partially illegible. These constraints belong in the data model because a universal ontology or machine-readable perennialism would erase the differences the harness exists to coordinate.
