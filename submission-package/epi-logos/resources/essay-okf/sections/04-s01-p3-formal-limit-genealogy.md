# §0/1 · #3 — The Formal-Limit Genealogy

## Claim
A sufficiently articulate system cannot convert every condition of its articulation into an ordinary internal term without remainder.

## Warrant
[Russell and Whitehead](../references/sources/russell-1908-theory-types.md) restrict paradoxical re-entry; [Gödel](../references/sources/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions.md) proves scoped internal limits; [Wittgenstein](../references/sources/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus.md) reaches silence and practice; [Whitehead](../references/sources/whitehead-1978-process-reality.md) makes non-closure creative; [Spencer-Brown](../references/sources/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form.md) and [Varela](../references/sources/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference.md) admit re-entry; [The Blind Spot — Frank, Gleiser, Thompson](../references/sources/frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot.md) restores the experiencer.

[Paradox](../arguments/15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge.md) shows how this limit moves across registers without becoming one homogenised theorem. [Cusa's](../arguments/15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge.md) polygon approaches the circle while retaining the difference between finite measure and infinite form; [Flasch's account of Eckhart](../references/sources/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart.md) follows a language that affirms, qualifies, and returns each predicate to what exceeds it; poetry keeps the opening alive where a terminating definition would close. The anonymous medieval infinite-sphere image is used with its attribution intact: Cusa receives and transforms it rather than originating it.

## Tension / limit
Gödel performs a formal proof; Cusa and Eckhart perform mathematical-apophatic and linguistic disciplines; Whitehead supplies process metaphysics, and Bohm's process bridge enters only at #5→0. Their distinct objects and methods prevent their convergence from being mistaken for one theorem and give the wider architecture its strength.

## Anchor and transition
**Mathematical anchor:** undecidability under stated formal conditions. **Images:** the ladder, the re-entering mark, the polygon approaching the circle, the sphere with centre everywhere and circumference nowhere. The positive response is [§0/1 · #4 — Gebserian Diaphaneity](05-s01-p4-gebser-diaphaneity.md).
