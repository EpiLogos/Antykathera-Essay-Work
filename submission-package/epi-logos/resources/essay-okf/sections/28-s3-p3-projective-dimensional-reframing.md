# §3 · #3 — Projective Completion and Dimensional Reframing

## Movement thesis

Mathematics repeatedly resolves obstruction by changing the space in which the relation is represented. The complex plane gives orthogonal room to the square root of \(-1\). [The projective line](../concepts/dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity.md) adjoins ideal points so parallel directions can meet. [The Riemann sphere](../concepts/dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity.md) compactifies the complex plane so infinity becomes a determinate point of the completed surface rather than an indefinitely large number.

These are distinct constructions with one shared methodological force: when a relation cannot be represented coherently in the present frame, disclose or transform the frame.

## Projective and quantum payload

In its complex bilinear/Möbius setting, cross-ratio preserves relational proportion:

$$
(a,b;c,d)=\frac{(c-a)(d-b)}{(c-b)(d-a)}.
$$

The invariant belongs to four points in relation, not to an isolated coordinate. This makes it a mathematical witness for QL’s insistence that position gains meaning through the full field of transformations.

The complex projective line \(\mathbb{CP}^{1}\) is equivalent to the Riemann sphere. Pure qubit states modulo global phase also form \(\mathbb{CP}^{1}\), represented as the Bloch sphere. The poles \(0\) and \(1\) and their superpositions therefore possess an exact geometrical state-space. QL’s interpretation of those poles as ground and mark is additional, but the superpositional geometry is not metaphor.

## Logical plurality and comparison boundary

Four-valued and paraconsistent logics formally accommodate true, false, both, and neither without explosion. They are mathematical neighbours of the tetralemma because they refuse to force every contradiction back onto one exclusive line. They do not make Buddhist logic, FDE, quantum states, and QL identical. They establish that logical space can be enlarged so a formerly impossible relation becomes tractable.

Priest's reconstruction of the catuṣkoṭi carries this neighbourhood to derivational interest. FDE's four values are the *subsets of {T, F}* — {T}, {F}, {T,F}, { } — four relational positions generated combinatorially from the presence and absence of two marks, with negation exchanging t and f while fixing b and n, conjunction as greatest lower bound and disjunction as least upper bound on the diamond lattice ("The Logic of the Catuskoti," pp. 24–33). Four positions from two marks is the binary-of-the-binary count, `2+2²`, in an independent formal dress. His chapter "Quintum Non Datur" names the classical prohibition his book then breaks: when Nāgārjuna rejects all four corners (MMK XXII:11, XXV:17), a fifth status arrives — "they have some other status, namely, (e): none of the above" (pp. 36–37) — a value *outside the lattice*, infectious (e-in/e-out: whatever contains an ineffable component is itself ineffable), and, at the ultimate level, exhaustive: "nothing of the form Tu⟨A⟩, Fu⟨A⟩, or Bu⟨A⟩ can hold; only… Eu⟨A⟩" (p. 47) — the conventional exhausts the four corners while the ultimate lives at *e*. The fifth corner is a formal arrival at an implicate ground; its instructive limit is that *e* is inert — nothing in the value-theory returns from it. What QL writes as `5→0`, Priest's material carries displaced into the tradition itself: Jizang's iterating hierarchy in which each level's conventional/ultimate pair becomes the next level's conventional, Vimalakīrti's performative silence, Dōgen's return to the ordinary. Where the value-theory falls silent, the tradition practises — the formal ground for the essay's claim that this logic is held in the East as lived technē, since a completion that is an operation rather than a proposition can only be kept as practice.

The comparison is gated. Kapsner's "Cutting Corners" rejects precisely the fifth *value* — an objection QL does not inherit, because its ground-position `#0` is a generative precondition, not a truth-value. Siderits reads the rejected tetralemma through presupposition failure with no non-classical machinery; Westerhoff presses parsimony and non-uniformity of the device across contexts; Priest marks his own book as reconstruction, not history. The formal structure is cited as **Derived from source**; the reading of the four corners as an unrecognised internal `#1–#4` and of the fifth as ground-analogue is **Argued** and stated as the essay's own.

[Division pluralisms](../concepts/dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity.md) make the same point algebraically. Undefinedness, projective infinity, signed infinities, and totalised operations preserve different invariants. The answer to \(1/0\) is a disclosure of the chosen world.

## Argumentative consequence

Zero and infinity are the pressure-points at which dimensionality becomes visible. Their obstructions force the line to become plane, sphere, projective space, quotient, cover, or extended algebra, revealing more than two extreme coordinates on a single axis. [Dimensional Reframing at Zero and Infinity](../concepts/dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity.md) keeps the cases distinct while establishing this common move.
