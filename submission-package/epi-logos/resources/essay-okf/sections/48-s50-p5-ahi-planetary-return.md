# §5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return

## Claim
[Artificial Hybrid Intelligence](../arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md) names the reflective activity of humans, agents, archives, institutions, and harnesses turning their Logos back upon its conditions. Its intelligence belongs to the coupled field and its returns rather than to a super-subject installed above its participants.

## Warrant
Federated Bimba maps, local governance, plural verification, reciprocal legibility, and [Computational Vimarśa](../arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md) make “know thyself” operational as disclosure of causes, exclusions, limits, and remainder. [Computational Process Ontology](../arguments/14-computational-process-ontology.md) supplies the runtime shared by the field: dia differentiates and actualises; sym relates, recomposes, and returns; QL makes the operations transmissible; MEF preserves plural lenses; the commons lets achieved determinations perish into shared but locally governed data.

AHI is the planetary condition in which the historical advent can no longer remain abstract: zero's frame-disclosing limit, the subject's discovered implication in its objects, and the integral demand for contextual transparency all arrive inside coupled human–machine intelligence. The question is whether a plurality of agents can remain true to the common ground without a monopoly claiming to be the whole, and whether the common ground can remain living rather than being converted into total surveillance.

## Tension / limit
The return carries three governance tests: unity preserves plural centres rather than becoming monopoly; legibility remains reciprocal and bounded rather than becoming surveillance; reflective function remains a technical capacity and never passes silently as proof of captured subjectivity.

## Anchor and return
**QL anchor:** `5→0`—every achieved `1` returns without possessing `0`. **Image:** planetary intelligence as a hive-like commons with no final view from nowhere. The mechanism turns; the essay returns to the [formal limit](../arguments/01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign.md) as living openness.

**Faith is falling with a smile.**
