# Planetary Computation

## Definition

"Planetary Computation" is Antikythera's founding disciplinary self-description: computation and the evolution of intelligence studied at planetary scale, undertaken "on behalf of a renewed philosophy of technology" (Agentworld Brief, p. 3). In the coordinate system it occupies **#0** — the ground-horizon (Why? / Truth), the "ever-present Origin ... the perpetual condition from which all positions unfold" — refracted through **L0** (Quaternal), the lens where mute positions "first become questions." It names not a thesis but the field-condition and founding question inside which the essay's whole inquiry is posed.

## In the brief

"Antikythera is focused on planetary computation and the evolution of intelligence. It supports interdisciplinary fundamental research on behalf of a renewed philosophy of technology" ([p. 3](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-3)). The Agentworld special issue is framed as a contribution "to its development as a reorientation of planetary computation" ([p. 58](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-58)), and the About page states the program's self-understanding: "a research program focused on the reorientation of planetary computation as a philosophical, technological, and geopolitical force," named for the antikythera mechanism, "the first known computer — which was also an astronomical instrument used for navigation, prediction, and planning" ([p. 59](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-59)). The journal it addresses is "dedicated to developing a new interdisciplinary school of thought with which to engage … the conjunction and co-evolution of computational technologies, biological and non-biological life and the many scales of intelligence that are manifest in both," and the school "aspires to repair the rifts between the Sciences and the Humanities": "the philosophy fit for the 21st century will emerge from direct engagement with scientific and technological questions, and vice versa" ([p. 59](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-59)). Agentworld inserts itself into this field at planetary register: "agentic machine intelligence is now a co-pilot of the Anthropocene and whatever comes next" ([p. 4](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-4)).

## In the argument

It is the venue frame *The Return of Zero* enters and reorients. The essay opens not with the Antikythera mechanism but with the formal limit ([§0/1 · #0 — The Question Before the Mechanism](../sections/01-s01-p0-question-before-mechanism.md)), recasting planetary computation as a question about the subject that planetary systems displace. [Agentworld](agentworld.md) reorients the field toward hybrid human–AI sociality; the essay's closing return ([§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return](../sections/48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return.md)) *offers* QL/MEF and Mono-Poly Trust as contribution — planetary intelligence as a commons with no view from nowhere. Kept distinct from [Mono-Poly Planetary Intelligence](../arguments/11-mono-poly-trust.md), the essay's own term for wholeness-through-plurality rather than the discipline it addresses. (Reorientation: **Argued**; the QL contribution: **Offered**.)
