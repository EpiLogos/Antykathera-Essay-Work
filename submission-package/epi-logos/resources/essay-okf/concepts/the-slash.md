# The Slash (AND/OR)

## Definition

The stroke between numerator and denominator, **working double** (*Argued*): arithmetically division / fraction / ratio; logically **AND/OR** — a conjunction that does not erase disjunction, so `0/1` conjoins ground and mark yet keeps them distinct. Placed here (*Argued*) at the **P2 dynamis** — canon's `−/+` differentiating operator (How? / Word, Square C) — because the slash is the operator *par excellence*, the stroke common to all six determinations (`0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx`); refracted through **L0.5** (*pros hen* / analogia), so holding both readings is analogia, not identity. The Śaiva gloss (*Offered*): it is the **pramāṇa**, the means, between numerator (*pramātṛ*, knower) and denominator (*prameya*, known). No node was dedicated to it because every surface treats it as a component of [the matheme](../arguments/12-core-theorem-bridge.md); this names the component.

## In the argument

The slash is the operative core of [Sym-Ballein](../arguments/03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein.md): in `(0/1)/(1/0)` each inner stroke holds indefinite and definite together, the outer relates their conjugate orientations — [§2 · #2 — Sym-Ballein](../sections/21-s2-p2-sym-ballein.md). Because the mark *retains its crossing*, the return to zero is transformation, not annihilation — [§3 · #2 — Re-entry](../sections/27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex.md). The same stroke is the quotient folding `ℝ²` into the torus — [§3 · #5→0 — Arche-Topos](../sections/30-s3-p5-arche-topos.md) (*Argued*). Its **structured irrationality** yokes `0/1` (defined) to `1/0` (division by zero, undefined) across one stroke — against the cancelling line of [Dia-ballein](../arguments/03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein.md), `(+1)/(−1)`, whose signed poles erase their ground.

The slash also has a lived, comportmental form (*Argued*): Heidegger's *Gelassenheit* is the AND/OR practised toward one's own instruments — "We can affirm the unavoidable use of technical devices, and also deny them the right to dominate us," a comportment "which expresses 'yes' and at the same time 'no'" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 54). Simultaneous affirmation and denial without contradiction, because the two operate at different levels of the relation: use conjoined, domination disjoined. Releasement is the slash worn as a stance — which is how [§5→0 · #2](../sections/45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement.md) carries it into the politics of technical life.
