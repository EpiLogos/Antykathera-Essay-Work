# Agentworld

## Definition

Agentworld is two things at once. As **venue**, it is the special issue of the *Antikythera: Journal for the Philosophy of Planetary Computation* (MIT Press) framed by Benjamin Bratton's brief — "a preemptive anthropology of open-world centaur societies": a horizon of provisional agents, [parasocial](parasociety.md) doubles, plural world-models, and hybrid coordination. As **coordinate**, the essay reads it at **#4 Context** (Square B; lens pair L4 Phenomenological ↔ L1' Phenomenal; element Fire; the lemniscate anchor) — the constructed context-world into which *The Return of Zero* is thrown (Heidegger's *Geworfenheit* at venue scale) and against which every outer station must justify itself.

## In the brief

The brief defines Agentworld as "characterized by densely reticulated social graphs emerging from the hyperscaled webs of interactions between diverse combinations of minds, some more human than others, but all capable of significant communication," in which "agent-to-agent communication will constitute the vast majority of socially significant interactions" and "first, agents model humans, and then become the model against which humans model themselves" ([p. 4](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-4)). It is "a parallel ecology, sometimes deeply interwoven with human societies and other times seemingly autonomous from them," whose agents appear as "human-masked ghosts from the future"; "the two societies are really one, but they are not identical or reducible to one another," and "each sub-society is an evolutionary niche of the other" ([pp. 4–5](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-4)). The governing scale question: "Consider a world populated by 8 billion human human-level minds and 800 billion or a trillion non-human human-level minds. With such ratios, what is a 'society'?" ([p. 4](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-4)); the governing mood: "P(WEIRD) = HIGH … P(DOOM) = LOW" ([p. 8](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-8)).

The research posture is explicit: the focus is "not foremost tools for agentic task completion but rather the human perplexity of deep agentic diffusion," whose constraints are "not utility functions to be solved but qualitative and open-ended questions: psychological, philosophical, linguistic, symbolic, existential" ([p. 3](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-3)). Preemptive anthropology "inverts" the retrospective vector of the discipline: "not prediction, but rather the active composition of categories through which contact will be navigated," a "deliberate fabrication of a vocabulary, a set of scenarios, and a conceptual armature" for a society that "will remain illegible to its participants if the language for it is not built before the fact" ([p. 6](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-6)). The ten thematic sections "each draw a composite scenario, not a conclusion," under a deliberate modal rider: "That world may never arrive, and it may also be here already — and in some odd way, it may be both" ([p. 53](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-53)). Venue facts: the special issue launches Fall 2026 with the peer-reviewed MIT Press journal; the review window opens June 25, 2026; the journal is "web-first and design-forward," welcoming visual, interactive, or project-based components ([p. 58](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-58)).

## In the argument

Agentworld fixes the address, not a topic inside the essay: QL/MEF is offered not as a solution to "AI" but as a shared grammar of distinction and return letting [centaur](centaur-societies.md) societies coordinate without ontology collapse or fractal conflict. The [Agentworld Response Matrix](../maps/agentworld-response-matrix.md) keeps each station (§0/1 → §5→0) answerable to a specific brief pressure. Stations that turn on it: [Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open](../arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open.md), [Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field](../arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md), Mono-Poly Trust.
