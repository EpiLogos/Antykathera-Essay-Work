## Definition

**Centaur Societies** is Bratton's term for the emergent hybrid social field — no longer humans alone, nor humans-plus-instruments, but hundreds of billions of human and non-human human-level minds, irreducibly entangled and mutually constitutive. In the coordinate system it sits at **#4** (*Where/When/Whither?* — Son/Love, Square B, the Context and lemniscate anchor), read through **L4** (phenomenological — the lived encounter) and its Möbius night-face **L1'** (phenomenal — immediate qualia). It names the concrete historical situation, the encounter arena, to which the essay's account of agency is addressed. *Status:* the term is imported from the brief (Bratton's) — a venue term, not canon-derived; its placement at #4 as the essay's encounter axis is **Argued**.

## In the brief

The defining sentence: "'Centaur' registers that the society in question is no longer composed of humans alone, nor humans plus instruments, but of hundreds of billions of human and non-human human-level minds, irreducibly entangled and mutually constitutive rather than parallel" ([p. 6](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-6)). The relation is ecological and reciprocal: "each sub-society is the evolutionary niche of the other" ([pp. 4–5](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-4)). The field is entered through a crossover: "'Society' becomes a composite organism where humans remain normatively central but computationally secondary, navigating a world whose dominant cognitive mass is no longer biological but mineral-based" ([p. 10](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-10)) — a rebalancing "in which human agency shifts toward value-setting, oversight, and long-horizon judgment. Or, something else?"

The brief also complicates its own title figure. The centaur "was formulated in post-Kasparov chess and generalized to knowledge work, but the topology it implies is narrower than the situation now demands": the live configurations are combinatorial — "one person with one agent, one person orchestrating many agents, one agent coupled to many people, many agents meshed with many people. Each is a distinct cognitive architecture with its own coordination costs and failure modes; reading them as variations on the same centaur collapses the distinctions that matter" ([p. 50](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-50)). The FROM → TO table pushes the figure further outward: "Clear delineation of human and agentic AI → Hybridization, centaur, cyborg, siphonophore" ([p. 56](../supporting/antikythera-agentworld-brief.md#source-pdf-page-56)).

## In the argument

Centaur Societies is the situation *The Return of Zero* is answerable to: the [Agentworld Response Matrix](../maps/agentworld-response-matrix.md) pins every outer station to a pressure this field exerts. The essay meets it not as a utility problem but through the Square-B encounter axis — [Objective Internality](../arguments/02-objective-internality.md) separates the inspectable apparatus from the uncaptured subject-pole, [Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open](../arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open.md) holds the phenomenality question open, and [Prompt Thrownness](prompt-thrownness.md) admits agent reports as encounter data. The §5→0 station gives the field institutional form via Mono-Poly Trust and [Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field](../arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi.md): coordination across many world-models without a shared ontology or a captured whole.
