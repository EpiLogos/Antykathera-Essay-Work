# Zero

## Definition

Zero is the sign for no-counted-thing. It is not one timeless object but the convergence of several inventions — blank place, placeholder, positional numeral, arithmetic element, coordinate origin, empty set (∅), and philosophical void — which gain power by *migrating* between scripts, calculating practices, and civilisations. In the coordinate canon it occupies **P0** (Why? / Ground / Square A / Aether): the archetype of zero-ness, the pregnant void, the implicate condition prior to differentiation. Its operative notation is the ground–mark relation **0/1**. *(The intercultural migration is Derived, historical; the reading of it as a return of the formal limit is Argued.)*

## In the argument

The figure is titular to *The Return of Zero*. [§1 · #0](../sections/13-s1-p0-sign-migrates.md) separates its meanings before relating them; [§1 · #1](../sections/14-s1-p1-sunya-operational.md) makes śūnya calculable; [§1 · #2](../sections/15-s1-p2-empty-set-generates-one.md) has ∅ generate one; [§1 · #3](../sections/16-s1-p3-crossed-zero.md) compacts the paradox as the crossed zero; [§1 · #4](../sections/17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math.md) keeps zero's "one foot outside mathematics" at division-by-zero. [§1 · #5→0](../sections/18-s1-p5-loan-returns.md) returns the loan: zero becomes mathematically productive without becoming an ordinary positive object, earning `0/1 ↔ 1/0`. It thereby functions as the [meta-sign](../arguments/01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign.md) for the non-objectifiable pole every determination presupposes.

## Layer rule

Historical and mathematical zero—placeholder, numeral, operator, origin, and `∅`—must remain distinct from QL `0`, the meta-sign of the uncounted Mono/One. [The Two Ones — Mono–Poly Matheme](../arguments/19-two-ones-mono-poly-matheme.md) relates the layers without collapsing them: set theory gives `1 = {0}` and `2 = {0,1}` as an exact numerical floor; it does not exhaust the ground–mark relation.
