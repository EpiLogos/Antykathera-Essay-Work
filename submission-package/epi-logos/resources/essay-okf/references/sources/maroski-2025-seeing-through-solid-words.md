## Essay use

Maroski provides a scholarly secondary carrier for a narrow account of Gebserian transparency as form rather than content, and for the non-successive relation among consciousness structures. It supports §0/1 #4's methodological transition and qualifies any fusionist reading.

**Claim boundary:** it does not establish QL/MEF, a view from nowhere, or an identity of mathematics, phenomenology, psychology, myth, and design. Direct quotation from Gebser awaits a licensed primary edition.
