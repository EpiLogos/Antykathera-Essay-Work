# Taylor — Mono-Poly, the Two Ones, and the Whole Field (2026)

## Provenance identity

Internal authorial synthesis recovering the `0/1` root of mono–poly: the two ones, the natural/whole-number threshold, the relation of Mono/One to Poly/All, the two orientations `0/1 ↔ 1/0`, and their refraction through the eight determinations, god-image, political organisation, trust, myth, and hybrid intelligence.

The document is a reusable internal derivation surface. It does not convert its external references into public warrant. The core QL derivation remains governed by [Taylor — Core Theorems Pithy (2026)](taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md). Historical mathematics, myth, Śaiva doctrine, political theory, and Agentworld claims retain their own source houses.

## Essay use

Use the source to prevent four recurrent errors: treating mono–poly as a synonym for trust; treating dia/sym as the original relation rather than two accountings after `0/1`; reducing the two ones to `±1`; and treating `X/x` or calculus as detachable analogies rather than determinations of the same authorial field.

The source supplies the derivational basis for [Mono-Poly: Whole and Many](../../arguments/11-mono-poly-trust.md). The declared consumer route is §1 → §2 → §3 → §4 → §5 → §5→0; individual section insertions remain governed by their own canonical movement nodes and claim-status boundaries.
