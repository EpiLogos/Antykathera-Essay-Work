# Taylor — The Advent of Zero, Subject, and Integral Logic (2026)

## Provenance identity

Internal authorial historical synthesis of zero's operational migration, the modern subject's perspectival formation, Freud's disciplinary minting of the unconscious, Jung's psychic-fact register, Neumann's post-Jungian consciousness history, Gebser's mental-rational and integral mutations, Agentworld's technical threshold, and QL as the explicit return of `0/1` through dia-ballein and sym-ballein.

The manuscript carries the essay's historical argument. It does not turn Kaplan, Brahmagupta, Bhāskara, Freud, Jung, Neumann, or Gebser into sources of QL's native derivation. Historical mathematics, psychology, and consciousness studies retain their own evidence surfaces and source boundaries.

## Essay use

Use this source when a passage needs the full historical movement rather than one local resonance: zero entering rational calculation; the subject entering modern philosophical and scientific representation; the unconscious entering psychoanalytic science; the mental-rational conversion of an internal `0` into external `+1/−1`; the integral return in which the context of perspective becomes explicit; and the arrival of QL as a shared logic for human–machine intelligence.

It safeguards three distinctions: `X/x` is authorial QL notation before its psychic refraction; integral consciousness is a higher historical mutation, not a mere accumulation of perspectives; and `0/1` precedes the two logics while becoming historically explicit through their relation.
