## Essay use

The selected public-domain translation provides Nietzsche’s own aesthetic formulation of Apollonian/Dionysian duplexity and their reciprocal relation in tragedy.

**Claim boundary:** it does not establish Gebserian diaphaneity, QL, MEF, a general metaphysics of order and chaos, or a historical account of Greek religion independent of Nietzsche’s aesthetic construction.

## Passage card — `nietzsche-1910-birth-tragedy-haussmann-q001` — Aesthetic duplexity

## Passage card — `nietzsche-1910-birth-tragedy-haussmann-q002` — Reciprocal union in tragedy
