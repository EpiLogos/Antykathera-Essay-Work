## Essay use

Specialist source for the medieval European reception and demonisation of unfamiliar numeral forms. It can correct simplistic zero-history narratives and support the Salem/cipher episode after the exact manuscript/image trail is established.

**Claim boundary:** this manuscript and cipher tradition warrant a local historical case. Any claim about “Europe's response to zero” as a whole requires a broader corpus across periods, regions, and practices.
