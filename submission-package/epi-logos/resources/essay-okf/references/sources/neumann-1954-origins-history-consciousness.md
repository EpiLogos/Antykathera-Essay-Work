## Essay use

Neumann supplies the post-Jungian historical morphology required by the zero–subject braid: mythic image, ego formation, and consciousness-development are treated as a historical psychic drama. Its contribution is a neighbouring history to Gebser's structures and to zero's mathematical itinerary; it does not identify their methods, prove a universal stadial scheme, or derive QL.
