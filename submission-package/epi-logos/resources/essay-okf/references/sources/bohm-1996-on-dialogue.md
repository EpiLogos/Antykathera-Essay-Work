## Essay use

Primary source for dialogue as meaning moving through participants, suspension of assumptions, tacit thought, common participation, and a shared field that does not require identical opinions. It supplies the human practice precedent for sym-ballein and Epi-Logos.

**Passages needed:** *dialogos* etymology; stream of meaning; suspension; tacit assumptions; collective thought and *koinonia*.

**Claim boundary:** Bohm's dialogue suspends assumptions so meaning can move through a group; consensus and guaranteed truth lie outside that definition. QL/MEF extends the practice architecturally and must be presented as the essay's system.
