## Essay use

Primary source for Russell's diagnosis of reflexiveness/self-reference in the contradictions and for ramified type theory's response. It warrants the historical and formal claim that logical levels are introduced to block vicious circularity. The essay's reading of this as a choice about re-entry is its own argument, not Russell's.

**Passages needed:** the common characteristic of the contradictions; the vicious-circle principle; the relation between apparent variables, orders, and types.

**Claim boundary:** present Russell's technical problem and the structure of type theory before developing the philosophical contrast with Whitehead. The source warrants neither a psychologising claim that Russell was “afraid of paradox” nor the verdict that type theory is failed repression.
