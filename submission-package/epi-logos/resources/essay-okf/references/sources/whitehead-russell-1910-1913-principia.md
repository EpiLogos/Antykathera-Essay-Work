## Essay use

The work establishes the actual closure project to which Russell's type theory and Gödel's title belong. It also makes the Russell–Whitehead narrative historically concrete: both participated in the logical enterprise before Whitehead's later process metaphysics.

**Passages needed:** only if the essay discusses a specific proposition, type-theoretic rule, or the work's stated aim. The general narrative may be supported by the work plus an intellectual history.

**Claim boundary:** *Principia* warrants claims about the joint logical project. Whitehead's later philosophical divergence requires his later works and responsible biographical or historical support.
