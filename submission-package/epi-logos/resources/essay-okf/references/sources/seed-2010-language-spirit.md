## Essay use

Institutional source for the history and form of the Language of Spirit dialogues: Leroy Little Bear and F. David Peat's 1992 approach to Bohm, SEED's later foundation, and the combination of Indigenous talking-circle and Bohmian dialogue practices.

**Claim boundary:** the institutional brochure warrants the programme's stated aims and documented existence. Claims about Indigenous participants' knowledge require participant-authored sources that preserve local sovereignty, names, and contexts; a generic “Indigenous worldview” is inadmissible.
