## Essay use

### Source's relevant contribution

- A historical witness to Jung and Pauli's attempts to think the relation of psyche, matter, archetype, and mathematical form without collapsing their disciplinary differences.

### Licensed essay use

- Contextualise the §4 psychoid problem and qualify the claim that number can be treated as merely subjective convention or merely physical fact.
- Preserve QL's arche-topos and numerical derivation as Taylor's argument; this correspondence neither derives nor verifies it.
