## Essay use

The closest existing psychological source for the chaos-dynamical reading of archetypes, complexes, bifurcation, feedback, boundaries, and individuation. It lets the two logics be related to symmetry breaking and higher-order composition within a patterned but non-repeating field.

**Claim boundary:** Van Eenwyk proposes the strange attractor as a model for archetypal dynamics. Mathematical identity would require a specified state space, dynamics, observables, and empirical test; keep analogy, model, and technical identity distinct while taking the model's explanatory force seriously.
