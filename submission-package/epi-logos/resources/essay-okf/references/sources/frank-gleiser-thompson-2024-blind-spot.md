## Essay use

Contemporary philosophy-of-science diagnosis of the substitution by which scientific abstractions are treated as more real than the experience that makes observation, measurement, and justification possible. It gives a field-facing formulation of the subject/experience problem and directly touches AI, mind, and planetary science.

**Passages needed:** the “surreptitious substitution”; experience as condition of science; AI/mind; Earth/planetary system; the authors' positive account of scientific knowledge.

**Claim boundary:** the authors warrant the blind-spot diagnostic within their own philosophical programme. `0` as experiential or subject pole, QL, Kashmir Śaivism, and idealism are the essay's argued developments and must be identified as such.
