# Traversal Ledger

A traversal ledger keeps the shape of one reader's encounter with a linked work. It belongs to the reader. It neither changes the published pages nor becomes an authority over them.

Use the ledger when a reader wants to retain, resume, share, or transform a reading. Do not create one merely because a link was opened.

## The movement vocabulary

- `enter` — begin from an asset, diagram, heading, block, question, or conversation.
- `follow` — take a link or declared continuation.
- `branch` — leave the current line while retaining the point of departure.
- `rejoin` — return from a branch to a retained line.
- `revisit` — encounter an earlier asset again without yet completing a return.
- `question` — place a live question into the traversal.
- `response` — retain a response that materially changes the reading.
- `annotate` — record a reader-owned perception, association, or difficulty.
- `return` — close a circuit by relating the present movement to an earlier event or asset and naming what has changed.
- `close` — rest or complete the traversal while retaining its remainder and possible continuations.

Linear reading is an ordered sequence of events. Non-linear reading adds branches and rejoins. A toroidal traversal includes a `return` whose changed understanding becomes a possible new `enter`.

## What each meaningful event retains

Record only what is needed to recover the movement:

- event number and kind;
- the asset entered or left;
- heading, block, diagram, or other selector where relevant;
- the prior event when this is a branch, rejoin, revisit, or return;
- why the movement was taken;
- what the reader carried into it;
- what changed or remained unresolved.

Use a stable asset ID and publication release when the vault provides them. Also keep the path and selector seen by the reader so the record remains intelligible if titles or slugs change.

## `0/1` and `5→0`

The binding states the reader's live pressure, the entry offered by the work, and the intended depth of the encounter. It can be a sentence.

The return is not a summary. It states the semantic difference produced by the traversal, what the reading still cannot close, and the next opening now available.

## Reader ownership

Obsidian Publish is ordinarily read-only. Keep a durable ledger in a reader's copied vault, a private companion session, or an exported Markdown note. Conversation and annotations are private unless the reader deliberately chooses another disclosure.

An agent may maintain the ledger during a conversation, but should show the reader the resulting path in readable prose and make the Markdown available for keeping. It must append a new event rather than silently rewriting the path already taken.

## Optional Epi-Card return

At a meaningful return, a frozen ledger snapshot may become an Epi-Card reader-encounter capsule. The card consumes the traversal; it does not replace the ledger. The reader may carry the achieved movement, unresolved remainder, and next ground into a later traversal.

Use `TRAVERSAL-TEMPLATE.md` as the copyable form.
