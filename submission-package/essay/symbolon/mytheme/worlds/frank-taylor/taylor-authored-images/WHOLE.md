# Taylor — Poetry and Authored Images

<a id="taylor-authored-images-whole"></a>
## #0 — A word that can return to its speaker

This record serves the essay's account of return as something a word or an image does to its own speaker, and it works in the mytheme register: Taylor's poems and authored images act as whole works, and what each discloses is read from what it does.

In one poem a game reaches checkmate and asks to be played again. In another a definition lets air through its panes, and in a third a speaker who has understood capture finds himself seeking its soothing once more. Each poem lives the relation in its sounds, intervals, pronouns and endings before any account collects it. The twenty-three poems stay individual works within this authored whole, and a shared philosophical field gives them relations without making them fragments of an unwritten master poem.

Taylor's [Personal Poetry Corpus](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#complete-poems) gathers ten undated earlier works, the six-work composition running P0 to P5, and seven dated journal works from March to June 2026. We keep the compilation's order, so the June sequence is not presented as coming before the March poems, and bracketed first-line titles mark works for which no authorial title was found. Each quotation keeps the compilation's wording and lineation, and later authorial corrections stand beside their own poems, so that text, revision instruction and commentary stay separate.

These works come from Taylor's own situated world. The language is English, the dated journal encounters and the June composition supply particular occasions of production, and no birthplace or geographical route follows from a file location. In [Neumann's shared archetypal ground](../../../archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-round), which grounds this comparison, genesis passes through world-parent relation, opposition, differentiation, separation and renewed interplay. That common morphology is distinct from the geography of particular tellings and from the order and duration of their transformations: it makes relations comparable while Taylor's dated works keep their own times, so they do not become stages of a universal cultural chronology. Neumann's psychological morphology and Taylor's native derivation carry different warrants.

A local image is read against a native field that is complete before the reading begins: `/ = −/−`, `0/1`, `?/!`, `−/+`, `X/x`, `AM/IS`, `∞/dx`, `1/0`. Taylor unfolds these determinations in his [core theorems](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), where each parent opens its full four-and-two articulation and return permits the inverse passage, so that the sequence is neither a miscellaneous list nor a one-way ladder. Being at #0 and #1, Becoming at #2 and #3, and Knowing/unKnowing at #4 and #5 give the sequential harmonics, and Essence at #0 with #5, Constitution at #1 with #4, and Text/Texture at #2 with #3 give a different and complementary reading. `X/x` is Taylor's notation, and neither Neumann nor a poem derives it after the fact. Their own changing forms give the sixfold its movement, and the twenty-three works are not distributed among six prefabricated meanings.

A bird, a vessel or a divine name takes its office from the words, the actions and the ending of the work in which it appears, and the same name in another work can enter another relation. Fixing one identity across the stories would erase those changes before anyone could meet their meaning.

## #1 — Play, knowing, and the humanity that makes its forms

Ten earlier works move among address, recognition, institutional desire and release, and length is part of what each does: the two-line fatigue of waking and the longer rain against the window of freedom work differently, and each begins again in its own voice.

<a id="taylor-ft-p01"></a>
### FT-P01 — Play and Play

> Somehow, being right here  
> I am out there, everywhere,  
> Nowhere really,  
> And elsewhere completely.  
> Coming and Going;  
> Checkmate.  
> Another game?  
> I might as well stay knowing  
> If I set you going  
> You'll come again.  
> Let us play and play.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q001) · Extracted authorial poem.

From being right here the voice goes out there, everywhere and nowhere, reaches checkmate, and then asks for another game. Staying in knowing and setting another going belong to one invitation, because the other, once set going, will come again. Checkmate ends a game and leaves the playing, so the ending is what allows the invitation to be made, and the last line's repetition, play and play, reopens what the apparent ending had closed. The address to *you* is part of that reopening.

<a id="taylor-ft-p02"></a>
### FT-P02 — Mystery Is Infinitely Deeper

> Mystery is infinitely deeper than the simply unknown or unknowable;  
> It is the relative knowability of the Unknown  
> And the ultimate unknowability of the Known;  
> The Known alone is the wrong focal point for Knowing, and it will always spit back at us the empty husk;  
> Knowing itself is the Mystery,  
> The conjugal Joy of the Known and the Knower, eternally trussed.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q002) · Extracted authorial poem.

First the Unknown receives a relative knowability and the Known loses final possession, since its ultimate unknowability is asserted in the same breath. When knowing fixes only on its object, the Known sends back an empty husk. The last two lines restore the act and its participants: Known and Knower are held in a conjugal relation whose joy belongs to Knowing. Mystery therefore deepens as that relation becomes articulate, and a longer inventory of unknown things would never reach it.

<a id="taylor-ft-p03"></a>
### FT-P03 — It Takes One to Know One

> It's certainly true that it takes one to know one.  
> Truer still that it takes the One to Know the One.  
> Thus True Knowing takes one and makes one no one, just so that no-one, One, knows One.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q003) · Extracted authorial poem.

Three times the familiar saying changes case and agency. One person recognises another, then the One knows the One, and then knowing takes the separate claimant through no-one, so that no-one, One, knows One. Its repeated word keeps several offices at once, and the individual knower possesses none of them in full. Commas and capitals perform these turns, and spelling *one* uniformly would remove the differences through which the recognition proceeds.

<a id="taylor-ft-p04"></a>
### FT-P04 — The Zero and the One

> The 0 is the One, the 1 is the All! Thus the All is none, once and for all.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q004) · Extracted authorial poem.

In a single breath the poem sets the whole One at `0` against the enumerated All at `1` and then releases the All's claim to finality through *none* and *once and for all*. Its wit works only because the Two Ones keep different offices. Its use of number is symbolic, and the native derivation carries the argument, so the poem is no independent arithmetic proof.

<a id="taylor-ft-p05"></a>
### FT-P05 — Every Field Is a Way

> Every field is a way,  
> Every way a field  
> Born to yield  
> Truth unconcealed  
> So vistas open  
> In the day's veil  
> And paths lead on  
> Ever availed  
>
> As is the seal  
> Wont to entail  
> The opening of sight  
> And sense's portrayal  
> Too the bird in flight  
> The wind regales  
> The end all and be all  
> From Real to re-All

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q005) · Extracted authorial poem.

Field becomes way and way becomes field before either can close into an object, so the first stanza makes disclosure a movement through vistas and paths. In the second stanza a seal has the office of opening, and sight becomes flight in the wind. The last line returns *Real* as *re-All*, so that the poem re-enters the field of particulars from the reality they disclose, and the rhymes of *yield*, *veil*, *entail* and *portrayal* carry that transition in the sound.

<a id="taylor-ft-p06"></a>
### FT-P06 — Searching High and Low

> Searching high and low,  
> near and far for a clue;  
> Who's to know, and  
> In deed,  
> Who ever knew?  
> The Truth's as much what ever was  
> As what's ever anew.  
> No secret is safe with I;  
> About Time the Truth flew,  
> For the many  
> As much as for the few.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q006) · Extracted authorial poem.

Searching turns from where the clue lies to who knows, and the Truth proves to be as much what ever was as what is ever anew. At the close the secret leaves exclusive custody, since the truth flies for the many as much as for the few, and the capital *I* of *No secret is safe with I* and the play on *Time* belong to that turn from seeking outward to making what is known available.

<a id="taylor-ft-p07"></a>
### FT-P07 — I Dream Of

> I dream of a truly Humanised spirituality,  
> And of the truly Spiritualised human —  
> Failing this, there will be nothing and no one to seek.  
>
> I dream of institutions which respect their constitution,  
> And don't devour the humanity that made and makes them

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q007) · Extracted authorial poem.

A humanised spirituality and a spiritualised human are made reciprocal tasks in the first stanza, which adds that without them there will be nothing and no one to seek. The second stanza gives that reciprocity an institutional demand. Humanity both made and makes the institution, and the present tense stops its originating act from becoming an exhausted historical credit, so that an institution consuming this continuing humanity devours its own constitution, the word the poem chooses for what an institution should respect. The dream is an aspiration with a social consequence.

<a id="taylor-ft-p08"></a>
### FT-P08 — Waking Up

> Waking up is just a matter of getting tired  
> Of all the bullshit.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q008) · Extracted authorial poem.

One line raises the expectation of an account of awakening and brings it down to getting tired, and the next names what the speaker is tired of. Two lines are enough because the poem offers no ladder of superior attainments after the exhaustion, and its last word drops the decorum through which the same evasion could have continued.

<a id="taylor-ft-p09"></a>
### FT-P09 — Freedom

> Tell me; how is freedom free  
> If it be  
> Bound by law and  
> Contingency?  
> Freedom is a rule to itself  
> Or better yet  
> The Rule of The Self.  
> It's no pittance felt  
> Of irony dealt  
> In a house of liberation  
> Admonition, pelt lifted,  
> Rain against the window  
> Of Soul;  
> The eye of the wind within  
> Shan't know the pigeonhole;  
> The message comes through regardless.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q009) · Extracted authorial poem.

Freedom is put under examination: how can it be free while bound by law and contingency? The answer changes the office of rule, from a rule to itself to the Rule of The Self, and the house of liberation then meets rain at its window. By the end the wind, the eye and the message pass beyond the pigeonhole that would classify their arrival. This is a movement in the experience of freedom, and the poem makes no legislative claim that any particular action is exempt from consequence.

<a id="taylor-ft-p10"></a>
### FT-P10 — Beyond All Borders

> Beyond all borders, Knowing without defense,  
> Beyond all realities, Being, True without pretense.  
> Without defense, no wars for waging, nor could there be waging against.  
> Without pretense, no amount of slighting can even slightly sully sense.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q010) · Extracted authorial poem.

A first pair of lines releases Knowing from defence and Being from pretence, and the second pair says what each release makes possible: without defence there is nothing to wage war for or against, and without pretence no slighting can soil sense. The last line tests the claim in sound, since *slighting* comes within a syllable of *slightly* and still cannot give injury the final authority over what is real.

## #2 — Two roads, six works, and the return through the loop

P0 to P5 belong together as one composition, and P3 and P4 are a pair: the first unfolds the productive play of distinction and the second addresses its severing misuse, and Taylor's six-route programme keeps those complementary movements together. A return through the sequence adds no seventh poem.

<a id="taylor-ft-p11"></a>
### FT-P11 — P0: The two roads of two

> 2  →  (−1) + 1  =  0  
> 2  →  0/1  →  00, 01, 10, 11  +  0, 1  =  4 + 2

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q011) · Extracted authorial poem.

Both routes begin with two. On the first road the two poles are read as opposed signed ones and cancel to zero. On the second the two keeps `0` and `1`, generates their four ordered pairings `00, 01, 10, 11`, and keeps the generating two alongside the four, which is `4+2`. The two printed rows are the whole poem, and what separates them is the fate of relation under the two operations, which is cancelled on the first road and retained on the second.

<a id="taylor-ft-p12"></a>
### FT-P12 — P1: What binary cannot compute

> What binary cannot compute,  
> What thinking cannot know,  
> Where two see the common root,  
> There our powers grow.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q012) · Extracted authorial poem.

A common root enters where binary computation and thinking each meet their stated limits. Where two see it, our powers grow, and the poem moves into the first-person plural to say so. Coming after the numerical fork of P0, it makes the increase of power depend on a relation that the isolated poles could not supply. That claim is native and poetic and reports no benchmark about computing machines.

<a id="taylor-ft-p13"></a>
### FT-P13 — P2: Where life leads

> Where life leads,  
> There words transfix.  
> Where life flows,  
> There words shall slip.  
> Where language stays,  
> Understanding frays,  
> Let language cave,  
> And lay bare the way,  
> Over the edge, under the lips.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q013) · Extracted authorial poem.

Where life leads, words transfix, and where life flows they slip. When language stays where life has moved on, understanding frays, and so language has to cave and lay the way bare. *Cave* turns from a possible enclosure into a yielding, and the last line goes over the edge and under the lips, which brings the physical place of speech into speech's release. Its own words make the approach to the limit that it describes.

<a id="taylor-ft-p14"></a>
### FT-P14 — P3: 2 to two ones

> 2 to two ones, one or the other one, summed to none, already a one and done-er.
>
> 2 through the two ones, the whole and natural number, where one as first is both rooted in none and number, the 0 and 1, One and All, never rent asunder. Never falling into enmity, two plays itself apart and becomes again projected by it's chiaroscuric/contrastive arts, two upon itself, plus itself, 4+2. Looking back with love it wraps itself up, 6 perfection seeking recollection of the one that is it's two, ring around the Rosie until you fall down through the loop.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q014) · Extracted authorial poem.

Prepositions carry the fork. In the first paragraph two goes *to* two ones, one or the other, and sums to none, and in the second it goes *through* the Two Ones, the whole and the natural number, never rent asunder. Differentiation is playful and productive here: contrast projects two upon itself, plus itself, while keeping its generating pair, and love turns the resulting six toward recollection of the one that is its two. A children's ring ends the poem by falling down through the loop, which changes what perfection means, since completing the figure makes return possible and sets up no finished enclosure.

<a id="taylor-ft-p15"></a>
### FT-P15 — P4: There’s one underlying issue in our age

> There’s one underlying issue in our age. One could say, One is the underlying issue of our age… Let me rephrase, how the One is to one, this underscores and is setting our stage. Collectively we haven’t a frame open enough to let the evening wane or the one air in through released panes, to be breathed all the same. Are you one? Are we one whole together? Galaxies, planets, seasons, angels, demons, bodies, bots, the weather? Where between each one is One’s tether? Two is the first sign that one must endeavour, married to the bond by a lifetime of together, time dividing not for war but for dreaming towards forever. The one issue; we forgot that one really is two. We almost always see to two as two, because mind must split, divide and polarise, to know is to sever. What amounts, by all accounts, isn’t very clever. Firstly, it is completely derived. So late on the scene did this thinking arise, yet its high water mark is hubris despite; each apotheosis a reason to demonise. Secondly it measures by limiting a sky, by making a cloud where an aeon could fly, blocking light to reify. Third it seals with patterned definition, neither side holds the other, nor self recognition. Alive is a logic landing humility as provision, as primary and axiomatic, as prerequisite of precision. We start with the sky open wide, with the one truth for which thought has no vision, ever aloof, zero-and-one as one impossible act of re-cognition.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q015) · Extracted authorial poem.

From a single issue of our age the poem turns *one* into *One*, then widens the scene through panes, air, planets, bodies and weather. Two makes the bond perceptible, and time can divide for dreaming toward forever and not for war. A critique of severance then gives three reasons against the cutting mind: it is derived and arrived late, it measures by limiting the sky, and it seals each side with definitions in which neither holds the other. Humility enters as a prerequisite of precision. Read with P3, the poem keeps productive differentiation and exposes the cut that forgets its bond, and the open sky at the end answers the blocked frame at the beginning.

<a id="taylor-ft-p16"></a>
### FT-P16 — P5: God is not a thing to argue over

> God is not a thing to argue over, to believe in or deny; it is a word with many contexts, evolutions and definitions, that points somewhere no word can stand. Every word is born of difference, hold the different ones in hand; left and right run away from each other, but together master the span. We define God in terms that require no belief; we use numbers as symbols to express what words alone couldn't reach. Before All, God has always been the One, and yet we're beached, 2000+ years and the formula unimpeached. Number is the language of demonstrative proof, not age-doctored doctrines distilled from real visionary excretes. We offer a coherent and evolving account of God, one that includes it's own release; whereof one must be silent, there one shouldn't speak. And yet the essence of the project is to find the limit, so, to speak.

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q016) · Extracted authorial poem.

In this poem God moves from an object of dispute, belief or denial to a word with many contexts whose pointing exceeds the place any word can stand. Every word is born of difference, and left and right, which run away from each other, together master the span. Number enters the account as symbol, and the account includes its own release. Its close turns the injunction to be silent into speech through three small pauses, *so, to, speak*, which make finding the limit an act of language. Historical duration and doctrinal polemic inside the verse are Taylor's utterance, and quoting the poem establishes none of them as independent history.

## #3 — Dated encounters and the speaker changed by speaking

In the journal poems a speaker undergoes what he can articulate. March gives contradiction a social and bodily pressure, May develops an unnamed referent, difference and feeling, and June lets a fitting form be returned. These changes happen within particular works and dates, and no developmental ranking is imposed on them.

<a id="taylor-ft-p17"></a>
### FT-P17 — [Bullshit and horror so plain and clear]

> Bullshit and horror so plain and clear,  
> everyday normality still, for most, remaining dear,  
> yet not much more than façade on veneer,  
> and yet I love them all, despite my hatred and their fear

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q017) · Extracted authorial poem.

Horror and everyday normality share the same four lines, and *façade on veneer* doubles the thinness of what still remains dear to most. Its ending does not allow the speaker the comfort of leaving its inhabitants behind: he loves them all, alongside *my hatred* and *their fear*, and the pronouns keep that difficult implication personal. In the journal the poem comes after Taylor's note that nothing changes while all is change, and his admission that the next move remains possible without his being able to predict its outcome.

<a id="taylor-ft-p18"></a>
### FT-P18 — [The scratching is the itch]

> The scratching is the itch, what a stitch up!  
> I could see from an early age the world rearrange the way a wish cut,  
> such that our basic needs became the feed of our own eventual disgust,  
> beyond that which we discuss,  
> the feed stock of what makes ugly the lives, half-dead, that we live-up,  
> and I could never live it down, so taking to task the takedown  
> I did propound in my heart the twisted means by which desires abound  
> and thus could let one outmatch their amount,  
> yet I was swallowed up nonetheless,  
> in need of soothing my distress,  
> the winds of change and the naivety razed left me, despite knowing better from best, one and the same with the washing of the brain,  
> My failed test, another expiremental success,  
> Only now split within, between the kid and sin,  
> two hearts in one breast as the great ones did hint,  
> but by dint of this duality my reality did rescind -  
> I am blessed to know those who can hold open the door to my own way,  
> mostly they live in the realm that is no-way, the regions of life to which modernity has no say,  
> bar those whose role in the game is to curtail for their own sake.  
> But time has conspired to set the pot ablaze, fired to let the colours bake into the vessel of a new age,  
> set against and upon the stage that is built of impotences own rage, funnily enough it's saving grace.  
> I could lament my not having acted sooner, my having kept the cards close to my chest but then I'd have been another assumer, another reporter or truther, another speaking from, not to, distress.  
> Time has conspired because time is all our breathing,  
> and I am a bitter pill, sweet coating, relieving,  
> I am a symbol unbelieving, of the hauling and the heaving,  
> and I must be on the line if I am to bait the nourishment for our healing.  
> I was told today that a choice must be made between what sooths me to sleep and what let's me entrain  
> those very forces whose captured uptake has left the cleft wide open without the voice of the age...  
> And how has it left me? Returning to the same old pattern, recapitulating the stagnation  
> I rail against in what happens to simply be the inertia of the everyday "I must have (x)". But it's not eradication I seek, for underneath the sense of me has handed over to the action's own speech, of which my entire soul as aperture is a mouth to be breached,  
> and I know that what will settle is a natural mettle, and an incoussiant valuation of what takes me from my station to my peak.  
> But without the choice I am idle, with it I'm besseched.  
> It is no trite accretion of the image of the anointed, nor bland convalescence to the inversion appointed, but a true reconciliation in me of the brothers,  
> for how else can I become the right light unto others?

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q018) · Extracted authorial poem.

His attempt to understand manufactured desire is itself swallowed by his need for soothing, and knowing better does not spare him the capture he describes. Kid and sin become two hearts in one breast, and the door held open, with the vessel fired to let the colours bake, lets him imagine a different use of the same forces. A later distinction between speaking from distress and speaking to it changes the speaker's office. Time, breath and the bitter pill bring that change toward a choice between soothing sleep and entrainment, and even when the choice appears, stagnation returns. The last movement explicitly seeks no eradication of what he rails against, and asks for a reconciliation of the brothers within him, so that he can become a right light to others, and the final revised witness preserves this aftermath, which an excerpt ending at the kiln would lose.

<a id="taylor-ft-p19"></a>
### FT-P19 — [The unchanging is beyond consequence]

> The unchanging is beyond consequence, but is the opposite of inconsequential... What allows for an essence is essentially inessential... What builds for itself a journey is perpetually residential...  
> Sojourning through time yet unmovingly presidential... Silent but in structure reverential... Context total yet totally incidental... Foundation of mind, unfound by the mental...

[Complete source witness](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus/taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus.md#taylor-2026-personal-poetry-corpus-q019) · Extracted authorial poem.

This poem withholds its referent and lets seven clauses disclose what depends on it. *Beyond consequence* does not mean inconsequential, residence stays within the journey, movement through time leaves what presides undisplaced, and the last clause places the mind's foundation beyond the mind's finding. Taylor explicitly refused to replace the withheld referent with *ātman* or *witness*, because naming either would draw it into the distinctions the poem is traversing, and the journey through such distinctions is both necessary and self-negating.

A later authorial instruction in the same conversation places the context clause before the silence clause, so that context carries #4, silence #5 and the final mind clause the `5→0` return. The compilation's two-line witness above still puts silence before context, and the revised order is stated here as Taylor's instruction and is not silently substituted for the verse. The positions then express a succession of dependent relations. Withholding the referent keeps the parent capacity to distinguish effective before either term is named. An unchanging condition makes consequence possible without becoming one more consequence, and essence permits a determinate constitution without being another item within it. Journey and residence join movement to its remaining ground, and sojourning and presiding hold temporal particulars within a continuing pattern. A context that is total yet incidental contains a particular without being exhausted by it, and silence withdraws the claim to possess the condition while structure keeps its relation articulate. Last, the mind's unfound foundation returns from its determinate contents to the condition of their disclosure.

## #4 — Images that retain the relation they expose

In prose the images give the same field material operations beyond verse: a token breaks, a dot is erased, an optical figure turns, attention is drawn into a concealed centre. Each has its own conditions and aftermath, and their adjacency gathers Taylor's authored use of them without making a historical emblem, a psychological operator and an institutional diagnosis evidence of one kind.

<a id="fish-water"></a>
### The fish describing water

A fish tries to describe the water it swims in. Every distinction it makes is already sustained by what it is trying to put before itself as an object, and leaving the water to get an independent view would end the life from which the describing is done. This turns the question back on the describing subject: how does its own act disclose the condition it cannot encompass? Taylor's direct manuscript puts it as a fish describing water while swimming in it, and the hand that writes that the hand cannot grasp itself is still a hand grasping.

In Taylor's [*The Definition of God*, Draft 3](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md) definition meets its condition from within. It first asserts a bounded account, finds that even the declaration *God is the indefinable* is another mark, a determinate sentence with a full stop, and succeeds by acknowledging that failure. Assertion, failure and productive paradox become one contextual movement in which the defining subject recognises its own presence within what it was trying to define, and the [faithful definition](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) **extends** that recognition by returning to the act and its condition. We earn the first-person recognition through the attempt, and abandoning definition at the outset would not obtain it.

<a id="cage-window"></a>
### A definition made into a cage or a window

A frame can enclose what it claims to show, or it can delimit an opening through which we see beyond it. A cage makes its own bounds the creature's world, while a window keeps a definite frame and admits light and air from what exceeds it. Both need the frame, and removing all determination would remove the window with the cage.

Taylor gives definition this double possibility. Its assertion meets its failure first, and the acknowledged failure then lets it succeed in pointing beyond its grasp, through opposed claims and their containing field to first-person recognition and return. Panes, sky and limits share this opening operation in his poems while each keeps its own movement. What separates the two outcomes is the difference that a [living symbol](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) **defines**: it keeps the passage its form makes possible, while an idol closes around what the frame was meant to disclose.

<a id="kaleidoscope"></a>
### The kaleidoscope and its changing centre

Each turn of a kaleidoscope rearranges the relations among the visible pieces, and the pieces do not license every possible description, since what can be seen depends on their arrangement and on the instrument through which it appears. Taylor's P2 encounter with Singh's introduction to the *Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa* takes this as the image of a hermeneutics that is liberal and rigorous together: Abhinavagupta says that a sutra contains manifold meanings and can be interpreted in various ways, which does not mean arbitrarily. A word becomes a local orbital centre around which other meanings gather, and turning the relation brings a different coherence into view.

Centralising a notion recognises its identity with the Supreme, and decentring it affirms the Supreme's transcendence of that particular form, and both actions belong to the authored image. A centre can therefore be real in its local office without becoming the final centre of everything. An accompanying mandala opens concentrically from a home toward foreign contexts and returns to its anchor. Taylor's notebook adds a cymatic comparison, in which sand or iron filings on a vibrating plate pass from harmonic order through chaos into order at a higher level of complexity as the pitch rises. That comparison makes patterned alignment imaginable, and the notebook establishes no physical law by which philosophical truth increases with frequency. Its pun on *buoyancy* and a boy on the sea is Taylor's phonetic play on the word sustaining a crossing, and no historical etymology.

Taylor receives the manifold interpretation that [Singh's introduction to Abhinavagupta's commentary](../../../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/abhinavagupta/abhinavagupta-singh-1988-paratrisika-vivarana/abhinavagupta-singh-1988-paratrisika-vivarana.md) **sources** through the kaleidoscope, the orbital centre and the relation of centralising to decentring, and the introduction and the commentary remain different textual contributions. [Vāk's determinate articulation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A06-Vak.md) **grounds** his word-centred turn: a word can become a real local centre while it opens onto the wider field that gives it meaning, and recentring changes the relations that the next utterance can inherit.

<a id="necker-cube"></a>
### The cube that reverses while its field remains

Lines of a cube admit a change of orientation: a face that had receded comes forward, and the earlier view can return though the marks themselves are never replaced. While one reading fills the field, the alternative appears only through a flip. Taylor's core uses that reversal to approach a further change, in which a context holds the readings in relation and neither has to become the final account of the figure.

Taylor places this reversal within a passage through *I*, *you* and *it*, followed by the release of their exclusivity, recognition of one appearing act, and return. Context is no fourth competing face of the cube, because it holds the differently oriented readings in relation. [Diaphaneity](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) **defines** how a situated view becomes transparent to those conditions, and a [world-atlas](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **extends** the point by keeping the situated readings and their relations available where another victorious picture would only replace them. This contextual relation makes no experimental claim that incompatible depth percepts are literally simultaneous.

<a id="centre-everywhere"></a>
### Centres without a final circumference

From within an experience the world is given through this centre. Meeting another internality changes the demand, since the other is also a centre whose world cannot be exhausted by its appearing in mine, and further internalities can be nested within and across these relations. None of their reality waits for a last external spectator who would enclose them all.

An image of a centre everywhere gives this plurality an indefinite circumference: actual centres and their differences stay effective without a final enclosing view, and aperspectivality makes a perspective transparent to the conditions and the other perspectives it cannot monopolise. [Objective internality](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **grounds** the relation in articulated means. In a local interpretation the judging agent is a relative functional knower, its representations are what it knows, and its memory, rules and instruments are the means, and the whole apparatus serves as a means within a person's containing inquiry. A further inspection can make the local judging centre known while the present act of knowing keeps its own office. <a id="broken-symbolon-tessera"></a>
### The broken token and the kept counterpart

A token is divided and its halves are carried apart. Each party keeps a piece whose broken edge refers to the absent other, and recognition later happens through their fit. A break does more than damage a complete object, because keeping the fracture lets a relation be authenticated across absence. Time in between matters, since trust has to endure until the reunion makes its basis present.

Taylor's programme of the copula and of *ballein* gives this material scene an authored formal return. A party's half refers beyond itself, and a heap of pieces cannot perform their particular fit, so recognition needs the parties and the kept counterpart. A [symbolic account](../../../../episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md) **defines** this fracture as a means of identifying a relation, and the [root Symbolon](../../../../README.md) holds ground and mark together through their differentiating slash. This scene develops that operation and supplies no duplicate derivation and no invented ancient tale.

An [undertaking across time](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) **grounds** the interval: it stays exposed to a counterpart whose arrival its account cannot manufacture, keeping the half makes a possible reunion recognisable, and trust carries the relation through the time before that recognition becomes available.

<a id="erased-taijitu"></a>
### Two dots, one orientation, and the erased other

In the taijitu each of the black and white fields keeps a dot of the other within it. In Taylor's diagrammatic use those retained others make the whole living, since the distinguished sides do not sever their mutual implication, and his quilt counts the figure so: both dots make the living whole, one dot an orientation, and no dot a dead binary, so that monopoly is a taijitu with the dot erased. Erasing the dots changes the operation, and the two fields stay visibly opposed while they can no longer retain the other.

Taylor distinguishes annihilation from arrogation at this danger. On one route the opposition cancels into nothing, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and on the other a pole claims to be absolute and suppresses its dependence, which is appropriation, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from one pole and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the other. An outer form can survive while the reciprocal relation that made it generative disappears. [The two logics](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) **ground** the distinction: healthy polarity is held through its axis, cancellation is told from appropriation, and the held break returns through recognition and compound. Taylor's construction of two dots, one dot and no dot is QL imagery and is attributed to no ancient Taoist text.

<a id="bohr-taijitu-emblem"></a>
### Bohr's emblem as a separate reception witness

A reported Bohr taijitu emblem belongs to a particular historical reception of a received figure, in an armorial setting, whereas Taylor's alteration of the dots constructs a diagram that exposes an operation. Its exact provenance, motto and reproduction need their own witness, and no unverified image or motto is reproduced here.

Complementary experimental arrangements and the selection made within one measurement do different work. Across arrangements, the differently disclosed aspects belong to the complete account of a phenomenon, and selecting one arrangement gives a determinate orientation while it withholds what it does not disclose. In the quilt's terms the local measurement cut is dia-ballein and the phenomenon across arrangements is syn-ballein: wave and particle exclude each other under any single arrangement and are jointly necessary for one complete description. Taylor's comparison holds both operations together, neither collapses into one generic cut, and the historical emblem is not identified with his erased-dot construction.

The native [eight determinations](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **ground** the comparison by keeping relation, orientation, disclosed mark and withheld condition available through it. Physics carries its own experimental and formal warrants, and the emblem derives nothing of the native field.

A [local criterion](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md) **qualifies** the same relation in practice: it keeps its competence while its dependence on a selecting arrangement becomes available. A corrected result under a fitting arrangement differs from an exposed fault in the arrangement itself, and either finding can condition the next use, including the reasoned retention of an adequate condition.

<a id="black-sun-radiating-star"></a>
### The black sun and the radiating star

Attention and labour move toward a centre. At the public edge their use is presented as work done for others, and deeper in, the power to decide what is done with it is less visible. Taylor's black-sun image gathers these movements as capture: energy is received without a circulation that renews the people and relations supplying it, and in Taylor's social-power unit the black sun captures needs and sells them back. A radiating star changes where the intake goes, so that what reaches the centre becomes available outward as generative power.

In Taylor's social-power reading, capture and circulation unfold through the Work and Play inversion, with the six offices of Power in order: Play opens the freedom of an interior life, Need makes its dependence felt, Sacrifice concerns what is given and received, Decision orders that exchange, Love keeps the persons in relation, and Work makes the relation publicly available. Healthy work expresses the life that sustains it, and healthy play permits creation and contemplation. A coequal Name series, Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son and Image, articulates what this lived movement of Power brings into expression. Power includes provision and care, and the black sun is its capture and no part of the existence of Power itself.

Under the inversion the public work channel becomes performance and effective decision moves into concealed play, so that we see the orbits and never know there is a sun. Taylor's diagnosis gives the separation a changing depth through four concentric rings. Outer philanthropy supplies the visible face of benevolence, and he compares it to Neptune's distant orbit. Concentrated capital in the middle ring supplies stabilising gravity, compared to Jupiter. Inner fixers mediate between power and its instruments, under the image of Mercury's proximity. An innermost corona is a different kind of relation, in which compromising material binds participants through their capacity to expose one another, so that mutual vulnerability can make departure costly. These are differentiated offices in Taylor's adopted diagnosis, and the astronomical imagery makes their visibility and proximity imaginable without establishing the institutional claims by physical law or proving a universal history of power.

This contraction reaches identification as well. A person can be permitted an identity as worker, consumer or public profile while the depth of that life stops counting. A false accounting excludes interior experience because it cannot be entered in the authorised ledger, and then treats the exclusion as the absence of reality, so that what a person has suffered, loved or understood is disqualified before the account is made. Its return therefore concerns who may determine what I am as well as where labour and attention flow, and recognition can widen the relation within which a particular identity is held without requiring the person to disappear into a larger collective.

Attention passes through three stages. Cynicism still directs energy toward what it distrusts, exhaustion then brings disengagement, and the resulting blankness leaves a field ready for re-inscription, in which control works by deciding which questions become available before any answers are offered. Exposing the performance has therefore not returned power to those who sustain it, and a changed relation has to let work, attention and judgment return as their generative capacity. That is the consequence of the radiating star. A newly exclusive framework would repeat the demand for allegiance, and the authored return changes our relation to frameworks and our ability to answer them.

[Delegated labour](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) **grounds** the black sun in the economy of receiving: the output the centre acquires depends on capacities and costs its recipient did not originate. [A usurped measure](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md) **qualifies** the capture by hiding that dependence when it counts only what the centre can possess, and returning work, attention and judgment to those who sustain them changes the relation between receiving and decision. Particular political or criminal allegations in the raw unit, universal historical claims and psychiatric equivalences each need their own evidence, and this image establishes none of them. The separate spectral-chain tellings keep their own sequences, and they supply no synthetic poems and do not replace the political movement set out here.

<a id="god-city-bomb-surveillance-ai"></a>
### City, bomb, surveillance, and the counterfeit image

A city shelters a plurality of lives and gathers the energy that sustains them, and its organised power can also turn against its inhabitants. A bomb makes a destructive possibility material, since the built field can be annihilated. Surveillance can leave the buildings and the daily activity in place while it captures the people who move among them, so a city that still looks as if it functions does not show that its constitutive relation is still free.

Extending the image to AI and the deepfake brings in another power. An image can stay persuasive after its claimed source has been broken or counterfeited, which differs from destroying the city and from monitoring an actual person, because the encounter is now directed toward an appearance whose asserted origin cannot be trusted. Taylor's quilt reads the deepfake as a counterfeit symbolon, one half that presents itself as returnable to a counterpart or originating event that never stood behind it, and it holds claims about social trust effects for their own evidence. Material destruction, capture of persons and the counterfeit source-image are three distinct failures in the programme's movement through technoscientific power.

The God-image enters as the cultural question of what a collective places at the source and the end of its organised power. A destructive or a constructive capacity leaves its end underdetermined, so that having the means makes their exercise a consequential choice. A [mediating office](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority.md) **defines** one answer: it receives permission and stays answerable to the originating relation it serves. An [instrument that initiates disclosure](../mirror-that-moves-first/WHOLE.md) **compares** as a second: it exteriorises its measure, lets its image condition the next encounter, and discloses source, angle and measure before demanding conformity. Counterfeiting an image's source performs a different operation from either.

God, Shadow, Friend and Alien, the four operators that follow, belong to one encounter with alterity. Their different offices keep the word *Other* from covering over the difference between a power within the psyche and another person who meets it.

A [reflective encounter](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) **extends** the difference among destruction, capture within a continuing habitat and counterfeit originating witness: correcting a representation is effective only if the next encounter receives the corrected source or relevant condition, and an adequate condition can be retained with its warrant instead. A stronger claim that processing has changed needs the particular change and the later act that inherited it.

<a id="god-other"></a>
### God as Other

A finite mind confronts source, law or archetypal totality as something that exceeds its possession, and the image gives that excess an address in God as Other. A divine-mind reading, in which the divine Self meets its own manifestation, changes the register of the relation, and the finite mind cannot claim that office for itself because it has produced an image of the divine.

<a id="shadow-other-self"></a>
### Shadow as other-self

What the ego disowns returns as internal difference: its completed self-picture cannot accommodate what nevertheless acts within its life, and the shadow's otherness makes that incompleteness effective, so that recognition needs a changed relation to what had been excluded. This is an encounter within psychic life, and it does not make another person only a projection of the ego.

<a id="friend-myselfs-other"></a>
### Friend as my-self's-other

A friend shares a world and exceeds the image I make of them. Their response can alter our relation because its source is not exhausted by my account of it, and friendship needs that excess to stay active, since the other here is another centre. Assimilating the friend to an inner figure would remove the interlocutor through whom the relation changes.

<a id="alien-enemy"></a>
### Alien or enemy as externalised uncertainty

Denied uncertainty can be assigned to an external negative pole, and the enemy then seems to carry what the self's account has refused to include. That operation is a projection and a severance, converting an unresolved relation into a supposed property of the other, and recognising it restores responsibility for the account doing the assigning. Real dangers and real adversaries remain real, and recognising the projection does not turn them into inner figures.

These four relations **ground** [self and other without possession](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md): an exceeding ground, a disowned psychic power, a responding friend and a projected enemy each put different pressure on a finite self-account. Jung's inner face and the interpersonal pressure associated with Levinas keep their distinct sources, and the four offices are neither fixed native determinations nor four universal character types into which every person is sorted.
