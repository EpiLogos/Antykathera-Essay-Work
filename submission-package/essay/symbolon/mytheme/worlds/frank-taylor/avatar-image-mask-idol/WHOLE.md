# Avatar · image · mask · idol

This record serves the essay's account of how a mediating form keeps or loses its source, in Taylor's authored ecology of avatar, image, mask and idol. It lays three layers together: the mythemic (a presence taking a face, a mask worn for admission, an emblem that outlives the person it claimed), the mathemic (the signed polarity and the eight determinations) and the epistemic (the argument records, Ovid, Job, *The Prisoner* and the sources). Each layer amplifies the others and keeps its own office. Its status is Argued.

## #0

Presence becomes addressable through a form. A face turns towards someone; a name lets that person be called again; a gesture carries an intention into a shared situation. A role gives an undertaking a recognisable place. Someone can now be met, remembered, trusted, opposed or answered. Its finitude lets this encounter occur here, between these participants. Its success can also turn into possession when the presentation acquires authority over the life it makes available.

Finite form mediates the encounter. A person does not first become complete in isolation and subsequently add a public mask. Language, bodily expression, histories of recognition and shared practices participate in the life that appears. Yet no achieved presentation finishes that life. The person named can answer differently, the person remembered can have changed, and the one approached through a role can question what the role permits. Maintained personhood is this consequential continuity through different appearances, including the capacity to alter how one is received.

The seams ledger **sources** Taylor's [avatar–image–mask–idol programme](../../../../../quilt/final-argument-quilt-2026-08-23/MYTHEME-AND-DEEP-SOURCE-SEAMS.md), which develops one authored ecology through four distinguishable offices. Avatar concerns presence becoming effective in a finite appearance. Image concerns the particular formation that gathers attention and value. Mask concerns the presentation through which participation becomes possible. Idol concerns the closure by which a successful formation takes possession of the authority it was meant to mediate. These offices can meet in one form, and their relation makes no historical claim that four traditions tell one story.

[Taylor's core](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **grounds** the complete relation, which unfolds as parent `/ = −/−`, `0/1`, `?/!`, `−/+`, `X/x`, `AM/IS`, `∞/dx`, and return `1/0`. A form arises within that movement and carries its source into a particular appearance. Question can address assertion; an instance can expose the capacity through which it was formed; the personed encounter can change the account; the achieved account can return as a condition of another determination. Avatar, image, mask and idol distinguish what happens to presence, force, participation and authority within this relation, and the four offices add no positions to the native eight.

## #1

The avatar brings a wider presence into a particular encounter. In this authored use, descent names the movement into a form that can act: a voice rather than every possible voice, a gesture rather than intention without expression, an entrusted representative rather than an undertaking with no bearer. Presence is not made unreal by having an appearance, and it acquires an occasion on which it can matter.

That occasion keeps a difference of office. A representative can speak authoritatively within a commission, and the statement stays answerable to what was entrusted. Even the original instruction is already a formulation and cannot exhaust the intention simply because it was written first. As [A28 defines authored delegation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md), a human, institution or agent occupies a source-position relative to a commission. That bearer remains situated within a wider field, and the prompt through which they commission another act is already a determination. An encounter can therefore return upon the formulated instruction or its governing purpose without making the earliest wording an absolute source. Authority can be exact and effective without becoming absolute sourcehood.

An avatar's body belongs to the encounter it enters. A voice needs someone who can hear it; a gesture acquires consequences in a shared situation; an entrusted representative inherits a commission and answers through what the representation does. The source relation passes into action, where another participant can accept, question or refuse it, and that response becomes part of the form's history. Taylor's avatar names this effective finite presence. The histories of Sanskrit *avatāra* and theological descent would need their own doctrines and passages before this authored relation could be attributed to them.

An image makes that presence available under a particular arrangement, and the arrangement can be faithful without being exhaustive. A remembered face, an emblem or a written account selects, and selection is what makes recognition precise. Source and reflection also have a direction. A reflection can be encountered first in time while remaining dependent on what it reflects. Within a declared Context Frame, an achieved image or source record can genuinely occupy **Bimba**, the local original against which further readings are compared. That same field is **pratibimba**, situated reflection, relative to its wider sources. In this [Bimba–Pratibimba relation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md), which **defines** the two offices, the local original gives agreement and error a definite reference, and what those comparisons return can revise that original itself. Its authority is real at the scale of the inquiry. Idolisation begins when it prevents its own source relation from being questioned or changed.

In a local comparison the interpreting agent holds the functional knower office. Its reference image, remembered encounters, rules and instruments are the means; the represented person, source claims and alternative readings are known. An agent's selection can itself become known in a further inquiry. In the containing person's Life, this complete apparatus serves as means through which an appearance can be met and judged. These relative offices let the image act without confusing its useful authority with the whole relation in which the person meets it.

## #2

An image gathers force. Attention returns to it; desire and fear organise themselves around it; expectations begin to make some possibilities vivid and others difficult to receive. A picture that once expressed a situation can become a measure for the next situation. The person making or interpreting it is changed through that activity, and the formation acquires a history whose later efficacy includes what its earlier appearances have done.

In [Taylor’s reading of symbolic transformation](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics.md), which **sources** the point, archetypal capacity, particular image and organising complex keep different offices within this reciprocal movement. An archetype is not one finished portrait; an image is not the whole capacity from which further images can arise; a complex organises a field of response around a consequential formation. Attention, libido, desire and valuation name different aspects or registers of that efficacy, and they do not become interchangeable physical energies.

In the [Neumann articulation](../../../archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md), which the whole **compares**, the round holds the parental relation before its differentiated figures appear. Nourishing and engulfing faces of origin leave dependence with contrary possibilities, and separation of the world parents opens a world in which powers can be distinguished. A hero gains an effective position within that world, then meets the limits of identifying life with the victorious image. Transformation returns the achieved differentiation into relation with what it had subordinated or excluded. Each formation carries a capacity that its own established image can later confine.

Taylor's encounter with [Neumann](../../../../episteme/sources/psychology/neumann/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness.md) **extends** the relation through the whole native field: relation, conscious circumstance, question and assertion, withdrawal and extension, determining capacity and instance, personed context, differential horizon and return. Complementary folds stay active within this movement: question answers in a personed world, felt force acquires a readable pattern, and the conscious circumstance retains its inexhaustible depth. Conditions of appearing precede an ego's achieved self-image. Neumann's ego-development sequence has a distinct psychological office, and it limits this native relation to no ego chronology and assigns no culture a position on that sequence.

An image's efficacy reaches the body as well as its account. In Taylor's *Symbolon Dynamics*, libido concerns psychic investment and valuation; prāṇa concerns living circulation through breath, rhythm and differentiated current; Śakti concerns manifestation-power, including the concealment of source within its formation and its return through recognition. A repeated image can gather desire, alter the rhythm of attention and make a once-unavailable response possible. These registers disclose different operations of that efficacy. Their coordination belongs to Taylor's synthesis, and their names license no common physical measurement and transfer no tradition's evidence into another.

An image's success therefore creates the pressure that will test it. A form that has held a life together has a claim on attention, and revising it can threaten a valued coherence, an institution's continuity or a familiar account of oneself. What is tested is what this coherence can receive. If a new encounter changes the meaning, valuation or jurisdiction of the image, symbolic efficacy remains alive. If every encounter must confirm it, the interpreter's apparent fidelity begins to protect the image from the relation that made it significant.

[A20 defines image, valuation and possession](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md) through this change in authority. An image mediates while its source relation stays active, and idolisation appropriates that relation. A vivid or beloved image is no idol for that reason, and a quiet, abstract or apparently rational account escapes the test no more easily. What decides is authority: whether the encountered source can still alter what the image is allowed to mean and do.

## #3

An image becomes a mask when it offers a way to enter a social field. Language, manner, clothing, credentials and the expectations of a role make someone legible to others. A mask can protect an intimacy, sustain a public obligation or let a person exercise capacities that would otherwise have no recognised place. Learning to wear it is an achievement of participation, and nothing here requires a person to be completely exposed in order to be real.

Pressure appears when access depends on the presentation so completely that its bearer must administer life according to the recognising field's measure. What began as an interface becomes an internal criterion. Such a person anticipates what will count, edits what can be shown and may come to receive an unapproved response as a defect in the self, so the mask keeps opening doors while narrowing the life that can pass through them. This is one possible passage within self-administration, and a clinical diagnosis or a claim about every social role would need evidence of the particular person and situation.

The mask and chameleon seam keeps a specific pressure: adaptation can grant access while requiring a bearer to reproduce the measure that renders parts of that bearer illegible. A surrounding field acts through the person's acquired skill in navigating it, and participation can become self-administration through that success. Racial and colonial histories involve specific coercion, language, bodies and institutions; a Fanonian or biographical comparison must preserve those conditions rather than replace them with this general relation.

The distinction is sharply staged in [*The Prisoner*](../../../../episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/mcgoohan-markstein/mcgoohan-markstein-1967-the-prisoner/mcgoohan-markstein-1967-the-prisoner.md). After resignation, an unnamed intelligence agent is abducted into the Village and designated Number Six. Changing occupants hold the office of Number Two; the identity of Number One remains the question. When Six asks who One is, the answer assigns Six his place. Numbering gives the administrative office continuity across its occupants and gives the captive an identity the institution can administer. So the question about power's source is diverted into power's classification of the questioner.

In its concluding *Fall Out* the series returns this question through masks and Six's own face, then through escape and an unresolved London return. That inward turn does not cancel the Village's coercive apparatus or declare captivity imaginary. Our finale sequence follows written witnesses; its exact audiovisual continuity requires timecode collation. In Taylor's refraction the return reaches both the institution and the claimant to sovereignty within the person. Replacing an administrator or removing a mask does not alone make the governing relation answerable. Number Two and Number Six retain their dramatic offices; they supply no proof of the native `4+2` generation.

## #4

An idol closes the path through the image. Its bearer stays visible, perhaps more widely represented than before, while losing the ability to change the terms under which the representation acts. A category is allowed to know the person before encounter, and contrary speech is then classified as further evidence of the category. An image need not become inaccurate in any detail, since its protected jurisdiction lets even an accurate local description govern what exceeds that description.

Laid beside the signed arithmetic, the idol is appropriation held in place. Its category counts the whole span of the relation from its own pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and whatever the person says is charged to them from the other end, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, so that every reply adds to the deficit the category already attributes. A mask is the milder form of the same operation, in which the bearer reproduces the measure and counts themself from the measure's pole. This amplification lays the mythemic figures, the mathemic operation and the epistemic record of the protected account together. Its status is Argued, and the account of contrary speech as further evidence is the page's own.

Ovid's [Apollo and Daphne sequence](../../../../episteme/sources/classical-philology/ovid/ovid-1921-metamorphoses-miller/ovid-1921-metamorphoses-miller.md) **embodies** this continued efficacy exactly. Apollo's boast after Python leads to Cupid's opposed arrows. Daphne's prior refusal of marriage and Peneus's grant of that wish precede the pursuit. Apollo's powers do not make his desire hers. Exhaustion, appeal and transformation end with the laurel: the changed body is still felt as living, recoils from the kiss, and is claimed for Apollo's emblems. The ending also lends the tree an appearance of assent, and recoil and apparent assent together hold the ending in tension without turning the pursuit into uncomplicated consent. The authored image–idol reading concerns the emblem's power to continue after possession of the living other has failed. Roman literary telling, its Greek figures and its later interpretation retain different places and times.

The [biblical Job witness](../../../../episteme/sources/biblical-studies/anonymous/biblical-job-kjv-ebible-eng-kjv2006/biblical-job-kjv-ebible-eng-kjv2006.md) tests a different image: an account of divine justice. Councils, successive losses, bodily affliction and a wife's challenge precede the friends' explanations. Eliphaz, Bildad and Zophar first mourn and sit silently with Job; their later advocacy must be read against that initial accompaniment. Job's protest and defence of integrity resist an explanation which would require him to become the guilty person it needs. Wisdom in chapter 28 exceeds extraction and purchase; Elihu's separate intervention precedes the whirlwind. Divine speeches bring a world and creatures beyond Job's administration without explaining the councils to him. His response is followed by the friends' rebuke and their need for his intercession. Renewed household and social life do not erase the first children's deaths.

A defence of the account changes when Job can be heard and its advocates must receive his mediation. Jung's [*Answer to Job* comparison, §§614–617](../../../../episteme/sources/psychology/jung/jung-1969-psychology-religion-cw11/jung-1969-psychology-religion-cw11.md#jung-1969-psychology-religion-cw11-q002) **sources** a separate office: he reads the treatment of the faithful witness as a crisis of the God-image requiring self-reflection and remembrance of Sophia. That is an attributed psychological interpretation. Sophia is not introduced as an actor in biblical Job 28, and Jung's reconstruction does not become Taylor's native derivation. These three registers meet through a stated relation without losing their tellings.

## #5→0

Regard returns through the form to the participant who can answer it. An answer need not confirm the first question. It can disclose an omitted circumstance, refuse an imposed role, correct an attributed statement or show that the criterion itself has misnamed the encounter. The result is a new determination with a changed history. Maintained personhood does not require an essence displayed without mediation; it requires that continuity of address not become ownership of the person addressed.

The [Arbitration / Hybris / Regard / Anamnesis relation](../../../../episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md) **defines** six connected operations for this return. Resolution-in-Reconciliation returns the settlement with its history. Regard receives the person affected by the judgment; anamnesis lets the achieved history participate in another act. Their conjunction is an operational relation, with each word's lexical ancestry remaining distinct.

The [Symbol / Account / Trust relation](../../../../episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md) **defines** four further operations. A symbol answers to the source it claims to bear. An account makes that claim inspectable while the person's standing continues beyond the report. Trust keeps correction, refusal and consequences capable of reaching the governing office. What the image has done re-enters its source-field as a condition of its next use. Provenance, signatures, audits and credentials can strengthen specified evidential relations. Persons and institutions undertake responsibility for how those relations are used and for the answer they receive.

An entrusted avatar can therefore stay powerful through situated delegation while its source office is inspectable and revisable. A mask can continue to enable participation while allowing its bearer to exceed and alter its terms. An image can remain beloved while acquiring another meaning, or survive as an acknowledged historical sign after its former symbolic life has ended. A received answer can warrant correction, renewed reliance on a fitting condition, or withdrawal. Neither fidelity nor return obliges the bearer to continue an injurious encounter. Withdrawal can itself be the answer the representation must learn to receive.

Person, image and shared field return still distinct, with the warranted judgment carried into the next encounter. Self and Other, as [A27 defines them](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md), belong within one whole while no participant owns the other's reply, and [Objective Co-Internality](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **extends** this: one encounter enters several participants' later conditions. Neumann's differentiated participation offers a psychological comparison for this change without making ego chronology the limit of the native operation. The image has carried a presence into life, and its continuing integrity lies in letting that life answer back.
