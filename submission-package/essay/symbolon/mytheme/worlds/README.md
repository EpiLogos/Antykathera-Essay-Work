# Whole Mythemes by situated world

Each whole has one home. These entrances follow evidenced source traditions and authored worlds, and the [geographical](../atlas/geography/README.md) and [temporal](../atlas/temporality/README.md) routes show other relations among the same bodies. The two [shared archetypal wholes](../archetypal-ground/README.md) keep their top-level office.

- [Hellenic tellings](hellenic/README.md): 2 wholes. Homeric song and the Attic civic foundation remain distinct witnesses.
- [Roman Latin tellings](roman-latin/README.md): 2 wholes. Ovid and Apuleius supply different Latin works.
- [Biblical Job](biblical/README.md): 1 whole. The biblical telling, Jung’s interpretation and Taylor’s three-office reading retain separate attribution.
- [Late-antique Gnostic worlds](late-antique-gnostic/README.md): 2 wholes. The Hypostasis tractate and Irenaeus’s hostile report of Valentinian teaching are independent carriers.
- [Chinese Huayan](chinese-huayan/README.md): 1 whole. The Dushun-attributed contemplation carries the jewel net; attribution remains disputed.
- [Analytical psychology](analytical-psychology/README.md): 2 wholes. Jung’s symbolic history and Pauli’s dream/commentary retain separate authors and source passages.
- [Francophone anticolonial encounter](francophone-anticolonial/README.md): 1 whole. Fanon’s situated racial-colonial inquiry retains its own speaking position.
- [German literature](german-literature/README.md): 1 whole. Goethe’s poem remains a complete poetic movement in its German witness.
- [British television](british-television/README.md): 1 whole. The Prisoner is a television work with selected written episode tellings and an independently housed programme identity.
- [Frank Taylor’s authored world](frank-taylor/README.md): 10 wholes. These are Taylor’s compositions and interpretive wholes; their independent source traditions remain named inside them.
