# Indra’s jewel net

This record tells the jewel-net contemplation whole, in the Huayan carrier, and then lays three layers beside it: the mythemic (a chosen jewel that carries all the others), the mathemic (`0/1`, `(0/1)/(1/0)` and the signed polarity) and the epistemic (the Huayan sources and the argument records). Each layer amplifies the others and keeps its own office. The Chinese contemplation speaks in its own doctrine, while the notation is the essay's, and the comparison between them is the essay's. Its status is Offered.

<a id="indra-selected-telling"></a>
## #0 — A jewel is chosen within the net

The net belongs to Indra's celestial image-world, and its development here belongs to Chinese Huayan: the contemplation transmitted in the *Huayan wujiao zhiguan*, conventionally attributed to Dushun, whose authorship remains disputed. Thomas Cleary’s English excerpt in *Dharma Rain* (2000), pages 58–60, is the selected translation; its earlier container is *Entry into the Inconceivable* (1983), pages 66–68. The Chinese doctrinal work and its English transmissions sit at different times. Indra’s celestial setting gives the teaching its image-world. The [Dushun-attributed transmission](../../../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dushun/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra.md) preserves their attribution and edition limits.

It begins with knowledge. If phenomena arise through conditions, how can they be known? The answer places knowing within their relation: the contemplating eye encounters a field in which phenomena enter one another; it cannot secure an independent beginning outside that field and then look down upon the result. A jewel net gives that encounter a visible body.

Its jewels are clear. In every jewel the others appear, and what appears includes their reflections of one another. Choose a jewel in the southwest. Choosing gives attention an exact place to begin. All the other jewels are reflected there together. Each of those jewels also reflects the whole, including the selected jewel. Following a reflection therefore brings the point of departure back into view within the very field it discloses. So the net opens again inside its own appearance.

Nothing in the operation reserves this capacity for the southwest jewel. Begin elsewhere and the same relation holds. Every jewel supplies a particular entrance; none has to travel to a privileged centre before the others can become present. It makes the claim through simultaneous manifestation and the absence of coming and going. Its ordered sentences teach attention how to follow what the image presents together, and their sequence is no physical account of signals taking successive journeys.

<a id="indra-recursive-entry"></a>
## #1 — Entering the others without leaving this one

Now the contemplation asks the reader to remain within the selected jewel. To enter all the jewels from here is to discover their presence in this one; in those jewels this one is present again. Here the movement reverses without losing its starting place. Reflections contain reflections which contain the first reflection, with no final image at which the relation becomes exhausted.

An interlocutor presses the difficulty. How can one enter all while remaining here? His reply makes remaining here the condition of entry. Leaving the jewel behind would abandon the very relation through which the others are disclosed. Another question follows: if the many are present within this one, why speak of a net made from many jewels at all? The answer treats the one in its comprehensive aspect: its formation includes the many, and without that constitutive participation the net is not preserved. The movement keeps bringing the apparent opposition between one jewel and many jewels back to how each is present through the others.

The final test gives the contemplation a mark: put an ink dot upon the southwest jewel. It appears throughout the reflected field. Its many appearances do not require someone to have gone around physically marking every jewel. Begin with another jewel and the relation can be followed from there as well. That mark makes a return inspectable: something done at this definite place becomes present through the others, and the others disclose this place in turn.

Indra supplies the celestial name; the jewels, their clarity, their reflected images, the selected direction, the ink mark, the questioner and the answering contemplative voice each have work to do. This is the whole action of a teaching image. Its development occurs as each question changes what the initial sight of a shining net can mean.

The expanded sequence occurs in the Chinese text, T45 no. 1867, 513a20–513c16: selected jewel, reciprocal entry, questions, ink-dot test and closing qualification. The [CBETA primary-text transcription](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cbeta-org/xml-p5/master/T/T45/T45n1867.xml) makes that whole contemplative movement available. This telling is a prose paraphrase; Cleary’s selected printed English wording remains uncollated.

<a id="indra-simile-and-distinctness"></a>
## #2 — The image teaches where its own likeness ends

The teaching does not end with the ink dot, and it turns upon its own simile. Jewels have images which enter one another while their material substances remain separate; the phenomena for which the image is being used interpenetrate more completely. So the resemblance is partial, and the restriction belongs to the source's own movement, immediately after its most forceful demonstration. It prevents the observer from taking the optical apparatus as a complete account of the reality contemplated.

Its closing verses direct the teaching toward Vairocana’s activity for beings. Formless reality manifests forms; the immeasurable is present through particular appearances. The contemplative return has a purpose beyond admiration of an ingenious arrangement: the image is offered to open understanding, and its own inadequacy becomes part of that offering. Its jewel substances do not establish an ontology of permanently isolated things. Equally, the source’s stronger claim about interpenetration needs to be understood within Huayan, rather than translated without remainder into another tradition’s account of identity.

[Van Norden and Jones](../../../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/van-norden/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep.md) distinguish two contributions in their [fixed Fall 2024 Huayan account](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/buddhism-huayan/). In §1.3 each thing depends upon the others for existence and identity. Their §3 account of Zhiyan’s Ten Mysteries retains distinction within mutual inclusion and determination. Dependence and retained distinction together explain why the jewel’s comprehensive appearance belongs to a plurality whose members remain definite.

Each jewel is definite enough to be selected, reflected and marked. Its definiteness participates in the field which appears through it. Both the inward depth of the reflection and the outward plurality of the net are active. [C24 defines fusion](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C24-Fusion.md) as erasing the differences through which such inclusion works. So the comparison holds each local entrance together with its constitutive participation, allowing Huayan’s stronger account of interpenetration its own doctrinal scope.

The [homology and analogy field](../../../../episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) **defines** the crossing through homological agreement and analogical proportion. A definite local member discloses its constitutive relations, including their return through that member; this is the preserved operation. A different image can carry that operation in a specified respect while its objects remain different. Here the source makes the comparison more exact by naming the respect in which its own material likeness fails: reflected images enter one another while jewel substances remain separate. Stronger interpenetration belongs to the phenomena contemplated. [A12 defines whole and many](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) so that they retain constitutive participation, and [A13 extends this](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) by keeping determination and gathering distinct. This Huayan passage can correct a comparison which treats its partial optical likeness as complete.

## #3 — The definite jewel carries its constituting field

[A11 defines the two Ones](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A11-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md) as `0 = One`, Self-Identity, and `1 = All`, Self-Difference, and the concept record [C49 defines their reciprocal offices](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C49-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md), which let the condition become manifest through a determination that depends on it. One is no privileged jewel standing outside the net, and All includes the relations through which every local entrance becomes this one. The [Two Ones Matheme](../../../../matheme/ql/two-ones.md) **derives** the exact numerical and relational construction. A chosen jewel figures an already active relation becoming locally available, and each of the [native core](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) and [Definition of God](../../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md) **grounds** the determining relation and the finite account's answerability within it.

[A12 defines whole and many](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) as holding two aspects together: under its inclusive aspect the whole contains real particulars, and under its generative aspect it is their constituting relation. [Mono](../../../../matheme/mono-poly/mono.md) **derives** expression and return through `0/1` and `1/0` as different orientations. A jewel's presence within the others' reflections gives that return an image, since its local, comprehensive appearance keeps the dependence through which it is a member of the net.

Three distinguishable operations cross here. [A08 defines constitutive exclusion](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) as making a positive reference determinate through its relation to non-application, and [apoha](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C18-Apoha.md) **grounds** the account of nominally and verbally bound negations that work together without cancelling into an isolated positive. Dependent arising concerns conditioned occurrence, and saṃkalpa builds a world through constructive intention. Huayan adds recursive mutual inclusion: choosing a definite jewel keeps its constituting field active. So the crossing gathers the three while each keeps the object and relation it explains.

[A05 defines luminous appearing and reflexive articulation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md), and that sharpens the reflective crossing: Śaiva [Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C13-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **grounds** self-apprehension, retention and free formation as inseparable powers. A jewel displays reflected contents, and the reader's contemplation apprehends and follows their relation, so the two have different offices. An inert optical picture performs display, while the Śaiva living-mirror account develops an appearing that knows itself and generates its appearances. Exactness comes through what each does: recursive mutual inclusion in the net, self-apprehending articulation in the Śaiva account, and a situated reader whose understanding changes through the teaching.

[A18 derives the eight determinations](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md), which unfold `−/− → 0/1 → ?/! → −/+ → X/x → AM/IS → ∞/dx → 1/0`, under the parent `/ = −/−`, and the jewel teaching gives that traversal a body. A determination emerges within relation, so the selected point becomes question and response. Its contrast stays held, and the indefinite particular `x` takes part without exhausting `X`. Address carries the relation into an encounter, the local differential stays exact within inexhaustible articulation, and the return reopens the source relation through the achieved appearance. Choosing, questioning, following a definite contrast and meeting its limit change the reader's relation to the first appearance, and `X/x`, `AM/IS` and `∞/dx` keep their capacity and particular, personed and differential offices within that traversal.

This contemplation has six successive turns: selection, recursive entry, qualification, authored determination, answerable use and return. Its teaching movement and the eight determinations have different counts and offices, and the native `2+2²=6` keeps two terms and their four ordered pairings. Each of [the two logics](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md), [Dia / Syn](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C50-Dia-Syn.md) and their [formal construction](../../../../matheme/dia-syn/README.md) **defines** local cut and gathering explicitly: choosing a jewel makes attention determinate and keeps the field it distinguishes active. Polar relation, additive cancellation and directed appropriation stay different operations. Simultaneous reflection and the reader's passage through the teaching likewise have distinct temporal offices.

## #4 — A reflection becomes answerable for its use

The [Symbol, Account and Trust field](../../../../episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md) **extends** the image into four consequential operations. **Symbol answers to source.** This net discloses a source-specific relation, including the passage's qualification of its own simile. As [A20 defines image and valuation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md), even an impressive image stays alterable by what it represents, and its beauty can open attention while fidelity requires the relation through which the represented field can answer.

**Account does not replace source.** A description of what is reflected in one jewel is an articulation within the field. [C38 defines Bimba–Pratibimba](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) as a corresponding reference office: a locally agreed reference can truly be Bimba for a judgment while remaining pratibimba relative to wider sources. In a local comparison a judging agent holds the functional knower office, its reference, memory and rule are means, and the alternatives compared are known. That agent and apparatus can in turn be means within a person's containing inquiry, and [A26 grounds these nested offices](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) together. A wider source can correct the locally governing reference without making its earlier authority unreal.

**Trust keeps the return route active.** Using an account involves committed reliance on a relation it cannot exhaust. [A23 grounds the separation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) of making dependence inspectable from undertaking its continuation. A jewel's clarity shows the reflected relation, and an actual commitment needs a bearer able to answer for its use and consequence. [A27 extends this](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) by keeping belonging consequential: the Other can be within Mono while exceeding my possession, and a human participant's refusal can still change the encounter.

**Account re-enters source-field.** After an image guides action, its consequences become conditions of the next account. In the ink-dot episode a local mark appears throughout reflection, and conduct has another return: an encountered consequence can correct an answer, task, world-model, source, permission, evaluator or commission at the depth it warrants, and a fitting condition can instead be retained with reasons. Each of [objective co-internality](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) and [reciprocal world-constitution](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C42-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **defines** how these crossings are real through distinct local worlds. A later act inherits the corrected or justified condition, and the contributor can answer again. A reflected mark displays shared appearance, whereas an institutional return needs this actual receiving and later use.

The four operations hold together: the whole image gives the Symbol something definite to disclose, its textual limit corrects the Account, reliance carries the account into use, and the effects of use make the image and its sources answerable in a later encounter. That comparison has changed through the return: the likeness now keeps the relation and the exact respect in which its material cannot carry it.

The recursive net **compares** with [Ares, Aphrodite, Hephaestus and Poseidon](../../hellenic/ares-aphrodite-hephaestus-poseidon/WHOLE.md#ares-guarantee-release), and the two nets perform different acts. Mutual implication makes a local entrance disclose its constituting field, and capture organises bound participants as public evidence. Homer's bonds culminate in Poseidon's undertaking and release, and this contemplation returns through the jewel's reciprocal entry and the limit of its likeness. Laid beside the matheme, the difference is where the zero stands. In the jewel net relation is internal to every node, and the net keeps `(0/1)/(1/0)` active with nothing outside it administering the relation. In the Hephaestian mesh two exposed terms are held while a tribunal outside occupies the zero, and the essay's plate of the two nets writes that arrest as `(−1)+/−(+1)`. The comparison is an amplification in which two mythemic images, two mathemic expressions and two epistemic records are laid side by side, its status is Offered, and keeping both actions complete lets it distinguish relation from containment.

## #5→0 — Return to the jewel whose brightness depends on the others

The technical and ethical return asks what a local office does with the conditions it receives. [A29 derives delegated power and labour](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) as making the acceptance of a product answerable to the labour, costs and consequences borne elsewhere, and [commons](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly.md) **extends** that answerability by keeping practical powers available to their participants: to set terms, to refuse, to revise, to leave and to reconnect. The jewel net gives this inquiry a whole image. A definite participant's capacity depends on the relation it can help sustain, and institutional answerability depends on which affected participant can actually exercise each power.

[C62 defines planetary computation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C62-Planetary-Computation.md) as placing that question within material and institutional conditions, where energy, labour, infrastructure and authority need actual inquiry into their production and distribution. [A35 extends compassion](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md) to these origins and keeps them consequential. When sustaining people or environments bear excluded costs, the return reaches the terms of the commission able to answer for them, and loving sensitivity becomes practical through that route and through the next act it changes or justifiably sustains.

The whole **returns-to** [constitutive exclusion](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/10-s0-p3-apoha.md), which makes the selected member answerable through its contrast, whereas Huayan inclusion makes its participation visible through reciprocal reflection. It **returns-to** [the two-net encounter](../../../../../section-rooms/03-two-logics/movements/22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia.md), which places that positive recursive whole beside capture and release. And it **returns-to** [the sovereign commons](../../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/46-s50-p3-4-2-mono-poly.md), where locally authoritative centres share consequences and keep the power to contest a reference field, so the institutional architecture has to enact those powers through actual permissions, receiving offices and answerable crossings.

[A36 defines integral zero](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) as keeping an achieved Symbol capable of another traversal. The [Neumann archetypal relation](../../../archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-return-bond) **compares** here: it differentiates a local life and returns it through a bond that sustains further becoming, and this jewel contemplation gives relation, differentiation and return their Chinese body: a selected jewel, the reflected others and a reader whose encounter changes through their participation.

Return now to the southwest jewel. Its mark stays particular, its reflection among the others makes the starting point present again, and the source's final qualification keeps the viewer from possessing the teaching through a completed optical picture. What has changed is the encounter, since the chosen jewel can no longer be understood apart from the relations its brightness makes visible. It leaves a definite place from which to look again.
