# Chinese Huayan

The contemplation attributed to Dushun carries the jewel net, and its attribution is disputed. Indra's celestial setting and Indian name come from a different tradition than the Chinese Huayan doctrinal carrier. Cleary's translations of 1983 and 2000 are two containers of the text, and neither dates the narrated heaven.

Each of the [geographical route](../../atlas/geography/README.md) and the [temporal route](../../atlas/temporality/README.md) **returns-to** these same wholes.
