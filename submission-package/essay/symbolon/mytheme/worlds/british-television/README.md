# British television

*The Prisoner* is a television work. It enters here through selected written episode tellings and through a programme identity housed independently of them. The Village is the narrated place of confinement, and the British production world is a different geographical relation. The 1967–68 series and its episode order precede the later episode summaries and Taylor's reading of them.

Each of the [geographical route](../../atlas/geography/README.md) and the [temporal route](../../atlas/temporality/README.md) **returns-to** these same wholes.
