# Biblical Job

The biblical telling, Jung's interpretation and Taylor's three-office reading are attributed separately. Job's narrated land and divine council belong to the story, and the modern settings in which it is interpreted are different places. The KJV digital witness, the biblical narrative and Jung's later reception also sit at different times.

Each of the [geographical route](../../atlas/geography/README.md) and the [temporal route](../../atlas/temporality/README.md) **returns-to** these same wholes.
