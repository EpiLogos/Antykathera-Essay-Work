# Analytical psychology

Jung's symbolic history and Pauli's dream and commentary have separate authors and separate source passages. Modern psychological work is the setting in which Jung writes, and it differs from the many regions whose images he interprets and from Pauli's dream scene. *Aion*'s historical and aeonic interpretation concerns an age, while Pauli's egg sequence is one person's psychic sequence and keeps the letter and appendix witnesses that date it.
