# Mytheme

**Where you are:** [Reading root](../../README.md) › [#1 Symbolon](../README.md) › `#3` Mytheme

Mytheme carries the forms in which a relation can be seen, heard, suffered, remembered and transformed. Myth, narrative, poetry, media, art and music belong here when their **whole form** performs a movement of the argument. In the Vāk layering of the registers this is **madhyamā**: the formed visual and narrative operations built through the visionary logic that [Matheme](../matheme/README.md) carries.

The carrier is accordingly the whole relational image, story, scene or artwork in which the elements act together, and a detachable character, object or symbol is read only inside it. The Ares–Aphrodite–Hephaestus–Poseidon field is one whole before "Hephaestus" becomes an index of technē. Apollo, Eros, Daphne and Peneus form one whole before "Daphne" becomes an index of alterity or of the tree. Job's divine council, losses, friends, Wisdom, whirlwind and restoration form one whole before Leviathan, Satan or Sophia are amplified separately.

Mytheme works in laminar relation with the other two layers of the field. [Matheme](../matheme/README.md) supplies the operations (the signed polarity, cancellation and appropriation in both chiralities, `(0/1)/(1/0)`), [Episteme](../episteme/README.md) supplies the systematised records and sources, and the whole image supplies what neither can: the action of persons through which a relation is undergone. Each layer amplifies the others, in the sense of Jungian amplification, by laying parallels beside a figure until its operation shows. A mytheme discloses through the action of its whole image, which is a different office from formal proof and from historical attribution to the source that carries the image.

## Whole first, amplification outward

The operational order runs from the source outward and returns to it:

```text
source story / image / work
        ↓
whole relational Mytheme
        ↓
story-internal amplification
figure · object · animal · plant · element · place · action · sequence · affect
        ↓
cross-story comparison
        ↓
character / motif / archetype / elemental index
        ↓
higher-order constellation
        ↺
return to every whole from which the abstraction was drawn
```

A symbolic lexicon built in this order cannot stand in for meeting the image. "Poseidon," "serpent," "tree," "water," "king," "mother," "child," "sun," "shadow," "archon," "mask" and "aeon" can become powerful cross-Mytheme carriers, and each earns its range by comparing the different offices it performs inside intact wholes. None starts with a fixed meaning.

An archetypal index is therefore derivative and generative at once. It reveals a recurrent relation across variations because the variations remain recoverable, so the stories disclose the figure progressively and the figure does not explain the stories. A `constellations/` layer for figures, motifs and peoples gathered from exact whole occurrences is the ratified next surface and is not yet instituted; no index precedes the wholes it would be drawn from.

## Human amplification

Every interpretive Mytheme relation carries a relation-local boolean:

```yaml
human-amplified: yes | no
```

The boolean records one thing: whether Frank has supplied or ratified this symbolic, Jungian or Symbolon interpretation. Factual and source recovery keep their ordinary evidential status.

- `human-amplified: yes` gives the interpretation human authorial standing. The system may source, deepen, connect and articulate it without changing its office.
- `human-amplified: no` marks a plausible amplification the system has produced that still awaits Frank's encounter and ratification. It stays visible as a prompt and does not propagate as settled authorial meaning.

With this flag the system can carry out serious Jungian amplification while the human symbolic encounter at the centre of the project stays Frank's. A whole record may therefore hold fully sourced narrative material, several ratified interpretations, and a bounded queue of unratified symbolic possibilities.

## Record forms

The register holds five linked record types and keeps one direction of derivation among them.

1. **Whole Mytheme record**, the primary carrier. It tells the source work, story or scene fully enough that every active relation and transformation stays available, and it carries the relation-local human-amplification ledger for the project's interpretations.
2. **Story-internal element index.** A character, object, animal, plant, element, place, number, gesture or motif as it acts inside one named whole.
3. **Cross-story figure or motif index.** It gathers one figure or motif across two or more recovered wholes and records how its office changes.
4. **Higher-order archetypal constellation.** Serpent, tree, water, king, child, mother, shadow, trickster, sun, sea and similar fields, inferred through patterned variation across whole Mythemes.
5. **Comparative authored Mytheme.** A relation the project composes among otherwise distinct source wholes, with the composition itself marked as authored.

The last four never replace the first. Every abstraction carries reverse links to the exact whole-story moments from which it was earned, and every interpretive edge keeps `human-amplified: yes/no`.

A Whole Mytheme record identifies the work or source tradition and preserves the actors and relations its movement materially needs. It follows the sequence and transformations far enough for the ending and aftermath to qualify the beginning, and it states whether the whole figures, embodies, presages, inverts, occludes, gathers or returns a relation. It names the essay blocks it serves and the limit of the inference carried from image to argument. Its sixfold is derived locally from the whole and its relations, with the source sequence preserved, and the six positions carry no universal gloss.

Variant traditions stay whole in their own right. Where the project composes them, the composition is an authored cross-source relation and never a synthetic pseudo-original myth.

Frank's poems and first-person writings keep their wording, order and voice. Excerpts point to an authoritative complete text and carry a rights decision. External works carry creator, edition or release, scene locator and rights status through Episteme.

## Cross-register routes

Abstractions of character, motif and archetype remain Mytheme indexes while they gather images, and their conceptual definitions belong to Episteme only when the project deliberately institutes one. The operation that holds image and formal relation together returns to [Symbolon](../README.md).
