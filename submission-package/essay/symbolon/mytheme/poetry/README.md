# Poetry

Poetry carries Frank's poems and the external poems that perform necessary work in the essay. A complete poem is the primary object; excerpts remain linked to that whole and preserve lineation, punctuation, title and version.

Each record gives author, authoritative text or source, version, rights, exact essay use and the relation enacted by image, syntax, sound or turn. Frank's wording is never rewritten by an agent. A poem can embody or transform a relation without becoming evidence for a historical or formal claim.

Voice-calibration excerpts used during writing remain outside the public corpus unless the poem itself is admitted here.
