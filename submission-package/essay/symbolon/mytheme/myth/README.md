# Myth

Myth carries inherited **whole stories and scenes** whose actions disclose a live relation. The field now holds the whole Ares–Aphrodite–Harmonia and Hephaestus–Poseidon relation; Apollo, Eros, Daphne and Peneus; the project-composed Apollo–Dionysus–Daphne comparison; Job together with the later Jungian interpretation layered over it; the ouroboros, torus and trickster whole; the Attica contest; the *Hypostasis of the Archons* cosmology; and other mythic fields admitted by exact recovery.

The story is primary, and a record follows enough of its beginning, actors, setting, actions, reversals, transformations, verdicts and aftermath that the operation shows **between** the figures and is never assigned to them as a fixed attribute. Variant tellings stay distinct wholes until an authored comparison joins them.

Only after that recovery can a character or symbolic component be addressed on its own. Athena, Poseidon, Apollo, Daphne, serpent, tree, water, net, olive, laurel, whirlwind, Leviathan, Norea, Sophia, veil and ruler or archon can then be indexed across stories, provided the index says what the figure does in each whole, where its office changes, what relation recurs through the change, and how to return to the intact telling.

Higher-order archetypal fields are built the same way. "Serpent" has no dictionary meaning that decodes Cecrops, Python or the ouroboros. Cecrops as earth-born serpent-human, Python within Apollo's story and the ouroboros in its own whole are each recovered locally first, and only their differences and recurrences can then disclose a larger serpent field.

Every **project interpretation** attached to those wholes or to later indexes carries `human-amplified: yes/no`. `yes` means Frank has supplied or ratified the symbolic or Jungian appointment. `no` means the system has produced a candidate amplification, which stays available for Frank's encounter and does not propagate as if it had authorial standing.

Myth discloses liveability, enclosure, transformation, conflict, inheritance, return or failure through the action of its whole image, and that is a different office from proving a Matheme. Read together, the three layers amplify one another: the signed arithmetic names what the persons are doing, the records say whom and what the story is about, and the whole telling shows the operation undergone.
