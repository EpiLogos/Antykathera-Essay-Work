# Myth

Myth carries inherited **whole stories and scenes** whose actions disclose a live relation. The current field includes the whole Ares–Aphrodite–Harmonia / Hephaestus–Poseidon relation; Apollo–Eros–Daphne–Peneus; the project-composed Apollo–Dionysus–Daphne comparison; Job together with the later Jungian interpretation layered over it; the ouroboros / torus / trickster whole; and other mythic fields admitted by exact recovery.

The story is primary. A record follows enough of its beginning, actors, setting, actions, reversals, transformations, verdicts and aftermath that the operation is visible **between** the figures rather than assigned to them as fixed attributes. Variant tellings remain distinct wholes until an authored comparison joins them.

Only after that recovery may a character or symbolic component become separately addressable. Athena, Poseidon, Apollo, Daphne, serpent, tree, water, net, olive, laurel, whirlwind, Leviathan and similar figures can then be indexed across stories. Such an index must say what the figure does in each whole, where its office changes, what relation recurs through the change, and how to return to the intact telling.

Higher-order archetypal fields are built the same way. “Serpent” is not assigned a dictionary meaning and then used to decode Cecrops, Python, ouroboros or another image. Cecrops-as-earth-born serpent-human, Python within Apollo's story, and the ouroboros in its own whole are first recovered locally; only then may their differences and recurrences disclose a larger serpent field.

Every **project interpretation** attached to those wholes or later indexes carries `human-amplified: yes/no`. `yes` means Frank has supplied or ratified the symbolic/Jungian appointment. `no` means the system has produced a candidate amplification which must remain available for human encounter rather than being propagated as though it already carried authorial standing.

Its current human-amplified core includes Poseidon's sea/well as the archaic-magical/unconscious depth retained at the centre of Athena/Minerva's rational-civic form, the toroidal reading of that relation, and the olive's root→fruit→pressing→oil→anointing→flame/light chain. System-generated readings of Cecrops, the flood and other elements are retained separately as `human-amplified: no` until Frank works them.

This story-first law lets amplification expand the Mytheme world without atomising it.
