# Neumann images and plate relations

This record serves the shared genesis and return that every situated whole in the Mytheme register is read through. It lays three layers together: the mathemic (the signed polarity, cancellation and appropriation, `(0/1)/(1/0)` and the eight determinations), the epistemic (Neumann's account and the essay's records) and the mythemic (the images of round, parents, hero and paper in action). Each layer amplifies the others and keeps its own office. Its status is Argued.

<a id="neumann-round"></a>
## #0

The world has parents before the person who tells them apart can stand outside them. In Neumann's creation sequence heaven and earth, nourishment and danger, containing body and emerging child are not yet separate enough for a world to be lived in. Their possibility is held together in the round. Its enclosed outline carries the life that will differentiate it: womb, egg, enclosing waters, the serpent with its tail in its mouth. The future ego depends on this containing life long before it can represent the dependence, so the first pressure in the image is genesis, the way a world and a centre within it come apart through a relation neither makes alone.

What genesis carries here is **shared archetypal structuration**: genesis, world-parent relation, opposition, differentiation, separation and renewed interplay. It is the ground on which geographically situated tellings and temporal developments become comparable, because geography gives each telling its people, language and home, temporality gives its transformations their order and duration, and this structure is common to them. The essay's native QL account owns the universal field. Neumann's developed psychological morphology gives that field an imaginal and bodily refraction, and the images themselves show what the field is like to undergo. His sequence is therefore a refraction of the structure, and it supplies neither the structure's derivation nor a geographical address for it.

[Neumann's source house](../../../episteme/sources/psychology/neumann/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness.md) **sources** the telling: the uroboros, the Great Mother, the separation of the World Parents, the birth of the hero, the slaying of mother and father, the liberation of the captive and the treasure, and then the Osiris transformation. His book presents these as stages in the development of consciousness, and that stadial claim is Neumann's. What the essay claims is the priority of relation, which does not require every society or person to pass through this single historical sequence, and the source's historical limits do not shrink the shared operation to a local European image.

In the first round the paternal and the maternal are still together. Within the uroboros both are contained without either yet holding the exclusive office it will receive after separation. Calling it the mother stresses enclosure and nurture, and calling it the unity of the parents stresses the difference it holds before that difference becomes an opposition for a subject. No outline alone can decide between the two, and Neumann's movement begins when what contained the ego becomes something the ego can face, desire, fear and resist.

What follows paraphrases the canonical source account together with the page-anchored encounter preserved beside it, which is Taylor's reading of Neumann. That encounter is not a verified transcript of Neumann, so its source leads and Taylor's authored responses are kept apart throughout, and the quotation and collation debts are stated once, at the end.

<a id="neumann-metabolism"></a>
## #1

The round lives by taking into itself. Mouth meets tail, and eater and eaten belong to one body. Hunger keeps this body from being an inert totality: what sustains it passes through it, changes it and is released. Taylor's encounter, especially in the leads at pp.27–33, gives the round an interior movement through digestive and generative images: intake and expenditure, fullness and need, birth and reabsorption, systole and diastole. An infant's dependence on nourishment makes the movement intimate, since before an independent ego can say what the mother is, its life is already sustained through her.

As the Great Mother becomes the first object-face of the unconscious, the encompassing life acquires an address. What gives birth can take back, and shelter can become engulfment. Nurturing and terrible aspects belong to one relation whose terms are changing: the child needs the source from which it also needs to differentiate, and the mother as life-giver and the mother as devourer make that ambivalence visible without making any actual mother wholly kind or wholly destructive. Neumann's archetypal office is larger than a biographical parent, though it is met through the bodily dependency that makes the image compelling.

Receiving is an operation, and the metabolic passages matter for that reason. Food becomes the body of the receiver and alters it. Through Prajāpati, hunger, life and death, and the offering of the world to itself, the leads place creation inside this exchange. At p.29 the transmitted form is that Prajāpati creates the world as an offering to himself, horse and universe joined in the sacrificial image, and the passage received through Neumann and Deussen connects *aditi* with becoming an eater of the world. Taylor takes the receiving of a world into an inquiry about how experience forms an operative interior. At p.27 he brings Bhairava and Śaiva devouring into the same inquiry: Mono–Śiva receives and assimilates Poly–Śakti forms, and their integration can issue in samāveśa. His relation makes the receiver answerable to what the received world enables it to become. These Indian passages reach us through Neumann and the copied encounter, and their primary textual and translation homes are separate; what the encounter supplies is the pressure of a life whose generation includes expenditure and whose survival cannot consist in indefinite retention.

Taylor's authored refraction sharpens the difference into nourishment against monopoly. A self-eating round can figure assimilation that renews life, and the Saturnine devourer can figure taking that allows no new birth. What separates them is what the taking makes possible next. A structure that consumes its conditions while preventing their renewal cuts off the generative relation it lives on. Read through the signed arithmetic, intake and expenditure are the two poles of a held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, and the devourer is a pole that has taken the whole span for itself. That relation **qualifies** [monoisation and counter-generation](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C26-Monoisation-Counter-Generation.md): incorporation becomes counter-generation when the receiving centre monopolises the power of renewed emergence. Taylor develops the distinction between renewing nourishment and consumption that blocks generation from the bodily encounter, and no QL political or technical claim is attributed to Neumann.

At the leads marked pp.34–35 the same encounter introduces centroversion. Inner and outer movements are gathered toward the formation of a whole personality, which gives the later heroic struggle a destination beyond escape. A person who serves only an external collective, or is possessed only by an internal power, has not yet made the relation their own. A centre has to form without taking the conditions of its formation for its property. Neumann's book reaches that problem through separation and conflict, and centroversion lets it be heard already in the nourishing round.

<a id="neumann-world-parent-separation"></a>
## #2

Separation of the World Parents opens the interval in which a world can appear. Heaven becomes distinguishable from earth, and light can enter the space between them. An undifferentiated enclosure becomes an articulated field with directions and contrasts. Within this opening the ego emerges as a position able to discriminate, and what appears outside it becomes nameable because the same act of differentiation has given the observer a place.

At the encounter's p.105 lead the creation scene has its own sequence: the primordial person recognises itself and then divides into the paired figure of man and woman, held until then as if in an embrace. Self-recognition comes before sexual separation in this telling, so the androgyne has a relational office: the two who become partners arise through the differentiation of a prior togetherness. This image places sex and gender inside a genesis of relation, and its use here depends on keeping the before, the embrace, the division and the changed relation afterwards.

Neumann's psychological reading stresses the achievement of ego identity, the discriminating consciousness that says I am I. Taylor's response separates that achievement from the more primordial self-recognition of the native relation. Prakāśa–vimarśa does not first arise when an ego takes possession of an identity, because the finite experiencer and its inner instrument are determinations within the field of appearing. This distinction **returns-to** [the tattvic differential field](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) and **qualifies** [the bounded subject-position](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) and [the inner instrument](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md). Acquiring a determinate I is a real formation, and it cannot retrospectively produce the awareness in which the acquisition appears. Taylor's correction keeps the acquired identity inside that awareness, and the psychological and Śaiva sources keep their own evidence requirements.

Light brings more than a collection of visible objects. In the leads at pp.106–109 an oriented world appears: above and below, before and behind, inside and outside, I and you, sacred and profane, good and evil. Boundaries make a precinct, a possession, a law and an order of action possible. Some of these institutional consequences are Taylor's authored extension of the encounter and are not a verified universal history derived from Neumann. Their formal pressure is exact, since a measure both reveals a field and selects what will count within it, and [Māyā as operative measure](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) **qualifies** this refraction: differentiation is productive, and its danger begins when the selected measure hides its dependence on a wider field.

No fixed dictionary of light as good and dark as bad can hold the image of light as good and dark as bad. Light names disclosure from one position, and what that position leaves unlit belongs to the same field. Father and mother, active and receptive, inner and outer change office with the direction of the relation. Taylor's p.108 appointment of night at #0, day at #5 and four directions at #1–#4 gives the encounter a 4+2 articulation: the spatially specified field is held through implicate conditions of appearance and return. Four directions orient the emerged centre, and night and day keep that centred field related to the conditions through which it appears and returns. It fits this myth and does not assign every creation myth six episodes or make time and space interchangeable.

<a id="neumann-twins-retained-relation"></a>
The twins show the danger of separation in two faces within one genesis. Taylor's [core theorem account, IV](../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **sources** the named Ohrmazd and Ahriman appointment: the broken halves of a prior `0` become enemies when their division is made to deny their belonging. [Q27's energetics and Neumann return](../../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md) **grounds** the same operation at the good and evil twins. Their opposition has real force, and what changes is how the relation between them is held. Through `/` their different valences still have one ground, `(−1)/(+1)`. When the stroke becomes addition between isolated signs, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, the meeting cancels them. When it becomes subtraction, each twin counts the whole span as the other's departure: `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from one end and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the other, so that each meets the other only as what must disappear for the governing side to be whole.

Return has to preserve the two who can recognise their shared genesis. Blending them would destroy the difference through which recognition occurs, and a victory that abolishes the other's ground would perpetuate the divorce. Their prior `0` returns as their condition and not as the null result of their collision. Here is the pressure the hero will meet: what kind of separation permits a life in relation, and what kind makes continued separation its war? This authored image **returns-to** [Two Logics of Two — Dia / Syn](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) through the changed operator, and **returns-to** [Primordial Symbolon and Its Eight Determinations](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) without substituting two gods for its eight determinations. The names Ohrmazd and Ahriman come from the core, and no independent Zurvanite telling, chronology, ritual or account of an orthodoxy has been recovered for this page. Its judgment that the orthodoxy splitting them into a war is the fall administering itself is its mythemic interpretation, and historical attribution is a separate task. Neumann's comparative morphology supplies the shared separation field and does not source Taylor's notation.

Gaining a world brings the wound of separation. Neumann's guilt and fall motif, met at pp.114–115, joins creative light to the loss of original containment. An emerging ego suffers the break it has to accomplish, and its weakness and dependence do not vanish because it can name itself, since discrimination exposes them differently. Separation cannot simply be reversed without surrendering the differentiated life it enabled. This is why the story has to pass through the hero: something must bear the cost of leaving while finding a relation to what has been left.

<a id="neumann-hero-centroversion"></a>
## #3

The hero is born in the world the separation opened. Neumann's sequence gives him two emancipations, the slaying of the mother and then the slaying of the father. His struggle with the mother answers the engulfing origin from which he has to become distinct, and his struggle with the father answers the authority that can arrest that emergence in an inherited order. Had the telling stopped after the escape from the mother, the father's law could simply have inherited the power of enclosure. Keeping both struggles in sequence makes emancipation answer to the real formation of a centre and not to the replacement of one captor by another.

Violence belongs to the mythic and psychological telling, and its operation is differentiation from an overwhelming archetypal claim. Laid beside the signed arithmetic, a victory that abolishes the mother's ground counts the whole span from the hero's pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the engulfed ego's earlier position is the same span charged from the other end, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. What the telling asks of the hero is the held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, in which the centre keeps its relation to the origin from which it differentiated. Neumann's historically gendered morphology stays visible as his account. Taylor's relational reading follows the changing offices in the drama, including the earlier androgyne and the later possibility of partnership, and removing those changes would turn a developing relation into a static classification of persons.

Next come the captive, the treasure and liberation, which in the book's order precede the Osiris transformation. The achieved power to separate is now tested by what it releases and what becomes available through release. A captive freed into another form of possession would leave the deeper problem as it was. That judgment is the essay's authored pressure on the image: heroic efficacy has to be measured by the relation it makes possible, including the freedom of what it meets. It supplies no missing passage to Neumann's account.

In the Osiris division the book reaches its culminating transformation. Death and transformation keep the developmental movement from closing as the hero's permanent occupation of a victorious identity. Source navigation warrants this office and sequence, and a detailed retelling of the Osiris episodes needs primary tellings recovered separately. What matters here is the consequence: an ego strong enough to emerge has to become capable of transformation beyond its established form.

Centroversion gathers the movement. Forming a whole personality needs traffic between the centre and the powers from which it differentiated, and both engulfment and sovereign isolation obstruct it. Engulfment gives the centre away, and isolation makes its achieved form the measure of the whole. A centre develops by admitting what changes it while keeping enough differentiation to respond. This source-specific psychological account **returns-to** [individuation and recognition](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md). [Jung's *Aion*](../../../episteme/sources/psychology/jung/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2.md) **qualifies** the psychic distinction between ego and encompassing Self, and it neither authors the notation `X/x` nor proves Taylor's derivation after the fact.

Taylor's encounter leaves the distinction between the hero and the spiritual avatar as a live question. Heroic sacred marriage and uroboric reabsorption cannot be merged because both images contain union: one follows differentiation and can sustain relationship, and the other can abolish the separate position through which relationship is lived. Whether a particular avatar drama has a different office needs its own whole telling, and the encounter does not settle the identification. What this story has earned is a return through differentiated participation, and it does not entitle us to treat all transformations as one event.

<a id="neumann-paper-eightfold"></a>
## #4

Taylor's paper scene makes the return something a reader can perform. Begin with a blank sheet and write `1`. Attend to the paper on which the mark appears and write `0`, then attend to the act that distinguishes and relates them and inscribe the slash. Once written, `0/1` is itself a determinate figure on the sheet, and relative to the field through which it appears it too can be taken as `1`. Erase it. What disappears is the inscription, and the capacity for appearing, distinguishing and writing is not cancelled with it. The sheet is an image within that capacity and is not the literal substance of unobjectifiable awareness. A changed reader can begin again carrying the operation just performed, and paper, mark, slash and erasure bring recognition into an act the reader performs.

<a id="neumann-incest-erasure"></a>
The scene carries the more intimate pressure of Taylor's p.17 [authorial encounter](../../../episteme/sources/psychology/neumann/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness/neumann-1954-origins-history-consciousness-NOTES.md), which **sources** the connection between uroboric incest and erasure. In the encountered image desire turns toward being dissolved and absorbed into the round. Taylor reads the `1` slipping back into `0` and makes that movement performable on the page. First mark, acknowledged ground and slash have become one written figure, and erasing it releases its claim to stand as the ground it has named. Another inscription can then be received, and the capacity to distinguish stays live.

Laid beside the signed arithmetic, the difference between the two dissolutions is exact. Uroboric reabsorption surrenders the differentiated position altogether, which is cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, with the zero as a terminus. Enacted erasure releases a formed claim and keeps the capacity to form another, so its zero is a ground returned through. Sacred marriage has a third office: differentiated partners sustain a generative relation and do not disappear into undifferentiated containment. At p.17 the encounter connects the incest motif to *hieros gamos* while questioning the priority given to hero and spiritual avatar, and that question stays open. Taylor's amplification distinguishes the release of a formed claim from the loss of differentiated capacity, and it does not settle the hero and avatar hierarchy.

<a id="neumann-self-rolling-wheel"></a>
The next page lead gives the renewed act a moving image, the self-rolling wheel. Taylor's p.18 encounter places En-Soph, plenitude and nothingness beside the wheel and follows its rolling as a continuing self-composition about the unobjectified opening. A formed round has to keep being made. Its movement is the winding and weaving of a relation, and it is no finished circle bestowed once and left inert. Paper that can take another inscription and a wheel that keeps composing itself meet at this renewal, because each achieved figure belongs to the field through which another figure can appear.

So the wheel **returns-to** [the uroboros's interposition](../uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-interposition): a locally completed round can keep the passage through which it was made and return with a difference. Its opening stays constitutive without becoming another patch of surface. Taylor's self-composition gives the rolling wheel the work of Text/Texture, in which an achieved relation becomes the means of another formation. En-Soph's historical terminology, Neumann's exact p.18 wording and the encounter's proposed Whitehead comparison keep their independent source standing, and a self-rolling image establishes neither perpetual mechanical motion nor a topology theorem.

This scene **figures** [the Two Ones](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A11-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md) and **returns-to** their concept, [0 = One, 1 = All](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C49-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All.md). `0` bears the uncounted One, and `1` bears All, the whole manifest field as well as each indefinite particular within it. That contrast does not reduce to blank material and one isolated item. The first mark opens the possibility of a world of marks, their plurality is real, and their common condition does not become one giant object beside them. Consequently the scene **returns-to** [Mono/Poly](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md): the whole is structurally prior to its determinations, and each determination contributes a real difference to the field that returns.

[The native theorem spine](../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **grounds** the complete movement. [Six Positions, Eight Turns](../../../matheme/ql/eight-determinations.md) **defines** the exact relation between the positional body and its bracketed traversal:

`/ = −/− → 0/1 → ?/! → −/+ → X/x → AM/IS → ∞/dx → 1/0`.

The eight are determinations of one relation, with the parent relation and the return bracketing six interior determinations. They are not eight Neumann stages, and the sixfold narrative movement has a different office. The mythemic refraction follows their distinctions and does not derive them from an image. Laid beside the images, each takes a bodily or psychic form:

| Determination | What it does | Where Neumann's images carry it |
|---|---|---|
| `−/−` | The undelimited parent relation. | The unbounded, unformed fullness of the encounter; En-Soph at the p.18 lead and tohubohu at p.106, each with its own tradition. |
| `0/1` | Distinct offices emerge within one relation. | World-parent genesis. |
| `?/!` | Question and assertion: a determinate answer whose question stays live. | The opening of light. What appears can be specified because the field was capable of determination before the specification. |
| `−/+` | Withdrawal and extension: polarity as force. | Hunger, intake and expenditure. The two directions work through each other, and abolishing their distinction would abolish the operation. |
| `X/x` | A determining capacity receives an indefinite particular without being exhausted by it. | Ego and unconscious, or ego and encompassing psychic whole, as a named psychic refraction after the relation has been derived. This is Taylor's native QL notation. |
| `AM/IS` | First-person presence and third-person predication, met through the second-person address that changes both speaker and what can be said. | Recognition becoming sayable. The primordial I-am and the bounded ego's identity are not silently equated. |
| `∞/dx` | The inexhaustible field meets exact local determination. | The great round figures the field carried in each finite expression, which stays exact in its own office. An image of a circle establishes no analytic theorem. |
| `1/0` | The determination returns toward the condition it cannot contain. | The descent and return, the transformed personality and the erased inscription, each leaving a difference that has been undergone. The return is no longer the unconscious containment that preceded separation. |

Its formal universality belongs to QL, and Neumann's images give these operations their source-specific bodily and psychic life.

A supporting native field gives both parents a complete 4+2 in inverse orders. Essence persists while Structure and Formality exchange offices. Being at 0/1, Becoming at 2/3 and Knowing/unKnowing at 4/5 form the three harmonics, and the complementary folds of Essence at 0+5, Constitution at 1+4 and Text/Texture at 2+3 are a different operation. Both distributions coincide at 2/3 without being identical, which keeps a parent, a gender or a single symbolic episode from being frozen into one slot. [The primordial Symbolon and its eight determinations](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **grounds** that complete derivation, and the image returns its lived cost through nourishment, separation, conflict and changed recognition.

That cost requires both Name and Power. Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son and Image are accompanied by Play, Need, Sacrifice, Decision, Love and Work, because the visible image arrives through something lived: a need met, an alternative relinquished, a decision carried, a relation sustained. Power includes generativity and care as well as its possible capture, so the Great Mother cannot simply stand for pathology, and the father's authority does not automatically stand for truth. Formation and return **figure** the coequal series by following what formation costs and what its return releases. A formed image can enter another life, and it stays answerable to the work through which it came.

Taylor's toroidal correction belongs at this point of developed relation. Neumann's circular perfection remains the source image, and the authored refraction gives the uroboros a hole and a traffic through which taking becomes transformation. Position can present the round as a finished circle. Opposition exposes head and tail, appetite and food. Superposition makes their self-eating relation active in `(0/1)/(1/0)`. Interposition articulates the four explicate movements together with the implicate axis and opening, with #5 enfolding what has been traversed. A trickster's circle or sphere can conceal that operative opening, so the distinction concerns what the image lets the reader do, and it does not claim that every circular drawing is topologically a torus. Independently, the [uroboros, circulation and trickster whole](../uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md) **tests** whether shared archetypal structure has been mistaken for a closed picture, and it owns the developed drama to which this local relation returns.

In that correction Anuttara/Paramaśiva bears the unobjectifiable opening and Māyā bears measure in manifestation. Taylor's toroidal correction carries position, opposition, superposition and interposition through the differentiated image and its opening. Neumann is not credited with toroidal mathematics. [Homology and analogy](../../../episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) **qualifies** the crossing: the relation has to state which operation is preserved, which register changes and which evidence stays with its original source.

<a id="neumann-return-bond"></a>
## #5→0

Separation returns as a question of love or war. Taylor's p.121 encounter makes knowing-as-cutting answerable to knowing-with: a distinction can individuate two terms in their relation, or claim that its cut has abolished what relates them. The marriage image carries the indelible bond of a shared genesis, and even denial takes place through the field it denies. This is a formal and mythemic assertion about relation. Separation made the world and the responsive centre possible, and its return has to preserve that achievement.

That exact difference **returns-to** [the two logics of two](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md). Polar distinction is necessary. Its collapse into cancellation or appropriation is a different operation: `(−1)+(+1)=0` cancels the signed terms, while `(−1)−(+1)=−2` and the inverse subtraction `(+1)−(−1)=+2` carry the two orientations of appropriation. Through the native relation `(0/1)/(1/0)` the directions stay together without turning QL's slash into ordinary division by zero. Taylor's guilt reading places the loss in a determination disowning its `/0`, and the relation itself has not been annihilated. Taylor's cut-and-bond, love-and-war reading asks the differentiated terms to recognise their relation without surrendering their difference, so the claim survives with productive difference and with the right of actual others to answer on their own terms.

Now the differentiated world sees its own means of seeing, and it **returns-to** [contextual transparency](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) and **figures** [diaphaneity](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C09-Diaphaneity.md): light, boundary and orientation become available together with the source relations and exclusions that made them effective. [Gebser's source house](../../../episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin.md) **compares** a distinct account of consciousness structures and integral transparency. Its contextual return can illuminate this passage, and neither source supplies a one-to-one identity between Gebser's structures and Neumann's stages, so the shared field keeps the two accounts in their own temporal and epistemic offices.

A centre's power to discriminate **returns-to** [local arbitration](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md), **qualifies** [the complex](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C31-Complex.md) and **extends** the question into [image, valuation and possession](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md). An image helps organise action, and possession begins when that organisation claims the authority of its own source. Great Mother as engulfing power, father's arresting law and victorious hero are different possible occupants of that office, and none is condemned for giving a world its form. [Arbitration, hybris, regard and anamnesis](../../../episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md) **qualifies** the return of judgement: the determining centre has to recognise the conditions of its measure and allow encounter to alter it. Each of [the living symbol and idol](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) and [the archetype](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C32-Archetype.md) **qualifies** the source and image distinction: an image stays alive while the relation can change its meaning, and its power of disclosure is lost when its present interpretation is made final.

A liberated relation **returns-to** [trust, faith and the formal limit](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) and [self and other without possession](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md). A shared origin removes no risk and gives no participant ownership of another. Trust has force because the response cannot be manufactured in advance, and recognition includes the possibility that an actual other changes the terms of the encounter. Captive and heroic union carry this pressure as images, and they prove neither the historical nor the ethical claims of every story to which those names have been applied.

For [mind as worldhood](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md), to which this whole **returns-to**, the developing centre and its world form together through discrimination, valuation, inherited relation and response. The shared field supplies more than a sequence of private mental contents, because it is a condition of intelligible individuation. A technical extension asks how an agent's context, memory, tools and collective relations can make that formation inspectable. Taylor's p.35 encounter keeps introverted and extroverted relations active within centroversion: material received from either direction contributes to a provisional whole that can still be formed further. An agent's operative interior, the human who commissions and receives its acts, and the shared inherited field have different offices in that relation, and a coherent persona becomes more exact when those constituting relations can enter its next act. Taylor's encounter also proposes reversing the individual-to-collective developmental route, and that proposal asks how a formed individual could alter the encompassing collective, which needs a mechanism beyond the psychological account of centroversion received here.

This same distinction **returns-to** [idealism and order of dependence](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md). In the essay the primordial relation is ontologically and epistemically consequential, and it is not the chronological manufacture of the world by a private ego. No centre can make the conditions of appearing its possession. Its return therefore **returns-to** [compassion as loving sensitivity to origins](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md): what has become this person or this act includes nourishment, deprivation, dependence, separation and labour, and knowing those origins becomes compassion through the orientation that refuses to consume the being it understands. Responsibility remains, and a more adequate field becomes available in which to exercise it. [Taylor's Draft 3 source house](../../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md) **extends** that return through the offered work and the reader's office of remembrance.

Finally, this whole **qualifies** [the first advent of zero](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A10-Advent-of-Zero.md) and **returns-to** [the advent of integral zero](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md). They let the exact sign re-enter a living symbolic relation after its mathematical office has been earned, and the return keeps that office: as the transformed centre keeps its differentiation, the sign becomes Symbol again without ceasing to be sign. What comes back is the capacity to begin with the source relation visible in the determination: a mark made, a world opened, a separation borne, an image released to be answered.
