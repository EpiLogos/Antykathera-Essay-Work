# Six Positions, Eight Turns

## #0 — Keep the two address systems distinct

The input is the six-position body that [binary self-relation](binary-and-binary-of-binary.md) generates. The [core theorem spine](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), §II, **derives** two readings of it: the qualitative determination of a knowing-act, and the processual slash-form at each position. This record sets the two tables side by side, so that a sign repeated in both cannot make one operation stand in for the other.

## #1 — The qualitative body

| Position | Determination | Distinct operation |
|---|---|---|
| #0 | `0/1` | Conscious circumstance |
| #1 | `?/!` | Questioning and asserting |
| #2 | `−/+` | Reserve and issue |
| #3 | `X/x` | Determining capacity and recurrence |
| #4 | `AM/IS` | Personed context |
| #5 | `∞/dx` | Local exactness and unbounded horizon |

[The definition projection](../definition/six-determinations.md) derives each transition rather than assigning these meanings from the count alone.

## #2 — The slash-form reading

| Position | Slash-form | Office in the processual reading |
|---|---|---|
| #0 | `(0/1)/(1/0)` | Full parent relation |
| #1 | `0/0 = %` | Native indeterminate potency |
| #2 | `0/1` | Expression |
| #3 | `1/0 = ?/!` | Incomputable inverse |
| #4 | `1/1 = 100%` | Achieved wholeness |
| #5 | `(1/0)/(0/1)` | Full inverse parent |

The `%` reading of `0/0` is native notation, since ordinary division assigns it no stable scalar. The qualitative `?/!` at #1 and the processual `?/!` at #3 have different offices, and their difference is part of the field's design and calls for no repair of the numbering.

## #3 — Bracket the six

The parent `/ = −/−` precedes the qualitative body, and `1/0` returns its achieved expression towards ground. Thus the traversal has eight turns while the positional body has six:

$$
-/-\;\to\;[0/1,\;?/!,\;-/+,\;X/x,\;AM/IS,\;\infty/dx]\;\to\;1/0.
$$

The brackets display a distinction of address. The inverse relation can be active locally within the sixfold and globally as the returning threshold, so the forms of `1/0` need not be one serial stage.

## #4 — Harmonics and inverse phase

The qualitative forward pairs are Being `0–1`, Becoming `2–3`, and Knowing/unKnowing `4–5`. Complementary folds are Essence `0+5`, Constitution `1+4`, and Text–Texture `2+3`. A pair shared by both readings remains one pair doing two relational jobs.

[Process](../process/README.md) **extends** the full chain and its inverse-phase positions, and definition and quilt keep the short chain. File 2's inverse-phase primes and File 3's Night-pass primes are told apart by their source-local tables, and sharing a glyph gives no ground for merging them.

## #5→0 — Return with the correct address

The result is a cross-readable field in which each sign keeps the operation that places it. A local `?/!` can now be followed to its definition or to its process, and following one leaves the other where it was.
