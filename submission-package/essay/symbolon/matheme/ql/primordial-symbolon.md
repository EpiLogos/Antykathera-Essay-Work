# `0/1` ↔ `1/0` — The Native Relational Derivation

## #0 — The terms and their relation

Let `0` name the unobjectifiable ground and `1` the determinate articulation. Let `/` name the activity that differentiates and relates their offices. The input is a knowing-act, and the two terms are no independently supplied numerical magnitudes. [The root `0/1`](../../0-1.md) **grounds** their reading as singular One and polyvalent All, and the [core theorem spine](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), §IV, **sources** the formal unfolding.

## #1 — Reverse the orientation

`0/1` reads the ground through its expression. Interchanging the roles in the inscription gives `1/0`, the expression turning towards its ground. Both terms remain in the relation, with their positions across the slash reversed. QL therefore writes `0/1 = 1/0` as a relational identity: the same relation read from its two ends.

Ordinary field arithmetic gives the two a different result: `0/1=0`, while `1/0` has no defined field value. The native relation puts this defined/undefined seam to work and gives zero no multiplicative inverse.

## #2 — Relate the two orientations

Apply the slash to the whole pair of readings:

$$
\frac{0/1}{1/0}.
$$

The numerator and the denominator each carry a complete relation. The outer slash holds them through AND/OR: their difference lets one orientation be selected, and their belonging lets both be held as one field. The result is a relation with its own contrary reading available inside it.

## #3 — Unfold the internal pairings

The two retained terms admit four ordered pairings: `0/0`, `0/1`, `1/0`, `1/1`. Keeping the two terms as well as their four relations gives `2+2²=4+2`. [Binary and binary-of-binary](binary-and-binary-of-binary.md) derives the count explicitly.

The returning parent `(1/0)/(0/1)` carries the same whole in inverse phase, and its reversal changes order and orientation and adds no second system. [The eight-determination projection](eight-determinations.md) **extends** the qualitative body, and the pair-count itself yields six interior positions.

## #4 — State the vantages

The [Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) distinguishes the short definitional chain from the full processual one. Definition and quilt carry `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 0/1`; process and music earn `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 1/0 = 4′+2′ = 5′→0′ = 0/1`.

The primes in File 2 label inverse-phase positions, and File 3 uses primes for its Night-pass sequence, which is a different source-local office. A shared glyph gives no ground for exchanging the two tables, and the full chain gives none either.
