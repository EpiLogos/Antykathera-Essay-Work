# §0 — −/− = AND/OR / X/x

## #0 — Two marks before their names

Strip the assigned meanings of `0/1` back to the minimal inscription `−/−`. Its inputs are two marks of the same kind, a difference of placement, and the relating stroke. The marks have no independently assigned positive or negative values at this stage.

The [experienced relation](the-matheme.md) stays in play through this reduction. In File 1 §0 of [Taylor's Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md), taking away the terms' names leaves the [relating stroke](../../the-slash.md) as the one thing left to inspect, and it shows itself to be the means by which the two marks are distinguished.

## #1 — Placement differentiates

The two dashes can be recognised as the same mark because their identity survives their different positions. The slash makes that positional difference visible within one expression. The relation therefore has two simultaneous requirements: the marks remain identifiable, and their distinction remains operative.

Both requirements hold before we choose names such as knower/known or ground/mark. Those names determine a relation that is already operating in the inscription, and the differentiating is in force before its terms acquire them.

## #2 — OR and AND

Read through **OR**, either position can be selected: this mark or that mark, this side of the relation or its other side. Read through **AND**, their co-presence is held as one relation. Selection does not manufacture the field of alternatives from which it selects; holding does not abolish their difference.

The slash thus carries `AND/OR`, which is selection among alternatives together with their holding in one relation. A Boolean connective is a different thing: it returns a truth value according to its truth table, and the table is the rule by which the result follows. The result of the slash is an inscription that can select and hold distinguishable aspects of itself.

## #3 — One letter, two cases

Write the relation as `X/x`. Capital `X` names determining capacity; lower-case `x` its determinate face. The same letter retains the identity that a difference of case makes articulate. The operation enriches placement with an intentional office: anything capable of determination and something becoming determined.

The slash must go on conducting both belonging and distance. If the instance exhausted the capacity, no further determination would be possible, and if the instance were severed from the capacity, it would no longer make that capacity legible. [The X/x root](../../X-x.md) **embodies** the relation across recurrence and personed life.

## #4 — The copula becomes necessary

Identifying the same across distinct appearances requires an account of identification itself. [The copula projection](copula.md) extends the minimal graphic material: parallel strokes form `=`, and the slash through them forms `≠`. Difference can now be written as identity under cut.

This transition earns the next formal development. The native derivation makes explicit the relation required for something to appear *as* something, and every particular claim of identity then has to say what is being identified and in which respect, since two inscriptions that look alike prove nothing about the thing identified.

## #5→0 — Relation returns enriched

Bare distinction has become intentional relation and identification. In the short definitional chain `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 0/1`, counting unfolds this retained relation into its operative pair and four appearances.

[Identification through difference](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md) **derives** how the same is held across its distinct appearances. The [eight determinations](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **extend** the relation into its qualitative body through an [ordered traversal](../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md). The parent `−/−` stands outside the six interior determinations and gives their differentiations a common source.
