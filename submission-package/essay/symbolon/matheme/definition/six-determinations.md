# §5 — The Six Determinations

## #0 — The conscious circumstance enters the worked copula

The input is lived `0/1`, received through the slash already worked out as `/ = ( = =/≠ ≠ )`. The operation is to derive the qualitative relations through which this knowing-act becomes accountable. The count of six gives six places and says nothing about what occupies them, so the meanings have to come from the act itself.

In File 1 §5 of [Taylor's Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md), a poem unfolds the one knowing-act through ground, questioning, force, recurrence, personed context and horizon. The [core theorem](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), §II(c), **defines** their ordered sequence, and the [twelvefold setting](../../eight-determinations.md) **extends** it by giving each determination its conjugate face.

## #1 — Ground becomes answerable

**0/1 — Ground.** Awareness is the condition in which following, consequence, and appearance are given. It is encountered through what appears while remaining unobjectifiable as another appearing term. The poem's first line relates being beyond consequence to being consequential in every appearance.

**?/! — Definition.** Once something appears, it calls for account. Asking what it is has already made a provisional mark; asserting an answer remains exposed to whether it runs true. The second line, concerning what allows an essence, refuses to promote that enabling condition into one further essence.

The step from ground to definition is forced by the operation: a field that is merely present becomes an accountable field once it sustains a question and a mark.

## #2 — The mark gains force and recurrence

**−/+ — Dynamis.** A mark makes a difference by withholding and issuing. The poem's journey and residence hold extension with reserve in one pulse. The positive appears through the ground's own self-relation, rather than being imported as an independently existing opposite.

**X/x — Pattern.** Force becomes recognisable when it can recur. The temporal particular *x* makes its determining capacity *X* legible; one letter in two cases preserves their relation. The poem's sojourning and presiding conduct this difference. The small *x* also carries multiplication: an instance can compound and generate further instances.

So force acquires a recognisable form, and the form keeps the force through which it lives.

## #3 — Pattern is personed and opens a horizon

**AM/IS — Context.** A recurring form is lived from within. First-person presence and third-person describability meet through second-person relation. The poem's total yet incidental context is the world accompanying every appearance without ordinarily appearing as one more object.

**∞/dx — Realisation.** No finite context exhausts the field in which knowing occurs. The differential makes local change exact; the unbounded names the depth no local mark contains. The poem's silence and reverence articulate this precision without possession.

Personed knowledge thereby stays answerable to a depth it cannot enclose. The six together form one qualitative body in which each determination has the place its operation gives it, so none of them can be swapped for another as a metaphor for awareness.

## #4 — Brackets, harmonics, and folds

The parent `−/−` precedes these six qualitative determinations. The return `1/0` turns their achieved expression towards its ground. Together they give eight turns, **(4+2)+2**, while the interior positional body remains six.

The forward harmonics pair Ground and Definition as Being, Dynamis and Pattern as Becoming, and Context and Realisation as Knowing/unKnowing. Complementary folds pair `0+5` as Essence, `1+4` as Constitution and `2+3` as Text–Texture. These pairings make the sequence traversable in both directions and cross-readable. One boundary protects the reading: `?/!` at qualitative #1 and the incomputable `1/0` at processual #3 are two real mappings with different offices, so the slash-form table of the processual sources and this qualitative sequence are two tables, and neither is the other.

## #5→0 — The foundation is recognised through finding

The poem's closing movement says that the mental cannot find the foundation of the mind, since every act of finding already works through the condition it would turn into an object. [1/0](../../1-0.md) **derives** the return of the achieved articulation through this recognition. The short definitional chain recognises the achieved relation at this point, and process and music carry the inverse-phase circuit through its further transformations.

The [catuṣkoṭi crossing](catuskoti-crossing.md) **extends** these relations from description into lived recognition. The [eightfold relation](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) and its [ordered traversal](../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/25-s3-p0-eight-determinations.md) fix every determination's exact place, so the account can become more articulate while its condition never becomes one more content.
