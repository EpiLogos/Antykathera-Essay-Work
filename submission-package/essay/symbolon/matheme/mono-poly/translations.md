# Translations — Correspondence with Its Proof Boundary

## #0 — State the two signatures

A translation begins with a source notation, a target notation and the relation proposed to survive the passage. The [core spine](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) and the [Binary house](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) **source** a field rich in correspondences, and this page makes their different proof burdens operational.

Three claims are kept apart: an internal derivation, an exact construction in another formalism, and the essay's interpretation of the correspondence. Any one can succeed while another stays Argued or Offered.

## #1 — Perform an exact reduction

`64/36=16/9` reduces a fraction by dividing numerator and denominator by 4. Its inverse reduces `36/64=9/16`. No interpretive bridge is needed for either equality.

The core's ratio-family operation also cross-compares components: `(4:2)` against `(3:3)` produces `(4/3,2/3)`. Here “against” means coordinatewise comparison. Ordinary division of the two scalar ratios would instead give `(4/2)/(3/3)=2`. Naming the operator preserves the authored pair without misreporting scalar arithmetic.

## #2 — Build and test a representation

The binary pairing set has four members; adding the two single terms as a separate level gives six. A six-position display can represent this count. The choice of positions is a construction whose extra relations must be stated.

A musical example maps a cyclic index `k` to pitch class `2k mod12`. For `k=0,…,5`, the image is `0,2,4,6,8,10`, returning at 6. This map preserves the six-step cyclic successor as a whole-tone step. It does not preserve ordinary numerical addition without modular interpretation, and it says nothing yet about just-tuned interval products.

## #3 — Name the argued recognition

In the native field, `0/1` and `1/0` are obverse readings of one relation. In ordinary arithmetic, they are not equal real numbers: the latter is undefined. The relational identity is stated in its native signature, with the arithmetic asymmetry preserved as the formal pressure it uses.

Similarly `1={∅}` is exact in von Neumann's construction, and reading that singleton as a symbolic linker is an explicit synthesis. The set-theoretic theorem establishes the construction, and the unobjectifiable subject and each symbolic use of a mark need their own arguments.

## #4 — Record what a translation leaves out

A pitch-class reduction forgets octave height. A rounding map can forget small frequency differences. A scalar sum forgets its summands. These operations are useful within scope, but an inverse cannot recover discarded information without additional data.

The register therefore retains source terms alongside a translated result whenever return needs information the map omits. The same rule distinguishes the Two Locks: replacing File 2 inverse-phase primes with File 3 Night-pass primes would alter their offices, even though a typographic normalisation might make the pages look uniform.
