# §1 — The Jungian Architecture

## #0 — The Self as equation

The question following the etymological circuit is who values, measures and loosens. In the [third quilt](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md), native `X=x` relates unexhausted reserve and conscious specification through the [complete psychic operation, §VII](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md). Its psychological reading lets the determined self-description return into the wider life it articulates. The equation and slash notation are authorial QL, not Jung's written formula.

`X` is unexhausted reserve; `x` the capacity for conscious specification, of which a particular ego is one determination; `=` the equating power. The equation makes identity-through-difference available as an operation rather than reducing reserve and expression to two independent things.

## #2 — Move from content to context

Take a settled self-description, “I am always the competent one.” It specifies an `x` and organises actual conduct. When an excluded limitation appears, adding another flattering predicate can leave the same equation of selfhood intact. The context move asks how the description is being sustained: by which relations, expectations, capacities and exclusions.

The parentheses in `X=(x)` give that determination its bounded place. They can be permeable conditions for recognition or become walls defended against the excluded field. The native operation works the former: the particular remains actual while its conditions become legible. [X/x](../ql/x-x.md) gives the broader specification derivation.

## #3 — The fourth changes the office of the three

The mediated triad is real, but the context that holds it cannot simply be another term doing the same job. The 3–4 pressure appears here: three modes recognised together require the change of standpoint that makes their shared field available. The three harmonics similarly remain distinct while context holds their relation.

The authorial reading of Maria's axiom takes its alchemical circuit: one becomes two, two becomes three, and the one returns as fourth. The sesquitertian comparison gives the completing relation the ratio `4/3`, with inverse `3/4`. Multiplying three equal units by `4/3` gives four; the difference is one third of the original three. That exact proportional construction is distinct from a proof of psychological completion by arithmetic.

A quantum measurement analogy would require its own formal and empirical relation; the psychic operation does not depend on claiming that identity.
