# §3 — The Topological Form

## #0 — The quotient relation

Let integer pairs act on the real plane by translation: `(m,n)·(x,y)=(x+m,y+n)`. Declare two points equivalent when their difference lies in `ℤ²`. The quotient is the torus, `T²=ℝ²/ℤ²`, equivalently `S¹×S¹` through `(x,y)↦(e^{2πix},e^{2πiy})`.

This is the exact input and operation beneath File 3's topological quilt in the [Binary Explication](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md). The [native topological reading, §VIII](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **derives** the identification of points under a stated relation together with the cover through which their difference stays available. [Hatcher’s topological reference](../../episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/hatcher/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology.md) **sources** the selected construction at the level of paraphrase, and no uncollated passage is quoted.

## #1 — Count the presentation and the quotient separately

A fundamental square has four corners and four oriented boundary edges. Opposite edges are paired to form the torus; all four corners become one vertex. The resulting cell structure has `V=1,E=2,F=1`, hence `χ=1−2+1=0`.

The curated `4g+2g` reading at genus 1 counts four boundary sides in the presentation and two independent generators, and File 3's corner-based positional display is a source-local construction view. The quotient has one vertex, and its Euler characteristic is 0, so six is no Euler characteristic and counts no four distinct vertices. The sixfold is the native coordination of the explicit presentation with the generating relations, and each has its own office.

## #2 — Follow a loop through its cover

A torus loop based at the image of `(0,0)` lifts to a path beginning at `(0,0)` in the plane and ending at an integer pair `(m,n)`. That displacement records its winding class. For example, `t↦(2t,t)` projects to a closed loop with class `(2,1)`. Its cover endpoint differs from its start even though its surface address returns.

The two generator classes `(1,0)` and `(0,1)` are independent and commute; `π₁(T²)≅ℤ×ℤ`. A mixed loop `(1,1)` uses both. The cover is infinite-sheeted: every surface point has infinitely many lattice-related preimages. The native two-face reading is not a claim that this cover has two sheets.

## #3 — Retain the different topological images

The hole, the surface and the circulation give the quilt's ground, manifestation and returning relation a topological body, and the harmonics, the catuṣkoṭi and the Borromean reading coordinate those aspects in the authorial register. A Borromean link is a three-component link construction in its own right, and two torus generators are not yet that construction.

A sphere has trivial fundamental group and is still topologically rich in other respects, and the torus's noncontractible loops give the contrast that matters here. They carry no physical circulation and prove no universal energy conservation. The Lacanian demand/desire comparison relates an articulated demand to the desire that exceeds what it asks for, which is a psychoanalytic relation and differs from a loop's lifted displacement. Independent winding keeps its exact topological result, and the proposed cross-reading stays answerable to the particular Lacanian passages.

## #4 — Give pitch its quotient

For positive frequency `f` relative to `f₀`, let `u=log₂(f/f₀)`. Octave equivalence identifies `u` with `u+n`, producing `ℝ/ℤ≅S¹`. Thus `f₀`, `2f₀` and `4f₀` share a pitch class while remaining different register heights. Keeping height restores the covering coordinate; a helix can display the relation.

In the twelve pitch classes, stepping by 2 partitions the field into `{0,2,4,6,8,10}` and `{1,3,5,7,9,11}`. These are two six-orbits of one cyclic action, and the two independent generators of a product torus are a separate structure. [Music's substrates](../music/chromatic-substrates.md) **extends** the selected tonal construction, and its bimba/pratibimba reading is native.

The proposed toric-code witness concerns global logical cycles under specified coding assumptions. Local errors can occur and accumulate into logical errors, so “no local disturbance can reach” the information is no unrestricted immunity theorem. That engineering witness needs its own evidence, and the quotient alone does not supply it.
