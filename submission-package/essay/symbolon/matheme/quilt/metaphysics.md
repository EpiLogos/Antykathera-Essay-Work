# §4 — Kashmir Shaivism: The Metaphysical Architecture

## #0 — Prakāśa-Vimarśa

Luminous awareness and its self-apprehending power enter the [metaphysical quilt](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) as a relation, whose [tattvic movement, §X](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) gives ground, inward differentiation, directed manifestation, separating measure, bounded knowing and recognition their distinct offices. The sixfold is QL’s compression of that movement; the numerical correspondences below retain their separate warrant.

The native operation relates awareness and manifestation without turning either into a self-sufficient substance. `0/1` and `1/0` give the subjectward and objectward readings. Their relational identity is not ordinary scalar equality, and reflective awareness is not reduced to one detached object among its contents.

## #1 — Trace the six major turns

| Position | Śaiva movement | QL compression |
|---|---|---|
| #0 | Paramaśiva / Anuttara | Unobjectifiable ground |
| #1 | Śiva–Śakti | Consciousness and manifestation-power internally differentiated |
| #2 | Sadāśiva / Īśvara | “I am this” / “This am I,” two orientations |
| #3 | Sadvidyā / Māyā | Balanced relation and separating measure |
| #4 | Five kañcukas + Puruṣa | Contracted powers and situated knower |
| #5 | Pratyabhijñā | Recognition retaining the differentiation |

The table compresses the major movements; it does not replace the thirty-six categories or attribute a six-position organisation to the Śaiva authors. Downstream of the contracted knower, the field includes Prakṛti, the inner instrument, sensory and active faculties, subtle elements and gross elements.

## #2 — Make contraction concrete

The five kañcukas specify limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and ordering or necessity. In a situated act these appear as what can be done, known, sought, done now and done under these conditions. These five limits give a life its finite ratios of capacity, knowing, desire, time and order. Their relation makes bounded activity possible; they are not five independent prisons added to an otherwise complete object.

Within the inner instrument, buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates under “I/mine,” and manas coordinates alternatives. A judgement, an appropriation and a shifting alternative can each become available to experience. Their objective internality is therefore distinguished from the experient-position to which they appear. An artificial agent can occupy the local functional knower’s office when it selects and judges through its available means, with the task, alternatives or result as known. Within the containing person’s inquiry, that agent and its processing become part of the means through which a world is disclosed. Reopening the local centre to inspection makes it known in another act without cancelling its prior functional office. The whole relation is Life/Mind; the first-person fact of appearing remains the condition through which judgement, appropriation, alternatives and their inspection become available, rather than one more component in their inventory.

## #3 — Work recognition and its approaches

The return makes those determinations legible as the field's own articulations. It does not erase a finite capacity or turn an individual into an unlimited empirical agent. The claimed continuity concerns the ground of appearing through its determinate modes.

The authorial quilt reads the three upāyas through three harmonics: āṇavopāya through individual effort/Being, śāktopāya through means and thought/Becoming, śāmbhavopāya through direct recognition/Knowing-unKnowing. The harmonic placement is the declared QL mapping; the traditions' practices retain their own sources and cannot be generated from the positional labels.

The native witness argument is exact in its stated register: an experienced or asserted cessation cannot, by that act, remove the presupposed position of appearing. The [immutable-subject projection](../definition/immutable-subject.md) works its fact/content distinction. The [Spandakārikā house](../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/kallata/spandakarika-dyczkowski-1992-stanzas-vibration/spandakarika-dyczkowski-1992-stanzas-vibration.md) has no acquired passage for the exact Kṣemarāja wording, so no direct quotation is promoted here. The internal argument survives that attribution debt.

## #4 — Hold the Lacanian encounter and the numerical boundary

The Lacanian encounter places *mi-dire*, the not-all and unwritable rapport beside recognition. Its operation keeps articulated half-saying distinct from the position for which articulation occurs. The proposed relation of not-all to Anuttara keeps articulated half-saying answerable to its condition; it is not a classical quantifier equivalence or a theorem Lacan derived from Gödel. Recognition is not introduced as one more exempt signifier founding a totality.

Likewise `36=6²` is exact arithmetic, while the identification of the thirty-six tattvas with a complete six-by-six QL map remains a proposed numerical resonance without a worked category-level correspondence. `64+36=100`, `16+9=25` and `16/9` are respectively sums and a ratio, with scale mediation required before a100-unit count is called 100% wholeness. Keeping count, sum, ratio and scale mediation distinct preserves each equation’s exact force within the native relation. The proposed tattvic correspondence retains its separate category-level question.

## #5→0 — Enriched 0/1

The result is differentiated manifestation returned through its source-relation. The quilt keeps `0/1=4+2=5→0=0/1`; the later Night pass has its own prime meanings. The metaphysical vibration names manifestation without displacement of its ground; physical string vibration has a separate mechanical description and is a named refraction.

[The eight determinations](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) retain the whole knowing relation through each local operation. [Integral Zero](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) returns the achieved determination through its still-active condition; [recognition](recognition.md) gathers that differentiated field without consuming its distinctions. The complete tattvic field remains the depth beneath this sixfold compression.
