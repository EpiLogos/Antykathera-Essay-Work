# §4 — Kashmir Shaivism: The Metaphysical Architecture

## #0 — Prakāśa-Vimarśa

Luminous awareness (*prakāśa*) and its self-apprehending power (*vimarśa*) enter the [metaphysical quilt](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-binary-explication/taylor-2026-binary-explication.md) as one relation. Its [tattvic movement, §X](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) **derives** the distinct offices of ground, inward differentiation, directed manifestation, separating measure, bounded knowing and recognition. The sixfold is QL's compression of that movement, and the numerical correspondences below carry their own warrant.

The native operation relates awareness and manifestation, and neither becomes a self-sufficient substance. `0/1` and `1/0` give the subjectward and objectward readings. Their relational identity is no scalar equality of ordinary arithmetic, and reflective awareness is no detached object among its contents.

## #1 — Trace the six major turns

| Position | Śaiva movement | QL compression |
|---|---|---|
| #0 | Paramaśiva / Anuttara | Unobjectifiable ground |
| #1 | Śiva–Śakti | Consciousness and manifestation-power internally differentiated |
| #2 | Sadāśiva / Īśvara | “I am this” / “This am I,” two orientations |

| #4 | Five kañcukas + Puruṣa | Contracted powers and situated knower |
| #5 | Pratyabhijñā | Recognition retaining the differentiation |

The table compresses the major movements, and the thirty-six categories stay in force beside it; the six-position organisation is QL's, and the Śaiva authors did not give it. Downstream of the contracted knower the field includes Prakṛti, the inner instrument, the sensory and active faculties, and the subtle and gross elements.

## #2 — Make contraction concrete

The five kañcukas specify limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and ordering or necessity. In a situated act they appear as what can be done, known, sought, done now and done under these conditions, and together they give a life its finite ratios of capacity, knowing, desire, time and order. Their relation makes bounded activity possible, and a complete object is not shut inside five independent prisons.

Within the inner instrument, buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates under “I/mine,” and manas coordinates alternatives. A judgement, an appropriation and a shifting alternative can each become available to experience. Their objective internality is therefore distinguished from the experient-position to which they appear. An artificial agent can occupy the local functional knower’s office when it selects and judges through its available means, with the task, alternatives or result as known. Within the containing person’s inquiry, that agent and its processing become part of the means through which a world is disclosed. Reopening the local centre to inspection makes it known in another act without cancelling its prior functional office. The whole relation is Life/Mind; the first-person fact of appearing remains the condition through which judgement, appropriation, alternatives and their inspection become available, rather than one more component in their inventory.

## #3 — Work recognition and its approaches

The return makes those determinations legible as the field's own articulations. A finite capacity stays finite and no individual becomes an unlimited empirical agent, because the continuity claimed lies in the ground of appearing through its determinate modes.

The authorial quilt reads the three upāyas through three harmonics: āṇavopāya through individual effort/Being, śāktopāya through means and thought/Becoming, śāmbhavopāya through direct recognition/Knowing-unKnowing. The harmonic placement is the declared QL mapping; the traditions' practices retain their own sources and cannot be generated from the positional labels.

The native witness argument is exact in its stated register: an experienced or asserted cessation cannot, by that act, remove the presupposed position of appearing. The [immutable-subject projection](../definition/immutable-subject.md) works its fact/content distinction. The [Spandakārikā house](../../episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/kallata/spandakarika-dyczkowski-1992-stanzas-vibration/spandakarika-dyczkowski-1992-stanzas-vibration.md) has no acquired passage for the exact Kṣemarāja wording, so no direct quotation is promoted here. The internal argument survives that attribution debt.

## #4 — Hold the Lacanian encounter and the numerical boundary

The Lacanian encounter places *mi-dire*, the not-all and the unwritable rapport beside recognition. Its operation keeps articulated half-saying apart from the position for which articulation occurs. The proposed relation of the not-all to Anuttara keeps articulated half-saying answerable to its condition, and it is a proposal of the essay: the relation is no classical quantifier equivalence, and Lacan derived no such theorem from Gödel. Recognition is not one more exempt signifier founding a totality.

`36=6²` is exact arithmetic, and the identification of the thirty-six tattvas with a complete six-by-six QL map is a proposed numerical resonance that still lacks a worked category-level correspondence. `64+36=100`, `16+9=25` and `16/9` are two sums and a ratio, and a count on a hundred-unit scale needs the scale to be named before it is called 100% wholeness. Keeping count, sum, ratio and scale separate keeps each equation's exact force within the native relation.

## #5→0 — Enriched 0/1

The result is differentiated manifestation returned through its source-relation. The quilt keeps `0/1=4+2=5→0=0/1`, and the later Night pass has its own prime meanings. The metaphysical vibration (*spanda*) names manifestation with its ground still in place, and physical string vibration, which has its own mechanical description, is a named refraction of it.

[The eight determinations](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **ground** the whole knowing relation in each local operation. [Integral Zero](../../../section-rooms/arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) returns the achieved determination through its still-active condition, and [recognition](recognition.md) **extends** the gathering of that differentiated field with its distinctions kept. The complete tattvic field remains the depth beneath this sixfold compression.
