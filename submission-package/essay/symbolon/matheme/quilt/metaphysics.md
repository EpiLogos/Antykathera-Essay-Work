## #2 — Make contraction concrete

The five kañcukas specify limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and ordering or necessity. In a situated act these appear as what can be done, known, sought, done now and done under these conditions. The core treats them as the finite ratios of a life, not five independent prisons added to an otherwise complete object.

Within the inner instrument, buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates under “I/mine,” and manas coordinates alternatives. A judgement, an appropriation and a shifting alternative can each become available to experience. Their objective internality is therefore distinguished from the experient-position to which they appear. This distinction remains active when the essay compares artificial agents: further inspectable machinery does not by accumulation establish an unobjectifiable subject.

## #3 — Work recognition and its approaches

The return makes those determinations legible as the field's own articulations. It does not erase a finite capacity or turn an individual into an unlimited empirical agent. The claimed continuity concerns the ground of appearing through its determinate modes.

## #4 — Hold the Lacanian encounter and the numerical boundary

The candidate places *mi-dire*, the not-all and unwritable rapport beside recognition. Its operation keeps articulated half-saying distinct from the position for which articulation occurs. Recognition is not introduced as one more exempt signifier founding a totality.

The core's distinctions preserve the equation's force without turning it into an external proof of doctrine.
