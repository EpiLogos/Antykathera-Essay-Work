## #2 — Read between without flattening

The intelligence sixfold names the activity of reading-between, through, according-to, together, out-from and down into an inventory. The corresponding terms are intelligence, dialogue, analogy, syllogism, eclectic and catalogue. Their positions express an operational ordering; the candidate's broader common-root claims retain the philological tasks stated in the etymology projection.

A worked comparison takes the number and topology columns at return. Modulo 6, a six-step count returns to residue 0. On the torus, a loop returns to its surface point while its lift retains an integer displacement. Both supply a specified return, but they retain different information. Their common operation is expressed more exactly by naming this difference than by calling both simply “the same zero.”

## #3 — Join activity, content and structure

The source's intelligence/real convergence puts Activity at Becoming, Content at Being and Structure at Knowing. Reading-between is an activity; the determined field is its content; the grammar gives their relation a repeatable form. The three are distinguishable offices of the encounter.

The property/relational-order contrast in the candidate's account of *real* does philosophical work: a determination can be possessed as an isolated thing or returned through the proportion and conditions that make it actual. The proposed historical genealogy of *res*, *ratio* and *ṛta* does not establish that philosophical relation by itself. The [Mono/Poly argument](../../episteme/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) supplies its earned native ground.

## #4 — From Logos to Code

The candidate's lineage moves through universal notation, mechanical reckoning, formal limit and the runnable letter. Its operative claim is that a notation can execute count, ratio and state-transition while making the limits of that execution explicit. [Gödel's page](../formal-neighbours/godel-incompleteness.md) states the exact arithmetic assumptions; incompleteness is not a theorem that every syntax/semantics pair is defective or that an ordinary software bug is inevitable by Gödel's proof.

The computational musical ontology joins derived ratios with timed, performable relation. Current code must demonstrate its actual operations before a technical promotion is made. The declaration of an ontology has its own Argued standing, while claims that a particular runtime implements it require receipts. Neither a working instrument nor a richer artificial objective internality manufactures evidence of phenomenal subjectivity.

The core's six O:I centres remain distinct loci of inquiry and implementation. A minimal test of an explicit operation cannot silently become the maximal claim of realised subjective or planetary unity. [Operational parity](../computation/operational-parity.md) gives that technical burden its own page.
