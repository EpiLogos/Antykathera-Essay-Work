# Energy-Based Inference and Revisable Encounter

## #0 — Define the energy landscape

An energy-based model assigns a scalar `E_θ(m,y)` to configurations, with observed context or representation `m`, candidate output `y` and parameters `θ`. Inference seeks `argmin_y E_θ(m,y)` over a declared feasible domain. [LeCun and colleagues’ technical construction](../../episteme/sources/computer-science-ml/lecun/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning.md) distinguishes this compatibility energy, minimised in inference, from the loss through which its parameters are learned.

The [additive quilt's encounter proposal](../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md) **sources** the experiment: a result that resists is carried back to the representation or evaluator that made it intelligible, and if the resistance exposes a failure there, the next inference inherits the warranted revision. The [native process](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) supplies the relation being tested, in which an achieved determination stays joined to the means through which it is returned. The experiment asks whether that relation can run through an energy-based model.

## #1 — Work inference on a fixed landscape

Take a real candidate `y` and energy `E_θ(y)=(y−θ)²`. With `θ=2`, the unique minimiser is 2 because the square is nonnegative and vanishes there. An inference process that searches more carefully can recover 2 more reliably, but its additional search does not change what the energy counts as best.

If the feasible domain is restricted to `{0,1}`, the minimum occurs at 1. The domain therefore belongs in the specification alongside the energy. A reported optimum without its feasible set omits a constitutive condition.

## #2 — Separate learning from prediction

For a training target `t`, choose the illustrative loss `L(θ)=(θ−t)²`. A gradient step is `θ′=θ−η·2(θ−t)`. With `θ=2,t=4,η=1/4`, the updated parameter is 3. Inference under the changed energy now selects 3 on the real line.

This calculation distinguishes optimisation over `y` from learning over `θ`. It is a fully specified elementary construction, not a claim that every EBM uses squared error or this update. Training data, loss, optimiser and feasible parameter family must be declared for another model.

## #3 — Let the encounter alter the question

The proposed second-order transition is

`(m,θ,E_θ) → (m′,θ′,E′_{θ′})`.

A new observation can expose that the old candidates or evaluator omitted a material relation. For example, an allocation optimiser minimising delay may learn a parameter while leaving unrepresented an obligation to a newly affected participant. Revising the representation to include that obligation, or replacing the objective under an authorised governance process, changes what counts as an answer.

The example separates two things: a better solution to a fixed need, and a changed evaluative frame. A parameter update alone shows only the first, because it can stay wholly inside the old objective while the allocation optimiser still ignores the newly affected participant.

## #4 — Make resonance discriminate

A claim of resonance with Bimba or a psychic field has to name the represented objects, the energy, the inference, the learning and the return rule. Observable differences then follow from asking what counter-evidence changes: only the selected candidate, or the parameters, or the represented alternatives, or the authority and objective of evaluation. Each of these is a different depth of return, and the experiment can tell them apart.

A controlled comparison holds compute and access constant and enables one specified return pathway. A null result is kept. The rival explanations are extra context, extra search, changed training data and a permissive evaluator, and the design has to exclude each. The word *energy* here names a scalar compatibility function, which is a different object from physical energy, libido, value or lived recognition, and the page claims nothing across that difference.

## #5→0 — Return the landscape through its ground

The result is a testable design distinction between movement inside a field and revision of the field's conditions. Revision of the conditions stays accountable to sources, affected parties and whoever holds authority over the objective, so openness to revision is no licence for objective changes that nobody governs.
