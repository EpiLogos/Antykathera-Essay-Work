# Dia/Syn — the two logics of two

The matheme's own operations are two. **Dia-ballein** is the cut, which contrasts, selects and excludes. **Syn-ballein** is the gathering, the throw-together that composes while it retains its source, its inverse reading and the context it affects. Beside them stands the **chronic pair**: the synchronic holding of a configuration, and the diachronic passage through change, bifurcation and return. Together these logics are how the symbolon's details become expressible as real relations between notations, and in the register they work at the `/` position.

The grounding comes from the whole essay space and no single file supplies it. The [core-theorems spine §IV](../../episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) derives syn-ballein and dia-ballein and the fork of diabolon and symbolon, and it holds the sentence "the devil is the literalist of the minus sign". The central plan's Two Logics of Two develops them as the two complete accountings of polarity. The `C50 — Dia/Syn` concept gives the Syn pole full strength as the owner of syn-ballein, and C23 to C26 are its failure modes. C50 #4 holds the synchronic/diachronic crossing, and the Symbolon Dynamics pair of landscape and trajectory supplies the temporal reading.

## Structure

- **[dia](dia.md)** — the cut: contrast, discrimination and selection, in a productive form and a counterfeit one. Severance (C23) is the counterfeit, and the [[symbolon/matheme/quilt/README.md|quilt]] register develops it.
- **[syn](syn.md)** — the gathering: retention through composition, as when a broken token keeps both halves and the fracture. Its failure modes are fusion (C24), counterfeit gathering (C25) and monoisation (C26). Whether gathering at full strength *is* the eros of logos remains a pending A-candidacy.
- **[chronic](chronic.md)** — synchronic landscape, the configuration held together, and diachronic trajectory, the change, bifurcation and return. These are two readings of one traversal and the operative pair of Symbolon Dynamics.

The pair feeds [[symbolon/matheme/computation/README.md|Computation]] with its two runtime operations, and it fills the slash position of the [[symbolon/matheme/README.md|matheme spine]]. The generative direction runs from gathering to cut, because a cut can only be made inside a field that something has already held together.
