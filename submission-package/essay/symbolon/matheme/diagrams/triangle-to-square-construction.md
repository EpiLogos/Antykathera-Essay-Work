## Proposition

On the unit circle with `N=(0,1)`, `E=(1,0)`, `W=(−1,0)`, `S=(0,−1)`, the native acquisition order N, E, W, S builds first the triangle N–E–W, with interior angles summing to 180° and area 1, and then, with S, the square N–E–S–W, with angles summing to 360° and area 2. The edge E–W, which bounded the triangle, becomes an interior diagonal shared by the complementary triangles N–E–W and S–E–W.

## Inputs

The centre `O=(0,0)`, the four stations, and the acquisition order N, E, W, S.

## Transformations

Draw the radius to N. Add E and W and join N–E–W. Add S and join N–E–S–W. Read E–W as an edge in the second figure and as a diagonal in the third.

## Invariant

The earlier relation E–W survives the change; its office in the whole changes.
