# A20 — Image / Valuation / Possession

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. Q27, Jungian image material and the Symbolon field are developmental provenance.

## Argument

An image becomes psychically powerful not simply because it represents something, but because valuation gathers around it. Image is a formed appearance; valuation is the assignment or precipitation of significance, attraction, threat, authority or desire; possession occurs when the image no longer functions as a participant in relation but occupies the office of what it claims to disclose. The sequence is therefore relational before it is moral: image → value → source-claim.

The distinction between living symbol and idol becomes exact here. A living image gives determinate form to something whose further determination remains active. It can be interpreted, answered, revised and returned to its sources. An idol may be visually identical. What changes is the office: the image is treated as exhaustive presence. In psychic life this can occur when a self-image, enemy-image, beloved-image, institutional image or image of God accumulates enough valuation that encounter is forced to conform to it. The modelled Other replaces the Other.

Possession is thus not limited to spectacular pathology. It occurs whenever a valuation regime protects an image from reciprocal correction. A person may possess an image as property while being possessed by the criteria that make the image non-negotiable. The same pattern can occur technically: a profile, score, embedding, persona or prediction becomes an “operational truth” and begins to determine access or response even after the living source has changed. The problem is not representation; the problem is closure of the return route.

This yields a precise ethical criterion. The more consequential an image becomes, the more its provenance, uncertainty, update path and relation to the represented source must remain available. Otherwise valuation silently turns representation into jurisdiction.
