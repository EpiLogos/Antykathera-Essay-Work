# A20 — Image / Valuation / Possession

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. Image → valuation → source-claim is a relational operation; historical icon/idol doctrines, clinical claims and particular technical outcomes retain their own evidence tasks.

## #0

An image becomes consequential through the significance gathered around it. Attraction, threat, authority, desire and remembered injury give a formed appearance force in the next encounter. [[A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime|A19]] grounds the local arbitration regime; A20 follows one of its principal media. Image supplies the appearance, valuation weights its significance, possession changes its office: what the image represents must now answer to the image.

The image-maker can be possessed by the criteria through which the image is possessed. A self-image, enemy-image, beloved-image or institutional image becomes non-negotiable; contradictory experience then arrives as a defect of the person or world that fails to resemble it. This closure belongs to the relation, not to a particular visual style or the intensity of feeling alone.
