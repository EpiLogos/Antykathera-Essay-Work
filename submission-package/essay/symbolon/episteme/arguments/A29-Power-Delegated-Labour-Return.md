# A29 — Power / Delegated Labour / Return

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument.

## Argument

Power is the capacity to make a difference in a shared field. Delegation converts that capacity into an office: one participant entrusts another with labour, decision or transformation that will affect conditions held in common. The ethical and political question is therefore not whether power exists but whether the path from source to delegated labour and back to consequence remains visible.

Labour is delegated whenever effects are produced by someone or something other than the immediate beneficiary or source of the task. Human institutions have always depended upon such arrangements; contemporary computation multiplies them through platforms, models, data labour, infrastructure and automated agents. The finished result can make this distributed production disappear. An answer looks instantaneous; a product looks self-contained; an agent appears autonomous. A29 requires the account to carry the labour and authority by which the result became possible.

Return is what distinguishes delegation from extraction. A delegated office must return evidence of what it did, effects that matter to those who authorised or were affected by it, and where appropriate value, revision or refusal. Without return, power accumulates at the mediator. The local office can then become a protected account: it receives inputs from a commons while treating outputs, criteria or surplus as its private source-property.

This is why Commons / Non-Monopoly is not an optional political appendix to the formal field. Mono/Poly already established that local determinations depend on relations they do not generate. A29 translates that ontology into institutional consequence. Common conditions need not abolish private or local offices; they do prevent a derivative participant from claiming the whole field as its own creation.

## Pressure and distinctness

It is distinct from C53 Power / Delegated Labour and C54 Commons / Non-Monopoly, and from A24's measure-usurpation: A29 focuses on capacity, work and return rather than criterion alone.
