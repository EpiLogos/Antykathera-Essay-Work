# A03 — Immutable Gap / Formal Limit

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument; historical Argument 03 and the live `01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign.md` carrier remain provenance.

## Argument

A determination never coincides with the condition under which determination occurs. This non-coincidence is the immutable gap. It is “immutable” because the gap is not missing information that a larger inventory could finally supply. When the determining condition is represented as another object, the representation becomes one more determination inside a still-present field of determining. Reflection can deepen indefinitely, but each successful inclusion generates another edge between what is included and the act or context of inclusion.

The formal limit is therefore internal to the success of representation. A better model can disclose more of its context, sources, body, prompt, memory, language, institution or world; it should do so. Yet the demand for reflexive disclosure cannot be converted into a fantasy that context will eventually become one final object containing the act by which it is contextualised. The limit gives the essay a rigorous prohibition against exhaustive objectification while leaving ordinary and scientific determination intact.

The live developed carrier gives `0` a precise meta-sign office here. A determinate zero can mark the condition that cannot enter the represented field as one more ordinary item. This does not identify the QL `0` with the empty set or with a mystical nothing. The set-theoretic floor `0 = ∅`, `1 = {0}` demonstrates how emptiness becomes countable inside a formal system; the meta-sign tracks the additional reflexive difference between represented emptiness and the condition under which that representation is made. A10 will tell the historical story of zero becoming an operative mathematical sign; A36 will tell the distinct return in which that same sign can become Symbol again without ceasing to be sign.

## Pressure and distinctness

A01 and A02 force A03. Faithful definition requires an account of why the source cannot be possessed by definition; identification-with-difference requires an account of the seam that no identity abolishes. A03 supplies the formal reason. It is not a permission slip for arbitrary mystery. The unknown gains no content from the limit. What follows is more exact: any claim that crosses the gap must name the mediation, evidence and standing by which it does so.

This argument also blocks the common mistake that “more context” alone achieves contextual transparency. A total database may still hide the act of deciding what counted as data. Diaphaneity in A04 is the positive response: the context can become partially visible through the determination without being falsely completed as an object.
