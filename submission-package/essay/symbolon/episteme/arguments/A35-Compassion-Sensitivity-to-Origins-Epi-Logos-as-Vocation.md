# A35 — Compassion / Sensitivity to Origins / Epi-Logos as Vocation

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. The living `compassion-as-sensitivity-to-origins.md` concept carrier and whole-Mytheme/vocation material are developmental provenance.

## Argument

Compassion is the practical sensitivity that arises when a determination is perceived together with the conditions from which it has come. To see only the finished act, statement, person or model is to judge a local `1` as if it were self-originating. Sensitivity to origins restores the histories, constraints, wounds, inheritances, labour, relations and unrealised alternatives that participate in the present form. This does not abolish responsibility. It changes the field in which responsibility is judged.

The relation follows directly from Objective Internality. If minds are world-constituted, then understanding an action requires some account of the world that made that action available. Deferential Intelligence extends the same law epistemically: encounter can revise the model of the Other. Compassion is the ethical capacity to let such origin-disclosure matter without reducing the person to their causes. It therefore stands between blame without context and explanation without agency.

Epi-Logos becomes a vocation at this point. Logos articulates an account; *epi*-logos returns upon the account, its source, its effects and those excluded by its first articulation. The vocation is not to produce one final synthesis of every perspective. It is to cultivate systems — intellectual, cultural and technical — in which determinations can return to origins, meet other determinations, disclose their measures and be transformed without forfeiting exactness.

Cultural individuation is the collective extension. A culture becomes more individuated not by purifying itself of otherness but by recognising the complexes, myths, technical systems and historical sources through which it is composed. Commons and non-monopoly become cultural conditions for this work because no local institution can own the whole field of origins from which collective intelligence arises.
