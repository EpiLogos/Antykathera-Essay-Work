# A21 — Individuation / Recognition

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. Jungian individuation and Pratyabhijñā/recognition traditions are source-distinct developmental warrants.

## Argument

Individuation is not the production of an isolated self. It is the process by which a local subject-position becomes more capable of bearing the relations through which it is itself without either dissolving into them or claiming ownership of them. Recognition names the return by which a determination is known in relation to its source and history. The two operations meet because a person becomes more individuated when the complexes, images, inheritances and differences that constitute a life can be recognised without any one of them being installed as the whole.

Jung's individuation and Śaiva *pratyabhijñā* are not the same doctrine. The essay refuses that collapse. Their operational homology is narrower and stronger: what has become estranged, autonomous or locally self-grounding can return through recognition without ceasing to have the determinate form acquired in the interval. Return is therefore not regression to an undifferentiated origin. The achieved difference is carried back into a wider self-relation.

This solves a problem generated by A19 and A20. A complex cannot simply be expelled, because the history to which it answers remains part of the person. An image cannot simply be declared false, because it may disclose something real while overclaiming its jurisdiction. Recognition changes their office. What possessed can become participant; what had been projected can be re-situated; what had been excluded can be encountered as an active condition of the present field.

The same structure prevents individuation from becoming self-possession. A subject cannot finally turn itself into a complete self-thing. The more adequate achievement is a self-relation capable of holding its own non-coincidence. C36's *complexio oppositorum* is therefore not an achieved synthesis that terminates difference. It is the containing capacity through which opposed determinations can transform the field that contains them.

## Pressure and distinctness

A19–A20 make local possession possible; A21 gives the return operation. It is distinct from C34 Individuation and C19 Recognition, which are reusable concepts, and from A35, where cultural individuation and compassion become a collective vocation. It also prepares C64 Paradox: some oppositions are transformed not by choosing a side but by changing the containing field.
