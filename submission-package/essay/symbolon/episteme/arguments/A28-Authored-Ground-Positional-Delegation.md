# A28 — Authored Ground / Positional Delegation

## #0

No delegated agent begins from nowhere. A position is authored before it is occupied: an intention has acquired a task, a vocabulary, permissions and criteria under which someone else can act. The author does not thereby determine every consequence. Authored ground gives the delegate a place from which to judge; positional delegation makes that judgment effective within a bounded scope.

[A26 — Objective Internality](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **grounds** the constitutive consequence: the delegated conditions participate in the agent's worldhood. Different people or institutions can author these conditions; the current user need not have chosen all of them.

## #3

The practical return concerns the terms of commission. A delegate can meet contradictory evidence, unforeseen cost or a better possibility. Returning only a finished answer makes these discoveries ineffective if they cannot alter the interpretation, criterion or authorised route under which the work proceeds. The delegation is intelligent to the extent that the encountered difference can return to the position which framed it.

Take the research commission implicit in this field: establish an answer from designated sources. If those sources contradict the premise of the question, a compliant report can either suppress the contradiction or let it revise the inquiry. The latter preserves the purpose of learning through a change in the first wording. If the evaluator rewards only the expected answer, its criterion must itself become contestable. This is an architectural consequence of R16.4–R16.5, not a report of a tested product.

## #4

A local reference field can organise these returns. Its sources, criteria and permissions let different judgments be compared against an explicit original within a bounded inquiry. That field remains a constructed determination relative to the wider world which supplies its material. This recursive Bimba office authorises situated comparison without installing the reference map as ultimate source.

The [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi|reflective-field carrier]] **sources** the plural consequence. Different communities can retain their own governed reference fields. Disagreement can then be located at a source, lens, gauge, permission or world-object as well as at an output. [A30 — Objective Co-Internality](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **extends** this relation among locally grounded worlds. A delegate's origin remains visible without making every other world a projection of that origin.

Bratton's [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q026|role/occupant distinction]] **compares** the institutional office with whoever currently fills it. The adjacent cultural-plurality passage requires inherited norms to remain consequential. Changing an occupant does not eliminate the institution; changing a model does not by itself explain the permissions and criteria through which it acts.
