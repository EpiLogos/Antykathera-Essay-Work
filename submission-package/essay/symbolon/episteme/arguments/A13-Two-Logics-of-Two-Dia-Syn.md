# A13 — Two Logics of Two — Dia / Syn

## #0

Difference already occurs within a relation. Before signs are assigned, the slash holds the capacity to distinguish:

$$
/ = -/-.
$$

[[A11-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All|A11]] grounds the two ones in their distinct firstness; [[A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|A12]] grounds the One/All relation which the two logics now make operative. A13 begins at the pressure of accounting for their difference. Comparability precedes opposition: the act that recognises two as opposed has already related them. That common operation remains active even when its products are treated as independent camps.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C18-Apoha|C18 Apoha]] defines how excluded alternatives remain constitutive of a determination. A boundary selects something real. Its consequence depends on whether the selection retains the field through which it acquired its sense and power.

## #1

Dia-ballein gives the difference direction. Assignment of signs produces a centred polarity:

$$
(-1)/(+1).
$$

Zero remains the axis of tension and connection. Measurement, comparison, refusal, critique, classification, ranking and decision require this capacity to distinguish. A signed direction means through the centre and the counter-direction it has not selected. The pole's local definiteness therefore carries a relation; it does not have to wait for a later reconciliation to acquire one.

The collapse changes the operation performed between the terms:

$$
(-1)+(+1)=0,
$$

$$
(-1)-(+1)=-2,\qquad (+1)-(-1)=+2.
$$

Addition cancels the opposed values. Subtraction measures their whole span from one orientation and attributes that magnitude to its pole: appropriation under the negative or positive sign. Cancellation and appropriation are different outcomes; neither is equivalent to the polar slash. The equations are ordinary signed arithmetic. Their reading as two failures of relational accounting is the native QL matheme sourced by [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|Taylor's theorem spine]], especially IV, within the complete I–XI and eight-determination field.

## #2

Sym-ballein retains what the cut discloses and lets the determination read back through its own condition. The inner slash works as AND/OR: conjunction retains both terms, disjunction preserves their difference. `0/1` reads the One through its appearing All; `1/0` reads the appearing All toward the One which it cannot contain. Their identity is the relation's identity across its obverse orientations:

$$
0/1 = 1/0,\qquad (0/1)/(1/0).
$$

The outer slash relates those orientations themselves. This is QL relational notation: ordinary arithmetic's defined `0/1` and undefined `1/0` remain the formal seam through which the return operates. The finite expression encounters the condition that cannot be made into another finite expression. Sym-ballein defines towards wholeness by retaining that encounter; a claim to possess wholeness would close the return it names.

The distinction's self-relation can now be counted. The two singles `{0,1}` remain, together with their four pairings `{00,01,10,11}`:

$$
2+2^2=2+4=6=4+2.
$$

The four pairings do not replace the original two. The sixfold holds both the poles and the ways their relation can relate itself. This is why the two logics yield a process body: the achieved determination can become accountable to its source and inverse rather than end at its output.

## #3

The complete computational pulse is **divide / relate / recombine / return**. Recursive division makes subproblems tractable because their answers can be recomposed. If the capacity to combine is retained privately while everyone else remains divided, division becomes a means of governing the gap. That political extension concerns the ownership of the relating office, including institutions, labour, material conditions and coercion; a psychological description alone cannot establish its historical instances.

Q27's “two monocultures” gives the failure a precise recurrence. Each camp suppresses its internal plurality, needs the other as its negative identity, treats internal dissent as contamination, and reads the other's hardening as confirmation that further exclusion is necessary. More visible antagonism then coexists with less Poly. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C26-Monoisation-Counter-Generation|C26]] defines this counter-generation; [[A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime|A19]] extends the question into the local regime which decides what can count. The return asks what field was divided and who controls its recomposition. “Divine and concur” recovers that third relation; it does not calculate a midpoint between the camps. Judgment of unequal facts, power and responsibility remains possible within the recovered field.

A problem can close when its stated task is resolved. A constitutive predicament persists because each pole supplies something the other needs. Forced restabilisation treats the latter as though one final answer could discharge it, expelling the relation as a remainder. [[A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure|A24]] tests the authority of that closure. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C23-Severance|C23]] defines loss of the field through exclusion; [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C24-Fusion|C24]] defines loss of difference through enforced coincidence. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C25-Counterfeit-Gathering|C25]] defines their encounter where a comprehensive enclosure claims to be a living whole.

## #4

The whole mythic relation figures how gathering can either preserve or arrest differentiated powers. Ares gives refusal, defence and the capacity to remain distinct; Aphrodite gives attraction and meeting. Harmonia carries the form their relation can generate while retaining tension. Hesiod's genealogy also includes Panic and Fear, so the ancient family cannot be reduced to a guarantee that union produces concord. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-philology/hesiod/hesiod-1920-theogony-evelyn-white/SOURCE|Hesiod's source house]] sources the genealogy at 933–937; the two-logics reading remains the essay's own mythemic development.

Hephaestus's bonds gather the lovers by immobilising them. Helios supplies detection; the gods' assembled gaze turns capture into public evidence; bride-gifts and the adulterer's fine convert grievance into account; Hermes exposes the incentive by welcoming the bonds if they bring Aphrodite. The successful apparatus still cannot conclude its business. Poseidon risks his own liability as surety, and Hephaestus accepts the relation his trap cannot manufacture. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-philology/homer/homer-1919-odyssey-murray/SOURCE|Homer's source house]] sources that detection–capture–account–surety–release sequence. The interpretation as counterfeit gathering and trust is Argued; Homer does not state QL or a theory of surveillance.
