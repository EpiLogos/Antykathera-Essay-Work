# A12 — Mono/Poly — One / All, Whole / Many

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument; historical Argument 04 remains provenance, with Alpha/Delta matheme residue preserved rather than silently absorbed.

## #0

Mono/Poly is the ontological consequence of the Two Ones: Self-Identity becomes Self-Difference, one relational whole differentiating through many real expressions. The whole is structurally prior because no part creates all the relations and conditions through which it becomes this part. This priority names dependence, not an earlier giant being that distributes fragments of itself, and not a total constructed by adding independently grounded atoms after the fact.

The [Q27 aspectual correction](../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md) also retains Mono as inclusive whole or super-object insofar as every real particular belongs within one reality. Here object does not mean something placed before an external knower. That extensional inclusion and the generative relational condition are compatible under their distinct aspects; neither makes another person a component of my private model. The processual superject refraction keeps its separate Whitehead source task.

## #1

The many have reality as the whole's actual differentiation. Each indefinite `x` remains locally exact and consequential; its source-relation is what makes this exactness possible. The One is not the residue obtained by subtracting every difference. It remains present through the power to articulate and integrate difference. The All includes the whole possibility of determinations as well as their local instances, so no bounded account of all its objects encloses the One that makes accounting possible.

[A08](A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) gives a determinate semantic route: exclusion makes a mark intelligible within a field of alternatives. Its account of Pind's preclusion remains source-specific; the native whole-first consequence is the essay's Argued extension. A local term can be empty of independent self-subsistence and still act with real force. Śaiva self-articulation supplies a distinct refraction through A05/A09, not an interchangeable source for Buddhist semantics.

## #2

`0/1 ↔ 1/0` retains both directions: the One manifests through the All, and the All returns through the One. Dependency is reciprocal in different respects—being and determining reality in one direction, manifestation and knowability in the other. Q27 explicitly preserves trust in this primordial slash. A12 asks what kind of whole can differentiate through real plurality; A23 asks how finite beings inhabit that same relation through knowing, relying, risking and returning. Trust is not an optional feeling appended to an otherwise neutral whole.

## #3

Monopoly begins when a local `1` installs itself in the source-office. A ruler, platform, institution, theory or model then represents the conditions that sustain it as its own authority. The [late Q27 cultural sequence](../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md) specifies what follows. Culture already contains many practices, idioms, histories and criticisms. Monoculture suppresses their internal legitimacy; excluded difference returns as counterculture because it can no longer appear as variation within the official account.

The counter can perform a necessary correction: it shows the established one that it is not the whole. The candidate notation `(1)` pictures a local determination enclosed by its forgotten condition. It remains an explanatory political/mythemic candidate, not a new theorem primitive. The relevant movement is the occlusion of the source-relation that makes a local measure seem simply natural, neutral or universally valid.

## #4

Counterculture can itself monoise. Each camp compresses its internal plurality, treats dissent as contamination by the other, and needs the opposed camp to stabilise its identity. Two monocultures then masquerade as plurality: the shared field appears only as their battlefield. This is more precise than treating every difference or conflict as pathology.

Retained signed polarity, `(-1)/(+1)`, still has an axis and a relation; conflict can remain transformative. Cancellation removes difference, appropriation gives one pole the span, and war demands a winner who can exclude the other from participation. The political third returns to the field through which both terms arose. It does not average their positions or require equal judgments about their truth, ethics, power or responsibility. Recovering origins makes discrimination more answerable; it does not suspend it.

## #5→0

Commons and protocols can hold common conditions while local contexts retain genuine authority within scope. That is a concrete test of Mono/Poly: can an institution return a determination through its sources, exclusions, material dependencies and affected participants, or does it install its output as the final source of its own warrant? A common grammar can coordinate divergent worlds without appointing a ruler, crowd or model as their super-subject.

The same return limits what apparatus can achieve. Observability can expose or verify a relation without producing its living fidelity; a commons preserves conditions for trust without manufacturing trust. The source's Ares–Aphrodite–Harmonia–Eros–Hephaestus circuit carries this as a whole mythemic relation, whose detailed source-bound restoration belongs with A24. A29 develops institutional power and A35 cultural individuation. Neither wider historical genealogy nor a clinical theory of crowds is established by the native operation alone.
