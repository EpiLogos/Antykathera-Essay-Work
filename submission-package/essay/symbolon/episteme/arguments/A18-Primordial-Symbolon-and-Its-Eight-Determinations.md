# A18 — Primordial Symbolon and Its Eight Determinations

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument; historical Argument 10 remains provenance. This is the substantive Symbolon argument; C61 is the disclosure/routing architecture, not a duplicate theorem.

## Argument

The Primordial Symbolon is the relation that can separate, invert, question, polarise, pattern, person, differentiate and return without ceasing to be one relation. Its eight determinations are not eight topics laid beside one another. They are one transverse traversal:

```text
−/−
0/1
?/!
−/+
X/x
AM/IS
∞/dx
1/0
```

The traversal begins with **`−/−`**, relation capable of difference before positive terms have been assigned. **`0/1`** determines ground and mark: the uncounted condition and the determinate appearance. **`?/!`** turns the relation into epistemic tension, question and assertion, opening and achieved answer. **`−/+`** projects difference into signed polarity and measurable orientation. **`X/x`** distinguishes determining capacity from indefinite particular without making the former an object possessed by the latter. **`AM/IS`** brings first-person presence and predicative determination into one contextual field: “am” and “is” become mutually legible without equivalence. **`∞/dx`** places a finite differential within an inexhaustible horizon of variation and traversal. **`1/0`** is the inverse return: manifestation turns back toward ground, not by deleting the journey but by carrying the achieved determinations into a changed reading of the primordial relation.

The eightfold therefore performs the essay's central law of living symbol. A symbol is alive when the manifest form remains related to an overdetermining source it does not exhaust. The Symbolon can become a determinate mark without becoming an idol because every stage preserves a route of return. Conversely, when a local determination occupies the source-office, the Symbolon becomes counterfeit: severance, fusion, counterfeit gathering, monoisation and protected account are not arbitrary moral labels but characteristic failures of the relation.

This also clarifies QL's role. QL is the wider formal and research field through which many exact forms are generated and compared. The eight determinations are one privileged transverse traversal inside that field. They do not redefine QL as a whole, and C61's Symbolon Disclosure Architecture exists only to route such formal, mythemic and epistemic expressions without confusing their standing.
