# A26 — Objective Internality — Mind as Worldhood

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. Direct developed carrier: `02-objective-internality.md`.

## Argument

Objective Internality means **Mind as Worldhood**. An operative interior is not a sealed mental container surrounded by optional metadata; it is dynamically constituted through the context-world in which remembering, perceiving, deciding, speaking and acting become possible. Language, memory, tools, other people, institutions, permissions, materials, environment, history, training and inherited categories are not merely external influences on an already finished mind. They participate in the form of the operative interior itself.

This claim must be held together with the immutable gap. The subjective pole is not thereby converted into an inspectable bundle of contextual determinants. A model of a person's memory, body, language, relations and environment remains a model. Objective Internality names what can be positively investigated about world-conditioned interior organisation while preserving the unobjectifiability of subjectivity as such. The live “Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open” carrier states the same proof boundary for artificial systems: performed subjectivity, objective internality, functional agency, recursive self-description and phenomenality are distinct questions.

The argument therefore reverses the ordinary explanatory direction without discarding it. A person forms a picture of the world; but the world also forms the conditions under which that person can picture. Language precedes many of the distinctions a speaker later experiences as intimate thought. Institutional permissions change the action-space of an agent. Tools extend and reorganise memory and perception. Other people supply names, norms and counter-perspectives. Objective Internality reads these not as a list of causes outside the mind but as constituents of a situated field of possible judgment and action.

Artificial agency makes the distinction operationally vivid. Prompt, model, context window, memory store, tool permissions, system rules, retrieval sources, organisational role, hardware and interaction history jointly constitute a changing agent-world. These are inspectable and causally consequential. They can be tested without declaring that the system therefore has, or cannot have, phenomenal subjectivity. This is why Objective Internality is both philosophically substantive and technically productive.
