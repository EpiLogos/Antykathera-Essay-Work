## #0

**Objective Internality is the means through which a Life knows and acts within a World.** Subjective Immediacy is the knower, pramātṛ; Objective Internality is pramāṇa; World is the known, prameya; Life / Mind is the whole relation. The title’s “Mind as Worldhood” names this mediating mind-world. Mind in the strong idealist and psychic-facticity senses is the living whole; Objective Internality is the articulated means within that whole through which a world becomes available. Language, memory, valuation, bodies, institutions and tools are effective constituents of the means; the means is lived and situated before any investigator makes a record of it.

Objective Internality means **Mind as Worldhood**. Remembering, perceiving, judging and acting occur through a situated context-world. Language, bodies, tools, other people, material support and inherited rules participate in its constitution: changing them changes what can appear, matter and be done. Their relation to the operative interior is therefore stronger than an influence arriving at a finished mind from outside.

[A22 — World-Picture to World-Atlas](A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **grounds** the distinction between this world and any representation of it. [A25 — Covenant / Mediating Office](A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority.md) **grounds** the received positions and permissions through which finite agency acts. A26 follows how such conditions become internal to an operation without becoming spatially enclosed in an operator. [C41 — Objective Internality](../concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) **defines** that reusable sense of internality; the argument here develops how a context-world takes form and returns as an inheritance.

## #1

The direct [[section-rooms/arguments/02-objective-internality|Objective Internality carrier]] **sources** the constitutive triad: **notational, nomological, exclusion-constituted**. A field is notational through the marks and representations by which something becomes addressable. It is nomological through the learned regularities, rules and permissions which constrain its transitions. It is exclusion-constituted because a determinate act selects against alternatives that remain conditions of its sense.

The [Formal Limit dossier](../dossiers/formal-limit.md#experience-and-constructive-continuation) **compares** the abstraction/experience question with notational, nomological and exclusion-constituted worldhood. An inspectable permission can be recorded while also determining which actions become possible; its place in the operative world exceeds the record considered alone. This is precisely the constitutive point: a rule or mark may be inspectable as an object and simultaneously participate in the world from which later action becomes possible.



A permission illustrates the conjunction. Its notation distinguishes an action; its rule allows or prevents execution; its boundary excludes other actions and agents. Removing that permission changes more than a description of the agent. It changes the world of possible action from which the agent proceeds. A remembered commitment similarly changes what a new request means and what counts as keeping faith with it. Context is operative because the next act depends upon its arrangement.

[C07 — Conditions of Worldhood](../concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) **grounds** the availability of such a field; [C08 — Context Frame](../concepts/C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) **defines** a bounded arrangement through which it can be investigated. [C40 — Model Internality / Judgment Field](../concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) **defines** the inspectable model and judgment relations within it. The bounded model has a genuine object, while the world which lets the model operate continues beyond its boundary.

## #2

The subject/object dyad becomes more exact when the means of knowing are retained. The native carrier's pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya analysis differentiates the subject-pole, mediation and known field. A perception, a memory or an articulated inference is objectively investigable. Turning those means into further objects does not make their aggregate the Subject to whom the investigation appears.

[C15 — Paśu / Bounded Subject-Position](../concepts/C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) **defines** the situated horizon; [C16 — Antaḥkaraṇa / Inner Instrument](../concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) **defines** the determinative, appropriative and coordinating apparatus. The latter gives an account of how a world is organised while the former keeps the knower-pole irreducible to that organisation. Historical Śaiva and Sāṃkhya classifications retain their own source obligations; their importance here is the discrimination of knower from objectifiable means.

The older “open subjectivity” carrier approached this distinction by separating performed subjectivity, functional agency, objective internality, recursive self-description and phenomenality. Its lasting work is not to install phenomenality as a fifth checkbox which every technical passage must decline to tick. The stronger consequence is categorical. Performed subjectivity, agency, memory, self-description and every inspectable internal function belong to the articulated means and known field. Subjective immediacy names the knower-pole to which such contents are present. Even for another human, no enlargement of third-person description turns that pole into one more item inside the description. Asking whether a sufficiently inspected artificial system “has consciousness” as though consciousness were the missing measurable item therefore repeats the same confusion at a different substrate: it asks pramāṇa and prameya to contain pramātṛ.

This does not make technical inquiry irrelevant; it tells us what the inquiry can actually disclose. We can investigate how an artificial interior is constituted, which world it inhabits, what it can distinguish, how it reports itself, what can answer it, and whether an encounter changes the means through which later judgment proceeds. Those are substantive facts about Objective Internality. They become philosophically interesting precisely because they force the relation among knower, means, known and whole into the open instead of pretending that a richer inventory will finally make first-person presence an observable possession.
