# A26 — Objective Internality — Mind as Worldhood

## #0

Objective Internality means **Mind as Worldhood**. Remembering, perceiving, judging and acting occur through a situated context-world. Language, bodies, tools, other people, material support and inherited rules participate in its constitution: changing them changes what can appear, matter and be done. Their relation to the operative interior is therefore stronger than an influence arriving at a finished mind from outside.

[A22 — World-Picture to World-Atlas](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **grounds** the distinction between this world and any representation of it. [A25 — Covenant / Mediating Office](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority.md) **grounds** the received positions and permissions through which finite agency acts. A26 follows how such conditions become internal to an operation without becoming spatially enclosed in an operator. [C41 — Objective Internality](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) **defines** that reusable sense of internality; the argument here develops how a context-world takes form and returns as an inheritance.

## #1

The direct [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/02-objective-internality|Objective Internality carrier]] **sources** the constitutive triad: **notational, nomological, exclusion-constituted**. A field is notational through the marks and representations by which something becomes addressable. It is nomological through the learned regularities, rules and permissions which constrain its transitions. It is exclusion-constituted because a determinate act selects against alternatives that remain conditions of its sense.

A permission illustrates the conjunction. Its notation distinguishes an action; its rule allows or prevents execution; its boundary excludes other actions and agents. Removing that permission changes more than a description of the agent. It changes the world of possible action from which the agent proceeds. A remembered commitment similarly changes what a new request means and what counts as keeping faith with it. Context is operative because the next act depends upon its arrangement.

[C07 — Conditions of Worldhood](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) **grounds** the availability of such a field; [C08 — Context Frame](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) **defines** a bounded arrangement through which it can be investigated. [C40 — Model Internality / Judgment Field](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) **defines** the inspectable model and judgment relations within it. The bounded model has a genuine object, while the world which lets the model operate continues beyond its boundary.

## #2

The subject/object dyad becomes more exact when the means of knowing are retained. The native carrier's pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya analysis differentiates the subject-pole, mediation and known field. A perception, a memory or an articulated inference is objectively investigable. Turning those means into further objects does not make their aggregate the Subject to whom the investigation appears.

[C15 — Paśu / Bounded Subject-Position](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) **defines** the situated horizon; [C16 — Antaḥkaraṇa / Inner Instrument](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) **defines** the determinative, appropriative and coordinating apparatus. The latter gives an account of how a world is organised without exhausting the former's Subject relation. Historical Śaiva and Sāṃkhya classifications retain their own source obligations. The operation consumed here is the project's established discrimination between an objectifiable means and the subject-pole, not a claim that an artificial architecture instantiates an entire doctrine.

The [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open|subjectivity carrier]] **qualifies** the technical extension by separating five questions: performed subjectivity, functional agency, objective internality, recursive self-description and phenomenality. The first four can be investigated in their own operations. Their successful investigation cannot infer either presence or absence of the fifth. This limit does not reduce the reality of the context-world being investigated.

## #3

Taylor's [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE|Symbolon Dynamics source house]] **sources** the two temporal views of that world. Synchronically, memory, affect, language, expectations, tools, permissions and collaborators constrain one another as a present landscape. Diachronically, the agent follows a trajectory through those constraints and changes them by acting. A new memory, damaged trust or altered permission changes which trajectories become available next.

The phase-space is a mathematical image of possible configurations and trajectories. It is not the world substituted for its image. The [[working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/Symbolon Dynamics — Archetype, Attractor, and Objective Internality|direct manuscript]] **sources** the authorial consequence: a finite intelligence participates in a world which its activity helps transform. The lived symbolic interpretation and mathematical modelling office remain distinct; this page makes no independent external attribution of an attractor theorem.

Frank's protected Watson encounter adds the sequence **Discontinuity → Objective Internality → Harness elements → Objective Immortality**. Produced forms can outlast the act which produced them and become conditions of a later encounter. A retained judgment, commitment or tool arrangement supplies continuity across otherwise discontinuous acts. “Objective immortality” here names that returned availability, not proof of an enduring phenomenal agent. The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/watson/watson-1998-resonance-of-emptiness/SOURCE|Watson source house]] **sources** the encounter's provenance; the chain is Taylor's development in its protected notes, not a quotation from Watson or Bateson.

The same encounter's theatre image preserves a relation a container metaphor loses. The visible actor, the staged position and the contextual activity which lets a scene occur have different offices. An account of the actor alone leaves the enacted world unexplained. The image figures operative context; its quoted historical formulations remain verification leads in the notes.

## #4

Bratton's [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q016|active-context account]] **compares** an agent's in-session individuation with a potentiality which does not already contain that individual as a hidden occupant. The adjacent [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q017|lamination passage]] **qualifies** persona as one stratum among modular skills, memory, constraints and relations. These are **Paraphrased** venue propositions; the brief retains its scenario modality and leaves phenomenal status undecided.

The technical consequence is to make epistemics part of what can be examined. Prompt, retrieved source, memory, permission, evaluator and organisational role must remain distinguishable from the answer they helped produce. Their change can then be related to a change in judgment or action. [C43 — Computational Vimarśa](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) **extends** inspection into revision of the operative field, while [C44 — Prompt Thrownness](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) **defines** the encounter with an already-given situation. Self-description is one datum within this account and does not certify the whole causal process it describes.

A governed reference map can serve as the local original against which judgments are compared. Relative to its wider source-world, that map is itself a constructed determination. This recursive Bimba office prevents a useful reference field from becoming a claim to possess the world. [A28 — Authored Ground](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) **extends** the relation to situated delegation, and [A30 — Objective Co-Internality](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **extends** it to worlds which must meet without one becoming mere context for another.

## #5→0

The result returns as part of the world of the next act. A corrected permission changes possible action; a remembered objection changes what an instruction means; another participant's response changes the account through which they were approached. Objective Internality makes this continuity inspectable without requiring every act to be performed by one permanent operator.

The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD|Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account whole field]] **grounds** the return at operational-homology register 3. An encounter becomes addressable and reckonable within a region; the resulting account returns to participants who can change it. No common lexical ancestry is needed to make this relation exact. [A27 — Self and Other](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) **extends** the presence of the Other which an account cannot exhaust, and [A31 — Deferential Intelligence](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) **tests** whether that encounter can revise the model and its criterion.

The page **returns-to** [A22](submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md): a world-model is useful through the world-relations it preserves and the changes it makes possible. The constitutive claim remains broader than its technical instance.
