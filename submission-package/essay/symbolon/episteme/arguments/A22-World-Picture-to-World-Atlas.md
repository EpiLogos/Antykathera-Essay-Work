# A22 — World-Picture → World-Atlas

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument. The atlas is the essay's constructive operation. Historical Objective Internality is a direct developmental carrier, not a second canonical identity for A22.

## #0

A world-atlas makes situated accounts traversable. Each picture remains placed; its overlaps, transformations and obstructions become part of what it says. [[A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency|A04]] grounds contextual transparency; [[A16-Arche-Topos-as-Differential-Field|A16]] grounds differential placement; [[A20-Image-Valuation-Possession|A20]] shows why a consequential image must remain answerable to its source. Their epistemic consequence changes the relation to picturing itself.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/heidegger/heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology/SOURCE|Heidegger’s world-picture source]] sources a specific historical diagnosis in q012–q014: the modern event is the world grasped as picture together with the representer becoming its normative centre. That paraphrased diagnosis exceeds the simple existence of pictures. Lovitt's wording is verified, while print collation and several page locators remain pending. The atlas does not appear in Heidegger as this essay's constructive technical proposal.

## #1

A picture has a frame before its contents are assembled. The box-lid image gives a jigsaw its finished arrangement in advance; every piece is then judged by the place that arrangement assigns. Inquiry becomes different when travel discloses the frame's own conditions or encounters a piece whose relation cannot be settled by the lid. Collecting more pieces cannot alone correct the criterion of fit.

Q27's stained-glass development supplies the fuller operation. Light, panes, lead cames, aperture, viewer and cast image work together. Each pane transmits, colours and interrupts; the cames preserve seams and non-equivalences. Move the light or viewer and the visible pattern changes through the same constructed window. A source's difference is therefore information for the composition, not an impurity to be removed by averaging. The complete image is a context of transmission, not a pile of decorative fragments.

## #2

An atlas requires declared conditions of transition. For local charts with coordinate maps `φᵢ` and `φⱼ`, an overlap permits the transition `φⱼ ∘ φᵢ⁻¹` on the relevant domain. This mathematical operation has explicit hypotheses: the maps must have the required inverse and common region, and the transition must satisfy the structure being studied. A collection of incompatible descriptions does not become a mathematical atlas by being stored together.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE|Symbolon Dynamics]] sources the author's distinction between this chart operation and attractor dynamics. A basin organises trajectories; an atlas coordinates partial disclosures. Symbolic movement can illuminate both while leaving their mathematical objects distinct. In the epistemic extension, a transition must state the question, scale, assumptions, preserved distinctions and changed criterion. An obstruction is a result to expose: these accounts cannot yet be composed for this purpose under these conditions. It is not a reason to manufacture agreement.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C37-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas|C37 World-Atlas]] defines this reusable demand. Adequacy remains discriminating: some accounts fail their own tests, some address different objects, and some genuinely conflict. The atlas must make those differences intelligible enough to guide a next encounter.

## #3

The corrected Bimba relation prevents the atlas from claiming the place it has denied to its pictures. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map|C38 Bimba–Pratibimba]] defines Original and reflected determination. Every produced world-picture, including an instrument's reference map and this atlas, is pratibimba. It can function as a local reference field for downstream views without becoming Bimba in the originary sense. Q27's later explicit correction supersedes its earlier sentence assigning the produced picture to Bimba.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses/SOURCE|The MEF source house]] sources the internal lens architecture; its older no-local-file statement is stale against the recoverable twelve-lens reference. That reference's L4/L4′ correction gives a precise transformation: each carries the whole rotated Name/Power constellation, with opposite faces grounding the view. Changing lens changes the governing relation rather than deleting the rest of the field. The architecture's historical attributions, musical subset claims and practical utility require separate warrant; its inherited six subheadings do not govern this page.

## #4

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/SOURCE|Gebser’s source house]] compares perspectival sector and aperspectival co-presence through its page-specific paraphrase map. The essay's `180°→360°` development changes the unit from a selected view to the field of positions and transformations that makes views possible. It does not grant an observer an unconditioned panorama. Frank's direct P5 Gebser carrier makes context a being-with the text and subtext, invested in outcome and care; that authored interpretation is not a quotation from Gebser.

The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD|Encounter whole field]] derives the return through **Region → Name/Countenance → Account**. A placed and counted participant remains a face able to answer the count. The operational homology does not claim a common word-root for name and number. The account gains maturity when encounter can alter its frame, not merely populate the existing categories more completely.

## #5→0

The invariant remains `world ≠ world-model`. A model's rich representations and a federation's many models do not exhaust the world in which they were learned and into which their actions return. [[A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood|A26]] grounds the active objective internality which precedes representational technique. A disclosed harness records some conditions of an event; it does not contain all the material, institutional and interpersonal relations that made the event possible.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE|Agentworld]] compares divergent worlds sharing a grammar of interaction. Common grammar permits disagreement to acquire shape; it does not make different ontologies identical. [[A31-Deferential-Intelligence|A31]] tests whether that disagreement can revise the governing view; [[A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity|A33]] tests whether the revision changes actual operation. Their consequence returns to the atlas as a changed route, sharper obstruction or newly situated account.
