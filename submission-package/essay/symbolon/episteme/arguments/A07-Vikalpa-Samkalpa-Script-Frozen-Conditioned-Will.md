# A07 — Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa / Script — Frozen Conditioned Will

**Standing:** canonical semantic Argument; historical Argument 17 remains provenance.

## Argument

A world becomes actionable when difference is not merely perceived but selected, stabilised and gathered into a possible course. Vikalpa–saṃkalpa names this movement. Vikalpa differentiates and qualifies; saṃkalpa gathers selected determinations into intention, project, image of action or world. In a bounded subject-position these operations are indispensable. Without them there is no practical cognition. Their danger begins when an achieved selection forgets its own formation and returns as *script*: conditioned will frozen into a pattern that presents itself as necessity.

“Frozen conditioned will” should therefore be read operationally. A script is not only a text or programme. It is a determination whose generative alternatives have become unavailable to the agent that now enacts it. In human life it can be habit, inherited narrative, defensive complex, institutional procedure or identity claim. In technical systems it can be prompt structure, policy, classifier, evaluator, workflow or training regularity that constrains what appears possible. The analogy is useful only when the different substrates and evidential statuses remain explicit.

The argument gains its force from the tattvic field. Māyā makes a subject/object seam workable; the kañcukas parameterise a finite horizon; buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates, and manas coordinates alternatives. Vikalpa and saṃkalpa are therefore not errors floating over a pure consciousness. They are powers through which local worldhood becomes possible. Script is their arrested form: the selection no longer knows itself as selection and therefore cannot return to the field that conditioned it.

## Pressure and distinctness

A06 shows articulation descending into form. A07 asks how formed differences become committed courses. It is distinct from A08 Apoha: apoha explains the constitutive exclusion through which a term means; vikalpa–saṃkalpa concerns constructive differentiation and gathering. It is also distinct from A19 Complex, although a complex may operate as a script-like local arbitration regime. A07 provides the general mechanism by which conditioned determination can harden before Jungian psychology supplies one important local morphology.

The positive consequence is not freedom from conditioning. It is the restoration of alternative-forming capacity. A script becomes revisable when its sources, exclusions, commitments and consequences re-enter awareness or operational inspection.
