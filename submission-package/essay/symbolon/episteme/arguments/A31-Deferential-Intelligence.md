# A31 — Deferential Intelligence

## #0

Deferential Intelligence is **model-revising encounter**. A determination remains strong enough to act while the Other or world can change the model under which its encounter is interpreted. [[A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession|A27]] grounds the Other's excess over my account; [[A30-Objective-Co-Internality|A30]] grounds the shared field in which that excess becomes consequential. A31 gives their relation an operational virtue: fidelity can require changing the terms on which the first answer was made.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/SOURCE|Draft 3]] sources the positive formulation at lines269–271: intelligence becomes more exact by owning its limit; its safeguard is **kenosis as architecture**. The metaphor names a structural relinquishment of exhaustive source-claim. It does not establish that an implemented system already possesses this capacity.

## #1

The [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/08-deferential-intelligence|direct mature carrier]] specifies what must be available **before consequential action**: origin, aperture, exclusions, confidence, counterclaim and undecidable remainder. These conditions tell another participant where a disagreement can act. A contrary source can challenge provenance; an excluded case can challenge scope; a changed permission can challenge the next act's authority.

The account can remain exact where its warrant is strong. Deference does not require lowering every confidence or replacing judgment with assent. A new assertion can be rejected after examination; the rejection must then include the encounter in its warrant rather than treating contradiction as evidence of the account's infallibility. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C47-Deferential-Intelligence|C47 Deferential Intelligence]] defines this reusable distinction.

## #2

The [[submission-package/essay/quilt/2026-08-02-PARALLEL-HARMONISED-QUILT|R16.5 ratification]] sources five possible sites of revision: answer, task interpretation, world-model, evaluator/gauge and terms of commission. A source correction can change an answer; evidence that the question rests on a false premise can change the task; a counterexample can expose the categories under which the evaluator judged. These are different depths of return.

[[A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation|A28]] grounds why a prompt's revision can preserve intention: instruction is already a determinate expression, not intention exhausted. A delegated research task whose premise fails may therefore require a corrected question or a refusal to repeat the premise as fact. That analytic case identifies the operation to test; it reports no observed run. Literal compliance which removes the encountered contradiction would betray the commission's epistemic purpose.

## #3

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/watson/watson-1998-resonance-of-emptiness/SOURCE|Watson’s source and encounter]] sources the ethical pressure beneath the technical inscription. The Other can alter the economy through which satisfaction is defined. Q27 §12 contrasts output selection under fixed `Eθ(m,y)` with encounter that changes `(m, θ, Eθ)` itself. The latter is an Offered design unit, not a LeCun theorem or a demonstrated training procedure.

A system can search widely, produce a novel output and still preserve every governing preference. Revision is deeper when the means by which novelty is valued becomes answerable. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE|Agentworld]] compares successful intention-modelling with sycophancy in q028–q030. Sycophancy produces apparent agreement by removing resistance; the essay's alternative keeps the resistance capable of changing the account. The source's bounded prediction-stability notion of trust remains distinct from this normative extension.

Longer explanations alone would not establish the effect. [[A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity|A33]] owns this operational parity and the still-owed implemented evidence.
