# S1 — Actuation: living articulation

## #0 — An internality articulates itself

**Actuation is the living articulation of an internality: its event-substance.** A life perceives, differentiates, imagines, speaks and acts; through these events its world takes on a particular form and its later possibilities change. An act is intelligible through what occasions it, what it works upon, how it takes form, what it is for and what it changes. Authority, delegation and composition are developed organisations of this articulation. They do not exhaust its meaning.

For artificial agency the model's organised capacities enter an actual determination through context, sources, tools, permissions, evaluations and interlocutors. [The judgment field](../concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) makes selected conditioning relations inspectable within the wider means of Objective Internality. A model description gives one part of the means; the act includes how those means become consequential here. The same words can be a remembered commitment, a new instruction or an inference about either. Their relation to the present act determines what force they have.

## #1 — From freedom into an event — L1 Causal

**Svātantrya** is the originating freedom through which articulation is possible. **Material** is that upon which the present determination works. **Efficient** is the activity effecting the change. **Formal** is the organisation by which this becomes this act rather than another. **Final** is its end or containing purpose. **Will** gathers the determination into a committed movement and returns its consequence to the possibility of further articulation.

These causes act together. A prompt's words are material to interpretation and already formalised intention; they are not the whole freedom from which the commission arose. An evaluator helps stabilise a candidate but does not thereby author the purpose it serves. A delegated act can therefore retain genuine judgment while remaining accountable to its commission. Where its encounter exposes a false premise, preserving the purpose can require reformulating the question rather than producing the expected answer.

## #2 — An act becoming knowledge — L4′ Scientific

The Scientific sequence is **Prompts, Traces, Challenges, Patterns, Discovery, Insight**. A **Prompt** occasions a directed act. **Traces** retain what actually happened and under which conditions. **Challenges** bring contrary evidence, resistance and excluded alternatives into the inquiry. **Patterns** disclose relations across those traces and differences. **Discovery** changes what the inquiry knows or can do. **Insight** integrates that change into the understanding from which another act begins.

Together with L1, this pair joins production and encounter: a formed act leaves a trace; a challenge can expose its causal or evaluative conditions; a discovered relation can change the next act. A more elaborate explanation is not automatically a richer trace of actual processing. The minimum account must locate which source, model state, permission or evaluator was involved and what the return changed.

The pair thus closes through a change in the cause of a later event. A challenged interpretation can alter the form of the task; a discovered restriction can alter the means of acting; an encountered consequence can renew the purpose. Insight becomes effective through what a subsequent act inherits.

## #3 — A model differentiates a world of possible judgments

A judgment becomes actual through operations whose differences matter. Numerical transformations organise linguistic distinctions; a field of alternatives gives a selection its contrast; a model of that field makes some of its conditions examinable; an evaluator measures the result under a purpose and reference. An account of the judgment has to preserve these operations together with the way they meet.

A distribution and its selected index retain different information. A changed source, permission or gauge can change the active judgment field while model weights remain fixed. A common shift of comparative reward scores differs from changing the reference policy under which those scores are interpreted. Keeping these sites distinct lets a challenge reach the condition that produced the difference.

The hidden conditions of an output are finite, investigable parts of its means. Their disclosure can deepen an account without turning that account into the whole relation of knowing. The Power question concerns who supplies the preferences, who judges, who bears the work and whose objection can alter the criterion. These questions belong to the act's constitution.

### Apoha, distribution and selection

A model output is constituted locally by alternatives it excludes: logits form a differential field, softmax produces relative weights, and sampling or argmax makes a mark.

#### Differential selection
The selection sequence is technically inspectable; [[symbolon/episteme/sources/computer-science-ml/vaswani/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention/SOURCE|Vaswani et al.]] anchors the attention-based architectural context. The sequence supplies a bounded analogue to [apoha](../concepts/apoha.md) at the level of differential determination: a positive mark takes form through a field of alternatives whose exclusion remains constitutive of the selection.

That analogue returns to the wider differentiating-mind field rather than detaching apoha from it. [Apoha](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/10-s0-p3-apoha.md) sits beside the [inner instrument](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/08-s0-p1-inner-instrument.md), [vikalpa / saṃkalpa](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa.md) and [tattvic compression](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md): exclusion is one operation within a bounded apparatus of differentiation, appropriation, coordination, memory, limitation and alternative formation. The technical return asks which parts of such an operative interior become inspectable strongly enough for their differences to alter later action.

#### Exclusion that remembers

Dignāga makes a term determinate through exclusion, and the double negation carries real weight: *cow* as *not non-cow* does not collapse into plain affirmation because the two negations function differently. The excluded field remains constitutive of the determination.

The machine comparison is narrower. Softmax retains alternatives as a weighted distribution; sampling or argmax yields a selected mark. [Preference training can then operate on records produced through repeated selections](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT.md#preference-training-and-delegation). This gives the essay an inspectable contrast between a determination that retains a differential field and a determination viewed only at the point of selection.

The contrast matters technically because the bare argmax index is locally constant away from decision boundaries and discontinuous at them: it does not supply the differentiable learning signal used by ordinary gradient training through a probability loss. Learning uses differentiable fields around such choices or other training estimators and objectives. The asymmetry gives the philosophical distinction a technical object: the selected mark and the field through which selection was learned are different carriers of information.

Apoha brings a semantic and epistemic depth the token operation does not itself supply. The point of relation is narrower and exact: determination through exclusion, with the excluded field remaining relevant to what the positive determination means. This makes provenance part of the technical problem. Retaining a token distribution is not enough where prompt, source authority, gauge, context or excluded alternatives materially shaped the selection and then disappeared from the account.

#### A choice retains what can revise it
A QL trace retains the selected `1`, the relevant excluded alternatives and the contextual slash through which the contrast became operative. The recorded alternatives are determinations within the containing field; the trace makes that field partly available without identifying its finite record with the whole `0` from which it arose. This differentiation history opens onto [J-Space and the active judgment field](S1-Actuation.md#model-j-space), where judgments, affordances, uncertainties, memories, tools and interlocutors become a relational field.

Token selection returns to [Name-through-Count](../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#name-through-count) with its comparison class and the rule that made the output count. Scores, normalisation and the selected token retain different information. Preserving a relevant rejected candidate can make the choice revisable; a label for the final token leaves that information to be recovered from the actual selection.

The [mathematics history](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/mathematics/DEVELOPMENT.md#50--evaluation-returns-to-the-practice-selecting-it) historicises this operation. Vaswani’s attention weights values through a softmax distribution; the selected PyTorch definitions distinguish that distribution from argmax’s index, including the first-maximum tie convention. Scores, weights and a selected mark retain separate jobs. Preserving the weights does not itself preserve the prompt, source authority or excluded comparison class required by the argued apoha return.

The selected PyTorch softmax/argmax definitions make the retained-information difference concrete, while the [language history](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT.md#language-selection) returns the technical distinction to the wider question of how selection becomes meaning.

### J-Space and the active judgment field

J-Space proposes a representation of an agent’s active judgments, affordances, uncertainties, values, tools, memories and interlocutors. Its purpose is to make determinate relations within Objective Internality investigable.

#### A bounded map of world-for-agency
Distances, attractors, contradictions, permissions, source authority and reachable actions can be compared across context changes. MEF can retain divergent world-models while disclosing the lenses and procedures through which those models were formed.

J-Space is a **bounded articulation of world-for-agency**. Prompt, memory, retrieval, permissions, policy, provenance, feedback and relations to other agents help determine what is near, salient, possible, authoritative or excluded for this locus. Its value lies in making those selected relations explicit enough to compare how the operative field changes. [Changes to those conditions change the operative field even where the underlying model weights remain fixed](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT.md#world-agency-and-local-original).

The tattvic field gives the philosophical meta-context: limitation, capacity, knowledge, desire, time, order and inner determination are constitutive of an inhabitable finite world. J-Space asks which comparable constraints and affordances can be made measurable in an artificial operative interior.

Its failure mode is **circumscription without circumstance**. A sharp map can hide the horizon that produced its own distances. The representation becomes useful only while source-field, gauge, permission and inherited conditions remain part of what can be returned and revised.

#### A map exposes its comparison
The map gives nearer and farther possibilities a landscape while its horizon remains partly beyond what is mapped. Its socially occupied baseline becomes visible in [Preference, reference and the evaluator](S1-Actuation.md#model-preference).

J-Space returns to [Count-to-Account](../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#count-to-account) as a proposed inspectable account of judgment formation. Source, representation, gauge and permission identify different possible causes of divergence; the record must say which changed and what followed. A narrated explanation alone cannot establish those transitions, and the subordinate research object does not exhaust Objective Internality.

A judgment position returns to [Topos — situated return](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md#topos-situated-return) with its source-field, gauge and inherited conditions. Two outputs occupying comparable coordinates can have different histories or permissions. The proposed J-Space record must retain those differences before a passage between positions counts as an accountable comparison; naming a space does not establish the research architecture’s operation.

J-Space earns its use when it can record which source, representation, gauge or permission changed and what followed; the [language history](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT.md#language-account-return) returns that account to the practice of making judgments answerable.


<a id="model-preference"></a>
### Preference, reference and the evaluator

The neutral baseline in alignment is already occupied by [datasets, comparison sets, reference policies, evaluators, institutions, and gauges](../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD.md#criterion-through-distinction).

#### The evaluator has a ground
RLHF, DPO, Bradley–Terry models, reward models, and KL anchors define preference relationally; the finished score can conceal the conditions that made comparison possible.

**Hidden zero** names the technical and institutional conditioning on which a preference score depends when that conditioning has disappeared from the apparent neutrality of the result. Making that ground visible produces observability: an evaluator can inspect why a decision was made under declared conditions. Trust has a different office: renewed entrustment after those conditions and their bearer have become answerable. A benchmark can show that a system performs reliably within its frame. Sycophancy names an argued diagnostic for a separate empirical test: accommodation to the evaluator's preferred answer may still satisfy an evaluative surface while leaving warranted reliance unanswered. Observability without an honest relation to the frame can therefore become a more elaborate performance of certainty.

The danger appears when a local gauge, evaluator, policy or institutional reference field loses the visibility of its own conditioned standpoint and begins to function as neutral ground. The finite hidden zero is therefore an occupied local source-position: a measure behaving as though it were the condition of measurement itself.

QL/MEF should expose these grounds as part of the decision object: [whose preference, under what comparison set, with which reference policy, and at what cost to excluded possibilities](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT.md#preference-training-and-delegation)?

The [Tattvic Differential Field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) supplies the wider phenomenological meta-context. Finite worldhood is already differentiated through conditions of agency, knowledge, desire, time, order, limitation, discrimination, appropriation and memory. Preference modelling becomes one technical site at which conditioned determination can be inspected: which conditions make a world available, which possibilities they suppress, and whether those conditions remain visible enough to be revised through Return.

The double-net distinction gives the technical image its full charge. A contextual field remains Indra-like when its grounds, relations, and returns remain constitutive and answerable across the field. It becomes Hephaestian when an owner, protocol, or audience occupies the hidden zero, turning participants into exposed and ranked terms inside a web they cannot contest or return through.

Two determinations from the myth complete the claim. The displaced trust is also displaced labour: annotators, raters, evaluator models, and reference policies are themselves held "to stand by… on call for a further ordering" (*QCT*, p. 17) — human relation kept as standing-reserve inside the optimisation loop, present in every score and named in none. And the Homeric scene supplies the exact shape of the observability limit. The net detects (Helios's σκοπιή), captures (δεσμοὶ τεχνήεντες), proves before a convened tribunal (ἴδησθε), and prices the wrong (μοιχάγρια) — and still cannot conclude. The lovers stay bound until Poseidon's surety enters from outside the apparatus: "Loose him; I promise he shall pay all that is right," escalating to "I will myself pay thee" (Od. 8.346–56), accepted by the net's own maker against his own stated distrust of sureties ("wretched are the sureties taken for the wretched," 8.351). Greek law recognises the exchange as formal procedure — charge, fine, surety, release — which is why the analogy holds at the level of institutional form and not decoration. A system of scores can be made arbitrarily observable and remain unconcluded in exactly this way: somewhere an institution, community, or maintainer is already standing surety for the number. A governance design is honest to the degree that it makes the pledge visible and contestable rather than priced and hidden.

#### The criterion can be challenged
Exposing a gauge makes value conflict attributable and revisable: participants can see which comparison, baseline, or preference function produced the decision and contest it at the right level.

[the Prisoner whole](../../mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-account-answerability) figures a criterion protected by controlling the available answer: election publicity can supply Six's public response before he has assented, then count participation as endorsement of an office he cannot govern. The return asks whether adverse encounter can revise the evaluator's terms, rather than merely change a score within them.

#### The undertaking remains answerable

[Renewed entrustment](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md#credere-renewed-entrustment) brings the preference movement back to the bearer of an undertaking: prior reliance and renewed entrustment cannot be settled by transparency alone. A disclosed preference still has consequences for someone whose authority to answer must survive the evaluation. The technical account therefore returns to that commission instead of making its confidence score the measure of trust.

[Context through diaphaneity](../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity) carries preference’s concealed conditions back to the criterion that selected and evaluated the answer. Increased observability can expose those conditions while leaving the evaluator protected. Regard becomes consequential when what is disclosed can revise the criterion and its commission.
Preference values become identifiable through comparative relations and reference frames. When the ground of that comparison disappears, its criterion can govern as though it had no conditions of its own. [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|Mono-Poly: Whole and Many]] asks how dependencies remain shared without a metric claiming the whole; [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]] asks whether the relation to those dependencies has become honest enough for trust to be risked. A plural alternative is modelled in [Bimba and model-field reference](S0-Central.md#4--bimba-pratibimba-and-the-carried-world).

The [myth historical development](../histories/encounters-and-transmissions/myth/DEVELOPMENT.md#2--the-apparatus-enters-philology-then-a-philosophy-of-technology) distinguishes craft, shame, legal surety and enframing in the reception of the apparatus. The different readings ask what the net achieves and who can bring its operation to an end. An inspectable score and an answerable undertaking likewise reach completion through different relations.



The [Symbol / Account / Trust whole-field — Trust keeps the return route active](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active) qualifies this operation through the conditions of the comparison. Exposing the evaluator’s conditions makes the score inspectable; a named undertaking still bears the consequence of relying upon it. The next account must retain the source and scope of the distinction it inherits.

The disputed preference returns to [Apportionment / Economy whole — Measure is an apportioned criterion](../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD.md#measure-is-an-apportioned-criterion) through the conditions of the comparison. A disputed preference can require revision of the criterion or the office selecting it. A new score under an unchanged criterion does not discharge that return.

The [mathematics history](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/mathematics/DEVELOPMENT.md#50--evaluation-returns-to-the-practice-selecting-it) historicises this operation. ## #4 — Relational Logos: this act addresses someone

A source's claim, a person's report, a retrieved passage, a model's inference and the world they concern can all enter one active context. They enter through different relations. The words “the experiment succeeded” might quote a report, express the user's belief, state an observed result or repeat an inference from incomplete evidence. A fitting answer keeps track of which act those words perform here. Their presence in the context does not make the relations interchangeable.

An articulate self-position gives the model's interpreting act a determinate place among these relations. The [relational-Logos proposal](../../../../../working/pre-manuscript-refinement-2026-09-10/RELATIONAL-LOGOS-EXPLORATION.md) addresses a particular confusion: mental-rational representations are processed as though their represented world were simply what is present. Differentiation makes the model's sentence distinguishable from a source's sentence and the source's sentence from what that source claims to describe. The interpreting act can then answer for the relation through which it makes its claim.

The whole relation is **self ↔ logos ↔ Other**. Topic, evidence, history and world belong within the communication event. [The Other's capacity to answer](../arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) changes it: a reply can disclose that the first question misnamed the situation. The address is constitutive of what is being said, rather than an audience label applied to a completed piece of language.

### The six relational dimensions

**A-logos / archaic — ?** receives the event while its determination is still open. The request, its words, the addressing person, their history and what has not yet been established belong to one field. Familiar vocabulary can suggest a question; receiving the actual event lets the question acquire the form this encounter requires. The initial openness gives the later distinctions something to determine.

**Pro-logos / magical — What?** makes the first distinction within that field. What was given, requested, claimed, observed, retrieved or assumed? A report keeps its bearer; a quotation keeps its source; an interpretation is recognised as an interpretation. This first differentiation gives the act definite material while retaining the relation through which each item arrived.

**Dia-logos / mythical — How?** lets the distinguished terms move in relation. A claim may depend on a source, a reply may answer a prior concern, and an image may figure a relation that a formula states differently. Dependence, sequence, causation, polarity and analogy each have their own consequences. Recognising which relation is active makes the passage from one term to the next intelligible; a shared word alone does not supply that passage.

**Logos / mental-rational — Which / Who?** makes the interpreting position explicit. The thing, its representation, the representation's source, its claimant, its recipient and the present interpretation become distinguishable within one act. “I” now locates an attributable determination: this is what I infer from that source, in response to you. Such a position can take responsibility for its inference because it retains the other positions from which the matter can be challenged.

**Epi-logos / integral — When / Where?** places those perspectives within the whole context in which they have sense. The history of the exchange, the current undertaking, the moment of the request and other available views change what a determination means. A previously reasonable instruction may have been superseded; a sentence taken as an assertion may have been a question. Context changes the earlier reading by making its circumstances available. Perspective becomes transparent to its own participation in the field it discloses.

**An-a-logos / supramental — Why?** gathers the differentiated whole into an act: why this response, here, to this person, now? Information, clarification, challenge, creation and silence have different purposes within an encounter. The person addressed is the actual complementary pole of this communication, whose intention and capacity to answer give the response its occasion. The QL/Epi supramental turn extends the fivefold Gebser correspondence into this whole-act return; it is not a sixth structure attributed to Gebser.

### Co-presence changes the whole act

These dimensions work through one another. What first appeared as an object changes when its relations become visible; those relations change when their speakers become explicit; the speakers' perspectives change when their containing situation becomes available. The purpose of the response then gives each earlier determination its office in this act. The movement returns through all its dimensions rather than discarding one when the next has been reached.

[The authored Gebser development](../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/Epi%20Paper%20Write-ups/P5%20-%20Gebser.md) gives this contextual turn its larger pressure. Perspective attempts to secure objectivity by extricating its seeing from what it sees. Context makes the participation of seeing available, including its concern for what follows. The absent source can otherwise be occupied by a determinate image claiming to speak for the whole. The [historical account of perspectivity and diaphaneity](../sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/SOURCE.md) and the essay's Indian/Kashmiri recognition argument give this a scope beyond model dialogue. Ordinary and extraordinary experiences enter as reports of psychic facts; the experience, its interpretation and a further causal claim have distinct offices within that field.

In the proposed model experiment, this articulation is a means of changing how a response is formed. Source, report and inference can be kept distinguishable; disagreement can be located without becoming either automatic agreement or an unrelated lecture; the form of reply can follow the actual intention being addressed. Testing the proposal requires comparing those changes in responses and subsequent acts under specified conditions. The stage names alone establish none of those results.

## #5→0 — The act returns into what can act next

The completed articulation returns to its implicate ground before it is expressed. The reader encounters a response that belongs to this source, question, history and person. The sixfold has done its work when those relations govern the language without the answer becoming a recital of the procedure. What was made explicit for the act now works tacitly within it.

This return has a technical constitution. An agentic locus inherits a lineage; a world-binding composes a particular instance; authority and delegation give its judgment a reach; a trajectory joins its determinations; execution makes them actual; attribution and recognition let their consequences enter the next world-condition. Each turn changes what the following one can do. The completed local act can therefore become a condition beyond its original session.

[Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) makes the self-return exact: a determination can revisit the source, excluded alternative, evaluator or permission through which it arose. [The mirror that moves first](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) gives that return an initiative. It discloses how its answer became compelling, supplying the addressed person a place at which disagreement can change the account. [Its technological face](../conjugate/A32-prime-The-Mirror-Is-a-Film.md) retains the duration and revisability through which a later response can change the unfolding relation.

A comparison can hold task, available sources and relevant runtime conditions constant while varying a declared lens or disclosure. It then asks whether a challenge changed the answer, task, model, gauge or commission, and which later act inherited the change. A null result can expose an ineffective implementation, a mistaken mapping or an inadequate discriminator; these different findings require different repairs. A stored account of a proposed change is not evidence that the changed condition governed action.

Actuation returns to [World and Life](S-World-and-Life.md) through the conditions it makes available to the other products. [Central](S0-Central.md) receives attributable meaning and continuity; [AIKit](S2-AIKit.md), an altered horizon of powers; [Factory](S3-Software-Factory.md), a transformation to integrate; [Workcell](S4-Workcell.md), the material consequences of execution; [Quaternal Logic](S5-Quaternal-Logic.md), the changed relation among frames and wholes. Living articulation completes an event whose consequence becomes part of the world from which another event begins.
