## Source scholarship

Westerhoff's objections: Madhyamaka uses the catuṣkoṭi in different ways, so it has no single logical form for one logic to capture; a dialetheist reading risks immunising itself against textual counterexample, and interpretive parsimony favours classical logic where it suffices; it is unclear where Priest's "natural reading" ends and authoritative interpretation begins. He closes positively, likening the project to reconstructing the Antikythera mechanism. He relays Priest's wording with Priest's pages (to be collated against the book before any use): Dōgen as "a focal point of all the rays of our narrative" (142); the book "is not a scholarly work" (xvii); the coda's question (149).
