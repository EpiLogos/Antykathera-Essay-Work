## Passage card — `godel-1931-undecidable-propositions-q003` — consistency as a hypothesis (note 46)

> Die Existenz widerspruchsfreier und nicht ω-widerspruchsfreier ϰ ist damit natürlich nur unter der Voraussetzung bewiesen, daß es überhaupt widerspruchsfreie ϰ gibt (d. h. daß P widerspruchsfrei ist).

- **Locator:** note 46, p. 190, original German article (the note's marker follows the sentence that says the extended system ϰ′, obtained by adjoining the negation of the undecidable sentence, is consistent but not ω-consistent).
- **Status:** quotation-ready (read from the page image, p. 190).
- **Verification:** read by `s01-sourcelock` (G2), 2026-10-04; transcribed from the page image of the Internet Archive scan.
- **Source relation:** extracted.
- **Evidential action:** qualifies (the existence of consistent but not ω-consistent systems is proved only on the hypothesis that consistent systems exist).
- **Consumers:** essay §0/1, M04, marker `s01-godel` (the note's second locator).
- **Use boundary:** the note concerns only the existence of consistent, non-ω-consistent systems, which it makes conditional on the consistency of P. It is not a statement of the hypotheses of the incompleteness theorem as such (those are in Satz VI).
