## Source scholarship

MacTutor states that the 6174 property was "first discovered by Kaprekar in 1946 and he announced it at the Madras Mathematical Conference in 1949", and that he published on it in *Scripta Mathematica* in 1953 ("Problems involving reversal of digits"). The 1949 Madras announcement agrees with the 1961 *Scripta Mathematica* notice; the 1946 date and the 1953 paper are MacTutor's alone and are not used. Print sources behind the page: S. P. Arya, *Mathematics Student* 55 (1987): 241–43; R. C. Gupta, *Indian Journal of History of Science* 41, no. 2 (2006): 223–26 (not consulted).
