> "p | q may be interpreted as the proposition neither p nor q; in other words, | has the properties of the logical constant neither-nor. This logical constant we may symbolize by [Sheffer's sign], and for obvious reasons we may name rejection."

- **Locator:** § 2, "Application to Primitive Logical Constants," p. 487.
- **Status:** quotation-ready (the bracketed insertion replaces Sheffer's special rejection sign, which the text layer does not render reliably).
- **Verification:** transcribed from the AMS PDF text layer and read against the printed page, agent, 2026-10-05. Also on p. 487: negation defined as p rejected with itself, and disjunction as the rejection of (p rejected with q) with itself.
- **Source relation:** Extracted.
- **Evidential action:** supports.
- **Consumers:** §3 · #0 (reworked draft, 2026-10-05): a single binary operation from which negation and disjunction, and so every truth-function, are defined.
- **Use boundary:** functional completeness of one connective within two-valued logic; the parent relation `/ = −/−` is the essay's own and stands before any assignment of truth-values.
