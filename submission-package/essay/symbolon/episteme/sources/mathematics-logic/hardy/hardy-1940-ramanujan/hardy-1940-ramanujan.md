## Source scholarship

Hardy's first lecture recounts receiving Ramanujan's letter of 1913, sorting its formulae into the known, the false and the unprecedented, and inferring the writer's honesty and power. Earlier in the same lecture Hardy records that Ramanujan had become a clerk in the office of the Port Trust of Madras in 1912 (exact page between pp. 3 and 7 not yet fixed).
