## Source scholarship

The paper’s introduction presents the argument as a reaction against “exaggerated subservience to the ‘Copernican principle’”: we should not assume a privileged central position, but our situation cannot be privileged in no sense, since specially favourable conditions are prerequisites of our existence. Carter then distinguishes predictions that need no anthropic principle, those that need a “weak” principle, and those that need an extended “strong” principle.
