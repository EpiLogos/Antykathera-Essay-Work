## Source scholarship

Reversibility is central: gift/counter-gift and the undoing of accumulated value oppose a system that can circulate terms without receiving that challenge. The Preface also subjects its own theoretical terms to removal; the symbolic cannot simply become another guaranteed positive category. Death therefore cannot be reduced to the referent's disappearance: the book's programme includes the exchange of death and the separation of living and dead.

The three orders specify mutations in the law of value. Counterfeit belongs to the Renaissance/classical period through the Industrial Revolution; production to industrial organisation; simulation to the code. This is Baudrillard's historical construction, with independent historical corroboration still owed. Its own discussion warns against idealising the restricted, hierarchical circulation of earlier signs.
