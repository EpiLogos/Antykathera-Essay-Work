### Passage card — `brhadaranyaka-upanisad-muller-1884-q001` — the teaching by “No, no!”

> Next follows the teaching (of Brahman) by No, no!^1 for there is nothing else higher than this (if one says): ‘It is not so.’ Then comes the name ‘the True of the True,’ the senses being the True, and he (the Brahman) the True of them.

- **Locator:** *Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad* 2.3.6 (the last paragraph of section 6; section 6 begins on p. 107 and this paragraph is on p. 108, under the running head “II Adhyaya, 3 Brahmana, 6”). Müller's footnote ^1 to “No, no!” reads: “See III, 9, 26; IV, 2, 4; IV, 4, 22; IV, 5, 15.”
- **Status:** quotation-ready (the footnote marker after “No, no!” is printed in the scan and appears as ^1 in the OCR text; wording read in the scan text).
- **Verification:** read 2026-10-04 (g5a). Müller renders *neti neti* as “No, no!”, not “not this, not this”; the essay's gloss “not this, not this” is the common alternative rendering (e.g. Mādhavānanda) and is not Müller's.
- **Source relation:** extracted.
- **Evidential action:** supports (the formula withdraws predicates; here it follows the two forms of Brahman, the material and the immaterial).
- **Consumers:** essay §0/1, M03 marker `s01-nirguna`.

## Essay uses

M03, marker `s01-nirguna`: *neti neti* as the withdrawal of each proposed predicate so that no final predicate grounds the Absolute; the cross-references in Müller's footnote supply the other four occurrences (3.9.26, 4.2.4, 4.4.22, 4.5.15).
