## Source scholarship

Of 42 appellants, 39 had their convictions quashed (para. 447(ii)); three appeals were dismissed (para. 447(i)). The court held the Horizon prosecutions an affront to the conscience of the court and found that reliability of Horizon data was essential to the prosecution cases (paras. 121, 137).
