## Provenance and standing

The book was not read. Identity was confirmed from the publisher and library records, and its relation to MKULTRA from a CIA review: Jay Watkins, review of *Operation Paperclip*, *Studies in Intelligence* 58, no. 3 (September 2014), https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Review-Operation-Paperclip.pdf, which says the book treats Hubertus Strughold, Kurt Blome and Reinhard Gehlen and describes Operation Bluebird as later called MKULTRA. That is the reviewer's summary; verify it in Jacobsen's text.
