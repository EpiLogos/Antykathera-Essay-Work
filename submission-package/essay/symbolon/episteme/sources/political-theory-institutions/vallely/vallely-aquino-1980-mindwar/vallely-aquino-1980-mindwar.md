# Vallely and Aquino — From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory (1980)

## Bibliographic identity

Paul E. Vallely and Michael A. Aquino, "From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory" (Presidio of San Francisco, CA: Headquarters, 7th Psychological Operations Group, United States Army Reserve, 1980), https://archive.org/details/from-psyop-to-mind-war-the-psychology-of-victory. The printed byline names Colonel Paul E. Vallely, commander, first, with Major Michael A. Aquino, PSYOP Research and Analysis Team Leader; the Internet Archive metadata misspells Vallely. The paper was an internal talking paper that Vallely asked Aquino to write "to encourage some future thought within the PSYOP community" and circulated to government offices and commands; it was not published as an article, and no *Military Review* reprint was found. An introductory note by Aquino dated November 2003 gives his rank as Lt. Colonel, Military Intelligence, USAR-Ret.
