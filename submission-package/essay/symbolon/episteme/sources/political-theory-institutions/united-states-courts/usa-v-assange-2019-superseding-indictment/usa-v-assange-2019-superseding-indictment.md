## Source scholarship

The caption lists eighteen counts: Count 1, conspiracy to receive national defense information, 18 U.S.C. § 793(g); Counts 2-17 under 18 U.S.C. § 793(b), (c), (d) and (e) (obtaining and disclosing national defense information); Count 18, conspiracy to commit computer intrusion, 18 U.S.C. §§ 371 and 1030. The filing supersedes the indictment first returned in this case number in 2018 and unsealed on April 11, 2019.
