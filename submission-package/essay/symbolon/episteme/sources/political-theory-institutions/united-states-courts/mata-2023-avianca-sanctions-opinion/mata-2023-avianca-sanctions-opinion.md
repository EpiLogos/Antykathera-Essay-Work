## Source scholarship

The opinion makes findings of fact after a June 8, 2023 hearing: Schwartz drafted the March 1, 2023 affirmation in opposition to the motion to dismiss using ChatGPT (¶¶ 5–6, 11, 38–40); the cited decisions did not exist (¶ 36); when the court's orders of April 11 and 12 required copies, an affidavit filed April 25 (signed by LoDuca, drafted and compiled by Schwartz) annexed only excerpts (¶¶ 13–14, 18–22); Schwartz had no full text of any such decision and cited and quoted only from excerpts the chatbot generated (¶ 40). The court sanctioned the respondents under Rule 11, 28 U.S.C. § 1927 and its inherent power (conclusions of law, from p. 21).
