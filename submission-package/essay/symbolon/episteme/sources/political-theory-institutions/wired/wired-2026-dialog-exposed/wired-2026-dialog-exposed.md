### wired-2026-dialog-exposed-p002 — Offices named and the directory/registration distinction (added 2026-10-04, source-locking)

**Locator:** Paragraphs beginning "The group, called Dialog" (2006 founding; invitation-only); "A directory in the website's code" (independent verification); "A source separately provided WIRED with the registration list" (222 names; "active member"/"guest"; August 12-16 retreat near Dublin); "Together, alongside the mundane fare" (NATO commander on the registration records; the website directory "names sitting Trump administration officials, two US senators"); "Those executives appear side by side" (Treasury secretary and Army secretary named in the directory); "The registration records appear to show".

**Located operation:** The article gives the 2006 founding as by Peter Thiel (the second WIRED article adds Auren Hoffman). The sitting senators (two) and sitting administration officials come from the website directory of 113 names, not from the 222-name retreat registration list; the registration list is cited for a NATO supreme allied commander and for further figures absent from the directory. The offices named are the Treasury secretary (a cabinet office) and the Army secretary (a service secretary, not a cabinet office); the article does not speak of "cabinet officers" in general. The registration records are said to "appear to show" attendance, and the 2026 retreat had not yet taken place when the article ran.
