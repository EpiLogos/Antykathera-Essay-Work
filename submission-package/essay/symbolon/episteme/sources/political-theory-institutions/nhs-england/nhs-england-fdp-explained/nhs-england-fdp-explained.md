### nhs-england-fdp-explained-q001 — What the platform is: connecting existing systems

**Locator:** Headings "Our purpose" and "What is it?"; and under "Our mission", "A fully connected NHS".

**Exact text:** "The NHS Federated Data Platform connects vital health information across the NHS, helping staff deliver better care for patients and work more efficiently."

"core infrastructure: the platform primarily connects existing systems and data, breaking down silos to create a unified view of information"

"The NHS Federated Data Platform brings together information from different systems across the NHS, enabling hospitals and ICBs to use and build digital solutions that improve service efficiency with patient care prioritised."

**Context and use boundary:** The "What is it?" list also names "product solutions" (applications such as "‘waiting list management’ and ‘discharge planning’") and "analytical capabilities". Under "Connecting multiple legacy systems" the page says the platform "doesn’t replace – it sits on top of multiple systems".

### nhs-england-fdp-explained-q002 — Instances, and NHS bodies as data controllers

**Locator:** Heading "How does it work?", first paragraph.

**Exact text:** "Each NHS organisation, for example a trust or integrated care board (ICB), will have their own ‘instance’ of the NHS Federated Data Platform for which they are the data controller and NHS England is responsible for the national instance."

**Context and use boundary:** The following paragraphs say instances can connect and share information "where data protection law allows it", and that nationally shared planning data passes through separate privacy-enhancing technology to remove identifiers.
