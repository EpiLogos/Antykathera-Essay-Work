## Source scholarship

Reports that Representatives Thomas Massie and Ro Khanna, after viewing the unredacted files at the Department of Justice, said the names of six men had been redacted, and that Khanna read those names on the House floor; the department's response (Deputy Attorney General Blanche) was that redactions were legally required to protect personal information and that at least one named person appeared unredacted in the files. The piece also gives approximate volumes released and unreleased; those figures were not relied upon pending a primary check.
