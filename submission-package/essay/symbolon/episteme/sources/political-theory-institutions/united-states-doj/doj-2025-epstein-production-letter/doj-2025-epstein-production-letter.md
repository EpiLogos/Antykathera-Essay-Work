### doj-2025-epstein-production-letter-p002 — Page-level locator and limits on "near-total" (added 2026-10-04, source-locking)

**Locator:** Letter "Page 1 of 6" to "Page 6 of 6"; p. 1: production "in compliance with" the Act, which "President Donald J. Trump signed ... on November 19, 2025"; p. 2: the Review Protocol's five grounds for redaction or withholding (victim identifying information; child sexual abuse material; material that would jeopardise an active investigation or prosecution; images of death, physical abuse or injury; properly classified national-defense or foreign-policy information) and a "limited amount" of privileged material withheld, with a privilege log promised "in due course"; p. 3: over 1,200 victim or relative names identified and redacted, the EFTA eight-digit Bates numbering and the note that a missing number means a document was pulled for further redaction.

**Located operation:** Pages 1-4 are the correct span for the note (process, categories, numbering); pages 5-6 describe collection sources and are not needed. The letter describes the redactions as limited to the statutory grounds and privilege and calls the privileged withholding limited; it does not quantify redactions and does not support a description of them as "near-total".

**Status:** paraphrase-only; located context verified from the downloaded PDF.

**Provenance:** Official six-page DOJ letter (Deputy Attorney General Blanche to Members of Congress), downloaded and read 2026-10-04; the Act is Pub. L. No. 119-38, approved November 19, 2025 (see [[us-congress-2025-epstein-files-transparency-act]]).
