## Source scholarship

DOJ announces a further release of more than three million pages (with more than 2,000 videos and 180,000 images), bringing the total published under the Act to about 3.5 million pages. It states that duplicates, material protected by deliberative-process and attorney-client privilege, statutory exceptions (depictions of violence) and documents unrelated to Epstein or Maxwell were withheld, that redactions were to be limited to the protection of victims, and that more than 500 attorneys and reviewers took part. It gives no count of redacted pages.
