# Nationaal Archief — Octrooi voor de VOC (1602)

## Bibliographic identity

The original charter is Nationaal Archief, The Hague, Archief van de Verenigde Oostindische Compagnie (VOC), 1602–1795, inventory 1.04.02, no. 1: "Octrooi verleend door de Staten-Generaal betreffende de alleenhandel ten oosten van Kaap de Goede Hoop en ten westen van de Straat van Magallanes voor de duur van 21 jaar" (https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/1.04.02/invnr/1). The institutional description is "Octrooi voor de VOC (1602)," Bronnenbox, Nationaal Archief (https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/beleven/onderwijs/bronnenbox/octrooi-voor-de-voc-1602), with the finding aid at https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/zoekhulpen/voc-1602-1800. No transcription of the charter was obtained.

### nationaal-archief-voc-octrooi-1602-p001 — Charter, monopoly and powers

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/beleven/onderwijs/bronnenbox/octrooi-voor-de-voc-1602; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** Bronnenbox page and finding aid (no pagination).

**Located operation:** The charter is dated 20 March 1602 and grants exclusive trade for 21 years between the Cape of Good Hope and the Strait of Magellan. The finding aid states that the VOC may conclude contracts with foreign rulers, build forts, administer justice, hire soldiers and wage war (Dutch: contracten sluiten met buitenlandse vorsten, forten bouwen, recht spreken, soldaten inhuren en oorlog voeren); the essay's sentence closely paraphrases it. "Appoint governors" and "defensive war" are not in this wording and would need the charter text, article by article.

**Status:** paraphrase-only

**Source relation:** Paraphrased.
