## Source scholarship

A British government consultation. The Foreword characterises SLAPPs as abuse of process to "harass, intimidate and financially and psychologically exhaust" an opponent and says they are typically initiated by reputation management firms and brought by "extremely wealthy individuals and corporations"; chapter 1 says that individuals or organisations wishing to prevent information reaching the public eye engage reputation management firms or legal professionals, and that the desired outcome is to prevent further investigation.
