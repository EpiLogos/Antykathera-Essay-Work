## Source scholarship

The S-1 is the company's own sworn disclosure to the regulator at the moment of going public. It names the three Founders (Karp, Cohen, Thiel), states the 2003 founding and the counterterrorism origin, and sets out the Class F share structure through which the Founders were to hold up to 49.999999% of total voting power. The founders' control structure and the founding narrative are company statements; the essay's reading of them as captured power is its own inference.

### palantir-2020-form-s1-q001 — The Founders named, with their Class F voting control

**Locator:** Preliminary prospectus cover page (unnumbered; "Subject to Completion, dated August 25, 2020"), paragraph beginning "We have two classes of common stock".

**Exact text:** "All shares of Class F common stock will be held by a voting trust established by Alexander Karp, Stephen Cohen, and Peter Thiel (our “Founders”) pursuant to a voting trust agreement (the “Founder Voting Trust Agreement”)."

Same paragraph: "So long as our Founders who are then party to the Founder Voting Agreement and certain of their affiliates collectively meet a minimum ownership threshold on the applicable record date for a vote of the stockholders, the Class F common stock, together with the Founder Voting Trust Agreement and the Founder Voting Agreement, will give these Founders the ability to control up to 49.999999% of the total voting power of our capital stock."

**Context and use boundary:** The Class F stock was to be authorized "subject to stockholder approval"; the realized percentages are blank in this filing and appear in later amendments and the 424B4. Quote as the August 25, 2020 filing's statement of intent.

### palantir-2020-form-s1-q002 — Thiel as co-founder and chairman since 2003

### palantir-2020-form-s1-q003 — Founding date and counterterrorism origin

**Locator:** "Business" — "Overview", p. 121 (the same sentence appears in "Prospectus Summary", p. 1). Related: "Management’s Discussion and Analysis" — "Crisis & Instability", p. 93.

**Exact text (p. 121):** "We were founded in 2003 and started building software for the intelligence community in the United States to assist in counterterrorism investigations and operations."

**Context and use boundary:** The MD&A overview (p. 86) adds: "We founded the company in 2003 to build software for use in counterterrorism operations." The filing names Gotham (2008) and Foundry (2016) as the two principal platforms. The founding narrative is the company's self-description.
