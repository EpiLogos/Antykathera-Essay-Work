### us-house-2011-hrept-112-47-in-god-we-trust-p001 — Dates of the motto

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-112hrpt47/html/CRPT-112hrpt47.htm; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** Report text (no page pinned).

**Located operation:** The report says the motto first appeared on the 1864 two-cent coin, was made the national motto by Public Law 84-851 (36 U.S.C. § 302), approved July 30, 1956, and first used on paper money in 1957 on the one-dollar silver certificate, which entered circulation October 1, 1957. The BEP history adds that a 1955 law required the motto on all currency and that it appears first on series 1957 $1 silver certificates, then on 1963 Federal Reserve notes. The Library of Congress post cites the 1864 act (13 Stat. 54) and the 1956 joint resolution (70 Stat. 732) and says the motto did not appear on all circulating currency until 1966. Every date in the M05 note is supported.
