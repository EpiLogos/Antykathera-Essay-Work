Authors (University of Denver) are named on the publisher's page. No copy of the book itself was consulted; identity and table of contents come from the publisher's page, read 2026-10-04 (contents: 1 "The Onslaught of SLAPPs," with sections "The Definition: What Are 'SLAPPs'?" and "The Process: How SLAPPs Work"; 2 the right to petition; 3 real estate SLAPPs; 4 public-servant SLAPPs; 5 eco-SLAPPs; 6 "Not in My Backyard" SLAPPs; 7 rights SLAPPs; and later chapters on responses). ## Source scholarship

The first book-length study of SLAPPs. Its definition is chapter 1; the essay's gloss ("a suit brought to burden critics with the cost of defending themselves more than to win") rests on the book's account that the filer's goal is to chill, not to win, and is the author's compression. The page for the definition has not been located.
