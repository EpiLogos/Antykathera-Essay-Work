## Source scholarship

The essay builds on the French verb *différer* in its two senses, to differ (distinction, discernibility) and to defer (delay, a detour that puts off presence), and on the silent *a* that written *différance* adds and speech does not give. Meaning in a system of signs is produced by differences among signs and by reference onward, so that no concept is present in itself.

## Passage card — `derrida-1982-margins-differance-q002` — concept in a chain; no self-present signified
