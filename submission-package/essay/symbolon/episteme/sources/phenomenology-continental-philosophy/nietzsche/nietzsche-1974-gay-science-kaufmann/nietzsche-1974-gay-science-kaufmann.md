## Essay use

Primary source for the scene in which a madman lights a lantern in the morning, goes to the marketplace, and announces the death of God to people who do not believe in God. The essay uses only the setting: that Nietzsche placed the announcement in a marketplace (*Markt*). The essay's later reading of why the news belongs in the sphere of money is its own argument.

**Claim boundary:** the scene is a parable told of a madman, and those who hear him are non-believers; it supports the claim about where Nietzsche set the death of God, not any claim about economic doctrine.
