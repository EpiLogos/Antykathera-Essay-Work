### brady-2017-emotion-diffusion-moralized-content-p001 — Moral-emotional words and diffusion (Twitter data)

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1618923114; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** Abstract and results (pp. 7313–18; no page pinned).

**Located operation:** The study uses Twitter data, not Facebook, and finds that each additional moral-emotional word in a message increased its diffusion by about 20%. It supports the claim that affective intensity helps content spread; it is not evidence about Facebook's emoji weighting.
