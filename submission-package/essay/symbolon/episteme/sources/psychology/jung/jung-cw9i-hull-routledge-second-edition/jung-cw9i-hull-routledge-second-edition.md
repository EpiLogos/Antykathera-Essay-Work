### jung-cw9i-hull-routledge-second-edition-p005 — The constant axial system: invariable geometric proportions

**Locator:** ¶155, continuing; user-local PDF page 91 (printed p. 80).

**Located operation:** Continuing the crystal comparison of p001, Jung says the axial system determines only the stereometric structure, not the concrete form of the individual crystal, which varies endlessly in size and in the development of its planes; what remains constant is the axial system, or rather the invariable geometric proportions underlying it; the same holds of the archetype, which has an invariable nucleus of meaning in principle and never as regards its concrete manifestation.
