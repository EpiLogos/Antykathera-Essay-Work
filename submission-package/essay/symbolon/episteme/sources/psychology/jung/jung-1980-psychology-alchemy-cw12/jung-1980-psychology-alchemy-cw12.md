### jung-1980-psychology-alchemy-cw12-p003 — The axiom of Maria

**Locator:** ¶26; PDF pp. 53–54. Index-inferred printed p. 23 ("one … as fourth (axiom of Maria), 23, 26"; "Tiamat, 23, 25").

**Located operation:** Jung calls the saying of Maria Prophetissa one of the central axioms of alchemy, in his rendering one becoming two, two three, and out of the third the one as the fourth; alchemy is an undercurrent to surface Christianity, compensating its tension of opposites as a dream compensates consciousness; the axiom interpolates the even numbers (feminine, earth, the underworld, evil) between the odd numbers of Christian dogma, personified by the serpent of Mercurius (¶26).

### jung-1980-psychology-alchemy-cw12-p004 — The inferior function brings up the Self (three and four)

**Locator:** ¶31; PDF pp. 56–57. Index-inferred printed pp. 25–26 ("inferior/fourth/undifferentiated [function], 26"; "axiom of Maria, … 26").

**Located operation:** Beyond what p002 records (four as the minimum determinants of a whole judgment; vacillation between three and four), ¶31 says that the few cases producing three showed an unconsciousness of the inferior function; that the least differentiated or inferior function is so contaminated with the collective unconscious that on becoming conscious it brings up, among others, the archetype of the self, Maria's "one as the fourth"; and that four signifies the feminine, motherly, physical, three the masculine, fatherly, spiritual, so that the uncertainty between three and four is a wavering between spiritual and physical.

**Status:** locator-verified; paraphrase-only.

**Verification:** as p003 (bracketed marker [31]).

**Source relation:** Paraphrased; Jung's gendered number-symbolism is his and stays source-specific.
