### mumford-1995-air-evaluation-remote-viewing-p001 — History of the programme

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://documents3.theblackvault.com/documents/stargate/pdfs/CIA-RDP96-00791R000200180006-4.pdf; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** chap. 1, "Background and History," pp. 1-3 to 1-4; Utts, chap. 3, p. 3-4; Hyman, chap. 3, from p. 3-43; Executive Summary, p. E-1.

**Located operation:** The report describes "Star Gate" as a Defense Intelligence Agency programme using the paranormal, primarily remote viewing (p. 1-3); says the CIA's own programme began in 1972 and was discontinued in 1977, DIA direct involvement began about 1985, the CIA declassified its past programme efforts in 1995, and the CIA's Office of Research and Development contracted AIR in June 1995 (p. 1-4). Utts (p. 3-4) dates government-sponsored research to the early 1970s at the Stanford Research Institute (in existence until 1989), after which sponsorship moved to SAIC under Edwin May; Hyman dates the SRI programme to 1973–89 with continuation at SAIC 1992–94. Utts's definitions separate telepathy (the source is another person), clairvoyance (real time, no human source) and precognition, and the report treats telepathy mainly through ganzfeld experiments, not remote viewing. The report uses the terms remote viewing, psychic functioning, anomalous cognition and paranormal; it does not describe the programme as testing telepathy and clairvoyance as instruments of espionage.

**Status:** locator-verified. No quotation admitted.

**Source relation:** Paraphrased. The essay's span "1972 until 1995" runs across successive sponsors and is not one continuous funding line (CIA 1972–77; Army and DIA later; Star Gate ended after the 1995 review).
