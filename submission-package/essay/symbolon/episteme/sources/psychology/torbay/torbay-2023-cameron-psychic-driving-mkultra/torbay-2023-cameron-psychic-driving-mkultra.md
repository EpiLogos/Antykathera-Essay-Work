### torbay-2023-cameron-psychic-driving-mkultra-p001 — Subproject 68 and Cameron's methods

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://doi.org/10.1177/0957154X231163763; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** sections "MK Ultra" and "Psychic driving and depatterning" (pages within 320–30 not pinned).

**Located operation:** In January 1957 Sidney Gottlieb launched MK Ultra Subproject 68, which gave Cameron (Allan Memorial Institute, Montreal) about $69,000 over the following years, routed through the CIA front organisation, the Society for the Investigation of Human Ecology. *Psychic driving* is the repeated playing of recorded verbal statements meant to change behaviour; *depatterning* is prolonged sleep, electroshock and sensory deprivation meant to reduce patients to an infantile state to be rebuilt as the experimenter wished (Torbay cites Cameron and Pande, 1958, for the depatterning methods). Torbay adds that Cameron allegedly never knew the money came from the CIA, so the CIA funded the work without that being established as Cameron's knowledge.

**Status:** locator-verified at section level. No quotation admitted.

**Source relation:** Paraphrased.

**Consumer:** M05 note `s01-mkultra`.

**Use boundary:** The 1977 Senate hearing does not mention Cameron, Allan Memorial, depatterning or psychic driving, nor Subproject 68 by name (see [[senate-1977-project-mkultra-hearing]]); Torbay is the source for that attribution. Torbay does not treat the 1988 *Orlikow* settlement or Canadian compensation.
