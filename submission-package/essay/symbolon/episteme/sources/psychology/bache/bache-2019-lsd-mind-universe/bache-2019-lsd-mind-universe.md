## Essay use

First-person archive of 73 high-dose LSD sessions over twenty years, reporting recurrent individual, collective, archetypal, and cosmic registers. It reintroduces a displaced experiential domain into the psychoid evidence field.

**Claim boundary:** the work supplies supportive first-person phenomenology. Ontological conclusions require additional arguments and should be tested against critical psychedelic science, neurophenomenology, pharmacology, set/setting, memory, and retrospective interpretation.
