## Source scholarship

A prospective online study: individuals planning to use a psychedelic completed questionnaires one week before (N = 654) and one day, two weeks, four weeks and two years after a psychedelic experience. The authors report that lifetime psychedelic use correlated with baseline nature relatedness, that nature relatedness was significantly increased at two weeks, four weeks and two years, and that the increase correlated with increases in wellbeing and depended on the extent of ego dissolution and on the perceived influence of natural surroundings during the acute state.

## Passage card — `kettner-2019-egoism-ecoism-q002` — qualification: self-selection and causal wording

**Paraphrase:** The sample is self-selected (people already planning to use a psychedelic) and uncontrolled. The authors’ conclusion speaks of evidence for a “context- and state-dependent causal effect”; the essay’s prose keeps the more cautious “links”.
