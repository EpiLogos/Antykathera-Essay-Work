## Passage card — `atilf-tlfi-saveur-q001` — saveur: from Latin sapor

**Paraphrase:** The entry gives French *saveur* (Old French *savor*) as from Latin *sapor, -oris*, “taste, flavour of a thing; smell, perfume; something good to taste,” figuratively *homo sine sapore*, “an insipid man”; senses attested from about 1160 are “seasoning, condiment” and “quality perceived by the sense of taste,” with figurative uses in the thirteenth century and later.
