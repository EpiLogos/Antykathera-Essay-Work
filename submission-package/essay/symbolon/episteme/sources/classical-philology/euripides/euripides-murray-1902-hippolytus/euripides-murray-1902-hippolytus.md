## Source scholarship

The Nurse’s speech to Phaedra (lines 433–81) argues that love is a god’s power and that resisting it is not wise; line 474–75 is the line Liddell–Scott–Jones cite (as “E. *Hipp.* 474”) in their entry on *hybris* for the word predicated of an act.

## Passage card — `euripides-murray-1902-hippolytus-q001` — 474–75: “nothing other than hybris, to wish to be stronger than the gods”

> λῆξον δ’ ὑβρίζουσ’· οὐ γὰρ ἄλλο πλὴν ὕβρις
