# Swedenborg — Swedenborg's Dream Diary, 1743–1744 (2001)

[Source index](../../../README.md)

## Bibliographic identity

*Swedenborg’s Dream Diary*, by Emanuel Swedenborg with an introductory essay and commentary by Lars Bergquist, translated by Anders Hallengren (West Chester, PA: Swedenborg Foundation, 2001; Swedenborg Studies 11; ISBN 978-0-87785-198-1). It renders Swedenborg’s manuscript journal of April 1743–October 1744, known in English as the *Journal of Dreams*; the Swedish edition from which it is translated is Bergquist, *Swedenborgs drömbok* (Norstedts, 1989). Publisher, translator, editor, year and ISBN confirmed from the Swedenborg Foundation and Swedenborg Society catalogue entries, 2026-10-04 (agent G6b, 2026-10-04); the book itself was not seen. This is the one edition selected for the note’s statement about the journal.
