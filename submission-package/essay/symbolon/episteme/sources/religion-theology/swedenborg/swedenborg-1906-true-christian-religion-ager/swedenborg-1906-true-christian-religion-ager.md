## Passage card — `swedenborg-1906-true-christian-religion-ager-q001` — the Lord’s call and the opening of the spiritual world

**Paraphrase:** Swedenborg testifies that the Lord manifested himself before him, sent him to the office of teaching the doctrines of the new church, afterwards opened the sight of his spirit and admitted him into the spiritual world, so that he saw the heavens and the hells and talked with angels and spirits, and this continuously for several years. He adds that from the first day of that call he received nothing of the church’s doctrine from any angel, but from the Lord alone while he read the Word.

- **Locator:** §779 (section VIII of the work: the Second Coming of the Lord is effected by means of a man to whom the Lord has manifested himself in person).
- **Status:** paraphrase-only.
- **Verification:** Paragraph read in the Ager translation (online), 2026-10-04; the quoted fragment “the Lord manifested Himself before me, His servant, and sent me to this office” was confirmed in the Ager text, the rest is paraphrase. agent G6b, 2026-10-04.
- **Source relation:** Paraphrased.
- **Evidential action:** supports.
- **Consumers:** essay §0/1, M04, marker `s01-swedenborg`.
- **Use boundary:** Warrants the prose’s statement that Swedenborg himself described a sustained engagement with the spiritual world (conversation with angels and spirits) as a vocation.
