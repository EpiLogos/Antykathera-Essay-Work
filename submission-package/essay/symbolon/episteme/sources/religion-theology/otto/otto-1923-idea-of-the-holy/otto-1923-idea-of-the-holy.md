# Otto — The Idea of the Holy (Harvey translation, 1923)

## Bibliographic identity

Rudolf Otto’s *Das Heilige* (German original 1917) first appeared in English as *The Idea of the Holy*, translated by John W. Harvey from the ninth German edition (London: Oxford University Press, 1923). Library catalogue records agree on translator, press and year (checked by web search 2026-10-04). Jung’s own bibliography in *Psychology and Religion* (CW 11) lists the work as “The Idea of the Holy. Translated by John W. Harvey. 4th impr., Oxford, 1926”, so the printing Jung cites is a later impression of the same translation. This house records the term’s origin only; no page of Otto has been consulted.

## Essay use

Origin of the term *numinous* that Jung adopts. The essay (M04, `s01-numinous`) names Otto as the source of the term through Jung’s own attribution at CW 11 ¶6 (located in `jung-1969-psychology-religion-cw11`, card p009). No Otto passage is quoted or paraphrased.

## Open acquisition and verification

Otto’s own definition of the numinous and the *mysterium tremendum et fascinans* formula would need a consulted copy and a page locator before any claim is made about Otto’s wording; the essay does not make one.
