## Essay use

Primary source for meaning in use, rule-following, language-games, and forms of life. It helps move the formal-limit genealogy from an abstract calculus toward the living practice that gives a rule its application.

**Passages needed:** the rule-following discussion, language-games, agreement in forms of life, and any line used to link meaning to practice.

**Claim boundary:** later Wittgenstein relocates rigour within use, practice, and form of life, thereby blocking the treatment of language as a self-applying autonomous calculus. QL's subject-pole is the essay's further argument and must be warranted independently.

## Passage card — `wittgenstein-1953-philosophical-investigations-q001` — rule-following and interpretation
^wittgenstein-1953-philosophical-investigations-q001

*(Locator card; the text is in copyright and is paraphrased, not transcribed.)* The regress of interpretations: an interpretation by itself does not fix what it interprets, so a further interpretation can always be asked for (§198, with the question whether a rule can fix its own application, §§199–200). The paradox of §201: no course of action could be determined by a rule, because every course of action can be made out to accord with the rule. Its resolution in the same remark: there is a way of grasping a rule which is not an interpretation, but which is exhibited in what we call “obeying the rule” and “going against it” in actual cases. §202 adds that to think one is obeying a rule is not to obey it, so that obeying is a practice.

- **Locator:** §§198–202, with §201 as the centre; the wording of §201 was cross-checked against its quotation in the secondary literature (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Ludwig Wittgenstein,” rule-following section, which cites §§185–243 and presents §201 as the climax; and a published discussion quoting the grasping-a-rule clause). Not transcribed from a copy of the 4th edition.
- **Status:** locator-verified; no excerpt (in copyright).

- **Argument function:** knowing how to go on is not secured by adding interpretations; there is a grasp of the rule exhibited in following it.
