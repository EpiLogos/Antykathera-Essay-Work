## Passage card — `faggin-2022-beshara-interview-q001` — the Lake Tahoe experience

**Paraphrase:** Faggin says that during a vacation at Lake Tahoe, where the family had a home and spent the Christmas holidays skiing, he woke in the night and experienced himself as love, a powerful, scintillating white energy that was self-knowing and came from him; the energy exploded and he saw that everything is made of that same love; he experienced himself as the world made of that energy observing itself, and was both the observer and the observed; it was a very short experience that changed his life and set him on a path to understand consciousness.
