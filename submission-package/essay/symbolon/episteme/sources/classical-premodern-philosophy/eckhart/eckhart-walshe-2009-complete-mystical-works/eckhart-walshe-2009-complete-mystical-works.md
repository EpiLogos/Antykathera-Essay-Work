# Eckhart — German sermon Pr. 52, “Beati pauperes spiritu” (Walshe translation)

## Bibliographic identity

*The Complete Mystical Works of Meister Eckhart*, translated and edited by Maurice O'C. Walshe, revised with a foreword by Bernard McGinn (New York: Crossroad Publishing Company, 2009), a single-volume revision of Walshe's earlier three-volume translations (Eckhart Society announcement, 2009; publication date 1 November 2009, read 2026-10-04, g5a). The German sermon cited is “Beati pauperes spiritu” (Matt. 5:3), number **52 in Josef Quint's critical edition** of the German works (*Meister Eckhart: Die deutschen und lateinischen Werke: Die deutschen Werke*, vol. 2, Stuttgart: Kohlhammer) — the sermon known as the poverty sermon (*Armutspredigt*); several scholarly pages surfaced by search confirm “Quint Nr. 52” (2026-10-04). Walshe's translation numbers his sermons by Quint's order, so the sermon appears there as Sermon 52; this was supported by catalogue-level search results but **not checked against the translation's table of contents**.

The debts file's remark that “Quint Pr. 52 is not Walshe Sermon 56” records an earlier working note that mixed two different sermons; the corrected note cites only Pr. 52 and states the numbering once.

The standard locus in Pr. 52 is the prayer that God make the speaker free of God (German “Darum bitte ich Gott, daß er mich Gottes quitt mache”), with the distinction between God as origin of creatures and what lies above that (the *Gottheit*); this wording was read only as quoted in secondary pages, not in a text under copyright terms that allow transcription, and is not admitted as a quotation.

## Essay uses

M03, marker `s01-nirguna`: “Meister Eckhart approaches the divine depth through his distinction between God, active in creation and relation, and the Godhead, *Gottheit*, where the distinctions through which God becomes nameable return to unity.” The note points to Pr. 52 as a locus for the distinction; the essay's gloss is the author's reading, with Flasch (`flasch-2015-meister-eckhart`) as the secondary witness.
