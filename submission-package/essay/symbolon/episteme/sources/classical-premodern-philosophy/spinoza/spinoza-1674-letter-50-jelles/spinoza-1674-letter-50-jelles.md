## Essay use

Letter 50 is the Western geometric articulation of negational determination at §0/1: finite figure becomes determinate through negation. It enters beside Dignāga's *apoha*, which articulates the semantic operation of exclusion. The two sources stand on equal footing while doing different work: Spinoza opens the finite, geometrical form of the cut; apoha opens its conceptual and linguistic form.

The relation of determination to provenance, alternatives, revisability, `0/1`, and the QL process is the essay's own development. Spinoza returns later through the *Ethics*, where geometric epistemology and metaphysics can be developed on their proper terrain.
