### q001 — Multiple senses ordered to a primary reference

**Paraphrase:** Healthy uses refer differently to health through preservation, production, indication or receptivity; medical uses likewise relate to an art. Aristotle coordinates different senses of being through a primary reference and identifies substance as the primary object of the inquiry. The differentiated relations belong to one investigation without having an identical definition or function.

**Locator:** IV.2, opening argument; project locator 1003a33–1003b19.

**Verification:** selected W. D. Ross online translation read; paraphrase-ready, no print or Greek collation and no exact quotation claim.

### q002 — Every capacity has its incapacity (Θ.1)

> "And 'impotence' and 'impotent' stand for the privation which is contrary to potency of this sort, so that every potency belongs to the same subject and refers to the same process as a corresponding impotence."

**Locator:** IX (Θ).1, Ross's Part 1; Bekker 1046a29–32 (the Greek sentence beginning καὶ ἡ ἀδυναμία καὶ τὸ ἀδύνατον is printed at 1046a30–31 in the Perseus Greek text, with ὥστε … ἀδυναμίᾳ completing it).

**Status:** quotation-ready for the Ross online wording above; Bekker locator checked against the Perseus Greek text (Ross, Oxford 1924, via Perseus 1999.01.0051), 2026-10-05.

**Verification:** Ross translation read at https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/metaphysics.9.ix.html; Greek line placement read at https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0051%3Abook%3D9%3Asection%3D1046a. Verifier: agent, 2026-10-05.

**Source relation:** Paraphrased in the essay (no quotation printed). Aristotle states the conceptual relation; its application to capability systems is the essay's own.

**Consumer:** §5 · #2 (reworked draft, 2026-10-05).

<a id="aristotle-metaphysics-ross-q003"></a>
### q003 — Rational capacities are for contraries; something else decides (Θ.5) — qualifying pair

> "There must, then, be something else that decides; I mean by this, desire or will."

**Locator:** IX (Θ).5, Ross's Part 5; Bekker 1048a8–11 (ἀνάγκη ἄρα ἕτερόν τι εἶναι τὸ κύριον· λέγω δὲ τοῦτο ὄρεξιν ἢ προαίρεσιν, 1048a10–11 in the Perseus Greek). The preceding sentence holds that non-rational potencies produce one effect each while rational ones produce contrary effects.

**Status:** quotation-ready for the Ross online wording above; Bekker placement checked against the Perseus Greek text, 2026-10-05.

**Verification:** as q002. Verifier: agent, 2026-10-05.

**Source relation:** Paraphrased. This qualifies q002: a capacity for contraries is not yet an act; the decision belongs to desire or choice, not to the capacity. The essay's use (potency includes the power not to enact; selection is a further act) is argued from it.

**Consumer:** §5 · #2 (reworked draft, 2026-10-05).

## Passage card — `aristotle-metaphysics-ross-q004` — non-contradiction and excluded middle
^aristotle-metaphysics-ross-q004

> “It is, that the same attribute cannot at the same time belong and not belong to the same subject and in the same respect; we must presuppose, to guard against dialectical objections, any further qualifications which might be added. … For it is impossible for any one to believe the same thing to be and not to be, as some think Heraclitus says.” … “But on the other hand there cannot be an intermediate between contradictories, but of one subject we must either affirm or deny any one predicate.”

- **Locator:** *Metaphysics* IV.3, 1005b19–25; IV.7, 1011b23–24 (Bekker numbers per the standard edition; the Internet Classics Archive text carries part numbers only).
- **Status:** quotation-ready (wording); Bekker line numbers from the standard reference.
- **Verification:** agent, 2026-10-05; transcribed from W. D. Ross’s translation at the Internet Classics Archive, https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/metaphysics.4.iv.html (book IV, parts 3 and 7).
- **Source relation:** extracted.
- **Evidential action:** supports (the two governing principles of classical two-valued logic, with Aristotle’s own qualifications “at the same time” and “in the same respect”).
- **Consumers:** §2 · #1 (reworked draft, 2026-10-05).
- **Use boundary:** reading truth and falsity as a polar axis through an undecided zero is the author’s.
