## Source scholarship

The jewel-net passage (513a28–513c10) follows a discussion of how knowledge accords with mutually entering dharmas (513a20–27). It selects one jewel in the southwest, follows its recursive reflections, answers a sequence of objections about how one can enter all while remaining in one and why many jewels are needed if one contains all, gives the ink-dot test, and then qualifies its own simile: the jewels only take in each other's images while their substances remain distinct, whereas the dharmas interpenetrate in their whole substance. Closing verses on Vairocana follow (513c11–16).

## Passage card — `huayan-wujiao-zhiguan-cbeta-t1867-q001` — entering all without leaving the one

> 既於一珠中入一切珠，而竟不出此一珠；於一切珠入一珠，而竟不起此一珠。

> 只由不出此珠，是故得入一切珠。若出此一珠入一切珠者，即不得入一切珠也。何以故？離此珠內無別珠故。

- **Locator:** T45, no. 1867, 513b09–b15.
- **Status:** quotation-ready (Chinese text); the English rendering used in the essay is the essay's own and is marked as such.
- **Verification:** transcribed from the CBETA XML (line-break markers 0513b09–0513b15), agent, 2026-10-05; apparatus readings not collated against a Taishō page image.
- **Rendering (essay's own):** "Having entered all the jewels within one jewel, one never goes out of this one jewel; entering the one jewel within all the jewels, one never rises from this one jewel." "Only because one does not go out of this jewel can one enter all the jewels. If one went out of this one jewel to enter all the jewels, one could not enter all the jewels. Why? Because apart from this jewel there is no other jewel."

## Passage card — `huayan-wujiao-zhiguan-cbeta-t1867-q004` — the simile qualified (limiting passage)

> 法不如然，喻同非喻，一分相似故以為言。何者？此珠但得影相攝入，其質各殊。法不如然，全體交徹故。

- **Locator:** T45, no. 1867, 513c04–c06.
- **Status:** quotation-ready (Chinese text); English rendering the essay's own.
- **Verification:** CBETA XML, agent, 2026-10-05.
- **Rendering (essay's own):** "The dharmas are not like this: the simile is the same as no simile, and it is spoken only for a partial resemblance. Why? These jewels only take one another's images in, while their substances each remain distinct. The dharmas are not like this, because their whole substance interpenetrates."
