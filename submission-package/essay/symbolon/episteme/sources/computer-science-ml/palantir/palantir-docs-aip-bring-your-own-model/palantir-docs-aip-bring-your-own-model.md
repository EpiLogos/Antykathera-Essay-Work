### palantir-docs-aip-bring-your-own-model-q001 — Registered models: customers connect their own LLMs

**Locator:** Opening paragraph under "Bring your own model to AIP".

**Exact text:** "Bring-your-own-model (BYOM), also known as registered models in the Palantir platform, is a capability that provides first-class support for customers who want to connect their own LLMs or accounts to AIP across Palantir developer products."

**Context and use boundary:** Under "When to use" the page says Palantir "generally recommend[s]" its own provided models (from OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, xAI, GCP Vertex, or self-hosted open-source models such as Llama) and recommends registered models "only when you cannot use Palantir-provided models for legal and compliance reasons, or when you have your own fine-tuned or otherwise unique LLM".

### palantir-docs-aip-bring-your-own-model-q002 — Externally hosted models; administrators register them

**Locator:** Headings "Model sources" and "Permissions and enablement".

**Exact text:** "REST API source: Connect an externally hosted model provider or account through a REST API source in Data Connection."

"Registering models: Only Enrollment administrators can register new models or edit registrations in Control Panel."

**Context and use boundary:** The alternative source is a "Compute module", a model server the customer hosts "on your own hardware and GPUs, on-premise or air-gapped". Once registered, "access is governed solely by the registered model enablement configured in Control Panel."
