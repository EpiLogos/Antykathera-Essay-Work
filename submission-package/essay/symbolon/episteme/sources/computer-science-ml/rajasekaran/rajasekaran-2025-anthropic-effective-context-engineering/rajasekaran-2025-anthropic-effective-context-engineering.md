### rajasekaran-2025-anthropic-effective-context-engineering-p001 — Definition of context engineering

**Provenance:** as recorded for this source: edition status: selected; URL https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents; accessed 2026-10-04.

**Locator:** Opening section (web page; no pagination).

**Located operation:** The article defines context engineering as the strategies for curating and maintaining the optimal set of tokens during LLM inference, including information that lands there outside the prompts, and as the art and science of curating what goes into the limited context window. Paraphrase: deciding which information reaches the model's limited context at each step, beyond the wording of the prompt.
