## #1

The closing social image rejects the monolithic intelligence explosion because intelligence in the developed field is already relational, distributed and historically entangled. A step-change in agency therefore appears through coupling among many systems, institutions, people, artifacts and inherited worlds rather than through one isolated mind simply scaling until it becomes the whole.

The integral society is not an aggregate super-mind. It is a field in which demographic, cognitive, institutional, technical and first-person registers can remain related without one register appropriating the others. [Symbolon Disclosure Architecture](../concepts/C61-Symbolon-Disclosure-Architecture.md) keeps their warrants distinct; [Pros Hen / In Quantum](../concepts/C63-Pros-Hen-In-Quantum-Focal-Return-Qualified-Predication.md) keeps predicates attached to their respect; [The Two Ones](../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/Epi%20Paper%20Write-ups/Mono-Poly%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Two%20Ones%20and%20the%20Whole%20Field.md) prevents any counted one from claiming the unity of the whole.

## #2

In the frame this field runs on, the return has a face: the film recognized as film while the accounting stays precise — the light never confused with what it shows, and never absent from it. The exactness is retained at every joint, not surrendered at the close: the ratios still compute with their hypotheses held, the constituted measure still measures within published limits, the registration is still camera-work, exact and inspectable, and the loop is still audited as film while it runs. The usurpations stay refused in the same motion, through its own determining relation — no measure becomes ontology, no world-model becomes the world, no institution becomes the planetary Subject, no aggregate counts itself as the field's unity — and each distinction retains the warrant of the operation which earned it. ## #4

What does the whole apparatus serve? The ethic gives the criterion: work clear in intention, right in form, and capable of giving back to life more room to know, act, create and be.

Room is made by return. A determination that gives back what it draws releases its sources from the fixed role of input and allows the world to become again a source of further determination. Answerable definition enlarges room to know. Maintained identification gives room to be without freezing identity. Authored session gives room to act within entrusted purpose. A nourished commons gives room to create without consuming the conditions of creation.

These are not four boxes to assign to the field. They are four consequences of one return: the developed apparatus serves Life only where its power increases the living world's capacity for further meaningful determination.

The [Advent of Zero manuscript](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-advent-zero-subject/SOURCE.md) gives the return its historical body: the integral mutation is a higher reorganisation, not an accumulation of perspectives. The integral zero is zero in its office: the uncounted condition present in the count without becoming one of the counted — what the apparatus turns toward and meets as the incomputable, not as an object called "the ground." Zero as One / One as All retains that relation: neither a generic ground-label thinned of texture nor a total inventory of isolated units.
