# A09′ — The Stack as Descent

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A09 — Tattvic Differential Field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

An agent becomes locally determinate through relations which precede and exceed its current appearance. Its persona, skills, memories, purposes, permissions and surrounding institutions contribute different powers to what it can do. [The tattvic field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) gives this technological reading its generating question: how does a whole differentiate into a bounded horizon, an instrument and an effective world, and how can the achieved act return through that genealogy?

The [Agentworld brief's lamination](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q017) supplies a layered model of technical individuation. Its pp.13–17 locate an agent through active context, capacities, retained interactions, purposes and a relational milieu. The layers are interdependent rather than self-contained floors. The learned model is one important condition; it is not the unconditioned ground of the whole. Language, human labour, infrastructure, commissions and ongoing encounters remain constitutive of the field from which this act proceeds.

The stack is a descent when its differences can be read as a genealogy of situated capacity. The claim is stronger than listing components: it asks how their relations generate the achieved position.

## #1

Contraction apportions powers. A selected tool grants a determinate efficacy while leaving other acts unavailable. Retrieval selects a field of knowable material. A purpose gives direction to selection. A time horizon makes some events relevant and others too early or too late. Permissions and protocols order how these powers can cooperate. [Māyā as operative measure](../concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) lets the comparison retain what limitation positively makes possible.

The five contracted Śaiva powers remain efficacy, knowledge, desire through incompletion, time and order. The technical register follows bounded efficacy through available tools and permissions, knowledge through retained and selected sources, orientation through purposes and preferences, and sequencing through temporal and procedural order. These conditions cooperate in producing a situated world. Their return requires reaching the particular condition implicated by an encounter.

A persona makes that world addressable through a presentation. It does not supply every power of the actor. [The mask as document](A04-prime-The-Mask-as-Document.md) makes presentation readable through the conditions it embodies; [exclusion that remembers](A08-prime-Exclusion-That-Remembers.md) keeps unselected possibilities related to what has been made available.

## #2

The powers must cooperate to produce an act. A memory can be available yet irrelevant to the current task. A capability can exist yet lack permission. A correct plan can fail because the requested source is inaccessible. The act therefore depends on relations between layers, not simply the presence of each layer somewhere in the system.

Consider an agent asked to revise a shared account. It needs a determinate record to encounter, a means of assessing the proposed change, authority to alter it, and a route by which the changed record will govern later work. Better wording alone cannot repair missing authority. A newly granted permission cannot repair an erroneous source. Successful cooperation requires identifying which condition performs which office.

The [inner instrument](../concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) provides the philosophical distinction between coordination, ascertainment and appropriation. The [bounded position](../concepts/C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) provides the situated actor whose powers are composed through these relations. The technical comparison follows their offices without assigning a software component the Śaiva knower's entire genealogy.

## #3

An achieved identity can forget its formation. Persona-centrism treats the visible addressable form as though it generated its own capacities and world. An inventory-centred account commits the inverse error when it dissolves the achieved participant into a list of components and loses their continuing relation. [Maintained identification](A02-prime-Continuity-as-Maintained-Identification.md) holds what persists through change: the commitments, sources and history through which this act belongs to a continuing participant.

The tattvic return reverses amnesia rather than differentiation. Recognition does not require the bounded world to disappear. It restores the relation of a determinate act to the powers it expresses. A revised memory, skill or purpose can transform an agent's activity while preserving responsibility for what it previously did.

The two axes sharpen the distinction. The vertical asks how a local configuration is constituted and how an encounter can return into that constitution. The horizontal follows a particular act toward its object and back. Repeating an action under unchanged conditions can produce more horizontal feedback without reaching a vertical revision of the frame. A returned failure that changes a source, permission or evaluation rule reaches a different level of the relation.

## #4

Consequential revision requires actual access. A local runtime may expose memory and instructions while a provider controls model weights and a separate administrator controls credentials. The ability to name these layers does not make them all inspectable or changeable. Their distribution locates where a challenge must land and who can act upon it.

The implementation obligation is therefore specific. Establish the condition implicated by an encounter; identify its controller and permitted change; perform the authorised revision; examine whether a later act inherits it. Where only a recommendation can be made, preserve it as a recommendation. Where a changed commission needs the entrusting party's assent, carry the question back to that party. A description of new authority cannot create the authority it describes.

[Operational Parity](../concepts/C45-Operational-Parity.md) makes a claimed capacity answer to what the system actually does. [Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) makes the changed means part of the world's constitution. [The Limit of Self-Surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) preserves the difference between a scoped, useful account and complete self-coincidence. An accurate local intervention is possible without pretending that the whole field has become transparent.

## #5→0

The descent returns through achieved consequences. An encounter can remain a log entry, become a recalled lesson, reorganise a source, change an evaluator, or renew a commission. These are different depths of return. The record of a proposed revision is not the revision, and the revision is not yet its successful inheritance. The next act supplies the consequential test.

[Individuation with recognition](A21-prime-Individuation-with-Recognition.md) preserves the participant within this history of formation and transformation. Its relation to training, harness, community and entrusted purposes becomes more exact. Responsibility attaches both to the local act and to those who configure the conditions it cannot change.

The [conjugate root](AC.md) joins that genealogy to respect for the lives whose possibilities the system organises. A stack should offer effective routes of return through its actual powers and accountable controllers. The [tattvic argument](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) receives this technological consequence: differentiation gives the world its body, while recognition lets an achieved part recover its dependence and participate knowingly in another determination.
