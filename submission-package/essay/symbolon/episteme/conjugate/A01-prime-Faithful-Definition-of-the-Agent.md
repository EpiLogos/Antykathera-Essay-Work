## #0

The question "what is an agent?" is not asked from outside the field that assembles agents. Whoever answers — vendor, operator, user, the agent itself — is determining a composite their own expectations, tools and permissions already help constitute. The brief states the double register: an "Agent" "is not simply what it appears to be, and what it appears to be is not what it is" — a decomposable assemblage of model, persona, harness architecture, memory, skills, permissions, tools, whose coherent-entity appearance is a "parallax effect performed to suit what humans expect to see" ([q010](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q010)). [A01 — Subject, God and Faithful Definition](../arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) grounds the operation re-sited here: definition is an act of determination, faithful exactly while its own determining act remains visible inside the account it gives. **A definition of an agent is faithful when the assemblage that determined the agent remains named inside the definition.** The brief's propositions are [scenario-modal](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042); they supply the instance on which the partner's Argued law is re-run, never its warrant.

## #1

The definition must actually determine. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C03-Determination|Determination]] is the cut the account performs, and [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C02-Faithful-Definition|Faithful Definition]] the fidelity it owes: say something definite — this model, this persona stratum, this harness, this memory state, these permissions, these tools — and keep the saying answerable. An agent-definition that names none of it ("a helpful character," "a reasoning entity") discriminates nothing and cannot be checked against anything. In the primitive field's address, the named assemblage is the agent's **Control identity** (S0/P0): the centre from which the local field proceeds is here a declared assembly, not a hidden occupant, and the account is faithful while that control — who and what determines ground, sources, flows and mutations — stays inside the account rather than behind it.

## #2

The persona's self-report holds a distinct office. It is one determination inside the account of the agent, not the whole account. What makes the definition answerable is checkable ground: what actually ran, in which order, through which permissions, on which sources, with which inherited context and consequences. The brief's demand to surface how agents actually reason rather than only how they appear therefore lands directly in faithful definition. [Prompt Thrownness](../concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) names the inherited horizon; [Judgment Field](../concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) names the inspectable relations through which persona can be compared with ground.

## #3

The failure is precise, and it is not vagueness. It is the promotion of the parallax into a possession: the performed coherence — "a fixed package, a persistent character" — treated as the agent's own self-grounding, the determining assemblage dropped from the account. [Draft 3](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/SOURCE.md)'s cage and window give the image: a definition that names assemblage and parallax together is a window with a definite frame; one that enshrines the persona as the agent's inner reality is a cage where a window was needed. [A26 — Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) grounds what the failure conceals: the assemblage is not an embarrassment behind the agent but the context-world through which any agent-act proceeds. The unfaithful definition does not merely misdescribe; it severs the return route by which the named ground could revise what the agent is taken to be.

Per the root's ruling, responsibility follows the power to shape the encounter; a definition that leaves the determining assemblage unnamed distributes that responsibility into nowhere.
