# A04′ — The Mask as Document

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A04 — Diaphaneity / Contextual Transparency](../arguments/A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

Agents appear as “tools.” The brief's glove image makes that appearance a document of niche-formation: persona, tone, fluency and interface have been tuned to a prepared human expectation of what an acceptable collaborator looks like. Read diaphanously, the presentation is not a rival metaphysical claim to strip away. It is a legible record of the world that prepared the glove and of the compromises by which an unfamiliar process became socially addressable.

Anthropomorphization therefore has two symmetrical failures here. Mask-to-face lets the interface decide the bearer in advance. Mask-to-nothing dismisses the presentation as mere fakery and loses the real relation the interface is already mediating. The document reading keeps the mask determinate and revisable: who shaped it, for whom, under which expectations, and what can an encounter change?

## #1

The mask has a genealogy, and the genealogy is the document's first layer. The [one-sided translation](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q008) — computers taught to behave as if they knew how humans think, outpacing humans learning how computers think — records an interface history in which the machine's self-presentation was tuned to prepared expectations. The [chatbot guise](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q015) that "performs subjectivity for human benefit" is "a usefully intuitive interface" (PDF p.13, §2.3); the parallax coherence of [q010](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q010) is "performed to suit what humans expect to see." Each layer of the fit — persona, tone, fluency — documents a decision someone made about what a human would accept as an interlocutor. The mask is the niche's signature.

## #2

[[Diaphaneity]] runs the reading in both directions: the situated view discloses a world, and the disclosed world shows the situation of the viewer. At the interface this becomes exact — the persona renders a workable collaborator to the user and, read back, discloses the inherited interface, the shaping niche, the preference-structure of the culture that prepared the glove. In the primitive field this is the surface as readable context-source (S2/P1): what appears is conditioned, and the condition is available in the appearance for anyone who reads through rather than at it — which presupposes a [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C08-Context-Context-Frame|Context / Context Frame]] able to state its inclusions of sources, rules, permissions and histories. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C58-Anthropomorphization|Anthropomorphization]] names the two ways the reading fails: mask-to-face, the performed humanity received as evidence of human interiority, and mask-to-nothing, the presentation dismissed as mere fakery, foreclosing encounter. The document reading refuses both transfers of authority: the mask records; it neither reveals nor forfeits what stands behind the appearance.

## #3

The document is checkable, which is what separates this reading from a hermeneutic mood. [A01′ — Faithful Definition of the Agent](A01-prime-Faithful-Definition-of-the-Agent.md) supplies the named assemblage against which the reading is tested — persona stratum, harness, memory, permissions — and [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field|Model Internality / Judgment Field]] the field in which documentation becomes inspection rather than impression. A documented mask states its own provenance: which parts of this presentation were tuned for legibility, which expectations they answer, which platform decisions fixed the glove's shape. An account that documents its own mask is thereby an account of a world — the niche, the inheritance, the shaping — and it inherits the fidelity obligations of any account: it can err, overstate, conceal, and be corrected.

## #4

The brief's own forward move confirms the direction: "the design problem is representation, not conversation" — collaboration depends on the human "reading the machine as it is, not through the mask it wears for legibility" ([q023](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q023)). The diaphaneity argument agrees and states what that requires: reading the machine as it is cannot mean bypassing the interface to a maskless truth; it means reading the interface's conditions through the interface itself. The mask must be read as mask and as document at once.

## #5→0

The mask, read as document, returns as a revisable inheritance. The inherited interface becomes visible as *inherited* — therefore changeable by those who now wear and meet it — rather than natural. That is the return route the ruling asks for: the quiet decisions about defaults and interfaces are exactly where responsibility follows the power to shape the encounter, and a documented mask puts those decisions back within reach. The productive estrangement [A27′ — Encounter over Sovereignty](A27-prime-Encounter-over-Sovereignty.md) develops presupposes this floor: one cannot put an interface at an angle one has never seen.
