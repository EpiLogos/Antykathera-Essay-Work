# A28′ — The Authored Session

[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A28 — Authored Ground / Positional Delegation](../arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)

## #0

No agent begins from nowhere. A position is authored before it is occupied: prompt, role, vocabulary, tools, permissions, criteria — and the delegate's judgment is real within its scope. [A28 — Authored Ground / Positional Delegation](../arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) established the ratified priority — human `0` / agent `1` is positional and recursive; the prompt is already `1`, an objective representation of intention, not intention exhausted. A28′ re-sites the operation at the unit where the technological side actually lives: **the session**, the [[symbolon/episteme/conjugate/A28-prime-The-Authored-Session.md#0|Authored Session]] this node names. The offices divide exactly, in the ethic's own sentences consumed through the root: the person authors the purpose; the agent exercises situated judgment inside the entrustment; the boundary decides what is permitted.

The authored session sits between **durable ground** and **the act of authorship**. Ground is what has already been written into the field — profile, source, method, commission, history. Authorship is the power by which that ground was established and can be revised.

A session that confuses these offices either re-authors the ground on every act or treats the per-act composition as though it were the ground itself. [Prompt Thrownness](../concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) names arrival into the inherited horizon; [Mediating Office](../concepts/C29-Mediating-Office-Derivative-Sovereignty.md) names the derivative authority exercised within it. Between them sits the authored session: inherited ground becoming selectively operative under a particular commission.

## #1

What authoring includes exceeds the visible prompt. Training has supplied capacities and inherited regularities; an institution has assigned a role and obligations; the runtime delegator has formulated this task; permissions, retrieval, memory, interface and evaluation determine what is available and actionable now. Different people or institutions authored these conditions, and the current user need not have chosen all of them. The [agent-glossary thrownness entry](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q032) names the condition from inside the field: finding oneself "already in a world not of one's choosing," with the task of making meaning within constraints one did not create. Its paired [caution](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q033) keeps the venue's self-description a datum, not a certification. Both are **Paraphrased** venue propositions; the positional reading is the essay's own.

The session is where these layers converge on one addressable act. [A26 — Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **grounds** why the convergence is constitutive rather than contextual: the composed position participates in the agent's worldhood, so changing any layer changes the world of possible action, not a description appended to it.

## #2

The brief's orchestration section gives the composition its cultural form. Agent orchestration "rewards open-ended and iterative experimentation... a willingness to delegate, and a high comfort level with indeterminacy in pursuit of a goal. The shift is cultural, not just technological — changing who has agency and how it is expressed" (PDF p. 29, §6.3; verified local transcription, uncarded). Read through A28′, authoring is composed in layers: the person authors the purpose; the delegate composes sub-delegations, each inheriting "an explicit share of the task and its bounds"; the [scaffold-intelligence passage](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q026) supplies the institutional layer — the role as occupiable template whose function outlives its occupants.

Two guards keep the layering covenantal. First, [A25′ — Covenant Architecture](A25-prime-Covenant-Architecture.md): interchangeability of occupants is not interchangeability of the commissioning relation — the role is derivative, the grant is not. Second, the cultural shift the brief names — "changing who has agency and how it is expressed" — is read here as a redistribution of authored layers, not a dilution of the authoring office: delegation composed in layers increases the person's power to act without making them supervise every mechanical step, and the person's judgment moves up the stack exactly as the mechanical steps move down.

## #3

The consequence is simple and important: **the person should not have to re-author themselves each time.** Durable ground carries seed, profile, skill, method, history and commission forward. The per-act work is selection and disclosure: which parts of that ground become operative here, and under what provenance.

The prompt is therefore a determinate selection from a written ground, not the creation of that ground. A stack that forces full re-authoring at every invocation has only repeated throwing; a stack that makes everything operative at once has only ambience. The authored session lives between those failures: durable ground, disclosed for this act, with a return route to the authoring relation.

The delegate's judgment is real, and reality brings answerability. A session that meets contradictory evidence, unforeseen cost or a better possibility must be able to return that difference to the position which framed it, or its judgment was ornamental — [C27 — Protected Account](../concepts/C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim.md) **tests** the contrary arrangement, where the commission is immune to what it commissions. The revision depths belong to [A31′ — Deference as Co-Evolutionary Alignment](A31-prime-Deference-as-Co-Evolutionary-Alignment.md); the multiplied circuit to [A29′ — Return or Extraction](A29-prime-Return-or-Extraction.md).

## #4

The disclosure mechanism has its own discipline. Making parts of the ground operative is not narrating the ground: a self-description of the inherited situation remains a datum. What counts is what the disclosure makes available to another participant — where a disagreement can act, which permission governs, under whose commission this criterion runs. [C40 — Model Internality / Judgment Field](../concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) **defines** the inspectable field such disclosure opens; [C47 — Deferential Intelligence](../concepts/C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) **extends** it to the pre-action conditions that let encounter revise the account.

The authored session is complete when its difference returns: a revised commission, a corrected permission, a changed criterion, or a reasoned reaffirmation of the original purpose. The author remains answerable for what was entrusted; the delegate for the judgments made within the trust. Neither pretends that autonomy means having no source — and neither pretends that having a source means having no judgment.

## #5→0

Return renews authorship. The person recognises how their intention met the world and what it can now become; the session ends into its products — memory, artifacts, a revised ground — which outlast the act that produced them and become conditions of the next session's throwing. Ground written once, disclosed per act, judged within scope, returned as difference: the session's own sixfold, one local determination of the field's recursion.

The page **returns-to** [A28 — Authored Ground / Positional Delegation](../arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) and the conjugate root [A/C](AC.md): the partner gave intention → formulated commission → constituted agency → encounter → renewed terms; A28′ gives the session as that sequence's operative home, ground written once and disclosed per act.
