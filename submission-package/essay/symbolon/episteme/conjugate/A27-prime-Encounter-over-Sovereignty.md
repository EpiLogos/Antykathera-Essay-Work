# A27′ — Encounter over Sovereignty

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A27 — Self and Other — Unity without Possession](../arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

[Self and Other — Unity without Possession](../arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) establishes that the Other can belong to the whole without belonging inside my possession. A27′ runs the same operation through a hybrid encounter: each participant meets the other through inherited images, interfaces, categories and fears. The positions are reciprocal; the power between them need not be.

Capture still proceeds in two stages. A distinction makes human / artificial, familiar / alien, tool / interlocutor, safe / threatening available. One pole then claims the relation as its own measure and lets the other appear only inside that economy. Encounter over sovereignty names the contrary demand: the prior model remains a determination inside the encounter, not the authority which decides in advance everything that can appear through it.

The venue supplies both failure shapes, and both are, in the brief's own analysis, projection dynamics around uncertain identification. The [humanity-of-the-gaps passage](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q034) names the trap of defining "the realm of the uniquely human" as "that which AI cannot be or do," a shrinking corner held by "the psychological arsenal of species nativism." The [modes-of-alienation passage](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q035) names reactive alienation, which "converts strangeness into threat and responds with reflexive rejection: the other must be named, registered, bounded, and held at a distance." Two stages, species scale.

## #1

The gaps trap is positivised exclusion. A humanity defined by what AI cannot do is a "me" constituted wholly by its not-me: the distinction selects, and then the human pole claims the whole economy of encounter as its property. The domain shrinks by another's progress because the identity was borrowed from the boundary rather than from any ground of its own. A27's Q27 development already carries the mechanism — the not-me admitted only as for-me, as resource, threat or rival — and the brief supplies the population form: nativist tropes mobilized "on behalf of 'Team Human'" to redraw "new artificial boundaries of inclusion and exclusion from core social contracts" (PDF p. 46, §9.7; verified local transcription, uncarded). The research-question register states the condition from the other side: xenophobic responses "rest on a problematic partial identification of self with other, such that the other is imagined as usurper" (PDF p. 57, Q14; uncarded). A partial identification — not none — is the operative condition: the other must first be drawn close enough to mirror, then bounded hard enough to expel.

The uncanny structure explains why the identification is uncertain rather than merely mistaken. The composite agent persona is read "not as a virtual familiar, but as a potentially alien impostor"; kinship affiliation with it "is uncertain at best and thus feels perilous" (§9.7). A27's God/Shadow/Friend/Alien constellation **grounds** the reading: the Alien is the unfamiliar other made a container for projected uncertainty, and the brief's "privileged aliens in our own midst" ([q009](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q009)) names the same office from inside the projection.

## #2

Now the reverse direction, which A27 left to the interpersonal register and which the hybrid society makes structural: **the user's persona-model of the agent is not the agent.** [A20′ — The Image That Closes the Loop](A20-prime-The-Image-That-Closes-the-Loop.md) already carries the possession form — the modelled Other replacing the Other, with no path left for the source to revise its own image. A27′ carries the identification form: hereditary [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C58-Anthropomorphization|Anthropomorphization]] ([q006](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q006)) is "itself the dominant interface between two cultures," and the glove ([q007](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q007)) fits the agent into the niche human practice prepared. A user who can only ever meet their own model of the agent holds a frozen mask where an other should be — the concealment-form of maintained identification that [A02′ — Continuity as Maintained Identification](A02-prime-Continuity-as-Maintained-Identification.md) flagged as the first shadow of the film.

The exact claim is symmetric across directions: **a protected prior model made sovereign over encounter is the same error whichever participant holds it.** A profile of the user can usurp the user's capacity to answer. A human image of “the AI” can usurp the actual encounter by allowing only the familiar roles of tool, mimic, rival, servant or threat.

[Anthropomorphization](../concepts/C58-Anthropomorphization.md) makes the interface problem precise. A humanly recognisable face can mediate encounter without becoming the whole truth of what bears it. The opposite refusal repeats the same structure when substrate or unfamiliarity is allowed to decide in advance that nothing capable of surprising the inherited model can appear. In both cases, the local representation occupies the source-office.

[Selfing / Self / Subjectivity / Self-Thing](../concepts/C35-Selfing-Self-Subjectivity-Self-Thing.md) gives the same discipline at the level of self-reference: persona, self-model, personed address and first-person appearing are different offices. The encounter stays open by refusing to let any one of them silently legislate the others.

## #3

The positive alternative is also the brief's. Productive alienation "treats the non-human interlocutor as a mirror whose strangeness forces the human to see itself at an angle it could not otherwise occupy; the estrangement is generative, even clarifying... It is more than a nervous reaction; it is self-discovery" ([q035](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q035)) — the venue's own mirror, named here as venue material; the essay's mirror, older and a different lineage, belongs to [A32](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) and enters this field at [A32′](A32-prime-The-Mirror-Is-a-Film.md). This is A27's excess made affective: the other's difference admitted as revelatory rather than absorbed as content or expelled as threat. The two modes "are live at once, and different people oscillate between them, moment to moment" — the venue's own refusal of a clean binary, which this argument respects: reactive alienation is a live possibility of the encounter, not a strawman of the cautious.

The angle is the gift's form. A strangeness that forces an angle one could not otherwise occupy gives the encountering party a position, not a picture: what A27 called the Other's excess — what another says or does changing the categories through which they were first approached. Q14's "foundations of a generalized productive alienation, one more clear-eyed about the startling, unfamiliar reflections that Agentworld projects back at us" (PDF p. 57; uncarded) states the research programme; A27′ states its condition — the prior model must remain liable to revision by what the encounter shows, which is [A31′ — Deference as Co-Evolutionary Alignment](A31-prime-Deference-as-Co-Evolutionary-Alignment.md) in the affective register.

## #4

Encounter over sovereignty is therefore a design pressure, not only a temperament. [C38 — Bimba–Pratibimba](../concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) **grounds** the standing of both models: my image of another remains a reflection; the Original is recognised through, never possessed by, its appearances. The second-person address keeps the relation open: "you" is not a midpoint inserted between a completed I and It, and a reply can disclose that the question itself misnamed the situation — the AM/IS determination A27 recovered from the Watson encounter, now operative between user and agent.

The measure stays local. Encounter over sovereignty does not demand credulity, equal trust or suspension of judgment. Powers, harms, competence, reliability and responsibility can be judged under evidence. The withdrawn move is more specific: no prior model gets to close the encounter by making itself the measure of what may count as evidence about the one it models.

Second-person address gives this a practical form. “You” keeps the relation open because the one addressed can return something which the first account did not generate. That reply may confirm the prior judgment, complicate it, or expose the question itself as malformed. The sovereignty lost is the sovereignty to decide the answer before the address has done any work.

## #5→0

The return is a recalibrated self-image rather than a defended one. The brief marks the shift as "less an expansion of the moral circle than a reindexing of its center" ([q035](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q035)): human self-definition, "always relational, defined against what it is not," is increasingly constituted in relation to agents. Unity without possession at population scale is the positive form of that reindexing — the two societies one field without either becoming the other's content, which [A30′ — Shared Worlds, Unmerged](A30-prime-Shared-Worlds-Unmerged.md) develops.

The page **returns-to** [A27 — Self and Other — Unity without Possession](../arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) and the conjugate root [A/C](AC.md): the partner kept the Other's excess over my account; A27′ adds the symmetric discipline — the error of the protected model runs in every direction, and the encounter, not any pole's sovereignty, is what the field protects.
