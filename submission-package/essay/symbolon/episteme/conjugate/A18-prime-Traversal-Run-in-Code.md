### What the named act costs and returns

A18’s whole Name series — Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son, Image — retains its coequal Power series — Play, Need, Sacrifice, Decision, Love, Work. The technological act has both faces: its articulated name and the capacities, expenditure and consequences through which that articulation becomes effective. A named result cannot substitute for the power that made and sustains it. The four offices developed here arise within this particular act; they leave the two complete series and the eight determinations intact.

Sacrifice becomes determinate expenditure. The act takes time, attention, energy and labour from a field which had other possible uses for them. A request answered at negligible visible cost can still depend upon work and infrastructure outside the session’s view. The act must be able to state what was committed and what remained unavailable to it. This is the [Apportionment / Economy whole field](../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD.md)’s **delegated labour distributes capacity and return** operation at evidence register 3. Particular costs require their actual records; the native requirement to retain expenditure does not wait upon inventing a measurement.

Decision gives that expenditure a course. A branch is selected, an operation is authorised, and an alternative remains unactualised. The outcome must retain the criterion under which this course was taken and the office which had authority to choose it. An output can be exact while still concealing this act of selection. Returning a disputed result therefore requires access to the criterion and commission, not only another run within the same selected course. [A28](../arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) keeps intention, prompt and execution distinct; [A24′](A24-prime-The-Usurping-Criterion.md) tests the criterion’s claim to become its own source.

Love retains the other upon whom this course takes effect. A representation of a person, community or encountered world can guide an act without exhausting the relation it represents. Their answer must be able to alter what is requested, measured or executed.

This is the productive Power office already named in A18, made technically consequential through [Self and Other](../arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) and [Deferential Intelligence](../arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md). Love at this register is performed by retained alterity: changed authority, changed action, changed commission or changed terms of encounter when the Other's reply requires it. A declaration of affinity which cannot alter the relation is sentiment without the office.

Work returns the achieved form to the common field. The return includes the usable result, its provenance, the costs borne elsewhere, the consequences encountered and a route for revising the next commission. [A29](../arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) distinguishes those flows. A product delivered while its costs and correction rights remain excluded has completed only part of this return. E6’s **commons preserves the wider economy** operation makes the distinction explicit: a local share may remain real without taking ownership of the generative whole.

The circuit is concrete enough to examine in an actual execution: what was committed, what course was chosen, whose answer could change it, and what came back with the result. These questions do not add four new joints to the native traversal. They develop its already declared Name/Power face within one technical process. No deployed implementation is certified by having written them.
