## #0 — An act inherits a relation

A request already joins a person, a concern and a world before an agent produces a result. The request becomes executable through language, a model, retrieved material, tools, permissions and a setting in which an answer will be used. Those conditions are active in the answer: changing a source, an available tool or the authority to act can change what is produced even when the sentence requested remains the same. [Code as the Age's Logos](A14-prime-Code-as-the-Ages-Logos.md) develops this composition through the acting ensemble; [the Agentworld account of provisional individuation](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q017) names its lamination of persona, skills, memory, drives and milieu.

The [eight determinations](../arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) make the whole relation traversable. Before its terms are assigned, the parent stroke holds the possibility of relation: `/ = −/−`. Conscious circumstance then becomes answerable definition; definition acquires force; force becomes a recognisable instance; the instance enters personed context; exact local determination discloses its wider horizon; the achieved form turns toward its condition. The signs compress that sequence:

$$
/=-/-\;\longrightarrow\;0/1\;\longrightarrow\;?/!\;\longrightarrow\;-/+
\;\longrightarrow\;X/x\;\longrightarrow\;AM/IS\;\longrightarrow\;\infty/dx
\;\longrightarrow\;1/0.
$$

The parent and return bracket six qualitative determinations: `(4+2)+2`. They do not add two positions to the sixfold page, and they do not turn the six into a checklist of software components. One component can participate in several operations; one operation can pass through several people and components. The technological question is what changes at each joint and how its change can become answerable in another act.

## #1 — Circumstance becomes a claim

Consider a constructed inquiry: a person asks an agent for a walking route to a place. Their destination, present circumstances and reason for going precede the route. The agent receives an articulated request and a particular field of available information. A result appears within that relation. The person for whom it appears, the means of finding and presenting it, and the place to which it refers have distinguishable offices.

This is the conscious circumstance named by `0/1`: appearing belongs within awareness; a determinate mark can be addressed without becoming the entire ground of its appearing. At technological grain, the means disclose a route through a finite context. The lived knower is not made into a component of the map. The request also carries conditions the local run did not originate: the person's purpose, the map's making, the world it maps and the authority under which its information was acquired. [The venue's nucleation-seed account](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q016) gives the session its particular instance within such inherited capacity.

The route becomes an assertion: this path reaches the destination. Its question remains active: according to which map, at what time, under which access conditions, and for this person? An assertion makes enough of the field determinate to be used; a returned question can expose a condition that its first wording concealed. `?/!` names this mutual answerability. Asking the question already acknowledges a provisional mark, and giving the answer retains the question of whether it runs true.

A trace can identify the source consulted, the query made, the candidate routes and the selected path. [The limit of self-surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) carries the further distinction: a retained trace and the account produced from it are separately examinable. Their availability lets a finding be checked; the sentence which says the route was checked is itself one more claim to check.

<a id="2"></a>
## #2 — A claim takes force and becomes a pattern

Selection makes the route consequential. The run allocates time, retrieval and attention; it commits to one path while leaving others unactualised. A permission may let the agent suggest a route while withholding permission to book transport or change a shared itinerary. Reserve and issue, contraction and extension, are co-present powers of the act. What is withheld helps determine what can be released. The sign `−/+` names this pulse before its local quantities are mistaken for the whole movement.

An explicit budget or gate can be inspected where it exists. Other conditions may belong to a provider or institution and become known through their effects. An answerable act retains the difference between a condition it can inspect, a condition it can change and a condition whose change requires another's authority. Declaring every joint inspectable would conceal precisely the dependencies which inquiry needs to recover.

A particular route also manifests a recurring capacity. Mapping, searching, comparing and communicating can produce different routes in different settings. The particular result makes those capacities legible, while their further possibilities exceed this result. That relation earns `X/x`: determining capacity and indefinite particular, one letter differently articulated. Its recurrence is productive when the next instance can inherit a changed condition; it freezes when each repetition has to preserve the first instance as its governing model. [Purpose that survives the metric](A07-prime-Purpose-That-Survives-the-Metric.md) follows this difference between an entrusted course and conditioned will fixed as necessity.

The Agentworld harness/ensemble passage places capability in relations among model, tools, memory, shared knowledge and institutional interfaces ([source, §10.2](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q038)). That is a particular technical object for investigating recurrence. The capacity is tested through the distinctions the acting ensemble can actually carry into its next instance, rather than being located in an isolated output or assumed from a component's name.

## #3 — The addressed person changes the horizon

The person reaches a locked gate. The suggested route remains a statement about the place; their encounter gives it a consequence which the statement had omitted. Their reply can now change the relation: this route is unavailable to me here, now. The first-person circumstance, second-person address and third-person account operate together. `AM/IS` compresses their personed copula. AM names lived presence; IS names what can be said of a thing; the slash retains the address through which a reply can change the account.

The persona which presents the answer belongs among the means of this encounter. It can make an agent recognisable and addressable, while a person's reported experience and the third-person description retain their different offices. Assigning AM to a persona layer would put an interface in the place of the first-person relation it mediates. [Encounter over sovereignty](A27-prime-Encounter-over-Sovereignty.md) develops the positive demand: another's answer can revise the model through which they were first approached.

The gate report is locally exact while its horizon remains wider. It establishes a closure at a time and place; it need not establish every access rule, all future closures or the entire origin of the map. Exactness in the local difference and unbounded determining depth meet in `∞/dx`. The calculus supplies a precise comparison: a derivative fixes local change while integration restores a family `F(x)+C`; choosing a particular member requires a further condition. A reported gate is not thereby a derivative. The shared operation is that a determination can be exact while leaving a condition of its provenance to be supplied through another relation.

The achieved result now turns toward what made it possible. The map may be stale, the permission may be different from the user's assumption, or the commission may need to change. The mark cannot acquire its whole ground by making that ground another mark. The return `1/0` names this pressure: in arithmetic the quotient has no ordinary field value; in the knowing relation, an achieved account becomes answerable to a condition it does not contain. Recognition makes that relation available for revision. [Traversal with return](A17-prime-Traversal-with-Return.md) follows the difference carried by the circuit.

## #4 — What the named act costs and returns

<a id="a18p-name-power"></a>
An act becomes nameable through a whole descent from what precedes a saying to its achieved reflection. Truth is the field presupposed by the request: there is a destination, a person and a world before a route is said. Mind first receives that circumstance as something to understand. Word differentiates it into a request whose terms can be answered: this destination, this departure, this person's conditions. Logos gathers those terms proportionately, relating a possible path to time, access and purpose. Son brings the articulated relation into a someone's life: the route becomes this person's journey, where its claimed adequacy can be lived or resisted. Image is the achieved reflection through which that journey can be addressed again—the route and its returned account, including the gate which changed it. Each determination receives what precedes it and changes what the next can receive. An image earns its completion through that descent; a polished route display alone cannot perform the personal embodiment or gathering it presents.

The other face begins from the freedom to undertake a course and follows what actualising it demands. Play is the availability of a beginning: the person can go, stay, choose another destination or find an unexpected way. The possibility exceeds the route subsequently fixed. Need gives that openness its particular lack and demand: the person is here and seeks to arrive there, with conditions the journey must receive. At technological grain an entrusted request carries this demand into retrieval and planning; a system's objective names one means of receiving it. The lived lack and freedom remain those of the person whose course the means serves. Need directs the next expenditure without exhausting the possibilities from which it arose. Sacrifice, Decision, Love and Work develop what taking up that demand costs, commits, holds and achieves.

These relations earn the whole series: Truth/Mind/Word/Logos/Son/Image and Play/Need/Sacrifice/Decision/Love/Work. Binary Explication distinguishes the emanative Name face seen from outside and the reversionary Power face felt from inside. Their convergence keeps each articulated determination together with its effective demand. In the route inquiry the spoken request and its reception are one relation, gathered proportion and decision another, personal embodiment and concern another, achieved reflection and returned work another. Software participates in the means carrying these relations; allocating the twelve names to twelve components would discard their co-presence.

Sacrifice becomes determinate expenditure: time, attention, energy and labour become available to this course at the expense of other uses. Some expenditure occurs outside the session, in making and maintaining the map and its infrastructure. Its actual magnitude needs an actual record. [Apportionment and economy](../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD.md) retains delegated labour's capacity and return so that what costs little to the user does not thereby appear costless to the field.

Decision gives expenditure its course. The selected route inherits a criterion—shortest distance, accessible passage, least cost—and an authority to use that criterion. A locked gate can require new information under an adequate criterion. It can also disclose an inadequate commission: shortest distance failed to receive the person's mobility or the purpose of their journey. [Authored ground and positional delegation](../arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation.md) keeps intention, request and execution distinguishable. [The usurping criterion](A24-prime-The-Usurping-Criterion.md) names the failure when a local measure claims authority over the purpose it was meant to serve.

Love retains the other affected by the course. Their reply can revise a permission, a proposed action or the commission; it can also refuse the proposed remedy. The route user's experience matters in forming another route, while the landholder's authority and other people's access retain their own places in the inquiry. [Deferential Intelligence](../arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) develops this model-revising encounter. Affinity which leaves another's answer without consequence does not perform the office.

Work returns the achieved form to the common field: a usable revised route, the dated gate report, its source, the costs and decisions that shaped the remedy, and a route by which a subsequent encounter can revise it. [Entrusted power and return](../arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return.md) distinguishes delivery of a product from the wider return of capacity and correction rights. A local result remains real while the common field retains what other participants need to answer for its use.

The whole recognitive grammar also keeps its distinct anatomy. In the crossed-zero series, an inner instrument first occupies the apparent subject; a differentiated object-world stands opposite it; mediation becomes visible; subject-side and object-side recognition become available together; unity is affirmed with the differentiations retained:

$$0\rightarrow Ø\rightarrow X\rightarrow Ø/X\rightarrow(0/Ø)/(1/X)\rightarrow1\;\circlearrowleft\;0/1.$$

A technical account contributes inspectable means within that relation. The sequence does not diagnose a persona as a subject merely because it presents a coherent face. Its technological consequence is to keep the means visible in the relation they organise: map, report, permission, model and criterion can be addressed at their actual loci. [The disclosure architecture](../concepts/C61-Symbolon-Disclosure-Architecture.md) receives that consequence.

## #5→0 — A returned challenge enters another act

The gate report changes the next operation only when its consequence reaches the relevant locus. Updating the map can correct stale information. Changing a routing rule can stop a passage being recommended under an inappropriate condition. Clarifying a commission can make a different destination or mode of travel appropriate. Each requires its own authority; none can be replaced by displaying a corrected sentence. The result and the changed conditions must remain related through what is subsequently done.

The eight joints remain active together. Being joins conscious circumstance with answerable definition. Becoming joins force with recurrence. Knowing/unKnowing joins personed context with the exact local difference whose horizon exceeds it. Across them, Essence folds ground with realisation, Constitution folds definition with context, and Text–Texture folds force with pattern. These relations make a whole act available in more than one direction. They do not ask every future run to repeat a fixed workflow.

Primordial possibility and consequent reception therefore retain different offices. Whitehead's [process account](../sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md) places actuality's achievement and its reception into further becoming within his own metaphysics. Here the technological return is examined through the actual inherited and changed conditions of another act. A shared record can carry the correction to other users and agents; whether it does so is an execution finding which the constructed inquiry has not supplied.

The return reaches [Integral Zero in the hybrid society](A36-prime-Integral-Zero-of-the-Hybrid-Society.md) when the developed apparatus can receive its ground again with what the encounter changed. It returns through [the Primordial Symbolon](../arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) and [the shared ethical field](AC.md): the determination remains joined to its making, another's reply can alter the relation, and the acquired difference becomes a condition of another beginning.
