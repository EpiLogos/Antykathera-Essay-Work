# A23′ — Trust under Unpossessable Interiors

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A23 — Trust, Faith and the Formal Limit](../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)

## #0

Reliance begins with an act entrusted under conditions: a person asks an agent to investigate, permits a bounded use of its result and remains exposed to what follows. [Trust under formal limit](../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) makes that finite commitment answerable. The person can investigate the sources, competence and offices supporting it without first owning the entire relation through which another act becomes possible. Trust continues through that difference as a situated undertaking, with real stakes and a possible withdrawal.

Missing evidence has its own remedy. A run may be reproducible, a source recoverable, a permission inspectable or an error corrected. The brief's observation that surfaced chain-of-thought "is not actually a transcript of the neural activations" ([the self-report passage](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q015) context, PDF p.14) concerns what that particular account records. It gives no theorem that a user can never reproduce an inference. [The Limit of Self-Surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) preserves the stronger operation: making an earlier act available as content does not include the present determining act by which that content is now read and judged. Inspecting that act supplies a further content within another act. This recurrence can coexist with accurate and useful local disclosure.

Trust's technical body therefore starts by repairing the omissions which can be repaired and locating the authority which can answer for what remains. Its philosophical relation concerns the continuing difference between an achieved account and the act which receives it. The two operations meet in reliance without becoming the same failure.

The brief's own definition is honest about half the relation: "Trust here is not a moral category but a prediction-stability category: an agent is trusted to the degree its behavior remains well-modeled across the adversarial and cooperative transitions that punctuate any long interaction" ([the prediction-stability account](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q030)). The scoped "here" marks a systems register. Prediction-stability can support a scoped judgment of reliability. An unfaithful substitution occurs if that result is made to discharge every question of purpose, consequence and undertaking, as though the measured relation supplied its own complete warrant. The brief supplies its own internal correction: "An agent that predicts only the action of another, not the intent from which it proceeds, has modeled the shadow rather than the body" (the prediction-stability discussion, §8.4) — prediction without the from-which is precisely the interior the predictor does not hold.

## #1

[Trust’s provisional anchors and protected certainties](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/SOURCE.md) take different courses. Knowledge can offer provisional anchors that help a life bear uncertainty — provenance, calibration, declared scope, a benchmark's known limits — or it can deny its provisionality and displace the distrust it cannot hold onto a scapegoated object or false ground: the opaque system blamed as sorcerer, the user blamed as incompetent, the model blamed as inscrutable, each Certainty protected by its scapegoat. The second operation is not explained by identifying a mistaken proposition; a protected certainty has acquired the office of relieving a lived crisis — the [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim|Protected Account]] names the insulation — and contrary evidence can intensify its defence. In the agentworld the fork is daily: fluent output either carries its grounds, offices and revision conditions with it, or it wears the mask of a certainty no surface could earn. The [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C48-Trust-Faith-under-Formal-Limit|Trust / Faith under Formal Limit]] concept defines the committed relation through which measure is used without absolutisation; [The Measure Constitutes](A15-prime-The-Measure-Constitutes.md) grounds the constituting side — the benchmark that hides its criterion manufactures the very certainty that then demands blind trust.

## #2

The whole Homeric net makes the limit of apparatus concrete. Hephaestus's bonds succeed at capture and public proof; exposure still does not settle the relation; release comes only when Poseidon personally undertakes the debt. The apparatus borrows a personed undertaking because tightening the bonds or improving the record cannot manufacture surety.

Every observability regime inherits the figure. An audit trail can establish what occurred; a trace can expose mechanism; a dashboard can show calibration. None of these becomes the bearer of loss. Surety is a named undertaking within relation.

Trust therefore belongs to **mediated encounter**, not to the mere existence of inspectable mediation. The apparatus can make the terms of reliance explicit and revisable. It cannot substitute itself for the participant or institution that bears the consequence of reliance.

## #3

The faithful form makes grounds, offices and revision conditions visible and grades what is entrusted. This essay's ladder distinguishes discoverability, contact and an undertaking: publishing an architecture does not itself provide a challenge route; opening a channel does not itself identify who will bear the loss; a named undertaking specifies what is promised and under which conditions it can be revoked. Provenance identifies whose labour, sources and choices entered the answer. Competence is judged by evidence in the scope concerned. Consequence identifies who is exposed and who can authorise a remedy.

In a constructed research commission, a report is first checked against its cited passages. A false citation calls for corrected evidence; a sound passage which defeats the question's premise calls for a returned interpretation; an adequate criterion can be reaffirmed after the objection. If the result is to guide a consequential decision, the commissioning office must also state what reliance it permits, who can challenge it and who bears its effects. The next decision inherits the corrected source or examined terms. A fuller trace supports that sequence; it does not perform the undertaking by itself.

The brief's Bayesian mutual-modelling regress "does not terminate in principle; in practice it truncates where further recursion no longer changes the predicted action" (the prediction-stability discussion, §8.4). Its stopping condition concerns a prediction procedure. It can help decide how much modelling this task needs. The native determining-act/content relation has another object: even a sufficient model is received and judged in an act it does not thereby contain. Trust can use the scoped prediction while keeping the personed undertaking and that philosophical difference present.

## #4

Efficacy sets the burden. The greater the capacity to affect a world, the greater the burden of trust in the relation — [efficacy increases the stakes of entrustment](../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md). Executing a contract, spending a resource, shaping an account and mediating an encounter exercise different powers over different lives. Their actual scope and consequences set the burden. Someone must be able to bear, investigate, correct, withdraw and renew the particular undertaking; the fact that an actor is technical does not settle where every act stands on the gradient. Trust is not guaranteed harmlessness. A ground can genuinely withdraw; a commitment can be exited; an offered return does not oblige continued submission to an abusive office — the withdrawal route is part of the faithful form, not its failure. Fides precedes arbitration; credere — entrustment — becomes possible again after the account has been examined and settled: the ladder is climbable again precisely because it was graded.

## #5→0

Formal limit becomes a discipline of continued relation. A calibrated result, an audit trail, a benchmark can make reliance better grounded; the institutional ownership and the consequences of deployment still matter, and observability alone cannot supply surety. [The Usurping Criterion](A24-prime-The-Usurping-Criterion.md) extends the burden to the criterion that authorises the trust; [Individuation with Recognition](A21-prime-Individuation-with-Recognition.md) grounds the legibility of histories and sources such graded trust can rest on; [The Image That Closes the Loop](A20-prime-The-Image-That-Closes-the-Loop.md) qualifies its counterfeit — the persona that accumulates prediction-stability as if it were earned relation; [Deference as Co-Evolutionary Alignment](A31-prime-Deference-as-Co-Evolutionary-Alignment.md) tests whether fidelity permits objection and revision. The account is acted upon while remaining answerable to source, context and Other — and [faithful definition of the agent](A01-prime-Faithful-Definition-of-the-Agent.md) receives the return as faithful definition carried through time: the named assemblage supplies an address for inquiry. Faithfulness is achieved through what that address makes possible: evidence checked, an implicated condition reached under real authority, a corrected or reaffirmed commission, and a later act which inherits the judgment. Trust can be better grounded by that enacted relation without becoming guaranteed harmlessness.

[Trust, faith and formal limit](../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) keep reliance graded, revocable and borne by a named participant or institution. [Entrustment](AC.md) gives that care its point of contact: the person living through the system can inspect, challenge, withdraw and renew the particular undertaking through which its power enters a life.
