## #0 — The forming relation becomes executable

[Computational process ontology](../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) establishes the priority: an achieved thing inherits the relations through which it became actual and contributes conditions to what follows. Computation makes exact, repeatable transformations available for execution. Code becomes the logos of this age where those formative distinctions can themselves be articulated, enacted, inspected and revised. The answer returns through the means which produced it.

The quotient gives this forming relation an exact topological expression. On the plane, declare `(x,y) ∼ (x+m,y+n)` for integers `m,n`. Taking each equivalence class as one point produces `ℝ²/ℤ²`, the torus. Infinitely many representatives receive one surface-address under a definite rule of identification. The [core theorem](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md) reads the slash here as the slash of `0/1`: a field becomes determinate through a relation, rather than a finished thing acquiring relations afterwards. The [toroidal argument](../arguments/A17-Toroidal-Circulation-and-the-Arche-Topos.md) develops the resulting quotient, cover and return.

A harness makes constitutive relation technically consequential through composition. It joins a model to tools, memory, sources, permissions and interfaces so that an act becomes possible. This is the technological continuation of the forming relation. A literal quotient of technical states would additionally need its own specified equivalence relation and preserved operations; composition by itself supplies neither. The generative claim already has force: the capability acts through an organised field, and altering a constituting relation can alter what that capability does.

<a id="1"></a>
## #1 — Capability becomes actual in an ensemble

[Agentworld's harness-centric account](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q038) locates deployed capability in the relations among weights, tools, memory, shared knowledge and institutional interfaces. It directs inquiry toward the ensemble and the commons through which participants act. That is a claim about the conditions of capability; a particular architecture still has to show which of its conditions changed the act under examination.

The same model can participate in different operative ensembles. Giving it a new source changes what can be retrieved. Giving it a tool changes what operation is available. Granting or withholding permission changes whether a proposed operation can become an act. A change in any one relation need not alter every answer; it changes the relevant possibilities of reception and action. Usage can also leave a record, improved tool or revised procedure which another run inherits without any change to the base weights.

[Objective Internality](../concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) names this constituting means of world-disclosure. [Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) names the reflexive return through which an achieved determination reaches a condition of its subsequent making. The ensemble is not complete merely because its components have been named. Its operative relations must be followed.

<a id="2"></a>
## #2 — A trace connects the result to another possible act

[The venue's representation problem](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q023) asks how participants can encounter an agent's actual operation rather than only the appearance it presents for legibility. The process grammar makes this an inquiry into what the result inherited, selected, excluded, stabilised and changed. An explanation can be accurate yet leave the making inaccessible. A trace becomes a route of return when a participant can use it to reach a relevant condition.

In the constructed route-planning case, the trace identifies the map version and access rule used to choose a path. A site report challenges the map. The returned correction must reach the representation used in the next selection; merely appending the report to an unused log changes no such condition. A disputed permission must reach its actual authority. The same visible route can therefore prompt different corrections because its information and its authorisation have different sources.

[The constituting measure](A15-prime-The-Measure-Constitutes.md) distinguishes what an evaluator makes count. [Traversal with return](A17-prime-Traversal-with-Return.md) carries the encountered consequence back to model, criterion or commission. [Parity](A33-prime-Parity.md) asks where a claimed return changes the operation and which comparison could discriminate it. Together they turn legibility into a possible intervention rather than a terminal description.

## #3 — Alignment reaches the conditions it declares

An account which understands agency through inheritance, exclusion, consequence and return commits its technical design to those relations. Its alignment claim cannot be discharged by displaying the cuts while preventing every consequential challenge from reaching them. The operative question is whether a returned encounter can revise the relevant means, under attributable authority, and whether the next act inherits that revision.

[Agentworld's co-evolutionary account of alignment](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q040) places contact and mutual adjustment inside the problem. The present grammar specifies what such adjustment has to reach. Correcting an observation, revising a model, altering an evaluator and changing a commission are different operations. Their differences must survive the account of successful adaptation.

The representation and its return also belong to relations among participants. A planner can expose why it selected a route while the affected person contests the aim for which that route was selected. Their powers are not interchangeable. [Shared worlds without merger](A30-prime-Shared-Worlds-Unmerged.md) keeps the encounter capable of changing both sides without giving either side complete possession of the other.

<a id="4"></a>
## #4 — The context problem becomes a design object

The contemporary importance of code lies in this articulation of conditions. Memory, selection, permission, evaluation and institutional purpose acquire technical forms which can enter further acts. AI makes the problem of context available as the active organisation of a field, rather than merely surrounding prose. The [core theorem's process development](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md) gives the organising sequence: differentiated possibility becomes an achieved form, the form has consequences, and the returned encounter can change the means of further determination.

The [Meta-Epistemic Framework](../concepts/C39-Meta-Epistemic-Framework.md) makes a source, lens, gauge and counter-reading available within that sequence. A changed lens must change what is asked or admitted; a changed criterion must change how a relevant later act is judged. The harness is the execution environment in which those relations can be enacted and examined. Its record does not include every material, social or historical condition on which it depends. It gives participants definite points at which the larger dependence can become answerable.

This is why the venue's demand for [conceptual systems adequate to the present](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q003) is also a practical demand. The concepts must articulate differences the technical field actually performs, and the field must be able to return consequences which sharpen or correct their proposed translation.

## #5→0 — The completed form becomes inherited possibility

The arc's related arguments now acquire a common operation. [The uncounted entering the account](A10-prime-Uncounted-Enters-the-Account.md) reopens what a determination omitted. [One field with two faces](A11-prime-One-Field-Two-Faces.md) and [the defence of plurality](A12-prime-Against-Monoculture.md) keep differentiated participants within a shared relation. [The cut and the gathering](A13-prime-The-Cut-and-the-Gathering.md) preserve selection and recomposition as cooperating powers. [Placedness](A16-prime-No-View-from-Nowhere.md) makes the act's situation consequential. [The traversal run in code](A18-prime-Traversal-Run-in-Code.md) carries the achieved distinctions into another cycle.

The result returns into the means of another act. Its source can be corrected, its measure contested, its permission revised, its remaining question inherited. Each change needs the route through which another act will receive it. [Epistemic Cultivation](../concepts/C46-Epistemic-Cultivation.md) follows this continuing work: an intelligence becomes more exact by changing its means in response to their use.

The technical return therefore reaches [the process argument](../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) and [the shared field](AC.md) with a definite contribution. Code can make formative relations explicit and repeatable; an encounter can then reach those relations rather than only their latest answer. The achieved `1` becomes part of a changed field of possibility. The age's logos is this articulation made consequential and capable of return.
