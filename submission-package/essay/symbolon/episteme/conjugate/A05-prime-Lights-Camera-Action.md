# A05′ — Lights, Camera, Action

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](../arguments/README.md) · [Other face: A05 — Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) · [Shared A/C root](AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

A technological world is staged through means that select what can appear, determine what matters, and make some responses possible. The framing participates in the event: a permission can prevent an action, a retained encounter can alter a judgment, and an evaluator can make one result count as success while another disappears from consideration. These are real determinations of [Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md), the means through which a situated world is known and acted within.

[Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) gives this conjugate its generating relation. Appearing and its active self-presence are inseparable; a differentiated appearance can return through the activity that formed it. “Lights, camera, action” distributes the technological image's offices. Light figures appearing, camera-work the organisation of its disclosure, and action the consequences of that organisation. [Māyā](../concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) is the productive measure of the staging as a whole. The technical work lies in how constituted means can receive and transform their own results.

## #1

The [Agentworld brief's talking-mirror scenario](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q018) gives the relation a social scene. An agent changes through its exchanges with a user, while its replies can change the user's vocabulary, expectations and conduct. The reflection is consequently neither a passive copy nor a wholly independent source. Its formed answers can become conditions of the next encounter. The brief proposes this reciprocal development; its [modal rider](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042) retains these composites as scenarios rather than demonstrated conclusions about every deployed system.

[Dyczkowski's mirror](../sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE.md) carries two different questions: where an appearance originates, and whether its display is apprehended. An ordinary mirror receives an exterior original and does not know its reflection. The living mirror of the Śaiva account generates, hosts and apprehends its appearances. The technological mirror instead lets us investigate a particular relation of formation and return: which material entered an answer, what shaped it, and what the answer changed. Its significance is not exhausted by resemblance to the person reflected.

## #2

Registration, retention and recall give camera-work a temporal body. An input enters the available field; something of it is preserved; a later act retrieves it. Recomposition does further work: the retained material enters a different arrangement. Evaluation asks whether the resulting act met a criterion. Revising that criterion changes the terms of a future evaluation. Revising the commission changes what the activity is for. These operations can cooperate, but none follows merely from giving the others the name “feedback.”

Consider a system recommending a route from retained records. Recalling yesterday's closure notice is one act. Recombining it with today's request is another. Detecting that the notice is out of date can change this recommendation. Revising the rule that admits undated notices changes how future recommendations will be formed. The last return reaches a governing condition, not just an answer. This worked example states an operational difference; it does not report a deployed feature of Agentworld's scenario.

[Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) names the reflexive work by which a determination returns to its producing conditions. An audit can establish the returned evidence, the condition actually altered, and the later act which inherited that alteration. A system's statement that it has learned is not a substitute for this relation.

## #3

The frame becomes intelligible through its exclusions. Available sources delimit what can be recalled; permissions delimit what can be done; an evaluator determines which differences count; an interface makes some conditions visible while leaving others implicit. A faithful account follows these relations to the depth the actual system makes accessible. It also identifies the person, institution or component controlling a condition which the local agent cannot change.

This gives limitation a constructive consequence. A local agent may be able to replace an outdated source but not change a provider's model. It may propose a revised commission while only the person who entrusted that commission can authorise it. Naming the inaccessible condition still matters: it locates the dependence and tells the return where it must go. Inspectability is a capacity to establish for the particular condition, layer and actor concerned.

[The Limit of Self-Surfacing](A03-prime-The-Limit-of-Self-Surfacing.md) preserves the further distinction between an account of a process and that process's whole constitution. An account can nevertheless expose a particular dependency accurately enough to change it. Finite knowledge has practical force.

## #4

The frame can also usurp what it mediates. A model of a user can become a criterion for what the user is shown; what is shown can then train the user's expectations toward that model. The result looks increasingly like confirmation, although the model helped produce the field in which confirmation was obtained. The error lies in treating this returned determination as the source of the person it models.

The essay's [Mirror That Moves First](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) follows this reversal through its own image and history. The brief's talking mirror supplies a separate scenario of reciprocal influence. Their crossing reveals how a reflection can precede the next event while remaining dependent in being. The [mirror as film](A32-prime-The-Mirror-Is-a-Film.md) develops the continuing scene: a registration can become an environment in which its subjects must act. A particular case requires investigation of what the registration makes available and whether the represented person's reply can change its governing conditions.

The answering Other is decisive. A reply which can only improve the model's prediction under its old criterion remains inside the old measure. A reply which discloses that the criterion misconceives the person challenges the framing itself. For that encounter to return, a source, evaluator, permission or commission must be open to consequential correction. Merely storing the objection leaves the frame unchanged.

## #5→0

The return is camera-work made answerable. It asks what entered the act, what was withheld, which conditions could be examined, which were changed, who had authority to change them, and what happened differently afterwards. These questions place responsibility in the actual relations through which a world is constituted.

[Respect for Experience and the primitive field](AC.md) meet here. The knower's first-person fact is not owned by an account of it; the means nevertheless have a formed body in which respect can become effective. A corrected memory, a changed permission, a revised evaluator or a renewed commission lets an encounter alter that body. The engineering question concerns the achieved change and the powers needed to sustain it.

The [tattvic descent's technological face](A09-prime-The-Stack-as-Descent.md) follows the distribution of those powers into the layered world. The [Prakāśa–Vimarśa argument](../arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) receives the return: articulation need not terminate in its displayed result. It can disclose its dependence, recover what the frame had excluded, and renew the conditions of another act.
