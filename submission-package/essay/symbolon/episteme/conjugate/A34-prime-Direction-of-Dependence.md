# A34′ — The Direction of Dependence

## #0 — A forecast changes its future

A forecast can affect the event it forecasts. An anticipated condition informs an intervention, and the intervention becomes one of the conditions of what follows. [Agentworld's relay scenario](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q022) makes this passage explicit: models enter the worlds later models receive. Its descriptive, predictive and speculative modalities give representations different ways to become consequential.

The question is what depends on what in that passage. [The idealist order](../arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md) locates the whole inquiry within Life / Mind: Subjective Immediacy is the knower, Objective Internality the constituted means, and World the known. An inspectable relay is a determinate articulation within that relation. Its causal power makes the distinction more consequential, not less.

## #1 — Five directions within one event

Consider a constructed greenhouse relay: a temperature forecast leads to a vent being opened; the achieved temperature then enters a subsequent forecast. The current model depends causally on the data and processes through which it was produced. A later physical condition depends partly on the action that model informed. These causal directions can form a feedback circuit.

Epistemically, the forecast may be the operator's first access to a possible future condition. Logically, the predicted result follows from a calculation under specified assumptions. A transcendental question asks what makes the situation available as a possible object of experience. Ontologically, model, intervention and account of both are determinate through the source–means–known relation. None of these directions can be read simply from which item is printed first in a diagram.

[The reflective field](../concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) holds epistemic precedence and causal initiative together with dependence in being. [Qualified predication](../concepts/C63-Pros-Hen-In-Quantum-Focal-Return-Qualified-Predication.md) keeps the respect explicit. A forecast may be prior to an intervention and dependent on an earlier measurement. Its authority to recommend an action is another relation, entrusted by a particular commission. Being causally influential and being authorised to decide are different facts.

## #2 — The means participates in the outcome

The greenhouse's achieved temperature must be understood with the vent intervention included. Comparing it with an untreated forecast while erasing the action would misdescribe what was tested. The representation has participated in the production of the later object. Its contribution can be investigated through the recorded state, assumptions, instruction, action and result.

The encounter can expose an error in a reading, a response model, a target or the permission to act. These corrections return to different conditions. A changed estimate does not itself confer actuator access. A new target written in a report does not change the target used by the controller. [Traversal with return](A17-prime-Traversal-with-Return.md) follows the passage until the relevant revision is inherited by the next act.

Causal reciprocity therefore belongs inside the positive order of dependence. The World includes the effects of its representations; its means can themselves become objects of inquiry and transformation. The concrete object does not become unreal because mediation participates in its formation. Nor does the representation become the origin of the whole field because it has changed one event within it.

## #3 — The source can itself be situated

A shared model may serve as the reference from which several projections are made. In that relation it genuinely occupies the Bimba office. Its own measurements, inherited concepts and commission can remain visible as wider sources to which it is a pratibimba. [The disclosure architecture](../concepts/C61-Symbolon-Disclosure-Architecture.md) preserves this nesting of real source offices rather than requiring a local reference to pretend it has no history.

A later agent may encounter the projection before it encounters the reference. The projection can even cause the reference to be revised. [The mirror that moves first](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) describes that initiated return. What changes is an actual relation of knowledge or action. The projected form's success does not make it ontologically self-originating.

Agentworld's §5.3–5.4 extends the case to relays composed by different actors and resolutions. A local description may be another system's input, a prediction another's object, an intervention another's unrecorded condition. There need not be one office able to inspect or revise all the legs. A meaningful account identifies the relation it can follow and the party or condition on which a further change depends. The research question about value, decay and deception remains a question about the actual courses of these relays, not a verdict that any circuit with a return is trustworthy.

## #4 — The slash becomes available

The native recognition sequence gives this investigation a fuller order:

**`0 → Ø → X → Ø/X → (0/Ø)/(1/X) → 1 ↺ 0/1`.**

It begins with unobjectifiable presence and follows its occlusion by a formed means. In `Ø`, a particular way of determining passes as the whole I; `X` is the cut and named object-field produced through the same activity. `Ø/X` makes the mediating slash explicit. At `(0/Ø)/(1/X)`, unoccluded presence and apparent self, unified manifestation and its differentiated field are available together through the outer relation. The achieved `1` affirms Self as one being, integrating what had appeared as bound self and opposed world. Its return as `0/1` recognises their always-related source and manifestation. [The crossed-zero matheme](../../matheme/ql/crossed-zero-re-entry.md) develops the complete traversal.

The technical inquiry makes a specific part of this relation practical. “The model says so” can initially conceal the observations, choices and commission under which the sentence was formed. Investigation separates these operations. Recognition can also reach the investigator's identification with a preferred model or result. The instrument's answer is then available as a determination within an encounter, capable of being corrected by something its own frame had excluded.

A further self-description is still a description. Its usefulness lies in what it makes available for inquiry and change. The Subject-condition is the condition to which the entire inquiry appears; it is not a withheld field in the configuration file. The idealist order is therefore preserved through increasing technical disclosure: the means can become more articulate without becoming the whole relation by accumulation.

## #5→0 — The commission receives its consequence

The return is enacted when the relevant correction changes a subsequent possibility. A reading is corrected at its source; a model changes its inference; an evaluator changes what counts as success; a commission changes what the system is for. A local system may enact some of these changes and return others to the people or systems who hold the relevant authority. An accurate account keeps the proposed revision, its enactment and its inheritance distinct.

[The Advent of Zero](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-advent-zero-subject/SOURCE.md) returns exact knowledge to the context in which it becomes a term. Technical exactness can intensify that return: the more clearly a result's formation is available, the more precisely an encounter can reach the condition needing change. The return also has a manner. [The commons behind the system](A35-prime-Commons-Behind-the-System.md) receives the question of how an act sustains the relations it draws upon, and [the final technological return](A36-prime-Integral-Zero-of-the-Hybrid-Society.md) gathers the instrument back into its purpose.

[The shared A/C field](AC.md) holds source, means, known and whole together. The relay is a real determination within it. Its ability to change later events persists; its dependence is now something its further activity can acknowledge rather than conceal.
