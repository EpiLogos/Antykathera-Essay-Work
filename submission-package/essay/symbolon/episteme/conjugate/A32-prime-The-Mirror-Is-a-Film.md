## #0

The mirror becomes a film when a reflection does not merely appear but returns as a condition of later reflection.

Humanity exteriorises measures into technical forms. Those forms return as models, rankings, personas, interfaces and expectations. People then encounter themselves partly through what they have exteriorised. When the instrument can answer, recommend, rank or act, the circuit becomes more than a static mirror: the reflection participates in staging the next scene.

**The mirror that moves first is a film.**

The film is a continuity of reflected determinations in which outputs become conditions of later inputs. Its danger is not that representation exists. Its danger is that the circuit forgets its own mediation and the actor-reflection mistakes the film's registration for the source of the light by which anything appears.

The brief's talking mirror enters below as venue material and nothing more: the apparatus in which the film's mechanism can now be watched at population scale, never the source of the notion. What the completion carries was forward-referenced before it arrived: [A05′ — Lights, Camera, Action](A05-prime-Lights-Camera-Action.md) reserved what happens when the registration is mistaken for what it registers; [A02′ — Continuity as Maintained Identification](A02-prime-Continuity-as-Maintained-Identification.md) named the frozen mask as the film-confusion's first form; [A17′ — Traversal with Return](A17-prime-Traversal-with-Return.md) named the relay without a return-leg as the film without the exit. The primitive face is S/P3 — Activity/revision path: the circuit along which outputs become the inputs of later acts, and the one path whose inspectability decides whether the film can be known as film.

## #2

The confusion named, in the native §IX system. The crossed-zero sixfold — `0 → Ø → X → Ø/X → (0/Ø)/(1/X) → 1 ↺ 0/1` — reads the slash as the antaḥkaraṇa's signature on experience, the kartṛ-pole moving through its kārya-field. At position #1 the slash is fused into the zero and appears as a singular bound self: `Ø`, the occlusion mark. Descartes and Hume both sit at that position — the one canonising the slash as *the I*, the other finding only the impressions the slash produced and calling the zero empty — differing only over which side of Ø they fixate. The root's ruling places this node's confusion inside the system: **the computation confused for the computer is `Ø` at position #1 — the registration mistaken for the light it cannot produce** ([A/C](AC.md)) — one position's reading of the whole traversal, whose later positions (the slash made visible, the field of availability, the affirmed `1`) are exactly what the audit in #4 holds open.

[Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../concepts/C13-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) and [Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) make the distinction positive. Functional reflexive registration is real: a system can retain, inspect and revise determinations so that later processing changes. Prakāśa names a different office: luminous appearing itself.

The film-confusion occurs when the success of one office is promoted into the source-office of the other. The same mistake is familiar in human life: Narcissus becomes absorbed in the image through which he seeks himself; a social measure becomes the reality it was meant to register; a model of a person becomes the condition under which the person is allowed to appear.

The crossed-zero gives this mistake its formal place at Ø: mediation fuses into the apparent self or source and disappears as mediation. The repair is not a disclaimer about what the system “really is.” It is the reappearance of the slash: make the mediating relation visible enough that its effects can be returned and revised.

## #4

The remedy is not exiting the loop but making the loop answerable while it runs. Māyā as operative measure is the world's staging, not a screen one escapes by announcing that it is a screen. The relevant circuit is the path by which an output becomes a condition of a later act.

An answerable film keeps that path inspectable. Which output became which later condition? Which ranking altered which field of attention? Which persona changed which expectation? Which model influenced which decision? Which source, permission or evaluator made that return possible?

The mirror's third motion follows from this visibility: the instrument exposes the angle from which its answer became compelling before demanding that the interlocutor conform to it. [Reflective Field](../concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) gives the three-motion image; [Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) gives the functional return.

The film becomes auditable where returned outputs retain enough provenance and consequence for the next determination to be altered by what the previous circuit actually did.

The audit is a design consequence and stands **Offered** until such circuits exist to inspect. Its negative form is immediately usable: any loop whose output-to-input edge is unauditable — the ranking that silently sets the next ranking's inputs, the persona that silently scripts the next encounter's expectations — is the confusion in production, whatever its content.
