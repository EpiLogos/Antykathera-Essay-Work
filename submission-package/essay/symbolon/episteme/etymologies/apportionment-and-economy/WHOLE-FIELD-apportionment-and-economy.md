# Whole Field — Apportionment / Economy

Apportionment makes a finite share effective within the economy which sustains it. Five operations carry that relation through capacity, measure, delegated labour, commons and planetary computation. Name/Power gives the act its expenditure, authority, affected other and returned result; the six sections follow this whole movement without adding another operation. The protected [HISTORY](HISTORY-apportionment-and-economy.md) retains the authorial lexical inquiry; [the historical branches](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-apportionment-and-economy.md) distinguish the separately warranted senses and institutions through which its terms acquired their uses.

## #0 — The share within its sustaining order

<a id="whole-field-claim"></a>
### Whole-field claim

**Apportionment** names the act of distributing a finite share, capacity, role or measure within a wider field. **Economy** names the ordering/management of a relational whole through which such distributions become operative. The canonical relation lies in the pattern the two words name together, and it does not require that they share one root or one political theory: finite agency repeatedly appears as a distribution of powers inside an order that no single share exhausts.

An allotment changes what someone can do. It sets aside time for a task, entrusts an office with authority, or establishes the unit under which unlike things can be compared. The share acquires a determinate boundary; the relations sustaining it continue across that boundary. Economy follows those relations through use, consequence and renewed distribution. An account of the allotted product therefore owes an account of the powers and conditions through which it became available.

The five operations below follow this pressure from finite capacity to planetary consequence, and three kinds of evidence are in play. A word's descent and its attested uses have lexical witnesses of their own. The performed allocation and its consequence establish a different relation, which holds even where a Latin reconstruction is conjectural, since finite agency and its economy do not wait on the etymology. A phonic or poetic crossing can reopen attention, and its historical attribution answers to lexical evidence. An attested Greek word names a distribution, and whether the distribution is just is a further inquiry.

## #4 — The economy exceeds its owner and its interface
