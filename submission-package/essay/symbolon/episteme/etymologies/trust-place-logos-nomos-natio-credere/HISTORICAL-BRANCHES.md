# Fides / Topos / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere — Historical Branches

The [sixfold trust relation](WHOLE-FIELD.md) joins prior reliance, situated place, articulated account, instituted rule, inherited belonging and renewed entrustment. Taylor's [inherited word-field](HISTORY.md) and [twelve-branch historical tree](../../histories/places-and-peoples/language-law-nation-centralisation/HISTORY.md) give these relations particular temporal and institutional settings. Lexical descent, attested sense and an authorial comparison have different warrants. A documentary intervention can establish a prescription without establishing its whole reception; an incomplete transmission chain leaves resemblance a comparison rather than a cause.

## The six lexical inputs

The selected online dictionary consultations date to 8 September 2026. They give the bounded senses below. A primary citation embedded in a dictionary identifies a passage; it does not make the dictionary a collation of that ancient edition.

### Fides / fidere

Lewis and Short associates *fides* with *fido* and differentiates reliance, credibility and fidelity. Cicero's *De officiis* 2.9.33 and *Pro Sulla* 3.10 appear as its examples of confidence linked to competence/justice and credibility accorded to testimony. The [fides entry](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/SOURCE.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q001) gives those selected semantic witnesses; the neighbouring stringed-instrument homograph has a separate entry. The online lexical witness does not verify each ancient context or a musical derivation.

Prior reliance makes an inquiry possible; examination makes its bearer, scope and consequence explicit. These temporal offices can differ within overlapping trust words. Their distinction is Taylor's relational construction. Particular Roman fidelity/protection/public obligation and later theological covenants have their own texts and institutions; the selected dictionary senses do not establish their whole histories.

### Topos

LSJ's τόπος distinguishes region/place, position, a passage in a text and a rhetorical topic. Aeschines 3.78 and Aristotle's *Rhetoric* 1358a14 are its citations for different uses. The [selected LSJ transcription](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/SOURCE.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q001) bears this semantic range; the separately displayed Bailly entry leaves etymology uncertain. Ancient contexts and deeper descent remain independent of that selected transcription.

Taylor's *Arche-Topos* makes placing itself a relation through which observer, object, path and horizon become determinate. The native field is an authorial compound, rather than an attested ancient word. Topology, observer-position and jurisdiction have different objects: a formal quotient/lift proof keeps its mathematical assumptions, while authority over a place depends on its particular law. A stronger historical connection among these uses needs an actual passage and transmission chain.

### Logos

LSJ's [λόγος](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/SOURCE.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q002) differentiates reckoning, account, proportion, explanation and discourse. Euclid V definition 3 is its cited ratio witness, while account and argument have different contexts. Neither “word” nor “reason” translates every office. The dictionary supplies selected lexical evidence, without independently collating each ancient source or a whole philosophy of Logos.

An achieved articulation makes a relation available to others; Epi-Logos returns it to its source, exclusions and effects. Greek prefix semantics can constrain that construction without proving its philosophical return. Exact *epi-* history, poetic re-entry, Vāk and the Heidegger/Chang reception retain different source chains. No Greek–Sanskrit borrowing follows from their operational comparison.

In [personed articulation](../../dossiers/indian-philosophy.md#2--speech-forms-a-world-which-can-answer-its-speaker), a speaker forms an expression and receives an answer within a circumstance. Vāk's formative, mental and uttered offices differ from the Greek attested account-word. Their comparison preserves the different source histories while making the act of address consequential to what is said.

### Nomos / nemein

LSJ's [νόμος](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/SOURCE.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q003) relates to νέμω and differentiates usage/custom from enacted law or ordinance. Conventional νόμῳ can oppose φύσει in its cited contexts. The differently accented νομός, pasture/district, has a separate entry. The selected verb witness also gives distribution of portions/honour and holding a portion; these offices retain distinct uses rather than one inevitable pasture-to-state chronology.

An ordering rule distributes authority, rights and permissions. Custom, enactment, jurisdiction and sanction do different work in a political case: a shared custom need not confer the same power as an enacted law, and jurisdiction determines where a decision can bind. The selected lexical offices make that distinction intelligible without establishing every historical step or the verb's complete semantic genealogy.

### Natio / nasci

Lewis and Short's [natio](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/SOURCE.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q003) connects with *nascor* and gives birth, stock, kind and people. The Alatius transcription was consulted after the earlier Perseus headword endpoint failed. Its examples also concern animals and things; one English translation does not settle civic identity. Cicero's grouping and a modern nation-state have different historical objects.

Neo-nativity is Taylor's diagnosis of a historically achieved institutional alignment presenting itself as original natural belonging. Ancestry and continuity remain real within that relation. A given medieval or modern use of nation, people, birth or citizenship acquires its force through particular institutions and practices; Latin morphology alone does not supply their chronology.

### Credere / creditum

Lewis and Short's [credo](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/SOURCE.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q002) differentiates lending, entrusting for protection, confidence and belief, with *creditum* as a loan. Cato's *De agri cultura* 5.4 is its citation for delegated lending authority, not an independently collated ancient passage here. The nineteenth-century comparative etymology does not supply a current specialist reconstruction.

After examination, Credere receives a commitment with better knowledge of its bearer, scope and possible failure. Its entrusting senses are independently attested; the before/after-arbitration relation is Taylor's construction. The deeper heart/place proposal remains unverified in a specialist historical-etymological source. Renewed commitment remains possible through the attested sense while that specific descent stays unsettled.

## French documentary sequence

Two interventions give the centralisation branch independent primary inputs. They are separated by different political institutions and more than two centuries; no continuous enforcement history is inferred between them.

In the 1539 justice ordinance, [articles 110–111](../../sources/political-theory-institutions/francois-i/francois-i-1539-villers-cotterets/SOURCE.md#francois-i-1539-villers-cotterets-q001) prescribe clear judgments and French in the listed judicial acts, addressing uncertainty attributed to Latin wording. Courts, registers, contracts, commissions and instruments pronounced, recorded or delivered to parties give the prescription its particular reach. This is the official transcribed intervention; local compliance and everyday language use require different evidence.

In [the 4 June 1794 speech](../../sources/political-theory-institutions/gregoire/gregoire-1794-unite-de-langue/SOURCE.md#gregoire-1794-unite-de-langue-q001), Grégoire argues that inadequate French compromises public acts, while excluding citizens without it recreates hierarchy. He advocates linguistic uniformity and removal of regional idioms, meeting an objection from southern attachment and literary traditions. The remedy combines an egalitarian justification with linguistic elimination. The official supplied transcription marks omissions; reported counts are the speaker's claims, and the complete report/decree is not established by the excerpt.

Legal legibility can enlarge participation while the prescribed language becomes a condition of standing. The actual difference depends on how affected people used, translated or could not enter the procedure. A historically effective return has a dated challenge and institutional reception: an application can be corrected under a fitting rule, the rule revised where it fails, or a challenged condition retained with an answerable reason. Those outcomes have different consequences for later acts. The two documents do not establish all local implementation, petitions, counter-testimony, the full 1794 report/decree or later schooling and reception.

## Twelve branches and their distinct return tasks

The twelve branches preserve the different objects Taylor placed in the historical tree. The scoped French documents establish particular interventions; other causal or transmission claims retain their named witness limits. What makes these histories comparable is the relation among place, articulation, rule and belonging, rather than an assumption that every population follows the same sequence.

### I — Occitania and Capetian/French incorporation

Military, dynastic, administrative and pedagogical powers give Occitan incorporation different forms: the 1209 crusade, 1229 settlement, 1271 incorporation, 1539 legal-language intervention, 1794 programme and later schooling are separate events. The acquired ordinance and speech establish only their specified points. Treaty/jurisdiction clauses, regional legal records and learner testimony govern stronger accounts of their effects. Altered access or suppressed speech can change the relation among place, articulation and rule; a national narrative cannot settle those consequences by calling the result one people.

### II — Posquières, Lunel and the Jewish/Kabbalistic field

Languedoc's learned relations exceed political borders. RABaD III, Isaac the Blind, ibn Tibbon translations and Maimonidean reception have different actors and textual offices. A manuscript, translation preface, correspondence or documented teacher/text itinerary can establish an actual route to Spain or Italy; a missing chain leaves resemblance a comparison. The branch makes territorial identity porous through transmission without supplying an undocumented causal bridge.

### III — Renaissance transmission and mathematical office

Florentine patronage and Ficino/Pico translation, Visconti–Orléans dynastic claims and Pacioli/Leonardo mathematics have different causal offices. The particular Minervois *biscione* remains unidentified and cannot carry a route by resemblance. Commission/translation prefaces, dynastic instruments and the relevant 1494 *Summa* / 1509 *Divina proportione* passages govern stronger attribution. Printed codification differs from invention of double-entry bookkeeping. A qualitative/quantitative mathesis comparison becomes historically consequential through a documented change of use rather than a list of Renaissance names.

### IV — Yates, Hanegraaff and rejected knowledge

The qualified Yates thesis and later historiography of rejected knowledge concern different interpretations of exclusion. Censorship, curricular classification, secrecy, manuscript loss and disciplinary rejection are distinct events. A particular decision can change the standing of a particular teaching; the generic category “esoteric” cannot by itself explain disappearance or continuity. Stronger histories depend on the selected scholarship and the documents of the actual institutional act.

### V — Jung's Aion and psychic history

Jung's Pisces/Christ–Antichrist, Gnostic/alchemical and psychic-historical construction organises an image of an epoch. Its image can disclose how an account becomes intelligible while remaining distinct from evidence that it caused European events. Exact *Aion* passages have their selected source scope; a Reformation, outward-conquest or nature-relation causal claim has different historical witnesses. The full mythic operation therefore remains available without becoming one independently established European chronology.

### VI — Italy's literary and administrative languages

Literary Tuscan, national construction, Manzoni's 1868 intervention, schooling and regional spoken language involve different institutions and temporalities. A literary norm's prestige differs from a person's ability to enter an administrative office through it. Commission reports, educational rules and local literacy/use evidence govern those particular consequences. Language can become a condition of belonging without one literary standard alone proving a nation.

### VII — Germany, Volk and racialised classification

Herder's plurality, Fichte's political situation and Grimm's philological/legal/mythic work retain distinct positions. A linguistic description, people-category, ancestry, biological classification and juridical exclusion become consequential through different acts. Shared terms do not establish shared doctrine or inevitability. At each stronger transition the particular passage, classificatory instrument or law identifies who can be admitted, excluded or injured.

### VIII — Anglo-Celtic jurisdiction

Welsh legal incorporation and 1847 education reports, Irish plantation/property relations and Scottish schooling/Gaelic histories have different jurisdictions. Sanction, economic incentive, migration and bilingual practice likewise act differently. A law/report, its local administration and contrary testimony determine which act English makes possible or bars in each case. Their relation can be examined without explaining every language change by one cause.

### IX — Hebrew revival and Zionism

Hebrew revival joins households, schools, press, the 1913 language conflict and political institutions through different acts. Textual continuity and diasporic alternatives remain consequential. Palestinian Arabic sources, land/displacement histories, citizenship instruments and lived testimony place the standing of those affected within the attempted alignment of language, people, law and territory. The branch's particular records govern its chronology; counterhistories belong within its evidence rather than outside its national object.

### X — Abrahamic Word, law and people

Torah/halakhah with diaspora, Christian Logos across Latin/vernacular institutions and Qur'anic Arabic with multilingual umma and differentiated fiqh give Word, law and people different relations. A concrete covenantal/legal case gives source, obligation and mediating office their particular bearers. Peters remains a later comparative lead. These traditions complicate territorial coincidence; they do not provide one priest-to-king-to-politician chronology.

### XI — Post-war recombination

US race law, Nuremberg, denazification and Paperclip/Gehlen have different personnel, legal acts and receiving institutions. Selective retention and real rupture can coexist within post-war recombination. Each claimed continuity or break depends on the named actor, decision and altered authority in its particular record; an undifferentiated account of the whole order would conceal those different operations.

### XII — Computation makes the sequence executable

A computational mark becomes a name; policy selects an allocation; action changes a world; an affected response can reach the answer, source, evaluator, permission or commission responsible. In a local comparison, the determining agent is the functional knower, sources/procedures/tools are means, and differentiated options are known. The apparatus itself serves as means within the person's containing inquiry, where its judgment can become an object of inspection. An authorised correction or fitting-condition retention becomes inherited by the next act. A stronger claim of changed processing needs the versioned commission, selection, action, response and actual changed condition. Compliant output alone leaves that institutional answerability unestablished; no completed trial is reported here.

[These different historical formations](../../histories/places-and-peoples/language-law-nation-centralisation/DEVELOPMENT.md) distinguish incorporation from textual transmission, literary from administrative language, national from transregional belonging and an inherited category from its later institutional or computational use. Their return keeps the bearer, exclusion and undertaking particular. Incomplete witnesses bound each causal claim while the comparison retains those actual differences.

## Rings, sources and return

Participation, ancestry, rational administration and later recomposition organise different meanings of history. They can be co-present rather than one compulsory succession. Historical inquiry concerns an actor, text/office and consequence; archetypal reading concerns the image through which that action becomes intelligible, such as motherland, blood, chosen people, purity, betrayal or sacrifice. A fruit becomes a seed when an achieved category enters the next generation as unexamined inheritance. Recovering its formation changes how it can be used while preserving the life that grew through it.

In [Homer's captured-lovers scene](../../sources/classical-philology/homer/homer-1919-odyssey-murray/SOURCE.md), Poseidon changes assurance about Ares into his own contingent liability; Hephaestus accepts and releases. The later cards keep their web-verification/Perseus-reread limit. [Ostrom's local rule-making](../../sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/SOURCE.md) makes participation and nested governance consequential to a particular ecology and social setting. [Taylor's Chang encounter](../../sources/chinese-philosophy/chung-yuan-chang/chung-yuan-chang-tao-a-new-way-of-thinking-2014/SOURCE.md) gives loving sensitivity its authored relation to origins gone, coming and present, while nested quotations remain uncollated. In [Mono/Poly](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-mono-poly-two-ones/SOURCE.md), a common whole and real local many retain different offices before the institutional comparison. These sources give different means of relation, rather than interchangeable guarantees.

In [renewed entrustment](WHOLE-FIELD.md#credere-renewed-entrustment), a settled account carries examined history and responsibility into another commitment. Its bearer can answer for continuation without owning every future outcome. Source correction, adequate application, revision of a failing rule or reasoned retention reaches the next act at the relevant condition. The specific lexical, documentary, theological and implementation limits remain boundaries of those claims, rather than substitutes for the positive trust relation.
