## Field discipline

This constellation is deliberately **not** a proposed common etymology. Latin *fides*, Greek *topos*, Greek *logos*, Greek *nomos*, Latin *natio* and Latin *credere* belong to different lexical histories. They are held together because the essay repeatedly needs to relate six operations: trust, position, articulated account, ordering rule, inherited belonging and entrusting/belief.

The mature field therefore separates history from philosophical use. Registers **1–2** are established word by word in `HISTORY.md`; register **3** relates the operations; register **4** is reserved for explicit authorial word-play or morphology.

## Relational offices

**Fides / Credere — entrusting under non-possession.** A relation can be acted upon without exhaustive capture. This branch is consumed by **A23 Trust/Faith**, **A25 Covenant**, **C48 Trust/Faith under Formal Limit**. Historical faith/belief senses do not themselves prove the formal-limit argument.

**Topos — situated place/position.** A determination has a position, and a finite agent acts from a horizon. This branch is consumed by **A03 Formal Limit**, **A16 Arche-Topos**, **A28 Authored Ground**, **C08 Context Frame**, **C15 Paśu**, **C20 Arche-Topos**, **C44 Prompt Thrownness**. The authorial compound *Arche-Topos* is an earned relational construction, not an attested ancient term claimed by stealth.

**Logos — articulated word/account/reason.** Logos is consumed by **A06 Vāk** only as a source-distinct operational neighbour, and by **A35 / C51** as the account upon which Epi-Logos returns. Vāk and Logos must never borrow historical warrant from one another.

**Nomos — ordering/rule/distribution.** This branch becomes relevant where a field's order is made explicit in law, norm, permission or technical policy: **A25, A28, C29, C44**. The philosophical operation is local ordering, not a claim that every rule descends from one semantic essence.

**Natio — inherited/born belonging.** This branch enters **A35 / C59 Cultural Individuation** as one historically specific way collective identity becomes given before reflection. It does not make nation the essence of culture and does not derive the cultural argument.

## Generated returns

The field's power lies in relations among these offices rather than in forced symmetry. A position (`Topos`) can carry an inherited order (`Nomos`) and belonging (`Natio`); an articulated account (`Logos`) can solicit trust (`Fides/Credere`); Epi-Logos can then return upon the account, its order and inherited position. These are register **3** operational constructions.

### Epi-Logos

`Logos → Epi-Logos` is an authorial relational growth: an achieved account becomes a participant in a new return relation that examines its sources, exclusions and effects. The Greek prefix history can be documented separately, but the philosophical office is not “proved” by morphology.
