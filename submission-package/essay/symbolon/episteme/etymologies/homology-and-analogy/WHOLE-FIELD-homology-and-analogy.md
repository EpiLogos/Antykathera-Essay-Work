# Whole Field — Homologia / Analogia

Agreement and proportion let distinct fields become comparable through the relations each actually performs. Their [lexical histories](HISTORY-homology-and-analogy.md) supply the historically warranted meanings through which these operations are named.

## #0 — Distinct fields entering one comparison

<a id="why-this-field-exists"></a>
### Why this field exists

The essay moves among mathematics, Śaiva metaphysics, Jungian psychology, language, topology, music and technical computation. Without a disciplined relational vocabulary, similarity quickly becomes false identity. **Homologia / Analogia** provides the grammar by which forms can answer to one another while remaining source-distinct.

Historical Greek meanings and morphology retain their documented witnesses in [the lexical history](HISTORY-homology-and-analogy.md). A correspondence among mathematical, psychic, symbolic and technical acts is established through the operation preserved across those acts.

The attested senses of the two words are what make the field's two questions available. In the LSJ entry, *homologia* runs from a jointly stated account, through assent and admission, to the compact imposed in surrender. To ask whether two fields agree is therefore already to ask who speaks, under what compulsion, and whether the terms are shared or dictated, and the word carries its own test for false agreement. *Analogia* begins as mathematical proportion and widens to correspondence between any related terms, so to ask whether two fields are analogous is to ask in what respect a ratio holds between their terms. Homologia asks whether both fields hold the crossing; analogia asks what the crossing is held in respect of. The range of senses is attested, and it is the reason the field puts both questions to every case in the order it does.

The field has **two members**, Homologia and Analogia. Agreement, proportion and research boundary are three offices of their use; the boundary is the dyad’s answerability to its objects. Unfolding this answerability through six positions leaves its cardinality intact. The [historical and relational branches](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-homology-and-analogy.md) recover how the terms acquired their uses; their [protected history](HISTORY-homology-and-analogy.md) retains the encountered evidence.
