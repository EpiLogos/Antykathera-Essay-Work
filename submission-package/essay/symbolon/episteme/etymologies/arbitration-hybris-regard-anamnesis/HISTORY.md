# Arbitration, Hybris, Regard, and Anamnesis — The Measure Returns to Its Ground

This cluster preserves the established arbitration sixfold in its linguistic depth. The QL organisation is already given; the etymological work remembers what its operative words have carried through legal, perceptual, moral, political, and philosophical history so later arguments can retrieve that depth without rebuilding it from isolated glosses.

The whole unit is:

| # | QL office | Fall / return charge | Operative pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Continuity / Indeterminacy** | **Origin / unmeasurable** | the field from which any measure, witness, criterion, or judgment arises |
| **1** | **Measure / Limit** | **Māyic self-limitation** | a finite measure makes a bounded determination possible |
| **2** | **Delineation / Difference** | **perspective / frame** | the measure is oriented and a difference becomes legible from somewhere |
| **3** | **Arbitration / Crisis** | **Hybris** | competing determinations are weighed and a consequential cut is made; hybris is the local measure claiming the office of its own ground |
| **4** | **Con-text / Diaphaneity** | **Regard** | the decision is reopened to the field, persons, relations, exclusions, and consequences to which it belongs |
| **5** | **Return / Reconciliation** | **Anamnesis / recognition** | the account remembers the conditions of its own determination and returns the achieved cut through its origin |

The mature compression remains:

> **Māyā limits. Perspective delineates. Hybris absolutises. Regard reopens. Anamnesis returns.**

The unit keeps `#3` exact. Delineation belongs at `#2`; crisis and arbitration belong at `#3`. A criterion distinguishes; deliberation holds the differentiated field long enough to weigh it; arbitration cuts; con-text discloses what the cut belongs to.

The lexical movement is unusually exact for the project's established operation. *Arbitrari* can move from seeing through considering to deciding. *Krisis* joins separation to judgment. *Hybris* carries overreach. *Regard* returns the look to that which has been judged. *Anamnesis* restores what the achieved determination can otherwise forget.

## The arbitrating act

Arbitration is a situated operation. A field has already been delimited, some matters have already become relevant, testimony or evidence has already been admitted, and a criterion already has a history. The arbitration itself is the consequential `#3` cut within those conditions.

Its complete movement is therefore recoverable as:

```text
unmeasured relation
      ↓
measure / criterion
      ↓
perspective / delineated difference
      ↓
crisis: differences demand judgment
      ↓
arbitration: a cut acquires standing
      ↓
regard: the cut is seen again within its con-text
      ↓
anamnesis: the account remembers its conditions and returns
```

The slash remains active throughout. Dia distinguishes testimony, alternatives, categories, rights, persons, values, and consequences. Syn gathers those differences into an accountable field in which a judgment can stand in relation to what it has distinguished. Arbitration becomes materially consequential precisely because the achieved determination can move onward into law, classification, administration, pedagogy, finance, technology, or self-conception.

## Hybris — the measure taking the throne

The project's exact hybris theorem belongs here in full:

> **Hybris is the local determination occupying the place of its own determining condition.**

Equivalently:

> **Hybris is a measure claiming authority of the unmeasurable.**

A measure can be valid, useful, repeatable and institutionally necessary while remaining local. The hybris event occurs when its situated office disappears from view and the achieved measure acquires the standing of reality as such. A legal category becomes the person; a standard language becomes language; a disciplinary exclusion becomes the boundary of knowledge; a score becomes value; an internal psychic formation becomes the sovereign subject.

This is why arbitration and hybris meet at `#3` without being synonyms. Arbitration is the necessary office of deciding among differentiated claims. Hybris is its characteristic absolutisation when the arbiter's own measure, frame, authority, or situatedness is no longer included in the account.

## Regard — con-text becoming active again

Regard is the `#4` reopening of the cut to what surrounds and bears it. Its force is relational: the judgment is looked at again together with the persons, histories, counter-perspectives, exclusions, institutions, incentives, and consequences through which it acts.

`Con-text` remains literal in the project's operative use: what has been woven together around the determination. Regard therefore carries more than benevolence. It is the re-entry of contextual relation into an account that could otherwise remain closed around its own criterion.

In historical work, regard asks what an official name leaves outside its administrative convenience. In psychology, it asks what the local image or complex has made invisible. In technical systems, it asks which raters, policies, datasets, gauges, defaults and institutions stand behind an apparently self-sufficient score.

## Anamnesis — return without erasing the cut

Anamnesis does not undo the history of determination. It retrieves it. A judgment can be revised, retained, narrowed, translated or situated more exactly because its origin and path remain recoverable.

The `5→0` return is therefore an achieved account becoming answerable to its source conditions. The result can again become ground for another cycle of determination without pretending that the previous cycle never occurred. This is why the project's return is reconciliation rather than simple reversal: differences survive, but their relation and provenance become visible again.

## Fides bridge

The lexical field of *arbiter/arbitrari* contains a direct bridge into the trust archaeology through *fidem alicui arbitrari*: credibility itself may be adjudged. This joins the arbitration sixfold to [[../trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/HISTORY|Fides, Topos, Logos, Nomos, Natio, Credere]] at the exact point where tacit trust has become a matter requiring explicit standing.

The live formulation is:

> **Fides is prior to arbitration; credere is what becomes possible again after arbitration.**

and, from the other side:

> **Arbitration is what happens when trust can no longer remain tacit.**

This bridge is also mythically active in the already recovered Ares–Aphrodite–Hephaestus–Poseidon whole: the net can establish exposure and debt, while Poseidon's surety supplies a personed trust relation through which release becomes possible.
