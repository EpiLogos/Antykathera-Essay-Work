# Historical and Relational Branches

Witnessing, weighing, deciding and returning a settlement are different acts within one relation. Their six generated relations, `6 / 6′ → 6+6′`, unfold in the [whole field](WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md); their received word histories remain in [HISTORY](HISTORY-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md). An attested sense, an institutional act, a philosophical comparison and a poetic recollection each contribute what the others cannot, and a decision becomes answerable through the particular witness, authority and history it receives.

## Witness, weighing and the authority to decide

Latin *arbiter* carries beholding, hearing, witnessing and judgment; *arbitrari* carries perception, consideration, weighing and decision. A witness supplies an encountered particular; weighing assesses its force; judgment states what follows; an authorised decision gives that judgment consequence. A person can perform more than one of these offices, and testimony still needs its authorisation from outside itself, and perception remains short of adjudication.

[Lewis and Short’s selected entries](../../sources/classical-philology/lewis-short/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary/lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary.md#lewis-short-1879-latin-dictionary-q004) distinguish observation, consideration, judicial decision and testimony among Latin uses. *Arbiter* separately names witnessing and an appointed deciding office. Its deeper going/coming reconstruction remains a historical dictionary claim requiring specialist review. [LSJ’s κρίσις entry](../../sources/classical-philology/liddell-scott-jones/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon/lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon.md#lsj-1940-greek-english-lexicon-q004) distinguishes separating, judging, choice and judicial proceedings within its own Greek citation chain. The semantic offices can be compared while their different histories remain effective.

Credit accorded to someone is the office of **fidem alicui arbitrari** in Lewis and Short, *arbitror* II.A, citing Plautus, *Bacchides* 3.6.41. **Fides is prior to arbitration; Credere is what becomes possible again after arbitration. Arbitration is what happens when trust can no longer remain tacit.** [Renewed entrustment](../trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md) follows examination of an undertaking’s bearer and terms. The crisis makes the deciding act explicit within reliance already enacted.

A pledge differs from a score of credibility. In [Poseidon’s undertaking](../../sources/classical-philology/homer/homer-1919-odyssey-murray/homer-1919-odyssey-murray.md#homer-1919-odyssey-murray-q008), *Odyssey* 8.344–66, the demand to release Ares becomes Poseidon’s own liability if Ares flees the debt, and Hephaestus releases the bonds. Surety gives another person’s obligation a bearer who can answer for it. Debt, guarantee and release thus change the relation through distinct acts. Comparing credit accorded with this undertaking follows their different practical offices; the Latin phrase is not a translation of the Greek exchange.

Each carries its own evidence, and the missing historical witness of the one cannot be borrowed from the other.

A deciding office admits evidence under a rule, exercises particular means of enforcement and gives its judgment consequence for an affected party. Arbitration, adjudication and governance differ in scale and authority. Joining or dividing those powers changes who can act, who must answer and where a challenge can land.

Attentiveness can become socially consequential without turning this lexical history into a single circuit followed by every speaker.

Through **Con-text-through-Diaphaneity**, a judgment becomes answerable to the field that bears it. Context includes the observer’s investment as well as what is observed. The Other exceeds the idea through which they have been received. Knowledge about someone and answerability to that person have different objects: an exhaustive description can still leave the person’s reply without consequence. Heidegger’s ontological inquiry and Levinas’s interpersonal demand therefore remain distinct at the point where the encounter tests the account.

The media, practices and institutions of [language, symbol and dialogue](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/HISTORY-language-symbol-dialogue.md) give address particular means. ## Anamnesis, recognition and a history which remains effective

A previous determination becomes an intelligible condition of the next one. Its trace and consequence remain available while its authority can be retained, narrowed or revised. ## Poetic memory and the return to the whole

The dramatic sequence—Māyā limits, perspective delineates, hybris absolutises, regard reopens, anamnesis returns—lets poetic memory hold the movement in one breath. Its compressed images retain their force through the developed relations they recall. Graphic or phonic re-entry acquires meaning through an earned relation; a resemblance of sounds alone supplies no common origin for the words.

An attested sense lets a word be used exactly; a dated act makes an institution’s consequence available; a philosophical comparison changes which relation can be understood; poetic memory lets that relation remain present through another utterance. In the [whole field](WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md), these differences return to witnessing, weighing, deciding and reconciliation without losing their respective warrants. The next judgment can now receive more of the source, person and history through which its criterion acquired authority.
