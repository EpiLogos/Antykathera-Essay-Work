# Whole Field — Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account

## #0 — Encounter before its completed account

<a id="what-holds-the-field-together"></a>
An encounter gives an account something to answer to. What arrives is already situated, yet the descriptions, names and measures available here do not decide everything it can disclose. A person can answer a question by changing what the question asks. A source can correct the category under which it was retrieved. A consequence can expose a condition omitted from the decision that produced it. Encounter names this relation before its possible reduction to the report of what happened.

The priority is operative, not a claim that people first met without language and only later invented it. An inherited name can make the meeting possible. Its use nevertheless takes place within a relation which can exceed and revise that use. [The with-relation](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-encounter-region-name-count/taylor-2026-encounter-region-name-count.md) is prior to treating its opposed terms as independently complete. Opposition remains real; it takes place within the facing relation it articulates.

The word-histories give this relation several distinct forms. *Encounter* comes through forms of meeting toward or against; numerical *count* through reckoning; *countenance* through holding and bearing; titled *count* through companionship and office. The [four histories](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#four-con-histories) carry different actions: facing against, reckoning together, holding oneself before another, and going with a companion. The common *with* is differentiated by what happens within it. Opposition turns a relation into counter-position; reckoning makes its terms repeatable; bearing gives it a personed appearance; accompaniment can become an entrusted office. Encounter holds the possibility of each, including hostility and refusal.

The six terms therefore grow as Encounter → Region → Name → Count ↔ Countenance → Account → Encounter. The return arrow is as necessary as the forward ones. A report becomes an inherited condition of another meeting: it directs attention, grants credibility, authorises action or forecloses a possibility. If its effects cannot reach back into its terms, an account of encounter becomes a means of preventing encounter.

<a id="canonical-operations"></a>
The four generated operations are **Encounter-in-Region**, **Name-through-Count**, **Count-through-Countenance**, and **Count-to-Account**. Each changes the relation between its terms: a meeting becomes situated, a name becomes addressable, a counted participant becomes able to answer, and a reckoning becomes accountable to the encounter it has organised.

## #1 — Region makes the meeting consequential

### Encounter-in-Region

A relation is situated before it is abstracted. Region holds the conditions through which this person, mark or event can appear as something and admit a response. Language, memory, bodily capacities, material arrangements and permissions are active in the meeting. Removing a permission changes which act is possible; changing a remembered source changes what can count as warranted. They compose the encounter, and an encounter described without them is missing parts of itself.

The [conditions of worldhood](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) constitute this availability. A [context frame](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) temporarily holds a selection of those conditions together, while what it excludes can still affect the act. A frame is useful because it lets an inquiry proceed and its conditions become readable. Its finite inventory does not exhaust the region in which it works.

The region branch forks between a directed line and a facing expanse. The Latin *regio/regere* history supports direction and delineation; the *country/contra* history retains what lies opposite. The [region's openness](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#region-direction-and-open-expanse) is the philosophical turn within these histories: what faces us can gather and abide within an expanse whose possibilities exceed a line drawn through it. A rule directs movement through this field and makes some of its relations repeatable. Rule is therefore one office within a relation; an account of the rule cannot substitute for the field it orders.

A [world-atlas](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) retains the consequences for representation. Different maps can disclose different relations, and an obstruction to translation can be a finding. Source, object, lens, gauge and permission give disagreements different conditions and different possible repairs; a corrected map or a reasoned retained relation need not resolve them into one picture. The relation also reverses: [the notational, nomological and exclusion-constituted world participates in producing the agent who models it](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md). Within a local comparison, the determining agent is the functional knower, its memory, language, rules and tools are means, and the differentiated situation is known. In a person's containing inquiry that whole apparatus is itself means. [Objective Internality](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) keeps these recursive offices within Life / Mind; inspecting the local centre makes it known in a further act without exchanging that object with the first-person condition of its appearing. The [inner instrument](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) differentiates determining, appropriating and coordinating within the relation. Changing available memory, language or permissions changes what becomes possible from within that world.

The region also has a history. A saved judgment enters what a later participant inherits, while that participant's action can change the available field. Across [co-internal worlds](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md), a return can revise a local map, change a relation between maps or change the rule of circulation. [These are distinct scopes of change](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C42-Objective-Co-Internality.md). In an [Agentworld](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C57-Agentworld.md), an exchanged message acquires a durable world-constituting office where its returned record or permission alters what can next be done. A shared container can hold those messages while leaving their participants without authority to correct how they are used.

## #2 — Name becomes addressable through distinction

### Name-through-Count

A name reaches something through an achieved distinction. Two people can bear one name; repeating the word does not individuate its bearers. One person can be addressed under different descriptions; multiplying descriptions does not automatically multiply persons. Count here includes the prior discrimination which makes a bearer available as this one. Naming makes that distinction portable and repeatable, while the region retains the circumstances in which it was made.

[Name, instance and circumstance](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md) form one identification. The instance is held as this very one through a difference of presentation; the name makes it articulable; the circumstance determines the respect in which another presentation can count as the same. Counting has constitutional priority here because a repeated sound cannot on its own distinguish its bearers. [Reckoning](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A15-Ratio-Rationality-Measure-Reckoning-Harmony-Account.md) makes that discrimination available for repeated comparison. [Determination](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C03-Determination.md) makes the distinction repeatable: something can recur as another instance while retaining the capacity and circumstances by which it is recognised.

Selection also gives the name a field of non-application. [Constitutive exclusion](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) participates in what a term makes available as its referent. In [apoha](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C18-Apoha.md), a thing qualified by preclusion differs from mere preclusion, and nominally bound exclusion differs from denying a proposition. The positive term has its reach through a contrast that participates in its meaning. Its account must retain the exclusion that actually constitutes this reach.

Through [vikalpa–saṃkalpa](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md), differentiated alternatives become a gathered course. A selection narrows the field and makes action possible. Its account must retain enough of the alternatives to explain why this course was formed and how it can be reconsidered. Storing every imaginable alternative is neither required nor possible; retaining a consequential excluded condition is a definite obligation.

The [advent of zero](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A10-Advent-of-Zero.md) changes addressability at the level of arithmetic permission. A place-mark, an arithmetic operand and a boundary expression have different offices. Giving an empty position a sign does not settle every operation in which zero appears. The admission of a mark changes what can be reckoned, but the account must say which operation changed. The name makes a sign addressable; the admitted rule determines what can be done with it.

The Chinese branch gives naming an adjacent limit. Laozi 25 names an otherwise unnamed way and places the ruler among four greats; 32 joins the arrival of names to a capacity to stop; 28 places formed vessels and offices beside a return to the unworked. [These distinct acts of naming, stopping and returning](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#chinese-facing-and-non-severing-return) give determination a source which its achieved form can retain. Naming becomes fruitful through its capacity to retain its source and limit.

[Enumeration, exclusion and recognition](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/indian-philosophy/DEVELOPMENT-indian-philosophy.md) give determinate reference different anatomies within Indian philosophy. Sāṃkhya’s functional triad differentiates the instrument from its witness; Pind’s qualified referent retains the positive work of preclusion; the Śaiva account makes finite capacities effective through contraction. Name-through-Count holds these differences together: identify what a term makes addressable and the contrast through which it applies before comparing its return. Enumeration, preclusion and contraction each explain a different way a determinate reference becomes possible.

A [repeated sound-name and its situated bearer](../../dossiers/indian-philosophy.md) belong to one address through a determining circumstance. In an I–you–it exchange, speaker and addressee change which relation a word enacts. The Vāk descent differentiates formative power, mental articulation and utterance: the spoken name is one determination of a fuller capacity to articulate. A returned determination retains the relevant excluded field and reaches the implicated answer, source, task, world-model, evaluator, permission or commission. Corrected reference under an adequate criterion differs from revising a criterion that fails; a fitting condition can be retained for reasons the encounter supplies. The next act inherits that warranted consequence. The same name can remain exact through a changed context, while a familiar name can require a revised account of whom or what it addresses.

[The reception of zero](../../dossiers/zero-reception.md) involves changes of permission within a receiving practice. An internal place-marker, an absent astronomical measure and an arithmetic operand can share a later name while doing different work. Name-through-Count receives the rule that makes this sign usable here; Count-to-Account receives the carrier and restricted inference by which another reader can check it. Nothaft’s critique of a modern reception myth changes the historical account without changing the independently established arithmetic. The revised historical account returns a changed understanding of the receiving practice.

Admission into an operative vocabulary changes what can become a subject of inquiry. In the [histories of zero and subjectivity](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/zero-subject-advent/DEVELOPMENT-zero-subject-advent.md), zero acquires specific arithmetic permissions, while disciplinary accounts make psychic processes available for investigation. These are different objects and different histories of admission. Their relation brings out a recurrent limit: the represented item does not take over the determining capacity through which it becomes addressable.

## #3 — Count makes a relation repeatable

Counting makes a difference usable across instances. It requires a unit or criterion, a field in which the unit applies, and a way of retaining what the result means. The same people can be counted by attendance, entitlement or affected consequence and yield different accounts. A town's electoral roll, its census and its list of households touched by a flood can each be arithmetically correct while a conclusion carries a figure from one criterion into another's work, as when the roll is quoted for how many people the flood affected. Exactness therefore includes the relation counted, not only the numeral obtained. Read through the signed arithmetic, a count is a cut `(−1)/(+1)` drawn across a population: those the criterion includes stand on one side, and those it leaves out stand on the other. The cut collapses by cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, when the excluded drop out of the result as if they had never been in the field. It collapses by appropriation when the counted are taken as the whole of the field: the figure holds `+2` and the uncounted, whose existence the figure denies, hold `−2`. This is the essay's reading and is Argued, and it is the reason the field gives a count its criterion and its excluded remainder.

[Six ways of reckoning](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#six-ways-of-counting) distinguish the acts of making countable: fitting into an order; straightening or ruling by a measure; pruning and selecting together; telling a succession; gathering and laying out; declaring together in enumeration. Each gives a count a different operation. Fitting gives each part a place through proportion or recurrence. Straightening supplies a line against which another instance can be compared. Pruning selects what will enter the count and leaves a consequential remainder. Telling preserves a succession which a total alone can discard. Gathering holds the differentiated terms together. Declaring-together makes the resulting relation available for another to receive and recount.

A count that includes only its finished numeral has lost these acts of making countable. In [the copular construction](../../../matheme/definition/copula.md), two primitive operations compose four distinguishable appearances; in [the harmonic recount](../../../matheme/definition/harmonics-and-recount.md), the appearances are held together with their manner of composition. `4+2` includes what makes the field countable within its count. The field's linguistic movement follows the same demand: the reckoning must be able to account for its own selections and relations.

A [present configuration and its actual trajectory](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics.md) disclose different aspects of the counted field. A census of present positions cannot replace the history of how those positions became operative. Equally, a narrative of arrival does not specify every current transition. An account needs the relevant two temporalities where the history has changed what is now possible.

[Dia-ballein](../../../../section-rooms/03-two-logics/movements/20-s2-p1-dia-ballein.md) makes the analytic cut productive: a count retains the criterion and excluded alternative through which it became exact. Its failure begins where the common operation disappears from the result. Through [syn-ballein](../../../../section-rooms/03-two-logics/movements/21-s2-p2-sym-ballein.md), `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the achieved branch carries the problem it answered and the wider state it changes into recomposition. The seam stays visible, and that is how a result becomes answerable. A fusion of counted and uncounted would remove the very differences that recomposition has to hold.

[Heidegger's Memorial Address](../../sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/heidegger/heidegger-1966-discourse-on-thinking/heidegger-1966-discourse-on-thinking.md) compares calculative and meditative thinking, both explicitly legitimate in their own offices. [His Logos essay](../../sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/heidegger/heidegger-1975-early-greek-thinking/heidegger-1975-early-greek-thinking.md) compares gathering as letting what is gathered lie together. Calculation can complete a determination; gathering returns that determination to the field in which its completion matters. The two acts become mutually consequential when the completed count can alter how the field is next approached.

## #4 — Countenance lets the counted answer

### Count-through-Countenance

Countenance returns the counted participant as address, face and bearing. The person described can answer the description from a situation which it has not enclosed. This is an operation within a shared field. [Being within Mono differs from being within my model](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md). Common reality enables encounter while conferring no possession of the other.

The historical *continentia* line concerns holding and bearing. The neighbouring [questions of formed appearance, face, presence and role](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#countenance-bearing-face-and-role) arise through *facies* and *prosōpon*, each with its own linguistic history. Their juxtaposition makes the philosophical pressure visible: an appearance can disclose someone without becoming the whole of that person's address. The title or mask under which someone is recognisable can organise a real meeting and still prevent their response from reaching the terms of recognition.

The `AM/IS` relation carries this operation through first-person presence, third-person sayability and the second-person slash through which they meet. A statement about someone can be exact; the one addressed can nevertheless change its use or expose what it omits. The second-person relation is active: a reply can change the question, and another's recognition can change how the speaker recognises themselves. Countenance is the personed form of context because the lived bearing and its sayable appearance become available to one another through address.

[Image and counter-image](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) develop this returning relation. The face given to another returns through a look, word, recollection or echo. The returned image can make an aspect of the self available that its first account missed. The source, its appearance and the other's response remain differentiated within the encounter; their circulation is what makes recognition possible.

[Deferential intelligence](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) makes address consequential for the model receiving it. An objection can change the answer, task, world-model, evaluator or commission; those are different scopes of revision. Choosing a better output under a fixed evaluation differs from [changing the representation or evaluator itself](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md). Deference remains discriminating: an unsupported objection can be rejected with reasons, while an established correction must be able to reach its actual cause.

An artificial agent becomes recognisable through forms of address which can include [anthropomorphization](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C58-Anthropomorphization.md). A recognisable voice, retained memory and changed conduct perform different offices in an encounter. The name and persona make the agent addressable; the history gives a later act something to inherit; conduct makes the relation consequential. [Maintained identification](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/conjugate/A02-prime-Continuity-as-Maintained-Identification.md) holds these differences through the actual arrangements by which a continuing agent is recognised and entrusted with work. Its powers, permissions and effects remain available to challenge by those who encounter it.

Count ↔ Countenance is therefore reciprocal. Counting permits someone to enter an account as a distinct participant; address tests whether the count and its criterion received what matters. Where address is refused, the signed arithmetic names the failure: the counter's position grows to `+2` and the counted person, reduced to an entry, sits at `−2` with no face from which to answer. Countenance is what lets the pair return to clean oscillation, `(−1)/(+1)`, in which each can correct the other. A missed participant can be admitted under a fitting rule; an inadequate criterion can be revised; a challenged condition can be retained for an answerable reason. The next reckoning inherits the relevant correction or justified retention. This restores the relation in which a count can be right and its application answerable without requiring every encounter to replace its criterion.

## #5→0 — Account returns its conditions to encounter

### Count-to-Account

Reckoning becomes an account when its criterion, provenance and consequence become available for challenge and revision. [Numerical count and account share a lexical history](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#native-field-and-source-preserving-return). An account also tells what the count means: which unit was selected, who or what was included, what was left out, and what follows from the result. A fluent explanation can still conceal which source, exclusion or permission actually caused a result. An account must preserve those distinctions if it is to return to their causes.

Within [MEF](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C39-Meta-Epistemic-Framework.md), different perspectives change questions, evidence or judgments while retaining their warrants. A [judgment field](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) makes the production of a determination inspectable. A source correction, an altered gauge and a revoked permission act at different sites. Relative scores, a distribution and a selected output likewise preserve different information; their differences are conditions of an exact account of selection. An explanation which substitutes the selected mark for the whole differential field has concealed part of what it must answer for.

[Operational parity](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity.md) requires a claimed functioning distinction to make a discriminable difference. A relevant comparison must be able to disclose its consequence. When the comparison fails, the result returns to the [implementation, mapping or test](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C45-Operational-Parity.md), with those failures distinguished. For a proposed implementation, the test must establish whether the returned difference changed the next operation. A record of the proposed test and evidence of its result have distinct offices in that account.

<a id="native-field-return"></a>
The [eight determinations](../../../matheme/ql/eight-determinations.md) give this return its full differential movement. The unassigned relating stroke permits a first distinction; conscious circumstance gives that distinction a lived field; question and assertion make it determinate enough to answer; withdrawal and extension give it direction; recurrent capacity becomes recognisable through an instance; personed context makes the account someone's; an unbounded field receives a local expression; and the expressed determination turns back toward its condition. Naming, counting, countenance and account each involve several of these operations. Their connection is active through the whole passage.

The inversion between naming and gathering now becomes exact. Naming selects a mark from its region. Gathering receives that mark together with the differences, exclusions and history by which it became meaningful. The first makes a determination available; the second makes that determination answerable to its source. In the QL traversal, the question/assertion of `#1` and the telling of `#5` are opposite faces of one differentiating act. Their return changes the next question rather than taking back the answer already earned.

The act of accounting belongs inside this movement. To say “one” introduces its saying into the field of the one being named. To count that saying makes a further determination. A final numeral cannot substitute for the activity through which the count continues. The [one-and-its-saying relation](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#chinese-facing-and-non-severing-return) therefore gives recounting a constitutive consequence: the account returns through recognition of its activity, not by pretending that one more counted item finally encloses the activity of counting. [The personed copula](../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C06-Copula-Identification-with-Difference.md) makes that return explicit when the equating itself is recognised as “what I am.”

[Idealism's order of dependence](../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/47-s50-p4-idealism-horizon.md) makes the consequence exact at the scale of a world-account. An increasingly complete representation remains an act within the whole relation it articulates. The account gains the power to disclose its condition and to act responsibly within it; completeness of representation does not reverse that dependence.

The circumstance which makes a repeated expression apply to this bearer is active in [language and dialogue](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-account-return). The later Wittgenstein locates use in practice; Lacan’s address retains speaker, room and audience. Their different approaches keep the situation of speaking consequential for what is said. Count-to-Account returns that determining situation with the telling: an addressee must remain able to correct the terms of the address.

In [mathematics](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/mathematics/DEVELOPMENT-mathematics.md), naming an absence differs from establishing the rules that admit an arithmetic participant. Subsequent calculations retain their domain, selected unit and forbidden inference. Count-to-Account returns those conditions to the next user, including the difference between an attention distribution and a selected index.

A recorded score governs action through [technical and political conditions](../../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#preference-training-and-delegation) which the numeral alone does not contain. Dataset, comparison class, evaluator and permission have different offices. Returning their provenance makes an output attributable; a subsequent objection must still be able to reach the office that can change its use.

An [attributed participant](../../dossiers/oi-technical-responsibility.md#oi-source-projection-contribution-route) can answer from a situation which exceeds their stored profile. Contribution, metric and Encounter retain contributor, basis and mediation. An attributed contribution establishes who supplied it; understanding and endorsement concern further acts whose presence must be established in their own terms. The next account must give a correction an address at the source, criterion or permission it challenges.

### Facing, naming and the memory of language

The [historical branches](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md) let the words carry different kinds of memory. A descent explains how a later form came from an earlier one. An attested use shows what an expression did in a particular setting. A shared operation lets independent histories illuminate one another; the English sounding proximity of count and countenance can make the relation audible. The field needs these differences because language bears both inherited form and the capacity to make a new relation speak.

The Chinese [鄉 witness](../../sources/classical-philology/chinese-university-hong-kong/cuhk-multifunction-chinese-character-database/cuhk-multifunction-chinese-character-database.md#cuhk-multifunction-chinese-character-database-q001) gives facing a graphic body: figures meet across a food vessel. The [wider character field](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#chinese-facing-and-non-severing-return) brings orientation, hospitality and response into the encounter reading. Region becomes thinkable as a relation made inhabitable. The shared vessel matters because those who face one another also meet through something that gathers their attention and action.

Naming can then be read from both ends. The Laozi passages bring an unnamed way into tentative designation, situate the ruler among the four greats, place vessels and office beside simplicity, and connect named action with a stopping limit. [The three selected chapters](HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-encounter-region-name-count.md#chinese-facing-and-non-severing-return) thus hold determination and its source in different relations. The returning account practises the field's non-severing order: a name or office can organise life while releasing the claim to own the expanse it orders. The achieved distinction remains; its source becomes available again.

### A carried account becomes a technological world

[Central](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/37-s5-p0-math-moves-meaning.md) carries meaningful continuity. A name, source or commission must remain identifiable through revision, and its revised account must reach what the next act inherits. Count-to-Account therefore supplies more than an archive: the history and criterion of an identification remain available to someone who can resume, correct or end the undertaking.

[Actuation](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax.md) makes the carried field consequential as an event. The comparison class, score, distribution and selected mark do different work within a judgment. J-Space's proposed account distinguishes source, representation, gauge and permission as possible causes of divergence. A returned challenge reaches the condition it exposes: an answer or source can be corrected under a fitting evaluator; a failing evaluator or permission calls for a different return. The warranted change or reasoned retention becomes a condition of the next judgment. For the stronger claim of changed processing, the comparison must show which operative condition changed and which later determination inherited it.

[AIKit](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/39-s5-p2-j-space.md) discloses potency as a changing horizon. What exists, what is available, what is relevant, what is permitted and what becomes operative are different countable relations. A catalogue that merges them can be numerically exact while misrepresenting the means a life can bring to bear. Encounter returns an altered account of fitness and availability to the horizon from which the next act selects.

[Software Factory](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero.md) receives that return as transformation. A gate asks whether a result meets a criterion; recognition distinguishes an implementation defect, a misread task, a failure in the criterion or commission, and a fitting condition that can be retained. Those are different depths of receiving an encounter. The count becomes accountable where those affected can contest its measure and the next build inherits the correction or justified continuation warranted by their answer. A stronger claim of paradigm transformation needs the actual change in the governing pattern and its later effect.

[Workcell](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields.md) keeps the account situated. A local reference, model or instruction is enacted through particular hosts, resources, permissions and resistances. A changed account must reach this material here. Another participant's reachability opens a possibility of encounter while leaving their authority over their own participation intact.

[Quaternal Logic](../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/42-s5-p5-research-vectors.md) relates these local wholes without consuming their differences. The terms remain able to answer from the relations that give them meaning. Seam retention, gauge disclosure, lens refraction, topological continuity, recursion and deferential return become specific research questions: does the difference actually change the operation which claims to receive it? Null results belong to the returned account as surely as confirmations.

<a id="return"></a>
### Return

An account enters the next encounter with the authority it has earned and the conditions under which it earned it. If a source changes, the corresponding conclusion must be available for revision; if someone contests their classification, the criterion must be reachable; if a consequence exposes an omitted relation, the field must admit it as a condition of the next act. Account is complete locally through this capacity to return, not through an assertion that nothing remains outside it.

[Number Six](../../../mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-account-answerability) arrives into a captured world whose names and counts are already operative. The Village runs the first two operations in full: it situates him in a region and makes him addressable as a number whose position repeats. It withholds the other two. Its recognition of him is an appropriation in the signed arithmetic, in which the administration's account holds `+2` and Six, held as a counted item, holds `−2`; and a reply that the Village files as another fact about the prisoner is a cancellation, since it adds nothing to the region.
