# Whole Field — Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account

**Standing:** mature T09 Etymology whole-field. The established directory name is retained for provenance; the canonical whole-field has grown beyond `encounter-region-name-count` to include **Countenance** and **Account**. `HISTORY.md` remains the historical/lexical evidence carrier.

## What holds the field together

This is not a claim that six English words descend from one hidden root. Their historical lines must remain individually evidenced. The whole-field is valuable because the words expose successive offices in the making of a knowable and governable relation:

```text
Encounter
   ↓
Region
   ↓
Name
   ↓
Count
   ↔
Countenance
   ↓
Account
```

**Encounter** is relation before it has been exhausted by the terms under which it will later be reported. **Region** is the situated field in which an encounter can take place and alternatives can remain active. **Name** makes a participant addressable. **Count** makes distinctions reckonable and repeatable. **Countenance** returns the counted participant as address, face, bearing or irreducible encounter rather than mere quantity. **Account** gathers what has been named and counted into a report, reckoning or intelligible relation capable of being trusted, challenged and revised.

The arrows are **relational form growth**, not asserted philological descent. The field can grow differently where history earns different branches.

## Canonical operations

### Encounter-in-Region

A relation is situated before it is abstracted. This operation is consumed wherever world/context is treated as constitutive rather than decorative: **A22, A26, A30** and **C07, C08, C41, C42, C57**.

### Name-through-Count

Naming makes a distinction available to reckoning, but reckoning selects from a wider region. This operation is consumed by **A08 Apoha**, **A10 Advent of Zero**, **A15 Ratio/Rationality**, **C03 Determination**, **C17 Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa** and **C18 Apoha**.

### Count-through-Countenance

The participant who has become countable can return as an Other capable of revising the count. This is not anti-quantitative; it is the refusal to let quantity exhaust address. It is consumed by **A27 Self and Other**, **A31 Deferential Intelligence**, **C47 Deferential Intelligence** and **C58 Anthropomorphization**.

### Count-to-Account

Reckoning becomes an account when the criterion, provenance and consequence of the count are made explicit. **A15**, **A33 Operational Parity**, **C39 MEF**, **C40 Model Internality** and **C45 Operational Parity** consume this operation.

## Evidence registers

- **1 Philological descent:** only for documented word histories in `HISTORY.md`.
- **2 Attested semantic field:** recorded senses of each word, without forcing one essence.
- **3 Operational homology:** all canonical cross-field operations listed above unless separately elevated by evidence.
- **4 Poetic / phonic re-entry:** explicit authorial returns such as the proximity of *count* and *countenance* may be philosophically generative but are never historical proof.

The count/countenance relation is particularly important here: its value survives even if no genealogical relation is asserted. It is an operational and possibly phonic re-entry whose warrant must stay in the correct register.

## Return

The field begins in encounter and reaches account, but the account is not terminal. A mature account returns to encounter. If the Other, region or consequence changes the model, the field begins another traversal. This recursive return is why the whole is an Etymology reasoning office rather than a glossary.
