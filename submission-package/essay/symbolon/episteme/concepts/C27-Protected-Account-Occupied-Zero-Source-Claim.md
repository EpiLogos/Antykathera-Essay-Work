# C27 — Protected Account / Occupied Zero / Source-Claim

## #0

A Protected Account is a determinate account whose governing criterion is insulated from encounters that could revise it. Occupied Zero names the QL office-confusion: a local `1` acts as uncounted source `0`. Source-Claim names the assertion, explicit or implicit, through which that occupation is justified.

## #1

An account initially makes something knowable and actionable. It selects a circumstance, names and counts, establishes a comparison, and authorises consequences. The result is a real achievement with a particular scope. Its source includes the relations, exclusions, labour and permissions through which it became authoritative; none disappears because the account is exact.

## #2

Protection changes the relation between error and criterion. Every discrepancy is charged to what is measured while the measure cannot itself be questioned. A complex can reorganise experience around its defended image; an institution can accept complaints only in categories that protect its mandate; a technical evaluator can alter the answer while remaining outside the permitted revision. These are distinct psychic, institutional and technical applications of the operation, not one empirical mechanism proved by shared vocabulary.

## #3

QH makes the technical distinction concrete. Source, contextual gauge, permission, evaluator and agent are different causal offices. A system can disclose reasoning or improve an output while the commissioning purpose and fixed evaluator remain untouched. C40 locates the judgment field; C45 asks which disclosed difference actually changes an operation. Narrated self-criticism alone does not establish that the relevant criterion has become revisable.

## #4

A stable standard differs from a protected account through its declared warrant, scope and return conditions. It need not change whenever challenged: evidence can vindicate it, and a bounded task can require consistency. Protection begins when no encountered resistance can reach the office authorised to reconsider those conditions. QH R16.4 names the returning material: error, cost, dissent, unforeseen possibility and consequences of labour must be able to change model, measure, instruction, institution or purpose.

## #5→0

C47 reopens the account through model-revising encounter; C28/C29 bind authority to a received relation and an accountable office. The result is a renewed determination with a visible history, not the abandonment of judgment. Trust revision §0 keeps the ethical fork live: a knowledge-account can provisionally support trust or export distrust onto another who bears its failures. An account returns when it answers for its own conditions as well as judging what comes before it.
