# C62 — Planetary Computation

**Definition.** Planetary Computation is computation understood as a world-scale material, institutional and ecological field: devices, networks, models, data, energy, labour, standards, logistics, states, corporations and users form infrastructures that alter the conditions of collective cognition and action.

The distinction exists because “AI” can be treated as a bounded model object while its real conditions and consequences spread across the planet. C62 returns computation to Objective Co-Internality, delegated labour and reflective-field dynamics.

Its operation is **distributed computation/labour → persistent infrastructural effects → altered cultural/world conditions → new computational inputs and agencies**.
