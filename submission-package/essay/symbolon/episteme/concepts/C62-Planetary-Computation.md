# C62 — Planetary Computation

## #0

Planetary Computation places computation within the material, institutional and ecological world that makes it possible and that its operations change. Hardware, energy, data, labour, standards, networks and public powers constitute this field together. The [direct developmental carrier](planetary-computation.md) preserves its first office as Antikythera's disciplinary horizon; the essay's reorientation of that horizon is Argued, and its QL/MEF contribution is Offered.

## #1

The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE|Agentworld brief]] begins within a programme for renewed philosophy of technology, then asks how human and artificial agencies alter sociality. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C57-Agentworld|Agentworld]] owns that encounter and its persistent local worlds. C62 keeps the conditions which exceed a single agent, session or platform in the account.

A completed computational output inherits more than the prompt that requested it. Its production depends on physical capacity, institutional permission and distributed work; its use can change whose work is required next and under what terms. These dependencies are the object of inquiry. No quantitative energy, labour or supply-chain claim is established merely by listing them.

## #2

The [[submission-package/essay/quilt/2026-08-02-PARALLEL-HARMONISED-QUILT#2.3.6 Aristotle, Gebser, and Epi-Logos|ratified causal articulation]] separates four questions. Material asks what the system consumes and is made from. Efficient asks which agents, incentives and deployment chains act. Formal asks which structures, protocols and rules organise action. Final asks what good the activity serves. The articulation is settled authorial architecture; exact Aristotle passages remain a separate source gate.

These offices prevent final purpose from becoming a slogan detached from production. A declared vocation must answer to the actual distribution of labour, permission, benefit and consequence. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C53-Power-Delegated-Labour|Power / Delegated Labour]] locates that test where a commissioning office receives products while costs are displaced down the chain.

## #3

Consequences re-enter the field. Changed infrastructure affects who can participate; a standard alters what can be exchanged; a model's categories reshape the records through which successors learn. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality|Objective Co-Internality]] specifies reciprocal constitution, and [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First|Reflective Field]] specifies the model's return into what it models.

The scale does not create a planetary Subject. It expands the field over which relations must remain legible. A highly connected system can coordinate correction or amplify a protected criterion. The number of participants does not determine which operation occurs.

## #4

The brief's q039–q041 poses infrastructure, learning and coupling as commons questions. Its scenarios establish a research pressure rather than the governance that would discharge it. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly|Commons / Non-Monopoly]] asks for common conditions in which plural local worlds retain authority, source and powers of refusal and revision.

The source-return must reach the institution holding the computational mirror. A platform cannot satisfy it solely by exposing user actions while keeping its own gauges and permissions beyond correction. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return|Delegated Labour / Return]] carries that asymmetry; [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First|The Mirror That Moves First]] carries the initiating obligation.
