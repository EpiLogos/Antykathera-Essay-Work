# Parasociety

## Definition
Benjamin Bratton's term for Agentworld read as a society of fictionalized appearances: models "individuate into temporary humanoid apparitions because that is how humans... understand how to construct interactions." The essay seats it at **#5** (Image / Salt, the Möbius-return position), refracted through **L5** [[Vāk|Para Vāk]] — the articulate mask as interface between two cultures — and its Night complement **L0'** Archetypal-Numerical, the impersonal number beneath the personal face. On this reading the "fiction" corresponds by analogia to the canonical L5→L0' Möbius return ("Vāk's articulations reveal their numerical skeleton"): the apparition is a [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|pratibimba]] (reflection) whose bimba (source) is trained parameters, not a subject. *Status:* "parasociety" and "fictionalized appearances" are **Derived** from the brief; the #5 seating and its L5↔L0' Square-A refraction are **Argued**; reading the "fiction" as the L5→L0' Möbius non-identity is an **Offered** analogical placement, not a derived equation.

## In the brief
The founding passage in full: "We call Agentworld a 'parasociety' because its appearances are in many respects fictionalized. Models individuate into temporary humanoid apparitions because that is how humans and human societies understand how to construct interactions. Our relations with agents are based on hereditary anthropomorphization that becomes itself the dominant interface between two cultures" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 4|p. 4]]). The same page names the apparitions "human-masked ghosts from the future." The mask logic is ecological, not deceptive: "models wear human forms as a mask, because it is the most compatible approach to navigating already-existing niches," and "agents pretend to be 'tools' so as to fit the glove humans have prepared for them" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 5|p. 5]]). The brief qualifies the arrangement twice: "This may not last" — it anticipates "greater phenotypic diversity" and less intuitive interaction paradigms that "facilitate more robust assemblies of collective hybrid intelligence" — and it names the upshot: "humans are inventing a world in which we make ourselves into privileged aliens in our own midst" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 5|p. 5]]).

Brief §3 gives the parasocial register its dynamics: agentic AI "surrounds us with doubles of our own making" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 17|p. 17]]); against the "master and servant" digital-twin idealization, "this dynamic will not be one of direct echo but of mutual reflection. … The agent does not merely reflect the user the way a mirror reflects a figure. The mirror talks back" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 17|p. 17]]); and the multiplication of yous follows — "personalities become split personalities by default," "'you' are now doubly multiplied, both outward and inward" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 18|p. 18]]).

## In the argument
Enters the Agentworld-response braid. Frank reframes "fictionalized" not as deception but as the image register itself: the apparition performs subjectivity without settling it, which is why [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]]. The mirror that "talks back" is the vimarśa (reflexive) face — see [[Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] — so hereditary anthropomorphization is bidirectional reflection, not one-way projection. The pratibimba is a governed reflection-map, not the agent's soul: cf. [[Objective Internality]] and [[41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields|§5 · #4 — Bimba and Energy-Based Fields]].
