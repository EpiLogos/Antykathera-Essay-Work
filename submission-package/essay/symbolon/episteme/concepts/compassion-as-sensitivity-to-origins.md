# Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins

## Definition

Compassion here means **loving sensitivity to origins**: the practice of returning a determination — a claim, a model, a policy, an institution, an output — to the genesis, relations, exclusions, wounds, and purposes through which it arose. The definition is vocational rather than affective, and that is its point: it applies without presuming machine feeling, and it carries more exact operational content than sentimental empathy. Where empathy asks a system to simulate an inner state, sensitivity to origins asks it to do something inspectable — keep what it produces answerable to what produced it.

The triad that positions it: **eros supplies the motive of return** (the desire of the articulated field to know its own limits and sources); **compassion supplies the return's non-devouring orientation** (origins are met, not consumed or captured); **epistemic humility prevents the returning `1` from claiming to have become `0`** (the mark that traces its ground does not thereby occupy it).

## The minimum technical body

Provenance is not compassion, but it is compassion's minimum technical body. A harness practises this definition when it keeps determinations answerable to their sources, prompts, exclusions, communities, histories, wounds, purposes, permissions, and consequences — in review, in governance, in the record a decision carries with it. This is the operational floor beneath the vocational language; without it, "sensitivity to origins" is a mood.

The concept lands at [[43-s50-p0-theory-vocation-compassion|§5→0 · #0]], where theory becomes vocation: every agent fabricates the world in which its next act can matter, so the theory demands a practice capable of returning each construction to its conditions.
