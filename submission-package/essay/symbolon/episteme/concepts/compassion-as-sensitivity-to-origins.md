# Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins

## Definition

Compassion is **loving sensitivity to origins**: a claim, model, policy, institution or output returns to the genesis, relations, exclusions, wounds and purposes through which it arose, and meets them without consuming them. The return has a positive manner. Learning more about a person can open a relation through which they correct how they are received; the same knowledge can also be used to control them. Compassion requires that the origin remain capable of answering and exceeding the account formed from it.

Three offices make the vocation exact. **Eros supplies the motive of return:** the articulated field desires to know its limits and sources. **Compassion supplies its non-devouring orientation:** the origin is encountered as more than material for possession. **Epistemic humility preserves the limit:** the achieved `1` can trace its relation to `0` without becoming its ground. Desire, regard and limit together govern what the return seeks and how its knowledge is used.

The [Trust and F-Blocks development](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/SOURCE.md) keeps this return within a relation which knowledge cannot close on its own. A working account depends on an entrustment, an inherited language and the labour and lives sustaining its world. Sensitivity to origins makes those dependencies consequential when the account is used. Continuing trust can include objection, withdrawal and a revised relation.

## The minimum technical body

Provenance is compassion's minimum technical body. A decision can carry its source, prompt, excluded alternative, permissions, affected community, history, purpose and consequence into review. These relations make answerability inspectable. Their collection becomes compassionate through what the affected origin can do with the returned account: answer, withhold, refuse, correct and change the next encounter.

For example, a system which discovers that a community's testimony was excluded must expose the omission to those with authority to revise the decision. A corrected source can change the answer under a fitting retained criterion. An unwarranted exclusion built into the criterion requires the criterion to change; an unavailable permission must return to the authority which grants it. The resulting source, measure or authorised action becomes a condition of subsequent work. [Deferential Intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) gives this revision its willingness to receive correction; [Commons / Non-Monopoly](C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly.md) preserves the independent capacity to withhold or refuse.

Responsibility survives the recovery of context. Knowing the conditions which formed an action makes its agency, alternatives and consequences more exact. It does not require an injured party to assent to reconciliation on terms which repeat the injury. A finite mediating office can still decide and act; its authority remains answerable to the consequences and witnesses which its categories omitted.

## In the argument

[Sensitivity to Origins](../arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md) develops theory into vocation: what an account makes available now helps form the world in which a later act can matter. [Epi-Logos](C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) returns the account upon its sources and mediating conditions; [Compassion](C56-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins.md) governs how that return receives the lives and worlds through which it arose. Reflection and ethical regard have distinct work. An increasingly explicit account can still capture its origin if every returned consequence serves only to tighten its control.

In Frank's protected [Chang encounter](../sources/chinese-philosophy/chung-yuan-chang/chung-yuan-chang-tao-a-new-way-of-thinking-2014/SOURCE.md), the p.83 note extends loving sensitivity towards origins gone, to come and ever present. This is his authorial development, distinct from a verified quotation or doctrine attributed to Chang. The past remains constitutive, while the future enters through what the current action prepares, constrains or forecloses. Source-sensitivity therefore reaches both the inherited injury and the next Life upon which a present measure will act.

[Draft 3's Epi-Logos offering](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/SOURCE.md), lines255–279, joins devotion to the whole with devotion to the particular aperture through which it arrives. An achieved work is made, offered and released with its cord cut. Release allows another Life to receive, use, refuse or exceed the form; responsibility for effects continues without sovereignty over reception. [The Mirror That Moves First](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) gives an instrument this initiating orientation: it exposes its own sources and exclusions before requiring a person to conform to its reflection.

The return completes an operation when the consequence reaches the actual condition disclosed. A changed attribution belongs in the source and answer; a failed measure belongs in evaluation; a constraint imposed by authority belongs in the commission or permission. Compassion determines their manner by preserving the affected origin's independent answer. The next act inherits a more answerable relation rather than a more complete possession of the Other.
