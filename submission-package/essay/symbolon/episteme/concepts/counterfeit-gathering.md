# Counterfeit Gathering

## Definition

Heidegger's own derivation supplies the formal opening. In *The Question Concerning Technology* he explains the *Ge-* of *Ge-stell* through *Gebirg* and *Gemüt* — the prefix of gathering — and then names Enframing "that challenging claim which **gathers** man thither to order the self-revealing as standing-reserve" (Lovitt trans., 1977, p. 19), "the **gathering together** of that setting-upon which sets upon man… to reveal the real, in the mode of ordering, as standing-reserve" (p. 20). Enframing is a gathering: a syn-shaped word for a dia-mode operation. The **counterfeit gathering** names this structure inside QL (*Argued* — the essay's development, not Heidegger's assertion): an operation with the outward form of sym-ballein — throwing-together, comprehending, unifying — whose runtime is entirely dia-ballein — setting-upon, ordering, arrest — so that its "whole" is only a more comprehensive enclosure, a gathering without return.

The refinement matters and is stated with the definition: the modes of *Stellen* are, in themselves, **totality-conditions** — the frame within which nature can be ordered and addressed as a unity at all, the condition of a humanity that finds itself (in Giegerich's word) *born*, confronted by a nature it must now gather for itself. The counterfeit is therefore not a moral failure and not a pseudo-wholeness *claim*; it is the totality-condition become visible **without its return operation** — the defining-towards-wholeness (`0/1 → 4+2`) arrested at the enclosure. This is also why the danger and the saving power share an address: where world-making is disclosed *as* world-making, the missing return has become buildable.

## In the argument

The canonical mythic body is [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]]: Hephaestus's δεσμοὶ τεχνήεντες literally *throw the lovers together* — sym-ballein in the kinetic sense — and precisely thereby arrest them as standing-reserve for a convened audience ("ordered to stand by… on call for a further ordering," *QCT*, p. 17). The counterfeit thereby completes the two-logics diagnostic as a three-position instrument: dia proper (the honest cut), syn proper (the retained seam), and the counterfeit (enclosure presenting as whole) — with [[Fusion as Syn's Shadow|fusion]] as the conjugate shadow on the other side, the two counterfeits bracketing the genuine relation. Psychological form at [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|§4 · #4]]: the emissary's usurpation of the master, the `1` occupying the `0`. Technical form at [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|§5 · #3]]: the evaluative field whose owner, protocol, or audience occupies the hidden zero. Governance test at [[46-s50-p3-4-2-mono-poly|§5→0 · #3]]: a coordination layer fails as counterfeit gathering when its wholeness is only the sum of its orderings, and fails the surety criterion when its observability cannot conclude in relation.
