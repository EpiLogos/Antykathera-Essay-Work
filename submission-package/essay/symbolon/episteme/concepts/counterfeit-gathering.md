# Counterfeit Gathering

## Definition

Heidegger's own derivation supplies the formal opening. In *The Question Concerning Technology* he explains the *Ge-* of *Ge-stell* through *Gebirg* and *Gemüt* — the prefix of gathering — and then names Enframing "that challenging claim which **gathers** man thither to order the self-revealing as standing-reserve" (Lovitt trans., 1977, p. 19), "the **gathering together** of that setting-upon which sets upon man… to reveal the real, in the mode of ordering, as standing-reserve" (p. 20). Enframing gathers by ordering the self-revealing as standing-reserve. The gathering word and the arrested return meet in one operation. The **counterfeit gathering** names this structure inside QL (the essay's development of Heidegger's ordering account): an operation with the outward form of sym-ballein — throwing-together, comprehending, unifying — whose ordering holds differentiated terms available for further ordering while excluding their return upon the arrangement itself. Its "whole" is then a more comprehensive enclosure, a gathering without return. A real cut, rule or ordering does not by itself make this failure; the failure occurs where the common source and a consequence capable of reaching it disappear from the account.

The modes of *Stellen* are, in themselves, **totality-conditions** — the frame within which nature can be ordered and addressed as a unity at all, the condition of a humanity that finds itself (in Giegerich's word) *born*, confronted by a nature it must now gather for itself. The counterfeit is therefore not a moral failure and not a pseudo-wholeness *claim*; it is the totality-condition become visible **without its return operation** — the defining-towards-wholeness (`0/1 → 4+2`) arrested at the enclosure. This is also why the danger and the saving power share an address: where world-making is disclosed *as* world-making, the missing return has become buildable.

## In the argument

Hephaestus's δεσμοὶ τεχνήεντες [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|bind Ares and Aphrodite]] after they lie together; the fine forged bonds prevent movement and escape, and the convened gods become spectators of their exposure. Taylor compares the held availability with "ordered to stand by… on call for a further ordering" (*QCT*, p. 17): the technological reading concerns the captivity and its arranged audience, not a Homeric theory of standing-reserve. Poseidon's promise to bear the debt reaches the maker and obtains release where exposure and account alone do not. The two-logics diagnostic therefore distinguishes a real differentiating cut, a [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|gathering that retains its seam]], and an enclosure whose order suppresses return. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/fusion-as-syns-shadow|Fusion]] loses the differences a gathering would relate. The [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|emissary's usurpation]] makes a local `1` occupy the `0`; the [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|evaluative field]] hides the owner, rule or audience that governs the judgment. A [[46-s50-p3-4-2-mono-poly|coordination layer]] remains counterfeit where its members can only comply with the orderings. Surety becomes consequential when a person can stand for another at risk and the responsible office can receive that relation, answer it and release the capture.
