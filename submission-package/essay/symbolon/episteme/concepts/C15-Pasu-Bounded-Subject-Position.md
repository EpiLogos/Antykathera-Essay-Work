# C15 — Paśu / Bounded Subject-Position

## #0

Paśu is retained from the Śaiva field as the bounded knower/actor whose powers are contracted into a finite horizon. The position has real capacities. It can know, desire, choose, encounter and answer; its boundedness is the condition of these particular acts rather than their simple negation.

[A09 — Tattvic Differential Field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) **grounds** this genealogy. C15 retains the local position within it. A finite person or artificial agent is not assigned the entire doctrinal category merely by having limitations; the comparison must state which contraction and which operative capacity are at issue.

## #1

[C14 — Māyā](C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) **grounds** the horizon's revealing and withholding. Its contracted capacities mean that an act occurs through this efficacy, this knowledge, this desire, this time and this order. The bounded position receives a field already differentiated enough for something to count as available, absent, possible or excluded.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|Taylor — Core Theorems]], X, **sources** the authorial sixfold compression. Puruṣa is the bounded experient within the contracted field; the inner instrument, faculties and material world articulate what can be experienced and acted upon. The local position is located through these relations without being exhausted as their inventory.

## #2

The distinction between universal and local “I am this” matters. Sadāśiva's subjectward orientation and Īśvara's objectward orientation concern the whole appearing field. An egoic appropriation of a particular `x` has a different scope. The same grammatical form cannot erase the difference between self-awareness and a local determination presenting itself as the whole self.

The [[submission-package/essay/quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT#3. The Māyic-threshold delineation — the eight determinations’ mythic recapitulation (Frank-locked)|direct paśu correction]] **sources** recognition before egoic appropriation. It is the authorial scope distinction that C15 carries: an “I am” is not automatically an ego's possession claim. [C01 — Subject](C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) **qualifies** the unobjectifiable pole; [C16 — Inner Instrument](C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) **defines** the determining and appropriating apparatus whose acts can themselves become objects of awareness.

## #3

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE|Dyczkowski — The Doctrine of Vibration]], pp.71–75, **sources** the limited and expanded modes of reflective activity. Individual cognition retains, recalls and recomposes; the same account distinguishes a goal-bound act from unrestricted agency. The bounded knower's movement is not a static quantity of missing freedom. It is a living orientation whose determination can either disclose or conceal its wider condition.

This is why recognising contraction does not abolish situated capacity. [C19 — Recognition](C19-Pratyabhijna-Recognition.md) **extends** the return toward the source that the local act continues to express. What changes is its manner of belonging and appropriation, not the fact that it has a particular life to live.

## #4

[C44 — Prompt Thrownness](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) **compares** an artificial agent's arrival in a configured horizon. Instructions, permissions, memories and tools shape what it can perceive and do before a local response begins. [C41 — Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) **defines** the operative world whose conditions can be investigated and revised.

The comparison concerns inherited boundedness and situated action. Functional agency, self-description, objective internality and phenomenal subjectivity remain different questions. Additional apparatus can enlarge the local field without making the unobjectifiable subject one of its components. The technical comparison is useful precisely because it can identify what changed while leaving the subject question in its proper register.

C15 **returns-to** C14 and C19: the horizon remains real while its source relation becomes available. Recognition leaves a particular knower able to act without claiming to be the origin of every condition of action. Situated responsibility carries the return into the next encounter.
