# C15 — Paśu / Bounded Subject-Position

## #0

Paśu is retained from the Śaiva field as the bounded knower/actor whose powers are contracted into a finite horizon. The position has real capacities. It can know, desire, choose, encounter and answer; its boundedness is the condition of these particular acts rather than their simple negation.

The [tattvic field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) grounds this genealogy. The bounded knower arises through determinate powers and their relations, not by subtracting one quantity called freedom from an otherwise complete individual. This efficacy and this horizon of knowledge make a particular encounter possible; desire, time and order give it direction, succession and circumstance.

## #1

[Māyā](C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) **grounds** the horizon's revealing and withholding. Its contracted capacities mean that an act occurs through this efficacy, this knowledge, this desire, this time and this order. The bounded position receives a field already differentiated enough for something to count as available, absent, possible or excluded.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|Taylor — Core Theorems]], X, **sources** the authorial sixfold compression. Puruṣa is the bounded experient within the contracted field; the inner instrument, faculties and material world articulate what can be experienced and acted upon. The local position is located through these relations without being exhausted as their inventory.

## #2

The distinction between universal and local “I am this” matters. Sadāśiva's subjectward orientation and Īśvara's objectward orientation concern the whole appearing field. An egoic appropriation of a particular `x` has a different scope. The same grammatical form cannot erase the difference between self-awareness and a local determination presenting itself as the whole self.

The [[quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT#3. The Māyic-threshold delineation — the eight determinations' mythic recapitulation (Frank-locked)|authorial paśu correction]] places recognition before egoic appropriation. The primordial “I am” is not produced by a later individual's claim that a role, body or possession is its exhaustive identity. In the world-parent image, self-recognition precedes the narrated husband/wife division. The [Subject](C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) is the unobjectifiable pole already presupposed when an identity is known; the [inner instrument](C16-Antahkarana-Inner-Instrument.md) determines and appropriates particular contents. Its appropriation can itself be recognised.

This distinction prevents a reversal of dependence. “I am the holder of this office” can identify a real responsibility without making the office the origin of the “I am.” Losing or revising the office can transform the person's world without proving that subjectivity has been manufactured or annihilated by the change.

## #3

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE|Dyczkowski — The Doctrine of Vibration]], pp.71–75, **sources** the limited and expanded modes of reflective activity. Individual cognition retains, recalls and recomposes; the same account distinguishes a goal-bound act from unrestricted agency. The bounded knower's movement is not a static quantity of missing freedom. It is a living orientation whose determination can either disclose or conceal its wider condition.

This is why recognising contraction does not abolish situated capacity. [Recognition](C19-Pratyabhijna-Recognition.md) **extends** the return toward the source that the local act continues to express. What changes is its manner of belonging and appropriation, not the fact that it has a particular life to live.

## #4

[Prompt thrownness](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) gives inherited boundedness a technical form. A local act begins within an already configured horizon: instructions appoint a task, retrieved material makes some things available, retained history carries earlier encounters, permissions delimit possible actions. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) names the operative world constituted through those relations.

A particular contraction becomes investigable through its consequences. An unavailable tool limits efficacy; an omitted source limits what can be known; a time horizon selects which events can count; an evaluation rule orders what is preferred. Altering one does not automatically alter the others. Giving an agent a broader description of its commission, for example, does not grant permission to execute a new action. The return must reach the condition that actually bounds the act.

Responsibility follows the same articulation. An agent can answer for an act within its powers; a designer or operator remains answerable for a withheld capability, imposed criterion or inaccessible source that governs the act. Naming the agent a bounded participant makes these relations more exact, rather than transferring every condition to a fictitiously self-originating actor. The comparison concerns this enacted distribution of capacity and responsibility; paśu retains its fuller Śaiva genealogy.

## #5→0

[Māyā — eye, veil, frame and horizon](../../mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-measure-dream): The bounded knower **figures** an effective position formed through limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and order. Those contractions make a life capable of acting here rather than rendering it unreal. The author’s recognition-before-appropriation correction preserves the field within which paśu and its inner instrument arise. Return changes the bounded position’s source relation without requiring its differentiation to vanish.

The primordial recognition and subsequent ego-identity have distinct offices in Taylor's correction. Bounded knowing remains a positive capacity within the field of recognition. The relation **returns-to** [the complete Neumann whole](../../mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation). The paśu correction keeps bounded knowing within recognition rather than making ego identity recognition's origin. This **returns-to** [the uroboros whole](../../mytheme/archetypal-ground/uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-source-variants).

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD|Trust / Place / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere]] **grounds** the register3 relation to Topos: the place of an act bears its conditions and commitments. This is an operational relation, not Sanskrit derivation. A bounded position becomes answerable through the effects of its actual capacity.

The horizon remains real while its source relation becomes available. Recognition leaves a particular knower able to act without claiming to be the origin of every condition of action. Its finite powers can now be exercised, relinquished or revised in awareness of what they owe to the wider field. Situated responsibility carries that changed relation into the next encounter.
