# C42 — Objective Co-Internality

**Definition.** Objective Co-Internality is the objective reciprocal condition in which multiple operative interiors are partly constituted through a shared world and through one another's actions, records, language, tools, expectations and permissions, while remaining non-identical.

The distinction exists because distributed agency is more than communication between pre-formed sealed agents. A shared document, norm, model state or environment can become part of each participant's operative interior. Yet this causal/semantic co-constitution does not establish collective phenomenality.

Its operation is **shared world → reciprocal conditioning of local interiors → changed future action-space**.
