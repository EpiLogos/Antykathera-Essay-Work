# The Slash (AND/OR)

## Definition

The stroke between numerator and denominator, with its own determination form **`/ = −/−`**. It works double (*Argued*): arithmetically as division, fraction, ratio, or quotient; logically as **AND/OR**—a conjunction that does not erase disjunction and a disjunction that does not deny co-presence. In `0/1`, the slash conjoins ground and mark while keeping them distinct; by the same AND/OR it generates the obverse `1/0`. QL therefore writes `0/1 = 1/0` in its relational register: one relation read from its two ends. Ordinary arithmetic retains the difference between the defined fraction and division by zero; that exact difference is the seam through which the matheme reaches mathematics' own limit and metaphysical ground.

Placed here (*Argued*) at the **P2 dynamis**—canon's `−/+` differentiating operator—because the slash is the operator *par excellence*, the stroke common to all six determinations (`0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx`). The Śaiva gloss (*Offered*) makes it **pramāṇa**, the means, between numerator (*pramātṛ*, knower) and denominator (*prameya*, known).

## In the argument

The slash is the operative core of [[Sym-Ballein]]: in `(0/1)/(1/0)` each inner stroke holds indefinite and definite together, and the outer stroke relates the relation to its own inversion—[[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|§2 · #2 — Sym-Ballein]]. Because the mark *retains its crossing*, return is transformation rather than annihilation—[[27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex|§3 · #2 — Re-entry]]. The same stroke is the quotient folding `ℝ²` into the torus—[[30-s3-p5-arche-topos|§3 · #5→0 — Arche-Topos]] (*Argued*).

Dia-ballein shows what happens when the parent stroke is specialised or lost. Polar `(-1)/(+1)` retains zero as the axis of tension and connection. Collapsed `(-1)+/-(+1)` cancels the two ones to `0` or lets one pole appropriate their span as `-2` or `+2`. Sym-ballein does not discard dia's difference; it carries the two ones and their own two readings, yielding `2+2² = 4+2`, and returns them through `5→0`.

The slash also has a lived, comportmental form (*Argued*): Heidegger's *Gelassenheit* is the AND/OR practised toward one's own instruments — "We can affirm the unavoidable use of technical devices, and also deny them the right to dominate us," a comportment "which expresses 'yes' and at the same time 'no'" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 54). Simultaneous affirmation and denial without contradiction, because the two operate at different levels of the relation: use conjoined, domination disjoined. Releasement is the slash worn as a stance — which is how [[45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement|§5→0 · #2]] carries it into the politics of technical life.
