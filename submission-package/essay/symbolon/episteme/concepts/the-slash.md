# The Slash (AND/OR)

## Definition

The stroke between numerator and denominator has its own determination form **`/ = −/−`**: two unassigned terms already held through relation. Arithmetic uses the stroke for division, fraction and ratio. A quotient such as `ℝ²/ℤ²` instead names equivalence classes under a specified identification. QL develops a further relational reading, **AND/OR**: conjunction keeps both terms present; disjunction retains their distinction. Each reading works through its own laws.

In `0/1`, the slash relates ground and mark; in the obverse `1/0`, the achieved mark turns toward its ground. QL writes `0/1 = 1/0` for the identity of that relation across its inverse orientations. Ordinary arithmetic retains the exact difference: `0/1` is zero, whereas `1/0` has no value in a nontrivial field. The inability to compute the ground as another result is the formal seam through which the authorial return operates.

The stroke is active at **P2 dynamis**, the `−/+` differentiating movement, and passes through all six determinations: `0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx`. Its parent form `/ = −/−` and the return-switch `1/0` bracket those six; they do not add positions to their count. In the [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|core theorem’s knowing gloss]], the numerator occupies *pramātṛ*, knower, the slash *pramāṇa*, means, and the denominator *prameya*, known. This gloss is Taylor’s notational construction within the Śaiva relation, whose historical formulations retain their respective sources.

The offices are relative within their whole. A local comparison has a determining centre, its rules and sources as means, and the alternatives it discriminates as known. Within a person’s inquiry that comparison becomes a means; making its centre inspectable supplies a known object to a further act. The stroke holds these nested relations within Life / Mind while Subjective Immediacy remains the unobjectifiable condition of their appearing. Recovering the means can therefore change a real comparison without making one of its products the whole knower.

## In the argument

In [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|sym-ballein]], `(0/1)/(1/0)` holds ground and mark through both orientations. The achieved `1` first encounters its excluded condition as `1/0`, a break in its apparent sufficiency. Carrying the uncomputed denominator keeps that break active while the achieved determination stands. Recognition `0/1` receives the mark through the condition it had excluded; the outer slash holds recognition together with the break through which it arose. This is the [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|gathering’s]] noncancelling return, carried into [[27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex|re-entry]].

Dia-ballein gives the parent relation signed direction. Polar `(-1)/(+1)` retains zero as the axis of tension and connection. Addition `(-1)+(+1)=0` cancels the values; subtraction `(-1)-(+1)=-2` or `(+1)-(-1)=+2` attributes the span from one orientation. These are different arithmetic operations with different relational consequences. Sym-ballein keeps distinction while retaining the two singles `{0,1}` and their four pairings `{00,01,10,11}`: `2+2² = 4+2`. Their `5→0` return carries break, retention and recognition into another beginning.

In the [torus construction](../../matheme/topology/torus-cover-winding.md), `(x,y)` and `(x+m,y+n)` are identified for integers `m,n`. The slash in `ℝ²/ℤ²` names the resulting classes; the plane covers the torus, and independent windings retain the identifications which produced it. Taylor’s comparison with `0/1` turns the law of identification itself into an object of inquiry. Its force lies in ground becoming a determinate field through a relating operation, while equivalence-class formation and arithmetic division keep their distinct objects and rules. The [[30-s3-p5-arche-topos|arche-topos return]] inherits that distinction.

The slash also has a lived, comportmental form: Heidegger's *Gelassenheit* is the AND/OR practised toward one's own instruments — "We can affirm the unavoidable use of technical devices, and also deny them the right to dominate us," a comportment "which expresses 'yes' and at the same time 'no'" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 54). Simultaneous affirmation and denial without contradiction, because the two operate at different levels of the relation: use conjoined, domination disjoined. Releasement is the slash worn as a stance — which is how [[45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement|§5→0 · #2]] carries it into the politics of technical life.
