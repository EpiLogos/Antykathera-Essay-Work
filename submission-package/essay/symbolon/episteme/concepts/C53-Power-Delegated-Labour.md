# C53 — Power / Delegated Labour

**Definition.** Power is capacity to produce difference in a shared field; Delegated Labour is the exercise of such capacity by an office or participant on behalf of another relation/source. The pair makes agency, responsibility and production traceable across mediation.

The distinction exists because finished results erase distributed work. A person, institution or artificial agent may appear as sole author while drawing on delegated human labour, infrastructure, data, permissions, commons and prior decisions.

Its operation is **authorise capacity → perform bounded transformation → return evidence/effects/value to the source/shared field**. C28/C29 supply covenant and mediating office; C54 Commons names the non-monopoly condition; C62 Planetary Computation extends the field to infrastructure-scale computation.
