# C32 — Archetype

**Definition.** Archetype names a generative pattern of possible form that becomes knowable through images, affects, motifs and recurrent organisations rather than as a directly inspectable content-object. The canonical concept retains the Jungian office while remaining open to carefully bounded cross-cultural comparison.

The distinction exists to separate the generative pattern from any one image or complex. An archetypal image is determinate; the archetype is inferred through the family and dynamics of its manifestations. Treating one image as the archetype repeats the living-symbol/idol error.

Its operation is **pattern-through-variable manifestation**. C33 Image/Valuation names a manifest carrier; C31 Complex names a local psychic regime; C30 Psychoid Number asks whether some archetypal/formal organisation can bridge psychic and physical descriptions; C61 routes the evidence without merging registers.

Nearest neighbours are C30, C31, C33 and C36 Complexio Oppositorum.

**Argument consumers:** A16, A19–A21, A32, A35. **Movement consumers:** Jung/Pauli, complex, individuation and Mytheme movements.

**Etymology relation:** *Homologia / Analogia*; consumed operation: multiple manifestations may be related by operational/focal analogy without being declared identical. Register **3**. Return C32 → C33/A20 → C34/A21 → C32.

**Source/tradition standing:** Jungian archetype theory primary. Cross-cultural myth and formal comparison require independent evidence; resemblance alone does not establish universality.

**Carrier disposition:** CREATE as canonical C32; older archetype/Mytheme notes remain radial-depth provenance rather than atomised replacements.
