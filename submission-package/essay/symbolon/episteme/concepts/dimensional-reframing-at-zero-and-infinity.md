# Dimensional Reframing at Zero and Infinity

## The method proposition

When a relation is obstructed in its present representational space, mathematics preserves it by transforming the space — enlarging, folding, identifying, or re-situating its field. Complex extension, projective completion, compactification, quotienting, covering, and differential limiting are genuinely different operations, but each makes a prior impossibility legible without pretending the obstruction never existed. Zero and infinity are the recurrent pressure-points at which the current space discloses its limit: **the return to zero is where a system declares its law.** This is the mathematical form of the immutable gap — not an occult elsewhere, but the non-eliminable edge at which every determinate account reveals what it presupposes.

## Case ledger

Each case names its operation and the invariant it preserves; each is Derived only within its own definitions.

- **Signed line → complex plane.** The equation `x² = −1` has no home on one signed line; adjoining an orthogonal dimension makes `i² = −1` representable as quarter-turn rotation. Opposition becomes phase.
- **Affine line → projective line.** Parallel directions never meet in affine space; homogeneous coordinates adjoin ideal points where they do. Infinity becomes a *position*, not an oversized number, and cross-ratio survives every projective transformation.
- **Complex plane → Riemann sphere.** Stereographic projection compactifies the unbounded plane by one point at infinity; `CP¹` gives the completion its exact form, and division's runaway values acquire a pole rather than a breakdown.
- **Covering plane → quotient surface.** `T² = R²/Z²`: identification (an equivalence relation, not arithmetic division) folds an infinite cover into a compact surface while every surface-point retains its infinite lattice of pre-images. Infinity and zero are joined by identification rather than cancellation.
- **Inexhaustible field → local change.** `∞/dx`: differential and limit procedures relate what no finite mark contains to exact local determination — precision without possession.
- **Division by zero → division pluralisms.** Ordinary fields leave `1/0` undefined; the projective line adjoins one point at infinity; wheels, meadows, and transreal arithmetics preserve different operations by accepting different consequences. The plurality is the finding: an exceptional operation discloses which structure a formalism has committed to preserve. Bhāskara II's ***khahara*** — his term for the quantity with zero divisor, compared in his text to the unchanging infinite — is the historical witness at this edge (exact wording remains verification work; the concept-load stands).

## Status boundary

The cross-case proposition — mathematics often preserves an obstructed relation by changing its space — is **Argued**; it does not follow from any single case. "Zero generates dimensionality" is too broad and never appears without a named construction. QL's `1/0` is an oriented meta-sign, not ordinary division: the essay treats the affine obstruction as philosophically exact, never as licence to assign an illicit value.
