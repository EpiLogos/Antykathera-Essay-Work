# C45 — Operational Parity

## #0

Operational Parity applies when software claims to instantiate a philosophical or formal distinction. The claimed difference must have an identifiable operational locus and a consequence that inspection or a meaningful test can discriminate. A philosophical distinction can remain valid without being encoded; parity governs the technical promotion.

The restored paradigm language gives this criterion a new load-bearing case. If a system claims not merely to *represent* its paradigm but to make that paradigm inspectable and refinable, the claim must identify **which enacted mediating relation can be changed through the representation and what later operation changes with it**. A map that is accurate but causally disconnected from the field it maps may be valuable; it has not yet established paradigm-level Epi-Logos.

## #1

Name the operation carrying the distinction: retrieval, storage, routing, permission, exclusion retention, evaluator, collaboration or return. A new label on an unchanged process supplies no such locus. The process test in A33 requires an inherited field, a selected branch, a stabilising rule, retained counter-reading, changed future conditions and return into revisable data.

For a paradigm claim, add the relation between **explicit articulation and enactment**. What does the representation point to in the live field? Is that relation attributable? Can a relevant feature of the enactment be changed through the articulated object? Does the changed field then alter what is retrieved, permitted, judged, expressed, built or materially executed? If no such path exists, the representation may disclose the paradigm to a reader while remaining operationally external to the paradigm it describes.

## #2

A comparison must preserve the relevant conditions while testing the claimed difference. The mature Deferential Intelligence carrier proposes consequential decisions with and without disclosure of origin, aperture, exclusions, confidence, counterclaim and undecidable remainder. The outcome concerns delegation, correction and answerability, not the frequency of humility words. This is a proposed real-function comparison, not a result already demonstrated.

The same logic applies to Epi-Logos. Compare an explicit paradigm representation that is **read-only** with one whose authorised changes can revise a governing relation—source, permission, evaluator, composition, material binding or containing frame—while task and other relevant conditions are held as stable as the test permits. The relevant result is not that users report the second interface as “more transparent.” It is whether a returned distinction produces a traceable change in subsequent operation and whether that change can itself be inspected and contested.

## #3

The metric's own source and gauge belong inside the claim. A test can detect compliance with a stated criterion while the criterion excludes the affected reality it purports to serve. A24's arbiter has a jurisdiction and an inherited measure; that measure must remain open to regard and return. Exposing it is different from certifying the metric as the whole.

A paradigm test therefore cannot be graded solely by the paradigm's own unchanged evaluator. If the claim concerns the evaluator becoming revisable, success cannot mean only “the evaluator approved the revision.” The test needs an externalised before/after operation and a route by which affected source, user, collaborator or material consequence can contest the change. Co-internality makes the test relational; it does not imply a neutral view from nowhere.

## #4

A15's exact arithmetic supplies the discipline for technical promotion: a sum, quotient and ratio are different operations even where the same numbers recur. Operational parity applies the same demand to philosophy and code. If a formal or philosophical distinction is said to motivate a feature, identify the technical operation corresponding to that distinction and the consequence by which its presence or absence can be discriminated. A successful implementation check establishes that stated operational consequence. The metaphysical argument retains its own warrant; the implementation neither borrows nor cancels it.

The six-product field gives paradigm-level parity concrete loci. [Central](../products/S0-Central.md): did meaningful ground/source change? [Actuation](../products/S1-Actuation.md): did the judgment or evaluator relation change? [AIKit](../products/S2-AIKit.md): did availability, relevance, permission or selection change? [Factory](../products/S3-Software-Factory.md): did evidence precipitate into changed developmental form? [Workcell](../products/S4-Workcell.md): did the material world, binding or observed consequence change? [QL](../products/S5-Quaternal-Logic.md): did the relation among frames or the containing reading change? A claimed “paradigm update” must name its locus rather than invoke the whole sixfold rhetorically.

## #5→0

Operational Parity **returns-to** [Count-to-Account](../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#count-to-account) where the named distinction has a testable locus in retrieval, retention, evaluation or action. The result must distinguish an implementation error, an inadequate operational mapping and a test that cannot discriminate the claim. Keeping those outcomes separate allows revision without making either technical success or failure a blanket verdict on the philosophical field.

A failure returns to a named claim: the implementation may be wrong, the operational mapping inadequate, or the proposed test unable to discriminate the distinction. Those are different findings. C46 preserves their provenance in the next revision; C47 lets encounter alter the governing model or paradigmatic condition. Parity succeeds as a discipline when its own criterion can be revised without erasing what the prior test actually showed.

For the Expression programme, the negative test is especially useful. Rendering the paradigm as glyph, text, image, field or transition establishes **expression**. It establishes **Epi-Logos** only where manipulating or contesting that expression can return to the governed structure and where the changed structure changes a later act. Conversely, a non-visual configuration change can satisfy the operational return without satisfying the expressive ambition. The two accomplishments should be measured separately before they are joined.
