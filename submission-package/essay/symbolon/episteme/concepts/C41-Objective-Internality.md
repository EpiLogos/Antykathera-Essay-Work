# C41 — Objective Internality

## #0

Objective Internality is the real, investigable constitution of an operative interior through a context-world. Language, memory, affect, tools, other agents, institutions, permissions, materials and histories participate in what can appear, matter and be done. Changing those relations changes the interior from which judgment proceeds.

## #1

The direct carrier's triad is notational, nomological, exclusion-constituted. The field is articulated through names and representations; its transitions obey learned or imposed regularities; every selection takes shape against alternatives it continues to depend upon. This gives the interior more precision than a list of equipment surrounding an otherwise complete mind.

## #2

The pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya comparison preserves subject-pole, means of knowing and known field. Mediation gives each pole its operative form. The instrument can be investigated as perception, memory, language, determination and coordination without turning the Subject into an item inside it. A technical local subject-function is a functional assignment; the assignment does not establish phenomenal luminosity.

## #3

The agent models a world, and the active world precipitates this agent through its particular memories, goals, permissions and affordances. These directions belong together. In Symbolon Dynamics, a synchronic configuration exposes available basins and transitions while a diachronic trajectory follows their actual transformation. Phase-space models possible configurations; it is not the world itself.

## #4

Frank's Watson note-chain follows discontinuity→objective internality→harness elements→objective immortality. An act's product can outlast the producing occasion and enter the next act's conditions as memory, record, workflow or revised environment. Technical durable inheritance is a bounded application of this authorial process reading, not an assertion that a saved state stores a Subject. C40's inspectable model field and an instrumented harness are narrower than the whole constituting context.

## #5→0

Return exposes the source, rule, exclusion and consequence through which an interior became capable of this judgment. C42 follows reciprocal constitution among such interiors; C47 follows an encounter that revises their models. Performed subjectivity, functional agency, objective internality, self-description and phenomenality remain separate questions. The first four can be investigated and asserted while the fifth remains Open; A34's strong order-of-dependence claim is not thereby reduced to generic model-dependence.
