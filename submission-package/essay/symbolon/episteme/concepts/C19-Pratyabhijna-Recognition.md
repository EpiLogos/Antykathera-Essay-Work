# C19 — Pratyabhijñā / Recognition

## #0

Recognition is the return in which a present determination is known through the source, identity or history from which it had become estranged. The difference acquired in the passage remains. Returning is neither cancellation of what happened nor acquisition of an untouched beginning hidden behind it.

[A21 — Individuation / Recognition](../arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) **grounds** the argument. C19 keeps the return distinct from simple recollection, correct classification or the satisfaction of having an existing account confirmed. Pratyabhijñā retains its specific Śaiva source-office within this broader authorial movement.

## #1

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE|Dyczkowski — The Doctrine of Vibration]], pp.71–75, **sources** the distinction between memory and recognition's wider direction. Retained traces can be recalled and recomposed within a contracted field. Recognition concerns the integral self-awareness whose activity appears at contracted and expanded levels. A remembered object and recognition of the act's source relation are therefore not interchangeable achievements.

The vertical ascent/descent and horizontal movement toward/from an object preserve the return within manifestation. [C13 — Prakāśa / Vimarśa](C13-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **grounds** the luminous/reflexive relation; [C15 — Paśu](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) **grounds** the bounded knower whose particularity survives recognition.

## #2

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|Taylor — Core Theorems]] **grounds** the native return-switch. The achieved `1/0` turns toward the ground it presupposes and meets what it cannot exhaust as a further object. The `X/x` relation retains a locally real instance; its recognition does not replace the instance with a largest or final determining image.

[C06 — Copula](C06-Copula-Identification-with-Difference.md) **defines** identity through difference. The passage from `x` through `X` and back to `x` returns to a particular life with a changed relation to its determining capacity. Enlarged understanding does not require a person to cease being this person.

## #3

Frank's protected encounter with [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE|Van Eenwyk — Archetypes and Strange Attractors]], pp.69–73, **sources** the known→unknown→known movement. A symbol carries the person beyond the organisation in which it was first understood and returns with that organisation changed. The source's copied quotations remain leads; the native QL cross-reading belongs to Frank's encounter.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE|Taylor — Symbolon Dynamics]] **extends** the reciprocal transformation. A symbol changes the interpreter, and the changed interpreter alters what later symbolic encounters can do. Recognition is not the instant at which all future images become fixed; it changes the conditions of their future intelligibility.
