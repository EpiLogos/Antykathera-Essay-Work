# C09 — Diaphaneity

**Definition.** Diaphaneity is contextual transparency: the capacity of a determination to disclose enough of the mediation and conditions through which it appears that view and viewing-position can be read together.

The distinction exists because transparency is often imagined as disappearance of mediation. C09 means the opposite. A diaphanous medium does not vanish; its shaping role becomes legible through what it mediates. Perspective can then disclose both an object and the position from which the object is seen.

Its operation is **two-directional reading**: agent → world and world/context → agent-position. The concept therefore links formal limit to epistemic practice. C05 says the gap cannot be closed; C09 says the conditions on each side can become increasingly explicit.

Nearest neighbours are C08 Context Frame, C10 Mediation / Pramāṇa and C40 Model Internality. Its failure mode is opaque mediation masquerading as direct access.
