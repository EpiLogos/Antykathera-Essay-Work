# C09 — Diaphaneity

## #0

Diaphaneity is the capacity of a determination to disclose its mediation: what appears can be read together with the position, means and conditions through which it appears. The medium's shaping role becomes visible in what it lets through. Its disappearance would remove the very relation that has become intelligible.

[C05 — Immutable Gap](C05-Immutable-Gap.md) **grounds** the enduring non-coincidence which this does not abolish. [A04 — Diaphaneity](../arguments/A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) **grounds** the positive argument. C09 retains the operation of reading in both directions: a situated self sees a world; the disclosed world also shows the situation of that self.

## #1

Perspective renders a world from a position. Its achievement includes determinate depth, proportion and a field that can be communicated. Its conditions can nevertheless disappear behind the scene it renders. Adding more detail to the scene does not necessarily reveal that disappearance.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/SOURCE|Gebser — The Ever-Present Origin]] **historicises** this problem through the emergence of landscape and perspectival optics. His aperspectival direction releases perspective from exclusive validity while retaining its achievement. The source relation is **Paraphrased** for the historical account and **Argued from** it for the essay's contemporary contextual continuation.

## #2

A contextual reading must be involved in what it sees. Taylor's direct [[working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/P5 - Gebser|P5 — Gebser]] **sources** the pressure as being with the text and subtext. Care for the situation's outcome belongs to the seeing; impartiality does not require indifference.

Its absent-Father image makes the pathology exact. An unavailable final standard remains active as a demand; a surrogate image can then occupy its office. Apparent aloofness becomes a way of ruling while disavowing participation. This is Taylor's authorial development, not a quotation from Gebser. [A24 — Usurpation of Measure](../arguments/A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md) **extends** the office/standard consequence, while C09 retains the task of making the hidden position readable.

## #3

The corrected stained-glass image gives the operation a whole body. Originating white light figures Bimba; its full ordered spectrum figures QL. Differently made and positioned panes, their densities, angles, colours and lead seams figure MEF's refracting apparatus. The coloured image cast into the room is Pratibimba. Recognition reads the one light through the lawful differences of the display.

The seams retain source distinctions and translation limits. The projected image changes with sun, pane, room and viewer. Its changes disclose relations that a single fixed view concealed. The Original is not an average of images or a final picture assembled from their sum. QL belongs to the light's internal order; reducing it to the lead framework repeats the assignment explicitly corrected in the developmental quilt.

[C39 — MEF](C39-Meta-Epistemic-Framework.md) **defines** the refractive office; [A22 — World-Atlas](../arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **extends** the coordination of differing views. The image figures the operation; it does not use physical optics to prove metaphysics.
