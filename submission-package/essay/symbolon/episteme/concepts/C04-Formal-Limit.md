# C04 — Formal Limit

## #0

A formal limit is generated by an operation's own structure. The operation can succeed, become more exact and admit more information while reproducing the boundary in question. In the essay's primary case, representing the conditions of representation produces a further representation. The act now making it has not become identical with its represented content.

[C03 — Determination](C03-Determination.md) **grounds** this distinction in the positive achievement of a mark. [A03 — Immutable Gap / Formal Limit](../arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) **grounds** the full argument. C04 retains its criterion: distinguish what further inquiry can supply from what that very inquiry necessarily enacts again.

## #1

Attempt the inclusion. Name the language, instrument, prior distinctions and position through which an account was made. Each can become more legible. Now include the act of making that enlarged account. It too becomes something represented, while the present act of representing continues. The new account genuinely knows more; it has not closed the difference between a representation and its occurrence.

Draft3's opening names the exact turn: **“every circle is a closure in, not around, the reality it designates.”** The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/SOURCE#taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3-q002|Draft3 source house, q002]] **sources** the authorial image at “The Edge of the Rational,” line13. The circle figures inclusion that cannot enclose its own drawing. Its work is philosophical, not a geometrical theorem that lines cannot themselves be drawn.

## #2

An omitted measurement and the act of measurement therefore make different demands. The omitted measurement can be supplied. Representing the act makes its conditions available without moving the present act outside all conditions. Calling both “unknown” loses the distinction on which the argument depends.

[C05 — Immutable Gap](C05-Immutable-Gap.md) **defines** the recurrent non-coincidence. It is not a last unexplained capacity which future science can remove. “God of the gaps” and “humanity of the gaps” each stake their referent on a shrinking inventory of unexplained functions. Here the subject-condition is implicated in explaining any function, including one whose mechanism has become completely inspectable. This neither grants nor denies phenomenality to a particular artificial system.

The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|core theorem spine]] **grounds** the distinction through its performed definition, crossed-zero movement and full eightfold. Parent relation, conscious circumstance, question/assertion, polarity, `X/x`, `AM/IS`, `∞/dx` and inverse return retain their separate operations. The limit belongs to their living relation; it is not eight repetitions of an inability to know.

## #3

Formal comparisons make this criterion more exact when their operations remain distinct. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/russell/russell-1908-theory-types/SOURCE#russell-1908-theory-types-q001|Russell's theory of types]] **compares** the reflexive predicament with a specific response: restrict which totalities can contain members defined through those totalities. Its relative types concern variables and ranges of significance. The response reorganises allowable formation; it is not a psychological diagnosis of its author.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/godel/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions/SOURCE|Gödel's 1931 article]] **qualifies** the mathematical comparison by fixing its jurisdiction in effectively axiomatized systems sufficiently expressive for arithmetic, with the relevant consistency hypotheses. Incompleteness is not a theorem that every account, model or closed physical system fails. The native argument remains Argued; Gödel does not supply a proof of the Subject, God or QL.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/SOURCE|Spencer-Brown's calculus]] **compares** a third operation: distinction and its re-entry. The unmarked state belongs to that calculus; it cannot silently be substituted for QL's unobjectifiable condition. These comparisons are source-specific relations, not interchangeable authorities for one slogan.

## #4

Changing a formal container can change what is expressible. That makes the container's rules more consequential, not disposable. A new regime must state its operations and the laws it retains. [A10 — Advent of Zero](../arguments/A10-Advent-of-Zero.md) **historicises** this pressure through zero's changed operational permissions. C04 does not treat ordinary undefined division as a verdict on every historical zero-denominator practice.

[C09 — Diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md) **extends** the positive response to the reflexive limit: the mediation becomes more transparent within the account. More contextual data alone cannot perform this change. The account has to disclose how its position participates in what it makes available. The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD|Fides / Topos / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere whole-field]] **grounds** the Topos relation here at evidence register3: the placing-act is situated even when it places its own conditions. Lexical history remains a separate evidential office.

## #5→0

The limit returns inquiry to a task it can perform: give an account, expose its determining relations and let what returns alter it. [C02 — Faithful Definition](C02-Faithful-Definition.md) **defines** that responsibility; [A23 — Trust and Faith](../arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) **extends** it into continuing without possession of final ground. A commitment can be investigated, corrected or withdrawn. Formal non-possession does not certify a relation as harmless.

C04 **returns-to** [A01](../arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md), where the definition's failure of enclosure becomes its performed fidelity. [A36 — Integral Zero](../arguments/A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) **extends** that return at the far end: mathematical exactness survives when the achieved sign discloses the relation it cannot exhaust.

**Depth Restoration:** active inclusion, closure-in rather than closure-around, correctable omission versus constitutive limit, and differentiated formal responses are restored. C05 owns the enduring non-coincidence; A03 owns the full formal braid. No identity change.
