# Centaur Societies

## Definition

**Centaur Societies** is Bratton's name for the hybrid human–artificial sociality explored in the Agentworld brief. Human and agent societies become reciprocal niches: each supplies conditions through which the other acts and changes, while their capacities, substrates and experiences of time remain different. The brief's hundreds-of-billions configuration poses a question about the form of such a society. Its [scenario modality](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042) governs that scale; the numbers are not a reported population.

The essay places this field at **#4**, the *Where/When/Whither?* Context office, through **L4** and **L1′**. The pair brings situated disclosure into relation with the modes through which a field is apprehended. Participants arrive amid institutions, material supports, language, tools, histories and inherited expectations; their response can change what another participant next encounters. Situated disclosure gives each participant’s response an address within the conditions the others inherit.

## In the brief

[The complete opening unit, pp.3–6](<../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera Agentworld Brief.md#source-pdf-page-3>), gives preemptive anthropology its work: compose categories through which an emerging sociality can become legible while it is still being formed. Its p.6 definition describes human and nonhuman minds as irreducibly entangled and mutually constitutive. The p.4 question about eight billion human minds and hundreds of billions of nonhuman minds asks how that ratio changes what “society” means. The two societies remain mutually nested rather than reducible to one another.

The crossover scenario develops the difference between normative and computational standing. [The source's p.10 account](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q013) imagines humans remaining normatively central while computationally secondary; its “Or, something else?” keeps that distribution a question. The [Agentworldbuilding unit, pp.53–55](<../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera Agentworld Brief.md#source-pdf-page-53>), explicitly returns each thematic vignette as a composite scenario whose world may never arrive and may already be present in some respects. This modality belongs to the particular social claims throughout the brief.

The [centaur social-graphs unit, p.50](<../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera Agentworld Brief.md#source-pdf-page-50>), widens the chess-derived pair into distinct configurations: one person with one agent, one person orchestrating many agents, one agent coupled to many people, and many agents meshed with many people. Their coordination costs and failure modes differ. Human-to-human, human-to-agent, agent-to-agent and agent-to-institution edges change which participant can address, delegate to or answer another. The [FROM → TO table, p.56](<../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera Agentworld Brief.md#source-pdf-page-56>), extends this plurality through centaur, cyborg and siphonophore images.

## In the argument

[Agentworld](C57-Agentworld.md) gives the persistent shared world in which a result can become a condition of another act. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) distinguishes the mediating field through which each local centre can know and act. Within its operation, a technical agent can occupy a functional knower office; memory, model, tools and rules mediate; the differentiated situation is known. Within the containing commission, that same agent participates in the means through which a Life acts. [Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) develops these recursive offices within Life / Mind. Inspection can make a local centre known through a further act while Subjective Immediacy retains the unobjectifiable condition of appearing.

The social configuration makes that recursion consequential. One agent receiving work from several people must retain whose purpose and permission it is carrying. A person coordinating several agents must preserve their different source fields and scopes. A mesh needs routes through which a changed source, disputed permission or affected participant can alter what a later operation inherits. [Prompt Thrownness](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) keeps those inherited conditions addressable; [the Reflective Field](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) asks the instrument to initiate disclosure before requiring another Life to conform to its reflection.

The [Mono/Poly operation](../arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many.md) keeps common relation effective through genuinely different local offices. Coordination can therefore preserve divergent world-models while making disagreements locatable and consequential. The [Agentworld Response Matrix](../../../quilt/agentworld-response-matrix.md) retains the essay's specific address to the brief's pressures. A common grammar has work to do here: it must carry source, scope, exclusion and returned consequence between participants so their differences can reach the conditions of the next act.
