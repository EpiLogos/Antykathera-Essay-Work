## Definition

**Centaur Societies** is Bratton's term for the emergent hybrid social field — no longer humans alone, nor humans-plus-instruments, but hundreds of billions of human and non-human human-level minds, irreducibly entangled and mutually constitutive. In the coordinate system it sits at **#4** (*Where/When/Whither?* — Son/Love, Square B, the Context and lemniscate anchor), read through **L4** (phenomenological — the lived encounter) and its Möbius night-face **L1'** (phenomenal — immediate qualia). It names the concrete historical situation, the encounter arena, to which the essay's account of agency is addressed. *Status:* the term is imported from the brief (Bratton's) — a venue term, not canon-derived; its placement at #4 as the essay's encounter axis is **Argued**.

## In the brief

The defining sentence: "'Centaur' registers that the society in question is no longer composed of humans alone, nor humans plus instruments, but of hundreds of billions of human and non-human human-level minds, irreducibly entangled and mutually constitutive rather than parallel" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 6|p. 6]]). The relation is ecological and reciprocal: "each sub-society is the evolutionary niche of the other" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 4|pp. 4–5]]). The field is entered through a crossover: "'Society' becomes a composite organism where humans remain normatively central but computationally secondary, navigating a world whose dominant cognitive mass is no longer biological but mineral-based" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 10|p. 10]]) — a rebalancing "in which human agency shifts toward value-setting, oversight, and long-horizon judgment. Or, something else?"

The brief also complicates its own title figure. The centaur "was formulated in post-Kasparov chess and generalized to knowledge work, but the topology it implies is narrower than the situation now demands": the live configurations are combinatorial — "one person with one agent, one person orchestrating many agents, one agent coupled to many people, many agents meshed with many people. Each is a distinct cognitive architecture with its own coordination costs and failure modes; reading them as variations on the same centaur collapses the distinctions that matter" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 50|p. 50]]). The FROM → TO table pushes the figure further outward: "Clear delineation of human and agentic AI → Hybridization, centaur, cyborg, siphonophore" ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 56|p. 56]]).

## In the argument

Centaur Societies is the situation *The Return of Zero* is answerable to: the [[Agentworld Response Matrix]] pins every outer station to a pressure this field exerts. The essay meets it not as a utility problem but through the Square-B encounter axis — [[Objective Internality]] separates the inspectable apparatus from the uncaptured subject-pole, [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]] holds the phenomenality question open, and [[Prompt Thrownness]] admits agent reports as encounter data. The §5→0 station gives the field institutional form via [[11-mono-poly-whole-and-many|Mono-Poly]] and [[Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field]]: coordination across many world-models without a shared ontology or a captured whole.
