# C23 — Severance

**Definition.** Severance is the counterfeit operation in which achieved difference is treated as loss of constitutive relation. A distinction becomes an independently grounded term and the excluded or source field is externalised.

The concept exists to distinguish healthy Dia from its collapse. Cutting, classifying and separating are often necessary. Severance begins only when the cut claims ontological or epistemic independence from the relation that made it possible.

Its operation is **difference → relation-amnesia → self-grounded pole**. C18 Apoha shows why determination still depends on an excluded field; C50 Dia/Syn supplies the healthy conjugacy; C27 Protected Account is a frequent institutional result.

Nearest opposite failure is C24 Fusion. Both destroy relation: severance by abolishing connection, fusion by abolishing distinction.
