# C54 — Commons / Non-Monopoly

**Definition.** Commons / Non-Monopoly names a shared field of conditions that can support many local offices without any one participant claiming that the field's generative source is its exclusive property.

The distinction exists because Mono/Poly requires an institutional consequence. Local ownership, privacy, authority and bounded stewardship can all be legitimate; monopoly in the canonical sense begins when a local office converts dependence on a shared field into a claim to own the field's source conditions.

Its operation is **maintain common generative conditions → permit differentiated local use/office → require return/non-appropriation of source relation**. C26 Monoisation names the failure; C53 Power/Delegated Labour shows how value flows; C59 Cultural Individuation gives the collective-development consequence.

Nearest neighbours are C26, C53, C59 and C62. Commons is not compulsory collectivisation; it is a relational constraint against source-usurpation.
