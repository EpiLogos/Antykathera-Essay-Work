# C29 — Mediating Office / Derivative Sovereignty

**Definition.** A Mediating Office is a position authorised to transform, interpret or decide within a bounded scope. **Derivative Sovereignty** names the real but non-source authority such an office can possess.

The distinction exists because mediation is often described either as neutral transmission or as total control. C29 allows genuine discretionary power while preserving its dependence on a prior covenant/source relation.

Its operation is **receive delegated measure → exercise situated judgment → return decision/effects to source field**. C28 supplies the primary relation; C27 names failure when the office protects its account; C53 describes the labour/power distributed through the office.

Nearest neighbours: C28 Covenant, C40 Model Internality, C44 Prompt Thrownness and C53 Power. An evaluator, judge, model, steward or agent can all occupy mediating offices without those roles being identical.

**Argument consumers:** A19, A24–A25, A28–A31, A33. **Movement consumers:** arbitration, agent delegation, evaluation and commons movements.

**Etymology relation:** *Apportionment / Economy*; exact operation: authority is an **apportioned** capacity inside a wider economy of relation. Register **3**, with legal/political lexical histories separately sourced. Return C29 → A25 → A28/A29 → C47/A31.
