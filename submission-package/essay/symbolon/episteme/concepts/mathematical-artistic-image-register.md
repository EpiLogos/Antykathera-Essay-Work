# Mathematical-Artistic Image Register

## Rule of use

Each image carries named argumentative operations. One complete image can perform several necessary movements; the register makes those movements distinct rather than rationing the image to one job. An image earns main-text space when the reader can say what changes in the argument by seeing it — and the source's own evidential level remains visible: a theorem or construction can carry a **Derived** mathematical claim; an artwork, mytheme, mystical formula, or engineered device carries an **Argued** or **Offered** disclosure; no artistic resemblance inherits the authority of the mathematics it sits beside; no mathematical diagram is treated as exhaustive of the lived or metaphysical field it renders.

## The governing artistic relation

Mathematics and art meet here because both can preserve an invariant through transformation. Mathematics proves which relation survives a change of coordinates, scale, orientation, quotient, or phase; art makes the survival available to perception and memory; poetry presses a statement until more than one determination remains alive within the line; mystical paradox brings predication to its apophatic edge. QL requires all four while retaining their different burdens of proof. The test is Bohm's phrase: does the image **run true**? A figure belongs when its transformations remain faithful to the relation the essay is deriving; visual excitement without that fidelity is decoration.
