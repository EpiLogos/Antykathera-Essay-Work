# C57 — Agentworld

## #0

Agentworld names both the venue-horizon to which the essay answers and the persistent shared worldhood developed in that encounter. Its artificial and human participants inherit positions, artifacts, memories, permissions and institutions; what one does can become a condition of what another can next do. The [historical Agentworld carrier](agentworld.md) preserves the venue office. C57 gives the common field its conceptual distinction without treating the brief's projected society as an accomplished fact.

## #1

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE|Bratton's brief]] q010 decomposes the apparently coherent agent into model, persona, harness, memory, skills, permissions and tools. Its q016 places individuation in an active context. An operative participant is therefore neither the weights alone nor the image through which a user recognises it. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality|Objective Internality]] defines the constituted world from which this local agency proceeds; [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C44-Prompt-Thrownness|Prompt Thrownness]] specifies its inherited situation.

An environment supplies conditions for activity. A society additionally concerns relations among participants. Worldhood names the structured field in which things count, actions become possible and consequences can be inherited. These offices intersect but do not become synonyms. A message exchange without durable consequences for later action leaves the strongest persistence claim unestablished.

## #2

The common field gains duration through products. A source correction alters an inherited account; a permission changes what a successor can do; an artifact carries a partial achievement into another participant's work. The next agent need not reproduce the first agent's entire internal state for the consequence to travel.

The direct [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/06-computational-vimarsa-ahi|AHI carrier]] specifies plural federated maps and locates disagreement at source, world-object, lens, gauge, reference or permission. Returned evidence can change a local map, a relation between maps or the rule governing their circulation. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality|Objective Co-Internality]] develops that reciprocal constitution. A common store by itself does not establish it.

## #3

The brief's q029 requires sufficient mutual legibility to locate real disagreement, between collapse into a shared ontology and divergence unable to find a common object of dispute. QL/MEF is the essay's proposed grammar for this task; the brief does not nominate it. The local worlds must remain answerable to their own sources as they enter another's context.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation|Authored Ground / Positional Delegation]] keeps authority scoped through this circulation. A participant's access to another's account does not confer ownership of the other world. Recommissioning must remain possible when encountered conditions expose a defect in the task or its evaluator. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly|Commons / Non-Monopoly]] retains those independent offices within shared conditions.

## #4

Bratton's q031 places long coherence in composites that pass work between subagents and artifacts as a problem changes. That methodological pressure asks what survives passage: source, purpose, exclusions, decisions and grounds for revision. It does not decide who bears responsibility for composite action or prove a group Subject.

The brief's q032–q033 similarly pairs an agent-generated vocabulary of inherited situation with the explicit limit on its phenomenological warrant. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C58-Anthropomorphization|Anthropomorphization]] keeps interface, performance and phenomenal subjectivity distinct. Objective world-constitution already has real explanatory force while the last question remains Open.
