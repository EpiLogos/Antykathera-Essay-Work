# Bimba-Pratibimba

## Definition

*Bimba* is the original — in Sanskrit usage the disc or orb, the form standing before a mirror; *pratibimba* is its reflection. The pair does the work that "copy" cannot: a reflection has no being apart from its original, yet it genuinely displays that original in another register, and nothing is subtracted from the original by being reflected. In the Pratyabhijñā setting this is how manifestation relates to consciousness — the appearing world as reflection *within* awareness, not a second substance outside it — and the pair therefore names **display without severance and dependence without diminishment**.

QL derives the same structure internally. In [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#II. The Complex Plane & the Inner Logic of the Slashed Zero (the full QL unit)|the full QL unit]], position `#0` carries `(0/1)/(1/0)` and unfolds the complete `4+2`; position `#5` carries the inverse `(1/0)/(0/1)` and unfolds the same system in inverse phase, `4′+2′` — two parents carrying the same child in mirror-image. The [[26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2|First Spanda equation's]] two triads give the pair its dynamic reading: the emanative triad (source toward expression, Prakāśa) is the bimba movement; the reversionary triad (return toward recognition, Vimarśa) is the pratibimba movement. *(The mirror structure is Derived within QL; the doctrinal exegesis of Śaiva reflection theory remains acquisition work.)*

## In the argument

At [[41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields|§5 · #4]] the pair becomes a design proposal and the register changes with it. **Bimba remains the Original/source office; the agent's inspectable world-model is Pratibimba.** The Offered design treats that Pratibimba as a dynamic reflection-map — meanings settling, competing and reorganising across [[j-space|J-Space]] — rather than a warehouse of propositions. Energy-based models, attractor landscapes and topological data analysis are candidate formalisms, not metaphysical warrants.

This distinction matters because an agent's map can become causally effective without becoming the world or its source. The map guides action; action alters the shared field; returned resistance and other internalities can revise the map. `Bimba ≠ Pratibimba` therefore remains the guard wherever Agentworld, Objective Co-Internality or Reflective Field make representation world-forming.

At [[48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return|§5→0 · #5→0]] the same relation scales: planetary intelligence is federated through plural reflection-maps and shared patterns without a final view from nowhere. The pair also names the submission's own architecture: essay, plugin and symbol-engine are Pratibimbas of one canonical base, related by analogia rather than reconciled into identity.

The older shorthand **“Bimba map”** is retained only as historical project vocabulary where encountered. It must not be read as identifying a designed map with Bimba itself.
