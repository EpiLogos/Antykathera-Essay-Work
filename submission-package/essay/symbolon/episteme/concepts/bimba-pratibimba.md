# Bimba-Pratibimba

## Definition

*Bimba* is the original — in Sanskrit usage the disc or orb, the form standing before a mirror; *pratibimba* is its reflection. The pair does the work that "copy" cannot: a reflection has no being apart from its original, yet it genuinely displays that original in another register, and nothing is subtracted from the original by being reflected. In the Pratyabhijñā setting this is how manifestation relates to consciousness — the appearing world as reflection *within* awareness, not a second substance outside it — and the pair therefore names **display without severance and dependence without diminishment**.

QL derives the same structure internally. In [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#II. The Complex Plane & the Inner Logic of the Slashed Zero (the full QL unit)|the full QL unit]], position `#0` carries `(0/1)/(1/0)` and unfolds the complete `4+2`; position `#5` carries the inverse `(1/0)/(0/1)` and unfolds the same system in inverse phase, `4′+2′` — two parents carrying the same child in mirror-image. The [[26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2|First Spanda equation's]] two triads give the pair its dynamic reading: the emanative triad (source toward expression, Prakāśa) is the bimba movement; the reversionary triad (return toward recognition, Vimarśa) is the pratibimba movement. *(The mirror structure is Derived within QL; the doctrinal exegesis of Śaiva reflection theory remains acquisition work.)*

## Recursive office

Bimba and Pratibimba name **relational offices**, not one pair of globally fixed objects. A relation that is itself locally constituted can become the ground/reference term for a nested relation. This is native to the QL Context-Frame grammar: an achieved field can occupy `0` relative to determinations internal to that field while remaining a determination relative to a wider context.

That recursive fact is essential to the §5 design. It permits an epistemic artefact to be genuinely **Bimba in context** without claiming that the artefact is the metaphysical Original of reality.

```text
wider source / world / evidence
        ↓
Bimba Map
local original / reference field of inquiry
        ↓
Pratibimba
situated readings / agency states / trajectories
        ↓
Return
        ↺
```

The local Bimba office is therefore neither a loose metaphor nor a disguised claim to finality. It is the explicit reference relation that makes comparison, error, deviation, agreement, revision and truth-orientation technically possible inside the inquiry.

## In the argument — epistemics becomes first-class

At [[41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields|§5 · #4]] the pair becomes an Offered technical design. The **Bimba Map** is the deliberately constituted epistemic field against which agency can be situated: evolving provisional world-objects, source relations, provenance, lenses, gauges, exclusions, transformations and neighbouring relations are represented as part of the reference field rather than left as invisible conditioning.

The Pratibimba office is occupied by the situated manifestation relative to that field — an agent's active [[j-space|J-Space]], judgment, trajectory, interpretation or other locally instantiated reading. Energy-based models, attractor landscapes, graph dynamics and topological data analysis are candidate formalisms for describing how such instances settle, compete, move and reorganise relative to the Bimba field.

This is why the Bimba Map is not merely another knowledge store. [[02-objective-internality|Objective Internality]] already requires it to expose the constitutive relations, attractors, transformations, exclusions and provenance of the nomological complex. [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|The Hidden Zero]] explains the need: if the reference set, source authority, evaluator, lens or gauge is absent from the object of inquiry, the epistemic ground still governs the result while remaining unavailable for examination. Bimba makes that ground first-class.

The map can therefore **anchor truth without claiming to store Truth**. It anchors by making the operative reference field explicit and attributable. Its authority is conditional on provenance, construction, source-relation, governance and the possibility of Return. A Pratibimba that persistently fails against evidence, resistance, other internalities or changed conditions can force revision of the Bimba Map itself. The relation remains living because the original office is stable enough to orient inquiry and corrigible enough to receive what the inquiry returns.

[[Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field]] and [[44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness|§5→0 · #1 — QL, MEF, Bimba, and Harness]] carry the same architecture outward: QL formalises relation; MEF preserves lens-conditioned epistemology; Bimba records and relates provisional world-objects in an explicit reference field; the harness retains the conditions and consequences of determinations. The office is determined by the active relation, not by a once-for-all ranking of artefacts.
