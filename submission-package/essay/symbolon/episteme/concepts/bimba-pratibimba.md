# Bimba-Pratibimba

## Definition

*Bimba* is the original — in Sanskrit usage the disc or orb, the form standing before a mirror; *pratibimba* is its reflection. The pair does the work that "copy" cannot: a reflection has no being apart from its original, yet it genuinely displays that original in another register, and nothing is subtracted from the original by being reflected. In the Pratyabhijñā setting this is how manifestation relates to consciousness — the appearing world as reflection *within* awareness, not a second substance outside it — and the pair therefore names **display without severance and dependence without diminishment**.

QL derives the same structure internally. In [the native mirror relation](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md), position `#0` carries `(0/1)/(1/0)` and unfolds the complete `4+2`; position `#5` carries the inverse `(1/0)/(0/1)` and unfolds the same system in inverse phase, `4′+2′` — two parents carrying the same child in mirror-image. The [[26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2|First Spanda equation's]] two triads give the pair its dynamic reading: the emanative triad (source toward expression, Prakāśa) is the bimba movement; the reversionary triad (return toward recognition, Vimarśa) is the pratibimba movement. The native parents and triads derive their mirror relation within QL. Dyczkowski's selected conscious-manifestation account supplies its own distinct original/reflection argument; primary Utpaladeva and Abhinavagupta edition collation remains separate.

## Recursive office

Bimba and Pratibimba name **relational offices**, not one pair of globally fixed objects. A relation that is itself locally constituted can become the ground/reference term for a nested relation. This is native to the QL Context-Frame grammar: an achieved field can occupy `0` relative to determinations internal to that field while remaining a determination relative to a wider context.

That recursive fact is essential to the §5 design. It permits an epistemic artefact to be genuinely **Bimba in context** without claiming that the artefact is the metaphysical Original of reality.

```text
wider source / world / evidence
        ↓
Bimba Map
local original / reference field of inquiry
        ↓
Pratibimba
situated readings / agency states / trajectories
        ↓
Return
        ↺
```

The local Bimba office is therefore neither a loose metaphor nor a disguised claim to finality. It is the explicit reference relation that makes comparison, error, deviation, agreement, revision and truth-orientation technically possible inside the inquiry.

## In the argument — epistemics becomes first-class

At [[41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields|§5 · #4]] the pair becomes a proposed technical design. The **Bimba Map** is the deliberately constituted epistemic field against which agency can be situated: evolving provisional world-objects, source relations, provenance, lenses, gauges, exclusions, transformations and neighbouring relations are represented as part of the reference field rather than left as invisible conditioning.

The Pratibimba office is occupied by the situated manifestation relative to that field — an agent's active [J-Space](j-space.md), judgment, trajectory, interpretation or other locally instantiated reading. Energy-based models, attractor landscapes, graph dynamics and topological data analysis are candidate formalisms for describing how such instances settle, compete, move and reorganise relative to the Bimba field.

The constituted reference field makes the active [[02-objective-internality|world of inquiry]] available: sources, relations, attractors, transformations, exclusions and provenance can enter the comparison that they condition. In the [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|hidden-zero problem]], an absent reference set, source authority, evaluator, lens or gauge still governs the result. Exposing these means gives the local judging agent something determinate to inspect and use. That agent occupies the relative functional knower office; the map, comparison rule, memory and tools are means; the addressed world-object and returned interpretation are known. In the person's containing inquiry, the whole apparatus occupies a means office. A further inspection can make the local judging centre known without turning the immediate condition of knowing within Life into a stored item.

The map **anchors truth without claiming to store Truth** by making the operative reference field explicit and attributable. Its authority depends on its actual sources, construction, commission and route of return. A Pratibimba that fails against evidence, resistance, other internalities or changed conditions returns the fault to the relevant office. A factual correction can alter an answer or world-object under a fitting comparison rule; failure of that rule can require revision of the evaluator or commission by capable authority. A fitting condition can also be retained with reasons. The next inquiry inherits the returned judgment and source history. The original office remains stable enough to orient comparison and available to receive the consequences of that comparison.

## Across scales

Within [reflective coordination](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md), QL formalises relation; MEF preserves lens-conditioned epistemology; Bimba relates provisional world-objects in an explicit reference field; the [harness](../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md) retains the conditions and consequences of determinations. [Federated Bimba Maps](../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return.md) compose locally governed reference fields through provenance, declared translations and reciprocal answerability. Their wider composition retains each local source office and the authority capable of receiving its return.

The submission architecture supplies another scale of the same relation: essay, plugin and symbol-engine may be Pratibimbas of a canonical base when that base occupies the Bimba office for their comparison. The office is determined by the active relation, not by a once-for-all ranking of artefacts.
