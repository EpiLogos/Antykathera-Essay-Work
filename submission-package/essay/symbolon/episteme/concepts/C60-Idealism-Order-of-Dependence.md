# C60 — Idealism / Order of Dependence

## #0

The essay's idealism states a positive order of dependence: **Subject / Consciousness → mind as objective internality → object as determinate appearance**. The arrows articulate dependence, not chronological manufacture by a private ego. C60 makes the order and the predicates exact; [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence|A34]] develops the argument. Treating idealism as only a discipline of comparing views would remove the position that the [[quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT|explicit Q27 correction]] requires the field to carry.

## #1

An inquiry encounters determinate evidence within appearing, discrimination and knowing. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability|Subject / Defined Indefinability]] names the unobjectifiable condition through which objects become available. A brain, model, state or introspective report can be made an object of further inquiry. Its additional detail does not by itself make it the condition of object-availability.

The authorial criticism concerns that change of office: an objective account treats its own determinate terms as sufficient to generate the field their assertion already presupposes. Science retains its exact transformations within that field. The ontological inference criticised by the essay is its inflation into a self-grounding account of appearing as such.

## #2

[[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality|Objective Internality]] gives the middle term its positive reality. Attention, memory, valuation, language, contrast and selfing make an inhabitable world of determinate relations. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood|Conditions of Worldhood]] distinguishes what lets things count and actions become possible. Objectivity includes resistance, persistence, shared measurement and consequence; recognising its condition does not abolish these achievements.

The private ego is itself a determinate formation within this field. “World in mind” therefore does not make the world obedient to private imagination. The difference between a lived world and a world-model remains operative even where a model changes conduct and thus the world it later models.

## #3

Different dependencies can be reciprocal without sharing one direction. Epistemically, a reflection can be encountered before its source is understood. Ontologically, the source is prior to that reflection. Causally, the reflection can change later conditions through the action it informs. Logically, one proposition can depend on premises. Transcendentally, inquiry can disclose a condition of the possibility of an object.

[[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First|The First-Moving Mirror]] supplies the concrete crossing: the model may lead the next encounter without becoming the ontological author of its source. [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/C63-Pros-Hen-In-Quantum-Focal-Return-Qualified-Predication|Focal and Qualified Predication]] keeps each “prior” or “depends” attached to its operative respect. Distinguishing these orders carries the argued ontology; it does not suspend it.

## #4

The [[section-rooms/arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open|open-subjectivity carrier]] separates the position from the localisation question. Artificial agency can have an inspectable constitutive interior while the presence and partition of phenomenal subjectivity remain Open. Functional complexity alone settles neither its presence nor its absence.

Historical witnesses retain their own disputes. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/berkeley/berkeley-1734-three-dialogues-wilkins-2002/SOURCE|Berkeley's selected dialogue]] q001 gives Philonous's conditional rejection of material substance; q002 gives Hylas's immediate objection. They are different speakers in one argument, not two interchangeable authorial conclusions. Neither establishes QL or the phenomenal status of an engineered system. The Q27 coordination of analytical idealism and Śaiva articulation retains separate textual and inferential tasks, including exact Kastrup recovery.

[[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood|Objective Internality]] and [[symbolon/episteme/arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality|Objective Co-Internality]] return this ontology to the construction of consequential worlds among agencies. Relations contribute to their constitution; they are not merely later connections between finished isolated units. Local agency persists through that dependence rather than disappearing into a super-agent.
