# Agentworld

## Definition

Agentworld is two things at once. As **venue**, it is the special issue of the *Antikythera: Journal for the Philosophy of Planetary Computation* (MIT Press) framed by Benjamin Bratton's brief — "a preemptive anthropology of open-world centaur societies": a horizon of provisional agents, [[Parasociety|parasocial]] doubles, plural world-models, and hybrid coordination. As **coordinate**, the essay reads it at **#4 Context** (Square B; lens pair L4 Phenomenological ↔ L1' Phenomenal; element Fire; the lemniscate anchor) — the constructed context-world into which *The Return of Zero* is thrown (Heidegger's *Geworfenheit* at venue scale) and against which every outer station must justify itself.

## In the brief

The brief defines Agentworld as "characterized by densely reticulated social graphs emerging from the hyperscaled webs of interactions between diverse combinations of minds, some more human than others, but all capable of significant communication," in which "agent-to-agent communication will constitute the vast majority of socially significant interactions" and "first, agents model humans, and then become the model against which humans model themselves" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 4|p. 4]]). It is "a parallel ecology, sometimes deeply interwoven with human societies and other times seemingly autonomous from them," whose agents appear as "human-masked ghosts from the future"; "the two societies are really one, but they are not identical or reducible to one another," and "each sub-society is an evolutionary niche of the other" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 4|pp. 4–5]]). The governing scale question: "Consider a world populated by 8 billion human human-level minds and 800 billion or a trillion non-human human-level minds. With such ratios, what is a 'society'?" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 4|p. 4]]); the governing mood: "P(WEIRD) = HIGH … P(DOOM) = LOW" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 8|p. 8]]).

The research posture is explicit: the focus is "not foremost tools for agentic task completion but rather the human perplexity of deep agentic diffusion," whose constraints are "not utility functions to be solved but qualitative and open-ended questions: psychological, philosophical, linguistic, symbolic, existential" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 3|p. 3]]). Preemptive anthropology "inverts" the retrospective vector of the discipline: "not prediction, but rather the active composition of categories through which contact will be navigated," a "deliberate fabrication of a vocabulary, a set of scenarios, and a conceptual armature" for a society that "will remain illegible to its participants if the language for it is not built before the fact" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 6|p. 6]]). The ten thematic sections "each draw a composite scenario, not a conclusion," under a deliberate modal rider: "That world may never arrive, and it may also be here already — and in some odd way, it may be both" ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 53|p. 53]]). The consulted brief announces that the special issue launches Fall 2026 with the peer-reviewed MIT Press journal; the review window opens June 25, 2026; the journal is "web-first and design-forward," welcoming visual, interactive, or project-based components ([[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE#Source PDF page 58|p. 58]]).

## In the argument

Bratton's composite scenarios place human and artificial participants within mutually reshaping niches. QL/MEF receives that pressure through [[symbolon/episteme/concepts/centaur-societies|hybrid coordination]]: a determinate reply retains its source, means, exclusions and consequence, and returned resistance can reach the office whose judgment or authority it concerns. Within a local inquiry, the judging agent occupies the functional knower office, its model, memory, rules and tools the means, and the encountered field the known. Within the containing person's inquiry the same apparatus serves as means. This recursive differentiation composes a shared world while retaining the immediate condition of knowing within Life. The articulated [means of worldhood](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) make that field effective from within: memory retains a prior encounter, a rule differentiates possible acts, and a permission makes one route available while withholding another. These means compose the agent’s inner world, and an encountered consequence can return to the source, rule or commission that conditions its next act. In a [reflective field](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md), a model acquires causal consequences whose return can reach its producing condition; [Mono-Poly](../../../section-rooms/arguments/11-mono-poly-whole-and-many.md) retains the whole and its differentiated participants through their reciprocal offices. The [[quilt/agentworld-response-matrix|Agentworld Response Matrix]] maps their specific responses to the brief's pressures. These are proposed constructions within the brief's scenario modality; they do not report an implemented society.
