# C07 — Conditions of Worldhood

**Definition.** Conditions of Worldhood are the relational, material, linguistic, historical, institutional and technical conditions through which a world becomes inhabitable and actionable for a situated agent. They are broader than any one world-picture or model.

The concept exists to keep A22 **World-Picture → World-Atlas** distinct from the worldhood that makes pictures possible. A world-model represents selected relations; worldhood includes the conditions under which representation, salience, action, resistance and encounter occur at all.

C07 operates by **reversing explanatory direction**. Instead of asking only how an agent models a world, ask how the world/context constitutes what the agent can distinguish, remember, say and do. This is the reusable conceptual hinge for A26 Objective Internality.

Nearest neighbours are C08 Context / Context Frame and C41 Objective Internality. C37 World-Picture → World-Atlas is a representational transformation; C07 is the broader world-making condition.
