# C01 — Subject / Defined Indefinability

**Definition.** The Subject is the pole to which appearances, determinations and acts are present, but which cannot be exhausted by being represented as another appearance, determination or act. **Defined indefinability** is not vagueness: it is the positive determination that representation of the Subject remains representation *for* a still-present subject-pole.

The distinction exists because the essay must speak exactly about subjectivity without converting subject into a hidden object. A profile, body, memory, model, self-concept or inner instrument can all be investigated. None is thereby identical with the Subject as such. The operation of C01 is therefore reflexive discrimination: whenever the account turns the knower into something known, it asks whether the knowing pole has simply been smuggled into the object-description.

Its nearest neighbour is **C35 Selfing / Self / Subjectivity / Self-Thing**, which separates several senses usually compressed into “self”. Its formal complement is **C03 Determination**: the Subject is encountered through determinations yet is not one more determination on the same footing. **C04 Formal Limit** and **C05 Immutable Gap** state the consequence of that asymmetry.
