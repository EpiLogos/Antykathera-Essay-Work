# C48 — Trust / Faith under Formal Limit

## #0

Trust is the dependency already enacted in `0/1`, before it becomes a belief about a neutral world. The All depends on the One for being and determining reality; the One becomes manifest and knowable through the All. A finite being relies on language, memory, bodies, others and material continuity before any finished account can certify them.

## #1

Knowledge is a determinate achievement within this relation. It can provide a provisional anchor whose scope, mediation and correction remain available. Belief stabilises an orientation within such an aperture; confidence concerns a judgment of reliability. These do not exhaust the prior dependency through which knowing and acting are possible.

## #2

The formal limit makes non-possession explicit. No completed statement contains the act and world through which it becomes meaningful. Faith is lucid continuation through that limit, with grounds, scope and revision conditions retained. It is not guaranteed harmlessness: the historical trust carrier preserves that a ground can withdraw and a relation can require changed or withdrawn reliance.

## #3

The trust revision restores knowledge's fork. An account can support reliance while admitting its provisionality, or it can conceal uncertainty and fasten the unresolved distrust onto a scapegoat, enemy or idol. The second operation replaces the live relation with an object claiming final certainty. This is the author's diagnostic; particular social histories require their own evidence.

## #4

Arbitration arises when tacit trust no longer suffices. A covenant or institution can stabilise delegated relation, but the mediating office receives a prior settlement and remains answerable to it. A25 preserves primary arbitration before king, priest or politician. Protocols, audits and records can support renewal; their operation still depends on persons and institutions able to bear the commitment.

## #5→0

Reliance returns through encounter and can be investigated, corrected, withdrawn or renewed. C47 specifies the power of later encounter to revise the model; C28 the delegated relation; C02 the faithful account. Trust and Mono/Poly share a root while doing different canonical work: whole/form/power in one case, finite knowing, risking and fidelity in the other. The apparatus can make the terms of reliance inspectable without manufacturing the living relation it serves.
