# C48 — Trust / Faith under Formal Limit

## #0

The [trust revision](../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/SOURCE.md) begins from the dependency already enacted in `0/1`, before trust becomes a belief about a neutral world. The All depends on the One for being and determining reality; the One becomes manifest and knowable through the All. A finite being relies on language, memory, bodies, others and material continuity before any finished account can certify them.

## #1

Knowledge is a performance within this prior reliance. An "I know" presents a determinate proposition, while the act of knowing already depends on a practical verdict about what works, what fails and where reliance can be placed. The certainty of the proposition can conceal this dependency; receiving its provisionality lets knowledge become an anchor for trust whose scope, mediation and correction remain available. Belief stabilises an orientation within such an aperture; confidence concerns a judgment of reliability. These do not exhaust the prior dependency through which knowing and acting are possible.

## #2

The formal limit makes non-possession explicit. No completed statement contains the act and world through which it becomes meaningful. Faith is lucid continuation through that limit, with grounds, scope and revision conditions retained. A ground can withdraw, and a relation can require changed or withdrawn reliance. Lucid continuation receives that possibility within the undertaking itself: its fidelity can include investigation, refusal and renewed terms.

## #3

Knowledge gives this dependence a fork. An account can support reliance while admitting its provisionality, or it can conceal uncertainty and fasten the unresolved distrust onto a scapegoat, enemy or idol. The second operation replaces the live relation with an object claiming final certainty. The account's certainty can then increase while its capacity to receive the source's answer diminishes. This is the authorial operation of displacing distrust; particular social histories retain their own evidential warrants.

## #4

Arbitration arises when tacit trust no longer suffices. A covenant or institution can stabilise delegated relation, but the mediating office receives a prior settlement and remains answerable to it. [Covenant and primary arbitration](../arguments/A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority.md) give king, priest or politician standing within that received relation. Protocols, audits and records can support renewal; their operation still depends on persons and institutions able to bear the commitment.

## #5→0

[E3’s fides before arbitration](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md#fides-before-arbitration) distinguishes enacted prior reliance from the renewed entrustment of Credere after an undertaking has returned consequences. Faith’s lucid continuation under formal limit retains its own office beside knowledge, belief and confidence. Neither lexical proximity nor an instrument’s confidence converts these relations into synonyms or supplies automatic grounds for trust.

The [Symbol / Account / Trust whole-field — Trust keeps the return route active](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active) **qualifies** this operation at evidence register 3. The inspected obligation and the act of entrusting remain distinct; reliance can continue through investigation, correction and refusal without requiring possession of its ground. The resulting distinction **returns-to** that whole field with the consumer’s own source and evidential limits retained.

Reliance returns through encounter and can be investigated, corrected, withdrawn or renewed. [Deferential encounter](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) makes later experience capable of revising the model; [covenant](C28-Covenant-Primary-Arbitration.md) binds the delegated relation; [faithful definition](C02-Faithful-Definition.md) keeps the account answerable to its source. Trust and Mono/Poly share a root while doing different canonical work: whole/form/power in one case, finite knowing, risking and fidelity in the other. The apparatus can make the terms of reliance inspectable without manufacturing the living relation it serves.

[the Job whole](../../mytheme/worlds/biblical/job/WHOLE.md#job-measure-trust-return) **figures** trust through an address that survives the failure of its first explanation. Protest and questioning remain movements within the relation; the friends' certainty does not by itself make their defence faithful. The return is to revised reliance after encounter, rather than a guarantee against loss. This appointment leaves refusal, withdrawal and protection from harm available where a living relation requires them.

[the Prisoner whole](../../mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-account-answerability) **figures** assurances whose working conditions remain withheld from the person asked to rely on them. Arrival's escape route returns Six to confinement; an electoral mandate later fails to give his command operative force. Trust therefore requires an answerable undertaking, not simply another account promising freedom. The possibility of refusing the offered relation belongs to this return where assurance itself administers capture.
