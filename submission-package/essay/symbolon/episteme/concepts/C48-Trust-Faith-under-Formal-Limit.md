# C48 — Trust / Faith under Formal Limit

**Definition.** Trust / Faith under Formal Limit is committed reliance upon a mediated relation whose source cannot be exhaustively possessed, while retaining explicit grounds, scope and revision conditions.

The distinction exists because action cannot wait for impossible total certainty. Testimony, memory, instruments, other persons and institutions all require reliance beyond direct possession. C48 distinguishes such reliance from credulity by making mediation and correction part of the trust relation.

Its operation is **assess warrant → commit under non-exhaustion → remain answerable to later source/encounter**. C04 supplies the formal limit; C10 mediation makes the means explicit; C28 Covenant stabilises trust into delegated relation; C47 Deferential Intelligence makes later revision possible.
