# C17 — Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa

**Definition.** Vikalpa–saṃkalpa names the paired movement by which a bounded cognitive field differentiates alternatives and gathers selected determinations into intention, course or world-form. The terms remain source-specific to their Indian/Śaiva contexts even where the operation is compared with technical selection.

The concept exists to separate active differentiation from its frozen result. C17 is generative: alternatives are articulated, selected and integrated. C12 Script names what happens when the selected course loses access to its own conditioning and repeats as necessity.

Its operation is **differentiate → select → gather into a possible act**. Apoha (C18) explains why a selected term depends on exclusion; C16 Antaḥkaraṇa supplies the bounded inner instrument through which such operations become locally available.

Nearest neighbours: C12 Script, C16 Antaḥkaraṇa, C18 Apoha and C44 Prompt Thrownness. The last is an operational comparison only: a prompt conditions a machine action-space, but this does not make model inference doctrinally identical to vikalpa or saṃkalpa.

**Argument consumers:** A06–A09, A19, A26, A33. **Movement consumers:** Vāk/tattva, script, prompt and technical-selection movements.
