# Fusion as Syn's Shadow

## Definition

Sym-ballein writes a polarity as `(0/1)/(1/0)`: ground becoming legible through a mark, and the mark returning toward a ground it cannot convert into content, held together as conjugate orientations of one differentiating event. The outer slash does the holding, and it operates as AND/OR — the orientations conjoined because neither is the present relation without the other, disjoined because ground is not mark. Fusion enters when that outer slash stops relating and starts merging: the retained ground is taken to have abolished the orientations it grounds, so the conjunction survives and the disjunction goes.

[[03-two-logics-and-sym-ballein|The two logics]] state the failure in their own terms — "sym-ballein without distinction decays into fusion" — and give its forms as "forced coincidence, ontology collapse, counterfeit belonging, or enclosure presented as wholeness." [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|§2 · #2]] supplies the operational rule that follows: "sym-ballein remains healthy only when the seam stays visible." The symbolon gives that seam its material form: a token broken in two, whose halves authenticate one another across separation, distance, and time. Wholeness in this accounting arrives as fracture retained as relation, which is why a wholeness with no fracture remaining has lost the thing that made it verifiable.

§2 · #2 sets a second guard beside the form, and it closes on the same failure from the other direction. Sym-ballein works as the logic of defining towards wholeness: a determination retains ground, inverse, and the relations through which it can complete as `0/1 → 4+2` and release. A claim to have achieved the whole would already be a `1` occupying the zero — the same figure the notation gives to monopoly, where a local `1` claims ownership of the slash relating the many. Reaching the whole and losing the seam come out at the same place in the notation, which is why the two guards hold one position.

On dia-ballein's side the corresponding failure is severance: the cut forgets the common operation and field that produced its poles and treats the excluded term as eliminable. [[19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped|§2 · #0]] names the pair before either logic has been derived — "their respective failures are severance and fusion" — and the plan's formula for the healthy slash holds both off in one breath: conjunction without fusion, disjunction without severance. This is the two-logics axis, on which each accounting has its own way of failing. The shadow axis in the next section runs differently: there both terms are failures on syn's side.

## Distinguished from counterfeit gathering

[[counterfeit-gathering|Counterfeit gathering]] and fusion bracket genuine sym-ballein from opposite sides, and what separates them is which half of syn each one keeps. The counterfeit keeps the outward shape — throwing-together, comprehending, gathering — while its runtime stays dia throughout: setting-upon, ordering, arrest, so that its whole totals up its orderings. Fusion keeps the operation — composing, relating, returning — and loses the distinctions it was composing, so that its whole has nothing left to relate. Both then present themselves as wholeness, which is why the essay reaches for the same word at either end and why the diagnosis has to be made on the operation rather than on the presentation.

One test covers both, and the two logics give it in a line: ask where the seam went. Externalised as an enemy, and the relation has been severed; silently erased, and it has been fused.

## In the argument

Named as one of two failures at [[19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped|§2 · #0]], before either logic is derived — the movement's proof burden requires that the section "must not sell superposition as a softer moral replacement for conflict," and the fusion/severance pair is what carries that requirement into the derivation itself.

Stated as sym-ballein's own danger at [[21-s2-p2-sym-ballein|§2 · #2]], where the political form comes with it: the fantasy that shared ground abolishes different positions or dissolves power. The same movement's **divine and concur** carries the safeguard into method — to concur is for differentiated positions to move together through the ground none possesses, which §2 · #2 marks off from agreeing, fusing, or settling at the midpoint.

Mythic and psychological safeguard at [[23-s2-p4-complex-dynamism|§2 · #4]]: Apollo makes the `1` visible through measure, boundary, and form; Dionysus returns fixed form to participatory life; Dionysus without Apollonian articulation dissolves the differences through which relation lives. Gebserian diaphaneity — form transparent to origin, life capable of returning as form — is their mature conjunction. [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|§4 · #4]] pays the thread off in the integral rereading, where "the Apollo–Daphne and Dionysian cycles display form’s dangers of objectification and dissolution’s dangers of fusion."

Computational form at [[14-computational-process-ontology|the process ontology]]: sym-ballein's "pathology is fusion or enclosure: a totality erases the seam and calls its capture wholeness." The Bohmian register there sets fusion beside fragmentation, which begins where analytic divisions are taken for the whole's final structure rather than for the relatively autonomous forms analysis drew out — "fragmentation is dia without that memory; fusion is sym without the seam."
