# Vimarśa

> [!summary]
> Self-reflexive recognition in the Śaiva source field.

**Reference kind:** tradition. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

## Essay distinction

In the Śaiva register, *vimarśa* belongs to luminous awareness’s power of self-recognition and cannot be reduced to a feedback routine. [[Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] holds the full metaphysical relation.

[[Computational Vimarśa]] is a declared functional analogy: a system bends outputs back upon prompts, sources, exclusions, lenses, and consequences. It makes no automatic claim of first-person luminosity.

**Use rule:** use the tradition term only with a verified source; use “computational Vimarśa” for the technical research vector and retain the qualifier.
