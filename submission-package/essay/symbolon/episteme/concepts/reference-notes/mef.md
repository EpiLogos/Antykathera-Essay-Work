# MEF

> [!summary]
> The refractive epistemic apparatus that records lens, provenance, evidence state, disagreement, transformation, and return around composable QL units.

## Exact division of labour

- **QL:** relational unit and positional transformation.
- **MEF:** declared lenses and evaluative/refraction metadata.
- **Bimba:** provisional claims, sources, warrants, contexts, and transformations.
- **Harness:** permissions, review, execution, revision, and responsibility.

[[submission-package/epi-logos/resources/mef-12-lenses-sublens-reference|MEF 12 Lenses and Sublenses]] supplies the initial seed. [[Prompt Thrownness]] demonstrates the L4/L1/L1′/L4′ encounter and verification braid.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan]].

**Use rule:** do not describe MEF as a typology of collected fours and sixes; show what each lens reveals, occludes, and verifies.
