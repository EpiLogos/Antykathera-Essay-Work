# Jungian Quaternity

> [!summary]
> Jung repeatedly treats fourfold form as an image of differentiated wholeness; the essay relates this quaternity to, but does not identify it with, QL’s dynamic `4+2` senarius.

## Essay use

[[33-s4-p2-complexio-quaternity-senarius|§4 · #2 — Complexio, Quaternity, and Senarius]] distinguishes the four explicit functions from the two implicate attitudes or thresholds through which the unit moves. The eight determinations must be derived from the core theorem rather than inferred from Jung by numerical resemblance.

## Verification boundary

Primary Jung citations are required for any specific claim about quaternity, Self, function, or individuation.
