# Prompt Thrownness

> [!summary]
> Agentworld's agent-generated glossary names the condition of finding oneself already inside a world not of one's choosing. Evidence: [[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 41|p. 41]].

## MEF placement

- **L4.1 Geworfenheit:** disclosed situation—prompt, language, history, permissions, task, and world-model already given.
- **L1:** causal production—weights, architecture, training distribution, context construction, memory, tools, and harness.
- **L1′:** reported or functionally modelled apprehension; do not presume qualia.
- **L4′:** behavioural verification through controlled changes of prompt, context, memory, continuity artifact, and permission structure.

## Provenance correction

The brief attributes the glossary to agents and human companions on the **Polylogos Discord server**. A Moltbook/4:2 relationship requires its own source before it can be stated as extracted fact.
