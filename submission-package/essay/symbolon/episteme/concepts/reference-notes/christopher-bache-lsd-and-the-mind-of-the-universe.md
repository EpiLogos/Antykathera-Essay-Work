# Christopher M. Bache — LSD and the Mind of the Universe

> [!summary]
> A systematic first-person archive of 73 high-dose LSD sessions conducted across twenty years, interpreted through collective, archetypal, and cosmic registers of mind.

## Bibliographic record

Christopher M. Bache, *LSD and the Mind of the Universe: Diamonds from Heaven*, foreword by Ervin Laszlo (Park Street Press, 2019). Paperback ISBN 978-1-62055-970-3, 352 pages. Metadata verified against the [publisher's record](https://www.innertraditions.com/books/lsd-and-the-mind-of-the-universe).

## Permitted argumentative load

Bache belongs in the experiential archive surrounding [[Psychoid Number]] and [[Arche-Topos as Differential Field]]. The work documents repeated reports of transitions from individual consciousness through collective and archetypal strata toward what Bache interprets as a unified field or mind of the universe. It can establish that disciplined first-person psychedelic inquiry produces persistent, structured claims relevant to the psyche/matter boundary and the “world is mind” horizon.

It does not prove the QL theorem-units, the ontological truth of a cosmic mind, or the identity of psychedelic imagery with mathematical structure. Its role is **supportive disclosure**: to return an academically displaced region of experience to the evidential field and let the derived architecture state clearly what such experiences would mean if their reported structures survive comparative and critical analysis.

## Research use

- Compare Bache's recurrent collective/archetypal strata with Jung's collective unconscious and psychoid archetype.
- Track whether death–rebirth, field-unity, luminosity, and nested scale appear as stable phenomenological attractors rather than isolated motifs.
- Keep pharmacology, set/setting, interpretation, memory, and retrospective narrative available as causal and epistemic counter-accounts.
