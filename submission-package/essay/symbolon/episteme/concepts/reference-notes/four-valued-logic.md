# Four-Valued Logic

> [!summary]
> Four-valued systems can distinguish true, false, both, and neither, preserving inconsistency or incomplete information without classical explosion.

## Essay use

The family supplies a formal neighbour for QL’s refusal to reduce every opposition immediately to one classical binary. It belongs in [[28-s3-p3-projective-dimensional-reframing|§3 · #3 — Projective Completion and Dimensional Reframing]] beside [[Division Pluralisms]], not as proof that QL is identical to Belnap, Dunn, or a tetralemma.
