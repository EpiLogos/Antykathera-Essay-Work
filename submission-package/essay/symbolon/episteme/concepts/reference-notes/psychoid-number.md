# Psychoid Number

> [!summary]
> Jung, Pauli, and von Franz locate number and archetypal order at the threshold where psyche and matter have not yet divided into independent explanatory domains; QL derives a formal differential field for that psychoid problem.

**Reference kind:** psychology. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan]].

## Argumentative load

The historical source claim is that Jung, Pauli, and von Franz treated archetype and number as ordering factors whose reach exceeds a purely subjective psychology. The essay does not ask those authorities to prove QL. The [[Core Theorem Bridge]] supplies the derivation; the psychoid lineage supplies the already-articulated problem to which [[Arche-Topos as Differential Field]] gives a formal answer.

Psychological image and mathematical regularity are therefore read as distinct deposits of one differentiating field. Their distinctness matters: the psychoid claim would be emptied if psyche were reduced to physics, physics to imagination, or both to a catalogue of numerical correspondences.

## Supporting disclosures

[[Christopher M. Bache — LSD and the Mind of the Universe]] and other psychedelic, psi, visionary, and synaesthetic records return displaced experiences to the phenomenological archive. They disclose regions requiring placement within a psychology adequate to the psychoid; they do not replace theorem, controlled study, or source criticism.
