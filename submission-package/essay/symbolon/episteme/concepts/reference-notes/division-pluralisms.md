# Division Pluralisms

> [!summary]
> Division by zero has no single context-free resolution: ordinary fields leave it undefined, while extended or projective structures may adjoin signed infinities, one point at infinity, or other exceptional values under altered rules.

## Essay use

The plurality is evidence that an exceptional operation discloses the structure a formalism preserves. It supports [[Dimensional Reframing at Zero and Infinity]] and [[17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math|§2 · #4 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics]].

## Claim boundary

QL’s `1/0` is an oriented meta-sign, not ordinary division. Every mathematical example must name its structure and changed algebraic laws.
