# Cymatics and Standing Waves

> [!summary]
> Acoustic standing waves generate visible nodal organisation in a material medium under particular boundary conditions. This provides a bounded visual-auditory bridge for the arche-topos.

## Use rule

- **Derived after citation:** standing-wave and nodal-pattern physics.
- **Argued:** one relation can receive audible and visible renderings.
- **Offered:** pre-modal psychoid unity of seeing, sounding, counting, and naming.

Cymatics does not prove that sound, light, and consciousness are physically identical. The medium and boundary conditions are constitutive and must remain visible.
