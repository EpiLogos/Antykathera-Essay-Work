# Gebser Diaphaneity

> [!summary]
> Diaphaneity names the integral transparency through which structures of consciousness become present with their origins and limits visible, rather than being totalised by the mental-rational perspective.

**Reference kind:** philosophy. **Evidence status:** bibliography-seed.

**Master context:** [[The Return of Zero — Central Argument Plan#§0/1 · #4 — Gebserian diaphaneity and the integral demand]] and [[Computational Process Ontology]].

## Function in the essay

Gebser gives the process lineage its stage-shift without turning it into a ladder of obsolete epochs. The archaic, magic, mythic, mental-rational, and integral structures are modes of consciousness whose relations can become diaphanous; integration means transparency and co-presence, not the rational accumulation of enough parts to simulate the whole.

This supplies the governing rule for cross-register argument. Mathematics, phenomenology, psychology, myth, and technical design remain non-identical apertures. Each must become transparent to the origin, limits, exclusions, and other structures that condition it. MEF instantiates this demand by attaching claims to lenses and keeping divergence visible.

## Limits

Exact Gebserian terminology, chronology, and quotations require edition-specific verification. The essay may use diaphaneity as a sourced philosophical warrant for transparent co-presence; it should not recruit Gebser as prior authority for QL's formal count.
