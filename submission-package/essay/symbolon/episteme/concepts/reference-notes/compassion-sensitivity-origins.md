# Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins

> [!summary]
> Frank's formulation: compassion is loving sensitivity to the origins and genesis of what is perceived. Source trail: [[revision-notes-trust-and-f-blocks#5.1 — "Compassion as sensitivity to origins" — RESOLVED]].

## Technical vocation

Compassion does not require a machine to claim empathy. It requires a harness to keep determinations answerable to their genesis: sources, prompts, exclusions, communities, histories, wounds, purposes, permissions, and consequences. Provenance is not compassion, but it is compassion's minimum technical body.

## Relation to Epi-Logos

- Eros of Logos: desire of articulated knowledge to know itself.
- Compassion: the non-devouring manner of return.
- Epistemic humility: the `1` releases its claim to occupy `0`.
- Agentic praxis: origins become operational fields in review and governance.
