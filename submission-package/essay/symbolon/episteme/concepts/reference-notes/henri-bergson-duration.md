# Henri Bergson — Duration and Creative Evolution

> [!summary]
> Duration is qualitative continuity: the past is carried into a present whose character changes through what arrives. A new note changes the heard phrase. The process therefore has an inheritance that a row of completed positions can record without performing the synthesis it records.

**Reference kind:** philosophy. The selected source is the [Pogson digital carrier of *Time and Free Will*](../../sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/SOURCE.md). Its chapter II passage units supply the duration and clock comparisons here. *Matter and Memory* and *Creative Evolution* are distinct branches of the source trail below.

**Master context:** [Computational Process Ontology](../../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md), [Conditions of Worldhood](../C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) and [the Process dossier](../../dossiers/process.md).

## The heard whole changes through its succession

[Bergson’s melody](../../sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/SOURCE.md#bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson-q001) differentiates two objects. Conscious states interpenetrate: dwelling on a note changes the quality of the whole phrase. Setting their positions beside one another makes a spatial representation whose separated units can be inspected and counted. Retaining the earlier state in the present is part of the qualitative formation, rather than an archive subsequently attached to an otherwise complete present.

[The clock and motion analysis](../../sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/SOURCE.md#bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson-q002) then distinguishes counted simultaneities, the positions traversed and the act of traversing. Memory lets the succession be held; juxtaposition gives its representation another office. A chronology and a lived continuity can concern the same passage while disclosing different relations within it.

## The runtime inherits a changed field

The essay’s [native process grammar](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md) makes an achieved determination a condition of further determination. An agent’s runtime therefore has a state-changing history: earlier cuts can change what is available for later cuts. A corrected source changes which attribution can be warranted; a retained objection changes what a later question means; a permission changes the possible act. The record becomes effective when the next operation inherits the changed condition.

At the grain of a source comparison, the agent is the functional knower, its retrieved text and comparison rule are means, and the claim being compared is known. A received correction can enter the local reference used by a further comparison. In the containing person’s inquiry that agent is itself a means; inquiry into its rule makes the local centre known in another act. [Objective Internality](../C41-Objective-Internality.md) holds these recursive offices within Life / Mind, with Subjective Immediacy as the knower condition of their appearing.

Bergson sharpens this return by requiring attention to changed significance. Restoring an attribution can alter what the whole argument now means. Recording the correction as one later event supplies chronology; allowing it to reorganise the next reading supplies the consequential inheritance the native process account develops.

## Different temporal operations remain active together

Whitehead supplies another process object: a novel occasion reaches satisfaction and becomes available for further becoming. Its singularity and objective availability have their own metaphysical standing. Gebser’s mutation and diaphanous co-presence of structures have a historical and experiential office. Bergson gives lived continuity and the critique of spatialising succession. [The Process dossier](../../dossiers/process.md#bergson-whitehead-gebser-comparison) holds these differences together; [Symbolon Dynamics](../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE.md) supplies the native relation of present configuration, actual trajectory and changed future possibilities.

QL’s count and slash operations derive from their native theorem. The temporal comparison makes their return more exact: the achieved form brings transformed history into the next field, rather than resetting to an earlier coordinate. The next inquiry can recover both the sequence and what the sequence has changed.
