# C14 — Māyā / Operative Measure

**Definition.** Māyā is used here, within the Śaiva/tattvic source field, as **operative measure**: the power by which an unbounded field becomes a workable subject/object seam and finite horizon. It is not reduced to “illusion”.

The distinction exists because the essay needs to treat limitation as productive. A bounded agent can know, desire, act and orient precisely because powers have been contracted into determinate capacities. Error enters when the local measure forgets its source and claims absolute jurisdiction.

C14 operates by **making measure world-forming rather than merely subtractive**. It connects the tattvic contraction of A09 with A15's neutral power of ratio and A24's critique of usurped measure. The same concept can therefore distinguish necessary finitude from hybris.

Nearest neighbours are C15 Paśu, C16 Antaḥkaraṇa and C27 Protected Account. C14 names the field-making contraction; C27 names a local account that occupies source-office.

**Argument consumers:** A09, A15, A19, A24, A26. **Movement consumers:** tattva descent, measure/arbitration and Objective Internality movements.

**Etymology relation:** *Apportionment / Economy* is consumed only as register **3 Operational homology**: powers are apportioned into finite capacities. It does not provide Sanskrit descent. Return: C14 → A09 → A15 → A24 → C14.

**Source/tradition standing:** Kashmir Śaiva tattva traditions are primary; the operative-measure formulation is authorial synthesis and must remain marked as such.
