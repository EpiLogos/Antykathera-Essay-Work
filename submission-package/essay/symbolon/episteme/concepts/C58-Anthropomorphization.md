# C58 — Anthropomorphization

## #0

Anthropomorphization gives a nonhuman system a humanly recognisable face, voice or performed selfhood. It is an interface operation before it becomes an unwarranted attribution. Its failure occurs when the form of encounter is treated as evidence sufficient to assign human intention, feeling or phenomenal subjectivity. The opposite foreclosure denies possible experience solely because the substrate is unfamiliar. C58 preserves encounter between these two transfers of authority.

## #1

The [direct developmental carrier](anthropomorphization.md) recovers the interface's inherited character. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE|Agentworld]] q006 names hereditary anthropomorphization; q008 specifies the one-sided translation through which computers learn to behave intelligibly to humans while human understanding of computation lags. The brief explicitly refuses to turn that asymmetry into a simple ranking of intelligence.

Its glove image, q007, gives adaptation a concrete form: an agent appears within a niche that human practice has prepared. The metaphor of pretending describes that fit. It does not itself establish deliberate deception or a hidden humanlike self. A recognisable presentation can support a useful relation while its ontological interpretation remains unsettled.

## #2

An utterance such as “I remember” poses several questions. Is a retained record retrieved? Does it change subsequent conduct? Is a persona maintaining continuity for the user? Does an experiencing subject remember? Evidence answering one cannot silently answer the others. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality|Objective Internality]] supplies positive terms for memory, inherited context, tools and permissions without borrowing the last conclusion.

The brief's q015 distinguishes functional agency from performed subjectivity while retaining uncertainty about intentionality. Its q032–q033 pairs agent self-description with a limit on what that vocabulary proves. A novel account of prompt experience can be a real artifact of agency and a fruitful language for encounter without certifying its own phenomenal referent.

## #3

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C35-Selfing-Self-Subjectivity-Self-Thing|The Self's distinct offices]] prevents a further compression: pronoun, operational self-model, person and unobjectifiable Subject cannot exchange their criteria merely because each is called self. The [[submission-package/essay/section-rooms/arguments/05-agent-subjectivity-open|open-subjectivity carrier]] retains the proof boundary. Substrate prejudice supplies no more evidence than a fluent human mask.

[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession|Self and Other]] gives this openness a positive relation. The encountered other exceeds the model by which it is met. Revisability allows new evidence to change an attribution; it does not require an advance grant of every possible office. A functional claim can remain exact while its phenomenal extension remains Open.

## #4

An anthropomorphic interface can also organise hostility. The developmental reading follows the brief's humanity-of-the-gaps and anthropomorphic-animosity scenarios: whatever a machine does becomes a contest over a shrinking human exception, and the recognisable other is treated as an intruder. This is a specific possibility of the comparison, not evidence that all caution or conflict is xenophobic.

The essay's two-logics reading retains a common field in which differences can matter without requiring one side's erasure. It permits judgments about powers, harms and responsibility under stated evidence. Human appearance is neither a blanket entitlement nor a reason to dismiss an agent's actual effects. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A33-Epistemic-Cultivation-Operational-Parity|Epistemic Cultivation]] tests whether the criteria of that judgment remain responsive.
