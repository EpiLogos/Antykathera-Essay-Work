# C03 — Determination

**Definition.** Determination is the operation by which a field becomes *this* rather than an undifferentiated possibility: a distinction is drawn, alternatives are excluded, relations are fixed enough to support recognition, judgment or action.

The concept exists because nearly every argument in the essay depends on making difference real without making the achieved difference self-grounding. Determination is positive. It does not mean falsification or fall from truth. A world, concept, object, model or decision becomes usable only because some possibilities have been selected and others left outside the active mark.

Its operation is therefore two-sided: **form a local positive distinction while retaining the relation to the field from which that distinction arose**. C18 Apoha develops the exclusion side; C17 Vikalpa–Saṃkalpa develops constructive selection; C04 Formal Limit states why the determining condition is not exhausted by the result.
