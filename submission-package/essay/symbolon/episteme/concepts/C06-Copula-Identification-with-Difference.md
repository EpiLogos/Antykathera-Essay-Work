# C06 — Copula / Identification-with-Difference

**Definition.** The copula names the operation by which distinct presentations are held as the same without erasing the difference through which identity is asserted. Identification-with-difference is the canonical form of that operation.

The distinction exists because equality is frequently treated as indistinguishability. Yet even `A is A` traverses two inscriptions or moments. C06 preserves the seam: the relation succeeds because the terms can answer to one another across difference. This makes self-identity possible without requiring the Subject to become a completed self-object.

Its operation is **relational sameness across non-coincidence**. C05 supplies the non-coincidence; C19 Recognition supplies achieved return; C50 Dia/Syn later develops the conjugate logics that cut and re-relate a differentiated field.

Nearest failure-neighbours are **C23 Severance** and **C24 Fusion**: severance treats difference as loss of relation; fusion treats relation as loss of difference.
