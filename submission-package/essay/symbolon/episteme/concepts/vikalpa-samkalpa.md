# Vikalpa / Saṃkalpa

## Definition

Both terms grow from the root `√kḷp` — to fit, arrange, order, form, prepare. The root matters: it means neither "carry" nor "cut" but *construct*, so everything it generates is an **arrangement** — revisable formation, not irreversible severance. The prefix `vi-` (apart, asunder, in two directions — an etymological cousin of *dvi*, two) yields ***vikalpa***: forming-apart, the articulation by which a field is rendered as qualified alternatives, this-rather-than-that determinations, conceptual constructions. The prefix `sam-` (together, convergent) yields ***saṃkalpa***: together-formation, the gathering of selected differences into an intention, resolve, commitment, or workable world. Sanskrit thus holds the essay's two logics inside one root — dividing and uniting as two modes of a single constructive power — which is why the pair can serve as an exact cognitive cartography rather than a decorative gloss. *(The etymological reading follows [[taylor-claude-2026-derivational-chat|the derivational chat record]] — internal provenance; standard lexical glosses support the operational use, and doctrinal exegesis of the classical treatments remains acquisition work.)*

Two boundary terms complete the family. ***Nirvikalpa*** names the non-conceptual register — cognition, or awareness, without constructed alternatives — used in the essay strictly as a comparative limit, never as a shortcut around argument. ***Śuddha-vikalpa*** ("purified thought-construct") is the Pratyabhijñā refinement: a formation whose content is non-dual and whose function is to corrode its contrary formation — the entrenched conviction of limitation — until the series consumes itself. Thought as medicine for thought: a vikalpa aimed at its own obsolescence. It is the doctrinal ancestor of the essay's demand that determinations carry their own revision conditions.

## In the argument

At [[09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa|§0 · #2]] the pair makes cognition constructive without being arbitrary: each act inherits a field, makes a cut, and alters the field available to the next act. Artificial agency becomes philosophically relevant exactly here, because a model objectively exhibits the sequence at the functional register — differentiations become latent relations, selections become outputs, outputs alter context — while whether the operation is lived through a phenomenal inner world remains a separate, open question. In the [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#XI. Computational Process Ontology — QL Formalises the Process, MEF Runs It|computational-process cartography]], vikalpa is the cognitive face of dia-ballein (distinguish, qualify, exclude, select) and saṃkalpa the world-forming face of sym-ballein (relate, recompose, contextualise, commit); [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/apoha|apoha]] supplies their semantic law, and the [[13-tattvic-differential-field|tattvic field]] places all three inside manifestation rather than treating them as late mental commentary. The pathologies are symmetrical: a vikalpa that forgets it was produced claims self-subsistence; a saṃkalpa that forgets its exclusions presents its arrangement as the only possible world. The thread pays off at [[38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax|§5 · #1]], where differential selection gives the same operations a technical body.
