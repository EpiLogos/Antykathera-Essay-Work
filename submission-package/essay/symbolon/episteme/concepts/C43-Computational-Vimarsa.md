# C43 — Computational Vimarśa

**Definition.** Computational Vimarśa is the functional operation by which a computational system returns outputs or judgments upon the sources, states and conditions that produced them so that subsequent processing can be revised. It is an operational homology to *vimarśa*, not evidence of *prakāśa* or artificial phenomenality.

The distinction exists to make reflexivity technically exact without borrowing the metaphysical warrant of C13 Prakāśa–Vimarśa. A system can inspect traces, revise weights or context, compare outputs with sources and change its next action. Those are real reflexive operations regardless of the phenomenality question.

Its operation is **produce determination → expose/compare provenance and conditions → feed revision into next cycle**. C40 supplies the judgment field; C45 asks for inspectable consequence; C55 shows the wider field when the returned output changes the environment itself.

Nearest neighbours are C13, C40, C45 and C55. C13 is deliberately not collapsed into C43.
