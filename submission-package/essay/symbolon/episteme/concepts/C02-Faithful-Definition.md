# C02 — Faithful Definition

**Definition.** A faithful definition determines strongly enough to discriminate its object while preserving the conditions and formal limit of the determining act. It is neither exhaustive possession nor deliberate vagueness.

The distinction exists because definition can fail in opposite directions. An account that refuses determination cannot guide thought; an account that treats its achieved boundary as the whole of what is named converts clarity into enclosure. C02's operation is **accountable determination**: identify what the term includes and excludes, state the position and mediation through which the distinction is made, and retain a route by which renewed encounter can revise the account.

The nearest complement is **C03 Determination**; the governing limit is **C04 Formal Limit**. **C21 Living Symbol / Idol** supplies the symbolic failure mode: the same form becomes idolatrous when it occupies the source-office rather than remaining a disclosure of it. **C48 Trust / Faith under Formal Limit** extends faithful definition through time and action.
