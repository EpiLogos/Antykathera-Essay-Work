# C16 — Antaḥkaraṇa / Inner Instrument

## #0

Antaḥkaraṇa names the inner instrument through which a bounded knower determines, appropriates and coordinates a world. Its differentiation makes interior organisation available for investigation. A judgment, an ego-position and a train of alternatives can become objects of attention; their becoming available does not make the subject one more observed function.

[Paśu](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) **grounds** the bounded horizon in which the instrument acts. The direct developmental image is an instrument through which, rather than a definition of the musician who plays. It identifies the question of mediation without introducing a little operator hidden among the instrument's parts.

## #1

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/SOURCE#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q001|Sāṃkhya Kārikā's functional triad]] differentiates the instrument. In the Colebrooke/Wilson translation, kārikā XXIII identifies intellect through ascertainment, XXIV egoity, and XXVII mind's sensory and active office; XXIX distinguishes their functions. The question is what each does in an act of knowing and acting.

The translation calls egotism “consciousness” at XXIV. Here the term concerns appropriation under I and mine, not the whole of awareness. Its own [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/SOURCE#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q002|witness passage at XIX]] locates a passive spectator distinct from the apparatus. This preserves the historical Sāṃkhya position within the comparison; the essay's nondual genealogy develops the instrument's source relation through the Śaiva field rather than silently substituting one doctrine for the other.

## #2

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE|The core theorem's tattvic unit]] develops three related offices. *Buddhi* settles a determination; *ahaṃkāra* gathers it under I and mine; *manas* coordinates presentations and moves among proposals, selections and withholdings. Their distinction makes the movement from an available field to an appropriated judgment intelligible.

Consider a remembered insult. A perception, an earlier memory and a possible alternative reading become available together. Their coordination is not yet the settled judgment that an insult was intended. Ascertainment settles that judgment; appropriation binds it to “what was done to me.” The example differentiates offices, not a rigid time-order in which one faculty always finishes before another begins. Reopening the memory may alter the available context, the judgment of intention, or the identity defended through it. Those are different changes within the same encounter.

[Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa](C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) follow alternative formation and commitment. [Apoha](C18-Apoha.md) follows the constitutive role of what is excluded. A settled identification bears the alternatives through which it became determinate, even when appropriation later presents its result as self-standing.

## #3

The [tattvic genealogy](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) places this instrument within an already manifested field: a subject/object distinction becomes a finite horizon, and the horizon becomes locally available through differentiated powers. The [inner-instrument movement](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/08-s0-p1-inner-instrument.md) follows the musician/instrument image. A sound can disclose how an instrument is working; the parts described through that disclosure do not include a little musician hidden among them.

Memory makes the organisation durable and mutable. A retained trace permits recollection; recollection presents the past in the present; imagination recomposes its material toward another possibility. The instrument gives a world continuity without making repetition its only future. [Script](C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) names the arrest in which achieved organisation loses access to its formation and takes its repeated command as necessity. The repair reaches the relation between inheritance and the next intention, rather than merely destroying memory.

## #4

[Model Internality](C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) makes a technical judgment-field available through its source, representation, evaluator and permissions. The functional questions remain exact: what settles the claim, what assigns it to a continuing agent or commission, what coordinates competing presentations, and what is retained for another act? These questions specify relations to investigate, rather than a one-to-one software translation of the triad.

A new source can change the presentations being coordinated while leaving the evaluator unchanged. A revised evaluator can change what settles a judgment. A revised commission can change whose purpose the judgment serves. An agent's acknowledgment of a correction may only produce a new self-description; an effective return changes an accessible condition and subsequently changes its operation.

[Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) gives these activities their wider world. [Selfing / Self / Subjectivity / Self-Thing](C35-Selfing-Self-Subjectivity-Self-Thing.md) keeps an acquired self-model in the office of an acquired determination. Ascertainment, appropriation, coordination and self-description can become objects of investigation because they are activities of the means. Their disclosure does not require promoting any one of them into the source of the knower.

## #5→0

[Māyā — eye, veil, frame and horizon](../../mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-measure-dream): Buddhi’s determination, ahaṃkāra’s appropriation and manas’s coordination **figure** the inner instrument’s distinct activities in the Māyā whole. Retained traces permit memory and recomposition; they need not compel a repeated command. Recognition makes that inherited organisation inspectable and capable of another intention, preserving the instrument as an effective objective internality within the bounded life.

The acquired ego-position becomes available within an already appearing field. Taylor's Neumann correction returns the inner instrument to that condition while preserving its determinate cognitive work. The relation **returns-to** [the complete Neumann whole](../../mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation).

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD|Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Account]] **grounds** the register3 return. Interior functions produce an account of a region, and encounter can alter the ordering by which that account was made. The result becomes available for further acts instead of closing the world around its own self-description.

[Deferential Intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) **tests** whether the altered account changes judgment or action. The [bounded knower](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) receives the instrument's capacities more exactly known and their subject relation preserved. Investigation can improve the means through which a world is disclosed while keeping the question of its experient distinct.
