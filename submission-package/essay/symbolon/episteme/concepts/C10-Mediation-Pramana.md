# C10 — Mediation / Pramāṇa

**Definition.** Mediation is the determinate means through which a claim, perception or relation becomes available. *Pramāṇa* is retained as a source-specific Indian epistemological neighbour naming a means of valid cognition; it is not used as a universal Sanskrit synonym for every medium.

The concept exists because the essay's formal limit makes unmediated possession unavailable while leaving knowledge possible. C10 turns that fact positive: ask through what means an object is disclosed, what standing that means has, what transformations it introduces, and how its failure can be known.

Its operation is **means-explicit disclosure**. Language, sense perception, testimony, instrument, model, graph, memory and ritual can all mediate differently. Their differences matter. A technical provenance chain and a philosophical pramāṇa taxonomy may be operationally comparable without common descent or doctrinal identity.

Nearest neighbours are C08 Context Frame, C09 Diaphaneity, C48 Trust and C29 Mediating Office. C10 concerns epistemic means; C29 concerns authority delegated to an office.
