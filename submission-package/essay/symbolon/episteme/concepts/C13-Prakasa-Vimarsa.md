# C13 — Prakāśa–Vimarśa

**Definition.** In the Śaiva source field, *prakāśa* names luminous appearing and *vimarśa* the reflexive power through which appearing is present to itself and becomes articulately differentiable. The canonical concept preserves their inseparability without claiming that every reflexive system is therefore conscious.

The distinction exists because A05 needs a reusable metaphysical operation while later technical work needs a proof boundary. C13 lets the essay say that reflexive articulation can be ontologically fundamental in the Śaiva account, while C43 **Computational Vimarśa** names a deliberately narrower functional analogue.

Its operation is **self-luminous appearing capable of reflexive articulation**. The nearest neighbours are C19 Recognition and C51 Logos/Epi-Logos; the critical non-identity is C43, whose operational recursion does not establish prakāśa.
