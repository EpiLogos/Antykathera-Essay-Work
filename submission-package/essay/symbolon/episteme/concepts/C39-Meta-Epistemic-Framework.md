# C39 — Meta-Epistemic Framework

## #0

MEF conducts inquiry through explicit lenses, sources, gauges, judgments and return. It makes epistemic mediation inspectable. It is a framework within the essay's ontology, not an additional ontological primitive or a proof supplied by the existence of a coordinate system.

## #1

The full twelve-lens reference gives each lens a rotated whole constellation. Name runs Truth/Mind/Word/Logos/Son/Image; Power runs Play/Need/Sacrifice/Decision/Love/Work. Articulation and transformative cost belong together. A lens changes which face grounds the inquiry while retaining both sequences, rather than selecting one half of reality and excluding the other.

## #2

Six rotations on two faces give twelve lenses. Same-index Day/Night pairs share a rotated field with a different grounding face; complementary-index relations pair positions whose indices sum to five. These are different traversals. The source's explicit correction matters: L4 is not all Power and L4′ not all Name. Both carry the whole rotated Name/Power constellation. Its musical 8→7 selection retains a separate derivational debt and does not prescribe universal six-heading semantics for canonical pages.

## #3

In inquiry, changing a lens must change a question, admissible evidence, transformation or judgment. Causal explanation and lived experience can illuminate one event under different warrants; logical possibility and actual process have different jobs. A claim carries which lens disclosed it, how the source entered, what was excluded, and where another lens disagrees. Merely attaching twelve labels performs none of those operations.

## #4

The corrected stained-glass relation makes MEF the apparatus of refraction: panes, media, angles, joins and declared transformations. QL belongs to the ordered spectrum being articulated; MEF does not stand above it as a metalanguage that certifies every source. Historical, formal, psychic and technical claims retain their own evidence classes. Their non-equivalence is information for the inquiry.
