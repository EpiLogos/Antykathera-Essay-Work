# C34 — Individuation

**Definition.** Individuation is the process by which a local person or culture becomes more capable of bearing the differentiated relations through which it is itself, without either dissolving into those relations or installing one part as the whole.

The distinction exists to resist the modern shorthand that treats individuation as increased personal independence. The canonical operation is integrative but not fusional: complexes, images, oppositions and inherited conditions are recognised, re-situated and allowed to transform the containing self-relation.

Its operation is **differentiate → encounter autonomous parts/opposites → recognise provenance → recompose without erasure**. C19 Recognition supplies the return; C31 Complex and C33 Image supply local formations; C36 Complexio Oppositorum names the containing capacity that can bear unresolved opposition.

Nearest neighbours are C19, C31, C35 and C36. Cultural extension appears at C59 rather than being smuggled into the individual concept.

**Argument consumers:** A21, A27, A35–A36. **Movement consumers:** Jung/recognition, Self/Other, cultural vocation and final return.
