# Dialogues

Dialogues records the formation of the work's own ideas through dialogue — especially dialogue between Frank and AI assistants. These records are **provenance of thinking, never evidence**. A dialogue record answers how an idea came to be held, tested, or transformed; it cannot make a claim about the world, a text, or a tradition.

Each record declares participants, date, the raw transcript locator, a contribution ledger (which formulations are Frank's, which are the assistant's), and an acceptance state: `raw`, `quilt-proposed`, or `canon-referenced`. The quotation gate never applies: quotations of the dialogue are quotations of the conversation, never of the named public works, and every assistant-provided historical, textual, or technical statement is a verification lead until it resolves to a real source record.

The register keeps this distinct from Sources. Sources are warrant machinery: what may be cited, quoted, and attributed. Dialogues are formation records: what the work became through encounter. Where a dialogue contributes an idea that enters the argument, the accepted formulation is argued in its canonical node and sourced there.
