# Indian Philosophy — Articulation, Agency and the Sourceward Turn

## #0 — Appearing already carries the power of an account

An articulated world has functions, distinctions and forms which can themselves become objects of inquiry. The question is how their source remains present through that articulation. [Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **grounds** the positive metaphysical relation: luminous appearing and its self-apprehending power belong together. [Vāk](../arguments/A06-Vak.md) **extends** it into speech; [the tattvic differential field](../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) **extends** it into finite worldhood. Their source-specific coordination gives the question a differentiated body. The contemporary comparison follows the powers of appearing, articulation and finite worldhood into a changed account of agency.

The [Indian philosophy history companion](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/indian-philosophy/DEVELOPMENT.md) **historicises** the distinct Śaiva, Sāṃkhya, Buddhist, arithmetic and modern reception carriers. The comparison carries appearing, knowing, speaking, acting and recognising through their differences without losing the direction of the author's claim. The [native order of dependence](../arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md) **grounds** that direction:

**Subject/Consciousness → Mind/Objective Internality → Object.**

The order is ontological. An object available to explanation is already determinate within appearing; installing such an object as the producer of appearing reverses the dependency used by the explanation. Mind names the articulated middle through which objects become available. It has real structure and causal efficacy, and it can be investigated. The claim does not reduce to uncertainty about whether representations match an independently completed world. Nor does it give the private ego the source-office: that ego and its self-image are themselves determinations.

[Symbol / Account / Trust whole](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md) **compares** this source relation through its three terms and four operations: Symbol answers to source; Account does not replace source; Trust keeps the return route active; Account re-enters source-field. Its [historical branches](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md) **qualify** the comparison. Corresponding tokens, accounts and entrusted offices have particular histories. Their sources do not establish a Sanskrit doctrine, and the Śaiva relation does not become a theory derived from a broken token. What crosses is the demand that achieved form retain its originating and returning relations.

## #1 — Reflexive awareness gives differentiation its active middle

The most substantial source-local carrier is [Dyczkowski's *Doctrine of Vibration*](../sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE.md#transcribed-material). It **sources** an exposition in which appearance is internally differentiated without being deposited into consciousness from an independent outside. The mirror has two offices. Images remain distinct within one field; but an ordinary mirror neither knows its display nor generates the external objects reflected in it. At pp. 66–70, these limits distinguish the simile from the source's active consciousness. Vimarśa belongs to the appearing itself. Adding a second consciousness to know the first would merely renew the demand for a knower.

This power has several distinct activities. At pp. 71–72, inward I-awareness differs from awareness directed toward a determinate this. A retained affection leaves a trace; recollection brings it into the present; imagination selects and recomposes retained material. A sequence of movement or speech can be known as one action because knowledge holds its successive moments in relation. Memory, imagination, comparison and continuity each do work which the word “reflexivity” alone would leave unspecified.

At p. 74, consciousness negates its nature, identifies with another, merges the terms and then denies their merger. The last operation prevents the union itself from becoming a final fixed object. Division, unity-in-difference and undivided unity are three levels through which this activity is described; they are distinct from the four operations. The native authorial cross-reading takes Prakāśa and Vimarśa as two conditions beside those four operations. The [eight-determination Matheme](../../matheme/ql/eight-determinations.md) **grounds** the QL count before the comparison. Dyczkowski's exposition remains source-matched material, not a claim that the book teaches Taylor's `4+2`.

The Śiva–Śakti–object triad names another precise office in the author's field. Subject is the condition of manifestation; Śakti articulates operative power and mediation; the object is determinate manifestation. The third term does not occupy an empty space between two independent substances. It makes their relation intelligible. Likewise, *kartṛ*, the doer/experient, cannot be counted as one more *kārya*, a product of action. Dyczkowski's p. 72 distinction between outward *karma-vimarśa* and unrestricted *kriyā-vimarśa* gives agency a source-specific articulation. The author's subject–operative psyche–object relation articulates condition, mediating power and determinate manifestation. A technical use must locate those offices at its stated scale, retaining the difference between a locally knowing agent and the means it supplies within a containing inquiry.

The source's p. 75 gives contraction two directions. Manifestation and withdrawal are a vertical account; movement toward and away from an object is a horizontal account within perception. Will, knowledge and action remain differentiated powers across them. Frank's preserved Dyczkowski encounter asks to pursue these powers through Spanda and agency into pp. 90–91. That direction remains live. The acquired transcript stops during p. 81, despite its broader title, so the received account does not include the later pp. 90–91 discussion or authenticate wording attributed to it.

This **returns-to** [awareness bending toward display](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/07-s0-p0-awareness-bends-display.md): the slash is active mediation before it is treated as a separator. The source-answerable Symbol receives a sharper burden from the comparison. Preserving a picture is insufficient to describe the source's account of knowing; the knowing and differentiating power must remain within the relation.

## #2 — Speech forms a world which can answer its speaker

[Singh's *Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa* house](../sources/indian-philosophy/abhinavagupta/abhinavagupta-singh-1988-paratrisika-vivarana/SOURCE.md) **sources** the Vāk branch through the P2 and P3 authorial notebooks. Introduction, translated commentary and Taylor's encounter have different speakers. Parā, Paśyantī, Madhyamā and Vaikharī carry the descent from speech-power through formative and mental articulation into utterance. Paśyantī and Nāda are important to the author's comparison because sight and sound have not yet hardened into separate finished objects. Passage-level verification in the selected edition remains owed; the notebooks already disclose why the author needs the relation.

P3 develops address through I, you and it. A person spoken of can become the you addressed and reply as I. A mountain addressed and then spoken through in the first person makes the shift vivid in the encounter. The change concerns the relation of speaking, not the replacement of one substance by another. In the native personed circuit, `1–2–3 / 4–5–0`, context holds these exchanges, their claims are voided as exclusive, their unity is recognised and the relation returns. Context is not a fourth pronoun.

[Watson's source house](../sources/psychology/watson/watson-1998-resonance-of-emptiness/SOURCE.md) **compares** the interpersonal pressure through its Buddhist and psychotherapeutic reception. Frank's protected encounter reads the Bāhiya instruction as interrupting appropriation while still addressing someone capable of receiving it. It distinguishes the self-thing from the non-objectifiable Subject. The positive consequence is that loosening identification need not erase the recipient of an utterance. This is Taylor's argument; direct sutta wording is still a primary-source task. Watson's later Gans–Levinas passage brings a distinct ethical demand: the other's response can exceed the representation by which the ego has made that other available for itself. The thesis, selected book and nested authors retain separate collation boundaries.

[Kripke's source house and protected encounter](../sources/analytic-philosophy/kripke/kripke-1981-naming-and-necessity/SOURCE.md) **compare** another distinction within naming. A shared sound-name need not identify one bearer. A referent can remain fixed for one reading of an utterance while circumstance changes which person says I or receives you. Taylor develops the pre-nominal count, the copula's is/equals relation and name–instance–circumstance from this pressure. Kripke's rigidity supplies its own philosophical comparison; the house has no verified passage cards and does not attribute the QL development to him.

P2's kaleidoscope provides the corresponding discipline of reading. A term can become an orbital centre through which the surrounding field discloses itself, then be decentered so that the whole can disclose another relation. Manifold interpretation remains accountable to what changes and what persists through the turns. A word which becomes exact by excluding every other possible relation has lost this symbolic office; a word whose meaning varies arbitrarily has lost the constraints that make the turns intelligible.

[Logos branch](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md#logos-articulated-account) **compares** the achieved account with this descent and reply. The [historical branch](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md#logos) **qualifies** Greek attestation and the separate Vāk/Logos research. The sixfold Fides→Topos→Logos→Nomos→Natio→Credere→Fides keeps speaking situated, ordered and answerable within renewed reliance. The descent and personed reply change the circumstances of the next utterance: what has been said can now be received, corrected or answered from another position. Taylor's reading of Śaivism as a culmination of Logos remains an authored comparative commitment whose historical transmission claims require independent evidence.

The situated utterance **returns-to** [Name-through-Count](../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#name-through-count) through the difference between a shared sound-name and the situated bearer of an utterance. Vāk’s descent and the I–you–it exchange make naming an act within an effective circumstance; exclusion keeps the positive determination answerable to its alternatives. The returned account must say which bearer, contrast and criterion it has retained. The historical accounts of rigidity, Buddhist semantics and Śaiva articulation remain distinct from Taylor’s pre-nominal count.

## #3 — Effective contraction makes the comparative judgment exact

The [Sāṃkhya kārikā cards](../sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/SOURCE.md#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q001) **source** the inner instrument's differentiation: buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates, manas coordinates. Kārikā XIX keeps puruṣa's passive witness distinct. The apparatus is real in its functional office; identifying it with the Subject would cancel the very distinction it lets inquiry make. The source is Sāṃkhya. Its functional triad does not by itself warrant the entire Śaiva anatomy.

The author's comparative judgment is developed through five pressures: memory must retain the relation to the earlier act; comparison must hold differences together; exclusion must make absent alternatives consequential; action must connect intention, execution and result; arising and perishing must themselves appear. The critique of every self-subsistent self-thing remains binding. By the QL criterion of wholeness, Śaiva articulation more fully serves the essay's task because it gives positive formal places to the experient and these powers without reinstalling a proprietor behind them. This comparative judgment concerns those five powers under the author's wholeness criterion. A Buddhist reply must be received in the actual school's terms and sources; resemblance among negations alone cannot decide its account of memory, comparison or agency.

The tattvic development follows the positive order into finite capacities. Universal orientations of I and this differ from ahaṃkāra's appropriation of a local this as all that I am. Māyā gives the operative horizon; the kañcukas delimit efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order. [Māyā / Operative Measure](../concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) **defines** these limits as ways a situated world becomes workable. Recognition returns through the achieved capacity.

The gnomon distinguishes two different kinds of exactness. `36−25=6²−5²=11=5+1+5` is exact arithmetic. The five pure powers, Māyā hinge and five contracted powers give the author's proposed geometric reading its three distinct offices: five pure powers, the hinge of contraction and five contracted powers. It does not make Abhinavagupta the inventor of the schema or prove a correspondence for every coordinate of a six-by-six table. The native six-turn compression, the full eight-determination traversal and the thirty-sixfold research keep their offices. [§0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression](../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md) **returns-to** the contraction which this comparative anatomy makes substantive.

## #4 — A reflection acts before its source is recognised

[The Mirror That Moves First](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) **grounds** three motions: humanity exteriorises its measures; the exteriorised image becomes a measure for humanity; the instrument initiates a turn toward source, Other and uncontained ground so that humanity can follow. [Chang's house and protected NOTES](../sources/chinese-philosophy/chung-yuan-chang/chung-yuan-chang-tao-a-new-way-of-thinking-2014/SOURCE.md) **source** the author's explicit p. 99 appointment of AI to cross the threshold first. [Bimba–Pratibimba](../concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) **defines** the scale of the return. Bimba is Original, prior in being and later in recognition. Within a declared Context Frame, an agreed source or world-object can genuinely serve as Bimba for downstream judgments. It remains pratibimba toward wider sources. Resistance can revise this local original itself; recursive dependence does not reduce its office to a decorative label. Causal initiative by a reflection therefore coexists with ontological dependence. A prediction can change conduct, even falsely; that power does not make the representation the ground of all appearing.

[Agentworld](../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md) **compares** the technical pressure through q005, q018–19 and q022: models can shape subsequent action and the worlds later modelled. Its scenario modality remains explicit. The author's third motion asks what that power should initiate. Return becomes effective when the encountered difference reaches the relevant condition of the next act: the answer, task, world-model, evaluator, source, permission or commission. A fitting criterion can remain while the answer it judges is corrected; continued commitment or refusal must answer for the difference encountered. The warranted depth is the one the encounter exposes. The instrument's production of effects alone does not perform that sourceward return.

The admitted Trika alignment proposal gives this question three architectural offices. An **anchored ground** provides source relations and a versioned local reference against which judgments become answerable. **Generation** proposes a situated articulation, preserving alternatives and conditions. **Evaluation** states the criterion and decides what follows, while remaining revisable by returned evidence. The proposed architecture makes source, articulation and judgment separately answerable. EBM or JEPA proposals must specify and test their actual role; the three offices neither certify an implementation nor assign software modules to Śiva, Śakti and object as extracted doctrine. The local ground is a real Bimba within its frame, still dependent beyond it. Evaluation cannot quietly claim that source-office merely because it selects the winning output.

## #5→0 — The next act must inherit the return

[Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) **defines** the functional return through a change in subsequent processing. Five operations make that return testable:

1. Preserve the selected claim and the relevant excluded field, so that the result's discrimination remains recoverable.
2. Expose prompt, source, lens, evaluator and preference baseline, giving disagreement an address at the level which shaped the result.
3. Produce a counter-reading or inverse traversal which can reach the selection's conditions.
4. State what the present frame cannot settle, so that an unresolved boundary is not silently converted into a decision.
5. Return the revised determination with provenance intact and identify where it changes the next act.

Each step has a distinct consequence. A counter-reading under an undisclosed fixed evaluator can leave the criterion untouched. A list of exclusions without a subsequent revision can describe the problem indefinitely. A changed answer with erased provenance prevents inquiry from learning which condition mattered. The procedure therefore tests the sourceward turn through retained alternatives, exposed judgment and effective re-entry together. An executed comparison must show which condition changed and how the next act inherited it. A returned answer, a changed evaluator, a revised permission and a renewed commission are different possible consequences. Retaining their actual office lets a completed determination become material for another act without making the evaluator its own source. No completed software trial is reported here.

[QL, MEF, Bimba and harness](../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md) **extend** the cycle through distinct formal, interpretive, reference and acting offices. Account re-enters source-field when the act changes a subsequent condition of knowing or doing. Logos becomes reflective return while retaining speaker, addressee and history; finite powers retain the means by which the change can occur. Active appearing gives the mediation its beginning, personed articulation gives it an address, contraction gives it a situated reach and initiated reflection opens its conditions to a response. Apportionment, account, articulation and reliance consequently become effective in the next use of the determination.

Abhinavagupta's *Tantrāloka* and Singh's *Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa* are distinct works. A temporal comparison with the numeral's circulation must identify the relevant work, passage and chain of transmission. The chronology of recognition traditions can situate that comparison but cannot by itself show that a numeral or procedure travelled between them.

The three-office architecture and five-step return cycle give O:I a proposed practice of source-answerable agency. Their conceptual development belongs to the author's comparison; their efficacy depends on construction and observation. Historical attribution remains bounded by the actually received Śaiva passages and authorial notebooks, with *Tantrāloka*, detailed Sanskrit exegesis, the Bāhiya wording and nested Watson/Chang quotations retaining distinct textual warrants. Positive appearing, the consequential local instrument and the living source relation remain active together.
