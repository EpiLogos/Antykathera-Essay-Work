# Mathematics — Changed Permissions and Retained Conditions

## #0 — A place must be readable before it can operate

A written absence does work only within a practice that can read it. An empty internal place in a positional numeral separates magnitudes; a sign for an absent astronomical measure belongs to a table and its units; an arithmetic zero can participate in stated operations. These achievements concern different permissions. In this [history of mathematical permissions](HISTORY-mathematics.md), admitting an absence lets a practice perform an operation while retaining the conditions through which its result can be read.

[Kaplan, in the selected 2000 printing](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is.md), follows the distinction through Babylonian, Hellenistic and Indian sequences. His pp. 4–27 separate internal positional marking from astronomical notation; pp. 36–75 follow the Indian arithmetic development. These are historical syntheses with particular tablet, inscription and dating claims still requiring specialist primary collation. A circular sign alone cannot establish descent between practices, and a missing written final zero shows nothing about the sophistication of its users' mathematical world, because notation depends on the reader's context and the operations it serves.

[Zero's arithmetic advent](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A10-Advent-of-Zero.md) changes a permission within a mathematical practice, rather than retrospectively ranking civilisations. An operation can be newly available even where its eventual symbol resembles an older mark. [Naming an absence and admitting it to a count](../../../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count) are related achievements whose rules remain inspectable. A [situated place and articulated account](../../../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md) give a reader the context through which the sign becomes operative. A tablet, table or rule does not carry every condition of its reading on its face. These are authorial operational relations, not claims that the Greek or Latin field generated Indian arithmetic.

This history therefore proceeds through acquired operations and their returns. Its later conjunction of ancient arithmetic, twentieth-century logic and contemporary learning is a retrospective argument built from distinct historical branches, and it traces no continuous causal descent from a Babylonian separator to a transformer. A historical practice and a comparison between operations answer different questions. Lexical descent needs its actual transmission, and a poetic image can renew a relation without being its ancestor.

## #1 — Zero enters calculation and exposes its laws

In [Colebrooke's translation](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara.md), three different acts bring zero into operation. Brahmagupta's rules at printed p. 339, chapter XVIII, rules 31 and 34, give zero additive and multiplicative behaviour. Bhāskara's *Bīja-gaṇita* I.14 at p. 137 retains a fraction with zero as denominator. I.16 at p. 138 compares its invariance under addition or subtraction of finite quantities with divine immutability through creation and destruction. The theological comparison is itself part of that translated witness. It is a comparison inside the text, and the essay's Subject needs its own proof, while Sanskrit philology awaits a critical edition beyond the selected English witness.

The laws do different work. Addition by zero preserves a finite quantity; multiplication by zero makes different finite quantities agree at the result. Over the real numbers, `6·0 = 17·0` supplies no cancellation back to `6 = 17`. The information lost in multiplication cannot be recovered by treating zero as an invertible factor. Under ordinary field laws, assuming `0x = 1` conflicts with `0x = 0`. Retaining `a/0` consequently requires a declared change in what expressions mean or which transformations are permitted. Zero has two offices in the matheme's signed arithmetic as well, and an arithmetic admission of zero as an operand gives rigorous laws to only one of them: zero is the terminus at which `(−1)+(+1)` arrives, and it is the axis through which `(−1)/(+1)` keeps its sign. This is Taylor's reading and is Argued, and the second office is left to the philosophical argument.

[Dutta's 2023 reconstruction](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/dutta/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india.md) examines restricted cancellation, cross-multiplication and postponed evaluation for zero-divided quantities. This modern reconstruction is distinct from both Colebrooke's translation and ordinary real-number arithmetic. It gives an exact reason to reopen Kaplan's adverse treatment of the Indian rules, because a retained exceptional expression has to be tested within its declared regime, and unrestricted division by zero throughout mathematics remains unlicensed.

[Khahara](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/khahara.md) retains a zero-denominator expression under particular permissions and places invariance beside a theological image. Its selected English passages establish that juxtaposition; the Sanskrit terminology and wider reception need their own witnesses. [Brahmagupta's chapter-and-rule formulation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/brahmagupta.md) fixes the arithmetic acts within a broader chronological inquiry. [Admission to calculation](../../../../../section-rooms/02-return-of-zero/movements/14-s1-p1-sunya-operational.md) gives zero a new office; [a refused or altered law](../../../../../section-rooms/02-return-of-zero/movements/17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math.md) exposes the algebra containing that office. [Changing an algebra, adjoining a geometric point and changing coordinates](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C52-Dimensional-Reframing-at-Zero-and-Infinity.md) answer different obstructions.

The transmitted sign also changes its social permission. Kaplan's pp. 90–115 lead through Arabic and Latin numerical practices, teaching and commercial calculation. [Nothaft's 2020 study](../../../sources/history-philosophy-of-science/nothaft/nothaft-2020-satanic-ciphers/nothaft-2020-satanic-ciphers.md) examines a particular medieval reception of ciphers; it warrants no Europe-wide prohibition and no single religious attitude toward zero. Use of numeral signs and use of positional zero remain distinct in the proposed [Gerbert](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/gerbert-sylvester-ii.md) attribution. The proposed [Salem codex](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/salem-codex.md) testimony has no established manuscript, dating, folio and edition here. Repeating the story as a fact would erase the distinction the history exists to keep.

## #2 — A generator has an exact domain

Modern constructions give zero new offices without supplying missing ancient events. Under the finite von Neumann construction, `0 = ∅`, `1 = {0}`, `2 = {0,1}`, and `n+1 = n ∪ {n}`. Membership records the predecessors within each succeeding ordinal. The empty set is a subset of every set; it is not a member of every set. It is a member of every nonzero finite ordinal under this construction. This [ordinal construction](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/von-neumann-ordinals.md) makes membership exact; attribution of its first publication to von Neumann still needs the original historical witness.

In Taylor's [advent of zero](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A10-Advent-of-Zero.md), formal absence has an exact identity and can become the member around which the first positive ordinal is formed. The subject comparison concerns a condition's relation to what it makes available. The empty set remains an object inside set theory and is not thereby the unobjectifiable Subject. The construction earns the comparison by its operation, and its force owes nothing to a claim that nothing physically creates a universe.

Kaplan's pp. 203–215 place further constructions nearby. Between boundary markers `0/1` and `1/0`, the mediant inserts `(a+c)/(b+d)` between appropriate neighbours. Between `1/2` and `2/3`, for example, it produces `3/5`. The markers' role differs from ordinary division, and mediant addition differs from addition of rational values. The empty product has value one because it preserves the multiplicative composition law when no factors are present. Neither operation is ordinal succession. In [Kaplan's selected printing](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/kaplan-nothing-that-is.md), these distinct constructions give a boundary, a multiplicative identity and a successor their respective offices.

Boolean generation provides another precise economy. Write `p ↓ q = ¬(p ∨ q)`. Then `p ↓ p` yields negation; negating `p ↓ q` yields disjunction; `(p ↓ p) ↓ (q ↓ q)` yields conjunction. This NOR construction has a specified truth-function and two-valued domain. It is not a NAND formula, a history of all logic, or the QL eightfold. [Sheffer's 1913 paper](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/sheffer/sheffer-1913-five-postulates/sheffer-1913-five-postulates.md) belongs to the single-connective programme; its exact historical notation and priority chain are separate from this modern truth-table construction. A modern truth-table demonstration establishes expressive sufficiency while leaving historical notation and priority undecided.

[Russell's 1908 types](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/russell/russell-1908-theory-types/russell-1908-theory-types.md) and [Principia](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/whitehead/whitehead-russell-1910-1913-principia/whitehead-russell-1910-1913-principia.md) address the admissibility of expressions and the reconstruction of mathematics; [Gödel's 1931 paper](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/godel/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions.md), with [Raatikainen's account](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/raatikainen/raatikainen-2026-godel-incompleteness-sep/raatikainen-2026-godel-incompleteness-sep.md), addresses provability under stated arithmetical and effectiveness conditions. Original first-theorem hypotheses and later strengthenings must be distinguished; the second theorem concerns formalised consistency under its own conditions. [Wittgenstein's Tractatus](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus.md) makes saying, showing and the status of its elucidations a different philosophical problem. These [different formal and philosophical limits](../../../dossiers/formal-limit.md) do not become one incompleteness theorem by converging. The [determining act](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) keeps its native argument while encountering those particular limits. Technical undecidability does not itself establish the entire native field.

## #3 — Re-entry, representation and retained passage

[Spencer-Brown](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form.md) develops a calculus from distinction; [Varela](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference.md) develops reflexive indication with a distinct formal treatment. Their historical relation opens the question of a distinction returning into its own field. It does not mean every self-referential expression has one temporal solution. Their distinct calculi require their own expressions, rules and transformations; a general appeal to re-entry does not substitute for either demonstration.

In [Kauffman's selected 2014 iterant exposition](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/kauffman/kauffman-2014-iterants-fermions-dirac-arxiv/kauffman-2014-iterants-fermions-dirac-arxiv.md), the swap relation `[a,b]η = η[b,a]` and the period-two construction occur at pp. 27–28. The operation can be checked in a finite representation: let `D = diag(1,−1)` and let `η` exchange the two coordinates. Then `η² = I`, `ηD = −Dη`, and `(Dη)² = −I`. Alternation together with the shift produces the square-root-of-minus-one relation. The phase is operationally represented; it is not inferred from an evocative drawing of a loop. Kauffman's separate non-trivial-kernel qualification also prevents a selected representation from claiming uniqueness.

[Alternation together with shift](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex.md) makes the represented phase exact. The native slash keeps its authorial derivation, while the representation supplies a mathematical neighbour. In the [primordial Symbolon's](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) [quaternal relation](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C11-Quaternal-Logic.md), the complete eight determinations, both complete inverse parents, harmonic and complementary pairings, and coequal Name and Power remain active. Four successive complex quarter-turns do not replace those relations or generate their historical provenance.

Other changes of space have different invariants. In [NIST's selected DLMF release](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/nist/nist-dlmf-2026-complex-variable/nist-dlmf-2026-complex-variable.md), the extended complex plane has an ideal point and cross-ratio remains invariant under bilinear transformations. Adjoining its ideal point does not turn every formerly undefined arithmetic expression into a field operation. [Hatcher's quotient and lifting constructions](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/hatcher/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology.md#hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology-p002) supply the relation between a loop and its lifted path. Taylor's [toroidal construction](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A17-Toroidal-Circulation-and-the-Arche-Topos.md) uses the explicit carrier `T² = ℝ²/ℤ²`: integer translates are identified, and one torus point has infinitely many lifts.

A closed loop can lift from `(0,0)` to `(2,1)`. That displacement records its winding class; it does not retain every turn, speed or encounter of the path. The universal covering plane is distinct from a torus covering a Klein bottle in two sheets. A coordinate change, a quotient and a cover therefore return different information. A [preserved relation under transformation or a similar operation under different conditions](../../../etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) makes a comparison answerable to both its retained form and difference. [Dimensional reframing](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C52-Dimensional-Reframing-at-Zero-and-Infinity.md) and [a paradox reaching its containing field](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C64-Paradox-Transforming-the-Containing-Field.md) can transform a frame while retaining the exact achievement of the former one.

## #4 — Commensuration makes a remainder consequential

In [Scholtz's tuning account](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/scholtz/scholtz-1998-algorithms-diatonic-keyboard-tunings/scholtz-1998-algorithms-diatonic-keyboard-tunings.md), the fifth `3/2`, fourth `4/3`, tone `9/8` and octave `2/1` have their specified ratios. Twelve pure fifths exceed seven octaves by `(3/2)^12 / 2^7 = 531441/524288`. Equal temperament distributes that discrepancy logarithmically across the fifths. The tuned result changes the intervals according to a declared criterion. This operation differs from Taylor's [exact octave-completing product](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A15-Ratio-Rationality-Measure-Reckoning-Harmony-Account.md) `16/9 · 9/8 = 2`: the latter retains the factor completing an octave, while temperament adjusts a cycle of fifths. Six pure `9/8` tones do not simply equal an octave.

[An exact ratio, a winding class and a musical resolution](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/29-s3-p4-topology-music-resolution.md) retain different kinds of return. The listener's recognition can bring the achieved relation to rest; the formal comparison does not require indefinite production of cycles. A [selected denominator and purpose](../../../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-apportionment-and-economy.md#e6-ratio) make the ratio answerable to the work for which a share is apportioned. The history of reckoning and exchange has its own lexical and Aristotelian witnesses. No ancestral relation between every term for harmony and number is supplied by these equalities.

A material instrument can coordinate several cycles without making them one cycle. The [selected Freeth et al. 2006 abstract](../../../sources/history-philosophy-of-science/freeth/freeth-et-al-2006-antikythera/freeth-et-al-2006-antikythera.md) reports fragment imaging, further inscriptions and recovered celestial functions; the [2021 model](../../../sources/history-philosophy-of-science/freeth/freeth-et-al-2021-model-cosmos/freeth-et-al-2021-model-cosmos.md) proposes a front display constrained by inscriptions and fragments. Its nine outputs and nested arrangements are a reconstruction, not an intact ancient device recovered in full. The [mechanism](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/concepts/reference-notes/antikythera-mechanism.md) retains different evidential scopes in the fragment report and reconstructed front display. In [situated coordination](../../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement.md), an indication can be exact within the instrument while the reader still owes an account of evidence, reconstruction and what the display cannot decide. This retrospective encounter with ancient craft is not a claim that tuning or topology chronologically produced that mechanism.

[Measure is apportioned through a selected unit and purpose](../../../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD-apportionment-and-economy.md#measure-is-an-apportioned-criterion), making commensuration exact within that relation. Tuning a cycle and retaining an octave-completing factor keep different operations; a comparison class and reference policy likewise keep different roles. A disputed result must return to the particular measure it uses rather than to an unspecified ideal of greater precision.

## #5→0 — Evaluation returns to the practice selecting it

Modern machine learning gives these questions new operational sites. In [Vaswani et al.](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/vaswani/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention.md), scaled dot-product attention applies `softmax(QKᵀ/√dₖ)` to weight values. The weighting is distinct from selection of a maximal index. Under [PyTorch's version-fixed definitions](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/pytorch/pytorch-2-9-softmax-argmax-api/pytorch-2-9-softmax-argmax-api.md), softmax normalises over a selected dimension and argmax returns an index, using the first maximum in a tie. [Scores, a distribution and a selected mark](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax.md) have different offices. Retaining the distribution does not alone retain the prompt, source authority or excluded comparison class. The apoha relation is the essay's argued comparison and carries no claim of historical transmission into the algorithm.

[Bradley–Terry](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/bradley/bradley-terry-1952-paired-comparisons/bradley-terry-1952-paired-comparisons.md) supplies the paired-comparison history; [DPO's selected passage](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/rafailov/rafailov-et-al-2023-dpo/rafailov-et-al-2023-dpo.md) makes the dependence on reward differences explicit. Adding the same constant to two compared rewards cancels in their difference. This gauge freedom is distinct from replacing DPO's reference policy: a changed reference policy can change the optimisation problem. [InstructGPT](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/ouyang/ouyang-et-al-2022-instructgpt/ouyang-et-al-2022-instructgpt.md) has its demonstration, ranking, reward-model and reinforcement-learning pipeline; DPO's direct preference objective is a different training procedure. An inherited comparison form does not make their entire algorithms equivalent.

In [LeCun and colleagues' selected 2006 prepublication text](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/lecun/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning.md), inference varies candidate values under an energy function; learning changes the function through a loss. Low compatibility energy, a training loss and a preference score have different jobs. A [constructed Bimba reference](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields.md) gives the proposed energy-field programme a comparison field through those distinct jobs. Its reference field can genuinely govern local comparisons while remaining answerable to wider sources; an energy function does not create that epistemic authority by itself.

A [computational account](../../../../../section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) retains the operation performed, its comparison field, criterion and judging office. The [finite conditions of preference](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero.md) can then become inspectable without being identified with native Zero. [Contextual disclosure](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity) becomes consequential when an exposed difference reaches the condition it concerns: an answer, task or world-model can be corrected under a fitting criterion; a failed source selection, evaluator, permission or commission may need revision at that depth; a fitting condition can be retained with reasons. The warranted correction or retention must be inherited by the next act. A stronger claim of changed processing still needs an identifiable producing condition to have changed. [Trust](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active) adds the undertaking through which someone bears what follows from reliance. [Renewed entrustment](../../../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#credere-renewed-entrustment) follows inquiry while retaining a person's commitment, rather than being derived from a confidence score. In the [investigation-and-faith relation](../../../aphorisms/investigation-and-faith.md), inquiry makes reliance determinate and faith undertakes what that reliance involves.

Mathematics has supplied exact distinctions throughout this passage. Their use becomes answerable through the acquired permission, the law retained or changed, the evidence bearing the attribution and the consequence received. [A count becomes an account](../../../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-to-account) when its selected unit, interpretation and use are related to the conditions which made it possible. The next calculation can keep its precision while becoming more faithful to the practice and world through which it operates.
