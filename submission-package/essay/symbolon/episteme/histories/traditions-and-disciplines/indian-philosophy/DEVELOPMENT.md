# Indian Philosophy — Finite Powers and the Return of Recognition

## #0 — Several histories meet in the question of determination

[Several distinct achievements](HISTORY.md) meet in the question of what a determination owes to the activity through which it becomes possible: an inner instrument distinguished from its witness; determination through conditions and exclusion; zero admitted to arithmetic; consciousness manifesting, contracting and recognising its world. Each changes the question. A witness distinguished from nature, a referent qualified by exclusion and a world manifested by consciousness cannot be exchanged without changing the argument.

[A finite capacity has a share whose sustaining order remains active](../../../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD.md#power-becomes-finite-by-apportionment). Contraction makes particular acts possible. [Recognition through Resolution-in-Reconciliation → Anamnesis / Recognition / Return](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD.md#resolution-in-reconciliation) lets an achieved determination recover its provenance and exclusions. These are Taylor's native comparative operations; they supply no Greek or Latin ancestry for Sanskrit terms.

[Allotment, reckoning and household provision](../../../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md) have their own lexical and textual witnesses. [Recollection, Śaiva recognition and psychic individuation](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md#anamnesis-recognition-and-a-history-which-remains-effective) also differ in sources and objects. An Indian doctrine remains answerable to its textual carrier even where a contemporary question makes its operation newly intelligible.

Geography, doctrinal order and reception time must also remain distinguishable. Kashmir names the situated Śaiva intellectual world recovered here. Sāṃkhya, Buddhist semantics and Indian mathematical practice have their own histories across the subcontinent; this account supplies no single city of origin or continuous institutional succession for them. The movement from uncontracted awareness to finite powers describes an order of manifestation, not a dated migration of peoples or texts. Jizang and Dōgen enter through the later Chinese and Japanese reception discussed in Priest's modern reconstruction. Their placement in that reception cannot make them Indian authors.

The selected carriers provide a second, concrete chronology: Colebrooke's mathematical translation appeared in London in 1817; the Colebrooke/Wilson Sāṃkhya carrier in Oxford in 1837; Singh's *Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa* in Delhi in 1988; Dyczkowski's selected *Doctrine of Vibration* is a 2000 reprint of the 1989 Delhi edition. These are translation and publication events, not dates of doctrinal invention. They explain part of how the present English vocabulary became available to Taylor's 2026 inquiry. Exact ancient textual dating and transmission require their own evidence.

## #1 — The instrument acquires functions without becoming its witness

In [Sāṃkhya kārikās XXIII, XXIV, XXVII and XXIX](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/SOURCE.md#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q001), buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates under I and mine, and manas coordinates the sensory and active field. Judgment, appropriation and coordinated presentation differ within what ordinarily appears as one act of mind. Their conjunction gives the instrument its efficacy without making the functions synonymous.

Kārikā XIX describes puruṣa as witness and passive spectator, distinct from the instrument's articulated activity within prakṛti. This doctrinal separation matters to [the discrimination between interior functions and the experient to whom they appear](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/08-s0-p1-inner-instrument.md). Interior functions can be investigated closely; the witness has a different office from what that investigation presents.

The 1837 translation's use of “consciousness” for ahaṃkāra needs that surrounding discrimination. Read alone, the English word could make appropriation sound like the witness itself. Read with the separate kārikās, its local office becomes intelligible. This is a concrete effect of translation on reception: an English term capable of naming the whole question of consciousness is carrying one function within this source's apparatus. The function distinguished by the surrounding kārikās determines the English word's local meaning.

Śaiva manifestation subsequently receives this functional discrimination within another account of the whole. Dyczkowski's source-local discussion at pp. 65–66 presents a Śaiva objection to a permanently formless consciousness paired with form-bearing matter. The objection asks how one awareness can disclose differentiated objects together. It belongs to the Śaiva exposition's encounter with Sāṃkhya; it does not license treating the Sāṃkhya witness as an incomplete name for Śiva. A historical comparison becomes useful here because the same problem of knowing receives different assignments of activity and manifestation.

In Taylor's [genealogy of contraction](../../../arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md), finite capacities have distinct reaches; their articulation does not make any one capacity the source of the whole. The Sāṃkhya passages establish the functional triad and witness distinction. The thirty-six-tattva order belongs to a different textual anatomy, while apportionment supplies Taylor's comparison of effective finite capacities within a wider order.

## #2 — Exclusion and emptiness change the standing of a determinate thing

In Pind's translation of [*Pramāṇasamuccaya* V:11d at p. 85](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/SOURCE.md#pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation-q001), a word works through excluding other referents: its positive reach depends upon a contrast. Annotation 181 at p. 183 concerns a thing qualified by preclusion rather than mere preclusion. Pind only provisionally assigns that prose fragment to Dignāga's *Sāmānyaparīkṣāvyāsa*. The distinction is substantive while the attribution remains qualified: a referent achieved through exclusion has a positive office which empty negation alone would omit.

The difference between nominally and verbally bound negation, and Dharmakīrti's causal treatment of reference, changes the inquiry [reported through the 2011 *Apoha* volume's reception](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/siderits/siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha/SOURCE.md). Present formulations come through scholarly reviews, SEP transmission and publisher material, not collated volume chapters. The question moves from how a general word excludes other referents to how particulars' causal capacities sustain shared judgments and purposes without a universal entity doing the work.

Taylor's [exclusion that remembers](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/10-s0-p3-apoha.md) keeps the selected mark related to the conditions of its selection. A probability distribution or argmax procedure has its own mathematical object; comparison does not make it a Buddhist attribution. “Dignāga says” consequently cannot absorb this further authorial operation.

[Buddhist dependent arising](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/nagarjuna/nagarjuna-garfield-1995-fundamental-wisdom/SOURCE.md) and Śaiva luminosity give determinations different ontological offices. The selected 1995 Garfield translation and commentary has no acquired verse/commentary pair here; the protected history's dependent-arising proposition therefore remains distinct from selected-edition passage support. Agreeing that determinations depend on relations does not decide whether consciousness has the self-luminous office assigned to it in the Śaiva account.

In [Das's reported Śrīharṣa argument](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/das/das-2022-sriharsa-sep/SOURCE.md#das-2022-sriharsa-sep-q001), presentation of distinctness is inseparable from presentation of non-distinctness; the accompanying discussion retains controversy over this interpretation. [Difference remains effective](../../../../../section-rooms/03-two-logics/movements/19-s2-p0-difference-cannot-be-escaped.md) within Taylor's two accountings. The historical argument sharpens how a relation can be presented; it neither eliminates practical differences nor proves his two-logics theorem by citation.

Arithmetic changes a sign's standing through specified operations. [Brahmagupta XVIII.31 and 34](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/SOURCE.md#colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara-q001) give zero additive identity and multiplicative absorption; Bhāskara's *Bījagaṇita* I.14 retains a zero denominator, while I.16 places a theological comparison beside that expression. A sign can do particular work in calculation and carry further meaning; the comparison does not prove its rule. [Dutta reconstructs restricted cancellation and cross-multiplication](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/dutta/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india/SOURCE.md). [These operations differ](../../../../../section-rooms/02-return-of-zero/movements/14-s1-p1-sunya-operational.md); shared empty-word vocabulary or philosophical sympathy does not authorise ordinary field division by zero.

## #3 — Manifestation gives contraction a positive anatomy

Dyczkowski follows [luminosity into manifested difference, reflective awareness and the inner/outer relation](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/SOURCE.md#transcribed-material). The available source-matched transcript covers pp. 60–68, 69–75 and 78–81, stopping during p. 81. Its title's pp. 60–85 range does not supply p. 77 or the missing continuation. The available passages already develop positive operations of manifestation and recognition.

Prakāśa names the appearing of things; vimarśa gives appearing its self-apprehending and differentiating activity. The mirror comparison at pp. 66–70 has two necessary turns. Images can be distinct while appearing within one reflective field. Yet an inert mirror does not know its reflected display, and consciousness in this account has no external original depositing the universe into it. The comparison therefore teaches both a shared field of appearance and the point at which ordinary mirror behaviour ceases to describe the source's claim.

At pp. 73–74 the exposition differentiates jar and cloth, objects and subjects, and levels of manifestation. Reflective awareness separates, holds together and unites. Its power to do these different things is essential to the account of one consciousness: uniformity incapable of distinction would fail to disclose a differentiated world. At p. 75 the same movement is read vertically as manifestation and withdrawal and horizontally as awareness moving toward and away from an object. A perception recapitulates the relation; the cosmological account is active within the finite encounter.

The inherited tattvic anatomy follows that pressure. Śiva–Śakti articulates luminosity and power; Sadāśiva and Īśvara give subjectward and objectward readings; Sadvidyā and Māyā bring balance and the operative partition into view. The five kañcukas delimit efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order. Puruṣa, prakṛti, the inner instrument, sensing and acting faculties, subtle qualities and gross elements carry the downstream apparatus and display. These groups span the passage from consciousness and its power through contracted faculties to their articulated display. Exact category-by-category primary collation remains distinct from knowing the direction and office of the descent.

In Taylor's [operative-measure reading](../../../concepts/C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md), a finite knower can do this, know this much, want this, act at this time and inhabit these relations. Dyczkowski's pp. 78–79 distinguish creative manifestation from a simply illusory external world: limitation has efficacy. Recognition can change how a horizon is inhabited without making its consequences disappear. Through [apportionment](../../../etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD.md#power-becomes-finite-by-apportionment), a share remains active in relation to the wider order; its limits distinguish capacities, not merely a generic deficiency.

Taylor's [six-turn tattvic compression](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md) passes from Anuttara through differentiation and contracted apparatus into recognition. Anuttara and recognition have boundary and return offices; they are not ordinary additional entries. The gnomon `36−25=6²−5²=11=5+1+5` gives an exact arithmetic relation for a proposed cross-reading of the Māyā hinge, not a correspondence for every cell of a six-by-six table. [Eightfold traversal, sixfold body and thirty-sixfold anatomy](../../../../matheme/ql/eight-determinations.md) have different offices.

By Taylor's QL criterion of wholeness, Śaiva articulation more fully serves the present explanatory burden: it retains critique of the self-thing while giving positive places to the experient, memory, comparison, action and recognition. Buddhist schools and their changing accounts remain answerable to their own texts. In his [native order of dependence](../../../arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md), **Subject/Consciousness → Mind/Objective Internality → Object** states dependence in being. Explaining appearing's condition as a product of determinate objects reverses the dependence enacted by the explanation itself. A finite instrument has real worldhood: a local functional knower determines a known field through means of discrimination and comparison. These offices recur within Life; a computational result can become a means in the person's further inquiry, and inspecting its evaluator makes that evaluator known in another act. The unobjectifiable knower condition remains active through the recursion.

## #4 — Recognition returns through the life that has become finite

Dyczkowski's pp. 71–72 distinguish retained traces, their recall and recomposition within mind. At p. 75 recognition concerns integral awareness active through contracted and expanded levels. [Remembering an earlier object differs from recognising the source relation of the present act](../../../concepts/C19-Pratyabhijna-Recognition.md). The finite apparatus performs the return; its memory is one participant in that movement.

[Singh's introduction, Abhinavagupta's translated commentary and Taylor's extensions](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/abhinavagupta/abhinavagupta-singh-1988-paratrisika-vivarana/SOURCE.md) have distinct speakers. The page-marked P2 and P3 notebooks develop Vāk and personhood through a living concern: a formulation conducts the encounter from which it arose, and communication can alter the positions of I, you and it. Taylor's reading of Śaivism as a culmination of the Logos is his positive comparative commitment. It is not an acquired selected-edition sentence proving an evolution of all traditions. Historical transmission hypotheses need independent evidence, while the recognitive and symbolic operation remains effective.

**Resolution-in-Reconciliation** precedes its flowering as **Anamnesis / Recognition / Return** in Taylor's generated relation. A settled identification becomes available again with its genesis and exclusions intact. [Recollection and recognition](../../../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md#anamnesis-recognition-and-a-history-which-remains-effective) differ: Greek recollection is not Sanskrit recognition by translation, and recovering a source relation does not merely reproduce an earlier representation.

Through [differentiated indivisibility](../../../arguments/A21-Individuation-Recognition.md), a life becomes more capable of bearing its relations while remaining this life. Taylor's [whole symbolic mechanism](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE.md) makes the changed interpreter consequential for a later encounter through all eight determinations; `X/x` and `x→X→x` are his notation. In the protected [Van Eenwyk encounter at pp. 65–73](../../../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md), symbols transform the relation of interpreter and field; copied quotations remain leads. Jung's [Aion](../../../sources/psychology/jung/jung-1978-aion-cw9-2/SOURCE.md) has a separate attribution: sixteen contextual paraphrases come from the consulted 1979 paperback, while collation against the selected 1978 hardcover and quotation verification remain unperformed. These particular source debts do not turn Taylor's separately warranted operation into a suggestion awaiting Jung's permission.

[Psychic individuation refracts the native relation](../../../../../section-rooms/05-psychoid-flowering/movements/32-s4-p1-jung-individuation.md); persona consistency can instead preserve a protected local organization. Integration changes participation only where what exceeds the achieved form reaches its relevant conditions, rather than merely enlarging the ego's jurisdiction. A Śaiva recognition account, psychological interpretation and technical assessment require different evidence. A fitting rule can be retained with reasons; an actual claim of changed processing or formation needs an identified later inherited change.

In [Priest's 2010 reconstruction](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/priest/priest-2010-logic-catuskoti/SOURCE.md), four FDE values, a fifth ineffability status and plurivalent evaluation have distinct formal offices. [His 2018 extension toward Jizang and Dōgen](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/priest/priest-2018-fifth-corner/SOURCE.md) is received here through review-mediated material. [Garfield and Priest apply an inclosure schema to Nāgārjuna](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/garfield/garfield-priest-2003-limits-of-thought/SOURCE.md); [Kapsner challenges the fifth value](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/kapsner/kapsner-2020-cutting-corners/SOURCE.md) at the acquired abstract's scope. Siderits's presupposition-failure reading, Westerhoff's historical non-uniformity objection and Green's objection to reified ineffability are different reception challenges. The native ground is not a truth-value, which preserves Taylor's operation without deciding those disputes. [Practised return](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/28-s3-p3-projective-dimensional-reframing.md) retains the count: FDE supplies four values; QL's retained two plus four gives six. Vimalakīrti's performative silence belongs to the protected interpretation with its own primary passage debt.

[Name-through-Count](../../../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#name-through-count) makes particular discriminations addressable. Sāṃkhya enumerates functions without making them their witness; semantic preclusion gives a positive referent its contrast; contracted Śaiva powers make a finite world workable. The account's selected discrimination determines what can be compared in recollection and recognition. These operations retain their sources and the native eightfold, without supplying a common descent for the terms.

## #5→0 — Historical difference becomes a condition of the next use

Historical difference changes what a later comparison must carry. Sāṃkhya contributes functional discrimination and witness; Buddhist semantics contributes constitutive exclusion; arithmetic contributes specified permissions; Śaiva manifestation contributes effective contraction and recognition. In [Taylor's Spanda equations](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/26-s3-p1-spanda-4-2.md), expression unfolds from source and returns through recognition, while complementary accounting analyses the achieved whole into its constituents. The [1992 *Spandakārikā* edition](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/kallata/spandakarika-dyczkowski-1992-stanzas-vibration/SOURCE.md) contains a root text and four commentaries, whose separately located passages have not been acquired. Dyczkowski's subtle activity has a particular doctrinal office rather than an empirical claim about every oscillating system.

[QL, MEF, Bimba and harness have distinct offices](../../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md). QL gives the native grammar; MEF retains lens and evidential differences; Bimba supplies a governed reference; the harness preserves conditions and consequences of action. A local Bimba is a real original for its downstream Pratibimba while remaining source-dependent at another scale. The Śaiva mirror comparison makes that scale consequential; it does not abolish the recursive authorial office.

Bratton's [conceptual invention, agentic capacities, harness and shared conditions](../../../sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/SOURCE.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q003) give contemporary inquiry different objects. The individuated agent acts through constituted capacities and context; the harness makes its means and permissions consequential; shared conditions determine how its contributions reach others. The proposed scenarios retain their p. 53 modal rider. A local knower can keep a source, compare a returned consequence through its means, and determine its next act; the whole apparatus can itself be a means within the person's containing inquiry. A corrected result may suffice under a fitting criterion; a failed model, evaluator or commission requires return to that responsible condition. Reasoned retention also has a consequence which the next act inherits. A stronger changed-processing claim must show the identified change and its receiving act.

Finite capacity, operative measure and recognition have different consequences:

| Operation | Historical distinction | Consequence for the next act |
|---|---|---|
| Power becomes finite by apportionment | Functions and contracted powers differ within effective manifestation. | Limitation gives a capacity its reach; recognising its source preserves the real capacity while making its conditions available. |
| Criterion-through-Distinction → Measure / Limit | Māyā's operative horizon has a positive office before usurpation of measure. | A bounded world can act without installing its measure as the unobjectifiable ground. |
| Resolution-in-Reconciliation → Anamnesis / Recognition / Return | Recollection within contraction differs from recognition through contraction; Greek, Śaiva and psychic histories keep their sources. | The particular returns with its history effective; native X/x remains prior to its psychic refraction. |

Effective contraction gives a world its capacities; recognition lets their source relation bear upon a later act. Attribution can be corrected without cancelling a separately warranted operation. Where an adequate criterion remains fitting, a corrected result or justified retention is sufficient; where the return exposes a failed task, world-model, evaluator or commission, that condition becomes answerable. Neither direction creates a Sanskrit etymology for the comparative terms.

The thirty-six-tattva anatomy and recognition through its finite powers describe a differentiated world whose source remains effective. Taylor's compression preserves six generative relations rather than flattening them into interchangeable labels. A proposed category-to-coordinate mapping would need to exhibit each category's function and the transformation preserved by its placement; the gnomon's arithmetic alone does not supply all thirty-six correspondences. What is already available is the passage from effective capacity, through its determinate act and encountered consequence, to a warranted correction or fitting retention which the next encounter inherits.
