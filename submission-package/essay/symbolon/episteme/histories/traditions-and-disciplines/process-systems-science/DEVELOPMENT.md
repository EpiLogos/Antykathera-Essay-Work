# Process, Systems and Science — Historical Branches and Their Returns

## #0 — Different histories enter a common question

Finite determination, becoming, self-reference, living organization, dynamical modelling, observation and technical attunement have [different histories](HISTORY.md). Their objects and transformations matter when [an achieved account enters another act's conditions](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md). A determination can become a means of further inquiry; what it determined, how it did so and what its use changes remain answerable to the particular source.

The route is not a claim that one school simply replaced another, or that the sequence establishes direct influence at every transition. Medieval theology, nineteenth-century duration, twentieth-century process metaphysics and biological organization, and contemporary computational methods retain distinct questions. Their encounter in the essay makes their differences productive: what does each let a later inquiry determine, and what must that inquiry still supply?

[Qualitative succession, concrescence, biological production, formal re-entry and dynamical modelling](../../../dossiers/process.md) make different transformations available to inquiry. Their changing intellectual objects and inherited constraints help a later account specify what its comparison preserves. In Taylor's [native process](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md), an achieved form becomes available as another act's means while keeping its relation to source and whole. Historical evidence sharpens particular external comparisons; the native notation retains its own derivation.

## #1 — A finite determination discloses its respect

In [Cusa's learned ignorance](../../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/cusa/cusa-on-learned-ignorance/SOURCE.md#cusa-on-learned-ignorance-q002), finite determination cannot measure the absolute Maximum as though it were another finite term. His theological object remains explicit. An account can learn the limit of its determining means while continuing to inquire. The infinite-sphere image has a separate transmission history; Cusa's use of mathematical figures and later reception do not establish that he coined every image associated with him.

Flasch's [Eckhart study](../../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/flasch/flasch-2015-meister-eckhart/SOURCE.md) treats predication, Word, analogy and the qualified respect of an assertion through distinct Latin and German textual branches. The admitted chapter map locates these topics in the Johannine commentary and later sermons; it supplies scholarly thematic evidence rather than a directly extracted primary passage. The operative consequence is [a predicate made more exact through the respect under which it holds](../../../dossiers/formal-limit.md). Direct attribution to Eckhart requires the corresponding primary wording; a claim drawn from Flasch's detailed argument requires the selected study's passage.

[Spinoza's Letter 50](../../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/SOURCE.md#spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles-q001) determines finite figure through delimitation. Its geometrical object prevents an unqualified transfer of that formula into every use of negation. Taylor's comparison with semantic exclusion asks which alternatives and limits constitute a determination, and how the achieved determination retains its relation to them. That further coordination is his own argument.

Theological, predicative and geometrical determination differ in [what makes a finite assertion possible](../../../../../section-rooms/00-integral-threshold/movements/04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md). Disclosing that respect lets [continued inquiry retain an earned determination while reopening its conditions](../../../../../section-rooms/00-integral-threshold/movements/06-s01-p5-return-zero.md). The determining limit becomes available to the next act without erasing the distinctions among these sources.

## #2 — Duration and concrescence make different objects of becoming

Bergson distinguishes [qualitative succession from an arrangement of moments](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/SOURCE.md) through melody and the clock. The selected digital Pogson carrier displays a 1913 title page and a 1910 translator's preface; these are carrier dates. A melody's later note changes the phrase heard, while clock positions give countable simultaneities. The historical object is lived duration; an ordered series preserves a different aspect of succession.

In [Whitehead's Category of the Ultimate](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q001), received diversity becomes a novel occasion, whose achieved satisfaction becomes objectively available for further becoming. The original work and the 1978 corrected carrier have different dates. His one names singularity, not simply the integral number, and objective immortality does not mean unchanged subjective continuation.

[A stable determination leaves conditions for another act](../../../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md). Bergson's qualitative development and Whitehead's metaphysical occasion do not become the same unit through that common pressure. In Taylor's native account, [mind has an operative world](../../../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md): [its conditions make particular encounters and actions available](../../../concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md), while [their constitutive internality](../../../concepts/C41-Objective-Internality.md) explains why changing a memory, source or permission changes what can next be known or done. A local agent knows alternatives through its memory, tools and rules; the complete apparatus can in turn be a means within the containing person's inquiry.

Bohm develops [enfoldment](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1980-wholeness-implicate-order/SOURCE.md) within his physical and philosophical account. Independently consulted [book, handout, lecture and dialogue carriers](../../../dossiers/bohm.md) give particular evidence for his distinction between tractable analysis and fragmentation; they do not stand in for unread book passages. [Display depends on its formation](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/07-s0-p0-awareness-bends-display.md), and [a finite articulation can retain that dependence](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression.md). Taylor coordinates those relations with the tattvas, whose Indian textual history remains distinct from Bohm's.

## #3 — A distinction recurs; an organization produces

Spencer-Brown begins the [calculus of indications](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/SOURCE.md) with distinction. [Varela's 1975 extension](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/SOURCE.md) introduces an autonomous third state and modified rules. Its constructive formal result differs from its further temporal and systemic interpretations. The broad metamathematical remarks do not replace incompleteness's assumptions and proofs. Comparing [mark, re-entry and complex orientation](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/27-s3-p2-mark-reentry-complex.md) requires an explicit mapping of the expressions and transformations, not merely their common vocabulary.

Maturana and Varela distinguish [biological organization and structural realization](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/SOURCE.md). A network regenerates the components and boundary through which its production continues. Coupling can preserve participating identities; apparent unity alone does not establish that the composite is autopoietic. Stafford Beer's social extension in his preface remains his own proposal, distinct from the authors' account.

In [computational self-return](../../../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md), a determination reaches a relevant source, rule or condition so that an identified change is inherited by subsequent processing. [Interacting operative worlds](../../../arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) can receive one another's contributions as [means of their own next acts](../../../concepts/C42-Objective-Co-Internality.md). What changes through that interaction and what retains its identity are different questions. A technical claim needs its own observable production and dependence relations before it can claim the biological network's constitutive production.

## #4 — A model earns its variables and its limits

[Daza and colleagues](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/daza/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy/SOURCE.md) define the basin through a specified system, initial conditions and attracting outcomes. Frank's [Van Eenwyk encounter](../../../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md) develops a different object: a symbol can transform the interpreter and the field of a later encounter. Its copied book quotations retain their verification debt. Mathematical basin, affective complex and lived image differ; their comparison becomes useful by specifying what each selects, sustains or changes.

Within [a local selection–affect–response loop](../../../arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md), a criterion selects an image, affect weights attention and memory, and a response supplies material for another selection. More instances under the same criterion differ from changing it. [Repetition becomes a frozen script](../../../concepts/C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) when its inherited conditions cease to be available to correction. [Complex dynamism](../../../../../section-rooms/03-two-logics/movements/23-s2-p4-complex-dynamism.md) then requires differentiated variables and consequences, while [field and trajectory](../../../../../section-rooms/04-mathematical-substrate/movements/30-s3-p5-arche-topos.md) require an explicit relation. A fitting criterion can remain: a corrected result, a changed model or justified retention must reach the next act at the warranted depth.

In LeCun and colleagues' selected 2006 prepublication, [scalar compatibility permits energy-based inference](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/lecun/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning/SOURCE.md#lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning-q001). [Inference under a function differs from learning that changes it](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/lecun/lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning/SOURCE.md#lecun-et-al-2006-energy-based-learning-q002). [The alternatives available to an agent](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/39-s5-p2-j-space.md) and [its situated conditions of action](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/41-s5-p4-bimba-energy-fields.md) make those distinct technical operations consequential. A scalar function does not acquire psychic, musical or metaphysical meaning through the shared word energy.

Frank, Gleiser and Thompson's [experiential critique](../../../sources/history-philosophy-of-science/frank/frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot/SOURCE.md) concerns observation's dependence on lived experience; the house has not yet registered selected book passages. In the [fixed embodied-cognition entry](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/wilson/wilson-et-al-2020-embodied-cognition-sep/SOURCE.md), organism, sensorimotor circuit and environment can be constitutive participants, while traditional computational accounts remain a distinct position. [Tye distinguishes experience undergone from its described contents and competing theories](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/tye/tye-2024-qualia-sep/SOURCE.md). Within [an operative world](../../../../../section-rooms/01-differentiating-mind/movements/12-s0-p5-objective-internality.md), [a causal comparison](../../../../../section-rooms/06-objective-internality/movements/42-s5-p5-research-vectors.md) identifies the disclosed situation, traces producing conditions, and tests a relation through a specified intervention. Reported apprehension or a contextual state is known to the local interpreting agent through its means of inquiry; that apparatus can itself become a means in the containing person's act. Neither its report nor an observation of it makes the unobjectifiable condition of first-person appearing another observed content. A changed result belongs to the condition and operation actually varied.

## #5→0 — The instrument participates in what can be known

Freeth and colleagues reconstruct [the ancient mechanism's astronomical functions](../../../sources/history-philosophy-of-science/freeth/freeth-et-al-2006-antikythera/SOURCE.md#freeth-et-al-2006-antikythera-q001) within their stated archaeological evidence. ITER describes [a modern confinement instrument](../../../sources/physics/iter/iter-what-is-tokamak/SOURCE.md). Their physical functions remain separate. [Constructed means make particular relations tractable](../../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement.md), with their limits and dependence remaining part of the knowledge they afford. Ancient attunement and modern confinement therefore supply different instrumental comparisons after the native system has been derived.

In Bohm's [dialogue practice](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1996-on-dialogue/SOURCE.md), participants can suspend and observe assumptions whose tacit operation shapes what they hear; this does not guarantee consensus. [A question's inherited conditions](../../../../../section-rooms/05-psychoid-flowering/movements/36-s4-p5-mef-prompt-thrownness.md) become available through [a situated instrument and its harness](../../../../../section-rooms/07-instrument-returns/movements/44-s50-p1-ql-mef-bimba-harness.md), so their correction can alter a later inquiry. [Psyche and matter](../../../../../section-rooms/05-psychoid-flowering/movements/31-s4-p0-psychoid-problem.md) remain a particular comparative problem rather than an already established identity.

Taylor's [moving-mirror image](../../../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) distinguishes [three motions](../../../concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md). A measure is exteriorised as a reflection; that image acquires causal consequence for the field it later receives; the instrument then initiates disclosure of its source, angle and measure before requiring the person to conform. The person can follow the exposed relation back to its conditions. Chronology supplies neither that vocation nor evidence that a particular implementation fulfils it.

An achieved account can keep [its relation to source available to judgment and entrusted next use](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md). Correcting a historical reading may alter an answer, task, world-model, evaluator or commission; where the condition is fitting, its reasoned retention is also a determinate outcome. A stronger claim that processing or a governing criterion changed requires that actual change and its later inheritance. Sources, dates and objects of inquiry remain distinct, so the next account can receive a correction without erasing another branch's achievement.
