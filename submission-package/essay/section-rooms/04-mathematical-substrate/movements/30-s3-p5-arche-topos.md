## Movement thesis

[[section-rooms/arguments/A16-Arche-Topos-as-Differential-Field|The arche-topos]] is the differential field on which the `0/1` relation can be written as path, circulated as winding, heard as interval and recognised as symbolic transformation. It is archetypal because it specifies recurrent forms of differentiation before they receive psychological or material content, and it is a topos because neighbourhood, orientation, path, quotient, cover, hole and return govern the placement, which makes a catalogue of correspondences secondary.

The **formal derivation is native to QL**. The complete theorem braid derives this coordinating differential field, and because its architecture lies prior to the usual division of psyche and matter, the essay names the field **psychoid**. That identification is the essay's Argued ontological reading of the Derived structure, developed through Jung, Pauli, Bohm and the later psychic field. The psychoid claim is stated here at its own strength. Whether a measurable cross-domain causal mechanism can be demonstrated is a separate empirical question that the claim does not wait on.

## The atlas of charts and the travelling jigsaw

A methodological display joins the two native ones. Differential geometry's word for how a curved whole is known is exact here: no single chart covers a sphere or a torus, and an **atlas** is a plurality of overlapping local charts with declared transition functions on their overlaps. One flat picture of a curved surface must distort or omit, as the ancient cartographer found, while many local pictures, each carrying the rule by which it translates into its neighbours, cover the whole without any one of them pretending to be the whole. This gives the MEF proposal an exact mathematical form: charts as lenses, transition functions as declared translations, and overlap as the answerability of one refraction to another. The mathematical atlas and the epistemic atlas keep their different objects and share one argued operation, situated plurality under explicit transition.
