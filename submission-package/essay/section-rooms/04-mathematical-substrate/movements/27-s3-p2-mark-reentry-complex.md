## Movement thesis

A determination fed back as the condition of the next either hardens into a fixed point or stays in phase, depending on whether each reckoning from one pole is returned to be re-read from the other. The manuscript develops this through three constructions that share one structure: Kaprekar's routine (an iterated map with two attractors), re-entry (a mark that contains itself, made definite by a successor), and iterants (alternation with its shift, which yields the imaginary unit and so phase). Each shows that difference is the condition of circulation, and that a return can arrive transformed where cancellation would arrive at nothing.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form|Spencer-Brown]] begins with the injunction to draw a distinction. The mark creates inside and outside together, and calling and crossing specify how marks condense or cancel, so a distinction is already an operation and a static pair of values comes second.

## Warrant: the iterated map, Kaprekar's routine

D. R. Kaprekar's game (announced at Madras in 1949, published that year as "Another Solitaire Game", returned to in 1955 as "An Interesting Property of the Number 6174") takes any four-digit number whose digits are not all alike, arranges its digits descending and ascending, and subtracts. From 2026: 6220−0226=5994; 9954−4599=5355; 5553−3555=1998; 9981−1899=8082; 8820−0288=8532; 8532−2358=6174; and 7641−1467=6174 again. Every such number arrives at 6174 within seven subtractions, and only the ten repdigits go elsewhere, to 0000 in one step. All ten thousand strings were run: the longest journey is seven subtractions, 2,184 strings need seven, and 0000 and 6174 are the only numbers the routine leaves in place. In the language of iterated maps, `x₁ = f(x₀)`, `x₂ = f(x₁)`, a state with `f(x) = x` is a fixed point, a state or set toward which trajectories tend is an attractor, and its basin is the set of starting points that lead to it. The routine has two attractors, both fixed points, and divides the space of four-digit configurations into their two basins without remainder: everything with no difference falls to 0000, everything with any difference is drawn to 6174.

That is a structural theorem with no reading attached: difference is the condition of circulation. What the author reads into it is offered separately, in the first person: [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-6174-ql-complete-mapping/taylor-2026-6174-ql-complete-mapping|the 6174 mapping]] **sources** the reading, which the manuscript keeps among the deep sources of the matheme. At the fixed point the six appear together: the descending arrangement 7641 at **#0**, ground; the ascending 1467 at **#5**, synthesis; the digits 6, 1, 7, 4 of the result at **#1**–**#4**. The routine states in arithmetic that the explicate four are the measured gap between the two implicate orderings, `#0−#5 = #1#2#3#4`, `7641−1467=6174`; the four re-sort into the two orderings, so at the fixed point the six run in place. The seven-step bound is read as one cycle of six plus the twist that closes the fifth into the ground, and the core theorems already found seven in the sixfold (the four pairs `0+1+2+3` and the two singles `0+1`): the structure contains one more than it counts, and the seventh is the surface's return. The digits sum to 18, and `6174 = 18 + 18² + 18³`. Every identity is checked; the mapping of six arrangements to six positions and the reading of seven as cycle-plus-return are **offered**; the routine is a fact of four-digit decimal arithmetic, and its three-digit cousin settles on 495. The basins are named Anuttara (the void-frame to which undifferentiated configurations return) and the Self (the configuration to which every differentiated one is drawn), and the partition they name is Kaprekar's arithmetic.

**The routine through the two logics.** Subtraction is the operation by which dia-ballein appropriates a span: `(+1)−(−1)=+2` counts the whole relation from one pole and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the other, the healthy polarity `(−1)/(+1)` hardened into a quantity one end can own. Kaprekar's subtraction measures the span between the greatest and least orderings of the same digits and never lets either keep the result; the span is handed back as a new whole and sorted again from both ends. Held inside a re-reading that returns the result to both orientations, the subtraction settles into a configuration in which the measured polarity is the configuration itself: syn-ballein's form, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, with dia-ballein's measuring stroke operating inside it. A subtraction allowed to keep its answer would run one way into a standing quantity.

**Script and basin.** §0/1 · #5→0 left this section the dynamics of conditioning: a script is an articulation that has become a condition of further activity, and its danger is frozen conditioned will. **I argue** that an iterated map is a script in the mathematical register. At 0000 it rests because nothing differs, at 6174 because its difference weighed against itself returns the same difference; a life can settle into either, a habit so uniform that no encounter registers or a pattern so balanced that every encounter confirms it. A fixed point is a determination that has become the condition of its own repetition.

## Warrant: re-entry, signed alternation, iterants

Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form* lets a distinction re-enter the space it distinguishes; an expression that contains itself has no stable value and receives an imaginary state, a state of time rather than space. In the two values, `r = 1 − r` has no solution (0 gives 1 on the right, 1 gives 0), so a static logic calls it a contradiction. Give it a successor, `r(n+1) = 1 − r(n)`, and it runs: `0, 1, 0, 1, …`. Delay supplies a domain in which the operation runs; no hidden Boolean value is discovered. That is the sense in which re-entry makes time. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference|Varela]] formally introduces an [autonomous third state](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference.md#varela-1975-calculus-self-reference-q001), so that self-reference enlarges the arithmetic and does not unroll into a sequence. Alternation and a third value are two responses to one difficulty; the manuscript follows the temporal one because it leads to phase.

Written with signs, `+1, −1, +1, −1, …` shows both logics at once. Held, it is `(−1)/(+1)` given time to move in. Cancellation sums it in pairs, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the whole oscillation disappears with the information about which value came first. Appropriation freezes it at one value, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from the positive end and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the negative, and the oscillation hardens into an opposition in which one pole holds the whole span. Both collapses lose the **phase**, the information about where in its cycle the oscillation stands: `[+1, −1]` and `[−1, +1]` are one process seen one step apart.

**Kauffman's iterants** make the pair of views an algebra: the two views and the shift between them become square roots of minus one. With `D = diag(1, −1)` (keep the first entry, flip the second) and `η` the swap, `D² = η² = I` and `Dη = −ηD`. Their composite `J = ηD` is the matrix `[[0, −1], [1, 0]]` with `J² = −I`, `J⁴ = I`; on a pair, `(x, y) ↦ (−y, x)`. `J` acts as `i` acts on the plane. A quarter-turn is built from two operations that do not commute, and their failure to commute turns alternation into rotation. Kauffman notes that the representation is not unique; the argument needs only that one exists and can be checked.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/kauffman/kauffman-2014-iterants-fermions-dirac-arxiv/kauffman-2014-iterants-fermions-dirac-arxiv|Kauffman's iterants]] **source** the construction; the **offered** signed-dia reading proposes the seam unrolled in time, poles alternating, re-entry making the relation operative, complex orientation recording the phase a one-dimensional line cannot. §0/1 · #1 recalled Pauli's `i`; `i` keeps the phase both collapses discard. Multiplication by `i` carries a determination a quarter-turn, `1 → i → −1 → −i → 1`: opposition along one signed line becomes orthogonal relation across two axes, dia-ballein's line remaining as the diameter, the poles now able to pass into each other by turning where they would collide. The signed seam, unrolled in time, becomes phase, and a return arrives with its orientation transformed and its passage recorded. A fourfold is generated by rotation of one relation and need not be assembled from four independent contents, which §4 · #2 needs for Jung's quaternity. The orthogonality of the base frame's `3² + 4² = 5²` (§3 · #1) and of `i² = −1` (rotation into the dimension perpendicular to the real line) are each derived; **I argue** they are one structural fact, as the core theorems state: manifestation requires a perpendicular dimension, a turn into what the line cannot contain, which generates the line's own opposite.

**Running true.** For `z = re^{iθ}`, multiplying by `e^{iφ}` changes the angle and leaves the modulus: the point moves and its distance from the centre does not. This is the local mathematics of the wheel and thread that §0/1 · #5→0 took from Bohm and Krishnamurti, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue|the Bohm–Krishnamurti wheel and thread]] **figure** the rotation: the thread corresponds to the modulus. It shows that an invariant relation can be borne precisely through transformation; the modulus is the invariant of one specified transformation and defines nothing about truth, while Bohm and Krishnamurti ask the fuller question of what fidelity is.

**Winding and its lift.** A path of nonzero complex numbers that returns to its start does not reveal at its endpoint how many times it circled the origin. Lift the angle continuously and the difference between final and initial values is `2πk`, `k` the number of windings; it belongs to the path with its lift and is no property stored in the final point. A process that claims to remember its return has to keep the history somewhere (a lift, a counter, a record), because arrival alone has forgotten it. The involution of §3 · #1's parents is the same lesson from the other side, recording which phase is active and never how many reversals; §3 · #4 builds a surface on the distinction.

## Warrant: the complex, and the agent's loop

The word *complex* has a second life here. Jung's complex (§0/1 · #3 through Van Eenwyk's dynamical reading; §4 · #1 develops it) is a feedback loop of the kind the routine performed, and read through the two logics it appropriates: each new encounter is counted from its own pole, the friend's silence entered as a `−2` against the self's `+2`, and the counting confirms the pole. **I argue** the psychic complex is an oscillation arrested at one pole and the complex plane an oscillation kept in phase, its two views joined by a turn. What changes a psychic organisation is the symbol, which disturbs the categories through which the gathering occurs and opens a different regime, as a perturbation can carry a trajectory across a basin boundary.

An agent that writes, reads what it wrote and writes again runs the same loop: §0/1 · #2's legal brief, in which a fabricated citation that fitted the argument directed the next one, is a basin in which coherence itself has been recruited to the error. Routine, complex and agent share the dynamics, and the organisation drawn toward can be the empty fixed point of an output that no longer registers its sources or the balanced fixed point of an account in which every piece fits. The remedy follows from phase: keep, beside each claim, the source it was set against and the alternative it excluded, so that a later step can read the claim through its other orientation and not only confirm it.

## Tension / limit

Basin vocabulary is defined in the arithmetic and has to be carried into persons with its conditions attached. The attractor literature applies the notion typically to dissipative dynamical systems and notes systems, the open Hamiltonian ones, for which it is meaningless, so calling a habit an attractor is an analogy until the states, the rule of evolution and what is measured are specified. A literal dynamical model of a particular complex would owe those three; the structure of the loop needs no model to be recognised. The mapping of Kaprekar's six arrangements onto the six positions and the reading of seven as cycle-plus-return are offered, and the 6174 text is recorded in its house as provided by the author, so whether it is his own composition or a dialogue output is still to be confirmed (DEBTS). The `3-4-5` and `i²` identification is argued, and the draft asks whether it should stand as Derived. Varela's own temporal and systemic readings leave the characterisation of frequency to further work ([the second passage](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference.md#varela-1975-calculus-self-reference-q002)); the [frequency-to-retention task](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/formal-limit.md#frequency-retention-and-signed-dia-research) (what recurs, how frequency is measured, what retains the prior state, how retention changes the next crossing) stays a research task that the manuscript's successor rule `r(n+1) = 1 − r(n)` approaches and does not discharge.
