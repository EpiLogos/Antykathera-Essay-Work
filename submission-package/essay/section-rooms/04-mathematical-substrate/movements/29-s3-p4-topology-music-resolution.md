## Claim

Topology and music are two exact forms of return through retained difference, and the manuscript coordinates them as the spatial and temporal rendering of one QL return. On a torus a loop closes locally while its lift on the cover keeps the displacement, and a doubled fourth is completed to the octave by an exact whole tone, so the system reaches octave return through its remainder and not by eliminating it. What joins them is a case, a runner on a four-hundred-metre track: she is back at the finishing line each lap (a point of a closed loop) and her watch reads four hundred, eight hundred, twelve hundred metres (a number on an unbounded line), and both descriptions are correct. Winding vector and frequency ratio are different mathematical objects; what the comparison relates is an operation, each register making return-with-difference exact in its own material, which is analogy in the strict sense of a proportion between two exact relations.

## Warrant: topology first

**Circle, cover, lift.** `S¹ = ℝ/ℤ`: identify `x` and `x + n` for every integer `n`. The slash here is a quotient. That line is the circle's cover, the wrapping map is the covering map, each place has infinitely many distances above it (one per lap), and a journey on the circle lifts to a journey on the line once a start is chosen. A return that arrives at the same place ends at a different number, the difference a whole number of laps, the winding. Hatcher's proof that the fundamental group of the circle is the integers runs through this lifting; it is the claim that a return can arrive at the same place and keep its passage, stated as a theorem. §3 · #2 found the same in the lifted angle of a complex number.

**The torus.** `𝕋² = ℝ²/ℤ²` (§0/1 · #1's gluing of opposite edges, `a b a⁻¹ b⁻¹`), with `π₁(𝕋²) ≅ ℤ × ℤ`. Two counts must be kept apart. `4+2` counts the presentation and its circulation: four sides before identification, two independent loops after. The Euler count, by contrast, takes the cells of the finished surface: four corners become one vertex, four sides two edges, one face, so `χ(𝕋²) = 1 − 2 + 1 = 0`. Reading the first as QL's sixfold depends on keeping the difference; the orientable genus-`g` polygon has `4g` sides and `2g` generators, giving `4+2` at `g = 1`, and QL adopts that as an **offered** structural correspondence while the derivation of the sixfold stays with the native binary self-relation. In the core theorems the cover and quotient are the topological carrier of `∞/0`: every torus point sits under an infinite lattice of points of the plane, the quotient gives that infinity one determinate address while keeping it in the fibre, and the surface is compact with `χ = 0`. Infinity and zero are joined by identification where arithmetic would have cancelled one against the other. The slash is the quotient's, the appointment the essay's, the topology Hatcher's.

**Winding kept, timing lost.** The loop `γ(t) = [2t, −t]` begins and ends at one point of the torus while its lift from `(0,0)` ends at `(2,−1)`: twice around one generator and once back around the other, unchanged by any deformation fixing the endpoints. That class keeps the topology of the journey and loses its timing and every turn; a claim that a return "remembers" has to say which it keeps. A straight flow `t ↦ [t, αt]` closes when `α` is rational and winds densely, never closing, when `α` is irrational: one finite surface carries cadence and inexhaustible traversal together.

**Sphere and torus (offered).** On a sphere every loop shrinks to a point. We want the ego to be a sphere, a form in which every round trip comes to `1 + (−1) = 0`: dia-ballein's cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, written into the fabric. Individuation finds that the ego inhabits a torus, with two independent circuits it did not author (in the Śaiva language of the core theorems, the temporal life-circuit *kriyāśakti* and the archetypal depth-circuit *jñānaśakti*, commuting as the two generators of `ℤ × ℤ` commute). Solving for `x` becomes finding one's winding class `(m, n)`, definite and holding infinitely many concrete paths; a person is a way of going around. Its opposite fault is a local closure presenting itself as the whole, a torus drawn to hide its opening. §4 · #1 takes the reading into individuation.

**Māyā as topology.** A torus inherits a flat geometry from its cover, so no measurement in a small region distinguishes it from the infinite plane; only a journey that winds around discloses that the world is closed and has an opening. A many-to-one map conceals the infinity it identified: the quotient makes the surface finite, habitable and navigable and hides the cover, which is §0/1 · #5→0's Māyā as operative measure.

## Warrant: double covers and the processual chain

§0/1 · #4 promised rotation, projection and double covering; the third belongs here. Next the torus double-covers the Klein bottle (quotient of the plane by translations plus a glide, a shift with a reflection): each point of the bottle has two points of the torus above it, related by the orientation-reversing glide, a two-sheeted cover distinct from the plane's infinite one. A Möbius band shows it in a strip: once along the middle arrives inverted, twice arrives as set out. Hamilton (1844) extended the complex numbers by `i² = j² = k² = −1` with `ij = k` and `ji = −k`; a unit quaternion rotates space and `q`, `−q` give the same rotation, a 2-to-1 homomorphism (Stillwell). With `q = cos(θ/2) + u sin(θ/2)`, a full turn `θ = 2π` takes `q` from `1` to `cos π = −1`, the same rotation carried by the opposite quaternion, and only `θ = 4π`, 720 degrees, returns it to `1`. Two circuits restore what one inverts.

**I argue** the matheme's processual chain has the shape of a double cover. The short definitional chain `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 1/0` becomes, in the full process,

$$
0/1\;\longrightarrow\;4+2\;\longrightarrow\;5\!\to\!0\;\longrightarrow\;1/0\;\longrightarrow\;4'+2'\;\longrightarrow\;5'\!\to\!0'\;\longrightarrow\;0/1,
$$

the first circuit unfolding the relation and returning it inverted as `1/0`, the second unfolding the inverse phase `4′+2′` (the whole held at **#5** in §3 · #1's table) and returning it to the orientation it began from. Both chains are vantages on one relation. Rotation gave the passage from perspective to context its turn, projection a completed space, and the double cover gives the return, in which context comes home through the inverse of the perspective it began from and recognises that inversion as part of the same relation.

## Warrant: the physical witness, the tokamak

[[section-rooms/arguments/A17-Toroidal-Circulation-and-the-Arche-Topos|The tokamak]] **embodies** toroidal circulation in matter. Charged particles follow field lines, a straight magnetic bottle leaks at its ends, and a ring removes the ends. A ring alone is not enough: the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics names the two combined fields, "a ring-shaped field produced by plane external coils; secondly, the field of a current flowing in the plasma. The field lines of the combined field are then helical." The French institute gives the same construction in the topologist's words, a poloidal field added to and perpendicular to the toroidal field, so that "the two fields generate the helicoidal structure of the field lines". Particles wind round the long way and the short way at once; the two coupled fields are the torus's generators made of magnetism. The machine witnesses toroidal circulation in matter, and the force of the argument comes from the topology already derived.

## Warrant: music as the second rendering

Two notes an octave apart sound as the same note. With pitch measured as `log₂(f/f₀)`, an octave is one unit and octave equivalence identifies pitches a whole number of units apart: pitch class `= log₂(f/f₀) mod 1`, which is `ℝ/ℤ`, the runner's circle. Pitch class is her place on the track, pitch height her distance on the watch: a melody that comes home to its tonic an octave higher returns to the same class and keeps the octave. One periodic coordinate gives a circle and not a torus, so the identity of structure holds on one circle and stops there.

Now the remainder of §3 · #1 can be heard. Two fourths, `4/3 · 4/3 = 16/9`, the Pythagorean minor seventh, fall a whole tone short of the octave, and `16/9 · 9/8 = 2/1`: C, the B-flat above it, and the C above that, the last step a whole tone. A Pythagorean keyboard builds its notes from powers of `4/3` and `3/2` folded into the octave, and its whole tone is `9/8` (Scholtz). That whole tone does no damage to the octave; it is how the octave is reached. **The system reaches octave return through rather than by eliminating its remainder.** The arithmetic is derived. That `9/8` is a remainder and that the return is completed through it is the argued reading, with a counterpart in each earlier register: the winding the loop keeps, the phase the oscillation keeps when its sum has cancelled, the constant the derivative lacked and a further condition supplies.

**The comma is another remainder.** Twelve pure fifths folded down by seven octaves do not meet: `(3/2)¹²/2⁷ = 531441/524288 ≠ 1`, the Pythagorean comma, which Scholtz describes as "the difference between 12 consecutive fifths and seven octaves". Equal temperament flattens the fifths by `1/12` of a comma so twelve equal fifths equal seven octaves, paying for the closed circle with fifths that are no longer pure. Whole tone and comma differ in office: the whole tone is the interval through which a doubled fourth completes an octave, the comma a discrepancy a closed cycle of fifths must distribute. One is completion, the other the cost of forcing a different completion. A tuning that treated the whole tone as a defect would read it as cancellation reads a polarity, a shortfall summed away, and would find no octave; tempering the comma is syn-ballein's way with a remainder, neither summed away nor left to fall on one interval, kept and distributed across the cycle.

## Perfect six and offered amplification

The elementary identities cluster around six: `1+2+3 = 1·2·3 = 6`; `0+1+2+3 = 6` while `0·1·2·3 = 0`; `6 ≡ 0 (mod 6)`; the `3-4-5` triangle with area 6 and perimeter 12. These are genuine facts, and their arrangement as a QL `2+4` family, and standard trigonometry's six named functions as a sixfold symbolic plate, are **offered** amplification. M29's prose does not carry the trigonometric sixfold (the plan's §3 · #4 lists it); the note keeps the item here, and a plate earns its place where the generating operations stay visible beside the numerical resonance.
