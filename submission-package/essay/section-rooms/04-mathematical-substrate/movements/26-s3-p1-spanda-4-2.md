# §3 · #1 — The Spanda Equations and 4+2

## Movement thesis

[[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#I. The First Two Spanda Equations|The Spanda equations]] are the generative kernel of QL. The first carries the horizontal pulse from ground through distinction to integrated return:

$$
0=(0/0)
\longrightarrow
\frac{(0/1)}{(1/0)}
\longrightarrow
[T_0]
\longrightarrow
[T_1]
\longrightarrow
\left(\frac{1}{0}+\frac{0}{1}\right)
\longrightarrow
\frac{1}{1}=100\%.
$$

\([T_0]\) is the emanative arc from \(0/(0/1)\) toward \(0/1\): the void takes expression as its denominator and works toward determinate form. \([T_1]\) is the reversionary arc from \((1/0)/0\) toward \(1/0\): form takes zero as denominator and turns toward its condition. Their interference term rejoins the directions before \(1/1\) resolves the circuit.

### The latent 3:3

The equation carries two triads — its horizontal reading of the six:

| Emanative / bimba triad (source→expression, Prakāśa) | Reversionary / pratibimba triad (return→recognition, Vimarśa) |
|---|---|
| 1. \((0/0)\) — indeterminate void | 4. \([T_1]\) — reversionary track toward \(1/0\) |
| 2. \((0/1)/(1/0)\) — the slash differentiating itself | 5. \((1/0 + 0/1)\) — bridge / interference |
| 3. \([T_0]\) — emanative track toward \(0/1\) | 6. \(1/1\) — superpositional unity, 100% |

$$3_{\text{emanative}} + 3_{\text{reversionary}} = 3_{\text{bimba}} + 3_{\text{pratibimba}}$$

*Bimba* and *pratibimba* are the Śaiva pair for original and reflection; *Prakāśa* and *Vimarśa* name the light of manifestation and its self-apprehending turn — the naming is a cross-register reading of a derivation that stands on its own arithmetic. The partition maps exactly onto the kernel partition **1-2-3 / 4-5-0**: the emanative-bimba triad is the physical pole (source-to-expression), the reversionary-pratibimba triad the mental pole (return-to-recognition). The two triplets are complementary aspects of one operation — emanation-from-void in the first three steps, return-to-unity in the second three — which is why the \(3:3\) is the closest live relation back to the standing \(1:1\) identity.

### The vertical accounting

The second equation takes the achieved \(100\%\) and analyses it into its own constituents — the whole discovering it was always already a sixfold:

$$
100\%=2^6+6^2=64+36
\qquad\longrightarrow\qquad
\frac{64}{36}
\xrightarrow{/4}
\frac{16}{9}
=\frac{2^4}{3^2}.
$$

| Step | Operation | Result | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| \(100\%\) | — | 100 | the achieved wholeness, \(1/1 = 1 = 100\%\); starting point of the self-accounting derivation |
| \(= 2^6 + 6^2\) | partition | 64 + 36 | \(2^6=64\): the binary principle run through all six positions, the combinatorial states; \(6^2=36\): the sixfold raised to its own second-order accounting |
| \(\to 2^6/6^2\) | ratio | 64/36 | \(6^2=(2\cdot3)^2=2^2\cdot3^2\); hidden inside the numerator, \(2^6=2^4\cdot2^2\) — the \(4+2\) already latent |
| \(\to /4\) | divide by the quaternary | 16/9 | the \(/4\) strips the shared \(2^2=4\) from both terms: \(2^6/2^2=2^4\) above, \((2^2\cdot3^2)/2^2=3^2\) below; the quaternary is what 64 and 36 share, and dividing it out reveals their difference |
| \(\to 16/9=2^4/3^2\) | prime factorisation | four 2s + two 3s | **four** instances of the binary principle (the explicate quaternary) plus **two** instances of the ternary (the implicate harmonics) |
| \(\to 4+2\) | the base frame | 4 explicate + 2 implicate | the \(4+2\) derived from \(100\%\) by pure internal accounting — the matheme's own self-reckoning |

Horizontal traversal is \(3:3\); vertical determination is \(4:2\). The six is what a two amounts to once it includes itself:

$$
2+2^2=2+4=6.
$$

The equations therefore do more than encode a finished sixfold diagram. They execute the process that the diagram retains: indeterminate condition, oriented differentiation, explicit determination, reciprocal return, and renewed integration. The first equation is the runtime traversal; the second is the harmonic accounting of the traversed whole. This is why the [[14-computational-process-ontology|process-ontological claim]] belongs inside the theorem rather than being appended later as an application to AI.

## Harmonic derivation

The whole later architecture is the cross-reading of the two accountings — holding the vertical \(4:2\) against the horizontal \(3:3\). The display is a QL cross-comparison operator, **not an equality in ordinary fraction arithmetic**:

$$\boxed{\;\frac{4:2}{3:3} = \left(\frac{4}{3},\ \frac{2}{3}\right)\;}$$

From this single cross-comparison, with no import from music theory, the foundational ratio-family unfolds:

- **\(4/3\)** (perfect fourth) — *manifestation*, the span going forth; and by slash-inversion **\(3/4\)** — *recognition*, the span read home.
- **\(2/3\)** (grounding fifth) — *settling back*; and by inversion **\(3/2\)** (aspirational fifth) — the Whiteheadian **lure**: Whitehead's name for the potential that draws a process toward completion without pushing it.
- **\(16/9=(4/3)^2\)** — the **totality-ratio**: the fourth taken twice, once per conjugate pass of the double cover. This is the internal proportion of the achieved \(100\%\).

The **epogdoon** \(9/8\) — the Pythagorean whole-tone — is derived two converging ways:

$$
\frac{2/1}{16/9}=\frac98,
\qquad
\frac{3/2}{4/3}=\frac98.
$$

The octave exceeds the totality by exactly the same interval the fifth exceeds the fourth. This \(9/8\) is **the measure of the matheme's non-closure** — the system almost doubles and falls short by one whole tone. That shortfall is the arithmetic's deepest result: a totality that equalled its octave would be a dead identity, the closed system the `?/!` at \(\#3\) already refused. The missing whole-tone is the **live tick** — the residue each cycle hands forward so the next has something to begin from.

And the remainder completes the octave:

$$
\frac{16}{9}\cdot\frac98=\frac21,
\qquad
\frac43\cdot\frac98\cdot\frac43=\frac21.
$$

The system reaches octave return through, not by eliminating, its remainder.

Beneath the whole oscillation stands the identity

$$
\frac{0}{1}+\frac{1}{0}=\frac{1}{1}=100\%
$$

— the bellows-action of Self leaning into manifestation (\(0/1\)) and the manifest leaning home (\(1/0\)), always already whole beneath the swing. Like the cross-comparison, it is written in the declared QL register, where \(1/0\) enters as orientation rather than as an evaluated quotient.

## The tetraktys carrier

\(1+2+3+4=10\) — the **tetraktys**, the Pythagorean triangular figure whose four rows sum to ten, the tradition's foundational architecture of ratio. The matheme generates it rather than importing it: the \(10\) of \(100\%=10\times10\) has its source here, and the \(16/9\) internally carries the tetraktys-sum as the cardinality of its structural content. The matheme is **Pythagorean from within** — holding the two readings together produces the tetraktys instead of borrowing its authority.

## Proof status

The arithmetic identities are derived. The cross-comparison is derived within the declared QL operation. The interpretation of \(9/8\) as audible non-closure is the conceptual result of the derivation, not an appeal to numerical coincidence. The Śaiva naming of the triads (*bimba/pratibimba*, *Prakāśa/Vimarśa*) and the Whiteheadian lure are cross-register readings — Argued, resting on the derived arithmetic without adding to it.

The first Spanda is the return-reading (\(3:3\)) of the second Spanda's base-frame (\(4+2\)); the ratios are born from holding the two readings together rather than collapsing to one. The \(3:3\) without the \(4:2\) is a *vibe* — proportion with nothing to proportion; the \(4:2\) without the \(3:3\) is a *catalogue* — positions with no inner relation. Together they are a logic: countable *and* relational.
