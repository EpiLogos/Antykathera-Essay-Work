# Bohmian Enfoldment and Dialogical Return

## Claim

Bohm's implicate/explicate order gives the essay a continuous middle between non-objectifiable ground and determinate appearance. `0` becomes discussable as ordered implication without being turned into a hidden object; `1` becomes an explicate event without being treated as an independently self-grounding substance; `/` becomes the holomovement through which enfoldment and unfoldment remain one process.

The tattvas give this relation its full anatomy. Every explicate object carries an enfolded genealogy of luminosity, reflexive power, subjectward and objectward orientation, operative differentiation, contraction, cognition, sensation, action, and material articulation. [[Tattvic Differential Field]] therefore specifies the architecture Bohm leaves general.

## Running true

The relation is valid only if its explications run true to what they implicate. The four visible determinations must unfold from the two operative marks rather than being added as a concordance after the fact. Bohm's spinning-wheel image gives this standard a kinetic body: the wheel turns, the thread remains straight, and truth names fidelity through transformation.

The complex plane supplies the mathematical carrier. Phase rotates through `1→i→−1→−i→1` while modulus remains invariant. The thread makes invariant-under-transformation perceptible; the proof and definitions remain those of complex analysis itself.

## Dialogue as social holomovement

Bohmian dialogue carries the same relation into collective intelligence. Meaning moves through distinct participants, brings tacit assumptions into articulation, and composes a shared field without forcing identical views. The long Bohm–Krishnamurti conversations enact this between physics, philosophy, and contemplative inquiry. The [[SEED — The Language of Spirit Dialogues (2010)|Language of Spirit dialogues]] later carry the method into intercultural encounters between Indigenous and Western knowledge-worlds.

This grounds Epi-Logos as more than an information architecture. A Bimba commons should let claims unfold, circulate through plural lenses, expose their tacit and historical grounds, and return for correction by the people and worlds from which they arose. Shared intelligence is a dialogical holomovement, not a central model aggregating finished statements.

## Agentworld consequence

The same decision-procedural grammar can be shared while world-models remain divergent because the grammar governs movement rather than ontology: disclose, distinguish, circulate, listen, compose, return. Human and artificial participants need not be declared the same kind of subject to enter one inspectable process of meaning-formation.

## Claim boundary

Bohm's physics, Krishnamurti's inquiry, Śaiva tattva theory, Indigenous knowledge, QL, and agent architecture remain distinct. The bridge is operational and *analogical*: each concerns how a whole becomes locally articulate, how fragmentation arises, and how differentiated meaning can return through a shared field.
