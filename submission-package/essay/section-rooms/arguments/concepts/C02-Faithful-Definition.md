# C02 — Faithful Definition

## #0

A definition is faithful when it gives a positive account of what it names and includes, inside that account, the act by which the account is made. Take the case that forces this, the definition of the Subject, which the essay states in one sentence: the Subject is the whole that any coherent representational system participates in, depends upon and necessarily fails to contain. That sentence determines something definite, because it says what the Subject is, distinguishes it from the parts and the systems it holds, and states a relation of dependence. It is also a representation, produced by a system of representation and offered inside the very whole it says no representation can contain. Its author stands where the sentence says no one can stand: outside. A faithful definition accepts this implication, and its fidelity lies in the relation it performs. So the determination discloses its source through a finite form and stays answerable to what that form cannot possess, so the defining act belongs to the reality being defined and acquires no outside position by describing that reality more fully.

[The performed definition of God and Subject](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) **grounds** this by making the defining act part of what is defined. What the performance earns is an exact distinction: **non-exhaustion can be exact**. An account so made can identify, discriminate and commit without making its own boundary the boundary of what gives it standing.

## #1

A definition has to determine. It says what is claimed, distinguishes it from alternatives, and gives the distinction a field in which it can be understood. "Something beyond words" fails here for want of content, since a reader cannot tell the proposed relation from any other. A faithful account takes the risk of saying something definite, and the risk is real because a definite claim can be wrong.

A [determining cut](C03-Determination.md) separates what is named from its alternatives, and the question this record asks is what the cut does to its own conditions. When God names the whole in which the defining takes place, or Subject names the one for whom the distinction appears, treating the referent as one further bounded object changes its office, because the whole that includes the defining has become a part of it. A more elaborate description of the object repeats that change at greater length. Appending "this description is incomplete" repairs nothing if the account goes on operating as though its source were contained, since the disclaimer then sits beside the claim as one more item.

What mends this is a definition that makes its own implication consequential: this is the account, this is the act of accounting, and the second has not been absorbed into the first. That requires no impossible inventory of every condition. It requires the definition to preserve the determining relation through the determination it makes, so a reader can see who is defining, from where, and with what claim committed.

## #2

Draft 3 of the *Definition of God* gives the pressure a bodily exactness: **"a fish trying to describe water while swimming in it."** The sentence about the whole is another event within the whole, and its inability to occupy an exterior is no missing observation that one more sentence would supply. The [formal limit](../A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) recurs whenever a further representation tries to capture the condition of representing, because that further representation is itself made within the condition.

[The definition's failure](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md#taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3-q005) has a specified object, the attempt to exhaust or possess the source in a predicate. That attempt differs from a [remediable absence of information](C04-Formal-Limit.md), which another observation can supply. After any such observation the limit reproduces itself, because the observation takes place within the relation it describes, as a new measurement of a room is taken from inside the room.

## #3

A faithful definition does more than report its limit. It reaches the limit through its own claim and lets that encounter change how the claim is held. Draft 3's definition succeeds through the failure of final predication, because the defining act becomes visible within what the definition means. Its image of the failure is **"a cage where a window was needed"**, and the fault it names lies in the image's claim to totality, since making an image is no fault.

A [living symbol](C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) gives a determinate disclosure through which its source can still answer, and an idol requires the source to answer to the achieved image. A window has a particular frame, and fidelity does not ask for the frame's removal. It asks the account to let through what it frames, including whatever changes the terms under which it was first approached.

The self-opening thought is therefore a strong definition, and a weak one only on the mistaken assumption that strength means closure. Draft 3 calls it **"a determinate proposition built to carry its own negation as cargo."** [Open conceptuality](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3.md#taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3-q022) carries the determination through affirmation, through negation and through the relation that keeps both available, and the negation it carries is aimed at the proposition's pretension to finality. Such an account stays responsible for what it asserts while giving up the claim that assertion possesses its condition.

## #4

This responsibility yields a practical test. A faithful account lets another reader recover what it determines, how it arrived there and what would require correction. An erroneous distinction requires revision, a disputed historical attribution requires its textual warrant, and a finite model's success requires its domain to be stated; invoking an ungraspable whole discharges none of these obligations. A definition must answer in the same way for the act through which it makes its claim.

An [account](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md) answers to its source through the claims, distinctions and consequences it makes available for return. Fidelity keeps two commitments together: the whole exceeds the frame, and this particular frame is where the encounter is taking place. An account that forgets the first becomes an idol, and one that forgets the second evades the work of saying anything answerable.

[Commitment through time](../A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit.md) **extends** the test into a continuing relation, in which [reliance](C48-Trust-Faith-under-Formal-Limit.md) must receive the consequences and corrections its use brings back. Naming a limit does not risk a relation. Fidelity requires that what returns can matter to the account and to the one who gives it.

## #5→0

An account that describes the whole movement is still an account. Draft 3 makes the next turn explicit: the fourth position still speaks *about* God, and first-person recognition changes the register. Whoever attempts the definition recognises their own implication in what was being defined, and recognition makes one consequence unavoidable, that describing answerability cannot stand in for becoming answerable.

The [eight determinations](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **derive** how a formed claim stays related to its generation and its inverse return. That defining movement passes through relation, ground and mark, question and assertion, polarity, the indefinite particular, `AM/IS`, the infinite and finite articulation, and inverse return, and each turn changes how a formed claim stays related to its determining act and to what can answer it. At the [return through Integral Zero](../A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) the achieved sign bears its source-relation without losing its determinate operation.

The [defining act](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) must do what it says, and its achieved form then becomes available for use, encounter and correction. A window stays a window when the light arriving through it is allowed to alter the account.
