# Psychoid Number

## Definition

*Psychoid* names Jung's approach toward an archetypal ordering factor whose reach is not exhausted by conscious psychic contents. His correspondence with Pauli investigates relations among psychological images, mathematical objects and physical events while retaining different methods and objects. The proposed underlying unity, *unus mundus*, belongs to that psychophysical inquiry; naming it does not demonstrate an event-level identity or imply that the pair coined the term. The number program treats number as a primitive ordering factor, making it a proposed point of contact because it appears as inward form and outward quantity. One carries unity and first distinction, two duality and relation, and three synthesis or the dynamic third in this qualitative comparison. These offices remain distinct from the scalar quantities used in a calculation. Jung's rejection of absorption into physics and Pauli's resistance to calling the whole ordering field psychic preserve the productive difference. Marie-Louise von Franz's *Number and Time* develops a further number program requiring that work's own argument. In [the native Pythagorean operation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), identity-as-difference counts its own relations: `1+2+3 = 1×2×3 = 6`, zero's dual nature and the 3-4-5 triple in the base frame. These formal relations generate the sixfold within QL; their equality does not by itself verify a psychophysical correlation.

## In the argument

A psychological image and a physical event can raise a question about ordering that neither disciplinary description alone exhausts. The native sixfold and its relations supply their own generated structure, and their coordination with that psychophysical question requires an argument about the relation preserved across the two descriptions. [[30-s3-p5-arche-topos|The arche-topos]] receives distinct paths, positions and returns within one determining field. [[31-s4-p0-psychoid-problem|The psychoid problem]] asks how psychological and physical descriptions can meet there without one absorbing the other. Bohm's implicate order supplies a distinct process account, while [[A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|Śaiva differentiation]] articulates manifestation within its own conscious whole. A shared numerical pattern can make a comparison precise; the produced image, mathematical object and material event retain their specific source histories and consequences. The comparison would lose its object if those different offices became a catalogue of interchangeable numerical correspondences.
