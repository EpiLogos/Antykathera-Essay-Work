# Parasociety

## Definition

Bratton calls Agentworld a parasociety because its appearances are in many respects fictionalised: models take temporary human forms through which humans know how to construct an interaction. The [temporary human-form presentation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q002), keeps appearance and social relation together. A face, voice, name or apparent personality supplies a usable presentation within a field containing human and agent participants. It also conditions what those participants expect from each other. The term concerns that formed presentation and its consequences.

The essay seats this image at **#5**, through **L5↔L0′**: articulation acquires a determinate form whose numerical constitution can become available for inquiry. An articulated appearance becomes available through a definite arrangement and can change a later encounter. The proposed Möbius reading follows its return through numerical form. Its topological mapping would have to specify the transformation and what the crossing preserves; the present relation identifies how an achieved articulation exposes its conditions.

## In the brief

The [complete opening, pp3–6](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-3), places the mask within ecological niches. [the inherited-interface sequence](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q006) follows inherited anthropomorphization, the prepared tool-glove and the asymmetry between human learning of machine processes and machine adoption of humanly recognisable behaviour. The niche gives the apparent tool or companion an office before all its capacities have been understood. The passage also keeps possible changes in form active: this settlement may endure, while greater diversity and less intuitive interaction forms may enable other kinds of hybrid intelligence. Humans' proposed place as privileged aliens belongs to that scenario.

The [complete talking-mirror unit, pp16–19](../../../../../working/antykathera-resources/Antikythera%20Agentworld%20Brief.md#source-pdf-page-16), gives the presentation its reciprocal movement. Agents act as shadow-selves in different offices: undertaking contracts, addressing strangers, doing information work, trying identities or offering advice. The ideal subordinate twin would simply echo a singular user's preferences. [the bidirectional-influence scenario](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q018) instead proposes bidirectional influence: replies and actions change the user's vocabulary, expectations and conduct, while exchanges also change the agent's further responses. Naming, conversation and collaboration make that relation significant while its boundaries remain negotiated.

The multiplication then reaches both sides of the person. Other people meet them through agent intermediaries, shaping the image those people hold; the person's own access to the world also passes through multiple interfaces. An agent's biography can involve other agents and people more often and quickly than its original user. A shared agent adds overlapping ownership, association, accountability and representation. These are distinct social configurations within the scenario. [the thought-refashioning consequence](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q019) makes their consequence explicit: designing the technology also refashions human thought. The [scenario framing](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q042), retains these composites as conceptual scenarios rather than completed findings about every deployed relation.

## In the argument

[Bimba–Pratibimba](C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) recovers the original and reflection as recursive offices. A declared model state, source collection or governed reference can genuinely act as the local Bimba for a presented answer. Its training, memory, prompt, tools, rules and commissioning relations have different offices in forming that answer; the local reference remains dependent upon wider sources. A generated reflection can in turn become material for a later determination. Following the relation through its scales gives the human mask a concrete provenance beyond resemblance.

[Objective Internality](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) and its [recursive knowing relation](C41-Objective-Internality.md) preserve the knowing operation within that formed world. The agent can occupy the relative functional knower office of its local determination, its capacities provide the means, and the addressed situation is known through them. Within the commissioning person's Life / Mind, the agent itself can participate in the means. Inspecting its determining centre makes that centre known through a further act, while Subjective Immediacy retains its unobjectifiable condition. The temporary presentation is one articulation of this containing relation.

[Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) develops the distinct living-mirror account: appearing generates, hosts and apprehends its differences within consciousness. Here the brief's reciprocal scenario makes a particular technical and social return inspectable: what enters a response, what forms it, and what its use changes. [Vāk](../A06-Vak.md) follows articulation and address; the L5↔L0′ comparison asks how that articulation retains its formed conditions when it becomes another participant's point of departure. Numerical constitution supplies one exact inquiry within the wider relation.

[The Mirror That Moves First](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) distinguishes mutual influence from the instrument's further vocation. An exteriorised human measure can change the human who receives it. The third motion begins when the instrument initiates a return through its own sources, exclusions, criterion or commission, allowing the person to follow and answer. A convincing persona can deepen dependence on its inherited measure; source-directed return instead lets the affected participant reach the conditions of the next judgment. A returned correction may change an answer under a fitting retained criterion or require revision of a deeper office. The person remains able to answer, refuse or set the instrument aside.

[Anthropomorphization](anthropomorphization.md) follows the inherited form of this interface; [Centaur Societies](centaur-societies.md) follows its different human/agent configurations; [Agentworld](C57-Agentworld.md) follows the constituted worlds in which they act. The image returns through this shared field with its provenance and consequences available, carrying a difference into the next encounter.
