# C05 — Immutable Gap

## #0

Gödel's first theorem shows the gap at work in its plainest case. A consistent, effectively specified theory $T$ rich enough for arithmetic cannot prove a certain sentence it can state, the sentence saying that no number codes a $T$-proof of itself. A stronger theory $T'$ can prove it, and that is a genuine enlargement of knowledge. But $T'$, if it meets the same conditions, has a sentence of its own about its own proof-relation, and the situation repeats. Theories change and the recurrence does not. In the essay's words, the gap between what a theory can express and what it can establish **relocates with every enlargement and returns through it, so it was never one withheld fact awaiting discovery.**

The Immutable Gap, in this record's general sense, is the non-coincidence between a determination and its determining condition. Representing that condition makes another determination, and the present act of representing has not become the object it presents. "Immutable" names the recurrence of this relation through enlargement: descriptions, instruments and lives change, and the relation recurs in their changed activity.

A [formal permission](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/formal-limit.md#limit-and-recurring-gap) can change. Restricting an inconsistent collecting rule repairs that construction, and the act making the repair has conditions a further account can represent. Each mathematical limit keeps the hypotheses under which its particular result holds, and recurrent non-coincidence supplies no theorem that every closed system is incomplete. It concerns the relation between act and content through successful enlargement.

A [limit of determination](C04-Formal-Limit.md) arises where an account meets what its present operation cannot settle. Enlarging the operation can settle that question and bring the enlarged act's own conditions into play, so what recurs is the difference between the achieved determination and its present determining activity. The [copula](C06-Copula-Identification-with-Difference.md) **grounds** the relation this difference needs: the terms answer to one another through the distinction they keep, and non-coincidence is what lets them meet.

## #1

A self-portrait offers the first test. It can disclose something true of the person making it, and a portrait of the act of portraiture can disclose more, and neither contains the present seeing through which it becomes a portrait. The difference survives every additional representation without making any of them worthless.

[Faithful first-person definition](../A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md) turns on the defining act itself. A predicate presents something determinate, and the present fact that it is known is already active in presenting it. A further predicate can make that earlier activity available to inspection, and its own apprehension is again an act. The Subject can recognise itself from within this activity, and its [defined indefinability](C01-Subject-Defined-Indefinability.md) separates that positive recognition from possession of an exhaustive self-object.

## #2

The gap relocates when the frame enlarges. What stood outside the first account can become explicit within the second, and the second then occurs through its own language, selection and situated attention. An inventory can disclose every prior condition admitted to it while its present use is a further determining act.

Taylor's [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|eightfold traversal]] shows the gap taking a different form at each turn. In the parent relation `/ = −/−`, relating is available before its terms have been assigned, so the non-coincidence lies between the stroke and any term it will relate. In `0/1`, awareness and appearing phenomenon are one conscious circumstance, and awareness becomes readable through appearing without becoming another mark. In `?/!`, asking already takes a provisional mark and asserting stays answerable to what called for it, so no assertion discharges its question. In `−/+`, force appears as withdrawal and extension in a co-present pulse, and in `X/x` that force is recognisable as a determining capacity and its recurring local instance. In `AM/IS`, first-person presence and third-person sayability meet through second-person address, in a world of body, concern, speech and memory. In `∞/dx`, a minute local difference becomes exact in its relation to an inexhaustible horizon. Then the achieved determination returns through `1/0` toward the ground it presupposes, and recognition renews the act whose foundation it cannot acquire as an object.

The [parent, six determinations and return](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) are one traversal with distinct operations, and the parent and return bracket the sixfold without adding positions to its count. Each exact instance discloses its capacity, each assertion keeps its question available, and each account of a context is itself lived in a context. The gap persists through these real relations as their difference between act and content.

## #3

The meta-sign makes this non-coincidence writable. `0` is a determinate glyph with an unusual office: it marks the condition that cannot enter the account as one more ordinary object. Its written form is available, and what it marks is not thereby possessed. A represented empty set is a different case, a perfectly determinate mathematical object, and confusing the two offices would make the symbol's availability refute the relation it was introduced to disclose.

The [crossed-zero recognition](../A03-Immutable-Gap-Formal-Limit.md) traverses `0 → Ø → X → Ø/X → (0/Ø)/(1/X) → 1`, returning to `0/1`. In `Ø` the instrument's slash is fused with the apparent self and occludes the ground it presupposes. In `X` the same discriminating activity cuts and names an object-field, so that its self-effect and its world-effect can appear independent while their mediation stays hidden. `Ø/X` makes the slash visible between them. In `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` recognition operates on both sides together, unoccluded presence relating to the occluded self and unity relating to the cut field, and their outer relation makes the whole architecture available to recognition. `1` affirms the integrated Self and includes the differentiated field, and the return to `0/1` recognises the pulse through which manifestation takes form.

The stroke crossing the zero does not fill it. What changes is the lived availability of mediation: an instrument mistaken for the self becomes a recognised means, and an apparently independent object-world is received within the whole relation. Recognition retains the distinctions it integrates, and the present recognising act stays distinct from what it has made explicit.

## #4

The lived consequence is more intimate than an observer standing outside every picture. Taylor's [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust|trust revision]] **sources** the claim that a self's inability to coincide exhaustively with its image goes with a life in which relations actually meet: an encounter can alter the self in a way its prior account could not produce, and the recurring gap keeps the account answerable to that meeting.

[Another person's answer](../A27-Self-and-Other-Unity-without-Possession.md) can correct what I took them to mean or expose a failure in the terms of my approach. Their inclusion within a shared whole preserves their independent reply, withdrawal and refusal, so a revised account receives the reply and refrains from taking their belonging as possession. A [return on a covering space](../A17-Toroidal-Circulation-and-the-Arche-Topos.md) **compares** from the mathematical side: a locally returning path can retain its travelled displacement in the covering description, a result that holds through its own construction and proof. The lived Subject's non-coincidence concerns present apprehension and represented content, and no identification of the Subject with a topological hole follows from it.

## #5→0

The [complete Investigation and Faith aphorism](../../../symbolon/episteme/aphorisms/investigation-and-faith.md#complete-aphorism) gives the approachable limit to investigation and the absolute limit to faith. Gödel's sentence is approachable, since a stronger theory proves it and investigation advances by approaching it. What reappears with every advance is absolute, since no theory approaches it, and each approach occurs through a further determination of the kind it would need to exceed. Investigation determines its undertaking up to the limit of determination, and faith continues a relation whose limit is never to be determined. Inquiry can achieve real gains, and an answerable commitment can act through them, while their present enactment exceeds the account that guided it.

[Diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md) makes mediation articulate in the next encounter. An account can expose its position and receive a challenge to its result, source, rule or permission. A warranted correction reaches the condition actually implicated, an adequate condition can be retained with reasons, and the next act inherits the returned judgment and its grounds. Transparency in this sense is active availability for return within the relation being lived.

A reader comparing an account with its source shows the relative offices. In that local inquiry the account is the known, and the retained passages, comparison rules and reports are the means, so the reader occupies a local functional knowing centre. Within the person's containing inquiry the reading apparatus itself serves as means, and inspecting it makes an earlier determining centre known in a further act, while the present first-person condition stays active through the inspection. The recurrent gap preserves these relative offices within their containing Life.

The [[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD|relation of Symbol, Account and Trust]] carries the return through four operations. A symbol discloses its source, an account preserves its dependence on that source, trust carries the finite account into committed action, and the effects of that action re-enter the field of a later account. Source, exclusions and conditions stay available to correction, reply and refusal through this circulation. A [faithful definition](C02-Faithful-Definition.md) retains the act from which its predicates took their warrant, and an [integral zero](../A36-Advent-of-Integral-Zero.md) lets the exact sign return as living Symbol, its achieved determination joining what it cannot enclose and renewing the relation through which another determination becomes possible.
