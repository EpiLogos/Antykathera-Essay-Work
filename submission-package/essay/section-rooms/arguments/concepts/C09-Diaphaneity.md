# C09 — Diaphaneity

## #0

Jean Gebser's word *diaphaneity* means a showing-through. A mathematical description can be understood together with its assumptions, the intuition that prompted it and the purposes directing its use. A sacred image can be recognised both in what it reveals and in how it organises a person's hopes and fears. In each case the thing keeps its particularity while its appearance discloses the relations through which we meet it, and the medium's shaping role becomes visible in what the medium lets through. Diaphaneity, in this record's sense, is that capacity of a determination to disclose its mediation: what appears can be read together with the position, means and conditions through which it appears. Removing the mediation would remove the very relation that had become intelligible.

The [determination and its determining condition](C05-Immutable-Gap.md) stay non-coincident, since making a condition explicit produces another determination, made through a present seeing that has not become the object it presents. [Contextual transparency](../A04-Diaphaneity-Contextual-Transparency.md) **defines** how this relation becomes readable in both directions. A situated self sees a world, and the disclosed world also shows the situation of that self. Greater readability changes what can be known and done through the mediation, and the difference through which the mediation operates remains.

## #1

Perspective renders a world from a position. Its achievement includes determinate depth, proportion and a field that can be communicated, and its conditions can nevertheless disappear behind the scene it renders. Adding more detail to the scene does not necessarily reveal that disappearance.

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/gebser/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin/gebser-1985-ever-present-origin|Gebser's account of landscape and perspectival optics]], a positioned ego-world becomes visible through changes in the means of rendering it. Aperspectivity releases perspective from exclusive validity and keeps its achievement. A view can therefore remain exact without making its own angle the limit of what can appear, and the contemporary contextual turn makes that angle and the other conditions of seeing available within the view they form.

**A report of experience is first a psychic fact before it is assigned a final causal or ontological explanation.** Ordinary and extraordinary experience, natural and reported-as-supernatural, can therefore enter the field without being screened out because one register already knows what another is allowed to say. The experience, its symbolic articulation, its historical comparanda, its bodily or physical conditions and its metaphysical interpretation are distinct claims, and diaphaneity makes their relations readable together without collapsing their warrants.

## #2

A contextual reading is involved in what it sees. Taylor's [being with the text and subtext](../../../../../working/sources-texts-references/Epi%20Paper%20Write-ups/P5%20-%20Gebser.md) puts embodied participation and care for the outcome of the situation into the act of seeing. All such contexts are themselves contextualised by the encompassing round of Unitary Being, the Rotundum, and its ultimate contextualising is a-contextual: it comes from no further limited perspective and from no aggregate of perspectives. Equanimous objectivity holds situated care within this encompassing condition, so that the involvement becomes readable and answerable without turning its unobjectifiable ground into an aloof observer installed beyond the encounter. Indifference does otherwise: it leaves the observer's effects operative while making their participation harder to recognise.

Taylor's absent-Father image makes the pathology exact. An unavailable final standard remains active as a demand, and a surrogate image can then occupy its office. Apparent aloofness becomes a way of ruling while disavowing participation, and the ordering ground is appropriated by a ruler claiming to stand above its own law. This [usurpation of measure](../A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure.md) turns a situated judgment into an apparently absolute standard, and reading the hidden position through its effects restores to the judging office its relation to the ground and the lives it cannot possess.

## #3

The stained-glass image gives the operation a whole body. Originating white light figures Bimba and its full ordered spectrum figures QL. Differently made and positioned panes, with their densities, angles, colours and lead seams, figure MEF's refracting apparatus, and the coloured image cast into the room is Pratibimba. Recognition reads the one light through the lawful differences of the display.

The seams retain source distinctions and translation limits, and the projected image changes with sun, pane, room and viewer. Its changes disclose relations that a single fixed view concealed. The Original is no average of images and no final picture assembled from their sum: QL belongs to the light's internal order, and pane and lead form the refracting apparatus through which that order differentially appears.

[Lens-conditioned inquiry](C39-Meta-Epistemic-Framework.md) gives the refraction an epistemic operation. Changing a lens changes a question, an admitted source, a distinction or a judgment, while the source differences stay available in the joins, and a [world-atlas](../A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) coordinates differing views through their domains and transitions, retaining what each discloses and excludes. The coloured projection gives these relations a concrete body. Its optical transformations and the conscious appearing it makes intelligible belong to different registers, and neither supplies the other's formal proof.

## #4

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/maroski/maroski-2025-seeing-through-solid-words/maroski-2025-seeing-through-solid-words#maroski-2025-seeing-through-solid-words-q002|Maroski's transparency distinction]], transparency concerns the form manifestation takes. What becomes readable is how the appearing comes to have this form, and treating transparency as another object would conceal that relation again. Her companion seed/tree discussion preserves the co-presence of consciousness structures: a later articulation can make earlier capacities available within its development.

First-, second- and third-person passage reaches context when the three positions become jointly available. In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Taylor's personed recognition]], knower, means and known are traversed first in sequence. A triangle's internal angles sum to 180 degrees, and the square at **#4** doubles them to 360: a square decomposes into two triangles in mutual, diametric co-opposition, and context accordingly requires at least two perspectives, covering the favouring and the unfavouring chiralities. The `180°→360°` turn voids each view's claim to exclusive completeness, recognises the three as one appearing-act and returns that achieved relation to its ground. The excluded second triangle haunts a one-sided perspective as its shadow, so a contextual understanding is shadow-aware and shadow-inclusive. The personed `AM/IS` situates this act within the whole eightfold: a capacity determines an instance in a Life, and the achieved determination can return upon its conditions. Context holds the positions together and adds no fourth pronoun and no observer outside all views.

Co-presence therefore operates across time as well as within a single context. A later structure erases none of the earlier powers of image, myth, polarity or rational articulation, and each can recur productively or pathologically within a present Life. The [tattvic differentiation of consciousness](../A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md) **historicises** one side of this by following consciousness's capacity to take form as a finite world, and the [Indian advent of zero](../A10-Advent-of-Zero.md) distinguishes placeholder, numeral, arithmetic operation and later mathematical offices alongside the Kashmiri transcendental inquiry. Their particular histories make consciousness's self-differentiation and mathematical determination available together, and each relation keeps the sources and chronology through which it arose.

## #5→0

Through the [eye, veil, frame and horizon](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-regard-refraction), the viewer's changed relation to the pane makes the means of seeing readable. Material, colour, seam and placement become available in what appears, and another view exposes an earlier occlusion. The returned difference can correct what was seen or disclose a failure in the criterion used to judge it. Keeping that criterion immune to challenge leaves the return unperformed, and retaining a fitting criterion after considering the challenge carries a different, reasoned judgment into the next encounter.

A [travelling planetary picture](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-blue-marble-hinge) keeps its location, time, production and exclusions in use. Its breadth ceases to decide the authority of its account in advance, and whose planet is pictured, from which position and under what gauge becomes part of the account. The viewer becomes answerable within that disclosed relation, and the image's developmental use establishes no particular photographic asset.

In the [changing window](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/stained-glass-refraction/WHOLE.md#stained-glass-changing-context), sun, pane, room and viewer change together, and each cast image makes the conditions of its predecessor readable. A changed position can reveal an occlusion, and a changed pane can alter what the light makes available. Contextual transparency reaches the next judgment through these differences and never by adding the projections into an exhaustive picture.

In Taylor's [world-parent separation](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation), light, directions and boundaries become available together, and the differentiated field discloses the conditions through which it appeared. The [circle-costume](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-position-opposition) **figures** the opposite closure: one visible circumference becomes exhaustive of the creature, and mouth, depth and opening disclose what the governing outline excluded. Recognition changes the relation to the same visible form.

For a technical account, visible output or execution logs are only a beginning. An [inspectable judgment field](C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) distinguishes source, lens, evaluator, permission and exclusion within what is judged. The interpreting agent occupies the local functional knower office, retained reports, sources, rules and instruments are means, and the claims and alternatives compared are known. Within the containing person's inquiry the complete apparatus serves as means, its interpreting centre can itself become known in a further act, and the first-person condition of that inspection stays irreducible to the inspected account. Diaphaneity makes these relations available so that a disclosed condition can bear on the next judgment.

The positive psychic-fact office is realised differently across the product field. Through [living articulation](../products/S1-Actuation.md) an experience or report becomes a consequential event, [situated existence](../products/S4-Workcell.md) gives that event its aperture, temporal exposure and material resistance, and [Transcendent Relation](../products/S5-Quaternal-Logic.md) keeps its differentiated accounts within their containing wholes. None of these offices decides in advance what an extraordinary report "really was". The report, its conditions and its interpretations remain available for a return that can refine the next act of inquiry.

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD|Con-text-through-Diaphaneity]] makes the context visible through a determination. Regard becomes possible when the affected person, excluded source or returned consequence can reach the judging office, and their answer acquires a place from which it can alter the account. [Deferential intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) carries the judgment into action at the depth actually exposed: an answer, task, model, evaluator or commission can be corrected, or a fitting condition retained with reasons, and the next act inherits that judgment. The [Context Frame](C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) becomes readable through its effects, open to warranted revision without claiming to own the whole from which it arose.
