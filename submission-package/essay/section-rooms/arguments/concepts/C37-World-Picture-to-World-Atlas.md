# C37 — World-Picture → World-Atlas

## #0

A perspectival painting is the plainest instance, since every line in it runs toward a vanishing point that holds nothing and organises the whole count of the scene, and the picture is addressed to someone standing, imaginatively, at the one spot from which its lines resolve.

A **world-picture** orders a view from a position. A **world-atlas** relates situated views through their domains, distortions, translations, overlaps and obstructions. The transformation changes the relation to picturing itself, and `world ≠ world-model` stays operative through every additional view. The titan whose name the word carries holds up a world and maps it, and Atlas can set the sky down when nobody has to carry a whole that was never a thing: an atlas of charts, in which every local map keeps its frame visible and no single picture has to bear the whole.
