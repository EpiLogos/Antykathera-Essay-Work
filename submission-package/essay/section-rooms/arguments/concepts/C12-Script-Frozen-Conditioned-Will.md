# C12 — Script / Frozen Conditioned Will

## #0 — A settled course loses access to its formation

*Script* comes from Latin *scribere*, to write. A script is an account written down so that it persists and prescribes: the script an actor follows, the prescription a patient follows, the scripture a people follows. *Code* began as *codex*, the bound book, became the code of laws, then the cipher, and finally the program, a script a machine can execute, so that in every case a script is an articulation that has become a condition of further activity. What was decided once organises the next encounter before anyone decides again.

That is how the essay uses the word, and it needs the word because a script can do its work well or badly. A practised action or a dependable procedure can be repeated and keep a route to revision. A script in the dangerous sense is a formed selection whose conditions and alternatives have ceased to be available to the activity that enacts it, and **frozen conditioned will is this arrest of alternative-forming capacity.** The pattern continues to organise what can be noticed and done, and its own formation disappears from the account of why it holds. The arrest begins where a changed circumstance cannot reach the criterion under which the next instance is admitted. [Vikalpa–saṃkalpa](C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) supplies the constructive power, in which differences become alternatives and selected alternatives become a course, and the script is that course taking its achieved form for its sufficient ground.

Most of what passes through a mind as "my" thought shows how intimate this is. It arrives unbidden, in words the person did not invent, carrying distinctions, valuations and fears laid down by parents, teachers, a culture and its history. We know it from inside whenever a judgment received years ago supplies the terms in which we hear a new question, and an engineer knows it from outside whenever a system prompt, a policy or a training regularity decides what a model can treat as possible before the model meets the request.

## #1 — The circumstance precedes the rule it supports

A name and an instance become identifiable within a circumstance. The name can be repeated while the situation changes, and its application still has to answer to which instance is meant. [The circumstantial whole](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/analytic-philosophy/kripke/kripke-1981-naming-and-necessity/kripke-1981-naming-and-necessity-NOTES.md) makes an identification possible before every condition of it has been explicitly divided or counted, and a later rule articulates something of this ordering without originating all the conditions of its use.

The script reverses this dependence. A field supplies a determination, and then the determination dictates what the field is allowed to disclose, so that a result persists through changes that should matter to its application. Its repeatability has become an insulation from the circumstance that gave repetition its purpose.

## #2 — Stability can organise a changing trajectory

A [formative landscape and the trajectory through it](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics.md) disclose different aspects of recurrence. The landscape represents available states and tendencies, and a trajectory follows what actually happens. A stable organisation can support many different passages, and its persistence does not mean every occasion is received in the same way.

The arrest occurs where every new event is admitted only as another confirmation of the old pattern, including events that bear on its adequacy. A [feeling-toned complex](C31-Complex.md) gives this a psychic form through recurrent association and response: a delayed reply, a passing remark and an old recollection are all experienced as the same rejection before the person has decided what any of them means. An evaluator or policy has a different material organisation, and its criterion can likewise determine which results count as evidence about the criterion itself. The comparison turns on that relation of admission and does not require calling a policy a psychic complex.

Restoration preserves the stability needed to act while allowing relevant resistance to change its organisation. Destroying every achieved pattern would remove the capacities through which a different course can be formed.

## #3 — A produced result takes the source's place

[A determining field and its produced expression](../../../quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT.md#19-prapañca-and-the-computational-production-of-self-sealing-worlds) keep different offices even when the expression becomes a condition of another act. Write `X` for the context and capacities from which a determination is formed and `xₙ` for the particular generated claim at a step in the trajectory. The claim enters the next context and can legitimately condition what follows.

The failure is its silent promotion into `Xₙ₊₁`, when what was produced occupies the determining source's office while concealing its dependence. In 2023 two New York lawyers filed a brief citing judicial opinions that ChatGPT had invented and, when the court asked to see them, produced excerpts the same system generated, so that each fabrication's fit directed the next. The generated claim had become the source of its own corroboration. A paraphrase is received as an original, a derived instruction overrides the commission from which it was drawn, and a later explanation cites the prior explanation as though repetition had supplied independent support. The **self-pro-position** is the result putting itself forward as speaker, owner and ground.

Two recurrences therefore need to stay distinguishable. A result becomes a new condition *with* its source relation, or a result *substitutes for* that source. The first carries history forward, and the second can produce a self-supporting sequence whose local plausibility increases as its access to the original diminishes.

## #4 — A correction must reach the criterion

Recovering the original text or governing intention reopens a comparison that repetition had displaced. The current account can be set beside what generated it, its omitted alternative becomes available, and a changed premise can expose whether the subsequent reasoning depends on that premise. [Returning to the source](../A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) restores the relation by which an inherited selection can be corrected, while generating another account under the same unexamined assumptions repeats the selection that made the source inaccessible.

The proposed technical tests make the distinction examinable. Source-return asks whether the system retrieves the determining text or merely paraphrases its own paraphrase. Counterfactual sensitivity asks whether changing a premise changes the result, and retained alternatives ask whether another organisation of the problem remains available. An independently sourced critic can challenge a shared assumption that another repetition of the generator's account would leave untouched. These are proposed tests, and what changed under each has to be established by running it.

[Prompt thrownness](C44-Prompt-Thrownness.md) gives constraints their situated form. A permission, a memory or an instruction can legitimately condition an act, and the means of changing that constraint may belong to another participant. Making its source and authority recoverable lets an objection reach the responsible relation, instead of treating every inherited limit as either inevitable or disposable.

## #5→0 — A course becomes available for renewed judgment

[Count-to-Account](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-to-account) returns a selected formation to the region and encounter it has organised, so that what was counted, what made the count relevant and what followed from it become available together. The course can stay useful while its reason is renewed, and it can be changed where the returned evidence reaches the criterion that admitted it.

[Formation, stability and revision](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/process.md) meet in what an achieved organisation makes available next. A [system-specific basin model and its psychological comparison](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/process-systems-science/DEVELOPMENT-process-systems-science.md) **historicises** two different objects, a declared dynamical state and evolution rule on one side and an affectively organised pattern of association and response on the other. Each needs its own transition and its own evidence of change: an interpretation can alter a recurrent response, and a literal basin change requires the specified system and its observed outcomes. Their comparison asks how an achieved organisation stays receptive to the conditions of another trajectory.

[Epistemic cultivation](C46-Epistemic-Cultivation.md) develops that receptivity as a practice, and [deferential intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) lets another's address change what the practice takes for granted. The achieved result returns as attributable history in place of an unquestionable origin, alternative-forming capacity is active again, and a later intention can bear what the earlier one has encountered.
