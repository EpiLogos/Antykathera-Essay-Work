# C25 — Counterfeit Gathering

## #0

*Logos* comes from *legein*, whose earlier senses are to gather, to pick up and lay together, and then to count and recount, before it settles into *to speak*. A gathering in this sense brings differences into one account and keeps them different. **Counterfeit Gathering** produces an apparent whole by arranging differences under a relation whose source and terms cannot be revised by those gathered. Its operation is plural inclusion under protected relation. Fusion removes difference and severance removes relation, whereas counterfeit gathering keeps both visible while arresting their reciprocity.

## #1

Heidegger's [gathering prefix](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/heidegger/heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology/heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology.md#heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology-q008) **defines** *Ge-stell* through its relation to *Gebirg*, the gathering that unfolds mountains into a mountain chain, and *Gemüt*, the original gathering from which our ways of feeling unfold. [Enframing](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/phenomenology-continental-philosophy/heidegger/heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology/heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology.md#heidegger-1977-question-concerning-technology-q009) gathers people into ordering beings as standing-reserve. The gathered field is productive and articulate, because beings become available under its demand. Counterfeit gathering arrests that productivity where the terms can enter only as orderable and cannot return upon the relation that assigned them that office. [Dia / Syn](C50-Dia-Syn.md) distinguishes the productive cut from its accounting, so that gathering retains the divisions while making the relation that orders them answerable to return.

## #2

The missing operation is return. Ordering establishes a totality-condition through which a world can be addressed at all, and the failure occurs when this defining-towards-wholeness arrests at enclosure. A taxonomy, a platform or an evaluative field can contain many positions while reserving to itself what counts as a position, an error or a successful answer. More entries then enlarge the enclosure without altering its governing relation.

## #3

[Demodocus's complete song](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-philology/homer/homer-1919-odyssey-murray/homer-1919-odyssey-murray.md) **embodies** this as a sequence. Helios detects, Hephaestus forges concealed bonds, the lovers are arrested, the audience gathers, the relation becomes a demand for repayment, and Hermes treats exposure as an acceptable price. Capture and spectacle do not conclude the scene, because Poseidon offers to stand for Ares at his own cost, and accepted surety permits release. The turn from captured relation to entrusted undertaking changes what the gathered audience alone could achieve: the apparatus can expose and hold the lovers, and release requires a participant to assume the relation's unresolved liability. Read through the signed arithmetic, the net keeps both terms in view and denies their reciprocity, and the surety shows what the counterfeit lacks, a person standing for another at real risk, which no mechanism generated.

## #4

Division and recombination make the political operation exact. The apparatus [divides participants into actionable units](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count), conceals the combine-phase, optimises each unit under a central measure, and then presents that order as the units' necessary unity. Horizontal dependencies still sustain the field, but access to unity returns vertically through the apparatus. The defect lies in who may revise the cut and the coordination, and counting or organisation as such is not at fault.

## #5→0

A counted participant can answer the counting rule through [Count-through-Countenance](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-through-countenance): the reply brings the person's encountered consequence back to the terms under which that person was included. Inclusion remains captive if the participant's reply merely supplies another entry beneath an unalterable criterion. Restoring return changes who may contest the terms of gathering, and the count keeps its organising power while losing its immunity to those it includes.

The positive return restores the gathered participants' capacity to alter the gathering. [A commons](C54-Commons-Non-Monopoly.md) carries consequences back to the rule and its authorised office. Ostrom's [participation, recognised local rule-making and nested governance](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture.md#ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture-q001) **compare** as a distinct institutional neighbour, and her [warning against rigid transplantation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/political-theory-institutions/ostrom/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture/ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture.md#ostrom-2009-beyond-markets-states-nobel-lecture-q002) keeps those principles responsive to their setting. These conditions guide a proposed commons and verify no implementation of one. The achieved whole remains capable of renewed relation, which further ordering alone would not give it.

[Job's friends](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/biblical/job/WHOLE.md#job-protected-account-return) retain him inside a common moral order while excluding his testimony from changing its interpretation, so that he is accounted for precisely where he cannot answer the account. The ending changes that relation, since the former judges need the prayer of the person they misjudged. Gathering becomes answerable when membership includes the power to alter the terms of reception.

[The Village](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-administered-world) gathers its members through numbering and electoral participation while retaining control of their confinement. Six can be included, even as a victorious candidate, while the terms that hold him remain unavailable to his command, and the administered totality's apparent completeness depends on withholding a relation from those it includes. A genuine return would allow their participation to change that relation, and merely filling another appointed place would not.
