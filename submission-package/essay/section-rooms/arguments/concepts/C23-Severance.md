# C23 — Severance

## #0

Two independent measurements of one object, each recorded with its instrument, unit and conditions, are in hand, and one of them is inconvenient. Delete it and the remaining figure stands as though the other had never been taken: the source and the alternatives through which the figure arose are externalised, and the figure presents itself as independently grounded. That is **severance**: achieved difference misread as the loss of constitutive relation. The cut still depends on what its account declares absent, and the deleted measurement goes on conditioning what the surviving one means.

[Cutting and gathering](C50-Dia-Syn.md) keep differentiation and its constitutive relation together. Distinction, classification, refusal and separation can all be necessary, and severance adds a further claim, that the produced term no longer bears the relation through which its independence became possible.

## #1

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Core Theorems I and XI]] distinguish a living signed polarity from its collapsed results. `(−1)/(+1)` holds opposed values around a shared axis. Addition gives `(−1)+(+1)=0`, one subtraction gives `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and the inverse gives `(+1)−(−1)=+2`. These are distinct arithmetic operations, and each retains or removes different information about the relation.

In [the two-logics reading](../A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md), cancellation and appropriation can be mistaken for completion of the relation. The arithmetic results stay correct, and the philosophical loss occurs when the collapsed result is treated as all there was to know about the field and the operation that produced it. When computable determination is made the sole criterion of reality, the same arithmetic runs on the means of knowing themselves: the failed return toward the condition of determining is dismissed along with what it was trying to reach, the `0/` is jettisoned, and the account proceeds among the surviving `1`s as though their capacity to appear, relate and become known needed no further thought.

## #2

A term's [semantic non-application](C18-Apoha.md) remains part of how it applies. Removing traces of relevant alternatives from an account does not abolish their constitutive work, and exclusion remains an operation when its achieved reference is subsequently taken as a positive given.

[Recursive source substitution](C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) repeats the further loss: a generated determination enters a later context as independently sourced, and repetition strengthens that appearance. Recovering the source can change the inherited course while retaining the valid work the course achieved.

## #3

The core's divide-and-conquer example makes the productive cut exact. Dividing a problem creates tractable subproblems, and combining their results is part of the method's success. Severance occurs when decomposition is taken as the whole intelligence of the operation and the products' recomposition is lost or monopolised.

An [occupied return](C22-Counterfeit-Symbolon.md) retains the power to combine while treating divided participants as self-standing and replaceable. A system boundary has a different possible office: it can state what crosses, under which authority and how the result bears on a wider task, and keeping those conditions answerable lets a bounded result return into recomposition.

## #4

In Taylor's [[quilt/27-07-26-QUILTING-FOR-FULL-ARGUMENT#10. Ares and Aphrodite: divide/unite become felt love/war logics|division-and-union comparison]], refusal, defence, truth-telling and the capacity to say no give differentiation ethical force. The ability to meet requires beings capable of remaining distinct, and relation cannot compel continued proximity or agreement.

Severance begins where the other's removal is imagined to complete the whole, or an autonomous position denies what sustains it. [Fusion](C24-Fusion.md) makes the opposite mistake by requiring difference to vanish. A legitimate separation keeps its history, obligations and consequences intelligible while the separated terms retain their force.

## #5→0

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD|Proportion among differentiated terms]] returns through the respect in which they stay related. Reconnection states what is shared and what remains distinct, since a real conflict keeps its particular stakes and a vague assertion of unity cannot do their work.

The productive [cut](C50-Dia-Syn.md) enters a [living relation](C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) where its source and consequences can be recovered. A divided task can return an incompatible premise to the commissioning condition, and a corrected source or authorised task can then enter recomposition while the local result remains credited. The next act inherits what the return warranted, and distinction thus serves inquiry, autonomy and action without claiming to create an independently grounded world.
