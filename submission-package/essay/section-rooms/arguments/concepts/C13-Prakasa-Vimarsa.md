# C13 — Prakāśa–Vimarśa

## #0

*Prakāśa* is luminous appearing, the light of consciousness by which anything appears at all. *Vimarśa*, from the root *mṛś*, to touch, feel, perceive, reflect or examine, is the power by which that light grasps itself and whatever occurs within it. Without vimarśa the light would amount to phenomena appearing to no one, like light reflected in an ordinary mirror or crystal. An appearance is therefore known in the act of appearing, and it is not first displayed to an absent observer and then lit by another awareness, because requiring a second awareness to reveal the first would demand a third to reveal the second, and so on without end. The reflexivity belongs inside the occurrence. Their inseparability is the living relation through which a difference can appear, be recognised, retained and articulated.

In [luminous self-presence](../A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md), appearing is illuminated, difference apprehended, an affection retained and a new act formed. Reflection, cognition, freedom and recognition distinguish these relations within one field, and self-presence is already operative in the experience from which the inquiry begins.

## #1

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration|Dyczkowski source]] develops the relation through cognition and the living mirror. Subject, object and means attend one act together at pp. 63–64, and blue and yellow are then known as different within one cognition at pp. 65–66. Homogeneous awareness could not distinguish them, and unrelated acts could not hold their difference together, so formlessness and the capacity to bear forms belong to the same knowing.

The mirror has two limits. An ordinary mirror depends on an exterior original, while the Śaiva field of consciousness generates and hosts its appearances, so that its original is imported from no second universe outside consciousness (pp. 66–68). An ordinary mirror also displays without apprehending what it displays, and the crystal's reflected blue is not the crystal's recognition of blue (pp. 69–70). Vimarśa names living self-apprehension and adds no further reflection to the first.

[Mediation](C10-Mediation-Pramana.md) receives the epistemic consequence: the mode of disclosure belongs to the act through which a differentiated world is known.

## #2

Reflexivity has a temporal body. An experience leaves a residual trace, and recollection brings the earlier affection into the present as past, while imagination recomposes retained material into a new appearance (pp. 71–72). A trace, its present recollection and a new formation are distinguishable even when they concern the same material. Without this continuity a rapid sentence would dissolve into unrelated sounds and a running movement into unrelated positions.

Internal *aham-vimarśa* is self-awareness without a determinate thought-object, and outward reflection apprehends a differentiated *this*. Its outward direction does not carry it outside the awareness in which the object becomes known, and these two orientations let one field sustain both self-presence and object-directed cognition. [Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa](C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) **develop** the discriminating and formative powers through which a particular determination arises within it.

## #3

The source distinguishes a goal-bound act from the activity through which goals become possible. *Karma-vimarśa* names outward unfolding, and *kriyā-vimarśa* names unrestricted internal agency before a determinate goal (p. 72). The *kartṛ*, doer or experient, is not exhausted by the *kārya*, the product, and a product's passing away can itself be experienced, the witnessed cessation continuing to presuppose its experient (p. 80).

*Svātantrya* names the freedom of this conscious activity, and vimarśa names its reflexive self-awareness. Will, knowledge and action differentiate its operation. [Māyā](C14-Maya-Operative-Measure.md) carries those powers into finite efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order, so that the particular act becomes possible through contraction while its source exceeds the accomplished result.

## #4

Four reflective acts produce three levels of difference in the p. 74 account. Consciousness negates its own nature, identifies with another, holds the two together, and negates the merged determination. The first two cooperate in constituting *bheda*, the separated individual and its objects. Holding their relation gives *bhedābheda*, unity-in-difference through the means of knowledge, and return beyond the merged determination gives *abheda*, undivided unity. The fourth act is no fourth level appended to these three: it releases the achieved union from becoming another final object.

The author's `4+2` reading holds the four acts with prakāśa and vimarśa as their two inseparable conditions. The [native eight-determination theorem](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) supplies its own count and return, and the source contributes this distinct reflective operation.

Two axes further differentiate the movement (p. 75). Manifestation and withdrawal are vertical, as diversity unfolds and is gathered into unity, and perceptual approach and return are horizontal, as an encounter unfolds through a differentiated world. The first concerns the world's manifestation and the second an object-directed act within it. [Vāk](../A06-Vak.md) **develops** articulation within this living movement, and [recognition](C19-Pratyabhijna-Recognition.md) returns a contracted act to the source it has continued to express.

## #5→0

Through [Māyā's eye, veil, frame and dream](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-measure-dream), a bounded life knows a world whose condition can withdraw from its horizon. The living mirror generates, hosts and apprehends its appearances, and the inert crystal only displays them, so that recognition concerns the source relation of an experienced world and has nothing to do with multiplying pictures within it.

[Indra's jewel-net](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-native-relation) **figures** the whole relation through mutual reflection. Its jewels disclose one another, and the Śaiva account adds the retention, recomposition and self-apprehension of vimarśa. The [Mother, Assumption and chiasm whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mother-assumption-chiasm/WHOLE.md#mother-sophia-logos-shakti) brings active bearing and recognition into the crossing, since a receiving matrix participates in what appears. Its Christian sequence and the Śaiva sequence keep their own figures and histories, and the comparison follows their operations without assigning them interchangeable offices.

[Computational Vimarśa](C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) gives reflexive mediation a technical body. Retention allows a result to be recalled, recomposition allows another result, and review returns a consequence to what made the determination possible. Within that review the local interpreting agent is the functional knower, retained source and context together with the comparison rule are means, and the result examined is known. Within the containing person's inquiry the reviewing apparatus itself serves as means. The [native knowing relation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) holds these relative offices within Life / Mind.

A corrected answer or application can follow under a fitting rule. If the rule, representation, permission or commission itself failed, the return reaches the office able to change that condition, and if inquiry warrants retaining it, the later act inherits the reasons for that judgment and the source relation through which it was reached. A claim that processing changed has to identify what changed and where the next operation received it, and reasoned retention is not reported as a new engineering intervention.

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust|Symbol / Account / Trust]] carries the corresponding relation: an articulation answers to the appearing it discloses and refrains from usurping its source. The return preserves the differentiated powers while releasing an achieved determination's claim to contain the whole.
