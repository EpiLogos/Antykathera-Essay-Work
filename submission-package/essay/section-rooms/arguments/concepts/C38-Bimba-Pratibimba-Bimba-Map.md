# C38 — Bimba–Pratibimba / Bimba Map

## #0

*Pratibimba* is the Sanskrit word for a reflection, and *bimba* for the original that is reflected. **Bimba** is Original, and **pratibimba** is its situated display or reflection. The ontological relation preserves display without severance and dependence without diminishment. A **Bimba Map** is the authorial technical design by which a locally governed reference field can orient multiple judgments. These offices must stay explicit whenever the same term crosses scale.

## #1

Ontological Bimba is prior in being and later in recognition: the Original is recognised through proportions, inversions and differences among appearances that do not exhaust it. Dyczkowski's source-matched mirror discussion places manifestation within consciousness, with no separately existing exterior original required. The doctrine is a source-specific neighbour of the native QL mirror relation, and it is no ancient description of a database.

## #2

The native parents `(0/1)/(1/0)` and `(1/0)/(0/1)` carry the complete body in inverse orientations, and the first and returned accounts preserve their source relation. The optical relation places QL in the light's ordered spectrum, MEF in the refracting apparatus and pratibimba in the cast image. A technical world-picture is thus a reflection relative to the wider world, and renaming it cannot make it metaphysically Original.

The paradigm/project relation gives reflection a consequential office within the world it discloses. A **Project** is a determination thrown forth through a **Paradigm** into a World, so that a pratibimba can become an effective condition of what happens next, and the `/↔1` relation names this projection and enactment. A revised map can change a route and become the reference for later journeys, and its authority in those journeys stays derived from the encountered world, sources and rules through which it was made. Its causal effectiveness and its dependence are simultaneous features of the same projection.

## #3

Recursive locality nevertheless gives a real reference office. Inside a declared Context Frame, a source record or an agreed world-object can serve as Bimba for judgments compared with it, and that same field remains pratibimba relative to wider sources and reality. Within the [recursive Bimba relation](bimba-pratibimba.md), local authority can be strong and operational while its determining sources remain available beyond that office.

This is also a paradigmatic office. A local Bimba can organise source, gauge, lens and admissible relation strongly enough to condition a subsequent inquiry. The healthier case keeps that organisation visible as mediation and lets returned resistance revise the local original, and the counterfeit case appears when the map becomes the sole authorised route through which its wider source is permitted to appear. A [world-atlas](../A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) relates projections through transitions that remain answerable to the paradigms that made them legible.

## #4

The map exposes provisional world-objects, source authority, lens, gauge, exclusions, transformation and governance as parts of the inquiry. This makes disagreement locatable, since two judgments can diverge because their evidence differs, because they refer to different objects, or because permission and evaluation differ. A federation can keep several locally authoritative maps with declared transition rules and need not require one universal map. In a local comparison the judging agent is the relative functional knower, source, memory, lens and rule are means, and the alternative judgments or addressed field are known. Within the containing person's inquiry that whole apparatus serves as means, and its local determining centre can become known in another inquiry without losing the office it occupied in the first.

At this level **holographic** does not mean that a local map contains the whole. It means that the local reference can retain, at its own scale, the relation among source, mediation and projected determination. A Bimba Map is strongest when this whole-relation remains recoverable while the map performs its finite office, and its recursive source-dependence is part of its authority and no defect to conceal.

## #5→0

Baudrillard reverses Borges's map fable and then contests even its remaining map/territory distinction. A made map nevertheless serves as a genuine local Bimba within a Context Frame while remaining pratibimba toward wider sources, and the images below each show that two-sided office from a different side.

The [window's ordered light and situated view](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-regard-refraction) differentiate Bimba as Original order, QL as the full ordered spectrum immanent to light, MEF as refractive operation and pratibimba as situated projection. Because the image is read through that order, returned comparison can revise the local original without making the produced artifact metaphysically Original or stripping finite reference of its governing office.

In [Indra's jewel-net](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-account-and-consequence), one jewel presents the others, including their return through it, and so gives recursive local reference a contemplative image. In the technical comparison an agreed record can serve as Bimba for a judgment while remaining pratibimba relative to wider sources, and that local authority remains revisable. The reflected ink mark alone establishes no institutional correction.

In the [mirror's initiated return](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mirror-that-moves-first/WHOLE.md#mirror-recursive-bimba), the reflection moves first in time and keeps its place in the order of dependence. An agreed record or world-object orients judgments that are its pratibimbas, the same reference stays pratibimba toward wider sources, and persistent resistance can revise the local original itself. The mirror's initiative preserves both its authority to orient comparison and its answerability beyond that office.

The [travelling jigsaw's made world-picture](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-blue-marble-hinge) shows the office at planetary scale. A bounded view of Earth can gather attention around a shared planet, and whose planet is pictured, computed by whom and on whose gauge stay part of the account. The reference picture serves as local Bimba for situated readings while remaining pratibimba toward wider sources, and it can itself be corrected.

The [stained-glass encounter](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/stained-glass-refraction/WHOLE.md#stained-glass-return) makes the recursion explicit: an achieved reference can be Bimba for its local readings and pratibimba within a wider relation. Recognition can revise the local original itself while preserving enough stability for comparison and error to have a definite address.

[Account does not replace source](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#account-does-not-replace-source) gives this office a consequential distinction. A local Bimba governs reference within a bounded inquiry while remaining source-dependent and revisable within a wider frame, and an encountered correction can reach that local reference itself, the corrected account retaining which source, respect and authority made the correction warranted.

Through [paradigm, project and enactment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md#1--paradigm-through-project-projection--enactment), a projected reference orients future determinations and becomes causally active while retaining its source-dependence. Paradigmatic reflexivity makes the map's source, gauge and exclusions answerable, and a failure reaches the condition able to correct it, or a fitting condition is retained with reasons. A later act inherits the relevant correction or warranted retention.

Returned evidence can revise a situated judgment, a relation between reference fields, or the local Bimba itself. Stability provides orientation, and corrigibility preserves truth-seeking, so a map anchors by attributable source relations and contestable construction and does not store Truth. Through [Objective Co-Internality](C42-Objective-Co-Internality.md), distinct local worlds make their consequences conditions of one another's next acts, and the [Reflective Field](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) lets a map turn its own authority first toward its source.

An achieved [avatar, image or mask](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/avatar-image-mask-idol/WHOLE.md) can serve as local Bimba for further readings within a declared Context Frame, and it remains pratibimba toward its wider sources, so that the comparisons it makes possible can return to revise the local original itself. The four-office ecology keeps that authority effective, and idolisation concerns immunity from sourceward correction, while the fact that a bounded reference governs an inquiry is no fault.
