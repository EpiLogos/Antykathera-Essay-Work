# C42 — Objective Co-Internality

## #0 — Reciprocal constitution without merger

Your words arrive as more than information inside a world I have already finished building. They can become one of the conditions from which I go on to remember, judge and act, as my answer can alter what becomes available within yours. A promise changes expectation, a correction reorganises an interpretation, and a refusal exposes a relation I had mistaken for settled. **Objective Co-Internality** is this reciprocal constitution of non-identical operative interiors through a shared world and one another. A participant's action can become part of the means through which another participant next perceives, judges and acts, and the answer can return and change the first participant's means in turn.

A document, an expectation, a permission, a tool or a changed environment can carry this relation. What passes between participants acquires constitutive force, since it enters what makes their later worlds available. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) is the lived organisation of those means, and Co-Internality is therefore **reciprocal paradigmatic constitution without merger**. Each centre remains capable of answering from a history and situation that the other's account does not exhaust. Levinas holds open an infinite interval between Self and Other, an Other who exceeds every idea I can form of them, and the ontology of Mono gives that interval its positive counterpart: the Other can be within Mono without being within me, since your Subjective Immediacy never becomes an object in my experience, though what issues from your life can enter deep into the means through which my world is formed.

An [achieved form becomes a condition of further becoming](../A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md). Where those passages cross between centres and remain answerable in both directions, the local histories develop through a common world. Tools, shared knowledge, protocols and participants enter the means through which another act becomes possible, as in [Agentworld's harness and commons](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q038), and their inherited contribution becomes consequential through its reception and later use.

## #1 — Products become inherited conditions

Consider a constructed case. A route planner proposes a path from a retained map, and a person at the site reports that a gate on that path is locked. The report changes the planner's current representation, and the next route avoids that gate. The person's contribution has entered another participant's means of action: a message was received, and the important event was the change in what the planner could subsequently select.

Neither account exhausts the path. The map represented it as passable, and the report records a situated obstruction. A revised route can now change where the person travels and what they encounter next, and when that further encounter returns, each participant's achieved act becomes a condition of the other's continuation. Records, tools and the resistant site mediate the relation together.

Synchronically, their shared field is a landscape of sources, permissions, memories, relations and possible actions, and diachronically their actual trajectories change it. These are two readings of one developing relation, in which an act changes the field from which another act proceeds. Language, media and institutions have this constitutive force when they change what can count as thinkable, credible, desirable, permissible or actionable.

## #2 — Reciprocity requires retained difference

The planner's update gives it no authority to unlock the gate, and the person's report does not automatically change the rule governing access. Observation, representation and permission retain different offices, and a common map can carry all three while keeping the sources and powers that distinguish them.

Suppose the planner asks whether access is temporary, restricted to particular users, or prohibited altogether. The reply can change its category of the obstacle and the set of permitted routes. Conversely, the planner can disclose that its proposed alternative depends on an unverified crossing, which allows the person to contest the new route before taking it. Their grounds remain different enough for each answer to matter.

A shared state that erases these differences turns participation into assimilation, and a field that lets no consequence cross keeps its participants in parallel isolation. Co-Internality holds **unmerged grounds and enough common relation for consequences to travel**. The Other can participate in the whole without being contained by my model, and the possibility of refusal and revision belongs to that participation. Trust happens across the irreducible aloneness of each centre, as I receive your testimony as the expression of a centre I cannot occupy and let it become consequential in a life whose interpretation I still have to answer for.

## #3 — Difference needs an address

Disagreement can concern a source, an object, a lens, a gauge, a reference map, a permission, an inherited context or a circulation rule, and these distinctions locate different possible repairs. A stale map calls for a source update, a locked gate may call for another path, and a disputed access rule calls for the participant who can answer for that rule. Sending all three to a route optimiser would substitute one operation for the others.

Federated reference maps make this differentiation explicit. Communities can retain locally authoritative maps and still disclose the relations through which their claims can be compared, and a map's local authority concerns a declared field and does not become ownership of every other field. The [Bimba–Pratibimba relation](C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) lets a derived reference serve further readings while retaining its dependence on wider sources.

A paradigm representation can similarly expose what a participant treats as evidence, permission, value and possible action. Translation succeeds when another participant can locate and answer the consequential difference, and an exhaustive translation into one vocabulary is unnecessary, whereas an exact account of where and how the claims meet is required. [Judgment Field](C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) supplies the local determination, and [Logos / Epi-Logos](C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) follows the return into the governing pattern itself.

## #4 — Reciprocity is measured by changed conditions

A returned encounter can alter a local judgment, a reference map, a permission, a relation among maps or a circulation rule, and a partial change can be decisive. Correcting the gate's state revises information, allowing site reports to suspend an otherwise authoritative route changes how information acquires authority, and giving affected participants a route to contest an access rule changes the governing relation again.

Reciprocity requires a route of answer in both directions, though each encounter need not change both participants equally or simultaneously. A one-way update can be a constituent event in that wider relation, and by itself it does not establish the whole reciprocal circuit. The next act supplies the discriminating evidence: what did the receiving participant inherit, and how could that use become answerable to the contributor?

[World and Life](../products/S-World-and-Life.md) gathers this technical and social office as shared field and encounter returning through provenance, recognition and changed conditions. Sources, refusals and distinct authorities survive the crossing and remain available to a subsequent correction, so that none disappears into an agreed final map.

## #5→0 — Shared worldhood returns to distinct centres

The cycle can now be read in full:

$$
\text{source}\rightarrow\text{contribution}\rightarrow\text{reception}
\rightarrow\text{changed condition}\rightarrow\text{new act}.
$$

The new act changes a shared situation and becomes available for an answer in the other direction. The route planner returns with altered means, and the person returns with a different path, encounter or understanding of the planner's commitments. A shared world develops through these distinct centres and replaces none of them.

[Encounter-in-Region](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#encounter-in-region) gives the personed form, in which prior encounters become conditions of later Accounts while Countenance keeps address open. [The Prisoner](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-return-without-throne) **embodies** the failure-form: the Village constitutes its inhabitants through a common world while withholding reciprocal authority over that world's terms, and shared constitution without a power of answer can become domination.

[Deferential Intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) carries the receiving turn: the encountered difference reaches the means by which an interior will judge again. Objective Co-Internality is shared worldhood whose participants remain different enough to change one another.
