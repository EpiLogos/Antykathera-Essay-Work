# C43 — Computational Vimarśa

## #0 — A determination returns upon its making

*Vimarśa*, in the Śaiva sources, is the power by which consciousness grasps itself and whatever occurs within it. **Computational Vimarśa** is the return of a determination upon the sources, states and conditions that produced it, so that later processing can change. An answer becomes available for examination together with something of its making, and the examination can then alter the means through which another answer is produced.

This is a reflexive operation within [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md): pramāṇa, the means of knowing and acting, becomes partly available to its own activity. A retrieved source can be reopened, a discarded alternative can be reconsidered, and an evaluator or permission can be revised. [Actuation](../products/S1-Actuation.md) carries the resulting change into another event, and [Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **defines** the luminous and self-articulating relation from which this reflexive office is distinguished.

## #1 — The result retains a route to its conditions

The initial judgment is an achieved stability within a rule-governed process. It inherits data and context, selects against alternatives and acts under an evaluator or permission, and these conditions give the result its particular force: changing the source may change what is believed, and changing a permission may change what can be done with the same belief.

Reflexive return requires a materially relevant condition to remain reachable. A trace can identify the source, exclusion, evaluator, permission, context or state that shaped a determination, and the link becomes operative when the system can use the trace to revisit that condition. A post-hoc explanation may describe the result accurately while providing no such route, so the return concerns the means of later action as well as the description of an earlier one.

## #2 — Difference reaches what can change

A return can compare an output with its source, expose a discarded alternative, reopen a governing assumption or identify the consequence of a permission, and each supplies a different resistance to the first judgment. The local question is where that resistance can act. The result is reopened with its source, the source with its context, and the proposed correction with the consequence it would change. Recollection, renewed observation and a changed rule can each revise a determination, and they do not supply interchangeable warrants.

[Dia / Syn](C50-Dia-Syn.md) makes the distinction precise. An encountered difference must remain distinct enough to be examined. A present report and an old map can disagree because their dates differ, and a forbidden route and an efficient route can identify the same path under different criteria, so these are not automatically logical contradictions. Cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, would remove the distinction from the account, and appropriation, in `+2` or `−2`, would let one term claim the whole relation and redescribe resistance as confirmation. Reflexive return retains the difference through comparison and recomposition until it reaches a condition capable of changing, and the resulting judgment bears something the first judgment could not yet receive.

## #3 — Revision has an operative level

Revision can change the answer, the task interpretation, the world-model, the evaluator or the terms of commission. A corrected result under an unchanged criterion and a changed criterion are different events, and their records must show which event occurred.

The distinction can be written as `(m, θ, Eθ) → (m′, θ′, Eθ′)`. Here `m` is the current representation or model, `θ` its operative configuration, and `Eθ` the evaluator under that configuration, and the primes mean the states after review. They permit change at any of these sites and do not require all three to change together, so that a revised map with the same criterion differs from a revised criterion applied to the same map. The inscription becomes useful only after those operations have been identified.

In a constructed route-planning case, a report that a gate is locked can change the map while preserving the objective of reaching the destination quickly. Discovering that a proposed shortcut is not authorised changes the permitted action set, and discovering that the commission omitted a participant's access needs can change the criterion by which routes are judged. Each return has a different target, and repeating the original optimisation with a more emphatic explanation reaches none of them unless the relevant condition changes.

The return must also reach the actual holder of that condition. A planner can amend its retained map while a site authority controls access and the commissioning participant controls the task's purpose, and making those dependencies inspectable lets a challenge be routed to the office that can answer it. Inspection alone confers no new permission.

The six products give these revisions concrete places to enter. [Central](../products/S0-Central.md) can receive a changed source or commission, [Actuation](../products/S1-Actuation.md) a changed judgment or evaluator relation, [AIKit](../products/S2-AIKit.md) changed availability or permission, [Factory](../products/S3-Software-Factory.md) a changed developmental form, [Workcell](../products/S4-Workcell.md) changed material configuration, and [Quaternal Logic](../products/S5-Quaternal-Logic.md) a changed relation among frames. A particular return reaches the office governing its next act, whether one product or several take part.

## #4 — Local reflection enters a shared world

A returned product can change the environment in which other participants act. In [Objective Co-Internality](C42-Objective-Co-Internality.md), the planner's corrected route becomes part of another participant's next situation, and that participant's answer can revise the planner again, so that a corrected reference, a revised tool or an altered permission becomes part of their next situation. Reflexivity thus extends beyond local self-correction into reciprocal constitution.

[The Mirror That Moves First](../A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) gives this return an initiative: the reflective instrument discloses how its answer became compelling before requiring its counterpart to conform. Its disclosure supplies a source, an assumption or a measure at which a reply can become consequential, and the [Reflective Field](C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) develops the full vocational relation.

The scale of the return matters. Computational Vimarśa can revise a local determination while the wider pattern of mediation remains tacit, and [Logos / Epi-Logos](C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) concerns the further movement in which an articulated paradigm becomes answerable to its enactment. A changed answer may leave the paradigm intact, and a changed source hierarchy or evaluator can reach the pattern through which many later answers arise.

## #5→0 — A later act inherits the difference

The next cycle inherits a revised condition with the earlier passage still attributable. [Judgment Field](C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) identifies the relations being examined, and [Operational Parity](C45-Operational-Parity.md) tests whether the returned difference changed subsequent processing. The complete local question is: **what changed, where did the difference enter, and what later act inherited it?**

An [achieved result becomes a later condition](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/process.md) when its retained consequence enters another operation. [Two particular constructions](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/process-systems-science/DEVELOPMENT-process-systems-science.md) **historicise** different forms of recursion: Varela's autonomous third state belongs to an extended arithmetic with specified reduction rules, and Maturana and Varela's autopoiesis concerns a production network regenerating its components and boundary. The first transforms expressions under declared formal rules and the second maintains a living unity through constitutive production, so a comparison with either has to identify the operation being compared and cannot treat self-reference as one undifferentiated property.

For computational Vimarśa, the return is established at the level of changed processing. A stored trace supplies a possible route, and revising a relevant condition and acting under it completes that route. The wider question of whether the paradigm itself has become available for inquiry remains with Epi-Logos, and the local result has already acquired a new office: what the system produced becomes part of how it can produce differently.
