# C16 — Antaḥkaraṇa / Inner Instrument

## #0

A musician plays through an instrument whose sounded response makes its working available. Describing the instrument's parts, its strings and its resonance, locates no miniature musician among them. *Antaḥkaraṇa*, the inner instrument, is the Śaiva and Sāṃkhya name for the means through which a bounded knower determines, appropriates and coordinates a world, and the image carries its point: the differentiation of the instrument makes interior organisation available for investigation. A judgment, an ego-position and a train of alternatives can become objects of attention, and their becoming available leaves the subject outside the list of observed functions.

A [bounded knower](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) acts through a finite horizon of capacity, knowledge, desire, time and order. The instrument articulates the means of this particular act, and the image makes mediation consequential while preserving the one for whom its sound appears.

## #1

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q001|Sāṃkhya Kārikā's functional triad]] **sources** the differentiation of the instrument. In the Colebrooke/Wilson translation, kārikā XXIII identifies intellect through ascertainment, XXIV egoity, and XXVII mind's sensory and active office, while XXIX distinguishes their functions. The question each time is what the office does in an act of knowing and acting.

The translation calls egotism "consciousness" at XXIV, and here the term concerns appropriation under I and mine and does not stand for the whole of awareness. Its own [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/isvarakrishna/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika/isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika#isvarakrishna-colebrooke-wilson-1837-sankhya-karika-q002|witness passage at XIX]] locates a passive spectator distinct from the apparatus. The historical Sāṃkhya position keeps this dualist shape in the comparison, and the essay's nondual genealogy develops the instrument's source relation through the Śaiva field.

## #2

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|The core theorem's tattvic unit]] develops three related offices. *Buddhi* settles a determination, *ahaṃkāra* gathers it under I and mine, and *manas* coordinates presentations and moves among proposals, selections and withholdings. Their distinction makes the movement from an available field to an appropriated judgment intelligible.

Consider a remembered insult. A perception, an earlier memory and a possible alternative reading become available together, and their coordination is not yet the settled judgment that an insult was intended. Ascertainment settles that judgment, and appropriation binds it to "what was done to me". The example differentiates offices and sets no rigid time-order in which one faculty always finishes before another begins: reopening the memory may alter the available context, the judgment of intention, or the identity defended through it, and those are different changes within the same encounter.

The three offices are also the moments in which a determination signifies and becomes significant for a bounded life. *Manas* holds the presentations among which something could count; *buddhi* settles what it counts as, and that settling is the instrument's signification; *ahaṃkāra* binds the settled determination to I and mine. Appropriation is one office within significance and never its whole, because a meaning can make sense, draw a person in, orient an act and carry worth while being shared rather than owned. In the remembered insult, significance reaches past appropriation: the same judgment can matter as a wound to be defended or as something two people now need to understand together. Possession begins where *ahaṃkāra* takes the whole significance into its office. [Signification and significance](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#signification-and-significance) develops the distinction, and [the psychic equation](../../../symbolon/X-x.md) shows its rigid form, an ego taking its own value for the equation that produced it.

[Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa](C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) follow alternative formation and commitment, and [Apoha](C18-Apoha.md) follows the constitutive role of what is excluded. A settled identification bears the alternatives through which it became determinate, even when appropriation later presents its result as self-standing.

## #3

Through [tattvic differentiation](../A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field.md), a subject/object distinction becomes a finite horizon, and the horizon becomes locally available through differentiated powers. A [musician sounds the instrument](../../01-differentiating-mind/movements/08-s0-p1-inner-instrument.md), and its response makes how it is working available to attention, so that the parts described through that disclosure include no little musician hidden among them.

Memory makes the organisation durable and mutable. A retained trace permits recollection, recollection presents the past in the present, and imagination recomposes its material toward another possibility. The instrument gives a world continuity without making repetition its only future, and [Script](C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) **qualifies** this by naming the arrest in which achieved organisation loses access to its formation and takes its repeated command as necessity. The repair reaches the relation between inheritance and the next intention, which destroying memory would leave untouched.

## #4

[Model Internality](C40-Model-Internality-Judgment-Field.md) makes a technical judgment-field available through its source, representation, evaluator and permissions. The functional questions stay exact: what settles the claim, what assigns it to a continuing agent or commission, what coordinates competing presentations, and what is retained for another act. These questions specify relations to investigate and offer no one-to-one software translation of the triad.

Within a technical comparison the local interpreting agent functions as knower, its source, memory, comparison rules and tools as means, and the addressed situation as known. Within the containing person's inquiry that whole apparatus occupies the means office, and the agent's relative determining centre can itself become known through a further act. These nested relations preserve the first-person condition of their apprehension within I-Consciousness, and the Sāṃkhya functions and witness keep their historical doctrinal offices.

A new source can change the presentations being coordinated while leaving the evaluator unchanged, a revised evaluator can change what settles a judgment, and a revised commission can change whose purpose it serves. A mistaken answer can be corrected under the fitting conditions already available. Return follows the fault exposed, and it need not replace the criterion in every encounter: the implicated source, result, model, permission or commission must be answerable, and a fitting condition can be retained with reasons. A new self-description does not complete that route by itself, and the later act inherits the warranted correction or retention, so that a claim of changed processing must identify the particular change actually inherited.

[Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) gives these activities their wider world. [Selfing / Self / Subjectivity / Self-Thing](C35-Selfing-Self-Subjectivity-Self-Thing.md) keeps an acquired self-model in the office of an acquired determination. Ascertainment, appropriation, coordination and self-description can become objects of investigation because they are activities of the means, and their disclosure requires no promotion of any one of them into the source of the knower.

## #5→0

Through [Māyā's eye, veil, frame and dream](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/maya-eye-veil-frame-horizon/WHOLE.md#maya-measure-dream), a bounded life receives a differentiated world. Buddhi settles a determination, ahaṃkāra appropriates it, and manas coordinates its presentations and alternatives. Retained traces permit recollection and recomposition without compelling a repeated command, and recognition makes that inherited organisation available for another intention while preserving the instrument's efficacy within the particular life.

The acquired ego-position arises within an already appearing field. In the [world-parent separation](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-world-parent-separation), primordial self-recognition precedes the paired separation, and Taylor's correction keeps that recognition distinct from the ego's later appropriation of a determinate I. The inner instrument can determine and appropriate a world without retrospectively becoming the origin of the appearing in which its acquisition is known.

An [Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Account](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md) turns these functions toward what their account addresses. Ascertainment settles which relation is being named, coordination keeps relevant alternatives available, and appropriation can bind the conclusion to an office or identity. Another person's answer can correct the attribution, reopen an omitted source or expose a rule that made their difference inadmissible, and the resulting judgment becomes available to a further act together with the reasons for correction or retention, so that the world does not close around its self-description.

[Deferential Intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) receives the difference at its warranted depth. The [bounded knower](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) returns with the instrument's capacities more exactly known and their subject relation preserved, and inquiry can correct a judgment under adequate means, revise the means whose failure it exposed, or reaffirm their fitting use with reasons. The particular life continues through these discriminated capacities and obligations.
