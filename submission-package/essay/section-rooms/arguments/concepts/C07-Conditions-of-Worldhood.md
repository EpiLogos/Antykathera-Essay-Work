# C07 — Conditions of Worldhood

## #0

To paint a room in perspective an artist first decides where it is seen from, and every other decision follows: the far wall shrinks against the near one in exact proportion to its distance, the floor's edges converge on a point on the horizon, a figure's height is fixed by its distance from the chosen eye. The picture discloses a coherent space, and it also places its viewer at the one spot from which all its lines resolve. A finished picture can conceal the position that made its precision possible and present a view from somewhere as simply how things are. What it leaves out is worldhood, the relation in which anything becomes a world for someone, and the essay's claim is that this relation stays when every single world-picture has been set down.

A world becomes inhabitable through the conditions that let something appear, matter and admit a response. Language, memory, bodily capacity, tools, rules and other participants supply more than contents for a finished picture: they condition what can be distinguished and which actions are possible from here. [World-Picture / World-Atlas](C37-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **defines** the contrast on which this depends. A picture selects relations within a world, an atlas coordinates pictures and their transformations, and worldhood includes the conditions under which either can be made, encountered and used.

## #1

The explanatory direction reverses. An agent models its world, and the context-world also constitutes this agent's capacity to model anything. Change the memory available to it, the language in use, the permitted instruments or the interlocutor, and the question itself can take on different terms. The world is active within the standpoint from which it is represented, as the room is active in the perspective that depicts it.

[Objective Internality](../A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **grounds** this reversal, because it is **notational, nomological and exclusion-constituted**. Marks make relations articulable, rules constrain what can follow, and exclusions let this actual determination emerge against alternatives. Together the three constitute a field from which an agent can distinguish, judge and act, and a visible output alone does not disclose the world from which it became possible.

## #2

Internal and external are distinctions relative to an operation. A permission imposed by another institution can be constitutive of what an agent can do, and a remembered encounter is internal to a later decision while carrying a history the deciding agent did not author. The field's interior is bounded by neither the skin of a device nor the location of a memory store.

[Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) in the essay's full sense includes the language, memory, tools, permissions and histories through which this context-world becomes available. A [Context Frame](C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) holds a bounded selection of those conditions together so that a particular inquiry can proceed and its means become inspectable. Neither the frame nor its inventory exhausts the conditions of worldhood, and what a frame leaves outside can remain materially consequential within it, as a rule enacted elsewhere still binds the decision made here.

## #3

Worldhood has a present configuration and a formed history. A map of currently possible transitions answers one question, and the actual passage by which this configuration arose answers another. The sequence can change the field of possibilities, and the changed field conditions the next sequence.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics|Symbolon Dynamics]] **sources** the two temporal readings: synchronic configuration discloses which transitions are possible now, and diachronic trajectory retains the passage through which those possibilities took form. A phase-space models configurations and trajectories and does not replace the world whose activity it models. An image can transform its interpreter, mathematical dynamics follows a specified system, and a computational state records a particular configuration of an executed process, so a comparison among them has to keep these different objects of inquiry in view.

## #4

Another locally grounded world can affect the conditions from which this one next acts, and that is stronger than adding another opinion to a store. A contribution can alter what counts as evidence, which distinction is available, or whether a route of action remains legitimate, and it must stay answerable to the source-world from which it came.

[Objective Co-Internality](../A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **extends** worldhood to this case by relating participants who remain locally grounded while their contributions can change one another's conditions. A shared grammar lets their worlds meet, and their distinct source-relations, permissions and powers of refusal stay effective.

Through [Diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md), the resulting account discloses the position, means and conditions through which it was produced. A changed source, permission or standpoint becomes readable in what appears and consequential for the next judgment, and making an output observable is a different achievement from exposing the worldhood of its production.

## #5→0

An account enters [the conditions of further process](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/process.md) when it changes what can afterwards appear, matter or be acted upon. [Bergson's melody](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson.md#bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson-q001) **compares** precisely, in qualitative terms: an arriving note reorganises the heard whole, so the return concerns a change of significance and no mere added item. His [clock analysis](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson.md#bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson-q002) separates counted positions from the conscious synthesis that retains a succession. [Whitehead's satisfaction and objectification](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/whitehead-1978-process-reality.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q002) distinguish an occasion's own becoming from its functioning in another's formation, and [objective immortality](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/whitehead-1978-process-reality.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q003) names its achieved availability beyond individual absoluteness. [The distinct duration and concrescence histories](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/process-systems-science/DEVELOPMENT-process-systems-science.md) **historicise** these two objects of process so that both stay available together.

The native constituting relation makes a changed inheritance consequential. A corrected source can change what an earlier argument now means, and a retained memory or an altered permission can change which comparison or action is possible next. Recording these changes yields an inspectable chronology, and following how they reorganise reference, salience and access shows their work within worldhood. In a local comparison the agent is the functional knower, its retained sources and comparison rule are means, and the inherited claim and returned text are known. A corrected source becomes a genuine reference for the next act once it enters the means that act actually uses. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) keeps these offices recursive: in a person's containing inquiry the comparing agent and its record are themselves means, and inspecting that agent makes it known in a further act. I-Consciousness holds these relations while Subjective Immediacy names the knower-condition of their appearing, and the return changes the constituted field from which another determination can arise.

[Bohm's enfoldment comparison](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm.md) makes a form's dependence on its field concrete. In the Peat interview a distributed dye and holographic information show concealed organisation to be different from absence, and the postmodern-science essay gives enfolding and unfolding priority in Bohm's proposed interpretation. Each source keeps its scope: a region is no exhaustively readable copy of the universe, and a physical illustration does not establish the full ontological claim. What the native relation adds is that worldhood concerns what can appear, matter and be acted upon, beyond the resulting picture.

An achieved account can change attention, action or infrastructure and so enter the conditions of later accounts. Return is causal as well as interpretive, because what was produced becomes part of what can next produce a difference. Within [[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD|Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account]] a region holds the conditions through which someone or something can appear, be named and admit a response. Naming makes an achieved distinction addressable, counting retains its criterion, and countenance lets the counted participant answer, so the account returns these determinations to another encounter whose participants can correct how they are named, counted or received.

[World-Picture to World-Atlas](../A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **returns-to** this record's relation by keeping coordinating views answerable to the worldhood that makes their differences consequential. Within the [Order of Dependence](../A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md), an inspectable context-world belongs to the mediating field through which an agent acts, and increasing its inspectability clarifies the means of world-disclosure without turning worldhood into a completed inventory.
