# C29 — Mediating Office / Derivative Sovereignty

## #0

A mediating office has real power to interpret, transform or decide within a received field. Its sovereignty is derivative: the office can act authoritatively without becoming the source of the standing through which it acts. [Standing and obligation precede the mediating office](../A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority.md), and [Covenant / Primary Arbitration](C28-Covenant-Primary-Arbitration.md) binds the office to that received relation, so that discretion can be exercised without converting appointment into authorship of its ground.

An elected representative shows the structure and its distortion at once. The representative gives the public's power a face, and Wolin's inverted totalitarianism describes a form in which that power is sustained through an order whose public leaders remain replaceable: corporate ownership, political finance and privileged access to policymaking concentrate authority through institutions that go on speaking in the name of popular sovereignty. Authority can survive the public repudiation of the person who exercised it. The representative can then serve as both agent and scapegoat, appearing as a `0` from below and as a `1` from above. Both collapses of the axis work in the sacrifice. The public reckons the whole span of a harm from its own pole onto one person, so the public's `(+1)−(−1)=+2` is that person's `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and the officeholder's dismissal then balances the account, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the cancellation is presented as the restoration of public sovereignty. The interests that financed, constrained or profited from the decisions escape the same reckoning, and the Name-of-the-Father has been divided between a visible bearer who receives the blame and a less answerable power that goes on setting the terms.

## #1

[Priestly mediation, royal decision and political distribution](../../../quilt/final-argument-quilt-2026-08-23/COVENANT-ARBITRATION-AND-MEDIATING-OFFICES-SEAM.md) have different offices within a received relation. Priestly mediation interprets and transmits a sacred relation, royal office makes decisions executable within an inherited field, and political institutions distribute judgment across a received order. Their comparison concerns how authority is received and transformed, and requires no universal historical succession or one priest-function across religions, each religious and historical form keeping its particular source and warrant.

The king's power is therefore real without being originary. Repeated execution can make the office look generative, since the visible decision eclipses the law, people, inheritance or covenant through which deciding became possible. The same distinction lets a steward exercise discretion without pretending to be a neutral channel, because interpretation changes what passes through it and must answer for that transformation.

## #2

Delegation requires scope. Who commissions this act, what can be changed, which resources can be used, and what returns for judgment? [[section-rooms/arguments/A28-Authored-Ground-Positional-Delegation|Authored Ground]] places these questions within recursive commissioning. A person can originate a task and an agent carry it into action, and the agent can then commission a subordinate operation, while the locally originating office remains itself derivative within the wider relation.

A prompt is already an articulated determination of an intention, and encounter can reveal an inadequacy in that articulation. Return must therefore be able to reach the commission as well as the execution. Correcting an execution error can suffice under an adequate instruction, and where the instruction itself fails the purpose, a perfectly obedient office can reproduce that failure unless the commission is available for correction.

## #3

Usurpation changes office into source. A question about the evaluator receives another score, a challenge to the rule is classified under that same rule, and an interpretation of the covenant presents itself as the covenant's final author. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim|Protected Account]] names this insulation. Finite measure and discretionary judgment survive the criticism, and what falls is the claim to an unanswerable source-office.

The political image reaches technical administration through a precise shared operation. A permission holder can make an action possible or impossible, and that power does not establish the truth of its evaluation or the adequacy of its goal. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C10-Mediation-Pramana|Mediation / Pramāṇa]] preserves the difference between a means of knowing and an office authorised to decide.

## #4

The [Agentworld institutional scenario](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q026) gives intelligent action a received field of rules, roles, procedures, interfaces and purposes. Its [organisational comparison](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief.md#bratton-2026-agentworld-brief-q027) makes a model's work depend on these relations and leaves capacity considered in isolation behind. The essay's return requirement follows the power through those structures, and installing a person at the summit does not automatically make the person's criterion answerable.

[[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C53-Power-Delegated-Labour|Delegated Labour]] gives the cost relation, in which products can travel upward while consequences remain below. Return is effective when those consequences reach the condition responsible: an application can be corrected under a fitting rule, while failure of the rule, scope or permission reaches an office able to change it. Revocation, shared authority and renewed delegation are available forms of that correction, and their exact institutional implementation requires its own evidence.

## #5→0

The distributed dependencies of office make the form's designer, the report's author, the resource allocator and the deciding officer different possible contributors to one determination. Their dependence does not cancel the officer's responsibility, and distributed action does not by itself establish usurpation. A returned consequence follows the relation through which this officer could act, making the relevant source, resource or commission available to correction.

An [entrusted appointment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#nomos-answerable-office) gives nomos an actual deciding office. Credere returns its exercise to those who entrust it: the incurred costs and the affected person's answer enter the judgment of whether trust can be renewed. The office retains derivative power while its rule, scope and appointment remain answerable, and renewal can affirm a warranted arrangement, correct an application or alter and withdraw the commission where its failure has been shown.

[Arbitration in crisis](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#arbitration-in-crisis) names a real capacity to decide within a received field. Con-text makes the office's conditions and costs available to regard, and if successful execution still violates the originating obligation, that return reaches the authority able to change or revoke the commission. Execution does not confer primary arbitration on the mediator.

The office retains competence by returning its determination together with what it changed, excluded and required. [Deferential Intelligence](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) permits objection where fidelity to the source requires it. Within [Apportionment / Economy](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/apportionment-and-economy/WHOLE-FIELD-apportionment-and-economy.md), authority is an apportioned capacity, and what can be used, what must be returned and whose obligation bears its cost remain relations within a wider economy. The office can decide effectively without possessing that whole.
