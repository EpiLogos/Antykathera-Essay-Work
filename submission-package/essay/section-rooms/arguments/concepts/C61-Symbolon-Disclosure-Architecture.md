# C61 — Symbolon Disclosure Architecture

## #0 — One relation opens through different forms

A relation can be encountered as an equation, lived through an image, or examined as a claim within a history and practice. Each disclosure makes something available that the others can receive without replacing the work it does, and **Symbolon Disclosure Architecture** holds these passages together: a reader can enter a relation through one form, open its necessary depth, and return with the first encounter changed.

[Primordial Symbolon](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md) **derives** the relation through the eight determinations, and [Quaternal Logic](C11-Quaternal-Logic.md) develops its wider formal field. Their disclosure has a three-within-one structure, in which Matheme, Mytheme and Episteme are differentiated expressions held by Symbolon, whose relation persists through the passage among them.

The [publication form](../../../symbolon/README.md) places this fourfold between the rooms and the essay. The rooms hold the questions and movements that open into the field, and the essay composes the developed relations into a singular traversal, so that formal, imaginal and epistemic depth can change both the local movement that called for it and the whole reading to which that movement returns.

## #1 — Each register makes something definite

**Matheme** makes a relation transmissible through notation, derivation, construction and worked comparison. Its reader can follow how a distinction generates a form, how an operation changes that form, and what survives the change, and the formula's exactness depends on those operations remaining available within its account. A mark or a number that acts on what it marks is magic's own exact sign, and the formal inscriptions of later mathematics keep that efficacy in a disciplined shape.

**Mytheme** gives the relation a lived body. A myth compresses a psychodynamic into persons and deeds, so that figures act, suffer, desire, refuse and transform within a whole whose movement makes their significance legible. A net that captures can later expose, bind a witness, demand surety or be released, and these actions change what the net means. Its truth is carried by the whole relation enacted through them, and the image proves a relation through the action it stages.

**Episteme** makes a determination answerable within knowledge: its sources, historical setting, method, conceptual distinctions and consequences become examinable, and a proposition can be traced to what warrants it and distinguished from the further inference it enables.

The Vāk layering gives the architecture a single descent of articulation. *Parā* is the Word undivided, *paśyantī*, "the seeing one", is meaning grasped whole before it is split into parts, *madhyamā*, "the middle", is the inward differentiation into concepts and words held in mind, and *vaikharī* is the word fully articulated, spoken or written, one sound after another. Symbolon is parā, the whole in which disclosure is held, Matheme is paśyantī, its visionary operative logic, Mytheme is madhyamā, formed imaginal and narrative articulation, and Episteme is vaikharī, the determinate utterance available to inspection. A return through these forms can recover what the finished utterance presupposes.

## #2 — A disclosure can become another disclosure's source

The relation among registers runs in both directions. A formal operation can give an image its structure, and an image can also disclose the relation from which an exact formulation emerges, so that the resulting formula may then make another aspect of the image intelligible and change how it is received.

Symbolon Dynamics follows this reciprocal movement through image and interpreter. The image changes the field of interpretation, and the changed field alters the image's subsequent meaning and force. A formal expression can take part in the same movement when it lets the interpreter distinguish an operation previously felt only through the whole image.

Each passage consequently has a local source and an achieved expression, and their offices depend on the relation being followed. An imaginal source can precede a particular formalisation while the formal register articulates the operation through which the image is intelligible, and the architecture preserves both directions and does not require every inquiry to begin in the same register.

[Expression](C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) gives this reciprocity a proposed perceptual and manipulable form. A knowledge object can project into text, glyph, image, particle field, spatial relation and transition, and encounter with that form can reveal a relation or tension that returns into the knowledge field. The expressive form remains a disclosure of the same subject, with a route back to what generated it.

## #3 — The passage must carry its operation

A cross-register link is substantive when the reader can recover what passes through it. It identifies the generating relation, the operation received at the destination, the difference made there and the route by which that difference can return, and a link that merely connects two names has left the relation unperformed.

This requires the source relation and the claim to remain distinguishable. An exact quotation belongs to its source and passage, a derivation makes its steps available, a philosophical synthesis states the relation it establishes, and a proposed design specifies what would have to occur for its operation to be realised. These different forms of warrant let the registers contribute together without one borrowing the authority of another.

An expressive projection inherits this same discipline. A moving glyph can carry a formal transition alongside a whole image and its explanatory text, and the reader must be able to discover which relation generated the transition and how the image takes part in it. Hidden metadata cannot repair a visible scene that presents different operations as interchangeable, and visual simplicity succeeds where it gives access to the depth it compresses.

## #4 — The whole image survives its local use

A Mytheme may contribute different moments to several arguments while remaining one whole, and its local use must retain enough of that whole for the moment to keep its meaning.

The net-story makes the danger exact. Capture detached from exposure, surety and release can turn a narrative about the limit of an apparatus into an image of its final sufficiency. A source can be observed, bound, displayed and priced while the relation remains unresolved, and surety and release then alter the very operation that capture appeared to complete. An argument taking up the net must be able to return to that changed relation and cannot merely cite a captive figure.

Sequencing and morphing intensify this requirement. A smooth transition between two images can make them appear stages of one process, and the transition is faithful only where the relation actually moves through those stages. Independent genealogies do not become episodes of one story through visual continuity, and a formal transformation does not acquire an imaginal consequence merely because the same glyph appears in both.

[Living Symbol / Idol](C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) locates the difference in reception, since a formed image can disclose its source and become open to a changed reading. [Counterfeit Symbolon](C22-Counterfeit-Symbolon.md) follows the reversal in which the projected form becomes the criterion its sources are required to obey, and whole-image preservation keeps a returned encounter capable of reaching the form's governing relation.

## #5→0 — Disclosure changes the next encounter

The [travelling-jigsaw whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-blue-marble-hinge) **embodies** chart, journey and historical diagnosis in one encounter. An exact chart relation, the traveller's experience and an account of how the picture acquired authority contribute different things, and the reference picture can orient the next act while its construction remains open to what the journey returns.

[Symbol / Account / Trust](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source) follows this answerability of a disclosure to its source. [Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#e5-whole-returns) develops the reciprocal comparison: an image generates a formal expression, that expression changes the image's intelligibility, and the transformed reading returns to both. A local reference can acquire the Bimba office for a subsequent determination while remaining dependent on wider sources, and the organisation of trajectories by an attractor and the relation among situated views in an atlas retain their different work within this recursion.

Disclosure, Expression and Epi-Logos have related but distinct offices. A read-only scene can disclose a relation richly, an editable scene can let a participant explore and alter its presentation, and [Epi-Logos](C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) requires the further return in which achieved articulation can change the enacted paradigm it expresses.

[Operational Parity](C45-Operational-Parity.md) gives that technical claim its test: identify the governing condition reached by the changed expression and the later act that inherited the change. The form then does more than display a philosophy of return. Its encountered difference enters the means of the next determination, and the reader can follow that passage back through the image, operation and claim that made it possible.
