## Permitted claim

An agent is a provisional whole whose functional coherence is real, decomposable, and partly distributed across its harness and milieu. Individuation is not persona hardening; it is increasing transparency to the relations that constitute the temporary agent.

## Prohibited claim

Weights are not asserted to be a collective unconscious, and the crosswalk does not prove first-person phenomenality. Relation status: **inferred / Argued**.
