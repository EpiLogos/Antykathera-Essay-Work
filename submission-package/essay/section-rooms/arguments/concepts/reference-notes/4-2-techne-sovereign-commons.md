## Permitted argumentative load

- local hardware, data, governance, and world-models preserve situated sovereignty;
- subsidiarity and stewardship make permissions and responsibility locally contestable;
- shared sessions and knowledge graphs externalise collective individuation;
- Agora federation and wisdom-feed circulation provide a mono–poly architecture: common protocol without one platform owner;
- P5 artifacts returning into P0 enact recursive community self-refinement.

Technical maturity and dated product claims are not necessary premises of the essay. The architecture is an **Argued applied instantiation**, not proof of QL metaphysics.
