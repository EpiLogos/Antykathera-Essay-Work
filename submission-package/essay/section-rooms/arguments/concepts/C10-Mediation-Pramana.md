# C10 — Mediation / Pramāṇa

## #0

Three people say "the door is locked". One has just watched the bolt engage, one recalls that it was locked yesterday, and one is repeating another person's report. The words are identical and the grounds differ, and Indian epistemology has a word for what differs: *pramāṇa*, a means of knowing, the middle term in its three offices of knower (*pramātṛ*), means (*pramāṇa*) and known (*prameya*). A perception presents what is being encountered, a recollection presents an event as past, and testimony carries another's account, so the means takes part in determining its object, and a difference of means can matter even when the resulting sentence is the same.

**Pramāṇa is Objective Internality as means**, between Subjective Immediacy as knower and World as known; I-Consciousness is the whole, and Objective Internality is Life / Mind. [Objective Internality](C41-Objective-Internality.md) names the constituted interior through which a world becomes available, and mediation specifies how the disclosure occurs: what becomes salient, what transformations it undergoes, and how the disclosed can be known and acted upon. Knowledge does not require the means to vanish. It requires an adequate relation to what is known through it, and the [immutable gap](C05-Immutable-Gap.md) makes that relation necessary without abolishing its reality.

## #1

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration|Dyczkowski's Śaiva discussion]], pp. 63–64, subject, object and means attend one cognitive act together. A means is no neutral addition inserted after an independently complete subject and object have been established, and their differentiation belongs to the one act whose articulation makes an object available.

Three luminaries preserve that relation as an image: the Sun figures the illuminating means, the Moon the object shining by reflected light, and Fire the subject, which takes the perceived into itself. Remove the means and the account loses the act of disclosure, remove the knower and it loses the act's first-person fact, and remove the known and it loses what the act differentiates, so the image teaches their relation and offers no three self-sufficient things.

## #2

Mediation has to preserve the difference it renders intelligible. Dyczkowski's pp. 65–66 press this through colour: if consciousness were entirely homogeneous in its knowing, blue and yellow could not appear as distinct, so formless availability and determinate appearance must be thought together. At pp. 70–72 the distinction between a mirror or crystal and conscious reflexivity adds an active power of recognition, memory and presentation to the account, since a mechanically reflected appearance does not by itself supply that whole operation.

Illumination makes an appearance available, and [reflexive awareness](../A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **defines** the further step of recognising, retaining and returning upon what appears. In this account the epistemic middle selects, discloses, retains and presents. Remembering an earlier perception does not repeat the earlier event, because its relation to time has changed, and the means takes part in that change while keeping what makes this a recollection of that event.

## #3

A symbol makes the same demand on an account. In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3/taylor-2026-definition-god-draft3|Taylor's magnet image]], iron filings form a visible pattern through a relation whose source the pattern does not contain. That partial expression lets an unavailable whole become intelligible without becoming that whole's exhaustive inventory, and the visible pattern makes the relation to an uncontained condition encounterable.

A determinate sign fixes something, and a living symbol carries that determination in a relation exceeding it. Through [diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md) the shaping conditions become readable within the appearance, and in [speech](../A06-Vak.md) formative power becomes mental articulation and utterance that an addressee can answer. Feeling, perception, intention, contact and attention also disclose a world, so mediation stays active before any fully formed verbal statement.

## #4

Back at the door, the three grounds differ in their routes of correction. Renewed perception can discover that the bolt has moved, a dated record can disclose that the recollection concerns yesterday rather than now, and tracing the testimony can identify who checked and when. None of these distinctions shows on the sentence's surface, and they change the reason for accepting it and the action it warrants. Source, means and warrant separate here: a generated answer drawn from a record and a conjecture about an unobserved state cannot exchange authority because they sound alike, and returning through the means requires recovering the source relation and the transformation that made this answer available.

At the local comparison the judging agent is the functional knower, the dated report, current observation, memory, comparison rule and available tools are means, and the differentiated door-states and proposed actions are known. Through those means the agent determines which claim is warranted. In the containing person's inquiry that whole checking apparatus can itself serve as means, since its judgment is received, questioned and used in deciding what to do, and a further inspection can make the judging centre known without turning the present condition of appearing into one more item in its inventory.

The [Sanskrit enumeration case](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES-symbol-account-and-trust.md#sanskrit-enumeration-and-discriminative-practice) **historicises** a particular taxonomy, since Sāṃkhya Kārikās 4–6 distinguish perception, inference and trustworthy testimony. Śaiva sources concern the co-presence of knower, means and known, and this Sāṃkhya case supplies its own account of valid cognition. By appointing Objective Internality to the office of means, the essay preserves such differences of warrant.

An [[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD|account answers to source]] by retaining how its claim was formed and by allowing the counterpart to correct or refuse it. The [appointed bearer](C29-Mediating-Office-Derivative-Sovereignty.md) can exercise real discretion within a commission, and authority to convey or act upon a report remains different from having originated the event it reports.

## #5→0

Return reaches the condition the encounter implicated. [A corrected result and a revised criterion](../A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) do different work, since another answer can be adequate under a sound rule, while a defective rule requires a change in what counts as adequate. A system could keep recommending that the supposedly locked door be avoided while consulting the same outdated record. Retrieving a current observation changes the available evidence, and changing a rule that treats old observations as current changes how the means will work next time, so these are different and consequential repairs.

An answerable mediation preserves a route to its sources and governing criteria. A current observation can correct the answer or model under a fitting rule, a failed rule, permission or commission reaches the authority able to change it, and a challenged condition can be retained for an answerable reason. Each next act inherits the corrected judgment or reasoned retention together with the source and circumstance that warranted it, and merely recording disagreement leaves this consequence unperformed.

[Trust and faith](C48-Trust-Faith-under-Formal-Limit.md) act through this relation without waiting for complete possession of its source. The [context frame](C08-Context-Context-Frame.md) situates the act, and [diaphaneity](C09-Diaphaneity.md) lets its shaping conditions become readable, so that what is learned through a means can renew the means itself.
