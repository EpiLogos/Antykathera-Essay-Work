# C32 — Archetype

## #0

An image appears with a force its visible content cannot contain. It gathers associations, redistributes feeling and draws a life along paths no single interpretation commands. In Jung's concept an **archetypal image** presents a relationship in a form through which it can be felt, the protecting or judging father, the abandoned child, the hero whose victory would establish his worth, and its force can become numinous, seizing consciousness beyond any deliberate act of choice. Jung distinguished such images from the archetype as such: the images are very varied structures that all point back to one essentially irrepresentable basic form, a psychoid factor at the invisible end of the psychic spectrum, which does not itself reach consciousness. He compared that form to the axial system of a crystal, which preforms the crystalline structure in the mother liquid without having any material existence of its own, and in itself the archetype is empty and purely formal, a possibility of representation given in advance.

**Archetype** therefore names the generative organisation made legible through such variable manifestations, and the archetype as such does not appear as a final content-object beside them. The [arche-topos](../A16-Arche-Topos-as-Differential-Field.md) is the wider field of possible differentiation, and archetypal organisation gives this field a specifically psychic and symbolic office.

## #1

A dream, a god, a number, a symptom, a fantasy, a narrative, a concept or a bodily intensity can become an [archetypal manifestation](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics.md). The particular image gives an unknown ordering relation a local face, and its excess over a settled interpretation keeps that face open to further manifestation. [Image and valuation](C33-Image-Valuation.md) concern the determinate image and its investment, and this particularity stays distinct from the generative organisation.

The broken symbolon's seam makes the relation tangible. One half carries the shape of a counterpart it cannot manufacture alone, and recognition depends on their relation. An image can similarly make its absent or unexhausted condition operative, a [living symbol](C21-Living-Symbol-Idol.md) keeps this relation active, and an idol installs its image as the exhaustive identity of its source.

## #2

In native QL, `0/1` retains unrepresentable ground and representable mark through their relating stroke, and `X/x` differentiates determining capacity from its indefinite particular. Archetypal recurrence is Taylor's psychic instantiation of this already derived relation: across distinct images the determining capacity becomes legible without becoming another image. The notation is authorial QL and Jung's name is not attached to it.

Through [the eight determinations](../A18-Primordial-Symbolon-and-Its-Eight-Determinations.md), pattern retains its life: appearance questions, attracts and repels, gathers into recurrence, stakes a first-person relation and effects a particular transformation before returning toward its source. This is the author's cross-register movement. It makes the sequence no historical Jungian derivation and reduces symbolic experience to no six labels.

## #3

An attractor and a trajectory articulate different aspects of the dynamic comparison. A trajectory is an actual history through a state-space, and the attractor organises recurrent behaviour that no single orbit exhausts. In the authorial psychic reading, what persists can be the organisation of transformation itself, so historical novelty does not have to be the abandonment of every recurrent form.

[Complexes](C31-Complex.md) stabilise local affective organisations, and symbols can intensify them, expose their boundaries, connect separated regions or enable a new regime. In [Taylor's Van Eenwyk encounter](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors.md), an image's response can reorganise associations and change the trajectory through which another image is encountered. A recurrent image is not automatically a strange attractor, and the technical definitions of attractor, basin and bifurcation keep their mathematical requirements.

## #4

Amplification and transformation do different work. Amplification moves among manifestations so that one partial view becomes readable through another, and transformation changes the lived trajectory and its possibilities. A [world-atlas](../A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) relates partial disclosures through their transition conditions, while the basin/trajectory comparison follows the changed history through an organising field. An atlas is not an attractor, and a shared motif alone establishes neither a transition map nor a common causal mechanism.

[Homological agreement and analogical proportion](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md) preserve the whole relation in each telling, with its actors, transformation, aftermath and limit, and the myth's historical transmission and the operation it performs remain different questions. [Psychoid number](C30-Psychoid-Number.md) concerns efficacy across psychic and physical descriptions: a number's occurrence in both gives that inquiry a particular object, and efficacy requires evidence of what its use changes.

## #5→0

In the [mother–assumption chiasm](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mother-assumption-chiasm/WHOLE.md#mother-image-recognition-return), the encountered body, cope and corrected reading give determining capacity a situated maternal manifestation. Neumann's morphology provides shared archetypal structuration, and the local encounter keeps its own history and cannot become a universal template.

In [Neumann's morphology](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-round), the round, the nurturing body, the world-parent relation and the transformed centre give shared genesis changing parental, bodily and heroic offices, and their situated tellings and temporal transformations retain their own histories. The [uroboric source variants](../../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/uroboros-trickster/WHOLE.md#uroboros-source-variants) let digestive enclosure, the world-parent bond and differentiating passage contribute through those distinct operations, so that they do not become interchangeable pictures.

An archetypal interpretation returns to the life it has interpreted. [Image and valuation](../A20-Image-Valuation-Possession.md) make its acquired force answerable to what it opens or excludes, and [individuation](../A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) transforms the relation among agencies. The outcome is no completed catalogue of the archetype. A life recognises its own achieved differentiation within a wider ordering relation and becomes capable of another encounter.

[Cultural sensitivity to origins](../A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md) keeps the interpreted life and its sustaining conditions able to answer again, so that one culture's image remains a particular contribution and never the universal form to which other lives must conform. Through pattern's variable manifestations, [symbolic disclosure](C61-Symbolon-Disclosure-Architecture.md) differentiates formal relation, psychic transformation, whole telling and warranted historical account.
