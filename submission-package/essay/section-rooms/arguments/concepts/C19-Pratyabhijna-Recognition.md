# C19 — Pratyabhijñā / Recognition

## #0

Most of us have met, after many years, a friend who steps off a train and is for a long moment a stranger: the walk is different, the voice has dropped, she laughs at things she would once have scorned. Is she still the person we knew? Over the evening that follows, "she is" turns out to be a description we had kept, "she is not" turns out to be our measure and no verdict on her, and the person we are recognising stands in a register neither answer reaches. Recognition in this sense is the return in which a present determination is known through the source, identity or history from which it had become estranged. The difference acquired in the passage remains, so that returning neither cancels what happened nor finds an untouched beginning hidden behind it.

In [individuation and recognition](../A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) the local life returns capable of a changed relation to its own determining history. Recollection retrieves an earlier content and classification identifies it under a term, while recognition can expose the means through which the content and its bearer had been understood. *Pratyabhijñā*, the Śaiva word the essay translates as recognition, retains its account of integral self-awareness within this broader movement, and what it changes is the mode of identification, where reflection had only changed the image. The images I hold of myself stay active, and their claims to be "the I that knows the image" are relinquished.

## #1

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration|Dyczkowski's pp. 71–75 account]], retained traces can be recalled and recomposed within a contracted field, and recognition concerns the integral self-awareness whose activity manifests through contracted and expanded levels. The recollected object and the recognition of the knowing-act's source relation are different achievements.

Ascent and descent relate unity and differentiated manifestation, and the horizontal movement approaches and withdraws from an object. Both keep the knowing-act within manifestation. [Prakāśa and Vimarśa](C13-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **ground** the account by holding luminosity and reflexive activity together, and the [bounded knower](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) retains particularity through recognition.

A Life can remember the rules, concepts and history through which its world has been organised without yet recognising **those means as means**. Its [lived mediation](C41-Objective-Internality.md) makes a world familiar through attention, comparison, memory and valuation, and recognition makes that determining pattern available within the relation: what appeared self-evident can be examined through its means, while the articulated pattern stays within the Life and World it mediates.

## #2

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy|native return-switch]] turns achieved `1/0` toward the ground it presupposes, and the determining condition cannot be exhausted as another object. `X/x` retains a locally real instance whose recognition returns a changed relation to its determining capacity, and substitutes no final determining image for that life.

[Identity through difference](C06-Copula-Identification-with-Difference.md) lets the passage from `x` through `X` return to this particular `x`. The life keeps its history and its capacity to act while recognising more of the relation through which it became intelligible, and enlarged understanding does not require it to cease being this life.

The same law prevents "paradigm recognition" from meaning possession of a master schema. A diagram of the paradigm is another `1`, and its value lies in making relations within the means available for comparison and return. The crossed-zero movement matters because `Ø/X` recognises mediation as mediation and `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` makes both sides of the relation available together, so recognition is a changed relation to the means of world-disclosure and installs no final meta-model above them.

## #3

In Frank's protected encounter with [[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors|Van Eenwyk]], pp. 69–73, the symbol passes from known to unknown and returns with the interpreter's organisation changed. The QL cross-reading belongs to Taylor's encounter, which reads symbolic transformation through `x→X→x`.

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics|Symbolon Dynamics]], image and interpreter transform reciprocally: an encountered symbol can change how later images are received, and the changed interpreter alters their range of significance. Recognition changes future intelligibility without fixing every later image in advance.

Changing one statement about a paradigm can leave its enactment untouched. Its return is real when the changed understanding alters salience, interpretation, permission, response, relation or the attractor field through which a later encounter is organised, and the explicit articulation is then a **carrier of recognition** because it takes part in this changed future. Otherwise it remains a description of a paradigm and falls short of a recognised paradigm.

## #4

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD|Resolution in reconciliation]] returns a determination through its history as **Anamnesis / Recognition / Return**. The judgment can be retained, narrowed or resituated while its earlier consequences remain effective, and remembering how it arose gives that authority a relation it can answer.

[Individuation](C34-Individuation.md) carries the psychic movement into a life capable of bearing its differentiated relations. Greek anamnesis, Śaiva pratyabhijñā and Jungian individuation have distinct histories and evidence, and their comparison follows changed relation and return, since a shared word or image alone cannot establish doctrinal identity.

The six-product field gives recognition differentiated destinations in place of one abstract "feedback" channel. [Central](../products/S0-Central.md) can recognise a source, commitment or history differently, [Actuation](../products/S1-Actuation.md) the conditions of an act, [AIKit](../products/S2-AIKit.md) the actual horizon of powers instead of the imagined inventory, [Factory](../products/S3-Software-Factory.md) a transformed intention or form, [Workcell](../products/S4-Workcell.md) the material situation and resistance previously treated as background, and [QL](../products/S5-Quaternal-Logic.md) a containing relation within which a local distinction acquires another scale. Recognition need not change every office. It must reach the office whose inherited determination is being returned.

## #5→0

The return must reach an act. [Deference](C47-Deferential-Intelligence.md) lets an encountered difference reach the model, evaluator or course that gives a result practical force, and correction can revise the exposed condition or preserve a fitting one with reasons. A system that accepts only confirmations of its correctness closes this route.

A [computational return](C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) identifies what changed, where the difference landed and what later act inherited it. The local evaluating agent knows an encountered discrepancy through its sources, comparison rule and tools, and the alternatives form its known field. Its apparatus also serves as means within the containing person's inquiry, so that this local recognition preserves the whole relation of I-Consciousness and the unobjectifiable condition to which the inquiry appears. At [paradigm scale](C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md), an articulated pattern becomes answerable to the lived pattern it mediates: recognised means can change salience, permission and further interpretation while the explicit model remains a particular articulation.

The [bounded life](C15-Pasu-Bounded-Subject-Position.md) returns with particularity and achieved history intact. Its [individuation](../A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) becomes consequential where the source relation can enter the next encounter, and releasing the result into another's use continues that recognition: the changed known meets what it cannot yet contain through a changed capacity to receive it.

The [Śaiva historical comparison](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/indian-philosophy/DEVELOPMENT-indian-philosophy.md#4--recognition-returns-through-the-life-that-has-become-finite) **historicises** Dyczkowski's distinction between recollected content and recognitive return through this finite life, and its comparison with resolution in reconciliation keeps the difference between Sanskrit recognition and Greek anamnesis.
