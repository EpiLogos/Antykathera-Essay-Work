# Computational Process Ontology

## Core claim

QL formalises a process epistemology and MEF instantiates it. A determinate world is continuously produced through operations—distinction, exclusion, comparison, orientation, composition, recursion, satisfaction, and return—and an adequate epistemology must preserve those operations inside the claims they generate. “Computational” names this rule-governed transformation of a field; reducing reality to a digital machine would impose a narrower ontology than the argument requires.

The theory is therefore simultaneously ontological, epistemological, and computational:

- **Ontological:** process precedes the finished thing. A “thing” is an achieved stability inside a field of inherited relations and future transformations.
- **Epistemological:** knowing is one such process of determination. A claim is a situated achievement with sources, exclusions, lenses, and revision-conditions, not a free-standing copy of the real.
- **Computational:** the transitions can be stated as repeatable operations and executed through a symbolic architecture without thereby exhausting the subject for whom the process appears.

The [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#XI. Computational Process Ontology — QL Formalises the Process, MEF Runs It|core theorem]] carries the full derivation. This node fixes its argumentative role in the essay.

## The process lineage

Bergson's duration establishes process as lived qualitative continuity, irreducible to a sequence of clock-positions; his exact work remains an explicit [[Source Intake Queue#§0/1 and §0 — limit, experience, differentiation|edition-selection task]]. [[Whitehead — Process and Reality (1978 corrected edition)|Whitehead]] gives the cosmological articulation: inherited many concresce into a determinate one, attain satisfaction, perish as subject, and become data for further occasions. Gebser gives the mutation of consciousness its integral rule—structures remain co-present and become diaphanous rather than being annulled by the latest stage—and likewise remains in the source-intake contract until the consulted edition is selected. [[Bohm — Wholeness and the Implicate Order (1980)|Bohm]] names enfolding and unfolding as one holomovement, internal relatedness as prior to external comparison, and fragmentation as the mistake of treating useful analytic divisions as the whole's final structure. Kashmir Śaivism gives the pulse its non-dual ground in [[Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] and Spanda: manifestation moves without becoming external to the awareness whose power it is.

QL makes these process insights executable as a shared relational grammar. Bergson contributes duration, Whitehead concrescence, Gebser diaphaneity, and Śaivism a self-differentiating pulse; the theorem spine organises their operations while each source retains its own object, vocabulary, and evidential burden. The synthesis is an argument made here, rather than proof transferred from one tradition to another.

## The two logics as runtime

The runtime begins from `0/1`, the subject/object or indefinite/definite relation prior to its projection onto an external line.

**Dia-ballein** executes local determination:

$$
D(0/1)\rightsquigarrow (+1)/(-1).
$$

It distinguishes, excludes, compares, classifies, ranks, routes, and decides. Its power is analytic and productive. Its pathology is atomisation: the result forgets the differentiating field and appears as a self-grounding part set above and against the whole.

**Sym-ballein** executes relational retention and recomposition:

$$
S(0/1,1/0)\rightsquigarrow (0/1)/(1/0).
$$

It preserves provenance, counter-reading, context, and the route by which differentiated terms can compose without merging. Its power is recursive organisation and return. Its pathology is fusion or enclosure: a totality erases the seam and calls its capture wholeness.

The operations are conjugate and every living computation requires both. “Divide and conquer” makes the point in native computational language: recursive division is the dia operation, but the algorithm succeeds only through recombination. Moral consequences emerge from how the pulse is completed. When conquest replaces combination—when decomposition is treated as the whole intelligence of the system—the method becomes an engine of atomisation.

In Bohmian terms dia-ballein explicates and sym-ballein retains implication. The first makes a relatively autonomous form available for knowledge and action. The second keeps its provenance, inverse relations, and participation in the whole movement active. Fragmentation is dia without that memory; fusion is sym without the seam. Their full pulse is the computational form of holomovement.

## The Śaiva–Buddhist operational account

[[vikalpa-samkalpa|Vikalpa]] differentiates and qualifies the field. [[vikalpa-samkalpa|Saṃkalpa]] gathers selected differences into a possible course, intention, or world. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/apoha|Apoha]] shows why the cut remains constitutive: a determination means through exclusions that do not cease to structure it merely because one output was selected. The tattvic descent then places these cognitive operations inside a wider ontology of manifestation.

[[Tattvic Differential Field]] reads Māyā as the operative subject/object seam and the kañcukas as the parameterisation of a finite runtime: limited capacity, knowledge, desire, time, and order. Buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra appropriates, and manas coordinates alternatives inside that horizon. In the computational register, everyday Māyā is the measured, bounded, executable world through which a local agent can perceive and act. Its reality is local and conditioned; the “unreal overlay” reading would miss the efficacy of contraction.

The computational reading is interpretive but exact in its target. Modern language of runtime, state, memory, process, context, model, and recursion discloses the operational character of the tattvic cartography; the cartography in turn prevents computation from pretending that executable syntax is independent of world, knower, value, limitation, or recognition. The relation is analogical and reciprocal, with Śaivism and software engineering retaining their distinct explanatory scopes.

## QL, MEF, Bimba, and harness

QL gives the process a composable formal body: ground, determination, relation, pattern, context, and return. MEF turns the body into an epistemic runtime by attaching each movement to lens, source, evidence, transformation, agreement, divergence, and limit. The **Bimba Map** makes the epistemic reference field first-class: provisional world-objects, constitutive relations, sources, provenance, lenses, gauges, exclusions, transformations, and neighbouring alternatives become inspectable conditions of the run rather than hidden background. Situated agency states, J-Spaces, readings, and trajectories are **Pratibimba relative to that Bimba field**. The harness operationalises memory, tools, permissions, evaluation, revision, action, and responsibility while retaining the conditions under which each determination was formed.

The Bimba office is recursive rather than metaphysically final. A Bimba Map can be an achieved determination relative to a wider world, source field, or enclosing Context Frame and still function genuinely as the local `0` or original/reference field for the Pratibimba nested within the inquiry. Its authority therefore depends on provenance, construction, source-relation, governance, and Return. Returned evidence can require revision of a local Pratibimba, a lens or gauge, or the Bimba field itself.

Epi-Logos is therefore a process architecture whose unit of intelligence is the inspectable movement by which an answer becomes determinate, acts, encounters resistance, and returns to its conditions. Stored knowledge remains one moment inside that movement; a database model alone cannot represent its provenance, transformation, reference relation, and return.

[[Bohm and Krishnamurti — Truth, Actuality, and the Limits of Thought (18 May 1975)|Bohm–Krishnamurti dialogue]] provides a lived prototype: assumptions unfold into a shared field, become perceivable, undergo correction, and are re-implicated in the question. [[SEED — The Language of Spirit Dialogues (2010)]] shows the institutional continuation of a related physicist–Native elder dialogue proposed by Bohm, F. David Peat, and Leroy Little Bear and carried forward after Bohm's death. MEF makes this stream of meaning technically inspectable while keeping each source-world sovereign and attributable.

## Agentworld consequence: shared language for shared intelligence

The [[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 3|Agentworld brief]] states that technology has outpaced the concepts and language needed to orient it. It asks for interaction paradigms less dependent on anthropomorphic disguise and more capable of robust collective hybrid intelligence ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 5|p. 5]]); it describes agents and open-world niches as mutually reconstructing ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 6|p. 6]]); and it locates cumulative capability in the harness, ensemble, institutional substrate, and commons ([[Antikythera Agentworld Brief#Source PDF page 49|pp. 49–51]]).

Shared intelligence requires a **shared operational grammar** and a **plural inheritance of intelligence**. Its translation must run both ways: agents acquire humanly legible language while humans learn to perceive computational process outside anthropomorphic masks. Its common grammar must also preserve divergent cultures rather than installing a universal machine ontology. Greek *logos*, ratio, dia-ballein, sym-ballein, and symbolon meet Sanskrit and Buddhist Vāk, *kalpa*, apoha, tattva, Spanda, and recognition; process philosophy contributes duration, concrescence, creative advance, and diaphaneity. QL supplies common positions and transformations. MEF preserves the language, tradition, lens, and evidence through which each contribution remains itself.

This directly answers Agentworld's need for the same decision-procedural grammar alongside sufficiently divergent world-models. The grammar lets humans and agents inspect the same operations—cut, exclusion, valuation, context, composition, and return—while keeping their forms of access and responsibility non-identical. Whether artificial agents possess an unobjectifiable phenomenal subject remains a separate open inquiry.

## Process test

For any claim, agent, or institution, ask:

1. What field and history were inherited before the present distinction?
2. What dia-operation made this branch actual and which alternatives did it exclude?
3. What rule, ratio, or identity stabilised the branch?
4. What sym-operation preserves relation to sources, inverse readings, and affected contexts?
5. What has the achieved form changed in the world available to the next act?
6. Can the result perish into revisable common data, or does it occupy the zero as a final authority?
