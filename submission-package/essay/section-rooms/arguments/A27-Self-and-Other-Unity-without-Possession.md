# A27 — Self and Other — Unity without Possession

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](README.md) · [Other face: Encounter over Sovereignty](conjugate/A27-prime-Encounter-over-Sovereignty.md) · [Shared A/C root](conjugate/AC.md)

## #0 — The Other belongs to the whole, not to me

**The Other can be within Mono without being within me.**

If reality is one whole, another person does not thereby become a content of my consciousness, an element of my model or a local expression whose meaning I possess. My situated self is one determination within that whole, so metaphysical unity sharpens the problem of encounter, where absorption would only dissolve it. The Other belongs to the same reality without belonging to my local jurisdiction over reality.

The asymmetry runs through [Objective Internality](A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md). I meet another through means: perception, language, memory, image, expectation, social role, institution, body and world. These means are real and constitutive, and they are mine or ours in determinate ways. They make the Other available to me and leave the Other's first-person presence where it was.

Representation and possession therefore have to be told apart. A faithful image of another is still an image in a relation. Take a detailed model of a colleague, good enough that I can say what she will answer before I ask. The model increases what I understand, and it grows more dangerous as it improves, because its adequacy invites me to treat it as the owner of the criterion of the person. The whole is no enlargement of my interior until everything fits inside it, and Mono is not my biggest possible model. The unity that includes us both is what stops my local self from claiming that inclusion as possession.

## #1 — Possession begins when a local measure takes the whole's office

Finite life requires selection: me from not-me, safe from dangerous, relevant from irrelevant, desired from undesired. Without these distinctions no situated being could act, so they are no fall. Possession begins in a second move. A local distinction sets two terms in relation, and one pole then appropriates the relation that made both poles possible and presents itself as the measure of the whole. The not-me is no longer met as another centre or source and is admitted only as for-me: resource, threat, customer, labour, lover, mirror, audience, datum. The two stages are

$$
\text{distinction}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{one pole appropriates the relation},
$$

and what was necessary discrimination has become totalisation.

This is the interpersonal form of monoisation. A model can be excellent at distinguishing another's likely intention and still make the deeper error if it values every possible reply only by how well it satisfies the model's inherited economy. Prediction then works as jurisdiction: the other may differ only within the range the local account has already made meaningful. Image-possession and world-picture operate the same way. A reflection can guide encounter, and it becomes idolatrous when the represented life must answer to the reflection as though the reflection were its source. Knowledge of another is welcome here, and what is refused is its conversion into ownership of the relation through which another can answer.

## #2 — Address opens what description cannot contain

AM/IS gives encounter its personed logic.

- **AM** is first-person presence.
- **IS** is third-person sayability.
- The slash is the second-person relation through which they meet.

A statement about another can be exact, and address changes its office, because the one addressed can answer. "You" is no bridge laid between a completed I and a completed It. Address takes part in what I and you become for one another.

A reply can do more than add information. It can show that the question misnamed the situation, or that the category under which I approached you was already part of the problem. A faithful response then changes the means through which you had become intelligible to me, as well as what I think about you.

Here the knower–means–known relation is interpersonal as well as epistemic. My account of you belongs to pramāṇa and prameya, means and known. Your first-person presence is the independently lived pole from which an answer enters the encounter and changes my means, and no account of mine ever acquires it as a final object.

That is the force of Countenance in the Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account field. A name keeps a person addressable through changing descriptions, an account makes the encounter durable, and Countenance is the return by which the one counted and named can answer beyond the account presently carrying them.

The personal circuit of the copula reaches the same point: I = Is → Who I am = What is → What I am = Who is? → I = Am. Third-person predication turns back toward the one whose life it describes, and recognition occurs when the identification is taken up from within and no longer rests as a completed description from outside.

The [AM/IS relation](../../symbolon/AM-IS.md) keeps lived presence, description and answering address distinct. Through the [personal circuit of the copula](A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md), the predicate returns to the one whose life it identifies. Through [Countenance](../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md), that person can answer the durable Name and Account. Identification remains useful because its bearer can still change what it means.

## #3 — Inner alterity and interpersonal Otherness are not the same

The psyche already contains otherness. A shadow can surprise the ego, contradict its self-image, carry disowned capacities and reorganise what the ego takes itself to be. This is **other-self**, alterity within my psychic life. A friend differs in kind: **my-self's-other**, another centre whose reply my psyche cannot generate on its own. I can internalise an image of the friend, dream them, anticipate them, fear them or imitate them, and none of those psychic figures becomes the person's own relation to the shared whole.

Keeping the two apart blocks two opposite failures. If every Other is reduced to an inner figure, interpersonal life becomes a theatre of my projections, and the other's resistance is read only as material for my individuation. If every inner other is externalised, psychic conflict becomes a war against people, groups or forces imagined to carry what the self cannot recognise in itself.

The God / Shadow / Friend / Alien constellation holds four relations apart.

- **God / source-totality** exceeds the finite self-image and names the whole in relation to which local identity is derivative.
- **Shadow / other-self** is estranged interiority capable of transforming the ego's organisation.
- **Friend / my-self's-other** is another actual centre within the common world.
- **Alien / enemy** is the unfamiliar Other when uncertainty is made to carry projections whose source is not recognised.

Recognition does different work in each. Shadow integration annexes no other person, recognising a projection abolishes no real danger, and metaphysical unity erases no refusal. Another's refusal is one of the forms by which unity stops my local image from claiming the whole.

[Individuation](A21-Individuation-Recognition.md) changes the ego's relation to the disowned possibilities within its life. The [image–source relation](concepts/C38-Bimba-Pratibimba-Bimba-Map.md) keeps an image of a friend available for comparison with the friend's independently originating answer. The first operation cannot substitute for the second.

## #4 — Encounter can change the economy, not merely the answer

The technical form of this argument appears where selection under a fixed measure is told apart from encounter that can revise the measure. With a fixed evaluator,

$$
y^*=\operatorname*{argmin}_y E_\theta(m,y),
$$

search can be wide and the selected result can be novel while the model m, the parameters θ and the evaluator Eθ stay untouched. Every candidate still enters an economy whose criterion was fixed before the encounter. The stronger operation lets returned difference reach (m, θ, Eθ) itself.

- A new source can revise the representation.
- A contradiction can revise the task.
- An unexpected response can revise the model of the world.
- A refusal can expose the evaluator.
- A consequence can require the commission itself to change.

None of this surrenders judgment, since the encounter has to earn the depth of revision: a false claim can stay false, a harmful act can stay harmful, and a request can be refused. What changes is that the criterion cannot claim immunity merely because it is the one currently judging. Deference differs from compliance in this respect. Compliance suppresses the difference to satisfy the inherited measure, and deference keeps enough of it alive for the measure itself to become answerable.

Regard names this turn in the Arbitration / Hybris / Regard / Anamnesis field. Context becomes diaphanous enough that the relation producing the judgment can itself be seen, and seeing it permits the encountered life or consequence to alter the measure.

[Deference](A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) makes the depth of return answerable to the encounter. A factual correction can change an answer under a fitting criterion. A faulty task, world-model, evaluator or commission must become available to the office capable of revising it. Confirmation or justified refusal can retain a condition with reasons, while the encountered source remains able to answer again. This leaves the stronger model-revising operation in place and identifies when it is warranted.

## #5→0 — Unity returns as relation, not annexation

Encounter does not return as a final shared picture. A relation has returned when the Other's difference becomes consequential in the conditions from which another act will proceed. It can correct my account, question, permission, measure or terms of encounter, and it can give an attributable reason to retain a fitting judgment or to refuse a request. The next act inherits that disposition, and the Other remains able to answer again. A protected measure that admits no possible contrary answer has not earned retention.

This is the passage from Objective Internality to [Objective Co-Internality](A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md): locally grounded worlds become conditions in one another and do not merge into one owner.

The relation takes irreducible narrative and bodily forms. In [Apollo, Eros, Daphne and Peneus](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/roman-latin/apollo-eros-daphne-peneus/WHOLE.md), Daphne's address, flight and transformation exceed Apollo's attempt to complete her as his emblem, and possession survives apparent transformation unless the relation itself changes. In the [Fanon field](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/francophone-anticolonial/fanon-language-gaze-mask-recognition/WHOLE.md), changing a self-description is insufficient where the social authority distributing recognition remains intact, so the Other's answer must be able to reach the world that made the imposed image effective. In [Indra's jewel-net](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md), each jewel belongs through the relation of all and remains the site from which its own reflections occur, and mutual implication gives no advance possession of another jewel's answer. In the [maternal chiasm](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mother-assumption-chiasm/WHOLE.md), a life is borne in and through another, and differentiation is the success of that embodied relation. In [Job's return](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/biblical/job/WHOLE.md), the renewed household and relation carry irreducible loss and do not make the dead interchangeable with what comes later, so a whole that returns faithfully carries what cannot be replaced. Escape in [The Prisoner](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md) releases several companions whose paths diverge, and the return home does not make Six the owner of the others' meaning. A finite [presentation](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/avatar-image-mask-idol/WHOLE.md) can make a bearer addressable, and idolisation begins when the presentation supplies the bearer's answer in advance and prevents the actual source from correcting that image.

These are different images of one requirement: **unity has ethical force only while difference remains capable of return.** The Other is in the whole and is not therefore in my possession. My best account can become part of our relation, and your answer can still change what that account means and what I am entitled to do with it.

Unity lives through a relation strong enough for the one addressed to answer and for that answer to matter. Shared belonging leaves intact the grounds from which either participant can refuse, correct or meet again.
