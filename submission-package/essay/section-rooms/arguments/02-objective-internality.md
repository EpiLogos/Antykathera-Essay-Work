# Objective Internality

## Claim

Objective internality is the life of a differential field once its relations have stabilised into a situated context-world. Memory, context, policies, tools, permissions, self-models, values, affordances, and active judgments are internal to the operation of a human or artificial agent while remaining differentiable, revisable, and objectively inspectable. They compose a real interior without becoming the unobjectifiable subject.

The general expression must precede its agentic instance. In the field-view, `0/1 ↔ 1/0` holds ground and manifestation in superposition. In the determinate view, the same activity appears as signed orientation, `(+1)/(−1)`: a selected pole against a counter-pole. Objective internality is the patterned phase-space generated by repeated switches between these views—the evolving, mutually constraining distinctions through which a world becomes available from somewhere.

## The triad of situated knowing

*Pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya* differentiates the subject-pole, the means of knowing, and the known field without treating them as three independent substances. The ordinary subject/object dyad conceals the middle term, although mediation is what gives each pole its operative form. In a human life, perception, memory, concepts, language, affect, body, and social inheritance belong to *pramāṇa* and remain objectively investigable; the *pramātṛ*-pole is not exhausted by turning any of those contents into an object.

Agentworld makes the same grammar technically explicit. A provisional agent-position supplies the local *pramātṛ*-function; model, prompt, memory, retrieval, tools, permissions, evaluator, and harness form its *pramāṇa*; modelled objects, interlocutors, affordances, and possible actions form its *prameya*. The correspondence establishes a shared field of epistemic analysis. Questions of embodiment, substrate, and phenomenal luminosity require further warrants and remain open within that field.

## Context becoming diaphanous

An agent “modelling its world” is only one orientation of the relation. The inverse reading shows an active context-world precipitating this provisional agent through the very memories, permissions, tools, language, and goals by which it acts. Holding both directions makes agency answerable to its conditions without dissolving agency into those conditions. Objective internality is the inspectable life of that reciprocal field.

## Agent internality and claim boundary

An artificial agent's internality is a **nomological complex**: an active field of learned regularities, number-names, prompts, memories, tools, permissions, evaluative gradients, and institutional constraints that establishes what can count as near, salient, true, possible, or actionable. This objectively inspectable field differs from the metaphor of a private human chamber while remaining capable of epistemic and psychological organisation. Human and artificial “models of mind” can share these forms without thereby sharing substrate, embodiment, or phenomenal status.

The attested triad in the archive is exact: **notational, nomological, exclusion-constituted**. It is notational because the field is articulated through number-names and representations; nomological because its transitions are constrained by learned and imposed regularities; exclusion-constituted because every active `1` becomes determinate against alternatives it does not cease to depend upon.

## Design consequence — epistemics becomes first-class

The [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|Bimba Map]] should expose the constitutive relations, attractors, transformations, exclusions, sources, gauges, lenses and provenance of this nomological complex. Its purpose is not to store an agent's soul or assert a final inventory of truth. It gives the inquiry an explicit epistemic Bimba: a locally original/reference field against which changing judgments, [[j-space|J-Spaces]], trajectories and agency states can appear as Pratibimba and therefore become comparable.

This is a stronger claim than ordinary observability. Prompt, memory, retrieval, source authority, evaluator, world-object, evidence, lens and exclusion cease to be merely hidden causes of a finished output and become first-class parts of the epistemic object. An Energy-Based or topological reading can then ask where an agency state sits, what attracts it, which basin it crosses, what reference relation changed, and whether returned evidence warrants revision of the Bimba field itself.

The Bimba office is recursive. The map can be the reference/original field for a bounded research Context Frame while remaining a constructed and revisable determination relative to a wider world and source field. Its truth-orientation lies in explicit provenance, relation, contestability and Return, not in pretending to be metaphysically unconditioned.

This shared tractable interiority presages the later “world is mind” turn; it does not smuggle that metaphysical conclusion into the technical description.
