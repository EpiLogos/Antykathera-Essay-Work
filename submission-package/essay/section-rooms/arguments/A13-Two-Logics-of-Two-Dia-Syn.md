# A13 — Two Logics of Two — Dia / Syn

<!-- paired-field-navigation -->
[Argument field](README.md) · [Other face: A13′ — The Cut and the Gathering](conjugate/A13-prime-The-Cut-and-the-Gathering.md) · [Shared A/C root](conjugate/AC.md)
<!-- /paired-field-navigation -->

## #0

This record argues, in the episteme register, that a polarity can be accounted for in two complete ways, and that the choice between them decides what a distinction does to the terms it separates. The signed arithmetic and the count that follows from it are Derived. Their readings in psyche, politics, myth and technical systems are Argued, and each carries its own source.

Difference occurs inside a relation, and Taylor's comparability principle says why: "Two things cannot stand in opposition unless they share the ground that makes them comparable; difference without a common register is not difference but simply the *unrelated*, and the unrelated cannot oppose one another because they cannot meet, even in difference." Every opposition therefore testifies to the unity it appears to refuse, which is the sense in which opposition betrays, and the camps that afterwards insist on separate ground have already placed their opponents on one axis in order to oppose them. A bare relating stroke, before either side has a sign, is written `/ = −/−`, and every polarity we draw is that stroke once signs are assigned.

Its parties can be neither exchanged nor pulled apart. The [[A11-The-Two-Ones-0-One-1-All|two Ones]], the singular One (`0`, Self-Identity) and the polyvalent All (`1`, Self-Difference), each have a firstness of their own, and the [[A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|One/All relation]] grounds why they belong together nonetheless: the whole is prior to its parts by dependence, since no part produces all the relations and conditions through which it is this part, and the parts are the whole's actual differentiation. A distinction drawn between One and All, or between any two terms derived from them, therefore either keeps that belonging in view or loses it, and the two logics are the two ways of doing each.

In the apoha account of meaning, [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C18-Apoha|constitutive exclusion]], a term picks out its object by excluding what it is not, so the excluded alternatives stay active in the sense of the determination: a boundary selects something real. What follows from a boundary depends on whether the selection keeps the field through which it acquired its sense and its force. The core derivation states this as the criterion for everything below. No fall occurs in drawing the boundary; the fall lies in how the two are accounted for, according to whether the act of distinction includes what makes it possible or severs itself from its own condition.

## #1

Dia-ballein, from *diaballein*, to throw apart, is the first accounting, and it gives difference direction. Assigning signs to the bare stroke produces the polar line

$$
(-1)/(+1),
$$

in which zero remains the axis of tension and connection. Each pole is definite because it is read from that shared zero, so a pole's local definiteness already carries its relation to the counter-direction it has not selected and does not wait for a later reconciliation to acquire one.

Everything that has to be exact depends on this logic. A measurement reads a signed difference from a zero, a classification takes one side of a criterion, a critique or a refusal names what it declines, and a decision commits to one branch and gives up the other. At its simplest, two alternatives and a stated criterion that selects one of them give an output that is exact relative to the pair and the criterion, with the unselected alternative excluded in this selection and not declared nonexistent in every account, and larger cases keep that shape. Divide and conquer makes a problem tractable by splitting it into subproblems that can be solved independently, and Bratton's *Agentworld* brief (§7.3) treats managed conflict, which it illustrates with predator–prey modelling, courtroom adversaries, peer review, red-teaming and adversarial training, as a central mechanism by which composite systems adjust and refine themselves. An account that could not cut would be unable to say what it claims or what it leaves out, and a gathering without a cut would have no criterion and no means of committing to anything. Dia-ballein is therefore indispensable, and as the core derivation puts it, dia-ballein is not a fall merely because it cuts.

Dia-ballein can also collapse its own polarity, by replacing the held stroke with arithmetic between isolated terms, and each such operation answers a real question while hiding another. Adding the signed units gives cancellation:

$$
(-1)+(+1)=0.
$$

A pound borrowed and a pound repaid leave a balance of zero, and so does a ledger in which nothing ever passed between the two parties. The sum answers how much remains owing; it never promised to say whether anyone helped anyone, whether repayment came gladly, or whether the debt was all that passed between them. Error enters when a settled balance is taken to settle the relation, and nothing in the sum prevents it, since many different pairs sum to zero and the result no longer records which pair produced it.

Subtraction measures the span between the poles from one end, and the end it is measured from claims the whole span as its own magnitude, which gives the two chiralities of appropriation:

$$
(+1)-(-1)=+2,\qquad (-1)-(+1)=-2.
$$

Each equation is exact, and each is the same distance charged from the opposite pole, so one party's gain is the other's loss. The result also forgets where it began, because the pairs (1, −1) and (4, 2) both give a difference of 2. In the political case of the sacrificed representative both operations act in turn. The public reckons the whole span of a harm from its own pole onto one person, and its `+2` is that person's `−2`; the officeholder's dismissal then balances the account as `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the cancellation is presented as the restoration of public sovereignty, while those who financed, constrained or profited from the decisions escape the same reckoning.

A healthy polarity holds its span through the zero, each sign keeping its sense through the other, and `±2` is the image of imbalance: the duality hardened into opposition. A held polarity oscillates, each position passing into relation with the other through the stroke, as a breath goes out and comes back, and appropriation stops that movement at one end and fixes the span as a standing quantity. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/spencer-brown/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form/spencer-brown-1969-laws-form|Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form*]] sources the formal shape of the oscillation, since an expression that re-enters its own space takes an imaginary state in time and so alternates in value; reading hardened opposition as that alternation arrested is the essay's own step.

Logically and etymologically this is the structure of the diabolical: a cognition that divides and then forgets the whole it divided, taking its own operation for the nature of what it operates on, and discarding what it cannot include so that a false separation of identities can be kept. Greek already names a division done between persons, since *diaballein* also meant to set at variance and to accuse, and *diabolos* is the accuser. Turned inward it becomes repression: the `−1` is the share of the psyche denied a sign, stored in the shadow and returning with interest, which is Jung's *enantiodromia*, the compensatory revenge of the censored pole. These psychic and etymological readings are Argued, and the signed arithmetic they read is Derived.

## #2

A determination meets the return at the point where it claims to be sufficient. Take the position that counts the manifest mark, the determinate and countable thing, as the whole of reality, and call its mark the materialist `1`. What that position has excluded, the condition under which anything could be marked, comes back first as a break inside its own reckoning, and written as the mark's division by its condition the break is `1/0`. Ordinary arithmetic gives this quotient no value, since no number multiplied by zero returns one, and the native notation keeps that inability exact: the determination cannot count the condition of its determining as one more determination. Whatever it offers as an answer is another `1`, so the unobjectifiable condition has again been approached through something objectifiable.

A determination can answer the break in three ways. By cancellation it settles the break in a terminal zero: the failed computation is dismissed together with what it was reaching for, and the account carries on among the surviving ones as though their capacity to appear and be counted needed no further thought. By appropriation it gives the whole span to one pole as `±2`. By retention it leaves the uncomputed `/0` beneath the one, so that the determination goes on standing while its ground stays active in how it is understood and used, and this retention is the recognition written `0/1`: the mark is received through the condition it had treated as absent. Return enters as a break before it can be recognised as a relation.

Syn-ballein, from *symballein*, to throw together, is the accounting that takes the third course, and it began as a practice with objects. A coin, a knucklebone or a tile was broken between two friends, each keeping a half, so that fitting the fracture later proved the belonging. The halves had to remain two for the fitting to mean anything, so fusion would have destroyed the proof as surely as loss; what the practice yields is recognition without merger. Its two parties are not a term and some other one that negates it, but one and its own other, co-equal portions of a single thing, and written as the minimal distinction between integers this is `0/1`.

Our notation holds that logic because the slash works in two registers at once. In arithmetic it is division, the most domesticated sign in the stock. In logic it is AND/OR, a stroke that declines to decide between conjunction and disjunction: conjunction retains both terms, and disjunction preserves their difference. Under AND/OR, `0/1` reads the ground as expressed through the distinction, and the same stroke generates the obverse `1/0`, the distinction held within and by the ground. Each is the one relation seen from its other end and not a second relation, and QL writes their sameness across the two orientations, with the outer slash relating the orientations themselves:

$$
0/1 = 1/0,\qquad (0/1)/(1/0).
$$

Ordinary arithmetic defines `0/1` and leaves `1/0` undefined, and the notation keeps that asymmetry in place of smoothing it away. Its full form joins arithmetic's most docile fraction to its one forbidden gesture across a single stroke, with an outer slash that is an operation of QL relational notation and no ordinary division, so that a finite expression meets, inside its own writing, the condition it cannot turn into another finite expression.

Written this way, a distinction can count itself. It is a two, ground and mark; held under AND/OR it must also distinguish its own two readings, `0/1` and `1/0`; and a two applied to a two gives the four pairings `{00,01,10,11}`. Keeping the two singles `{0,1}` alongside the four pairings gives

$$
2+2^2=2+4=6=4+2.
$$

Nothing is added in this count. Six is what a two amounts to once it includes itself, so the sixfold holds the poles together with the ways their relation can relate itself, and the achieved determination can become accountable to its source and its inverse instead of ending at its output. This is why the two logics yield a process body.

Both lines pass through a zero, and that decides the difference between them. In dia-ballein the zero is annihilation, the terminus at which a sum of opposites arrives. In syn-ballein it is ground and threshold, a death the account returns through. The core derivation compresses this into one sentence of Taylor's: the devil is the literalist of the minus sign, who reads zero as cancellation where Logos reads it as return.

Syn-ballein keeps the cut's result and makes the result carry what it answered: the problem, the alternatives it excluded, the counter-reading that can contest it, and the wider state it changes. Take two independent measurements of one object, each recorded with its instrument, unit and conditions. A gathering first keeps both records and then asks whether a comparison or a conversion is valid. If the units convert, a common display can show how the two relate while the originals stay; if the conditions differ materially, that difference stays in the result. Deleting the inconvenient measurement loses a term, replacing both with one unsupported figure merges them, and displaying both while withholding the condition that makes one of them inapplicable keeps the appearance of a gathering with none of its work. Because the cut stays, syn-ballein is the logic of defining towards wholeness and not a way of having it: a claim to have achieved the whole would itself be a `1` occupying the zero, and the retained seam is what keeps wholeness a direction of definition instead of a possession.

One diagnostic covers both accountings: where did the seam go? In the token the seam is the fracture, and in the notation it is the stroke with its undefined side. A seam can be externalised, as when the fracture is relocated into an enemy, and it can be erased, as when the halves are melted into a single lump. Only a seam that is kept leaves the relation able to return.

## #3

A computation divides a problem, relates the parts, recombines the results and returns the combined answer to the question that was asked, and recursive division succeeds only because the sub-answers can be put back together: the splitting is half the method. Where the capacity to combine is held privately while everyone else stays divided, division stops being a method and becomes a way of governing the gap between the parts.

Politics runs the same pulse, and there it asks who holds the relating office. Which institutions, labour, material conditions and coercion put recombination into a few hands has to be shown from evidence in each historical case, and a psychological description alone cannot establish an instance. In mass-formation psychology, [Le Bon](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/le-bon/le-bon-1895-crowd-popular-mind/le-bon-1895-crowd-popular-mind.md) grounds its first step by observing that a crowd need not gather in one place: a scattered population becomes a psychological crowd when its members, under the same conditions, acquire a common direction of feeling, thought and action. [Jung](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/jung/jung-2013-undiscovered-self-routledge/jung-2013-undiscovered-self-routledge.md) sources the transfer that follows: the larger the crowd, the more negligible the individual, so responsibility is collectivised and the State is made a quasi-person that wills, knows, protects and punishes, while the people and offices composing it recede behind its mask. [Desmet](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/desmet/desmet-2022-psychology-totalitarianism-web-essay/desmet-2022-psychology-totalitarianism-web-essay.md) sources the affect that prepares the transfer, namely isolation, loss of meaning, and free-floating anxiety and aggression, which find relief when a narrative supplies them with a target. Joined, these give a circuit: a population is atomised, its unbound affect is fixed on an excluded enemy, responsibility is delegated upward, and the apparatus offers itself as the one shared subject. [Iakovou's institutional qualification](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/political-theory-institutions/iakovou/iakovou-2022-misuse-totalitarianism/iakovou-2022-misuse-totalitarianism.md) **qualifies** the circuit by insisting that mass psychology cannot replace institutions, parties, offices and the material organisation of authority. What the circuit shows is how the capture of the relating office can feel, to those captured, like collective purpose; which regime did the capturing is left to evidence.

Taylor's quilting of 27 July 2026 describes how the circuit closes on a culture. A culture that compresses its internal plurality under one measure generates a counterculture, the lost many returning in signed form, and the first counter may be the field's own self-correction, since it tells the established one that it is not the whole. Trouble begins when the counter monoises in turn: each camp then compresses its own plurality, needs the other as the negative identity through which it knows itself, treats internal dissent as contamination, and reads the other's hardening as proof that further exclusion is necessary. Each camp also counts the whole span between them as the other's departure, `+2` from one end and `−2` from the other, which is appropriation reckoned from both poles at once. Antagonism becomes more visible while plurality shrinks, and the field that generated both camps can be thought only as the battlefield between them. This [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C26-Monoisation-Counter-Generation|counter-generation]] makes the opposed camp a condition of each camp's hardened identity, and each camp works as a [[A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime|local arbitration regime]] that decides which contrary evidence can count inside it.

Syn-ballein asks what field was divided so that these terms now appear as mutually exclusive wholes, and who controls its recomposition. "Divine and concur" names that practice: to divine is to find, in the apparent self-sufficiency of a pole, the relation it has obscured, and to concur is for differentiated positions to move together through a ground that none of them possesses, which is neither agreement nor fusion. It calculates no midpoint, since a centrist position stays on the signed line and only chooses a place between `−1` and `+1`. It also leaves judgment intact: unequal facts, power and responsibility can still be weighed within the recovered field, and recovering where the terms came from makes the weighing more answerable.

A problem can close when its stated task is resolved. A constitutive predicament persists because each pole supplies something the other needs, and forced restabilisation treats it as though one final answer could discharge it, expelling the relation as a remainder. [[A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure|Arbitration]] becomes usurpation at small scale where the closing authority makes its own measure unavailable to the relation it judges, as when someone asks who gave a score its weight and is handed another score. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C23-Severance|Severance]] loses the field through exclusion, so that a produced term stands as though the source and alternatives through which it arose were absent, as the deleted measurement does. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C24-Fusion|Fusion]] loses the difference through enforced coincidence, as the single unsupported figure does, and with it the distinct address through which a reply could change anything. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C25-Counterfeit-Gathering|Counterfeit gathering]] keeps the terms and the relation in plain view while those gathered cannot revise the relation's terms or its source, as the display of two records with the disqualifying condition withheld does. Severance and fusion meet in a comprehensive enclosure that claims to be a living whole while withholding the return that could change its measure.

## #4

Two Greek tellings, read together, show what each logic does when it meets a case. Their office is mythemic: each whole image discloses a relation through the action it stages, while the formal derivation keeps its own warrant and nothing here attributes the two logics to Hesiod or Homer.

In [[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-philology/hesiod/hesiod-1920-theogony-evelyn-white/hesiod-1920-theogony-evelyn-white|Hesiod's genealogy]], *Theogony* 933–937, Aphrodite bears Ares Panic and Fear, the terrible gods who drive men's ranks into disorder in war, and also Harmonia. Strife and beauty together therefore bear concord and terror together, and the ancient family cannot be read as a guarantee that union produces concord. In the Homeric song of *Odyssey* 8, which the [[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-philology/homer/homer-1919-odyssey-murray/homer-1919-odyssey-murray|Homeric sequence]] sources, the lovers are again Ares and Aphrodite, and Aphrodite's husband is Hephaestus. Helios, who had seen the lovers together, tells Hephaestus, and the smith answers with his craft: he forges bonds that cannot be broken or loosed and are too fine for any god to see, catches the pair in bed, and summons the gods to look. The goddesses stay home for shame; the gods who come laugh, and Hermes says he would gladly bear three times as many bonds, with all the goddesses watching, to lie beside Aphrodite. Hephaestus demands the bride-gifts back, and the gods price the adultery as a fine. Poseidon, the one god who does not laugh, promises that Ares will pay, and since a pledge for someone who can run away is worth little, Hephaestus asks how Poseidon himself could be held to it, whereupon Poseidon undertakes to pay if Ares flees. Hephaestus accepts and looses the bonds. Ares goes to Thrace and Aphrodite to Paphos, and the song does not say whether anyone ever paid.

Ares gives refusal, defence and the capacity to remain distinct, and Aphrodite gives attraction and meeting; their relation bears Harmonia while it keeps its tension, in the manner of the bow and lyre of Heraclitus B51. Hephaestus answers that living relation with an apparatus built wholly from dia-ballein's means. Helios detects, the bonds capture, the gods' assembled gaze turns capture into public evidence, and bride-gifts and the adulterer's fine convert grievance into account. The lovers are held as exposed terms while their maker, the tribunal and its spectators stand over them as a zero occupied from outside. Every one of these operations succeeds, and the whole still cannot conclude: a trap can hold a debtor but cannot make a debt good, and the lovers stay bound until someone steps in from beyond the apparatus. Bonds secure bodies, and the promise accepted from Poseidon makes their release possible, so capture and undertaking are different kinds of work. Hephaestus himself distrusts such a promise and accepts it because nothing else can open the bonds and close the account. His net gathers by arresting: it throws the lovers together and so immobilises them, keeping both terms in view while denying their reciprocity. The essay's name for this is counterfeit gathering, and the surety shows what the counterfeit lacks, a person standing for another at real risk, which no mechanism generated. Homer's own word for the device is *dolos*, a snare, and *diaballein* does not appear in the episode, so calling Hephaestus's public exposure *diabolē* in the classical lexicon's sense, an accusation staged as exposure that sets the parties at variance before witnesses, is the essay's naming and is Argued.

Taylor's [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics|Symbolon Dynamics]] sources a second distinction that cuts across the first. Synchronic co-presence and diachronic transformation are two ways of asking about one movement, a configuration held together and a trajectory followed through change, and they are a different pair from cutting and gathering. A photograph-like arrangement can hide the field that produced it, a narrative can carry its provenance, and a completed change of perspective differs from the contextual holding of the perspectives together. Neither temporal view decides by itself whether a relation is retained.

Inside a single life the same relation appears in [[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust/taylor-2026-revision-notes-trust|the trust-revision account]], which sources this reading. Taylor writes: "I have never coincided with myself... and yet my life is all coinciding." A self never exhaustively matches its own representation, because self-reflection returns the self as its own other, and the coincidences that do occur arrive through encounters that reflection could not have performed alone. An image becomes an idol when it claims to coincide exhaustively with what it images. The correction keeps determinate identity, which remains necessary, and lets the image answer to an encounter that can change it.

## #5→0

Dia differentiates, syn gathers without abolishing the differentiation, and neither is self-sufficient, which is the reusable form [Dia / Syn](concepts/C50-Dia-Syn.md) **defines**. What passes into the rest of the field is therefore one standing question: what must be distinguished here, and what relation among the distinguished terms must stay active if the distinction is to return rather than harden? [Computational Process Ontology](A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) **extends** the question into the transformations of a world, and [Deferential Intelligence](A31-Deferential-Intelligence.md) **extends** it to judgment that must return authority to what it depends on.

Lacan's cut makes differentiation consequential within signification, and Taylor's accounting asks of any such cut whether it retains the relation it differentiates; the [language history](../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-address) **historicises** the Lacanian attribution on its own terms, keeping Lacan's bar, Dignāga's exclusion and Taylor's slash as three inscriptions with three genealogies. Heraclitus B51 holds differing agreement in tension, what differs agreeing with itself through a backwards-turning fastening like that of bow and lyre, and the [transmitted philosophical account](../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/ancient-philosophy/DEVELOPMENT-ancient-philosophy.md#ancient-tension) **historicises** that relation, preserving Mackenzie's wording and distinguishing it from both tuning ratios and Aristotle's qualified non-contradiction. Taylor's [bow–lyre image](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/hellenic/ares-aphrodite-hephaestus-poseidon/WHOLE.md#ares-bow-lyre-hamartia) **extends** the fragment: a bow forgets that it is also a lyre when its tension collapses into a target, and the mark then misses its ground. B51 supplies the differing agreement, and the lexical history of *hamartia* has to establish separately that the word means missing the mark.

Three authored images each ask which operation a cut performs. Taylor's [erased taijitu](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/taylor-authored-images/WHOLE.md#erased-taijitu) **figures** both failures. Each field keeps a dot of the other, and that retained other keeps the whole living; erasing the dots leaves the fields opposed but takes away their capacity to hold one another, whether the opposition is cancelled into nothing or one pole claims to be absolute and suppresses its dependence. Sharper outlines do not restore the plurality erased inside them, and the diagram is Taylor's QL imagery with no claim to interpret an ancient Taoist text. A [Necker-cube sequence](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/taylor-authored-images/WHOLE.md#necker-cube) **compares** successive reversal with contextual holding: while one reading fills the field the other appears only by a flip, whereas context holds the differently oriented readings in relation, with no experimental claim that incompatible depth percepts are literally simultaneous. Taylor's corrected eagle in the [Mother, Assumption and chiasm whole](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mother-assumption-chiasm/WHOLE.md#mother-eagle-valence) **figures** signed power. Its two heads direct one body in opposed orientations, and the question is which operation the body carries: `(−1)/(+1)` holds the polarity, addition cancels it, and the opposed subtractions give `−2` or `+2`, one valence enlarged by negating the other. A sign carries no moral identity, so the body is judged by the operation it performs.

Other images show a cut that sustains the relation it makes. The [Indra's jewel-net whole](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-native-relation) **embodies** selection and gathering together: attention distinguishes one jewel and then follows its constitutive relations without erasing the distinction, so the definite jewel carries its constituting field, while the reflection is simultaneous and the reader's traversal successive. The [Valentinian Sophia–Horos–Achamoth whole](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/late-antique-gnostic/valentinian-sophia-horos-achamoth/WHOLE.md#valentinian-authorial-return) **compares** Horos's sustaining separation, which lets Sophia remain in her conjunction, with the Aeons' joint contribution to the Soter: a boundary can hold a relation, and a composed whole can keep its contributors distinct. That source's final destruction of matter keeps its own sequence and is not converted into a noncancelling return. A [historical matching token](../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source) **grounds** the same point in practice, since matching requires the parts to stay distinct while their fracture makes a shared relation recognisable, and the parties and the institution determine what the recognition can warrant. In a present account, likewise, the source and whoever stands for its use stay distinguishable, and returning to that relation makes the account answerable to what it depends on.

Between persons the two logics are felt as war and love. The [Ares–Aphrodite relation](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/hellenic/ares-aphrodite-hephaestus-poseidon/WHOLE.md#ares-guarantee-release) **compares** Harmonia and the separate genealogies of Eros with Homer's capture and release, and in Homer the bond is loosened through an undertaking whose payment the song never narrates. The [Eros–Psyche union](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/roman-latin/eros-psyche/WHOLE.md#psyche-native-return) **tests** the same pair on a different route, through four labours, a descent, a waking and an acknowledged continuation, with Apuleius's legal idiom of possession kept in its account of union. Taylor's [indelible-bond reading](../../symbolon/mytheme/archetypal-ground/neumann-images/WHOLE.md#neumann-return-bond) of Neumann **grounds** cutting and knowing-with in a shared genesis: a cut changes the relation, and the claim to have abolished the relation is what produces the fork between love and war, while what was cut remains a condition of how the differentiated powers can meet again.

[The Prisoner's whole](../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-asymmetric-count) **figures** the administered counterpart, a cut whose authority is placed outside challenge. The Village distinguishes and recombines its numbered inhabitants, yet Number Six cannot change the relation that counts him, so the powers of distinction and combination stay real while their source and return are withheld, and Taylor locates the counterfeit reversal in the asymmetry between being counted and answering the account. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief|Agentworld]] **tests** the technical consequence through the brief's narrow corridor, "same decision-procedural grammar, sufficiently divergent world models" (§8.4). A common grammar that preserves divergent models keeps premises contestable, and agreement is no evidence of a shared field where the method suppresses the difference that would test it. This is the essay's response to the brief, and it takes the brief's own modality, since its section on Agentworldbuilding offers the ten thematic questions as composite scenarios and not as conclusions.

[[symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy|Operational homology]] **compares** these passages through what each preserves of differentiation and return. Common lexical descent, doctrinal identity and empirical causal sameness each need evidence of their own kind, so the Greek descent of *dia-ballein* and *syn-ballein* and claims about particular political formations remain separate historical inquiries.

Superposition belongs first to the dual-non-dual ontology, before any comparison with a physical formalism: ground and manifestation are distinguishable orientations of one appearing-act, each given through the relation that prevents either from becoming self-sufficient, and `0/1` and `1/0` write that co-presence in inverse directions. Quantum state space, complementarity and non-classical logics offer formal neighbours with operations of their own. [Priest's fifth corner](../../symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/priest/priest-2018-fifth-corner/priest-2018-fifth-corner.md) answers Nāgārjuna's rejection of all four corners of the catuṣkoṭi with a fifth value for the ineffable, and the catuṣkoṭi crossing in [Matheme](../../symbolon/matheme/definition/README.md) takes the same limit of predication through IS, IS-NOT, BOTH, NEITHER and SILENCE. The two readings part at one joint, which the source house records: Priest's fifth value is a value, while QL's **#0** is a generative precondition and no truth-value, so the published objections, from Siderits, Westerhoff, Green and Kapsner, bear on the fifth value's standing as a value and leave untouched the ground QL names.

QL also serves as an attractor-space for the *pros hen* analogia, by which mono–poly, subject/object, one/many, self/other, order/chaos and Apollo/Dionysus can be read together without being declared synonymous. In Taylor's worked example, a person bears health, a diet contributes to it and a complexion signs it; all three are called healthy through their ordered relation to one focus, and none of them has health in the same way. The QL positions work likewise as transcendental attractors around which distinct mathematical, psychological, metaphysical, linguistic and technical expressions constellate. A taxonomic box would demand identity of content, whereas [focal relation](concepts/C63-Pros-Hen-In-Quantum-Focal-Return-Qualified-Predication.md) constrains movement and preserves differences of register.

The diagnostic travels with the argument and asks a different question in each register. In the crowd it asks where the relating office went, in the erased taijitu where the dots went, in the Village where the source of command went, and in the token whether the fracture still fits. Wherever the seam has been externalised as an enemy or silently erased through fusion, the relation has lost its ground.
