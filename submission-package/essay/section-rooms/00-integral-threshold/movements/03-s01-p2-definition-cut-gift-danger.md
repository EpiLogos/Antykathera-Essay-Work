# §0/1 · #2 — Definition as Cut, Gift, and Danger

Movement 03 of 48 · [This room](../ROOM-00-integral-threshold.md) · [← Previous](02-s01-p1-define-subject.md) · [Next →](04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md)

## Claim
Determination is negational and productive. It gives a knowable object by distinguishing it from what it is not, and it becomes dangerous when the achieved term forgets the field and activity from which it was cut and presents itself as their source. Anyone who has said "I know myself" and been wrong in the same sentence has met both faces: a description gives something definite to work with, and the means through which we came to know ourselves quietly assume the authority of the one who knows.

## Warrant
The language of knowing already carries the predicament. Latin *scire*, to know, lies on the standard account beside a root meaning to cut or split, so knowing cuts: it separates a voice from noise and an explanation from an excuse. *Scire* also enters *conscire*, to know together, and *conscientia*, where shared knowledge, inward awareness and moral testimony were joined. The essay names the knowing at the root of that family **withness**, the witnessing present in every act of knowing, by which a life is known from within as it is lived, and conscience is the voice in which the person is returned to it. The ego-self appropriates the knowing that occurs through its aperture, and then "I know what I meant" ends the listening. English has a pun for the turn: the *con-* of knowing-together becomes a con, the confidence trick that wins a trust it means to use, in which the knower is persuaded that the image it recognises itself in is what it finally is. In Jung's *Red Book* a god set outside the self tears us loose from it, and a god moved into the self leaves "only the one god despite his multiplicity", which is `0/1` in vernacular speech. Written out, the concealment is `0/1` with the `0/` occluded, leaving the determinate self-image to present itself as the source of the activity it only expresses.

Two witnesses show that the cut is productive and not merely linguistic. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles|Spinoza]] gives the Western geometrical articulation: in his letter to Jelles, a figure exists only in finite bodies, and because figure is determination and determination is negation, the outline makes the shape by marking where it ends. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation|Apoha]] gives the semantic articulation: Dignāga holds that a word excludes other referents, so *cow* is articulated as *not non-cow*, and Pind's translation has the referent as a thing as qualified by preclusion, not mere preclusion. A real particular is reached through a conceptual distinction whose generality depends on what it excludes. The operations differ and neither is subordinated to the other, and together they establish the cut as a condition of intelligibility.

Meaning carried by cuts slides and gets fastened. Derrida's *différance* names the differing and deferring by which a sign refers onward through other signs, and Lacan's *glissement* names the signified slipping beneath the signifier, with the *point de capiton*, the quilting point, as the place where a chain of signifiers takes hold. Once *protecting my child* has acquired a fixed meaning, a parent can hear a fifteen-year-old's request to take the train alone or stop it before it is finished, and the difference lies in how the meaning was fixed. Fixing of this kind governs institutions as well as households. A parent who withdraws from the encounter in which authority could be questioned keeps the power of the relation and refuses its reciprocity, so the finite `1` plays the `0`. Kelo's neighbours in New London received a public warrant that concealed how alternatives were selected and costs assigned, and Carlin's line that "it's a big club and you ain't in it" names the operation: the club keeps syn-ballein for its members while dia-ballein holds everyone outside apart on the axis. A system that cannot mark the limits of its determinations turns provisional distinctions into natural facts and governs through them. Technical classifications do this at machine speed, since they produce governable objects by stabilising boundaries.

Beneath all of it runs trust, which names an already-enacted dependence, while knowledge opens an aperture on that dependence and belief steadies an orientation within that aperture; the account becomes dangerous when continued reliance is made conditional on believing it incapable of failure, and its uncertainty is then carried by the difficult child, the disloyal citizen, the witness declared inadmissible. Latin *fides* comes before arbitration, since a witness must be heard before testimony is weighed, and three operations hide in any authoritative account: a criterion distinguishes, a delineation gives the difference a workable boundary, and an arbitration decides through the boundary. A definition remains a gift while it can receive a consequence that redraws its line.

The previous movement made the mediating act visible in ordinary language without a formal sign, and that withholding stays in force. We first need to see what a cut does: it gives a determinate object, leaves a field of excluded alternatives, and can conceal the activity by which its boundary acquired authority. Only after the formal-limit sequence has made that recurrent condition unavoidable will Zero enter as a proposed meta-sign.

## Tension / limit
The cut remains necessary, and the God-image shows how far its danger reaches. The definition of the Subject was first written for God, and theodicy tests it: evil set outside the whole needs a second source, and suffering defined away protects an image at the cost of the One. Whatever we say of God we must eventually withdraw, since the predicates are forms through which the Subject becomes articulate to itself. *Neti neti*, "not this, not this", does this withdrawal from the side of the source, taking back each proposed predicate so that none grounds the Absolute (*nirguṇa Brahman*), and the *catuṣkoṭi* unfixes a proposition's claim to have exhausted the alternatives. Their offices differ: apophatic refusal keeps the ground from becoming a final predicate, and the tetralemma keeps a proposition from occupying the whole field of possibility.

## Anchor and transition

The cut's gift and danger return to [E2's criterion through distinction](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#criterion-through-distinction). Criterion distinguishes, Delineation gives that difference a workable boundary, and Arbitration decides through it, and usurpation occurs when the deciding boundary claims exemption from the continuity that enabled it. Productive definition remains capable of receiving a consequence that redraws its line.

**Relational anchor:** field — differentiating act — determinate mark.
