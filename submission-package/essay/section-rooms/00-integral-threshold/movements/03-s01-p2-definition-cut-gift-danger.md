# §0/1 · #2 — Definition as Cut, Gift, and Danger

Movement 03 of 48 · [This room](../ROOM-00-integral-threshold.md) · [← Previous](02-s01-p1-define-subject.md) · [Next →](04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy.md)

## Claim
Determination is negational and productive. It gives a knowable object by distinguishing it from what it is not, and it becomes dangerous when the achieved term forgets the field and activity from which it was cut and presents itself as their source. Anyone who has said "I know myself" and been wrong in the same sentence has met both faces: a description gives something definite to work with, and the means through which we came to know ourselves quietly assume the authority of the one who knows. We follow that assumption from the self to the household, the state and the God-image, and state its operation in both logics: dia-ballein `(−1)/(+1)` collapses into cancellation and appropriation, and syn-ballein `(0/1)/(1/0)` keeps the shared zero operative through the difference. When the `0/` drops out of an account, a finite `1` plays the `0`, and authority passes to whoever can present an interpretation as the source speaking.

## Warrant
Our language of knowing carries the predicament. Latin *scire*, to know, lies on the standard account beside a root meaning to cut or split, so knowing cuts: it separates a voice from noise and an explanation from an excuse. *Scire* also enters *conscire*, to know together, and *conscientia*, where shared knowledge, inward awareness and moral testimony were joined. Perkins in 1596 explained *conscire* as two parties knowing a secret together, one of whom is God, and Augustine's *Confessions* X had already confessed that something of a person remains unknown to the person's own spirit while God knows it entire. At the root of that family lies knowing-with, which the essay calls **withness**: the witnessing present in every act of knowing, by which a life is known from within as it is lived; conscience returns the person to it. An ego-self claims sovereignty when it treats the aperture through which witnessing occurs as its possession, and "I know what I meant" ends the listening. Hobbes in 1651 watched the word turn the same way, from shared testimony to private self-certification, and English has a pun for the turn: the *con-* of knowing-together becomes a con, the confidence trick that wins a trust it means to use, in which Mono is taken in by a determination within Poly. A verdict such as "I'm just not a numbers person" shows the con in a life, because an account of habits becomes the condition under which the life may understand itself. Jung's *Red Book* gives it at the scale of a god: a god set outside the self tears us loose from it, and a god moved into the self leaves "only the one god despite his multiplicity", which is `0/1` in vernacular speech. Concealment is `0/1` with the `0/` occluded, so that the determinate self-image presents itself as the source of an activity it only expresses.

A cut gives *here* by giving *there*. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles|Spinoza]] states it geometrically in his letter to Jelles: figure exists only in finite bodies, determination is negation, and the outline makes the shape by marking where it ends. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation|Dignāga's apoha]] states it semantically: a word excludes other referents, so *cow* is articulated as *not non-cow*, and in Pind's translation the referent is a thing as qualified by preclusion, not mere preclusion. These two operations differ and neither is subordinated to the other, and together they establish the cut as a condition of intelligibility. Meaning carried by cuts slides and gets fastened. Derrida's *différance* names the differing and deferring by which a sign refers onward, Lacan's *glissement* the signified slipping beneath the signifier, and the *point de capiton* the place where a chain takes hold. Once *protecting my child* has acquired a fixed meaning, a parent can hear a fifteen-year-old's request to take the train alone or stop it before it is finished. A household, the *oikos* of economy, apportions time, attention and permission by such meanings, and at political scale the disagreement over what a policy does becomes a contest over whom one plays for: law emerges as sporting where it should be supporting.

Dia-ballein and syn-ballein name what a distinction does between people. *Syn-ballein* throws together; *dia-ballein*, thrown apart or across, also meant setting at variance, accusing and slandering, and *diabolos*, the accuser, became the Devil. A **held polarity** `(−1)/(+1)` keeps its stroke, so that either sign has its sense through the other. Two operations collapse it. Cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, is the balance of a borrowed and a repaid pound, and it answers how much remains owing without saying whether the relation is settled. Appropriation reckons the span from one pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` or `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and becomes a claim to own the relation. `±2` is the image of imbalance, duality hardened into opposition, and a polarity that oscillates cleanly is what Spencer-Brown's re-entering distinction gives when time enters. Red against blue is appropriation reckoned from both poles at once, each counting the whole span as the other's departure, and the common life the policy was meant to serve drops out of both accounts. Syn-ballein keeps the shared condition through the difference: the symbolon's pieces stay distinguishable and their reunion shows why their edges fit. Reading `1/0` explains why the zero keeps dropping out. `1/0` asks the determinate `1` to account for the `0` it proceeds from, any answer is another `1`, and when computable determination is made the sole criterion of reality the failed return is dismissed with what it was trying to reach. That is dia-ballein at work on the means of knowing themselves, cancelling the condition it cannot compute.

When the `0/` drops out, someone holds the authority it gave. A parent who says "because I am your father" invokes the order of kinship through which the instruction has standing, and Lacan's Name-of-the-Father names this symbolic function, which binds the bearer as it authorises them. Wolin's inverted totalitarianism describes authority that survives the repudiation of the person who exercised it, since power concentrates through institutions whose public leaders are replaceable. A representative can then serve as both agent and scapegoat, and both collapses operate in the sacrifice: the public's `(+1)−(−1)=+2` is the officeholder's `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, one span charged from its other end, and the officeholder's dismissal balances the account, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, presented as restored sovereignty. What they represented is protected, because the investigation of responsibility stops at the bearer. Two moments compose the appropriation: the account occludes the `0/`, and a quilting point binds the remaining terms to an authoritative interpretation which a ruler, parent or institution then identifies itself with. John's Gospel marks the contrast between kinds of sovereignty by their relation to origin, and the Father/Son/Spirit relation reads as `0/1` with the stroke as the relation the tradition names the Paraclete; the Father's name is itself an expression in the field of the `1`, and the usurper claims source-authority as the possession of a worldly position. Christ's legacy became an idol, a `1/0` that devolved into another `1`, and *hamartia*, missing the mark, locates the error: the finite `1` is installed as the Father whose name it bears.

We first wrote the definition of the Subject for God, and theodicy, Leibniz's name for the vindication of God's justice in a world of suffering, tests it. Once goodness is made constitutive, evil must count as evidence against God, be thinned into unreality, or stand as a second principle, and each breaks the One; the definition closes those exits. Pressed on their own predicates, omnipotence, omniscience, omnipresence and omnibenevolence each generate a shadow (self-limitation, a knowledge of what it cannot reach, a capacity for absence, a ground from which goodness and its negation differentiate), and two further attributes complete them: **omnivalence**, sovereignty over the whole valence-field as the condition from which valence differentiates, and **omni-lemmicity**, the capacity of every predicate to become its own issue. Received theology arrives at four and two without instruction. Our answer to theodicy is that God need not be protected from the death of a God-image: as Subject the referent's *is* cannot be denied, and as God every image of it can be, and must be. *Nirguṇa Brahman* and *neti neti* withdraw each predicate from the source's side, and Eckhart's God and Godhead distinguish active naming from the ground to which distinctions return. Exodus gives the name as *ehyeh asher ehyeh*, and since *ehyeh* had entered earlier as "I will be with thee", the name Moses receives answers to a presence already pledged, a being-with: withness as God's own name. Its psychological counterpart is Jung's shadow, "a moral problem that challenges the whole ego-personality", which projection places in the other, so that an image of my goodness that expels its evil must put it somewhere.

A finite `1` **plays the `0`** when a person or office borrows the position of the source and treats withdrawal from the encounter as a prerogative: the aloof parent keeps the power of the relation and refuses its reciprocal demands, and unanswerability becomes a means of rule. Its cases are institutional. At Fort Trumbull, New London, an approved redevelopment plan took homes by eminent domain and *Kelo v. City of New London* (2005) held the taking a public use; the gain was appropriated for a private development, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, the same span charged to the homeowners as `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and responsibility for their loss was cancelled to `0`. Reputation management and the SLAPP let wealth keep consequential dealings from scrutiny. Carlin's "it's a big club and you ain't in it" names the operation: the club keeps syn-ballein for its members while dia-ballein holds everyone outside apart on the axis. In June 2026 records verified by WIRED named the participants of Dialog, an invitation-only society, and the leak shows a topology, positions that appear separate in public sharing a bounded private field that the governed can neither enter nor inspect. Added to these are the heavy official redaction of the Epstein files, and Snowden and Assange as the cost of lifting a veil. A digit is a finger and *manipulation* descends from *manipulus*, a handful, so the first countable `1` is the first digit of manipulation. Nietzsche's death of God belongs to the marketplace, and in the household the God-image's failure to arrive is felt as abandonment, which a surrogate exploits by claiming the Father's authority while reproducing his absence.

Witnesses can be shut out only where something was trusted to begin with. **Trust** names the already-enacted dependence; **knowledge** opens an aperture through which it can be understood; **belief** steadies an orientation within that aperture so that we can act; and **faith**, in the essay's sense, lives the dependence through the account's limitation, with its grounds, scope and conditions of revision retained. A con works on this dependence, and an account that refuses to acknowledge its uncertainty has it carried by the difficult child, the disloyal citizen and the witness declared inadmissible. Latin *fides*, reliance and the credit granted to a word, comes before arbitration, since a witness must be heard before testimony is weighed, and the arbiter was first a witness. Three operations hide in any authoritative account: a criterion distinguishes, a delineation gives the difference a workable boundary, and an arbitration decides through the boundary. A definition remains a gift while it can receive a consequence that redraws its line. At its close stands the question formal reason asks of any rule: does it include itself among what it governs?

## Tension / limit
Necessary though the cut remains, the God-image shows how far its danger reaches. Whatever we say of God we must eventually withdraw, since the predicates are forms through which the Subject becomes articulate to itself. *Neti neti* and the tetralemma do related but different work. Apophatic refusal keeps the ground from becoming a final predicate; the tetralemma keeps a proposition from occupying the whole field of possibility. Their offices differ and neither is made to stand for the other. Institutional cases keep their own carriers: the *Kelo* opinion for the taking, WIRED's reporting for Dialog, the released records for the Epstein redactions, and the essay's `+2`/`−2` reading of each is **Argued**, an amplification through the signed arithmetic that attributes nothing to the sources themselves. Latin and Greek histories (scire, conscientia, *diaballein*) carry lexical history, and the theological readings, the `0/1` reading of the Trinity among them, are the essay's own.
