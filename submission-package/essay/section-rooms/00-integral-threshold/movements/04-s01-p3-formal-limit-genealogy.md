# §0/1 · #3 — The Formal-Limit Genealogy

## Claim
A sufficiently articulate system cannot convert every condition of its articulation into an ordinary internal term without remainder.

## Warrant
[[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/russell/russell-1908-theory-types/SOURCE|Russell]] restrict paradoxical re-entry; [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/godel/godel-1931-undecidable-propositions/SOURCE|Gödel]] proves scoped internal limits; [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus/SOURCE|Wittgenstein]] reaches silence and practice; [[whitehead-1978-process-reality|Whitehead]] makes non-closure creative; [[spencer-brown-1969-laws-form|Spencer-Brown]] and [[varela-1975-calculus-self-reference|Varela]] admit re-entry; [[frank-gleiser-thompson-2024-blind-spot|The Blind Spot — Frank, Gleiser, Thompson]] restores the experiencer.

[[15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge|Paradox]] shows how this limit moves across registers without becoming one homogenised theorem. [[15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge|Cusa's]] polygon approaches the circle while retaining the difference between finite measure and infinite form; [[Flasch — Meister Eckhart: Philosopher of Christianity (2015)|Flasch's account of Eckhart]] follows a language that affirms, qualifies, and returns each predicate to what exceeds it; poetry keeps the opening alive where a terminating definition would close. The anonymous medieval infinite-sphere image is used with its attribution intact: Cusa receives and transforms it rather than originating it.

## Tension / limit
Gödel performs a formal proof; Cusa and Eckhart perform mathematical-apophatic and linguistic disciplines; Whitehead supplies process metaphysics, and Bohm's process bridge enters only at #5→0. Their distinct objects and methods prevent their convergence from being mistaken for one theorem and give the wider architecture its strength.

[E3’s fides before arbitration](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md#fides-before-arbitration) gives the enquiry its prior reliance and its subsequent renewed entrustment: formal investigation already undertakes a practice before it can arbitrate its results. Russell’s, Gödel’s and Wittgenstein’s distinct limits retain their own scopes. Fides and Credere articulate the project’s relational passage through those limits; Latin lexical history neither derives nor proves the formal results.

The [complete Investigation and Faith aphorism](../../../symbolon/episteme/aphorisms/investigation-and-faith.md#complete-aphorism) **returns-to** the authorial relation carried through A03 and A23: investigation makes determinations; [faith consciously continues through their non-exhaustion of ground](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md#trust-keeps-the-return-route-active). **Mathematical anchor:** undecidability under stated formal conditions. **Images:** the ladder, the re-entering mark, the polygon approaching the circle, the sphere with centre everywhere and circumference nowhere. The positive response is [[05-s01-p4-gebser-diaphaneity|§0/1 · #4 — Gebserian Diaphaneity]].
