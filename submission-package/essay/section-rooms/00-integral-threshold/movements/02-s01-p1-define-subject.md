# §0/1 · #1 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object

## Claim
The Subject is the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event: the opening through which a determinate world is given. Every definition of it is made by an identifying activity, and that activity cannot be one of the things it identifies. Treating the condition as a hidden substance inside the world would turn it into another object and repeat the error under examination, so the definition can only be enacted, and it fails in a particular and instructive way: each attempt to capture the knower produces one more describable relation and leaves the knower where it was.

## Warrant
Identification begins in counting. Two parents who argue over a name pass the baby between them and both know the same child has come back into their arms, so beneath the name lies a count, *this one* and *this one again*. Kripke's rigid designator, a term that picks out the same individual in every counterfactual situation in which that individual exists, works only because something has already picked out the one to be held. Naming therefore needs three things together: a name, which articulates; an instance, which holds what is identified; and a circumstance, which makes the relevant sameness and difference intelligible. A pair of dice shows what the circumstance does. Tracked by which die shows which face, the pair has thirty-six states; drop the labels and the same throws are twenty-one outcomes; keep the sum and they are eleven. Each count answers a different question, and the distinctions an account keeps decide what its number can say.

The copula does the same double work, joining a determination to what was encountered. Frege asked why "the morning star is the evening star" informs where "a = a" cannot, and answered that two names present one planet in two ways, so an identity is informative across a difference of presentation. Written with the simplest marks, two dashes with an oblique stroke between them, `-/-`, the equals sign is two parallel strokes, and drawing the cut through it gives the sign for non-equality. Equality and non-equality are therefore related by a stroke that both separates and joins them, `=/≠`, and read as AND/OR it holds identity as what is kept and difference as what lets anything be articulated. Four articulated appearances (the two poles and the two faces of the middle) composed by two operations come to `4+2`, and the account has had to include its own identifying act to give itself. Any definition of the Subject meets the same demand.

The sentence "I see the world" shows where the demand lands. It looks like an *I* and a *world*, and it contains a knower, what is known, and the seeing through which the two become available in relation. Turn it on itself, "I am aware of seeing these words", and the seeing, which was simply how the page was there, becomes one more thing presented to me. A painter's self-portraits make the point: a second canvas that includes the painter painting the first discloses more of the activity and still leaves the present seeing in which both canvases are met. Descartes performed the turn in 1641, and his doubt stops at its own performance, since being deceived is still occurring, and the further determination of what the *I* is, a thinking thing, is a second and weaker achievement. Watching thought changes my relation to it without manufacturing the knowing in which the change shows; otherwise every watcher would need a watcher. Lacan's *glissement* and Derrida's *différance* name the sliding of the signified beneath the signifier, and the quest for the Subject slides in the same way beneath neural correlate, pneuma or noun. *Pratyabhijñā*, recognition, changes the mode of identification, where reflection had only changed the image.

Śaiva epistemology names the three moments *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa* and *prameya*. *Prameya* is the definitive pole of what can be made known; *pramātṛ* is the indefinitive pole present in knowing and never exhausted as another known; *pramāṇa* carries their asymmetric relation. The familiar subject/object pair is therefore a compressed triad, and it hides the seeing, thinking, measuring or valuing through which knower and known become available together. The later essay names these offices **Subjective Immediacy**, **Objective Internality** and **World**, held within **Life / Mind** as their whole relation. At this threshold the point is that the means is constitutive without being the Subject, and the known world is real without disclosing itself apart from the relation through which it is known.

## Tension / limit
"Non-objectifiable" sets a discipline of inquiry: relations and effects remain describable, and the pole cannot be captured as an occult object or exempted from scrutiny. What a particular means consists in is the work of §0, which earns that positive anatomy and does not presuppose it here.

[the Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-asymmetric-count) **figures** this limit in the opening exchange: a question about the source of the numbering receives the speaker's assigned number as its answer. The first-person refusal keeps the source-question distinct from the predicate through which the person has been identified, and the narrative's return of a concealed Number One leaves the non-objectifiable Subject unreplaced.
