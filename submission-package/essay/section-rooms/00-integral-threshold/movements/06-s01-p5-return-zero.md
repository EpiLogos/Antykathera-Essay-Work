## Claim
Zero first enters as a promissory meta-sign for the condition a system cannot count as one more object, and the first notation it earns is `0/1`. Mathematics and linguistic meaning are one at bottom, because both rest on identifying through difference: a name holds its bearer through every change of appearance, and a count holds its unit through each distinguishable instance. The formal-limit sequence has established a recurrent remainder without claiming that mathematical zero, emptiness, consciousness and subjectivity are identical, and `0` is the sign this section proposes for what that remainder has in common across them.

## Warrant
Artificial intelligence has made the unity of number and meaning a working fact: a process made of arithmetic over learned numbers reconstructs enough linguistic and conceptual intelligence to enter an argument, and the Transformer's attention, in which every position compares a query with every key and mixes values by softmax weights, makes context itself a calculation. Meaning was never independent of relations that can be counted. The correspondence between Jung and Pauli is the richest witness to the same question. Pauli's exclusion principle determines each quantum state by exclusion, Heisenberg's uncertainty and Bohr's complementarity show descriptions that exclude one another within any single arrangement and are both required, and Jung's psychology has no Archimedean point outside its object, so every experience is a psychic fact before any question of cause is settled. Where the physicist found complementary descriptions held around an observer who cannot step outside them, the psychologist found opposites held around the empty centre of the mandala. Both located the meeting point in number: Pauli proposed *homo-usia* between the archetypal world and physis, expressible only in concepts neutral between psyche and physis, and mathematical concepts are such concepts. QL is the inventory Jung asked for, begun where his letter could not begin, at zero, and its derivation is the essay's own.

Logos names the relation by which the world is intelligible as belonging to the world's own activity, and it widens to **Code** by way of script, the account written down so that it can run without its author. Conditioning is scripting. Apoha shows a term made determinate by what it excludes, *vikalpa* is the mind's construction of such terms, and a script turns dangerous when an achieved selection forgets its formation and the conditioned self speaks as though it were the uncreated one, which is frozen conditioned will. The Śaiva power that sets up such a world is Māyā, read as operative. Read through the symbolon, one occurrence then holds three offices apart: Subjective Immediacy is the knower, *pramātṛ*; Objective Internality is the means, *pramāṇa*, which is what the essay calls Life and Mind; and World is the known, *prameya*. Written as `0 / 1`, with the slash as Objective Internality, the whole of the three is I-Consciousness, and one word, *consciousness*, has been made to carry all four, which is why accounts of it talk past one another.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm's implicate order]] supplies the process bridge into §0. The remainder becomes the enfolded whole active in each local unfolding, which gives the zero-space a generative role an inert blank could never perform. The next station gives this implicated whole its differentiating anatomy through the tattvas.

## Tension / limit
The proposal is positional: its historical and mathematical warrants are deferred to §1, and its present force comes from the formal-limit derivation together with the convergence of the Jung–Pauli correspondence and the machine case on number as the common medium.

## Anchor and transition
