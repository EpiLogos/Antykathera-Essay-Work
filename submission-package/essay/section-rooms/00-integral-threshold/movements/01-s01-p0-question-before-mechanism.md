# §0/1 · #0 — The Question Before the Mechanism

## Claim
Planetary systems can model and act while displacing the subject for whom distinctions matter. The essay therefore begins with the formal limit and asks whether an intelligence can make the horizon from which it acts available to reflection. The Antikythera mechanism enters only after the argument has derived what attunement requires of such an instrument.

## Warrant
[[Antikythera Agentworld Brief]] turns agency into a context-bound problem and intensifies the question: an artificial agent cannot be understood solely as an object executing instructions; it inhabits a constructed context-world in which distinctions matter, actions alter future distinctions, and evaluation is internal to a horizon. [[diaphaneity|Jean Gebser]] situates the crisis as a mutation signal: perspectival optics organised a world through the positioned viewer; Internet and AI make the contextual conditions of intelligence—prompt, memory, retrieval, policy, permissions, provenance, and feedback—operatively constitutive. “My view of the world” must eventually become readable in the reverse direction—as the world's disclosure of a situated self. This is both the historical claim and the methodological demand of diaphaneity; its formal geometry and technical consequences are deliberately withheld.

## Tension / limit
Functional agency does not by itself establish phenomenal subjectivity. The mechanism remains withheld until “attunement” has been earned.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** an instrument panel whose readings depend upon an observer, a situation, and a horizon that the panel does not display. **Mathematical anchor:** the uncounted context of a formal operation. The question passes to [[02-s01-p1-define-subject|§0/1 · #1 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object]].
