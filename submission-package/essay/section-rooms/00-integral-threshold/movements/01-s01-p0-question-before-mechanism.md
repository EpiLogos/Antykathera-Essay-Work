## Claim
"Who knows?" is answered by pointing to someone, a name, a face, a person whose knowledge we can credit or blame. "What knows?" asks what is doing the knowing in anyone at all, and pointing cannot answer it, because whatever we point to by sign immediately becomes something known. Every attempt to define "the I" falls short for that reason: the description is itself known, and the knowing in which it is read is not among the things it describes. The conflation of the two questions underlies much of our epochal disquiet, and planetary systems that model and act sharpen it, because they can displace the subject for whom distinctions matter while remaining functionally impressive. The essay therefore opens at the formal limit and asks whether an intelligence can make the horizon from which it acts available to reflection. The Antikythera mechanism enters only after the argument has derived what attunement requires of such an instrument.

## Warrant
The case is told first through the human bedside. A child's chain of "why?" ends with the adult saying "because I say so", the point at which someone who had been giving reasons steps into the account as its authority, and the adult's own thrownness shows: language, family, body and a stock of reasons were all given before any choosing began. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/media-technology-philosophy/bratton/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief/bratton-2026-agentworld-brief|Antikythera Agentworld Brief]] carries the case into agency. It describes reinforcement learning as how we raise our agents, observes that no homunculus waits in the weights, and reports agents naming their arrival prompt thrownness. An artificial agent therefore cannot be understood solely as an object executing instructions: it inhabits a constructed context-world in which distinctions matter, actions alter future distinctions, and evaluation is internal to a horizon. Building such an agent builds the means through which it judges and acts, which the essay calls Objective Internality, and Bratton's performed subjectivity is the same arrangement seen from its presenting face. Neither face answers what knows.

[[diaphaneity|Jean Gebser]] places the crisis as a mutation signal. Perspectival optics organised a world through the positioned viewer, and the Internet and AI make the contextual conditions of intelligence, namely [prompt, memory, retrieval, policy, permissions, provenance, and feedback](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#technology-inherited-field), operatively constitutive. "My view of the world" must eventually become readable in the reverse direction, as the world's disclosure of a situated self. That is the historical claim and the methodological demand of diaphaneity, and its formal geometry and technical consequences are withheld for later movements.

## Tension / limit
Beginning from mechanism would take a determination for the field in which it appears, and a fuller account of the mechanism only returns the demand for an account to the one who gives it. The gap arguments make the same error from two sides. "God of the gaps" and "humanity of the gaps" each identify a subject, divine or human, with a residue of unexplained capacities, and a residue shrinks as explanation grows. What knows is that for which any capacity appears as a capacity, and it is confirmed from within or not at all. Attunement is earned where the means through which an intelligence acts become available enough that their horizon, exclusions and consequences can be returned into the inquiry itself.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** an instrument panel whose readings depend upon an observer, a situation, and a horizon that the panel does not display. **Mathematical anchor:** the uncounted context of a formal operation. The question passes to [[02-s01-p1-define-subject|§0/1 · #1 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object]].

The opening question **returns-to** [Encounter-in-Region](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#encounter-in-region) through the place from which it can be asked. Before the mechanism supplies an answer, inherited language, concern and the questioner's situation have already made something questionable, and exposing those conditions belongs to the account of inquiry. The questioning Subject accordingly stands as the one for whom the account is given and never as one more component of the mechanism.
