# §5→0 · #3 — 4:2 Technē and the Sovereign Commons

## Claim
[[42-techne-2026-sovereign-commons|4-2 Techne — Sovereign Commons]] realises a mono–poly architecture: one common field strong enough to carry shared protocols, memory, consequence and collective work, composed with locally governed centres strong enough to retain material and **epistemic sovereignty**.

The epistemic term has now acquired a precise essay-native office. A sovereign centre does not merely own data or maintain a private world-model. It must be able to constitute, inspect, govern and revise the reference conditions by which claims and actions become assessable there. [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|The Bimba Map]] is the Offered §5 architecture for that office: a local reference/original field of sources, provisional world-objects, provenance, lenses, gauges, exclusions and transformations against which situated agency appears as Pratibimba.

The resulting commons is therefore a federation of **epistemic grounds**, not a merger of world-models into one superior map.

## Warrant
The local 4:2 Technē source is explicit about decentralised/community-led infrastructure, local governance, memory and epistemic sovereignty. The next step is a developed essay relation rather than a quotation from that source: §5 gives epistemic sovereignty technical form by making the local ground of determination first-class in Bimba. [[Mono-Poly: Whole and Many|Mono-Poly Planetary Intelligence]] then supplies the whole/many relation through which such grounds can compose without surrendering the local source-office.

```text
                 shared commons
      protocol · memory · provenance · consequence
          translation · reciprocal legibility
              /          |          \
        Bimba A      Bimba B      Bimba C
      local ground  local ground  local ground
          ↓              ↓             ↓
     Pratibimba      Pratibimba     Pratibimba
          ↺              ↺             ↺
             reciprocal Return
```

The common layer can carry objects, relations, obligations and translations across these fields, but it does not become a master Bimba standing above them. A determination crossing from one field to another must retain enough provenance to disclose which sources, gauges, lenses, permissions and local meanings made it intelligible in its originating context. Reciprocal legibility therefore means the ability to locate and contest a determination across epistemic grounds, not the requirement that every ground become the same ontology.

This gives Objective Co-Internality an institutional body. Each centre remains capable of an internally real, locally governed field of determination; those fields nevertheless condition one another through shared infrastructures, decisions, agents and consequences. The commons is real precisely because effects cross the local boundaries. Sovereignty is real precisely because the crossing does not erase the authority and responsibility of the local field to say how those effects are read, admitted, refused and returned.

Mono–poly supplies the political depth. Authority can accumulate upward while its material and epistemic conditions run downward through labour, communities, maintainers, data, energy, ecology, memory and inherited Logos. Sovereign-commons governance makes those dependencies visible, reciprocal and answerable to the people and contexts that sustain the shared layer. [[Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]] remains distinct: an inspectable Bimba field can make grounds answerable without manufacturing the surety by which another participant entrusts itself to the relation.

This is the institutional praxis of divining the third. It gives the hidden `0/1 ↔ 1/0` relation durable procedures: the governed can inspect and contest the conditions under which an institution acts; an institution can return authority, data, benefit and explanation toward the people and material worlds that sustain it. Because the local Bimba office is recursively constituted rather than absolutely possessed, Return can reach beyond correction of a Pratibimba and require revision of the governing reference field itself. A commons in which the local epistemic ground cannot be revised has converted sovereignty into a Protected Account.

## Tension / limit
Mono without poly becomes platform monopoly; poly without mono becomes incompatibility and fractal conflict. Epistemically, those failures become more exact:

```text
one master Bimba / evaluator
→ local grounds become mere Pratibimba of the platform
→ commonality becomes epistemic capture

isolated local Bimbas without translation or common consequence
→ disagreement cannot become shared evidence or obligation
→ sovereignty becomes non-relation
```

The viable interval requires locally governed reference fields **and** crossings through which provenance, contradiction, action and consequence can return. Local Bimba does not mean local infallibility; its sovereignty includes responsibility for admitting the evidence by which its own ground may need revision.

[[Hephaestus's Net: The Failed Relation]] remains the offered test against a coordination layer that mistakes capture for relation, and the test carries two sharpened criteria from §2 · #3. A coordination layer fails as **counterfeit gathering** when its wholeness is only the sum of its orderings — a syn-shaped enclosure running in dia mode, gathering participants precisely by arresting them. It fails the **surety criterion** when observability cannot conclude in relation: in the Homeric scene the completed apparatus of detection, capture, proof and judgment still cannot end the episode; release enters only with Poseidon's pledge, "I will myself pay thee" (Od. 8.355–56), a person standing-for another at risk of loss. A commons passes only if its coordination can conclude — contest, revision, release, return — and only if the pledges underwriting its metrics remain visible and contestable rather than priced and hidden.

The [[Dushun (attributed) — The Jewel Net of Indra (Cleary, 2000)|Huayan jewel net]] supplies recursive mutual reflection, and [[Van Norden and Jones — Huayan Buddhism (SEP, 2024)|Van Norden and Jones]] show why that inclusion retains the distinction of its members. The sovereign-commons reading develops this into mutually answerable epistemic centres; Hephaestus's bonds name the enclosure in which visibility makes every centre capturable.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** a mesh of locally grounded Bimba fields with reversible crossings: common enough for shared consequence, sovereign enough for difference to remain a source of correction. The architecture opens the cosmological horizon in [[47-s50-p4-idealism-horizon|§5→0 · #4 — Idealism as Horizon]] as an **Offered** inference; its proof burden lies with the idealist and Śaiva arguments developed there.
