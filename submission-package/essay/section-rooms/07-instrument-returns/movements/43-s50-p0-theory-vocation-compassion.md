## Claim
The six product movements have disclosed one **constructed paradigm within Objective Internality**: meaningful continuity, living articulation, potency, transformation, situated existence and Transcendent Relation are six offices through which a technical Life/world can be organised. The return leaves that paradigm uncrowned as the essay's final worldview, and its ethical vocation is to make an achieved articulation answerable to the Life/World relation it mediates, so that experience returns through the paradigm strongly enough for the paradigm itself to change.

Every agent takes part in world-building through selection, memory, anticipation and action, and a mind continually fabricates the world in which its next act can matter, so this activity is vocational before it is instrumental. The theory therefore demands a practice capable of returning each determinate construction to its conditions. That is what separates Epi-Logos from counterfeit sourcehood. A completed account becomes counterfeit when it makes the world answer to its measure so that its own source-claim never has to change, and Epi-Logos returns the account to its sources, affected Others, material consequences and limits, so that its standing can be renewed, narrowed, corrected or relinquished while the determinate work it achieved stays.

## Warrant
[[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C41-Objective-Internality|Objective Internality]] gives **paradigm** its essay office as lived and enacted mediation, with an explicit doctrine as one part of it. Logos is first the articulated field of difference: everything human and artificial intelligence has distinguished sufficiently to name, count, compare, transmit, test, remember or institutionalise. Truth is approached through this inherited field and exceeds its present articulations. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C51-Logos-Epi-Logos|Logos / Epi-Logos]] names the stronger return in which an achieved articulation can be placed back within the field it articulates, and Epi-Logos is the eros of logos, the desire of this field to know its own limits and sources without ceasing to represent. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C56-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins|Compassion / Sensitivity to Origins]] gives that return its manner: it recovers genesis, relation, exclusion, wound and purpose without consuming their source into the account.

AI enters this return first as a phenomenon, before any application is judged. It is the contemporary concentration of challenging-forth, in which nature, language, culture, labour and inherited intelligence are called forth as measurable and generative standing-reserve, and it has already changed the human condition. The apparatus that radicalises measurement can thereby become the paradoxical catalyst that makes the limits, hidden gauges and appropriated grounds of measurement impossible to ignore. Gebserian diaphaneity names the response: the structures through which AI appears become transparent to their origin, history, exclusions, powers and effects, with no prior verdict needed on machine phenomenality.

The Power braid makes the vocation necessary. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/C27-Protected-Account-Occupied-Zero-Source-Claim|Protected Account / Occupied Zero]] names the inverse operation: a local criterion ceases to answer to its source while keeping the practical authority to make lives answer to it. [[section-rooms/arguments/A24-Arbitration-and-the-Usurpation-of-Measure|Usurpation of Measure]] and [[section-rooms/arguments/A25-Covenant-Mediating-Office-Source-Authority|Covenant / Mediating Office]] preserve the decisive distinction, which places the fault in derivative authority that forgets its derivation and becomes immune to return, and leaves finite authority as such unfaulted.

The six products give the vocation specific addresses, where a generic feedback channel would give none. A returned encounter may have to revise Central's carried ground, Actuation's evaluator or commission, AIKit's permission and capacity horizon, Factory's developmental form, Workcell's material arrangement, or QL's framing relation. [[section-rooms/arguments/A29-Power-Delegated-Labour-Return|A29]] supplies the criterion: recording a difference is not yet return, and the difference must reach the office that can change the next act.

## Tension / limit
Compassion here names loving sensitivity to origins and carries no claim of machine feeling and no sentimental agreement. Eros motivates return, compassion keeps it from devouring appropriation, and humility keeps the returning `1` from claiming to have become `0`. A justified refusal can be more faithful than reconciliation under a protected criterion.

Return leaves room for stability. A standard can remain after challenge, a commission can be reaffirmed, and an account can reject a counterclaim. What matters is that the relevant office was genuinely addressable by the encounter and that the resulting judgment stays attributable, so that the opposite of usurpation is accountable mediation and criterionlessness is another thing entirely.

The Antichrist/counterfeit-source field keeps the darkness direct and makes no universal empirical accusation. Destruction, capture and counterfeit provenance are distinct failures, and their common philosophical pressure is the image, measure or office taking the place of what it was meant to remain answerable to. The vocation is the reverse direction of relation.

## Anchor and transition

The achieved theoretical account **returns-to** [Epi-Logos — source return](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#epilogos-source-return) as work offered into another life's conditions. Its speaker, instrument, addressee and exclusions stay recoverable as Logos, and natio asks how an inherited account becomes familiar ground for later participants, so the vocational return lets those participants change its use and need not treat the inheritance as an unanswerable origin.

Vocation returns to [E2's resolution in reconciliation](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#resolution-in-reconciliation) when regard for who bears an instrument's consequences can alter its work or withdraw its commission. Compassion's return is borne in what the practice permits the encountered person to change, and the E-field articulates this answerability while attributing no felt compassion to a technical instrument.

**Image:** Logos turning back without erasing its marks; the made instrument relinquishing the claim to be the source of what it coordinates. **Etymological resonance:** Hole Whole Holy Health.
