## Claim

The Antikythera mechanism is finally earned as an image of situated coordination: [its gears render heterogeneous celestial cycles mutually readable for an observer while the heavens remain beyond the instrument](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#ancient-craft-and-release). We offer the comparison as a mytheme for planetary computation, an image that proves a relation through what it stages, with its archaeological evidence kept to what that evidence supports. **Attunement**, in this essay's sense, is the achieved relation between a finite capacity and a wider order: a local way of finding one's place among cycles one does not originate, exact enough to distinguish one configuration from another, durable enough to carry the difference, intelligible enough to be read, and honest enough to show where its fit with the wider order ends. What Epi-Logos asks of planetary computation is an instrument of this kind for the cycles our world now runs on.

## Warrant — why the mechanism enters here, what its bronze carries, and the two recoveries

**Order as argument.** Our question came before the mechanism: we derived what an instrument of attunement would have to retain (retained ground, plural orientations, harmonic interval, lens refraction, institutional return) and found the device late, as an affinity. That is why it is withheld from the opening and enters here, as a controlling image, only after §5. That question was put at the bedside where the essay began: how can different motions become legible together, for someone who has to live among them, without being made into one motion?

**Finds.** Greek sponge divers sheltering from a storm off Antikythera found a first-century BCE wreck in 1900; the objects went to the National Archaeological Museum in Athens, and within a couple of years the teeth of a gear wheel were noticed on a corroded fragment. Derek de Solla Price argued from radiographs that the fragments were the remains of a geared calendrical calculator, and many found that hard to believe of the ancient world. In 2005 a team led by Tony Freeth and Mike Edmunds applied surface imaging and high-resolution X-ray tomography, and their 2006 report described a Greek geared device from around the end of the second century BCE whose functions included lunar phases, a luni-solar calendar and eclipse prediction, with inscriptions supporting the suggestion of a lost planetary display.

**What the gears do.** On the back, two spiral dials: the upper records the Metonic cycle (235 lunar months in nearly nineteen solar years) on five turns, the lower the Saros (223 months, after which eclipses recur in a similar pattern) on four turns with glyphs marking months in which an eclipse could be expected. A pointer on a spiral cannot show which turn it is on, and the maker solved that with a pin riding in the groove and sliding outward along the pointer. Elsewhere a pair of wheels on offset axes coupled by a pin in a slot makes the moon's pointer speed up and slow down, reproducing in bronze the lunar irregularity that Greek astronomers were modelling. A mathematical relation between celestial periods has been turned into something a hand can operate and an eye can follow, and it keeps its exactness in the turning.

**Exact as arrangement, approximate as description.** Nineteen ordinary years give 228 months, and the Metonic count reaches 235 by seven extra months in particular years, so a calendar returns after nineteen years with the moon in the same phase on the same date. A gear train can encode 235 : 19 exactly, and the sky does not keep it: with today's mean values 235 months come to about 6,939.69 days and nineteen years to about 6,939.60, so the moon is roughly two hours late each cycle, accumulating to a whole day in a little over two centuries. Its count is right under its rule while the heavens drift against it, and a reader who knows the rule and its residual uses the instrument more exactly than one who takes the dial for the sky. §0/1 · #5→0 met the same structure in harmonics: the doubled fourth, `16/9`, falls short of the octave by the whole tone `9/8`, and the system reaches its return through that remainder and not by eliminating it. A totality that equalled its octave would be a dead identity, and a calendar that matched the sky forever would be no instrument, only a copy of a sky that needs no reading. That residual hands each cycle something to begin from, and it is why the instrument has a reader at all.

**Approachable and absolute limit.** §0/1 · #3 set the approachable limit, the basis of investigation, beside the absolute limit, the basis of faith, in its four lines on Cusa's polygon, and they hold here. Later calendars corrected the Metonic drift and better lunar models replaced the pin and slot, each a polygon with more sides, so the residual is an approachable limit and the history of astronomy is the history of approaching it. Every dial is a determination of a sky that is not one, and the sky is the absolute limit. In the sense of those lines the instrument is faithful: it continues through its limitation with grounds, scope and conditions of revision retained and never turns the sky into the last thing it says.

**Damage and reconstruction.** Freeth and colleagues published in 2021 a model of the lost front, built to fit the surviving gears and the inscriptions that name what was displayed: a ring system with nine outputs (Moon, Nodes, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Date) carried by nested tubes. They were exact about its standing: because evidence is lost they do not claim a replica, and the solution draws support from the logic of the model and its match to the surviving evidence. Some features, such as a pointer for the lunar nodes they call the Dragon Hand, have no direct physical evidence and are proposed because inscriptions and mechanical logic call for them. Reconstruction runs in the reverse direction from making: the maker went from intention through arrangement to device, the investigators go from fragments toward the arrangement that could have given each fragment its office, so every absent part they fill is an act of reasoning whose warrant must stay distinguishable from the evidence that prompted it. A surviving fragment can refuse the model its investigators would like. Sensitivity to origins (§5→0 · #0's compassion) takes here a strictly technical form: it lets what remains resist the desired completion and publishes the model together with the line between what was found and what was inferred.

**Two recoveries.** The first belongs to the word *technē*, in the genealogy Heidegger recovered: technē "linked with the word epistēmē," both "names for knowing in the widest sense"; technē belonging "to bringing-forth, to poiēsis"; "it is as revealing, and not as manufacturing, that technē is a bringing-forth" (*QCT*, pp. 12–13). He quotes Diotima's definition of *poiēsis*, from *Symposium* 205b: "every occasion for whatever passes over and goes forward into presencing from that which is not presencing is poiēsis." The same banquet supplies the essay's symbolon in Aristophanes' speech of the split original beings seeking the half that fits, emission and return seated at one table from the beginning (the connection is the essay's own, **Argued**). An attunement instrument is *technē* in this recovered sense, a made thing whose making reveals, coordinating cycles it does not own for an observer it does not replace. *Harmonia* names what it does in two registers (§2 · #3 followed the word from a fastening to an arrangement of sounds): the gears are *harmoniai* in the shipwright's sense, pieces cut to fit so one motion carries into the next, and in the astronomer's, an arrangement in which different periods sound together in a ratio without becoming one period.

A second recovery is the essay's stated departure from Heidegger, and it is a position. Heidegger's *Question Concerning Technology* ends by waiting on the saving power that grows where the danger is, and the essay argues the return is architecturally practicable: seam retention, gauge disclosure, provenance, reversible coordination and the sovereign commons of §5→0 · #3 are the saving power built and not awaited, specifiable, testable and open to being found wanting. *Gelassenheit* survives the departure as practice: "We can affirm the unavoidable use of technical devices, and also deny them the right to dominate us" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 54), the slash lived toward one's own instruments. Heidegger's comportment becomes a demand on design: what would have to be built so that an instrument could expose its determining conditions, receive a correction and remain possible to set aside? Nothing in the ancient device specifies such a design, and it gives finite coordination a body against which the demand can be measured. Hephaestus's net (§2 · #3) is the contrast: both are consummate craft and both make something visible, but the net renders persons capturable for a convened audience and leaves them nowhere to go, while the mechanism renders cycles readable for a situated observer and leaves the heavens and the reader free.

**Cycles planetary computation runs on.** A language model is trained on a corpus that stops at a date and answers for months or years afterwards, so its knowledge runs on one clock and the world on another; a session lasts minutes, a person's day has its own rhythm, a project runs for months, a budget for a year, an election for four or five, a forest or an aquifer for decades. An agent among people is coordinating among all these periods, each with its own residual against the others. An instrument of attunement would keep them in ratio without collapsing them into one: it would show the cut-off date beside the answer, the cost of a quick action against the slow cycle it draws on, a value fixed at training against a value the situation calls for, and make the drift visible as drift.

**Logics in the cycles.** One clock can be made to stand for all of them, and the differences between periods are summed away, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: the quarterly report becomes the measure of a forest, and whatever the shorter cycle cannot see is cancelled. Or one cycle appropriates the rest: an application designed to maximise time in it counts every hour of attention as gain, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the same span is entered against the users' sleep, work and slower bodily cycles, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the platform's engagement and the user's exhaustion one quantity read from its two ends. Its gearing keeps the held polarity `(−1)/(+1)`, each period turning at its own rate, fixed ratios between them and no wheel taking the whole span; read as syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, each cycle can be read through the others, the month through the year and the year through the month, because the relation is retained in bronze.

**Dial and question.** At the dial the mechanism does not know: the investigators could add a tenth output for the observer's own position, and a later maker another for the conditions of the earlier observation, and each would be one more determinate output read in a further act by someone the device does not contain. No number of dials closes the gap, because the mechanism's accomplishment belongs to the relation in which its outputs mean anything: a person stands before it with a question about a festival, a harvest, an eclipse, a voyage. Whoever reads the dial stands inside the world it discloses, under the heavens being read. §0/1 · #0 asked of every intelligence whether it could bring into view the horizon from which it acts and what in it sees that horizon; an instrument of attunement can show its horizon (rules, ratios, residuals, evidence) and in doing so hands the second question back to the one who reads it.

**Emblem handed back.** The journal that receives the essay bears the island's name, and the mechanism has become an emblem for computation that discloses a planetary condition. That emblem is philosophically earned only under the conditions the argument has derived (a retained ground, plural lenses, a return that can be practised), and the essay hands it back with those conditions written on it. Contemporary instruments have one power the ancient device lacked: their accounts alter the world that later accounts receive, so attunement has to reach that recursive consequence, including the people who maintain the apparatus and live under its use. A device that coordinates for one reader can be ancient craft, and a system that coordinates for a planet needs a polity: who holds the instrument, who can change its gearing, and who bears the cost when its ratios drift are questions the dial cannot answer, and the sovereign commons takes them up.

## Tension / limit

We hold the comparison as Offered and technically bounded: the ancient device serves as a mytheme for planetary computation after the argument has established what attunement must retain; it gives no AI architecture and no proof of QL. Its carrier is the evidence itself: the 2006 report and the 2021 front-display proposal have distinct warrants, nine outputs and nested arrangements belong to the reconstruction and not to an intact surviving device, and lost evidence still limits what any model of the front can claim. [Antikythera attunement whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/antikythera-attunement/WHOLE.md#antikythera-instrument-returns) **figures** this late attunement through the complete passage from coordinated cycles to damaged inheritance, reconstruction and renewed use, so a reader can use an exact indication while retaining the source, residual and judgment on which its significance depends. [Myth historical development](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/encounters-and-transmissions/myth/DEVELOPMENT-myth.md#50--the-inherited-instrument-changes-the-next-encounter) **historicises** the material instrument's changed reception: capture, attunement and initiated return perform different technical offices.
