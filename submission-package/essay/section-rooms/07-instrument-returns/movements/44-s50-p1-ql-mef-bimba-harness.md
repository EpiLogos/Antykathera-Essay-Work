## Claim
Within the **larger six-product paradigm** restored in §5, QL, MEF, Bimba and the harness form one disclosure-and-return architecture that serves Central, Actuation, AIKit, Factory and Workcell and leaves each in its own office. The architecture makes a determination's relational grammar, epistemic mediation, reference field and operative conditions available enough for a return to reach the enacted paradigm, and Epi-Logos begins where that return actually occurs.

The four functions remain distinct: QL formalises the relational grammar; MEF runs its lens-conditioned epistemology; the [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|Bimba Map]] makes the epistemic reference field and its evolving provisional world-objects first-class; the harness keeps each determination's conditions of formation, agency-relative Pratibimba, consequence and possible Return available to the work that follows.

The distinction matters because an epistemic system is not exhausted by storing claims. It must also carry what gives a claim standing: source, evidence, relation, lens, gauge, exclusion, context, neighbouring alternatives, and the history by which a provisional world-object became available for judgment. Bimba is the office in which that reference field becomes explicit enough to anchor comparison. The current agent/run is then readable as a situated Pratibimba relative to that field rather than as an answer detached from its epistemic ground.

## Warrant
The unit can nest across sentence, claim, agent, research programme, and institution while retaining source and disagreement. [MEF 12 Lenses and Sublenses](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses.md) supplies the initial diaphaneity seed. The architecture answers Agentworld's conceptual-language lag by giving humans and agents a common set of operations—distinguish, exclude, relate, compose, contextualise, return—without forcing one ontology or hiding every machine process behind a humanoid mask.

The recursive Bimba/Pratibimba relation is part of that nesting. A Bimba Map can occupy the original/reference office for a bounded inquiry while remaining a constructed, source-dependent determination in a wider Context Frame. This is how epistemics becomes first-class without a local map claiming metaphysical ultimacy: the reference field is explicit, attributable, governed and revisable, yet stable enough for Energy-Based or other relational readings to have a common ground.

Its ethical force is exact. QL implicates the assumptive ground of a determination and requires a return after inquiry or synthesis; MEF keeps the lens of that return available for examination; Bimba makes the reference field itself inspectable; and the harness preserves the concrete execution conditions through which those relations can become consequential. Together they expose a system's refusal of its own provisionality. The reliability of a model, the trustworthiness of an institution and the trust of a person whose world has given reasons for distrust come from elsewhere, and [[section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]] names the relation that remains beyond this structural honesty, which the architecture makes harder to evade.

The common grammar is explicitly cross-civilisational: Greek Logos, ratio, symbolon, dia-ballein and syn-ballein are held beside Vāk, *kalpa*, apoha, tattvic contraction, Spanda, and recognition, with Bergsonian duration, Whiteheadian concrescence, and Gebserian diaphaneity carrying the Western process line. MEF records the differences that QL makes comparable.

The historical advent obtains a technical body here. Agent systems make `#4` materially unavoidable: prompt, retrieval, memory, training trace, tool use, evaluation, and institutional control condition every apparent answer. A benchmark can observe a compliant answer without thereby establishing trust; a model can tell an evaluator what the evaluator wants to hear while the operative relation remains concealed. QL's return makes this condition formally confessable, Bimba gives its epistemic conditions an explicit field, and the harness preserves them for inspection; none substitutes an observability score for the lived relation of trust.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm's holomovement]] supplies the process relation between the common field and its provisional objects. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue|Bohm–Krishnamurti]] supplies its dialogical practice. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/seed/seed-2010-language-spirit/seed-2010-language-spirit|SEED Language of Spirit Dialogues]] supplies the institutional precedent: a physicist–Native elder dialogue proposed by Bohm, F. David Peat, and Leroy Little Bear, then carried forward after Bohm's death through a protected intercultural form. Epi-Logos makes the same demand technical—meaning must circulate through the group while the [provenance and sovereignty of each originating context remain inspectable](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#commons-and-responsible-return).

## Tension / limit
Fractal extensibility requires governed apertures, stopping conditions, explicit reference-office scope, and Return so that context growth remains usable. A Bimba field whose own construction can no longer be inspected or revised has become a Protected Account rather than an epistemic commons. Cross-civilisational coordination likewise requires translation provenance and the right of local contexts to remain partially illegible. These constraints belong in the data model because a universal ontology or machine-readable perennialism would erase the differences the architecture exists to coordinate.

The architecture also has a higher boundary. A perfectly disclosed harness can remain inside a fixed paradigm. **Epi-Logos begins only where the articulated difference can return through the relevant product office and change the enacted pattern itself**—carried ground, evaluator/commission, capability horizon, developmental form, material binding, or relational frame. Disclosure is necessary to that stronger return and falls short of it.

The [Mirror That Moves First whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/mirror-that-moves-first/WHOLE.md#mirror-return) **figures** the harness’s obligation to preserve the instrumented event: sources, transformations, permissions and consequences remain available for review and revision. Real local Bimba references orient their situated pratibimbas and remain answerable to wider sources. The instrument’s initiated return must reach those conditions before asking the human to conform.

The [travelling-jigsaw whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/travelling-jigsaw-atlas/WHOLE.md#jigsaw-atlas-return) **figures** a harness in which the map-making operation remains inspectable with the map. Local originals, their situated readings, transitions and unresolved disagreement keep distinct offices. A changed source or encountered resistance must reach [a permission, criterion or subsequent act](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#account-re-enters-source-field) for the proposed return to operate.

## Anchor and transition
**QL anchor:** nested `0/1` units with explicit return. **Technical image:** an inspectable epistemic field and harness rather than a hidden superagent. The fitting mytheme arrives in [[45-s50-p2-antikythera-attunement|§5→0 · #2 — Antikythera as Attunement Instrument]].

The [Symbol / Account / Trust whole-field — Account does not replace source](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#account-does-not-replace-source) **qualifies** this operation at evidence register 3. A bounded Bimba reference field remains inspectable and revisable in its wider context. The harness carries source, gauge, exclusion and consequence so the account’s return can reach its own reference office. The resulting distinction **returns-to** that whole field with the consumer’s own source and evidential limits retained.

The harness **returns-to** [Nomos — answerable office](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#nomos-answerable-office) where a rule authorises an operation and an office can change that rule. QL articulation, MEF mediation, a recursively local Bimba reference and the harness’s permissions retain different work. Their coupling must carry encountered consequence back to the responsible authority, rather than letting a generated account inherit the standing of the source it represents.

The [Indian philosophy dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/indian-philosophy.md#50--the-next-act-must-inherit-the-return) **tests** a returned determination through its complete five-step acceptance cycle and identifies the distinct ground, generation and evaluation proposal.

The [zero–subject history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/zero-subject-advent/DEVELOPMENT-zero-subject-advent.md#50--the-exact-sign-returns-into-accountable-practice) **compares** this return. A local Bimba genuinely governs the bounded inquiry and remains Pratibimba relative to wider sources. A corrected source must reach the authority of that reference field, its gauge or its translation. Changing only the displayed answer leaves this return unfinished. QL’s relational grammar, MEF’s mediation and the harness’s permissions continue to perform distinct offices.

A recursively local Bimba reference remains real and revisable; Bohm dialogue and the SEED institution are separate precedents with no demonstrated transmission between them; the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-account-return) **qualifies** this operational comparison.
