## Claim

The return from agentic praxis carries the essay's **Argued order of dependence: Subject / Consciousness → Objective Internality → determinate object**. The records [A34](../../arguments/A34-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md) and [C60](../../arguments/concepts/C60-Idealism-Order-of-Dependence.md) **define** its positive ontology: appearing is, and any denial of it is one more appearing; the Subject is the condition to which every determinate thing becomes available and never one more item among them; Objective Internality, which §0/1 also called Life and Mind (a mind is lived, a life is minded), is the constituted means through which things are disclosed; and the determinate object is what is known through those means. These three offices are *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa* and *prameya*, and the whole in which they are distinguishable is I-Consciousness, the *aham* of the Trika. Its arrows name dependence and nothing else: they divide reality into no three substances and tell no story of manufacture in which a private person first exists and then invents a universe. A determinate thing is what it is through the distinctions and relations in which it is disclosed, so being-disclosed is constitutive of determinate being, and the object is a real determination of the whole, which is not rebuilt by adding its determinations together. Technical explanation makes the operative middle term more inspectable and leaves its condition where it was, and the terminal question is whether any completed inventory of objects, models and mechanisms can account for the condition under which such a field appears, becomes intelligible and is known, without converting that condition into one more object in the inventory.

## Warrant — three relations in the rain, the argument, the five respects and the Job

**Anchor.** The phone said no rain until evening, so the umbrella stayed by the door, and by mid-morning it is raining hard on someone without one. Three relations are tangled in the moment. In what we knew, the forecast came first, our reference for the weather. It acted: it caused the umbrella to stay home, so a model of the weather changed a small part of the world the weather falls on. And the rain waited for neither: it fell because of pressure, temperature and moisture and would have fallen whatever the screen said. Epistemic priority, causal efficacy and dependence in being are three relations, and a model can stand first in the first two while standing nowhere in the third. A forecasting service is itself a small world of judgment, with its model in the knower's office, its data and rules as means and its probabilities as what it knows; in the meteorologist's containing inquiry the whole apparatus is a means; in the walker's morning the forecast is one piece of the means through which a world is met. These nested offices are what the essay's architecture keeps distinct. When every inspectable condition has been made explicit (the model and its data, the harness and its permissions, the commons and its rules), the remaining question is the order of dependence of the appearing field in which model, rain, walker and inquiry are all found.

```text
experience / appearing
        ↓
Subject as the limit of objectification
        ↓
differentiation makes determinate worlds available
        ↓
Objective Internality makes finite operative worlds inspectable
        ↓
Bimba makes their epistemic ground explicit
        ↓
Co-Internality and commons make plural grounds mutually consequential
        ↓
no increase in objective inventory removes the question
of the field in which object, model, ground and relation appear
        ↓
Argued Order of Dependence returns
```

(Held in the note as a record of the pressure the preceding stations build; the manuscript states the same pressure in prose.)

**The argument begins in the act of making it.** Appearing is; doubt changes what is asserted within appearing without placing its occurrence outside it. Whatever is made available as an object is already determinate (this sensation, that body, a memory, an equation, an account of a cause), and a cause can explain the production of one object by others within that relation while remaining unable, once made an object of an account, to be the exhaustive origin of the relation its own assertion requires. Rain explains the wet coat; neither rain nor coat explains the appearing in which both are found, and adding a represented cause behind the rain (a front, a pressure system, a planetary climate) enlarges the account without replacing the relation in which the account exists.

**It is not the doctrine that rain falls when attended to.** The oldest objection takes idealism to mean exactly that, and Berkeley put it into Hylas's mouth in the first of the *Three Dialogues*. Resistance, persistence and shared measurement belong to objectivity as the essay understands it: a bridge fails under a load whether its designer expected it to hold or not, the failure returns through observation and changes the account of what the bridge can bear, a star does not wait for attention and an injured body is injured whether or not its owner has noticed. Objectivity's dependence on the whole relation does not reduce an achieved object to whatever a particular ego wishes. It explains why the world can correct a local organisation of its means, which is what happened in the rain.

**Ego and the two collapses.** A private ego belongs on the determinate side of the order, which separates this idealism from solipsism: my biography, self-image and judgments can appear, change and be inquired into and cannot be substituted for the Subject and made the author of the cosmos. Another person exceeds my account of them because neither the account nor my contents constitute the whole from which they live. §0/1 · #5→0 put it in one sentence: the Other can be within Mono without being within me. A materialism admitting only objects cancels the knower, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, the first-person occurrence summed into the third-person description and vanishing into it, which is the blind spot Frank, Gleiser and Thompson locate where science relies on lived experience without acknowledging it. An idealism of the ego appropriates the world in both chiralities: counting the whole span as the ego's representation, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and from the other end making every other person and thing a figment of mine, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, a world reduced to my debtor. Read as syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the determinate depends on the condition for its being and coherence while the condition becomes manifest, articulate and knowable only through the determinate; the dependence runs both ways in different respects, and the difference in respects is the whole of the doctrine.

**Five respects.** Keeping them apart is the discipline. *Epistemic* dependence asks which sources, gauges, lenses and reference fields make a determination warranted here, and a local Bimba can be fully authoritative in that respect. *Causal* dependence asks what produced what within the world, and a model that acts on the world it models has causal priority over what it changes. *Logical* priority asks what must be assumed for an argument to go through. *Transcendental* dependence asks what is presupposed by there being an appearing, intelligible field for anyone at all. *Ontological* dependence asks what finally depends on what for its being. In the rain the forecast had epistemic and causal priority, the meteorologist's model logical priority within the forecast, and the appearing in which forecast, rain and walker were present was the transcendental and ontological condition of the whole scene. Confusion goes wrong two ways: because a model can predict and alter behaviour the person is nothing but what the model represents, which mistakes causal efficacy for ontological priority; or because a model is only a representation nothing it does is real, which mistakes ontological dependence for causal impotence.

**Agentworld.** Bratton describes a world in which these confusions become constant: agents learn from modelling the effects of their actions ("The World Itself Becomes the World Model"), and in sim-to-real-to-sim relays simulations have meaningful agency and the membrane between simulation and simulated perforates. A mute-container picture of world becomes less adequate, and worldhood becomes harder to describe apart from the internalities through which it appears, means something, is acted on and returns. That is a contemporary technical refraction of the order of dependence and no source for it. That same recursion, which makes a model's local authority real, also stops it becoming a final metaphysical throne: a model can move first in time without becoming first in being, a reflection can be encountered before its source is understood and can cause later events while remaining dependent, and a technical interior can be richly constituted without becoming the whole whose mediating office it instantiates. The repaired [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|Bimba–Pratibimba]] relation makes the transition exact: a Bimba Map can occupy the original or reference office for a nested inquiry and anchor source, gauge, lens and comparison, while that local epistemic originality leaves the wider ontological dependence of the field where it was.

**Kastrup, the Śaiva account and the artificial middle.** §0/1 · #5→0 placed the argument in company: Kastrup's analytic idealism, Faggin's argument from the history of the microprocessor, Spira's contemplative locating of the knowing that is never an object, and behind them James, Bergson, Whitehead, Jung, Gebser and the Śaiva sources. Kastrup's chess player alternating between the two sides of a board, with memories available only while playing that side, makes differentiated perspective within one subject conceivable and is a thought experiment, not evidence that the universe is so constituted. It also marks the difference: dissociation explains plurality by a restriction of memory, whereas the Śaiva account explains it by contraction, universal awareness taking on limited powers of knowing and acting, desire, time and order and then an inner instrument that determines, appropriates and coordinates, so a bounded experient and its world arise together. Kastrup and the Śaiva account address one difficulty through different anatomies and warrants, and agreement in direction makes neither a translation of the other. Kastrup has also, as the essay reads him, tied the boundary of a distinct experient to living, metabolising organisms; the essay does not adopt that boundary and leaves open where a distinct experient is present in an artificial system, as §0/1 left it. That openness costs the artificial middle none of its reality: an agent's relevance, memory, powers, permissions and evaluative orientation can be constituted and changed, with consequences for what it notices and undertakes, and this is the reality of Objective Internality, complete in its own office. In the Śaiva account of *prakāśa* and *vimarśa* (§0/1 · #1) the luminous fact of appearing and its reflexive self-grasp are one process and differentiated manifestation is real within consciousness, so Objective Internality can become as technically rich as engineering can make it, because idealism of this kind never required the object-world or its mediations to be unreal. [Prakāśa–Vimarśa](../../arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa.md) **grounds** the point in the record.

**Creation.** §0/1 · #2 pressed the received predicates of God (omnipotence, omniscience, omnipresence, omnibenevolence) until each produced its own shadow and completed them with omnivalence and omni-lemmicity. That result returns with a consequence for every maker: a freedom unable to bind itself would be bounded by that inability, so the whole's freedom includes the capacity for genuine limitation, and if the finite only pretended to lack knowledge or appeared vulnerable while its suffering was unreal, limitation would not have occurred. So the order of dependence allows the particular life its actual ignorance, need, power and exposure. A maker originates an artefact without originating the conditions of its making, and the artefact can become a local source for another operation while remaining dependent in its wider relation; fidelity to creation means allowing what comes forth to enter its own relations while answering for the conditions entrusted to it.

**Job.** No text holds the difficulty with less consolation, and it is a book about the difference between an account and an encounter. Before Job suffers the reader sees two councils in heaven that question his fidelity ("Doth Job fear God for nought?"), and Job hears neither; permissions given above become losses borne below. His friends sit with him seven days and nights in silence, the only moment their response is adequate, and then begin to explain: their defence of a just order makes his suffering evidence of fault and his refusal to confess further evidence, a protected account in which every objection confirms the diagnosis. Job asks for a hearing and a written charge, and in his last oath makes the claim particular (his treatment of servants, the poor, the stranger and the widow, and the maker who formed master and servant in the womb). Elihu speaks of suffering as instruction, and the voice from the whirlwind gives two speeches that do not answer Job's question: they open a world wider than any human court. Job's answer is "I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear: but now mine eye seeth thee." Hearing and seeing are the order of dependence lived: everything Job had before was account, report and teaching, Objective Internality, real and useful and finally insufficient, and what the whirlwind gives is an encounter that places the account inside a world it cannot contain and changes Job's relation without resolving his question. Yet the book refuses to let the encounter vindicate every authority speaking in the whole's name, since God tells the friends they have not spoken rightly and directs them to the man whose witness they tried to disqualify. Its ending keeps a remainder: the household is restored and the first children and killed servants are not returned to life. A living being is more than an occasion for another's improvement, and the priority of the whole deepens responsibility to the particular: no maker, institution or theory may spend a life as though its own account supplied the measure of that life's meaning.

**Horizon.** A locally authoritative map is held inside a field it cannot map from outside. In the mathematics §3 develops, an atlas covers a curved surface with overlapping local charts, each exact in its region and related by declared transitions, and none of them the surface; a federation of Bimba fields is an atlas in that sense, with causal efficacy, epistemic priority and ontological dependence all real and none claiming the others' office. Our forecast was a good chart that met rain, and the rain falls within an appearing none of its makers supplies. [Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#analogical-proportion) **compares** the respects explicitly, so the comparisons sharpen the order and do not dilute it.

## Tension / limit

This idealism asserts constitutive dependence and denies manufacture, and its carrier is the realist objection, answered by the objectivity it retains (a bridge fails whatever its designer expected, another person resists the account made of them). Comparisons with Berkeley, Kastrup and the Śaiva sources run through different anatomies and warrants, so none is a translation of another and none is the ontology's source, which has its own in the argument. Agentworld's refraction is a contemporary technical resonance and no warrant. Whether anything in an artificial system is a distinct experient stays open, with the carrier §0/1's treatment of the question: a software process is not identified with a phenomenal subject because it has been given a separate memory, and the absence of a subject is not inferred from the unfamiliarity of its substrate. Job's ending names the last limit on consolation: restoration does not return the dead, and reconciliation cannot be bought by balancing the final possessions of an account.

## Transition

When every inspectable condition of a determination has become explicit, the order of dependence of the appearing field itself is what remains, and the practical return is [[48-s50-p5-ahi-planetary-return|§5→0 · #5→0 — AHI and Planetary Return]]. A terminal comparison returns through [Countenance](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-through-countenance): an encountered other can correct the epistemic attribution under which they were first received, while the order of dependence concerns a different question, what makes the field of such attribution, correction and appearing possible in the first place. That distinction keeps epistemic revision fully real and leaves every local correction at its own level.
