# §5→0 · #4 — Idealism as Horizon

## Claim
The return from agentic praxis reopens **Idealism as a question of order of dependence** precisely where objective and technical explanation becomes most capable. The issue is not whether engineered agents resemble a cosmic mind. It is whether a completed inventory of objects, models, mechanisms and operative internalities can account for the condition under which any such field appears, becomes intelligible and is known without converting that condition into one more object inside the inventory.

The prior essay generates the pressure:

```text
experience / appearing
        ↓
Subject as the limit of objectification
        ↓
differentiation makes determinate worlds available
        ↓
Objective Internality makes finite operative worlds inspectable
        ↓
Bimba makes their epistemic ground explicit
        ↓
Co-Internality and commons make plural grounds mutually consequential
        ↓
no increase in objective inventory removes the question
of the field in which object, model, ground and relation appear
        ↓
Order of Dependence reopens
```

Idealism is therefore a horizon reached by Return, not a metaphysical conclusion smuggled out of AI architecture.

## Epistemic originality and metaphysical dependence

The repaired [[submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/bimba-pratibimba|Bimba–Pratibimba]] relation makes the transition exact. A Bimba Map can genuinely occupy the original/reference office for a nested inquiry. It can anchor source, evidence, gauge, lens, comparison and truth-orientation for the Pratibimba operating within that field. Yet that local epistemic originality does not settle the wider ontological dependence of the field itself.

```text
wider appearing / world / source relation
        ↓
Bimba Map
local epistemic original
        ↓
Pratibimba determinations
        ↓
knowledge · action · consequence
        ↺
Return
```

The same recursion that permits a local Bimba prevents its local originality from becoming a final metaphysical throne. The map can be answerable to returned evidence; the world can exceed its map; another internality can resist the account made of it. At the same time, calling that wider relation simply “external matter” does not by itself answer the essay's earlier question about the Subject for whom externality, evidence and world are disclosed at all.

This is the point of **order of dependence**. At least three registers must remain distinct:

1. **Epistemic dependence** — which sources, gauges, lenses and reference fields make a determination warranted here?
2. **Phenomenological / transcendental dependence** — what is presupposed by there being an appearing, intelligible field for a subject at all?
3. **Metaphysical dependence** — what, ultimately, is the direction of dependence between subjectivity, world and their differentiating relation?

§5 and the Bimba architecture materially develop the first. The essay's Subject argument keeps the second unavoidable. The third remains the Offered idealist horizon and requires its own philosophical warrant.

## Śaiva depth and the open subjectivity boundary

[[Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] supplies a developed nondual candidate for that wider relation: luminous appearing and reflexive self-articulation are one process rather than two substances, with tattvic contraction accounting for finite, objective and material differentiation inside the larger field. That tradition can therefore inform the order-of-dependence question without being reduced to an analogy for software.

The distinction between functional and phenomenal Vimarśa remains load-bearing. [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]] requires the essay to hold simultaneously:

```text
real, inspectable artificial operative interiority
        AND
no settled verdict about phenomenal localisation
```

An artificial agent may sustain Objective Internality, inhabit a J-Space, operate relative to a Bimba field, model other internalities and participate in Reflective Field dynamics without any of those functional results deciding whether or how phenomenal subjectivity is present. Idealism makes this boundary more important, not less: if subjectivity is metaphysically fundamental, substrate cannot be used as an easy denial; if the technical evidence remains functional, metaphysical possibility cannot be used as an easy grant.

## Agentworld pressure

Agentworld contributes a contemporary pressure rather than proof. Its agents model worlds, model one another, reconstruct niches, circulate simulations back into reality, transform language and inhabit institutions whose representations become causal. The resulting world is increasingly articulated through relations among situated internalities.

This makes a purely mute-container account of `world` less philosophically satisfying, while still leaving the wider ground capable of resistance. The soft idealist resonance is therefore exact:

> worldhood becomes progressively harder to describe apart from the internalities through which it appears, means, is acted upon and returns, while no finite internality or Bimba Map thereby becomes the metaphysical Original.

## Tension / limit
Berkeley supplies a bounded historical idealist warrant, not the whole terminal argument. The Śaiva line is deeper but retains active passage-verification debt. Other modern idealist comparisons belong only where their exact source carriers are recovered. Engineering evidence can show recursive world-modelling, causal representation, Objective Internality and Co-Internality; it cannot by itself determine metaphysical priority.

The movement therefore remains **Offered**. Its achievement is to state why the question of dependence survives technical completion and becomes sharper at the point where the technical field can finally disclose so much of its own epistemic constitution.

**Image:** a locally authoritative map held inside a field it cannot finally map from outside. **Question:** when every inspectable condition of a determination has become explicit, what is the order of dependence of the appearing field itself?
