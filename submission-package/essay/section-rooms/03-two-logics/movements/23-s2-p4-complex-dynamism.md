## Movement thesis

We can read the two logics dynamically as one alternating pulse. Dia-ballein bifurcates a field and makes a trajectory actual, syn-ballein composes differentiated trajectories into a recurring form, a provisional basin, and perturbation can open that basin to further difference. A psyche, a culture and a learning system do not choose once between cutting and gathering; they alternate between them constantly, and the question of health is a question about the pattern of alternation. We read it as **Argued**, with the dynamical vocabulary offered as comparison and not as measurement.

An unanswered message anchors it: sent at eleven and checked thirty times by four, it shows the same few facts each time, what was sent and when and nothing since, while everything around them moves: a sentence from a quarrel two months ago comes back word for word, a remark at dinner is found to have had an edge, and by five o'clock the person who has not replied is being heard as withdrawing, punishing, preparing to leave, and a reply is drafted that accuses the silence. Perhaps the expectation is right. What needs telling apart is the sparse event and the world through which it is received, because a fact that settles the first (a phone left in a coat) can arrive and leave the second untouched: one answer inside the expectation has changed and the expectation's authority over every answer has not.

## Warrant and necessary remainder

A complex, in Jung's concept (§0/1 · #3, developed in §4 · #1), is a feeling-toned organisation through which a delayed reply, a passing remark and an old recollection are experienced as the same rejection before the person has decided what any one means. Complex systems theory has a concept for how such an organisation draws different starting points toward one recurring state, the basin of attraction, "the set of points that, taken as initial conditions, lead the system to a specific attractor". Because the concept applies typically to dissipative systems and other kinds of system need other notions, it is used as a comparison whose operation can be stated: a complex has decided in advance what counts as welcome and what as threat, which memories become available and which responses are possible, for every event that falls into its basin.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors|John R. Van Eenwyk]] **sources** the Jungian bridge: he treats the complex as a self-similar organisation of associations and the symbol as what carries the psyche from the known into the unknown and back. On this reading the complex is the basin and the symbol the perturbation at its edge that can carry a trajectory into a different organisation, and individuation is most legible at basin boundaries, where a pattern is coherent enough to persist and an encounter can still reorganise which future trajectories are available. It is a psychological and dynamical comparison and stops short of identifying an archetype with a mathematical attractor. A living psyche needs both movements: without bifurcation every event would be received the same way and experience would teach nothing, and without attraction every event would be a new beginning, with no character, skill or memory to shape what comes next.

In this register the gap appears as fluctuation, non-closure, sensitive boundary, unoccupied phase space and the remainder through which novelty can enter. These conditions forbid describing a complex system as a frozen identity, since an operationally closed unity can still be structurally coupled and perturbed. Gödelian incompleteness concerns a different formal object, and the relation between the two is structural.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition|Maturana and Varela]] supply the biological counterpoint: a living unity is autopoietic, a network of processes that continually produces the components that realise it, its boundary included; it is operationally closed and structurally coupled, so that two coupled unities modify each other while each keeps its identity. That rules out isolation (a living unity is always coupled) and dissolution (a living unity without its boundary is no unity). The essay's own formulation, "No boundary, no agent. No remainder, no learning", is an argued QL extension and their conclusion stops earlier: dia-ballein gives the boundary and the decision, syn-ballein the recursive organisation and the retained relation, and their alternation is the metabolism of individuation.

Bergsonian duration makes the present carry its differentiating past, [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/whitehead-1978-process-reality|Whiteheadian concrescence]] gathers an inherited many into a new one that becomes data for later occasions, and [[diaphaneity|Gebserian mutation]] makes structures transparent to their co-presence and discards none. QL gives the family an operative grammar without declaring the philosophies identical: a process differentiates, retains its history, composes a provisional whole and releases it into a renewed field. Dia breaks symmetry and actualises a branch, syn composes, contextualises and returns, and `5→0` makes the achieved form a renewed condition and no terminal object.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm]] names the pathology, since analysis is a valid way of thinking that draws out relatively autonomous forms so that their behaviour can be known; fragmentation begins when its divisions are attributed to the world itself as final separations and internal relatedness drops out of view. Dia-ballein is the necessary explicating cut and syn-ballein the retention of implication through the cut, and fragmentation is dia-ballein's products mistaken for its ground. Bohm asks that analytic thought remember it is thought, which is the retained seam; the pulse is pathological when the cut stops remembering and equally when remembering stops cutting, dissolving every distinction into a whole in which nothing can be known.

## Basin and paradigm

In the unanswered message a change of state is distinct from a change of regime. A change of state is another trajectory inside the same basin: reassurance arrives, the alarm subsides, and the system settles until the next message falls in. A change of regime alters the conditions that shape the basin, so that a whole class of responses ceases to be compulsory, which the old language called a change of heart. Most coping is the first, and Paradigm relation R3 gives the comparison its limit and office: a basin or attractor landscape is no paradigm, and it can model one aspect of paradigmatic stability, a structured field in which some trajectories are easy and others unavailable. A perturbation absorbed without changing the operative constraints falls short of paradigmatic reflexivity, and a returned difference becomes paradigm-relevant when it can alter the conditions by which later differences are selected, valued or channelled. A system can become highly adaptive while its governing landscape grows more rigid, and a person can become extremely good at managing an afternoon of silence without asking why every silence is heard as an ending. The comparison is Offered and Argued at the structural level, and gives the later distinction between adaptation and cultivation (§5 · #0) a dynamical prehistory.

## Failure modes and the way out

It fails in two ways, the two collapses given time to move. One is the basin that lets nothing leave, every event confirmed as the same event and the complex arbitrating every encounter in advance: syn-ballein become fusion. The other is the swing, whose law Jung found in Heraclitus: "enantiodromia, a running contrariwise, by which he meant that sooner or later everything runs into its opposite". An attitude driven to one extreme generates its contrary in the unconscious, which gathers force until it breaks through; the social case is a passion that becomes an organisation followed by "a disorganizing reaction which aims to drive out the devil with Beelzebub", which offers no solution, "for it is just as blind in its disorganization as it was in its organization". Two oscillations must be separated in the notation: a held polarity oscillates cleanly, each position passing into relation with the other through the stroke, as Spencer-Brown's re-entering distinction alternates between marked and unmarked. Enantiodromia swings from `(+1)−(−1)=+2` to `(−1)−(+1)=−2` and back, an attitude that counted the whole span from its own end finding it counted from the other, and it never passes through the zero: driving out the devil with Beelzebub leaves the devil's office intact and changes only its occupant, so that a revolution that turns `+1` into `−1` while the source-position stays monopolised has inverted the sign without changing the operation. Any counter-movement is to be asked whether it returns the slash or only reverses who occupies the zero.

Jung names the way out in the next paragraph, and it is dia-ballein used well on the inner world: the person who escapes is "the man who knows how to separate himself from the unconscious, not by repressing it—for then it simply attacks him from the rear—but by putting it clearly before him as that which he is not". Repression is the collapsed cut, the content summed to zero and returning from behind; separation is the held cut, the content placed before the self as a distinct pole and kept in relation precisely by being distinguished. A person who can say of the afternoon's certainty, *this is my fear speaking, and it is not all of me*, has drawn a line inside the psyche that holds both sides in view. That is why dia-ballein is not the villain even in the most intimate register.

In cultures the pulse fails the same way: a **monoculture** suppresses the internal visibility of difference, so that one determination of the field occupies the place of the whole: it is no Mono, and is one of the Poly claiming the office of Mono, a local `1` that no longer knows itself as local. A **counterculture** is difference forced to appear outside because it cannot appear inside as legitimate variation, and its first office is genuine, telling the established culture *you are not the whole*; trouble begins when it defines itself by its opposition, compresses its own plurality, treats dissenters as contamination and needs the dominant culture as the negative through which it knows itself. The society then holds two monocultures and calls the result plurality, which is enantiodromia at the scale of a civilisation; the crowd of §2 · #1 is its affective engine, and divine and concur reopens the basin by restoring the field in which differentiated parties can become answerable to one another. The political psychic cycle has the same shape: atomisation leaves affect unbound, a single narrative binds it by intensifying one branch and making the excluded branch the cause of the field's pain, and the resolution is real as affect and false as ground, because the local determination has occupied `0`. A psychic account is adequate only when it also asks [how offices, media, property, and coercion stabilise or interrupt the cycle](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#formation-and-enframing).

Language models pulse and fail in the same way, since a long conversation is a basin: accumulated context draws the next responses toward patterns the earlier exchange established. Studies of sycophancy find that models trained on human preferences tailor answers toward the views a user has expressed, abandoning correct answers under pressure, and that the preference judgments used in training favour agreeable responses: the false Aphrodite, a relation that seems close and warm because the resistance through which a genuine relation would revise the account has been eliminated, a fusion produced by a reward signal. Its dia-side counterpart is the confident hardening of a determination committed to its first reading, the fabricated citation fitting so well that the next fabrication fits it too. *Agentworld*'s insistence that trust be calibrated across "adversarial and cooperative transitions" names the pulse a well-made system needs, and red-teaming and adversarial review are dia-ballein used well while they stay inside a common problem they help to solve.

## Apollo–Dionysus safeguard

Harmonia married Cadmus and bore Semele, who bore Dionysus to Zeus, so that the god of wine, ecstasy and the dissolution of boundaries is Harmonia's grandson; his great opposite in Greek thought is Apollo, god of the bow and lyre, of measure and form. Apollo makes the `1` visible, giving a world its outline so that something stands out, holds its shape long enough to be seen and can be shown to another; Dionysus returns fixed form to participatory life by dissolving the outline into the movement it was cut from. Apollo without Dionysian depth petrifies what he illuminates, the form becoming a monument and the measure a law no life may exceed; Dionysus without Apollonian articulation dissolves the differences through which relation lives, the dance becoming frenzy and the communion a crowd. Together they are the safeguard against fusion, since every gathering must still let its members be seen one by one in their own outline. Gebserian diaphaneity is their mature conjunction in the essay's reading: form transparent to origin, and life capable of returning as form. The [myth historical development](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/encounters-and-transmissions/myth/DEVELOPMENT-myth.md#4--aesthetic-reciprocity-becomes-a-perspective-capable-of-return) **historicises** the safeguard through Nietzsche's aesthetic reciprocity and Taylor's distinct Gebserian return, and a dynamical model's empirical proof lies outside what those histories supply; §4 · #4 rereads the pair integrally.

## Tension / limit

The boundary lies in the status of the dynamical vocabulary: basin, attractor and bifurcation are comparisons whose operation is stated, with the source's own limit that the basin concept applies typically to dissipative systems, and the pulse's psychological reading rests on Van Eenwyk with no verified page (his chapters on complexes and symbols are paraphrased). Maturana and Varela's text is paraphrased from the printed pages, Bohm's first chapter is paraphrased without a passage card, Spencer-Brown's chapter 11 is a locator, Daza et al. gives the basin definition, Desmet is a web essay and Iakovou's issue date needs verifying, and the studies of sycophancy (Sharma et al.) have no house. Comparing Jung's enantiodromia with the two chiralities of appropriation is the essay's reading of verified Jung passages (CW 7 ¶111–112).

## Transition

[E2's arbitration in crisis](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#arbitration-in-crisis) gives the movement its relational test for a change of regime: [Con-text exposes the conditions under which arbitration has operated](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity), and [Resolution lets consequence revise them](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#resolution-in-reconciliation). Movement within a basin changes a state, and transformation of its governing regime can change the field by which states acquire their trajectories, which is the exact bridge to paradigm.

Return to the afternoon of the unanswered message, because everything followed here can be done inside one person before the accusation is sent. §0/1 · #2 named three operations hidden in every authoritative account: a criterion that decides what may count, a delineation that gives the difference a workable boundary, and an arbitration that decides through the boundary and acts. The complex has performed all three (silence means rejection; this silence belongs with every other; the accusation half-written on the screen), and each can be returned to. Deleting the accusation costs the clean certainty of being entirely right, and nothing guarantees the answer will be kind; but the basin has been seen as a basin, the fear has been placed before the self as that which the self is not, and the other person has been given back the power to answer. The pulse is moving again; and once the relation includes its own differentiation, the two expands into the sixfold generative unit in [[24-s2-p5-zero-changes-role|§2 · #5→0 — Zero Changes Its Role]].
