## Movement thesis

The two logics can be read dynamically as one alternating pulse. Dia-ballein bifurcates a field and makes a trajectory actual, syn-ballein recursively composes differentiated trajectories into a provisional basin, and perturbation can open that basin to further difference. Symmetry breaking and symmetry making thus alternate in the metabolism of complex form.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors|John R. Van Eenwyk]] gives the Jungian bridge. Complexes and archetypal patterns can be compared with attractors: trajectories are drawn into characteristic forms while staying sensitive to initial conditions and basin boundaries. Individuation becomes especially legible near those boundaries, where an established pattern is coherent enough to persist and an encounter can still reorganise which future trajectories are available. The comparison is psychological and dynamical, and it stops short of identifying an archetype with a mathematical attractor.

## Warrant and necessary remainder

In this register the gap appears as fluctuation, non-closure, sensitive boundary, unoccupied phase-space and the remainder through which novelty can enter. These dynamical conditions forbid describing a complex system as a frozen identity, since an operationally closed unity can still be structurally coupled and perturbed. Gödelian incompleteness concerns a different formal object, and the relation between the two is structural.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition|Maturana and Varela]] supply the biological counterpoint: operational closure together with structural coupling distinguishes a living unity from isolation and from mere external assembly. The essay's further formulation, "No boundary, no agent. No remainder, no learning", is an argued QL extension and their conclusion stops earlier.

The pulse belongs to a wider process line. Bergsonian duration makes the present carry its differentiating past, [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/whitehead-1978-process-reality|Whiteheadian concrescence]] gathers an inherited many into a new one that becomes data for later occasions, and [[diaphaneity|Gebserian mutation]] makes structures transparent to their co-presence and discards none of them. QL gives this family an operative grammar: dia breaks symmetry and actualises a branch, syn composes, contextualises and returns, and `5→0` makes the achieved form a renewed condition and no terminal object.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm]] separates analysis from fragmentation. Analysis explicates a relatively stable form so that its behaviour can be known, and fragmentation occurs when the analytic divisions are attributed to the whole as final separations and internal relatedness disappears. Dia-ballein is the necessary explicating cut and syn-ballein is the retention of implication through the cut, so atomisation is the runtime arrested between them.

## Basin and paradigm

The R3 paradigm relation gives the dynamical comparison a limit and an office. A basin or attractor landscape is no paradigm, and it can model one aspect of paradigmatic stability: a structured field in which some trajectories become easy, recurrent or intelligible while others become difficult, unstable or unavailable. Ordinary adaptation can move skilfully within such a landscape without changing the landscape.

Paradigm change is closer to a transformation of the regime that organises trajectories than to another successful trajectory inside the same basin. A perturbation absorbed without changing the operative constraints falls short of paradigmatic reflexivity, and a returned difference becomes paradigm-relevant when it can alter the conditions by which later differences are selected, valued or channelled. The comparison is Offered and Argued at the structural level.

This gives the later distinction between adaptation and cultivation a dynamical prehistory. A system can become highly adaptive while its governing attractor landscape grows more rigid, and epistemic cultivation will need the stronger capacity for encounters to expose and sometimes transform the conditions of adaptation themselves.

The political psychic cycle has the same dynamic shape. Atomisation and loss of meaningful relation leave affect unbound, and a single narrative binds it by intensifying one branch and converting the excluded branch into the cause of the field's pain. The resolution is real as affect and false as ground, because the local determination has occupied `0`. Divine and concur reopens the basin, restoring the relational field in which differentiated parties can become mutually answerable while their conflict stays what it is. The institutional question remains in force: a psychic account is adequate only when it asks [how offices, media, property, and coercion stabilise or interrupt the cycle](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#formation-and-enframing).

## Apollo–Dionysus safeguard

Apollo makes the `1` visible through measure, boundary and form, and Dionysus returns fixed form to participatory life. Apollo without Dionysian depth petrifies what it illuminates, and Dionysus without Apollonian articulation dissolves the differences through which relation lives. Gebserian diaphaneity is their mature conjunction in the essay's reading: form transparent to origin, and life capable of returning as form.

The [myth historical development](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/encounters-and-transmissions/myth/DEVELOPMENT-myth.md#4--aesthetic-reciprocity-becomes-a-perspective-capable-of-return) **historicises** the Apollo–Dionysus safeguard through Nietzsche's aesthetic reciprocity and Taylor's distinct Gebserian return. The source histories preserve articulation and participation, and a dynamical model's empirical proof lies outside what they supply.

## Transition

[E2's arbitration in crisis](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#arbitration-in-crisis) gives this movement its relational test for a change of regime: [Con-text exposes the conditions under which arbitration has operated](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#con-text-through-diaphaneity), and [Resolution lets consequence revise them](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD-arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis.md#resolution-in-reconciliation). Movement within a basin changes a state, and transformation of its governing regime can change the field by which states acquire their trajectories, which is the exact bridge to paradigm.

The complex-dynamical pulse shows that the two accountings cannot stay merely philosophical. Once the relation includes its own differentiation, two expands into the sixfold generative unit in [[24-s2-p5-zero-changes-role|§2 · #5→0 — Zero Changes Its Role]].
