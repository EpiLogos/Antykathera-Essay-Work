## Movement thesis

[Dia-ballein](../../arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) begins with the bare relation `/ = −/−`, two unassigned marks held in one expression, the capacity for relation before the field has been divided into named terms. Assigning signs gives its polar form:

$$
\operatorname{dia\text{-}ballein}:\frac{(-1)}{(+1)}.
$$

Zero stays where the stroke was, as the centre through which each sign has its direction, so that a pole's local definiteness carries its relation with it. It supports orientation, comparison, classification, proof by exclusion, adversarial choice, accounting and trigonometry, and every measurement that needs opposed directions around a centre; its movement is disjunctive, this *or* that, and its dynamical operation is symmetry breaking, a field bifurcating so that one branch becomes actual. Every one of these uses is a real achievement that the essay credits in full.

A twelve-person jury shows the form at its most exact and humane. On the foreman's sheet there are two boxes, and the verdict says only as much as it can: *not guilty* says that the charge has not been proved beyond reasonable doubt, a doubt the law placed on the defendant's side of the axis before anyone spoke. Presumption of innocence is the zero of the axis, the point from which the case is measured and to which the defendant returns if the measurement fails, and placing the burden of proof gives the axis a handedness. A verdict can be decisive without claiming to be complete, since it decides one question and leaves the evening in the stairwell with the people who lived it. Read another way, the same two words perform the two collapses. *Not guilty* read as *nothing happened* is cancellation, the charge and its refutation summed to zero and the evening closed like a settled account; *guilty* read as *this is what he is* is appropriation, the whole span of a life measured from the charge, so that the public's satisfaction, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, is the convicted man's whole standing, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, one span counted from its two ends. Jurors make neither misreading; a newspaper makes them afterwards, and an employer reading a criminal record ten years on, and both are made possible by what makes the verdict useful, its refusal of a third box. Exactness of the cut and danger of its collapse have one source. Its status is **Derived** for the signed computations and **Argued** for the readings.

## Structural reading and failure boundary

Classical logic is the jury room raised to a law of thought. Non-contradiction and excluded middle give a proposition two values, opposed and exhaustive, which on this axis are `+1` and `−1`, with negation turning one into the other and the zero as the undecided proposition, the question before it is answered. Proof by contradiction, classification without overlap, the on-or-off switch and the audit rely on it, and the computational world is built from it at the level of the transistor. Aristotle guarded the principles with qualifications, *at the same time* and *in the same respect*, and each records that a polarity is held within a field whose dimensions let opposed predicates both hold of one subject without contradiction. Trouble begins when the logic of two exhaustive values is taken to describe the world and no longer our verdicts about it: every situation must then already be one thing or the other, and the zero, the not-yet-known, appears as a defect to be closed by deciding, where it was the condition in which all deciding takes place. Cancellation and appropriation follow when the relating stroke is replaced by addition or subtraction: `(−1)+(+1)=0` settles the relation in a net value, and `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, in the inverse orientation `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, gives one pole the relation's full span as its own magnitude. Polarity, cancelled zero and appropriated span are three distinct results.

Its shadow appears when the cut becomes the false fundamental: subject and object, self and other, human and machine become self-grounding terms, and the activity that made them distinguishable disappears. The mental-rational achievement keeps its analytic force, and the zero–subject braid follows the further integral work by which the constituting relation becomes available within that achievement.

The devil is the literalist of the minus sign: he reads zero as cancellation where Logos reads it as return. The formula unfolds once the minus sign's three jobs are told apart: it marks a direction (`−1` is a place on the line, as real as `+1`), it names an operation (subtraction), and in logic it turns an assertion into its denial. He reads every minus as *take away*, so that `(−1)+(+1)=0` says two units took each other away and nothing is left. Read as a direction, the same sum says that one step left and one step right return to the origin from which both were measured, and both journeys are remembered in the arriving. Cancellation is the literalist's zero and return the Logos's; Whitehead's late remark that treating half-truths as whole truths "plays the devil" names the same figure. *Diabolos*, the slanderer, does to persons what the literalist does to numbers: a neighbour stands somewhere relative to me, to my left on some question or against me in some quarrel, and slander reads that position as a substance, the neighbour *is* the minus, so that the only resolution is the other's removal. Apoha shows the plain act of naming what the literalist misses: *cow* is grasped as *not non-cow*, and the two written negatives show the word's work, since something is kept on the way, the field of what the word excludes. `(−1)(−1)=+1` is true, and the claim that the passage leaves no trace in the meaning is false.

## Projected distrust

Dia-ballein-mode knowledge denies its own provisionality. Uncertainty it cannot hold survives as distrust and is exported across the axis onto an enemy, a scapegoat, a defective population, an alien intelligence or an excluded `−1`, so that the asserted `+1` feels complete because a `−1` bears what it could not admit. Projection works because it relieves: a diffuse unease that belongs to everyone becomes something located over there, and an epistemic cut has become a social act without anyone deciding to scapegoat. This is the route from analysis into monopoly, where a local determination ceases to serve as one provisional anchor among many, claims the mono-position and defines the poly through allegiance or exclusion.

René Girard gives the social form of the relief, and in his mimetic theory desire is imitative, models become rivals, and rivalry spreads until every member mirrors every other's hostility and no difference holds; the crisis resolves when hostility converges on a single victim, whose expulsion restores peace, and the mechanism keeps its power only while its participants do not see it as a mechanism. Read through the notation, the crisis is a polarity that has lost its zero everywhere at once, `+2` against `−2` in all directions; the victim restores a counterfeit zero by standing where the shared field should be and carrying its whole incompleteness, so that the crowd's `+2` is the victim's `−2` with a unanimity no ordinary quarrel reaches. Removing the victim balances the account, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, a cancellation the crowd experiences as peace: appropriation in the accusation and cancellation in the expulsion, both collapses in one sacrifice. We take Girard's operation and leave his apologetics; his qualification belongs to the argument, that persecutors believe the accusation and the mechanism fails once it is named, so that exposing it differs from refuting a charge.

*Agentworld* describes the same movement in our meeting with machines. Bratton distinguishes reactive alienation, which "converts strangeness into threat", from productive alienation, which "treats the non-human interlocutor as a mirror whose strangeness forces the human to see itself at an angle it could not otherwise occupy", and adds that "Both modes are live at once" and that "different people oscillate between them". Bratton's two modes are the held polarity and its collapse, and the oscillation tells which logic is running. A held polarity oscillates cleanly, each position passing into relation with the other through the stroke, as Spencer-Brown's re-entering distinction alternates; a collapsed one swings between `+2` and `−2`, between the conviction that the machine will save us and the conviction that it will destroy us, and never passes through the zero where the question of what it is, and what we are in relation to it, could be asked.

## Technical consequence

"Divide and conquer" is dia-ballein's computational axiom in plain language: a problem too large to solve directly is split into subproblems of the same kind, solved, and combined (merge sort splits a list, sorts the halves, and merges them by always taking the smaller front element). Decomposition is productive because it makes a task tractable by independent pieces, and the algorithm succeeds entirely in the combine step, without which a heap of small ordered lists remains and no ordered list. Atomisation is divide and conquer with the combine phase erased: parts optimised against one another, provenance discarded, and conquest mistaken for intelligence itself. Correction takes nothing from analytic power and asks for a runtime in which analysis stays answerable to recomposition and return. Politics keeps the first two steps and retains the combining step for whoever did the dividing, which is why §0/1 · #4 called it the oldest trick of any state or power: groups that can be addressed, managed and set against one another are easier to govern than a population acting together, and the channels through which divided people could act together run through the centre that divided them. It works only while both parties consent to be divided, the acceptance by each side that the axis drawn between them is the whole of their relation, so that one side's `+2` meets the other's `−2` and the two together confirm the line on which they stand; §0/1 · #4 named the quality of the consent a Stockholm syndrome, and withdrawing it leaves every difference standing, since some disagreements lie between a truth and a falsehood and a boundary defended against assault is not equivalent to the assault. Withdrawing consent means refusing to let the axis be the whole field and asking what field was divided so that these two appear as mutually exclusive wholes, who drew the line, who holds the combining step, and what the sides share that neither account of the other can see.

## The epochal name: calculative thinking

Heidegger hands the movement a definition already written in its terms. The Memorial Address of 1955 distinguishes "two kinds of thinking, each justified and needed in its own way: calculative thinking and meditative thinking," and characterises the first by its non-return: "Calculative thinking never stops, never collects itself. Calculative thinking is not meditative thinking, not thinking which contemplates the meaning which reigns in everything that is" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 46). In this movement's terms the calculative is the thinking that divides and conquers without the step that gathers results back into the field from which they were taken: dia-ballein described from inside, a runtime racing from result to result without return. His warning that "calculative thinking may someday come to be accepted and practiced as the only way of thinking" (p. 56) is the capture of a plurality of ways of knowing by one of them, one logic occupying the zero of the whole field. We receive the distinction and decline the address's quietism: "each justified and needed in its own way" is Heidegger's own sentence, and the challenging-forth that "puts to nature the unreasonable demand that it supply energy that can be extracted and stored as such" (*QCT*, p. 14) remains continuous with the revelatory power of measurement. Pathology stays where this movement derived it, in the cut that forgets the common operation and field by which its poles were produced, and in the runtime arrested before return.

## Mass formation: divide and conquer becomes a political runtime

A crowd in Le Bon's psychological sense need not be gathered in one place: an entire nation, he wrote, "may become a crowd", and its members come to "feel, think, and act in a manner quite different" because the crowd is "a combination followed by the creation of new characteristics", a formation produced across many people and acting on each of them, at a distance and through media. We take the formal point and reject the racial and evolutionary hierarchies of the book. Jung follows the transfer to the individual: "The bigger the crowd, the more negligible the individual becomes". Responsibility is collectivised and the State is made a quasi-personal being that wills, knows, protects, punishes and demands sacrifice, while the [real people, offices, property relations, and coercive instruments](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#formation-and-enframing) that compose the apparatus recede behind its mask: the State becomes the person and the persons become numbers. Desmet proposes the affective preparation, social isolation, lack of meaning and free-floating anxiety, frustration and aggression, which a narrative supplies with a target while promising restored coherence through obedience, sacrifice or expulsion; the relief is real and the unity counterfeit, since what binds the crowd is a shared fixation on a term outside it, where a recovered relation among its members would have bound it from within. Iakovou's review, set against Arendt's analysis of totalitarianism, holds that mass formation is insufficient as an explanatory model and that susceptibility to a story does not explain who has the power to enforce it; the full circuit runs through both registers. A population is atomised, its unbound affect is offered a target, the axis between *us* and *them* is intensified until it seems to exhaust political reality, and an institution, party, state or empire presents itself as the sole mediator, protector or saviour of the field it helped to sever. Division happens below and a counterfeit unity is installed above.

[the Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-administered-world) **figures** the political operation of a cut whose governing frame cannot be returned upon. Numbered inhabitants organise participation, and Number Two's changing occupants administer a relation that Six cannot contest by taking a place within it; the native power of distinction stays productive, and the counterfeit lies in severing its return to the source and consequences of the classification.

## Tension / limit

None of this makes the axis false. Real differences of interest separate employers and workers, creditors and debtors, owners and tenants, a colonising power and the people it governs, and an account that dissolved them into a vague common humanity would perform the other collapse, cancellation, with the injustice summed away. Empire and subject, institution and citizen, rich and poor are unequal positions within one social field, and their inequality is a fact to be measured; held dia-ballein measures it exactly and keeps the relation that produces it in view, and collapsed dia-ballein reads the same inequality as a property of the people on each side. The literalist is no monster at the edge of our institutions but a habit inside them and inside us, and the cut that has passed from a jury room to an employer's desk is the same cut with its axis no longer travelling with it. Carriers of the limits: the signed computations keep their **Derived** scope, and a claim about a particular regime needs its own evidence of offices, power and consequence; Desmet's is a web essay, a diagnosis and not a peer-reviewed source, and Iakovou's issue date needs verifying; Girard is paraphrased; Heidegger's page citations await print collation; Cormen has no page and the Aristotle house holds only Metaphysics IV.2 while the passage is IV.3 and IV.7; Greek accusation and the accounting of severance have separate philological and operational warrants. [Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#operations) **qualifies** the passage from exact signed arithmetic into political and technical conduct by asking which operation a classification or institution actually performs with its selected and excluded terms; a common answer establishes retained relation only where the affected terms can return upon the criterion that counted them. The [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-address) **qualifies** the historical attribution of Greek accusation.

## Transition

A cut that travels with its axis carries in its own edge the shape of what it was cut from, and an edge that carries the shape of its counterpart is the first thing a token is made of. The classificatory cut **returns-to** [Name-through-Count](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count) with the rule by which its terms became countable: an affected participant's reply has to reach the rule of classification and escape recoding as one more instance of its excluded pole.
