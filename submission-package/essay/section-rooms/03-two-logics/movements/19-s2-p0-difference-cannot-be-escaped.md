## Movement thesis

Non-duality keeps distinction inside the appearing-act from which its terms arise. Without difference nothing appears, no question can be asked, no action can occur and no world can become articulate, so the live question is what the products of differentiation do once the cut has been made. Danger lies in the amnesia that follows it, when the products forget the activity and field through which they arose.

Every binary has two provenances. Its terms can be treated as separately given values whose conflict is measured from outside, or as inverse determinations of one differentiating relation, and [[section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|Two Logics of Two]] names these as complete accountings of polarity: two ways a two can be true. Taylor's comparability principle states why the second provenance is always present: two things cannot stand in opposition unless they share the ground that makes them comparable, so every opposition testifies to the unity it appears to refuse.

Knowledge meets the same fork because it cannot eliminate its own provisionality. "Knowledge is a performance of trust wearing the mask of certainty" names the hidden floor: language, memory, method, witness, competence, institution and world have already been trusted enough for a proposition to count as known. The two logics describe what knowledge then does with that trust, and the bedside case that opened the essay shows both uses at once, an adult who cannot answer a child's last "why?" and says "because I say so".

## Natural-language derivation

A determination requires a difference, and a difference requires a field across which the distinction is made. Once made, the differentiated terms can be compared as opposed values, and their opposition leaves untouched their shared dependence on the originating distinction. Logic must therefore account both for the opposed terms and for the seam that makes them terms.

The first accounting writes two signed ones around a retained axial zero, `(−1)/(+1)`, and distinguishes what happens when that relation is cancelled, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, or its span appropriated, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` and `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. The second retains ground and mark inside each orientation and writes two conjugate ratios, `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

[[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|Mono–poly]] names the field/many relation: the common field is the mono, and the determinations belong to its poly. [[section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust]] is the lived relation beneath any act of knowing, made explicit when a rational account meets the condition it cannot fully certify, and it is a different thing from that topology. The issue is whether knowledge can receive that condition humbly and become a provisional anchor, or project its unheld distrust onto a term that then claims totality.

## Proof burden

Dia-ballein is necessary for decision and boundary, and syn-ballein is necessary for provenance and return, so the section sets the two logics side by side as two operations that a competent account needs together. Their failures are severance, where the cut loses the field, and fusion, where the relation loses its terms, and their coordinated movement is differentiation capable of recognising its own ground. Superposition, read this way, adds retained relation to the cut and leaves the cut's force in place.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#operations) **grounds** the comparison in this opening difference: terms must enter a shared relation before they can oppose one another, and the relation decides none of their claims in advance. What survives the crossing is comparability through retained difference, so the move into Dia must carry the field that made its signed alternatives possible. An agreed answer is a further event whose truth and conditions of assent require examination, since the mere existence of a common field is a weaker thing.
