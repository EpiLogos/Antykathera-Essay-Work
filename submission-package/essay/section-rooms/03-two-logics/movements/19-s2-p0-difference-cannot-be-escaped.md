# §2 · #0 — Difference Cannot Be Escaped

## Movement thesis

Non-duality keeps distinction inside the appearing-act from which its terms arise. Without difference nothing appears, no question can be asked, no action can occur and no world can become articulate, so the live question is what the products of differentiation do once the cut has been made: whether a distinction stays answerable to the ground and the activity that produced it, or its finished terms present themselves as self-sufficient things. Danger lies in the amnesia that follows the cut, when the products forget the activity and the field through which they arose.

Every binary has two provenances, since its terms can be treated as separately given values whose conflict is measured from outside ("One and *some* Other One"), or as inverse determinations of one differentiating relation ("One and its own Other"). [[section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|Two Logics of Two]] names these as complete accountings of polarity, two ways a two can be true. Taylor's comparability principle states why the second provenance is always present: two things cannot stand in opposition unless they share the ground that makes them comparable, so every opposition testifies to the unity it appears to refuse.

Knowledge meets the same fork because it cannot eliminate its own provisionality. "Knowledge is a performance of trust wearing the mask of certainty" names the hidden floor: language, memory, method, witness, competence, institution and world have already been trusted enough for a proposition to count as known. Collapsed dia-ballein exports the uncertainty this leaves across an axis onto an excluded term; syn-ballein distributes it across revisable anchors. We read it as **Argued**.

## Anchor cases

Two children and one slice of cake open the movement. Under cut-and-choose the elder cuts as near the middle as eye and hand can manage, because her brother will choose first and leave her the remainder; there are still two pieces, two children and two offices, and the rule changes where the cut answers. Change one thing and the same knife performs a different event: the elder who cuts and keeps the larger piece counts the span from her end, a tired parent who hands out the pieces gives a judgment neither child can return upon, and a parent who removes the slice leaves a balance of zero and a resentment the balance does not record. In every version a difference is made; what varies is whether the act that made it can still be answered by the people it divides. In the signed arithmetic of §0/1 · #2, cut-and-choose is dia-ballein held well, two offices on one axis with the cake as the zero both depend on and neither owns; the elder who keeps the larger piece has appropriated the span, and the parent who removes the slice has cancelled it. A rule that keeps the cut answerable to the one who did not make it is a small working syn-ballein.

Hesiod tells the first division between gods and humans as a cut of the same kind turned inside out. At Mecone the verb for the dispute, *ekrinonto*, belongs to *krinein*, whose senses run from separating to deciding, the *krisis* on which §0/1 · #4 closed. Prometheus portions the ox so that the best looks like the worst and the worst like the best, a *technē* bent to *dolos*; Zeus sees the trick and takes the fat-covered bones anyway, and the gods withhold fire; Prometheus steals it, and Hephaestus forms the "evil thing" that is the price of fire. In the children's rule the chooser's choice kept the cut honest; at Mecone the look of the portions captures the choice in advance, so that the relation survives in form and is emptied in substance. Read through the signed arithmetic, the mortals' `+2` is the gods' `−2`, one span reckoned from either end, and the shared zero, the sacrifice both took part in, has dropped out of both accounts. That Zeus chose the bones because choosing them gave him a grievance, a span counted from his own end, is the essay's reading of a card that has him plan mischief. Mecone is a mytheme in §0/1 · #4's sense, a whole image that proves a relation through the action it stages: a division can be performed as a relation and still be an appropriation, which is the con of §0/1 · #2 set at the founding of the relation between the human and the divine. Hesiod quarrels with one way of dividing, in which the presentation of the portions occupies the place where the chooser's free reply should stand.

## Natural-language derivation

A determination requires a difference, and a difference requires a field across which the distinction is made. Once made, the terms can be compared as opposed values, and their opposition leaves untouched their shared dependence on the originating distinction. Logic must therefore account both for the opposed terms and for the seam that makes them terms. The first accounting writes two signed ones around a retained axial zero, `(−1)/(+1)`, and distinguishes what happens when that relation is cancelled, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, or its span appropriated, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` and `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, so that one party's `+2` is the other's `−2`. The second retains ground and mark inside each orientation and writes two conjugate ratios, `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

Greek prefixes carry the difference in small: *dia* is through and across, and in compounds that pass through and part a whole it comes to mean apart; *syn* is with and together. Dia-ballein throws across, which is how a cut is made, and syn-ballein throws together, which is how a token is fitted. Each needs the other's object, since nothing can be thrown together unless something was thrown apart, and nothing apart unless a field held it together. Diachronic and synchronic cross these two: cut-and-choose is a sequence performing syn-ballein, a banquet table seated by rank is one synchronic arrangement performing dia-ballein in its collapsed form, and what decides between the logics is whether the relation that makes the terms terms stays active through the operation performed on them.

Śrīharṣa, the twelfth-century philosopher of the *Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya*, supplies the strongest case against difference itself, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/das/das-2022-sriharsa-sep/das-2022-sriharsa-sep|Nilanjan Das's account]] **sources** it: Śrīharṣa argues that "irrespective of how we define distinctness, no awareness—perceptual or otherwise—can present one object as distinct from another, without also presenting them as non-distinct." Every awareness of difference is also an awareness of relation, because a difference can be presented only within the one act that holds its terms together, which is the copula's double work of §0/1 · #1. For an Advaitin the inseparability counts against the final reality of difference; the essay reads it as the reason the distinction is never alone: the pot really is not the cloth, and the awareness that tells them apart really holds them as one field. Non-duality preserves distinction within the appearing-act from which its terms arise.

Dia-ballein keeps the field as the axis: the zero between `−1` and `+1` is the respect in which they are comparable and the point from which both directions are measured, the cake both children want. Syn-ballein keeps the field as one of the terms: in `0/1` the zero is written alongside what has become determinate within it. Dia makes its two out of two signed ones, each a `1` with a direction, and its zero lies between them or comes out as their sum. Syn makes its two out of the two senses of *one*, zero bearing the singular One of self-identity and one the manifest All of self-difference. In the first accounting zero is never a term and in the second it always is; §2 · #5→0 counts how the zero's office changes.

[[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|Mono–poly]] names the field/many relation: the common field is the mono and the determinations belong to its poly. [[section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust]] is the lived relation beneath any act of knowing, made explicit when a rational account meets the condition it cannot fully certify. A haematologist reading a blood count trusts the analyser's calibration, the technician, the label on the tube and the printed reference ranges, and the result is a certainty only in respect of everything she trusted; an agent answering with three citations trusts the retrieval, the documents' provenance and the instructions about what counts as a source. §0/1 · #2 named the four offices of the relation (trust, knowledge, belief, faith), and the field trusted in every such case is Objective Internality, the means through which a life knows and acts within a world. *Performance* is meant fully: knowledge is an act carried out before others and with them, under conditions it did not set, and its certainty is the mask it wears in order to act at all. Trouble begins when the mask is taken for the face.

They then differ in what they do with the uncertainty that remains. Collapsed dia-ballein exports it across the axis onto an excluded term, the enemy, the scapegoat, the defective population or the alien intelligence, so that the asserted `+1` feels whole because a `−1` bears what it could not admit: the public's `+2` is the scapegoat's `−2`. Syn-ballein distributes it across revisable anchors (a skill tested in use, a witness who can be questioned, a practice that can be corrected, an institution with recourse), so that no single object carries the whole weight.

Heraclitus gave the structure a Greek name. What differs agrees with itself, and the word for agreeing is *homologeein*, a common *logos* found within a difference; §2 · #3 hears the whole fragment. Any comparison, any judgment that two things are opposed, any quarrel over which is larger presupposes a common respect, and that respect is the zero the logics treat differently: held, it lets opposed things be compared, contested and fought over without ceasing to belong to one field; lost, it leaves camps that no longer recognise the common operation that produced them, and their opposition hardens into a fact about each. The same fork decides whether a society of artificial agents can coordinate without collapsing into one world-model or splintering into many that cannot measure their disagreement, the corridor §5 · #5→0 reads through these two logics.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#operations) **grounds** the comparison in this opening difference: terms must enter a shared relation before they can oppose one another, and the relation decides none of their claims in advance. What survives the crossing is comparability through retained difference, so the move into dia-ballein carries the field that made its signed alternatives possible.

## Tension / limit

Both logics need their full scope, because they distinguish operations and not moral camps. Cutting powers comparison, classification, measurement, proof by exclusion, decision and critique; without it nothing could be refused, no injustice could be named as injustice, and the verdict has to be reached before every uncertainty is settled. Its pathology begins where the cut forgets the common operation and field that produced its poles: the elder's knife does nothing wrong, and her keeping does. Syn-ballein fails in its own way, by fusion: a gathering can erase the differences it gathers and call the result unity, and be welcomed by the people it absorbs. Carriers of the boundary: Das records that this reading of Śrīharṣa "remains controversial"; the Hesiod cards cover lines 535–44, 551–57 and 561–72, so the quoted phrase "take which ever of these portions your heart within you bids" awaits a card; and the existence of a common field is a weaker thing than an agreed answer, whose truth and conditions of assent require their own examination.
