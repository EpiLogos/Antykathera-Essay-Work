## Movement thesis

[[section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|Syn-ballein]] **defines** superpositional polarity as the inverse orientations of the retained ground–mark relation:

$$
\operatorname{syn\text{-}ballein}:\frac{(0/1)}{(1/0)}.
$$

Each inner slash holds the indefinite and the definite together, and the outer slash relates their conjugate orientations. `0/1` is ground becoming legible through a mark, and `1/0` is the mark returning toward a ground it cannot convert into content. The two orientations are the same differentiated event and irreducibly inverse readings of it; the slash is AND/OR: classical logic reads the slash as OR, *one and not the other*, and syn-ballein reads it as the conjunction that does not erase disjunction, which §0/1 · #1 found inside the copula as `=/≠`. Read as OR, either orientation is taken in its own office and the difference stays consequential; read as AND, both are held within one act, since each orientation is the relation and neither is the whole of it. Ordinary arithmetic supplies the seam through which the readings stay different, since it defines `0/1` and leaves `1/0` undefined. We read it as **Argued**, with the asymmetry of `0/1` and `1/0` in ordinary arithmetic **Derived**.

On a maternity ward a midwife fastens a band on the baby and a matching band on the mother, and later a night nurse reads the two against each other and hands the child over: the bands keep mother and child two, can be carried apart through corridors and across shifts, and prove the match to a stranger who has no other way of knowing. A single band shared between them would prove nothing, and a mother who lost hers would have to prove her child slowly and in distress. Such a proof is exact because narrow, saying nothing of whether the number will ever mean anything to the person who wore it, and it is needed only for the hours in which the relation exists and cannot yet be read from either face. A device of trust is made for an interval in which those who must rely on a relation cannot see it directly.

## Warrant — the token, the logic and the seam

Its ancient form is the *symbolon*, a token broken between two parties and fitted later so that the match of the broken edges authenticates the relation: the difference between the halves is the condition of their recognition. Greek lexicography records a knucklebone or other small object broken by two guest-friends, each keeping a piece, so that whoever later presented the other piece could prove who he was; it could be inherited, carried by a messenger who had met neither party, and wait in a chest for a generation. Each half holds the exact negative of the absent half in its outline. [[section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|Syn-ballein]] is the logic the token embodies, and `(0/1)/(1/0)` is the whole of it in notation: the relation taking its own inverse as a term. This logic is complete in another sense than the classical one, since it sorts no cases into boxes and keeps the relation every sorting presupposes in view while the sorting goes on.

It is superpositional in the sense the dual-non-dual ontology gives the word: ground and manifestation are distinguishable orientations of one appearing-act, each given through the relation that prevents it from standing alone, because separately complete substances related afterwards would presuppose the joining they were meant to explain. Quantum state space, the complementary descriptions discussed by Pauli and Jung, and non-classical logics that allow a proposition to be both or neither are exact neighbours of the claim, examined in §3, and none supplies it; both bands are fully readable at once and neither makes the other redundant.

Syn-ballein is not *the whole*. It is the logic of defining toward wholeness, the accounting in which a determination keeps its ground, its inverse orientation and the relations through which it can complete as `0/1 → 4+2` and release, so that completion stays a direction travelled. A claim to have achieved the whole would already be a `1` occupying the zero, what §0/1 · #2 called playing the `0`; a token fused into one lump is no token, and a gathering that claimed no remaining seams would be an enclosure. Heraclitus carries the right gathering in adjacent fragments: B51's backwards-turning fastening gives retained tension its instrument in bow and lyre, and B50, in Heidegger's rendering of *legein* as "the Laying that gathers" (*die lesende Lege*, *Early Greek Thinking*, p. 66), gives Logos the shape of a gathering that lets what it gathers lie together before us without consuming it, as a reader gathers letters into a sentence without the letters ceasing to be letters. Its counterfeit, the net that gathers to arrest, waits in [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]].

## Provisional anchors and mono–poly trust

What a token carries across its interval is trust, and syn-ballein's second face turns toward the uncertainty knowledge cannot eliminate. Where collapsed dia-ballein finds one object to blame, syn-ballein finds many places to rest: competence tested in use, a relationship that has survived disagreement, a lineage, a practice, a piece of land, a ritual, a checked source, an institution with real means of redress. Each is a **provisional anchor**, provisional because it can be revised when it fails and an anchor because it holds while it holds, and their plurality is their strength: when one fails the possibility of trusting does not fail with it, and each can correct the others. [[section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]] names the living relation those anchors serve, and [[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|Mono–Poly: Whole and Many]] names the topology in which many anchors need not be absorbed by one false whole. Mono is the singular field of reliance already enacted before anyone certified it, and Poly the many anchors through which it becomes definite, local and revisable. Sever the Poly and trust does not disappear: it becomes available for capture by a single totalising object, a leader, doctrine, party, platform or model that offers to bear all the weight and asks exemption from the revision every anchor owes. That is the bargain the crowd of §2 · #1 accepted, and the monopoly of trust takes its name from *pōlein*, to sell: one seller where there should be many.

§0/1's vocabulary can be followed as six offices in the life of one entrustment: *fides*, reliance already enacted; *topos*, the place in which the relying happens; *logos*, the articulation that makes it examinable; *nomos*, the instituted effect that decides whose answer changes what is done; *natio*, the order into which later people arrive before they can examine how it was made; and *credere*, entrustment renewed after an undertaking has returned its consequences. A *nomos* that forbids its own revision has arrested the sequence before *credere*, a *natio* that presents its order as nature has hidden its *topos*, and a *logos* that claims to need no *fides* has taken the mask for the face; syn-ballein keeps the six moving.

## Divine and concur

Against divide and conquer stands **divine and concur**, a pun that argues. To divine, Latin *dīvīnāre*, is to perceive what is hidden, and divining here means finding in the apparent self-sufficiency of each pole the relation it has obscured or appropriated: the transcendental third, the `0/1 = 1/0` relation through which both poles were formed. That third is no third party in the middle and not the centrist's midpoint, which remains on the signed line; it is the field that was divided so that these two appear as mutually exclusive wholes, and the question that finds it is the one §2 · #1 left: who drew the line, who holds the combining step, and what do the two sides share that neither account of the other can see? *Conquer* descends through Old French from the Latin family of *conquīrere*, which Lewis and Short gloss as to seek for, to procure, and also to bring together and collect: the conqueror gathers by seizing, and the whole he assembles is held by the hand that took it. *Concur* is *con-currere*, to run together, the running of *currency*, what is present enough to pass from hand to hand. To concur is for differentiated positions to move together through a ground none possesses, as current money runs between people without belonging to any of them: conquest gathers by possession and concurrence by circulation.

Divine and concur can judge. Recovering the field in which an injustice was done may sharpen the injustice: one claim may prove false, a power may have to give up an office it had taken, and an injured person may decline reconciliation and be right to. What the operation refuses is the step by which the falsity or violence of a determination licenses the fantasy that the people, histories and needs from which it arose can be expelled from the real. It returns each institution, party and claim of authority to the people, labour, histories and material conditions it cannot own, and asks whose labour sustains the form, whose exclusion secures it, [which office authorises it](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#formation-and-enframing), which record makes it claimable, and by what path the governed may contest and alter it. It is a view from within the field and absolves no power, the practical condition under which a collective need not become a crowd in order to act together: reciprocal contestability, provenance and return.

## Computation, return and the counterfeit token

In a divide-and-conquer algorithm the combine step brings the subproblems' answers back into the problem they were cut from, and in a larger process everything that lets a result carry its provenance does the same work. A result that keeps the problem it answered, the alternatives it excluded, the inverse reading that could contest it and the larger state it changed can return, and a result stripped of these can only be obeyed. [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|The runtime]] **extends** this to Computational Process Ontology, whose primitives are the two logics: dia makes the local branch actual by distinguishing, comparing, excluding, ranking, routing and deciding, and syn keeps the branch's belonging to a wider field by relating, preserving provenance, inverting, recombining, contextualising and returning. A runtime of pure division atomises, and a runtime of pure gathering never decides. *Agentworld* locates the intelligence of an agentic society in the composing step: "Long horizons are a property of groups," Bratton writes, and what carries a long task is "the composite's capacity to recompose itself around an evolving problem, passing coherence from sub-agent to artifact to sub-agent without meaningful loss". In these terms the sub-agent is a cut, the artifact passed between sub-agents is the token across the gap, and the coherence that passes without loss is the retained seam. *Agentworld* raises without answering the question this movement keeps asking: who holds the combining step? An ensemble whose recomposition is performed by every participant with provenance visible to each is concurrence; one whose recomposition is retained by an operator, while sub-agents stay divided and their outputs pass through a channel only the operator can read, is divide and conquer with a better interface.

Return is an operation, and a file cannot perform it. In a constructed case, a research agent's reports carry beside each claim the retrieved page it rests on; a reviewer who finds a page that does not say what the claim says can have the fault recorded, so that the report is corrected and the next run proceeds under the same rule for what counts as a source, or can reach the criterion or permission that let the page count as evidence and change what the harness accepts as a citation from then on. Only the second is a return, because only there does the consequence arrive where the next act is decided. Maternity bands are read, and an account whose provenance is perfectly recorded and never consulted has kept the seam as a museum keeps a broken pot. Synthetic media are the token's modern counterfeit, presenting themselves as the half of a token whose other half never existed; once a culture knows the seam can be fabricated, a genuine record can be refused by claiming it was fabricated too, the advantage Chesney and Citron named the liar's dividend. Provenance systems, signatures and content credentials strengthen the conditions under which a sign returns to its source, yet none manufactures trust from inside the mark: somewhere a person or an institution has to stand surety for the account, and who that is and what they risk is the question §2 · #3's myth puts at its centre.

## Tension / limit

Syn-ballein's failure is **fusion**, relation preserved only by erasing the distinctions through which its terms could meet. A fused relation has no seam left to read: the two bands melted into one would match nothing, a marriage in which one partner's preferences have become the other's has stopped being a relation between two people, and a collective speaking with one voice because the others have learned to keep quiet has abolished the differences from which a reply could come. Fusion presents itself as love, consensus or the overcoming of division, and can be welcomed by those it absorbs; the test is the token's: can the halves still be told apart, and can one still refuse, answer back or leave? Carriers: Heidegger's derivation of *legein* is contested and carries the boundary his house states; the derivation of *conquer* through Old French needs an etymological dictionary card, and the Lewis and Short and LSJ entries are read without pages; Chesney and Citron has no source house; the research-agent case is constructed and rests on no source.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#operations) **qualifies** gathering through what it preserves: both determinate orientations, their inverse relation and the conditions under which their result can be contested, so that concurrence needs no single interchangeable voice and the achieved agreement still lets participants differ and their difference change the common undertaking. The [Symbol / Account / Trust whole-field — Symbol answers to source](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source) **qualifies** this operation at evidence register 3: the matching seam keeps an achieved sign answerable across separation, and provisional anchors stay able to receive a changed encounter, with the consumer's own source and evidential limits retained. A token's seam eliminates no power and guarantees no trust, and the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-dialogue) **qualifies** this operational comparison.

## Transition

Dia-ballein measures a real difference across a shared zero and collapses when it reads the zero as an ending; syn-ballein keeps the relation every measurement presupposes and collapses when it erases the difference that made the pair a pair. A life without the cut could not refuse and a life without the seam could not belong. What neither notation shows is what these operations feel like to the people performing them, and what happens when the two meet in one bed. The retained branch **returns-to** [Count-to-Account](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-to-account) when its source, exclusion and consequence remain available for the next act of gathering, and Greek myth gave the meeting of cut and seam a song: [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3 — Ares, Aphrodite, and Harmonia]].
