## Movement thesis

Syn-ballein, which the essay's links still carry as [[section-rooms/arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn|Sym-Ballein]], writes superpositional polarity as inverse orientations of the retained ground–mark relation:

$$
\operatorname{syn\text{-}ballein}:\frac{(0/1)}{(1/0)}.
$$

Each inner slash holds the indefinite and the definite together, and the outer slash relates their conjugate orientations. `0/1` is ground becoming legible through a mark, and `1/0` is the mark returning toward a ground it cannot convert into content.

Superposition belongs first to the dual-non-dual ontology. Ground and manifestation are real distinctions within one appearing-act, and each is present only through the relation that prevents it from becoming self-sufficient, because separately complete substances would already presuppose the relation being derived. QL gives this co-presence a mathematical and symbolic articulation native to the claim, and quantum state space and non-classical logics enter later as formal neighbours whose similarities and differences can be stated once the ontology has established its own meaning.

## AND/OR logic

The slash operates as AND/OR. The terms are conjoined because neither exists as the present relation without the other, and they are disjoined because ground is not mark and mark is not ground. Superposition therefore means simultaneous relational presence, and it is a different thing from probabilistic blur or fusion. At the inner level `0/1` is origin-to-expression, Power-to-Name, the field becoming a determination, and `1/0` is expression-to-origin, Name-to-Power, the determination becoming transparent to its field. At the outer level the two readings form one event and stay irreducibly inverse. The slash is the quotient in both senses: division discloses the relation, and quotienting constructs a new space by declared identifications.

## Symbolic force and fusion boundary

Syn-ballein is the root of symbolon: a token broken in two whose halves authenticate one another across separation, distance and time. The symbolon is materially a trust-device, and its fracture verifies the bond. Wholeness is fracture retained as relation, and pristine identity is a different thing.

Syn-ballein-mode knowledge offers provisional anchors. Embodied competence, relationship, lineage, land, ritual, family, source and institution can distribute the conditions in which uncertainty is borne, so that no single object occupies the whole. [[section-rooms/arguments/A23-Trust-Faith-and-the-Formal-Limit|Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]] names the living relation those anchors serve, and [[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|Mono-Poly: Whole and Many]] names the social topology in which many anchors need not be absorbed by one false whole.

In technical systems the same principle becomes provenance, reversible traversal, visible alternatives, inspectable seams and plural governance, so a decision can stay decisive while carrying where it came from. Syn-ballein is accordingly the compositional half of [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|the runtime]]. It keeps dia-ballein's branch and makes the branch carry the problem it answered, the alternatives it excluded, the counter-reading that can contest it and the wider state it changes. The result is an epistemic object whose relation to its own conditions stays executable, and a metadata log records the trail without performing the return.

Its danger is fusion, the fantasy that shared ground abolishes different positions or dissolves power, and syn-ballein stays healthy only while the seam stays visible. Syn-ballein is the logic of defining towards wholeness: the accounting in which a determination retains ground, inverse and the relations through which it can complete as `0/1 → 4+2` and release. A claim to have achieved the whole would already be a `1` occupying the zero, and the retained seam keeps wholeness a direction of definition and no possession. The Greek register holds this natively in adjacent fragments of Heraclitus. B51's backwards-turning fastening gives retained tension its instrument in bow and lyre, and B50, in Heidegger's rendering of *legein* as "the Laying that gathers" (*die lesende Lege*, *Early Greek Thinking*, p. 66), gives Logos the shape of a gathering that lets what it gathers lie together before us without consuming it. One movement ahead waits the gathering's counterfeit: the net that gathers to arrest.

## Divining the third: divine and concur

Syn-ballein inserts no third party at the centre of an already-given left/right spectrum. It divines the third: the transcendental relation that makes the spectrum, its institutions and its antagonisms possible in the first place. `0/1` is the common ground taking determinate form, and `1/0` is the formed determination returning to the ground it cannot own: people, labour, memory, material conditions and exclusions. The third is therefore no neutral party. It denies none of empire/subject, institution/citizen or rich/poor, it makes their unequal co-production visible, and it reveals where authority has detached itself from the conditions it governs.

"Divine and concur" names this method. To divine is to find, in the apparent self-sufficiency of a pole, the relation it has obscured or appropriated. To concur is for differentiated positions to move together through the ground none possesses, which is more than agreement, fusion or a settlement at the midpoint. The unitive praxis is the continual reorientation of `(−1)/(+1)` through `(0/1)/(1/0)`: opposition stays articulate, and each pole becomes answerable to the relation it neither created nor can exhaust.

The political difference is exact: divide and conquer makes the wound in relation governable: atomise persons, bind them to a target, and offer the apparatus as their only shared subject. divine and concur reverses the direction, returning the apparent subject (State, Party, market, institution, empire) to the determinate relations from which it draws force and legitimacy. It asks whose labour sustains the form, whose exclusion secures it, [which office authorises it](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#formation-and-enframing), which record makes it claimable, and by what path the governed may contest and alter it. It is a view from within the field, and it reconciles nothing at the price of absolving power. It is the practical condition in which a collective need not become a mass in order to act together: reciprocal contestability, provenance and return.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#operations) **qualifies** gathering through what it preserves: both determinate orientations, their inverse relation, and the conditions under which their result can be contested. This is why concurrence needs no single interchangeable voice. The achieved agreement must still let the participants differ and let their difference change the common undertaking, and Greek agreement, the historical token and the native slash each keep their own warrant, so that neither lexical kinship nor an agreed outcome performs the source-return alone.
