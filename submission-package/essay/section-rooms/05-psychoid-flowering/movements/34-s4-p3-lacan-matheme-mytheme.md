## Claim
The matheme compresses an invariant relation for transmission, and the mytheme lets that relation be lived, remembered and transformed through image. Neither suffices alone, because the barred subject marks that no formalisation closes over its own condition, so the transmission has to pass through both.

## Warrant
Lacan contributes the signifying cut, the quilting signifier `S1`, the subject barred by language, the logic of the not-all, and topology as a way to write relations that ordinary description obscures. Together they form a rigorous language of incomplete formalisation. The crossed zero can now unfold beyond its first graphic appearance as a psychoid sequence:

$$
0\rightarrow Ø\rightarrow X\rightarrow Ø/X\rightarrow (0/Ø)/(1/X)\rightarrow 1
\quad\curvearrowright\quad 0/1.
$$

| Sign | Office in the sequence |
|---|---|
| `0` | The unobjectifiable *kartṛ*-pole before it is mistaken for one object called "the subject". |
| `Ø` | That pole under the stroke of the *antaḥkaraṇa*, occluded by the inner instrument's identification with a determinate self-image. |
| `X` | The object-world deposited by discriminating activity. |
| `Ø/X` | The bar made visible as *pramāṇa*: the apparent subject and its world are recognised as the two deposits of one cutting and relating act. |
| `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` | The bar deepened into a meta-relation, in which unoccluded and occluded subject-side, integrated and differentiated object-side, and their inverse readings become available together. |
| `1` | Achieved recognition, which returns to `0/1` because manifestation was always *kartṛ/kārya*, knower and known held through the act of knowing. |

Lacan gives the local cut its precision: the barred subject, the signifier that quilts a field, and the lack no final signifier repairs. QL carries the cut through an ontological account of the thread it cannot signify. `S1`, `Ø` and `{0}` belong to distinct formal traditions, and each performs the structurally exact work of a first mark instituting a field whose ground it can gesture toward and cannot become.

## Tension / limit
`1/0` is an Argued resonance with the signifier's failed attempt to close over its own ground or lack. The bridge is the essay's, and Lacanian formalisation is argued with and is no retrospective endorsement of QL.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#e5-whole-returns) **qualifies** the transmission by keeping the work of each mark and image distinct. Lacan's cut and barred subject belong to their source-specific account, and the [native QL sequence carries its own positive source-relation and recognitive return](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source). A shared stroke or an incomplete formalisation is a common feature of two accounts, and the doctrines stay their own. Mytheme can supply the imaginal source of a matheme, and the resulting formal expression can return to change the image's reading, so the whole lived carrier is never an example exhausted by its notation.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** the stroke that first hides zero becoming the bar through which its occlusion can be seen. **Topological anchor:** lack written as structure; the local cut held by a global thread. Matheme and mytheme meet in [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|§4 · #4 — Apollo Through Dionysus]].

Lacan 1971–72, Price 2017, Darmon 1992 and Adleman/Vanderwees 2021 are separate carriers, and the native QL sequence is no Lacanian notation; the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-address) **qualifies** this historical attribution.
