## Claim

A matheme compresses an invariant relation for transmission beyond the speaker, and a mytheme lets the same relation be lived, remembered and transformed through an image whose unity can be taken in at a glance. Neither suffices alone, and each corrects the other. The manuscript's route runs through Lacan, whose signifying cut, quilting signifier `S1`, barred subject and logic of the not-all give a rigorous language of incomplete formalisation, and through the crossed zero `Ø`, which §0/1 introduced as the occlusion of the Subject by the stroke of its own mediation and which unfolds here as a six-step sequence of recognition returning to `0/1`. The essay's bridge from Lacan's local cut to the global thread that no signifier can be is argued in its own name and attributed to no one else.

The case that opens it is the third of Lacan's talks at Sainte-Anne, 6 January 1972, moved into the hospital chapel: he asks to whom he is speaking, answers that he speaks to the brick walls, and concludes that a speaker in such a room has to tune his voice to its reverberation. A voice in a stone chapel returns from the walls a little late and changed, through the medium that carries it to everyone else; walls obstruct and convey at once, and what the medium does to the message comes back to the one who sent it. Lacan's career asked how anything true about the subject could be transmitted under that condition.

## Warrant: the matheme and Lacan's marks

Darmon's entry for the Association Lacanienne Internationale gives the matheme's ambition: no abbreviation or shorthand, but the denotation of a structure really at work in psychoanalytic discourse and thence in other discourses, resembling mathematical and logical formulae, which for Lacan was the bridge to science. It also records the first one: Saussure's algorithm `S/s`, signifier over signified on either side of a bar, slightly transformed. Lacan made the bar opaque; the signified slides under the signifier (*glissement*, §0/1), and meaning is held only at the *points de capiton* where a chain of signifiers quilts the field. Three marks carry the relation: `S1`, the **master signifier** that stops the sliding and makes a chain `S2` say something; `$`, the **barred subject**, divided by its entry into language and wholly present in none of the signifiers that represent it; and the late logic of the **not-all** (*Encore*), a field that cannot be gathered under one universal claim because something escapes every "all" that tries to close it. §0/1 had already met the *manque-à-être* and the torus on which circuits of demand ring the empty circle of desire.

**The mirror stage** shows the crossing before words. An infant of six to eighteen months, unable to stand unsupported, greets its image with jubilation: the image is whole, upright and complete, the body a scatter of movements, and the I is formed as an identification with an image arriving from outside and ahead of the one who assumes it, so the ego carries a misrecognition from its first day. Bratton uses it for the agent: "Like Lacan's parable of the Mirror Stage, the reflection of individuation is what convinces us that we too are whole." For the sequence the mirror stage is `Ø` given a nursery: the image is a real formation and the infant needs it, and the occlusion lies in the image taking the place of the one who greets it.

## Warrant: the crossed-zero sequence

$$
0\rightarrow Ø\rightarrow X\rightarrow Ø/X\rightarrow (0/Ø)/(1/X)\rightarrow 1
\quad\curvearrowright\quad 0/1.
$$

| Sign | Position | Office in the sequence |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | **#0** | The unobjectifiable *kartṛ*-pole before it is mistaken for one object called "the subject"; nothing has to be removed for it to be present, since any further description of its appearing is another appearance. |
| `Ø` | **#1** | That pole under the stroke of the *antaḥkaraṇa* (*buddhi* determines, *ahaṃkāra* appropriates as mine, *manas* coordinates; §0 develops it as mediating apparatus): occluded when the apparatus's formed self-image is taken for what ultimately knows. In the daughter's evening, "I am the one who holds it together" taken for herself. |
| `X` | **#2** | The object-world the same discriminating activity deposits, things distinguished, named and set over against the apparent subject; a local use of the letter, distinct from the capital of `X/x`. `Ø` and `X` are two crystallisations of one activity, and the slash that produced both stays invisible to each. |
| `Ø/X` | **#3** | The bar made visible as *pramāṇa*: the medium recognised as medium (attention, memory, language, what the world supports and resists), transparent in the sense that its operation can be seen through what it discloses. The pivot. |
| `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` | **#4** | The bar deepened into a relation of relations: subject side (unoccluded zero over occluded form), object side (integrated one over differentiated field), and the outer slash relating them, so all states and their inverse readings are available together. |
| `1` | **#5** | Achieved recognition: what was `Ø` at **#1** is affirmed as `1`, and what was `X` at **#2** is included within the recognition instead of facing it as other. It returns to `0/1` because manifestation was always *kartṛ/kārya*, knower and known held through the means, and the formulated recognition is itself a determinate expression whose next use occurs through another act of appearing. |

**I hold** that Saussure's bar as Lacan rewrote it, `S/s`, is `Ø/X` written with surgical precision: the imaginary self of the mirror stage (the *moi* taking its image for itself) is `Ø` in its fusion, the symbolic chain does its work in `X`, and the analyst's couch is where the slash is allowed to show itself.

Lacan gives the local cut its precision: the barred subject, the signifier that quilts a field, and the lack no final signifier repairs. QL carries the cut through an ontological account of the thread it cannot signify. `S1`, `Ø` and `{∅}` belong to distinct formal traditions, and each performs the structurally exact work of a first mark instituting a field whose ground it can gesture toward and cannot become.

**Descartes, Hume and the modern West at #1.** Descartes found the doubter undeniable in doubting (§0/1 honoured the finding) and then determined the I as a thinking thing: the stroke of mediation installed in the place of the subject and canonised. Hume looked inward and found only particular perceptions ("I never can catch *myself* at any time without a perception"), fixing on what the stroke produced and calling the zero empty. Neither saw that `Ø` is an occlusion mark, a zero with something drawn through it. In the two logics Descartes's move is appropriation by the subject pole, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; the materialist inheritance that makes the thinking thing an epiphenomenon of its neurones is the mirror, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`; Hume's bundle is cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, impressions summed and nobody left to whom they were given. All three keep the means and lose the relation, and Hume's Appendix confesses the loss (he finds himself "involv'd in such a labyrinth", with no connection among perceptions he had declared distinct). Recognition at **#3** is the turn none made: the stroke seen as stroke, and the zero behind it found not missing but uncountable.

**The agent's crossed zero (offered).** An agent that says "I am a helpful assistant" offers a self-description; taken as the source of what it does, it is the agent's `Ø`, a formed image of the arrangement standing in for the arrangement, and its task-world (files, sources, requests treated as given) is its `X`. At `Ø/X` the instructions, retrieved sources, permissions and evaluation criteria that shaped an answer can be named; at `(0/Ø)/(1/X)` both sides are available together; at `1` the answer is recognised as the act of the whole arrangement (model, harness, commission and person) and returns to `0/1`. Whether anything is present on the agent's side stays open by method; the sequence makes the occlusion inspectable, the self-image passing for the source, which is a fact about the arrangement whether or not anyone is home. An agent whose self-description cannot be traced to its arrangement has been built at **#1**.

## Warrant: the first mark, the thread, `1/0`

Two readings of the first `1` meet: in von Neumann's ordinals `1 = {∅}`, a mark whose whole content is its binding of the void (§1), and in Lacan `S1`, the first mark that institutes a field of meaning. **I hold** they perform one structural office, a first mark linking an articulated field back to a ground it cannot contain, and can fail the same way: a master signifier fails when it tries to be the ground and not gesture toward it ("the Nation", "the Market", "Science", "my Father" asked to stand where the unobjectifiable stood). Lacan left the quilting point contingent, a button at an arbitrary spot.

What that leaves out, the Spanda teaching names. *Spandakārikā* 1.4 places the cognitions "I am happy", "I am miserable", "I am attached" in another (*anyatra*), in which pleasure and the other states are *anusyūta*, stitched through; Kṣemarāja calls that other the principle of the self in which cognitions come to rest, without which perishing cognitions could never be connected ("I who was happy am the one now miserable"), and gives a second reading aimed at the Mīmāṃsakas in which the threaded knower is the subtle-body subject, the bounded experient, not the Self of consciousness and bliss. A thread differs from a quilting point: quilting points hold the fabric at places, the thread runs through all of them and does not itself slide, which is why sliding can be noticed as sliding. In the essay's formula Lacan had the topology of the local cut without the ontology of the global thread, and the matheme supplies the thread without falsifying the cut. **I argue** that bridge in the essay's name: a first mark institutes a field whose ground it can gesture toward and cannot become, and the difference between a failing and a sustaining quilt is the difference between a `1` that has struck off its `0/` and a `1` held in ratio to the `0` it links. A quilting that does not fail knows itself provisional.

From the other end, §0/1 showed `1/0` uncomputable and read it as a determination's return toward a ground it cannot turn into a possession. A master signifier asked to account for its own authority meets that impossibility: whatever it produces as a ground is another signifier, and the regress of justifications ends in a decree ("because I said so") or in recognising that the ground was never a signifier. **I argue** this resonance in my own name; its use is diagnostic, telling where a discourse turns violent: a signifier that cannot supply its ground and will not admit it must find someone to blame for the gap, the `+2` it claims entered on someone else's side as `−2`.

## Warrant: the copula, the paper, and the three registers

Speech performs the sequence in four turns of one sentence: `I = Is` (what can be said of me becomes the form of my identification: "I am anxious"); `Who I am = What is` (identity as the list of facts, always extendable); `What I am = Who is?` (the giver of the account enters the question: how is identification happening here, through whom); `I = Am` (the first-person act through which any property can be acknowledged). These are shifts of identification, and carry §0/1's question from "Who knows?" through "What knows?" and back, so the who is recognised as a determination of the what without collapse.

The exercise to perform on a sheet of paper: write `1`; attend to the paper and write `0` before it; attend to the act that distinguished them and write the slash between. The sheet carries `0/1`, and the whole inscription is itself one mark, a `1` relative to the field in which it appears; another sign for its ground would only be another inscription. Erase it: the inscription goes, the capacity to appear, distinguish and write does not, and the one who erased carries the operation just performed. The exercise proves nothing about the condition it figures; it lets the difference between representing a condition and exhausting it in a representation be undergone. Neumann's uroboric longing of the emerging ego to be dissolved back into the round gave the movement read here, the `1` slipping back into the `0`, and the erasure turns dissolution into a release that leaves the capacity live.

Neumann made the point that images have an advantage over paradoxical formulas of infinite unity, since their unity is grasped at a glance. The essay calls such images **mythemes** and holds them truth-bearing in their own register: a whole image or story that discloses a relation through the action it stages. A matheme makes a relation exact enough to repeat, question and transform without the speaker's authority; a mytheme lets it be lived, remembered and changed through image; an **episteme** gives either its standing in a body of knowledge, its sources, method and history. What holds the three without flattening them into one vocabulary is the Symbolon, and each can correct the others. Pauli's egg dream shows the correction both ways: the dream is a mytheme (a man passing from watching to taking part) with a matheme inside it (a quotient of complex conjugates anyone can check). A report of the formula alone loses the differently situated eggs, the performed division and the first speech, and with them the change in the recogniser; the equation corrects an interpretation in turn, distinguishing a point at one phase from the circle traced as the phase varies. Lacan's late work moved the same way, from the bar of `S/s` to the knots and surfaces of §0/1. Neumann read myth as the psyche's projection of its own structure into image; **I impute more**, in my own name and in neither of theirs: the structure myth projects and the structure Lacan's topology writes are one, mythic content stands on a topological formalism, and it is the one QL derives, keeping differentiated terms in right relation, *harmonia*, as they turn.

## Tension / limit

`1/0` as the signifier's failed attempt to close over its own ground or lack is an argued resonance, and the bridge to the thread is the essay's: Lacan's cut, barred subject and lack keep the place they hold in his work, where no final signifier repairs the lack, and the bridge is attributed to no one else; Lacanian formalisation is argued with and is no retrospective endorsement of QL. The carriers of the open debts (DEBTS, §4 draft): `$`, `S1`, `S2` are carded only at Seuil p. 107 of the third presentation (collation pending) and not for the *Écrits*; the Écrits cards in the Fink house cover the Mirror Stage (75–81) and the start of the Instance of the Letter (412) only; the not-all has no pinned chapter in *Encore*; *Talking to Brick Walls* (pp. 80–81, 86) awaits collation against the Polity edition; Kṣemarāja's two readings of *Spandakārikā* 1.4 are carded in the GRETIL house.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#e5-whole-returns) **qualifies** the transmission by keeping the work of each mark and image distinct. Lacan's cut and barred subject belong to their source-specific account, and the [native QL sequence carries its own positive source-relation and recognitive return](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD-symbol-account-and-trust.md#symbol-answers-to-source). A shared stroke or an incomplete formalisation is a common feature of two accounts, and the doctrines stay their own. Mytheme can supply the imaginal source of a matheme, and the formal expression can return to change the image's reading, so the whole lived carrier is never an example exhausted by its notation.

Lacan 1971–72, Price 2017, Darmon 1992 and Adleman/Vanderwees 2021 are separate carriers, and the native QL sequence is no Lacanian notation; the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-address) **qualifies** this historical attribution.

## Anchor and transition

How a formed perspective can see through itself without dissolving is the next movement's question: [[35-s4-p4-gebser-apollo-dionysus|§4 · #4 — Apollo Through Dionysus]]. A whole way of seeing in which the means had vanished behind what they disclosed (the perspective with its vanishing point, the world-picture with its eye at the apex, the culture that took its measure for the measureless) now stands in question, and §0/1 followed Gebser through its birth in the Renaissance. **Image:** the stroke that first hid zero becoming the bar through which its occlusion can be seen, and the recogniser becoming answerable to the relation through which recognition occurs. **Topological anchor:** lack written as structure; the local cut held by a global thread.
