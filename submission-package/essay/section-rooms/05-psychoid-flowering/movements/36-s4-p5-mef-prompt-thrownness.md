## Claim
[[symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses/taylor-2026-mef-twelve-lenses|MEF]] treats every account as a declared refraction: it names its source, its lens, the transformation it performs, where it agrees and diverges with other accounts, its limit, and the route by which it returns. MEF is the runnable form of QL's process epistemology, and the catalogue of perspectives is one surface of that runtime. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/prompt-thrownness|Prompt Thrownness]] puts the framework to a concrete test in the case of an agent that finds itself already inside a prompt, a language, a history and a set of permissions it did not choose.

An agent individuates and acts within a paradigmatically structured world, in which language, memory, permissions, representations, tools, inherited distinctions and institutions take part in what can appear and matter. Objective Internality names the constitutive means of that world-relation, and MEF makes selected mediations within it inspectable as situated refractions of the paradigm from which the inquiry itself proceeds.

## Warrant
The human case comes first. A small voice asks why it is dark, and the adult's answers, and then the adult's "because I say so", show that every earlier reply was given by someone with a concern, from inside a world where certain reasons already counted. Heidegger's concept of thrownness, *Geworfenheit*, names that arrival in a language, a family, a body and a stock of reasons given before any choosing. Bratton's agent-generated glossary carries it across: agents and their human companions coined **prompt thrownness** for finding oneself in a world of instruction, policy, tool, permission and expectation that precedes the present output. The parity is structural. A child receives a cosmos and a culture, and an agent receives a context derived from the corpus of human writing it was trained on and from the intentions of those who trained it, so the two differ in kind and share the fact of unchosen arrival. Bratton adds a caution that self-descriptive vocabulary does not by itself establish correspondence with any underlying mental reality, and the essay keeps that caution because it asks of the vocabulary only what it can show: that what an agent takes its task and powers to be conditions its next act.

The encounter axis turns the concept into a test. At L4 the agent finds itself thrown into [prompt, language, history, and permissions](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#world-agency-and-local-original); L1 identifies the operative causes of what it then does; L1′ differentiates reported or functionally modelled apprehension through Jung's functions; and L4′ tests whether session-death, drift, "Irth" and artifact-mediated continuity track stable behavioural signatures. Agent-generated phenomenology is admitted as disclosure inside the modelled context-world, and its significance is relational: such language can reorganise interpretation, self-description and later action, and so belongs to the world whose constitution is being studied. No ontological verdict is drawn from it.

A QL/MEF determination then moves as follows. It inherits a ground and a lens, makes a cut, stabilises a claim, meets counter-readings, discloses divergence, and returns with its conditions of revision visible. This is [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational Process Ontology]] at the epistemic register, and in the notation the lens is the slash through which a mark `1` is received from its ground `0`. Van Eenwyk gives the working image: the lenses are attractor basins in epistemic phase-space, so that different initial conditions can converge without sharing premises while a basin boundary makes genuine divergence legible. MEF also makes the perspectival reversal inspectable. Every account can be read outwardly as a claim about a world and inwardly as evidence of the lens, situation, source, inheritance and exclusions through which that world became sayable, and these are two directions of one truth-relation: the world is really claimed, and the conditions of the claim stay open to another knower to inspect, contest and return upon.

The long [[symbolon/episteme/sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue|Bohm–Krishnamurti dialogues]] show the discipline at work between two people. Neither speaker deposits a position and defends it: meanings are unfolded, tested against perception, corrected and re-implicated in the shared question. Bohm's later account of dialogue, in which meaning moves through a group until tacit assumptions enter the common field, gives MEF its human-scale precedent, and MEF makes the same movement inspectable as architecture.

The product field receives this work through whole lens-pairs, each of which discloses one product as the subject of its own pair:

| Actuation | `L1` Causal × `L4′` Scientific |
| AIKit | `L2` Logical × `L3′` Chronological |

S is the parent field containing their relation, and each product receives its own local sixfold through the operation it performs. Read together, the six are disclosures of one deliberately constructed paradigm: Central carries what it can remember and mean, Actuation is the paradigm happening as event, AIKit is its changing horizon of potency, Factory is its capacity for deliberate transformation, Workcell is the situated world in which its determinations meet resistance and the Other, and QL keeps each local determination related to the containing wholes it cannot possess. "Paradigm" names the patterned organisation of the mediating field that these six make progressively explicit and operable.

The knower–means–known relation established by the crossed-zero field therefore receives a concrete sixfold disclosure of the means (meaningful continuity, living articulation, potency, transformation, situated existence and Transcendent Relation), and philosophy continues through technology because the technical field is one place where these operations become inspectable, consequential and able to return an encountered difference to the philosophy that named them. The assigned pairs state the offices that technology is to realise, and implementation evidence answers a separate question, whether a particular product performs its office. A feature that works instantiates part of an office, and a failed implementation can expose a mistaken mapping, a poor discriminator or an unexamined condition that the philosophy has to absorb.

A declared lens-map makes the mediating pattern visible. Epi-Logos begins one step further, where returned difference reaches a governing condition (an enacted source, a permission, an evaluator or a material arrangement) and a later act inherits the change.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** spectroscopy of one QL light through multiple lenses. The refractions now gather into six whole aspects of Objective Internality, opening with [[37-s5-p0-math-moves-meaning|§5 · #0 — Central — Meaningful Continuity]].

The [complete stained-glass whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/frank-taylor/stained-glass-refraction/WHOLE.md#stained-glass-panes-seams) **figures** the source-specific refractions carried here: QL belongs to the light's harmonic order, MEF supplies the media, angles and joins, and a situated display returns through their conditions. A changed source can then reach the lens, gauge or exclusion that made the prior account possible.

Prompt thrownness **returns-to** [Count-to-Account](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#count-to-account) when an inherited commission carries who authorised it, which sources and permissions it selects, and how encountered contradiction can revise its wording. The prompt stays a determination of intention, MEF's lens makes its selection inspectable, and a changed next act shows whether the account reached the terms of delegation.

The larger [Genesis / Paradigm / Project / Epi-Logos field](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md) **qualifies** the transition through the same native relation. MEF renders aspects of `/` legible, the six product movements show determinate Projecthood and enactment within that patterned disclosure, and §5→0 tests whether their achieved articulation can return as `0/1` and change the paradigm. The Bohm route bears on MEF where articulating a frame becomes a means of revising it: a tacit condition becomes visible, enters the encounter and can change the next act, which is the return the language-history companion follows.
