## Claim
Jung and Pauli approached a domain in which a psychological image and a physical event could not be causally reduced to one another and still displayed meaningful structural correspondence. The psychoid names the neutral or transgressive ground that problem requires, and QL gives that common movement a formal place: the Arche-Topos is a differential field prior to the ordinary psyche–matter division, able to receive distinct psychic and physical descriptions without assigning either final priority.

## Warrant
[[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/psychoid-number|Psychoid and number]], [[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/jung/jung-pauli-meier-2001-atom-archetype/jung-pauli-meier-2001-atom-archetype|Jung and Pauli]], archetype and synchronicity disclose the historical problem as a neutral or transgressive ordering ground across psyche and matter. Its two sides arrive from opposite directions. Pauli's exclusion principle determines each quantum state by exclusion, and Bohr's complementarity shows two descriptions of one object that exclude one another within any single arrangement and are both required. Jung's psychology has no Archimedean point outside its object, so every experience is a psychic fact before any question of cause is settled, and the mandala's empty centre shows opposites held around what cannot be filled. Each found, in his own register, that classical either/or could not describe what stood in front of him, and both located the meeting point in number.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm's common implicate order]] gives that ground a process form: mind and matter can be approached as distinct explicate orders whose relations a deeper holomovement carries internally, before any external comparison. The tattvas give that implication an anatomy, which Bohm deliberately leaves general, and QL gives its differentiation and return a notation. The [[section-rooms/arguments/A16-Arche-Topos-as-Differential-Field|Arche-Topos as Differential Field]] derives the native coordinating structure through the complete theorem braid, and the essay **argues that this derived structure is psychoid**: its organising relation precedes the split whose two sides later disclose it differently.

## Tension / limit
The positive claim is ontological and cross-register: the derived differential field is the psychoid architecture through which psyche and physis can be differentiated without becoming unrelated substances. Each witness contributes according to its own operation. Equations carry formal invariants, psychic images carry experienced meaning, synchronicity raises the question of acausal coordination, and physical observations bear empirical claims, and their difference is what lets the psychoid thesis organise a real crossing across them in place of a flat resemblance. Synchronicity and archetype enter in the source language of Jung and Pauli, and the essay coordinates formal and imaginal patterns at the level where the psyche/matter cut is itself under examination.

[Homologia / Analogia](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/homology-and-analogy/WHOLE-FIELD-homology-and-analogy.md#research-boundary) keeps the crossing exact by asking what each witness actually carries. The psychoid identification is the essay's argued coordination of the derived field across psychic and physical registers, and it is stated here at its own strength. A measurable causal process common to psyche and matter would be a further empirical discovery with its own evidence, which the ontological relation does not wait on.

The same field preserves the positive idealist order developed later: Subject / Consciousness → Mind / Objective Internality → Object. The psychoid field concerns how differentiated descriptions belong to one deeper ordering relation, and carrying that claim requires no separate machine-localisation problem.

**QL anchor:** two descriptions retained across one seam. **Image:** a pattern appearing on both sides of an unmastered threshold. The historical entry is psychoanalysis's formal admission of an unconscious before Jung insists that religious images are psychic facts and objectification cannot exhaust them. `X/x` gives the essay its own exact relation of determining capacity and local manifestation, and Jungian individuation becomes one psychic refraction of that relation in the next movement.
