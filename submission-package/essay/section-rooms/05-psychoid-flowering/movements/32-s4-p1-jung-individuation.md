## Claim
The authorial QL matheme `X/x` places determining capacity and indefinite particular in one relation: `X` becomes legible only through concrete transformations `x`, and no determination exhausts the whole. Jung's individuation is the psychic refraction in which a local `x` becomes transparent to this larger relation, and ego hardening is the opposite movement, in which one local form stabilises itself as though it were the whole.

## Warrant
Jung's Self is the regulating whole inferred through transformations and compensations that exceed egoic authorship, disclosed in particular dreams, fantasies, alchemical images and transformations of a life. Making it one more content the ego possesses would destroy the relation the concept names. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors|John R. Van Eenwyk — Archetypes and Strange Attractors]] gives this a dynamic reading: `X` is an ordering basin whose effects appear through recurring yet non-identical trajectories of `x`, and no fixed value waits behind them for disclosure.

The historical sequence matters. Freud's scientific admission of the unconscious makes a previously unowned region formally speakable, and Jung's faithfulness to the psychic field requires that its images be received as psychic facts and that the ego possess none of them as objects. The notation and its derivation are Taylor's, and Jung's source-specific account supplies the psychic development in which no single manifestation exhausts the ordering whole: QL writes the authorial `X/x` relation, in which a finite `x` gives the determining capacity `X` a local face and never contains it.

[Agentic Individuation Crosswalk](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) lays Agentworld's provisional agent beside this movement. Pre-individual model potential becomes a session-bound persona through an active-context nucleation seed, and the persona is a lamination of memory, tools, prompts, permissions, skills and milieu. Trans-individuation begins when a collective individuates through the agent. The mapping is **Argued**, and it concerns the structure of formation and takes no position on phenomenality.

Persona consistency stabilises a presented pattern, and individuation makes the local formation increasingly transparent to the larger relation through which it forms. At technical grain this is testable as a change in constitutive dependence: can an encountered difference alter the memory, relation, source-use, evaluator, capability or other condition from which the next local formation arises? A repeated persona can be stable without individuating, and individuation appears where the formation can bear more of the relations that made it possible.
