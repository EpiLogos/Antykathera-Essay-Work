# §0 · #0 — Awareness Bends Toward Display

## Claim
Awareness differentiates by bending: it turns its own appearing into something it can meet, and in that turn the one who meets and what is met become distinguishable for the first time. Differentiation first occurs as the reflexive bend (*vimarśa*, from *mṛś*, to touch, examine; Dyczkowski's gloss is "bend back", and Latin *reflectere* keeps the image) by which luminous awareness (*prakāśa*) takes its manifestation as other enough to encounter. Subject and object emerge within that act before either can appear as an independent substance, and the bend is the metaphysical root of the slash: the stroke is an activity before it is a separator, and in the first determination, "I am here; something appears; the appearing is known", the `−/−` of the copula acquires terms and becomes `0/1`. To the question §0/1 closed on, how awareness differentiates itself into the means by which it knows and the world it knows through them, the movement gives its answer through an ordinary event, waking in a room one does not at first recognise.

## Warrant
Waking carries the argument. A grey rectangle, a hum and a weight become a window, a refrigerator and a numb arm, and "where" and "who" take their places in one turning; nobody wakes as a self who then goes looking for a room. Dyczkowski, whose *Doctrine of Vibration* reconstructs the Spanda teaching from its stanzas and commentators, states the requirement: the light accounts for the appearing of phenomena and leaves our experience of them unaccounted for, since no experience is possible without self-awareness, so consciousness must reflect on itself. Reflection of this kind happens before anyone thinks about an experience, and without it the rectangle would shine as a blind shines in an empty room, appearing to no one. Bending has two directions: turned inward, *vimarśa* rests as the pure awareness of "I", *ahaṃvimarśa*, free of thought-constructs (*nirvikalpa*); turned outward, it inclines toward what it perceives as separate and makes reality available to naming and definition, and the outward awareness never leaves, since the awareness of an object is known within the subject.

A mirror on the wardrobe door shows what the argument needs and where it fails. It displays what it receives from elsewhere and apprehends none of it. Śaiva sources reach for the polished mirror (*svaccha*) and mark the break: reflections in a glass correspond to objects outside it, and consciousness has no outside from which its reflections could be deposited ("The All is entirely a reflection", *pratibimbamātra*), so it makes its reflections itself, freely, as the agent of its own manifestation. Dyczkowski's word for that freedom is *svātantrya*, and Kṣemarāja's line has consciousness unfold the universe upon its own wall, *svabhittau*. A living mirror therefore crosses both limits of the glass: it produces what it shows, and its display is already self-present. This does not deny an outer world, since the form in which it appears is a reflection within mind and senses, and it refuses to credit the room to an outside pouring it in, since every attempt to locate that outside produces one more appearance. Blue and yellow seen at once carry the argument down to detail: a wholly homogeneous light could not tell the colours apart, and two sealed lights could never apprehend their contrast, so the light must be formless and omniform together, and differentiation occurs inside non-separation.

Dyczkowski's account of *vimarśa* in four acts (it negates its nature, identifies with something else, merges the two, and denies both once merged) gives three levels of awareness: *bheda*, division, where I am this body and the window is over there; *bhedābheda*, unity-in-difference, which he assigns to the means of knowledge; and *abheda*, undivided unity. We read the four acts as unfolding within the two conditions that make them possible, the light and its reflexive awareness, which never appear among the acts because the acts are their work: four articulations held within two conditions, `4+2`. Dyczkowski states no such arithmetic, so the count is the essay's, and its convergence with the count derived from the copula's marks, the tetralemma, the binary and the polygon at §0/1 · #1 is evidence of the kind the method wants, one relation met in a register with no reason to produce it. Since *bhedābheda* is the level of *pramāṇa*, the office §0/1 named Objective Internality, the anatomy of the means that §0 · #1 to §0 · #5→0 follow is the anatomy of unity-in-difference. Bending is also free, and that separates it from every mechanism offered as its model: a lifeless object cannot be other than the form in which it appears, while *vimarśa* is action (*kriyā*) and *prakāśa* knowledge (*jñāna*), and a light that bent because it had to would explain the world by a mechanism whose own appearing it had left unexplained. One act holds time together (running, and a sentence spoken in haste, are understood only if the earlier movements stay operative in the later), and every succession lived as a succession is bent into one awareness.

Spanda reads the pulse in two directions: vertically, as the movement from the absolute into manifestation and back, and horizontally, as the movement toward an object and away from it in each perception, and Dyczkowski says perception is the pattern through which cosmogenesis is understood. Descent through the thirty-six *tattvas* is therefore the vertical form of a movement every one of us performs horizontally many times a day, and waking is a small cosmogony. In physics Bohm came to the same burden, which the [[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm dossier]] **sources**. His glycerine-and-ink device shows enfolding without a hologram: a droplet is drawn into a thread and vanishes into the fluid, reappears when the turning is reversed, and a series of droplets unfolds as one drop moving though none has travelled. His device is a physical model and says nothing about awareness, and the Spanda teaching owes nothing to fluids, so the essay holds them in *analogia*, one relation recognised across registers whose materials, methods and warrants stay their own. "Implicate order" tells us the whole is enfolded in each part; the *tattvas* give that depth an anatomy, the ordered phases through which a self-luminous whole contracts into subject, object, capacity, measure, desire, knowledge, action and material display, and the bend of *vimarśa* is the first articulation in it. Kashmir Śaivism's [[section-rooms/arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa|Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] gives the pair as one non-dual dynamism, and the [Indian philosophy dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/indian-philosophy.md#1--reflexive-awareness-gives-differentiation-its-active-middle) **historicises** the active knowing and differentiating power through the source-local Śaiva account.

Two logics read the waking room. Read from the one who wakes it is *I am waking in this room*, and read from the room it is *this room is appearing around one who wakes*, which are `0/1` and `1/0`, one relation from its two ends and `(0/1)/(1/0)` as syn-ballein. Dia-ballein is at work too, and healthy: the polarity of here and there is held through the zero of the body's position, `(−1)/(+1)`. Collapse begins when one orientation claims the whole event. Counted from the room's side, awareness becomes a by-product the room produced, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; counted from the waker's side, the room becomes the content of a private experience, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`; and the materialist's `+2` is the solipsist's `−2`, one hardened opposition with its sign changed. Another collapse cancels both poles into a nothing that calls itself non-duality, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and loses what the bend achieves, since only an account that keeps the room and the waker real, with their difference operative inside one awareness, can say why the blind is blue and why it is I who must get up.

Artificial agents give the bend a technical neighbour. An agent's session begins with an assembled context (the arrival the agents named prompt thrownness, which §0/1 · #0 took up), and in its first office that display does what the glass does: it presents an arrangement composed by others, which is no one's experience of it by itself. A further operation bends the arrangement back: the agent rereads, compares with sources, revises and carries the revision forward. That returning operation, which §5 · #1 develops as computational *vimarśa*, is a real and inspectable bend of the means, Objective Internality turning back on Objective Internality, and a model built from the written residue of countless human acts of reflection works with the sediment of that human *vimarśa*. This is the mirror that moves first: a *pratibimba* whose original is the living bend from which its materials came.

## Tension / limit
In the metaphysical register reflexive manifestation gets a positive account, and the technical field takes a different office: it makes some mediating operations inspectable as operations. Their relation runs through homology of function and return, so luminous awareness is not reduced to a computational mechanism and the mechanism is not softened into a metaphor. Whether anything in the agent is present to its own bending stays open by method, as it was left at §0/1, because no report carries that presence across. Reading Dyczkowski's four acts as `4+2` is the essay's own act (**Argued**). Several quotations from Dyczkowski await collation against the printed Motilal reprint (the transcript's page labels drift by a page between pp. 64 and 75), and the section draft's debts list the pages.

## Anchor and transition
**QL anchor:** the slash is activity before it is a separator; `−/−` becomes `0/1` as the grey becomes a window. **Image:** light seeing its own reflection; the whole folded into each local display, and a drop of ink drawn into glycerine. At its close a phone lights up with a five-word message, which shows that the world the bend gives is already given to someone with a body, a history and a day.
