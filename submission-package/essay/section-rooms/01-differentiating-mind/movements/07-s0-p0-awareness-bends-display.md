## Claim
The first differentiation is the reflexive bend by which luminous awareness takes its own manifestation as other enough to encounter. Subject and object emerge within that act, before either can appear as an independent substance, and the bend is the metaphysical root of the slash: the differentiating activity through which terms arise and acquire a relation, prior to any line drawn between completed entities.

## Warrant
Kashmir Śaivism's [[section-rooms/arguments/A05-Prakasa-Vimarsa|Prakāśa-Vimarśa]] names luminous appearing (*prakāśa*) and its inseparable self-apprehension (*vimarśa*) as one non-dual dynamism. Blue and yellow are known as different within one cognition: a wholly homogeneous knowing could not tell them apart, and two disconnected cognitions could not apprehend their difference together, so awareness must bear the distinction without dividing into two awarenesses. A physical mirror shows what the argument needs and falls short of it in two ways. It displays an image received from elsewhere and does not apprehend what it displays, and the power that generates Śaiva reflections also hosts them, with no outside from which a universe could be deposited into consciousness. The living-mirror argument crosses both limits: manifestation belongs to awareness's own activity, and its appearing is already self-present. The distinction between luminosity and self-recognition therefore arises within one activity, and treating them as independent substances would reintroduce the second consciousness that would need a third to reveal it.

[[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm's holomovement]] carries the same process burden in a Western lineage. The whole recurrently enfolds and unfolds, and apparently separate things are relatively stable explicate forms within that movement. Left general, "implication" is a depth-metaphor, and the tattvas answer by naming the ordered phases through which luminous self-relation contracts into subject, object, capacity, measure, desire, knowledge, action and material display.

The [Indian philosophy dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/indian-philosophy.md#1--reflexive-awareness-gives-differentiation-its-active-middle) **historicises** the active knowing and differentiating power through the source-local Śaiva account.

## Tension / limit
The metaphysical register gives a positive account of reflexive manifestation. The later technical field takes up a different office: it makes some mediating operations inspectable as operations. Their relation runs through homology of function and return, so that luminous awareness is not reduced to a computational mechanism and the mechanism is not softened into a metaphor.
