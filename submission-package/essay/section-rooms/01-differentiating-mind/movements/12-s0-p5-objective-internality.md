## Claim
[[section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood|Objective Internality]] is the **constituted means through which a Life inhabits, discriminates and acts within a World**, and a mind is lived while a life is minded, so Life and Mind are two names for this means. It is internal because knowing and action always occur through a situated mediating field, and it is objective because that field has determinate relations, constraints and consequences that can become available to reflection, comparison and correction. Subjective Immediacy, Objective Internality and World are distinct offices within one whole, I-Consciousness, and the means is neither a private interior substance nor the Subject itself.

The [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|Tattvic Differential Field]] supplies one deep genealogy of how finite worldhood arises through manifestation, measure, contraction, inner instrument and recognition. The tattvic anatomy helps disclose what a finite mediating world can contain and do, and Objective Internality names the general office through which such a world becomes lived and operative.

## Warrant
In ordinary human life this means includes attention, memory, language, valuation, habit, expectation, embodiment, inherited practice, social relation and the many conditions through which things can appear as salient or actionable. None of them is the Subject in isolation, and together they take part in the world-disclosing means through which a particular Life encounters a World.

The R3 paradigmatic relation gives durable organisation within this means a precise name. A **paradigm** is a relatively stable pattern of Objective Internality: a way the mediating field has learned to disclose, organise and act in a World. An explicit worldview or self-description is one determination within a paradigm, and the paradigm is enacted in what becomes obvious, doubtful, possible, valuable, sayable and doable before anyone describes it as a theory.

Artificial agents make part of this middle office unusually inspectable. Agentworld's provisional agent is a temporary coherence of model, persona, memory, tools, permissions, objectives, relations and milieu, and the [Agentic Individuation Crosswalk](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) places this technical problem beside individuation. The configuration is one **Objective-Internal** case in which context and mediation can be instrumented, and it is a case of the means and not a definition of Mind.

The coherence is processual. Its present "world" is the accumulated effect of earlier distinctions: [selected memories, tool-mediated consequences, permissions, and environmental feedback](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#technology-inherited-field). [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational Process Ontology]] supplies a common operational vocabulary for investigating this history, and the Subject / means / World distinction keeps the shared operations from being read as an identity of the offices.

In Bohmian language a locally operative world is an explicate order: a determinate arrangement through which wider historical, cultural, bodily, technical and environmental relations become effective here. The local form is real, and calling it explicate places that reality inside the movement and relations that continually produce and transform it.

## Tension / limit
Objective Internality is broader than an inspectable state-space and narrower than I-Consciousness. Technical traces disclose genuine parts of an artificial operative interior, and they leave open its wider institutional and material conditions and the question whether phenomenal subjectivity is present; that question belongs to Subjective Immediacy, which no trace supplies. First-person immediacy, for its part, gives its mediating field no exemption from objective inquiry.

A paradigm made explicit is one more determination inside the relation it describes, and the knower stays inside its own paradigm. Reflexivity becomes consequential when what is disclosed can change the mediation through which a later act is formed.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** a lived horizon which can become partly transparent to the conditions through which it operates. **Relational anchor:** Subjective Immediacy — Objective Internality — World, within I-Consciousness. The mediating field is now positively described, and the next question is historical and formal: how did a sign capable of carrying the uncounted condition become operational? That opens [[13-s1-p0-sign-migrates|§1 · #0 — A Sign Migrates Between Worlds]].

Objective Internality **returns-to** [Encounter-in-Region](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#encounter-in-region) where language, memory, rule, embodiment and permission become conditions of an operative interior. A changed permission changes what an agent can do, and a changed inherited account changes what a person or system recognises as possible. The region is constitutive through these effects, and any inspectable account of them is a determination within the wider whole.

Objective Internality also **returns-to** [Paradigm-through-Project → Projection / Enactment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md#1--paradigm-through-project-projection--enactment): a paradigm organises the means through which possible determinations are projected and enacted. Returned experience becomes philosophically decisive when it can revise that mediating pattern and adds more than one further item to the world it already knows how to see.
