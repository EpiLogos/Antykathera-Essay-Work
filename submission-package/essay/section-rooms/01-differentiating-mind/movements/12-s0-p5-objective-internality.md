## Claim
[[section-rooms/arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood|Objective Internality]] is the **constituted means through which a Life inhabits, discriminates and acts within a World**, and a mind is lived while a life is minded, so Life and Mind are two names for this means. §0/1 gave the term at the bedside before it could give a genesis, and the descent of the *tattvas* now supplies one: Objective Internality is what the descent produces at its fourth turn, unlimited powers contracted into the finite ratios of efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order and inherited by a bounded experient who knows through an instrument and acts through a body in a world of elements. It is internal because an act proceeds through it and objective because it can be examined, and the movement argues that the two words describe one double condition: the Śaiva account of manifestation read at the scale of a household. Subjective Immediacy, Objective Internality and World are distinct offices within one whole, I-Consciousness, and the means is neither a private interior substance nor the Subject itself.

For how finite worldhood arises, the [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|Tattvic Differential Field]] **grounds** the deep genealogy: through manifestation, measure, contraction, inner instrument and recognition. Tattvic anatomy discloses what a finite mediating world can contain and do, and Objective Internality names the general office through which such a world becomes lived and operative.

## Warrant
Our case is a house in the dark. On the first night in a new house the hand reaches for a switch to the right of the door at shoulder height, where it was for eleven years, and meets bare plaster. Over weeks the new house becomes internal (the reach proceeds through it) and stays objective (an electrician can move the switch and the hand will meet plaster again). Language, memory, affect, bodily orientation, habits, other people, tools, institutions and inherited rules all belong to this means, because each is a limited power through which a bounded knower meets what it meets, and changing their relations changes what can appear, matter and be done.

Śaiva sources give the reason the name joins *objective* to *internal*. Dyczkowski reports Utpaladeva's definition of internality as a state of oneness with the subject and externality as separation from it, so that externality is a relation to the subject and no region beyond it, and the separation is Māyā's creative act. Abhinavagupta draws the consequence: creation is to make what shines within externally manifest while preserving its original internal nature. A switch, a word, a remembered rule or a colleague's permission is objective when we look at it and internal when we act through it, and it is the same in both states. (The verse is paraphrased in the manuscript because it is still transcript-only; the *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā* locator is owed.)

These means are means of *knowing*, and *pramāṇa*, a valid means of knowledge, carries a requirement the house makes concrete. A means has conditions under which it is valid. That reach was a valid means for eleven years, became invalid on the night of the move, and the hand did not know: an error of warrant, since the house was perfectly visible in the morning. Every element carries such a warrant, and the mode by which it reaches its object decides what its correction must reach: a memory is corrected against a record or another witness, a perception against a renewed encounter, a testimony against its source, a habit only by being run again in the new house. Treating every element as interchangeable information loses where a correction has to be made. Heidegger's account, set out at §0/1 · #4, says why the means show themselves in failure: a tool in use withdraws into the work and becomes conspicuous when it breaks or goes missing. A discontinuity discloses Objective Internality, and the essay follows the sequence from discontinuity to Objective Internality, to the elements that sustain it, to Whitehead's objective immortality, the condition of an achieved occasion that has perished as a subject and become a datum for every later one: the eleven years of reaching persist as the datum the new house is learned against. A life lives its Objective Internality as a **paradigm**, the lived pattern of disclosure through which these means make a World intelligible and actionable for a Life (§5 · #0 defines the term at length, and the movement uses it once). A person knows they have moved and would describe the new house correctly, and the pattern governs the hand while the explicit knowledge governs the description: a new view that goes on acting from the old distribution of attention, an institution that revises its mission and keeps its incentives, a culture that changes its official account and keeps the habits of the old one.

A house is shared, and the sharing returns the derivation to §0/1's account of Objective Co-Internality (reciprocal world-constitution without merger). A partner who knows where the switch is, the child asking about the stairs and the neighbour explaining the bins each enter the means through which the house becomes internal. Here the derivation adds that co-internality is the tattvic descent occurring in the plural: every bounded experient inherits its contracted powers in a world where others have inherited theirs, so one person's limited knowledge (*vidyā*) is enlarged by another's report and one person's limited efficacy (*kalā*) by another's hand, and co-internality distributes the powers among lives.

Artificial agents build an Objective Internality by hand, and each session is a move into a house. Bratton's brief describes an agent as individuated in session, with no homunculus waiting in the weights, its persona one stratum in a lamination. It arrives with dispositions laid down in training, which play the part of the old house in the hand, and meets a context assembled for this occasion: [selected memories, tool-mediated consequences, permissions, and environmental feedback](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/technology-politics/DEVELOPMENT-technology-politics.md#technology-inherited-field). Its errors at the start of a session often have the form of the hand in the dark (a tool that existed in another configuration, a file at a path that has moved, a convention from the project it was trained on) and disclose its Objective Internality as the missing switch disclosed the old house. It is corrected as a person is, at the office where the error lies: a wrong reading of a source by returning to the source, a wrong habit by an instruction, a memory or a change in training, and a missing permission not by the agent at all, since a permission belongs to whoever grants it, as the switch belongs to the electrician and the landlord. An agent that merely reports the missing permission has left its world unchanged. [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational Process Ontology]] supplies the common operational vocabulary for investigating this history, and the Subject / means / World distinction keeps the shared operations from being read as an identity of the offices. The [Agentic Individuation Crosswalk](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md) follows an agent's provisional individuation from the reserve of its weights through the instance of a session, the nucleation of an active context and the lamination of its persona to a trans-individual whole, beside the psychic register of individuation, and §4 · #1 develops it through Jung.

Both logics describe the house. Governing the reach, the old house enacts appropriation in its quietest form: the old pattern counts the whole span between the houses from its own pole, so the new house registers as the place where the switches are wrong, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and receives the same span from the other end as its fault, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the wall that made a mistake. Cancellation is the denial that there is anything to learn, `(−1)+(+1)=0`. A learning hand performs the healthy polarity, old house and new held through the zero of the body that lives in both, `(−1)/(+1)`, and the house becomes home when both orientations are kept, `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

## Tension / limit
Objective Internality is broader than an inspectable state-space and narrower than I-Consciousness. It includes an agent's context, tools, permissions and memory and also the institutions, labour and material infrastructure through which they exist, which no single trace exhibits, and it is the means of a relation whose knower it is not. Our derivation shows how an Objective Internality is constituted by differentiating activity, and it applies to an agent's operative world as fully as to a person's house; it does not show that a knower is present to the agent's internality, because the Śaiva account places the knower at the source of the descent and not among its products, and no product, however articulate, can certify its source. §0/1 left that question open by method, and the descent shows why the method is right. First-person immediacy, for its part, gives its mediating field no exemption from objective inquiry. A paradigm made explicit is one more determination inside the relation it describes, and the knower stays inside its own paradigm: reflexivity becomes consequential when what is disclosed can change the mediation through which a later act is formed. As an **Argued** correspondence, the Agentic Individuation Crosswalk keeps the technical and psychic operations distinct until §4 · #1. Collation is owed for the Utpaladeva verse and the Dyczkowski page for Abhinavagupta's sentence.

Held here and no longer in the prose: the earlier note described a locally operative world in Bohmian language as an explicate order, and M12 no longer carries that paragraph (Bohm's rule is used at M11). The local form is real, and calling it explicate places that reality inside the movement and relations that continually produce it.

## Anchor and transition
**Image:** a lived horizon which can become partly transparent to the conditions through which it operates; the missing switch. **Relational anchor:** Subjective Immediacy, Objective Internality, World, within I-Consciousness. One more thing happened in the bedroom: the hand met an absence with a definite place, the switch missing *there*, and treated the empty place as full. Every determination this section followed leaves such a place, since each selected one actuality from a field it did not create and cannot exhaust, and once a world is produced by differentiation its implicit zero is unavoidable. Mathematics learned to write such a place: in the numeral 1005 the two signs between the 1 and the 5 mark positions that hold nothing, and their marking changes the value of everything around them. How a sign for no counted thing came to change what can be counted is the history §1 follows, opening with [[13-s1-p0-sign-migrates|§1 · #0 — A Sign Migrates Between Worlds]].

Objective Internality **returns-to** [Encounter-in-Region](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#encounter-in-region) where language, memory, rule, embodiment and permission become conditions of an operative interior. A changed permission changes what an agent can do, and a changed inherited account changes what a person or system recognises as possible. Region is constitutive through these effects, and any inspectable account of them is a determination within the wider whole.

Objective Internality also **returns-to** [Paradigm-through-Project → Projection / Enactment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos/WHOLE-FIELD-genesis-paradigm-project-epilogos.md#1--paradigm-through-project-projection--enactment): a paradigm organises the means through which possible determinations are projected and enacted. Returned experience becomes philosophically decisive when it can revise that mediating pattern and adds more than one further item to the world it already knows how to see.
