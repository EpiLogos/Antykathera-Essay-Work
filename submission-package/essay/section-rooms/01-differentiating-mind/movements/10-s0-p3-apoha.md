## Movement thesis

§0/1 opened the cut through apoha and Spinoza together, and here apoha receives its own historical and semantic force. A word becomes determinate by excluding what it is not: "cow" is articulated as *not non-cow*, and the selected `1` is intelligible against the field it excludes. [[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/apoha]] therefore does more than explain how concepts divide a pre-given collection. A determination is semantically dependent, because the contrast through which it applies stays constitutive of its meaning.

The double negative keeps the point exact, because in the Dignāga line received through the source house, the two negations do not collapse into a positive essence, and the scholarship carries the reason: in Dignāga's formulation, "a word talks about entities only as they are qualified by the negation of other things" (*Sāmānyaparīkṣā*, via Tillemans), and the two negations in "not non-cow" are of different kinds, nominally and verbally bound. Exclusion therefore does not disappear from the account when a positive term has been selected. The achieved term carries a relation to what it excluded in becoming determinate, and that is the precise semantic claim the essay takes from apoha.

Apoha can then be set beside other Indian and Chinese accounts of conditioned relation, with each keeping its own object. *Pratītya-samutpāda* concerns conditioned arising, which is the ontogenic expression of the same law that what arises does so through conditions; Huayan mutual dependence gives a further, differently situated account of relation and identity. The author's wider braid lets these operations illuminate one another where they resist isolated self-subsistence, and the formulation that emptiness is relational fullness (*śūnyatā* as *pūrṇatā*) is the essay's argued synthesis and no translation of one tradition's vocabulary into another's. This eastern sequence is the inner epistemic and ontogenic parallel to Gebser's diaphaneity, where a perspective becomes transparent to the context that makes it possible.

Apoha also gives an epistemic route into [[section-rooms/arguments/A12-Mono-Poly-One-All-Whole-Many|mono–poly]]. The poly is a plurality of determinations whose intelligibility depends on conditions, contrasts, histories and alternatives they did not create. The mono is the essay's wider relational claim that parts become intelligible within a field prior to their later recombination, and no Buddhist doctrine is credited with it.

## Warrant and comparison boundary

[[section-rooms/arguments/concepts/apoha]] supplies the classical differential-semantic account. Later technical selection systems will give a bounded comparison in which a selected output may retain or discard information about alternatives, and that computational payoff is withheld here, because §5 must earn the concrete model comparison from its own mechanics and receive softmax and argmax as results of that work.

Phenomenal existence trusts relational context in a different register. A gesture is made because the world is assumed coherent enough to answer it, and a word is spoken because a field of shared difference is trusted enough to carry it. Analysis decomposes and recomposes that prior coherence and never manufactures the whole from nothing. This is the essay's phenomenological extension and no doctrine attributed to Dignāga.

## Consequence

Every selection leaves a remainder, and every generated `1` depends on a field of unselected possibilities. An adequate account of determination keeps enough of the excluded field, the source conditions and the comparative frame for the selected mark to stay answerable. A product that has lost them can appear self-grounding while its intelligibility rests on alternatives no longer visible.

The [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dushun/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra/dushun-cleary-2000-jewel-net-indra|Huayan jewel-net passage]] gives a distinct relational image: reflections pass through every jewel and recur within the reflections themselves. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/van-norden/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep/van-norden-jones-2024-huayan-sep|Huayan scholarship]] states the native force more carefully, since each member depends upon the others for existence and identity while remaining distinct. The essay relates that operation to apoha's constitutive exclusion and to the prior movement's world-building pulse, with the traditions and claims kept separate. [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]] later sets this living web against Hephaestus's bonds after the Homeric scene has supplied the opposing mytheme.

The [Indra's jewel-net whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-whole-return) **compares** the selected jewel's constitutive field with this movement's account of determination. Its recursive inclusion joins the authored braid, with apoha's semantic exclusion, conditioned occurrence and saṃkalpa's world-building each kept distinct, and its complete contemplation supplies the positive image before the later two-net encounter.

## Anchor and transition

The determinate term **returns-to** [Name-through-Count](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count) with the contrast through which it applies. Recovering the materially constitutive non-selection makes the chosen name answerable and leaves its positive reach intact. Apoha's semantic operation and the native count have different objects, so a shared word for exclusion does not establish their relation, and neither does an inventory of every absent possibility.

**Conceptual anchor:** a positive determination whose excluded contrast remains constitutive. **Ethical image:** every mark carries a remainder. The full contraction is compacted in [[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|§0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression]].

Pind's two passages and the secondary account of nominal and verbal negation keep distinct evidential offices, and the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-exclusion) **qualifies** this historical attribution.
