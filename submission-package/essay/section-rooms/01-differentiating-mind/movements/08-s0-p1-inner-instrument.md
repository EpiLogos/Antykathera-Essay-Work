# §0 · #1 — The Inner Instrument

## Claim
*Buddhi*, *ahaṃkāra*, and *manas* are determinate internal functions—judgment, appropriation, and coordination—available to reflection as contents and operations. The subject remains the pole under which these functions and their products appear.

## Warrant
They can be reflected upon, conditioned, and functionally decomposed. The same distinction prevents identification of an artificial agent with its model, memory, policy, or self-description.

## Tension / limit
Functional correspondence does not establish ontological equivalence between a tattvic apparatus and a computational stack.

## Anchor and transition
**QL anchor:** objective functions occupy the marked side of `0/1`. **Image:** an instrument through which, not the musician who. Its operations become world-making in [[09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa|§0 · #2 — Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa]].
