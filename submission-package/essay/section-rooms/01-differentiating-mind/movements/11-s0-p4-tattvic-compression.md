# §0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression

## Claim
Kashmir Śaivism's thirty-six *tattvas*, its "thatnesses", can be carried argumentatively as six turns, and the movement argues that these are the six the descent cannot do without: each is forced by a demand that the preceding turns leave unmet, and together they give the anatomy by which an implicated whole becomes an inhabitable world for a bounded experient and returns to recognition. Names, descent through contraction and return through recognition are the tradition's; the arrangement of the turns into the six positions of Quaternal Logic is the essay's. Making tea gives the horizontal case: a kettle clicks off in an empty kitchen, a wanting finds its object, a cup comes forward at a distance across the worktop, the situation closes around a hand, a blue tin and four minutes, and the first mouthful is savoured, so in a few seconds a whole descent has run (§0 · #0 gave the method, reading the vertical through the horizontal).

## Warrant
The derivation is stated in [The Tattvic QL Unit](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), developed as an argument in [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|Tattvic Differential Field]], and traced to [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|36 Tattvas]]. Each turn answers what the last leaves unsupplied. **#0** is forced by the demand that appearing have a condition that is not one of the appearings: *Anuttara*, "that beyond which there is nothing higher", is the condition from which subject and object arise without being the highest of the objects, and placing it above them would use the distinction it names. **#1** is forced by the gap between a condition and an appearance, since a condition that stayed only a condition would leave the kitchen dark: Śiva is the light and Śakti the power by which the light knows, shows and turns toward display, distinct within one reality. **#2** is forced by the fact that one wanting reads two ways at once, *I would like tea* and *there is the tea*: Sadāśiva holds the universe toward the I-pole ("I am this") and Īśvara from the world's side ("This am I"), the relational identity `0/1 = 1/0`, without which an account would be pure subjectivism or pure objectivism, each of which §0 · #0 showed to be a collapse. This is also the Sadāśiva sentence the *ahaṃkāra* of §0 · #1 contracts: one grammatical form serves universal self-recognition and anxious appropriation, distinguished by how much of the world the "this" is allowed to hold.

**#3** is forced by distance: for the tea to be reached, I and this must be held apart enough for one to confront the other as something to determine. *Sadvidyā* holds I and this in balance, and Māyā makes their difference operative as separation. Māyā's place in `0/1` depends on the relation read (§0/1 · #5→0): beside *prakāśa* and *vimarśa* she stands toward the measured world, and in the triad of Parā Śakti, Māyā Śakti and Nara she is the slash. Where non-dualist Vedānta makes Māyā an indefinable principle producing an illusion, the Kashmiri Śaivas hold her to be the absolute's power to appear in diverse forms, the creative freedom and the deluding power being one power named by the insight with which it is met. **#4** is forced by limit: a separated knower facing a separated cup still has no situation, and tea is made under limits. Five *kañcukas* contract omnipotence, omniscience, plenitude, eternity and unrestricted order into the ratios of a particular life: *kalā* (efficacy: this hand lifts this cup), *vidyā* (knowledge: the tea is in the blue tin, the milk is not known), *rāga* (desire from a situation felt as incomplete), *kāla* (time: four minutes), *niyati* (order: kettle before cup). In the kitchen they show as five, since supplying more of one does nothing for another, and the count is the tradition's. Whoever inherits these ratios is *puruṣa*, the bounded experient, and from here the system unfolds *prakṛti*, the inner instrument whose offices §0 · #1 followed, the senses and organs of action, the subtle qualities and the five gross elements. **#5→0** is forced by the danger of a life lived inside its contractions without knowing them as contractions, where everything §0 · #1 to #3 found going wrong goes wrong. *Pratyabhijñā* returns the contracted field to its source without undoing the contraction: nothing finite is deleted, the cup is still a cup and the shoulder still aches, and what changes is the relation of the one living through them to their source. French *savoir* from *sapere*, which joins tasting and being wise, names it for the kitchen: recognition is that taste at the scale of a life.

Institutions apportion the same five powers, which is why the sheaths are no merely contemplative anatomy. A contract of employment carries a set of *kañcukas*: what the post may do (*kalā*), what it is told (*vidyā*), what it is rewarded for wanting (*rāga*), its deadlines (*kāla*), and the chain of procedure (*niyati*). Apportionment is the condition of any work getting done and becomes dangerous where one party sets all five for another and keeps the setting out of view. On the essay's reading of Horizon the subpostmasters could act on the branch accounts and not on the system that produced them, were told the figures and not what the Post Office knew of the system's faults, and found the order of proceeding put the burden of explaining a shortfall on them: a *puruṣa* held responsible for limits it had not set and could not see, so that tattvic anatomy makes the injustice nameable as a fault in apportionment, with the authorship of the sheaths concealed.

Read through [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational Process Ontology]], the compression is a runtime cartography and no static ladder: Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative, the *kañcukas* parameterise the run, and *antaḥkaraṇa* executes determination, appropriation, coordination, memory and alternative formation within the bounded horizon. Read through [[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm]], the full field is the architecture of implication that §0 · #0 found his language lacking, and the MEF's Bohmian rule sets two implicate offices against four explicate positions, `{#0, #5}` against `{#1, #2, #3, #4}`: Anuttara and Pratyabhijñā hold the implicate offices, and Śiva–Śakti, Sadāśiva–Īśvara, Sadvidyā–Māyā and the contracted world are the four explicate turns. Its contents are generative capacities and contractions, and none is a hidden object stored behind appearances, which is why the descent is a movement and not an inventory.

Two arithmetic readings follow. Sāṃkhya counted twenty-five *tattvas* and the Śaiva tradition thirty-six, a scheme traced in the early Śaiva scriptures well before Abhinavagupta, who systematised it. Eleven are added above the twenty-five as their genesis (the five pure *tattvas*, Māyā as the hinge, the five *kañcukas*), and written as squares the relation is `36−25 = 6²−5² = (6−5)(6+5) = 11 = 5+1+5`. Twenty-five counters laid as a five-by-five square need a row of five, a column of five and the corner where they meet to make six-by-six: the Greek gnomon. We offer the gnomon as a reading of what the arithmetic shows and claims only that the step from twenty-five to thirty-six changes dimension, from a fivefold measure to a sixfold one (**Offered**; nobody in the tradition built the system this way). Thirty-six is also six times six, and the essay offers that resonance as a conjecture: Kripke's two dice at §0/1 · #1 gave thirty-six states, and the harmonic derivation of §0/1 · #5→0 divided the whole, `100`, into `64` and `36`. Reading the full field as the sixfold taken through itself would make the thirty-six a square of the compression, and the conjecture's burden is to show category by category what the square makes intelligible beyond the coincidence of the number; until that work is done the six turns are used and the square is held open.

Both logics run through the descent at the turn where the tradition places Māyā. *Sadvidyā*'s balance of I and this is a held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, and Māyā makes it operative, which is healthy dia-ballein, the cut that lets a cup be reached. Collapse comes at **#4**, where the bounded experient inherits the contractions as its own: the cup, the kitchen and the four minutes become *mine*, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the world enters as what stands against me, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, so the experient's inflated claim and the world's apparent hostility mirror each other. Cancellation dismisses the finite world as illusion, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, Māyā read as a veil over nothing, which Dyczkowski reports the Spanda teachers rejecting. Recognition performs syn-ballein: "I am this" and "This am I" are each read through the other, `(0/1)/(1/0)`. Recognition also has to be told apart from *nirvikalpa* absorption. Meditative absorption can make the inward resting the whole of experience for a time, and if freedom is made to depend on keeping thought silent, the return of a thought is felt as a loss and practice becomes one more exclusion. *Pratyabhijñā* is re-cognition, knowing again what was already known, and the knowing happens amid activity: judgment continues, the instrument goes on settling and appropriating, the four minutes run out. A calmer morning might have helped the instrument hear the five words truly, and recognition would have changed something else, the demand that the verdict settle who the reader was.

## Tension / limit
Compression has to preserve provenance, and the movement keeps the offices of its claims apart. Forcing each turn is a derivation the essay makes (its status is **Derived** as an argument about what the descent cannot do without), the *kañcukas*' five-fold count and the thirty-six are the tradition's, the arrangement into six QL positions is the essay's, and where detailed exegesis is at issue the full tradition governs. As a reading the gnomon is **Offered**, the square of the sixfold is held open as a conjecture, and the Horizon apportionment is the essay's reading of the case. A linked QL plate and further-reading note keep the thirty-sixfold system available, and the sixfold unit carries the argumentative turns the essay needs. One historical claim is loosely supported at present: that the thirty-six predate Abhinavagupta (two reviews support the scheme's evolution across the early scriptural layers), and the Dyczkowski page collation the section draft's debts list is still owed.
