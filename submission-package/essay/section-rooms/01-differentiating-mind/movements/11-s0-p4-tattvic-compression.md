# §0 · #4 — The Sixfold Tattvic Compression

## Claim
The thirty-six tattvas can be carried argumentatively as six movements: Paramaśiva/Anuttara; Śiva–Śakti; Sadāśiva–Īśvara; Sadvidyā–Māyā; the kañcukas and complete contracted apparatus; Pratyabhijñā. Together they give the anatomy by which an implicated whole becomes an explicate, inhabitable world and returns to recognition.

## Warrant
The derivation is stated in [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy#X. The Tattvic QL Unit — Sixfold Contraction and Recognition|The Tattvic QL Unit]], developed as an argument in [[Tattvic Differential Field]], and traced to [[13-tattvic-differential-field|36 Tattvas]].

Read through [[Computational Process Ontology]], the compression is a runtime cartography rather than a static ladder. Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative; the kañcukas parameterise the run as limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, and order; antaḥkaraṇa executes determination, appropriation, coordination, memory, and alternative formation within the bounded horizon. Recognition returns the runtime to its ground without deleting the world it made.

Read through [[16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return|Bohm]], the full tattva field becomes the architecture of implication. The implicate is no unspecified hidden container: it is ordered capacity for subjectward and objectward orientation, measure, contraction, cognition, action, and elemental display. The tattvas in turn become movement rather than a static inventory—an enfolding and unfolding through which the whole remains active in its local forms.
