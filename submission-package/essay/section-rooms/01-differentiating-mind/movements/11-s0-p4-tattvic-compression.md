## Claim
The thirty-six tattvas can be carried argumentatively as six movements, and together the six give the anatomy by which an implicated whole becomes an explicate, inhabitable world and returns to recognition. Each movement answers one question about how a luminous field becomes a bounded experient in a world:

| Position | Movement | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `#0` | Paramaśiva / Anuttara | Undivided luminous freedom, for which no external measuring object stands outside. |
| `#1` | Śiva–Śakti | Luminosity and its self-apprehending power: distinction is nascent within non-separation. |
| `#2` | Sadāśiva–Īśvara | The reversible emphasis of "I am this" and "This am I", written `0/1 = 1/0` as the emanative and recognitive obverses of one relation. |
| `#3` | Sadvidyā–Māyā | Balanced identity differentiates, and Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative: this appears while that is withheld, which lets an agent discriminate and act. |
| `#4` | Kañcukas, puruṣa, prakṛti, antaḥkaraṇa, faculties, elements | Five contractions make agency, knowledge, desire, time and order finite, and limitation becomes an inhabitable context-world. |
| `#5→0` | Pratyabhijñā | The contracted field recognises its ground without cancelling the reality or value of its differences. |

## Warrant
The derivation is stated in [The Tattvic QL Unit](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md), developed as an argument in [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|Tattvic Differential Field]], and traced to [[section-rooms/arguments/A09-Tattvic-Differential-Field|36 Tattvas]]. What becomes limited at every step is a power of the field, and the limit stays effective within it, so a Life never stands outside a complete world and then adopts a worldview about it: its powers, limits, means of discrimination, temporal order and material situation take part in the very form in which a world becomes available.

Read through [[section-rooms/arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology|Computational Process Ontology]], the compression is a runtime cartography and no static ladder. Māyā makes the subject/object seam operative; the kañcukas parameterise the run as limited agency, knowledge, desire, time and order; and antaḥkaraṇa executes determination, appropriation, coordination, memory and alternative formation within the bounded horizon. Recognition returns the runtime to its ground without deleting the world it made.

Read through [[symbolon/episteme/dossiers/bohm|Bohm]], the full tattva field is the architecture of implication. The implicate is an ordered capacity for subjectward and objectward orientation, measure, contraction, cognition, action and elemental display, and it holds no hidden stock of objects behind appearances. The tattvas thus become a movement of enfolding and unfolding through which the whole stays active in its local forms, and a local act can bear that whole movement: a possibility takes form, becomes a particular encounter, leaves an effect and returns into the conditions of another act.
