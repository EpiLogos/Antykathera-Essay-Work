# §0 · #2 — Vikalpa and Saṃkalpa

## Proposition — a field becomes a possible course

Mind constructs a context-world through a pulse. [Vikalpa](../../arguments/concepts/C17-Vikalpa-Samkalpa.md) cuts a field into qualified alternatives, so that a possibility becomes thinkable as distinct from another, and *saṃkalpa* gathers selected differences into an intention, a resolve, a course or a workable world. Both are formed on the root *kḷp* (fitting, arranging, framing, preparing), which Monier-Williams divides between the prefixes: *vi-* for separation and distinction, *sam-* for togetherness and completion, so that *vikalpa* is a fitting-apart and *saṃkalpa* a fitting-together. From the same root come *kalpa*, a rule or precept and also an aeon, so one family of words keeps the fitting of a thought, the rule governing a practice and the span of a world. Cognition is constructive without being arbitrary: every act inherits a field, makes a cut, and alters the field available to the next act. Construction is also answerable, because it is done in and against a world that was already there, and the movement's positive thesis is that worlds made by *vikalpa* and *saṃkalpa* are real as worlds and empty of self-subsistence, while the power that makes them is the knower's own activity in contracted form.

In the case, two children take a sofa apart on a rainy Saturday: cushions become walls, the gap under the table a tunnel, the dog a dragon, and within twenty minutes there are rules and a password. They cut (this cushion could be a wall or a boat) and they gather (*this is our castle*), and the castle redistributes the room: the dog's place changes from pet to dragon, the kitchen's from where food is made to enemy territory, the parent's from the one who decides about lunch to a grown-up who needs the password. Nothing physical moved but the cushions, and what could appear, matter and be done had all changed, which is §0/1's worldhood (relational distribution more than what is distributed) watched in the making. Their field also answers back: a cushion stands as a wall and will not bear a child as a ladder, a sheet sags, and the rule that grown-ups may not enter holds until one enters. Spanda extends the same pulse to its widest form, in which the world is the *viśvavikalpa* of consciousness made under no constraint but its own freedom, and finite awareness makes its small worlds under the constraints that the universal making has laid down as the given world: a children's castle is a local *kalpa* fitted inside a larger one.

## Warrant — the act changes what the next act inherits

Positions on what such constructions are decide what world-making can be trusted to do. In the *Yogasūtra* *vikalpa* has its narrowest sense, a cognition that follows words and has no object, and Vyāsa's example ("The true nature of the Self is intelligence", in Woods's translation) shows a genitive dividing one thing into an owner and a possession, so on this view *vikalpa* manufactures distinctions that are not there and yoga stills it. Dharmakīrti's school gave conceptual construction a wider office and a sharper verdict: it does real practical work among particulars while its general objects and relations are constructed, and MacCracken records the view of relations as "relation-as-merely-epistemic", which "deals an injurious insult to realists, many of whom were dualist Śaivas". Pratyabhijñā thinkers answered from the other side: Utpaladeva argued that relation is the real power of consciousness, Śakti, and the Spanda teachers held that the separation of subject and object is the product of a creative act of the absolute, its Māyā, and no illusion superimposed on something else. Each can be heard in the castle without flattening: the yogin is right that "the dog is a dragon" has no dragon as its object; the Buddhist logician is right that the relations were constructed and come down at bedtime; the Śaiva is right that the construction was a real power really exercised, and that the power to construct relations is no further construction some other power could take down. Our position follows from §0/1's definition of the Subject. A world-making that knows itself as world-making can build castles and take them down, and only one that has forgotten itself turns a castle into a prison.

*Saṃkalpa* has an ordinary life as a resolve and, in ritual, the declaration of purpose with which an act begins. §0/1 · #2 followed one such resolve to a bedside ("I will listen"), and the same structure remakes a world when someone decides to leave a job: nothing in the office has changed but a decision, and the decision has redistributed relevance across everything in it. This pulse also has a temporal shape, the inheritance §0/1 followed through Whitehead's satisfaction and Bergson's melody. A resolve made at one moment conditions every later moment until fulfilled, abandoned or revised, and the danger lies in the inheritance itself: a resolve can outlive its circumstance. In the morning of the message a verdict became the resolve *I will defend myself*, which hardened into a rule of the instrument, *whenever someone says "can we talk", prepare a defence*. This is the script of §0/1 in its genesis, a *saṃkalpa* whose occasion has gone and whose authority has stayed, and [the constructive course and its arrest as script](../../arguments/A07-Vikalpa-Samkalpa-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) are one operation seen at two moments, with a rule keeping useful stability while its purpose and application stay revisable. The [process argument](../../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) follows the continuing work of that inheritance, and in [Dyczkowski's account](../../../symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/dyczkowski/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration/dyczkowski-2000-doctrine-vibration.md#page-71-continuation) remembrance and imagination keep distinct offices even where both draw on retained experience.

Both logics describe what a hardened resolve does to the people involved. In its living form the castle held a polarity through a shared zero, inside and outside, children and grown-ups, `(−1)/(+1)`, through the household they all belonged to. Hardened, it counts the whole span from one pole: inside becomes everything safe, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the excluded adult becomes the whole of what threatens, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the mirror of the children's `+2`, with the household dropped from the account. A parent who declares the castle just cushions has cancelled it, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and taught the children that what they make does not count. A parent who knocks and gives the password honours the rules long enough to negotiate lunch, and both orientations are kept, the household read through the castle and the castle through the household: syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`. *Śuddha vikalpa*, the purified thought-construct, is the Śaiva deliberate use of construction against construction, and its ordinary form carries a limit on its own authority ("this one, today, has knocked and given the password") instead of the opposite rule, "grown-ups can always be trusted", which is one more castle rule. It works as a medicine dosed to the condition and can be set down, and a faithful definition keeps its determining act visible for the same reason.

## Boundary — formation, meaning and manifestation

Construction proliferates, and the Buddhist name for proliferation that has lost its limit is *prapañca*: the elaboration of concepts charged by craving, appropriation and conflict, which Ñāṇananda (quoted by Watson) glosses as thought generating thought out of its own products. Śaiva sources use the word with another value, the chain of associations by which one thought leads to the next and by which the universe unfolds. What separates them is what each product carries: proliferation stays alive while each construction keeps its provenance, its relation to the field and the power that made it, and seals itself when its products appear independent of the field and begin to warrant the next construction without contact with what first warranted them. A mind turns *prapañca* into a prison when it forgets that the dragon was the dog and builds its next castle on the dragon.

Horizon is the institutional case. From 1999 the Post Office ran branch accounts on the Horizon system and held subpostmasters liable for the shortfalls it showed; the public inquiry estimates that approximately a thousand people were prosecuted on its evidence. Each conviction counted as evidence that the figures were sound, and the Court of Appeal, quashing thirty-nine in 2021, said that as each prosecution succeeded "the asserted reliability of Horizon was, on the face of it, reinforced". A common-law presumption that a computer operates correctly unless shown otherwise, which a consultation summarised as "the computer is always right", left a subpostmaster who had not been told what the Post Office knew of the system's faults without the means of showing it, and in 2019 the High Court found that the system's bugs had "actually" caused shortfalls. On the essay's reading the office able to reopen the figures was the office whose standing depended on their soundness; in the logics the institution held the whole span of every discrepancy, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the subpostmaster stood at the mirrored pole as its entire deficit, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, while the shared zero, a branch account both had reason to make true, dropped out. Naming the collapse, the Court of Appeal wrote that the Post Office "effectively sought to reverse the burden of proof".

Notation and reading of the trajectory are the essay's. Let `F` be the determining field from which a step proceeds and `cₙ` the claim produced at step `n`; the trajectory proliferates when `cₙ` is silently promoted into the next determining field, `cₙ → Fₙ₊₁`, so that a previous paraphrase warrants the next and their agreement replaces comparison with the original: a *pratibimba* has taken the place of its *bimba*. Horizon's figures show the trajectory needs no language model, and the failure needs no false datum, since a chain of fluent, mutually supporting elaborations can seal itself while an abundant generation can remain source-responsive. Two scales stay distinct: within one run an output returning as its own premise produces circular support (the New York brief that cited opinions ChatGPT had invented, from §0/1), and across generations of training, models trained on earlier models' outputs lose the tails of the original distribution (model collapse, after Shumailov and colleagues). Six working tests ask how quickly source links and distinctions decay across steps, how much later reasoning rests on earlier outputs, how many live alternatives the trajectory keeps, whether a criticism, retrieval or contradiction can move it into another organisation, whether it can recover the determining text, datum or rule or only paraphrase its own paraphrase, and whether extraction, paraphrase, inference, proposition and speculation stay visibly distinct. A critic that repeats the generator's assumptions deepens the same basin, and an independently sourced critic can open another. These tests are the essay's offering; the section draft's debts list reconciles their count and provenance with the working-session record they came from.

Three accounts keep their own objects. Saṃkalpa gathers differences into an intention; [apoha](10-s0-p3-apoha.md) makes the excluded contrast inside a determination explicit; and conditioned-arising accounts concern the conditions through which an event occurs. A Śaiva account of manifestation adds the power through which a differentiated world appears and becomes available to recognition, and the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-exclusion) keeps the historical differences among them in view. Objective Internality names the mediating office within the whole relation of knower, means and known: a model, memory or self-description is available within those means, and the technical comparison asks how a course is formed, how it meets the world and how its consequence changes another course, while the subject remains the pole for which they work.

## A technical event carries the same question forward

A language model performs *vikalpa* and *saṃkalpa* at working speed. It selects each next token from a weighted range of continuations, and the token it commits becomes part of the context from which the next selection is made, so every output is also the field of the next act (§5 · #1 follows the mechanics). Comparison between psyche and machine becomes exact by asking what each operation does: retaining an item differs from recollecting it, recomposing alternatives differs from choosing among them, and revising a judgment differs from revising its criterion. In an agent a selected source enters context, a comparison settles an answer, and a permitted tool act changes the field to which the next act responds, so changing a retained source or permission changes which next act is available.

## Return — the weave can receive another thread

Every cut the children made left something out: the cushion that became a wall did not become a boat, and the dog that became a dragon stopped, for the afternoon, being the family's dog. A world gathered by *saṃkalpa* is gathered from alternatives that *vikalpa* opened and then set aside, and it means what it means partly through what it set aside. Horizon's figures meant what they meant against an alternative the Post Office refused to keep open, that the shortfall lay in the software, and the convictions stood as long as that exclusion held. [Name-through-Count](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count) returns the formed name and intention to their circumstance: an obstacle can expose an omitted relation, a source can correct the account made of it, and another person can question the description under which they were addressed. §0/1 · #2 met the dependence in the Buddhist theory of *apoha*, and the next movement makes it exact: [apoha](10-s0-p3-apoha.md) shows how what was excluded participates in what the determination means.
