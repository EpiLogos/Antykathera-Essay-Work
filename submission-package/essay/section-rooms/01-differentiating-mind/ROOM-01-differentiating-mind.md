## Arrival

`0` marks the condition under which counting occurs, while every counted item occupies the position of `1`.

## Section wager

Mind is the movement by which an implicated whole becomes a determinate world for a situated subject. Bohm supplies the movement of enfolding and unfolding; the tattvas supply its differential anatomy, from undivided awareness through contraction into the faculties, elements, and context-world of finite experience.

**Present burden:** derive the agent's “objective internality” from differentiating activity rather than attaching metaphysics to a pre-given machine.
