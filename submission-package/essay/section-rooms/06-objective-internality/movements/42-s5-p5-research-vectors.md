## Claim

**Quaternal Logic is Transcendent Relation:** the movement through which unity differentiates without ceasing to be whole, difference enters relation, and an internality remains open to the larger wholes in which its distinctions arise. It is the culmination of the six product movements because each previous office is already relational: a ground belongs to histories, an act exceeds itself in consequence, a capability depends on context, a transformation enters a community, and a situation belongs to a wider world.

## Warrant — Para Vāk, archetypal number and the two logics

A document shows the nesting plainly. A sentence belongs to a claim, the claim to a research programme, the programme to an institution, and each of these is a whole at its own scale and a part at the next. The same unit can be read at each scale, with its source and its disagreements intact, and what returns to a scale is that scale's own difference. QL gives the nesting its grammar, and the product office asks what keeps a whole answerable to the wholes that contain it.

The whole pair is **L5 Para Vāk × L0′ Archetypal-Numerical**, and the two lenses read one sequence of six from two sides:

| Order | `L5` Para Vāk | `L0′` Archetypal-Numerical |
|---|---|---|
| first | Anuttara / Asambhava | Unity |
| second | Para Vāk | Duality |
| third | Paśyantī | Triad |
| fourth | Madhyamā | Quaternity |
| fifth | Vaikharī | Pentad |
| sixth | Mātṛkā | Hexad |

Para Vāk gives the vertical movement from what precedes determination through undivided word-power, vision, inner articulation and expressed articulation to the differentiating matrix of manifest signification. Archetypal-Numerical gives the formal movement in which one becomes difference, difference is mediated, mediation stabilises as field, the field is developed and the whole is articulated for recursive return. QL therefore carries more than formal reasoning: it is a language for how wholes become internally structured and how a whole at one scale can enter another relation with its integrity intact. The sixfold `{0,1}` with `{00,01,10,11}` formalises this, since singles and pairings are both required, and relation holds determinate terms together with the field of their conjunction and reduces neither to the other.

The two logics become operational here. **Dia** preserves the differentiating axis `(−1)/(+1)` and distinguishes its failures: cancellation into `0`, appropriation into `+2` or `−2` (one span charged from opposite poles), or retention of the returning `/0` until recognition can become `0/1`. **Syn** reads the slash as AND/OR and holds `0/1` and `1/0` as obverse orientations whose outer relation is itself meaningful. Self and Other show both in one pair. If I count the whole of what lies between us as your departure from me, I have appropriated the span, and the same span is your debt to me from the other end. If we agree and nothing of what passed remains owing, we have cancelled it. If each stays distinct and each reads the other through the relation that holds us, we have the syn-ballein relation. These logics form a different pair from the diachronic and synchronic, since succession and co-presence can each preserve or destroy relation.

A/C's local QL constitution makes the field addressable through Subject/Ref/QLAddress; QLForm, Lens and State6; Operator and VĀK instruction; Relation, Conjugacy, Cross relation and nesting; ContextFrame and MEF address; then Synthesis, provenance, conformance evidence and returned differentiation. The governing rule is **a primitive remains alive when its generating relation remains operable — what it relates, what difference it makes, how that difference becomes actual and what returns.**

The six inherited research vectors now return to this culmination as experiments in place of sovereign movements. Seam retention, gauge disclosure, lens refraction, topological continuity, QL recursion and deferential return test whether the distinctions developed across the six products actually alter operation, and their value lies in the possibility of a contrary result: a failed implementation can expose a weak mapping, a bad discriminator or a philosophical assumption that needs refinement.

Transcendent Relation reaches its ethical strength in co-internality. Self and Other can be distinct while belonging to a whole that contains their relation, and one Life can enter another's Objective Internality without becoming its possession. The Huayan net gives the relation an image, read here as an amplification that carries the essay's Argued reading. [Indra's jewel-net whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/chinese-huayan/indra-net/WHOLE.md#indra-native-relation) **embodies** it: each jewel reflects every other, the chosen jewel stays the definite entrance, and no jewel is the exterior from which the net could be judged. The wider whole is the relation through which local differences remain real and consequential, and no final super-agent is needed to own it.

## Tension / limit

**QL is faithful to Transcendent Relation only insofar as the differences it relates remain capable of answering it.** Traditions, persons and technical systems keep source, refusal and consequence within the relation. Para Vāk and the Archetypal-Numerical lens give the product its vertical and formal disclosure, and historical Kashmir Śaivism and the QL derivation are distinct grounds whose relation is carried through the operations they make available.

Recursive and nested technical architecture is a genuine refraction of Transcendent Relation where local terms can retain integrity, enter larger wholes, and return consequences capable of changing the relation itself. The operational and ethical test is whether difference remains consequential, or whether every Other is translated into a legible term of one enclosing frame.
