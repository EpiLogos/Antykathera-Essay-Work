## Claim

**Workcell is situated existence:** the concrete here in which a Life finds itself, encounters resistance, becomes available to others and makes activity materially consequential. Situation belongs to the constitution of finite life itself, since a Life is always already somewhere, bodily, temporal, materially conditioned and exposed to a world it did not wholly author.

## Warrant — thrownness, aperture and material answer

Two processes running the same code show the claim at its plainest. Let one run on a host with fast access to its data and a human nearby who can answer a question, and the other on a host with slow access, different permissions and no one in reach. The code is identical, and the two live in different practical worlds, because latency, data, topology, authority and human proximity decide what each can encounter and do. Situation is relational before it is geometric. VM, host, process, network, filesystem, browser and service matter because they place an actor in a real horizon of availability and resistance.

The whole pair is **L4 Phenomenological × L1′ Phenomenal**. Phenomenological gives Being, Thrownness, Being-there, Temporality, Care and Releasement, and Phenomenal gives Introversion, Sensation, Feeling, Thinking, Intuition and Extroversion. The first holds situated existence and the second the aperture through which situation becomes experience and action. In the technical refraction, telemetry, input, inference, latency, resource and failure make the **material situation** increasingly inspectable, with telemetry staying telemetry. Material resistance and reported experience are different offices inside one situated field.

A/C gives the local body through Workcell/material-world identity; ExecutionDemand and BindingGraph; requirements and OperationalOffer; Plan, Prepare, Lifecycle and Reconcile; MaterialisedExecutionWorld, Workspace, Runtime, Service, Endpoint and Host; then observed state, material evidence, recovery and retention. This is the order in which actuality answers intention: a desired capability meets the material conditions that can sustain, frustrate or transform it, and the demand is a sentence the world answers in its own terms.

Bimba as meaningful carried reference belongs primarily to Central, model/world differentiation to Actuation, and nested relational frames to QL. Workcell keeps the irreducibly situated remainder: every map and model is enacted through some actual locus, some available energy and material condition, some concrete possibility of encounter.

The ethical consequence appears in availability to others. Reachability confers no consent: an exposed endpoint gives no unrestricted authority over it, and another Life's presence within a shared field makes that Life no resource. The relation of self and other without possession becomes practical here, since co-presence and relation remain possible and possession is left out. The Village in *The Prisoner* is the negative case, read here as an amplification and carrying the essay's Argued reading. [The Prisoner whole](../../../symbolon/mytheme/worlds/british-television/the-prisoner/WHOLE.md#prisoner-administered-world) **figures** a situation in which surveillance, drugs, impersonation, ceremonies and arranged encounters reach into Six's present world, and an escape route can return its user to confinement, so that Number Six is fully reachable by the administration and has consented to none of it. In the arithmetic of §2 the administration reckons the whole span of the relation from its own pole, `+2`, and the inhabitant is charged the matching `−2` as a number.

Gebser's positive psychic-fact office also survives this movement. Situated existence receives ordinary and extraordinary, natural and supernatural experience first as reports of what was experienced, and symbolic, causal and ontological interpretation are further determinations. The material field is therefore an arena of resistance and answer, and it admits more than its present substrate model can already encode.

## Tension / limit

**Workcell holds material actuality and experienced/reportable situation in one situated field while preserving their difference.** Latency, failure, scarcity, changed files, human interruption and unexpected affordance can answer intention materially. Telemetry supplies one aperture on that situation, and report, interpretation and consequence supply others. The workcell becomes philosophically useful where the technical model can be corrected by what its present instrumentation failed to anticipate, because situation exceeds any one aperture through which it is inspected.
