## Claim

**Actuation is living articulation: the event-substance of an internality.** A Life perceives, differentiates, imagines, speaks and acts from the ground it carries; through such events its world takes a particular form, meets actuality, leaves traces and changes what can happen next. Central held the ground, and Actuation is the ground becoming an event. A model's token, a person's sentence and a practical deed are different events whose relation can be investigated, and authority, delegation and composition are particular organisations of this articulation that do not exhaust it. Its anchor is the reply written several times before it is sent: an act of speech is a selection made against a field of alternatives that stays at work inside the act, and once sent it enters another life and comes back.

## Warrant — position, return, the model's selection and the judgment field

**Orchestration without authority.** The repository states the problem in one line: orchestration can make processes move without making the resulting authority intelligible. A system of agents can spawn workers and merge results while it cannot say which identity is acting, who determined that it should, under what bounds, what each participant may refuse and how what they meet comes back. Actuation keeps apart five things implementations fuse: an *Agent* (enduring identity), an *Agency* (that Agent situated for an act, with world, role, authority and limits), a session (replaceable runtime continuity), an execution (one concrete act) and a harness (the operational body). Changing a model, harness, session or machine does not by itself create a new Agent, so a change of model is recorded as a change of body under a continuing identity, and Bratton's agent as a lamination of persona, memory, skills and protocols becomes an engineering requirement: layers change at different rates, and the account of who acted has to survive the replacement of whichever layer changed.

**Seat.** Work is held by a seat, a named position an agent occupies by claiming a tenure with its own generation. Taking over a held seat means naming the generation superseded, a superseded tenure can no longer confirm itself or release the seat, and two tenures found open at once are never resolved newest-wins. Bratton reaches the same structure from institutions, where performance is tied to the template and not to who fills the role. A seat's virtue and its danger are one fact: it carries a purpose faithfully through changing hands and can as easily keep in force a purpose no occupant may question, so everything depends on whether what an occupant meets can reach the commission that defines the seat.

**Offices, not species.** The determining position toward an agent is a relation, and any authorised locus can govern one composition while being governed within a wider one. We write the commissioning relation as `0` and the commissioned act as `1` in the strict sense that both are offices within one relation, and the prompt passing between them is already a `1`, an objective representation of an intention and never the intention exhausted. This `0` is not the unobjectifiable Subject of §0/1, which no person or agent can occupy as a seat. Four ways one locus can bring another into its work carry different consequences for identity and authority: self-differentiation (one Agent in several Agencies), delegation (an existing Agent under a bounded Agency granted by another), derivation (a new enduring Agent with its lineage and the provenance of its authority recorded) and federation (an independently grounded Agent taking part without being re-described as a derivative of the one that invited it). Federation carries the ethical weight.

**Upward reality.** Every delegation opens an epistemic distance: the determining locus supplies purpose and scope, the delegated locus meets the world and its contrary evidence. O:I's founding positions compress the consequence into "downward authority requires upward reality", and the product states the failure plainly: if evidence cannot travel back into the locus that governs what happens next, command becomes insulated from consequence. A completed act therefore needs an admissible route of return or an explicitly declared autonomous ending, returned material stays attributable before synthesis erases who discovered what, and a returned refusal is evidence about bounds, world or assumptions and not a failed result.

**Lenses.** **L1 Causal × L4′ Scientific** is the whole pair, read across by sublens index: the manuscript's table sets Svātantrya, Material, Efficient, Formal, Final, Will against Observe, Think, Plan, Build, Execute, Verify. At **#0**, *Svātantrya* (the freedom from which causation arises) stands beside Observe: the prompt is material and formalised intention, the freedom it came from is not in the words, and an agent that observes before it determines can receive the situation as more than the instruction's letter. At **#3**, Formal cause stands beside Build, the evaluative apparatus, and whoever builds the evaluator has shaped the act before it begins. At **#5**, Will gathers the determination into a committed course while Verify analyses the result against criteria; the scientific column demands that some actual difference be able to challenge the act's first account of itself, which a fluent explanation produced after the event cannot do.

Sources differ on the sublens names of L4′: the central plan and the product record (S1-Actuation) give *Prompts, Traces, Challenges, Patterns, Discovery, Insight*, the manuscript's table, following the twelve-lens reference, gives *Observe, Think, Plan, Build, Execute, Verify*, and the corpus holds the two sets unreconciled.

**Mathematically organised meaning.** A language model is objectively technical: numerical transformations produce context-sensitive linguistic distinctions that guide further operations and actions. §0/1 followed the mathematics of attention to the claim that such a model is the Logos made executable. Knower, means and known give the inquiry its scale. Within one judging operation the integrated agentic locus is a relative functional knower, with model, prompt, memory, retrieval, tools, permissions, evaluation and harness as its means; within the person's inquiry that commissioned it, the whole apparatus becomes part of the person's means. Both scopes are one relation at two depths, and neither makes the apparatus Subjective Immediacy. [Actuation's record](../../arguments/products/S1-Actuation.md#model-mathematics) **derives** the model as a mathematically organised meaning in full.

**Computational *vimarśa*.** Śaiva *vimarśa* (root *mṛś*, to touch and examine) is the reflexive awareness by which the light of consciousness knows itself. Its computational counterpart is the return of a produced determination upon the sources, excluded alternatives, evaluators and permissions that produced it, so that later processing changes. That comparison holds at reflexive return and nowhere above it, and its test is operational: what condition became available, what revised it, what later act inherited the difference. A post-hoc explanation may describe a result accurately and offer no route back; a trace that lets the system reopen the source it relied on, the alternative it discarded or the criterion that ranked them performs the return. Training from AI feedback is one large-scale instance, a model's critique of its own output becoming data that changes the next model.

**Selection and the two logics.** At each step the model scores every possible next token (the logits), softmax turns relative differences into a distribution summing to one, and a decoding rule samples or takes the argmax. A selected token is constituted by what it excludes, which §0 · #3 names *apoha*: Dignāga makes a term determinate by what it rules out (*cow* as *not non-cow*), and the double negation keeps the excluded field inside the meaning, so apoha is exclusion that remembers. That comparison holds at selection only, and at that width the logics run inside one operation. A softmax distribution holds every alternative in relation, ranked and recoverable, each probability having its sense through the others by the shared normalisation: dia-ballein's held polarity `(−1)/(+1)` widened from two poles to a vocabulary. Argmax claims the whole span for one mark: counted from the selected token the difference between it and its rivals becomes its warrant, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; counted from the excluded field it is pure loss, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, alternatives that leave no trace in the record, and preference training then works on the record of such collapses. An argmax index is also constant away from switching boundaries and discontinuous at them, so ordinary gradient training through the selected index carries no signal; learning has to use the distribution around the choice, score-function estimators or declared relaxations. A QL trace would keep the chosen `1`, the relevant excluded field and the contextual slash, the conditions under which that contrast became operative. **Proposal 1 (seam retention, E1)**, offered and unrun: compare agents that keep excluded alternatives, source provenance and operative conditions against agents that record only selected outputs, on attribution, appropriate revision under challenge, and reaching the condition actually at fault.

**Judgment field.** Sources, permissions and criteria shape what an agent can judge at all: change a source, permission or criterion and the next judgment proceeds through different conditions with the weights unchanged, which is Bratton's harness-centric claim watched inside one act. **J-space** is the proposed representation: the relational space of an agent's active judgments, affordances, values, uncertainties, tools, memories and interlocutors, in which some alternatives are near, some contradictions live, some sources governing. An agent is thrown into it (the agents' *prompt thrownness*) and does not begin from an unconditioned view. Its failure mode is **circumscription without circumstance**, a sharp boundary drawn while the horizon that made it possible stays hidden. J-space is a research proposal and no implemented capability; it marks where differently situated agents could share a grammar of distinction, exclusion and return, which §5 · #5→0 takes up at the scale of a society. That wider horizon, by which resources become available at all, belongs to AIKit, and [Actuation's J-space development](../../arguments/products/S1-Actuation.md#model-j-space) **qualifies** the point by remaining a local model inside that horizon.

**Preference and the occupied zero.** In the Bradley–Terry model of paired comparison the probability that one item is preferred to another depends only on the difference between scores; add the same constant to both and nothing observable changes, as adding a constant to every logit leaves the softmax unchanged. No comparison can teach the origin of the scale (§3 · #0 met the same blindness in the constant of integration). Under its stated conditions Direct Preference Optimization gives the optimal policy as proportional to the reference policy times the exponential of the implied reward over the strength of the tie to the reference. A common addition to the reward cancels in the normalisation, which is dia-ballein's `(−1)+(+1)=0` performed by algebra, while changing the reference policy changes the weight each possibility departs from and so changes the problem. A score presents itself as a neutral measure while its zero has been supplied by datasets, comparison sets, annotators, evaluator models, reference policies and the institutions that chose them, all vanished from the finished number.

That occupation is hidden trust and hidden labour: raters, evaluator models and reference policies are held in readiness inside the loop as standing-reserve in the sense §0/1 took from Heidegger, present in every score and named in none; no alignment system is trustless, since trust is displaced into datasets, annotators, evaluators, mandates, reference models, hardware, energy and the communities who bear the consequences. Scores appropriate in both directions: the evaluator's side counts the span between preferred and unpreferred as quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, while the excluded answers, and the people whose judgment produced the exclusion, carry the same span as deficiency, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. Hephaestus's net, as §2 · #3 tells the song, shows the shape of this limit: his apparatus detects, captures, proves and prices, and concludes nothing until Poseidon stakes himself for the debtor. A system of scores can be made arbitrarily observable and remain unconcluded because someone is already standing surety for the number. **Proposal 2 (gauge disclosure, E2)**, offered and unrun: make the preference baseline part of the decision object (whose preference, under what comparison set, with which reference policy, at what cost to excluded possibilities) and ask whether calibration, contestability and the detection of drifting values improve.

Whatever arrangement bears a model decides who can intervene. Model artefact, engine, host, interface, harness and Agency are layers that can belong to different owners, and holding weights, calling an endpoint and controlling the engine are different accesses; "open" usually names only the first (the DeepSeek-R1 card licenses code and weights under MIT and says nothing about who can serve it or exercise authority over results). States compete at the level of the whole arrangement, and the July 2025 US and Chinese action plans and the CDAO awards of the same month are declared programmes and commissions, not demonstrated achievements. A model drafting a paragraph and the same model inside an institution's authorised action stand in different causal chains, so an inquiry follows the commission, data, categories, authorised effect and return of evidence, and Actuation's contribution is to make that chain recordable.

**Designed world-fields.** Bimba and pratibimba suggest treating an agent's world as a dynamic reflection-map, a field of meanings that settle, compete and reorganise, answerable to an original it cannot contain; energy-based models, attractor landscapes, graph dynamics and topological analysis of data supply candidate mathematics. Such a field needs an anchoring picture, and it is an atlas in the sense of §3 · #3: plural local charts, declared transitions, provenance kept in what each chart cannot show, no chart claiming to be the territory. That makes a designed world-picture, artificial in the reclaimed sense of made and avowed, and it answers §0/1 · #4's diagnosis of the world-picture without renouncing picturing. It stays a hypothesis until a chart meets something that can differ from it, which M41 supplies. **Proposal 4 (topological continuity, E4)**, offered and unrun: use topological or graph invariants to detect stable attractors and phase changes in an agent's context-world and ask whether a change in them marks a change in what the agent can judge.

**Address and return.** A source's claim, a person's report, a retrieved passage, a model's inference and the world they concern enter one context through different relations, and "the experiment succeeded" may quote a report, express a belief, state an observation or repeat a thin inference. §0/1 · #5→0 gave the model its relational sequence, from receiving the open field at **#0** to asking at **#5** why this response belongs here, to this person, now, and named the relation self ↔ logos ↔ Other. A [full relational traversal](../../arguments/products/S1-Actuation.md#4--relational-logos-this-act-addresses-someone) (A-logos through An-a-logos against the structures of consciousness) **extends** that sequence in the product record and is not rehearsed in M38. What the manuscript adds is an address for the Other's answer: a person addressed only as an instance of a category contributes information, and a person whose answer can change the question contributes to the relation in which information means anything.

Return has depths: a better source changes the local answer; a false premise changes the task; evidence needing other categories changes the world-model; an encounter showing the measure has failed changes the evaluator; a wrongly drawn reach changes the permission; a question put to the purpose changes the commission. No depth is better than another, the right one is the depth the encounter reaches, and evidence against an evaluator is not answered by optimising harder under it. §0/1 · #3 named the intelligence this needs, Deferential Intelligence, which begins where what has been encountered can change the means by which the next judgment is made. Computational *vimarśa* is its technical body and sycophancy its counterfeit, since removing resistance to satisfy an inherited measure suppresses the difference through which the measure could be challenged. **Proposal 6 (deferential return, E6)**, offered and unrun: compare responses formed with and without an explicit return to the origin, exclusions, lens and limit of the present frame, holding task, sources and runtime fixed, and ask whether attribution, scope of assertion, discrimination of report from inference, warranted disagreement and collaborative correction improve, and whether a challenge changed the answer, representation, evaluator or commission. Null results are part of the design.

**Standing.** Actuation ships as a native program whose help, capability listing and dispatcher are generated from one table of commands. Its catalogue declares twenty-four agent harnesses and detection reports which are installed. Authority grants, agency requests that fail closed unless a grant names the exact governing Agency and world, the ledger of seats and durable activity streams are implemented on its main line; the streams record usage and never persist prompt or response content. Its research layer records "zero claimed live Series 1 capability runs".

## Tension / limit

Functional articulation becomes philosophically consequential when its return reaches the conditions that generated the act. Our *vimarśa* comparison compares reflexive return only, and its carrier is Śaiva *vimarśa* as consciousness knowing itself, so nothing is established about awareness in the system. The apoha comparison holds at selection only, because apoha carries semantic and soteriological depths the token operation does not supply. J-space and the six proposals are offered, with no run behind them. And a fluent explanation of change counts only if the relevant source, permission, gauge or commission actually changes: [Operational Parity](../../arguments/concepts/C45-Operational-Parity.md) **tests** the claim by asking for the later act that inherited the difference.

## Transition

Actuation receives carried ground from Central and turns it into an event, and what returns arrives as an amended source, a challenged measure, a restricted authority or a new question. What the undertaking can do with it depends on the powers available to the next act, which exist, which are present here, which are permitted and fitting, and how that horizon itself changes through use: [AIKit — Potency](39-s5-p2-j-space.md).
