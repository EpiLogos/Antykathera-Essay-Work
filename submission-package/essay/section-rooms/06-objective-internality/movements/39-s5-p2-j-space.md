# §5 · #2 — J-Space

## Claim
J-space is the relational field of an agent’s active judgments, affordances, uncertainties, values, tools, memories, and interlocutors; objective internality becomes measurable as transformations of this field.

## Warrant
Distances, attractors, contradictions, permissions, source authority, and reachable actions can be compared across context changes. MEF retains divergent world-models under a shared procedural grammar.

## Tension / limit
"Circumscription without circumstance" is the failure mode: a sharp boundary hides the horizon that produced it. The contemporary contextual mutation makes prompt, memory, retrieval, permissions, policy, provenance, feedback, and agent relation operational components of that horizon. J-space is a research representation of the agent's active judgments, affordances, values, uncertainties, tools, memories, and interlocutors; the agent's total world exceeds what the representation presently captures.

**Image:** a thrown landscape of nearer and farther possibilities. Its socially occupied baseline becomes visible in [[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|§5 · #3 — Preference Models and the Hidden Zero]].
