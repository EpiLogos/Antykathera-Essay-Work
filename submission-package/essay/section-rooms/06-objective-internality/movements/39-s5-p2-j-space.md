## Claim

**AIKit is potency: the changing horizon of what a Life can know, express, reach and bring to bear.** Capacity differs from possession. A capability can exist yet be unavailable, available yet irrelevant, relevant yet unpermitted, permitted yet unselected, selected yet unable to become operative. These distinctions belong to the philosophical office because they describe how possibility is disclosed from a particular internality.

## Warrant — logical possibility through time

A source-reading tool shows the whole chain. The tool exists in the wider repertoire and enters a given situation only when the interface it needs is available. The commission makes it relevant, the source's access conditions decide whether it is permitted, and the actor selects it from the available methods. Selection still leaves activation to be tested, and a missing dependency can stop the read. That resistance returns to the horizon: another permitted method may now fit better, or preparation may make the selected method usable in the next act. The capacity persisted throughout, and its availability, relevance, permission, selection and fitness each changed on their own.

The whole pair is **L2 Logical × L3′ Chronological**. Logical gives Tetralemmaic ground, IS, IS-NOT, BOTH, NEITHER and SILENCE, so that a real horizon contains presence, absence, co-presence, indeterminacy and the possibility that articulation is not yet fitting. Chronological gives Spirit, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter and Life/Aufhebung: capacities emerge, mature, become operative, recede, are relinquished, retained or transformed. The logical lens distinguishes the relations at each moment, and the chronological lens carries them through time, which makes potency developmental and no static menu.

J-space names the relational space of an agent's active judgments, affordances, values, uncertainties, tools, memories and interlocutors, and an agent is thrown into it without an unconditioned view. A judgment space can map what is near, salient, permitted or contradictory for an agent, and AIKit concerns the wider horizon by which such resources and methods become available at all. A richer representation is therefore no richer capability field, and the distinction keeps [Actuation's J-Space development](../../arguments/products/S1-Actuation.md#model-j-space) a local model inside the wider modal horizon. "Circumscription without circumstance" names the failure in which a system draws a sharp boundary while hiding the horizon that made the boundary possible.

A/C makes the office concrete through Profile/Scope/Resource identity; Context and projections; Capability/Skill/Method; ContextResolution and change horizons; compositions, sessions and world-inhabitation; then Trust, Familiarity, fitness observation, usage signal and semantic revision. The last movement is decisive: encounter must be able to reorganise the horizon, and what proved useful, unsafe, irrelevant, dormant or newly necessary changes what can fittingly be brought to bear next.

Power enters wherever availability becomes selection, since making one method salient already shapes the probable act. Permission protects whose world, source and authority a capability may legitimately enter, in addition to execution safety. Potency includes the positive power not to enact what happens to be technically possible. Read through the Homeric scene, which is an amplification and carries the Argued status of the essay's reading, Hephaestus holds two powers in turn: the craft that forged the bonds, and the power to loose them. The release arrives on Poseidon's pledge, and the song tells that the same hands which assembled the constraint opened it. The second power is the one a catalogue of what a smith can make leaves out.

## Tension / limit

A capability inventory counterfeits potency when it treats existence as equivalent to situated use. Each step from existence to operation is its own achievement, and fitness shown repeatedly earns a bounded standing and never unrestricted authority. An explicit capability map is itself a representation inside Objective Internality, and it demonstrates Epi-Logos only if returned encounter can alter the actual horizon by changing what later becomes available, salient, permitted or selected.
