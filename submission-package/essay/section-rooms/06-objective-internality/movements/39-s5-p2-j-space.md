## Claim

**AIKit is potency: the changing horizon of what a Life can know, express, reach and bring to bear.** Possession and potency differ, and the difference is the whole subject of the product. A capacity can exist and be unavailable here; be available and irrelevant to the present work; be relevant and forbidden; be permitted and not selected; be selected and fail to become operative. The repository writes the differences as one line, which the manuscript displays as the best single statement of the design: exists ≠ eligible here ≠ available from a provider ≠ selected ≠ projected into this client ≠ loaded into context ≠ invoked. An agent that cannot tell these relations apart believes it can do what it cannot, does what it may not, or loads everything it might need into a context that then has no room for the work.

## Warrant — Aristotle's potency, the two lenses and the work that moves a horizon

**Anchor.** A strong swimmer standing dressed on a pier can swim and is not swimming. The water is cold, the jacket heavy, a sign forbids it; when a child falls in every condition changes at once and a capacity becomes an act shaped by all of them. What a life can bring to bear is a horizon the situation draws, and the horizon moves.

**Aristotle.** *Metaphysics* Θ holds that every potency belongs to the same subject and process as a corresponding impotence: what can build can also not build. Rational capacities are capacities for contraries, as medicine can produce sickness as well as health, so something other than the capacity has to decide which contrary becomes actual, desire or will. A power that could not refrain would be a compulsion, and a power that decided itself would need no one to exercise it. AIKit takes both points into engineering.

**Lenses.** **L2 Logical × L3′ Chronological** is the whole pair. The logical column (APORIA, IS, IS NOT, BOTH, NEITHER, SILENCE) keeps each determination answerable to a stated scope, so asking whether a capability exists differs from asking whether it is available here or operative in this act. Its chronological column (Spirit, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Life as *Aufhebung*) makes the history of a power internal to what it is. Four rows show what an inventory hides. At **#2**, IS NOT with Summer: a refusal at the height of a power's usefulness is the hardest denial for a system to keep, and the pairing says why it must be kept, since a capability in full exercise most easily overreaches the commission it serves. At **#3**, BOTH with Autumn, the season of harvest and evaluation: where a capability is available to one participant and not another, both determinations belong to the field, and erasing the participant manufactures a contradiction where there is only plurality. At **#4**, NEITHER with Winter: a dormant method is neither available nor absent in the way an inventory records, and the history of its dormancy belongs to its fitness. At **#5**, SILENCE with *Aufhebung*: a capability that stays permanently active can prevent a more fitting relation from forming, so growth sometimes requires setting a form down while what it taught is raised into the next arrangement. Potency includes the capacity not to enact what one could, which is Aristotle's point returned as a design rule.

**Work changes the horizon.** In February 2026 the engineer Mitchell Hashimoto described how his own use of agents changed. Isolated exchanges were useful from the start, and fitting agents into his existing work was hard, so he deliberately did some tasks twice, by hand and with an agent, to learn where the agent helped and failed. What he built was an arrangement more than a set of prompts: explicit planning where a task was vague, instructions written against particular failures, tools that let an agent verify its work, and a protected attention. Nothing in the account concerns a better model; the same models had a different practical horizon in his earlier arrangement, and the competence lives in the arrangement, Bratton's harness seen from one desk and won by labour. A file of guidance in which every line records a bad agent behaviour is a horizon written down: each line records an encounter in which a capacity met a situation and failed, and changes what the next agent can fittingly do, so the file is potency in its chronological sense. His account sets its own limit: he runs one agent at a time because one balances his manual work against watching over it, so the horizon widened because a person spent attention and stays bounded by the attention a person can spend. (Paraphrase of the source house; no quotation is carried in the note.)

**A body for the arrangement.** Skills now travel in a common package, which the Agent Skills specification describes as a folder of metadata and instructions with optional scripts, references and assets, disclosed progressively: an agent meets a short description and loads the whole only when the task calls for it. Anthropic developed the format and released it as an open standard. Packaging makes knowledge easier to carry and disclose and establishes neither permission, nor successful execution, nor fitness in a new environment, so AIKit occupies the gap between a package that exists and a method that governs an act. It assembles a person's chosen methods (their own curated collection, each product's shipped methods, outside sources they have decided to trust) into a numbered generation and projects it into the folders each harness reads; a changed selection writes a new generation and keeps the old, and one act returns to it. Discovery is not adoption: AIKit inspects what a person already has without rewriting it, and acts outside itself only through procedures that record enough to undo, refusing to overwrite conflicts. The repository calls this a non-displacement principle as much as a safety feature.

**Usage clock and the two logics.** Bratton says the harness improves on a different clock, driven by usage rather than training, and AIKit is where that clock runs and where it can deceive. Beside each capability the product keeps observations of trust, familiarity, fitness and use, held to one rule: familiar destinations become easier to reach without learned frequency silently becoming trust or authored preference. Familiarity says a power has been met before; fitness asks whether it worked under the conditions that matter now; trust is extended and can be withdrawn; preference, in Central's sense, is something the person said. A toolkit ranked by use and read as a ranking of worth repeats at toolkit scale the operation §5 · #1 found inside a preference score: usage occupies the zero. From the most-used method's side the span between often and rarely chosen is evidence of quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; from the side of the rarely used, often the careful or slow method needed for an uncommon important case, the same span is a deficit, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and the method drifts to the bottom of every list, so the popular method's rise and the careful one's disappearance are one movement read from its two ends. A held polarity `(−1)/(+1)` keeps frequency and fitness as two poles of one assessment, each read through the other and both answerable to the purposes the work serves.

**Power at selection, and a case.** Power enters wherever availability becomes selection: making one method salient already shapes the probable act, so the question is whose purposes, permissions and consequences govern activation. Repeated successful use grounds an observation of fitness and grants no unrestricted authority. A constructed case shows the movement. A tool for reading sources exists in a person's wider repertoire and enters a situation when the interface it needs is present; a commission makes it relevant; the source's terms of access decide whether it is permitted; the agent selects it; activation is still to be tested, and a missing dependency can stop the read. That resistance returns to the horizon, and another permitted method may fit better or the dependency may be supplied for next time. Capacity persisted while availability, selection and fitness changed, and an account recording only "the agent has a source-reading tool" described none of it. AIKit can explain, for any resource, why it is active, absent or degraded in the present context, and a resolved context has succeeded when it keeps enough provenance to say why these capacities, sources and methods entered the field.

**A seat for the horizon.** AIKit can launch an agent into a position of the kind §5 · #1 described: it asks Central for the position, asks Actuation to open or refuse the tenure, and starts the harness with position and generation in its environment. Nothing is released when the harness exits, because leaving is an explicit act. AIKit resolves body and horizon, Actuation decides who may act in the seat, and Central says what the seat is for, so a Life can have the power to act without the right to act in every shared field, and the three questions have three answerable owners.

**Competence has an economy.** A portable method condenses someone's labour into a form another undertaking can use, and the condensation is a gift only if the form keeps its origin visible, lets contributors revise it, carries its permissions and returns later improvement to its field. A catalogue that takes a method in under its own name appropriates the span between maker and users: as an asset, from the catalogue's side, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; as labour that left without its author, from the maker's, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. An open format holds maker and user in `(−1)/(+1)` at the level of form, and form is not content, since an open format can carry closed methods. Conditions under which a commons endures, including Ostrom's finding that those a regime affects need standing in its rules, belong to §5→0 · #3; AIKit's narrower and prior contribution is to keep, for every method in a horizon, its source and the standing its owner gave it, so a commons can be assembled from contributions whose makers stay findable and answerable.

**Standing.** AIKit describes itself as a production-oriented alpha. Its main line supports deterministic resolution of capabilities scoped to a context, immutable generations applied atomically with exact undo, reversible procedures for changes outside itself, trust gates and quarantine for secrets, read-only discovery of skills across the folders different agents read, and projections for two major harnesses. A wider programme is developing toward a typed field of resources, richer navigation of knowledge and an explained history of disclosure, and the repository marks the line itself: current code tells what is real now, and the V2 corpus what the product is being developed toward. Its product record carries the technical constitution in its own sequence, from profile, scope and resource identity through context resolution and compositions to trust, fitness observation and semantic revision, which M39 does not rehearse.

Held in the note and not carried at M39: J-space, which the manuscript places at M38, names a local relational space of an agent's active judgments, and AIKit concerns the wider horizon by which resources and methods become available at all, so a richer representation is no richer capability field. [Actuation's J-space development](../../arguments/products/S1-Actuation.md#model-j-space) **qualifies** this as a local model inside the wider modal horizon. A Homeric amplification (Argued, the essay's reading) sits beside it: Hephaestus holds two powers in turn, the craft that forged the bonds and the power to loose them, and the release arrives on Poseidon's pledge, so the second power is the one a catalogue of what a smith can make leaves out.

## Tension / limit

A capability inventory counterfeits potency when it treats existence as equivalent to situated use, since each step from existence to operation is its own achievement. Fitness shown repeatedly earns a bounded standing and never unrestricted authority, and the usage clock is only as reliable as the return of what it counts to people who know why the counted things mattered. A capability map is itself a representation inside Objective Internality, and it demonstrates Epi-Logos only if returned encounter can alter the actual horizon, what later becomes available, salient, permitted or selected. Its standing boundary has as carrier the repository's own sentence about current code against the developing corpus.

## Transition

Potency, in this sense, is a Life's means seen from the side of what it could do next, and it changes with every act: what proved useful, unsafe, irrelevant, dormant or newly necessary alters what can fittingly be brought to bear. A horizon becomes intelligent where encounter can reorganise it.
