## Movement thesis

A sign for no counted thing changes what is counted. A child asked to write one hundred and five puts down what she heard, 1005, and the parent can explain the mistake only by the middle zero: it is the place of tens that are not there, and without it 105 reads as 15, so that a hundred has shrunk to a ten while nothing was added or taken away. Because the zero adds nothing to the sum and keeps every mark to its left in its place, absence stops being a blank outside the count and becomes a position inside it. We follow this event through history, and it gives the formal-limit opening its historical body: how thought can mark the condition it cannot count as one more object is answered by what human beings did, slowly and in several places, when they learned to put absence in a position.

"Zero" is several offices gathered into one sign: a column the counting board keeps empty, a separator inside a written number, a digit among digits, a number with rules of addition and multiplication, an empty set that begins a construction, an origin from which measurement starts. Each office is a permission granted by the practice that receives the mark, and the sign acquired them one at a time by migrating, across more than two thousand years and at least five traditions of reckoning. Called "zero" from the start the offices erase the history that made the sign; kept apart they show absence acquiring its powers in turn. Its historical record is **Derived**; the readings the manuscript marks as its own (the order of events in India, the Maya reading through `5→0`, the divergence from Rotman) are **Argued**.

| Practice | Office of the mark | What the manuscript uses |

## Anchor case

At the kitchen table, place value moves from the page into the reader: it is objective, since she could state the rule if asked, and internal, since within a year she reads 105 through it without looking, so that a received rule has joined the means through which she reads a world, as §0/1 defined Objective Internality. History runs that movement the other way: what practitioners carried silently was converted, mark by mark, into signs that could travel to readers who did not share their training. Board and pen mark the price: on a board an empty place is a line with no counters, and paper keeps no places of its own.

## Derivation — how the manuscript carries the claim

§0/1 borrowed zero as the sign of the Subject before showing where the sign came from, and every determination §0 traced leaves a zero behind it, the field it was cut from and the act that cut it. That zero was first written down in the history of number, and [[18-s1-p5-loan-returns|§1 · #5→0]] repays the loan.

Old Babylonian scribes had no sign for an empty place, so `(1,0,1)` and `(1,1)` could look alike on the tablet and only the problem in hand said which was meant; the Seleucid separator moved a little of that context onto the tablet, but stood only between signs, never closed a number, and nobody added to it or multiplied by it. Roman numerals need no zero because their value is fixed by shape, and Greek letter-numerals did not meet the problem in ordinary arithmetic; in Ptolemy's sexagesimal tables a barred circle says that a measure of this kind is absent, which is one later office of zero, while a number zero that can enter calculation is another. Whether the Greeks had zero is a badly posed question, since one verdict would erase that difference.

In India the change happens first in language. Āryabhaṭa states the decimal rule in words (499), and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/aryabhata/clark-1930-aryabhatiya/clark-1930-aryabhatiya|Clark's commentary]] **sources** two points: *kha*, space or place, became a commonest word for zero, and it is disputed whether a written zero existed then, a blank column on a ruled board perhaps standing for it. Verse used words of the sky and the open for the digit, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara|Colebrooke's English of Kṛṣṇa's gloss]] **sources** "the privation of specific quantity": a privation of that kind leaves the space in which quantities appear exactly where it was. *Śūnya* is also the root of Nāgārjuna's *śūnyatā*, on which [[symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/nagarjuna/nagarjuna-garfield-1995-fundamental-wisdom/nagarjuna-garfield-1995-fundamental-wisdom|Garfield's translation]] **sources** the reading that whatever arises in dependence is empty of self-standing nature, the condition under which anything appears. The essay's claim rests on the order of events, a vocabulary of emptiness, a positional notation, then explicit arithmetic rules, so that the sign entered the count still bearing the name of the openness in which counting happens. No text shows a mathematician choosing *śūnya* because of Nāgārjuna, and the claim needs none. Sambor (683), the Gwalior temple (876) and the Bakhshali leaves show a zero in ordinary written use by the eighth and ninth centuries, and the dispute over the radiocarbon date of the earliest leaf, which bounds no copying date, leaves that conclusion standing.

Maya scribes invented zero independently for a different problem, counting elapsed time. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/morley/morley-1915-maya-hieroglyphs/morley-1915-maya-hieroglyphs|Morley's handbook]] **sources** Zero Pop, the first day of a month recorded as the day just completed, and the sign's reading as "ending" in dates that close a period, when "no new period had commenced". One mark then carries completion and the point from which the next count begins, which §0/1 wrote as `5→0`. The essay's pairing is an argued reading of a 1915 interpretation that later epigraphy has refined.

The sign enters the counting house westward. The Latin *Dixit algoritmi*, a twelfth-century version of al-Khwārizmī's lost treatise, says that where no number stands the practitioners set *circulum paruulum in similitudine .o. litere*, so that the empty place was known to be empty, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/al-khwarizmi/alkhwarizmi-1857-algoritmi-de-numero-indorum/alkhwarizmi-1857-algoritmi-de-numero-indorum|the text]] **sources** *algorismus* and so *algorithm*; *ṣifr* became *cifra*, *cipher* and *zero*. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/leonardo-pisano/leonardo-pisano-1857-liber-abbaci/leonardo-pisano-1857-liber-abbaci|Leonardo of Pisa's *Liber abbaci*]] (1202) **sources** the nine *figure* and the tenth sign, the *signum* that lets the nine work, and his judgement that the counting board's "arcs of Pythagoras" were almost an error beside the Indian method. Zero was the price of leaving the board, repaid in calculation that could be checked afterwards, copied into a ledger and carried away.

[[symbolon/episteme/sources/history-philosophy-of-science/nothaft/nothaft-2020-satanic-ciphers/nothaft-2020-satanic-ciphers|Nothaft's examination]] **qualifies** the story that medieval Christians feared zero and banned it: the evidence for a demonised zero and for general bans is thin, and restrictions on figures in account books belong to fraud prevention. That story is itself *dia-ballein* in the oldest sense, an accusation thrown across a distance that travelled more readily than its evidence. Rotman's three meta-signs (zero, the vanishing point, imaginary money) organise a system of signs from a position the system cannot occupy, and each brings a subject who stands outside and sees the whole. The essay parts from him at one point, since for Rotman a signifying practice produces that subject, whereas for the essay the Subject is the condition under which any such position can appear. `0` and its determinations in the `1` hold his insight without his conclusion.

Migration bought independence from any compulsory metaphysics, since an astronomer in Baghdad, a merchant in Pisa and a temple official in Gwalior can all agree that 270 plus 30 is 300. The cost, which the manuscript calls the first advent's sacrifice, is that universality was bought by leaving the symbolic world in which the sign was meaningful, and *śūnya* travelled as words that meant only the empty place, and the sacrifice was necessary and real. The sign migrates again in a language model, which receives numbers as tokens: GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 cut a long number from the left, so 1005 arrives as 100 and 5, the child's error made by the machine's division of its input, and grouping the digits from the right in threes lifts accuracy because the groups then fall on the places. Each receiving practice grants some offices and withholds others, and the sign **returns-to** [Name-through-Count](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD-encounter-region-name-count.md#name-through-count): what a mark can do depends on what the count that receives it permits. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/kaplan/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is/kaplan-1999-nothing-that-is|Kaplan]] and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/rotman/rotman-1987-signifying-nothing/rotman-1987-signifying-nothing|Rotman]] remain the narrative and semiotic guides to the whole itinerary.

## Tension / limit

Zero's history is the writing-down of context, and the conversion cannot be completed, because the act that writes a context down stands in a further context of its own; division by zero shows this in [[17-s1-p4-zero-outside-math|§1 · #4]]. Three limits have named carriers: Kaplan is held as a locator copy, no copy of Rotman is in hand and his argument is paraphrased at the level of the book, and only Nothaft's abstract has been read, so page-level locators for those three remain open. The Maya reading rests on a 1915 interpretation, the chronology and office of zero in each tradition belong to specialists, and Rotman's semiotic subject-position is not the essay's irreducible subject-condition; the [language history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/language-symbol-dialogue/DEVELOPMENT-language-symbol-dialogue.md#language-dialogue) **qualifies** this operational comparison.

Held in the note and not carried by the manuscript prose: Plate 1 and the [zero-reception dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/zero-reception.md#3--gerbert-and-salem-require-different-recoveries) **compare** two Latin recoveries that need different witnesses. Numeral form, calculating apparatus and positional use are separate predicates; Gerbert needs an independently dated technical witness, and the Salem route has Cantor's edition pointer without a verified manuscript, folio or Latin collation, so recognising a shape settles neither operation nor transmission.

## Transition

A place held open is still only a place. What gives zero its force is that arithmetic can pass through it, adding zero, multiplying by it and, finally, trying to divide by it, and someone has to say what happens then. [[14-s1-p1-sunya-operational|§1 · #1 — Śūnya Becomes Operational]] follows what Brahmagupta and Bhāskara said.
