# §1 · #4 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics

## Movement thesis

Division by zero is the point at which a formal system has to declare its law. A calculator that answers "Error" to one divided by zero has reported that the question has no answer under the rules it was built to follow. In an ordinary field, where zero and one are different numbers, dividing one by zero would require a $q$ with $0\cdot q=1$, and the laws rule every such $q$ out. Mathematics has given the quotient a place by changing something else, and each change shows the frame becoming visible at zero: the projective line adjoins a point at infinity, the Riemann sphere closes the complex plane, wheels and meadows keep division total at a price in the familiar laws, floating-point arithmetic signs its zeros, and calculus replaces the vanishing ratio with a limit or an enlarged number system. Completion is always completion within a declared structure, and [[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|division pluralisms]] establish the methodological point: exceptional operations reveal the container.

Zero is wholly mathematical, since it is written, counted, operated on and indispensable to every positional notation and every construction of number. Yet the operation that returns a determinate quantity to zero cannot be completed without choosing or changing the space in which completion is sought, and that is the sense in which zero keeps one foot outside mathematics: "outside" means frame-disclosing. Inside a field the result is a proof; identifying this edge with the essay's immutable gap is **Argued**.

## Derivation

In a field, $0\cdot q=(0+0)\cdot q=0\cdot q+0\cdot q$, so $0\cdot q=0$ for every $q$, and `1/0=x` would force $0\cdot x=1$ and so zero equal to one. Adding a new element $q$ under the same laws does not help, because the argument applies to it too. Zero divided by zero fails differently: every $q$ satisfies $0\cdot q=0$, so the question has too many answers. "Undefined" covers two reasons, and Brahmagupta's rule that zero divided by zero is zero picked one answer from many. If an inverse $z$ with $0\cdot z=1$ is insisted upon with every other law kept, then $a=a\cdot1=a\cdot(0\cdot z)=(a\cdot0)\cdot z=0\cdot z=1$ for every $a$, and every number equals one: the zero ring, the structure with a single element. Kaplan's remark that dividing by zero would make all numbers the same is derived here.

Read through the logics, the zero ring is fusion, the collapse of the One and the All with no remainder, cancellation `(−1)+(+1)=0` run through every pair until nothing can be told apart, and it is the failure the symbolon was made to resist. An ordinary field refuses division by zero, which keeps every number distinct at the price that the determinate one can never return to zero by division: dia-ballein doing its proper work, a cut stated as a law. `0/1 = 1/0`, in the relational register of §0/1, assigns `1/0` no value in a field and says that one relation can be read from either end with the difference between the defined and the undefined retained as the seam; syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps the seam and crosses it as a relation. Any proposal is to be asked where the seam went: into a refusal that preserves difference by forbidding return, a merger that permits return by abolishing difference, or a relation that holds both.

Each enlarged frame carries its price: [[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|projective completion]] gives a nonzero number divided by zero a single point at infinity that cannot itself be added to infinity or multiplied by zero while keeping the familiar laws; the [[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|Riemann sphere]] makes zero and infinity the two poles of one globe and the reciprocal map total on points while division still fails when both parts vanish; wheels weaken `x/x=1` and `0·x=0`, and meadows set $0^{-1}=0$ and give $x\cdot x^{-1}=1$ only for nonzero $x$. A history leads from the projective point back to the painter's room: Desargues (1639) took the vanishing point of perspective into geometry as the point where parallels meet, and the projective line on which `1/0` has a value is its descendant. Rotman set zero beside the vanishing point, and they meet in one construction, §0/1 · #4 having found the knower made a place at the near end of Alberti's pyramid and projective geometry the far end, given the value of the forbidden quotient.

Machines had to make the choice explicitly, and IEEE 754 gives division by zero a law with two zeros, positive and negative, that compare equal and remember the side from which a quantity approached, so that one divided by positive zero is positive infinity and by negative zero negative infinity; zero divided by zero is NaN, the one value that tests unequal to itself, and Kahan defended the signed zero as "Much Ado About Nothing's Sign Bit". Read through the logics, the signed zero gives the polar axis `(−1)/(+1)` a memory at its turning point: the return through zero keeps the chirality of the approach, as a loop on the torus keeps its winding. NaN echoes Frege's zero as the count of what is not identical with itself, a rule of comparison at the edge where nothing meets nothing. Training a neural network meets the edge by accident when one division by a vanished quantity makes a NaN that spreads through the parameters, and engineers keep a small ε between every denominator and zero (Adam's $10^{-8}$, normalisation layers' order of $10^{-5}$): a law for what happens near zero, a distance kept from zero and never closed.

Calculus met the same edge three centuries earlier, when Newton and Leibniz computed a rate of change as the ratio of two increments shrinking to nothing, and Berkeley's *The Analyst* (1734) asked whether the increments, "neither finite Quantities nor Quantities infinitely small, nor yet nothing", might be called "the Ghosts of departed Quantities". Berkeley's charge was precise: a quantity divided by and then set to nothing is treated as something and as nothing in one calculation, and success did not make that coherent. Cauchy and Weierstrass replaced the ratio of vanished quantities with the value a ratio approaches, so the division by zero is never performed; Kṛṣṇa's argument about the *khahara* had reasoned in the same direction, a quotient approached through a process and never reached as a value. Robinson (1966) showed that nonzero infinitesimals have a consistent meaning in an enlarged number system, where $\frac{(x+\varepsilon)^2-x^2}{\varepsilon}=2x+\varepsilon$ and the standard part is $2x$. What differentiation loses, the constant integration cannot recover, is §3 · #0's matter.

The sources show the historical pressure. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara|Colebrooke — Brahmagupta and Bhāskara (1817)]] **sources** Bhāskara's zero-denominator expression in the selected translation, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/dutta/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india|Dutta — Zero-Divided Numbers in Indian Mathematics (2023)]] **tests** what happens when cancellation and cross-multiplication are restricted for such objects: the expression stays an object of a calculus with its own laws and never becomes ordinary field division, which is why the formal container has to be declared. This operation has a [mathematics history](../../../symbolon/episteme/histories/traditions-and-disciplines/mathematics/DEVELOPMENT-mathematics.md#1--zero-enters-calculation-and-exposes-its-laws) **historicises** it; the field-theoretic result stays contradictory for unrestricted division, and the altered calculus carries its own stated laws.

## Argumentative force

Pressure of the same kind appeared at the formal limit: a system cannot exhaustively account for the condition from which its accounting proceeds. §0/1 defined the immutable gap through Gödel as the non-coincidence between what a theory can express and what it can establish, which relocates with every enlargement of the theory. Division by zero is **the mathematical form of the immutable gap at this register**: a field refuses `1/0`, the projective line adds a point and cannot divide infinity by infinity, the wheel defines every division and weakens `x/x=1`, Robinson's infinitesimals make the derivative a true quotient and must take a standard part to return to the reals. Each enlargement makes the old impossibility possible and produces a new one, because the operation that returns a determinate quantity to its zero asks the system to account for the condition of its own computation, and that condition is not one more of its numbers. Mathematics warrants the field result, and the identification of this frame-disclosing edge with the essay's immutable gap is the essay's own Argued claim, bounded as it was for Gödel: the mathematics does not prove the notation, and it shows the return of the determinate one upon its ground recurring, in one register, through every attempt to remove it.

Zero's mathematical history thereby reaches the threshold of the later psychic one. Arithmetic admits zero and meets its exceptional edge; psychoanalysis admits the unconscious and meets a condition consciousness cannot master as one more object. They share a parallel of operations (a determinate system takes in a sign for what it cannot contain, gains power by operating with it, and at its edge discloses its own frame) and keep their own objects, methods and evidence; §4 makes the QL refraction explicit.

## Tension / limit

"Outside" means frame-disclosing and carries no supernatural sense. The boundary has three carriers: the field-theoretic proof (for unrestricted division in a field), Dutta's reconstruction (for the Indian regime), and the open locator debts: Kaplan held as a locator copy; projective line, Desargues, wheels, meadows, IEEE 754, Adam and PyTorch's ε defaults stated from memory of their sources; Berkeley §35 and Robinson to re-read; Kahan's chapter pages not in hand.

## Transition

The calculator's "Error" looked like a dead end, and followed through the zero ring, the projective line, the sphere, the wheel and the meadow, the floating-point standard, the training run and the calculus, it is among the most informative places in mathematics, where a system shows what it assumes, what it protects and what it has given up, and a sign that can do that has earned the right to stand for the condition §0/1 borrowed it to name. §1 can now repay its promissory use of zero in [[18-s1-p5-loan-returns|§1 · #5→0 — The Loan Returns]].
