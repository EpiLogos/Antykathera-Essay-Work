## Movement thesis

Division by zero is the point at which a formal system has to disclose itself. In an ordinary field `1/0` is undefined, because no element multiplied by zero returns one. Other systems give it a place by accepting other laws: the projective line adjoins a point at infinity, the Riemann sphere compactifies the complex plane, and wheels, meadows, transreal arithmetic and similar algebras preserve different operations at different costs.

[[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|Division pluralisms]] establish a precise methodological point: exceptional operations reveal the container, and completion is always completion within a declared structure. Mathematics succeeds here by making the axioms and consequences of each possible completion explicit.

## Derivation

Assume in a field that `1/0=x`. Then `0·x=1`, and `0·x=0` for every field element, so no such `x` exists. To give `1/0` a value the system must change. [[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|Projective completion]] may identify directions at infinity, the [[dimensional-reframing-at-zero-and-infinity|Riemann sphere]] gives complex infinity a geometric place, and totalised algebras alter distributivity, equality or exceptional values. Each resolution has a purpose and a cost.

The historical pressure is inspectable in the sources. [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/brahmagupta/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara/colebrooke-1817-brahmagupta-bhaskara|Colebrooke — Brahmagupta and Bhāskara (1817)]] preserves, in the selected translation, Bhāskara's zero-denominator expression, and [[symbolon/episteme/sources/mathematics-logic/dutta/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india/dutta-2023-zero-divided-numbers-india|Dutta — Zero-Divided Numbers in Indian Mathematics (2023)]] asks what happens when cancellation and cross-multiplication are restricted for such objects. Taken together they show why the formal container has to be declared, since the expression stays an object of a calculus with its own laws and never becomes ordinary field division.

## Argumentative force

Zero is entirely mathematical, and the return of a finite determination to zero reaches the edge at which the current mathematical world must refuse the operation or transform its own space. That is the sense in which zero keeps one foot outside mathematics. Zero is the meta-sign of that decision, and it makes the rules visible.

The same pressure appeared at the formal limit: a system cannot exhaustively account for the condition from which its accounting proceeds. **This is the mathematical form of the immutable gap at this register.** Under a declared algebra, division by zero exposes the laws and limits of the containing world, and the exceptional expression makes the frame itself consequential. The field-theoretic result warrants the mathematical operation, and the identification of this frame-disclosing non-coincidence with the essay's immutable gap is the essay's own Argued cross-register claim, in which the formal world and the condition of its permitted operations become visible together as two things.

Zero's mathematical history thereby reaches the threshold of the later psychic history, and the shared operation is formal admission followed by frame-disclosure. Arithmetic admits zero and meets its exceptional edge, and psychoanalysis admits the unconscious and confronts a condition consciousness cannot master as one more object. The two histories stay distinct, and §4 makes their QL refraction explicit.
