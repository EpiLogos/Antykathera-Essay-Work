## Movement thesis

Von Neumann's construction of the natural numbers gives the essay's zero/one relation a precise formal neighbour:

$$
0=\varnothing,\qquad
1=\{0\}=\{\varnothing\},\qquad
n+1=n\cup\{n\}.
$$

In this representation one is the first successor of zero, the mark of zero, the first relation in which emptiness is contained, named or counted, and each finite ordinal gathers the prior ordinal as part of the next. Zero and the operation that marks it are enough to begin the count, so no second primitive numerical object is needed beyond the empty set and the successor construction. The philosophical force lies in what this exact local construction lets the essay compare, and set theory is offered as a rigorous neighbour and no proof of Subject, consciousness or QL.

## Derivation

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &= \varnothing,\\
1 &= \{\varnothing\},\\
2 &= \{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\}\},\\
3 &= \{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\},\{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\}\}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each ordinal contains every one before it, so the empty set sits inside the body of every later number: zero remains structurally present in each finite ordinal, and the series is generated by repeating one act, the marking of what is already there. In this register `0` and `1` have exact set-theoretic meanings. The essay places the construction beside its own relational notation, and the pair of the empty object and its first successor shows one mathematically legitimate way in which zero stays present in what it generates. The slash of `0/1` comes from the native authorial relation developed out of the formal-limit and knower–means–known argument, and von Neumann's construction supplies the sets and leaves ordinary set-theoretic division out of it.

## Argumentative force and proof boundary

The essay has already derived phenomenologically a question: how can determination arise without pretending to have made its own condition disappear? The ordinal construction gives that question a rigorous formal neighbour. A later finite ordinal literally contains its predecessors by the chosen representation, while in the essay's argument an achieved determination depends on a ground and a mediating activity that it cannot convert into an ordinary object. The two claims differ, and a disciplined structural comparison runs between them: in both, the first one is the mark of a zero it does not use up.

Within set theory, `∅` is an exact object and `{∅}` its exact singleton. The psychoid and transcendental questions begin at the difference between that formally represented empty object and the unobjectifiable condition under which representation occurs, and keeping the two apart preserves the distinction the movement teaches. The authorial reading can then say: **mathematics gives a concrete case in which zero participates generatively in the construction of one and the natural numbers; QL asks a wider relational question about how ground, mark and mediation are retained in determination.** The first gives the later Matheme a mathematically exact surface against which its additional claims can be tested.
