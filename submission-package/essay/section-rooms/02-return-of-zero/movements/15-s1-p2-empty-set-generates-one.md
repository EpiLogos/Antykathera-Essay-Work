# §1 · #2 — The Empty Set Generates One

## Movement thesis

Von Neumann's construction of the natural numbers gives the essay's zero/one relation a precise formal neighbour:

$$
0=\varnothing,\qquad
1=\{0\}=\{\varnothing\},\qquad
S(n)=n\cup\{n\}.
$$

Nothing is added to the empty set: a relation is formed around it, and the braces show where the one appears. One is the first successor of zero, the void gathered into a unit, and every later number keeps zero inside it as the first member it gathered, so that zero and the operation that holds it are enough to begin the count. A desktop folder that reports "1 item" when it holds one empty folder makes the same count in a dialogue box. Set theory is offered as a rigorous neighbour; it proves nothing about the Subject, consciousness or QL. The construction is **Derived** (standard mathematics, displayed), and the readings the manuscript attaches to it are **Argued**.

## Derivation

$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &= \varnothing,\\
1 &= \{\varnothing\},\\
2 &= \{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\}\},\\
3 &= \{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\},\{\varnothing,\{\varnothing\}\}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The manuscript builds the result through Frege, then von Neumann, then the empty sum, product and joint denial. In *Die Grundlagen der Arithmetik* (1884) a number belongs to a concept, and the first number needs a concept under which nothing falls: "0 is the number which belongs to the concept 'unequal to itself'" (§74); one is the number belonging to the concept "equal to 0" (§77), under which exactly one object falls, zero itself. Frege chose self-inequality because logic alone certifies its emptiness: every other empty concept (the golden mountain, the present king of France) is empty because of how the world happens to be, while this one is empty because of what identity is. The essay reads the choice through the copula of §0/1 · #1, where `≠` is equality with the cut drawn through it: zero counts a self-relation that fails everywhere, and one counts zero, so that the first unit counts the result of the impossibility of self-difference. That reading is the essay's, and Frege's own reason for the choice supports it. His wider ambition ran into Russell's paradox, and the construction survived in the set theory built to repair it.

Von Neumann (1923) then gives each number as the set of all the numbers before it, and Zermelo's earlier choice, each successor the singleton of its predecessor, yields a sequence that behaves exactly as the numbers do. It is a representation and no claim that numbers are sets in some deeper sense; von Neumann's became standard because each number is literally the collection of the numbers it counts past. Two counts must be kept apart. §0/1's `2+2²=4+2` counts self-application of the binary (the four ordered pairings and the two terms), and the ordinal two, `{0,1}`, counts succession; both begin from the pair zero and one and find that the pair generates more than itself once it is held together.

The core theorems name the one of this construction the **symbolic linker**, the `1` that links an articulated field back to the ground it cannot contain: the one that counts is zero held. In the set-theoretic register the link is literal, since one contains zero as a member and is distinct from it. Three relations that speech runs together under "contains" do different work: membership carries each number into the next, inclusion keeps the earlier numbers inside the later, and non-identity stops the sequence collapsing into its beginning. Finite repetition does not by itself produce the collection of all finite numbers; the axiom of infinity does, and §1 · #4 returns to that point.

Kaplan sets two more beginnings from nothing beside the ordinals. An empty sum is zero and an empty product is one, because adding or multiplying by no further term must leave the total unchanged: absence takes its value from the identity of the operation that holds it. Arithmetic names zero the additive identity, and the office matches §0/1's `0`, the One that remains itself through every determination without becoming one of them. That the empty product is one shows the relation from the other side. His third scene is logical: joint denial, "neither … nor" (`p↓q=¬(p∨q)`), produces negation, disjunction and conjunction, hence every truth function. Peirce saw it around 1880, Sheffer proved it in 1913 for a single stroke, and Wittgenstein built the *Tractatus* on it. This generator gives apoha's exclusion and the tetralemma's NEITHER a neighbour in the logic of truth values, a field of affirmation generated from arranged denials, and it underlies every digital machine: the Apollo Guidance Computer used three-input NOR gates as its only logic element.

The three constructions begin from almost nothing under three laws and share a pattern §0/1 anticipated: a spare beginning becomes generative through an operation that retains it, and what is generated carries its beginning inside it.

## Argumentative force and proof boundary

Within set theory `∅` is an exact object and `{∅}` its exact singleton, and the unobjectifiable condition under which any representation occurs is not another such object. When the construction is written and followed, the set, the braces, the rule and the act of following are all open to examination, and each examination is a knowing; representing that knowing adds one more object and makes the knowing no member of any set. Set and Subject belong to different levels, and the construction gives a formal case in which a beginning that is nothing remains present in everything generated from it, and in which the first determinate unit is the act of holding that beginning: **mathematics gives a concrete case in which zero participates generatively in the construction of one and the natural numbers; QL asks a wider relational question about how ground, mark and mediation are retained in determination.**

Sheffer's result holds for truth functions, which combine determinate values and return determinate values, and the slash of `0/1` relates a determination to a condition that is no further input. No Boolean connective, however complete, can be that slash; Diagram 3 draws the seam. Sheffer's stroke is nonetheless the oldest notation in this essay's history, and the slash is the same thin mark asked to divide and join at once. Carriers still open: Frege's *Grundlagen* (German, §§74 and 77) has no house yet and the English renderings are the essay's; von Neumann and Zermelo pages are given as remembered; Kaplan is held as a locator copy and no wording is quoted; and the Apollo figure of some four thousand circuits is to be confirmed in Hall.

## Transition

One can now serve as a linker, a determinate unit through which a relation to zero is carried into everything that follows, and its power depends on what it links. Trouble comes when the one forgets that it is a holding, when the singleton that holds the void takes itself for the source of its unity and the mark of a relation passes for the thing related. [[16-s1-p3-crossed-zero|§1 · #3 — The Crossed Zero]] follows the sign that marks that forgetting.
