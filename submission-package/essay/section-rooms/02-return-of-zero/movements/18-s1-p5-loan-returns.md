## Movement thesis

The formal-limit opening borrowed zero as a sign for the subject that cannot be counted as one more object, and §1 returns the loan with mathematical interest. Zero entered positional and arithmetic practices without becoming an ordinary positive magnitude. Later formal constructions gave it further exact offices: in set theory the empty set serves as zero and successors generate the finite ordinals, so zero enters a generative relation while remaining exactly the object the construction defines. The crossed zero made the occluded mediating stroke visible as a problem, and division by zero exposed the law of the containing structure. The notation is now earned as the essay's own relational notation:

$$
0/1 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad 1/0.
$$

Kaplan's printed pp. 204–207 give the pair a concrete mathematical prehistory: treated as formal boundary fractions, `0/1` and `1/0` seed a mediant construction in which relations ramify between retained endpoints. The precise Stern–Brocot/Farey relation is a specialist verification task, and the essay's slash is a relational operation of its own that the mediant construction neighbours.

`0/1` names the indefinite condition becoming available through definite form, and `1/0` names definite form returning toward a condition it cannot convert into ordinary content. The slash is the act or means of differentiation and is not always ordinary division. It carries the question of provenance: from what field did this mark arise, what did it exclude, through what mediation did it become legible, and by what transformation can its consequence return?

The notation can now be named the root *symbolon*. It is overdetermined because `0`, `1` and `/` stay mathematical signs, logical roles, phenomenological poles and directions of return, each under its explicitly distinguished reading. It is generative because the two orientations can be inverted, related, and made to take their own relation as a further term. §2 distinguishes the logics that emerge from those operations, §3 derives their wider mathematical field, and §4 returns the symbols to the life of psyche.

This is the historical advent at the end of §1: an exact mathematical sign can return into a wider philosophical account and remain exact mathematics. The Indian hinge is historically specific, since mathematical zero and Kashmiri metaphysics develop in distinct practices within a shared intellectual world, so the essay can place their operations in relation without making either the disguised origin-story of the other. The next stations carry that braid into dia- and syn-ballein, the eight determinations, psychic life and technical worldhood.

## Synthesis and claim boundary

The return of zero is recognition of the zero retained in every one: achieved form returns to its conditions with its differentiation intact, and regression into blankness would cancel the relation and so fail to return. The subject stays uncaught because it is the pole and condition through which concealed and manifest objects alike become available.

## Release into §2

The recovered zero shows why "binary logic" is ambiguous. The next station first gives **polar dia-ballein** as `(−1)/(+1)`, where zero remains the axis of tension and connection, and its collapsed operations then cancel the poles into `0` or appropriate their span as `+2` or `−2`, each in its own chirality. **Syn-ballein** relates `0/1` and `1/0` and retains ground, mark and their inverse orientations within one differentiated relation. The question is therefore whether the cut stays answerable to its generating relation or is collapsed into operations on apparently self-grounding terms, and the answer is independent of whether zero is placed outside or inside.

The loan's return **returns-to** [Credere — renewed entrustment](../../../symbolon/episteme/etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD-trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere.md#credere-renewed-entrustment) through a determinate undertaking with a bearer and a consequence. Examining the account clarifies what is owed, and renewed entrustment lets the relation proceed beyond what that examination can guarantee. The six-term whole carries place, articulation, rule and belonging through that passage, so the return keeps the obligation in force and builds no confidence out of its record.

The [zero-reception dossier](../../../symbolon/episteme/dossiers/zero-reception.md#4--rotmans-meta-subject-is-a-distinct-interlocutor) compares this return with Rotman's semiotically produced observing role. The comparison sharpens the distinction between a formal observer-position and the essay's irreducible Subject, and it leaves zero's documentary reception open to revising the historical account. Mathematical exactness and philosophical return stay joined through relation and need no single provenance story.
