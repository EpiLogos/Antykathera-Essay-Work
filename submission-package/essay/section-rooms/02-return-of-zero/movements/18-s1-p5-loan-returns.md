# §1 · #5→0 — The Loan Returns

## Movement thesis

The formal-limit opening borrowed zero as a sign for the subject that cannot be counted as one more object. §1 returns the loan with mathematical interest. Zero entered a positional calculus without becoming a positive magnitude; arithmetic made it operative; set theory generated one and the naturals from its repeated marking; the crossed zero showed the mark retained within its ground; division by zero exposed the law of every containing system.

The notation is now earned:

$$
0/1 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad 1/0.
$$

Kaplan's printed pp. 204–207 give the pair a concrete mathematical prehistory: treated as formal boundary fractions, `0/1` and `1/0` seed a mediant construction in which relations ramify between retained endpoints. The precise Stern–Brocot/Farey relation remains a specialist verification debt. The construction does not make the essay's later slash equivalent to the mediant operation.

\(0/1\) names the indefinite condition becoming available through definite form. \(1/0\) names definite form returning toward a condition it cannot convert into ordinary content. The slash is the act of differentiation, not a decorative separator and not always ordinary division. It carries the question of provenance: from what field did this mark arise, what did it exclude, and by what transformation can it return?

The notation can now be named the root *symbolon*. It is overdetermined because `0`, `1`, and `/` remain simultaneously mathematical signs, logical roles, phenomenological poles, and directions of return. It is generative because the two orientations can be inverted, related, and made to take their own relation as a further term. §2 distinguishes the logics that emerge from those operations; §3 derives their wider mathematical field; §4 returns the symbols to the life of psyche.

This is the historical advent at the end of §1: a term that travelled from sacred and philosophical vocabularies through mathematical formalisation can now return from mathematics without being unlearned. `0/1` does not restore a pre-mathematical zero; it carries the exactness and the limit of the mathematical journey into the wider Logos. The following movements give the return its dia- and sym-ballein operations, its eight determinations, and its psychic and technical bodies.

## Synthesis and claim boundary

- History shows absence acquiring a position.
- Arithmetic shows zero acting without becoming positive quantity.
- Set theory shows the empty set generating one through linkage.
- Semiotics shows the first sign instituting the count it cannot derive from within.
- Exceptional division shows the return to zero disclosing the formal container.

The return of zero is recognition of the zero retained in every one: achieved form returns to its conditions with its differentiation intact. Regression into blankness would cancel the relation and therefore fail to return. The subject remains uncaught because it is the pole and condition through which concealed and manifest objects alike become available.

## Release into §2

The recovered zero reveals why “binary logic” is ambiguous. One binary opposes \(+1\) and \(-1\) around an external zero. The other relates \(0/1\) and \(1/0\), retaining zero within both orientations. Their difference determines whether intelligence treats conflict as the collision of self-grounding camps or as differentiation within a shared but irreducible field.
