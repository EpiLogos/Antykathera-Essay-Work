Differentiation cannot be escaped; the live question is whether a distinction can remain answerable to the ground and activity that produced it, or whether its finished terms present themselves as self-sufficient objects. ## II. The notation and its floor

- `0` is the meta-sign for the non-objectifiable condition present in every act of distinguishing — subject-pole, ground, the uncounted. It is never "nothing," never a blank, never one more object.
- `1` is whatever has become determinate enough to name, count, model, or govern — mark, phenomenon, object-pole.
- `/` is the differentiating activity itself: division and ratio in the arithmetic register, **AND/OR** in the logical register — conjunction that does not erase disjunction. The slash is not punctuation between finished things; it is the act that makes both positions legible.
- The bare relation is **`/ = −/−`**. Dia-ballein has two exact operations: polar `(-1)/(+1)`, where zero remains the axis of tension and connection, and collapsed `(-1)+/-(+1)`, where addition cancels to `0` and subtraction appropriates the span as `-2` or `+2`. Syn-ballein is **`(0/1)/(1/0)`**: under the slash's AND/OR, `0/1` generates the obverse `1/0`, and QL asserts `0/1 = 1/0` as the two orientations of one relation. Ordinary arithmetic's defined/undefined distinction is retained as the formal seam, not used to weaken the matheme.
- The generative count: `2 + 2² = 6 = 4 + 2` — two poles plus the four ways their relation can be related. Six is not a sacred number imported; it is what a two amounts to once it includes itself.
- The matheme's compression: `0/1 = 4+2 = 5→0 = 1/0`.
- The **immutable gap** names the recurrent non-coincidence between a representation and the condition under which representation occurs. It belongs to representation as such, so more information cannot close it. "God of the gaps" and "humanity of the gaps" make the same error: identifying the divine or the human subject with a shrinking residue of unexplained capacities.

## III. Attitude locks

**Subjective Immediacy is pramātṛ, the knower; Objective Internality is pramāṇa, the means; World is prameya, the known; I-Consciousness is the whole in which the three offices are distinguishable, and Objective Internality is Life / Mind.** The knower-pole is present in knowing without becoming one more known item. This is the category distinction the argument develops through the complete crossed-zero recognition sequence.

Make the positive proposition proper to each passage. Technical organisation calls for an account of what the organisation does. ### 3. Dia-ballein is not the villain

The two logics distinguish **operations, not moral camps**. Dia-ballein powers comparison, classification, proof by exclusion, measurement, decision, critique — it is indispensable, and the essay says so without embarrassment. Its pathology begins only when the cut forgets the common operation and field that produced the poles: fragmentation (Bohm), atomisation, projection of unadmitted uncertainty onto an excluded object. "Divide and conquer" fails as intelligence only when conquest is mistaken for the whole process — division without the combine-context-return that syn-ballein names. Any surface that moralises the cut ("the bad logic") or, symmetrically, softens syn-ballein into a vague holism, has lost the section's derivation.

### 4. Status discipline governs public warrant — never depth of understanding

Derived / Argued / Offered, and the evidential relations Extracted / Paraphrased / Argued-from / Resonant-with, discipline **what may be asserted publicly and on whose authority**. They do not license timidity about content. **`Argued` is a positive warrant:** once the essay has built the inferential chain, prose states the proposition at that strength. **`Offered` belongs to a conjecture, design, research vector or implementation whose truth or success genuinely remains to be established; it is not a courtesy downgrade for an unconventional native thesis.** A room or dossier must teach the full content of a concept, text, or derivation — what it says, what work it does, why it sits here — while marking exactly what may be quoted or publicly attributed. "Source not yet verified" constrains quotation and attribution; **it is never a reason to leave a concept untaught, an argument unbuilt, or an authorial synthesis half-said**. The recurring failure is source-administration or review hedges ("remains a constrained inference pending specialist evidence") occupying the place where the plan's actual philosophy should be. The plan states the pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya analysis as the essay's own argued philosophy; the source bank's job is to back its public presentation, not to suspend its meaning.

### 7. The zero is always occupied — trust and the hidden ground

Knowledge is a performance of trust wearing the mask of certainty: before any proposition is certified, a field of language, memory, method, witness, and institution has been trusted enough for it to count. Dia-ballein-mode knowledge denies this provisionality and displaces its unadmitted uncertainty onto an excluded object (enemy, scapegoat, alien intelligence); syn-ballein-mode knowledge distributes it across revisable anchors — **mono–poly trust**, one living field sustained through many anchors. The technical form of the same fact: preference objectives (Bradley–Terry, RLHF, DPO) are gauge-structured — only differences enter, so the ground level is unlearnable and the apparently neutral zero is **already socially and technically occupied** (datasets, annotators, evaluators, reference policies, institutions). QL/MEF's proposal is to expose that occupation as part of the decision object. The political counter-operation is **divine and concur**: not a third party between poles but the recovery of the `0/1 = 1/0` relation through which every determinate pole is formed — returning institutions to the labour, histories, and conditions they cannot own.

### 8. Attunement, not representation; return, not closure

Life is not the absolute representation of a structured-yet-fugitive truth (the dia-ballein category error) but attunement and alignment with it. Formally: the system reaches octave return **through, rather than by eliminating, its remainder** — the `9/8` by which the totality-ratio `16/9` falls short of the octave is the live tick that gives the next cycle something to begin from; a totality that equalled its octave would be a dead identity. Institutionally: every achieved `1` must return to the context, exclusions, and living relations from which it arose, or it occupies the zero as monopoly. This is one law in four registers; the surfaces should let it recur as seed, echo, development, payoff — never as slogan.

### 9. Legibility without capture

Syn-ballein in governance is reciprocal legibility, not total transparency: expose claims, permissions, sources, and decision baselines while leaving irreducible interiority and local sovereign context unappropriated. Hephaestus's net is the standing test — craft that encloses relation becomes surveillance; a technically perfect enclosure that "contains both sides" is still dia-ballein if the governed parties lose agency, provenance, contestability, and return.
