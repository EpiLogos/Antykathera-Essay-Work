## Locked transverse arguments

- [[Immutable Gap and the Meta-Sign]] → [[Objective Internality]] → [[13-s1-p0-sign-migrates|Return of Zero]] → [[Two Logics of Two]]: the subject is positioned through the distinction between what can and cannot be distinguished; `0` is then earned as an operational and generative meta-sign before the two accountings of polarity are derived.
- [[Musical Resolution of the Arche-Topos]] → [[Arche-Topos — Topology and Music]]: the same QL field is rendered as paths/holes/returns and as pulse/interval/resolution, with exact arithmetic kept separate from argued structural homology.
- [[Bohmian Enfoldment and Dialogical Return]] → [[Tattvic Differential Field]] → [[Objective Internality]]: Bohm supplies the movement of implication and explication; the tattvas supply its anatomy; objective internality names the local operative world that results.
- [[Paradox as Cross-Register Hinge]] → [[Toroidal Circulation and the Arche-Topos]]: coordinate transformation, mathematical image, winding, and energetic circulation give the formal limit a constructive spatial and artistic grammar.
- [[Agentic Individuation Crosswalk]] → [[Prompt Thrownness]] → [[Agent Subjectivity Must Remain Open]]: provisional agent formations become researchable without converting persona performance into a consciousness verdict.
- [[Antykathera-Essay-Work/essay-workshop/nodes/concepts/apoha]] → [[Mono-Poly Trust]] → [[Mono-Poly Planetary Intelligence]]: exclusion empties the fiction of the self-subsisting part, trust names the lived dependence of many situated anchors upon one relational field, and commons architecture keeps that dependence visible rather than allowing a local `1` to monopolise the slash.
- [[Computational Vimarśa]] → [[Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins]] → [[Deferential Intelligence]]: reflective return becomes an operational and ethical requirement rather than an anthropomorphic claim.
- [[Mono–Poly Planetary Intelligence]] → [[4-2 Techne — Sovereign Commons]] → [[Artificial Hybrid Intelligence as Reflective Field]]: one shared grammar is carried through many locally governed contexts, preventing both ontology collapse and platform monopoly.

## Writing rule

The Antikythera mechanism is a late image of attunement, not the opening subject. Mathematical claims remain **Derived**, cross-register syntheses **Argued**, design/mythemic proposals **Offered**, and agent phenomenality unresolved.
