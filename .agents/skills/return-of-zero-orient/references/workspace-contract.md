## Required relations

Sections must expose claim, warrant, tension/limit, transition, sequence, status, sources, and linked concepts or arguments where operative.

Arguments must expose the exact proposition, warrant or derivation, tension/counterpressure/proof boundary, dependencies, downstream consumers, source relations, and status. A title plus fluent paragraph is not an argument node.

Concepts must expose definition, exact work in the argument, differentiations or common confusions, section/argument consumers, sources, aliases, and status where the concept carries a claim.

Canonical source houses remain separate from essay synthesis while keeping bibliographic identity, source teaching and passage evidence together in one navigable work object. Use the relations Extracted, Paraphrased, Argued from, and Resonant with explicitly. Passage text has one canonical transcription in its source house; generated `PASSAGE-LEDGER.md` carries locators and states, never the transcription.

Declared transverse threads are canonical paths with a stable `thread_id`; member artifacts and source houses declare `transverse_threads`. They preserve one argument across stations and registers without replacing each member's own local proposition. The effect map follows only these declared relations and canonical links; shared words, tags, or adjacent folders never establish a thread.

An internal source house with a `local_copy` must describe the actual recoverability state of that file. Agents verify the path before describing source material as absent or locator-only.

Source-house reading sections keep citation and quotation readiness unchanged while exposing lawful reading routes, source-established material, reader exercises or inferences, movement consumers and next acquisition action. Reading prose cannot certify evidence; only a stable passage card with the required provenance can do so.

Room `READING.md` files must expose a short entry, a full sequence, named source IDs, exact quotation-ready passage IDs, movement consumers, pair-writing retrieval instructions, and unresolved debts. “Main source” is always local to a named room burden.

## Context receipt

A valid working context identifies:

1. entry node, its declared register (or the unratified-register debt), and canonical path;
2. previous and next movement when section-bound;
3. controlling arguments and concepts;
4. warrant and live counterpressure;
5. source houses and passage readiness;
6. unresolved links or missing surfaces;
7. the trace by which every item was reached.

Sol, Luna, writing, and review must all use this same receipt. They may extend it, but may not substitute private summaries for it.
