## Worst patterns found

1. **Nouning and link-label subjects.** Link labels and abstractions acting as agents ("the gathering arrests reciprocity", "the account protects itself", "X gives Y"), plus strings of "the X, the Y". Opening rate of "The …" on rebuilt pages fell from 24–38% to roughly 5–15%.
2. **Stacked disclaimers.** A negation or "remains distinct from…" after nearly every claim, in place of one boundary naming its carrier.
3. **Catalogue closers.** `#5→0` and "returns through the consumers" sections that listed ten to seventeen linked records, one line each, with no argument; rebuilt as grouped moves that say what each case adds.
4. **The argument never stated.** The myth or the formal operation was gestured at behind link names. Appropriation was often given without its second chirality or the mirror.
5. **Canon slips.** "Life / Mind as the whole" (now I-Consciousness as the whole, Objective Internality as Life / Mind), hyphen-minus polar forms, "sym-ballein" in prose, inline `\(…\)` maths.

## Three before/after excerpts

**1. Hephaestus whole, the net.**
Before: "This gathering arrests reciprocity: the apparatus holds the participants and their relation visibly together while preventing the movement through which the relation could answer. An account protects itself when it makes the display's measure immune to a reply."
After: "An account that cannot receive a reply from those it counts, and that fixes in advance how each reply will be heard, has the form C27 defines as a protected account. Hephaestus' grievance is legitimate. What goes beyond it is the conversion of one claim into an exhaustive account of two people and their future… Where Hesiod's pair bears Harmonia, the pair held in the net produces a debt."

**2. A13, appropriation.**
Before: "Subtraction measures their whole span from one orientation and attributes that magnitude to its pole."
After: "…which gives the two chiralities of appropriation: `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. Each equation is exact, and each is the same distance charged from the opposite pole… The public reckons the whole span of a harm from its own pole onto one person, and its `+2` is that person's `−2`."

**3. `matheme/dia-syn/dia.md` #2.**
Before: "Cancellation and directed appropriation have three distinct arithmetic results … The subtraction results are not one ambiguous ±2."
After: "Replace the held stroke with arithmetic between the two poles, and three exact results follow: `(−1)+(+1)=0`, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`… appropriation comes in both chiralities because either pole can do the measuring… one party's +2 is the other party's −2."
