# §2 — Two Logics of Two

## M19 — Difference cannot be escaped

The zero has returned to the count. It has not removed the difference by which anything can be counted, known or addressed. A word must hold apart from another word to say anything; a perception must distinguish something within what appears; a judgment must decide what follows and what does not. Even the assertion that all division is illusory distinguishes illusion from truth. The question is therefore what a distinction does with the relation through which it becomes possible. Does it make that relation more intelligible, or take its own achievement as permission to forget it? ^roz-s02-m19-claim01

Two ones are already active in this question. There is the One which remains itself through differentiation, and there is the one which stands forth as something definite. The first cannot be counted as an additional object alongside the second, because counting it would produce another determination within the field whose unity was in question. The second, however definite, arrives through circumstances which its contour does not contain: an act of attention, a language, a contrast, a history, a world. The notation `0/1` holds these different offices together. Zero names One as Self-Identity; one names All as Self-Difference. All means the whole in its differentiation, rather than a completed inventory assembled from originally independent pieces.[^native]

The reversal of ordinary expectation is exact. Zero bears One because the undivided condition is not one item. The first positive mark bears All because even this apparently solitary appearance implicates the field through which it appears. A red mark can be distinguished through its difference from the surrounding surface, through the capacity to see colour, and through the continuity which lets its appearance belong to one experience. The mark neither produces these conditions by itself nor stands outside them. Its independence is an achievement within their relation.

The two offices depend upon one another in different respects. Determinate appearance depends upon its ground for its possibility and coherence; the ground becomes manifest through determinate appearance. These are not two causes waiting for one another to begin. They are the constituting and manifesting directions of one event. Their difference matters because being manifest through something does not mean being exhausted by it. A particular word makes a thought available while leaving room for another word to disclose what the first could not. The failure of exhaustiveness belongs to the efficacy of expression: something real has become sayable, and its reality remains capable of further speech. ^roz-s02-m19-claim02

*Mono–poly* names this whole–many relation before either of the two logics operates upon it. The whole is prior to its parts as the relation through which they can be parts, not as a gigantic object existing before smaller objects. The many are correspondingly real. Their dependence upon a common reality gives them relations to one another; it does not make them interchangeable. Another person belongs within the reality I inhabit and can still exceed everything I know of them. To infer possession from common belonging would install my local account in the place of the whole.

That installation is the root of monopoly in the argument developed here. A determinate formation takes the source-office of what sustains it. An institution may depend upon language, labour, remembered obligations and a population's continuing participation, yet describe its authority as though these were its creations or disposable resources. Its power remains real. What has changed is the account of where that power comes from and who may question its use. The same distortion can occur in a self-image which makes every encounter answer to the person I have already decided to be. The One is then represented by one formation which denies the plurality through which it lives.

Difference thus gives non-duality its positive work. Unity has to be lived through distinctions which remain effective. A consent which cannot be distinguished from refusal is no consent; a reconciliation which erases the injury has not reconciled its participants. To retain difference is to retain the possibility that something can answer from beyond the account presently made of it. There is no need to leave the whole in order to answer it differently.

Comparison itself requires this discipline. *Homologia* brings agreement into question; *analogia* brings proportion and correspondence. Their attested senses give us different ways to ask how two accounts run together, while the present argument requires us to say what operation is preserved and in what respect. A balance returning to zero, a quarrel ending in silence and a person recognising a former enemy may all admit the word “resolution.” They have not thereby performed the same act. The comparison becomes fruitful when their differences let us discover what each resolution has accomplished and what it has left untouched.[^lexical] ^roz-s02-m19-claim03

Such discovery already relies upon a world. We trust enough of language to ask whether a sentence is true, enough of memory to compare this account with an earlier one, enough of another's testimony to begin examining it. Inquiry can correct each reliance; it cannot institute every condition of inquiry before it begins. Knowledge is a performance of trust when it undertakes this dependence. It wears the mask of certainty when it presents its achieved assurance as freedom from dependence altogether.

Knowledge, belief and faith therefore need different offices. Knowledge articulates and warrants a determination. Belief holds an orientation sufficiently steady to guide conduct. Faith consciously carries a relation where complete possession of its ground is unavailable. None gives an unsupported proposition the standing of proof. Faith can sustain examination precisely because fidelity to a relation may require changing what one believes about it. The scientist correcting a result has not abandoned fidelity to truth; the person withdrawing from a betrayed undertaking has not abolished the possibility of trust.[^trust]

The two logics begin within this common necessity. *Dia* makes a difference definite: it separates, measures, chooses and gives a decision its edge. *Syn* gathers what has been differentiated together with the relation that makes it intelligible. Each is a positive operation. Severance occurs when a cut discards its constituting relation; fusion occurs when gathering discards the differences it must hold. Neither failure can be repaired by abolishing the operation whose power it has misused. ^roz-s02-m19-claim04

## M20 — The cut and the power to count

Before assigning a sign to either side, write the relation as a stroke between two unassigned marks: `/ = −/−`. These dashes are not yet negative numbers. They indicate places which can acquire a determinate contrast. Assign signed units, and the relation becomes `(-1)/(+1)`: negative and positive held through a common zero. Each direction means something because the axis is retained. Signed measure has gained an orientation, and a difference has become exact. Dia has done necessary work.[^native] ^roz-s02-m20-claim01

Three operations must now be kept apart. Holding the polarity preserves the terms and their axis. Adding the signed values produces cancellation:

\[
(-1)+(+1)=0.
\]

Subtracting them measures the span in a chosen direction:

\[
(-1)-(+1)=-2,\qquad (+1)-(-1)=+2.
\]

These are correct arithmetic operations answering different questions. Addition gives the net value; subtraction gives a directed difference. Neither result contains everything that was present in the held relation. A deposit and a withdrawal can leave a zero balance while retaining different actors and consequences. A directed span can be measured from either endpoint without making that endpoint the source of the line. Cancellation becomes a philosophical failure when the net result is treated as the disappearance of the relation itself. Appropriation occurs when one pole takes the relation's whole magnitude and authority as its own. The error lies in the transfer of jurisdiction from the result to what made the result possible. ^roz-s02-m20-claim02

The same difference governs an analytic judgment. A distinction becomes useful under a criterion: this observation counts as evidence of this claim. That is *Criterion-through-Distinction*. It yields a measure and, with the measure, a limit to its application. The measure must then work within a frame: these observations, this time, this comparison class, these admitted witnesses. *Delineation-through-Difference* makes that frame available as a perspective. A criterion says how to judge; a frame says what has been brought before judgment. A correct decision under one frame may become false when silently applied to another.

The etymological field sharpens the passage rather than explaining it away. Greek *krisis* includes separation, choice and judgment. Latin *arbiter* distinguishes a witness from a deciding office; *arbitror* carries observation into weighing and judgment. What has been seen, what warrants an inference, and what an authority decides to do remain different acts. Their relation gives arbitration its responsibility. A witness may establish that something happened without being entitled to settle every consequence; a judge may have authority to decide without having personally witnessed the event. Keeping those offices distinct makes their cooperation possible.[^lexical]

A crisis arises when action cannot wait upon a complete account. Someone must select a course, with consequences for others. Arbitration is the positive exercise of that finite office. Its degeneration into *hybris* occurs when the office makes its criterion immune to the relation it judges. The decision then assumes more than practical finality for this occasion: it assumes authority to determine in advance what every objection can mean. A complaint becomes disloyalty; resistance becomes evidence of the defect which justified the original treatment. The criterion has ceased to be one accountable power within the field and claims to be the field's source.[^arbitration] ^roz-s02-m20-claim03

This is the force of the essay's *dia-ballein*: the throwing-apart which becomes diabolical when the common operation disappears from the account. The Greek verb's movement into accusation belongs to its lexical history; the diagnosis of a severed relation is the philosophical operation being developed here. It does not condemn distinction. On the contrary, it demands enough distinction to tell the judge from the witness, the measure from its application, and a disagreement from the conditions under which disagreement has become punishable.

Consider what happens between name, count and account. Two people may have the same name while remaining two; one person may carry several names while remaining one. Discrimination precedes the name's use as an address. Naming makes the achieved distinction repeatable, and counting lets it enter a common reckoning. Yet the person counted can reply. *Count-through-Countenance* names this return of an addressed participant whose answer can alter the description. The verbal proximity of count and countenance supplies no common etymological root: numerical reckoning and the history of bearing or appearance have different descents. Their encounter here asks whether the person made legible by a count can change the terms under which it counts them.[^fields]

A record becomes an account when it can answer that question. The record may correctly identify a person, an act and a result while concealing why this act was selected, who chose its criterion and what follows from its classification. Returning count to account preserves those conditions. It changes a numeral from an apparently self-sufficient fact into an exact result whose use can be judged. Its accuracy need not diminish when its authority becomes more intelligible.

The political danger of dividing is consequently more specific than the existence of opposed groups. A power can separate participants horizontally while retaining their means of combination elsewhere. Workers become individual performances, citizens become cases, institutions become competing units; the conditions which connect them remain under the authority of an office whose own work is absent from the count. Each participant appears dependent upon the centre for a unity which their uncounted cooperation already sustains. One counter-group may take the centre's place without changing this relation. Two opposing monopolies have then been produced where plurality was needed.[^power]

*The Prisoner* gives this asymmetry a world to inhabit. An intelligence agent resigns, is abducted and wakes in a room resembling his own. Outside it is the Village, a settlement from which he cannot leave. He is called Number Six. The visible administrator, Number Two, wants the reason for his resignation. Six asks who occupies the source of command; the recurring reply names the questioner instead: “Who is Number One?”—“You are Number Six.” The question about authority has been answered with a classification.[^prisoner] ^roz-s02-m20-claim04

The Village can arrange the circumstances in which Six tries to test its account. In “Arrival,” an apparent escape by helicopter is remotely controlled and returns him to captivity; his former colleague's reported death is staged; Number Two changes while the institution continues. In “Free for All,” Six is invited to contest the administrator's office. His publicity is prepared, the campaign supplies acceptable answers, and victory brings him to a position whose command cannot free the inhabitants. His assistant becomes the next Number Two. Participation has been gathered inside a procedure which retains the power to decide what participation can accomplish.

The final interrogation changes who bears its consequences. In “Once Upon a Time,” a returning Number Two undertakes a week of isolation with Six and the Butler. Nursery language, counting and enacted stages of life are used to reach the positions from which Six will answer. The struggle reverses; Two dies as the period ends. In “Fall Out,” an assembly praises Six's resistance, revives Two and offers Six a distinguished place. When Six tries to speak in his own person, the assembly drowns him out. He reaches One, removes successive masks and encounters a face identical to his own. He then escapes with the Butler, Two and Forty-Eight. The others diverge on the journey; Six's London door opens with the Village's familiar automatic obedience, and his return to the car recalls the beginning.

The unmasking does not undo the abduction, coercion or apparatus. It makes the desire to occupy the deciding position part of the relation from which escape is sought. Number Two's designation lets an office survive changes of bearer; Number Six's designation lets that office hold a person in an assigned place. The same abstraction liberates institutional continuity and constrains personal answerability. Leaving one enclosure is a real act, yet freedom also requires that its sovereign position cease to govern the next encounter from within. The whole proves the inadequacy of merely exchanging the occupants of an unchanged authority.

Heidegger's distinction between calculative and meditative thinking gives this question another exact setting. His “Memorial Address” grants that both kinds are justified and needed. The danger is calculation becoming the only admitted way of thinking, so that the meaning and use of its achievements never gather for consideration. His simultaneous acceptance of technical devices and refusal of their domination preserves that difference. The present argument carries it into the design of the device and institution: the result must retain a route through which its use and criterion can be questioned.[^heidegger]

A divide-and-conquer procedure succeeds because it also knows how to combine. The local solution must return to the problem whose division made it possible. Political and psychic severance interrupt that passage while continuing to depend upon it. The cut has made something definite; gathering now owes that achievement its relation. ^roz-s02-m20-claim05

## M21 — Gathering across the seam

A broken token keeps an absent counterpart active in the piece that remains. Its fracture has a shape which another piece can answer. Bring them together, and the fit can establish that they belong to this division of this token. The proof requires their difference. Lose either piece and the matching cannot occur; melt them into an undifferentiated lump and the seam which could authenticate the relation disappears. Recognition happens through the maintained break.[^symbolon] ^roz-s02-m21-claim01

The *symbolon* gives *syn-ballein* its material operation: a bringing-together whose fidelity depends upon what it leaves distinct. A half is more than a likeness of a lost whole. It carries the demand for a particular other, and can be corrected by the other's arrival. The relation is therefore One and its own Other, rather than one self-sufficient thing with an accidental second thing appended. “Its own” names the constituting relation, not an entitlement to possess the counterpart. Each is what it is through the division they can recognise together.

What the matching proves also has a limit. A genuine token can belong to an unjust arrangement; an authenticated undertaking can later be betrayed. The fit establishes the claimed relation of the pieces, not the goodness of everything done through them. Precisely because the token performs a determinate proof, another question can remain open. Fidelity loses its force when one successful test is made to answer questions it never tested.

The slash now holds more than the signed opposition of two terms. `0/1` reads the ground becoming available through its mark. `1/0` reads the achieved mark turning towards the ground it cannot enclose. These are opposite orientations of one relation, and their non-coincidence is essential to what they disclose. QL holds them together as `(0/1)/(1/0)`. The inner strokes keep ground and mark differentiated; the outer stroke lets each orientation encounter the other. Its AND retains their participation in one event; its OR retains the distinction by which the event can be read from either side.[^native] ^roz-s02-m21-claim02

This is a declared relational use of the signs, not a new rule for ordinary fractions. In ordinary arithmetic `0/1` is defined and `1/0` is not. The return retains that break: a determination directed towards its own condition does not obtain one more object which could finish its possession of the ground. The mark remains exact in its own office while the inverse reading makes its dependence active. Syn therefore does more than place two conclusions next to one another. It carries the means by which either can be returned to what it presupposes.

The difference can be felt in an argument between people. Gathering their statements does not yet gather their relation. One may speak under a threat the other controls; one may use a word whose accepted meaning already excludes the other's experience. A transcript can preserve every sentence while the encounter remains unavailable to revision. To gather the relation is to let these conditions become consequential for what can next be said or done. The result may be agreement, a clarified disagreement, a changed arrangement or a justified departure. Fusion would decide the outcome by removing the difference which was to answer.

Trust has a temporal form within this passage. *Fides* names the reliance already enacted when someone approaches a witness, uses a word or begins an inquiry. That reliance acquires a *topos*, a place from which an answer is possible: this meeting, this source, this person with a particular competence or vulnerability. *Logos* articulates what can be said and reckoned there. An account then takes effect through *nomos*, the rule or office which determines whose speech changes action. Repetition makes such arrangements part of an inherited belonging; *natio* draws attention to the world into which later participants arrive. They receive the arrangements as given before they can investigate how those arrangements were made.[^trustfield]

Examination brings that inheritance back into question. Its yield is *credere*: an undertaking made again with a bearer, terms and possible consequences. The word's senses of lending, entrusting and confidence keep commitment beside the account which informs it. Fides precedes arbitration; credere becomes possible again after arbitration. This before-and-after is the essay's construction of the relation, not a chronological derivation of the Latin words. It changes the argument because renewed commitment now carries what inquiry learned. Trust has passed through judgment without pretending that judgment can guarantee the future.

Its anchors can be plural. A skill tested in use, the testimony of someone who was there, a relationship which has survived disagreement, an inherited practice open to correction and an institution with effective recourse each sustain reliance in a different way. Their differences allow one anchor to be questioned without the entire world of trust collapsing. Monopoly severs that plurality and then offers one authority as the only cure for the uncertainty it has intensified. Mono–poly trust sustains one shared world through many answerable relations.

Symbol, account and trust consequently perform a circuit. A Symbol conducts a source through a determinate form. An Account makes the form's claims and conditions available for examination. Trust carries an examined account into an act whose future is not yet possessed. The act changes the field from which another account will be made. This last movement is decisive: a returned consequence must be able to reach the source, criterion or permission which can change the next act. Merely storing more information about what happened can leave the governing relation untouched.[^fields] ^roz-s02-m21-claim03

The counter-operation may now be named *divine and concur*. To divine is to recognise the constituting relation through which the opposed positions have become possible; to concur is to let their differences act within that recognition. This is the transcendental third already active in the two, not a third faction standing halfway between them. Its recovery can expose a grave inequality. One claim may fail, one boundary may need defence, one authority may have to relinquish a power. Common ground neither equalises warrants nor requires the injured to complete someone else's image of reconciliation.

A gathering can therefore be judged by its treatment of the seam. Does the other remain able to arrive, to correct the fit, to refuse the use being made of it? Or has the other merely been retained as material within a completed arrangement? The distinction is difficult precisely because an enclosure can contain both sides. A net will show how thoroughly bringing together can arrest a relation. ^roz-s02-m21-claim04

## M22 — What opens the bonds

Demodocus takes up the lyre. Among the Phaeacians, dancers gather around the singer, and Odysseus listens to the song of Ares and Aphrodite. The scene is already held within an encounter: a stranger hears a story of gods whose desires, injuries and arts are offered for human pleasure. What follows begins in secrecy. Ares brings gifts and shares Aphrodite's bed in Hephaestus's house. Helios sees them and tells the husband.[^homer] ^roz-s02-m22-claim01

Hephaestus receives the report in grief and anger. He goes to his forge, sets the great anvil in place and works bonds too fine to be seen, fastening them around the bed and suspending others above it. Their strength is concealed within their delicacy. He then appears to depart for Lemnos. Ares, watching for his departure, approaches Aphrodite and proposes that they take their pleasure while Hephaestus is away. She receives him. They lie down, and the craft closes around them. Neither can move or raise a limb.

Helios keeps watch and tells Hephaestus again. The two reports do different work: the first makes the affair known, the second brings the maker back when his device has succeeded. He returns before reaching Lemnos and calls the gods to see. His complaint is personal and public. Aphrodite scorns his lameness and prefers Ares's beauty and strength; he himself was born misshapen. He demands repayment of the bride-gifts from her father, Zeus, and intends to keep the lovers bound until they are returned.

The spectators do not receive the display in one way. The goddesses stay in their own houses for shame. The gods arrive, and laughter breaks out at the work of the lame craftsman who has caught the swift Ares. An unnamed god remarks upon the victory of the slow over the fast through craft and the fine owed by the adulterer. Apollo asks Hermes whether he would accept these bonds for the pleasure of lying with Aphrodite. Hermes would take three times as many, with every god and goddess looking on. More laughter follows. The evidence has become spectacle, and spectacle has failed to impose a common valuation. What Hephaestus displays as an outrage, Hermes can desire as an enviable bargain.

Poseidon does not laugh. He urges Hephaestus to release Ares and promises that Ares will pay what is due. Hephaestus objects. A promise on behalf of someone who may evade it is a poor bond; if Ares escapes, how could Hephaestus bind Poseidon before the gods? The objection matters. Hephaestus is still exercising judgment, and distrust is doing an intelligible job. The first assurance does not yet answer who will bear the loss.

Poseidon changes the undertaking. Should Ares flee without paying, Poseidon will pay himself. Hephaestus accepts. He loosens the bonds, and the lovers spring free. Ares goes to Thrace; Aphrodite goes to Paphos, where the Graces bathe, anoint and clothe her. The song returns to its delighted hearers. It reports the release and departures. It does not report the later payment of the debt or the restoration of the marriage. ^roz-s02-m22-claim02

The episode's accounts must remain distinct if its transformation is to be understood. Hephaestus demands bride-gifts from Zeus. The spectator names a fine owed by Ares. Poseidon finally assumes contingent liability should Ares default. The grounds and bearers of these claims differ. Treating them as one interchangeable quantity called debt would remove the very change through which release occurs. The question has moved from what can be shown about the lovers to who will answer for what happens after they cease to be held.

Every technical operation of the trap has succeeded. Detection found the affair; fabrication produced the bonds; concealment made them effective; capture prevented escape; the summons made the result public. Their success has not made the next relation. A net can hold a debtor without making a debt good, and a display can establish an act without supplying agreement about its meaning. The story proves this difference by letting the completed apparatus stand while the lovers remain bound. Continuation requires another act, and the poem makes its bearer audible.

Poseidon's surety places someone within the consequence of his own word. His undertaking does not abolish the uncertainty to which Hephaestus objects. It changes who is answerable within it. Hephaestus can now act upon a relation the bonds themselves did not create. He neither forgets the grievance nor discovers that his craft was worthless. He uses that craft to release what it held. Technē survives the transformation because the question governing its exercise has changed. ^roz-s02-m22-claim03

The philosophical force belongs to the whole action. Reducing Hephaestus to technology, Aphrodite to goodness and Poseidon to an abstract faith would lose his injury, her participation, the spectators' divergent desires and the specificity of the pledge. Nor does the tale say that any promise deserves acceptance. It stages an objection and a changed undertaking. Trust becomes possible through judgment, not through its suspension. This is mythemic proof: the relation becomes true to the reader through the acts, refusals and consequences which constitute it. Its warrant remains different from a mathematical derivation and from an assertion that every historical institution works this way.

Homer calls the device a *dolos*, a snare or ruse, and gives its bonds the epithet of craft. Calling its public operation *dia-ballein* is this essay's reading of accusation and division, not a claim that the poem uses that verb. The accusation can be warranted while the means of holding its subjects becomes inadequate to what must follow. The same scene thus preserves a legitimate difference and exposes the limit of possession. An image of both parties held together is not yet an image of their relation being free to change.

Heidegger's account of enframing helps specify this counterfeit gathering. What is ordered becomes available for further ordering; gathering itself can be the form of a setting-upon. The captives are brought together so completely that their remaining possibilities are placed at another's disposal. Read through the Homeric action, the decisive question is whether the arrangement can return what it holds to a relation in which its use remains answerable. The comparison is the essay's, while the poem retains the important fact that the maker finally opens his own bonds.[^enframing]

Harmonia enters through another telling. In Hesiod's *Theogony*, Aphrodite bears Ares Panic and Fear, who throw battle ranks into disorder, and Harmonia, whom Cadmus marries. The same genealogy later gives Harmonia and Cadmus children and carries their union into the life of Thebes. Harmony belongs within generation, marriage and historical succession; it is not a reward narrated at the end of Homer's trap.[^hesiod] ^roz-s02-m22-claim04

The siblings matter. The union of these powers does not automatically produce peace. It also bears the forms in which force and attraction can disorganise a world. Their fruitful relation therefore contains a task. Ares gives difference the capacity to stand, defend, contest and refuse; Aphrodite gives relation its attraction, joining and fertility. Harmonia requires both powers to remain effective without one consuming the other. A joining which makes refusal impossible has lost the difference from which concord could arise; a boundary which makes every approach an enemy has lost the relation in which it could mean anything beyond defence.

Eros keeps the between active, but his provenance cannot be supplied by merging the poems. Hesiod places Eros among the primordial powers and later has Eros and Himeros accompany Aphrodite's emergence from the sea. This differs from the Homeric complaint which calls her Zeus's daughter. The erotic power already exceeds the particular lovers through whom it acts. In the philosophical relation developed here, desire opens a being towards what it lacks; possession tries to close that opening by making the desired other a secured object. The desire which gives relation life cannot be made identical with its fulfilment in capture.

Heraclitus's bow and lyre give harmony another body. Their counter-tension keeps difference active within an instrument: the pull which might appear to oppose itself is what lets the bow act and the lyre sound. Agreement does not require the cessation of tension. Neither instrument becomes the other, and the work each performs depends upon its particular construction.[^bowlyre] The image makes Harmonia exact without reducing her to an abstract compromise. What is held together can acquire a capacity which neither pull has alone.

The author's *hamartia* re-entry puts an archer inside that relation. The shot may reach its intended mark while the account of success forgets the bow, its tension, its maker and the purpose which made the target worth hitting. The mark has been struck and the ground missed. This is a deliberate poetic operation, not a claim that a single Greek root proves the philosophy. It changes the question asked of success: what did the successful act retain of the conditions through which it could succeed? The bow can answer only because its tension has not been cancelled. The musician's return will later make that retained difference audible.

Another net opens from another source-world. In the Chinese Huayan contemplation traditionally attributed to Dushun, jewels reflect one another in Indra's celestial net. The attribution is disputed; the contemplation's action remains recoverable. Choose one jewel in the southwest. The others appear in it, and in their reflections the selected jewel appears again. Its particular position has become an entrance into a field which includes that position within its own appearances.[^indra] ^roz-s02-m22-claim05

The teaching asks how one can enter all the jewels while remaining in this one. Remaining here is how the entry becomes possible: abandoning the chosen jewel would abandon the relation through which the others are disclosed. A second question asks why many jewels are needed if one includes the whole. The answer returns to the one in its comprehensive aspect, constituted through the many it discloses. Any jewel can be selected; none owns the field by being selected first.

An ink dot is placed on the southwest jewel. The mark appears through the reflected field without someone travelling from jewel to jewel to mark each of them. The particular act returns through the others to its point of departure. Yet the source does not stop at this apparent triumph of its image. It states the image's limitation: the jewels' images enter one another while their material substances remain separate; the interpenetration of phenomena being taught exceeds that optical likeness. The closing movement turns towards Vairocana's activity for beings, the formless appearing through forms. The image opens understanding and gives up the claim to contain what it opens.

This self-limiting return is part of the teaching's strength. The chosen jewel remains definite, the mark remains particular, and the likeness remains answerable to what it is meant to disclose. The two-net comparison can now preserve two complete operations. Hephaestus's bonds gather people into an exposure from which release requires an accepted undertaking. The Huayan image gathers a local appearance through its constituting relations and finally makes its own material limitation explicit. Their juxtaposition is a proposed comparison between distinct tellings, not a shared genealogy or a doctrine secretly stated by both. Each corrects an easy reading of the other: mutual visibility supplies no human guarantee of trust, while the maker's release prevents craft from being identified with captivity alone.

Power remains present throughout. Someone sees, makes, desires, decides, bears a possible loss and performs a release. The circulation has costs as well as names. The capacity to make a thing definite requires expenditure; a decision makes one possibility actual; love keeps an other consequential; work gives achieved form back to a world. These positive powers are coequal with what the relation lets us name. The pathology begins when a finished account claims the achievement while withholding its sustaining labour, vulnerability or consequence from the terms of the next act.[^power]

The question which leaves the two nets is therefore practical and inward at once. What authority presently governs a form's use, and can the form's encounter change that authority? A psychic formation can hold experience as efficiently as a net holds its captives. Its release will likewise require more than a change in the objects being held. ^roz-s02-m22-claim06

## M23 — The complex changes its jurisdiction

A feeling-toned complex gives experience a recurrent organisation. Images, memories, bodily responses and expectations gather around a concern, making some things immediately salient and others difficult to notice. Its positive work is considerable. A life could not begin every encounter without memory of what mattered, what threatened it or what made response possible. The problem arises when this local organisation becomes the judge of the whole field in which it operates.[^complex] ^roz-s02-m23-claim01

Consider, as an illustrative case, a delayed reply. A person who has known abandonment may feel the silence as rejection before considering another explanation. The feeling directs attention; attention recalls confirming episodes; the recollections intensify the image; the person withdraws or makes an accusation. The response alters the encounter, perhaps eliciting the distance that the original feeling predicted. A complex has helped produce the world it then recognises. This does not establish that the other was innocent or that abandonment was unreal. It identifies the operation by which a particular injury can acquire jurisdiction over occasions whose differences it has not examined.

Image, valuation and possession are successive pressures within that operation. An image makes something available. Valuation gives it weight: it attracts, frightens, promises or repels. Possession occurs when the weight becomes a demand that the source conform to the image. A contrary reply can then be received only as denial, deception or further evidence of guilt. The account may grow more elaborate while losing the relation through which it could become more truthful. Its increasing explanatory reach conceals a decreasing capacity to be answered.

The source of this organisation cannot be recovered by merely collecting more of its conclusions. A person may know a great deal about their recurrent reactions and still allow the same criterion to determine every encounter. Recognition must reach the authority by which this image judges. That is a change of jurisdiction. The formation can retain its capacity to notice a danger while losing its claim to decide that every uncertainty is that danger. A boundary remains available, and another person's difference becomes perceptible within it.

Van Eenwyk's reading of analytical psychology through nonlinear dynamics gives this distinction a useful morphology. A phase-space represents the possible states of a specified system; a trajectory is a history through those states. An attractor is an invariant organisation towards which neighbouring trajectories tend, and a basin identifies the region from which they are drawn. Different trajectories can remain within one basin. A change of state therefore need not be a change of the organisation governing the states.[^dynamics] ^roz-s02-m23-claim02

The comparison with a complex becomes exact in that respect. Repeated associations can vary while returning to the same feeling-toned organisation. A new response can remain governed by the old criterion. More radical change concerns the available transitions and the conditions which make a particular organisation recurrent. The psychological comparison is an argued model, not a measured identification of a psyche with a specified differential equation. Its usefulness lies in letting us ask which change has occurred: movement within a habitual field, a crossing between organisations, or a transformation of the conditions under which the field is formed.

A symbol can intervene because it does more than furnish another image for an established classification. It makes something present whose force exceeds the available account, attracting a response which alters the interpreter. A dream may gather an adversary with an aspect of oneself; a story may make the captor's vulnerability and the captive's difference simultaneously unavoidable. The image cannot be kept unchanged if its implications are followed. The interpreter's response then changes the world in which the image will next appear. Symbolic life is reciprocal in this concrete sense: what has been expressed becomes a condition of its further reception.

Taylor's reading of Van Eenwyk follows this transformation from the known into the unknown and back. Return matters as much as departure. An experience which overwhelms a settled account may open a possibility, but the possibility has to find a differentiated life: something that can be said, undertaken, refused or practised. Otherwise the old formation resumes its authority, now enriched by a memory of an extraordinary episode which it can classify without being changed. The symbol's force has been received only when the ensuing relation can do something it previously could not.

Synchronic and diachronic attention disclose different aspects of that movement. One holds a configuration together in co-presence; the other follows its history of formation and return. They are not synonyms for syn and dia. A synchronic picture can hold a living plurality or freeze its members into opposed types. A diachronic narrative can recover the relation among successive acts or describe a chain whose common conditions disappear. Equally, analytic differentiation can clarify a present configuration or a past sequence, while gathering can operate across either. The temporal and logical distinctions cross. ^roz-s02-m23-claim03

The energetic side cannot be reduced to an inventory of positions. Libido concerns psychic investment and valuation: a possibility has force because it draws, repels or holds a life. Prāṇa brings breath, vitality and differentiated circulation into view; changing a rhythm of attention and breathing changes how a form can be sustained. Śakti names manifestation-power in the Śaiva register, including the formation, maintenance and concealment through which a world becomes available, and recognition through which its source-relation reopens. Spanda keeps withdrawal and extension within one pulsation. These registers describe different operations. Their coordination belongs to the argument that a pattern gives force a recurrent route while force lets a pattern intensify, vary and reorganise.[^dynamics]

An image can thus redistribute attention and affect; a mantra can join sonic form with breath and cadence; a conceptual distinction can release an investment which had made another thought impossible. None requires the claim that psychic valuation, vital circulation and manifestation-power are interchangeable physical quantities. The shared problem is how a formed route remains capable of transformation. If every difference of force is made to confirm one pattern, the pattern's apparent stability can be purchased by the loss of living responsiveness.

Judgment has to move within this transformation rather than stand outside it. *Continuity-in-Indeterminacy* preserves the life and history which no finished criterion exhausts. It allows a new understanding to concern the same person without requiring the old account to remain sovereign. *Criterion-through-Distinction* then makes the relevant question exact: was this silence evidence of rejection, or had uncertainty itself been treated as sufficient evidence? *Delineation-through-Difference* asks which circumstances and witnesses were included. The distinction between criterion and frame prevents a changed mood from being mistaken for a changed account.[^arbitration] ^roz-s02-m23-claim04

*Arbitration-in-Crisis* concerns the act that must follow. One can ask, wait, set a boundary or leave. The judgment acquires responsibility by acknowledging what it has decided and under which conditions. *Con-text-through-Diaphaneity* then brings those conditions into view through the decision itself: the past injury, the present relation, the assumption about silence and the power to alter the response become visible together. Regard is the practical flowering of that transparency. Another's answer can now reach the place from which the next act is made.

*Resolution-in-Reconciliation* completes a different task from merely ending the episode. What has been learned returns with its history. Anamnesis, recollection, makes the earlier formation intelligible within the changed life; recognition releases its claim to be the whole of that life. The person may retain the original boundary after discovering that it was warranted. They may also revise it, withdraw an accusation or end an undertaking. Reconciliation here concerns a relation becoming answerable through its differences. It cannot require renewed exposure to the injury from which the boundary rightly protects.

This is why individuation cannot be the inflation of one complex into an all-explaining Self. The ego's capacity to act, the complex's organisation of affect and the wider ordering relation within which both arise have different offices. Recognition lets a particular life become more itself by relieving a local formation of an impossible task: securing the whole against every future encounter. Its competence can remain where it belongs. The burden of total sovereignty can be relinquished.

Apollo and Dionysus give this danger of the opposite excess its own movement. In the philosophical Mytheme composed here, Apollo gives a view its communicable form. A contour holds long enough for another person to see what is meant and where disagreement begins. Dionysus opens the formed view to the life in which its maker participates. What the view excluded can alter the one who took it. Nietzsche's two artistic powers contribute their reciprocal expression: each becomes capable of speaking through the other's medium. Their meeting has a form because neither is simply abolished.[^apollo] ^roz-s02-m23-claim05

Release, however, can itself become an appropriating image. A declaration that all are one may demand that another's difference dissolve into participation. The author's introduction of Daphne checks both powers. Her office in this composed whole is the other centre whose response remains her own. Apollo cannot turn illumination into entitlement; Dionysus cannot turn participation into permission to absorb. This is an explicitly authored relation, not an ancient episode in which all three gods enact the argument together.

The two powers must therefore return differently. Form carries its dependence upon the life it expresses; participation acquires a form through which a particular person can answer for it. Daphne remains effective within their relation because an answer can change what is being asked, including the proposed terms of unity. Gebser's diaphaneity names the transparency through which a perspective can disclose its conditions without ceasing to be a perspective. Co-presence preserves the achievements of differentiation within a wider relation rather than dissolving them into a single indistinct state.

The complex's changed jurisdiction is this return in a lived form. Its history remains, its distinctions can still protect and orient, and its energetic investment can pass into another course. What changes is the claim that this one organisation must settle the meaning of all that can occur. Dia has given the life its boundaries and decisions; syn has restored the relation through which those boundaries can become appropriate. The next encounter can be genuinely new because someone remains to encounter it. ^roz-s02-m23-claim06

## M24 — Zero changes its office

Zero was already present in the polar axis. Its return cannot therefore mean importing a missing centre into an otherwise centreless dia. What changes is the work zero performs in the account. A net value can end a calculation; a limiting break can reopen the relation by which a calculation, image or judgment became possible. Cancellation reports the result of an operation. Recognition returns an achieved determination to its condition without cancelling what was achieved. The two zeros may share a sign while performing different offices. ^roz-s02-m24-claim01

That distinction can now be counted. Begin with the two terms `0` and `1`. Retain them as terms, and form their ordered pairings with themselves. There are four: `00`, `01`, `10` and `11`. Order matters because the relation read from one side is not interchangeable with its inverse. The two single terms and the four paired forms therefore give a typed body of six:

\[
\{0,1\}\;\sqcup\;\{00,01,10,11\},
\qquad 2+2^{2}=2+4=6.
\]

The disjoint-union sign keeps the types distinct: a single term and an ordered pair are different kinds of entry. The count has not changed the arithmetic fact that two plus two is four. It has specified another operation, retaining a two together with its four self-pairings. In the native QL reading, the four make relation explicit while the two retain the terms whose difference gives it a field. This is the generative `4+2`.[^native] ^roz-s02-m24-claim02

Writing the paired forms with slashes makes their relational reading visible: `0/0`, `0/1`, `1/0`, `1/1`. This notation retains the different pairings; it does not evaluate undefined ordinary fractions. Nor does the arithmetic alone establish that every real process has six parts. The derivation establishes the body of the operation which has been declared. Its philosophical force comes from what the operation retains: the difference, its self-relation, and the possibility of reading an achieved form through its other orientation.

The whole is consequently more than the catalogue of four results. The singles have not been consumed by their combinations; the source and returning orientations each carry the complete relation. `(0/1)/(1/0)` can turn as `(1/0)/(0/1)` without losing the body whose phase has changed. The first orientation makes a determination available; the return lets that determination become consequential for the next act. Its history travels with it.

The name `5→0` marks this passage from achieved form to renewed ground. It is not a reset which makes everything that happened irrelevant. Poseidon's undertaking remains after the bonds open. A revised judgment inherits the injury it can now place differently. A symbol changes the conditions of its own reception. In each case the result becomes part of what the next encounter presupposes. Return is generative because the same field can now sustain a difference it could not sustain in the same way before. ^roz-s02-m24-claim03

This also gives the political count its test. The Village can call someone Six while withholding the office from which the designation acquires power. The native sixfold retains its own generating relation; the administered number can conceal it. The television scene does not derive the arithmetic. After the derivation, it discloses what its counterfeit does: an answer about the result replaces an answer about the power to count. Restoring that question is part of returning the achieved form to its source.

A formal body is now needed because a relation which is to remain answerable must let its operations be distinguished and repeated. The sixfold answers how the two and its self-relation are retained. The eight determinations which follow answer how that body is traversed: from the unassigned relation, through conscious circumstance, question, force, recurrence, personed context and exact local difference, to the return which cannot make its ground an object. The threshold and the return bracket the six interior determinations; they do not supply two extra members of the same count.

Their mathematics will have to earn each further step. Re-entry must show what is retained when a form takes itself into account; an inverse must preserve the operation whose direction it changes; a local measure must carry the conditions under which it is exact. The Mythemes have made this relation live through injury, desire, craft, judgment and release. The formal work can now give that living distinction a body without pretending to possess the life it articulates. The stroke remains between the terms, carrying their difference into the next act. ^roz-s02-m24-claim04
