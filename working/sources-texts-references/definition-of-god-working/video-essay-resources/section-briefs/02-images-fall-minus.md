## 02. The Images That Constellate

### Visual Thesis

The invisible centre is never shown directly; it is inferred from the patterned orbit of images around it. The visual logic should move from field behaviour to symbolic behaviour: iron filings around a magnet, attractor paths around an unreachable form, crystal structure shaping visible matter, then mythic and scientific images appearing as different apertures of one basin. The key is not "many religions are the same"; it is that symbolic images are real, aspectual, and overdetermined because they refer beyond themselves.

### Must-Have Image Motifs

- Magnetic field patterning: iron filings, invisible cause made visible by arranged matter.
- Jungian archetype / mandala / psyche imagery, preferably historical and sober rather than occult wallpaper.
- Crystal lattice as invisible formal condition of visible form.
- Lorenz or strange attractor, ideally clean mathematical diagrams with phase-space trajectories.
- Polytheistic images: Zeus, Kali, Quetzalcoatl, kept specific and culturally grounded.
- Monotheistic image: Yahweh / divine whirlwind / Sinai / biblical manuscript rather than sentimental God-as-old-man kitsch.
- Scientific images as God-images: gravitational field, standard model, quantum-field or equation imagery.
- Newton prism / spectrum / colour wheel for Eckhart's `in quantum`: white light becoming aspectual colour.
- Sunlight becoming leaf/grain/body: source truly present under an aspect, "and no further."
- Torus / winding paths only as a light prefiguration of later topology.

### Avoid List

- Generic galaxy, nebula, "cosmic spirituality" backgrounds.
- AI-looking mandalas or symmetrical psychedelic ornaments without provenance.
- Flattening traditions into a collage of deities as if they are interchangeable masks.
- New Age chakra composites, glowing silhouettes, stock meditation images.
- Literal magnets used as gadget photography with no visible field pattern.
- "Science vs religion" imagery, boxing-match layouts, or split-screen clichés.
- Overly Christianised universalism, especially images implying all traditions secretly mean one doctrine.
- Random statues of gods without a clear aperture/function in the argument.

## 03. The Fall, And What It Reveals

### Visual Thesis

The fall is not distinction itself. Distinction is innocent: centre and circumference, knower and known, ground and mark. The fall begins when the image forgets it is an image, when aperture hardens into boundary, when the partial view claims to be the whole. Visually, this section should show living relation becoming a closed system: window into wall, lens into idol, symbolon into diabolon, observer into blind spot, subjectivity into extractable data.

### Must-Have Image Motifs

- Infinite sphere: centre everywhere, circumference nowhere; centre/circumference as co-constituting terms.
- Camera aperture, lens, window, mirror: the medium shapes appearance but cannot appear as content in its own frame.
- Ordinary closed sphere / circle as the fallen version: a circumference drawn somewhere.
- Broken token / tessera / symbolon: two halves proving prior belonging without merger.
- Number-line opposition: `1` and `-1` as poles that displace the zero point.
- Ares/Aphrodite/Hephaestus net: craft trying to capture living relation.
- Blind spot / observer problem: science as method versus institution claiming a view from nowhere.
- Jung / imago Dei / shadow: the expelled dark returning by enantiodromia.
- Idol or sealed wall: image no longer letting through what exceeds it.
- Surveillance / data extraction / mass statistics: subject treated as real enough to mine and unreal enough to owe.
- Palantir seeing-stone / Tolkien palantir, if usable via licensed illustration or manuscript-adjacent material; otherwise handle as a custom reference.
- Alchemy / transhumanism: philosopher's stone, opus, `solve et coagula`, but with the missing operator-transformation made visually clear.

### Avoid List

- Generic "fall of man" Eden imagery unless specifically used to show the partial becoming total.
- Moralistic devil/horns/fire images. The fall here is structural misrelation, not cartoon evil.
- Corporate dystopia stock imagery: hooded hacker, blue code rain, generic surveillance camera.
- Over-literal bank/building/bureaucracy shots with no symbolic charge.
- Images that imply science itself is false or fallen; the target is institutional totalisation, not method.
- Random black holes unless used very carefully for unacknowledged gravity/ossification.
- Palantir brand screenshots or modern copyrighted product images.
- Vague "broken mirror" clichés unless the fracture is tied to symbolon/diabolon.

- Infinite sphere logic: centre everywhere / circumference nowhere, then the fallen act of drawing the circumference somewhere.
- Aperture-to-idol sequence: window, lens, icon, wall, with the same frame gradually losing transparency.

## 04. Two Deaths, One Minus Sign

### Visual Thesis

This section distinguishes two zeros: zero as terminus and zero as threshold. The same death-force can cancel relation or return through it. The mythic images should make the arithmetic felt: Apollo and Daphne as `1 + (-1) = 0`, Echo and Narcissus as attritional cancellation, Zurvan as forgotten ground behind dualism, Ares/Aphrodite as the hinge where capture fails, Eros and Psyche as the redeemed line, and alchemical / Shaiva / Egyptian / Sumerian parallels as threshold-deaths that generate rather than erase.

### Must-Have Image Motifs

- Minus sign / subtraction / cancellation marks, but restrained and typographic.
- Apollo and Daphne: pursuit, transformation into laurel, Apollo grasping the tree.
- Echo and Narcissus: reflection without relation, voice without source.
- Zoroastrian dualism: Ohrmazd/Ahriman if historically grounded; Zurvan / Infinite Time if available.
- Ares and Aphrodite caught in Hephaestus' net: capture apparatus attempting to cancel living relation.
- Eros and Psyche: lamp, sleeping Eros, Psyche's labours, descent, death-sleep, immortal wedding.
- Four labours of Psyche: seeds, golden fleece, Styx water, Persephone's box.
- Shiva and Parvati / Ardhanarishvara: distinction-without-collapse embodied.
- Alchemical royal couple, nigredo, Rebis: death as putrefaction and return.
- Isis re-membering Osiris; Inanna's descent; taijitu with reciprocal seeds.
- Eros as metaxu: Poros and Penia, resource and poverty, desire as between.

### Avoid List

- Generic crucifixion-as-suffering imagery unless structurally tied to kenosis and threshold-death.
- Random skulls, graveyards, smoke, or "dark night" mood boards.
- Eroticised mythological imagery that distracts from relation, capture, and arithmetic.
- Simplistic yin-yang clipart with no provenance or historical weight.
- New Age alchemy posters, fake manuscripts, or unlabeled esoteric composites.
- Depictions of Daphne that make the scene merely romantic; the point is failed recognition and terminal zero.
- Treating Psyche as a princess/fairy-tale visual without the lamp, tasks, underworld, and death-sleep logic.
- Modern fantasy images of Zoroastrian figures unless license/provenance is unusually clear.
