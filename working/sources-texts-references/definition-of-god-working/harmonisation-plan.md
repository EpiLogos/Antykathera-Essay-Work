## 0. The governing orientation — what "harmonising" means here

Everything below executes four rules. They are the clarified §7 logic, stated as working instructions; every entry in §2 is designed under them.

**0.1 — One register: every myth is read through BOTH logics.** The two logics are the two arithmetics already on the page, and the identification is exact: **syn-ballein is the 0/1 logic; dia-ballein is the 1 + (−1) logic.** The draft states it itself — L43 gives the 1 + (−1) as "our way of accounting for what the Greeks named dia-ballein"; L45 writes the symbolon as 0/1. A myth, in this essay, is a site where both logics run. Each mythic passage reads its telling through the 1 + (−1) — what opposes, what cancels, where the −1 is exported, where the sum executes — *and* through the 0/1 — what is held, what returns through the zero, where the ground persists — so the mytheme is carried by the notation twice over and has no space to slip free of the matheme register the essay must **demonstrate as much as define**. The draft's strongest passages already perform the double-reading: Daphne told in the fallen arithmetic with the laurel's fragrance "smuggled past the arithmetic" — the 0/1 remainder inside a 1 + (−1) telling (L97); Zurvan's orthodoxy as the 1 + (−1) enthroned, with the 0/1 memory kept under the floorboards (L101); Demodocus staged outright as "the hinge where the two accountings face each other" (L103); Psyche's labours as the 0/1 telling that *contains* the dia-ballein inside itself (L105 — "as the redeemed logic must"). Every entry below therefore leads with its **notation-reading in both logics**, and §7.6's test is absorbed and sharpened: **an insertion passes when both arithmetics have been run through its face.**

**0.2 — Confidence: the essay defines its own mathematical-symbolic language and derives its validity.** L55 deliberately yokes the defined and the undefined across one stroke; L195 states the notation *is* the logic and generates the structure it names; L207 grades every register. Because the language's efficacy is derived, the essay stands in it without flinching. Consequence for this plan: **the defensive hedges are gone.** The kernel (0/1 + 1/0 = 1/1) is a statement *in* the essay's language, whose semantics (co-oscillation summing to unison) it has earned — the borrowed arithmetic's "undefined" is cited once as the contrast that shows what our slash does, never as apology. The mono-poly formula is asserted, not fenced. The L207 contract (Derived / Argued / Offered) is the only epistemic fence, and it is enough.

**0.3 — The seam: the two logics co-equal, in need of balance, imaged as the crucifix.** Dia-ballein — the 1 + (−1) — is a real power with its own truth: real division, real opposition, the negative that Heraclitus makes father of all (L49), the death inside every redeemed telling (L105's own law: the myth "contains the dia-ballein inside itself, as the redeemed logic must"). Syn-ballein — the 0/1 — is the held ratio, the ground kept through the distinction. The two need each other (L47) and neither may win out. The fall is not a logic; **the fall is one logic absolutised**: the 1 + (−1) run as the whole story — cancellation as the only account, the zero as terminus, the −1 exported (L43's "divides *and then forgets*"); or the 0/1 collapsed into fusion — con-fusion (Frank's own hyphenation, Book L62), the halves dissolved, "the proof destroyed as surely as loss would" (L45), the Klein shadow of §7.2. Balance, not victory — and the balance carries a precise asymmetry the draft's matheme already states: **the 0/1 logic can contain the 1 + (−1) as its own moment** (the redeemed line passes *through* zero — 5→0 — it does not avoid it; the nigredo is real, the descent is real), **while the absolutised 1 + (−1) has no place for the ground at all.** That asymmetry is the whole content of "syn-ballein's primacy" (notes §1-F5): **primacy of containment, never victory** — the war kept in the boat, Set at the prow (L217), the capacity for negation held inside the whole (L87). The **crux/crucifix is the image of the held crossing**: two beams, neither reducible to the other, crossing at a centre that is empty — and its arithmetic is the essay's own: two beams, four ends, one quintessent centre = the 2 whose self-relation gives 2², the empty fifth at the crossing = Jung's "circle divided into four with the centre" and the crossed threads in the telescope (L23), completed formally in the fundamental polygon's four directed edges from two generators, **aba⁻¹b⁻¹** (L197). The crucifix is the essay's 4-from-2 given its oldest face — and Frank's own turn derives it from the notation directly: "the / itself carries the inner 4foldness... the infinite plane 0 doesnt just lean but is defined in the /'s 4 sides" (BIG CHAT L464 ✓, his voice).

**0.4 — Harmonise first, insert second.** The pass exists because §7 clarified the thread late; the work is to make the thread **propagate from the founding sections (L41–59, where the throws, the accountings, and the language are established) through everything downstream**. The default action per section is *modulation* — re-tuning existing sentences (verb choices, the vector/orientation vocabulary, notation call-backs) so the seam runs. Insertions are the passages where the seam needs a carrier that doesn't yet exist — and each insertion is admitted as a **notation-reading** (0.1), not as a reference. All the mythic material explored in the revision work — Ares/Aphrodite, Apollo/Dionysus and Nietzsche, Sirens/Orpheus, the swans, Delphi, Hathor, Pisces, Father/Son/Zurvan, Klein/Möbius, the music movement entire — **is retained in this plan**, each with its in-register design and a stated weight; the keep-vs-too-much call is Frank's, via the decision-list.
