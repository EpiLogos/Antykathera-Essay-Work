# The Copula, the Two Throwings, and the Question of Identity

## What this file is

This manuscript synthesises a working dialogue of July 2026 that quilted three bodies of in-house material into the field of *The Return of Zero*: the copula derivation chain (the resource file "The Copula Derivation Chain: Name, Count, Account, Economy," carrying the D0–D9 derivation and its pools), the two-accountings logic of *The Definition of God — Draft 3*, and the essay's Heidegger threads (enframing, calculative and meditative thinking, the world-picture) as they were developed for [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]] and its neighbours. Its centre of gravity is a single movement: **the question of Being, pursued to its end through the copula, becomes the question of Identity — and the 0/1 notation supplies the account of identity that the completed question requires.** Around that centre it fixes four results: a notation ruling that separates the fallen from the unfallen accounting of opposition; the derivation of the 4+2 count from the slash's AND/OR; the lattice of *ballein* compounds as the modes in which the copular act can be thrown; and the double Heidegger — the early digger into the thinned copula and the late thinker of identity — whose own path bends east, with the scholarly controls that bend requires.

Deliberate exclusions, recorded so they are not mistaken for oversights: the Paramaśiva O-logic (the four operations on zero) and the personal-register circuit (I = Is → … → I = Am) are held out of this synthesis by authorial decision; they belong to *The Self-Proving Self* and are not consumed here. The tetralemma is handled in the essay's own meaning-field at [[28-s3-p3-projective-dimensional-reframing|§3 · #3]] and is referenced below only where the derivation touches it; this file must not become a second home for it.

Epistemic statuses follow the essay's contract — **Derived** (theorem or exact source operation), **Argued** (structural correspondence shown, each instance stated), **Offered** (contemplative or mythic fit, never proof) — with verification flags carried inline.

## 1. The two accountings and the slash law

*The Definition of God — Draft 3* states that everything turns on how "One and some Other One" are accounted for, and that there are, initially, exactly two such accountings. The first, fallen accounting renders the two ones as inverse quantities: "1 and −1, each the other's negation, whose sum is zero… Difference under this regime collapses into cancellation because this particular relation projects difference as a war of exclusions, and the ground from which both terms emerged disappears in the arithmetic, only to reemerge as the consequence of War, zero as annihilation generated by the collision of opposites" (Draft 3, the two-accountings passage). Its compression: "2 → 1 + (−1) → 0, a zero that is terminus, nothing left, the account closed." The second accounting writes the pair as 0/1 — ground and mark held through a stroke — and its zero is not a residue but a ground: "the diabolic zero is an annihilation, and the symbolic zero is a ground — the difference between a death that ends the account and a death the account returns through."

The notation ruling follows, and it is a ruling about the slash. Draft 3's own qualification-sentence carries it whole: "**The quality of the ones is determined not by a valence (+ or −) but by a relational orientation (0/ or /0).**" The unfallen accounting qualifies its terms by orientation to ground; the fallen accounting qualifies them by valence against each other. Therefore: the slashed signed pair, (+1)/(−1), is licensed **only** where opposition is still borne within the relation that produced it — the honest analytical cut, dia-ballein proper, distinction that has not forgotten its register. The fallen mode must be written slashlessly and additively, **(−1) + (+1) = 0**, because the fall *is* the deletion of the slash: opposition claimed without the shared register in which alone opposition is possible. Draft 3 gives the operation its theological notation in the Antichrist passage — "0/1 loses the '0/' and forces '1' into self-opposition, the '0' returning as the annihilating sum" — and its epigram in the line already carried by the essay: the devil is the literalist of the minus sign, reading zero as cancellation where Logos reads it as return.

The operator semantics that make this ruling derivable rather than stipulated live in *The Self-Proving Self*. There the slash is defined as the operator that "holds them as two while preserving them as one"; the "+" is "topological composition of explicate and implicate" — composition, expressly not cancellation, so that when the *slashed* inverse pair is added in the First Spanda Equation, the interference term (1/0 + 0/1) resolves upward to 1/1, wholeness, not downward to zero; and the zero reached at the sixfold's close is "not as cancellation but as *return*." The whole ruling compresses to one law:

> **Terms that carry their ground compose; terms stripped of their ground cancel.**

The comparability principle of the copula chain (its axiom D0: "Two things cannot stand in opposition unless they share the ground that makes them comparable") shows what kind of law this is. Since opposition without a shared register is impossible, the fallen accounting is not a rival ontology; it is **fraudulent bookkeeping of the only relation there is**. The ground denied does not vanish; it re-emerges "as the consequence of War" — exactly as the hidden zero of preference systems re-emerges as displaced trust ([[40-s5-p3-preference-hidden-zero|§5 · #3]]). Three failure signatures now bracket the two genuine logics, each with notation, mytheme, and technical form: **severance** (the fallen dia: slash struck, additive cancellation, the scapegoat's export), **fusion** (syn's native shadow: "fusion would destroy the proof as surely as loss would"), and **enclosure** (the counterfeit gathering of [[counterfeit-gathering]]: a syn-shaped apparatus run in dia mode). A compact mythic-epistemic face of the fallen/unfallen difference is held for its own development pass (the corpus quote bank's Venus vector, "morning-star–evening-star," with its anchors: Revelation 22:16, Isaiah 14:12 in the Vulgate, the Hesperus–Phosphorus one-body identification, and Frege's sense/reference puzzle, which concerns exactly those two names of one planet); it is registered here and not developed, per the sweep's own "not yet mined" status.

## 2. The AND/OR stroke and the 4+2 count

Draft 3 derives the sixfold from the stroke itself, and the derivation should be consumed from there rather than re-invented. The slash "works double: read as arithmetic it is the fraction-stroke… and as the sign for division; read as a logical operator it is AND/OR, a stroke that declines to decide between conjunction and disjunction and holds both." Under that stroke, 0/1 reads as the ground expressed through the distinction and 1/0 as the distinction held within the ground — "each the one relation looked through from its other end" — so the notation "generates its own counter-reading in the act of being written," the pair standing as (0/1)/(1/0), "the stroke now standing between the two orientations of itself."

The count then falls out: "A distinction is a two: ground and mark… A distinction held under the AND/OR must also distinguish its own two readings… and a two applied to a two is 2²: four — … the four pairings of ground and mark, every way the two can meet itself. The superposition keeps both counts alive at once, the original two AND its self-relation: 2 + 2², two and four, six positions in all. Nothing was added; the six is what a two amounts to once it includes itself." (An authorial margin-note directs the final tightening: state it in the binary register — the binary plus the binary-of-the-binary — which is also the formulation the central plan already carries.)

Two consequences are recorded here and consumed elsewhere. First, the four positions of the classical tetralemma — is, is-not, both, neither — are the four pairings of ground and mark discovered in the wild as *copular positions*: four ways the "is" can be thrown once the stroke holds conjunction and disjunction together. The essay's treatment of this, including the modern formal neighbour (First Degree Entailment's four values as the subsets of a two-element set, and the reconstruction that adds a fifth, silent value), lives at [[28-s3-p3-projective-dimensional-reframing|§3 · #3]] and is not repeated here; this file adds only the ledger line the derivation earns: **the catuṣkoṭi is the 4; the modern reconstruction reaches 4+1; QL derives 4+2** — both implicates, the pole-pair whose self-relation generated the four, which is why the completing traversal appears in the tradition only as practice. Second, the derivation is what licenses the lattice of the next section: if the four are the pairings of one throw's two readings, then the Greek prefix-family of the throw is the natural place to find the pairings' modes named.

## 3. The ballein lattice: modes of the throw

Greek builds a family of relation-words on *ballein*, "to throw," exactly as it builds a family of gathering-words on *legein* — and *The Self-Proving Self* has already stated the method for reading such families: in its catalogue of *\*leg-* compounds (dia-logos, ana-logos, syllogismos, eklektikos, katalogos, epi-legein), "the root supplies the picking; the prefix supplies the geometry." Applied to *ballein*: the root supplies the throw — the copular act, the casting of a relation — and each prefix supplies a geometry of that act. The etymologies are lexically solid; the assignments to the essay's logic are the project's own and carry **Argued** status.

| Compound | Literal sense | Mode of the act | Place in the logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| *syn-ballein* → symbolon | throw together | the halves' rejoining as proof of prior bond | the retained-seam logic; trust honoured |
| *dia-ballein* → diabolē | throw across / apart | distinction; and, in the classical lexicon, accusation staged before witnesses | the honest cut when slashed; public accusation as its social form |
| *pro-ballein* → problēma | throw before | the question cast ahead, catchable, resolvable | the interrogative determination (?/!); projection in the constructive sense |
| *para-ballein* → parabolē | throw beside | meaning set alongside rather than asserted | the mythemic mode: the parable teaches beside, where the accuser throws across |
| *amphi-ballein* → amphibolia | throw both ways | held ambiguity | the AND/OR itself, worn as a word |
| *meta-ballein* → metabolē | throw across-into-change | transformation through; "unmetabolised duality is un-thrown-through duality" | the processing return; with *kata-bolē* (the downward, founding throw — the New Testament's word for the foundation of the world) and *ana-bolē* (the upward throw) as its two arcs |
| *peri-ballein* → peribolē | throw around | the enclosure (the *peribolos* is the enclosure-wall) | the counterfeit gathering's own family name: the net that gathers by arresting |
| *ek-ballein* → ekbolē | throw out | expulsion, casting-out | the fallen accounting's social discharge: "the '−1' knowledge refuses to hold… gets exported onto a scapegoat" (revision notes) |
| *hyper-bolē / elleipsis / parabolē* | over-throw / falling-short / exact application | excess, deficit, and exact fit — the triad by which Apollonius named the conic sections | the two ways a throw misses its measure, bracketing the exact fit: fusion as excess of union, severance as deficit of relation, the true relation as the parabolic fit |

The sibling family is Latin *tradere*, "to hand over," root of both *tradition* and *treason*, with *betray* alongside: one root, both valences, exactly as with *ballein* — every handing-down capable of handing-over. The lattice is not a thirteenth register and must not organise prose; its home is the concept layer, the argument map, and one developed movement, per the essay's rule of three. What it contributes structurally is this: dia-ballein and sym-ballein are not merely two logics of two — they are two *castings of the copula*, and the remaining compounds name the other castings the one act admits, including the pathological ones (the around-throw of enclosure, the out-throw of the scapegoat) that the essay's technical sections diagnose.

## 4. Heidegger, the copula-digger

Heidegger enters this braid twice, early and late, and both entries concern the copula.

**Early: the thinning of Being into the "is."** The *Introduction to Metaphysics* (1935 lectures) devotes a chapter to the grammar and etymology of "Being" and finds that the verb "to be" is not one root but three, fused and levelled: *es-* ("life, the living… the self-standing" — the "is" of presence), *bheu-/bhū-* (Greek *phyein*, "to emerge, to hold sway" — the "is" of arising), and *wes-* ("dwelling, abiding"). "From the three stems we derive three initial and vividly definite meanings: living, emerging, abiding" (trans. Fried and Polt, 2nd ed., pp. 78–79). The smallest word is itself a gathered family — and what history has made of it is a "used-up term": the lectures open on the alternative, "Is 'Being' a mere word and its meaning a vapor, or is it the spiritual fate of the West?" (p. 41). Kant's thesis that Being is not a real predicate but mere positing (*Setzung* — a *setzen/stellen* word: the set-family entering the copula itself) belongs to the same history: Being restricted to the copula's thinnest register, position and predication, until it evaporates into the emptiest of concepts. Read against the copula chain's first derivation-step (the copula's two registers: predication and identification, "is" and "="), this is the collapse of the copula told as the history of metaphysics — the identification-register going dark while the predication-register does bookkeeping. And **calculative thinking is this collapse as a cognitive mode**: the "is" executed as positing and equation-of-marks, "never stops, never collects itself" (*Discourse on Thinking*, p. 46); meditative thinking is the copula's other register kept alive — the "is" dwelt in, *legein* as letting-lie-together, *Gelassenheit* as the released relation to what is. The two thinkings are the two registers of the one copula, which is why the essay's two logics and its Heidegger thread were always one thread.

**Late: the Being-question becomes the Identity-question.** *Identity and Difference* (1957, trans. Stambaugh) takes the principle of identity and shows it was never blank tautology: "The formula A = A speaks of equality. It doesn't define A as the same"; the fitting formulation is that "every A is itself the same *with* itself. Sameness implies the relation of 'with,' that is, a mediation, a connection, a synthesis" (pp. 23–24). Identity says belonging-together: "Man and Being are appropriated to each other. They belong to each other" (pp. 31–32); the *Satz* der Identität is read as *Satz* in the sense of leap — "the spring is the abrupt entry into the realm from which man and Being have already reached each other" (p. 32); and the name for that realm, *Ereignis*, "can no more be translated than the Greek Logos or the Chinese Tao" (p. 36) — Heidegger's own sentence placing his key word beside the Tao. The essay receives this as the decisive shift: **what is Being? becomes what is the "=" ?** — and it is here that the in-house account completes what the late Heidegger opened. The 0/1 gives identity's structure: the "=" is an act with a subject-pole (in *The Self-Proving Self*: "the equals sign is the Self in formal dress… the equation's own capacity to equate"), grounded in a non-coincidence it can neither close nor sever. Identity cannot simply equal itself — bare self-coincidence is empty (Frege's "Hesperus = Hesperus" says nothing; a mark equal only to itself has no ground to be equal *in*; the 1 never contains its 0) — and cannot simply not-equal itself — bare self-difference violates the comparability principle, since even non-coincidence needs the register in which the non-coinciding terms meet. Identity lives as the held both-and-neither; the strong copula "identifies what it distinguishes and distinguishes what it identifies, equally, in a single act." This is also the exact point of contact with the venue: the Agentworld brief's own sentence — an agent "is not simply what it appears to be, and what it appears to be is not what it is" — is the non-self-coincidence of identity stated technically, and provisional individuation is identity-as-act rather than identity-as-possession.

**The onto-theological guard.** The same volume carries the constraint under which the theological register is handled everywhere in this project's venue-facing work: the god of philosophy, *causa sui* — the perfectly self-coincident, self-caused identity — is the god before whom "man can neither pray nor sacrifice… can neither fall to his knees in awe nor can he play music and dance" (p. 72), and "the god-less thinking which must abandon the god of philosophy… is thus perhaps closer to the divine God." The essay's refusal to make God a term of its argument is therefore not caution alone but philosophical correctness: a self-coincident God-identity is exactly what the account of identity above rules out, and the register in which the divine name can be handled (where its identity includes its own non-coincidence) belongs to the parallel theological corpus, not to the essay's assertions. See §7 below.

## 5. The eastern turn, with its scholarly controls

Heidegger's path itself bends east, and the essay may use this — carefully — as the Western terminus turning toward what apoha-type and Taoist thought had held all along: the determination that remembers its exclusions; the name that is not the ground ("'God' as a name *is not God*; as the Taoists say, 'It is before the name "God"'" — Draft 3's own sentence). The documented record: in spring 1946 Heidegger proposed to the Chinese scholar Paul Shih-yi Hsiao a joint German translation of the *Laozi*; they worked through that summer and completed eight of the eighty-one chapters — Parkes: "chapters of the Laozi containing the word *dao*" — before the project lapsed, the drafts unpublished and lost. Hsiao's own account ("Heidegger and Our Translation of the Tao Te Ching," in Parkes, ed., *Heidegger and Asian Thought*, 1987) records that Heidegger "asked penetratingly, tirelessly, and mercilessly — about every imaginable context of meaning in the mysterious interplay of the symbolic relations" of the text, and confesses "a slight anxiety that Heidegger's notes might perhaps go beyond what is called for in a translation." Heidegger's free rendering of chapter 15, which he had Hsiao brush in calligraphy: "Who can be still and out of stillness and through it move something on the Way so that it comes to shine forth? Who is able through making still to bring something into Being?" Alongside: the 1930 reading from Buber's *Zhuangzi* (the joy-of-fishes exchange, reported by Petzet), the "Dialogue on Language" with Tezuka, and the jug-emptiness passage of "Das Ding" beside *Daodejing* 11.

The influence claim has a scholarly control and the essay must carry it. Reinhard May's *Heidegger's Hidden Sources: East Asian Influences on His Work* (trans. Parkes, Routledge, 1996; German *Ex oriente lux*, 1989) argues from close textual comparison that Heidegger's formulations "on Being, Nothing, the clearing, and on the complex relations between language, Way, and Saying" were shaped — "on occasion almost word for word" — by German translations of Daoist and Zen texts (von Strauss's and Wilhelm's *Laozi*, Buber's *Zhuangzi*, Ohazama–Faust's *Zen*). Reception is divided and trending sceptical on the strong claim: Parkes himself hedges ("scholars… may persist in taking the similarities… as purely coincidental," and there is an alternative genealogy through the West's own reception of Asian thought since the eighteenth century); Lin Ma's *Heidegger on East-West Dialogue* (2008) is the forceful deflationary control; Stella Sandford presses that May's comparison is under-theorised. The essay's rule: the documented *encounter* (Hsiao, the owned translations, the dialogues) is solid and usable; May's inference from parallel to appropriation is **contested — cite as claim-with-control, never as fact**; and the essay's own use is neither — it needs only the emblem, which no one disputes: the West's deepest digger at the copula ended his path translating the Way, and named his own key word untranslatable "as the Greek Logos or the Chinese Tao."

## 6. The thrown and the set: two families under the one method

German runs on *werfen* (to throw) the operation Greek runs on *ballein*, and Heidegger's existential analytic is built from it: *Geworfenheit*, thrownness, the being-already-cast-into (which the essay already consumes technically as [[Prompt Thrownness]]); *Entwurf*, the projection or throw-forth of understanding (pro-ballein in German dress); and *Verfallen*, falling — the fall as the third structural moment. The fallen accounting of §1 thus has an existential-analytic twin, native and structural. Against the throw-family stands the set-family: *stellen* and its modes — *herstellen*, *vorstellen*, *bestellen*, *darstellen* — which *The Question Concerning Technology* gathers into *Ge-stell*, the enframing that orders everything to stand by. The relation between the families is the relation the essay's technology sections turn on: **the throw names the living act; the set names its arrest.** The two lattices cross at one position — *peribolē* in Greek, *Ge-stell* in German: the enclosure, the gathering that arrests — which is why the counterfeit gathering of [[22-s2-p3-ares-aphrodite-harmonia|§2 · #3]] can be named from either side. And the method itself — prefix-family as logic-lattice — is one Heidegger practises (on *stellen*, on *legein*, on the three stems of "being" itself) and the essay practises independently (on *ballein*, following its own *\*leg-* catalogue): a methodological homology worth one sentence in prose and no more.

## 7. The register constellation and the handling of the divine name

The materials gathered here run across three registers that the essay's register grammar ([[register-grammar]]) names matheme, mytheme, and episteme, with the symbolon as the fourth that gathers them. The copula derivation and the notation ruling are matheme-work; the two throwings, the fall, and the redemptive Venus vector are mytheme-work; the Heidegger history, the Kripke–Frege line of the copula chain, and the May controversy are episteme-work. "God" is the name that stretches across all three at once — which is precisely why, in the venue-facing essay, it is handled by discipline rather than assertion. The working rule (the corpus's own F1 copula rubric): bare "X is Y" assertions are a writing-fault unless earned by operation or citation. The essay therefore never writes the equation of Subject and God; it lets the totalising nature of the subject be *expressed as anatomy* — the definition of subjectivity at the formal limit, objective internality, and the tattvic detailing ([[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|§0 · #4]]) — while the traditions' own first-person utterances (the burning bush's *ehyeh asher ehyeh*; *aham brahmāsmi*) may be cited as what they are: the originary expression of the divine speaking from the I. The onto-theological analysis of §4 supplies the philosophical warrant for this silence: the God-name asserted as a term would be the causa sui, the self-coincident identity the whole account rules out. The register that can hold the divine name — where identity includes its own non-coincidence, where the complete valence-field and the complete lemma-space are internal to the divine — is the theological corpus (*The Definition of God*), developed in parallel and linked, not imported. This constellation of registers is under active development in parallel sessions; this file marks the seams and does not close them.

## 8. The Eckhart link

One lineage runs beneath several of this file's threads and should be held visibly: Meister Eckhart. *Gelassenheit*, Heidegger's word for the released comportment — the yes-and-no toward technology, received by the essay as the AND/OR slash practised as a stance — is Eckhart's word first (*gelâzenheit*, releasement, letting-be), a lineage the essay already touches through Kurt Flasch's study in the apophasis braid of [[04-s01-p3-formal-limit-genealogy|§0/1 · #3]]. Eckhart's *in quantum* ("insofar as") is analogia sharpened to an edge — Draft 3's own treatment: Being "said in many ways… senses really different, true in ordered relation to their primary" (*pros hen*), with Eckhart's operative delimiter as "that rule sharpened" — which is to say: the copula's many-senses-without-collapse, the omnilemmic structure of the "is" at the scale of a single qualifying phrase. The braid is thus closed at both ends: the same master stands behind the apophatic negation of §0/1 (predication driven through its own qualification) and behind the released comportment of §5→0 (the lived AND/OR) — one figure, entering twice, at the essay's opening limit and at its practical close.
