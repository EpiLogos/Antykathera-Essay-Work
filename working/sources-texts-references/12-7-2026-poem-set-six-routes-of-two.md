# The Six Routes of Two — Poem Set

Frank's own compositions, pulled verbatim from the canonical source (`test-site/src/poems.js`; "the verse, verbatim and immutable"). This is the **poetic parallel to [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy|the core theorems]]** — the same QL sixfold spine, said in the register of enacting verse rather than derivation. Each poem sits at a QL position (`P0–P5`) and grounds the section at that position; the walk runs **0 → 5** and wraps back through the loop.

A full 6-fold: **five verbal poems + the matheme as the sixth** (the poetic register of number). The **hidden seventh** is not a seventh poem but a seventh *position*: read under both number-bases (0-rooted 0–5, 1-rooted 1–6) the `4+2` set spans **0–6** — the 6 turning back to recollect the one that is its two (worked in verse at P3, in number at P0). This is Frank's "6 or 7."

**MUST-KEEP PAIRING (from source):** P3 (honours the cut — "two plays itself apart") and P4 (the critique of severing thought) belong near each other; individual poems lean, and the pair holds the balance.

## P0 — #0 · The Giving · *the two roads of two*

*Matheme: the body is the raw numbers themselves — the two directions taken from 2, set side by side. The register is the poetics of number.*

```
2  →  (−1) + 1  =  0
2  →  0/1  →  00, 01, 10, 11  +  0, 1  =  4 + 2
```

> The fork in raw number. The first road is **dia-ballein** — two rendered as inverse quantities summing to the terminus-zero. The second is **syn-ballein** — two carried through `0/1` into the four self-combinations plus the pair, the `4+2`. The generative road continues beyond this fork into the full return — `4+2 → 5→0 → 0/1` — the recollective wrap-back "with love" (the `→` is the turn where love enters the number). *Pairs with [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Theorem IV]] (Synballein & Diaballein) and the matheme of Theorem V.*

## P1 — #1 · Hero · *where the powers grow*

*The first line is the site's long-standing strapline; the poem is the negative ground — what the powers grow from.*

> What binary cannot compute,
> What thinking cannot know,
> Where two see the common root,
> There our powers grow.

> The definitional seed named apophatically: the power is located precisely at what binary cannot compute and thinking cannot know — the common root two sees when it does not sever. *Grounds the §0/1 threshold (the subject at the formal limit) and the non-dual register the whole site rides on.*

## P2 — #2 · Doorway threshold · *the word at its operational limit*

> Where life leads,
> There words transfix.
> Where life flows,
> There words shall slip.
> Where language stays,
> Understanding frays,
> Let language cave,
> And lay bare the way,
> Over the edge, under the lips.

> Process at the limit of the sayable: the word transfixes what is still, slips from what flows, and must *cave* — the surface opening to lay bare the way. (The site's interaction metaphor is sourced here: the surface caves open to the prose beneath.) *Grounds the passage where language meets its edge — Wittgenstein's silence, the `?/!` register.*

## P3 — #3 · Grammar (What QL Is) · *the two routes, in words*

*The pattern made visible — the number-drama told in language. Two stanzas: the broken two, then the true two.*

> **2 to two ones**, one or the other one, summed to none, already a one and done-er.

> **2 through the two ones**, the whole and natural number, where one as first is both rooted in none and number, the 0 and 1, One and All, never rent asunder. Never falling into enmity, two plays itself apart and becomes again projected by it's chiaroscuric/contrastive arts, two upon itself, plus itself, **4+2**. Looking back with love it wraps itself up, 6 perfection seeking recollection of the one that is it's two, ring around the Rosie until you fall down through the loop.

> The two routes in words. *To* (not *through*): 2 lands on two bare ones, "one **or** the other," summed to none — the cancelling route, spent in a single move. *Through*: the pair is traversed, the whole/natural-number distinction load-bearing (one "rooted in none and number," the 0-inclusive set). Two *plays* itself apart (generative, not fallen), "two upon itself, plus itself" = `4+2`; the "looking back with love" is the `5→0` return; "ring around the Rosie until you fall down through the loop" turns the nursery-rhyme's *all fall down* into the **Möbius pass-through** — the drop that completes the circuit. *Directly parallels [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Theorem IV]]'s two accountings and Theorem III's perfect-number recollection. **Must-keep pair with P4.***

## P4 — #4 · The Heart · *the One as the issue of the age*

*Diagnosis-in-context. One continuous piece; stanza breaks are the source's scroll-station divisions.*

> There's one underlying issue in our age. One could say, One is the underlying issue of our age… Let me rephrase, how the One is to one, this underscores and is setting our stage.

> Collectively we haven't a frame open enough to let the evening wane or the one air in through released panes, to be breathed all the same. Are you one? Are we one whole together? Galaxies, planets, seasons, angels, demons, bodies, bots, the weather?

> Where between each one is One's tether?

> Two is the first sign that one must endeavour, married to the bond by a lifetime of together, time dividing not for war but for dreaming towards forever.

> The one issue; we forgot that one really is two.

> We almost always see to two as two, because mind must split, divide and polarise, to know is to sever. What amounts, by all accounts, isn't very clever.

> Firstly, it is completely derived. So late on the scene did this thinking arise, yet its high water mark is hubris despite; each apotheosis a reason to demonise.

> Secondly it measures by limiting a sky, by making a cloud where an aeon could fly, blocking light to reify.

> Third it seals with patterned definition, neither side holds the other, nor self recognition.

> Alive is a logic landing humility as provision, as primary and axiomatic, as prerequisite of precision.

> We start with the sky open wide, with the one truth for which thought has no vision, ever aloof, zero-and-one as one impossible act of re-cognition.

> The contextual diagnosis: the age's one issue is *how the One is to one*. The three-part indictment of severing thought — it is derived (late, hubristic), it measures by limiting (the cloud where an aeon could fly), it seals by patterned definition (no mutual holding, no self-recognition) — sets humility as the provision, axiomatic and prerequisite to precision. *Grounds the §4 context station; carries the essay's cultural-crisis diagnosis. **Must-keep pair with P3.***

## P5 — #5 · The Question · *God, the limit, speech*

> God is not a thing to argue over, to believe in or deny; it is a word with many contexts, evolutions and definitions, that points somewhere no word can stand. Every word is born of difference, hold the different ones in hand; left and right run away from each other, but together master the span. We define God in terms that require no belief; we use numbers as symbols to express what words alone couldn't reach. Before All, God has always been the One, and yet we're beached, 2000+ years and the formula unimpeached. Number is the language of demonstrative proof, not age-doctored doctrines distilled from real visionary excretes. We offer a coherent and evolving account of God, one that includes it's own release; whereof one must be silent, there one shouldn't speak. And yet the essence of the project is to find the limit, so, to speak.

> Synthesis and release: God as the word pointing where no word can stand; number as the symbol reaching what words cannot; the account that *includes its own release* (Wittgenstein's silence honoured and then stepped past — "the essence of the project is to find the limit, so, to speak"). *Directly parallels [[working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy|Theorem V]] (the six-fold definition of God/the-Subject) — the poem is that theorem's synthesis said as verse.*
