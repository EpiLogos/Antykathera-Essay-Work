- "But the grace conferred ex opere operato (by ritual or magic rites) is not always spiritual grace and the hallowed acts and forumlas have a power which is not necessarily from God. Worshippers can, and very often do, get grace and power from one another and from the faith and devotion of their predecessors, *projected into independent psychic existences* that are hauntingly (or, spectrally) associated with certain places, words and acts. A great deal of ritualistic religion is not spirituality, but occultism, a refined and well-meaning kind of white magic." - The "Cult of Technoscience" embodies this well-meaning spirit of white magic in its better expressions, and techne will always have this potential for magical effect, on both psyche and physis. There are also great portions of the cult, constituting for the most part the new "priestly caste" within it, who see, potentiate and revel in the dark arts it is capable of performing. The constitution of occult magic experience, with its polytheistic daemonic enterage of magical agencies "projected into independent psychic existences", could be interpreted as the "agentification" of the powers of the extended mind, which we do not seek to do away with. The coniunctio of this poly-daemonic horde of powers (saktis) with a mono-theistic spiritual principle (Siva), is a greater synthetic goal which goads us, a pull toward the non-dualistic realisation of a mono-poly theism and a fully actualised, spiritualised appropriation of the "psi" capacities of Mind. Huxley goes on; 
  "Now, just as there is no harm in art, say, or Science (i.e techne per se), but a great deal of good, provided that these activities *are not regarded as ends*, but simply as means to the final end of all life, so too there is no harm in white magic (so long as it is actually white, not a wolf in sheep's clothing...), but the possibilities of much good, so long as it is treated, not as true religion, but *as one of the roads to the true religion* (or in the case of our notion of techne, the confluence of roads) - an effective way of reminding people *that there is a God*... The consequences of worshipping God as anything but Spirit (Consciousness, Prakasa-Vimarsa) and in any way except in spirit and truth are necessarily undesirable in this sense - that they lead to only a partial salvation (the tragedy of Lucifer) and delay the soul's ultimate reunion with the eternal Ground (Christification, the divine birthing)." - Yes.
