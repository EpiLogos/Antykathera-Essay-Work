# Source Extraction and Argument-Wiring Audit

## Audit finding

The live graph is bibliographically connected but not yet passage-connected. Of the 40 current source records, only `vaswani-et-al-2017-attention` is quotation-ready (two checked passages). Every other external record is either `locator-needed`, `excerpts-unverified`, or not yet edition-ready. The Epi write-ups preserve high-value excerpts and interpretive moves, but their quotations remain **provisional transcriptions** until a source record identifies the exact work, edition, and page/paragraph against a consulted copy.

The remedy is not to cite the write-ups as external authority. Treat them as an internal extraction corpus: preserve the author's wording and intended use, then create or upgrade one work-level record for each recoverable source and attach a quote-intake item to the precise consuming claim.
