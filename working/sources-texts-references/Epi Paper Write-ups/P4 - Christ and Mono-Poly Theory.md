Christ's symbolic monopolisation of the Divine Sonship may well be interpreted as the monopolisation, by the Logos, of Truth (i.e the Father) in language - the fixity and rigidity of meaning we ascribe to words and concepts echoes the inflexibility of the Divine Progenitive title. The doctrine of the Paraclete, as a dynamic mediating principle (the 3rd person), acts as a way to diplomatically apportion truth to a greater variety of "Sons", of divine words, a premonitory pointing toward a rising pluralism in the appellation and expression of Truth. Wisdom, Sophia, the feminine counterpart of Truth, fully realises his creative fluidity that must be applied in the expression and impression of Truth, in that Wisdom knows that Truth, metaphysically eternal, must be mercurial in its becoming, as certain and sure as it is sliding over and beneath itself, curtailing itself, failing to live up to itself. You could say that Wisdom is this very flowing of Truth, or rather flowing *with* Truth. Truth-with-flowing is what allows for a transcendent principle of Truth to become the true-ness of things and beings in manifestation; the masculine principle alone could not afford a living, dynamic reality, but must afford the very changefulness that sacrifices the perfection of Being-Truth for something greater; the completeness of *Truly-Becoming*.  Christs positive link to the Father expresses the power of fixed meanings to elucidate Truth, as Christ remains connected to Mary, the Divine Mother, and thus enlivens the Fathers atemporality with his dependence on the divine feminine, the creative principle par excellence. The Anti-Christ, on the other hand, represents the stagnation and fossilisation of "truths" that eventually befalls any attempt to monopolise Truth and Being with the Word in its temporal becoming - this is why the Anti-Christ becomes patriarchal, the Divine Fatherhood cannot be expressed when masculinity becomes onesidedly determinant of Truth. The special connection between Sophia and God the Father reflects the rooting and identity of Wisdom in Truth, above and beyond and before the Sonship of the Word, be it the risen, redeeming Son or the fallen one. This constellation gives us a guide to overcoming the Anti-Christian degradation of the Logos, in that it reveals the redeeming factor as *openness to Truth's change*, for which all so called truths are a candidate, including the most foundational. Indeed, it points us toward the recognition that for a truth to be True it must be changeful, and thus reveal its underlying, necessary provisionality, which is precisely the fear of the Anti-Christ, namely the terror that all his power and capacity to set the truths of the day are mere terrestrial provisions, touching not the root of Truth itself, which has the Wisdom to be No-thing at all, such that through the principle of change it can become All.
