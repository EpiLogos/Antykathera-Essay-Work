## Temporal / operational order

The surfaces are not simply ranked by date. Their operative order for quilting is:

```text
parent developmental provenance
           ↓
Parallel target harmonisation ──────┐
           ↓                         │
Plain-English whole-flow             │
           │                         │
           ├── Copula derivation     │
           ├── Agentworld pressures  │
           └── QL expression law     │
                     ↓               │
        complete prospective quilt field
                     ↓               │
     current granular corpus crosswalk
                     ↓               │
       transverse recensus/deep seams
                     ↓               │
       late Parallel discrepancy read ─┘
```

The side surfaces are not subordinate content summaries. Copula and Agentworld inject unique argumentative obligations; QL grammar constrains expression after content is resolved.

## Authority relation

The quilt surfaces remain pre-canonical/working. The central plan is structural authority, orienting principles mandatory/subordinate, live granular arguments/concepts/paths/sections carry current canonical work, canonical source houses govern public evidence, protected notes preserve authorial encounter, and the manuscript remains sovereign.

The quilt field can prove a propagation debt or expose a later development. It does not silently make the proposed relation canonical.

## Double reading of the Parallel Quilt

The Parallel Quilt must be read twice for two different reasons:

**First office — before granular crosswalk:** establish what the prospective harmonised whole actually asks of each current target and where selective formal/non-section propagation had already occurred.

**Second office — after granular crosswalk/deep recovery:** detect what the orderly harmonisation itself compressed, misplaced or failed to carry.

These readings are conjugate, not duplicates.

## Granular crosswalk statuses

Native 025 should use the following statuses at relation level:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `present` | quilt relation is already carried adequately by current granular surface |
| `partial` | recognisable carrier exists but later quilt operation/depth is missing |
| `prospective` | quilt relation has a proposed destination but is absent from current granular surface |
| `superseded` | quilt proposal was later corrected/withdrawn before propagation |
| `conflict` | current surface and live prospective relation make incompatible claims requiring adjudication |
| `deeper-current-carrier` | present current surface contains important work not represented adequately by the prospective quilt account |
| `source-debt` | relation is developed but public evidence/attribution remains incomplete |
| `expression-debt` | content standing is clear but expression/citation law needs repair or propagation |

A whole file is never labelled with one of these statuses globally. Status attaches to the specific relation between a quilt proposition and a current carrier.

## Anti-loss tests

Before any relation is called harmonised in the final T06D quilt, it must answer:

1. Which quilt surface(s) carry it and in what office?
2. What developmental contribution generated or corrected it?
3. Which current granular unit(s) carry it now?
4. What is the relation-level propagation status?
5. What later correction or source debt qualifies it?
6. Which downstream units depend on the determination?

This is the minimum sufficient contact for later synthesis.
