**Programme:** T17–T23 / P2 enrichment  
**Status:** mandatory enrichment input  
**Authority:** supplements the T09 canonical census and P1 movement propagation; does not reopen either  
**Purpose:** ensure that P2 enrichment recovers the authored depth of the essay rather than recursively summarising the already-compressed canonical layer.

## 1. P2 has two simultaneous axes

P2 is organised publicly by register:

```text
T18 — root Symbolon
T19 — Matheme
T20 — Mytheme
T21 — Episteme
        ↕
T22 — recursive fold-back
```

That register sequence is only one axis. Under it runs a mandatory **Argument-depth axis**:

```text
A01
A02
...
A36
```

Every canonical Argument receives its own recovery track. Register pages are then developed from the recovered Argument field rather than from thin A/C summaries alone.

This does **not** create 36 additional canonical record identities. The per-Argument recovery packet is a working/derivational receipt whose job is to restore depth into the existing canonical A page and route its recovered material into the correct Symbolon / Matheme / Mytheme / Episteme records.

## 3. Source houses and protected authorial notes

For every source house materially implicated by an Argument or by a record being developed from that Argument:

1. read the canonical `SOURCE.md`;
2. if a sibling `NOTES.md` exists, read it in full;
3. never edit, move, normalise, regenerate or delete `NOTES.md`;
4. treat `NOTES.md` as **authorial encounter / intention / quotation-lead provenance**, not as independent external evidence;
5. verify externally attributed claims against `SOURCE.md` readiness and its cited edition/passages rather than upgrading a note into evidence.

The point of reading the notes is not citation harvesting alone. They often preserve why a source mattered, the relation Frank saw, the pressure that caused the source to enter the essay, and formulations that later summaries can flatten.

## 4. Authorial-signal priority

P2 is explicitly a recovery operation against recursive AI compression.

When several carriers say roughly the same thing, prefer the richest recoverable **authorial signal** over the smoothest later summary.

Use this order as a provenance discipline, not as a mechanical truth ranking:

1. **Direct Taylor-authored material** — protected `NOTES.md`, authored essay/theorem/source files, authorial ratifications/corrections, and clearly identifiable Frank/user turns in dialogue/chat provenance.
2. **Direct developmental carriers preserving the original operation** — historical Argument files, named theorem/source carriers, raw working seams, primary brief material and other files whose provenance clearly precedes later harmonisation.
3. **The additive quilt**, especially Q27, read for distinct contributions and preserved formulations rather than only its later summaries.
4. **Later harmonisations / plain-English flows / AI syntheses** — valuable for relation, discrepancy detection and navigation, but secondary when they merely compress an earlier authored point.
5. **Generic AI paraphrase** — never preferred merely because it is cleaner, more systematic or more recent.

Where authorship inside a chat log or mixed document cannot be distinguished reliably, do **not** attribute a formulation to Taylor. Record the uncertainty and use the carrier as developmental provenance only.

A later formulation may genuinely improve an earlier one. The rule is therefore not “oldest wins”; it is: **do not let summarisation erase a distinct authored operation, image, pressure, qualification, asymmetry or wording that the mature argument still needs.**

## 5. Required A01–A36 recovery packet

Before bulk register dispatch, create a working packet for every canonical Argument under a clearly named P2 working directory, for example:

`working/p2-enrichment/argument-depth/A01.md` … `A36.md`.

Each packet must contain at least:

### Identity and current canon
- Argument ID/title;
- current canonical A page;
- current positive claim / distinct operation;
- P1 Movement consumers;
- current Concept and Etymology dependencies;
- current `Unresolved Delta` / `Depth Restoration` debt.

### Direct authored/developmental recovery
- direct Taylor/authored carriers found;
- historical Argument carriers;
- named theorem/source carriers;
- primary brief / deep-source / Mytheme seams where implicated;
- exact original formulations, images or pressures that materially exceed the current canonical summary.

### Quilt census
A six-row-or-current-live-count table recording for **every** file in `submission-package/essay/quilt/`:
- relevant anchors/search terms;
- distinct yield;
- whether the yield is authorial, developmental, source-facing, formal, mythemic or later synthesis;
- whether the current A page already preserves it.

### Source-house recovery
For every materially implicated canonical source house:
- `SOURCE.md` readiness and exact relation;
- whether `NOTES.md` exists and was read;
- authorial-intent/encounter yield from `NOTES.md`;
- evidence that is ready now;
- evidence/locator/edition debt that remains Open.

### Recovery result
- material to restore into the canonical A page;
- material that belongs instead in C/E, root Symbolon, Matheme, Mytheme, history/dossier/dialogue/source-facing Episteme or a supporting-visible carrier;
- genuine contradiction or structural pressure requiring T22 fold-back;
- material consciously *not* restored because it is superseded, unsupported, duplicative or merely an AI paraphrase of a stronger carrier.

## 6. Argument-first dispatch law

No bulk page-writing agent should receive only a canonical A/C summary and a register template.

Its packet must include the relevant **A-depth packet(s)** and the source/quilt carriers those packets identify. For a transverse page with several Argument consumers, include every materially bearing A packet rather than selecting whichever one gives the shortest context.

The enrichment question is not:

> “How can I expand this summary?”

It is:

> “What developed operation, source relation, image, formal structure or authored pressure exists in the underlying corpus that this canonical record is responsible for making available without duplicating another office?”

## 7. Relation to T18–T22

- **T18 root Symbolon:** recover whole-bearing relations from the A packets before writing roots.
- **T19 Matheme:** recover exact operations/theorem derivations/formal neighbours from the A packets and their direct carriers; never use a later prose analogy as proof.
- **T20 Mytheme:** recover the complete relational myth/image from the A packets and deep seams; preserve all materially active figures and relations rather than atomising a myth into topical illustrations.
- **T21 Episteme:** explicitly enrich A01–A36 and C01–C64 from the completed packets, alongside histories, etymologies, dossiers, lenses, maps, dialogues, figures and source-facing records. Arguments are not exempt from enrichment merely because a T09 first-materialisation page already exists.
- **T22 fold-back:** after each batch, return any recovered structural pressure into the governing decision/census layer before changing identity or creating a new canonical record.

## 8. Completion criterion

P2 cannot be accepted merely because every planned page exists.

Before T23/T24, the programme must be able to show:

- 36 completed Argument-depth packets;
- complete live `/quilt` coverage in every packet;
- relevant `SOURCE.md` + sibling `NOTES.md` coverage;
- clear distinction between Taylor-authored formulations and AI developmental interpretation where provenance permits;
- each A page revisited against recovered depth;
- recovered material routed to the correct register/publication office;
- unresolved source debt still honestly Open;
- no distinct authored operation lost because a later summary was smoother.

This is the intended meaning of **Depth Restoration** during P2.
