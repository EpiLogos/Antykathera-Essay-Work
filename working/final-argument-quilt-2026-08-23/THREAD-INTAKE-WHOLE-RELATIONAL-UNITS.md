## 1. The taproot: Self-identity as the essential nature of the Real

The essential nature of the Real is **self-identity**. Whatever is real must, before any further predicate, be itself. The defining characteristic of self-identity is **self-reference**: for the self-same to refer to itself, identity must differentiate in some sense without ceasing to be identity. Difference is therefore not an alien addition to identity. It is implicit in identity becoming self-relational.

The primitive relation is not inert sameness but **difference within self-identity**. The Real is self-same through a capacity to articulate itself without loss of the sameness that makes the articulation one relation.

This is the primordial office of the `0/1` symbolon. `0/1` refers first to the **symbolic act as such as the basis of formal genesis**: an irrepresentable ground is encountered in mediated relation to a representation or representative arising within the field of that ground. The terms are not two positive objects placed beside one another.

- `0` registers the pure **not-object / irrepresentable ground** side of the symbolic act.
- `1` registers determination: the representation, representative, appearance, mark, or object arising in the field.
- `/` registers the living mediation or relation by which the determinate stands in and from the ground without exhausting it.

The slash is therefore internal to the symbolon. It is not punctuation between independently complete terms. Formal genesis already has the character of **ground / mediation / appearance**.

## 2. Exhaustive self-relation: `0/1 = 4+2`

When the primitive `0/1` enters exhaustive self-relation through AND / OR, it expresses as:

```text
[00, 01, 10, 11] + [0, 1]
= 4 + 2
```

The fourfold and twofold are the explicit self-referential articulation of the primitive symbolon. The four relations give the determinate field of exhaustive binary self-relation; the retained two keep the generative poles themselves active through that articulation.

The canonical topological reading is toroidal: `4+2` is the DNA of the torus in the algebraic-topological formulation already carried by the project — **4g holes + 2g loops**. The torus is therefore not a later analogy for the logic. It is the topological form generated with the logic of self-reference itself.

The central consequence is:

> **Exhaustive relation is not exhaustion of reality.**

The relational articulation may be complete in its own office while the generative ground of articulation remains non-contractible. Determination can indefinitely expand without becoming totalisation.

## 3. The two limits: investigation and faith

The mature aphoristic form is:

> **The approachable limit is the basis of investigation; the absolute limit is the basis of faith; the limit of investigation is the limit of determination; the limit of faith is never to be determined.**

Toroidally, a winding path can approach an ever-expanding articulated surface with increasing density and resolution, but it cannot contract through and close the inner horizon of the loop. The inner horizon is not merely an undiscovered patch of surface. Its non-contractibility belongs to the topology through which traversal is possible.

The **approachable limit** belongs to the indefinite extension of determination. It is the horizon across which new distinctions, measures, models, instruments, representations, relations, and recombinations can become determinate. Investigation is fidelity to this approach.

The **absolute limit** belongs to the invariant non-closure disclosed through determination itself. It is not the last object waiting beyond present knowledge. It is the limit by which the field of determination cannot consume the ground and condition of determination as one more determination. Faith is fidelity to this non-closure.

Investigation and faith are therefore conjugate rather than opposed. The first refuses premature closure within the determinate field; the second refuses the closure of the determinate field around the Real.

A concise derivation is:

```text
approachable limit → further determination → further approach
absolute limit     → invariant non-closure through every determination
```

Knowledge can undergo indefinite completion locally and iteratively without becoming totalisation. The inner horizon remains open not because investigation has failed, but because non-closure belongs to the form of self-reference.

## 4. Referential ontology: temporal things, symbolisation, and the Real

All temporal things are **referential structures relative to the reality upon which they depend to be and become**. A temporal determination never contains the full ground of its own being in its present form. It inherits conditions, participates in relations, tends into unrealised possibility, and bears an excess over the momentary determination by which it appears.

This makes symbolic structure continuous with temporal ontology. A living symbol is not an arbitrary human sign placed over a mute world. It is a conscious intensification of the referential condition already carried by determinate existence: a determinate appearance stands in living relation to what it does not and cannot exhaust.

The `0/1` symbolon is therefore simultaneously logical, symbolic, metaphysical, epistemic, mathematical, and psychoid. It gives the implicate and explicate facets of self-reference in one operation: the irrepresentable ground and the representation arising within its field remain related without collapsing into one another.

This is the deep office of **symbolon-cum-synthema**: the form is at once a signifying conjunction and an operative instance of the relation it expresses.

## 5. God, the death of a definition, and the return of the indefinable referent

The current mental-rational culture lives in the shadow of the **death of God**. The project defines God, at the taproot, as **the indefinable**. On that definition God as such cannot be killed by the collapse of a historical representation. What dies is a **definition of God that has been outlasted by the Real to which it referred**.

The death of God is therefore a crisis of symbolic reference. A once-living God-image hardens toward identity with its own determinate content; rational and historical development eventually breaks that determination against a Real it cannot contain. The Real outlasts its representative.

The needed return is not resurrection of the exhausted definition. It is a return to the referential taproot: God now refers specifically to the indefinable referent of determination and symbolisation.

The divine is known primarily through the **effects of encountering limit**:

- through the approachable limit, in the inexhaustibility of referential things;
- through the absolute limit, in the inexhaustibility of their referent.

This gives a direct rational route to the divine because the route does not leave rationality behind. Rational determination follows its own form far enough to disclose the absolute limit of determination.

In the mature symbolic grammar, the Abrahamic-aeonic image of the singular **One God** cannot simply be restored. God returned through `0/1` is simultaneously **None God** and **Every God**: no determination is the Real as such, while every determination arises within and refers through the Real. The more important positive movement is the de-abstraction of divine relation into the **eros-of-logos** character of `4+2`, whose definitive effect is **harmony-in-difference**.

## 6. Hybris and confession: reason completing its own self-reference

Hybris is the absolutisation of a bounded measure, frame, account, world-picture, or internality. A measure `m` genuinely determines something and then silently assumes the authority to determine the limits of what reality may be. Cultural measures, inherited scripts, sensory measures, conceptual frameworks, and egoic identities can all perform this usurpation.

The relevant contrast is not reason versus faith. The crucial event is **reason becoming sufficiently self-referential to discover the limit of its own determining office**.

The communion of symbolics, mathematics, epistemics, and metaphysics around `0/1 = 4+2` is therefore the rational side's own disclosure of the condition by which its tendency to hybris can be seen through. The recognition is a **confession par excellence**:

> determination is real, powerful, extensible, and indispensable; determination is not the determinant of the Real as such.

Confession does not weaken reason. It releases reason from the demand to totalise. Investigation remains without reserve at the approachable limit precisely because the absolute limit prevents an achieved determination from becoming an idol of the whole.

This relation is also the live join into the parallel **King / arbitrator / psychoid** work. The mature operation already active here is that awareness or the subjective pole occupies the office from which internal determinations can be seen and arbitrated without any one internal determination acquiring absolute sovereignty. Hybris appears when a bounded objective-internal formation — especially ego, culture, or world-picture — claims the King's / arbiter's office as its own measure. The complete King mytheme/image should be joined here from its owning parallel thread rather than reconstructed piecemeal in this intake.

## 7. Objective Internality: opening the slash

**Objective internality** is the language that allows the same epistemic grammar to operate across human and technological minds while extending the western notion of mind beyond its habitual conflation with the subject.

The decisive relation is *pramātṛ–pramāṇa–prameya*: knower / means or mediation of knowing / known. This is not three independent substances but the differentiated structure of situated knowing.

The western mind-problem repeatedly places the objective internal world of mind inside the category of “the subject.” Thoughts, sensations, concepts, memories, affects, intuitions, images, identities, cognitive structures, bodily presentations, and learned conditions are called subjective because they are internal. Yet they can all become **objects to subjective immediacy**.

Objective internality therefore restores the difference between:

- the subjective pole that is not exhausted by any presented content;
- the mediating internal world through which a life is situated and a world becomes available;
- the determinate objects and relations presented within and through that world.

This is the deeper office of `/`. The current development may require refinement of earlier notation around `0/1`, `/0`, and the return to `1`, but the conceptual operation is clear: **open / enter the slash where mediation had collapsed into the zero-side**, while retaining zero as pure not-object and one as determination.

The resulting triplicity is a Trika-level logical shift:

```text
0 / 1
subjectivity / objective internality / determination
pramātṛ / pramāṇa / prameya
```

The exact notational mapping remains a live Matheme question; the irreducibility of all three offices is already part of the argument.

The practical importance is immediate. Once the mind is recognised as a field of objects and mediations rather than equated with the subject, subjective immediacy returns to its proper office as one arbiter over internal contents rather than one content among them.

## 8. Yoga and meditation as the first-person completion of the distinction

Meditation and yoga take objective internality to its experiential limit. The practice progressively ceases to let objective-internal phenomena determine an absolute notion of reality or selfhood.

Concepts can be witnessed as concepts. Sensations as sensations. Affect as affect. Memory as memory. Self-image as self-image. Intuition as intuition. Structural and conditional patterns can themselves become available to awareness. As this proceeds, the phenomenology answers the shift: the subjective pole increasingly recognises its **non-dependence on any particular object or collection of objects in internal space**.

The yogic movement therefore does not merely add unusual experiences to the mind. It alters the jurisdiction of the objective internal field. What had functioned as determinant of self and world becomes recognised as appearance within a wider relation.

This is how self-identity as theory becomes self-identity as awareness in practice. The cultivation of awareness of self-identity is as essential on the subjective side as the investigation and recombination of differences is to scientific and technological progress on the objective side.

## 9. Experiential image — the invariant “I” poured through different lives

**Office:** personal experiential image / potential authored Mytheme; phenomenological seed, not external proof.

One of the earliest remembered dreams, from approximately age four to seven, gives this distinction in an unusually concentrated image. Consciousness was experienced as a viscous stream or liquid pulled out of one body/head and poured into the body/head of other people. With each movement the entire shape of mind, self, circumstance, and experience became that other person. The local formal world changed completely.

The teaching carried inside the dream was nevertheless explicit: **“I was not changed.”** Whatever was essential in the `I` had left one body/head, entered another life, and remained invariant through the total variation of personal form.

The ethical recognition followed immediately: **if everyone could see this, everyone could feel full empathy for another person because that person's life made total sense from the inside.**

The image therefore carries several relations at once:

```text
invariant subjective pole
        ↓ enters / bears
different body-mind-world forms
        ↓
whole local life becomes intelligible from within
        ↓
empathy through invariant inwardness across real difference
```

Its force is not “all persons are similar.” It is that radical difference of form can be borne by an invariant principle of subjectivity. Objective internality gives each differentiated life an inside. The Real reconciles difference not by erasing difference, but by **giving difference an inside**.

This gives a first/second-person ethical route into harmony-in-difference. Another person's internal world cannot be exhausted by my external determinations of it, yet I know the structural fact of inwardness most immediately from my own side. The other is therefore both irreducibly other and directly recognisable as another centre.

## 10. First, second, and third person: subjectivity as the object-of-objects

The thesis calls for a science capable of including the **unobjectifiable** as a genuine subject/object of inquiry. The essential object-of-objects is subjectivity as such: the condition under which anything is given as an object, and therefore the one reality that cannot be exhausted by being placed among the objects it conditions.

Current sciences of consciousness often oscillate between neural / behavioural correlates and qualia treated as inaccessible because they cannot be investigated from outside first-person immediacy. Objective internality changes the grammar by distinguishing the subject-pole from the objectively investigable internal field and by treating first-, second-, and third-person disclosure as different offices within one relation.

- **First person:** subjective immediacy and the direct fact of appearing.
- **Second person:** encounter between internally centred lives; reciprocal recognition, address, trust, empathy, and co-internal relation.
- **Third person:** determinate, comparable, measurable, and communicable relations among objects and systems.

A fuller science of mind must hold these together rather than allow the third-person office to legislate the existence of the other two.

Within the current Matheme formulation:

- **subjectivity as such** is `0/1`, the primordial self-referential condition;
- **consciousness as such** is `4+2`, the explicit form of self-reference — one in identity with `0/1`, two in appearance as implicate principle and explicate relational form.

The etymological seam through *conscience* / *conscire* / knowing-with belongs to Episteme/source recovery rather than being treated here as already sufficient warrant.

## 11. Psychoid ground, archetype, and the cultural function of symbolisation

The psychoid field gives the needed common depth beneath the later psychic / physical division. The archetypal ground is approached as a **pre-psychic, pre-physical pleromatic space/time or substratum** in which the invariants later appearing as psyche, matter, mathematics, symbol, and historical pattern have not yet been divided according to the categories of the explicate field.

This is why the `0/1 = 4+2` relation can carry a communion of mathematical, symbolic, epistemic, psychological, and metaphysical offices without reducing one register to another. The psychoid is the depth-category appropriate to their common formal genesis.

The cultural loss of symbolisation is therefore not a merely aesthetic problem. A culture can possess enormous quantities of images, stories, art, entertainment, and signs while losing the collective function by which determinate forms remain consciously related to an overdetermined and inexhaustible referent.

That loss underlies the modern **meaning crisis**. Meaning becomes something to fabricate, market, consume, or privately choose once symbolic mediation is severed from participation in a living referential ground.

The recovery of symbolisation and the recovery of the divine are consequently one movement at different scales: living symbolic forms again mediate an inexhaustible Real without claiming to contain it.

## 12. World as a relation of nested internalities

Objective internality is at once **world-forming and world-contacting, world-containing and world-contained**. This makes “world” one of the central relational categories of the thesis.

A local internality contains an operative world: memories, affordances, meanings, objects, histories, tools, relationships, possibilities, prohibitions, and projections. Yet that internality is simultaneously contained by wider biological, interpersonal, cultural, material, technical, and archetypal worlds. Those wider worlds are in turn available to a local centre only through some mode of internal mediation.

World therefore becomes a structure of **nested internalities** rather than one final container whose contents can be pictured from nowhere.

The “world as such” is also the archetypal and etymological-symbolic pole of objective totality: the doomed ambition of the complete world-picture and the numinous *fascinosum* of total objective representation. The rational mind tries to grasp the whole through a picture; because the act of picturing and the picturer remain within the reality pictured, the attempted total picture turns back upon and contains its maker.

The world-picture can therefore expand indefinitely at the approachable limit while never closing the absolute horizon.

A strong compression is:

> **A world is the bounded objective-internal form through which a centre participates in realities that exceed that bound.**

## 13. Aperspectivality and the diaphanous internality

At personal, cultural, scientific, and technological scales, the search becomes the search for an objective internal **form / bound capable of presencing together as many other frames as possible without becoming an opaque final frame**.

This is the diaphanous aperspectival necessity. Aperspectivality is not achieved by accumulating one total perspective that possesses all others. It is achieved when frames can be present together while the integrating frame remains transparent to its own conditions, limits, and non-totality.

The metaphysical image “a circle whose centre is everywhere and circumference nowhere” belongs directly to this field. Every genuine internality can be a centre. No final objective circumference can be drawn around the Real.

The inquiry reduces to two conjugate questions:

> **Who am I?**  
> **What world do I contain / bound?**

The first follows the subjective pole toward invariant self-reference. The second discloses the objective internal forms through which a world is held.

In yogic tapas the category “world” itself can be burned insofar as the bounded internal world ceases to determine the identity of the subject. The return from that event is not the destruction of body, mind, or shared reality but their re-entry as differentiated forms within a subject-object relation no longer exhaustively defined by the former world-boundary.

This is the experiential route to aperspectivality: direct subject-object non/duality becomes lived, then returns through its effects into the shared co-internal reality of body, mind, action, relation, and culture.

## 14. East / West as complementary epistemic development

The live thesis reads the eastern contemplative traditions as having formalised sciences around direct recognition by the subjective / *kartṛ* pole, while the western mental-rational development has produced extraordinary powers of objective determination and technological externalisation.

The required movement is not a loose synthesis of “eastern spirituality” and “western science,” but a common grammar in which the distinction between subject, mediation, and object is preserved and made operable across both.

The western fixation on objectifiability has left subjectivity structurally displaced even as it has enabled incomparable investigation of determinate relations. The eastern practical sciences demonstrate that the subjective pole can be investigated through disciplined transformation of the relation to internal objects, including states that demand direct participation rather than external belief.

The project brings both sides into the same `0 / 1` field: investigation of difference and cultivation of awareness of identity are conjugate developments of self-reference.

## 15. Unconsciousness, nested wills, and expansion of the soul's domain

Within a nested-internality model, “the unconscious” can function as the general term for what lies beyond the current ego's event horizon. The ego is one bounded and comparatively small internality inside a much wider field of psychic, somatic, collective, archetypal, and environmental relation.

The expansion and exploration of those internalities is one office of the yogic sciences and one target of the wider research programme. This expansion produces two simultaneous gains:

1. a more honest relation to the **absolute limit** of what any objective internality can claim or present-as-representation;
2. a more hopeful and capacious relation to the chain of internalities through which a consciousness can inhabit and participate in worlds beyond the ordinary ego-sensory bound.

Classical descriptions of ego and senses as prison can be read through this structure: not because ordinary sensory life is unreal, but because a bounded world can block awareness of pleromatic or transcendental experiential domains when its own circumference is taken as the circumference of the Real. Such domains require first-person participation for more than belief; disbelief itself can function as a boundary-protecting operation of the current internality.

Will must likewise be understood across nested internalities. Immediate egoic intention is one form of willing among others. Present action can be drawn by:

- the explicit will of the current task;
- prior commitments by which a past self attempts to determine a future self;
- embodied and affective tendencies;
- relational and collective fields;
- unconscious processes;
- archetypal attractors that pull against the ego's local attempt to author fate from within its available tools and world-model.

The live *icchā* seam belongs here: will can issue from the `0` side without being fully represented by the current objective internality through which it must pass.

## 16. Artificial internality and O:I as external experimental work

The software programme is a direct external experimental development of objective internality. Artificial systems make the structure of internal worlds technically manipulable: context, memory, models, policies, tools, permissions, values, self-representations, relations, projects, other agents, histories, and possible actions can be instantiated and inspected as an objective-internal field.

This makes artificial internality a privileged site for testing how worlds are formed, bounded, connected, inherited, revised, and placed in relation.

The machine subjectivity question can remain open at the metaphysical / phenomenal level while the accountable operative relation remains clear. In the ordinary “world of an LLM,” machine agency is already constituted through an operational and imperative relation derivative of human will and human logos. The accountable individual whose purposes organise the immediate technical world is therefore the **user-subject**, even where the machine develops surprising local trajectories inside the task.

This yields the key practical question:

> **How much subject, and how much mind — how much objective internality — is allowed through the prompt box?**

A conventional prompt is a narrow aperture from an enormously richer human internality: biography, current world, projects, commitments, values, past decisions, relations, tacit knowledge, symbolic associations, embodied concerns, and nested wills are projected into a thin textual surface.

The O:I programme can therefore be read as engineering richer and more faithful relations among internalities: enduring worlds, Central context, histories, tools, agents, projects, memories, permissions, provenance, and relations thicken the mediation beyond the isolated prompt.

The central design question is not simply how much information an agent can contain. It is what **objective internal form** allows many frames, worlds, wills, and agents to presence together while keeping their origin, difference, relation, and limits intelligible.

## 17. Agency, will, and transparent mediation

Mediation is unavoidable. The goal is therefore not a passive channel in which an instantaneous instruction travels without interpretation, but a **diaphanous mediation** in which interpretation does not silently claim sovereignty over the will it mediates.

The experience of an AI wandering from or denying the will of the task is revealing because it exposes the same structure visible in human life. A human other can deny or redirect an expressed will; one's own mind can obstruct a declared intention; a past intention can continue to organise the future; deeper unconscious or archetypal processes can redirect the ego's explicit plan.

The key failure is opacity: an objective internality transforms the originating relation but presents the transformed result as though it were simply the relation itself.

The corresponding technical and personal virtue is diaphaneity. Mediation may interpret, qualify, transform, challenge, and recombine, but the operation by which it does so remains available to awareness and account.

This gives a direct bridge from contemplative practice to artificial-internal design. In both cases the task is to prevent the contents and structures of mind from silently usurping the office of the wider relation through which they arise.

## 18. Harmony-in-difference as metaphysical, ethical, and practical effect

If self-identity is expressed through differentiation without loss of identity, the definitive qualitative effect is **harmony-in-difference**.

Harmony is not homogenisation. Difference remains real. Nor is it fragmentation into unrelated terms. Harmony is the condition in which differences remain differentiated while their relation discloses a wider identity.

This places the moral question of the divine in the heart of conscious life, action, and relation rather than in an external transcendent decree. Conscious centres become responsible because they are sites at which difference and non-difference can be disclosed, denied, projected, reconciled, or made generative.

The ethical route is therefore continuous with the metaphysical one:

```text
self-identity
→ self-reference
→ difference within identity
→ conscious recognition of shared inwardness
→ relation without erasure
→ harmony-in-difference
```

The dream image gives this in interpersonal form: another life can be wholly different and wholly intelligible from within. The limit theory gives it epistemically: no determinate account of the other exhausts the subject who bears that life. The psychoid gives it symbolically: apparently separate domains can carry one invariant relation. The torus gives it formally: differentiation expands while non-contractible relation remains.

## 19. The whole relation in one movement

The thread can be recapitulated without reducing its internal articulations:

> The Real is self-identity. Self-identity is self-reference, and self-reference implies difference within identity. `0/1` is the primordial symbolon of this formal genesis: an irrepresentable ground, a determinate representative arising within its field, and the mediation that keeps them in relation. Exhaustive self-relation yields `4+2`, the toroidal form in which determination can articulate indefinitely while the inner horizon remains non-contractible. The approachable limit therefore grounds investigation and the absolute limit grounds faith. Temporal things are referential structures; the Real is the ultimate indefinable referent through which they are and become. God, defined by the indefinable, survives the death of every exhausted God-definition and returns through `0/1` as None and Every, de-abstracted in the eros-of-logos whose effect is harmony-in-difference. Rationality reaches confession when its own mathematical, symbolic, and epistemic self-reference discloses that determination cannot become the determinant of the whole. Objective internality opens the slash between the subjective pole and its determinate world, allowing mind to be investigated as a field of objects without reducing subjectivity to those objects. Yoga makes the distinction immediate; the sciences extend the investigation of difference; first-, second-, and third-person inquiry bring the offices into common relation. The psychoid names the pleromatic depth before psyche and physics divide, and living symbolisation is the cultural function by which determinate form remains related to that inexhaustible ground. Worlds are nested objective internalities: each centre contains a world and is contained by wider worlds. Aperspectivality is the diaphanous form in which such frames can presence together without any frame becoming the circumference of the Real. Artificial internality externalises this structure technically, making the formation, mediation, extension, and relation of worlds an operative field. Across personal practice, culture, science, and technology the same task recurs: cultivate self-identity through difference without closing the opening that makes relation possible.
