# Ought-Be Concept Architecture

Concepts are reusable distinctions. A Concept earns a first-class home when more than one Argument, Movement, register or historical field must use the same distinction without redefining it locally.

A Concept page does not become authoritative merely because a phrase has acquired a filename. Its reason must remain with the distinction:

```text
distinction
→ why the essay needs it
→ operation
→ consequence
→ neighbour
→ warrant
```

## Relation to Symbolon and Argument

- Symbolon owns whole relational unity.
- Argument owns claim, inference and standing.
- Concept owns a reusable distinction required by several claims.
- Etymology owns the whole lexical/historical field from which an Argument or Concept may consume an exact operation.
- Mytheme is an internal Symbolon register, not a Concept subtype.

A Concept may therefore point upward to a root Symbolon, sideways to neighbouring Concepts, downward to its source/warrant, and outward to all Arguments and Movements that consume it. It must not duplicate a root Symbolon page simply to create a Concept-shaped copy.

## Existing first-class Concepts — disposition

### Retain as canonical reusable distinctions

`Apoha`; `Bimba–Pratibimba`; `Diaphaneity`; `J-Space`; `Psychoid Number`; `Mathematical–Artistic Image Register`; `Counterfeit Gathering`; `Fusion as Syn's Shadow`; `Dimensional Reframing at Zero and Infinity`; `Compassion as Sensitivity to Origins`.

These already state a distinct operation and have multiple consumers. Their reciprocal links must be repaired against the theorem layer during rebuild.

### Retain as contextual/domain Concepts

`Agentworld`; `Anthropomorphization`; `Centaur Societies`; `Parasociety`; `Planetary Computation`; `Prompt Thrownness`.

These are real Concepts, but they do not define the metaphysical or epistemic theorem. `Agentworld` is the venue-scale P4 horizon into which the essay is addressed. Anthropomorphization preserves a constraint. The others organise the world/application side of the response.

### Legacy/index-only terms

The current `concepts/index.md` mixes first-class pages with legacy/reference-note names such as Zero, Slash, World-Picture, Machinic Gaze, Simulation, Pratyabhijñā and Axial/Katabatic Determination. The rebuild must resolve each against actual canonical ownership instead of generating pages from the index list.

Rules:

- **Zero**, **Slash**, `0/1`, `1/0`, **Self-Identity**, **Mono-Poly**, **Complexio Oppositorum** and **Eight Determinations** already have or require root-Symbolon/Matheme ownership; do not duplicate their whole form as ordinary Concept pages.
- **Pratyabhijñā**, **Prakāśa–Vimarśa**, **Vāk** and similar tradition terms remain Concept/Episteme only to the extent that their doctrinal distinction is sourced; they do not become aliases for QL positions.
- **Axial** and **Katabatic** must not remain one fused pseudo-concept if their operations differ. During canonical rebuild, retain them only if current prose demonstrates separate reusable work; otherwise leave them in reference/history material rather than canonicalising a compression.
- **World-Picture**, **Machinic Gaze** and **Simulation** require the same test: multiple real consumers and a stable distinction. Filename/index presence is not sufficient.

## Required reciprocal fields on a Concept page

Every rebuilt Concept must carry:

- positive definition;
- reason for the distinction;
- operation and consequence;
- nearest complementary/conjugate Concepts;
- theorem relations consumed (`CT-*`);
- Argument consumers;
- Movement consumers;
- Etymology operation(s), if any, with evidence register;
- Symbolon parent relation where applicable;
- source / tradition warrant and claim standing;
- unresolved delta.

## Context and recursion discipline

The Concept field must preserve recursive Bimba offices exactly:

```text
metaphysical:
Bimba = Original
Pratibimba = reflection

§5 epistemic:
Bimba Map = local reference/original field
Pratibimba = situated reading / agency relative to it
```

The second is possible because an achieved/contextual field may occupy a local `0` office under nesting. It does not revise the metaphysical distinction into “everything is Bimba,” nor does the metaphysical register prohibit a local epistemic original/reference field.

Likewise P4 is not simply a fourth topic. It is the contextual aperture at which positions and their relations become jointly available. CF5 is maximal recursive availability only where the concrete composition supports it.

## Concept rebuild order

1. establish the theorem-critical Concept homes above;
2. repair retained first-class pages against those homes;
3. resolve legacy/index-only terms by real consumers rather than token frequency;
4. rebuild `concepts/index.md` from actual canonical nodes and relations;
5. only then project Concept links into final Symbolon pages.

This prevents the downstream Symbolon rebuild from inventing its Concept ontology while rendering pages.
