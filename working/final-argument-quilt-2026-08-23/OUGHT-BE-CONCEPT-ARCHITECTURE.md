# Ought-Be Concept Architecture

Concepts are reusable distinctions. A Concept earns a first-class home when more than one Argument, Movement, register or historical field must use the same distinction without redefining it locally.

A Concept is not:

- a word promoted from Etymology because it is evocative;
- a local section-specific research representation promoted because it has a filename;
- an agent-generated phrase made canonical by repetition;
- a framework mistaken for an Argument;
- a root Symbolon copied into Concept shape.

The ratified Concept census contains **64 identities**. Their full top-level determinations and routing are in `ARGUMENT-CONCEPT-ETYMOLOGY-RECENSUS.md`.

## Relation to the rest of the architecture

```text
Argument
= claim / inference / standing

Concept
= reusable distinction required by several claims

Etymology
= whole lexical/historical relational field + exact consumed operation

Quaternal Logic
= general formal logic / research field which generates and relates multiple forms

Symbolon
= whole living relational unity
    ├─ Matheme
    ├─ Mytheme
    └─ Episteme
```

A Concept may therefore be consumed by several Arguments, live under a root Symbolon, take warrant from Episteme/source houses, and receive exact operations from Etymology without replacing any of those offices.

## Important office corrections

### Quaternal Logic

C11 is an umbrella formal/research Concept because the essay repeatedly needs to refer to the whole QL field. It does **not** absorb the distinct Argument/Concept identities it generates or coordinates. Two Ones, Mono/Poly, Dia/Syn, Spanda, the Eight Determinations, Psychoid Number, Arche-Topos and related forms remain independently addressable where they perform distinct work.

### Meta-Epistemic Framework

C39 is the full framework identity. It does not become an Argument simply because arguments depend on it. Its office is refraction: declared lenses, provenance, evidence state, disagreement, transformation and Return around a subject whose native identity remains intact.

### Conditions of Worldhood

C07 is broader than A22. Circumstance is one central operation within it; Heideggerian worldhood/Weltbild, thrownness, material/social context and world–agency conditions also participate. A22 remains the specific **World-Picture → World-Atlas** argument.

### Model Internality

C40 replaces J-Space as the general Concept. J-Space remains the particular §5 research representation of a model/agent judgment field and should survive as a linked research object rather than defining the whole concept.

### Paradox

C64 preserves the full paradox operation formerly over-promoted as an Argument. It is consumed wherever a current frame cannot retain all active determinations and must be transformed/reframed.

### Symbolon Disclosure Architecture

C61 is an architectural Concept, not a rival to A18 **Primordial Symbolon and Its Eight Determinations**.

```text
A18
Primordial Symbolon / substantive whole relation
        ↓
C61
how that whole is disclosed and routed
        ↓
Matheme / Mytheme / Episteme
        ↕
Argument / Concept / Section-Movement
        ↺ Return
```

The existing Mathematical–Artistic Image Register becomes one specialised production/admission surface under this architecture.

### Pros Hen / In Quantum

C63 keeps the two source traditions distinct while joining their operations at the QL level:

- *pros hen* — genuinely many determinations/readings oriented toward a focal One, especially at return/`#5`;
- *in quantum* — the ratio/“insofar as” under which a predicate is true, especially the ground/qualification office at `#0`.

Aristotle and Eckhart remain source-specific Episteme; neither is retroactively made a QL theorist.

## Required reciprocal fields on rebuilt Concepts

Every canonical Concept page must carry:

1. C-ID and positive definition;
2. why the distinction exists;
3. operation and consequence;
4. nearest complements/conjugates;
5. Argument consumers;
6. Movement consumers;
7. root Symbolon / QL / framework relations where applicable;
8. Etymology whole-field + exact operation/register where consumed;
9. source/tradition warrant and standing;
10. historical/current carrier disposition;
11. unresolved delta;
12. **Depth Restoration status**.

## Depth Restoration

The 64 descriptions establish identity and routing only. After structural rebuild, each Concept must be reopened against the original Q27 contribution(s), direct authored/source material, histories/Etymology/whole Mythemes, and only then QH/T06D harmonisation surfaces. A concise canonical definition may not silently replace a richer developed field.
