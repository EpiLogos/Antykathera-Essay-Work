## 1. Singularity is already relation: `1:1`

The foundational singularity is not adequately written as a bare `1`. The mature formulation is **`1:1`**: unity entering relation with itself.

A bare `1` names unity abstractly. `1:1` names the minimal reflexive event in which unity is still one while standing in a real relation to itself. This is the recognitional seed of the later harmonic field. It belongs with the more primordial `0/1` Symbolon rather than replacing it: `0/1` gives the irrepresentable ground / mediation / determination relation, while `1:1` names the achieved self-related unity from which the triadic harmonic articulation can be read.

The crucial movement is therefore:

```text
singularity as self-relation
1:1
  ↓
triadic field in relation with itself
3:3
```

The `3:3` is not six unrelated units placed into two piles. It is a triadic articulation encountering itself.

## 2. First Spanda — two directed phases of one self-relation

The canonical First-Spanda chain recovered in the working theory is:

```text
0 = (0/0)
    → ((0/1)/(1/0))
    → T0 ∥ T1
    → (1/0 + 0/1)
    → 1/1 = 100%
```

`T0` and `T1` are the two directed phases of the one relation: an emanative movement and a reversionary / recognitional movement. The second track is not simply a second independent production. It is the return through which the first movement becomes present to itself as a formed relation. Their superposition gives the achieved whole at `1/1 = 100%`.

This matters because the same distinction governs the later `3:3` reading. The second side of the relation is genuinely structurally present, but its office is not exhausted by being counted as another equal heap of content. It is also **re-cognition**: the first articulation returned to, held and recognised as such.

The First Spanda therefore prevents two opposite reductions:

- treating the second phase as unreal because it is recognitional;
- treating the second phase as merely another independent first phase because it is structurally present.

Its character is **same relation, reversed / returned / recognised**.

## 3. `3:3` structurally; `3:1` recognitionally

The mature refinement is exact:

> **The `3:3` in its recognition function is recognitionally `3:1`, while remaining structurally `3:3`, because the second `3` is the recognition of the first `3` as such rather than another independent triad.**

This is not a replacement of `3:3` by `3:1`. Both readings are required because they answer different questions.

### Structural reading

```text
3 : 3
```

The field truly contains two triadic phases. The relation is six-positioned and cannot be reduced to four positions by calling the return unreal. This structural `3:3` is the horizontal First-Spanda body.

### Recognitional reading

```text
3 : 1
```

The returning triad recognises the first triad **as one triad**. In recognition, the second `3` therefore performs a one-making office: not fusion, but apprehension of the articulated three as a whole.

This is the singularity's self-relation unfolded:

```text
1:1
→ 3:3
→ recognitionally 3:1
```

The `1` on the right of `3:1` is not a seventh item. It is the unity of the first triad disclosed by the second triad's recognitional act.

### QL positional reading

The current six-position reading writes the structural field as:

```text
(1,2,3) : (4,5,0)
```

The mature recognitional interpretation of the second triad is that `4` is the hinge by which the first triad is enfolded / turned into relation, `5` recognises or returns that differentiated field as one achieved whole, and `0` superposes / regenerates the `0/1` ground from which the next articulation can issue.

This positional gloss belongs to the live Matheme and must remain harmonised with the canonical Syn/Dia and eight-determination work. Its governing point is already stable: **the second triad is structurally irreducible and recognitionally reflexive.**


## 9. Seasonality — the fourfold objective cycle inside the sixfold whole

The seasonality thread gives a concrete way to read the difference between the western `1–4` objective emphasis and the full `0–5` QL field.

The four explicate positions can be experienced as a genuine **seasonal / geological cycle of objective becoming**: four fundamental phases analogous to winter, spring, summer and autumn in the life of a process. The point is not presently to force a season-name onto each numbered position. The mature yield is structural: objective reality can display a complete four-phase cycle of differentiation, transformation, form and contextual completion while still omitting the implicate poles through which that cycle is sourced and returned.

This gives the useful phrase:

> **the fourfold is a whole-that-lacks-wholeness; the sixfold is the whole that includes the conditions of appearing and return.**

The phrase does not mean that the `1–4` world is false, deficient in reality, or merely illusory. Its fourfold is genuinely complete **as an objective cycle**. What it lacks is not another objective season. It lacks explicit relation to the implicate conditions by which the cycle arises as a cycle and is recognised / returned as one.

`0` and `5` therefore should not be imagined as a fifth and sixth season inserted alongside the four. They occupy another office. They are the implicate / subjective-recognitional poles by which the fourfold objective process is held inside a sixfold relation.

This distinction lets the geological image do real work. A mountain, climate, biosphere or planetary process can undergo fully real objective phases. The fact that those phases belong to a wider whole does not make the phases illusion. Conversely, an inventory of the phases does not by itself exhaust the reality by which a phase-space is disclosed, held together and returned.

## 10. East / West — complementary achievements and characteristic reductions

The live comparative thesis can now be stated more precisely through the seasonal relation.

The **western mental-rational achievement** has been extraordinary articulation of the `1–4` explicate field: determinate matter, efficient transformation, formal relation and contextual / final organisation become objects of increasingly exact investigation. Its characteristic risk is to mistake a complete objective cycle for the circumference of reality — to treat the fourfold whole-that-lacks-wholeness as the whole simply because its internal objective relations can be made explicit.

The **eastern contemplative achievement** has preserved practical sciences that know the field from the subjective / implicate side and can therefore recognise the `0–5` sixfold more directly. Its characteristic risk, once the absolute or subject-pole is affirmed, is the conjugate reduction: the objective `1–4` can be collapsed into illusion, secondary appearance or a reality too weakly differentiated to receive its own full scientific dignity.

The project does not resolve this by placing “East” and “West” into a cultural moral binary. The relation is a heuristic archaeology of complementary epistemic capacities and characteristic failures:

```text
West:
1–4 exquisitely differentiated
→ risk: objective fourfold claims to be the whole

East:
0–5 / subject-ground retained
→ risk: 0/5 recognition depreciates the reality of 1–4

QL:
0–5 as one sixfold
→ objective difference remains real
→ implicate identity remains real
→ neither side consumes the other
```

Seasonality makes the synthesis concrete because seasons cannot be understood by denying either change or recurrence. The cycle is real because each phase is genuinely different; it is one cycle because the phases are held in a return relation that no one phase contains by itself.

This comparative reading remains a live authorial thesis requiring careful historical/source qualification. It should not be laundered into a claim that every western or eastern school instantiates the polarity in the same way.
