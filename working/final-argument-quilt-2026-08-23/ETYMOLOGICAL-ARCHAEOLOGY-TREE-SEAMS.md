## The tree morphology

### Roots — etymological archaeology

Root cultivation follows the word down through its documented descent, morphology, older senses, semantic branching, translation, legal/theological specialisation, grammatical shifts and material practices. A root is therefore a history of capacities that remain available to later language.

The existing four etymological registers remain the truth conditions of the roots:

- **philological descent** — documented cognation, derivation, morphology and language-specific history;
- **attested semantic field** — historically recorded senses and their changes;
- **operational homology** — a named operation recurring across independent words/practices/traditions;
- **poetic / phonic re-entry** — rhyme, pun, folk echo, sound or image that opens a live relation in the present.

The full archaeology keeps all four. Their evidential offices remain explicit so a productive pun can work as a pun and a historical derivation can bear historical weight.

### Mycelium / rhizome — lateral linguistic relation

Roots communicate laterally through word play, rhyme, idiom, phonetics, translation accident, metaphor, graphic resemblance, false friends and cross-language echoes. These relations matter because language can disclose a structural relation without requiring common ancestry.

The rhizome therefore records **how words meet**, including meetings created by later speakers and writers. It can join separate root systems without rewriting their genealogies. The etymology relation ledger states whether the connection is descent, semantic range, homology or phonic/poetic re-entry.

### Trunk — the forward, spiralic history

The trunk is the forward march of time through which the root-field acquires determinate historical bodies. Words enter institutions; institutions stabilise meanings; meanings reorganise action; persons transmit texts; laws establish names; conquests move borders; schools standardise speech; patronage carries manuscripts; financial techniques make accounts executable; technical systems turn categories into runtime decisions.

The movement is spiralic because earlier formations remain materially active inside later ones. History moves forward while repeatedly returning to inherited images, terms and unresolved relations under new conditions.

### Rings — retained formations

The rings record what earlier growth remains inside the later trunk. Magical participation, mythical kinship, theological covenant, philological classification, legal status and statistical administration can coexist within one modern national or technical category. A later form can reactivate an earlier ring without simply reproducing the earlier historical situation.

Rings are therefore especially important to the Jung/Gebser historical attitude. They let the quilt follow how psychic and symbolic structures remain active inside institutions whose explicit self-description has changed.

### Branches — regions and domains

Branches are historically specific domains: France/Occitania, German language-national formation, Italian standardisation, Anglo-Celtic jurisdiction, Zionism/Hebrew revival, the Languedoc Jewish/Kabbalistic field, Renaissance patronage, the history of rejected knowledge, post-war intelligence/scientific continuities, computational classification.

A branch has its own chronology, actors, sources, institutions and counter-histories. The common trunk gives comparison; it does not replace local evidence.

### Leaves — episodes, documents, objects and acts

Leaves are the concrete contacts through which the tree is evidenced: a treaty, law, school report, speech, translation, manuscript, marriage, ledger, census category, trial, passport, heraldic carving, dictionary, scientific paper, intelligence programme, model objective or administrative form.

The leaf is where a large historical/archetypal operation becomes inspectable. Every claim that depends on a local event should be able to return to such a leaf and then to its source house.

### Fruit and seed — achieved history becoming inherited ground

Historical processes produce relatively stable names, standards, institutions, identities, laws, credentials and narratives. These are fruit. Once inherited by a later generation they become seed: what had to be constructed can become obvious background.

This is the tree's `5→0` movement. A state-built standard becomes a native language; a negotiated border becomes “the country”; a racial classification becomes an apparently immediate perception; a disciplinary exclusion becomes common sense; a model default becomes infrastructural assumption. Anamnesis recovers the route by which the seed was made.

## Relational growth — the ideal form available beneath the tree

The tree is also generative in a second sense: **relations among established terms can grow new determinations**.

`[[RELATIONAL-FORM-GROWTH-GRAMMAR|Relational Form Growth Grammar]]` states the full law. Its first discipline is cardinality freedom. The archaeology may organise a relation as a 2-, 3-, 4-, 5-, 6-, 8-, 10-, 12-fold or another earned whole. Six is central to QL but is not imposed upon source material that bears another form.

Where an `N`-fold has a genuine conjugate `N′`, an ideal recursive fullness becomes available:

```text
N       articulated pole
N′      conjugate articulated pole
N+N′    N processual relations generated position-by-position
```

At the next scale:

```text
N      :     N+N′     :      N′
0      :       /      :       1
```

The named total field can then operate as a higher `0/1` Symbolon. The achieved whole is fruit; when it enters another relation it becomes seed.

### Two complementary recursion modes

The sixfold case makes two different growth operations especially legible.

**Conjugate generation — `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`**

Two complete sixfold faces are read through one another. Their homologous positions generate six processual/compositional determinations. This is the intensive articulation of the slash.

**Distributive recursion — `6×6`**

Each of the six parent positions receives a complete local sixfold. The result is six full sixes / thirty-six situated determinations. This is holographic or distributive depth: the whole cycle is refracted inside every local office while each local six retains its parent address.

The current theoretical orientation relates these to the two established sixfold readings without making them arithmetic identities:

```text
3:3 pressure
conjugate arcs / opposed-complementary traversal
        ↓
6 / 6′ / (6+6′)

4:2 pressure
full four-explicate + two-implicate six-body
        ↓
6×6
```

`3:3` makes face-to-face conjugacy, emanation/reversion and Bimba/Pratibimba traversal especially visible. `4:2` makes the complete six-body and its capacity to recur as a local whole especially visible. Both are readings of a body generated as `4+2`; neither exhausts the other.

A mature field can therefore grow **across** its conjugate face and **down** through the full sixfold depth of a position. The quilt may consume either scale so long as the return path remains intact.

## Historical-archetypal relation

The Jungian historical attitude carried by this seam has two explicit readings of the same tree.

**Historical Logos:** through which actual words, persons, institutions, places, documents, laws, money, marriages, technologies and events did this relation take this specific form?

**Archetypal Logos:** what psychic relation, image, affective charge or recurrent symbolic form is active through that historical body?

The first gives the chronological and documentary carrier. The second gives the inward and symbolic morphology by which historical facts become collectively meaningful and motivationally potent. Mytheme enters wherever an image itself carries enough relational structure to require whole-form recovery.

## Prospective Mytheme additions opened by this archaeology

### Athena/Minerva · Poseidon/Neptune · Cecrops · olive · arbiters · Athens

The ancient contest over Attica is a complete relational image and should be recovered whole. In the Pseudo-Apollodoran telling, **Cecrops**, earth-born king of Attica, witnesses the rival claims. **Poseidon** strikes the Acropolis and produces a salt-water sign; **Athena** plants the olive. **Zeus** appoints arbiters — in that version the twelve gods — and the decision goes to Athena because Cecrops bears witness to her planting. The city takes Athena's name; Poseidon's anger answers through flood. A Roman Hyginus telling carries the same broad contest under **Minerva / Neptune** with its own variant details.

The relational unit therefore includes claimant, counter-claimant, witness, gifts/signs, arbiters, verdict, naming, place and the defeated claim's continuing power. It is directly adjacent to the arbitration and `Topos → Logos → Nomos → Natio` etymologies because an inhabited place becomes a named civic field through witnessed competing claims and adjudication.

The name-etymologies of **Athena** and **Minerva** remain separate philological questions. No speculative derivation is required for the Mytheme to work.

### Olive — authored symbolic extension

The olive's further movement is an authored symbolic chain to be tested and developed beside the ancient telling:

```text
root → tree → fruit → pressing → oil → anointing → flame / light
```

This is not attributed to Pseudo-Apollodorus or Hyginus. It is the project's mycelial/Mytheme reading of the gift's material life: rooted continuity bearing fruit, transformation through pressure, oil as nourishment/anointing/fuel, and light as a later release of stored life.

### Poseidon across two whole stories — claimant and surety

Poseidon already has a materially indispensable office in Native 020's Ares–Aphrodite–Hephaestus whole: **surety**. The Attic contest adds another complete story in which Poseidon is a **claimant** whose claim is arbitrated and whose defeat does not simply vanish.

The relation between the two is an **authored cross-source Mytheme seam**:

```text
Attica: claim → witness → arbitration → verdict → unresolved force
Odyssey VIII: debt → distrust → surety → accepted liability → release
```

The stories remain separate. Their conjunction lets Poseidon carry both the force of claim and the personed standing-through which a frozen account can be released.
