## 3. Contextual-office law — do not globalise a local relation

A term's meaning is determined by the active relation and Context Frame in which it is operating. Higher-level metaphysical or theoretical meaning does not cancel a recursively nested technical office.

The canonical caution is the **Bimba Map**.

At the traditional/metaphysical register:

```text
Bimba      = original
Pratibimba = reflection
```

At §5's epistemic research register the same relation recurses:

```text
wider world / sources / returned evidence
        ↓
Bimba Map
explicit local original / reference field of inquiry
        ↓
Pratibimba
situated agency / J-Space / reading / trajectory
        ↓
comparison · consequence · Return
        ↺
```

The Bimba Map is intentionally **Bimba in this Context Frame**. It is the reference field an Energy-Based or other relational reading needs in order for distance, basin, settling, deviation, agreement and error to be epistemically interpretable. The map's being designed and revisable does not demote it to Pratibimba relative to the agents/readings it anchors. Conversely, its Bimba office in this inquiry does not assert that the artefact is the metaphysical Original of reality.

This is ordinary recursive QL discipline: an achieved determination can become contextual ground for an internal differentiation while remaining a determination relative to a wider field.

**Conformance rule:** before replacing a project-native term with a more globally tidy interpretation, identify the active relation, the office it occupies there, what depends on that office, and the wider frame relative to which the relation itself remains situated.

## 4. Tattvic conformance — transverse meta-context, already active

[[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|The Sixfold Tattvic Compression]] is a compression point for a field that remains active after §0. Its own warrant places Māyā, the kañcukas and antaḥkaraṇa across limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, order, determination, appropriation, coordination, memory and alternative formation.

### Cognition and exclusion

[[08-s0-p1-inner-instrument|Antaḥkaraṇa]], [[09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa|Vikalpa / Saṃkalpa]] and [[10-s0-p3-apoha|Apoha]] are nested operations of a bounded cognitive field. ### Limitation and inhabited worldhood

Māyā and the kañcukas carry the move from undivided capacity into situated limitation. Limitation is constitutive of a finite locus for whom there can be determinate knowledge, action, temporality, desire and world. That context remains active wherever Objective Internality and Agentworld ask what can be disclosed, excluded, remembered, intended, acted upon or returned.

### Phenomenological science and Theory of Mind

Agentworld makes recursive Theory of Mind a coordination problem while bracketing metaphysical conclusions from performed subjectivity. The essay does not accept Western ToM as the maximal science of mindedness. Yoga and the tattvic / antaḥkaraṇa traditions carry a developed phenomenological-practical field in which attention, cognition, embodiment, identification, limitation and recognition are investigated through disciplined practice as well as conceptual analysis.

```text

≠ possess that interior

behavioural / linguistic self-report
≠ exhausted account of phenomenality

Western Theory of Mind
= one bounded explanatory register

Yoga / tattvic / antaḥkaraṇa sciences
= a developed phenomenological-practical field
  that changes what counts as an adequate question about mind
```

This does not warrant an AI phenomenality claim. It prevents a contingent contemporary Western horizon from silently deciding the question in advance.

### Return

The tattvic arc culminates in recognition without deletion of the differentiated world. That relation remains active wherever a later local determination returns to source, context, neighbour or ground without annihilation.

The live §5 sequence is one developing research relation:

```text
#0  mathematically consequential transformation
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
#3  Hidden Zero — evaluator / gauge / reference conditions become visible
        ↓

        ↓
     Pratibimba — agency states / readings / trajectories relative to that field
        ↓
#5  experiments vary seams / gauges / lenses / topology / recursion / Return
```

This sequence is the technical body of **epistemics becoming first-class**. Prompt, memory, source authority, evidence, evaluator, lens, gauge, world-object, exclusion and context no longer remain invisible background to a finished output. They become representable parts of the epistemic object and can therefore be compared, governed and revised.

The Bimba Map is central to the passage from observability to epistemology. Hidden Zero shows that an undeclared reference field still governs. Bimba makes the reference field explicit. Pratibimba then makes a situated agency readable *relative to* that field. Energy-Based and topological methods become useful because their measurements are anchored to a declared epistemic ground rather than to a self-scoring model.

This sharpens Agentworld's comparatively tacit noun:

> **What is a world for an agent, what epistemic field makes that world determinable, and what is an agent for that world?**

O:I's research office is to make forms of that relation composable, variable and inspectable—not to declare a final answer.

### Worldhood includes limitation

A minimal world–agency relation requires a bounded locus to receive difference, mediate/determine it, act, and receive consequence. Maximal compositions may add persistent history, nested Context Frames, tools/senses, delegated powers, other internalities, institutions, permissions, refusals, common memory and frame-revising Return.

More context is not automatically more world. The tattvic field supplies the control: what is hidden, impossible, excluded, authoritative, remembered, salient, actionable and revisable helps constitute a finite world just as much as what is available.
