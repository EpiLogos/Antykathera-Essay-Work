## 1. Signal law — Unslop is a reading discipline before it is a rewrite discipline

External guide: `theclaymethod/unslop` (`cleanup`, `rewrite`, `core-contract`). It is used here only as an anti-residue guide; project-native writing law and source fidelity remain authoritative.

For every recovered carrier, first reduce the read to:

```text
what is being distinguished
→ why the distinction exists
→ what operation it performs
→ what it changes downstream
→ what neighbouring relation gives it meaning
→ what source or authored return presently warrants it
```

Then remove prose that no longer carries one of those functions.

Cut or compress:

- repeated declarations that material is “important”, “strong”, “materially deepened”, “worth making explicit”, or “a decisive correction” after the underlying relation is already shown;
- audit-language whose job ended when recovery was accepted;
- generic transitions that announce movement instead of performing it;
- repeated restatements of one relation under successively more abstract wording;
- canned `not X but Y` contrasts where no live ambiguity requires the contrast;
- implementation references used as rhetorical warrant for a philosophical relation already derived elsewhere;
- headings or lists that duplicate the structure already visible in the argument.

Preserve:

- technical and historical terms whose specificity carries the argument;
- provenance boundaries;
- genuine tensions and unresolved alternatives;
- equations, diagrams and compact relation-forms where they carry more information than surrounding prose;
- distinctions whose reason would disappear if compressed.

The standard is not minimal word count. It is **maximum recoverable relation per sentence**.

## 2. Link law — the wiki should disclose the argument’s topology

Wikilinks are semantic joints, not keyword decoration. A useful link should answer at least one of these questions:

```text
What does this receive from?
What does it differentiate from?
What operation does it hand onward?
What source warrants it?
What later argument consumes it?
What contrary / return path can revise it?
```

The audit should therefore prefer reciprocal relation over repeated mention. A canonical concept does not need to be linked every time its name appears; it does need links at the places where its office changes or becomes causally relevant.

Required backlink behaviour:

- [[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|Tattvic Compression]] should disclose later consumers where the contracted field actively structures cognition, limitation, worldhood or return.

Broken, ambiguous and duplicate local links are conformance failures. A backlink is valuable when it exposes dependency, inheritance, pressure or Return.

## 3. Tattvic conformance — transverse meta-context, already active

The current essay already gives the tattvas a wider office than Native 026’s compressed phrase “the tattvic / antaḥkaraṇa field” makes visible.

The local §0 carrier is explicit:

> the thirty-six tattvas are compressed as an argumentative anatomy by which an implicated whole becomes an explicate, inhabitable world and returns to recognition.

Its own warrant then connects Māyā, the kañcukas and antaḥkaraṇa to limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, order, determination, appropriation, coordination, memory and alternative formation. [[11-s0-p4-tattvic-compression|The Sixfold Tattvic Compression]] is therefore a **compression point for a field that remains active after §0**.

The conformance pass must preserve at least these existing transverse offices:

### Cognition and exclusion

[[08-s0-p1-inner-instrument|Antaḥkaraṇa]], [[09-s0-p2-vikalpa-samkalpa|Vikalpa / Saṃkalpa]] and [[10-s0-p3-apoha|Apoha]] are nested operations of a bounded cognitive field. Their later AI/LLM parallels should retain this context. §5 already proves that the relation is active rather than ornamental by returning to apoha in [[38-s5-p1-apoha-softmax|Apoha, Softmax, and Argmax]].

The technical comparison is strongest when it asks what kind of differentiation, exclusion, appropriation, memory, valuation and action become inspectable in an artificial operative interior. It weakens when one Indian term is paired with one machine mechanism as if the two were equivalent.

### Limitation and inhabited worldhood

Māyā and the kañcukas carry the move from undivided capacity into situated limitation. In the essay’s present use, limitation is not merely privation: it is constitutive of a finite locus for whom there can be determinate knowledge, action, temporality, desire and world.

That meta-context remains relevant wherever the essay asks what an internality can disclose, exclude, remember, intend, act upon or receive back. It therefore crosses the later Objective Internality, Co-Internality, Agentworld and commons material without turning those sections into a second tattva exposition.

### Phenomenological science and the Theory-of-Mind boundary

The Agentworld brief makes recursive Theory of Mind a practical coordination problem while bracketing metaphysical conclusions from performed subjectivity. The essay’s stronger boundary is not supplied by Western ToM alone. Its Indian/Yogic field carries traditions in which mind, cognition, embodiment, attention, identification, limitation and recognition are investigated through sustained phenomenological disciplines as well as conceptual analysis.

The conformance pass must therefore retain the essay’s live asymmetry:

```text
model another operative interior
≠ possess that interior

behavioural / linguistic self-report
≠ exhausted account of phenomenality

Western Theory of Mind
= one bounded explanatory register

Yoga and the tattvic / antaḥkaraṇa traditions
= a materially developed phenomenological-practical research field
  that changes what counts as an adequate question about mind
```

This does not make Yoga an automatic warrant for any AI phenomenality claim. It prevents the essay from silently accepting the contemporary Western theory-of-mind horizon as the maximal science of mindedness.

### Return

The tattvic arc culminates in recognition without deletion of the differentiated world. That relation remains available wherever later sections ask how a local determination can return to source, context, neighbour or ground without being annihilated. This is one reason the tattvas are meta-contextual across the quilt rather than a historical excursus confined to §0.

**Conformance requirement:** #39 must be able to represent this transverse office without creating a redundant “Tattvas” topic silo or forcing every later page to repeat the full thirty-sixfold field.

## 4. §5 / Agentworld — world and agency are the research relation

Native 026 correctly says that Agentworld broadens Objective Internality through sensorium, action, causal representation, institutions and world-forming Co-Internality. The §5 room supplies a more exact way to state what its six waypoints jointly do.

Current §5 sequence:

```text
#0  mathematics moves as meaning
#1  apoha / softmax / argmax
#2  J-space
#3  preference / hidden zero
#4  Bimba / energy-based fields
#5  six research vectors
```

Read together, these are not six illustrations of AI. They progressively make an artificial operative interior available as a research object:

```text
consequential transformation
→ differential selection
→ situated field of judgments / affordances / memories / tools / others
→ disclosed evaluative ground
→ governed world-model / reflection-field
→ experiments that vary seams, gauges, lenses, topology, recursion and Return
```

The Agentworld encounter sharpens the question because its central noun remains comparatively underdetermined:

> **What is a world for an agent, and what is an agent for a world?**

O:I’s research office is not to declare the answer. It is to make forms of that relation composable, variable and inspectable.

### Minimal and maximal forms are an experimental envelope

Do not treat them as an intelligence ladder.

A minimal world–agency relation requires enough structure for a bounded locus to receive a determinate condition, transform it, act, and receive consequence:

```text
bounded locus
→ available difference / context
→ mediation / determination
→ possible action
→ returned consequence
```

A maximal composition may add persistent history, self-situating Context Frames, heterogeneous tools and senses, delegated powers, other independently grounded internalities, institutions, permissions, refusals, common memory and a Return capable of revising the governing frame.

The research question is not whether more context automatically means more world. The tattvic field supplies the deeper control: **worldhood is articulated through limitation as well as availability**. What is hidden, impossible, excluded, authoritative, remembered, salient, actionable and revisable helps constitute the world of a finite agency.

### The six O:I products belong here as research powers, not allegorical labels

The O:I product field — Central, Actuation, AIKit, Factory, Workcell and QL-MEF — should enter §5 only where each product makes a distinct world–agency relation inspectable or variable. Their sixfold composition must be recovered from their authored roles and current implementation before final prose assigns them offices.

The governing relation is:

```text
essay distinction
→ technical responsibility
→ composable experimental condition
→ observed result / failure / null result
→ return capable of revising the proposed correspondence
```

No product warrants the philosophical argument merely by fitting a sixfold form. No six-product mapping should be frozen in #39 unless the product’s actual role explains why it occupies that relation.

### Agentworld is therefore challenged from within its own name

The venue gives rich accounts of agents, niches, simulations, institutions, language and hybrid intelligence. O:I can turn the comparatively tacit `world` into an experimental variable:

- what persists for this agency;
- what it can sense and act upon;
- what counts as local context;
- what gives an object or source authority;
- what lies outside its model but can still resist it;
- what other internalities can enter its field;
- what common institutions mediate consequences;
- what can alter the frame by which the agency determines at all.

That is the strongest contemporary office for O:I in the essay: **research infrastructure for investigating world–agency composition**, with Objective Internality and Co-Internality supplying the philosophical distinctions and returned technical evidence remaining capable of revising the correspondence.
