# 1. Current authorial taproot

## 1.1 Self-identity and the primitive relation

**Authored position:** the essential nature of the Real is self-identity. Self-identity becomes self-referential only through differentiation without loss of identity. Difference is therefore internal to self-identity rather than an alien addition.

The primitive symbolic relation is:

```text
0 / 1
```

where:

- `0` = not-object / irrepresentable ground;
- `1` = determination / appearance / mark;
- `/` = living mediation through which determination stands in and from ground without exhausting it.

This is ground / mediation / appearance, not two positive objects separated by punctuation.

## 1.2 `0/1 = 4+2`

Current authorial derivation:

```text
[00, 01, 10, 11] + [0, 1]
= 4 + 2
```

The four relations articulate exhaustive binary self-relation while the two generative poles remain active through that articulation.

**Authored consequence:**

> **Exhaustive relation is not exhaustion of reality.**

The formal articulation can be complete in its office without totalising the Real.

## 1.3 Investigation and faith

Current mature aphoristic formulation:

> **The approachable limit is the basis of investigation; the absolute limit is the basis of faith; the limit of investigation is the limit of determination; the limit of faith is never to be determined.**

Investigation and faith are conjugate. Investigation refuses premature closure inside the determinate field; faith refuses closure of that field around the Real.

This is an authorially supplied formulation and may enter the future Episteme aphorism ledger. It is not invented by the recovery process.

## 1.4 Referential ontology

**Authored position:** temporal things are referential structures relative to the reality upon which they depend to be and become. A living symbol consciously intensifies this ontological condition: a determinate appearance stands in relation to what it does not exhaust.

The `0/1` Symbolon is therefore logical, symbolic, metaphysical, epistemic, mathematical and psychoid without those registers becoming identical.

# 2. God, definition and confession

## 2.1 God as the indefinable

Current taproot definition:

> **God = the indefinable.**

The death of God is therefore not the death of the indefinable Real but the death of a determinate God-image / definition that the Real has outlasted.

The return is not restoration of the exhausted representation. It is return to the indefinable referent of determination and symbolisation.

The current aeonic symbolic development is:

- no determination is the Real as such;
- every determination arises within and refers through the Real;
- the older singular One-God image therefore returns through the non-dual binary as **None God / Every God**;
- the positive living form is the eros-of-logos character of `4+2`: harmony-in-difference.

## 2.2 Hybris and confession

**Authored position:** Hybris is the absolutisation of a bounded measure, frame, account, world-picture or internality.

Current pithy theorem:

> **Hybris is a measure claiming the authority of the unmeasurable.**

The corresponding rational confession is:

> determination is real, powerful, extensible and indispensable; determination is not the determinant of the Real as such.

This is reason completing its own self-reference, not reason being opposed by faith.

# 3. Objective Internality and the inside of difference

## 3.1 Objective Internality

The operative distinction is pramātṛ / pramāṇa / prameya:

```text
subjective pole / mediating internality / determination
knower          / means of knowing       / known
```

The western mind-problem often collapses mediating internal contents into “the subject.” Objective Internality restores the distinction between:

- the unobjectifiable subjective pole;
- the objectively inspectable mediating world through which a finite life or agent is situated;
- determinate objects/relations presented in and through that world.

This lets the same epistemic grammar operate across human and technical minds without deciding machine phenomenality by fiat.

## 3.2 Experiential image

The childhood dream/image remains authorial phenomenological ground, not external proof: consciousness is poured through wholly different body-mind-world forms while the recognition remains **“I was not changed.”**

Its mature yield is:

> **The Real reconciles difference not by erasing difference, but by giving difference an inside.**

This is a strong first/second-person bridge to Objective Co-Internality and compassion/harmony-in-difference.

> **0 is not empty because it is unmanifest; it is overfull relative to any single 1. A 1 is not self-grounding because it is determinate; it is the local determination of relations that exceed it.**

> **`0/1 ↔ 1/0` is proposed as a minimal formal grammar of determination-within-overdetermination: a determinate form arises from a source-field it does not exhaust, and through its conjugate orientation the determination is recursively returned into the field from which further determination becomes possible.**

Combined theorem:

> **Self-identity is the persistence of a relational whole through its own differentiating determinations; symbolisation is the form in which a determination carries its still-unexhausted relation to that whole.**

# 7. Agentworld authorial return — worldhood, Co-Internality and commons

The most recent authorial refinement is now part of Native 027 ground.

## 7.1 Agentworld as a soft idealist pressure

The venue's `Agentworld` names a hybrid ecology. The essay-native double reading is:

```text
Agentworld
= a world of agents
= a world increasingly agent-mediated / agent-articulated
```

This is **not** attributed as Bratton's intended pun and does not make Agentworld proof of idealism.

The stronger essay relation is:

> **what counts as a lived, actionable, social and represented world is increasingly inseparable from the internalities through which it is disclosed, modelled, acted upon, remembered and returned.**

Idealism here concerns order of appearing/intelligibility/dependence; it never licenses a local model to declare itself the Original.

```text
Bimba ≠ Pratibimba
Original ≠ world-model
```

## 7.2 Co-Internality as world-forming relation

Current authorial question:

> **How can many objective internalities co-produce consequential common conditions while no one internality, model, institution or ensemble is permitted to become the metaphysical owner of the world they share?**

The common field is operationally real without becoming a super-agent.

Theory-of-mind remains bounded by:

```text
A models B ≠ A contains B
```

Hence the target is reciprocal, bounded and revisable legibility among genuinely other internalities.

## 7.3 4:2 Technē agentic commons

The affiliated 4:2 Technē architecture remains Offered/applied rather than philosophical proof.

Its deeper commons relation is:

```text
Power
+ locally grounded plurality
+ shared relation
+ Protected Account / source integrity
```

Mono/Poly form:


An agentic commons is therefore a **world-forming relation among differentiated agents**, not a pool of agents.

Its governing institutional question is:
