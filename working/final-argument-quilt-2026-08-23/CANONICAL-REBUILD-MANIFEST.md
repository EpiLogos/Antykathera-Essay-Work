#### Form Growth law

Use `RELATIONAL-FORM-GROWTH-GRAMMAR.md` positively and permissively:

> **Fullness means maximum earned articulation with recursive return.**

Cardinality is earned by the actual whole. Never force an N-fold, conjugate or recursion simply because QL has an ideal geometry available.

Where a genuine developed `N` has a genuine conjugate `N′`, preserve the distinction:

```text
N          N′
 \        /
  \      /
   N+N′
```

`N+N′` means **N newly generated relational determinations**, not numerical aggregation and not a merged vocabulary list.

At the higher Symbolon scale:

```text
N      :     N+N′     :      N′
0      :       /      :       1
```

The generated slash may itself become articulated; the named achieved whole can become a higher `0/1` and enter another generation.

Keep conjugate generation distinct from distributive recursion:

```text
N / N′ → N+N′
asks: what does each position become THROUGH its conjugate?

N → N×N
asks: what does the WHOLE N-fold become inside this position?
```

For the arbitration exemplar, preserve exactly:

```text

Continuity                     Indeterminacy
Criterion                      Distinction
Delineation                    Difference
Arbitration                    Crisis
Con-text                        Diaphaneity
Resolution                     Reconciliation

               ↓ generated 6+6′ ↓

Continuity-in-Indeterminacy     → Origin
Criterion-through-Distinction   → Measure / Limit
Delineation-through-Difference  → Perspective / Frame
Arbitration-in-Crisis           → Decision / Hybris
```

The processual generated term is not replaced by the later semantic flowering. `Criterion-through-Distinction` remains the generated relation; Measure/Limit flowers from it. The same law applies throughout the field.

`3:3` and `4:2` remain orienting readings, not equations: `3:3` especially illuminates conjugate traversal; `4:2` especially illuminates complete six-body / holographic recursion. Neither proves or equals `N+N′` or `N×N`.
