## Conjugate and recursive readings

### `4+2`

The six named positions are the generative positional body. Four explicate + two implicate is a structural reading of the same six, not the eight-turn count.

### `3:3`

A horizontal/emanative–reversionary reading of the sixfold. It does not change the positions.

### `4:2`

A vertical/orientational reading, especially four explicate operations relative to two implicate conditions/poles. It is not synonymous with `4+2` generation.

### `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`

Conjugate generation: two complete sixfold faces generate six processual/compositional relations. The Arbitration Etymology field is the ratified exemplar; this does not make that one example the definition of QL.

### `6×6`

Distributive recursion: each position receives a full local sixfold. It is not a synonym for conjugate generation.

### P4 / CF5

At `#4`, perspective becomes context because prior positions and their relations become jointly available. This contextual aperture permits nesting. CF5 is the maximal recursive Context Frame only where the concrete composition earns that availability.
