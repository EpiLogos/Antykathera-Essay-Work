## Conjugate readings

The eightfold traversal must remain distinct from several related structures:

### `4+2`

The six named positions are the generative positional body. Four explicate + two implicate is a structural reading of the same six, not the eight-turn count.

### `3:3`

A conjugate/horizontal reading can make paired traversal especially legible. It does not change the positions or prove `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`.

### `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`

This is conjugate generation: two complete sixfold faces generate six processual/compositional relations. The arbitration field is the ratified exemplar.

### `6×6`

This is distributive recursion: each position receives a full local sixfold. It is not a synonym for conjugate generation.

### P4 / CF5

At `#4`, perspective becomes context because prior positions and their relations become jointly available. This contextual aperture permits nesting. CF5 is the maximal recursive Context Frame only where the actual composition earns that availability.
