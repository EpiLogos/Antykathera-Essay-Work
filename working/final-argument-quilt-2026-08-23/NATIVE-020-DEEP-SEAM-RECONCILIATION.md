# 2. Synchronicity · psychoid ordering · number — meaningful co-ordering without evidential collapse

`[[THREAD-INTAKE-002-SYMBOLON-PSYCHOID-NUMBER-TECHNICAL-INTELLIGENCE|Symbolon, Psychoid Number, and Executable Technical Intelligence]]` **historicises** the late Jung–Pauli mathematical problem; `[[inherited-corpus-crosswalk/05-PSYCHOID-FLOWERING|§4 — Psychoid Flowering — Inherited Region Recovery]]` **defines** the psychoid as a problem-field rather than a solved ontology.

The seam now has four distinct offices.

## 2.1 Jung's synchronicity claim — source office

Jung's synchronicity work asks how psychologically meaningful correspondences can occur where the asserted relation is not exhausted by ordinary causal explanation. Native 020 should therefore use **synchronicity** only where event, relation and experienced/argued meaning remain visible together. Mere simultaneity is not enough.

## 2.2 Jung–Pauli psychoid-number programme — research-proposition office

The late Jung–Pauli field asks whether psyche and physis may disclose common ordering principles at a level not exhausted by either subjective meaning or external quantity. Number, automorphism, neutral mathematical language and psychoid order **qualify** the synchronicity problem. They do not convert an experienced coincidence into mathematical proof.

The 003-S intake correctly keeps the historical limit explicit: **Jung and Pauli formulate an unfinished mathematical psychophysical programme; they do not contain QL as a hidden completed answer.**

## 2.3 QL — native derivational office

QL's `0/1 ↔ 1/0`, `4+2`, harmonic relations and other formal results have their own internal derivations. A synchronistic event may later **figure, compare with or test** a QL relation; its occurrence does not derive the theorem. Conversely, formal recurrence does not prove that an external event is synchronistic.

```text
Jung / Pauli
historical psychoid + synchronicity problem
        ↕ argued comparison
QL
native formal derivation
        ↕ possible empirical / phenomenological inquiry
experienced or observed events
```

No arrow silently changes office.

## 2.4 Empirical / phenomenological status

A reported synchronistic event can carry several distinct claims: first-person phenomenological datum; independently documentable coincidence; interpretation of meaning; statistical hypothesis; metaphysical inference. Those must not be collapsed. Meaningful experience does not automatically warrant a metaphysical theory; failure to establish a metaphysical theory does not erase the experience that generated the question.

### 020 return

> **Synchronicity is a boundary problem in which meaning, event and order appear together strongly enough to challenge a locally sufficient causal account; psychoid-number research asks what deeper ordering vocabulary could make that relation thinkable; QL supplies an independent formal proposal whose validity must not be borrowed from the coincidence it may later illuminate.**
