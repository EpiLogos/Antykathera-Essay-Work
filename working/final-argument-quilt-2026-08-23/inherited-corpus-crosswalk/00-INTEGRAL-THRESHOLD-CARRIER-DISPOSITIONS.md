The inherited movement #3 gathers several formally and historically distinct cases. Their current offices are:

Project use: a formal system can preserve articulation by restricting re-entry.

**Office:** `supporting-visible / formal-history witness`.

## Gödel

Sufficiently strong consistent formal systems have precise internal incompleteness limits under stated conditions.

**Office:** `supporting-visible / formal witness`.

## Wittgenstein

Project use: conditions of meaningful saying cannot all be treated as objects at the same level; formal rule does not determine its own application independently of practice.

**Office:** `supporting-visible / philosophical witness`.

Process/Creativity turns non-closure into becoming rather than merely restriction.

**Office:** `radial/depth` at opening, stronger later process role.

Project use: positive language and apophatic limit can cooperate; image/Word becomes transparent rather than idolised.

**Office:** `radial/depth`, possibly supporting-visible if final prose needs a theological witness.

Project use: exactness can grow together with exact knowledge of limitation.

**Office:** `supporting-visible` or radial depending final composition.
