
```text

#1 = 00
#2 = 01

#4 = 11
```

This compact relation is **Internal-derived** and remains legitimate as a census of the sixfold.

```text

  → 2⁶ / 6²
  → /4
  → 16/9
  = 2⁴/3²
  → 4+2
```

with four explicate powers and two implicate harmonics exposed by the internal accounting.

The active project coordinate authority independently states the current structural reason as:

- four explicate positions P1–P4;

The compact binary census and the richer topological/accountative derivation are therefore **compatible but non-identical offices**.
