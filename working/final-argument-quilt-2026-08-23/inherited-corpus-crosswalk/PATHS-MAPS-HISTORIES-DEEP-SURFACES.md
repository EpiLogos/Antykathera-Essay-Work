# First architectural lesson from the path/deep field

The inherited architecture contains at least four different kinds of carrier that low-context agents have repeatedly confused:

1. **argument nodes** carrying inferential operations;
2. **controlled concepts** carrying reusable definitions;
3. **transverse paths** preserving one relation across stations;
4. **source/reference surfaces** preserving authorial/external operations and evidence boundaries.

The future architecture should not promote every deep note into Concepts or stuff every transverse relation into one Argument. It must ask what kind of object lets the relation do its work with minimum duplication and maximum provenance.
