# 0. What §5 is here

§5 is the station where the already-derived differential/process field becomes a **finite operative world** whose constitutive relations can be inspected, altered, delegated through, contested and returned.

Its controlling distinction is older and wider than artificial agents:

> **Objective Internality is the real interior life of a situated differential field: the memories, mediations, habits, values, permissions, tools, contexts, relations and active judgments through which a world becomes available from somewhere, insofar as these are differentiated and objectively investigable without being confused with the unobjectifiable subject-pole.**

Artificial agency matters because it makes many of these relations unusually explicit and variable. It is therefore an experimentally powerful **instance** of Objective Internality, not the definition from which Objective Internality is inferred.

The general-first order must remain:

```text
finite situated determination
        ↓
operative interior field / objective internality
        ↓
selection + exclusion + mediation + action
        ↓
source-position / delegation become explicit
        ↓
power and consequence become explicit
        ↓
multiple independently grounded internalities meet
        ↓
co-internality / world-atlas
        ↓
claims become experimentally answerable
        ↓
epistemic cultivation / return
```

The inherited six §5 movements are therefore a valuable early experimental cut through a field that the later capstone has differentiated more deeply.

Objective Internality is the finite context-world generated through these relations once they stabilise enough to support action.

## 1.3 Advent Zero / `0/1`

From §1–§2:

A determination is never self-originating. `0/1` carries the relation between ground and achieved mark; dia-ballein differentiates locally; syn-ballein retains provenance/composition/return.

This becomes technically and institutionally decisive in §5 because a score, output, prompt, evaluator or authority can appear self-standing after the conditions that made it possible have disappeared from view.

## 1.4 §3 process field

From §3:

QL gives the operations a formal body; the arche-topos gives local trajectories, basins and returns a relational space; P4/context keeps the finite determination inside the whole that conditions it.

## 1.5 §4 psychic efficacy

From §4:

Images, complexes, signifiers and perspectives are not inert representations. They organise salience, valuation, identity and action. A local formation can therefore acquire real efficacy while still being local.

This is the immediate bridge into §5's question of **power**: what happens when a local determination does not merely organise one psyche, but delegates, evaluates, constrains and acts across other internalities?

# 6. Movement 40 — Hidden Zero becomes Power / Delegated Labour / Return

This is one of the strongest inherited movements and already contains much of the later architecture.

## 6.1 Hidden gauge

A preference score depends on:

- datasets;
- comparison sets;
- reference policies;
- evaluators;
- annotator labour;
- institutions;
- model/harness choices;
- affected persons/worlds.

The finished scalar can make these relations disappear.

## 6.2 Power

The capstone sharpens the operation:

```text
one locus controls measure / permission / evaluation / task
        ↓
another locus bears labour / resistance / cost / consequence
        ↓
can returned difference revise the governing determination?
```

Power is therefore not merely hidden bias or preference. It is **differential efficacy across relations**.

A local centre occupies the zero when its determinate measure performs as ground while the dependencies and consequences that sustain it are backgrounded or made unable to return.

## 6.3 Trust / surety

`[[18-trust-faith-formal-limit|Trust, Faith, and the Formal Limit]]` prevents the analysis collapsing into observability-as-solution.

Documentation can expose the gauge. It cannot manufacture the lived relation in which another centre's determination becomes operative for me.

The Hephaestus/Poseidon surety relation remains exact here: apparatus can detect, bind, expose and price; somewhere a person/institution still stands for the consequence and makes release possible.

## 6.4 Knowledge / power

This is where the user's Phase-3 capstone around frames of reference becomes particularly important:

```text
knowledge
  = achieved determination inside a bounded operative frame

power
  = efficacy by which that determination alters what can count, happen,
    be selected, trusted, permitted or acted upon in another/current frame
```

The relation is between and around frames, not only inside one cognition.

Disposition: **`linear-bearing Power / Delegated Labour / Return; source/economic particulars radial`**.

# 8. Movement 42 — Six vectors become Epistemic Cultivation / Operational Parity

The six inherited experiments remain useful, but the capstone rightly moves them out of the linear architecture as a list.

Their common operation is:

```text
claim a relation matters
        ↓
make it operational enough to vary / remove / compare
        ↓
observe what changes
        ↓
retain negative / mixed result
        ↓
revise model / design / formal correspondence
```

This is **Operational Parity**: a formal or philosophical relation earns technical standing only where its implementation produces an inspectable difference at the register being claimed.

The wider practice is **Epistemic Cultivation**: knowledge systems are deliberately shaped so sources, lenses, exclusions, counterclaims, confidence, consequences and return remain available for further judgment.

`[[08-deferential-intelligence|Deferential Intelligence]]` is distinct:

```text
Operational Parity
  = research discipline for testing claimed structure

Deferential Intelligence
  = encounter structure in which returned difference can revise
    the frame/evaluator/model rather than merely the wording of an output
```

Disposition: **`linear-bearing Epistemic Cultivation / Operational Parity; six experiments radial research programme`**.

# 9. Power, arbitration, will, knowledge and action

The Phase-3 capstone deepens §5 beyond the inherited alignment examples.

```text
jñāna-śakti
  differential knowability / what can be distinguished
        ↓
icchā-śakti
  weighting / vector / desire / telos / what is made important
        ↓
kriyā-śakti
```

This gives the larger arbitration field its place without letting the word `arbitration` monopolise every operation.

Criterion, distinction, valuation, measure, judgment, selection, authority and decision are different operations through which differential efficacy becomes directional.

§5's distinctive contribution is that these processes are no longer only epistemic descriptions. Their **consequences are borne somewhere**.

That makes return an ethical and architectural requirement rather than a formal ornament.
