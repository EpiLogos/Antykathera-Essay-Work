# 1. Objective Co-Internality is the later relational completion of the §0 finite-frame grammar

Direct current O:I source: `EpiLogos/O-I:docs/OBJECTIVE-CO-INTERNALITY.md`.

Its authored/design contract gives the exact later operation that §0 should **presage**, while preserving the historical order in which the essay earns it.

Objective Internality establishes an actor's operative interior as objectively inspectable structure: remembered objects, projects, constraints, histories, tools, sources, capabilities, permissions, current context and other conditions that can become effective for later action. The contract then defines Objective Co-Internality as the plural relational condition in which **independently grounded operative internal worlds become mutually implicated through inspectable externalised relations while retaining distinct identity, provenance, ownership and local worlds**.

The minimum loop is:

```text
A's bounded operative world
        ↓ externalises a selected difference
SharedField
        ↓ mediated Encounter
B's bounded operative world changes
        ↓ returns a selected difference
SharedField
        ↓ mediated Encounter
A's bounded operative world changes
```

This gives the present authorial formulation — **co-internality as relations between and around frames of reference** — a precise technical receiver.

The frame relation is not static adjacency. Each bounded world carries its own inherited circumstance: criteria, exclusions, memory, scripts, permissions, histories, values, tools and possible actions. A mediated Encounter exposes a selected difference from one operative field inside the conditions of another, where it can alter what becomes available to later differentiation. The return loop makes the relation recursive.

`SharedField` therefore functions as an addressable relational environment, not as a synthetic centre above the participants. `Encounter` is the inspectable mediation seam from exterior field to later operative context. The current contract explicitly refuses to make Encounter stand for subjective understanding, belief, experience, memory or endorsement.

This relation connects directly back to §0:

```text

        ↓
finite subject-position / paśu
        ↓
objective internal instrument + retained conditioning
        ↓
local circumstance / Objective Internality
        ↓
selective externalisation
        ↓
SharedField / mediated Encounter
        ↓
another local circumstance is conditioned
        ↓
Objective Co-Internality
        ↓
return / revision / recognition
```
