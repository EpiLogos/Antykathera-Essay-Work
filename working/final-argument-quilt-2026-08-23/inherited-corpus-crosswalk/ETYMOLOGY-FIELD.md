# Inherited Etymology / Word-Field Recovery

Status: first complete pass across the **current live cluster set**; architecture deliberately unfrozen.

## Why this field exists

The etymology system is one of the essay's ways of thinking. It does not provide a decorative root after an argument is finished. It lets an already-derived operation acquire linguistic memory, semantic tension, historical resistance and poetic re-entry while keeping the truth-condition of each relation explicit.

The present cluster directories are inherited working boundaries, not a target count. Native 018 must derive the future word-field architecture from the final argument.

## Four evidence / operation registers

The live etymology method repeatedly distinguishes:

1. **philological descent** — documented historical linguistic relation;
2. **attested semantic field / development** — historically available ranges and shifts;
3. **operational homology / analogia** — independent histories performing a named comparable operation while retaining their differences;
4. **poetic / phonic re-entry** — sound/image collision that genuinely discloses or remembers an operation without masquerading as descent.

Several clusters add technical equivalence or graphic/phonetic relations where appropriate. These registers are not a ladder from serious to decorative. They answer different questions and must remain typed so the essay can use poetic intelligence without laundering it into historical warrant.

# 2. Symbol / Account / Trust — Marks That Circulate

File: `symbol-account-and-trust/HISTORY.md`

- tally preserves a cut;
- symbolon preserves a break whose matching can attest relation;
- account orders marks into a telling;

The cluster's central test is whether circulation retains provenance, reciprocal address, risk and route of return, or whether the portable mark quietly occupies the zero.

# 3. Apportionment / Naming / Number / Oikonomia

File: `apportionment-and-economy/HISTORY.md`  
Status: living / active working field.

## Governing operation

How does a field distribute a share, make the share legible, let it circulate, and keep the allotment answerable to the whole that bears it?

This gives *oikonomia* an older/wider office than “market”: arrangement, measure, distribution and return within a household/common world.

## Distinctions recovered

- Greek `nemein / nomos` — distribute/assign/manage into law/custom/order;
- `oikonomia` — household arrangement/management;
- Latin `numerus` — possible wider apportionment relation, still lexical-source gated;
- Latin `nomen` — distinct name lineage, deliberately **not** collapsed into number;
- `nomisma` — coin/current money through custom/law;
- denomination — naming office joined operationally to numbered money;
- ratio/reason/arithmos/harmony — promising separate braid requiring verification.

The distinction between **name and number as different lineages meeting operationally** is itself important. Denomination makes their public encounter concrete without inventing a false cognacy.

## Argument pressure

- law, norm and authority;
- money/credit/accounting;
- Mono/Poly and commons;
- computation as account;

The Copula Derivation Chain's D8 gives this cluster unusually strong derivational relevance: determination → count/name → account → apportionment → economy → `5→0` audit.

# 4. Homologia / Analogia — Discipline of Comparison

File: `homology-and-analogy/HISTORY.md`  
Status: living / active working field.

## Governing operation

The project needs a precise way to say **what kind of sameness has actually been earned** when different traditions, sciences, images or words illuminate one relation.

This cluster therefore governs the comparative method rather than only one local argument.

## Comparison ledger recovered

- cognacy/descent;
- semantic development;
- formal equivalence/isomorphism;
- operational homology;
- analogia / proportional resemblance;
- poetic re-entry.

Each has a minimum burden and a forbidden shortcut.

## Why it matters

The project constantly composes Sanskrit, Greek, mathematics, psychology, physics, computation, myth and lived experience. Without a comparison grammar, rich synthesis either becomes timid or falsely univocal.

`pros hen` and analogia let several fields orient around a focal operation while remaining genuinely distinct. `Homologia` asks when several witnesses can actually be said to agree in a named operation.

## Architecture pressure

This field may deserve a central methodological etymology/history and perhaps relation to `register-grammar` rather than being merely one cluster among topical word histories. Its role is transversal and epistemic.

Native 018 must decide whether the present cluster is itself sufficient or whether comparison grammar should be distributed among:

- etymology method;
- register grammar concept;
- Core Theorem Bridge argument;
- final Symbolon/MEF method pages.

The operation must survive regardless of file disposition.
