## Why this field exists

The etymology system is one of the essay's ways of thinking. It does not provide a decorative root after an argument is finished. It lets an already-derived operation acquire linguistic memory, semantic tension, historical resistance and poetic re-entry while keeping the truth-condition of each relation explicit.

The present cluster directories are inherited working boundaries, not a target count. Native 018 must derive the future word-field architecture from the final argument.

## Four evidence / operation registers

The live etymology method repeatedly distinguishes:

1. **philological descent** — documented historical linguistic relation;
2. **attested semantic field / development** — historically available ranges and shifts;
3. **operational homology / analogia** — independent histories performing a named comparable operation while retaining their differences;
4. **poetic / phonic re-entry** — sound/image collision that genuinely discloses or remembers an operation without masquerading as descent.

Several clusters add technical equivalence or graphic/phonetic relations where appropriate. These registers are not a ladder from serious to decorative. They answer different questions and must remain typed so the essay can use poetic intelligence without laundering it into historical warrant.
