# Inherited Argument-Bearing Field

Status: active recovery; **not** an argument census and **not** a proposed future node list.

This ledger asks which distinct inferential operations are actually present across current arguments, movements, quilts, paths and deep surfaces. Existing argument filenames are provenance containers. Native 018 later determines which operations ought to receive canonical argument identities.

## Recovery rule

For every argument-bearing surface record:

1. the positive proposition / question / synthesis it performs;
2. why that operation is distinct enough to matter;
3. what it currently bundles together;
4. what later quilt/capstone developments change;
5. current consumers and transverse routes;
6. architecture pressure: `retain-operation / split-pressure / merge-pressure / rehome-pressure / boundary-open`.

No count is tracked as a completion target.

## §0/1-bearing argument operations recovered so far

### `01-immutable-gap-and-meta-sign.md`

**Current operation.** Establishes the invariant non-coincidence between a determination and the condition under which determination occurs. Objectifying the condition recreates the gap at another reflective level. `0` names this through a meta-sign; further contextual data cannot itself abolish the context/act problem.

**Why it exists.** The essay needs an invariant statement of why the ground cannot be captured by adding another represented item.

**Current bundled material.** Subject/non-objectifiability, zero/meta-sign, perspective/context, formal-limit witnesses, set-theoretic floor, Advent links and some diaphaneity pressure.

**Later pressure.** The Flow's experience-first and faithful-definition/God relation, capstone `1/0` self-authorisation problem and local-zero/authority work make the invariant more central while also exposing the breadth of the present file.

**Architecture pressure.** `retain-operation / boundary-open / possible-split-pressure`.

The invariant gap looks genuinely distinct. Whether “Meta-Sign,” Subject/Faithful Definition and contextual reflexivity remain inside the same future argument is unresolved.

### `15-paradox-as-cross-register-hinge.md`

**Later pressure.** `1/0` Return makes this field more central; faithful-definition could supply a prior enacted form of the same movement. Parallel Quilt also preserves the proposed performed-indefinability sequence.

### `18-trust-faith-formal-limit.md`

**Current operation.** Trust is the living relation already beneath knowledge. Formal limit exposes that dependency. Humility receives the limit without abandoning reason; faith names lucid continuation where the ground cannot be possessed. Documents, calibration and institutions can support conditions for trust but cannot manufacture it.

**Why it exists.** The formal-limit argument has a finite lived consequence. A determination can become a provisional anchor or an idol; the difference is relational, not merely logical.

**Current breadth.** Epistemology, psyche, crowd/politics, myth, AI reliability/ownership, Mono/Poly and final faith.

**Later pressure.** The late Job/Sophia/Yahweh work, trust intensified by efficacy, Deferential Intelligence as model-revising encounter, authority/local-zero and protected-account work substantially deepen this operation. The Symbol/Account/Trust etymology supplies a separate linguistic-economic carrier.

**Architecture pressure.** `retain-major-operation / strong-rebuild-pressure`.

The current node is important but likely no longer adequate to carry the full trust/dependency/authority/faith field by itself.

### `16-bohmian-enfoldment-dialogical-return.md`

**Current operation.** Supplies a process bridge: implicate/explicate order gives a continuous middle between non-objectifiable ground and determinate appearance; dialogue carries the same unfold/circulate/return relation into collective intelligence.

**Why it exists.** It prevents ground from becoming inert transcendence and makes “return” a movement through differentiated expression. It also gives Epi-Logos a social/practical precedent without making Bohm authority for QL.

**Current breadth.** Bohmian process, tattvic anatomy, complex-plane image, dialogue, intercultural encounter and Agentworld consequence.

**Later pressure.** The Flow uses Bohm at §0/1 and again later; the final architecture may need to distinguish process-ontological bridge from dialogue/praxis without losing their relation.

**Architecture pressure.** `strong-operation / possible-internal-split-or-register-rehome`.

**Current operation.** Keeps performed subjectivity, objective internality, functional agency, recursive self-description and phenomenality distinct. Operational evidence can support the first four without either proving or denying phenomenal subjectivity.

**Why it exists.** This is a **positive constraint** generated by the subject argument. It prevents both anthropomorphic grant and substrate-based foreclosure while leaving technical work fully assertable.
