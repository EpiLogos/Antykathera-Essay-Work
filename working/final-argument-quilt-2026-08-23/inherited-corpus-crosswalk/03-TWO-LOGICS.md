The mature answer is not “one bad logic and one good logic,” nor merely “classical logic versus superposition.” The section discovers two **conjugate operations of one differentiating activity**:

```text
prior relational field
        ↓
DIA — distinction / bifurcation / actualisation / selection
        ↓
determinate branch / interval / position / consequence
        ↓
SYN — relation-retention / composition / contextualisation / return
        ↓
provisional achieved whole
        ↓
perturbation / new difference / renewed DIA
        ↓
recursive process
```

This is why `[[CAPSTONE-DECISIONS#F1 — Restore dia/sym-ballein as the capstone's upstream engine|F1]]` restores Dia/Syn as the **upstream engine** of the capstone and why `[[CAPSTONE-DECISIONS#F20 — Root census resolves to five operations, not former seven|F20]]` gives Dia/Syn root-Symbolon office.

# 2. The controlling correction: living dia is not fallen dualism

The most important current correction comes from the developmental quilt's D02 and late counter-generation work.

The relation is:

```text
living dia-ballein
    = difference made actual while the slash/axis remains operative

collapsed dia
    = the slash disappears or is forced to yield a winner
```

A living signed polarity can therefore remain:

```text
(-1)/(+1)
```

with zero/slash retained as the axis of tension and connection.

The fallen accounting arises when that relation is collapsed into operations such as:

```text
(-1) + (+1) → 0     cancellation
(+1) - (-1) → +2    appropriation of the span
(-1) - (+1) → -2    inverse appropriation
```

The late developmental formulation is especially exact:

> dominant and counter terms can form a living signed dia-ballein `(-1)/(+1)`; **war begins when the slash is required to yield a winner**.

This supersedes any inherited wording in §2 that makes war, projected distrust, exclusion or atomisation intrinsic to dia-ballein itself.

### Current relation

```text
DIA
  → distinguishes
  → delineates
  → compares
  → excludes locally
  → decides / actualises
  → gives finite agency a branch it can act through

DIA without SYN / return
  → loses provenance
  → mistakes branch for ground
  → makes the cut invisible
  → may harden into severance / monopoly / scapegoating / conquest
```

The pathology is therefore **dia arrested before return**, not differentiation as such.

# 3. Syn-ballein: gathering that retains differentiation

Current authored terminology is **syn-ballein**. Historical files titled `Sym-Ballein` are expression/provenance debt and should not be renamed during recovery merely to disguise history.

The mature operation is:

```text
0/1
  ↔
1/0
        ↓
(0/1)/(1/0)
```

Its meaning is not “unity” in the abstract.

Syn-ballein:

- retains the differentiating seam;
- keeps the branch related to the field from which it arose;
- preserves exclusions, sources, counter-readings and affected context;
- composes differentiated results into a provisional larger whole;
- makes inverse traversal possible;
- lets an achieved form return as a renewed condition for later action.

Its characteristic technical expression is therefore not merely metadata retention. It is **executable relation to provenance and return**.

The inherited §2 #2 already states an important boundary correctly: syn-ballein is the logic of **defining towards wholeness**, not possession of “the whole.” A local claim to completed totality would itself be a local `1` occupying `0`.

### Failure mode

Syn without retained difference becomes:

- fusion;
- forced coincidence;
- ontology collapse;
- enclosure or counterfeit gathering;
- a whole that cannot be contested from within because its seam has disappeared.

The conjugacy is therefore symmetrical at the level of failure:

```text
DIA without SYN → severance
SYN without DIA → fusion
DIA ↔ SYN        → differentiated return
```

# 4. The AND/OR relation belongs to the slash, not to moral camps

The inherited movements sometimes associate Dia with OR and Syn with AND/OR. The stronger current theorem grammar requires more care.

`/ = −/−` is the parent relation capable of distinction before either term receives positive content. The slash itself carries **AND/OR**:

- **AND** — the relata belong to one operative relation;
- **OR** — they remain genuinely distinct and can be differently oriented/selected.

Dia and Syn are therefore different **operations upon/through the slash**, not simple logical connectives that divide AND from OR into moral or ontological camps.

Dia actualises distinction and branch. Syn retains/composes the relation through which the branch remains answerable to what it excluded and changed.

This also clarifies the strong-copula relation `= = =//=` recovered upstream: identity retains difference; relation is neither fusion nor external juxtaposition.

Ares is not “bad Dia” or war in essence. He carries:

- distinction;
- limit;
- refusal;
- defence;
- individuality;
- the capacity to hold a difference against absorption.

## Aphrodite

Aphrodite carries:

- attraction;
- relation;
- eros toward another;
- permeability;
- the possibility of conjunction and generativity.

Her failure mode is fusion/possession when relation loses distinction.

Harmonia is not midpoint or compromise. She is **differentiated concurrence**: a new capacity generated when real difference and real relation remain co-active.
