# 1. What §0/1 is here and why it exists

The inherited station has one real job: **establish the subject/formal-limit relation before the essay develops the differentiating machinery of mind or earns the full QL notation**.

The current room wager is already strong: exact inquiry must disclose its dependence upon an uncounted subject, context or act of determination. The station then turns this one pressure through six inherited positions:

1. an intelligence acts from a horizon it does not fully display;
2. the subject cannot be converted into one more object without repeating the act from another subject-position;
3. determination gives by cutting and therefore carries both epistemic value and political danger;
4. formal disciplines meet differently constituted limits to self-accounting;
5. integral/diaphanous awareness makes position and context available through the determinate view;
6. zero enters provisionally as the sign of the uncounted condition and hands the problem to differentiating mind.

The later Flow and harmonisation sharpen the opening substantially: the most fundamental entry is **immediate experience itself**, from which the subject-pole follows before Agentworld or technical context is introduced. They also add the strong authorial move that **to define God is to define the subject** and that a faithful definition shows its own limit rather than replacing the ground with an idol.

This means the inherited station contains a real internal chronology problem: its current #0 still opens technically/contextually, while the later prospective whole opens phenomenologically/experientially and uses the technical context as a later refraction of the same relation.

That is a quilting discrepancy to preserve for architecture, not a reason to silently rewrite movement 01 during recovery.

## 02 · `#1` — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object

### What the inherited movement actually says

The subject is the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event: the opening through which a determinate world is given. “I see x” already implies *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa*, *prameya*: knower, means/activity of knowing, known. The familiar subject/object pair compresses away mediation.

Any attempt to objectify the subject produces another appearing object inside a further act. The structural asymmetry remains.

### Why this distinction exists

This is the station's central ontological discipline. It prevents the subject from becoming:

- a hidden object inside experience;
- an aggregate of mental functions;
- a self-model;
- a capacity competitively possessed or absent;
- an unexplained remainder at the edge of science.

It simultaneously makes strong investigation of objective internal machinery possible because the machinery can be objectified without exhausting the subject-pole.

### Later prospective deepening

The Flow strengthens this into the experience-first derivation and states the more provocative authorial relation:

**to define God is to define the subject.**

The corresponding definition work frames successful definition as self-disclosing limitation: the definition remains faithful by showing that it cannot close over the condition it invokes. The idol is the achieved representation allowed to impersonate that condition.

The capstone's Idealism and machine-phenomenality split reinforce the same office: immediate phenomenal existence has first-person force; objective internality can be richly engineered; artificial phenomenal localisation remains Open.

### Relation state

`present / later-deepened`

The current movement is already a substantial carrier. The major missing pressure is the **experience-first + faithful-definition/God** architecture now visible in the later whole.

### Eight-determination pressure

`0/1` is central: unobjectifiable condition / determinate appearing. The mediation triad already gives the slash a pre-formal office. `AM/IS` is implicit in lived subject versus predicated representation. `1/0` appears as the impossibility of the representation self-authorising its own ground.

### Architecture pressure

The final architecture must decide whether the following are one argument or several related arguments:

- Subject as non-objectifiable condition;
- immutable gap / representation-condition non-coincidence;
- faithful definition / definition of God;
- Idealism / phenomenal priority;

The present `Immutable Gap and Meta-Sign` argument carries several of these at once, but file co-location does not settle the future boundary.

## 03 · `#2` — Definition as Cut, Gift, and Danger

### What the inherited movement actually says

Determination is productive and negational. Apoha gives a semantic operation of exclusion; Spinoza gives finite figure through determination/negation. The current movement already guards their difference.

A concept gives by making something knowable. It becomes dangerous when the achieved term forgets the field and activity by which it was cut. Technical classifications likewise stabilise governable objects. The problem is already political: a system that cannot mark the limits of its determinations can naturalise provisional distinctions and then govern through them.

The *symbolon* enters as the form that retains an absent counterpart rather than closing as a terminating concept.

### Why this distinction exists

The essay cannot simply condemn distinction. The cut is how intelligibility happens. What matters is whether the cut keeps its provenance, excluded field and route of return alive.

This is the seed of the later dia-/sym-ballein operational distinction and of the whole political/technical question of hidden gauges, authority and occupied zero.

### Quilt / capstone pressure

Parallel Quilt adds:

- `de-finition` as the making of a finite edge;
- definition as an aperture whose adequacy depends on retained provenance/excluded field/return;
- trust/name/account threads around the validity of a determination;
- encounter preceding definition as etymological ground.

The later capstone deepens this into local-zero / power / protected-account / model-revising-return relations.

### Etymological relation

`encounter-region-name-count/HISTORY.md` gives this movement a much deeper linguistic field than the current movement shows. Encounter is prior; naming/counting are cuts inside a facing. The `com-/contra`, `com-putare`, count/account and region/facing families trace how a prior with becomes distinction, reckoning, office and returned account.

The etymology does **not** prove the cut. It makes the operation audible and gives it historical/linguistic memory through separately typed relations.

`present / partial / later-deepened`

### Eight-determination pressure

### Architecture pressure

## 06 · `#5→0` — The Return to Zero

### What the inherited movement actually says

Zero enters only as a promissory meta-sign: the condition under which counting/determination occurs cannot simply be counted as another object. The historical and mathematical warrant is deliberately deferred to §1.

Bohm's implicate order supplies the process bridge into §0: the remainder becomes an enfolded whole active in local unfolding rather than an inert blank. The next station is to give that implicated whole a differentiating anatomy.

### Why this distinction exists

The station needs to release its achieved subject/formal-limit insight into a generative next question. Zero is introduced without prematurely claiming identity among mathematical zero, emptiness, consciousness and subjectivity.

It also preserves the important withholding law: the notation is not yet allowed to do work the reader has not earned.

### Later pressure

The Flow is stronger than the inherited movement in two respects:

1. it begins calling the subject the lived zero much earlier in plain English;
2. the later overall return is eventually sharpened through `1/0` first-contact with excluded ground.

But the local §0/1 office remains a **promissory** zero, not the final return theorem. The future architecture must preserve the difference between opening zero, historically/mathematically earned zero, local-zero diagnostic use, and terminal `1/0` Return.

### Relation state

`present / later-deepened / correct-withholding`

### Eight-determination pressure

`0/1` is present semantically but the full glyph remains withheld in prose. `1/0` is only a distant return pressure here. The immediate movement is `5→0`: achieved inquiry becomes renewed ground for differentiating mind.

### Architecture pressure

Potentially distinct future argument offices include:

- zero as meta-sign/unobjectifiable condition;

- `1/0` as formal/recognitional Return;
