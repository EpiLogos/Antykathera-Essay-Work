
## 3.1 Van Eenwyk / Symbolon dynamics

D01 recovers the sequence:

```text

  = feeling-toned attractor basin / locally stabilised psychic organisation
        ↓

  = deeper formative constraint / patterning capacity
        ↓
```

The point is not that a Jungian archetype **is** a mathematical strange attractor. The value is operational: psychic organisation may have stable basins, boundary conditions, energetic investment and transitions without being static or fully predictable.

Van Eenwyk's 1997 source house is citation-ready but still needs exact passage extraction for the specific complex/attractor/boundary claims.

## 3.2 Image and psychic power

The later quilt develops a crucial consequence:

```text
image / self-image / desired image / god-image / cultural image
        ↓
attracts libido / attention / prāṇa / will / valuation
        ↓
organises possible action around itself
        ↓
may become a local arbiter of value and identity
```

An image can initially open a real capacity. The developmental issue is whether the resulting activity becomes autonomous and answerable to the wider field, or remains beholden to ownership, access, status, allegiance, pleasure, recognition or permission promised by the image.

This gives §4 a direct route into the later power argument:

> **psychic power is partly the power of images and local formations to arbitrate what becomes salient, desirable, credible, shameful, possible or “mine.”**

## 3.3 Complex as frozen arbitration / valuation regime

The mature synthesis can therefore describe a complex as more than “strong feeling.”

A complex is a locally stabilised field in which:

- certain distinctions recur;
- certain valuations are pre-weighted;
- some cues immediately acquire disproportionate salience;
- a once-adaptive position is re-instantiated when present conditions resemble its basin;
- the local formation can act as though it were the determining whole.

This is the psychological version of a local `1` occupying `0`.

Freedom/individuation is correspondingly **de-monopolisation**: the complex becomes one real local formation inside a wider field rather than the invisible arbiter of the field.

## 3.4 Symbol versus idol

The distinction now becomes exact:

```text
living symbol
  = image/form that carries relation to source and can transform the field

idol
  = image/form whose local arbitration exceeds its provenance and begins to perform the office of ground
```

This joins the section back to Symbolon and ahead to Trust/Power.

## 3.5 Individuation

Persona consistency can therefore be technically useful while remaining distinct from individuation.

For engineered agents, **functional/technical individuation** can be studied as coherent field-organisation across context, memory, tools, permissions, relations and returned evidence. Phenomenality remains a separate Open question.

# 8. §4 #4 — Apollo, Daphne, Dionysus, Gebser and diaphaneity

The inherited movement already carries Gebser well, but the current quilt restores the missing mythic relation.

## Apollo

Apollo carries:

- measure;
- form;
- delineation;
- illumination;
- communicability;
- the achievement of a determinate perspective.

## Dionysus

Dionysus carries:

- participation;
- ecstatic release from fixed identity;
- dissolution of local enclosure;
- return to the living field from which forms arise.

## Daphne

Daphne gives the field its critical limit:

> **illumination that becomes possession drives the living Other into withdrawal.**

This is not an ornamental third figure. It is the direct mythemic bridge between:

```text
image / desired form
        ↓
grip / possession / capture
        ↓
living Other exceeds the image
        ↓
withdrawal / transformation
```

Daphne therefore belongs with the later image/complex/Levinas relation: the Other appears through an internal image but exceeds the image through which it appears.

## Integral relation

The mature whole is:

```text
Apollo — form / measure / communicability
        ↕
Dionysus — participation / living field / dissolution of closure
        ↓
Daphne — limit against form becoming possession
        ↓
Gebserian diaphaneity
  perspective becomes transparent to context, history, purpose and co-presence
```

Integral form neither abolishes measure nor worships dissolution. Form becomes transparent to participation, and participation returns as communicable form.

### `180° → 360°`

The §3 context geometry now receives its psychological/historical sense:

- `180°` — perspectival cut, local sector, viewpoint;
- `360°` — the local perspective includes its own conditions and counter-orientations as context.

This does not create a view from nowhere. It makes finitude **diaphanous**.

### McGilchrist gate

The inherited movement correctly keeps McGilchrist as a gated witness, not foundation. His master/emissary fable contributes **asymmetry / usurpation**:

```text

        ↓
```

That is the psychological form of `1` occupying `0`.
