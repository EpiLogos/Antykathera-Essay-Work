## Recovery laws

1. **Local integrity first.** Recover a movement, argument, concept, etymological history, mytheme, theorem or other surface on its own terms before using it to support a transverse synthesis.
2. **Distinct reason with every distinction.** Record why the unit exists and what unique operation would be lost if it vanished.
3. **File identity is provisional.** A useful operation may later be split, merged, rehomed or carried without its own page.
4. **Recovered/defunct material counts as ground.** The `concepts/reference-notes/` shelf and other deprecated/orphaned surfaces are candidate-bearing provenance, not disposable noise and not automatic future nodes.
5. **Etymology is co-equal architecture input.** Recover whole word-fields, their evidence registers and their argument routes. Do not reduce them to citation support. Earth/Taste/Wisdom is authorially ratified; Arbitration is now an active foundational seam.
6. **Native QL derivation is proof ground.** When a relation depends on the theorem spine or Copula chain, recover and link the exact theorem/D-step rather than treating QL as a summary label attached after external research.
7. **Whole myths before fragments.** A mythic operation is recovered as a relational field before deciding what a movement or argument should extract from it.
8. **Evidence debt is parallel.** Citation/quotation readiness constrains external attribution; it does not veto developed authorial or native theory.
9. **The eight determinations are a dynamic transversal.** `−/− · 0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx · 1/0` are followed where they operate rather than imposed as tags.
10. **Build the graph while thinking.** Every materially recovered operation receives traversable `[[wikilinks]]` to its strongest current carriers, typed relations, provenance/source routes and an expected return/backlink. Prospective identities link to stable working headings/blocks until the ought-be architecture ratifies canonical identities.
11. **Backlinks carry meaning.** Automatic Obsidian backlinks provide retrieval; canonical rebuilding also records why the linked surfaces belong together and where each relation returns.
12. **References attach to claims.** Native derivations, internal authorial source houses, external sources, etymological relations and research propositions keep distinct provenance offices and exact links.
13. **Current terminology is dia-/syn-.** New prose uses dia-ballein and syn-ballein; the noun remains Symbolon. Legacy `sym-ballein` filenames/titles are expression debt to be migrated after architecture ratification.
