## Why this directory exists

This is **not a preservation census of existing files** and it is not a proof that the current directory layout is correct.

The inherited publication body records several stages of development at once: some formal/non-section relations were propagated, granular movements and rooms remained substantially pre-propagation, recovered/legacy material sits beside developed nodes, and the quilt/capstone contains later argument that has not yet received canonical homes.

The task here is therefore:

```text
what was developed and why
        ↕
what the six quilt surfaces subsequently generated
        ↕
what the Phase-3 / §16 capstone now adds
        ↓
complete ground for deciding what SHOULD exist
```

Historical counts of arguments, developed concepts, paths, roots, histories or etymology clusters are navigation facts only. They carry no target authority.

## Recovery laws

1. **Local integrity first.** Recover a movement, argument, concept, etymological history, mytheme, theorem or other surface on its own terms before using it to support a transverse synthesis.
2. **Distinct reason with every distinction.** Record why the unit exists and what unique operation would be lost if it vanished.
3. **File identity is provisional.** A useful operation may later be split, merged, rehomed or carried without its own page.
4. **Recovered/defunct material counts as ground.** The `concepts/reference-notes/` shelf and other deprecated/orphaned surfaces are candidate-bearing provenance, not disposable noise and not automatic future nodes.
5. **Etymology is co-equal architecture input.** Recover whole word-fields, their four evidence registers and their argument routes. Do not reduce them to citation support.
6. **Whole myths before fragments.** A mythic operation is recovered as a relational field before deciding what a movement or argument should extract from it.
7. **Evidence debt is parallel.** Citation/quotation readiness constrains external attribution; it does not veto developed authorial or native theory.
8. **The eight determinations are a dynamic transversal.** `−/− · 0/1 · ?/! · −/+ · X/x · AM/IS · ∞/dx · 1/0` are followed where they operate rather than imposed as tags.

## Known count-supersession ground

The 2026-08-18 §16 capstone explicitly supersedes old inventory counts as completion boundaries. Its own README states that the notes are working material to be quilted into concept, argument or source architecture and are not themselves authority or automatic concepts.

The final decision therefore asks of every candidate-bearing item:

> What distinct work is here, where does the final argument need that work, and what canonical relation should carry it?

That question — not the current file count — governs this recovery.
