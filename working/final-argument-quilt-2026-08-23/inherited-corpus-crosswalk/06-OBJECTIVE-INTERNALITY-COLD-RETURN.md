# 6. Movement 40 — Preference Models and the Hidden Zero

## Hidden gauge retained

The movement correctly shows that apparently neutral or self-standing preference scores rely on determinate conditions:

```text
dataset · comparison set · evaluator · reference policy · institution · labour · gauge
```

DPO supplies a strong technical example of a reference policy and unavailable/estimated reference distribution. The political/metaphysical interpretation remains the essay's.

## Power recovered

The capstone makes the deeper operation explicit:

```text
one locus determines / evaluates / permits / ranks
        ↓
another locus bears labour / resistance / cost / consequence
        ↓
can returned difference alter the determining locus?
```

Power is **differential efficacy**: the capacity of one determination to shape what may count, happen, be selected, trusted, permitted or acted upon within or across operative frames.

This is why knowledge and power meet. Knowledge is a determination achieved inside a bounded frame; power is partly the efficacy by which that determination enters the operative conditions of another/current frame.

## Trust / surety retained as distinct

Making the gauge visible improves attribution and contestability. It does not manufacture trust.

The Hephaestus/Poseidon scene remains a useful Mytheme because apparatus can detect, bind, display and price while a relation of surety still has to bear the consequence of release.

Verdict: **movement retained and deepened into Power / Delegated Labour / Return; Trust remains distinct**.

# 7. Movement 41 — Bimba and Energy-Based Fields

## Technical proposal retained, bounded

Energy-based models remain a candidate technical formalism for configuration/compatibility landscapes. LeCun et al. supplies that exact technical object. The word “energy” does not create identity with psychic, social or metaphysical efficacy.

## Bimba correction retained

```text
Bimba      = Original
Pratibimba = reflection / refraction
```

A technical world-model is therefore a reflected chart whose conditions and provenance can be explicit; it is not the Original.

## World-Atlas recovered

The stronger model architecture is:

```text
plural local charts
+ explicit conditions
+ overlap / transition relations
+ no chart as territory / Original
```

## Objective Co-Internality recovered

This is generated from the relation between finite internalities:

```text
A is locally real and does not exhaust its ground
B is locally real and is not contained by A
A and B affect one another
        ↓
their relation becomes an operative field in its own right
```

Objective Co-Internality names **relations between and around independently grounded operative frames** while retaining source, local authority, provenance, alterity and Return.

O:I later gives one technical design grammar for this through Projection / SharedField / Contribution / Encounter, but that design does not define or exhaust the essay relation.

Verdict: **movement retained; Bimba/EBM become depth; Co-Internality and World-Atlas emerge as distinct linear operations**.

# 8. Movement 42 — Six Research Vectors

## The six experiments remain useful

The inherited research vectors preserve concrete candidate tests around:

- seam retention;
- gauge disclosure;
- lens refraction;
- topological continuity;
- recursion;
- deferential return / reflexivity.

None is discarded.

## Architectural correction

T06C F14 correctly removes the **number six** from the linear burden.

The individual experiments become radial fixtures under one stronger research relation:

### Epistemic Cultivation

Ways of knowing are themselves formed fields:

```text
source · corpus · lens · annotation · evidence · uncertainty · dialogue · evaluation · return
```

They can be deliberately authored, compared, contested and revised.

### Operational Parity

If a formal or interior relation is claimed to matter technically, vary or remove it and inspect what changes.

Negative, mixed and null results are genuine returned evidence.

### Deferential Intelligence

DI remains a third distinct operation: encounter becomes real deference where returned difference can revise the model/evaluator/frame/task/future action rather than merely making the output sound cautious or agreeable.

Verdict: **six vectors preserved radial; shared linear office becomes Epistemic Cultivation / Operational Parity; DI remains distinct**.
