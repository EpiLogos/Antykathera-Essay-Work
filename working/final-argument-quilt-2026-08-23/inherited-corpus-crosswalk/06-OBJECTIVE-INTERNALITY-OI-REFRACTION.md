# 2. Objective Internality — product inheritance, not product invention

The O:I founding document says directly that the Antykathera essay develops Objective Internality and that O:I inherits that research opening.

Its technical restatement is:

```text
objective/externalisable structure
  projects · histories · sources · memories · capabilities · permissions
  human-authored orientation · runtime/material relations
        ↓ selectively becomes operative
situated act
```

The product adds three useful design dimensions:

- **continuity** — what can persist and return across acts;
- **potency** — what can become causally available;
- **authorship/orientation** — what gives purpose, authority, priority, judgement and revision conditions.

These are product-design articulations of the broader §5 field, not its metaphysical definition.

Disposition: **`design commitment / later technical articulation of already-developed argument`**.

# 3. Positional Delegation — the product makes the relation explicit

O:I's human-agency and Actuation positions articulate the same structure recovered in §5 movement 39:

```text
human writes / chooses / determines
→ durable authored ground + authority
→ relevant parts become operative
→ delegated actor exercises situated judgement
→ world resists / answers / surprises
→ evidence and difference Return
→ human recognises / refuses / redirects / revises
```

The product position is especially useful because it preserves the distinction among:

```text
authorship
≠ observation
≠ generated interpretation
≠ retrieval
≠ permission
≠ returned proposal
```

This is a concrete design grammar for **Positional Delegation**.

It does not make the human metaphysical Zero. The founding positions themselves describe human authorship as one causally privileged source relation inside a wider World whose histories and conditions the human also inherits.

Disposition: **`strong design refraction of Authored Ground / Positional Delegation`**.

# 4. Power — “downward authority requires upward reality”

O:I's conjugate `2′` position gives the product-side reason for the essay's Power operation.

It locates power in the distribution of:

- actuation capacity;
- tool/material reach;
- authority;
- persistence/memory;
- source standing;
- ranking/surfacing;
- disclosure of authored material;
- delegation;
- evidence return;
- capacity to revise future conditions.

The decisive relation is:

> **downward authority requires upward reality.**

Delegation creates epistemic distance:

```text
determining locus
  gives purpose / scope / permission / authority
        ↓
labouring / delegated locus
  meets resistance / missing knowledge / consequence / unforeseen possibility
        ↓
Return path
  lets encountered reality alter the governing understanding
```

This is a strong technical/design refraction of **Power / Delegated Labour / Return** because it states why the return path exists, not merely that a logging mechanism should exist.

Disposition: **`authored design commitment / later product office for Power`**.

# 5. Objective Co-Internality — relation among differentiated Worlds

The O:I founding position and dedicated Objective Co-Internality contract both preserve the exact relation the capstone independently earned:

> one grounded World can become meaningfully available within another while both retain history, source authority, local organisation and capacity for Return.

The product grammar is:

```text
Self's operative world
→ selective Projection / Contribution
→ SharedField
→ mediated Encounter
→ Other's operative world changes
→ attributable difference returns
```

Important product non-identities include:

```text
Projection ≠ source
Contribution ≠ source object
SharedField ≠ Context
SharedField ≠ WikiSpace
Encounter ≠ subjective experience
ranking / metric ≠ canonical truth
```

These are technically useful because they preserve relation without converting the shared mediation layer into a super-world or super-subject.

This is one later implementation/design possibility for Objective Co-Internality. It does not exhaust the concept.

Disposition: **`strong design/contract refraction of independently earned Co-Internality`**.

O:I's dedicated epistemic-cultivation note states the cultural/design move plainly:

> epistemologies themselves can be authored, compared, contested, cultivated and returned.

The product field includes:

- source/corpus selection;

The circuit is:


The significant distinction is provenance: an epistemic artifact is intentionally formed and later made causally operative; it should not be redescribed as though the model spontaneously generated its entire epistemic environment.

# 7. Operational Parity — product-side falsifiability discipline

O:I's founding `4′` position says that a claimed archetypal/formal relation should make an operational difference if it genuinely organises the system.

This is the product meaning of **Operational Parity**:

```text
formal relation claimed
→ implement / vary / remove
→ inspect behaviour / composition / evidence / recurrence / interpretation
→ accept positive / negative / mixed / null return
→ revise correspondence where required
```

The product text explicitly preserves the possibility that:

- two formally different structures are operationally equivalent;
- a conventional recurrence outperforms a QL recurrence;
- a QL structure improves explanation without improving execution.

This makes Operational Parity a safeguard against turning QL into decorative inevitability.

Disposition: **`strong authored research discipline / later product experiment office`**.

# 8. Six centres — responsibility field, not ontology

Both O:I founding positions and README explicitly say the six product centres are:

- native instruments;
- centres of responsibility;
- independently useful;
- interoperable with heterogeneous alternatives;
- **not six mandatory boxes every installation must contain**.

Therefore the six product positions may later provide a particularly rich technical field for QL/Context-Frame reading, but they cannot be used as proof that §5 or technological agency “is sixfold.”

A correct relation is:

```text
already-developed formal / epistemic relation
        ↓ optional QL reading
six differentiated product responsibilities
        ↓ implementation and evidence
returned comparison
```

Native 026 will later test Context-Frame relational health across the essay without importing this product sixfold backward.

Disposition: **`bounded QL-informed product field / never ontological proof`**.
