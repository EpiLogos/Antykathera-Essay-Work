# 1. What §1 is here and why it exists

§0/1 provisionally used `0` as a meta-sign for the condition that cannot be counted as one more ordinary object. §0 then showed how a bounded, differentiated local world becomes operative. §1 now asks how **Zero itself becomes historically and formally admitted inside systems of inscription and calculation without becoming an ordinary positive magnitude**.

Its six-step movement is:

```text
absence / empty place becomes positionally legible
        ↓
zero becomes an arithmetic participant
        ↓
formal emptiness becomes a generator of one / succession
        ↓
the mark-and-ground relation receives a compact sign: Ø
        ↓
1/0 exposes the law / limit / containing structure
        ↓
0/1 ↔ 1/0 is earned as the native ground–mark / return orientation
        ↓
§2 asks what logics arise when difference is accounted from that relation
```

The station is therefore a **genealogy of operative zero** and the formal-historical advent of the relation later carried by QL. It is not the terminal ethical/technical Return of §5→0.

- quotient / covering constructions;
- totalised algebras such as wheels/meadows/transreal approaches;
- differential limiting;
- zero/infinity as recurrent pressure-points.

Its cross-case proposition is **Argued**, while each mathematical construction is Derived only in its own formal system.

The method can be stated without inflation:

```text
relation obstructed under current laws
        ↓
obstruction identifies what the current structure preserves
        ↓
formalism either refuses expression
OR
changes / enlarges / redefines containing space
        ↓
new relation becomes legible under declared new laws
```

This is a genuine mathematical neighbour of the QL context relation. The QL `1/0` is **not ordinary division by zero**; it is the oriented meta-sign for determinate form returning toward ground/condition and thereby exposing the frame under which the determination held.

This is also where the new Arbitration/Con-text field has a precise mathematical precursor: the exceptional expression does not decide its own meaning. The **containing structure / criterion** determines which operations count, and the exceptional case forces that criterion/context into view.
