# Ought-Be Etymology Architecture

Etymology has its own first-class office. Its canonical unit is the **whole relational field**, not the isolated word. Arguments and Concepts consume exact operations from that field without turning lexical history into theorem proof or turning every fertile word into a canonical Concept.

## Two identities on every use

Every architectural link into Etymology must name:

```text
whole field
+
exact consumed operation / term / relation
+
evidence register
```

The four evidence registers remain:

1. **Philological descent** — documented derivation/cognacy/morphology/history.
2. **Attested semantic field** — historically recorded senses without asserting one hidden essence.
3. **Operational homology** — the same named operation compared across distinct fields; no claim of common descent or doctrinal identity.
4. **Poetic / phonic re-entry** — authorial sound, pun, graphic or verbal return; never historical proof.

No register may borrow the warrant of another.

## Canonical mature fields and their Argument/Concept consumers

**Constraint.** This field does not license invented etymologies for `world`, `agent`, `internality` or other live Concept terms.

## Non-load-bearing / pending field

`earth-taste-wisdom` remains a proposal pending authorial verification. It may stay visible as a research field but must not enter the canonical Argument/Concept dependency field as lexical warrant until authorially resolved.

## Tree morphology and recursion

The archaeology keeps two compatible forms of depth:

```text
root → trunk → ring → branch → leaf → fruit/seed → 5→0
```

for historical development, and

```text
N / N′ → (N+N′)
```

for genuinely generated conjugate form. `6×6` is distributive recursion, not a synonym for `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`. `3:3` and `4:2` are readings that make different relations salient; neither is an arithmetic proof of a lexical field.

## Argument/Concept consumption contract

A canonical consumer must record, for example:

```text
Etymology whole: arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis
Operation: Arbitration-in-Crisis → Hybris
Register: attested hybris field + authorial QL appointment
Use: A24 / relevant Concept(s) / exact Movement
Return: Regard → Anamnesis reopens and returns the determination
```

A bare `[[Hybris]]` link is insufficient where the argument depends on the generated relation.

## Rebuild consequence

Do not explode mature fields into one-page-per-word canonical fragments. Local word records may exist for source work, but the whole-field page remains the source of relational meaning. Rebuild reciprocal routes from each field to Concepts, Arguments, Movements, Mythemes and histories; let final Symbolon pages consume those exact routes later.

Depth Restoration must reopen the actual lexical/source research behind any load-bearing etymological use rather than expanding a concise Concept/Argument definition by improvisation.
