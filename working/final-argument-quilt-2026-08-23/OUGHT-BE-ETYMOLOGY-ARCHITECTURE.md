# Ought-Be Etymology Architecture

Etymology is already structurally closest to its ought-be form. Its canonical unit is the **whole relational field**, not the isolated word. #39 therefore determines how Argument and Concept consume the field without turning lexical history into theorem proof or reducing a mature constellation to one evocative gloss.

## Two identities on every use

Every architectural link into Etymology must name:

```text
whole field
+
exact consumed operation / term / relation
+
evidence register
```

The four evidence registers remain:

1. **Philological descent** — documented derivation/cognacy/morphology/history.
2. **Attested semantic field** — historically recorded senses without asserting one hidden essence.
3. **Operational homology** — the same named operation compared across distinct fields; no claim of common descent or doctrinal identity.
4. **Poetic / phonic re-entry** — authorial sound, pun, graphic or verbal return; never historical proof.

No register may borrow the warrant of another.

## Canonical mature fields and theorem offices

### Encounter / Region / Name / Count / Countenance / Account

**Whole office.** Language enters as encounter becoming differentiable, nameable, reckonable and answerable without forgetting the prior region/with-relation.

**Theorem consumers.** CT-01, CT-03, CT-04, CT-06.

**Exact operations.** `/` as differentiating relation; name as pruning/differentiating cut; counter-facing; fitting/reckoning; countenance/domain; telling/gathering; `1/0` return. Naming and Logos are read as neighbouring directions of one differentiating/accounting operation where the local source warrants it.

**Constraint.** This field does not license invented etymologies for `world`, `agent`, or `internality`.

### Arbitration / Hybris / Regard / Anamnesis

**Whole office.** The ratified exemplar of conjugate generation:

```text
6                                  6′
#0 Continuity                      Indeterminacy
#1 Criterion                       Distinction
#2 Delineation                     Difference
#3 Arbitration                     Crisis
#4 Con-text                        Diaphaneity
#5 Resolution                      Reconciliation
```

Generated relations:

```text
#0 Continuity-in-Indeterminacy     → Origin / unmeasurable
#1 Criterion-through-Distinction   → Measure / Limit
#2 Delineation-through-Difference  → Perspective / Frame
#3 Arbitration-in-Crisis           → Decision / Hybris
#4 Con-text-through-Diaphaneity    → Regard
#5 Resolution-in-Reconciliation    → Anamnesis / Recognition / Return
```

**Theorem consumers.** CT-02, CT-04, CT-06.

**Exact operations.** bounded criterion; situated delineation; consequential arbitration; Hybris as local measure occupying source-office; Regard as contextual reopening; Anamnesis as provenance-bearing return.

**Source boundary.** *arbiter/arbitrari*, *krinein/krisis*, *hybris*, *regard*, and *anamnesis* have mixed lexical readiness. The project definition of Hybris is authorial even where the historical word-field is attested.

### Fides / Topos / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere

**Whole office.** Trust becoming an articulated, instituted common world and returning through entrustment:

```text
Fides → Topos → Logos → Nomos → Natio → Credere → Fides
```

Operationally: trust → emplacement → articulation → arbitration/distribution → belonging → entrustment → trust.

**Theorem consumers.** CT-02 and CT-06; CT-03 at Logos/Symbolon; CT-05 at `5→0` return.

**Exact operations.** `Topos → Logos → Nomos → Natio` is the historical fourfold within the sixfold; `Credere → Fides` is the return. The political-theological seam consumes source/covenant/standing/mediating-office/fidelity-or-usurpation from this field and its histories.

**Source boundary.** The sixfold is the project's historical-philosophical composition. Individual word histories require their own lexical warrant.

### Symbol / Account / Trust

**Whole office.** Separated token, mark, telling/reckoning, record, claim, credit and circulation remain answerable to the relation that gives the sign standing.

**Theorem consumers.** CT-03 and CT-06, with CT-01 as root relation.

**Exact operations.** broken token/relation; account as reckoning and telling; credit/standing; circulation without self-grounding.

### Homologia / Analogia

**Whole office.** Comparison is disciplined by the kind of relation actually claimed.

**Theorem consumers.** all cross-register projection, especially CT-03 and CT-05.

**Exact operation.** recurring operation does not imply common descent, doctrinal identity, or object identity. This field is the lexical Episteme guard for the Symbolon→Matheme/Mytheme/Episteme projection.

### Apportionment / Economy

**Whole office.** shares, value, naming/number, coinage, obligation and oikonomia under historically situated distribution.

**Theorem consumers.** CT-06 and §5 hidden-zero/common-world applications.

**Exact operation.** a measure/value system allocates standing and consequence; its source and arbitration must remain inspectable.

## Non-load-bearing / pending field

`earth-taste-wisdom` remains a proposal pending authorial verification. It may stay visible as a research field but must not enter #39 theorem dependencies as lexical warrant.

## Tree morphology and recursion

The archaeology keeps two compatible forms of depth:

```text
root → trunk → ring → branch → leaf → fruit/seed → 5→0
```

for historical development, and

```text
N / N′ → (N+N′)
```

for genuinely generated conjugate form. `6×6` is distributive recursion, not a synonym for `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)`. `3:3` and `4:2` are readings that make different relations salient; neither is an arithmetic proof of the field.

## Argument/Concept consumption contract

A canonical consumer must record, for example:

```text
Etymology whole: arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis
Operation: Arbitration-in-Crisis → Hybris
Register: attested hybris field + authorial QL appointment
Use: CT-06 / Argument 18 / relevant Movement
Return: Regard → Anamnesis reopens and returns the determination
```

A bare `[[Hybris]]` link is insufficient where the theorem depends on the generated relation.

## Rebuild consequence

Do not explode mature fields into one-page-per-word canonical fragments. Local word records may exist for source work, but the whole-field page remains the source of relational meaning. Rebuild reciprocal routes from each field to Concepts, Arguments, Movements, Mythemes and histories; let the final Symbolon pages consume those exact routes later.
