# Lexical Constellation and Relational Operators

This ledger separates three things that the recovered corpus often places close together: a word-history, an authorial constellation, and a relational operator. Their proximity can be generative; it is not evidence that they are identical.

## Register law

For every lexical relation ask:

```text
What term or field is present?
Why does the essay need it?
What operation does it perform here?
Which neighbour clarifies that operation?
What evidential register warrants the relation?
What does the operation return into?
```

The four Etymology registers remain philological descent, attested semantic field, operational homology, and poetic/phonic re-entry. A relation may be exact and useful in the latter two without becoming a historical derivation.

## Root lexical constellation

Native 027 returns the authorial constellation:

```text
axis
→ symbol
→ sumbolon
→ mythos
→ epos
→ etymon
→ logos
→ apeiron
→ horizōn
```

This is a **constellation of operative neighbours**, not a proposed chain of common etymological descent. Its architectural use is to keep orientation, joining, image/story, saying, word-history, articulation/account, indefiniteness and limit/horizon available around the same problem of determination and return.

At rebuild time each local use must name which relation is doing work. `symbol → sumbolon`, for example, may open the broken-token/joining history; `apeiron → horizōn` may open the limit field; neither permits the entire constellation to be cited as one lexical proof.

The secondary `{prei, Priya, Kratya, Trita}` constellation remains a recovered authorial/poetic field. `prei` is not to be detached and canonicalised as an independent lexical theorem. The current rough offices — epistemic-explicit / Sensual / tacit — remain comparative orientation only until their local source and authorial standing are made exact. `Sensual` is a conceptual office, not a lexical translation.

## Relational operators

### `/` — living mediation

`/` is the parent relational operator of the essay. It can function as distinction, ratio, division, mediation and the site through which AND/OR structure becomes explicit. Its meaning is determined by the terms and Context Frame it relates.

Architectural rule: never gloss `/` once globally and then infer every local use from the gloss. Record the local position, complement, conjugacy, nesting and Return.

### `0/1` and `1/0` — orientation and return

`0/1` carries unobjectifiable ground / determinate mark through the slash. `1/0` is the obverse movement by which achieved determination turns toward the condition it cannot possess. They are orientations of one relation, not two independent substances.

Consumers: CT-01, CT-02, CT-04, CT-05.

### `dia` — determination through distinction

`dia` is the differentiating operation: a field becomes articulable through cuts, exclusions, contrasts, perspectives and marks. Apoha supplies one epistemic determination of this operation.

Failure charge: severance — the cut forgets the relation/field that made it possible.

### `syn` / `sym` — relational gathering without fusion

`syn` / `sym` names gathering-together while differences remain. In the Symbolon field it supplies the return pressure of *sym-ballein*: separated determinations can be held in one relation without cancellation.

Failure charge: fusion / counterfeit gathering — the whole is declared by erasing differences or the source of relation.

`dia` and `syn` are conjugate operations. Neither is a moral good or bad term by itself.

### `name ↔ count ↔ account`

The mature encounter field distinguishes:

```text
encounter / with
→ region
→ name / differentiating cut
→ counter-facing
→ count / fitting / reckoning
→ countenance / personed domain
→ account / telling + reckoning
→ return
```

A name and an account are neighbouring expressions of determination: one differentiates enough to call; the other articulates enough to answer. Count mediates measure and recurrence. This relation supports CT-03 and CT-06 but does not reduce language to arithmetic.

### `criterion → delineation → arbitration → con-text → resolution`

The ratified arbitration field keeps these offices distinct. Criterion establishes an according-to-which; delineation gives a situated edge; arbitration makes a consequential cut in crisis; con-text reopens the decision to its bearing field; resolution can return through reconciliation.

The generated conjugates are the operative forms:

```text
Criterion-through-Distinction
Delineation-through-Difference
Arbitration-in-Crisis
Con-text-through-Diaphaneity
Resolution-in-Reconciliation
```

Hybris, Regard and Anamnesis are semantic flowerings of generated relations, not replacements for positions.

### `Fides → ... → Credere → Fides`

```text
Fides → Topos → Logos → Nomos → Natio → Credere → Fides
```

Trust is prior relational standing. Topos emplaces; Logos articulates; Nomos distributes/institutes standing; Natio gathers/narrates belonging; Credere entrusts toward a future return. The cycle is a project composition over separately warranted word-fields.

### `source → mediation → return`

The political/theological seam has one governing operator:

```text
source / divine relation
→ covenant / primary arbitration
→ standing / obligation / law / peoplehood
→ mediating office
→ interpretation / execution
→ fidelity to source OR usurpation of source-office
→ return / renewed arbitration
```

The mediating office may be priest, king, politician or another historically situated carrier. The theorem concerns answerability of office to source, not a timeless equivalence among those offices.

## Comparison operators

Cross-register projection must use explicit comparison operators rather than adjacency:

| Operator | Claim made | Claim not made |
|---|---|---|
| `derives from` | a documented or internally demonstrated dependence | resemblance |
| `attests` | a source bears the stated historical/lexical fact | theorem proof beyond the source |
| `instantiates` | a local case performs the already defined operation | identity with every other case |
| `formalises` | notation makes an operation exact in its declared formal domain | metaphysical proof by notation |
| `images` / `performs` | a Mytheme carries the same operation in imaginal form | equation between image and Matheme |
| `operationally homologous` | the same named operation recurs across distinct materials | common descent or doctrine |
| `resonant with` | a bounded neighbour illuminates a relation | derivation |
| `returns to` | a determination becomes answerable to source/context and can revise its field | simple reversal |

These operators are the connective tissue of the rebuilt wiki. They are not decoration: they state the relation that makes each link meaningful.

## Anti-collapse rules

- A root does not dictate a theorem.
- A rhyme is not a root.
- A mathematical likeness is not an ontological identity.
- A Mytheme performs; it does not prove.
- An Argument claims; a Concept keeps a reusable distinction; an Etymology field preserves lexical/historical depth.
- A local Bimba office does not become the metaphysical Original.
- A compressed agent sentence does not become a theorem or aphorism because it sounds memorable.
- `world`, `agent`, `internality`, `commons` and similar live Concept terms receive etymological treatment only when the actual lexical field earns it.

The purpose of the constellation is to increase relational precision: every link should say what kind of joining is occurring and what difference survives it.
