# 1. Linear-essential — current burden

The bounded inference presently requires the following operations to be intelligible in sequence.

## Opening / Subject / formal limit

- experience / appearing as received ground;
- Subject as defined indefinability / unobjectifiable determining pole;
- faithful definition and formal limit;
- promissory `0/1` relation.

Reason: the whole essay depends on distinguishing the condition of determination from another determined object.

## Advent Zero / symbolic-formal threshold

- absence/place becoming operative sign;
- zero and one as differentiated formal terms;
- Primordial Symbolon: determination remains related to the ground it does not exhaust.

Reason: the essay's formal language must emerge historically and conceptually rather than appear as free-floating notation.

## Dia / Syn / Return

- differentiation is necessary and not identical with severance;
- retained relation/provenance/composition is equally necessary;
- failed Dia and counterfeit Syn remain distinct pathologies;
- achieved determination can return without being annihilated.

Reason: this is the living process grammar used throughout formal, psychic, technical and institutional registers.

## QL / Matheme core

- sixfold positional body versus eight-determination traversal;
- First/Second Spanda and `4+2` under exact proof boundaries;
- P4 contextual aperture / Context-Frame recursion where needed;
- atlas / transition relation as exact neighbour for plural finite charts.

Reason: enough formal architecture must be visible for later MEF / Objective Internality claims to be more than metaphors.

The full mature QL mathematical corpus is not linear-essential.

## Psychoid / image / individuation hinge

- psychoid problem as non-reductive psyche–matter ordering question;
- `X/x` determining field / local manifestation;
- image / complex / symbol as efficacious local formations;
- individuation as local definiteness plus increased transparency to wider relation;
- diaphaneity / World-Picture → World-Atlas.

Reason: the formal relation must become psychologically and epistemically consequential before technical intelligence appears.

## MEF theory-first

- source / lens-position / transformation / divergence / limit / return;
- situated accounts become transparent to their conditions of production;
- MEF gathers prior stations rather than beginning as an AI method.

Reason: §4 needs a genuine theory of situated self-disclosing knowing.

## Objective Internality

- subject / mediation / object distinction through pramātṛ / pramāṇa / prameya;
- finite operative internality is real and inspectable without settling phenomenality;
- source/context/excluded field remain constitutive of determination.

Reason: this is the bridge that allows human and artificial minds to be discussed in one epistemic grammar without ontological flattening.

## Delegation / Power / Return

- source-position grants bounded authority;
- determination becomes efficacious across loci;
- consequences are borne somewhere;
- returned difference must be capable of revising the determining locus.

Reason: technical intelligence becomes politically and institutionally real here.

## Objective Co-Internality / World-Atlas

- independently grounded internalities remain locally real and non-contained by one another;
- relations among them form operative fields;
- their actions can co-produce common conditions;
- no local chart/institution becomes Original.

Reason: Agentworld and the commons now expose this as a world-forming relation, not merely interoperability.

## Terminal Commons / Trust / Order of Dependence / Return of Zero

- shared world must hold one/common and many/local without monopoly or fragmentation;
- trust/surety remains wider than observability;
- increasing objective completeness reopens rather than closes the order-of-dependence question;
- Reflective Field / Mirror That Moves First makes representations causally active and therefore answerable;
- terminal `1/0` returns achieved determination to ground/source/Other/consequence.

Reason: this is the actual telos of the essay rather than a product crescendo.

# 6. Handoff to #39

#39 should derive final node identities from **operations plus burden office**, not from existing filenames.

For every proposed node ask:

- must the reader traverse this to follow the bounded inference? → `linear-essential`;
- should the reader encounter it visibly because it concretises/tests the inference? → `supporting-visible`;
