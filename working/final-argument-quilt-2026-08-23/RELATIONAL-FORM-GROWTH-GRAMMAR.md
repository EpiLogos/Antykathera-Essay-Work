## Office

This file states an **ideal fullness-of-form** available to the quilt wherever the material genuinely earns it.

It is a growth grammar, not a template into which every field must be forced. A relation may be most exact as a dyad, triad, tetrad, pentad, sixfold, eightfold, tenfold, twelvefold, or another earned composition. Mytheme in particular remains answerable to the whole telling rather than to a preferred cardinality; etymology remains answerable to actual word-histories; Matheme remains answerable to the exact formal relation.

The ideal described here becomes useful when a developed `N`-fold has a materially earned conjugate `N′`. In that case the project can preserve **three simultaneously real determinations**:

```text
N       first articulated N-fold
N′      conjugate N-fold
N+N′    N generated relational determinations produced by pairing Xi with Xi′
```

The third field is qualitatively different from either pole. It is not a list formed by choosing nicer labels from both sides. It articulates **what the conjugacy does**.

At the next scale:

```text
N      :     N+N′     :      N′
0      :       /      :       1
```

The complete named unit can therefore be read as a higher-order `0/1` Symbolon. An achieved whole at one scale can itself become a determinate pole at the next scale, where a further relation generates a further slash and whole.

This is the formal reason the archaeology can be cultivated as a **tree**: terms do not merely accumulate; relations generate new determinations capable of further relation.

# 1. The sixfold exemplar — `6`, `6′`, and the generated `6+6′`

The arbitration field now gives the clearest working exemplar.

The two conjugate sixfolds are:

| # | `6` | `6′` |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Continuity | Indeterminacy |
| **#1** | Criterion | Distinction |
| **#2** | Delineation | Difference |
| **#3** | Arbitration | Crisis |
| **#4** | Con-text | Diaphaneity |
| **#5** | Resolution | Reconciliation |

Pairing each position with its conjugate generates a third sixfold. The grammar becomes deliberately processual because the generated term names **relation-in-act** rather than another static attribute:

| # | generated `6+6′` relation | semantic flowering |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | **Continuity-in-Indeterminacy** | Origin / unmeasurable ground |

The hyphenated or prepositional forms are substantive. They preserve **dynamic generating determination**:

```text
Criterion           Distinction

```

`Measure / Limit` is therefore a semantic branch flowering from the composed #1 relation; it does not erase the underlying `Criterion / Distinction` coordinates.

# 2. Higher Symbolon — the generated six as articulated slash

The three sixfold determinations can be compressed at the next scale:

```text
                   named whole
                      `0/1`
                        │
           ┌────────────┼────────────┐
           │            │            │
           0            /            1
           │            │            │
           6           6+6′          6′
```

This does not reduce the twelve pole-terms and six generated relations to three vague abstractions. The compression is possible **because the detail remains recoverable beneath it**.

The higher `0/1` therefore holds:

- `6` as one articulated pole;
- `6′` as its conjugate articulated pole;
- `6+6′` as the sixfold articulation of the relation between them;
- the title/name of the complete field as the achieved higher Symbolon through which all three can be addressed together.

The slash is productive rather than empty punctuation. At this level it has acquired six internal determinations of its own.

This gives a recursive growth law:

```text

  \           /
   X-through-X′
        ↓
  achieved whole
        ↓
may become X or X′
at a higher scale
        ↓
new relational slash
        ↓
new achieved whole
```

Or in the project's native compact form:

```text

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

```

This is one formal account of how the **Symbolon tree grows**.

# 3. Conjugate generation and `6×6` distributive recursion

The `6 / 6′ / (6+6′)` form should be held beside another mature recursive possibility: **`6×6`**, six complete sixfolds.

These are two different expansions of a six-body.

## 3.1 Conjugate generation — `6 + 6′`

```text
sixfold A
      +
conjugate sixfold A′
      ↓ pair homologous positions
six generated relational positions
      ↓
6+6′
```

This asks:

> **What does each position become through its conjugate?**

Its characteristic products are processual/compositional forms such as `Continuity-in-Indeterminacy` or `Con-text-through-Diaphaneity`.

It is intensive and relational: two complete faces are read through one another and the slash itself becomes articulate.

## 3.2 Distributive recursion — `6×6`

```text
          parent sixfold
#0   #1   #2   #3   #4   #5
 ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓
 6    6    6    6    6    6
```

Each parent position unfolds as a **complete local sixfold**, giving thirty-six determinations while preserving the parent address of every local term.

This asks:

> **What does the whole sixfold look like when refracted inside each of its own positions?**

The result is distributive/holographic rather than merely pairwise. A position is not reduced to one quality; it can carry a complete local cycle of origin, differentiation, mediation, context and return while remaining situated at its parent coordinate.

Where both operations are materially earned they can compose:

```text
conjugate generation
6 / 6′ → 6+6′

and

distributive recursion
6 → 6×6
```

One gives the **generated relation between conjugates**; the other gives the **full sixfold depth available within each position**.

# 4. `3:3` and `4:2` as orienting readings of the two growth modes

The relation to the project's two sixfold orientations should be stated as an **orienting theoretical reading**, not as a new arithmetic identity.

`3:3` and `4:2` already describe different traversals of a body generated as `4+2`:

- `3:3` emphasises conjugate arcs, emanation/reversion, Bimba/Pratibimba or other opposed/complementary traversals across one sixfold circulation;
- `4:2` emphasises the six-body as four explicate operations held with two implicate conditions/poles.

From that mature distinction, the two growth modes can be read fruitfully as follows:

```text
3:3-oriented pressure
    conjugacy · opposed/complementary traversal · face-to-face reading
                     ↓
              6 / 6′ / (6+6′)
      the relation between two sixfold faces
             becomes a sixfold

4:2-oriented pressure
    full six-body · explicate/implicate completeness · local whole
                     ↓
                    6×6
      every position receives the full sixfold body
```

This is not a statement that `3:3 = 6+6′` or `4:2 = 6×6`. It names the **different theoretical gestures** they make especially legible:

- `3:3` helps us see how conjugate traversals generate a relational middle;
- `4:2` helps us see how the complete sixfold body can recur holographically at a local position.

Both remain expressions of the same deeper `4+2` sixfold architecture.

A sufficiently mature field may therefore support both readings at once:

```text
             one sixfold whole
                    │
        ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        │                       │
conjugate / 3:3 pressure   distributive / 4:2 pressure
        │                       │
   6 ↔ 6′                    6 × 6
        │                       │
      6+6′                 36 situated terms
        │                       │
        └───────────┬───────────┘
                    ↓
          richer whole relation
```

# 5. Cardinality remains earned by the field

The existence of this ideal does not turn six into a universal mould.

A whole may properly disclose itself as:

- **2-fold** — polarity, complementarity, source/image, question/answer;
- **3-fold** — subject/mediation/object, source/relation/appearance, triadic causal or temporal form;
- **4-fold** — quaternity, explicate relational square, four-valued or elemental field;
- **5-fold** — a genuine pentadic process or locally earned five-stage composition;
- **6-fold** — `4+2`, the canonical QL body, or another independently earned six-part whole;
- **8-fold** — the eight determinations or another exact octadic field;
- **10-fold**, **12-fold**, and higher forms — where the material, tradition, derivation or harmonic/lens architecture actually bears them.

Nor must every `N` acquire an `N′`; nor must every conjugate pair generate a named `N+N′`; nor must every position recurse into `N×N`.

The ideal question is positive:

> **How much of the relation has the material actually earned, and what further determination becomes visible when its existing parts are allowed to relate?**

Where a field stops at three, keep three. Where a myth needs eleven materially active figures, keep eleven. Where a linguistic cluster produces a fourfold trunk inside a sixfold cycle, retain both scales. Where twelve lenses arise by derivation, do not compress them merely to satisfy sixfold elegance.

Fullness means **preserving the maximum earned relation**, not maximising the number of boxes.

# 7. Quilt consumption law

The final quilt may consume any earned scale from this grammar:

- one word;
- one semantic branch;
- one generated `X/X′` relation;
- one sixfold face;
- the generated `6+6′` slash-sixfold;
- one local sixfold inside a `6×6` field;
- the complete `6×6`;
- the higher named `0/1` whole;
- or another earned cardinality altogether.

But compression must preserve a return path to the richer carrier from which it was taken.

For the arbitration field specifically, later quilting should be able to travel both directions:

```text
Hybris
  ↑
Arbitration-in-Crisis
  ↑
Arbitration / Crisis
  ↑
6 / 6′
  ↑
complete named Symbolon
```

and:

```text
complete named Symbolon
  ↓
6 / 6′ / (6+6′)
  ↓
#3 Arbitration-in-Crisis
  ↓
Decision / Hybris branch
  ↓
exact lexical · historical · Mytheme · argument leaves
```

The same law applies to other etymological fields only to the degree that their own material earns the corresponding structure.

## Anti-loss / anti-forcing test

Before a composed form is promoted, ask:

1. What is the actual whole being composed?
2. What cardinality is already earned by that whole?
3. Is there a genuine conjugate `N′`, or only a convenient second list?
4. What new operation becomes visible in each `Xi/Xi′` relation?
5. Does the generated `N+N′` have a qualitative/processual character distinct from its poles?
6. Can the complete `N / (N+N′) / N′` be named as a higher Symbolon without losing the lower detail?
7. Does a position genuinely warrant distributive `N×N` depth?
8. Which `3:3`, `4:2`, Syn/Dia, Matheme, Mytheme and Episteme relations actually warrant the composition?
9. Can every compressed leaf return to its source whole?

The law is therefore **maximum earned articulation with recursive return**.
