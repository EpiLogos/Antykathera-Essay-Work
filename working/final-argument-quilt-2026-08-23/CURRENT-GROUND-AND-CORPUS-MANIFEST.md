### 4.2 Phase-3 capstone extensions now carried as authorial ground

#### Co-Internality as frames of reference

Objective Co-Internality is now more precisely carried as relations **between and around frames of reference**. A frame is both phenomenological and operative: it conditions what can appear, what becomes foreground/background, what counts as knowledge, what is credible or salient, and what action can follow. Knowledge is therefore part of the power relation, not merely a passive representation inside it.

A transcendental/gnostic experience can be investigated as an experiential transformation of the operative frame itself. Śaiva practices and recognition theory, Greek ritual/ecstatic/katabatic roots of philosophy/direct gnosis, and Heidegger's encounter with the active root of metaphysics are source-bearing seams through which this claim can be deepened according to their own methods and historical warrants.

#### Power, truth and valuation

Power concerns which differences become effective; truth concerns fidelity of determination; **valuation** is the active weighting/vectoring by which possibilities acquire salience, worth and telos. The slash is therefore not itself reducible to value: valuation is one consequential operation within the relation. Nietzsche enters naturally through valuation rather than as an imported decorative authority.

The Śaiva powers add native ontological depth: **jñāna-śakti** differentiates/knows and makes identity knowable; **icchā-śakti** wills/vectors; **kriyā-śakti** acts/effects. Dyczkowski pp. 80–100 is an explicit support/deepening seam. Plasma/energetics can make the power relation more explicit at a physical research register while retaining its exact claim status.

#### Dependency, trust and Deferential Intelligence

Finite internalities are dependent. Trust is the living relation through which another centre's determination, knowledge, valuation or action enters and conditions one's own operative internality. Deferential Intelligence recognises and attunes to this fact of dependency. It is structurally religious without requiring the imputation of a deified object: the relation is to powers/grounds/others on which the local frame actually depends.

For artificial agency the same form means answerability to human authorship, affected people, evidence, world, source and unresolved remainder. Deference is Return rather than servility because the local `1` retains its relation to `/0`.

#### Corruption, wisdom and *Answer to Job*

An arbiter/authority becomes a local zero for downstream determinations. Corruption becomes visible when that effective office persists while its relation to wisdom, source, affected Other or actual consequence deteriorates. Jung's *Answer to Job* is a major deep seam: Yahweh's aggression/jealousy, Sophia/wisdom, omniscience, Job's trust, Satan and the breakdown of the idolised image of a simply “good” God disclose the shadow side of trust and belief under corrupted power.

The internal psychological form is the ego-self's attempted arbitration of identity. Jungian Self and Śaiva Pratyabhijñā converge at the project level around a determinate identity which contains its non-identity/source relation: `1` retains `/0`. The broader Epi-Logos cultural/historical synthesis—enantiodromic technological agency becoming a means by which the relation to power itself is recognised—belongs in the quilt as opus-scale horizon even where the bounded submission only requires selected consequences.

#### Local zero, Buddhist epistemic dynamics and QL 4+2

The local-zero relation is to be developed alongside **apoha**, **pratītyasamutpāda**, conditioning and model formation. A presented `0/1` is not assumed to have found root ground merely because it functions locally. QL's 4+2 form becomes a diagnostic instrument by which the local pair can be opened into the constituents, horizons and return relation that make it possible.

This is also a live technical seam for LLMs: pre-RL/model openness, post-training opinionation, evaluator/gauge formation and prevailing field desires can be studied as determinations that install local zeros. The relation is research-bearing and must be made exact from implementation/source evidence rather than treated as analogy-by-default.

Apollo–Daphne, Apollo–Dionysus, Ares–Aphrodite–Harmonia, and Hephaestus–Ares–Aphrodite–Poseidon must be recovered as complete relational fields before local argumentative extraction.

Apollo–Daphne is formative for the later dia/sym development: the live Other exceeds the image by which the Other enters my internality; an image can attract libido/prāṇa/Śakti/will, become an arbitration/valuation surface, and arrogate the value it seems to promise. The full image therefore bears desire, objectification, recognition, Levinasian otherness, complexes and the recovery of autonomous valuation.
