# 4. Relational wiki law for Phase 3

The final wiki/page field should be largely **extrudable from the relations built during quilting**. Recovery work therefore has to build the graph while it thinks.

## 4.1 Every recovered operation gets a traversable carrier

For each material operation recovered from a movement, argument, concept, etymology, theorem, mytheme, source, quilt or capstone:

1. link the strongest **existing carrier** with a real `[[wikilink]]`;
2. link the exact **derivational/source carrier** where one exists;
3. name the relation the target performs;
4. identify the section/movement or transverse thread it serves;
5. record the inverse/return route expected of the eventual canonical target;
6. preserve its current relation-state (`present`, `partial`, `prospective`, `misplaced`, etc.).

## 4.2 Prospective identities are linked without prematurely freezing filenames

A newly discovered operation may deserve a future argument/concept/etymology/root/page, but T06D does not yet know its final canonical identity.

Until architecture ratification, link it to a **stable working ledger heading or block anchor** rather than inventing a final canonical filename solely to obtain a backlink. Example:

```text
[[01-DIFFERENTIATING-MIND#Script / conditioning bridge]]
```

At the ought-be architecture pass, give every ratified candidate a stable `candidate_id`. T09 then maps those working anchors/IDs to their final canonical records. This preserves graph continuity while keeping ontology genuinely open during recovery.

## 4.3 Relation records

Where a relation matters to future construction, recovery ledgers should capture at least:

| field | office |
|---|---|
| `from` | current operation/carrier |
| `relation` | what it does to/for the target |
| `to` | linked record or stable working anchor |
| `provenance` | authored position / native derivation / external source / inference / etc. |
| `state` | present / partial / prospective / misplaced / superseded / source-debt / etc. |
| `source` | exact source-house, theorem section, quilt section or evidence route |
| `return` | inverse relation the future canonical target must expose |

The existing publication relation vocabulary remains preferred: `derives`, `grounds`, `defines`, `historicises`, `sources`, `qualifies`, `tests`, `figures`, `embodies`, `extends`, `compares`, `presages`, `returns-to`.

If Phase 3 discovers a relation this vocabulary cannot express without loss, record the new term as `proposed-relation` with its reason; T07 decides whether it joins the enduring vocabulary.

## 4.4 Backlinks: automatic graph plus semantic return

Obsidian's automatic backlink is useful retrieval infrastructure; it is not by itself the whole semantic relation.

The canonical rebuild must therefore provide both:

- the outgoing `[[wikilink]]` that makes the graph traversable;
- an explicit typed relation / readable return route explaining **why** the pages belong together.

Every canonical argument, concept, etymology, root, Matheme, Mytheme or Episteme depth page should be able to answer:

> What calls me into the argument, what do I add, what do I depend on, and where do I return?

No canonical page should survive as an orphan merely because its prose is good.

## 4.5 References and sources

A reference is wired to the exact claim/operation it supports.

- **Native derivation:** link exact Core Theorem / Copula step and mark its QL status (`Derived`, `Argued`, `Offered`, `Open`).
- **Internal authorial source:** link the `SOURCE.md` and, where available, the exact passage/quote ID; this proves authorial provenance rather than independent external warrant.
- **External source:** mark `Extracted`, `Paraphrased`, `Argued-from`, or `Resonant-with`; preserve edition/page/quote debt separately.
- **Etymology:** mark philological descent, attested semantic development, operational homology/analogia, or poetic/phonic re-entry.
- **Research proposition:** link its supporting/witness surfaces while retaining the proposition's authored/inferential status.

Source debt never deletes a developed relation from the graph. It remains attached to that relation as work to be completed.

## 4.6 Page-extrusion acceptance

By the end of T09, every final argument/concept/etymology/root/depth record should be constructible from its relation neighbourhood with:

- positive proposition/definition;
- why the distinction exists;
- native derivation or argument basis;
- incoming and outgoing relations;
- exact station/movement targets;
- eight-determination / Symbolon relation where operative;
- Matheme/Mytheme/Episteme composition;
- source houses and evidence statuses;
- transverse-thread membership;
- explicit return routes;
- backlinks to every canonical surface that materially depends on it.

The later full pages should therefore be an enrichment and composition of already-known relations, not a fresh archaeological exercise.
