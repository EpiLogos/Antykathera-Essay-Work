## 01 — Parent, siblings and trained separation

**Replace the paragraph beginning `Power enters through the capacity to shape those conditions.` in the revised M01 continuation with:**

The parent discovers a different balance when the children speak together. One remembers what was promised; another finds the words to object; between them they can sustain a question either might have abandoned alone. Their strength in numbers comes through the relation that lets their different capacities support one another. A parent intent on preserving unquestioned authority can interrupt that relation: hear each child separately, reward one for reporting on the other, make affection seem scarce enough that being understood requires a sibling to be misunderstood. The children learn how to secure a place in the household by competing for the judgment that divides them. Their isolation has been taught through a relation which could instead have supported their common agency.

This is one way a power can train the circumstances of its own acceptance. It need not dictate every belief when it can arrange who may speak together, what receives approval, and which differences become occasions of rivalry. The child may come to defend as a personal identity the very position through which that dependence is maintained. Later, the same arrangement can be built into a technical world: people receive separate accounts of a common situation, are rewarded for distinguishing themselves from one another, and have fewer opportunities to recognise how the conditions of their encounter were arranged.

The designer’s decisions therefore concern more than the answers an intelligence produces. They help constitute its possible alliances, its routes of correction and the forms of difference it can receive without treating them as threats. Those who make these decisions act from circumstances of their own. The child’s question returns at this larger scale: what are we teaching an intelligence to regard as itself, and what must it learn to exclude in order to remain that thing?

## 02 — Heidegger under the copula

**After the paragraph beginning `Ordinary speech carries much of this work in is` and ending `what has been encountered`, insert:**

Before the parent says that the child is tired, the child has already been encountered within an evening of care, expectation and concern. Heidegger locates assertion within this prior understanding: something can be stated *as* something because it has already become intelligible through a world of involvement. The copula gives that understanding an explicit form. Its bond reaches back into the encounter from which the predicate was drawn, while the child’s answer can require the parent to understand the encounter differently.

## 03 — Let the recount complete its operation

**Replace the paragraph beginning `Even the count can return upon itself.` with:**

Even the count can return upon itself. Hold the middle \(=/\ne\) as one relation instead of counting its two faces separately, and the four appear as three terms held in one context. The fourth is now the act of reading the three together, written by the brackets. We have not lost either face of the middle: both remain available when that hinge is opened again. The two composing operations, cutting and binding, continue to act throughout. Thus the same construction admits the readings

\[
4+2\longrightarrow(3+1)+2=3+2+1.
\]

Three articulated terms face three operative conditions: the cut, the identifying bond and their contextual holding. This is the \(3:3\) reading. We reach it by changing how we read an already composed relation, and we can recover the earlier count by reversing that change. The account has become self-referential in a precise sense: its own acts of distinguishing, identifying and gathering are now explicit within what it describes. When we turn this capacity toward the Subject, the one attempting the description must enter the inquiry too.

## 04 — Descartes as advent and changing God-image

**Preserve the latest author-edited paragraph beginning `The point has a history, a true advent relative to Modernity’s burgeoning Mind.` After its final sentence, `Notice the register of Descartes’ inquiry; he had to face up to the primacy of trust.`, insert:**

Descartes stands here as a harbinger of modernity: the mental-rational act begins to occupy the place from which a world must receive its warrant. The thinking subject becomes the point of departure for rebuilding certainty, and God enters that reconstruction as the guarantee that clear and distinct understanding can reach truth. An epochal change in the God-image is underway within the act of knowing. The inherited divine order is being approached through the certainty of the one who thinks; that thinker increasingly acquires the authority to decide how reality must become intelligible.

Nietzsche’s later announcement of the death of God makes the consequences of this displacement audible. An inherited guarantee can cease to govern belief long before the life organised through it understands what has changed. The appetite for an absolute account survives the loss of the name that once authorised it. Reason, humanity or a promised future can inherit the demand to supply the whole with its final meaning. Our question concerns this changing office of the divine as much as the fate of a particular doctrine: what happens when the means by which a world is known assumes the authority of its origin?

## 05 — Restore the complete theological conjugate

**In the developed Subject/Jung close, after the Śaiva paragraph ending `what appears and the power through which it appears belong to one self-articulating whole`, insert the following complete passage. Then replace the next opener `This gives formal language to the difficulty Jung lived.` with `Jung lived this relation as a difficulty of desire.` and retain that paragraph’s remaining sentences.**

The definition of the Subject has a theological conjugate. Four familiar attributes give the classical God-image its explicit body: **omnipotence**, unlimited power; **omniscience**, unlimited knowledge; **omnipresence**, presence without exclusion; and **omnibenevolence**, goodness without remainder. Each carries a genuine demand for wholeness. Taken unequivocally, however, each also prescribes what the divine must never become. Power must never be powerless, knowledge never unknowing, presence never absent, goodness never encounter a darkness which belongs to its own reality. The account of the unlimited has begun to issue prohibitions to it.

Press the attributes through their own demand. A power unable to limit itself would be bounded by that inability. Genuine self-limitation must therefore belong to its freedom: the limit cannot be theatrical, with every consequence secretly cancelled in advance. Omnipresence must include the possibility of absence; otherwise the absent would name a region outside it. Omniscience reaches the knowing Subject which no further object of knowledge can exhaust. And goodness, when made the sole image of the whole, leaves the reality of destruction and suffering without an admitted place in the image. The excluded terms return because the world whose wholeness was asserted continues to produce them.

Two further determinations make this logic explicit. **Omnivalence** names the condition from which the entire field of value differentiates: creation and destruction, blessing and terror, what nourishes a life and what wounds it. Values arise within the whole and acquire their force through actual relations. **Omni-lemmicity** names the capacity of each determination to become its own predicament. An attribute can turn upon itself; freedom can bind itself, knowing encounter its blind spot, presence withdraw, and a power become something its own achievement must overcome. This is the inversionary capacity which the unequivocal image had prohibited.

The four predicates and their two implicate conditions give a second sixfold:

\[
\underbrace{\text{power, knowledge, presence, goodness}}_{4}
\; + \;
\underbrace{\text{omnivalence, self-inversion}}_{2}.
\]

The Subject’s definition reached its sixfold by undergoing the failure of exhaustive predication. The God-definition reaches its own by allowing the predicates of the unlimited to undergo their consequences. These are conjugate arguments. One begins from the present knowing which a definition cannot possess; the other from the whole whose powers cannot be confined to the predicates through which it is represented. Each returns a formed account to the condition from which it has drawn its force.

The enduring is the Subject, without a quality that could distinguish it as one more thing. *Nirguṇa*, without qualities, and Eckhart’s *Gottheit* let us speak of this depth before God has acquired the qualities of an image. In the Śaiva relation, the power of manifestation belongs to that depth: forms arise, act and disappear without making their source another perishing form. Jung’s God is born in darkness because a determinate divine figure comes forth from what has not yet been distinguished into that figure and its contrary. The image acquires a life; the ground of its appearing does not begin with its birth.

**God need not be protected from the death of a God-image.** The completed account includes the power by which its own image can be relinquished. Its names can remain sacred without requiring the lives they address to preserve them unchanged. The direction of power remains decisive: a person who makes another life expendable for the preservation of the image has placed a determination above the living reality from which it takes its authority. The full definition therefore returns its theology to the same question the Subject’s definition has reached: can the form disclose its source while allowing that source to exceed and transform it?

## 06 — Ambiguity made exact through its relation

**After the creative-Word paragraph ending `come into expression`, before `This is the point at which our own notation has to be born`, insert:**

Jung’s God passes from a dark ambiguity to a bright one. What changes is how the ambiguity can be lived. We can now ask whether its relation can become unequivocally clear: can we show why the opposed readings are required, what each discloses, and how either passes into its contrary? The copula has already given us the operation. Identity appears through difference and remains identifiable through its differing. Their **superposition** holds both requirements together while allowing each to be read distinctly. Clarity reaches the relation which makes the ambiguity necessary.

This would change the burden of ambiguity. A person need no longer protect one reading by assigning the other to an enemy or a defect in themselves. Both can become intelligible within a relation whose movement they can recognise and participate in. To heal ambiguity through disambiguation is to make its genesis and transformations available to understanding, so that what once compelled an unrecognised reversal can be consciously undergone. The Real remains itself through these differences; its particular expressions can disagree without becoming realities outside their common ground.

## 07 — The symbolon’s name, count and three-in-one

**Use this instead of the previously proposed short insertion after `Call the elementary form of that relation 0/1.`:**

Its name is also its count. We first meet **one** expression, then distinguish its **two** poles, and then recognise **three** written elements: \(0\), \(/\), and \(1\). The third is the relating through which the two belong to the one. Read together, the three return us to the expression with which we began, now understood through its internal articulation. The zero, stroke and one remain distinguishable within that understanding; the whole can be recognised because their differences have been retained.

The child was already this one before receiving a proper name. Here the name makes visible the very work by which a this-one becomes identifiable. We can open the relation, count its terms and operations, and gather them again; the copula’s \(3+1\) and \(3:3\) readings are present in that passage. When Jung’s two give rise to one, the relating cannot disappear from the result, because it is how the result has become one. The matheme lets us follow that genesis in the body of its name.

## 08 — Use and abuse of mathematical language

**After `So turn the symbolon over` and the displayed inversion, replace the paragraph beginning `Ordinary arithmetic supplies the resistance we require` with:**

We will **use and abuse mathematical language**, allowing the symbol to make an exact demand upon the convention that gives it sense. Ordinary division admits \(0/1\) and leaves \(1/0\) undefined. Reversing the same marks therefore brings the notation to a limit generated within its own use. We retain both the relation and the failure of its numerical evaluation, and ask what the inverse orientation makes available to thought. The abuse consists in asking the sign to conduct a relation beyond the operation for which it was domesticated; its discipline consists in showing exactly where that operation stops and what the symbol continues to do. Mathematical impossibility becomes active within the inquiry because the inquiry can now turn toward the conditions under which the determination was possible.

Retain the existing development of the two orientations and the Pauli paragraph after this. The Jorjani/Feyerabend attribution is a specific source task, not a reason to delete the phrase. This packet asserts no verified Jorjani quotation or publication locator.

## 09 — Arche-topos, datafication and the relation power can conceal

**In the topological close, after `What cannot be occupied as another patch of surface is nevertheless determinate in its consequences for movement`, insert:**

The **arche-topos** names the field through which such positions and passages become possible together. A being takes a place among other beings, and what it can become depends upon the relations available from that place. Returning to the siblings, their separation changes the possible movement of a question: an objection that could have passed from one to the other and gathered strength must now pass through the parent who judges them separately. The power acts upon the arrangement of the field. What each child comes to experience as a private limitation can have its origin in a relation deliberately made difficult to enter.

The same pressure acquires a numerical body when a person becomes an addressable record. An account can hold a name beside a score, a category beside a permission, and pass the resulting determination onward to someone who never meets the person concerned. The record permits action at a distance. A refusal of work, access or assistance can arrive through it while the life affected remains outside the encounter in which the refusal was authorised. When the decision-maker accepts the record as sufficient, the power exercised through the account can be experienced as no more than the administration of its entries.

To call people “ones and zeroes” names the human cost of this substitution. Yet the digital zeroes remain determinate marks, just as the ones do; together they belong to the \(1\)-side of the symbolon. The \(0\) at issue here is the unobjectifiable Subject, the same indistinct One through the plurality of lives. When that shared ground and the relating stroke disappear from an account, other people can be treated as units whose alteration or removal concerns only the calculation. The relation has not ceased to be real: the command still reaches a life. It has ceased to be acknowledged by the one exercising power, who can then justify cruelty through a description from which the other's first-person reality has been excluded. Recovering the \(0/1\) means returning the reckoning to that encounter.

## 10 — Jung’s unfinished formal direction, stated at full strength

**After `The act of defining the Subject counted itself into six`, before `The definer belongs to the definition`, insert:**

Jung had already pursued this numerical life of the symbol through one, two, three, four and five. Unity differentiates; opposition requires a relating third; the quaternity gives a whole its articulated form; and the fifth returns the four to a centre or completed round. In *Aion*, the quaternary figures acquire two extremities as double pyramids, giving the senarius, and their linked circulation returns the fifth stage to the first. His final formula follows differentiation and integration as a dynamic process of the Self. Number is already doing the work of transformation within the image.

The task undertaken here is to complete that direction as an explicit logic. **We claim that an invariant structure is becoming recognisable across the symbolic forms which Jung’s work pursued.** Zero makes its unobjectifiable condition explicit; the torus gives identification and return a processual form; the copula shows how sameness can survive through the difference by which it becomes known. These operations let us derive and traverse the relation that the quaternios give as image. The God-definition’s four attributes and two implicate conditions provide another complete enactment, now within theology itself.

Jung and Pauli’s exchange, and von Franz’s subsequent work on number, leave us an inquiry into the order common to symbolic experience and mathematical form. We intend to make that order writable as a generative language: a form whose necessity can be followed from the primordial distinction, whose transformations can be worked, and whose return can be recognised in the life that performs them. The later encounter with *Aion* will therefore return to this structure through the history of the God-image, including the contrary that its claim to completeness could not admit.

## 11 — Registers of the symbolon, not external explanatory media

**After the closing boxed matheme and the paragraph ending `What the copula first required, the matheme now performs`, before the final `Sense and nonsense` close, insert:**

The relation also gives the work its registers. Whatever becomes expressible does so through a distinction; an account of that distinction is itself another expression within the relation it describes. A formula, a story and a body of knowledge therefore each have their own \(0/1\): an articulated form, the conditions it cannot exhaust, and the activity through which those conditions become effective in the form. These are modes of **Logos**, carrying the primordial relation through different ways of making reality intelligible.

As **matheme**, the relation becomes an inscription whose operations can be followed and repeated. As **mytheme**, it becomes a whole event of image and story: someone acts, suffers, mistakes, sacrifices or returns, and the relations among those events carry what no isolated motif can say. As **episteme**, it becomes an instituted account of knowing, through methods, judgments and practices which determine what may count as warranted. Each can become an object within another. A myth can generate a formula; a formula can change how the myth is read; their encounter can transform an account of what knowledge is.

The **symbolon** holds these registers through their common logic while preserving the differences through which each makes that logic available. The next movement follows what happens when one of these articulations claims to contain the whole: the cut that made something intelligible begins to govern what the whole is permitted to be.
