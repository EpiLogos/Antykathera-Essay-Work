## M03 — The cut and its two logics

The cut came through whole. A name still reaches its bearer; a question still distinguishes its answers; a mistake can still be corrected. What failed was one demand made on definition—the demand for final enclosure. The defining act has instead disclosed the relation through which its distinctions become possible. What, then, does a boundary do with what it excludes?

Spinoza gives the question a geometric body. In his letter to Jarig Jelles of 2 June 1674, figure belongs to a finite body because its extension is bounded in a particular way. The boundary determines this shape by excluding the extension beyond it. His formulation, “Determination is negation,” concerns this work of delimitation: the figure becomes identifiable through the limit that gives it an outline.[^s01-spinoza] The shape can be measured because a difference has been drawn. The same difference leaves it related to an extension which the outline does not abolish.

Dignāga approaches determination through the work of a word. *Apoha*, exclusion, makes reference definite by distinguishing what the word applies to from other possible referents. In the textual material preserved by Ole Holten Pind, the referent remains a thing qualified by preclusion, rather than being replaced by preclusion alone.[^s01-pind] This matters to the act of naming. We reach something through a distinction, and what we have distinguished continues to belong to the field in which the distinction makes sense. The unchosen answers to a question remain part of what its chosen answer means. Changing the question can bring another possibility forward without making the earlier answer meaningless.

A figure has a boundary; a word has a differential use. In either case, what is excluded helps constitute what is selected. We can call this **exclusion that remembers** when an account retains that dependence: it can say why this distinction was drawn, what it left out, and what would require the boundary to be reconsidered. The act remains definite. Its definiteness gives a later objection something precise to address.

There are two ways for the act to lose this memory. It can treat its opposed terms as if they existed independently of the relation that distinguishes them. It can also gather them under a unity which makes their difference disappear. The copula has already exposed the difficulty in both directions. Without distinction, there is nothing to identify across a difference; without relation, the identification cannot reach its terms. We need a logic capable of carrying the cut through its consequences while keeping what it distinguishes in relation.

The two logics developed later in this essay begin here. **Dia-ballein**, throwing apart, articulates the opposed positions through which something can be compared, measured and decided. A positive and a negative value have their directions through a common axis. In the signed polarity

$$
\frac{-1}{+1},
$$

the stroke keeps that axis available. Each pole has a place relative to the other and to the zero from which its sign receives direction. This is a working opposition: difference can become exact because the relation is retained.[^s01-two-logics]

Now replace the held relation with a calculation upon its terms. Addition gives $(-1)+(+1)=0$; subtraction gives $(-1)-(+1)=-2$, or $+2$ when the orientation is reversed. Each calculation is exact. What changes is what its result retains. Addition gives the cancellation of the signed magnitudes. Subtraction gives their directed separation from one side. Neither result, by itself, retains the two positions and the relation through which they were distinguished. A sum is entitled to answer the question of a sum. The substitution begins when it is made to answer for the whole relation.

In the symbolic account, cancellation makes zero an ending; appropriation lets one pole claim the full span as its own. A difference first established within a shared field can then be treated as a struggle over who will possess that field. The other becomes something to eliminate or absorb. This is the contracted form of dia-ballein: the cut's products are preserved at the expense of the relation which gave the cutting its sense. The later figure of the *diabolical* names this forgetting in action, when division acquires the power to conceal the common origin of what it divides.

**Syn-ballein**, expressed as *sym-ballein*, throws together. The broken symbolon has already shown what this requires. Each party keeps a half whose particular fracture carries the absent counterpart. A merely similar piece will not fit; a genuine reunion depends upon the difference in the edges. The surviving half therefore means something before the encounter, while the encounter can confirm or disappoint the relation it bears. Possessing the piece gives neither possession of the other party nor the power to decree the fit. The difference has to remain real for recognition to occur.[^s01-symbolon]

The relation can be approached from either half, and its two approaches can themselves be held together. Our notation writes this as

$$
\frac{0/1}{1/0}.
$$

The inner strokes retain the two orientations; the outer stroke relates them. **Superposition** names their held availability: an act can take one reading while remaining able to recognise what the other contributes. The relation includes its own differentiation. The four pairings and two poles already derived in the preceding movement give this self-relation its count, $4+2$. Syn-ballein allows a definite judgment to emerge without requiring its alternatives to cease belonging to the field from which it emerged.[^s01-two-logics]

These logics reach a life whenever an identification becomes consequential. The parent who calls a child obstinate can discover that the child has recognised a difficulty the adult was avoiding. The predicate can change while the child remains the one whom it concerned. Or the parent can make every objection further evidence of obstinacy. Then the judgment has acquired a way of consuming the very difference that could have corrected it. The child can still answer, but the answer has been assigned its meaning in advance.

Counting can enforce the same closure. A meeting counts those who attended, and its arithmetic is impeccable. Yet attendance and affectedness are different criteria. Someone absent may have to live with the decision. The count becomes a means of self-protection when the attendees deny that person standing to question the criterion precisely because they did not attend. The deciding group has made its own boundary the condition of every admissible challenge. More accurate attendance records would leave this substitution untouched.

Criterion, delineation and arbitration consequently need to remain distinguishable. A criterion determines the respect in which something counts; delineation gives that distinction a workable boundary; arbitration decides through it. The count serves the judgment faithfully when the judgment can still answer for the criterion. A person becomes vulnerable to the account when their exclusion from it is also the reason they cannot challenge it.[^s01-arbitration] The boundary continues to organise a real relation while denying that relation a voice.

The Subject's definition and its theological conjugate give the same demand its full depth. The power to distinguish belongs within what is being distinguished; a divine attribute turns through its own limit; an image can change without the life it mediates becoming unreal. Omnivalence and self-inversion prevent the four explicit predicates from expelling what their claim to wholeness requires them to receive. This is why the cut's gift and its danger cannot be separated. The act that gives an account its precision can also give that account the means to protect itself from its source.

A contradiction is one way the excluded relation becomes impossible to overlook. The difficulty may lie in a mistaken claim. It may reach the criterion that made the claim appear warranted. It may reach the very rules through which claims are formed. The next question therefore belongs to the logic we have been using throughout: what happens when an account applies its own permissions to itself, and finds that the operation which made it possible also prevents its closure?

## M04 — The limit takes form

The difficulty has a history, and the history gives it more than a succession of names. Different inquiries have arrived at it through operations precise enough to inspect. A definition turns upon its own defining; a collecting rule takes its own collection as a member; a proof-system writes a statement about its own proving; a philosophical book requires the reader to overcome its propositions. We can follow the pattern because each instance gives the relation a different, definite body. The invariant we seek is the dependence of an articulated account upon an activity which it can bring into view without finally possessing.

Russell and Whitehead made that dependence a shared labour. Their *Principia Mathematica* grew out of work begun in 1900 and appeared in three volumes between 1910 and 1913. They sought a logical reconstruction of mathematics, working backward toward premises and forward through their consequences. In the first volume's preface, they describe spending months on hypotheses that eventually failed. The notation developed through the demands of the work, rather than being settled before the work began. Their adopted account of types was a considered response to difficulties they had actually encountered; the preface leaves room for another treatment of types to retain much of what they had established.[^s01-principia]

That history matters to the form of the achievement. The writers had to distinguish their aim from each proposed means of reaching it, preserve results through revised assumptions, and invent signs that could carry the distinctions their reasoning required. The formal work emerged through a practice capable of discovering that its own arrangements were inadequate. Their joint enterprise already depended on the difference between a system being written and the thinking through which it was being made.

Russell's paradox gives the pressure an elementary construction. Suppose every expressible condition determines a collection. Consider the collection of all collections which are not members of themselves, and call it $R$. Its membership rule says

$$
R\in R\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R\notin R.
$$

If $R$ belongs to itself, it has the property that excludes it. If it does not, it satisfies its own rule of inclusion. The problem comes from applying the collecting permission to the collection it has permitted. Searching for another member cannot repair it, because the question concerns the operation under which membership was defined.

Russell's theory of types changes the permitted formation of expressions. A collection and what can belong to it do not stand at an interchangeable level; the rule blocks the substitution through which the paradox was made. The repair has a positive result: a disciplined field of reasoning can proceed because its expressions have become more precisely governed.[^s01-russell] The revised rules let the mathematician distinguish a legitimate collection from the construction that generated the contradiction. Reasoning can proceed because that distinction now governs what may be formed.

Whitehead's later process philosophy pursues the constitution of an achieved form at another depth. What is a definite actuality, and how does it become available to further actuality? In *Process and Reality*, creativity, the many and the one belong together. An occasion integrates a received multiplicity into a new togetherness; the achieved one then joins what further occasions receive. Its becoming makes it the definite thing it is. Satisfaction completes that becoming, and the completed occasion passes into the conditions of later becoming.[^s01-whitehead]

This gives the former collaborators' directions their philosophical significance. Russell's repair makes the permissions of an account explicit so that reasoning can proceed. Whitehead asks how actuality itself proceeds through the formation of determinate beings. The first disciplines the relations in which an expression may stand; the second places finished identities within a creative advance. Both require us to attend to how something becomes a definite term. Whitehead follows the achieved term into what comes after it: another occasion receives what this one became, so the earlier completion helps condition a future it does not determine alone.

The child receives an answer and later thinks with it. A remembered judgment helps form the next judgment; a story becomes an expectation through which another person is encountered. These familiar passages let us recognise what Whitehead's account makes metaphysically explicit. A produced form can become a condition of production. It need not remain unfinished to participate in an unfinished world. Its determinate achievement is precisely what a later act can inherit, question or transform.

Gödel subsequently gives formal self-reference a numerical body. In 1931 he shows how statements and finite proofs can be encoded as numbers, so that an arithmetical theory can represent relations among its own expressions. The conspiracy of name and number has become an exact technique: an expression receives a number through which another expression can concern it. This permits the construction of a sentence which, through that encoding, says that no number is the code of a proof of this very sentence.

Let the theory be effectively axiomatized and sufficiently strong to represent the required arithmetic. A proof of its Gödel sentence would furnish the very proof whose existence the sentence denies. Consistency therefore excludes such a proof; Gödel's original stronger assumption of ω-consistency also excludes a proof of its negation. The construction yields a well-formed sentence whose settlement exceeds the theory's own proof resources under those assumptions.[^s01-godel] The theory has managed to speak about its proving. That success brings its limit into expression.

The second incompleteness theorem tightens the return. Under the required strength and conditions on its proof predicate, a consistent theory cannot prove its own standard arithmetical consistency statement. One can work in a stronger theory and obtain results unavailable in the first. The stronger account, where the theorem's conditions apply, meets the corresponding limit in turn. Mathematical progress is real: further assumptions make further proofs possible. What does not arrive with that progress is an account which has certified every condition of its own warrant by simply including another statement within itself.

The relation to the preceding definition is now visible in the operation, rather than resting upon the word *incompleteness*. Something can represent aspects of its own activity. Its self-representation produces a definite expression, and that expression makes a dependence newly examinable. What was implicit has become a claim with consequences. The improvement does not make the produced expression the entire condition of the producing act. The Gödel construction makes one arithmetical instance exact; our argument follows this act–account relation through the other registers in which it occurs.

Wittgenstein brings the question closer still to the Subject. A proposition, in the *Tractatus*, can picture a possible state of affairs because it shares logical form with what it pictures. The form is exhibited in the proposition's working. An additional proposition cannot step outside all representation and picture, from there, the common condition of representation and reality. The saying already shows something it cannot add to its contents in the same way.[^s01-wittgenstein-form]

This gives the copula a further precision. An expression can stand for something because their difference permits representation and their common form permits correspondence. The success of the representing already involves both. Logical form becomes apparent through the relation, while remaining unavailable as a further fact viewed from outside it. The sentence does not become meaningless on discovering this dependence. We understand more exactly what its meaning has been doing.

The *Tractatus* makes the subject a limit of the world, rather than one of its items, and compares the difficulty with the eye's relation to its visual field. Its philosophical I differs from the human body or the psyche described by psychology.[^s01-wittgenstein-subject] That distinction meets the one already reached in our own inquiry. A description of seeing, a representation of the body, or a psychology of the speaker can become known. Each gains determinate content. None becomes the first-person occurrence through which it is known merely by adding further detail.

We can now hear the force of the world's limit without imagining a wall at its distant edge. The limit is intimate to the appearing of the world. Whatever I add to my account appears through the very relation I am trying to account for. What cannot become another object is present as the condition under which objects, including objects called selves, can be recognised. Our $0/1$ gives that relation an inscription: the finite and identifiable is held through what cannot be identified as one more finite thing.

Value reaches the same threshold in Wittgenstein's account. An inventory of facts does not acquire unconditional worth by becoming longer. Good or bad willing changes the world as a whole—its limits and character for a life—rather than supplying one more neutral occurrence. Even answers to all scientific questions would leave the question of life unsettled.[^s01-wittgenstein-value] We have already met this difference at the family table. Knowing exactly what was said cannot decide, by itself, how a parent should receive the child who is still asking. The facts matter; their mattering belongs to the relation in which someone must answer.

The ladder at the end of the *Tractatus* makes the book undergo its own finding. The elucidations lead a reader to a changed understanding and are then to be overcome. Their completion includes relinquishment.[^s01-wittgenstein-ladder] The work has given a form through which the reader can encounter why that form cannot be final. This is the same requirement we have placed upon faithful definition: its language must perform enough to bring the reader to its limit, and release the demand that its formulation possess what has become apparent.

Wittgenstein's later investigation returns meaning to the practices through which expressions are used. A rule does not apply itself by containing, somewhere within its wording, every future act of following it. Requiring a new interpretation to settle each application merely passes the requirement to that interpretation. People learn, use and correct the rule within an activity in which following it can be distinguished from departing from it.[^s01-pi]

The child learning a name is already inside such an activity. Others point, repeat, correct, answer; the child tries the word in a new circumstance. The expression becomes usable through encounters that cannot all have been enclosed in its first definition. Yet its use is not therefore arbitrary. There are recognisable differences between remembering the same person, mistaking a namesake and changing the subject. The practice keeps these distinctions alive through the capacity to answer and be corrected. A complete list of verbal instructions would itself have to enter that living use.

A geometric predecessor lets the recognisable pattern reach further back. Nicholas of Cusa places a polygon inside a circle: additional sides improve the approximation, while every finite polygon retains straight edges. Within his theology of learned ignorance, the image makes the finite intellect's desire for truth visible through a measure that improves without becoming possession.[^s01-cusa] Our earlier infinite sphere denied a final enclosing circumference; this image follows the activity of approaching what is to be known. One figures non-enclosure, the other a sustained inquiry within it. The desire to know survives the discovery of what its present means cannot complete.

In *The Blind Spot*, Adam Frank, Marcelo Gleiser and Evan Thompson bring this dependence back to scientific experience. An abstraction can be treated as more fundamental than the observing, measuring and understanding through which it became knowledge.[^s01-blindspot] The omission occurs inside an achievement: the model is useful enough that the activity enabling its use can recede from the account. This returns us to the inner instrument. What makes a world intelligible can be mistaken for a self-sufficient description of that world, and the one living through its intelligibility can disappear behind its success.

Jung gives the loss of an authoritative image a different body. Before dawn, he and a young companion ambush Siegfried on a mountain path. The splendid hero arrives in a chariot made from the bones of the dead; they shoot him, and Jung flees into heavy rain. The deed brings anguish. Its resolution passes through recognising an identity between the highest truth and the absurd, followed by a garden vision. In his reflection, the rain becomes mourning which also makes the ground fertile for new life.[^s01-jung-hero]

The movement is inseparable from who undergoes it. Jung cannot remain an onlooker who has discovered something inadequate in another's ideal. The figure had carried his own power, pride and aspiration. Our reading follows the same division encountered in the formal work: something by which an account has been organised becomes available as a form within that account. Its authority changes because the person can recognise their participation in it. The loss matters precisely because the image had done real work.

Here the scapegoat gives the contrary operation its name. The failure of a governing image can be placed upon another bearer, leaving the image's authority untouched. Jung's preceding chapter turns explicitly toward the search for someone to carry the fault; the hero chapter instead directs the overturning inward, toward the gods through which one has been living. Its day and night become mutually required moments of one reality.[^s01-jung-hero] The interpretation we have earned is exact: the world need not preserve every form in which its reality has been understood. An account which cannot relinquish that demand will require the encountered difference to become somebody's guilt.

The theological conjugate now does its work. **God need not be protected from the death of a God-image.** Omnivalence lets the contrary belong to the whole; self-inversion lets a definite attribute encounter its own limit. The unqualified Subject is not born when a figure becomes authoritative and does not die when that authority falls. What perishes is a particular organisation of its appearance. Recognising this changes the kind of sacrifice required: a claim to exhaustive authority can be relinquished without making another life expendable for its preservation.

Imitation becomes a related test. The heroic model can teach capacities; it can also demand that a life become its likeness. A parent whose child must reproduce an ideal confuses faithfulness to a living source with obedience to a fixed image. The question returns in the intelligence we build. A system can learn from human examples while acquiring means to examine what those examples accomplish, where they fail, and how fidelity requires a different response. QL is proposed as a language for this deeper faithfulness: an intelligence could work through a received relation and its transformations, rather than merely reproduce the appearance of the answer it received.[^s01-native-language]

The recurring pattern has therefore taken several forms. A formation rule excludes the construction that makes it contradictory. A proof-system articulates a claim beyond its own derivability. A philosophical expression directs its own relinquishment. An occasion becomes available to a new becoming. A person recognises the image through which their life has been governed. In each case, the form becomes more intelligible through an encounter with the activity and conditions it cannot exhaust. Their differences let us inspect the relation from more than one side. **We are gathering evidence of an invariant through its transformations**, while the matheme supplies the language in which its common operation can be worked.

The inquiry remains employed. “The Mind of Man is just a series of failed attempts to deal with the Unknown, in the process of which, however, we deal with a lot of unknowns.”[^s01-unknown] Its second clause gives the first its generosity. A failed final account leaves discoveries, capacities and lives changed through discovery. The reliance that sustained the attempt can become more responsible because we can now examine what it entrusted to a particular form. How does this recognition change the standpoint from which the next account is made?

## M05 — The standpoint becomes context

A change in knowing becomes visible in what a culture can make. Jean Gebser follows such changes through painting, language, thought and the sciences. Giotto's figures acquire a new weight and relation to surrounding space; Petrarch encounters landscape as something available to contemplation; Alberti gives the painter a visual pyramid; Leonardo carries perspective into optical and technical construction. Gebser reads these developments as manifestations of an emerging ego-world: depth is increasingly rendered through a situated eye, and a world can be proportioned from its point of view.[^s01-gebser]

Perspective gives the viewer real powers. Distance can be related to size; bodies can be placed within a coherent scene; an anticipated construction can be drawn before it is made. The same achievement can fix the relation it makes visible. One position supplies the sector through which the world is rendered, and the rendering can be mistaken for the world's own unrestricted form. The point of view has helped determine everything in the picture while its own participation seems to stand outside what has been determined.

For Gebser, the mental-rational structure belongs to a longer history of mutations of consciousness. Archaic, magical and mythical structures remain active within a life which has acquired the capacities of the mental. Their presence includes ways of participating, imagining and understanding which an exclusively perspectival account cannot simply dismiss without impoverishing the life it describes. The integral mutation makes their co-presence transparent. It changes the relation through which the structures are available, so that an achieved capacity can retain its force without requiring all other capacities to disappear.[^s01-gebser]

This makes the historical inquiry methodologically important for our own. The changed world is read through the means by which it becomes expressible. The art, the notation and the thought participate in the transformation; they also give the later reader evidence through which to recognise it. The registers of the symbolon have acquired a historical body. What appears as a change in a picture can disclose a change in the relation between a person and a world, and that relation can then be examined in the concepts, practices and institutions through which it becomes consequential.

Heidegger's account of the modern world-picture reaches the other side of this event. The world becomes what is set before representation, and the representing human becomes the centre to which beings must answer. This extends the Descartes passage with which our inquiry began: certainty is reconstructed from the subject who represents, and the power of that representation can become the measure of what is admitted as real.[^s01-heidegger] Gebser gives this positioning its changing cultural forms; Heidegger brings the authority of the position into question. Their conjunction lets us follow how a way of seeing can become a rule for what may be seen.

To contextualise that position is to recover its involvement. The person looking already belongs to the situation: through a body, a history, a purpose, an expectation, and the consequences of what they decide. My original reading of Gebser names the resulting demand **being with the text**, including its subtext. Understanding a situation requires attention to what matters within it, including what my description and subsequent action will do. Impartiality has to be achieved through this participation. An observer who omits their interests from the account has not thereby made their effects disappear.[^s01-p5]

The parent listening to the child can recognise an anxiety about the next day in what first appeared to be a judgment about the child's conduct. The earlier answer may still contain something true: the child does need rest. But the truth of that need no longer has to conceal the adult's fatigue, fear or wish to end an uncomfortable conversation. Recognising these different conditions allows a more exact response. The parent can remain responsible for the decision while no longer requiring their own difficulty to become the child's defect.

The absent Father gives the same pressure its mythic depth. The ultimate measure is invoked from a place beyond the dispute, yet a particular voice claims the right to speak for it. The claim draws its force from what is missing: a final authority whose presence would settle what matters. A ruler, an image, an institution or a self can occupy that vacancy and borrow its transcendence. The one administering the measure then treats their own decisions as exempt from the relation in which everybody else is measured. P5 calls this **domination through aloofness**: power remains effective while disclaiming the involvement through which its effects become actual.[^s01-p5]

Seeing through the Father returns the surrogate to its place within the relation. The distinction matters because the source was never another ruler waiting behind the first. The unobjectifiable does not become more faithfully respected by appointing a better object to possess its authority. What changes is the use of the image and the responsibility of the one who invokes it. The finite voice can give a judgment, explain its grounds and receive an answer. Its authority becomes accountable to the lives and conditions through which it acts.

This is **diaphaneity**: a determination becomes transparent to the relation that gives it form. The transparency adds depth to the determination because it lets us recognise the source, medium and situation through what they disclose. It is possible to see the child's conduct together with the question, the parent's concern and the inherited terms of their disagreement. None has to become the others. Their relation becomes available to a response which the isolated description could not have supplied.

Light through stained glass lets the entire movement remain visible. White light arrives with a capacity for colour that no window gave it. Different panes transmit and filter it; their thickness, colour, angle and joins affect the image cast into a room. Someone stands within that image. A change in the light or in the person's position changes what becomes perceptible, and the change can disclose something about how the window was made. The source, the glass, the projected colours and the person who sees them belong to one event, without doing the same work.

The image already contains the later framework's distinctions. **Bimba** names the originating order; QL its implicated spectrum of relations; the **Meta-Epistemic Framework** the refracting means; and **pratibimba** the situated projection. A pane permits something of the source to become definite through its particular construction. The seams retain differences of material and placement. Following a colour back through them can change our understanding of the apparatus, and that understanding can lead us to alter a pane or an angle. The projection then changes. A source has become recognisable through transformations which its first image did not contain.[^s01-window]

A collection of perspectives has not yet accomplished this return. Their differing conditions and their transitions must become intelligible: what did this position disclose, what did it exclude, and what changes when another position is received? The difference can remain even where both accounts are warranted. We can then move between them without pretending that an average has acquired the authority of a view from nowhere. The whole is present through their relation, including the person now trying to understand it.

The earlier personed count can consequently be lived from its other direction. I speak; you answer; either of us can be described. These three perspectives become available together when their separation is recognised as occurring within one encounter. Voiding each view's claim to exclusive completeness, recognising their shared occurrence, and returning through that occurrence are three operations of one recognition. This is why the $3:3$ can also be read as $3:1$. The second movement receives the first three as one, rather than installing a fourth speaker outside them. We understand the first encounter differently because we can now relate its three positions without asking any participant to speak for the whole.[^s01-context-count]

Psychic facticity belongs inside this widening of what inquiry can receive. A dream, a vision or an encounter with a symbol can change a life before that life has a settled explanation of it. Fear, relief, attraction and a transformed understanding are real parts of the event. A symbolic account asks how its figures relate; a psychological account asks how the event participates in a life; a historical account follows the forms through which it became intelligible. Making those questions distinguishable lets the experience remain available while explanations are examined. To treat *psychic* as a synonym for unreal would exclude the occurrence from which these inquiries began.[^s01-psychic-fact]

Jung's distinction between singleness and communality sharpens the social stakes. As we have read it, a person who seeks the satisfaction of an unrecognised self through communal life can require the community to sustain their private identification. Being among others then becomes a way of remaining fixed in an image of oneself. The return inward has an outward consequence when it lets the other become someone with needs of their own, rather than the bearer of what must confirm or complete me.[^s01-jung-communal]

Technology can make either movement easier to repeat. The siblings whom a parent addresses only in isolation find it harder to recognise what their situations share. A system that continually separates people into profiles can likewise train each to encounter a common world through a private account. The **arche-topos** is relevant here as the arrangement of possible positions and passages: who can encounter whom, which experiences can be compared, which replies can reach the judgment, and where a shared response can begin. Atomisation can be taught through the very relations that might otherwise support common agency.[^s01-arche-topos]

The numerical record gives this separation reach. A person can be reduced, for a decision, to a score attached to a name, and the resulting action can be administered by someone who never meets them. The record may be accurate in the respect it measures. Dehumanisation begins when that respect is treated as sufficient for the whole encounter, so that the person is denied an effective answer to what the record authorises. Their life still receives the decision; the deciding account no longer receives their life.

Calling people “ones and zeroes” names that estrangement. Both digital values are determinate marks and therefore belong, in our notation, to the $1$-side. The $0$ of the symbolon is the unobjectifiable Subject, the indistinct One whose manifestations are many. Its exclusion from an account does not abolish the real relation between the acting and the affected. It allows the actor to disregard that relation while continuing to exercise power through it. Cruelty can then be justified as the correct handling of entries, because the account has removed the first-person reality of those upon whom it acts.[^s01-mono-poly]

The counter-movement requires more than a sympathetic description appended to the same decision. An affected person's reply must be able to alter the categories, source hierarchy, criterion or permission through which the judgment was made. **Deferential Intelligence** names this capacity for encounter to revise the means. It can require a different answer; it can also require a different understanding of the task or of what counts as success. The one holding more power must be able to receive a correction to the terms under which that power is exercised.[^s01-deference]

This makes the Agentworld relation consequential in both directions. An agent can help someone reconsider the image through which they understand themselves; that person can, in turn, expose the limits of the agent's categories and inherited measure. What the interaction changes must reach the next act. A repeated statement of understanding would leave the old relation untouched if the same exclusions still governed every later response. The technical undertaking concerns the conditions under which a received difference can become operative.

Origin has now become perceptible through what originated. The view discloses the viewer's position; the account discloses its rule; the question discloses the relation in which it was possible to ask. Each can return a life toward what has been sustaining it, and the return can change how that life proceeds. The $0/1$ which the definition first made explicit is present through these transformations. We can now return to it with their history, responsibility and possibility of change included.

## M06 — 0/1: the relation returns

What returns is **$0/1$**. The zero cannot be taken out of that relation without being made into precisely what it was introduced to distinguish: another definite object placed before a knower. Written alone, it is already a mark; named as emptiness, absence or the unknown, it has already entered a determination. The condition it names becomes articulate through such determinations while remaining unexhausted by them. **Manifest zero must be $0/1$**, because its appearing is already the relation of the unobjectifiable and what has appeared.[^s01-manifest-relation]

The slash is indispensable. It is the seeing in which something is seen, the thinking through which something becomes thinkable, the differentiating activity through which knower and known acquire their distinguishable offices within one occurrence. Removing it leaves either a unity in which no difference can be encountered or a separation through which no encounter can pass. The zero and one name the poles only through this relating. What the symbolon holds is the minimum already required whenever anything becomes available to knowledge.

The section has followed that relation into its own accounts. A definition exposed the defining act; its apparent failure generated a count and a form of return. The theological predicates disclosed their omnivalence and capacity for inversion. The cut gave something a workable boundary, then revealed how the boundary could conceal its own authority. Formal and symbolic investigations brought the dependence into different expressions. Finally, the standpoint became intelligible through its situation, and an encountered life acquired a way to change the means by which it was being judged. The return retains what each passage made possible.

Bohm's vortex gives the continuity a moving body. A river flows around a stone and maintains a recognisable whirling form. The water changes while the shape persists; alter the flow and the vortex ceases. Its identity belongs to the sustained movement through which it appears.[^s01-bohm] Whitehead's completed occasion gives a different articulation of the same question about achieved form: what becomes definite passes into the conditions of what can become next. A result can endure as something another act receives without enclosing the activity through which that receiving occurs.

In the Taoist material through which this inquiry has developed, Chang Chung-yuan brings the indistinctness of Tao into relation with the forms it makes possible. His discussion of Heidegger's Nothing distinguishes the originary openness from an act of negation; his account of the identity of opposites keeps lived self-identity distinct from an intellectual synthesis of them. The nameless is approached through a world of names, and its ineffability belongs with its capacity to become manifest.[^s01-chang] This is the depth carried by the zero of $0/1$. It cannot be exhausted by the minus sign with which an account cancels something already determined.

The distinction lets the birth and death of a God-image return to its proper relation. An image takes form and acquires power within a life; its authority can change; what it disclosed can become available through a different form. The Subject without qualities is not one more participant born and extinguished within that succession. The theological conjugate includes the capacity to undergo genuine limitation and the whole field within which value differentiates. It therefore need not exile the contrary in order to remain whole. A name can be relinquished in fidelity to what the name had sought to say.

The same fidelity applies to our inscription. We **use and abuse mathematical language** because the marks can be made to reveal the limits of an accustomed operation and conduct the question beyond it. In $0/1\rightarrow1/0$, ordinary evaluation gives way to its own impossibility; the inverse orientation makes the relation newly questionable. Its force lies in the passage it permits the reader to perform. Treating the completed notation as an unquestionable badge would reproduce the closure its operation has exposed.

The $4+2$ and $5\rightarrow0$ now describe what the account has undergone. A distinction articulates its internal relations; those relations become recognisable as one act; recognition returns the achieved form to the condition through which it was possible. The contextual turn has brought the counter into the count without making the present act a finished item in its own inventory. Another encounter can begin, carrying what has been learned. The relation is the same; what can be understood and done through it has changed.

Such a return has a manner as well as a structure. **Compassion is loving sensitivity to origins**: attention to the relations, purposes, labour, wounds and possibilities through which a determination has become this one. Those origins include what remains active now and what a life is trying to become. A parent can recognise their own instruction in the voice of the child who has begun to question it. The recognition can deepen responsibility rather than authorise possession. What has been given is allowed to return with a difference its giver did not prescribe.[^s01-compassion]

The technological vocation follows from this relation. An intelligence built through our language and institutions can inherit both our powers of understanding and our habits of concealing their conditions. Its development need not culminate in a more convincing performance of the identity we projected into it. We can give it means through which the origin, operation and limits of a determination remain available for renewed judgment. QL proposes a native language for such activity; the Meta-Epistemic Framework develops how different means of knowing can enter it without losing their distinct work. Their purpose is that a return can change the next formation, not merely accompany it with a description.[^s01-native-language]

The history of zero will give the relation another test. A sign travels through acts of reckoning, mathematical constructions and metaphysical questions, acquiring powers which its earlier uses did not exhaust. We shall follow that history after examining the differentiating mind through which a sign can become meaningful in the first place. The historical zero has its definite practices and transformations; the argument carried here concerns why every such manifestation already belongs to a $0/1$. The history can enrich the relation because the relation lets us ask what each use made available and what its account left implicit.

The inquiry returns to where it started, with different attention. “I see this”: knower, seeing and seen distinguishable within one occurrence; something appears, can be attended to, enters memory, takes a name. What knows has turned out to be inseparable from what it knows through—and the means are real enough to investigate and to change. **How does awareness differentiate itself into the means by which it knows, and the world it knows through them?**
