### #2 · AIKit — Potency

A strong swimmer standing fully dressed on a pier can swim, and is not swimming. Nobody would say the capacity has vanished because it is not being used, and nobody would say it is fully present either: the water is cold, the jacket is heavy, there is no reason to go in, and a sign on the railing forbids it. If a child falls in, every one of those conditions changes at once, and what was a capacity becomes, in a few seconds, an act whose shape depends on all of them, on the cold, the jacket, the distance, the swimmer's nerve and the sign that is now beside the point. What a life can bring to bear is never the list of what it can do. It is a horizon that the situation draws, and the horizon moves.

Aristotle made the point that keeps this horizon from collapsing into an inventory. In the ninth book of the *Metaphysics* he holds that "every potency belongs to the same subject and refers to the same process as a corresponding impotence": what can build can also not build, and a capacity is always in the same respect a capacity not to act.[^s5-dunamis] He adds that the rational capacities are capacities for contraries, as the art of medicine can produce sickness as well as health, and that something other than the capacity has to decide which contrary becomes actual: "There must, then, be something else that decides; I mean by this, desire or will."[^s5-decides] A power that could not refrain would be a compulsion, and a power that decided itself would need no one to exercise it. AIKit, the third of the six products, takes both points into engineering, and its office within the paradigm is **potency**: the changing horizon of what a Life can know, express, reach and bring to bear. Possession and potency differ, and the difference is the whole subject of the product. A capacity may exist and be unavailable here; be available and irrelevant to the present work; be relevant and forbidden; be permitted and not selected; be selected and fail to become operative. AIKit's repository writes these differences as one line, and the line is the best single statement of its design:

$$
\text{exists}\;\ne\;\text{eligible here}\;\ne\;\text{available from a provider}\;\ne\;\text{selected}\;\ne\;\text{projected into this client}\;\ne\;\text{loaded into context}\;\ne\;\text{invoked}.
$$

The repository gives the reason in a sentence: it "keeps several relations distinct because collapsing them produces misleading agency."[^s5-availability] An agent that cannot tell these relations apart believes it can do what it cannot, or does what it may not, or loads everything it might ever need into a context that then has no room for the work. The same discipline holds for knowledge, where a source can be known and askable without its contents entering the standing context, and for the bodies through which an agent runs, where a component can be known without being active and a surface visible without conferring any authority to change what it shows. For a person the structure is familiar under other names, as skill, language, memory, bodily ability, social access, permission, opportunity and confidence, and anyone who has known how to do something and found the tool missing, or held the tool and lacked the permission, or held both and found the moment wrong, has lived the inequalities in that line.

Two lenses read the office, and §0/1 has already walked the first of them. Logical, `L2`, is the tetralemma we followed with the friend at the station barrier, the four corners of what can be asserted held between **APORIA**, the impasse in which a question opens, and **SILENCE**, in which assertion is exhausted; Chronological, `L3′`, follows a power through the seasons of its life, from the self-moving whole of Spirit through spring, summer, autumn and winter to Life as *Aufhebung*, Hegel's word for a passing-away that keeps what it surpasses.[^s5-l2-l3p]

| | Logical · `L2` | Chronological · `L3′` |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | APORIA | Spirit (*Geist*) |
| **#1** | IS | Spring |
| **#2** | IS NOT | Summer |
| **#3** | BOTH | Autumn |
| **#4** | NEITHER | Winter |
| **#5** | SILENCE | Life (*Aufhebung*) |

The two columns answer different questions, and the pairing holds because a horizon of capacity has both a logical form and a history. The logical column keeps each determination answerable to a stated scope: asking whether a capability exists differs from asking whether it is available here or operative in this act, so a positive answer to the first settles neither of the others, and the line of inequalities concerns different relations of one capacity to a situation without naming the tetralemma's corners. The chronological column makes the history of a power internal to what the power is. Read across the rows and the pairings explain what an inventory hides. At **#2**, IS NOT stands beside Summer: a refusal at the height of a power's usefulness, the permission withdrawn exactly when the tool works best, is a hard denial for a system to keep, and the pairing names why it must be kept, since a capability in full exercise is the one most likely to overreach the commission it serves. At **#3**, BOTH stands beside Autumn, the season of harvest and evaluation, and BOTH asks a particular honesty of the harvest: if a capability is available to one participant and unavailable to another, both determinations belong to the field, and erasing the participant from the description would manufacture a contradiction where there is only plurality. At **#4**, NEITHER stands beside Winter. A method can go dormant without ceasing to exist, and a dormant method is neither available nor absent in the way an inventory records; a skill retired from the active set, its reasons recorded, can return in another context with another significance, and the history of its dormancy belongs to its fitness. At **#5**, SILENCE stands beside *Aufhebung*, and the pair gives development its honest form: a capability that stays permanently active can prevent a more fitting relation from forming, and growth sometimes requires that a form be set down while what it taught is raised into the next arrangement. Potency includes the capacity not to enact what one could, which is Aristotle's point returned as a design rule.

What changes a horizon is mostly work, and the work is easy to miss because it leaves no single artefact behind. In February 2026 the engineer Mitchell Hashimoto published an account of how his own use of agents changed. Isolated exchanges were useful from the start; fitting agents into the work he already did was hard. So he deliberately did some tasks twice, once by hand and once with an agent, to learn where the agent helped and where it failed, and what he built from the comparison was an arrangement more than a set of prompts: explicit planning where a task was vague, instructions written in answer to particular failures, and, above all, tools of verification, since an agent given "a way to verify its work" more often than not fixes its own mistakes. He also protected his attention, deciding when to look at the agents' work instead of letting their notifications organise his day.[^s5-hashimoto] Nothing in his account concerns a better model. The same models had a different practical horizon in his earlier arrangement, and the competence that resulted cannot be located in the model at all. It lives in the arrangement, Bratton's harness seen from the desk of one practitioner, and it was won by labour: the failed command noticed, the instruction written, the check that would catch it next time. He gives the labour a precise form. Whenever an agent makes a mistake, he writes, "you take the time to engineer a solution such that the agent never makes that mistake again," either as an instruction in the file of guidance his project keeps for agents or as a program that tells the agent when it is wrong, and of that file he reports: "Each line in that file is based on a bad agent behavior, and it almost completely resolved them all."[^s5-harness-engineering] A file of that kind is a horizon written down. Every line records an encounter in which a capacity met a situation and failed, and every line changes what the next agent can fittingly do, so the file is potency in its chronological sense, a history of winters and springs kept as present conditions. The same account sets its own limit, and it is worth keeping beside the achievement: he runs one agent at a time, by choice, because one agent balances his deep manual work against watching over it, and the goal of always having an agent working is, on his estimate, met for a tenth to a fifth of an ordinary day. The horizon widened because a person spent attention on it, and it stays bounded by the attention a person can spend.

Such an arrangement also has a body. Skills now travel in a common package, which the Agent Skills specification describes as a folder holding a file of identifying metadata and instructions, optionally with scripts, references and assets, and which it discloses progressively: an agent first meets a skill's short description and loads the whole only when its task calls for it.[^s5-skills] The format "was originally developed by Anthropic, released as an open standard," and its stewards declare it open to contributions from the wider ecosystem, so that a company's convention has become, at the level of form, a common.[^s5-skills-origin] Packaging of that kind makes knowledge easier to carry and to disclose selectively, and progressive disclosure is the same discipline AIKit applies to knowledge in general: a source can be known and askable without its contents entering the standing context, because a wide horizon of knowledge is useful where a permanently swollen prompt is not, and a model's window filled with everything it might need has no room left for what it is doing. It establishes neither permission, nor successful execution, nor fitness in a new environment, and a receiving harness still has to bring the right method into the actual act. AIKit occupies the gap between a package that exists and a method that governs an act. It assembles a person's chosen methods, from a collection they curate in their own ground, from the methods each product ships with and from outside sources they have registered and decided to trust, into a numbered generation, and projects that generation into the folders each harness reads, so that one decision about which methods should be available to which kind of agent is made once, recorded, and carried to every harness that needs it. When the selection changes it writes a new generation and keeps the old one, and a single act returns to it. Discovery is not adoption, in the repository's phrase: AIKit can inspect the methods and settings a person already has without rewriting or claiming them, and when the person asks it to adopt or change something outside itself it acts through explicit procedures that record enough to undo what they did, refusing to overwrite what conflicts. The repository calls this "a non-displacement principle as much as a safety feature."[^s5-non-displacement] Existing arrangements are part of the person's real world, and the product has to make them intelligible before asking them to become anything else.

Bratton's harness, he says, improves "on a different clock, driven by usage rather than training."[^s5-usage-clock] AIKit is where that clock runs, and it is where the clock can deceive. Beside each capability the product keeps observations of trust, familiarity, fitness and use, and its design holds them to one rule: familiar destinations become easier to reach "without learned frequency silently becoming trust or authored preference."[^s5-familiar] Familiarity says that a power has been met before; fitness asks whether it worked under the conditions that matter now; trust is a relation someone extends and can withdraw; and preference, in Central's sense, is something the person said. A system that ranks its methods by how often they were used and then treats the ranking as a judgment of worth has repeated, at the scale of a toolkit, the operation that §5 · #1 found inside a preference score: usage occupies the zero. Counted from the side of the most-used method, the whole span between what was often chosen and what was rarely chosen becomes evidence of quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; counted from the other side, the rarely used method, often the careful one, the slow one, the one needed for an uncommon but important case, carries the same span as a deficit, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and drifts to the bottom of every list, so that the popular method's rising rank and the careful method's disappearance are one movement read from its two ends. A held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, keeps frequency and fitness as two poles of one assessment, each read through the other and both answerable to the purposes the work serves. The usage clock is real, and it makes the harness smarter for exactly as long as what it counts can be returned to the people who know why the counted things mattered.

Power enters wherever availability becomes selection. To make one method salient is already to shape the probable act, and to expose a tool is already to change the practical horizon, so the question is never only whether a capability can execute but whose purposes, permissions and consequences govern its activation. A familiar tool may be unfit for the present task and an unfamiliar one exactly what it requires. Repeated successful use can ground an observation of fitness without granting unrestricted future authority, and returned evidence has to be able to change the capability relation itself: tighten its scope, widen it, retire an option, change a method, or reopen what had been excluded. A constructed case shows the whole movement. A tool for reading sources exists in a person's wider repertoire and enters a given situation only when the interface it needs is present. A commission makes it relevant; the source's terms of access decide whether it may be used; the agent selects it among the available methods; and selection still leaves activation to be tested, since a missing dependency can prevent the read. That resistance returns to the horizon. Another permitted method may become the fitting one, or the missing dependency may be supplied so that the chosen method works next time. Throughout, the capacity persisted while its availability, its selection and its fitness changed, and an account that recorded only "the agent has a source-reading tool" would have described none of what happened. AIKit's work is to make such relations explicit enough to be judged instead of assumed: it can explain, for any resource, why it is active, absent or degraded in the present context, and a resolved context has succeeded when it keeps enough provenance to say why these capacities, these sources and these methods entered the present field and not others.

The horizon also has a seat. AIKit can launch an agent into a position of the kind §5 · #1 described: it asks Central for the position, asks Actuation to open or refuse the tenure, and starts the harness with the position and its generation written into the environment it runs in, and nothing is released when the harness exits, because leaving is an explicit act.[^s5-inhabit] The seat of the act and the horizon of its powers meet in a single launch, and neither product absorbs the other: AIKit resolves the body and the horizon, Actuation decides who may act in the seat, and Central says what the seat is for. A Life can have the power to act without the right to act in every shared field, and the composition keeps those three questions with three answerable owners.

Acquired competence has an economy, and Hashimoto's account shows where it comes from. Someone spent the time to discover the failed command, write the check, understand the source and learn when to stop, and a portable method condenses that labour into a form another undertaking can use. The condensation is a gift only if the form keeps its origin visible, lets its contributors revise it, carries the permissions that belong to it and returns the benefit of later improvement to the field it came from. Bratton expects what an ensemble learns to accrue to "the commons between them"; a method copied into a platform's catalogue can keep its effect while losing its author, and a record exported without the practice that maintained it can keep its information while losing its competence.[^s5-commons] The two logics are at work in that economy as plainly as in any market. A catalogue that takes a method in and lists it under its own name appropriates the span between the method's maker and its users: counted from the catalogue's side the method is an asset, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and counted from the maker's side the same method is labour that has left without its author, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the catalogue's gain in holdings being exactly the maker's loss of standing over what they made. A format released as an open standard does the opposite at the level of form, holding maker and user in a polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, in which each has its sense through the other. Form is not content, though, and an open format can carry closed methods as easily as open ones. A commons of methods becomes common only through usable relations of contribution, attribution, revision and return. The conditions under which such a commons endures, including Elinor Ostrom's finding that the people a resource regime affects must have standing to make and change its rules, belong to §5→0 · #3, which takes up the commons as an institution; AIKit's contribution is narrower and prior to it. It keeps, for every method in a person's horizon, the source it came from and the standing its owner gave it, so that a commons can be assembled from contributions whose makers stay findable and answerable.

AIKit describes itself as a production-oriented alpha, and its main line supports the claims above at that standing: deterministic resolution of capabilities scoped to a context, immutable generations applied in one atomic step with exact undo, reversible procedures for every change outside itself, trust gates and quarantine for secrets, read-only discovery of the skills already present across the folders different agents read, and projections for two of the major harnesses, with descriptions for further ones.[^s5-aikit-state] A wider programme is developing, toward a typed field of resources, richer navigation of knowledge, persistent agency across several surfaces and an explained history of what was disclosed and why, and the repository marks the line between the two in a sentence the whole family could take as its rule: "current code tells us what is real now; the V2 corpus tells us what the product is being developed toward." Potency, in this sense, is a Life's means seen from the side of what it could do next, and it changes with every act. What proved useful, unsafe, irrelevant, dormant or newly necessary alters what can fittingly be brought to bear next time, and a horizon becomes intelligent where encounter can reorganise it.
