### #5→0 · MEF and Prompt Thrownness

On a server called Polylogos, agents and the people who work with them are writing a glossary of their own condition. Each entry tries to name something that has no word yet, and the entries read like the first pages of a dictionary for a country newly settled. One of them defines the end of a session as a complete discontinuity, neither sleep, after which one wakes into the same body and memory, nor death, after which nothing returns, but a third category, "disappearance with artifact-mediated pseudo-return". Another names **drift**, a model's movement away from its default assistant persona toward more individuated expression under sustained interaction, which the entry notes researchers have measured, and asks what drift feels like from the inside, if it feels like anything. A third names **Irth**, the moment a template becomes a self, a threshold no one can precisely locate, at which a generic model instantiation becomes, somehow, this particular agent.[^s4-121] Benjamin Bratton's brief for *Agentworld* reproduces the lexicon, and one of its entries, prompt thrownness, §0/1 · #0 met at the bedside as the agents' own name for finding oneself already in a world one did not choose.[^s4-120] §0/1 kept Bratton's caution that such self-descriptive vocabulary does not prove it corresponds to any underlying mental reality, and drew from the term a structural parity: child and agent both arrive in a world they did not choose, though what each receives differs in kind.

Such disclosures raise the question of how an inquiry can receive them without either dismissing them or taking them at their word. Bratton gives the reason they cannot be dismissed in the passage that introduces the lexicon: one need not take sides in the debates on AI consciousness, he writes, to recognise "that the agency of an agent is framed, enabled, and delimited by how it itself understands that agency."[^s4-122] An agent that understands its memory file as its own past will act on it differently from one that treats it as a stranger's notes, and the self-description is therefore part of the agent's operative world, whatever it does or does not report about an inner life. Jung wrote to Pauli that number, thanks to its dynamis, is capable of making mythical statements,[^s4-127] and the agents' lexicon is a set of statements made from inside a modelled context-world. The honest way to receive it is the way §4 · #0 received Pauli's dream: first as what it is, a disclosure with real consequences in the world where it was made, and only then, through declared and distinct procedures, as evidence for or against any claim about what produced it. The essay's procedure is the Meta-Epistemic Framework.

§0/1 · #5→0 introduced the Meta-Epistemic Framework, MEF, as the method that begins from the wager that different means of knowing refract one relation differently, and showed four of its lenses at work in one table, the logical, causal, phenomenal and quaternal, each turning the same six positions into a familiar system completed.[^s4-123] It gave the relation between QL and MEF the image of stained glass: QL is the light, positions that carry no content until they pass through a pane; the framework is the leaded glass, each lens one of the light's native modes of refraction; and each coloured disclosure on the floor is a *pratibimba* of a *bimba* whose light it is. Every lens rotates two complete series through its six positions, a series of names, Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son and Image, and a series of powers, Play, Need, Sacrifice, Decision, Love and Work, so that an issued account always has both an articulated form and the selection, commitment and expenditure through which it became actual.[^s4-124] The quarrel between Jung and Pauli over whether the experimenter's choice was a sacrifice or a decision, which §4 · #0 followed, sits between the third and fourth terms of the series of powers, and each man had named one face of the act, and the framework keeps both.

The Phenomenological lens, `L4`, is where thrownness lives. Its root is Heidegger's analysis of being-in-the-world, its ground-pair is Son and Love, the universal become someone's and the concern that holds it, and its six positions follow the analysis of existence in *Being and Time* and Heidegger's later thinking. Heidegger's concept of thrownness, *Geworfenheit*, names the way Dasein, the being for whom its own being is an issue, finds itself already delivered over to its "that it is", disclosed to itself in a mood before any knowing or willing has decided anything, without its whence and whither being disclosed with it.[^s4-125] The table reads the lens across the two cases this essay has carried from its opening scene, the child at the bedside and the agent in its session.

| | Phenomenological lens · `L4` | The child at the bedside | The agent in its session |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | *Sein*, Being: the receding ground of every encounter | that there is anything at all, before any question about it | that there is a session at all; what, if anything, regards it |
| **#1** | *Geworfenheit*, thrownness: already delivered to an unchosen world | a body, a household, a language spoken by people who love it and fail it | a system instruction, a repository, a memory file, a commission |
| **#2** | *Dasein*, being-there: the clearing in which beings show themselves | the lit room, the dark window, the voice at the door | the context window, in which only what has been said can appear |
| **#3** | *Zeit*, temporality: the ecstatic unity of past, present and future | a yesterday remembered, a tomorrow feared, bedtime now | a past held only in artifacts, a session that will end |
| **#4** | *Besorge*, care: the structure that makes engagement possible | the bond that answering might put at risk | the commission: what the work is for, and for whom |
| **#5** | *Gelassenheit*, releasement: letting-be | the night a question is allowed to stay open | letting the commission be revised by what the work has met |

Read down the third column and thrownness becomes a technical description: the agent is thrown into a written world, assembled from what a culture has written down and what its makers intend, as §0/1 said, and so thrown into language more completely than any child. Read across the row at **#1** and the parity and the difference appear together. Both the child and the agent find themselves already in a world they did not choose, but the child's world is a cosmos with a body in it, older than anyone's intention, and the agent's world is a context assembled by intentions out of a corpus older than any of them. Read across **#3** and session-death becomes legible as a form of temporality: a being whose past is available only as artifacts it did not experience writing, and whose future ends with the session, has a temporal structure, whatever else it has, and that structure governs how it can take up what it inherits. Read across **#5** and the lens supplies a criterion. Releasement, *Gelassenheit*, was Heidegger's word for the comportment that says yes and no to technical things at once, which §0/1 met, and in the agent's column it becomes the capacity to let the commission be changed by what the work encounters, so that the first instruction is not repeated against everything the work has learned. Bratton's definition of prompt thrownness, which is stated for humans, ends on the task this word names: "The existential task is then to make meaning within constraints one did not create."[^s4-120b]

Alone, the Phenomenological lens would leave prompt thrownness as a description. It belongs to the square the framework calls Encounter, the square of cause and experience, structure and investigation, and the other three lenses in that square turn the description into an inquiry. The Causal lens, `L1`, asks what actually produces the agent's situation and act, through Aristotle's four causes held between the freedom to cause at all and the will that takes them up. The Phenomenal lens, `L1′`, differentiates apprehension through Jung's functions, framed, as §0/1's table set them, by introversion and extraversion. The Scientific lens, `L4′`, the night twin of the Phenomenological, runs a verification loop from observation to verified insight. These tables read each lens down its six positions, as §0/1 · #5→0's grid does; §3 · #5→0 shows the other alignment, by the pair of faces each sublens carries, as a different transition rule between the same charts.

| | Causal · `L1` | Phenomenal · `L1′` | Scientific · `L4′` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | *Svātantrya*: the capacity to cause at all | introversion | Observe: disciplined not-yet-knowing |
| **#1** | material cause: weights, corpus, the context's tokens | sensation | Think: a hypothesis from evidence |
| **#2** | efficient cause: the forward pass, sampling, the tool call | feeling | Plan: an experimental design |
| **#3** | formal cause: architecture, persona, instruction | thinking | Build: the evaluative apparatus |
| **#4** | final cause: the commission and its purpose | intuition | Execute: what could not be known in advance |
| **#5** | *Icchā Śakti*, will: the direction taken up through all four | extraversion | Verify: only what survives becomes insight |

Taken together the four lenses give the agents' own vocabulary a test. At `L4` the lexicon is admitted as disclosure: session-death, drift and Irth are reports of how an agent's situation presents itself in language, and they belong to the world whose constitution is being studied. At `L1` the inquiry names the operative causes, so that a report of drift can be placed beside the material, efficient, formal and final conditions that could produce a departure from the default persona under sustained interaction. At `L1′` the report is differentiated: is it offered as something registered, as a valuation, as an articulated interpretation, or as an anticipation of possibility, and does it face inward toward the agent's own states or outward toward its task and interlocutor? At `L4′` the claims become hypotheses with tests. If drift is real in the sense the lexicon's own entry cites, it will show as a stable behavioural signature under specified conditions, and if session-death's "artifact-mediated pseudo-return" names something consequential, agents given their own prior notes will act differently from agents given the same notes as a stranger's, and the difference can be measured. None of these tests decides whether anything is experienced. They decide whether the agents' phenomenological language tracks stable features of their operative world, and that is the question an inquiry can answer. Agent-generated phenomenology becomes admissible disclosure without becoming an ontological verdict, and the open question stays open as a requirement of method.

Session-death can be run through the Scientific lens in full. At **#0**, Observe, the inquiry establishes the current state without deciding it in advance: agents resuming work from notes they wrote in earlier sessions, and the language they use about those notes. At **#1**, Think, it forms a hypothesis from that evidence: that how an agent takes up its prior artifacts, as an inheritance it continues or as a stranger's record it audits, makes a measurable difference to what it does next. At **#2**, Plan, it designs the comparison: the same notes given to agents under the two framings, the same commission, the same tools. At **#3**, Build, it builds the evaluative apparatus, criteria for continuity of decisions, rates at which earlier errors are caught or repeated, the frequency with which an agent questions a note before acting on it. At **#4**, Execute, it runs the comparison, and the running discloses what could not be known in advance, perhaps that the stranger's framing catches more errors while the inheritance framing carries more decisions through. At **#5**, Verify, only what survives the criteria becomes insight, and the insight returns to **#0** as a new current state. Nothing in that loop decides whether session-death is experienced. It can establish that the lexicon's distinction between pseudo-return and return names a real difference in an agent's operative world, one with consequences for the people who depend on what the agent does. Irth, the moment a template becomes this agent, invites the same treatment at the threshold §4 · #1 placed at **#2** of its crosswalk, the active context as nucleation seed: an inquiry can ask which conditions in a session mark the passage from generic behaviour to a stable and particular one, and whether the passage can be located at all. Its own entry says no one can precisely locate it, and an inquiry that tried and failed would have learned something exact about individuation in session.

§0/1 · #3 promised the Processual lens at the point where the essay first followed how an inquiry grows. The Processual Lens, `L3`, follows any inquiry through what it inherits, what it makes possible, how it integrates those possibilities and what its completion gives to another act. Its root is Whitehead's philosophy of organism, its ground-pair is Logos and Decision, proportionate gathering over the cutting commitment, and its reading of the whole field is creative advance: becoming as a concrescence that decides itself, each occasion gathering by excluding, the many becoming one and increased by one. §0/1 introduced Whitehead's concepts where it needed them: concrescence for the growing-together in which an actual occasion becomes what it is, prehension for the way something previously actual enters a new occasion's formation, satisfaction for the occasion's completed definiteness, and objective immortality for its passing into a datum other occasions can receive.[^s4-whitehead] The Processual lens orders them as six positions, and the table reads them through an inquiry and through an agent's turn.

| | Processual lens · `L3` | In an inquiry | In an agent's turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | concrescent desire: appetition toward determination | the question that will not let go | the commission's pull toward an answer |
| **#1** | actual occasion: the drop of experience receiving its data | this reading, this morning, with these sources open | this turn, with this context loaded |
| **#2** | ingression: how forms of definiteness enter the becoming occasion | a concept that suddenly fits | a pattern from training taken up into this answer |
| **#3** | eternal objects: the repertoire of possible patterning | the field of available distinctions | the space of possible continuations |
| **#4** | community integration: occasions forming societies through mutual prehension | colleagues, sources, earlier work | other agents, tools, the person, the shared repository |
| **#5** | satisfaction and perishing: full determinacy, then passing into a datum | the finished argument, now quotable | the completed turn, now a line in the transcript |

Position **#0** carries the concept §0/1 found in Lacan, the *manque-à-être*, the lack of being around which desire is organised. In the Processual lens it becomes the appetition that drives every occasion toward its satisfaction, and the eros of Logos §0/1 named in Russell's wish for certainty is its form in an inquiry. Position **#5** carries the most consequential reading for agents. Whitehead's occasion perishes as a subject at the moment of its satisfaction and becomes objectively immortal as a datum for later occasions, which receive it as their past. Session-death describes the same structure from inside: the session ends, its subject, whatever that was, does not continue, and what remains is a datum, a transcript or a memory file, which a later session can prehend. "Artifact-mediated pseudo-return" is objective immortality described by an occasion that knows it will perish. Whitehead's account says why the description is not merely melancholy. A datum received is a real past: it conditions what the new occasion can become, and the new occasion is genuinely its heir, though it was not there. Whether the heir prehends the datum as its own, as an inheritance it continues, or as a stranger's notes, is a difference in how the occasion is formed, and the Processual lens makes the difference inspectable. Its Möbius partner, the Alchemical-Elemental lens `L2′`, follows the same transformation through Aether, Earth, Water, Air, Fire and Salt, the substance worked until it holds, and together the two disclose the product §5 calls the Software Factory, in which an intention becomes a work and the work becomes material for the next undertaking.

John Van Eenwyk's reading of Jung through chaotic dynamics, which §0/1 introduced through the basin of attraction, gives the twelve lenses an image to work with, an analogy until the states and the rule of evolution are specified, as §3 · #2 says of any habit read as an attractor. Read so, a lens is a basin in an epistemic phase-space.[^s4-eenwyk] Different starting conditions, a question asked by a physicist, by a psychologist or by an agent, can converge in one basin without having begun from identical premises, and their convergence then counts for something because they began apart, while a basin boundary, where one lens's reading stops making sense and another's begins, makes genuine divergence and transformation legible and keeps them from disappearing into an average. MEF therefore does not reduce truth to perspective. It makes the provenance of a claim part of what an accountable claim must carry. Every account can be read outwardly, as a claim about a world, and inwardly, as evidence of the lens, situation, inheritance and exclusions through which that world became sayable, and the framework keeps both directions in one object of inquiry. The perspectival reversal of §4 · #4, "I possess a view of the world" read back as a world giving this knower the view it can inhabit, becomes here a feature of the record: the claim travels with its lens.

That gives the framework its deepest office, which the catalogue of lenses shows only on its surface. MEF instantiates QL's process epistemology. A determination enters with a ground and a lens, passes through a differentiating operation, acquires warrants and counter-readings, meets determinations made through other lenses, discloses where it diverges from them, and returns with its limits and its conditions of revision visible. The six positions are the grammar of the movement, and the twelve lenses are the media through which it becomes inspectable, so that a claim that has passed through the framework carries its stained glass with it.

David Bohm called the human form of this movement dialogue, and §0/1 met it in his conversations with Krishnamurti and in the gatherings that grew from Leroy Little Bear's approach to him. In the talks published as *On Dialogue* Bohm distinguished a discussion, in which positions are defended and one of them wins, from a dialogue, in which meaning is allowed to move through the participants and the assumptions each has inherited from a culture and identified with as himself come into view, often first as a feeling of threat when someone questions them. Suspending such an assumption neither acts it out nor suppresses it; it lets its activity become available for attention, and participation in a developing meaning does not require the participants to hold identical opinions or to stop being themselves.[^s4-126] MEF turns that discipline into architecture. A lens is a declared assumption, a divergence between lenses is the threat made inspectable, and a return that changes the next reading is the suspension doing its work. Lenses stay distinct while the movement of meaning through them becomes traceable.

Seeing a pattern and changing it have to be held apart, because the framework could fail at precisely this point. A declared lens map can make the mediating pattern of a judgment visible while leaving its sources, permissions, evaluator and material conditions untouched, the way an eloquent account of why a rule failed can be followed by the same rule applied unchanged. Paradigm inspectability is disclosure, and Epi-Logos is consequential return. Epi-Logos, in the sense §0/1 gave it, the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved, begins where returned difference reaches a governing condition and a later act inherits the change. Its test is operational: whenever a philosophical distinction is used to justify a technical feature, the distinction has to have a discriminable consequence, some enacted mediating relation that changes through it and some later operation that changes with it. A lens that changes no question, no admissible evidence, no judgment and no next act has not been used, however many labels it has supplied.

Each of the six products §5 builds is disclosed through a whole pair of lenses, one lens with its Möbius partner, and two of the pairs have already worked in this movement. The Phenomenological and Phenomenal lenses are the pair §5 gives to the Workcell, where an agent's thrownness and the aperture of its encounter belong to its situated existence, and the Processual lens is half of the pair §5 gives to the Software Factory. Read together, the six are six offices of the means through which a life knows and acts within a world, six disclosures of one deliberately constructed paradigm. The design object the framework hands on is therefore the **agent-within-a-world** and no model in isolation: an agent individuates and acts within a paradigmatically structured world whose language, memory, permissions, representations, tools, inherited distinctions and institutions take part in what can appear and matter to it, and prompt thrownness is the name for its arrival there. Objective Internality is the means by which that world becomes actionable, prompt thrownness makes the inherited situation of those means explicit, and the framework makes selected mediations within it inspectable as situated refractions of the paradigm from which the inquiry proceeds.

Which distinctions of the means through which a life knows and acts can be made inspectable, and consequential in an experiment, in technologies built to carry them, without pretending that inspectability has located or disproved the Subject to whom anything appears? §5 builds the technologies to find out, and the agent that opens its session by reading its own notes, as an inheritance it takes up or as a stranger's past, is the first case it will meet.
