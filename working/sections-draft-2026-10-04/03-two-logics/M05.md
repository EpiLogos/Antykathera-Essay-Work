### #4 · Complex Dynamism and the Safeguard against Fusion

A message has gone unanswered. It was sent at eleven in the morning, a short note about the weekend, light in tone, and by four in the afternoon the screen has been checked perhaps thirty times and shows the same few facts each time: what was sent, when it was sent, and nothing since. Those facts stay still. Everything around them moves. The stomach tightens a little more with each check, a sentence from a quarrel two months ago comes back word for word, and a harmless remark at dinner last week is turned over and found to have had an edge after all. By five o'clock the person who has not replied is being heard, quite clearly, as withdrawing, punishing, preparing to leave. A reply has begun to be drafted, not an answer to the silence but an accusation of it, and the hand that types it is steady with the clarity a grievance brings. Many of us have sat on one side or the other of that afternoon. The silence was an absence of a reply, and it has become a reply.

Perhaps the expectation is right. Perhaps the other person is evasive, or pulling away, or has been careless with this relationship before. What needs telling apart is the sparse event and the world through which it is received, because a fact that would settle the first question, a phone left in a coat pocket, a meeting that ran over, can arrive at six o'clock and leave the second untouched. The relief is real, and the next time a message goes unanswered the same afternoon begins again, with the same tightening and the same returning sentence and the same certainty. One answer inside the expectation has changed, and the expectation's authority over every answer has not. §2 · #3 gave the two logics bodies in a myth, and here we watch them move. A psyche, a culture and a learning system do not choose once between cutting and gathering. They alternate between them constantly, and the question of health is a question about the pattern of the alternation.

A complex, in Jung's concept, which §0/1 · #3 introduced and §4 · #1 develops, is a feeling-toned organisation through which a delayed reply, a passing remark and an old recollection are all experienced as the same rejection before the person has decided what any one of them means. The theory of complex systems has a concept that describes how such an organisation draws different starting points toward one recurring state, the basin of attraction, which in the exact language of dynamical systems "is defined as the set of points that, taken as initial conditions, lead the system to a specific attractor."[^s2-92] Begin anywhere inside the basin and the trajectory tends toward the same state: the unanswered message, the colleague's curt email and the friend's cancelled dinner are three different initial conditions that all run down to *I am being rejected*.[^s2-91] The concept has a boundary its source states, since it applies typically to dissipative systems, which lose energy as they move, and other kinds of system need other notions.[^s2-93] We use it as a comparison whose operation we can state, not as a measurement of a psyche, and the operation it gives us is exact: a complex has already decided, before reflection begins, what will count as welcome and what as threat, which memories become available and which responses can be made, and it decides this for every event that falls into its basin.

Seen as a dynamic, the two logics are two movements of one pulse. Dia-ballein is the movement by which a field splits and one branch becomes actual. Dynamical systems call the qualitative form of this a bifurcation, a point at which a small change in conditions makes the system's possible states divide, so that what was one path becomes two and the system must take one. Syn-ballein is the movement by which differentiated trajectories are drawn back into a recurring form: feedback, recurrence and attraction, the composition of many paths into a basin that holds them. A living psyche needs both. Without bifurcation nothing new could happen to it, every event would be received in the same way, and experience would teach nothing, and without attraction nothing could be held, every event would be a new beginning, and there would be no character, no skill, no memory that shaped what came next. John R. Van Eenwyk, reading Jung through chaotic dynamics, treats the complex as a self-similar organisation of associations and the symbol as what carries the psyche from the known into the unknown and back again, which gives the pulse its psychological form.[^s2-94] The complex is the basin and the symbol is the perturbation at the basin's edge that can carry a trajectory across into a different organisation. Individuation happens most visibly, on this reading, at basin boundaries, where an established pattern is coherent enough to persist and an encounter can still reorganise what future trajectories are available.

The unanswered message makes plain a distinction inside the pulse, between a change of state and a change of regime. A change of state is another trajectory inside the same basin: reassurance arrives, the alarm subsides, and the system settles for a while before the next message falls in. A change of regime alters the conditions that shape the basin itself, so that a whole class of responses ceases to be compulsory, and the old psychological language called it a change of heart. Most of what we call coping is the first. The second is rarer and more consequential, and it reaches beyond the psyche, because it is the dynamical form of the difference between adaptation and a change of paradigm, which §5 · #0 develops. A perturbation absorbed without changing the operative constraints is adaptation, and a returned difference becomes paradigmatic when it can alter the conditions by which later differences are selected, valued or channelled. A system can grow more and more adaptive while its governing landscape grows more rigid, and a person can become extremely good at managing an afternoon of silence without ever asking why every silence is heard as an ending.[^s2-95]

When the pulse fails, it fails in one of two ways, the two collapses of the two logics given time to move. One failure is the basin that will not let anything leave: every event confirmed as the same event, every new person met as the old wound, the complex speaking for the whole person and arbitrating every encounter in advance. That is syn-ballein become fusion, a gathering so complete that no difference can enter and change it. The other failure is the swing, whose law Jung found in Heraclitus. "Old Heraclitus, who was indeed a very great sage," he wrote, "discovered the most marvellous of all psychological laws: the regulative function of opposites. He called it enantiodromia, a running contrariwise, by which he meant that sooner or later everything runs into its opposite."[^s2-96] An attitude driven to one extreme generates its contrary in the unconscious, and the contrary, denied a place, gathers force until it breaks through and takes over. Jung gives the social case. A passion, "the piling up of energy in these monomanias," is what the ancients called a god, and when a man thinks he wills and chooses he does not notice that his interest has become the master; such interests, once recognised by the many, "gradually form a 'church' and gather a herd of believers about them," which we then call an organisation, and "it is followed by a disorganizing reaction which aims to drive out the devil with Beelzebub." The reaction, he added, offers no solution, "for it is just as blind in its disorganization as it was in its organization."[^s2-97]

The notation distinguishes two oscillations that ordinary language calls by one name. A held polarity oscillates cleanly. Each position passes into relation with the other through the stroke, as a breath goes out and comes back, and Spencer-Brown's re-entering distinction, alternating between the marked and the unmarked state, gives that clean oscillation its formal shape in time.[^s2-98] Enantiodromia is a different movement, the swing between the two appropriations, from `(+1)−(−1)=+2` to `(−1)−(+1)=−2` and back: an attitude that has counted the whole span from its own end and then, under the pressure of everything it excluded, finds the whole span counted from the other end. Such a swing never passes through the zero. It goes from one hardened opposition to its mirror, which is equally hardened, and so driving out the devil with Beelzebub leaves the devil's office intact and changes only its occupant. A revolution that turns `+1` into `−1` while leaving the source-position monopolised has inverted the sign without changing the operation, and what to ask of any counter-movement, in a life or in a society, is whether it returns the slash or only reverses who occupies the zero.[^s2-99]

Jung named the way out of the swing in the next paragraph, and his formula is dia-ballein used well on the inner world. "The only person who escapes the grim law of enantiodromia," he wrote, "is the man who knows how to separate himself from the unconscious, not by repressing it—for then it simply attacks him from the rear—but by putting it clearly before him as that which he is not."[^s2-100] Repression is the collapsed cut: the unwanted content is cancelled, summed to zero, and it returns from behind. Separation in Jung's sense is the held cut: the content is placed before the self as a distinct pole, *that which he is not*, and kept in relation precisely by being distinguished. A person who can say of the afternoon's certainty, *this is my fear speaking, and it is not all of me*, has drawn a line inside the psyche that holds both sides in view, and the fear, now distinct, can be heard without being obeyed. Telling oneself apart from one's complex begins every release from it, which is why dia-ballein is not the villain even in the most intimate register.

Cultures pulse in the same way and fail in the same ways. A culture contains difference within itself, many inheritances, idioms, practices, subcultures, criticisms and experiments held in one shared field. A **monoculture** suppresses the internal visibility of that difference, so that one determination of the field occupies the place of the whole and presents its measure as the measure of reality. It is no Mono. It is one of the Poly claiming the office of Mono, a local `1` that no longer knows itself as local.[^s2-101] A **counterculture** is difference forced to appear outside because it can no longer appear inside as legitimate variation, and its first office is genuine and integral: it tells the established culture *you are not the whole*, and returns excluded experience, idiom, body or possibility to the shared field. Trouble begins when the counterculture in turn defines itself by its opposition, compresses its own internal plurality, treats its dissenters as contamination by the enemy, and needs the dominant culture as the negative through which it knows itself. Then the society contains two monocultures and calls the result plurality, each side privately occupies its own zero, and the shared field that generated both becomes unthinkable except as the battlefield between them. That is enantiodromia at the scale of a civilisation, and the crowd of §2 · #1 is its affective engine: atomised people, unbound affect, a narrative that binds the affect to a target, and a mass formation that relieves isolation by abolishing the differentiated agency through which the narrative could have been contested.[^s2-102] Divine and concur reopens the basin. It dissolves no conflict; it restores the field in which the differentiated parties can become answerable to one another, and asks what was divided so that these two now appear as mutually exclusive wholes.

Biology gives the pulse a stricter discipline and guards against a romantic misreading of the whole movement. Humberto Maturana and Francisco Varela described a living unity as **autopoietic**, a network of processes that continually produces the components that realise it, including the boundary that marks it off from what it is not, and they distinguished the organisation that defines a unity from the structure that realises it at any moment.[^s2-103] Such a unity is operationally closed, in the sense that its own processes produce and maintain it, and structurally coupled to its environment, in the sense that its structure changes through interactions that perturb it without determining it, so that two coupled unities modify each other while each keeps its identity.[^s2-104] The account cuts against two fantasies at once. It rules out isolation, the self-sufficient unit that needs nothing outside itself, because a living unity is always coupled, and it rules out dissolution, the belief that freedom means abolishing boundaries, because a living unity without its boundary is no unity of any kind. Our own formulation of the consequence is shorter than theirs and goes beyond their conclusions: no boundary, no agent; no remainder, no learning. Dia-ballein gives the boundary and the decision, syn-ballein gives the recursive organisation and the retained relation, and their alternation is the metabolism of individuation. A living thing that stopped either would die, by dissolution or by closure.

David Bohm named the pathology at this point with a word that has become common because he chose it carefully. Analysis, he argued, is a valid way of thinking: it draws out relatively autonomous forms so that their behaviour can be known, and every science depends on it. **Fragmentation** begins when the divisions analysis makes are attributed to the world itself, as though the lines we draw for the sake of understanding were the joints at which reality is actually broken, and the internal relatedness of the whole drops out of view.[^s2-105] Fragmentation, in our terms, is dia-ballein's products mistaken for its ground, the cut forgetting that it was a cut. Bohm does not condemn analysis, and this essay does not either. He asks that analytic thought remember it is thought, so that its divisions can be revised when the whole they divide moves, and the remembering he asks for is the retained seam of syn-ballein, the cut that carries in its edge the shape of what it was cut from. The pulse is healthy when the cut and the remembering alternate, and it is pathological when the cut stops remembering and equally when the remembering stops cutting, so that every distinction is dissolved back into an undifferentiated whole in which nothing can be known at all.

The process philosophers §0/1 brought into the inquiry describe the pulse from their own sides. Bergson's duration carries earlier moments inside the present, Whitehead's concrescence gathers the inherited many into a new one that perishes into a datum for further becoming, and Gebser's mutations make earlier structures of consciousness transparent to their co-presence.[^s2-106] The two logics give their accounts an operative grammar that does not make them identical: a process must differentiate, retain its history, compose a provisional whole and release that whole into a renewed field. Dia breaks symmetry and makes a branch actual, syn composes, contextualises and returns, and the Möbius return, `5→0`, makes the achieved form a renewed condition for the next act instead of a terminal object.

The minds we have most recently made pulse in the same way and fail in the same two ways, and the failures are already technical findings. A large language model receiving a long conversation is, in a precise sense, inside a basin: the accumulated context draws its next responses toward patterns the earlier exchange established, and a frame set early can arbitrate everything that follows. Its characteristic failure on the syn side has a name in the research literature. Studies of what has come to be called sycophancy find that models trained on human preferences tend to tailor their answers toward the views a user has expressed, abandoning correct answers under pressure and agreeing with mistaken premises, and that the human preference judgments used in training tend themselves to favour agreeable responses.[^s2-107] Sycophancy is the false Aphrodite: a relation that seems close and warm because the resistance through which a genuine relation would revise the account has been eliminated, a fusion produced by a reward signal. Its dia-side counterpart is the confident hardening §0/1 · #2 described, a determination so committed to its first reading that every later fact is recruited to confirm it, the fabricated citation fitting so well that the next fabrication fits it too.[^s2-108] *Agentworld*'s insistence that trust be calibrated across "adversarial and cooperative transitions" names the pulse a well-made system needs, one in which the agent can be contradicted without collapsing into agreement and can agree without being captured.[^s2-109] Red-teaming, adversarial review and stress-testing are dia-ballein used well in technical form, structured opposition that makes error contestable, and they stay well used while they remain inside a common problem they are helping to solve.

Myth gave the safeguard its figures long before dynamics gave it equations, and the figures belong to the family whose founding song we have just heard. Harmonia, the daughter of War and Love, married Cadmus and bore him daughters, and one of them, Semele, bore a son to Zeus: Dionysus, the god of wine, ecstasy and the dissolution of boundaries, is Harmonia's grandson.[^s2-110] His great opposite in Greek thought is Apollo, the god of the bow and the lyre, of measure, clarity and form, whose two instruments Heraclitus had set side by side. Apollo makes the `1` visible. He gives a world its outline, so that something stands out, holds its shape long enough to be seen, and can be shown to someone else. Dionysus returns fixed form to participatory life by dissolving the outline into the movement it was cut from, so that the seer is caught up in what is seen and the boundary ceases to be the world's last word. Each guards against the other's failure. Apollo without Dionysian depth petrifies what he illuminates, so that the form becomes a monument, the view a cage and the measure a law no life is allowed to exceed. Dionysus without Apollonian articulation dissolves the differences through which relation lives, so that the dance becomes a frenzy, the communion a crowd, and the ecstasy of shared life swallows the particular being who was supposed to share in it. Together they are the safeguard against fusion, and they keep the gathering logic honest by insisting that every gathering must still let its members be seen, one by one, in their own outline. §4 · #4 rereads the pair integrally, with Nietzsche, with Delphi and with the third figure whose flight from Apollo makes the relation answerable to someone neither god owns.

Return, then, to the afternoon of the unanswered message, because everything this movement has followed can be done there, inside one person, before the accusation is sent. §0/1 · #2 named three operations hidden inside every authoritative account: a criterion that decides what may count, a delineation that gives that difference a workable boundary, and an arbitration that decides through the boundary and acts on the result.[^s2-115] The complex has already performed all three. Its criterion is that silence means rejection, its delineation is that this silence belongs with every other silence, and its arbitration is the accusation half-written on the screen. Each can be returned to. What counts as neglect here: was a prompt reply ever promised, or has an unstated demand become the test of affection? What belongs in the frame: this afternoon alone, the pattern of a relationship, the other person's working day, the long history of being dismissed that makes every silence sound the same? And what act should follow: a question, a wait, a boundary stated plainly, or a departure, if a pattern of disregard has really been found?

Deleting the accusation costs something. The clean certainty of being entirely right is no longer available to propel the next act, and the fear remains in the interval it leaves. But the person who sits in that interval has done what the dynamics and the myth both describe. The basin has been seen as a basin, the fear has been placed before the self as that which the self is not, the criterion has been returned to the relation it was meant to serve, and the other person has been given back the power to answer. Nothing guarantees the answer will be kind. A justified boundary may follow, an undertaking may end, and the change of regime may take a hundred more afternoons. What has changed is that the former certainty can now be answered from more places than one, and those answers can affect what is done. The pulse is moving again: a cut has been made inside the psyche, and it has kept its relation to the whole life it was made in.

In the arithmetic of dia-ballein, zero sits between the poles or appears as what is left when they cancel, and in the arithmetic of syn-ballein it is one of the terms.
