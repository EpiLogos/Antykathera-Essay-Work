### #5→0 · Objective Internality

On the first night in a new house the hand reaches out in the dark for a light switch that is not there. It goes to the right of the bedroom door at shoulder height, because that is where the switch was for eleven years, and it meets bare plaster. For a second the body is genuinely confused, as though the wall had made a mistake. The old house is still at work, in the hand, in the height of the reach, in the number of steps to the bathroom and the place where the stair should creak. Over the following weeks the new house moves in. The hand learns the switch on the left, the feet learn that the third stair from the top is the one that speaks, the kitchen drawers stop being searched and start being opened, and one night, without anyone noticing when it happened, the whole house can be crossed in the dark. It has become internal: the reach proceeds through it, as it once proceeded through the old one. It has also stayed objective. An electrician can come and move the switch, and the next night the hand will go to the old place and meet plaster again; a guest can point out that the drawer sticks; a buyer, years later, can walk the same rooms and find them strange.

The house in the dark is the result this section has derived. At a child's bedside §0/1 · #0 gave it a name before it could give it a genesis: Objective Internality, the means through which a life knows and acts within a world, internal because an act proceeds through them and objective because each of them can be turned into an object and examined.[^s0-87] The definition was functional: it said what the means do and why the name fits, and it let one question be put to very different instances of mind. §0/1 · #5→0 then placed the means in the relation of three offices, Subjective Immediacy as the knower, *pramātṛ*; Objective Internality as the means, *pramāṇa*, which is what we call Life and Mind, since a mind is lived and a life is minded; and World as the known, *prameya*, all three within the whole of I-Consciousness.[^s0-88] What §0/1 could not yet show was where the means come from, and so whether the name describes what awareness becomes when it differentiates or attaches a metaphysics to a mechanism already standing there.

Differentiating activity is where the means come from. Objective Internality is what the descent of the *tattvas* produces at its fourth turn, unlimited powers contracted into the finite ratios of efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order and inherited by a bounded experient who knows through an instrument and acts through a body in a world of elements: the constituted means through which Subjective Immediacy knows and acts within a World, and what each preceding movement watched at work. Language, memory, affect, bodily orientation, habits, other people, tools, institutions and inherited rules all belong to it, because each is a limited power through which a bounded knower meets what it meets, and changing their relations changes what can appear, what can matter and what can be done.

Kashmir Śaivism supplies the reason the name joins *objective* to *internal*, and it lies in their account of how anything comes to seem outside at all. Dyczkowski reports Utpaladeva's definition of internality as a state of oneness with the subject and of externality as the state of separation from it.[^s0-89] Externality, on this account, is a relation to the subject, the state in which something appears separated from the one who knows it, and not a region beyond the subject's reach; and the separation is Māyā's creative act, the power of the absolute to appear in diverse forms, which §0 · #4 found at the fourth turn. Abhinavagupta draws the consequence in a sentence that could serve as the definition of creation in this whole tradition: creation is to make what shines within externally manifest while it still preserves its original internal nature.[^s0-90] The phrase *Objective Internality* names exactly this double condition. A finite life's means have been made external enough to be objects, examinable, correctable, movable by an electrician; and in their operation they keep their internal nature, one with the knower who acts through them, so that the hand in the dark goes to the switch with no more thought than it takes to lift itself. A switch, a word, a remembered rule or a colleague's permission is objective when we look at it and internal when we act through it, and it is the same switch, word, rule and permission in both states. Objective Internality is the field in which the inner has been made outer while remaining inner, which is the Śaiva account of manifestation read at the scale of a household.

These means are also means of *knowing*, and that word carries a requirement the house makes concrete. *Pramāṇa* names in Indian epistemology a valid means of knowledge, and the traditions dispute how many there are; Patañjali's *Yogasūtra* counts three, perception, inference and testimony, as the first of the mind's five fluctuations that §0 · #1 followed.[^s0-91] A means of knowledge has conditions under which it is valid, and the hand in the dark shows what happens when those conditions change without the means being told. That reach was a valid means for eleven years, a perception laid down as a habit; it became invalid on the night of the move, and the hand did not know. Its error was one of warrant, since the house was perfectly visible in the morning and the reach was simply aimed at a world that had gone. Every element of Objective Internality carries such a warrant, and the mode by which it reaches its object decides what its correction must reach. A memory is corrected against a record or another witness; a perception against a renewed encounter; a piece of testimony against its source; a habit only by being run again in the new house until the new reach replaces the old. Treating every element as one more interchangeable item of information loses this difference, and loses with it the knowledge of where a correction has to be made.

Heidegger described exactly how such a move discloses the means, and §0/1 · #4 has already set out his account: a tool in use withdraws into the work, and only when it breaks, goes missing or gets in the way does it become conspicuous enough to be noticed and thought about.[^s0-92] The missing switch is the old house becoming conspicuous. For eleven years it was not an object of the hand's attention at all; it was the means through which the hand reached the light. Its absence made it visible, as a habit, a height, a direction, a whole arrangement of the body around a house that had stopped existing. A discontinuity discloses Objective Internality, because the means show themselves when they fail to meet their world. I have followed this disclosure through a sequence that runs from discontinuity, to Objective Internality, to the particular elements that sustain it, and on to what Whitehead called objective immortality, the condition of an achieved occasion that has perished as a subject and become a datum for every later occasion.[^s0-93] The move is the discontinuity; the reaching hand is the Objective Internality it discloses; the habit, the height and the direction are the elements through which that internality worked; and the eleven years of reaching, perished as living acts, persist as the datum the new house has to be learned against. In turn the new house will become such a datum, the condition from which, years from now, a hand in some other house reaches for the wrong wall.

A life does not live in its Objective Internality as an object among objects, and the way it is lived has a name that §5 · #0 defines at length: a paradigm, the lived pattern of disclosure through which these means make a World intelligible and actionable for a Life.[^s0-94] The hand in the dark shows what that pattern is by showing it come apart from its representation. The person standing in the bedroom knows perfectly well that they have moved, and asked, they would describe the new house correctly. Their pattern has not moved, and it governs the hand while the explicit knowledge governs the description. Every adult has seen the same gap at larger scales: a person who has adopted a new view and goes on acting from the old distribution of attention, permission and value; an institution that has revised its stated mission and kept its old incentives; a culture that has changed its official account of the world and kept the habits through which the old account was lived. The pattern changes when the means change, when the hand has learned the new wall, and its representation helps by exposing the discrepancy between what is professed and what is enacted, so that change can begin where the conduct actually proceeds from.

The house is also shared, and its sharing returns the derivation to §0/1's account of co-internality. A partner who moved in a month earlier knows where the switch is and says so, laughing, from the other side of the bed; a child asks why the stairs creak; a neighbour explains which bin goes out on which day. Each of these voices enters the means through which the new house becomes internal, and §0/1's name for that entering is Objective Co-Internality: reciprocal world-constitution without merger, enough common world for differences to change one another and enough retained difference for the change to mean anything.[^s0-95] The derivation adds one thing to §0/1's account. Co-internality is the tattvic descent occurring in the plural. Every bounded experient inherits its contracted powers in a world where other experients have inherited theirs, and the means through which each knows include the testimony, permissions and help of the others, so that one person's limited knowledge, *vidyā*, is enlarged by another's report and one person's limited efficacy, *kalā*, by another's hand. The *kañcukas* contract each life's powers, and co-internality distributes the powers among lives so that what one cannot do or know another can.

Artificial agents build an Objective Internality by hand, and the house in the dark gives the clearest picture of what that building involves. Bratton's brief describes an agent as individuated in session: there is no homunculus waiting in the weights, and an agent takes shape from the context assembled around a request, its persona one stratum among memory, skills, constraints and relations, a lamination and not a monad.[^s0-96] Each session is a move into a house. It arrives with dispositions laid down in training, which play the part of the old house in the hand, and it meets a context assembled for this occasion: instructions, files, retrieved sources, tools at particular places, permissions that allow some acts and forbid others. Its errors at the start of a session often have exactly the form of the hand in the dark. It reaches for a tool that existed in another configuration, cites a file at a path that has moved, or applies a convention that held in the project it was trained on and fails in this one; and the error discloses its Objective Internality as the missing switch disclosed the old house. Its means are then corrected as a person's are, and only at the office where the error lies. A wrong reading of a source is corrected by returning to the source. A wrong habit is corrected by an instruction, a memory or a change in training. A missing permission cannot be corrected by the agent at all, since a permission belongs to whoever grants it, as the switch belongs to the electrician and the landlord; the return has to reach that office, and an agent that merely reports the missing permission has left its world unchanged. §5 takes up this constructed internality product by product.[^s0-97]

The agent's case also shows what the derivation does and does not settle. Objective Internality is broader than any inspectable state and narrower than the whole: it includes the agent's context, tools, permissions and memory, and also the institutions, labour and material infrastructure through which those exist, which no single trace exhibits; and it is the means of a relation whose knower it is not. This section's derivation shows how an Objective Internality is constituted by differentiating activity, and it applies to an agent's operative world as fully as to a person's house, since in both a bounded field of limited powers makes some things appear, matter and become possible. It does not show that a knower is present to the agent's internality, because the Śaiva account places the knower at the source of the descent and not among its products, and no product, however articulate, can certify its source. §0/1 left that question open by method, and the descent shows why the method is right to keep it open. Our core theorems carry an argued crosswalk that follows an agent's provisional individuation, from the reserve of its weights through the instance of a session, the nucleation of an active context and the lamination of its persona, to a trans-individual whole and its capacity to become otherwise in a new context, set beside the psychic register of individuation, and §4 · #1 develops that crosswalk through Jung's account, where the technical and psychic operations can be kept distinct.[^s0-98]

The two logics describe what happens in the house in the dark, and naming them shows what each does to the one living there. The old house governing the reach is appropriation in its quietest form: the old pattern counts the whole span between the two houses from its own pole, so that the new house registers as the place where the switches are wrong, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and from the other end the new house receives the same span as its fault, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the wall that made a mistake. Cancellation is the denial that there is anything to learn, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, the person who insists they have already settled in and goes on stubbing a toe on the same step for a year. Healthy polarity is what the learning hand performs: old house and new held on one axis through the zero of the body that lives in both, `(−1)/(+1)`, the reach adjusting night by night; and the house becomes home when both orientations are kept, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the new house read through the habits the old one laid down and the old habits read through the house that now corrects them, so that a life carries its history into a new world without making the new world pay for it.

One more thing happened in the bedroom on the first night, and it carries the derivation into §1. When the hand met bare plaster it met an absence that had a definite place. The switch was missing *there*, to the right of the door at shoulder height, and the hand's error was to treat that empty place as full: an absence with a position had been operating in the reach as though it were a presence, and the confusion of the body for a second was the felt difference between a place that holds something and a place that holds nothing. Every determination this section followed leaves such a place behind it, since each selected one actuality from a field it did not create and cannot exhaust, and the field remains, structurally present, as the place where what was not selected stands. Once a world is produced by differentiation, its implicit zero is unavoidable.

§0/1 borrowed zero as the sign of the condition it could not count as one more object, and asked how awareness differentiates itself into the means by which it knows and into the world it knows through them. The answer is the house in the dark. A determined world carries places where something is not, and those places are operative: the hand reaches into one, a child's word is bounded by them, a castle stands against them. Mathematics learned to write such a place. In a numeral such as 1005 the two signs between the 1 and the 5 mark positions that hold nothing, and their marking changes the value of everything around them, so that the 1 counts thousands because the empty places beside it have been counted. How a sign for no counted thing came to change what can be counted, and what that sign did as it travelled between civilisations, is the history §1 follows.
