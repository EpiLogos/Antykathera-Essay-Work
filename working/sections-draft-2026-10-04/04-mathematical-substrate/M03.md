### #2 · Mark, Re-entry, Iterant and Complex Orientation

D. R. Kaprekar taught mathematics at a school in Devlali, in western India, for his whole working life, and played with numbers by hand. He announced the game that made his name at a mathematical conference in Madras in 1949, published it that year as "Another Solitaire Game", and returned to its destination in a note of 1955 called "An Interesting Property of the Number 6174". One of his games takes a minute to learn. Choose any four-digit number whose digits are not all the same, leading zeros allowed. Arrange its digits from largest to smallest, arrange them again from smallest to largest, and subtract the second arrangement from the first. Then do the same to the answer, and to the answer after that. Take 2026. From it we form 6220 and 0226, and their difference is 5994; from 5994, 9954 minus 4599 gives 5355; then 5553 minus 3555 gives 1998; then 9981 minus 1899 gives 8082; then 8820 minus 0288 gives 8532; then 8532 minus 2358 gives 6174. Do it once more and nothing changes: 7641 minus 1467 is 6174. The number has come home and stays there. Begin anywhere else and the same thing happens. Every four-digit number with at least two different digits arrives at 6174 within seven subtractions, and only the ten numbers made of one repeated digit go elsewhere, falling in a single step to 0000, where they stay.[^s3-kaprekar]

The routine is a small machine that feeds its own output back into itself, and that structure is what this movement studies. Mathematicians call such a process an iterated map: a rule `f` applied to a state gives the next state, `x₁ = f(x₀)`, and the next state is fed back, `x₂ = f(x₁)`, so that every determination becomes the condition of the one that follows. A state the rule returns unchanged, `f(x) = x`, is a fixed point; a state or set of states toward which trajectories tend is an attractor; and the attractor's basin is the set of starting points that lead to it.[^s3-basin] Kaprekar's routine has exactly two attractors, both fixed points, and it divides the whole space of four-digit configurations into their two basins without remainder. Everything that contains no difference falls to 0000; everything that contains any difference at all is drawn to 6174. The claim can be checked exhaustively by running the routine on all ten thousand starting strings: the longest journey takes seven subtractions, 2,184 strings need all seven, and 0000 and 6174 are the only numbers the routine leaves in place.

I have developed my own reading of the routine as the `4+2` of QL working as a dynamic, and I keep it among the deep sources of the matheme; it repays careful statement, because what it finds and what it offers are different in kind.[^s3-6174-mapping] At the fixed point the six terms of the sixfold appear together. The descending arrangement, 7641, gathers the four digits toward their peak, and I place it at **#0**, the implicate ground; the ascending arrangement, 1467, unfurls the same digits from their minimum outward, and I place it at **#5**, the implicate synthesis; the four digits of the result, 6, 1, 7 and 4, stand at **#1** to **#4** as the explicate four. The routine at its fixed point then states, in arithmetic, that the explicate four are the measured gap between the two implicate orderings:

$$
\#0-\#5=\#1\,\#2\,\#3\,\#4,\qquad 7641-1467=6174.
$$

The two implicate terms are the same four digits read from opposite ends, and their difference regenerates the four as a new configuration that sorts back into the same two readings, so that at the fixed point the six run in place, the ground and the synthesis ceaselessly producing the four as their differential and the four ceaselessly sorting back into ground and synthesis. The inversion in the reading pleases me because it is exact: the number greatest in value acts as ground, and the number least in value acts as synthesis, the way the Möbius return `5→0` turns what was completed into what begins. The seven-step bound has its own reading, as one full cycle of six positions plus the twist that closes the fifth back into the ground, and it meets a number our core theorems had already found inside the sixfold: summing the six positions as binary values, the four pairs `0+1+2+3` and the two singles `0+1`, gives seven, so that the structure contains one more than it counts, and the seventh is what the surface does, its return. The arithmetic adds further resonances, which I record and do not lean on: the digits of 6174 sum to 18, three times six, and `6174 = 18 + 18² + 18³`. Every identity in this paragraph is exact and checked. The mapping of the six arrangements onto the six positions, and the reading of seven as cycle-plus-return, I offer as a conjecture, because the routine is a fact of decimal arithmetic with four digits, its three-digit cousin settles on 495 instead, and no identity here proves anything about QL.

What the routine shows without any reading at all is the point that matters most, because it is a structural theorem and needs no numerology to carry it: difference is the condition of circulation. A configuration with no internal difference has nothing for the routine to work on and falls to zero in a single step; a configuration with any internal difference, however lopsided, is drawn through a bounded journey to the one arrangement whose difference reproduces itself when it is weighed against itself. My mapping names the two basins Anuttara and the Self, the void-frame to which undifferentiated configurations return and the configuration to which every differentiated one is drawn home, and the naming is mine; the partition it names is Kaprekar's arithmetic.

Kaprekar's operation is subtraction, the operation by which dia-ballein appropriates a span: `(+1)−(−1)=+2` counts the whole relation from one pole, and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` counts it from the other, the healthy polarity `(−1)/(+1)` hardened into a quantity one end can own. Kaprekar's subtraction measures the span between the greatest and least orderings of the same digits, and never lets either ordering keep the result: the measured span is handed back as a new whole and sorted again from both ends, so every reckoning from one pole is immediately re-read from the other. Held this way, inside a re-reading that returns the result to both orientations, the subtraction stops hardening into opposition and settles into a configuration in which the measured polarity is the configuration itself. The cut works inside a relation that keeps returning it to the whole, which is syn-ballein's form, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, with dia-ballein's measuring stroke operating inside it. A subtraction allowed to keep its answer would run one way into a standing quantity; a subtraction returned each time to both ends of the same digits comes to rest in a self-reproducing difference.

§0/1 · #5→0 left this section the dynamics of conditioning to work out, and the routine gives them their exact form. There a script was an articulation that has become a condition of further activity, an achieved selection that decides the next encounter before anyone decides again, and its danger was named **frozen conditioned will**, a selection that has forgotten its formation and presents its course as necessity. An iterated map is a script in the mathematical register. Each determination is fed back as the condition of the next, the trajectory is carried by its own previous results, and wherever it started it is drawn into a basin from which, once arrived, it does not move, and it can come to rest in two ways. At 0000 it rests because nothing in it differs from anything else, so the operation finds nothing to measure; at 6174 it rests because its difference, weighed against itself, returns the same difference. A life can settle into a script of either kind, a habit so uniform that no encounter registers, or a pattern so well balanced that every encounter confirms it, and both are fixed points. Basin vocabulary is exact in the arithmetic and has to be carried into persons with its conditions attached. The research literature applies the notion of an attractor typically to dissipative dynamical systems and notes systems, the open Hamiltonian ones, for which it is meaningless, so calling a habit an attractor is an analogy until someone specifies the states, the rule of evolution and what is measured.[^s3-basin-scope] The analogy is precise about one thing: a fixed point is a determination that has become the condition of its own repetition, and that is what frozen conditioned will is.

Re-entry is the name George Spencer-Brown gave to the same structure at the root of logic, and §0/1 · #3 met it there. In *Laws of Form* a distinction can re-enter the space it distinguishes, and an expression that contains itself has no stable value: it fits neither the marked state nor the unmarked one, and Spencer-Brown gives it an imaginary state, a state of time rather than of space.[^s3-spencer-brown] §0/1 · #3 read that alternation as the clean oscillation of a held polarity, and the hardened opposition as the oscillation arrested. Elementary arithmetic shows exactly what time adds. Write the self-containing expression as an equation in the two values 0 and 1, `r = 1 − r`. No value satisfies it, since choosing 0 makes the right-hand side 1 and choosing 1 makes it 0, and a static logic can only call the equation a contradiction. Give the operation a next step instead, `r(n+1) = 1 − r(n)`, and the same rule runs without difficulty: starting from 0 it produces `0, 1, 0, 1, …`, and starting from 1 it produces the same sequence shifted by one step. The argument and the result now occupy different moments, and each moment is definite. Delay has supplied a domain in which the operation can run; it has not discovered a hidden Boolean value that satisfies the equation. That is the precise sense in which re-entry makes time: the paradox of a form that is its own negation becomes, given a successor, an oscillation that carries its last state into its next. Francisco Varela took another road in 1975, adding to the calculus an autonomous third state that arises through self-indication and giving rules by which expressions containing it reduce, so that self-reference enlarges the arithmetic instead of unrolling into a sequence.[^s3-varela] A temporal alternation and a third value are two responses to one difficulty, and each must say what it keeps and what it adds; we follow the temporal one, because it leads to phase.

Write the alternation with signs instead of bits, `+1, −1, +1, −1, …`, and the two logics appear on it at once. Held, the sequence is dia-ballein's healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, given time to move in, each value passing into its counterpart through a common axis the way a breath goes out and returns. Cancellation sums it: add the terms in pairs and every pair gives `(−1)+(+1)=0`, so the summed account reports a zero in which the whole oscillation has disappeared, along with which value came first. Appropriation freezes it: stop the sequence at one value and reckon the span from there, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from the positive end and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the negative, and the oscillation has hardened into a standing opposition in which one pole holds the whole span. Both collapses lose the same thing, and the loss has a mathematical name. What the sum erases and the arrest freezes is the phase, the information about where in its cycle the oscillation stands. Seen from one step the sequence is `[+1, −1]`; seen from the next it is `[−1, +1]`; the two views are of one process, shifted in time, and keeping both is keeping the phase.

Louis Kauffman made that pair of views into an algebra. He calls an ordered view of an alternating process an iterant, and he reads `[a, b]` and `[b, a]` as one process seen with a shift of one time step; the two views, taken together with the shift, become the square roots of minus one.[^s3-kauffman] The construction can be checked with two small matrices. Let `D` be the operation that keeps the first entry and changes the sign of the second, and let `η` be the operation that swaps the two entries:

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Each undoes itself, `D² = η² = I`, but their order matters, since `Dη = −ηD`: sign-then-swap and swap-then-sign give opposite results. Compose them as `J = ηD`, and

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad J^2=-I,\qquad J^4=I.
$$

On a pair `(x, y)`, `J` gives `(−y, x)`; a second application gives `(−x, −y)`; four applications bring back the start. `J` behaves exactly as multiplication by the imaginary unit `i` behaves on the plane, a quarter-turn whose square is a half-turn, `i² = −1`. The swap alone has period two, and alternation alone would give back nothing but a back-and-forth. The quarter-turn has been built from two operations that do not commute, a sign and a shift, and it is their failure to commute that turns alternation into rotation. Kauffman adds that there is often a choice in making an iterant representation, so no single construction is privileged, and the argument needs only that one exists and can be checked.

§0/1 · #1 recalled Wolfgang Pauli reflecting on `i`, a symbol with no place among the ordinary numbers, which gives mathematical theorems a simple and distinct form and which, in the dream he was reading, united a pair of opposites into a whole. We can now say how. The imaginary unit keeps the phase that both collapses of the oscillation discard: it is alternation together with its shift, the two views of one process held as a single operation. On the complex plane, multiplication by `i` carries a determination a quarter-turn from the real axis, and four quarter-turns return it:

$$
1\;\longrightarrow\; i\;\longrightarrow\;-1\;\longrightarrow\;-i\;\longrightarrow\;1.
$$

Opposition along one signed line has become orthogonal relation across two axes. The line from `+1` to `−1` that dia-ballein drew is still there, now as the diameter crossed by a half-turn, and the plane adds the direction perpendicular to it, in which the two poles can pass into each other by turning instead of by colliding. This is the exact bridge from dia-ballein's line to complex orientation: the signed seam, unrolled in time, becomes phase, and a return to the origin arrives with its orientation transformed and its passage recorded, where cancellation would have arrived at nothing. The rotation also shows how a fourfold can be generated from one relation instead of being assembled out of four independent contents, which is what §4 · #2 needs when it comes to Jung's quaternity: four functions read as one orientation turned through its quarters keep what rotation keeps. Our core theorems find one more resonance here, which I offer. In the base frame of §3 · #1 the implicate three and the explicate four stood at right angles and the five joined them, `3² + 4² = 5²`; here the imaginary unit is rotation into the dimension perpendicular to the real line, and `i² = −1` generates the negative pole from a turn. Read together, both say that manifestation requires a perpendicular dimension, a turn into what the line cannot contain, which generates the line's own opposite. The orthogonality is exact in each case; their identification as one structural fact is the conjecture.

Complex numbers separate two aspects of a change that ordinary speech runs together. Write a nonzero complex number as `z = re^{iθ}`, a length `r` and an angle `θ`. Multiplying by `e^{iφ}` changes the angle to `θ + φ` and leaves the length exactly as it was: the point moves and its distance from the centre does not. This is the local mathematics of the spinning wheel and the thread running true that §0/1 · #5→0 took from Bohm's conversation with Krishnamurti and made its criterion of truth.[^s3-wheel] The thread corresponds to the modulus, retained while the argument turns, and the rotation shows that fidelity is compatible with motion and can consist in an invariant carried through it. The mathematics carries the image's structure and stops short of its meaning, since the modulus is the invariant of one specified transformation and defines nothing about truth; what it establishes is that an invariant relation can be borne precisely through transformation, so that a determination which keeps returning to its origin need not have stood still to be faithful.

A second fact about rotation matters more for what comes after. A path of nonzero complex numbers that comes back to its starting value does not reveal, at its endpoint, how many times it went around the origin. Once around, twice, or not at all, the final number is the same. Follow the angle continuously along the path, though, and lift it from the circle to the real line, where it is free to keep increasing, and the difference between its final and initial values is an exact multiple of a full turn, `2πk`, with `k` the number of windings. The winding number exists, and it belongs to the path together with its lift; it is not a property secretly stored in the final point. A process that claims to remember its return has to keep the relevant history somewhere, in the lift, in a counter, in the record of its passage, because the arrival alone has forgotten it. The inversion of the two parents in §3 · #1 showed the same lesson from the other side: reading `(0/1)/(1/0)` into its inverse and back restores every term, so the arrangement records which of two phases is active and never how many times the reversal has been performed. §3 · #4 builds a whole surface on this distinction, where every local return carries a lifted displacement on an infinite cover.

In this essay the word *complex* has a second life, and the dynamics of conditioning bring the two lives side by side. Jung's complex, which §0/1 · #3 met through Van Eenwyk's dynamical reading and which §4 · #1 develops into individuation, works as a feedback loop of exactly the kind the routine performed: an ambiguous encounter is selected through the complex's existing criterion, the selection recruits affect, affect weights attention and memory, and the response that follows produces fresh material read through the same criterion, which it confirms. A delay in a friend's reply is received as rejection, the hurt colours the next exchange, the next exchange goes badly, and the going-badly proves the rejection; coherence increases while the range of what can be encountered narrows, and the organisation settles into a fixed point that every new arrival confirms.[^s3-complex] Read through the two logics, the complex appropriates. Each new encounter is counted from its own pole, the friend's silence entered as a `−2` against the self's `+2`, and the counting confirms the pole. The complex plane and the psychic complex share a word and, I hold, a structure, which lies in what the plane adds to a line: **the psychic complex is an oscillation arrested at one pole; the complex plane is an oscillation kept in phase, its two views joined by a turn.** What lets a psychic organisation change, in Van Eenwyk's account and in Jung's, is the symbol, which disturbs the categories through which the gathering occurs and opens a different regime instead of adding one more confirming item to the old one, much as a perturbation can carry a trajectory across a basin boundary into another basin. A literal dynamical model of a particular complex would owe its state variables, its rule and its observables; the structure of the loop needs no model to be recognised.

Our machines run the same loop in plain view. An agent that writes, reads what it has written and writes again feeds its outputs back as its inputs, and §0/1 · #2 followed one consequence through a legal brief in which a fabricated citation, once it fitted the argument, directed the fabrication of the next. A fitting account is a basin: once an interpretation has decided what a supporting source should say, each step makes the interpretation harder to dislodge, because coherence itself has been recruited to the error. Routine, complex and agent share these dynamics. An achieved determination re-enters as the condition of the next, the trajectory is drawn toward an organisation that confirms itself, and the organisation can be either the empty fixed point of an output that no longer registers its sources or the balanced fixed point of an account in which every new piece fits. The oscillation teaches the remedy: keep the phase. A process that retains both views of its own alternation, what it asserted and what that assertion was set against, keeps the information that lets a return arrive transformed, where a process that sums its alternations or freezes them at one pole has discarded it. For an agent that means keeping, beside each claim, the source it was set against and the alternative it excluded, so that a later step can read the claim through its other orientation instead of only confirming it.

The line between what is shown here and what is read here runs as follows. The routine's behaviour, the Boolean recurrence, the iterant matrices, the complex rotations and the winding number are derived in their own mathematics. That the routine is the dynamics of conditioning, that the imaginary unit is retained phase, and that the psychic complex is an arrested oscillation are argued, each through the operation it shares with the mathematics. My mapping of the routine onto the six positions, seven as cycle-plus-return, and the identification of the Pythagorean right angle with the turn into the imaginary are offered. Complex orientation solved a limit of the number line by enlarging the space in which numbers live, and mathematics makes the same move wherever a relation meets an obstruction, which is where the projective constructions begin.
