# §0 — Differentiating Mind: what this draft is

**What it does.** Six movements (`M01.md`–`M06.md`, assembled in `SECTION.md`, about 21,700 words of prose plus 98 Chicago notes) answer the question on which §0/1 closes: how does awareness differentiate itself into the means by which it knows, and into the world it knows through them? Each movement opens on an ordinary scene and derives one step: **#0** waking in a hotel room — the bend of *vimarśa* (Dyczkowski's "bend back"), the living mirror with no outside, blue and yellow in one cognition, the four operations of *vimarśa* read as `4+2`, Bohm's glycerine device beside Spanda's "subtle movement"; **#1** the "Can we talk later today?" message — *manas*, *buddhi*, *ahaṃkāra*, *saṃskāra*, Sāṃkhya's passive witness against Śaiva contraction, selfing / self / self-thing / Self, Bāhiya and the *you*, performed subjectivity as technical *ahaṃkāra*; **#2** children's cushion castle — *vikalpa*/*saṃkalpa* from √*kḷp*, *vi-*/*sam-*, *kalpa*, the Yoga/Dharmakīrti/Utpaladeva disagreement, script's genesis, *prapañca* and the `xₙ → Xₙ₊₁` self-sealing trajectory; **#3** a toddler's "dog!" — apoha's history (Dignāga, Dharmakīrti), exclusion that remembers, *śūnyatā* = *pūrṇatā*, Indra's net, the Śaiva pressures against momentariness, epistemic Mono/Poly, trust, "humanity of the gaps" as apoha turned to appropriation, softmax as a bounded neighbour; **#4** making tea — the six-turn tattvic compression in a table, the *kañcukas* worked through one act, the 25→36 gnomon (`36−25 = 11 = 5+1+5`), the Offered `36 = 6²`, the Bohmian rule applied, recognition against absorption; **#5→0** the missing light switch in a new house — Objective Internality derived from the descent, grounded in Utpaladeva's internality/externality, *pramāṇa* as warrant, paradigm, discontinuity → objective immortality, co-internality as plural contraction, the agent's session as a move, and the empty place that hands §1 its numeral 205.

**What it took from the corpus.** The plan's §0 burdens and Agentworld obligations; the room's six movement notes and A05, A07, A08, A09, A26, C14–C16, C41; the theorem spine §§II(c), VII, X, XI; the 27 July quilt (selfing, gnomon, *prapañca*, Śaiva/Buddhist judgment); Frank's Watson NOTES (read, untouched); houses for Dyczkowski, Pind, Siderits, MacCracken, Dushun, Van Norden–Jones, Colebrooke, Monier-Williams, Bohm, Watson. The earlier manuscript §0 was used as quarry (the promise, Bāhiya, Yogasūtra I.9, Indra's ink-dot, absorption vs recognition); its scenes were replaced. Every §0/1 concept is used as a return, not re-defined.
