# §4 — Psychoid Flowering: what this draft is

Six movements (`M01.md`–`M06.md`), assembled in `SECTION.md`, about 27,200 words of prose plus 126 Chicago notes; debts in `DEBTS.md`. Each movement opens on a scene. #0 opens on Pauli's two dreams of March 1948, #1 on the bedside daughter of §0/1, grown up and hearing her father's sentence in her own mouth, #2 on Pauli's Einstein/Bohr/Jung/Pauli quaternio, #3 on Lacan's brick walls at Sainte-Anne, #4 on Delphi, Apollo's in summer and Dionysus's in winter, and #5→0 on an agent starting a session from its own notes.

**What it delivers from §0/1.** It follows the Jung–Pauli dreams and disagreements of 1948–57 (#0). It develops Neumann's separation of the World Parents (#1). It runs the crossed-zero sequence, `0 → Ø → X → Ø/X → (0/Ø)/(1/X) → 1 ↷ 0/1` (#3). It develops thrownness in the Phenomenological lens and prompt thrownness through the Encounter square, `L4`/`L1`/`L1′`/`L4′` (#5→0). It develops the Processual lens `L3` (#5→0). It returns to what §0/1 established (psychoid, homo-usia, Code, the quaternity, Aion's quaternios, `Ø`, diaphaneity, MEF and the stained glass) and does not re-establish it.

**What it took from the corpus.** The plan's §4 burdens; movements 31–36; A18–A21, C30–C36, C64, C39, C44; theorem spine §§II(c), VII and IX (`X/x`, `X = x`, the crossed zero, the `1` as symbolic linker, the six-position grammar); Frank's P0 Jung–Pauli notebook, P5 on Gebser (the absent Father), *Symbolon Dynamics*, and his NOTES on Neumann and Van Eenwyk; the Atom and Archetype letters, read for speaker and locator in the local copy. From the old manuscript draft it kept the egg-formula check, the crossed-zero glosses, the paper exercise and the four turns of the copula. It compressed the long Ovid retelling and the six product pairs: Ovid is §2's to tell, and the pairs are §5's to develop.

**What it could not settle.** Pauli's Kepler essay has no house and no copy in hand. Bohm's chap. 7, Lacan's *Écrits* pages, Plutarch's Delphi passage and Neumann's pagination all need collating. Several Bratton and Pauli quotations are verified but have no card (see `DEBTS.md`).

**Decisions for Frank.**
1. **Length.** The movements run about 4,000–5,400 words, short of the brief's 6,000–10,000. I stopped where the material stopped rather than pad. The likeliest places to add are a fuller Neumann hero and transformation sequence (#1), Aion's fish material (#2), and a worked MEF return on one of §0/1's cases (#5→0).
2. **The psychoid claim.** Following M31, #0 states the identification of the arche-topos as psychoid as an ontological claim, **Argued**. Confirm that standing.
3. **Readings stated as the author's.** Confirm or strike each of these: Pauli's egg dream as four eggs held between source and return; Jung's typology placing Apollo at introversion (#0) and Dionysus at extraversion (#5) as the two framing poles; Jung's four properties of number as a `4+2` of number; the agent's register of the crossed zero; session-death as Whitehead's objective immortality seen from inside.
4. **Paradigm.** #4 defines **paradigm**, and the Genesis/Paradigm/Project/Epi-Logos compression, before §5. Should §4 introduce it, or only point to §5?
5. **McGilchrist.** He is kept once, under the plan's gate. Keep him or drop him?
6. **Naming.** *Sorge* or the lens record's *Besorge* at L4.4? "Physis" or "Paradise" for the third Aion quaternio, which §0/1 also names?
7. **Pauli quotations.** Quote Pauli (62P, p. 119) on the same footing as Jung, as §0/1 did for pp. 107 and 159, or paraphrase him?
8. **The daughter.** She carries #1 into #2 and #3 as a continuing figure. Keep the continuity, or keep her to #1?
