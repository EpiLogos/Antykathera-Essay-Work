# §3 — Mathematical Substrate: what this draft is

**What it does.** Six movements (M25–M30, `#0`–`#5→0`, about 22,700 words of prose and 56 notes), written after the finished §0/1. Each opens from a scene: a deer on a lane at dusk, an accordion's bellows, Kaprekar's subtraction done on the year 2026, railway lines meeting at the horizon, a runner's lap counter, the travelling jigsaw. The draft returns to what §0/1 established (`0/1`, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, `2+2²`, the torus quotient and `π₁`, the dia-ballein chiralities, re-entry, Gödel as `1/0`, the 180°→360° turn) by stating only the result, and it delivers what §0/1 promised §3: the eight determinations as the construction and deconstruction of `0/1` (honouring Eckhart's *in quantum*); the full Spanda derivation of the harmonics, with three independent routes to `9/8`; "rotation, projection and double covering"; the dynamics of conditioning; the crossed zero's mathematical life; the atlas of charts under Atlas's name; and the arche-topos derived from QL, with Jung's requested inventory of numbers given as a table. Each movement ends with an audit sentence that marks what is Derived, Argued and Offered. The section stops at the arche-topos and hands psyche to §4. The Antikythera mechanism is not mentioned.

**What it took from the corpus.** It draws on the core theorems §I–VI, VIII, IX (partly) and XI, A18, A15–A17, A19, C20, C30, C31, C52, C64, the Matheme `process/`, `spanda/` and `definition/harmonics-and-recount` records, the 6174 mapping source, and the earlier manuscript §3, which was used as quarry for the projective, atlas, iterant and torus computations. Source houses used: Hatcher, NIST DLMF, Kauffman, Spencer-Brown, Varela, Priest (2010, 2018), Kapsner, Scholtz, Strang–Herman, Daza, ITER, Kaplan, Sheffer, Bradley–Terry, Dutta, Bratton, Jung–Pauli. All arithmetic was checked, including an exhaustive run of Kaprekar's routine.

**What it could not settle** (see `DEBTS.md`). Several works have no source house: Kaprekar's original paper, the Stern–Brocot reference, a Bloch-sphere text, the reviews by Siderits and Westerhoff, a quaternion text, a tokamak field-geometry source, the physics text on standing waves, and Spinoza's *Ethics*. The authorship of the 6174 text is unclear (its house lists no author). The movements run 3,400–4,600 words, below the brief's 6,000–10,000. I did not pad them. "The…" sentence openings are still at 16–25%, mostly where a mathematical object is the true subject.

## Decisions for Frank

2. **New argued claims.** Each needs accepting or striking:
   - the **mediant** `(0+1)/(1+0)=1/1` as the exact operation of the First Spanda's bridge, with the Stern–Brocot field generated between `0/1` and `1/0`;
   - the projective swap `[a:b]↦[b:a]` as the space of `0/1` and `1/0`, with `0/0` excluded;
   - the **processual chain read as a double cover** (Möbius band, the quaternions' 720°);
   - pitch class as the running track's `ℝ/ℤ`;
   - Kaprekar's subtraction as dia-ballein inside a syn-ballein re-reading;
   - the psychic complex as "an oscillation arrested at one pole" against the complex plane as one "kept in phase";
   - the numbers-inventory table in M30.
3. **6174.** It is placed as the opening of M27 and carries Offered status. Confirm the placement and the authorship.
4. **Third Spanda (`137`).** The old manuscript §3 used it, but it lies outside both the theorem spine and the Matheme field, so this draft leaves it out. Should it come back?
5. **Harmonic naming conflict.** The theorem and A18 name the adjacent pairs Being (0,1), Becoming (2,3) and Knowing/unKnowing (4,5), with folds Essence, Constitution and Text–Texture. CLAUDE.md's plugin note instead names Being as the adjacent family, Becoming as the mirror family and Knowing as the offset (1,2)(3,4)(5,0). The draft follows the theorem.
6. **Naming `#5`.** The draft uses Quintessence, and calls `∞/dx` the turn where the determination is realised. The theorem table says "Realisation" (ledger item 1.9).
7. **Placement.** The technical atlas sits in M28 and Atlas's return in M30. The plan had the atlas at #4/#5→0. The Bohm–Krishnamurti wheel returns briefly in M27, giving only the mathematics; the ledger had confined it to §0/1.
8. **Structure.** Movement headings are `### #n · Title` with `M25`–`M30` anchors, and notes are numbered `[^s3-1]`–`[^s3-56]`. Status is marked by one audit sentence per movement, with no inline labels.
