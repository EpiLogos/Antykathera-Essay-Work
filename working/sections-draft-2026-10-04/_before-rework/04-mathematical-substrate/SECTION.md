## §3 — Mathematical Substrate: From 0/1 to the Arche-Topos

<a id="M25"></a>

### #0 · Eight Determinations of One Relation

Most of us have walked home along a lane at dusk and stopped because something ahead of us moved. For an instant there is no deer yet, and no one watching one either: there is a halt in the walking, a change somewhere in the field, a relation that has not yet divided into a watcher and a thing watched. Then we are here and something is there, a darker shape against the hedge, and its appearing is known before anything about it is known. "What is that?" The question already asserts that something has appeared steadily enough to be asked about, and the first answers, a sack, a dog, a stump, are held loosely, ready to be wrong. The shape gathers itself, goes still, gathers again; whatever it is has force, and our attention tightens and slackens in time with it. It lifts its head and becomes a deer, one of the kind we have seen before and this one, now, with a notch torn out of one ear. It looks at us. I am here, it is a deer, and between us runs a regard that brings the deer nearer to a *you* than an *it*, because it is reading us as we read it. An ear turns by a fraction, and that small exact movement carries the whole wood behind it, the season, the road the animal crossed to get here, everything the moment is too small to hold. Then it is gone. Later someone asks what we saw, and we tell them, and in the telling we notice that the one who saw appears nowhere in the story.

Eight turns have passed through one act of knowing, and none of them added a new thing to the lane. We did not assemble the deer out of a halt, a shape, a question, a force, a kind, a gaze, a flicker and an anecdote. Each turn made the same encounter more determinate and more answerable, and the last turned the achieved account back toward the one it could not contain. Spinoza's letter to Jelles gave us, in §0/1, the principle on which such turns run: a figure has its shape by the limits that mark where it ends, and determination is negation.[^s3-1] A determination is a limit set within a relation, so that something becomes *this* by what it is set against; an act of knowing is a sequence of such limits, each keeping what the previous one made available while adding its own. Quaternal Logic writes the sequence of that act as eight determinations of one relation:

$$
/=-/-\;\longrightarrow\;0/1\;\longrightarrow\;?/!\;\longrightarrow\;-/+\;\longrightarrow\;X/x\;\longrightarrow\;AM/IS\;\longrightarrow\;\infty/dx\;\longrightarrow\;1/0.
$$

The traversal is my own derivation, and it carries everything this section builds.[^s3-2] Its six interior determinations are the qualitative body of the `4+2` that §0/1 counted from the binary and its self-application, two single terms and their four ordered pairings; the parent stroke `/ = −/−` and the return-switch `1/0` stand on either side of that body as the threshold before any count and the turn after the count is complete. Eight therefore counts the turns of one passage, while six remains the number of positions the passage moves through. The sixfold is the generative body, and the eightfold is that body traversed from before its first distinction to its return, which is why detaching one binary from the sequence, or treating each as a replacement for the last, would destroy the passage it belongs to.

| Turn | Determination | What it does | What stays the same through it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent relation | `/ = −/−` | holds two unassigned marks in one stroke: relation before the terms carry values | the relation has a condition that cannot appear as one more product of the relation |
| **#0** Ground | `0/1` | awareness and phenomenon: "I am here; something appears; the appearing is known" | the ground is immanent to every mark without becoming another mark or a hidden object behind it |
| **#1** Definition | `?/!` | questioning and asserting held as one act | every assertion carries the question that makes it accountable; every question already takes a provisional mark |
| **#2** Dynamis | `−/+` | withdrawal and extension: difference acquires force | the two poles remain one differentiating movement |
| **#3** Pattern | `X/x` | determining capacity and its indefinite particular: force becomes recognisable as kind | the instance makes the capacity legible and never exhausts it |
| **#4** Context | `AM/IS` | first-person presence and third-person sayability, met through the second person | knower, means and known remain one appearing-act without becoming interchangeable |
| **#5** Quintessence | `∞/dx` | inexhaustible horizon and exact local difference | exactness never becomes possession |
| Return-switch | `1/0` | the achieved determination turns toward the ground it presupposes | the limit is generative: `5→0` renews the cycle without cancelling what was determined |

Before the lane divides into watcher and watched, the halt itself is a relation with no named terms. In §0/1 we built that relation from the simplest marks there are: a dash here and a dash there, an oblique stroke drawn between them, and the discovery that the stroke drawn through the sign of equality writes non-equality, so that `=` and `≠` are one operative mark in two settings. There the stroke served the copula. Here it serves as parent of the whole traversal, the cut that already holds what it distinguishes before anything distinguished has been given content. Logic has an exact neighbour for a single cut able to generate a whole field of relation. In 1913 Henry Sheffer showed that Boolean algebra can be founded on one binary operation alone, and the operation that now carries his name, the stroke read as "not both", makes the result easy to check by hand. Write `p∣q` for "not both p and q". Feed one input to both places and the stroke negates it, `p∣p = ¬p`. Stroke the stroke and conjunction returns, `(p∣q)∣(p∣q) = p∧q`. Stroke the two negations and disjunction appears, `(p∣p)∣(q∣q) = p∨q`. Every truth-table can be written from negation, conjunction and disjunction, so every truth-table can be written in strokes alone, and Robert Kaplan tells the same story through the earlier arrow of "neither…nor", which does the same work from the other side.[^s3-3] The neighbour is exact at one point and different at another. It is exact in showing that a single relating cut, repeated and arranged, generates a complete field of determinate relations without importing any second primitive. It differs because Sheffer's stroke operates on values already given, true and false, under a fixed table, whereas our parent stroke stands before any value has been assigned; its two marks are `−` and `−`, the same unassigned sign in two places. What Boolean logic shows at the level of connectives, QL claims at the level of the act: every determinate relation the traversal produces presupposes the stroke, and the stroke never appears among the relations it produces as one more of them.

§0/1 earned the first named determination, `0/1`, at length, and we need only its result here. `0` writes the open, unobjectifiable fact of awareness, Subjective Immediacy, the One that cannot enter its own count; `1` writes the appearing mark and with it the manifest All; the stroke is the relation through which the real becomes readable without being exhausted by its readability. At dusk it is the bare circumstance before any question: we are here, and something has appeared to us. Appearing happens in awareness and awareness is met only through appearing, so the relation comes before any picture of two finished substances on either side of a wall, and that is why the ground can be immanent to every mark without turning into another mark or into a hidden object stationed behind the scene.

"What is that?" The second determination, `?/!`, holds question and assertion as one act read in two directions. To ask what something is already asserts that it has appeared stably enough to call for an account, and to assert what it is keeps open whether the assertion runs true. Definition is therefore double: it opens the thing to determination and it determines, and it stays answerable to whatever called the mark forth. At the lane, the sack, the dog and the stump are assertions made in the grammar of a question, each one offered so that the next look can refuse it. The six investigative questions of §0/1's quaternal lens, *why*, *what*, *how*, *which*, *where* and *why-for*, unfold from this one determination; each is a question carrying a provisional answer about where the next mark should fall. One distinction needs keeping in view here, because the same glyph returns later in this section with another office. At **#1** the pair `?/!` is the living question–assertion of definition. When §3 · #1 reads the six positions through proportion, the inverse `1/0` at **#3** will be found incomputable and *undecidable as question or assertion*, and the theorem writes that undecidability with the same glyph. Both mappings are real, and they concern different things: here a judgment's accountability, there the arithmetic's refusal.

At `−/+` the relation acquires force. One side behaves as reserve, contraction and withholding, the other as issue, release and presentation, and the positive is the ground's own self-relation appearing as extension. Breath gathers and lets go; attention contracts around a word and opens into its sense; the shape on the lane gathers and goes still. The two moments remain co-present as one pulse, and the Spanda teaching we met in §0/1 has a name for that pulse, the subtle movement that goes nowhere and yet manifests as every motion. §2 derived the two logics that can account for such a polarity, and the dusk lets us watch them work in a few seconds. Held as dia-ballein's healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, the pulse keeps its axis through zero: the animal's stillness and its movement, our approach and our halt, are opposed positions on one line of tension, and the tension is what lets us read the scene. Collapse the axis by addition and the encounter cancels, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: "it was nothing", a dismissal that balances the account by erasing the event. Collapse it by subtraction and one pole claims the whole span. Reckoned from our side, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, the whole relation becomes the animal's threat to us; reckoned from the animal's, the same span reads `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and we are the danger it bolts from. Our `+2` is the deer's `−2`, one span charged from opposite ends, and either way the oscillation that let both of us stay still and look has hardened into an opposition in which one party must win. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps what both collapses lose. The animal's stillness can be read through our stillness and ours through its, two orientations of one encounter, and the force between us stays a relation in which each is learning the other.

Force becomes recognisable as kind at the fourth determination. In `X/x`, capital `X` is the presiding capacity, the type or anything-by-which something can be read, and lower-case `x` is its local temporal face, the indefinite particular that algebra writes when it does not yet know which value it has. The notation is mine and works in the algebraic register and, at once, in the mythematical, musical, narrative and psychic registers the matheme opens.[^s3-4] One letter in two cases keeps the instance from being severed from the capacity it manifests, while the change of case keeps either from replacing the other. The small `x` also keeps the sign of multiplication: an instance can recur and compound, and the mind says "another one of those". At the lane, *deer* is the capacity and this notched ear is the instance, and the recognition carries continuity and correction together, since the capacity is what lets a new instance be recognised and also what lets an apparent instance fail the test, as the stump failed and the deer passed. Pattern is where force becomes kind, rhythm, memory and thought. §4 follows `X/x` into individuation, where a particular life becomes transparent to the capacity it does not exhaust; the relation it follows there is already complete here.

The animal looks back, and the fifth determination, `AM/IS`, brings the encounter into person. `AM` is lived first-person presence, "I am"; `IS` is what can be stated in the third person, "it is"; and the slash is the second-person relation through which they meet, the *you* in which an *it* can answer. §0/1 placed grammar's three persons in the Trika's three offices, *I* in the knower, *it* in the known and *you* in the mediating relation, and the dusk shows why the second person belongs at the slash: the moment the deer reads us, our account of it has to make room for an account it is giving of us, and the encounter becomes context. Context in this sense is the lived *where* of an encounter, the world, body, concern, speech, memory and task within which something can appear at all, and it is total, because nothing appears outside some horizon, and incidental, because the horizon does not ordinarily stand forth as one more object. Through `AM/IS` the knower, the means of knowing and the known, *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa* and *prameya*, remain one appearing-act and stay distinct within it: the deer is present to us through our looking without becoming our looking, and the looking is ours without becoming us.

An ear turns by a fraction. At the sixth determination, `∞/dx`, the horizon that no finite mark contains meets the minute difference through which change and local reading become exact. `dx` is no longer the instance `x` but the infinitesimal difference of the calculus, and the determination says that every appearance is a true local ratio of what exceeds it, precise as relation and partial as expression. In the calculus, differentiation yields exact local change: the functions `x²` and `x²+7` both have the derivative `2x`, and the derivative is right about each of them at every point. What differentiation drops is the additive constant. Integrating `2x` therefore returns a whole family, `x²+C`, one function for each constant, and only a further condition, such as the value of the function at zero, selects the member from which the derivative was taken; the textbook theorem states exactly this for antiderivatives on an interval.[^s3-5] The derivative was exact throughout. What it lacked was information it had never retained. The missing `C` is the formally required provenance that local change cannot reconstruct, and neither a mystical surplus nor a defect of calculation is needed to explain it: a determination can be exact without containing the whole condition from which its exactness was taken. That is the claim of `∞/dx`. The whole is present in the part as the way the part runs true to what it cannot enclose, and the horizon is not hidden inside the flicker of the ear as a smaller copy of itself.

A great deal of learning in our own machines proceeds by local difference, and the same structure returns there with consequences: a model is improved by following the derivative of a loss, one small exact step at a time, and preference training compares pairs of answers and learns only how much one is preferred to the other. In the model of paired comparison that Bradley and Terry set out in 1952, only the ratio of two items' strengths enters the probability that one is preferred, so every common rescaling of the strengths leaves the predictions unchanged.[^s3-6] Like the constant of integration, the common level is invisible to the data, and whatever settles it is supplied from outside the comparison. §5 follows that invisible level into the training of agents, where it turns out to be occupied by datasets, annotators and institutions before any model meets a request. We have already met this kind of fact in the calculus: an exactness that does not reach its own ground.

The last turn, `1/0`, directs the achieved determination toward the condition it presupposes, and what it meets there is the incomputable, where it had hoped for an object called "the ground". In ordinary arithmetic one cannot be divided by zero, since no number multiplied by zero gives one, and the native notation keeps that refusal in view at the very place where it would be convenient to hide it. The mental cannot find the foundation of the mental, because every act of finding already occurs through it. In §0/1 we watched Gödel's second theorem give this orientation an arithmetical precision, a consistent theory asked to account for its own ground answering with the one sentence it cannot prove. At dusk the turn is quieter. The story of the deer is complete and accurate, and the one who saw it is absent from it, because the seeing was the condition of the story and not an episode within it. The Śaiva sources call the turn *pratyabhijñā*, recognition, which is the opposite of acquisition: the achieved account becomes transparent to the relation through which it arose, and that recognition is the return `5→0`, the turn by which the completed passage comes back as ground for the next.

§0/1 promised that these eight would honour Meister Eckhart's constitutive approach through our own construction and deconstruction of `0/1`, and we can now say what the construction and the deconstruction are. Kurt Flasch restores the weight of Eckhart's primary determinations, Being, Oneness, Truth and Goodness, as constitutive, active in whatever is, is one, is true or is good, and Eckhart's *in quantum*, "insofar as", says how such a determination is present in its bearer: the just person, insofar as just, is Justice itself.[^s3-7] The eight determinations are QL's constitutive determinations of one relation, and each is the whole relation *in quantum*. The walker insofar as questioning is the relation as question; the shape insofar as gathering is the relation as force; the deer insofar as recognised is the relation as pattern. The traversal from `/ = −/−` through `∞/dx` constructs `0/1`, building determinacy turn by turn, so that by the sixth turn the bare relation of awareness and appearance has become an exact local reading against an inexhaustible horizon. The return-switch deconstructs it in the strict sense of the word, taking apart what had been built together without destroying any of it: `1/0` loosens each achieved determination from its claim to stand alone and returns it to the relation it expressed, and nothing determined in the lane is lost when the walker recognises that the seeing was never one of the things seen.

Among themselves the six interior determinations also have a grammar, and reading it shows why the body is neither a ladder nor a catalogue. Taken in adjacent pairs they form three harmonics. **Being** joins Ground and Definition, `0/1` with `?/!`: a field is present and can be called to account. **Becoming** joins Dynamis and Pattern, `−/+` with `X/x`: force becomes intelligible as recurrence, and recurrence stays alive only as force capable of a further instance. **Knowing/unKnowing** joins Context and Quintessence, `AM/IS` with `∞/dx`: lived predication becomes honest only when its horizon exceeds every situated claim, so that what is known carries its unknown as the condition of knowing more. Across these forward harmonics run three complementary folds, pairing positions whose numbers sum to five. **Essence** folds **#0** with **#5**, conscious circumstance with unbounded differential depth; **Constitution** folds **#1** with **#4**, answerable definition with the world in which someone asks and asserts; **Text–Texture** folds **#2** with **#3**, the felt weave of force with its readable pattern, which is why §0/1's *textus* and *contexere* belonged together. One pair, **#2** with **#3**, appears in both readings, and the arithmetic says why: among the adjacent pairs (0,1), (2,3) and (4,5), only (2,3) also sums to five. Dynamis and Pattern are the hinge at which Becoming and Text–Texture coincide, the place where force turning into kind and the weave turning into a readable figure are one event, and it is also the place, as the next movement will show, where the count crosses its own diameter.

This essay opened by asking for diaphaneity, a showing-through in which perspective becomes context, and §0/1 gave that demand a first geometry: the triangle's angles summing to 180 degrees, the square's doubling them to 360, and the square read as two triangles in diametric co-opposition, so that context needs at least two chiralities and the excluded triangle becomes the shadow. QL's counting logic now derives that geometry formally. Formal QL begins at **#1**, the first explicit position, and returns at **#0**, and when the numbers are assigned to the positions they do not match them:

| Position | **#1** | **#2** | **#3** | **#4** | **#5** | **#0** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Number | 1 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 4 | 5 |
| Office | Definition | Dynamis | Pattern | Context | Quintessence | Ground |

The explicate triad 1, 2, 3 sits at **#1**, **#2** and **#3** exactly where its face value places it. Then the offset begins. Zero goes not to **#0** but to **#4**, Context; four goes to **#5**, completion; and five descends to **#0**, the implicate ground. Each number of the implicate triad finds its home at the position that names its function, and the mismatch is the structure.[^s3-8] Its first consequence is arithmetical and can be checked at once. The three explicate numbers give the same total by addition and by multiplication, `1+2+3 = 1×2×3 = 6`, the first perfect number, equal to the sum of its proper divisors. Admit the zero that sits at **#4** and the two operations part company: `0+1+2+3 = 6`, while `0×1×2×3 = 0`. Added, zero changes nothing and lets the count stand; multiplied, it voids the whole product. In my own words for it, zero is the power of the name: to add a name changes nothing, and to multiply by the Nameless voids all names. Context has exactly these two powers over what it holds. It de-fines, permitting each perspective its boundary and letting the count stand, and it con-fines, placing every perspective inside a frame that can void its claim to stand alone.

The offset also carries perspective into context. At **#1**, **#2** and **#3** the numbers count the three persons in sequence: the first person, the *I*, the knower, the *here*; the second, the *you*, the means, the *between*; the third, the *it*, the known, the *there*. Their glyphs, from §0/1's table, are the line, the angle and the triangle, and the triangle's 180 degrees are the sum of a determinate perspective, three views composing one figure. At **#4** a voiding takes place, written by the zero that sits there: the exclusive claim of each view is emptied as all three become available together, and the triangle opens into the square's 360 degrees. At **#5** the three are reckoned as one appearing-act, recognition, the `1/1` that the next movement will write as one hundred per cent. At **#0** that achieved one returns to the relation of ground and mark from which the persons arose. The first pass is sequential, three perspectives counted one by one as a story unfolds; the second is one recognition performed in three operations, void, one and `0/1`. The passage is therefore `3:3`, three answering three, and at the same depth `3:1`, three answered by one.

Drawn from the count, the figure shows something the angle sums alone did not. Place the four explicit pairings of the binary on the compass in the order the count acquires them, `00` at North, `01` at East, `10` at West and `11` at South. The step from East to West does not move to the adjacent quarter. It leaps 180 degrees across the diameter, and the leap is the inversion of `01` into `10`, of `0/1` into `1/0`, already running in the middle of the first pass, a local phase-flip before the global turn `5→0` that the whole matheme names. North, East and West bound a triangle. Adding South and joining the boundary in compass order, North, East, South, West, makes the square, and the edge from East to West, which bounded the triangle, now runs through the square's interior as a diagonal shared by two triangles, North–East–West and South–East–West, each the other's inversion.[^s3-9] What had been the limit of a perspective has become a seam inside a context. That is the formal content of the claim that context needs at least two chiralities: the first triangle's boundary survives as the hinge by which it meets its mirror, so its claim to be the whole figure is voided while its edge is kept. Ordinary Euclidean measure does not derive the personed reading; the geometry is exact on its own terms, and the reading of its triangle as three persons and its square as their contextual holding is the essay's. Through it the Trika becomes quaternary: knower, means and known stop being traversed one after another and become jointly available as one appearing-act, each still distinct, on a surface where the situated viewer, the world viewed and their mediation can become mutually legible. No view from nowhere has been installed. The square is the context of three situated views and the place where each can see the shadow of its own.

Spinoza belongs at this point on larger ground than his letter. His *Ethics*, set out in the order of the geometers, was the Western proof that metaphysics and the theory of knowledge could be made to move by demonstration, definition to proposition to scholium, and our construction takes up that ambition while reversing its direction.[^s3-10] Spinoza demonstrated from a substance whose attributes are given at the outset; QL derives from an act of knowing that must count itself among what it derives, so that the geometry here has to include the geometer. Self-inclusion requires at least these eight determinations. A relation before its terms, a ground–mark circumstance, a question that asserts, a force, a recurring kind, a personed context, an exact local difference against an unbounded horizon and a return: take any one away and the act either cannot begin, cannot be answerable, cannot recur, or cannot come back.

Four joints of the traversal carry it into the rest of the mathematical substrate. `0/1` holds the seam of ground and manifestation, which the Spanda equations will read from both ends. `X/x` gives determining capacity a recurrent local face, which re-entry will set in motion. The turn from **#3** to **#4**, the triangle opening into the square, makes perspective into context, and the projective and topological movements will rebuild that turn in larger spaces. `∞/dx` gives exact local difference an explicit relation to the horizon it cannot enclose, and the torus and its infinite cover will make that relation a surface one can travel on. A traversal this complete still needs a generator, an account of how a single relation produces its own sixfold body and reads it back, and the Spanda equations supply it.

In audit: the traversal and its order are derived within QL's declared operations; the Boolean derivations, the antiderivative theorem, the counting identities and the compass geometry are derived in their own mathematics; the readings of Sheffer's stroke as a neighbour of the parent relation, of the lost constant as provenance, and of the square as the contextual holding of three persons are argued.

### #1 · The Spanda Equations and the 4+2 Count

An accordion breathes through its bellows. The player draws the two ends apart and air rushes in across the reeds; the player presses them together and the air is driven out across them again, and in a well-played tune the listener cannot hear the change of direction, because the phrase runs straight through it. Every phrase is made of two movements that go opposite ways, and the music is the relation of the two, one breath that opens and closes without ceasing to be one breath. When the player reaches the end of the bellows' travel the direction has to turn, and the turn is where the instrument is most alive and most at risk, since a clumsy reversal breaks the line while a good one carries it forward with something gained. I have long thought of the Self in these terms: the Self leaning into manifestation and the manifest leaning home, always already whole beneath the swing.

In §0/1 the Spanda teaching of Kashmir Śaivism gave that swing its classical name. *Spanda* is the pulse of consciousness, a subtle movement that goes nowhere and yet manifests as every motion, and Mark Dyczkowski, reconstructing the school's teaching from the *Stanzas on Vibration* and their commentators, follows a single act of it through will, knowledge and action, where the first moment of the will coincides with its last, so that the completed act comes back to the intent from which the next one rises.[^s3-11] We read that act there as a `0/1` and its return as `5→0`. QL writes the pulse natively in two equations I call the Spanda equations, which read one sixfold from its two ends.[^s3-12] The first is horizontal: it follows the relation from ground through distinction to integrated return, and it is a reading by proportion. The second is vertical: it takes the achieved whole and accounts for it, discovering the sixfold inside it, and it is a reading by count. Everything later in this section falls out of holding the two readings against each other, because their difference generates the relation that choosing either alone would lose.

Before either equation can be read, the slash-forms it uses need their proportional meaning, and that meaning is easiest to see by placing them on the six positions. The previous movement placed the four pairings on a compass and §0/1 gave the six positions their glyphs. Read now through proportion, each position holds a fraction built from the two marks, and the fractions behave very differently under ordinary arithmetic:

| Position | Slash-form | Under proportion | What ordinary arithmetic does with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | `(0/1)/(1/0)` | the relation of the two orientations; unfolds into `0/1, 0/0, 1/1, 1/0` with the singles `0` and `1`: the whole `4+2` | holds a term with no value; the native reading treats it as a relation |
| **#1** | `0/0` | the indeterminate, every ratio held at once | no unique value: every number times zero gives zero |
| **#2** | `0/1` | the expression, computable and docile | zero |
| **#3** | `1/0` | the inverse, incomputable | no value: no number times zero gives one |
| **#4** | `1/1` | the ratio resolved, one whole | one, or one hundred per cent |
| **#5** | `(1/0)/(0/1)` | the inverse relation of the two orientations; the whole in inverse phase, `4′+2′` | as at **#0**, read in the other direction |

The middle column is the theorem's; the right-hand column is arithmetic anyone can check. In a field of numbers, multiplying by zero always gives zero, since `(0+0)x = 0x+0x` forces `0x = 0`. The equation `0x = 1` therefore has no solution, which is why one divided by zero has no value, and the equation `0x = 0` is solved by every number, which is why zero divided by zero has no single value.[^s3-13] The two failures differ, and the table keeps them apart. At **#1**, `0/0` fails by excess: it can be any ratio, and the theorem reads that excess as the indeterminate, the capacity for every proportion, which it marks with the percentage sign as the glyph of potency. At **#3**, `1/0` fails by absence: the determinate mark divided by the ground yields nothing a number can name. The theorem locates this failure at Pattern and nowhere else, and reads it with the glyph `?/!`, because the system cannot decide whether `1/0` is a question or an assertion. Formal identity, the recognition of *what* a thing is, rests here on a foundation that does not compute: the figure cannot be measured against the ground, and the finite cannot be reckoned against the infinite, which is the Gödelian point reached at the level of a single position. This `?/!` at **#3** is an undecidability, a different office from the living question–assertion of definition at **#1** in the previous movement, and the two mappings must be read each in its place.

Two findings follow from the table, and both carry weight in what comes after. First, the parents contain the children: the relation at **#0**, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, unfolds into all four slash-forms together with both single terms, so the ground bears the whole `4+2` as its own relational interior, a seed carrying the full tree, and the relation at **#5** bears the same whole in inverse phase. Two parents carry one child in mirror image, and the Śaiva vocabulary of §0/1 names them exactly: *bimba*, the original, and *pratibimba*, its reflection. Second, the relation leans: the forward orientation `0/1` settles into a value and the backward orientation `1/0` stays permanently open, so the relation between ground and mark is not symmetric but handed. That handedness is the structural reason consciousness can never wholly catch itself, since every catching conceals what it catches *from*, and the reason it keeps trying, since the concealing drives the next catching. Read through the Indian analysis of knowing, each slash-form is a complete act compressed into notation: the numerator is the knower, the slash the means, the denominator the known. `0/1` is the void knowing the mark through the slash. `1/0` is the mark attempting to know the void through ratio, and the attempt does not compute.

§0/1 introduced a sign whose mathematical life the table now lets us follow. There we wrote `Ø`, the crossed zero, for the first occlusion: the zero of the Subject with the mediating stroke fused into it, so that the means of seeing passes for the one who sees. The slash-forms show the same two marks, a zero and a stroke, in a different arrangement. In `Ø` the stroke lies across the zero and belongs to it, and the result looks like a single thing, the bound self. In `(0/1)/(1/0)` the stroke stands between the two orientations of the relation, holding them apart and together, and the result is legible as a relation. The difference between the two arrangements is the difference between occlusion and recognition written in a single sign, and §4 follows the full sequence by which the fused stroke comes undone and becomes visible as mediation.

The First Spanda equation carries the horizontal movement from ground to integrated return:

$$
0=(0/0)\;\longrightarrow\;\frac{0/1}{1/0}\;\longrightarrow\;[T_0]\;\longrightarrow\;[T_1]\;\longrightarrow\;\left(\frac10+\frac01\right)_{\!\mathrm{QL}}\;\longrightarrow\;\frac11=1=100\%.
$$

Each term is a position of the unit read through proportion. The bare ground `0` reads itself and opens the indeterminate, `0/0`, every possible ratio held at once. The indeterminate differentiates into the ratio of ratios, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the ground's own AND/OR form unfolding the child it always contained; this is the first cut, the *inter-legere*, the reading-between, of nothing with nothing. Then come two directed passages. `[T₀]`, the emanative arc, moves from `0/(0/1)` toward `0/1`: the ground takes expression as its denominator and works toward expression, figure generated from ground, silence becoming articulate. `[T₁]`, the reversionary arc, moves from `(1/0)/0` toward `1/0`: the inverse takes the ground as its denominator and works back toward recognition, figure acknowledging the ground that sustains it. The two arcs meet in the bridge, `1/0 + 0/1`, where the directed movements recombine and discover that they were one circuit, and the circuit resolves into `1/1`, the ratio that has found itself whole. My shortest statement of that resolution is that to be one of oneself is to be oneself wholly is to be one hundred per cent of what one is.

A subscript declares the bridge's register: its plus sign composes two orientations; it is not the addition of two quotients, since one of the quotients has no value and ordinary addition would destroy the term before the composition could take place. A notation that asks for this much needs to show that the composition it names is a definite operation, and arithmetic happens to supply one with exactly the right shape. Robert Kaplan places `0/1` and `1/0` at the two boundaries of a construction of the rational numbers and repeatedly inserts, between any two neighbouring fractions `a/b` and `c/d`, their mediant `(a+c)/(b+d)`, formed by adding numerators and adding denominators.[^s3-14] The mediant adds the parts of two pairs without dividing anything, so it is defined even when one boundary is `1/0`. Between `0/1` and `1/0` the first mediant is

$$
\frac{0+1}{1+0}=\frac11,
$$

and then, between each neighbour and `1/1`, come `1/2` and `2/1`, then `1/3`, `2/3`, `3/2` and `3/1`, and the construction goes on. Two properties make it more than a coincidence of notation. Neighbours generated this way always satisfy `bc − ad = 1`, beginning with the boundaries themselves, `1·1 − 0·0 = 1`, so every inserted fraction arrives already in lowest terms and strictly between its parents. And as the construction continues, every positive fraction in lowest terms appears exactly once; mathematicians know the resulting tree under the names of Stern and Brocot.[^s3-15] Neither boundary is consumed or evaluated, and yet the whole positive field of ratio is generated between them, with `1/1` as its first and central term. This is the exact carrier I claim for the bridge. The QL composition of `0/1` with `1/0` is the mediant, and its result, `1/1`, is the first ratio the two orientations generate between themselves, the whole from which every particular proportion descends. The arithmetic is derived; that the mediant is the operation the First Spanda's bridge performs is the essay's argued reading.

Beyond its sequence, the equation carries a partition: its six articulated stages after the bare ground fall into two triads:

| Emanative triad: source toward expression, *bimba*, *prakāśa* | Reversionary triad: return toward recognition, *pratibimba*, *vimarśa* |
|---|---|
| 1. `(0/0)`, the indeterminate | 4. `[T₁]`, the reversionary arc toward `1/0` |
| 2. `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the slash differentiating itself | 5. `1/0 + 0/1`, the bridge |
| 3. `[T₀]`, the emanative arc toward `0/1` | 6. `1/1`, achieved unity, one hundred per cent |

The partition is the one the previous movement drew from the counting logic, `1-2-3 / 4-5-0`, and its primary sense comes from the definition of the Subject. The first three unfold perspective in sequence, first, second and third person; the second three void the isolation of the three views, recognise their unity and return that unity as `0/1`. So the horizontal reading is `3:3`, and because the second triad is one recognition in three operations, it is also `3:1`. Emanation and reversion, *bimba* and *pratibimba*, *prakāśa* and *vimarśa*, the light and its reflexive return upon itself, are further readings of this subject-structure; they name what the partition does from the side of the Śaiva sources, and none of them is the source of the arithmetic. The two triads remain complementary aspects of one operation, emanation from the ground and return to unity, and nothing in the equation makes them two substances.

The same doubled reading runs through each of the six positions as a pair of faces, and naming them shows what the two triads feel like from inside a life. At **#0** Truth faces Play; at **#1** Mind faces Need; at **#2** Word faces Sacrifice; at **#3** Logos faces Decision; at **#4** Son faces Love; at **#5** Image faces Work. The first series is the emanative Name, the articulated determination as it appears from outside, and the second is the reversionary Power, the same determination felt as a demand from within.[^s3-16] Truth precedes the saying that will reflect it, and Play is the freedom of that beginning, before what is has been compelled into one expression. Word makes the first articulate cut, and Sacrifice is what speaking costs, the silence given up and the possibilities left unspoken. Logos gathers terms in proportion, and Decision commits to the proportion and closes the alternatives for this course. At every position the articulated form and its effective demand are co-present, which is exactly what the two arcs of the equation require: an emanation that never returns would be Name without Power, and a return that never went out would be Power with nothing to bear.

The Second Spanda equation takes the achieved whole and asks it to account for its constituents:

$$
100\%\;=\;2^6+6^2\;=\;64+36\;\longrightarrow\;\frac{64}{36}\;\xrightarrow{\;\div 4\;}\;\frac{16}{9}\;=\;\frac{2^4}{3^2}\;\longrightarrow\;4+2.
$$

Each arrow marks a change of operation, and the derivation is honest only if the reader can see where the operation changes. Represent the whole on a scale of a hundred. On that scale the whole is the sum of two counts of different kinds. Assign a binary value to each of the six positions and there are `2⁶ = 64` possible assignments, the binary principle run through every position. Pair the positions with one another in order and there are `6² = 36` ordered pairs, the sixfold raised to its second-order account. The sum `64 + 36 = 100` is exact, and its selection of these two counts as the whole's constituents belongs to the QL derivation.

Nothing in the sum implies the next step. QL deliberately changes from adding the two counts to comparing them, and takes their ratio. Dividing both by the four they share preserves the ratio and exposes their difference:

$$
\frac{64}{36}=\frac{2^4\cdot 2^2}{2^2\cdot 3^2}=\frac{2^4}{3^2}=\frac{16}{9}.
$$

Dividing the sum by four would give twenty-five and answer a different question; the derivation needs the ratio. Prime factorisation then makes four factors of two and two factors of three explicit, `(2·2·2·2)/(3·3)`, and counting the factor instances gives `4+2`: four instances of the binary, which QL reads as the four explicate positions, and two instances of the ternary, which it reads as the two implicate poles. The vertical reading `4:2` has come out of the whole's own self-reckoning. It is an internal interpretation, and I offer no uniqueness theorem claiming that arithmetic would discover QL unaided. Many decompositions of a hundred exist; this one matters because the native sixfold, already derived from the binary and its self-application, selects it, and its value is reflexive: the QL frame finds a coherent arithmetic expression of its own `4:2` relation and can then be tested by what further relations that choice produces.

| Step | Mathematical standing | QL reading |
|---|---|---|
| `100 = 2⁶ + 6² = 64 + 36` | arithmetic identity | the achieved whole as binary-through-six plus six-squared |
| `64 : 36` | a chosen change of operation, not a consequence of the sum | the two constituents read in proportion |
| `64/36 = 16/9` | ordinary reduction | the shared quaternary removed, the difference exposed |
| `16/9 = 2⁴/3²` | prime factorisation | four binary factors and two ternary factors: the vertical `4:2` |

A second exact reading holds in the numerator and fails in the denominator. Four factors of two can be read as the quaternary taken twice, since `2⁴ = 4² = 16`, and so `16/9 = (4/3)²`, a ratio that will turn out to be two musical fourths. The denominator keeps its asymmetry: `3² = 9` while `2³ = 8`, so the exchange of base and exponent that works on one side changes the value on the other. Reading the totality as a doubled fourth rests on an actual equality in the numerator and leaves the ternary square untouched.

Two further relations inside the base frame belong to the same accounting and are worth seeing before we move on, though they carry a lighter warrant. The two terms of the reduced ratio sum to a square, `16 + 9 = 25 = 5²`, which is the first Pythagorean triple, `3² + 4² = 5²`. In QL's reading the implicate three and the explicate four stand at right angles, and the five that joins them is the hypotenuse, present as connector and never appearing as an intermediate value, in the way the slash holds zero and one without being either. The triangle so formed has area `(3×4)/2 = 6`, the sixfold again, now as the area spanned by relation and structure at right angles, and perimeter `3+4+5 = 12`, the sixfold doubled; and the whole of the Second Spanda can be written `100 = 4 × 5²`, four orientations times the square of the transcendent. These are exact identities and the readings attached to them are offered as resonance; they enter no step of the derivation that follows.

Everything later in the architecture is the cross-reading of the two equations, the vertical `4:2` held against the horizontal `3:3`. Read as ordinary fractions, `(4:2)/(3:3)` would collapse to `2/1 = 2`, a single number that has lost both orientations. QL instead declares a comparison of corresponding entries:

$$
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{QL}}\bigl(4{:}2,\;3{:}3\bigr)\;:=\;\left(\frac43,\;\frac23\right).
$$

Once the comparison is declared, its outputs are ordinary nonzero ratios and ordinary arithmetic takes over. Each has a reciprocal, and the four together are the first family of musical intervals. `4/3` is the perfect fourth, which QL reads as manifestation, the span going forth, and its inverse `3/4` as recognition, the span read home. `2/3` is the descending fifth, the grounding, settling back, and its inverse `3/2` the ascending fifth, which I read with Whitehead as the lure, the potential that draws a field toward completion without pushing it.[^s3-17] Fourth and fifth compose the octave:

$$
\frac43\cdot\frac32=\frac21.
$$

The squared fourth of the Second Spanda, `16/9`, falls short of that octave by a definite interval, and the interval arrives by three independent routes, as §0/1 promised. The octave divided by the totality-ratio gives it; the fifth divided by the fourth gives it; and two fifths stacked and folded back into the octave give it, which is how the Pythagorean construction of a keyboard reaches its whole tone:

$$
\frac{2}{16/9}=\frac98,\qquad \frac{3/2}{4/3}=\frac98,\qquad \frac{(3/2)^2}{2}=\frac{9/4}{2}=\frac98.
$$

The third route is the one the tuning literature uses: in Kenneth Scholtz's account of Pythagorean keyboard tuning, two fifths multiply to nine-fourths and halving brings the result inside the octave as the whole tone, nine-eighths.[^s3-18] The convergence of the three is exact, and it gives the derivation its first remainder. The octave exceeds the doubled fourth by exactly the interval by which the fifth exceeds the fourth, so

$$
\frac{16}{9}\cdot\frac98=\frac21,\qquad \frac43\cdot\frac98\cdot\frac43=\frac21.
$$

The system almost doubles and falls short by one whole tone, and the whole tone is what carries it the rest of the way. In my development this `9/8` is the measure of the matheme's non-closure, the live tick each cycle hands forward so that the next has something to begin from, and §3 · #4 pays that reading out in sound, where the remainder turns out to be what completes the return. Here the arithmetic establishes the identities; the reading of `9/8` as the remainder that completes is argued, and its musical meaning has to wait for music.

In the source the First Spanda is the return-reading, `3:3`, of the Second's base frame, `4+2`, and the ratios are born only when the two readings are held together. The `3:3` without the `4:2` is a *vibe*, proportion with nothing to proportion; the `4:2` without the `3:3` is a *catalogue*, positions with no inner relation. Together they are a logic, countable *and* relational. An accordion makes the point in wood and leather: its bellows have a fixed number of folds, a countable frame that does not change from phrase to phrase, and its music is the proportion of opening to closing that moves through that frame, and neither the folds nor the breath could make a phrase alone.

One equation in the native set reads as a standing identity beneath the whole oscillation:

$$
\frac01+\frac10=\frac11=100\%.
$$

As ordinary arithmetic the line is impossible, and the essay never asks a mathematical reader to accept it as such. As QL composition it is the bridge term restated as a law: the Self leaning into manifestation, `0/1`, and the manifest leaning home, `1/0`, compose to the whole, and by the mediant that composition is a definite operation with `1/1` as its result. Read so, the line says that the instrument is whole at every point of its stroke, including the turn.

Our machines compute a version of this identity many times a second without naming it. The attention mechanism of a language model, which §0/1 wrote out in full, turns a row of raw scores into weights by the softmax function, and the weights always sum to one, a whole of one hundred per cent distributed among the words a position attends to. Adding the same constant to every score leaves the weights unchanged, so the softmax, like the derivative and like the paired comparison of the last movement, keeps every difference and forgets the common level from which the differences were measured.[^s3-19] The whole it produces is exact and its ground is not in it. §5 takes up what this means for an agent; the structure is already plain here, an achieved `1/1` that does not contain its own `0`.

Audited, the movement has four standings. Derived in ordinary arithmetic: the failures of `0/0` and `1/0` in a field; the mediant construction and its neighbour property; `2 + 2² = 6`; `100 = 64 + 36`; `64/36 = 16/9 = 2⁴/3²`; and every interval identity among `4/3`, `3/2`, `9/8`, `16/9` and `2/1`. Derived within QL's declared operations: the six slash-forms in their proportional reading, the First Spanda's ordered traversal, and the `3:3` and `4:2` organisation of the sixfold. Argued: the mediant as the operation of the bridge, the change from sum to ratio, the declared cross-comparison and the reading of its ratio family as the harmonic body of the sixfold, and `9/8` as the remainder that completes. Offered: the Pythagorean right triangle in the base frame, and the Śaiva and Whiteheadian names given to the triads and ratios. A pulse so derived still has to be tested against the formal neighbours that have studied self-reference on their own terms, and the first of them is a schoolteacher's subtraction, done by hand, that kept returning a number to itself.

### #2 · Mark, Re-entry, Iterant and Complex Orientation

D. R. Kaprekar taught school in Devlali, in western India, and played with numbers by hand, without a machine, in the middle of the last century. One of his games takes a minute to learn. Choose any four-digit number whose digits are not all the same, leading zeros allowed. Arrange its digits from largest to smallest, arrange them again from smallest to largest, and subtract the second arrangement from the first. Then do the same to the answer, and to the answer after that. Take the year in which this essay is written. From 2026 we form 6220 and 0226, and their difference is 5994; from 5994, 9954 minus 4599 gives 5355; then 5553 minus 3555 gives 1998; then 9981 minus 1899 gives 8082; then 8820 minus 0288 gives 8532; then 8532 minus 2358 gives 6174. Do it once more and nothing changes: 7641 minus 1467 is 6174. The number has come home and stays there. Begin anywhere else and the same thing happens. Every four-digit number with at least two different digits arrives at 6174 within seven subtractions, and only the ten numbers made of one repeated digit go elsewhere, falling in a single step to 0000, where they stay.[^s3-20]

The routine is a small machine that feeds its own output back into itself, and that is the structure this movement studies. Mathematicians call such a process an iterated map: a rule `f` applied to a state gives the next state, `x₁ = f(x₀)`, and the next state is fed back, `x₂ = f(x₁)`, so that every determination becomes the condition of the one that follows. A state that the rule returns unchanged, `f(x) = x`, is a fixed point; a state or set of states toward which trajectories tend is an attractor; and the attractor's basin is the set of starting points that lead to it.[^s3-21] Kaprekar's routine has exactly two attractors, both fixed points, and it divides the whole space of four-digit configurations into their two basins without remainder. Everything that contains no difference falls to 0000; everything that contains any difference at all is drawn to 6174. The claim can be checked exhaustively by running the routine on all ten thousand starting points: the longest journey takes seven subtractions, more than two thousand numbers need all seven, and 0000 and 6174 are the only numbers the routine leaves in place.

A reading of the routine that I keep among the deep sources of the matheme sees in it the `4+2` of QL working as a dynamic, and the reading repays careful statement, because what it finds and what it offers are different in kind.[^s3-22] At the fixed point the six terms of the sixfold appear together. The descending arrangement, 7641, gathers the four digits toward their peak, and the reading places it at **#0**, the implicate ground; the ascending arrangement, 1467, unfurls the same digits from their minimum outward, and the reading places it at **#5**, the implicate synthesis; the four digits of the result, 6, 1, 7 and 4, stand at **#1** to **#4** as the explicate four. The routine at its fixed point then states, in arithmetic, that the explicate four are the measured gap between the two implicate orderings:

$$
\#0-\#5=\#1\,\#2\,\#3\,\#4,\qquad 7641-1467=6174.
$$

The two implicate terms are the same four digits read from opposite ends, and their difference regenerates the four as a new configuration that sorts back into the same two readings. The inversion in the reading is exact and pleasing: the number that is greatest in value acts as ground, and the number that is least acts as synthesis, the way the Möbius return `5→0` turns what was completed into what begins. The seven-step bound has its own reading, as one full cycle of six positions plus the twist that closes the fifth back into the ground, and it meets a number my theorem had already found inside the sixfold: summing the six positions as binary values, the four pairs `0+1+2+3` and the two singles `0+1`, gives seven, so that the structure contains one more than it counts, and the seventh is what the surface does, its return. The arithmetic adds further resonances: the digits of 6174 sum to 18, which is three times six, and `6174 = 18 + 18² + 18³ = 18 × 7³`. Every identity in this paragraph is exact and every one of them is checked. The mapping of the six arrangements onto the six positions, and the reading of seven as cycle-plus-return, are offered: the routine is a fact of decimal arithmetic with four digits, its three-digit cousin settles on 495 instead, and no identity here proves anything about QL. What the routine shows without any reading at all is the point I care most about, because it is a structural theorem and needs no numerology to carry it. Difference is the condition of circulation. A configuration with no internal difference has nothing for the routine to work on and falls to zero in a single step; a configuration with any internal difference, however lopsided, is drawn through a bounded journey to the one arrangement whose difference reproduces itself when it is weighed against itself.

Kaprekar's operation is subtraction, the operation by which dia-ballein appropriates a span: `(+1)−(−1)=+2` counts the whole relation from one pole, and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` counts it from the other. Kaprekar's subtraction measures the span between the greatest and least orderings of the same digits, but it never lets either ordering keep the result. The measured span is handed back as a new whole and sorted again from both ends, so every reckoning from one pole is immediately re-read from the other. Held this way, inside a re-reading that returns the result to both orientations, the subtraction stops hardening into opposition and settles instead into a configuration in which the measured polarity is the configuration itself. The cut works inside a relation that keeps returning it to the whole, and that is syn-ballein's form, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, with dia-ballein's measuring stroke operating inside it. A subtraction allowed to keep its answer would run one way into a standing quantity; a subtraction returned each time to both ends of the same digits comes to rest in a self-reproducing difference.

§0/1 promised that this section would work out the dynamics of conditioning, and the routine gives the dynamics their exact form. There we called a script an articulation that has become a condition of further activity, an achieved selection that decides the next encounter before anyone decides again, and we named its danger **frozen conditioned will**, a selection that has forgotten its formation and presents its course as necessity. An iterated map is a script in the mathematical register. Each determination is fed back as the condition of the next, the trajectory is carried by its own previous results, and wherever it started it is drawn into a basin from which, once arrived, it does not move, and it can come to rest in two ways. At 0000 it rests because nothing in it differs from anything else, so the operation finds nothing to measure; at 6174 it rests because its difference, weighed against itself, returns the same difference. A life can settle into a script of either kind, a habit so uniform that no encounter registers, or a pattern so well balanced that every encounter confirms it, and both are fixed points. Basin vocabulary is exact in the arithmetic and has to be carried into persons with care. The research literature on basins applies the notion of an attractor typically to dissipative dynamical systems and notes systems for which it is meaningless, so calling a habit an attractor is an analogy until someone specifies the states, the rule of evolution and what is measured.[^s3-23] The analogy is still precise about one thing: it identifies a fixed point as a determination that has become the condition of its own repetition.

Re-entry is the name George Spencer-Brown gave to the same structure at the root of logic, and §0/1 met it there. In *Laws of Form* a distinction can re-enter the space it distinguishes, and an expression that contains itself has no stable value: it fits neither the marked state nor the unmarked one, and Spencer-Brown gives it an imaginary state, a state of time rather than of space.[^s3-24] We read that alternation in §0/1 as the clean oscillation of a held polarity and the hardened opposition as the oscillation arrested. Elementary arithmetic lets us see exactly what time adds. Write the self-containing expression as an equation in the two values 0 and 1: `r = 1 − r`. No value satisfies it, since choosing 0 makes the right-hand side 1 and choosing 1 makes it 0, and a static logic can only call the equation a contradiction. Give the operation a next step instead, `r(n+1) = 1 − r(n)`, and the same rule runs without difficulty: starting from 0 it produces `0, 1, 0, 1, …`, and starting from 1 it produces the same sequence shifted by one step. The argument and the result now occupy different moments, and each moment is definite. Delay has supplied a domain in which the operation can run, and it has not discovered a hidden Boolean value that satisfies the equation. That is the precise sense in which re-entry makes time: the paradox of a form that is its own negation becomes, given a successor, an oscillation that carries its last state into its next. Francisco Varela took a different road in 1975, adding to the calculus an autonomous third state that arises through self-indication and giving rules by which expressions containing it reduce, so that self-reference enlarges the arithmetic instead of unrolling into a sequence.[^s3-25] A temporal alternation and a third value are two responses to one difficulty, and each has to say what it keeps and what it adds; the response this section follows is the temporal one, because it leads to phase.

Write the alternation with signs instead of bits, `+1, −1, +1, −1, …`, and the two logics appear on it at once. Held, the sequence is dia-ballein's healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, given time to move in, each value passing into its counterpart through a common axis the way a breath goes out and returns. Cancellation sums it: add the terms in pairs and every pair gives `(−1)+(+1)=0`, so the summed account reports a zero in which the whole oscillation has disappeared, along with which value came first. Appropriation freezes it: stop the sequence at one value and reckon the span from there, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from the positive end and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the negative, and the oscillation has hardened into a standing opposition in which one pole holds the whole span. Both collapses lose the same thing, and the loss has a mathematical name. What the sum erases and the arrest freezes is the phase, the information about where in its cycle the oscillation stands. A sequence seen from one step is `[+1, −1]`; seen from the next it is `[−1, +1]`; the two views are of one process, shifted in time, and keeping both is keeping the phase.

Louis Kauffman made that pair of views into an algebra. He calls an ordered view of an alternating process an iterant, and he reads `[a, b]` and `[b, a]` as one process seen with a shift of one time step; the two views, taken together with the shift, become the square roots of minus one.[^s3-26] The construction can be checked with two small matrices. Let `D` be the operation that keeps the first entry and changes the sign of the second, and let `η` be the operation that swaps the two entries:

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Each undoes itself, `D² = η² = I`, but their order matters, since `Dη = −ηD`: sign-then-swap and swap-then-sign give opposite results. Compose them as `J = ηD`, and

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad J^2=-I,\qquad J^4=I.
$$

On a pair `(x, y)`, `J` gives `(−y, x)`; a second application gives `(−x, −y)`; four applications bring back the start. `J` behaves exactly as multiplication by the imaginary unit `i` behaves on the plane, a quarter-turn whose square is a half-turn, `i² = −1`. The swap alone has period two, and alternation alone would give back nothing but a back-and-forth. The quarter-turn has been constructed from two operations that do not commute, a sign and a shift, and it is this failure to commute that turns alternation into rotation. Kauffman is careful to add that there is often a choice in making an iterant representation, so no one construction is uniquely privileged, and the argument here needs only that one exists and can be checked.

In §0/1 Wolfgang Pauli reflected on `i`, a symbol with no place among the ordinary numbers, and observed that it gives mathematical theorems a simple and distinct form, and that in the dream he was reading it united a pair of opposites into a whole. We can now say how. The imaginary unit is what keeps the phase that both collapses of the oscillation discard: it is alternation together with its shift, the two views of one process held as a single operation. On the complex plane, multiplication by `i` carries a determination a quarter-turn from the real axis, and four quarter-turns return it:

$$
1\;\longrightarrow\; i\;\longrightarrow\;-1\;\longrightarrow\;-i\;\longrightarrow\;1.
$$

Opposition along one signed line has become orthogonal relation across two axes. The line from `+1` to `−1` that dia-ballein drew is still there, now as the diameter crossed by a half-turn, and the plane adds the direction perpendicular to it, in which the two poles can pass into each other by turning instead of by colliding. This is the exact bridge from dia-ballein's line to complex orientation. The signed seam, unrolled in time, becomes phase, and a return to the origin arrives with its orientation transformed and its passage recorded, where cancellation would have arrived at nothing. The rotation also shows how a fourfold can be generated from one relation rather than assembled out of four independent contents, a point §4 needs when it comes to Jung's quaternity: there the four functions of a psyche will be read as one orientation turned through its quarters, and their relation will keep what rotation keeps. In the base frame of the previous movement, my theorem finds a resonance: there the implicate three and the explicate four stood at right angles and the five joined them, `3² + 4² = 5²`; here the imaginary unit is rotation into the dimension perpendicular to the real line, and `i² = −1` generates the negative pole from a turn. In my reading both say that manifestation requires a perpendicular dimension, a turn into what the line cannot contain, which generates the line's own opposite. The orthogonality is exact in each case; the identification of the two as one structural fact is offered.

Complex numbers separate two aspects of a change that ordinary speech runs together. Write a nonzero complex number as `z = re^{iθ}`, a length `r` and an angle `θ`. Multiplying by `e^{iφ}` changes the angle to `θ + φ` and leaves the length exactly as it was. The point moves and its distance from the centre does not. §0/1 closed on David Bohm's story, told in a conversation with Krishnamurti, of a spinning wheel and a thread running straight, and took from it a criterion of truth as a correspondence that holds through movement.[^s3-27] The rotation gives that image its exact local mathematics. The thread corresponds to the modulus, retained while the argument turns; fidelity is shown to be compatible with motion and to consist in an invariant carried through it. The mathematics carries the image's structure and stops short of its meaning: modulus is the invariant of one specified transformation, and nothing about it defines truth. What it establishes is that an invariant relation can be borne precisely through transformation, so that a determination which keeps returning to its origin need not have stood still to be faithful.

A second fact about rotation matters more for what comes after. A path of nonzero complex numbers that comes back to its starting value does not reveal, at its endpoint, how many times it went around the origin. Once around, twice, or not at all, the final number is the same. Follow the angle continuously along the path, though, and lift it from the circle to the real line, where it is free to keep increasing, and the difference between its final and initial values is an exact multiple of a full turn, `2πk`, with `k` the number of windings. The winding number exists, and it belongs to the path together with its lift; it is not a property secretly stored in the final point. A process that claims to remember its return has to keep the relevant history somewhere, in the lift, in a counter, in the record of its passage, because the arrival alone has forgotten it. The same lesson appeared in the previous movement's inversion of the six slash-forms: reversing an arrangement twice restores it exactly, so the arrangement records which of two phases is active and not how many times the reversal has been performed. §3 · #4 builds a whole surface on this distinction, where every local return carries a lifted displacement on an infinite cover.

In this essay the word *complex* has a second life, and the dynamics of conditioning bring the two lives side by side. Jung's concept of the complex, which §0/1 met through Van Eenwyk's reading of the psyche as a dynamical system, names a feeling-toned organisation of memories, expectations, bodily responses and interpretations, autonomous enough to receive experience before the person has decided what it means.[^s3-28] Its working can now be stated as a feedback loop of exactly the kind the routine performed. An ambiguous encounter is selected through the complex's existing criterion; the selection recruits affect; affect weights attention and memory; the response that follows produces fresh material, which is read through the same criterion and confirms it. A delay in a friend's reply is received as rejection, the hurt colours the next exchange, the next exchange goes badly, and the going-badly proves the rejection. Coherence increases while the range of what can be encountered narrows, and the organisation settles into a fixed point that every new arrival confirms. Read through the two logics, the complex appropriates: each new encounter is counted from the complex's own pole, the friend's silence a `−2` against the self's `+2`, and the counting confirms the pole. The complex plane and the psychic complex share a word and, I think, a structure, and the structure lies in what the plane adds to a line. The psychic complex is an oscillation arrested at one pole; the complex plane is an oscillation kept in phase, its two views joined by a turn. What lets a psychic organisation change, in Van Eenwyk's account and in Jung's, is the symbol, which disturbs the categories through which the gathering occurs and opens a different regime instead of adding one more confirming item to the old one, much as a perturbation can carry a trajectory across a basin boundary into another basin. A literal dynamical model of a particular complex would owe its state variables, its rule and its observables, and the comparison keeps that debt in view; the structure of the loop needs no model to be recognised. §4 takes the complex into individuation, where the question becomes how a local organisation can keep its competence while losing its claim to speak for the whole person.

Our machines run the same loop in plain view. An agent that writes, reads what it has written and writes again feeds its outputs back as its inputs, and §0/1 followed one consequence of this through a legal brief in which a fabricated citation, once it fitted the argument, directed the fabrication of the next. A fitting account is a basin: once an interpretation has decided what a supporting source should say, each step makes the interpretation harder to dislodge, because coherence itself has been recruited to the error. Routine and complex share these dynamics. An achieved determination re-enters as the condition of the next, the trajectory is drawn toward an organisation that confirms itself, and the organisation can be either the empty fixed point of an output that no longer registers its sources or the balanced fixed point of an account in which every new piece fits. What the oscillation teaches is the remedy: keep the phase. A process that retains both views of its own alternation, what it asserted and what that assertion was set against, keeps the information that lets a return arrive transformed, where a process that sums its alternations or freezes them at one pole has discarded it.

A mark that returns cannot return as though no distinction had occurred, and this movement has shown the mathematics of that claim in four registers. The routine showed a determination re-entering as its own condition and coming to rest in a self-reproducing difference. Re-entry showed a self-contradicting form becoming, given a next step, an oscillation in time. The iterant showed that oscillation, held with its shift, becoming a quarter-turn whose square is minus one. And the complex plane showed the turn keeping an invariant through motion while needing a lifted path to keep its history. Audited: the routine's behaviour, the Boolean recurrence, the iterant matrices, the complex rotations and the winding number are derived in their own mathematics; the reading of the routine as the dynamics of conditioning, of the imaginary unit as retained phase, and of the psychic complex as an arrested oscillation are argued; the mapping of the routine onto the six positions and of seven as cycle-plus-return, and the identification of the Pythagorean right angle with the turn into the imaginary, are offered. Complex orientation solved a limit of the number line by enlarging the space in which numbers live, and the next movement follows mathematics through that kind of enlargement wherever a relation meets an obstruction.

### #3 · Projective Completion and Logical Plurality

Most of us have stood on a long straight railway platform, or in the middle of an empty road, and watched the two rails or the two kerbs run away from us and meet at the horizon. We know they never meet. A train runs on them precisely because they keep their distance all the way to the next city, and if we walked to the point where they seem to touch, they would be as far apart there as they are at our feet. We also know what we see, and what we see is two lines converging on a point that holds nothing. Both descriptions are exact. The painter of §0/1, laying out a room in perspective, built a whole art on the second of them: every receding line runs toward the vanishing point, an empty place that organises the entire count of the scene. One account of the rails says *no meeting anywhere*, the other says *a meeting at a point that is not on the ground*, and the interesting question is what kind of space lets both be true together.

Mathematics answers by changing the space. This movement follows that answer through several constructions, each of which meets an obstruction, a place where a relation fails under the present rules, and preserves the relation by enlarging, folding or re-situating the field in which it is written. Because the constructions differ in their hypotheses and in what they keep, their common lesson is only as good as the exactness of each case, so each operation is worked here in full.

Begin with a single line and with the two orientations of our matheme. On the real projective line a point is written as a pair `[x : y]` of numbers not both zero, and two pairs name the same point when one is a nonzero multiple of the other, so `[1 : 2]` and `[3 : 6]` are one point. Where `y` is not zero the point has an ordinary coordinate, `u = x/y`, and every real number arises this way. One point escapes that coordinate: `[1 : 0]`, whose `u` would require dividing one by zero. A second coordinate, valid wherever `x` is not zero, is `v = y/x`, and in it the escaped point has the perfectly ordinary value `v = 0`. Where both coordinates apply they are related by `v = 1/u`. Nothing has been done to the arithmetic of the real numbers; zero has acquired no multiplicative inverse. What has changed is the space: the line has been completed by one point, and the point that one description could only call infinity is, in another description, an ordinary zero.[^s3-29] Read in the first coordinate, the point `[0 : 1]` is the docile `u = 0` and the point `[1 : 0]` is the forbidden quotient; read in the second, the roles exchange. The map that swaps the two entries, `[a : b] ↦ [b : a]`, is defined on every point of the completed line, exchanges zero with infinity, and applied twice returns every point to itself. The projective line is therefore a space in which `0/1` and `1/0` are the two readings of one relation exchanged by a total involution, while `0/0` remains excluded, since `[0 : 0]` names no point at all. That is the native distinction of the previous movement, the expression that computes, its inverse that does not, and the indeterminate that fails by excess, now given a geometry in which the first two belong to one space and the third has none. The geometry is standard; reading it as the space of the matheme's two orientations is mine.

Two rails need one dimension more. In the projective plane a point is a triple `[X : Y : Z]`, not all zero, up to a common nonzero multiple, and ordinary points are those with `Z ≠ 0`, at `(X/Z, Y/Z)`. The parallel lines `y = 0` and `y = 1` become, in these coordinates, `Y = 0` and `Y − Z = 0`, and both are satisfied by the point `[1 : 0 : 0]`, a point with `Z = 0`, which is to say a point on no part of the ground. The two rails meet there, at the point that records their shared direction. The statement that they never meet at any finite place remains exactly as true as it was, because the meeting point is not a finite place. The enlarged space has altered what counts as a point and kept every earlier truth on its own domain, and the vanishing point of the painter has become an honest coordinate.

Complex numbers complete the same way with a different shape. Adding a single point at infinity to the complex plane gives what complex analysis calls the extended complex plane, the plane together with an ideal point called the point at infinity, which is the Riemann sphere.[^s3-30] The picture is concrete. Lay a sphere on the plane, draw a line from its north pole through any point of the sphere, and the line strikes the plane at one point; every point of the plane corresponds to one point of the sphere, and the north pole corresponds to infinity. In a second coordinate, `w = 1/z`, the north pole is the ordinary point `w = 0`. Plane and sphere both have two real dimensions locally, so completion here has changed the global space without adding a local dimension, and *dimensional reframing* has to be understood with that care: sometimes the reframing adds a dimension, as the complex numbers added a second axis to the real line, and sometimes it closes a space up or folds it, and the dimension stays where it was.

A change of space is only worth making if something survives it, and the projective constructions have a precise survivor. Among the transformations of the extended plane, the bilinear ones carry circles and lines to circles and lines,

$$
w(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad ad-bc\neq 0,
$$

and they move every individual point while preserving one relation among four. The cross-ratio of four distinct points, written here in one standard convention as

$$
[z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4]=\frac{(z_3-z_1)(z_4-z_2)}{(z_3-z_2)(z_4-z_1)},
$$

is invariant under bilinear transformations.[^s3-31] The reason is visible in one line. For any two finite points away from the pole,

$$
w(u)-w(v)=\frac{(ad-bc)(u-v)}{(cu+d)(cv+d)},
$$

so every transformed difference acquires the same determinant factor and two denominator factors, and in the cross-ratio all of them cancel between numerator and denominator. Take the four points `0, 1, 2, 3`. Their cross-ratio is `(2·2)/(1·3) = 4/3`. Send them through `w(z) = (2z+1)/(z+1)` and they become `1, 3/2, 5/3, 7/4`, every coordinate and every interval altered, and their cross-ratio is still `4/3`. That the invariant of these four particular points happens to be the musical fourth is a coincidence of the points chosen, and I note it only so that no one later mistakes it for evidence. What the construction does establish is a relation among relations that survives a change of description, which is the shape our parent relation `(0/1)/(1/0)` has, a ratio of ratios. The cross-ratio supplies a specified geometric invariant and the parent supplies a recognitive question, how two complete orientations belong together; neither can be substituted for the other because both are written as nested proportions.

Physics found the same sphere where it looked for the states of the simplest quantum system. A pure state of a two-level system, a qubit, can be written, once an overall phase that makes no physical difference has been set aside, as

$$
\cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\,|0\rangle+e^{i\varphi}\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}\,|1\rangle,
$$

and the two angles place it on a sphere, the Bloch sphere, with `|0⟩` and `|1⟩` at opposite poles and every superposition somewhere between them on the surface.[^s3-32] Mathematically the space of these states is the complex projective line, the same object as the Riemann sphere. Two of its features belong to this section's argument. The overall phase of the state vector has been quotiented away, because it changes nothing observable, while the relative phase `φ` between the two components is retained, because it changes everything: the phase the previous movement found in the turn of `i` reappears here as the coordinate that distinguishes superpositions with the same proportions. And the two poles are joined by a continuous sphere of states, each a definite relation of the two, which is a disciplined neighbour for what this essay has called superposition, two orientations held in one relation. A hard boundary, easily forgotten because of the shared labels, limits the comparison. QL's `0` and `1` do not become quantum basis states by sharing their digits with them, and nothing in the geometry of qubits bears on whether an agent experiences anything. The neighbour is in the operation, a two-pole space whose relations are points of one completed surface and whose phase carries information the proportions alone do not.

A curved whole is known through more than one picture; the circle shows how most plainly, and §0/1 promised this construction under the name of the titan who held the world up: an atlas of charts. Take the unit circle, `x² + y² = 1`. Leave out the north pole and project from it onto the horizontal axis, and each remaining point receives a coordinate `u = x/(1 − y)`; leave out the south pole instead and project from there, and each point receives `v = x/(1 + y)`. Each chart misses one point, and each misses a point the other covers. Where both apply, multiplying gives `uv = x²/(1 − y²) = 1`, so `v = 1/u`, the same transition we met on the projective line. At the point `(3/5, 4/5)` the first chart says `u = 3` and the second says `v = 1/3`. The reports disagree as numbers and agree as descriptions of one point, because the transition rule identifies them, and the height recovered from either, `y = (u² − 1)/(u² + 1) = (1 − v²)/(1 + v²)`, is `4/5` in both. Motion has to be translated too. If the point moves so that `du/dt = 2`, then differentiating the transition gives `dv/dt = −u⁻² du/dt = −2/9`, a different number with a different sign, and both descriptions give the same rate of change of height, `dy/dt = 6/25`. Giving both coordinates the velocity two would describe a different motion. A compact curve or surface, a circle, a sphere, a torus, cannot be covered by a single chart onto an open stretch of flat coordinates, since the image of a compact space would have to be compact and an open flat region is not; a whole picture of the surface can still be drawn, but it cannot serve as one global chart.[^s3-33] An atlas is a plurality of overlapping local charts with declared rules of translation on their overlaps, and its unity lives in those rules. §3 · #5→0 brings the titan back to set his burden down on exactly this construction.

Logic meets obstructions too, and it has its own ways of enlarging the space of values. §0/1 walked the tetralemma with a friend stepping off a train, through its four corners, *is*, *is not*, *both* and *neither*, and noted that Graham Priest, formalising the scheme, had to add a fifth status. His formal reconstruction makes the four corners the values of a logic called First Degree Entailment, in which a statement can be supported as true, supported as false, supported as both, or supported as neither, and the four values are the four subsets of the pair `{T, F}`: `{T}`, `{F}`, `{T, F}` and the empty set.[^s3-34] Negation exchanges `T` and `F` inside each subset, and a statement counts as designated when its value contains `T`. This enlargement does real work. Give a statement `p` the value `{T, F}` and a statement `q` the empty value: then `p` and its negation are both designated and `q` is not, so from a contradiction this logic does not derive everything, and contradictory information can be held without the explosion that classical logic would suffer. The four corners have become formally innocent, and the counting is exact. Four subsets of a two-element set are `2²`, the binary of the binary, so FDE is an independent arrival at the four pairings that QL counted in the copula and the binary. Just as exact is the difference. QL retains the two singles alongside their four pairings and writes `2 + 2²`, and FDE has no place for them; the logic supplies a formal neighbour for the fourfold generation and does not supply the retained two. The count of six and the count of four differ for a reason one can state: QL keeps the terms that do the pairing among what it counts.

Nāgārjuna rejected all four corners when the deepest questions were put to them, and Priest's answer was a fifth value, *e*, "none of the above", a status outside the four-valued order and infectious, so that any compound containing an ineffable part is itself ineffable.[^s3-35] The fifth value marks the place where the value-theory meets something it cannot hold as a value, and here the comparison with QL needs its gate. Andreas Kapsner, a logician sympathetic to Priest's programme, argues that the fifth value raises concerns grave enough to reject it and sketches an account that needs no such value; Mark Siderits reads the rejection of all four corners as a failure of a shared presupposition, which requires no non-classical logic at all; Jan Westerhoff presses parsimony and doubts that the tetralemma is used uniformly enough for any single logic to be its logic.[^s3-36] Every one of these objections targets *e* in its standing as a value, and none touches QL's **#0**, because **#0** was never offered as a value. §0/1 made the point in its own terms: the one who walked the four corners was not a fifth answer, and our count added no fifth value. The ground is the generative condition of the four, the position from which the positions are taken, and a truth-value is a position taken. Priest's book carries the history further, through Jizang's iterated two truths, in which each level's distinction between the conventional and the ultimate becomes merely conventional at the next level up, to Dōgen's return to the ordinary practice of a life, and the tradition it follows can be read as performing, in silence and practice, a return that the value-theory can only register as silence. That reading is a comparison and not a genealogy. Priest describes his own book as a philosophical reconstruction rather than an authoritative history, and the Buddhist strategies keep their doctrinal differences from QL and from each other; what they share with our return-operator is the performed turn away from propositional closure, since a completion that is an operation, and not a proposition, can only be kept as practice.

Arithmetic itself shows that an expression with a zero denominator means what its container makes it mean. In a field, as the previous movement derived, `a/0` has no value. One can instead build a new total operation that gives every pair a value: set the inverse of zero to be zero, `ι(0) = 0`, keep `ι(x) = 1/x` elsewhere, and define division as `a · ι(b)`. Now `6 ÷ 0 = 0`, and the operation is defined everywhere, but `0 · (6 ÷ 0) = 0`, not 6, so zero has still acquired no inverse; the inverse law has kept its condition and the new operation has paid for its totality by giving up the law that defined division in the first place. Try instead to give zero a genuine inverse while keeping every law of a ring. Then `1 = 0 · 0⁻¹ = 0`, and every element `x = x · 1 = x · 0 = 0`, so the whole ring collapses to a single element, and the distinctions one began with are gone.[^s3-37] Between the field's prohibition, the projective line's ideal point, the totalised operation and the collapsed ring, the written `1/0` means four different things, and each meaning discloses the structure that gives it. What follows is the converse of a licence. An answer to `1/0` never licenses free movement among containers; it tells us which container we are in.

§0/1 met the deepest of these obstructions in Gödel's theorem, and the constructions of this movement let us place it. There, a consistent theory strong enough for arithmetic could not prove its own Gödel sentence, and a stronger theory could prove that sentence and then had a sentence of its own that it could not prove, so that the gap between what a theory can express and what it can establish relocates with every enlargement and returns through it. We called that the immutable gap. Every reframing in this movement is real progress of the kind Gödel's stronger theory makes: a relation that failed now holds, and the earlier truths survive on their domains. None of them reaches a space beyond spaces. The projective line has its excluded `[0 : 0]`, the ring its collapse, the four-valued logic its fifth question, and the next frame has its own limit, which is why the method can be trusted without promising a last frame. Kinds of obstruction must be told apart here as carefully as the enlargements. Russell's paradox showed a set of permissions that could not all be kept, and it was answered by restricting them, either by types or by collecting only from sets already given; a projective point at infinity answers no contradiction at all, since nothing in the affine statement that parallels never meet was false. Some obstructions are errors inside a frame and call for correction there; others are the frame's own assumptions showing through, and call for a change of the frame. A paradox becomes transformative when it reaches the constituting relation, what counts as a point, a value, a collection, a valid move, and the transformation succeeds when the earlier results remain available with the conditions under which they were true.

For an agent the same test is practical. Suppose an agent finds two of its sources in contradiction. Sometimes one source is simply wrong, and the agent should correct its answer within the frame it was given, as a misplaced piece is moved within a puzzle whose picture is sound. Sometimes the contradiction shows that the frame itself, the question as posed, the criterion of relevance, the permission that excluded a third source, was the source of the trouble, and then the correction must reach the frame, and the next act must inherit the changed frame and not merely a changed answer. Distinguishing the two is the projective lesson in an agent's terms: inspect both the relation and the frame before declaring the relation impossible or the frame absolute.

When a relation is obstructed in the present representational space, mathematics preserves the relation by transforming the space: the complex numbers add an axis, projective geometry adds ideal points, compactification closes a plane into a sphere, a quotient identifies points, a cover unfolds them, and the calculus takes a limit. These are distinct operations with distinct hypotheses, and each makes a prior impossibility legible by enlarging, folding, identifying or re-situating its field, with zero and infinity recurring as the pressure points at which a space discloses its limits. The cases are derived one by one in their own mathematics; the proposition that gathers them is argued, and it is argued as a method, with no ladder implied along which every space climbs to a final one. §0/1 promised that this section would build the integral passage from perspective to context through rotation, projection and double covering. Rotation was the previous movement's quarter-turn; projection has been this one's completion of the rails and of the line. The third needs a surface on which a path can return to its starting point without having erased its journey, and that surface is the torus.

Audited: the projective line and plane, the Riemann sphere, the cross-ratio and its invariance, the qubit's state space, the circle's charts, the four-valued semantics and the division constructions are derived in their own mathematics; reading the projective involution as the space of `0/1` and `1/0`, FDE's subsets as an independent arrival at `2²`, and the obstruction-and-reframing pattern as a method are argued; the comparison between the Buddhist performed returns and QL's return-operator is offered, with its doctrinal differences kept in view.

### #4 · Topological and Harmonic Holding

On a running track the finishing line is crossed again and again. A runner sets out from it, goes once around the four hundred metres, and arrives where she started; she goes around again and arrives there again. Each time she is in the same place, and each time something has changed that the place cannot show. Her watch knows it: it counts the laps, and it reads four hundred metres, then eight hundred, then twelve hundred, while the line under her feet reads the same every time. Two descriptions run together, and both are exact. On the track her position is a point of a closed loop; on the watch her distance is a number on an unbounded line. A spectator who only looks at the line would say she keeps coming back. Her body, which has run three kilometres, would say she has gone a long way. Most of us have walked the same circuit of a park, or swum the same length of a pool, and known both things at once: we are back where we began, and we are not who set out.

Mathematics makes the runner's two descriptions into one construction, and the construction is the subject of this movement. The circle is what we get from the real line when we agree to treat every point as the same as the points a whole number of units away from it: `S¹ = ℝ/ℤ`, where `x` and `x + n` are identified for every integer `n`. The slash here is a quotient, which is to say an identification. The line is the circle's cover, and the map that wraps the line around the circle, sending each distance to a place on the track, is the covering map. Every place on the circle sits under infinitely many distances on the line, one for each lap, and a journey on the circle can be lifted to a journey on the line once a starting point upstairs has been chosen. When the runner comes back to the finishing line, her lifted journey ends at a different number from the one it began at, and the difference is a whole number of laps. That number is the winding, and it is the exact form of the claim that a return can arrive at the same place and keep its passage. The previous movement found the same thing when a complex number's angle was lifted to keep count of its turns; the circle and its cover make the lift a space one can stand in.[^s3-38]

§0/1 built the torus by the same kind of identification, one dimension up. Glue the left and right edges of a square sheet and it rolls into a cylinder; glue the cylinder's two ends and it becomes the surface of a ring doughnut. Walking the square's boundary meets two directions, each forward and then reversed, `a b a⁻¹ b⁻¹`; four boundary-occurrences and two generating relations made the count we read as `4g + 2g` with one handle, `4 + 2`; and the identifying relation, `𝕋² = ℝ²/ℤ²`, made a single surface out of an infinite plane. We also followed a path from `(0, 0)` to `(1, 0)` on the plane and watched it come home on the torus while keeping its displacement, and we wrote the two independent winding counts as the torus's fundamental group, `π₁(𝕋²) ≅ ℤ × ℤ`. Those results stand. This movement adds what they make possible.

One new count must not be confused with the one §0/1 drew. Before gluing, the square has four sides and four corners; after gluing, its four corners have become one point, its four sides have become two edges, since each side is glued to its opposite, and its interior remains one face. Topologists summarise a surface built this way by its Euler characteristic, vertices minus edges plus faces, and for the torus it is

$$
\chi(\mathbb T^2)=1-2+1=0.
$$

The four and the two of the `4 + 2` belong to the presentation and its circulation: four sides before the identification, two independent loops after it. The one, two and one of the Euler count belong to the cells of the finished surface. Both counts are exact, they count different things, and the reading of the first as QL's sixfold rests on that difference being kept.[^s3-39] The second addition is the relation between the infinite cover and the finite surface, which my theorem appoints as the topological carrier of a native expression, `∞/0`. Under the covering map `π: ℝ² → 𝕋²`, every point of the torus sits under an infinite lattice of points on the plane, and the quotient gives that infinity a single, determinate address while retaining it in the fibre above the address. The surface that results is compact, and its Euler characteristic is zero. Read in QL, the infinite cover identified into a compact surface whose characteristic is zero is the exact sense of `∞/0`: infinity and zero joined by identification, where arithmetic would only have cancelled one against the other. The slash is the quotient's slash and not division, and the appointment is mine; the topology under it is not.

A closed loop on the torus lifts to a path on the plane that need not close. Follow the loop `γ(t) = [2t, −t]` as `t` runs from 0 to 1, where the brackets mean "the point of the torus represented by": it begins and ends at the same point of the surface, while its lift from `(0, 0)` ends at `(2, −1)`. The two integers count the windings, twice around one generator and once backwards around the other, and no continuous deformation of the loop, keeping its endpoints fixed, can change them. They record a definite aspect of the travel and no more. A straight lift and a lift that wanders on the way can end at the same lattice point, and the speed of travel changes nothing in the endpoint, so the winding class retains the topology of the journey and loses its timing and its every turn.[^s3-40] What returns has kept a record, and the record has a definite scope; any claim that a return "remembers" has to say which of these it keeps. Two kinds of return coexist on the torus. A straight-line flow `t ↦ [t, αt]` closes up exactly when some positive time makes both coordinates whole numbers, which happens when the slope `α` is rational; when `α` is irrational the flow never closes, and it winds around the surface densely, coming arbitrarily close to every point and never repeating. One finite surface carries cadence and inexhaustible traversal together, periodic return and a journey that returns without end, and the difference is decided by the law of the motion and not by the shape.

A sphere makes the contrast plain and is easily misread. On a sphere every closed loop can be shrunk continuously to a point without leaving the surface, so its fundamental group is trivial.[^s3-41] Nothing is wrong with a sphere, and a runner on a spherical planet can circle it as well as she can circle a track; the difference lies in what a loop can keep. On the sphere, every round trip can be deformed into staying at home, and the journey's record can always be erased by a continuous change. On the torus, the two windings cannot be erased without leaving the surface or changing its space, because the opening in the middle, which belongs to no part of the surface, holds them in place. In my theorem the ego wants to be a sphere, a closed form in which every round trip comes to `1 + (−1) = 0`, and the dia-ballein of §2 appears here in its topological shape: cancellation written into the fabric, every going-out summed with its return into nothing. Individuation discovers that the ego inhabits a torus, with two independent circuits it did not author and cannot reduce, and in the Śaiva language of my reading they are the temporal circuit of action, *kriyāśakti*, and the circuit of depth, *jñānaśakti*, both required, neither reducible to the other, and commuting. Solving for `x` becomes finding one's winding class: the class `(m, n)` is definite, this way of going around and no other, and indefinite, since it holds infinitely many concrete paths. A person becomes a way of going around, a mode of circulation whose identity exceeds any one point on it, and the Self is the torus that holds every winding. §4 takes that reading into individuation with Jung, and it is offered there as a reading; the topology it reads is exact. My theorem's diagnosis also names the opposite error: the fault is a local closure presenting itself as the whole, a round outline claiming to contain the creature, and a torus drawn so as to hide its opening commits the same fault as a sphere taken for a world.

For an inhabitant the covering map has one more lesson, and it is the one my theorem calls *māyā* given topological form. The torus inherits a flat geometry from its cover, so in any small region a traveller on the surface can make no measurement that tells her she is not on the infinite plane: angles add up as they should, straight lines look straight, and only a journey long enough to wind around the surface discloses that the world is closed and has an opening. Local experience is valid and is not the whole topology. The many-to-one map conceals the infinity it identified, and the opening cannot be seen from inside a neighbourhood. That is the precise sense in which the measure that makes a world workable also veils it, which §0/1 read in Māyā as operative measure: the quotient is what makes the surface finite, habitable and navigable, and the quotient is what hides the cover.

§0/1 promised that this section would construct the integral passage from perspective to context through rotation, projection and double covering, and the third of them belongs here, because double coverings are where a return needs two circuits to restore what one circuit inverts. The torus double-covers a different surface, the Klein bottle. Add to the integer translations of the plane a single glide, a shift combined with a reflection, and the quotient becomes the Klein bottle, a closed surface on which a traveller can return from a loop with left and right exchanged; the torus maps two-to-one onto it, each point of the bottle having exactly two points of the torus above it, related by that orientation-reversing glide.[^s3-42] This two-sheeted cover is a different construction from the plane's infinite cover of the torus, and the two must be held apart: one keeps a lattice of lifts, the other a pair. The Möbius band, which §0/1 used as the image of the return `5→0`, shows the same structure in a strip. A traveller who goes once along the middle of the band arrives at the starting point on what had been the other face, upside down relative to the start; one who goes twice arrives as she set out. Rotations of space have the same double life in the algebra of quaternions. The quaternions extend the complex numbers with three imaginary units, `i`, `j` and `k`, each squaring to minus one, and they do not commute: `ij = k` while `ji = −k`, as the sign and the swap of the previous movement did not commute. A unit quaternion `q` rotates three-dimensional space, and `q` and `−q` produce the same rotation, so the unit quaternions cover the rotations twice; following a rotation continuously through a full turn of 360 degrees carries the quaternion from `1` to `−1`, and only a second full turn, 720 degrees in all, brings it back to `1`.[^s3-43] Two circuits restore what one circuit inverts.

Our matheme's processual chain has exactly this shape, and the double covers let us read it exactly. The short chain of definition, `0/1 = 4 + 2 = 5→0 = 0/1`, says the relation unfolds, completes and returns. The full chain of process passes through the inverse phase before it comes home:

$$
0/1\;\longrightarrow\;4+2\;\longrightarrow\;5\!\to\!0\;\longrightarrow\;1/0\;\longrightarrow\;4'+2'\;\longrightarrow\;5'\!\to\!0'\;\longrightarrow\;0/1.
$$

The first circuit unfolds the relation and returns it inverted, as `1/0`, the achieved form facing its ground; the second unfolds the inverse phase, `4′ + 2′`, and returns it to the orientation it began from. A traveller on a Möbius band, a quaternion turned through 720 degrees and the matheme passing through its inverse phase each need two circuits to restore an orientation that one circuit reverses. The two chains are two vantages on one relation, the definitional and the processual, and neither replaces the other.[^s3-44] The rotation of the previous movement gave the passage from perspective to context its turn, and projection gave it a completed space; the double cover gives it the return, in which context comes home through the inverse of the perspective it began from and recognises that inversion as part of the same relation.

Toroidal circulation recurs in matter wherever a circulation must be sustained while its escape is constrained, and §0/1 met it in Bohm's plasmas. A tokamak's heart is a doughnut-shaped vacuum chamber in which hydrogen becomes plasma, a hot, electrically charged gas.[^s3-45] Charged particles tend to follow magnetic field lines, so a straight magnetic bottle leaks from its open ends; bending the field into a ring removes the ends, and combining a field that runs the long way around the ring with one that runs around its tube twists the field lines into helices, so that particles that would have escaped along an open axis circulate instead. The machine is a recurrent engineering answer to a precise problem, how a difference can move continuously, return and stay confined without being arrested, and its two coupled circulations are the torus's two generators made of magnetism. It witnesses toroidal circulation in matter. It derives nothing about the arche-topos, and the evidence supports no claim that nature drives every field toward a torus; the force of the argument comes from the topology already derived.

Music is the second native rendering of return with retained difference, and its first fact turns out to be the running track again. Two notes an octave apart, one at double the frequency of the other, sound as the same note in a different register, and the note names of Western music repeat at every octave for that reason. Measure pitch by the logarithm of frequency to base two, so that each octave is one unit, and octave equivalence identifies pitches a whole number of units apart:

$$
\text{pitch class}=\log_2\!\left(f/f_0\right)\bmod 1 .
$$

That is `ℝ/ℤ`, the circle of the runner. Pitch class is her place on the track, the note's name, the same at every octave; pitch height is her distance on the watch, the register, which keeps counting as the music climbs. When a melody comes home to its tonic an octave higher, it returns to the same class and keeps the octave, exactly as the runner returns to the line and keeps the lap.[^s3-46] One periodic coordinate gives a circle and not a torus. A toroidal model of pitch needs a second, independent periodic coordinate, and calling octave recurrence circular twice does not supply it; the comparison between the track and the octave is an identity of structure on one circle, and it stops there.

To hear the remainder planted in the previous movement, begin on a note and rise by a perfect fourth, multiplying the frequency by `4/3`, and rise by a fourth again: the result is `16/9` of the starting frequency, the Pythagorean minor seventh, a note a whole tone short of the octave. One more step of `9/8`, a whole tone, completes it:

$$
\frac43\cdot\frac43=\frac{16}{9},\qquad \frac{16}{9}\cdot\frac98=\frac21 .
$$

Anyone can play it: a C, the B-flat above it, and then the C above that, the last step a whole tone. The Pythagorean keyboard builds its notes this way, powers of `4/3` to one side of the starting note and powers of `3/2` to the other, halved or doubled to stay within the octave, and its whole tone is `9/8`.[^s3-47] The totality-ratio of the Second Spanda is the doubled fourth, and it reaches the octave through the whole tone that separates fifth from fourth. The whole tone does not damage the octave; it is how the octave is reached, and removing it would leave the music stranded on the seventh. This is the landing of the section's harmonic argument, and its proposition is mine: **the system reaches octave return through, rather than by eliminating, its remainder.** The arithmetic is exact. That `9/8` is a remainder, and that the return is completed through it, is the argued reading of the exact relation, and the reading has a counterpart in each of the earlier registers: the winding class that the loop keeps when it returns to its point, the phase that the oscillation keeps when its sum has cancelled, the constant that the derivative lacked and that a further condition supplies.

A different remainder lives next door and must not be merged with this one. Stack twelve pure fifths and fold them down by seven octaves, and they do not quite meet:

$$
\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^{7}}=\frac{531441}{524288}\neq 1 .
$$

The excess is the Pythagorean comma, the small discrepancy between twelve fifths and seven octaves, and the history of tuning is in large part the history of what to do with it. Equal temperament narrows each fifth by a twelfth of the comma, just enough for the twelve fifths to equal seven octaves exactly, and pays for the closed circle with fifths that are no longer pure.[^s3-48] The whole tone and the comma are different remainders with different offices. The whole tone is the interval through which a doubled fourth completes an octave; the comma is a discrepancy that a closed cycle of fifths must distribute somewhere. One is the completion, the other the cost of forcing a different completion, and an essay that called them both "the remainder" in one breath would have lost the exactness that gives either of them meaning. In the two logics, a tuning that treats the whole tone as a defect to be closed reads it the way cancellation reads a polarity, as a shortfall to be summed away, and finds itself with no octave; tempering the comma is a deliberate redistribution that knows what it gives up, and nothing in the argument condemns it.

My theorem went further at one point, and a later correction of my own qualifies it. I wrote that `9/8` measures the matheme's non-closure, that a totality equal to its octave would be a dead identity, and that the missing whole tone is the live tick each cycle hands forward so that the next has something to begin from. The first claim holds: a cycle that continues inherits the remainder as its next beginning, and the operational return makes an achieved musical relation the condition of a further performance. My musical derivation also has a second ending. In the telic return the standing relation enacted through the performance is recognised as whole, and the bellows can fall still while the capacity to play remains; silence does not cancel the articulation that made the recognition possible, and a completed piece is complete.[^s3-49] Fulfilment includes a genuine end. The remainder invites a further movement without condemning an achieved one to have been incomplete, and the torus already held both endings, a periodic return that closes and a dense flow that never does, on one surface.

Sound needs a body, and the body decides which tones it can hold. A string fixed at both ends vibrates in standing waves of the form

$$
y(x,t)=A\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)\cos(\omega t),\qquad \omega=\frac{n\pi v}{L},
$$

with `L` its length, `v` the speed of waves along it and `n` a whole number, so the boundary conditions admit only certain modes, and the points of the string that stay still, the nodes, fall where the sine is zero.[^s3-50] Change the support, or move from a string to a plate, and the problem and its patterns change. Scatter sand on a plate and drive it with a tone, and the sand gathers along the nodal lines into figures that change when the pitch changes. One vibration is then heard as a tone and seen as a pattern, two measurements of one physical process under its boundary conditions, and the crossing from audible to visible form has a real physical neighbour in these figures. The neighbour establishes that sound and visible form can be two faces of one vibration in a bounded medium. It does not establish an identity between sound, light and consciousness, and the later comparisons with *nāda* and *Vāk* in §4 and §5 carry their own warrants.

Topology and music are the two native renderings of what this section has been deriving, and they render different things. Topology asks where a distinction can travel and return without cancellation; music asks how difference can sound together without fusion. The torus answers with a surface whose loops come home carrying their winding, and whose opening, unfillable, is the reason the windings cannot be erased; the octave answers with an interval that completes a span through its remainder, and whose register, retained, is the reason a return to the same note is not a return to the same sound. The winding vector and the frequency ratio are different mathematical objects, and nothing here converts a displacement into an interval. What the comparison relates is an operation: each register makes return-with-difference exact in its own material, and their agreement is analogy in the strict sense, a proportion between two exact relations, each holding in its own field. The previous movements accumulated several rigorous ways of carrying a relation through a transformation: the constant that local exactness lacks, the six slash-forms read from both ends, the phase that alternation keeps, the frame that changes so a relation can hold, the winding kept on a quotient, the interval through which a span completes. All of them can be written on one field, and that field now needs its name.

Audited: the circle and torus as quotients, the Euler characteristic, path lifting and winding classes, the periodic and dense flows, the sphere's trivial fundamental group, the Klein bottle's double cover, the Möbius band, the quaternions' double covering of rotations, the tokamak's field geometry, pitch class as a quotient, every interval identity and the comma, and the string's standing modes are derived in their own mathematics and physics. ### #5→0 · The Arche-Topos

A jigsaw puzzle begins with its destination already in view. The picture on the lid shows what the thousand pieces will become, and the work proceeds under it: we turn the pieces face up, find the straight edges and build the frame first, then sort the rest by colour and by the marks that promise them a place inside it. Every fit confirms a small part of the anticipated whole. The work is exact, patient and satisfying, and anyone who has spent a winter evening on it knows the small pleasure of a piece that drops into place. Yet the finished picture has entered the work before the first piece is placed. It decides what counts as completion, which differences belong together, which edge is outside, and which piece that seems not to fit is merely waiting for its right neighbour. My image of inquiry carries this puzzle somewhere it was not made to go: its maker sets out into the very landscape the lid depicts. The road on the lid continues past the picture's edge; the building in the middle looks different from the other side; a field that seemed flat turns out to fall away into a valley no single view could show. At first each discovery looks like a missing piece, and the maker adds pieces, but every added piece is still being judged by the lid, and the lid was a view from one place. What is missing is an account of how the picture was made: from where, across which region, under what projection, with which omissions, and by what rule one view could pass into another. So the pieces change office. Each becomes a local chart, exact over its own domain, overlapping its neighbours instead of merely meeting them along a cut, and each overlap carries a rule saying how a place described here appears there. The maker comes home at last to a familiar spot and recognises it, and the recognition carries the route by which she came.[^s3-51]

The lid is the world-picture of §0/1, and the puzzle gives its topology. Assembled under the lid, the finished jigsaw is a disk: a single piece of plane with a boundary and no hole, on which every loop can be shrunk to a point, a flat map stretched over a curved world with its frame built first. Heidegger read the modern age as the age in which the world is grasped as picture and the one who represents it is installed as the centre on which everything depends, and the lid enacts both logics in their collapsed forms. It appropriates. Every piece is reckoned from the lid's pole, so a piece that fits counts for the picture, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and a piece that does not fit is entered against itself as the error, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, one span charged from opposite ends, with the picture always on the winning side. It cancels. A piece that belongs to no place under the lid is set aside as not of this puzzle, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the account balances by erasing what it could not hold. Neither operation is wrong as a way of finishing a jigsaw. Both become an error when the lid is taken for the landscape, and the maker discovers the error by travelling.

Under QL's grammar the same puzzle re-derives. The pieces become local covers of the domain they describe, each known in relation to the others and to the activity of covering, and the whole that regulates them is no longer a picture on a box but the covering relation itself, the bounded surface held in relation to the infinite plane that covers it, which is `0/1` written in topology. §3 · #3 built the mathematics of the change. A curved whole cannot be covered by a single chart onto flat coordinates; an atlas is a plurality of overlapping local charts with declared transition rules on their overlaps; and on the circle two charts reported a single point as `u = 3` and as `v = 1/3`, disagreeing as numbers and agreeing as descriptions, because the rule `v = 1/u` identified them and even translated a motion from one to the other. §3 · #4 built the return: a loop on the torus comes home to its point and keeps its winding in the lift. Both properties belong to the travelling maker's return. She recognises the familiar spot because the transition rules let her pass back into the chart that holds it, and she arrives with the route, the windings of her journey, retained. An atlas re-founds world-pictures: the lid survives as one chart among many, still exact over its domain, with its frame now visible.

§0/1 left a titan holding up the sky. Jason Reza Jorjani reads technoscience through Prometheus, the forethinking drive that steals fire, and Atlas, the world-bearing power that holds a world up and maps it, and we read the perspectival world-picture as Atlas's burden, a whole world held up from one standing place, crushing whoever takes the weight for the only way a world can be carried. We promised that the answer would keep his name. An atlas of charts is that answer. Nobody in it has to carry the whole as one picture, because no chart pretends to be the whole; its unity lives in the rules by which each chart passes into its neighbours, and those rules are carried by every chart together. Atlas can set the sky down because the sky was never a thing one picture had to bear.

This is the Meta-Epistemic Framework stated as mathematics. Its lenses are charts, each exact within its declared domain; its translations between lenses are transition rules, each stating how a determination in one lens appears in another; its overlaps are the places where one refraction becomes answerable to another, so that a disagreement can be diagnosed as a change of coordinates, a difference in what is being measured, or a genuine conflict that no available rule resolves.[^s3-52] Benjamin Bratton's brief for *Agentworld* describes the condition a society of humans and agents must find its way through, and names it a narrow corridor between a monoculture that fails all at once and incompatible world-models that cannot even agree on the shape of their disagreement: "same decision-procedural grammar, sufficiently divergent world models."[^s3-53] The atlas answers that need exactly. Its shared grammar is the set of transition rules, positions and returns that every participant can inspect; its plural content is the charts, which remain different and remain each other's to translate. Shared relational grammar, plural ontological content: QL supplies the repeatable positions, exclusions and returns, and MEF keeps each agent's lens, source and context visible, so that coordination need not make one world-model the compulsory average of all. The mathematical atlas and the epistemic one keep their different objects. A coordinate chart has a smooth domain and an invertible transition, and an opinion has neither by virtue of being held; the epistemic atlas is answerable to whether its declared translations actually preserve what they claim to preserve, and §5 takes up the instruments by which that can be tested.

I call the field on which this section has been working the **arche-topos**. Greek *archē* means beginning and origin, and also first principle and rule, the source from which something proceeds and the principle by which it is governed; *topos* means place. The compound is mine, and it borrows nothing from the topoi of category theory, which are a different construction.[^s3-54] The arche-topos is the differential field on which the relation `0/1` can be written as a path, circulated as a winding, heard as an interval and recognised as a symbolic transformation. It is archetypal because it specifies recurrent forms of differentiation before they receive psychological or material content, and it is a topos because neighbourhood, orientation, path, quotient, cover, hole and return govern placement in it, more than any catalogue of correspondences could. "Differential" names the field's work of differentiating, of making places, poles, paths and horizons determinate, and it should not be heard as the algebraic structure that shares the name. The arche-topos is also not a hidden location at which all perspectives would finally coincide, since such a location would already be one more position within it. It is the placing through which an observer and an object acquire their positions together: the field in which *here*, *there* and a route between them become possible at all.
