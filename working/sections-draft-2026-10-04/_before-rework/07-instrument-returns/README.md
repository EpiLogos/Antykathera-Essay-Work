# §5→0 — Epi-Logos and the Instrument's Return (draft)

**What it does.** Six movements, about 23,700 words of prose plus 136 Chicago notes, written in order in one voice after the finished §0/1. Each opens from a scene: a handover note between agent sessions, a Jung line that could not be found, the sponge divers at Antikythera, the Valencia Water Tribunal, a wrong weather forecast, and the bedside again, with the child's "why?" now put to a speaker on the shelf. The section closes the essay's `4+2+2` envelope. It delivers the mechanism §0/1 withheld (#2). It returns to "What knows?" through the order `1/0 → 0/1 → (0/1)/(1/0)` and the copula sequence `I = Is → … → I = Am`. It does not re-decide §0/1's answer. Whether the artefact knows stays open, and the instrument's office is the one §0/1 promised: to return a person to the knowing through which they meet it.
