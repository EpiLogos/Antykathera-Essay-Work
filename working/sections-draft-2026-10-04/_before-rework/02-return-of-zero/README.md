# §1 — The Return of Zero (draft)

**What the draft does.** Six movements (`M01.md`–`M06.md`, assembled in `SECTION.md`, about 22,900 words of prose and 77 Chicago notes) deliver the history §0/1 and the abstract promised, "from Babylonian positional absence to formal logic", as the repayment of the loan §0/1 took out when it borrowed `0`. #0 follows the sign's migration as a series of offices granted by receiving practices: Babylonian context, the separator, Ptolemy's circle, Sanskrit space-words and *śūnya*, the dated inscriptions, al-Khwārizmī, Fibonacci, Rotman's meta-sign, and the tokeniser. #1 reads Brahmagupta's fortune/debt rules (with the two logics written in situ), Bhāskara's suspended zero-multiple and *khahara*, the commentators' argument for the infinite, the Upaniṣadic *pūrṇa* verse, and the Abhinavagupta–Bhāskara braid (c. 975–1025 / 1150, no transmission claimed). #2 builds one from nothing: Frege's zero as the count of self-inequality (read as the crossed copula), von Neumann's ordinals, the identity element, joint denial and the NOR-gate machine. #3 returns to `Ø` with its one job (lock 5) and the history of the crossed sign (slashed zero, Ø, ∅, ⌀), Descartes and Hume at one position, the Spanda thread, the linking one and S1, and the persona's "you" turned "I". #4 derives division by zero, the zero ring as fusion, the reframings (projective line from Desargues, Riemann sphere, wheels, meadows), IEEE 754's signed zero and NaN, the ε in every model, Berkeley to Robinson, and the immutable gap's mathematical form. #5→0 returns the loan: the Stern–Brocot tree from `0/1` and `1/0`, the offices of each term, the break–retention test performed by Bhāskara's arithmetic, the paper exercise, Leibniz's binary read the other way round, Śiva/Śakti, ratio and *credere*, and the release into §2.

**What it took from the corpus.** Plan §1 burdens and the six movement notes; A10, A11, A15, A23, C52; the theorem spine §§II, IV, IX; *The Advent of Zero* manuscript; Frank's Kaplan marginalia (p. 73 "all numbers the same", chapter 15, the "synchronistic" Indian hinge, "returning the zero to the metaphysical domain"); the Colebrooke, Dutta, Kaplan, Scharf and Muller-Ortega houses; and §0/1's established terms, cited back as returns (Two Ones, `Ø`, Objective Internality, the dice, the copula marks, trust/*fides*/*credere*, interest as *inter-esse*, Alberti's pyramid, the immutable gap). The manuscript's earlier §1 was used as quarry for the mediant derivation and the ratio/reckoner paragraph.

**What it could not settle.** Web verification was cut short by a usage limit: many locators are missing and several quotations were transcribed from OCR or Gutenberg text without collation (see `DEBTS.md`). Rotman's book and Nothaft's article body were not read. The slashed-zero convention has no source yet. The prose runs about 3,100–4,700 words a movement, under the brief's 6,000–10,000: I deepened each movement with material I could verify and stopped there, since further length would have been padding or unsourced history. The places where more would carry are the Maya zero, Ptolemy's tables in a critical edition, Rotman read closely, and al-Khwārizmī's Latin text. #3 is short by design, because lock 5 reserves the full crossed-zero sequence for §4 · #3.

**Decisions for Frank.**
1. **Chronology.** Your note sets Bhāskara "around the same time" as Abhinavagupta. The draft writes the interval the canon records, about a century and a quarter, and uses your word "synchronistic" in Jung's strict sense. Is that right?
2. **New cases not yet in canon.** These are Leibniz's binary as *Imago Creationis*, read as the inverse of `0/1` (the release into §2); Bhāskara's *Līlāvatī* zero-multiple as the break–retention–recognition test done in arithmetic; Frege's zero as the count of the crossed copula; the zero ring as fusion beside the field's refusal as dia-ballein's proper cut; IEEE signed zero and NaN read against the polar axis and Frege; Colebrooke's "thrown together"; Desargues joining the vanishing point to `1/0`; Pacioli's ledger. 3. **Brahmagupta's vocabulary.** The fortune/debt reading carries the in-situ logics in #1. It rests on MacTutor until Plofker is checked.
4. **`Ø`'s history.** #3 adds the history of the sign (Weil's ∅, the slashed zero, Ø) and makes Descartes and Hume one position, from the theorem spine. Keep it at this length, or move more of it to §4?
5. **Form.** Movement headings follow §0/1 (`### #n · Title`, anchors M13–M18), and notes are numbered 1–77 across the section. The "The …" sentence-opening rate is about 12 per cent; a copyedit pass could lower it.
6. **Pointers.** "§0 has meanwhile followed awareness…" assumes §0 ends as the plan says. Check it against the §0 draft. The draft does not mention the Antikythera mechanism, and it leaves the terminal office to A36 / §5→0.
