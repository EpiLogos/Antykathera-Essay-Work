# Brief — drafting the seven later sections

Frank has finished §0/1, "The Integral Threshold". He wants to see what full drafts of the other seven sections look like when they are written to the same standard. These are drafts for his reading. They do not replace the sovereign manuscript and must not be written into it.

## The finished model to write after

- `submission-package/essay/CONFRONTING-THE-LIMIT-S01.md` is the finished §0/1: six movements of about 3,500–13,500 words each, about 62,800 words in all, with Chicago endnotes per movement. Read all of it before writing, for:
  - its voice, density and paragraph shape;
  - its "we" register;
  - its method of speaking through concrete scenes and cases;
  - its notation discipline;
  - every pointer it makes forward to your section. Search it for "§0", "§1", "§2", "§3", "§4", "§5" and "§5→0".

  Your section must deliver what §0/1 promises it, and must not re-establish what §0/1 already established. Cite back to §0/1 as a return, not a re-definition.
- `working/s01-hardening-2026-10-01/M01-REWRITE.md` … `M06-REWRITE.md` hold the same prose with working notes. `SECTION-UPDATE-LEDGER.md` beside them records the canon decisions taken during the §0/1 pass. These include:
  - *syn-ballein*, never "sym-ballein";
  - healthy polarity written `(−1)/(+1)`, with both appropriation chiralities `(+1)−(−1)=+2` and `(−1)−(+1)=−2`;
  - Subject and God as two signs of one referent;
  - no "conspiracy theory" as a term.

  These bind every section.
- `PROSE-STANDARD.md` governs all prose. Reread it before each movement. Its lessons sections, from M04 and from M01–M03, are the hard-won specifics:
  - one mode per scene;
  - no casting the reader as "you";
  - no fake-poetic lines;
  - no "x was there before y" or "x is y, without a and b" tics;
  - no nouning;
  - flowing paragraphs, not lists.

## What to produce

- Six movements for your section, `#0` to `#5→0` as the room defines them. Aim for the depth of §0/1: roughly 6,000–10,000 words per movement where the material carries it. Never pad. Each movement must:
  - open from a concrete case or scene;
  - define what it introduces at first use;
  - return to earlier sections where it draws on them;
  - point forward to the sections that develop what it opens.
- Chicago 18 endnotes per movement, in the clean reader-facing form used in `CONFRONTING-THE-LIMIT-S01.md`:
  - real locators only;
  - your own readings stated as the author's;
  - no internal language (card IDs, "no house", "to verify").
