## 4. Voice and quality

All seven drafts are learned, careful and honest about where their warrants stop. None reaches §0/1's hand, which has first-person asides, a colloquial edge, puns that argue, and sentences that run on because the thought is moving. All seven fall into PROSE-STANDARD's fault 3, the even "efficient" cadence, and each repeats one movement template.

On the second person, the drafts are cleaner than §0/1 itself. Only §5→0 casts the reader as "you", once.

### §0 · Differentiating Mind

It is the most disciplined on canon:
- the logics are written in place, with the mirror named, in every movement;
- the whole is right;
- the reader is never cast as "you" (both hits are Bāhiya's "you");
- claims are affirmed rather than hedged.

The faults:
- **One template per movement:** scene → etymology → Dyczkowski → Buddhist/Śaiva contrast → logics paragraph → "Artificial agents…" paragraph → hand-off.
- **"The…" openings** at about 28–34% of sentences, and about 50 uses of "exact" or "precise".
- **Internal language:** "Our theorem spine" ×5, where §0/1's own phrase is "our core theorems".
- **Back-narration:** §0/1 is "the threshold" 62 times.
- **Narrow registers:** almost no political or institutional cases.

**Strongest: #1 · The Inner Instrument.** One misread message is threaded through every office, and each failure is corrected at its own office. It carries the most authorial material: selfing, the self-thing, the *sell-thing* pun.

**Weakest: #4 · Tattvic Compression.** A table is then re-walked as a cast ("At #0… At #5→0…") and a *kañcuka* list. The tea scene is glue. The gnomon is the only derivation.

### §1 · The Return of Zero

It is lucid intellectual history with real derivations: the field proof, the mediant properties, the zero ring.

The faults:
- **One cadence:** paragraphs of 150–250 words throughout.
- **Every movement opens on the same device:** "Most of us…" with an everyday object.
- **"The…" openings** at about 22–36%. The README claims about 12%.
- **Hedging:** many "does not prove" and "we claim no such line of influence" sentences (faults 1 and 12).
- **Internal language:** "the theorem spine" ×3, "my notes on that chapter".
- **No process disclosure.**

**Strongest: #4 · Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics.** It derives inside the material and threads one claim through the projective line, IEEE 754, ε and Robinson, with the logics written in place: zero ring = fusion, the field's refusal = dia-ballein's proper cut.

**Weakest: #3 · The Crossed Zero.** It is the shortest, has the highest "The…" rate, re-explains §0/1, and takes §4's Descartes and Hume material.

The faults:
