# §2 — Two Logics of Two: what this draft is

Six movements (`M01.md`–`M06.md` = M19–M24), assembled in `SECTION.md` with Chicago notes grouped by movement (134 notes) and a source list per movement; verification debts in `DEBTS.md`. About 25,000 words of prose: #0 4,200 · #1 4,400 · #2 4,100 · #3 5,300 · #4 4,000 · #5→0 3,200. That is below the brief's 6,000–10,000 per movement. I stopped where the material stopped carrying weight rather than pad; the places that could take more body are named below.

**What it does.** It writes after the finished §0/1 and treats §0/1, #2's arithmetic of the two logics (held polarity, cancellation, both chiralities, `(0/1)/(1/0)`) as established, returning to it instead of deriving it again. It delivers §0/1's §2 pointers:

- the mythemic bodies War and Love, Ares and Aphrodite in Hephaestus's net (M22, whole song, *Odyssey* 8.266–366);
- the devil as "literalist of the minus sign", developed in M20 through the three offices of the minus sign and completed in M24 as "two deaths, one minus sign";
- divide and conquer working only while both parties consent to be divided (M20: consent is each pole accepting the axis as the whole of its relation; the withheld combine step);
- *krisis* and the dance or battle question, answered through Mecone (M19) and the Phaeacian dancers (M22).

The plan's other plantings are also delivered: "knowledge is a performance of trust wearing the mask of certainty" (M19); mono–poly trust and provisional anchors; divine and concur (M21); counterfeit gathering, the surety and Indra's net (M22); Bohm's fragmentation and the Apollo–Dionysus safeguard (M23); and `2+2²` with zero's change of role (M24).

**What it took from the corpus.** The plan's §2, the room and its six movement notes, A13/C50/C23–C25, the theorem spine (§§II, IV, XI) and the late quilt's §10 (Ares/Aphrodite, "war is the demand that the slash produce a winner", monoculture and counterculture). It also used the Ares/Aphrodite, Indra and Apollo–Dionysus–Daphne wholes and the old manuscript §2 as quarry: the unanswered message, the six arbitration operations, the typed count and the Prisoner. Source houses used: Homer, Hesiod, Mackenzie, Heidegger (×3), Das, Le Bon, Jung *Undiscovered Self*, Desmet, Daza, Nietzsche, Bratton (q016, q029–q031, q035, q039), Dushun, Van Norden & Jones and McGoohan. New material, all checked in hand where quoted:

- Jung CW 7 ¶78 (the opposite of Eros is Phobos, psychologically the will to power), read with Hesiod's Phobos as a child of Ares and Aphrodite;
- CW 7 ¶¶111–12 (enantiodromia; separating oneself from the unconscious);
- the Mecone division (*Theogony* 535ff., ἐκρίνοντο);
- *harmonia* as joint, compact and attunement (*Od.* 5.248; *Il.* 22.254–67);
- Odysseus stringing the bow like a lyre (*Od.* 21);
- Leviticus 25 (the jubilee as a zero of return);
- the counterfeit symbolon and the liar's dividend (Chesney and Citron).

**What it could not settle.** It could not settle the quotation states of Detienne–Vernant, Brown, Burkert and Phillips: Burkert and Phillips are left out, and the other two are paraphrased only. It could not check the LSJ senses for ἁρμονία, Van Eenwyk's pages, Bohm's fragmentation passage or several Homeric line spans (see `DEBTS.md`). Nor could it settle how much of §1's historical zero §2 may presume, since §1 is being drafted in parallel; M24 points to §3 for the eight determinations and gives only the parent/return bracket.

## Decisions for Frank

1. **Name.** The draft uses *syn-ballein* throughout, per your 2026-10-03 ruling. But the plan, room, movement titles and A13 alias still say *Sym-ballein*. Rename them, or keep them as aliases?
2. **Polarity order.** The plan's §2 writes `(-1)/(+1)`, while the CLAUDE.md notation lock and the M21 movement note write `(+1)/(−1)`. The draft uses `(−1)/(+1)`, with both chiralities. Confirm, and align the plan's §2.
3. **New mythic material.** Admit or cut Prometheus at Mecone (M19), the triple sense of *harmonia* and Achilles' refusal of a compact (M22), Dionysus as Harmonia's grandson (the bridge into M23), and the jubilee (M24). None of these is in canon yet.
4. **Jung CW 7.** ¶78 (Eros/Phobos/will to power) and ¶¶111–12 now carry M22 and M23. This needs a CW 7 house and cards, which would be a canon addition to A13/A19.
5. **Agentworld trust.** M22 tests Bratton's "prediction-stability" definition of trust against the net, arguing that the net's captives are perfectly predictable and still need a surety. This argues with the brief's scoped definition. Keep, soften, or move to §5 · #3?
6. **The Prisoner.** The plan's §2 does not name it. The draft uses it once, in M24, as the counterfeit of zero's role-change. Keep it here, or leave it to the A13 / C25 field only?
7. **Daphne and the late quilt.** Daphne appears only as a pointer to §4, and the late quilt's candidate `(1)` notation is not used. Confirm both.
8. **Length.** Do you want the movements brought nearer 6,000 words? If so, the honest places to grow are:
   - M20: a fuller Girard / scapegoat passage;
   - M21: a worked example of provenance in an agent harness;
   - M24: §1's zero history, once §1's draft is in hand.
9. **Canon updates implied.** If you accept the draft, these follow:
   - C25 (counterfeit gathering) gains M22 as its manuscript statement;
   - A13 gains the measured-two versus counted-two contrast (M24) and the minus sign's three offices (M20);
   - A23 gains "mono–poly trust" and the jubilee;
   - the presaging ledger records the trust wager as planted in M19.
