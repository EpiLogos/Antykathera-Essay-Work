# §5 draft — Objective Internality and Agentic Research

Exploratory draft for Frank, 4–5 October 2026. It governs nothing and was not written into the manuscript, the room, the movement notes or any source house.

**What it does.** Six movements, M37–M42, follow the room's product order: Central, Actuation, AIKit, Software Factory, Workcell and Quaternal Logic. Each is read through its whole MEF pair, set out as a two-lens table with the rows read across. The section's spine is the commission's phrase: O:I is **a harness before the harness**, the person-owned world agents act from, which persists while models and harnesses change. The phrase is set against Bratton's §10.2 ("capability … is a property of the harness") and his p. 56 row *model-centric → harness-centric*. The essay's step is to ask who holds what the harness draws on. M37 does the S-level entrance: the knower/means/known relation recalled from §0/1, the O:I acronym, and Genesis/Paradigm/Project as the compression. M42 does the section's 5→0 return through S. Every movement opens from a concrete case, often one observed on this machine on 4 October: the rendered floor, `actuation harness detect` (15 of 24 harnesses), the AIKit skill projection, the retirement of the prose gates, `workcell status`, and `ql matheme derive` printing §0/1's 100, 16/9 and 9/8. The technical claims were checked against each repository's README on `main` and the installed binaries, then cross-checked against the README audit (`working/oi-readme-audit-2026-10-04/`). One stale claim was corrected as a result: Central's README still lists NOW/DAY and machine Actions as open development work, but they are on `main`. Developing features are named as developing, and E1–E6 are named as offered and unrun.

**What it delivers from §0/1.** The "captured power in its own specificity" promise is met in M40. It runs from Thiel to Palantir to the NHS Federated Data Platform: a platform whose ontology persists while models are swapped is a harness before the harness held by a contractor. The text keeps the earlier draft's boundaries (no motive inferred, no claim that OPTICA uses an LLM, no adjudication of the contract). The Agentworld response is central throughout. M42 argues that Bratton's two pathologies, ontology collapse and fractal conflict, are dia-ballein's cancellation and its two-chirality appropriation, and that his "narrow corridor" is the held polarity within syn-ballein. M42 closes by answering the brief's propositions product by product. The text cites back to §0/1 for attention, the Logos cycle, the relational sixfold for models, Deferential Intelligence and co-internality, and does not re-derive them. It does not introduce the Antikythera mechanism.
