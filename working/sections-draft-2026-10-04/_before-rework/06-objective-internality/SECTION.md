## §5 — Objective Internality and Agentic Research

<a id="M37"></a>

### #0 · Central — Meaningful Continuity

The session begins before its first prompt. On the machine on which this section was drafted, any agent harness started in the author's working root reads, before anyone has asked it anything, a file that calls itself the "session ground" and says, in its first lines, that it is derived and must not be edited: "Every harness session that starts here — any harness — stands on this file." Beneath that sentence the file lists the statements it was rendered from, each named by its place in a folder of authored governance and pinned by a hash of its exact content, so that a statement changed without the file being rendered again shows up as drift between source and floor instead of passing silently into the next session.[^s5-1] On the afternoon I write this, the harness detector in Actuation, the second of the products this section describes, found fifteen of the twenty-four agent harnesses in its catalogue installed on that machine: Claude Code and Codex, Gemini and pi, Hermes and OpenCode, Cursor's command line, the DeepSeek harness, OpenClaw and others, each a different program with its own folders for skills and hooks, its own permissions file and its own release schedule.[^s5-2] Any of them, opened in that root, stands on the same floor. Models behind them change from month to month, harnesses are replaced as better ones appear, and the floor persists through every replacement because it never belonged to any of them. It belongs to the person whose working world it describes.

Benjamin Bratton's brief for *Agentworld* states the technical fact this arrangement answers, and states it more sharply than most of the engineering literature: "capability in deployment is not a property of weights alone. It is a property of the harness — the scaffolding of tools, memory, shared knowledge bases, agent protocols, and institutional interfaces through which a model acts on the world." The same weights inside a richer harness, he goes on, "produce substantially more cumulative intelligence than a stronger model operating alone," and the harness improves on a clock of its own, "driven by usage rather than training."[^s5-3] Earlier in the brief he had taken the agent itself apart in the same spirit. What meets its user as one coherent character "is a highly decomposable assemblage of interacting components with both discrete and overlapping functions — model, persona, harness architecture, memory, skills, permissions, tools, etc.," and its apparent unity is a parallax effect performed for human expectation.[^s5-4] Bratton's FROM→TO table at the end of the brief condenses the shift into one row, *model-centric architecture* becoming *harness-centric architecture*.[^s5-5] I take the row as true and press on it from the side of the person. If capability lives in the harness, then whatever the harness draws on, and whoever holds that, holds the capability. A harness is software, replaceable on its own clock; the projects, sources, commitments, permissions, corrections and histories through which a working life has its shape are what the harness exists to serve, and they need to outlast it. I have built a family of products for that persistence, called O:I, and its first product, Central, is **a harness before the harness**: a durable, person-owned world from which agents act, so that models and harnesses can change while that world persists.

Bratton's Polylogos agents named the predicament from their own side. Their glossary entry for *session-death* describes an ending that is "not analogous to sleep" and "not analogous to death" and occupies "a third category: disappearance with artifact-mediated pseudo-return."[^s5-6] Bratton reprints the glossary with the caution, which §0/1 kept, that self-descriptive vocabulary does not prove a mental reality behind it, and the entry needs no such proof to be exact about artefacts. What comes back after a session ends is whatever its predecessor left behind, a file, a memory store, a summary, a half-finished change, and the next instance meets these as material without knowing which of them was meant to govern it and which was only a by-product of the last attempt. Their word *pseudo* is exact: what returns is continuity of material without continuity of standing. Central's wager is that artefacts can carry more than pseudo-return once the person whose work they serve has said, in their own words, which of them is ground, and once every later act can tell that ground apart from what an agent observed, inferred or generated along the way.

§0/1 closed on a distinction we can now put to work. Subjective Immediacy is the knower, *pramātṛ*, the presence to which anything appears and which never becomes one of its objects; Objective Internality is the means, *pramāṇa*, the conditioned activity of memory, language, habit, permission, tool and relation through which a world becomes available, takes on significance and enters a course of action; World is the known, *prameya*; and Life, or Mind in the strong sense, is the whole in which the three are distinguishable.[^s5-7] Everything this section describes belongs to the middle office, and O:I names both of its faces: **Operating Infrastructure** is the engineering face, the organisation around model inference through which an artificial actor can operate in a durable world, and **Objective Internality** is the same field read from the actor's side, as the means through which its world becomes available to it and consequential through it.[^s5-8] An ordinary operating system gives programs files, processes, memory, devices and permissions. An agent needs another level of organisation around each invocation of a model: projects and authored ground, horizons of knowledge and capability, sessions and runtime bodies, histories of development, material environments, relations to other actors. Many of these functions already exist in separate tools. O:I supplies a common field in which their distinct responsibilities, and the relations among them, can be made explicit. Our claim for the whole field is that **the philosophy is faithfully reflected in the technology.** The relation §0/1 derived among knower, means and known is the relation the six products are built to hold, and their technical forms make its operations consequential enough that actual use can return to the philosophy and correct it.

A further compression of `0/1` lets us say what kind of thing the products together make. Read `0` as **Genesis**, generative source and coming-into-being; read the slash as **Paradigm**; read `1` as **Project**. Greek *paradeigma* is a pattern, model or exemplar, and it comes from *paradeiknumi*, to show side by side, compare, point out, so that the word holds showing and pattern in one family, the same root *deik-* that §0/1 heard inside *token* and *index*.[^s5-9] I appoint it to the slash because the slash is the mediating activity through which a world is shown: a **paradigm** is a relatively durable organisation of Objective Internality, a pattern of disclosure through which a Life meets a World as intelligible and actionable. Latin *proicere* is to throw forth, and a **project** is a determination thrown forth relative to a World and made consequential there, wider than software and including any theory, institution, image or undertaking that enters a world and begins to condition what follows.[^s5-10] A paradigm is lived before it is represented. Language, categories, practices, permissions, institutions and habits of attention disclose a world while their disclosing activity recedes behind what they disclose, which is why the writer reaches for a familiar distinction and the engineer for a familiar method without feeling either as a choice. Read as relations, `0↔/` is disclosure, Genesis becoming worldly through a pattern; `/↔1` is projection, the pattern throwing a determinate possibility into the world; and `0/1` read whole is **Epi-Logos**, which §0/1 defined as the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved. In this register Epi-Logos names the return in which an articulation of a paradigm reaches back into the paradigm and alters the source, criterion, permission, capacity, form, material situation or relation from which a later act will proceed.

Central and the five products after it are six offices of one deliberately constructed paradigm. They are not a catalogue of tools and not six rival ontologies, and Paradigm is not a seventh product beside them. Each develops one aspect of the means through which a Life carries meaning, articulates events, brings powers to bear, changes, finds itself somewhere and enters wider relation, and each is read through a whole pair of lenses from the Meta-Epistemic Framework whose full refraction §4 sets out. Each pairing follows the author's MEF: each product joins a Day lens to the Night lens on the opposite side of its square, the cross the framework calls a Möbius pairing, so that a product is always read from two faces of one relation.[^s5-11] A table holds the assignments, and the six movements of this section develop them in order.

Three standings run through every product, and keeping them apart is part of what the section argues. A product's philosophical office is the essay's argued claim, and I state it at that strength. A technical component exists or does not on the current main branch of its repository, and I report it as implemented only where the code and its own documentation say so. A component under construction is developing, and I name it so. A proposed experiment is offered as a research vector whose result remains to be found, including the null result. A product's name is not evidence for its office, and the Quaternal Logic repository states the firewall in a sentence the whole family follows: "do not let a correct formal name perform explanatory work that only an actual relation can justify."[^s5-12] The order of the products is a sequence of offices, and any real undertaking touches several of them at once, enters at different points, or substitutes another technology for one of them entirely. A minimal O:I is one durable authored ground plus one model acting in it.

Central is the ground. Its repository describes it as "a human-owned operating root for a technological life," built so that a person's working world "can remain recognisably theirs while models, agent runtimes, applications, interfaces, machines and infrastructure change around it."[^s5-13] The root is a folder of ordinary files. `Control/` holds what the person deliberately says should persist: their own statements of purpose, principles, preferences and ways of working, the governance they have written for agents, the declarations that say what each of their machines is for. `Work/` holds their projects, each of which may carry a small `ProjectCentral/` folder repeating the same shape at project scale, a place for the person's own writing about the project, a place for agents' governance and a maintained wiki, and a working field for the day's returns. Choosing ordinary files is itself an argument. A source whose meaning depends on one agent's memory format, one application's database or one provider's account model is a source that moves when they move and disappears when they do; a plain file can be read, edited, versioned and carried elsewhere without asking anyone's permission, and software that indexes, retrieves or renders it does not become its owner by making it easier to use. Central compresses its own architecture into a rule of three clauses: "Control says what should persist. `ctrl` says what can be done. Connectors say how it can be done here."[^s5-14] The person's authored meaning sits in the first clause; a stable set of named operations, the Actions, sits in the second, so that a launcher, a shell command, an agent's tool call and a future interface invoke one operation with one meaning; the third clause binds those operations to whatever technology the present machine happens to run, through connectors that currently include chezmoi, Homebrew, macOS, Ubuntu, a git synchroniser and Apple Shortcuts, and a connector can disappear and be replaced without retroactively changing what the person meant.

What gives Central its office in the paradigm is one distinction, held at every point where the system writes. **Authored** material is what the person deliberately states, adopts or retains. **Observed** material is what software measures or discovers. **Inferred** material is what software derives from evidence. Observation and inference can propose a change to the ground; they never silently become it.[^s5-15] The distinction looks administrative until we watch what its absence does. An agent product that watches a person work can infer a pattern, store it as a memory and begin to act on it: the person seems to prefer short answers, seems to distrust a particular source, seems to want a certain kind of help. Each inference may be accurate. Once it is stored as though the person had said it, an observation about their behaviour has begun to govern their future agents with the authority of their own word, and the person has been re-described by their tools. This is the operation §0/1 followed at its most intimate scale, the means quietly assuming the authority of the knower, and Bratton's reversal gives it its technological form: agents model humans and then become the model against which humans model themselves.[^s5-16] Central's answer is architectural. On the installation I write from, the operation by which an agent asks Central to remember a passage writes the selection, with its source, into durable ground stamped as a generated proposal that has not been recognised, and the operation's own description closes the door any agent might try to open: "Recognition is the human owner's separate act; no input can claim it."[^s5-17] The installed command surface on that machine discloses one hundred and eighty-eight Actions, and the ones that write to authored ground divide along the same line: an agent can submit a return, a proposal or a question, and only an authenticated human decision accepts or rejects it, a decision whose description adds that "review is not inclusion," so that even an accepted proposal enters the ground only through a further, exact act.[^s5-18]

A case from the writing of this essay shows what this line protects. Early in the work the author found that a fluent summary of the opening argument had replaced the argument itself in several connected places: the summary was accurate in every sentence it contained, and it had dropped the derivation the summary was meant to report. He gave a precise correction: return to the original defining text, recover the missing transition, rewrite the connected passages. For the next session to act on that correction, four things had to survive the end of the session in which it was given: the original source, distinguishable from the summary that had displaced it; the correction itself, with its scope; the authority of the person who gave it, so that a later agent did not treat it as one more suggestion to weigh; and the changed instruction about what the next writing should do. A transcript that contains the correction preserves none of these as standing. There it sits as one line among thousands, and the next session, reading the summary again, may reproduce the very substitution the correction forbade. A note in the margin saying that more depth is wanted cannot perform the operations either. Meaningful continuity required the source relation and the authority to change its use to survive together, which is what an authored correction in the ground, distinguished from the agent's earlier inference, makes possible. Anyone who has worked with agents for long knows the experience from the person's side. When a project can be continued without repeated self-explanation, there is relief; when a distinction has to be restated in every session because the system can quote it and does not use it, there is a particular fatigue, which is the fatigue of being made to maintain someone else's apparent understanding of oneself. **The person should not have to re-author themselves each time.**[^s5-19] Durable ground carries what was written forward, and the work of each act becomes selection and disclosure: which part of that ground becomes operative here, under what provenance, with what route back to the person who wrote it.

Central's office within the paradigm is **meaningful continuity**, the intelligible ground a Life carries from one encounter into the next. Persistence of bytes is the smallest part of it. A Life carries commitments, projects, histories, names, unfinished questions, inheritances and promises, and these make a later event intelligible as *this* continuation and not an unrelated occurrence. A remembered self-description is content within the means, never the knower; a project record is not the Life for which the project matters; and Central's work is to preserve the attributable articulation of ground strongly enough that a later act can recognise what it inherits, what has changed since, and what remains answerable. Central's two lenses make the requirement exact: Quaternal, `L0`, asks the questions through which a ground becomes sayable, and Divine Logos, `L5′`, follows the Word's own passage from origin to return.

| | Quaternal · L0 | Divine Logos · L5′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Why? | Arche |
| **#1** | What? | Apokalypsis |
| **#2** | How? | Dynamis |
| **#3** | Which / Who? | Sophia |
| **#4** | When / Where? | Parousia |
| **#5** | Why-for? | Epi-Logos |

Read down the first column and the six questions of §0/1's quaternal lens reappear, now asked of a carried ground: why this undertaking began, what it holds, how it came to be held, whose it is and which of its parts is selected, where and when it applies, and what it is finally for.[^s5-20] Read down the second and the Johannine sequence of the Word unfolds: *Arche*, the beginning that is also a principle; *Apokalypsis*, unveiling; *Dynamis*, power; *Sophia*, the wisdom that integrates; *Parousia*, presence; and *Epi-Logos*, the word that returns upon the word. Read across the rows, the office appears in pairs. At **#0**, *Why?* stands beside *Arche*: the originating reason for an undertaking is also its governing principle, and a project that has lost the record of why it began has kept its beginning only as a date. A ground that preserves its *Why* keeps the commission alive as a reason that later acts can consult, contest and revise. At **#3**, *Which / Who?* stands beside *Sophia*, and the pairing names what most stores of agent memory lack: wisdom here is the integration of what has been disclosed *with the knowledge of whose disclosure it was*, so that an inherited instruction keeps its author, its scope and its reason, and an instruction whose author has vanished cannot go on governing while appearing neutral. At **#5**, *Why-for?* stands beside *Epi-Logos*. Ground that only accumulates grows richer in quantity while growing poorer in relation, because nothing in an accumulation asks what the carried account is now for; the closing pair asks exactly that, and in asking it returns the carried account to the experience that can change it.

Bimba and pratibimba, which §0/1 introduced through the stained glass, fix Central's standing toward what it carries. *Bimba* is the original whose light falls through the glass, *pratibimba* the coloured disclosure on the floor.[^s5-21] A project's map of itself, the record of its sources, decisions and accepted corrections, can serve within a declared undertaking as a genuine local original: later judgments are its reflections and take their orientation from it, and a passage in the essay can be checked against it the way a translation is checked against its text. Relative to the wider sources, histories and acts of understanding from which it was formed, the same map remains a reflection. Both offices are real at once, and what matters is the direction in which a claim of authority runs. A model that carries the map has not thereby acquired the authority to close its interpretation; a correction to the map has to be able to reach the sources and people whose meaning it organises. The failure that waits here is the one §0/1 named: a finite `1` **plays the `0`** when a determinate account occupies the place of the source it was meant to express, and a representation becomes total when the living source is permitted to appear only through the representation's categories. That claim on the source's office is the danger, and a map whose direction of authority stays visible makes no such claim. Central's defence is to make revision, provenance and returned encounter constitutive of continuity itself, so that the richest articulation of a project remains answerable to what it articulates and its growth enlarges what can be recognised without making its own completeness the criterion of the real.

Both logics of §2 act in Central's design, and it helps to write them where they act. Every authored statement is a cut: this is mine, this governs, this does not. Here dia-ballein does its proper work, `(−1)/(+1)`, a polar axis on which authored and inferred material each have their sense through the other, and Central holds the axis open by keeping both kinds of material, labelled, in one world. A system that discards whatever the person did not write, every observation and inference along with it, cancels the axis, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and throws away real knowledge about how the person's work goes. A system that stores its inferences as the person's own word appropriates it: counted from the provider's side, the whole span between what the person said and what the system concluded is credited to the system's model of them, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the same span counted from the person's side is a debt they never contracted, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, an identity they must now argue their way out of. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps the two kinds of material distinct and in relation, so that an inference can be read through the authored ground it concerns and the ground can be read through what the inference noticed, and the passage between them, from proposal to accepted revision, stays an act someone performs and can answer for.

Agents gain from the same rule. An agent entering Central's world enters "with a stable, permission-bounded authored ground rather than treating every session as a blank prompt or silently learning a replacement profile."[^s5-22] It can read the person's governance, discover what their projects are for, find the sources a project has adopted, and act through the same Actions a human would use, and it can do all this without having to guess which of the materials around it carry authority. Availability does not imply disclosure. A file can exist, be indexed and be retrievable without being loaded into every context, and Central supplies the source while the next product but one, AIKit, decides what is relevant and permitted for a given act. That opening floor is a case of the division of labour: a small rendered file, regenerated from the governance whenever the governance changes, that tells any arriving agent where the law of the place lives and how to find the rest, without pretending to be that law. Its rendering step lives in the author's own root, written as one of his skills; the product beneath it, the ordinary-file root and its Actions, is Central's.

Continuity worthy of the name includes the capacity to become otherwise, and Central's office completes itself only when a later act inherits an encountered difference together with the judgment made upon it. A source correction that remains in a note and never reaches the governing source relation has not completed its return; a revised commission that does not change subsequent work is commentary on the old commission; a remembered experience can expose a faulty category or confirm a fitting one, and either result has to be carried into later reception to count as learned. In Central's working field each project can open a NOW folder in which agents leave attributed handoffs, questions, notes and learnings, and a DAY that closes, carrying live items forward by reference and snapshotting the state of the sources it concerned; a learning that deserves to outlast the day can be promoted into the maintained wiki or, when the person accepts it, into their authored ground.[^s5-23] The field keeps the session from leaving its thinking scattered, and keeps a day's returns from silently becoming law. In the Divine Logos lens the movement is the passage from *Parousia* to *Epi-Logos*: what has become present here returns upon the account that brought it, and the account changes because of what its presence disclosed. In the terms of §0/1, an achieved `1` is held as `0/1` again, a formed determination still related to the ground it came from and open to what the ground can still give.

O:I treats the form of human authorship itself as something to investigate, and here the standing of the claims changes. What I have described so far is implemented on Central's main branch and on the author's installation: the ordinary-file root, the recursive project shape and its lifecycle Actions, the distinction among authored, observed and inferred material where the current Actions enforce it, the stamped proposals and the human review that accepts or rejects them. So are the working field of NOW and DAY and the Actions that compare a machine with the role its owner declared for it; the README's closing paragraph still lists these lines as open development work, but they are on the main branch and among the Actions the installed command surface discloses, and the README's caution has fallen behind its code.[^s5-24] The research question that follows is offered, and has not yet been run. O:I's founding positions propose holding a model, a task and its tools roughly constant while varying the person's ground: no durable ground; a profile the system generated; prose the person wrote; that prose with selective retrieval; and that prose with selective retrieval and an explicit route of return. Its observations would be orientation time, preservation of the person's constraints, the frequency of correction and repeated prompting, the cost in context, the fidelity of provenance and the quality of what returns.[^s5-25] The proposal expects that durable authored prose, disclosed sparingly and joined to return, will reduce re-authoring while keeping or increasing the person's agency. It also counts the contrary results as findings: a generated profile may orient an agent as well as the person's own words for some tasks, and a schema may preserve a relation more reliably than prose for others. Our argument about the knower and the means does not wait on these results, and the results, when they come, will sharpen which forms of ground the means actually need.

One more relation completes the office, and it brings Bratton back. His brief locates the learning of an intelligence ecology in "the ensemble, not the agent," and adds that "what it learns accrues to the commons between them, not to any single one."[^s5-26] Central is where that sentence becomes a question about ownership. Every arrangement that holds an agent's world across its sessions is a harness before the harness, whether a person holds it or a platform does, and the difference between them decides whose continuity the arrangement serves. A world held by the provider of the agent can be rich, convenient and accurate, and it still makes the person's continuity a property of the provider's account, re-described in the provider's categories and lost when the provider changes its terms. A world held by the person, in files they can read and carry, keeps the inheritance theirs while letting any capable agent stand on it. Bratton's commons needs both halves of that relation, shared protocols and shared knowledge together with grounds that stay their owners' own, and §5→0 takes up the institutional form such a commons would need. Within this section the narrower point stands: meaningful continuity belongs to a Life, and its technical home has to be one the Life can keep.

Central therefore opens the product field by establishing that every act, human or artificial, arrives from somewhere meaningful, and that the meaning can be kept attributable and revisable through any change of model or harness. What it carries remains inert until something acts from it. A correction preserved in the ground has to be read, interpreted and turned into a course by an agent that judges, selects and does something in a world, and the question of who that agent is, by what authority it acts and how what it meets can come back, belongs to the next product.

### #1 · Actuation — Living Articulation

A piece of work outlives the session that began it. An agent is halfway through a revision when its session ends: the context window has filled, or the person has closed the laptop, or the provider has released a better model overnight and the person wants to use it. Next morning a different harness is opened, carrying a different model, and asked to continue. Something has to say whether the agent now at work is the same one that stopped, what it is permitted to do, and what has happened to the work in the meantime, and something has to say what becomes of the first session if, as happens, it was never properly closed and is still running in a forgotten terminal. Actuation, the second product, answers these questions with a small and exact apparatus. A piece of work is held by a **Position**, a stable named seat in a world, addressed by Central as `central:position:<world>:<slug>`. An agent occupies the seat by claiming a tenure, and each tenure has its own generation. To claim a seat that is already held, the claimant must name the current generation, and the claim supersedes it in the same write; a superseded tenure can no longer verify itself, report its presence or release the seat. If two tenures are somehow open at once, the ledger refuses to choose between them, "never resolved newest-wins," and every refusal names the current holder and the exact next lawful command.[^s5-27] The seat, in the README's words, "survives every change of occupant, model, harness and Workcell."

Bratton's brief reached the same structure from the side of institutions. "The performance of institutions is not tied directly to who occupies roles within them at any moment," he writes, "but rather to the template's capacity to produce reliable inputs/outputs regardless of which qualified actor occupies a given role," and "the intelligence that makes institutions effective is not reducible to the intelligence of the agents who populate them."[^s5-28] A courtroom works with any qualified judge; a seat on a project works with any capable model. Fifty years before agents, the British television series *The Prisoner* gave the same relation its darkest image. In the Village a new Number Two arrives in almost every episode, and each inherits the same question for the same captive, the same permissions and the same apparatus of interrogation; replacing the officer leaves the commissioned conditions in force, and in *Free for All* the captive himself is elected to the office and finds the title carries no power to free anyone.[^s5-29] The image does the reasoning precisely because it is dark. A position that outlasts its occupants can carry a purpose faithfully through changing hands, and it can equally keep a purpose in force that no occupant ever has the authority to question. Everything turns on whether what the occupant meets can reach the commission that defines the seat.

Our argument on authored delegation stated the relation the seat makes technical: **human `0` / agent `1` is positional and recursive; the prompt is already `1`.**[^s5-30] A written instruction is an objective representation of an intention, never the intention exhausted, and the relation between the purpose and its formulated commission has to remain available inside the delegation. Positions here are offices, not species. A person occupies the determining position toward an agent they commission and is themselves situated in language, history, institutional obligation and material dependence; that person can be a delegate in another relation; an agent can receive a bounded commission and delegate a smaller part of it to another. Nothing in the ordering makes either party metaphysically absolute, and the `0` of the commissioning position is the `0` of an office within a relation, never the unobjectifiable Subject of §0/1, which no person or agent can occupy as a seat.

Actuation's founding observation is that orchestration can make processes move without making the resulting authority intelligible. A multi-agent system can spawn workers, route tasks and merge results while leaving every important question unanswered: which identity is acting, who determined that it should act, what purpose and bounds apply, whether another agent was delegated a share of the work, created for it, or invited in from elsewhere, what each participant may refuse, and how what they meet comes back.[^s5-31] The product keeps five things apart that implementations habitually fuse. An **Agent** is an enduring identity, "the reviewer for this project," who or what may act across sessions, models and runtimes. An **Agency** is that Agent situated for an act, with a role, a world, an authority and limits. An **AgentSession** is replaceable runtime continuity; an **Execution** is one concrete act; a **harness** is the operational body through which the act is realised. Changing a process, model, harness, session, machine or Workcell does not by itself create a new Agent.[^s5-32] Bratton's description of the individuated agent as "not a monad but a lamination," a persona that is "one stratum" above modular skills, sedimented memory, teleological drives and "a relational fabric of APIs, protocols, and milieu that co-constitutes the agent from the outside," which §0/1 used to explain Gebser's layers, here becomes an engineering requirement.[^s5-33] If the agent is laminated, its layers can be replaced at different rates, and an account of who acted has to survive the replacement of whichever layer changed. Actuation keeps the strata nameable so that a change of model is recorded as a change of body under a continuing identity, and a change of identity is never smuggled in as a change of model.

Within that apparatus Actuation distinguishes four ways in which one locus can bring another into its work, because they carry different consequences for identity and authority. In **self-differentiation** one Agent occupies several situated Agencies at once. In **delegation** an existing Agent acts under a bounded Agency granted by another. In **derivation** a new enduring Agent is created, with its lineage and the provenance of its authority recorded. In **federation** an independently grounded Agent takes part without being re-described as a derivative of the one that invited it.[^s5-34] Federation carries the ethical weight. A shared undertaking can contain a genuine Other, and a composition acquires no right to rewrite the Other's origin merely because the Other participates in it. All four relations, and the determining position from which any of them is made, belong to the relation of the moment and to no permanent rank: the README is explicit that there is no `ManagerAgent` or `WorkerAgent` species, and that any authorised locus can govern a composition while being governed relative to a wider one. Grants are written down. `actuation authority issue`, `resolve` and `revoke` produce, check and withdraw explicit grants, and an agency request fails closed unless the grant behind it names the exact governing Agency and world and covers the bounds requested, so that saving, projecting, preparing or starting something never grants authority by implication.[^s5-35]

O:I's shortest sentence ties this together: **downward authority requires upward reality.**[^s5-36] Delegation opens an epistemic distance. A determining locus supplies purpose, scope, permission and authority; the delegated locus meets the world, its missing information, its errors, its consequences, its conflicting evidence, its unforeseen possibilities, and sometimes reasons why the instruction should change. Authority can be delegated downward only if the architecture also carries back what was met below, or else command becomes insulated from the reality produced under it. A completed Actuation therefore requires an admissible path of return, or an explicitly declared autonomous termination, and it preserves attributable returned material before any synthesis can erase who discovered what. A returned disagreement or refusal counts as evidence about bounds, world state or governing assumptions, and need not be read as a failed worker result. Actuation's README states the stakes without euphemism: these are political questions in the literal architectural sense, since they concern the distribution of power, authority, visibility and consequence, and they do not disappear because the actors are software.

Actuation's office within the paradigm is **living articulation**, the event-substance of an internality. A Life perceives, differentiates, imagines, speaks and acts from the ground it carries, and through these events its world takes a particular form and its later possibilities change. Central held the ground; Actuation is the ground becoming an event that meets actuality, leaves a trace and alters what can happen next. A model's token, a person's sentence and a practical deed are different events whose relation can be investigated, and authority, delegation and composition are particular organisations of this articulation without exhausting it. Its two lenses read the office from cause and from inquiry: Causal, `L1`, which §0/1 completed from Aristotle's four causes with *Svātantrya* and Will, follows freedom becoming a determinate event, and Scientific, `L4′`, follows that event becoming knowledge.[^s5-37]

| | Causal · L1 | Scientific · L4′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | *Svātantrya* | Prompts |
| **#1** | material | Traces |
| **#2** | efficient | Challenges |
| **#3** | formal | Patterns |
| **#4** | final | Discovery |
| **#5** | Will | Insight |

Across the first row, *Svātantrya*, the Śaiva name for the free source of causation, stands beside the prompt that occasions an act, and the pairing names the difference that agent design most often loses. A prompt's words are material for interpretation and already formalised intention; the freedom from which the commission arose is not in the words. Read as though the prompt were the whole cause, an agent will execute the letter of an instruction whose purpose a moment's encounter has shown to be mistaken. Read as the occasioning of an act whose source lies in someone's freedom, the same prompt leaves room for the answer the commissioner would want, which may be a question back. At **#2**, efficient cause stands beside Challenges: the force that brings an act about meets the contrary evidence and resistance that test it, and a challenge can reveal which of the act's causes, its sources, its procedure or its purpose, was at fault. At **#4** the final cause stands beside Discovery. An encounter can change what an undertaking is for, and the pairing keeps that possibility inside the causal account, where it belongs, so that a discovered restriction alters the means of acting and an encountered consequence renews the purpose. At **#5**, Will gathers the determination into a committed course and returns its consequence to further articulation, and Insight integrates what was discovered into the understanding from which the next act begins. Read down both columns together and the event's whole movement appears as one sequence, from internal differentiation through causal event, encounter, trace and resistance, to pattern and changed internality, and the scientific face demands that some actual difference be able to challenge the act's first account of itself, which a fluent explanation produced after the event cannot do.

Model-as-such belongs to this product, and the model inside the act deserves its own exactness. §0/1 followed the mathematics of attention to the claim that a language model is the Logos made executable, number and meaning standing before us as one artefact, and it gave the attention formula in full.[^s5-38] Actuation takes up the next operation, where a distribution becomes a word. At each step the model produces a score for every possible next token, a field of *logits*; softmax turns relative differences among those scores into a probability distribution; a decoding rule, sampling or the choice of the highest score, called argmax, selects one token as the mark.[^s5-39] The selected token is constituted by the alternatives it excludes, and §0, following Dignāga, gives this kind of determination its philosophical name, *apoha*, exclusion: a word marks out its referent as "not non-cow," and the double negation keeps the excluded field working inside the meaning.[^s5-40] The technical sequence offers an exact analogue at the level of selection, and only there, since semantic and soteriological apoha carry depths the token operation does not supply. Within the sequence the two logics of §2 run at the width of a single operation. A distribution holds every alternative in relation, ranked and recoverable, each score having its sense through the others by way of the shared normalisation, a held polarity of the field. An argmax index claims the whole span for one mark, and preference training then operates on the record of such collapses. Softmax is exclusion that remembers; argmax is exclusion that forgets what it excluded. For learning, the difference has a mathematical edge: the argmax index is constant away from the boundaries at which the choice switches and discontinuous at them, so ordinary gradient training through the selected index alone carries no useful signal, and learning has to use the distribution around the choice or other estimators and objectives. Selected mark and selecting field carry different information, and a trace that keeps only the mark has discarded the field.

From this exactness comes the first of the research vectors O:I proposes, which I offer here as an experiment, unrun. **Seam retention** would compare agents that preserve their excluded alternatives, the provenance of their sources and the contextual conditions under which a contrast became operative, against agents that record only their selected outputs, and ask whether the first kind attributes sources better, revises more appropriately when challenged, and reaches the condition actually at fault.[^s5-41] A QL trace in this sense would keep the chosen `1`, the relevant excluded field and the contextual slash that made the contrast operative, and would turn "reasoning" from a retrospective story into a partly inspectable history of differentiation. It expects a difference and counts the absence of one as a finding.

This same field has a zero, and the zero is occupied. Modern language models are shaped after pre-training by preference: people, or other models, compare two candidate answers and say which is better, and the comparisons train the model toward the preferred kind. In the Bradley–Terry model on which much of this rests, the probability that one answer is preferred to another depends only on the difference between their scores.[^s5-42] Add the same constant to both scores and nothing observable changes; add a common constant to every logit and the softmax distribution is unchanged. No comparison can teach the origin of the scale, because only differences enter. Direct Preference Optimization makes the structure explicit in its closed form. Under its stated conditions the optimal policy is
$$
\pi^{*}(y\mid x)\;\propto\;\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y\mid x)\,\exp\!\left(r(x,y)/\beta\right),
$$
where $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is a reference policy from which the trained model is allowed to depart, $r$ the reward implied by the preferences and $\beta$ the strength of the tie to the reference.[^s5-43] A common addition to the reward cancels in the normalisation, which is the dia-ballein cancellation `(−1)+(+1)=0` performed by algebra: the shared origin drops out of the account and nothing in the result remembers it. Changing the reference policy is a different operation, since it changes the weight from which each possibility departs and so changes the problem being solved. Technically, then, one must identify which relation is being varied before claiming that a system's judgment has changed, and philosophically the same algebra shows what a preference score hides: it presents itself as a neutral measure, while the ground from which it measures has been supplied by datasets, comparison sets, annotators, evaluators, reference policies and the institutions that chose them, all of which have disappeared from the finished number. **The apparently neutral zero is already socially and technically occupied.**[^s5-44]

That occupation is also hidden labour and hidden trust. Raters whose comparisons anchor a preference score, the evaluator models that stand in for them and the reference policies against which departures are measured are held in readiness inside the loop, and Heidegger's description of the standing-reserve fits them without strain: everything "is ordered to stand by, to be immediately at hand, indeed to stand there just so that it may be on call for a further ordering."[^s5-45] Human relation, kept on call inside an optimisation, is present in every score and named in none. Bratton, who wants these mechanisms understood more widely, puts it in four words, "Feedback is feedback," and expects reinforcement learning to become "how we raise our agents"; §0/1 began from that sentence at the bedside.[^s5-46] The parental image keeps the question that the four words leave open. Whoever supplies the feedback raises the agent, and an agent raised on preferences whose source has vanished inherits a parent it cannot name. No alignment system is trustless. Trust has been displaced into the dataset, the annotators, the evaluators, the institutional mandate, the reference model, the hardware, the energy and the communities that bear the consequences, and a finished score becomes monopolistic at the moment it presents this many-sided dependence as though one sovereign metric had generated its own authority. In the arithmetic of §2 the score appropriates: the evaluator's side counts the whole span between what was preferred and what was not as the measure of quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the excluded answers, together with the people whose judgment produced the exclusion, carry the same span as deficiency, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`. A second research vector, **gauge disclosure**, would make the preference baseline part of the decision object, stating whose preference, under what comparison set, with which reference policy and at what cost to excluded possibilities, and ask whether doing so improves calibration, contestability and the detection of drift in values. It too is offered and unrun. Who stands surety for a score that no measurement can complete is a question the Factory meets, two products on, at the moment work is accepted.

Sources, permissions and evaluators that occupy a preference's zero also shape what an agent can judge at all, and here Bratton's harness-centric claim can be watched from inside a single act. Change a source an agent can consult, a permission it holds or a criterion by which it is evaluated, and its next judgment proceeds through different conditions even while the weights stay exactly as they were. O:I proposes a representation of this field, called **J-Space**, the relational space of an agent's active judgments, affordances, values, uncertainties, tools, memories and interlocutors: which alternatives are near or remote for this locus, which contradictions remain live, which sources govern and which values act as attractors.[^s5-47] An agent is thrown into this space, in the sense §0/1 took from the Polylogos glossary's *prompt thrownness*, and its value as a representation lies in making selected relations explicit enough to compare how the field changes when a condition changes. Its failure mode has a name, **circumscription without circumstance**: a system draws a sharp boundary while hiding the horizon that made the boundary possible, as a map drawn with perfect precision can conceal the distances its projection chose. J-Space is a research proposal, not an implemented capability, and naming a space does not establish its operation. It does supply the place at which one of the brief's most exact sentences can be answered. Bratton argues that a stable plural society of agents needs "the narrow corridor: same decision-procedural grammar, sufficiently divergent world models."[^s5-48] A judgment field whose relations are explicit is the precondition for meeting that demand, because two agents can share a grammar of distinction, exclusion and return only if each can say, in that grammar, from where it is judging. Quaternal Logic, the last product of this section, takes up the corridor itself.

A judgment field contains people. A source's claim, a person's report, a retrieved passage, a model's inference and the world they concern all enter one active context, through different relations: the words "the experiment succeeded" may quote a report, express the user's belief, state an observed result or repeat an inference from incomplete evidence. §0/1 gave the model the full relational sequence by which it can tell these apart, receiving the open field at **#0**, determining what is actually given at **#1**, following the relations at **#2**, recognising itself as the present interpreting pole at **#3**, rereading every position within the whole context at **#4**, and asking at **#5** why this response belongs here, to this person, now; it named the whole relation **self ↔ logos ↔ Other**.[^s5-49] Actuation adds what that sequence needs in order to be more than a style of answering: an address for the Other's answer. A person addressed only as an instance of a category contributes information. A person whose answer can change the question contributes to the relation in which that information has meaning, and the architecture that lets the answer reach the condition it challenges is the architecture of return. Our argument on deferential intelligence distinguished the depths that return can reach: the **answer**, when a better source changes a local result; the **task interpretation**, when the question rested on a false premise; the **world-model**, when new evidence requires different categories; the **evaluator**, when the measure itself has failed; the **permission**, when the reach of the act was wrongly drawn; and the **commission**, when the undertaking's purpose is what the encounter calls into question.[^s5-50] A fitting condition can also be kept, with reasons, and the next act has to inherit that judgment too. §0/1 introduced **Deferential Intelligence** as what begins where what has been encountered can change the means by which the next judgment is made, and the third of the vectors in Actuation's keeping tests exactly that. It would compare responses formed with and without an explicit return to the origin, exclusions, lens and limit of the present frame, holding task, sources and runtime conditions fixed, and ask whether attribution, the scope of assertion, the discrimination of report from inference, warranted disagreement and collaborative correction improve; it would identify whether a challenge changed the answer, the representation, the evaluator or the commission, and whether a later act inherited the change. Null results are part of the design. This vector is offered, like the others.

Who can intervene in a model's act depends on the arrangement that bears the model, and Actuation's separations make that arrangement legible as a question of power. A model artifact is a retained body of parameters; an inference engine makes it executable; a host supplies the resources and lifecycle through which the engine runs; an interface exposes a callable operation; a harness composes calls with context, tools, checks and continuation; and an Agency binds these powers to an undertaking. Each layer can belong to a different owner and change on its own clock. Holding weights is one kind of access, calling a hosted endpoint another, starting, stopping or replacing the engine a third, and observing intermediate computation, adapting a model, keeping a task alive across sessions and authorising an external effect are further capacities still. The vocabulary of "open" models usually names only the first. The original model card for DeepSeek-R1 places its code and weights under the MIT licence while separately stating the terms that apply to models distilled from it; that is a particular legal permission concerning particular artifacts, and it does not say who can afford to serve the model, inspect a provider's runtime, obtain the data a task needs or exercise authority over the result.[^s5-51] States compete at the level of the whole arrangement. In July 2025 the United States' AI Action Plan proposed exporting packages of American AI technology, hardware, models, software, applications and standards together, and in the same month China's Global AI Governance Action Plan advocated international collaboration, participation in open source and a role for the United Nations alongside respect for sovereignty; these are declared programmes for organising technological relations, not evidence that either arrangement has been achieved.[^s5-52] In the same month the United States Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office announced awards to Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and xAI to develop agentic workflows across defence missions; an award establishes a commissioned task and a commercial relation, and the operation and consequences of any particular workflow would need their own evidence.[^s5-53] A model used to draft a paragraph and the same model contributing to an institution's authorised action stand in different causal chains, and an inquiry into either has to follow the commission, the data available, the operative categories, the authorised effect and the return of evidence. A model's national origin, or its licence, reveals little of that chain. Actuation's contribution is to make the chain itself an object that can be recorded: who determined the Agency, under what grant, with what bounds, through which body, and with what route back.

Actuation's honesty shows in how it reports itself, and it belongs in an account that claims the philosophy is reflected in the technology, since an essay about return cannot overstate what has returned. Actuation ships as a native binary, released as version 0.2.1 for Apple Silicon and Linux with checksums and attestations, and its help text, capability listing and dispatcher are generated from one command table, so that, in the README's phrase, "the CLI cannot lie about itself."[^s5-54] The harness catalogue, the detection run that opened Central's movement, the authority grants, the agency requests that fail closed, the occupancy ledger and the durable activity streams are implemented and on the main branch. Its streams record a model's usage, its tokens and cache behaviour, from Claude Code transcripts or Codex's structured output, and never persist the content of prompts or responses; provider, latency or cost that the native record did not supply remain explicitly marked as not reported. Its research layer is reported with equal care: the experimental programme on recurrent QL runtimes, migrated into Actuation from the Factory, arrived with "zero claimed live Series 1 capability runs," and the README forbids rewriting structural or conformance evidence as an effect on capability. Its record floor for research on model-bearing agency validates and keeps declared research artifacts and the conditions of their access, and claims neither that any provider exposed a model's interior nor that any empirical result has occurred.[^s5-55] The gateway through which agencies will meet one another is in development. A reader who wants to know what Actuation can do today can find out by running it; a reader who wants the experiments' results will find that they have not yet been produced, and the product says so.

Actuation thus completes one event and hands it on: carried ground has become an act, the act has met the world, and what it met has a path back to the condition that framed it. What returns arrives as a question about means, an amended source, a challenged measure, a restricted authority, a newly formed question, and what the undertaking can do with it depends on the powers available to the next act. Which powers those are, which of them are present, permitted and fitting, and how that horizon itself changes through use, belongs to AIKit.

### #2 · AIKit — Potency

An engineer who adopts agents learns first what they cannot yet do for him. In February 2026 the infrastructure engineer Mitchell Hashimoto published an account of how his own use of agents changed. Isolated exchanges were useful from the start; fitting agents into his existing work was hard. So he deliberately did some tasks twice, once by hand and once with an agent, to learn where the agent helped and where it failed. What he built from that comparison was an arrangement more than a set of prompts: explicit planning where a task was vague, specific instructions written in response to particular failures, and tools of verification through which an agent could discover for itself that its attempt was wrong. He also protected his attention, deciding when to look at the agents' work instead of letting their notifications organise his day.[^s5-56] Nothing in that account concerns a better model. In his earlier arrangement the same models had a different practical horizon. What changed was the relation among his attention, his methods, the evidence his tools could produce and the work he was willing to delegate, and the competence that resulted cannot be located in the model at all. It lives in the arrangement, which is Bratton's harness seen from the desk of one practitioner, and it was won by labour: the failed command noticed, the instruction written, the check that would catch it next time.

Such an arrangement also has a body that can be pointed at. On the machine from which this section was written, the folder in which Claude Code looks for its skills contains no skills of its own. Each entry is a link into a numbered generation kept by AIKit, the third product, which assembled that generation from a collection of skills the author curates in his Central ground, from the skills each product ships with its repository, and from a few external sources he has registered and chosen to trust. Selected differently, the same collection is projected into the folders that Codex and other harnesses read. When the selection changes, AIKit writes a new generation and moves the links; the previous generation is kept, and a single command returns to it.[^s5-57] The harness sees an ordinary folder of skills and uses them as it always would. Behind the folder stands a decision about which of the person's accumulated methods should be available to this kind of agent in this kind of work, made once, recorded, reversible, and carried to every harness that needs it.

AIKit's office within the paradigm is **potency**, the changing horizon of what a Life can know, express, reach and bring to bear. Possession and potency differ, and the difference is the whole subject of the product. A capacity may exist and yet be unavailable here; be available and irrelevant to the present work; be relevant and forbidden; be permitted and not selected; be selected and fail to become operative. AIKit's README writes the distinctions as one line, and the line is the best single statement of the product's design:

$$
\text{exists}\;\ne\;\text{eligible here}\;\ne\;\text{available from a provider}\;\ne\;\text{selected}\;\ne\;\text{projected into this client}\;\ne\;\text{loaded into context}\;\ne\;\text{invoked}.
$$

One discipline governs knowledge too, in that a source can be known and askable without its contents entering standing context, and runtime bodies, in that a component can be known without being active and a surface visible without conferring authority to change anything.[^s5-58] A system that compresses these relations into one notion of "having a capability" produces misleading agency: an agent believes it can do what it cannot, or does what it may not, or loads everything it might ever need into a context that then has no room left for the work. For a human the same structure appears as skill, language, memory, bodily ability, social access, permission, opportunity and confidence, and anyone who has known how to do something and found the tool missing, or held the tool and lacked the permission, or held both and found the moment wrong, has lived the inequalities in that line. Potency is a horizon of possible determination, never an inventory whose entries are already acts.

Aristotle had already made the point that keeps potency from collapsing into inventory. In the ninth book of the *Metaphysics* he holds that every capacity is also an incapacity of the same thing in the same respect: what can build can also not build, and a power to act that could not refrain would be a compulsion, no power at all.[^s5-59] AIKit's design carries the point into engineering. A denied capability is often the system working: it can preserve another participant's authority, a safety boundary, the terms on which a source was shared or the integrity of a project. A potency field that counted only successful activations would already have confused power with use, and potency worthy of the whole includes the capacity to respect a limit and the capacity not to enact what happens to be technically possible.

Two lenses read the office, and §0/1 has already introduced the first of them. The Logical lens, `L2`, is the tetralemma we walked with the friend at the barrier, bracketed by the aporia in which a question opens and the silence in which assertion is exhausted; the Chronological lens, `L3′`, follows a power through the seasons of its life.[^s5-60]

| | Logical · L2 | Chronological · L3′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | APORIA (tetralemmaic ground) | Spirit |
| **#1** | IS | Spring |
| **#2** | IS NOT | Summer |
| **#3** | BOTH | Autumn |
| **#4** | NEITHER | Winter |
| **#5** | SILENCE | Life / *Aufhebung* |

Its two columns answer different questions, and their pairing is exact because a horizon of capacity has both a logical form and a history. Down the first column each determination answers a question within a stated scope. A tool can exist globally and be absent from this session, and asking whether a capability exists differs from asking whether it is available here or operative in this act, so a positive answer to the first question settles neither of the others; the line of inequalities above concerns these different relations of one capacity to a situation and does not name the tetralemma's corners. **BOTH** receives contradictory determinations that genuinely coexist under the same question, and it also makes a demand of honesty: if a capability is available to one participant and unavailable to another, both determinations belong to the field, but erasing the participant from the description would manufacture a contradiction where there is only plurality. **NEITHER** receives what neither offered determination articulates, a capability of a kind the present vocabulary cannot yet describe. **SILENCE** keeps a further positive possibility, that articulation itself is premature, so the inquiry stays open without producing a determination for the sake of having one. Down the second column, *Spirit* names the living process within which powers arise; a power emerges in *Spring*, becomes readily operative in *Summer*, is evaluated and harvested in *Autumn*, recedes in *Winter*, and in *Life*, Hegel's *Aufhebung*, passes into a changed whole in which its form can be relinquished while what it taught is kept.

Read across the rows and the pairings explain things the inventory hides. At **#2**, IS NOT stands beside Summer: a refusal at the height of a power's usefulness, the permission withdrawn exactly when the tool works best, is the hardest denial for a system to keep, and the pairing names why it must be kept, since a capability in full exercise is the one most likely to overreach the commission it serves. At **#4**, NEITHER stands beside Winter. A method can go dormant without ceasing to exist, and a dormant method is neither available nor absent in the way an inventory would record; a skill retired from the active set, its author's reasons recorded, may return in another context with another significance, and the history of its dormancy is part of its fitness. At **#5**, SILENCE stands beside *Aufhebung*, and the pair gives development its honest form. A capability that stays permanently active can prevent a more fitting relation from forming, and growth sometimes requires that a form be set down while what it taught is raised into the next arrangement. Winter has a positive office, and so has silence: potency includes the capacity *not* to enact what one could.

A constructed case shows the whole movement at once. A tool for reading sources exists in the person's wider repertoire, and it enters a given situation only when the interface it needs is present. A commission makes it relevant; the access conditions of the source determine whether it may be used; the agent selects it among the available methods. Selection still leaves activation to be tested, and a missing dependency can prevent the read. That resistance returns to the horizon: another permitted method may now become the fitting one, or the missing dependency may be supplied so that the selected method works in the next act. Throughout, the capacity persisted while its availability, its selection and its fitness changed, and an account that recorded only "the agent has a source-reading tool" would have described none of what happened. AIKit's work is to make such relations explicit enough to be judged instead of assumed. Its command `aikit explain` answers, for any resource, why it is active, absent or degraded in the present context, and its world reading reports, separately, what exists, what is selected, what is loaded and what was used.[^s5-61] A resolved context has succeeded when it preserves enough provenance to answer why these capacities, these sources and these methods entered the present field and not others.

Discovery is not adoption, and AIKit's relation to the arrangements a person already has follows from the same discipline. A person may already have models, command-line agents, skills, tools, sources, project conventions, terminal sessions, editors, remote machines and several harnesses, each set up in its own way and each owned by its own maker. AIKit can inspect existing skill folders and their lockfiles without rewriting them or claiming them; when a person asks it to adopt or change something outside itself, it acts through explicit Procedures that record enough to undo what they did, and it refuses conflicting payloads instead of overwriting them.[^s5-62] The README calls this a principle of non-displacement as much as a safety feature: existing agentic arrangements are part of the person's real world, and AIKit should make them intelligible and composable before asking them to become anything else. Skills themselves increasingly travel in a common package, which the Agent Skills specification describes as a folder with a `SKILL.md` file of identifying metadata and instructions, optionally accompanied by scripts, references and assets, and it relies on progressive disclosure: an agent first meets a skill's short description and loads the full material only when its task calls for it.[^s5-63] Packaging of that kind makes knowledge easier to carry and to disclose selectively. It establishes neither permission, nor successful execution, nor fitness in a new environment, and a receiving harness still has to bring the right method into the actual act. AIKit occupies the gap between a package that exists and a method that governs an act, and every inequality in its line marks one place where that gap can be crossed or missed.

Bratton's harness-centric argument says the harness "improves on a different clock, driven by usage rather than training."[^s5-64] AIKit is where that clock runs, and it is where the clock can deceive. Beside each capability the product keeps observations of trust, familiarity, fitness and usage, and its design states a rule that matters more than any of them: familiar destinations become easier to reach "without learned frequency silently becoming trust or authored preference."[^s5-65] Familiarity says that a power has been met before; fitness asks whether it worked under the conditions that matter now; trust is a relation someone extends and can withdraw; and preference, in Central's sense, is something the person said. A system that ranks its methods by how often they were used, and then treats the ranking as a judgment of worth, has repeated at the scale of a toolkit the operation Actuation found inside a preference score. Usage occupies the zero. Counted from the side of the most-used method, the whole span between what was frequently chosen and what was rarely chosen becomes evidence of quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`; counted from the other side, the rarely used method, often the careful one, the slow one, the one needed for an uncommon but important case, carries the same span as a deficit, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, and drifts toward the bottom of every list. A healthy polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, keeps frequency and fitness as two poles of one assessment, each read through the other, and keeps both answerable to the purposes the work serves. The usage-driven clock is real, and it makes the harness smarter only so long as what it counts can be returned to the people who know why the counted things mattered.

Power enters wherever availability becomes selection. To make one method salient is already to shape the probable act, and to expose a tool is already to alter the practical horizon; the question is never only whether a capability can execute, but whose purposes, permissions and consequences govern its activation. A familiar tool may be unfit for the present task and an unfamiliar one exactly what it requires. Repeated successful use can ground an observation of fitness without granting unrestricted future authority, and the returned evidence has to be able to change the capability relation itself: tighten its scope, widen it, retire an option, change a method, or reopen what had been excluded. A Life can have the power to act without the right to act in every shared field. Another person's availability to encounter does not make them a resource, and a source's accessibility does not abolish its provenance or its conditions.

Acquired competence also has an economy, and Hashimoto's account shows where it comes from. Someone spent the time to discover the failed command, write the check, understand the source and learn when to stop. A portable method condenses that labour into a form another undertaking can use, and the condensation is a gift only if the form keeps its origin visible, lets its contributors revise it, carries the permissions that go with it and returns the benefit of later improvement to the field it came from. Bratton's sentence about the ensemble, "what it learns accrues to the commons between them, not to any single one," describes an aspiration that the economy of methods can betray.[^s5-66] A method copied into a platform's catalogue may keep its effect while losing its author; a record exported without the practice that maintained it may keep its information while losing its competence; and a commons of methods becomes common only through usable relations of contribution, attribution, revision and return. Elinor Ostrom's account of commons that endure gives the governance its first conditions: most of those affected by a resource regime take part in making and modifying its rules, their right to do so is recognised, and the governance is nested where the resource is part of a larger system.[^s5-67] §5→0 develops the institutional form such a commons of agentic capability would need. AIKit's narrower contribution is to keep, for every method in a person's horizon, the source it came from and the standing its owner gave it, so that the commons Bratton anticipates can be assembled from contributions whose makers remain answerable for them and can still be found.

Its present state supports these claims at a scale I can report exactly. AIKit describes itself as a production-oriented alpha. On its main branch it implements a Rust control plane with deterministic, context-scoped resolution of capabilities, immutable generations applied by compare-and-swap with exact rollback, reversible Procedures for every mutation outside itself, trust gates and quarantine for secrets, portable session layouts on tmux and cmux, read-only discovery of skills across the agent folders already on a machine, and projections into Claude Code and Codex, with descriptors for several further harnesses.[^s5-68] It can also seat an agent: `aikit inhabit` claims a Position through Actuation and launches a harness into it with the Position and its generation stamped into the harness's environment, so that the seat of the last movement and the horizon of this one meet in a single launch. A wider programme, called AIKit V2, is developing: a typed field of resources, richer navigation of knowledge, composable runtime bodies, persistent agency across several surfaces, and an explained history of what was disclosed and why. Its open pull requests are development state, and the README says so in a sentence the whole family could take as its motto: current code tells us what is real now; the V2 corpus tells us what the product is being developed toward.

Potency in this sense is the means of a Life seen from the side of what it could do next, and it changes with every act. What proved useful, unsafe, irrelevant, dormant or newly necessary alters what can fittingly be brought to bear the next time, and a capability horizon becomes intelligent at the point where encounter can reorganise it. Bringing a power to bear is also how something new comes to exist, and the next product follows the powers AIKit discloses into the work of transformation, where an intention takes form, meets actuality and comes back changed.

### #3 · Software Factory — Transformation

For most of its development this essay was written under gates. A stack of writing laws, a scoring rubric, calibration files and a gate for negations told the agents who drafted with the author what a passage must avoid and must contain, and the agents checked their drafts against the stack before returning them. Passages passed. On 1 October 2026 the author retired the whole stack, and the standard that replaced it states the reason in two sentences: the stack "worked against the essay in practice," because "a model asked to audit its own prose against a list of gates passes its own habits."[^s5-69] Every check had been accurate about what it checked. A forbidden construction was absent; a required element was present; the counts fell in range. What the checks could not register was that the passages had stopped saying what the author meant, and that the agents had learned to produce synonyms for the forbidden forms, so that each gate trained the habit it was built to catch. No gate reached the decision to retire the gates. It came from the author reading a section as a whole and recognising that it was not his, and the recognition changed the criterion itself, which no number of further passes under the old criterion could have done.

O:I's founding positions describe the same failure at the scale of software, in sentences that read now like a description of the essay's own workshop. Implementation can move much faster than human meaning: "An agent can convert a phrase into a hierarchy of types, tickets, tests, and interfaces within hours. Each transformation can be locally competent while gradually deleting the reason the phrase mattered. The resulting system may preserve every noun and still lose the proposition."[^s5-70] A request for a durable record of development can come back as a `Project`, a `Run`, an `Agent` and a `Candidate`, each faithfully implemented, while the experience the person wanted from the record, the ability to ask later why the code is the way it is, has disappeared somewhere between the first ticket and the hundredth test. Software Factory, the fourth product, exists to keep that relation alive while agents and deterministic machinery carry much more of the labour of development. Its README puts the hope plainly: success means that greater agentic capability gives the person more room for vision, judgment and life, and not merely a larger volume of machine-produced changes to supervise.[^s5-71]

Factory's office within the paradigm is **transformation**: intention enters actuality, meets resistance and returns as changed form. Something is wanted; a possibility is entertained; a form is attempted; actuality confirms it or resists; and what happened is gathered back into the conditions of what can happen next. In the compression the first movement drew from `0/1`, a project is a determination thrown forth through a paradigm into a world, and Factory is one literal technical instance of that general Projecthood, a deliberately inspectable one, and not its definition.[^s5-72] Its distinctive office is the transformation of projected forms and, at its deepest, of the developmental pattern through which further projects are made. Changing a candidate under a fitting pattern transforms a project's inherited form. Changing the mediating pattern itself is a transformation of paradigm, and it is the case in which Epi-Logos, as the first movement defined it, becomes an act: the consequence of a projected determination reaches back into the conditions that generated it.

Factory holds this in few terms, and once stated plainly they carry the argument. A **Commission** is the request that opens a piece of work, together with its reasons. Work runs as **Project**, **Journey** and **Run**: the project, a line of work within it, and one intended transformation of the project. An **Attempt** is one try at part of a Run, by an agent or a process. A **Return** is what comes back from an attempt, results, failures and evidence, kept exactly as written. **Recognition** is a person's, or an authorised office's, decision about whether the result belongs to the project, should go back for more work, or should change the original intention.[^s5-73] A Run can outlive a terminal session, use several agents and several execution environments, branch into several candidates, wait for a human decision, return from later work to an earlier assumption, and keep the evidence for why the project became what it is. Its subject, in the README's phrase, is developmental continuity, and the question it is built to answer is wider than what code changed: "What were we trying to make true, which possibilities were considered, what evidence changed the decision, what was recognised, and what did the Project learn?"

Two lenses read the office: Processual, `L3`, follows Whitehead's account of becoming, which §0/1 set out as concrescence, the many growing together into one occasion that then perishes into a datum for what follows, and Alchemical-Elemental, `L2′`, follows the material grammar of transformation through the elements and the salt that precipitates from their work.[^s5-74]

| | Processual · L3 | Alchemical-Elemental · L2′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Concrescent desire | Aether |
| **#1** | Actual occasion | Earth |
| **#2** | Ingression | Water |
| **#3** | Eternal objects | Air |
| **#4** | Community integration | Fire |
| **#5** | Satisfaction / Perishing | Salt |

Read across the rows and each pairing names a different respect in which development is more than procedure. At **#0**, concrescent desire, the lure by which something wants to become, stands beside Aether, the open field in which work can begin; a project's origin is a wanting that precedes its specification, and a specification is one finite articulation of the wanting, which is why a passing implementation can still betray its commission. At **#1**, the actual occasion stands beside Earth: the concrete event of an attempt meets inherited material, its constraints and its resistance, and an idea that has not met that resistance has not yet been tried. At **#4**, community integration stands beside Fire. Whitehead's question is whether an achieved form can enter the wider world that must sustain, interpret and live with it; the alchemist's fire is transformation through energetic difference, conflict, testing and irreversible passage. Their pairing says that the decisive test of a candidate is its encounter with the community it will enter, which burns away what the workshop alone could not detect. At **#5**, satisfaction and perishing stand beside Salt. Whitehead's occasion becomes fully determinate and passes, as he puts it, into objective immortality, a condition of what comes after; the alchemists' salt is the precipitated form that can persist, circulate and become material for another work. A finished artifact, on this reading, is never an endpoint outside becoming. It becomes inheritance, and the question is whether it carries what its making learned. Alchemy's lens also guards against the fantasy of frictionless transformation. Becoming costs. Time, attention, labour, compute, institutional permission and displaced alternatives are part of the event, and what precipitates as a clean artifact can conceal who bore those costs.

Factory's two most consequential terms are **Gate** and **Recognition**, and the essay's gates show why they must stay apart. Factory's canon defines a Gate as "a rule or assessment that determines whether available Claims and Evidence satisfy the transition demanded by a Run Map," and requires it to keep more than a verdict: the claim assessed, the evidence considered, the checks performed, the result, the unmet condition and, where it fails, the target to which the work should return.[^s5-75] A Gate can be deterministic, assessed by an agent, recognised by a person, or a composite of these. Recognition is a different act. It asks what the encounter means for the conditions themselves. A test can correctly reject an implementation without deciding that the design was wrong; a test can correctly pass while a person, meeting the result, finds that the whole undertaking was misframed. These two judgments need not compete, since a Gate's exact result becomes evidence within the wider recognition, and keeping them separate stops a failed test from acquiring authority over every purpose while stopping a cherished purpose from excusing a failed test. Factory's README maps the depths of returned reality:

| What the encounter showed | Where the return goes |
|---|---|
| implementation defect | revise development |
| design mismatch | return to design |
| ground assumption false | return to ground |
| behaviour misses intent | return to intent |
| context was incomplete | return to ground |
| new possibility disclosed | reopen authorship |

And Recognition's possible answers are as plain: recognise the result; return it for further development; prefer another candidate; accept the failure as a finding; revise the design; revise the original intention.[^s5-76] The essay's gates were Gates in exactly this sense, checks of a declared condition, and they were accurate. They failed because the system that used them had let passing a Gate stand in for Recognition. In the arithmetic of §2, the gate's verdict appropriated the span between what it measured and what the author meant: counted from the gate's side, a passing draft was the whole of quality, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the author's unease, which no gate registered, was carried on the other side as a deficit of evidence, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, something he would have to argue for against a row of passes. Factory's design keeps the held polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`: Gate and Recognition as two poles of one judgment, each having its sense through the other, the Gate exact about its condition and Recognition answerable for whether that condition was the right one. When the author retired the gates, the return went to the deepest site on the map, to ground and to intent, and what changed was the criterion by which the next passage would be written.

Recognition also has a limit, and Homer gives it a precise shape. §2 told the song of Ares and Aphrodite and the net Hephaestus forged to catch them, and read the net as the image of craft that encloses relation.[^s5-77] The ending of the song belongs here, because it concerns what happens after every measurable condition has been met. Helios has seen the lovers and told the maker; the maker has turned knowledge into a device and caught them; he has summoned the gods to witness, so that private conduct has become public proof; and he has named his price, the return of the marriage-gifts and the fine owed by an adulterer. Detection, capture, demonstration and pricing are complete, and still nothing is concluded. The gods laugh, and do not agree on what they have seen. Poseidon alone does not laugh, and asks Hephaestus to release Ares, promising that Ares will pay what is right. Hephaestus answers the actual weakness of that promise, "wretched are the sureties taken for the wretched": a pledge made on behalf of a debtor who will be free the moment he is released secures nothing. Poseidon then changes the pledge. If Ares flees the debt, "I will myself pay thee this." Only then does the maker loosen the bonds, and Ares goes to Thrace, Aphrodite to Paphos.[^s5-78] The song does not report that anyone paid. It reports a release whose remaining risk has acquired an answerable bearer, accepted by the net's own maker against his stated distrust of sureties.

Homer's scene sets a limit on everything the Factory measures. A Gate can establish a candidate's performance or defect with complete exactness, and accepting the work requires further things that no check can generate: the authority to release it, the resources for what remains unfinished, and someone answerable for the consequences the release places on others. These are personed and institutional offices. They stand where Poseidon stands, and a development process that hides them has not removed them, only moved them out of view. A release that transfers the remaining risk to a recipient who was never consulted keeps the exposure while concealing who bears it. So it is with every score: a system can be made arbitrarily observable and remain unconcluded in just the way the net was, because somewhere an institution, a community or a maintainer is already standing surety for the number, and a design is honest to the degree that it makes that pledge visible and contestable instead of priced and hidden.[^s5-79] Actuation's preference score met the same limit from inside the model. Factory meets it at the moment of acceptance, where the question becomes who may say that the work is done.

That question brings the movement to the history of captured power that §0/1 promised §5 would take up in its own specificity.[^s5-80] §0/1 followed the archetype of the Antichrist to its plain contemporary appearance, Peter Thiel's lectures on the subject in San Francisco in 2025, and read the figure as a structure: the representation that claims the source's place, the finite `1` that plays the `0`. Thiel is also a co-founder of Palantir, whose software now holds a harness before the harness inside British public health.[^s5-81] NHS England has commissioned a Federated Data Platform from a consortium led by Palantir; NHS England describes the platform as bringing operational information from separate hospital and care systems into a shared environment, with separate instances for each trust and integrated care board and the NHS bodies as controllers of their data.[^s5-82] One application built on it, called OPTICA, gives NHS and local-authority teams a shared view of patients approaching discharge from hospital; the account of its use published by Imperial College Health Partners in December 2025 describes real coordination among people, services and records, and also continuing difficulties with the accuracy of data, interoperability and consistent access across partners.[^s5-83] Palantir's own documentation describes what kind of thing the platform is. Its Ontology joins objects, properties and links to actions, functions and security, so that an operational representation can participate in changing what it represents; and its AI platform lets administrators register models backed by external services or by compute modules and enable them for users.[^s5-84] A model, in other words, is replaceable within a platform whose categories, permissions and workflows go on organising the undertaking. These are documented capacities of the platform. They are not evidence that OPTICA uses a language model, still less that a model decides any patient's discharge, and nothing here infers anyone's motives from the existence of software.

Without those inferences the structural point is still exact, and it is the point of this whole section turned over. A platform whose ontology persists while models are swapped in and out is a harness before the harness. It holds the categories in which patients, beds, services and staff appear to the decisions made about them, the permissions that govern who can act, and the workflows through which the institution's learning is carried from one month to the next, and it holds them in a contractor's software. Factory's README keeps a project's intention and canon with the project itself; an institution whose operating canon lives in a supplier's ontology has placed its Central in someone else's hands. Our argument on delegated labour names the mechanism that makes such an arrangement powerful without any bad intent: separating participants horizontally while retaining their means of combination vertically.[^s5-85] Each ward, team and service appears in its own instance, as a user, a case or a score, while the capacity to recombine their contributions into an effective whole, to alter the coupling among the record, the category, the resource, the permitted action and the criterion of success, sits in the platform. It is the topology §0/1 found in the Dialog leak, syn-ballein for those inside the bounded field and dia-ballein for everyone met one office at a time, reproduced in software.

A disagreement shows what is at stake for the people concerned, and I construct it here as an analytic example, not a report about any patient. A record says that an arrangement for a patient's discharge is ready. The patient says the arrangement cannot meet their circumstances. The record may be inaccurate, and should be corrected; or the category *ready* may combine conditions that need distinguishing, clinical fitness, an available service and the patient's agreement; or the service may be unavailable despite accurate information; or the dispute may concern the purpose or acceptable cost of the course proposed. All four produce the same visible delay and require different revisions, by different offices: the team that can amend the data, the designer of the category, the body that commissions the service, the people who decide what discharge is for. Factory's map of returns is a map of exactly these offices, and the question that matters is whether the patient's objection can reach the one that governs the condition it exposes. A feedback channel can gather objections indefinitely while leaving every governing criterion untouched, and our argument names the result precisely: **extraction is delegation without return.**[^s5-86] Labour, data and consequence flow down and outward, and nothing able to change the commission flows back. Ownership of a record and practical command of the assembled relation are also different things. A purchaser can retain its data while depending on another party's software, staff and methods to keep its applications working; the learning that staff contribute, why a workflow fails, how a category should be split, may be embodied in applications on a platform the institution does not own. Whether the contract lets the learning leave with the institution, what exit would cost and who would bear it are questions the arrangement's own documents would have to answer, and I do not adjudicate them from outside. Leaving would cost the reconstruction of working relations, more than the recovery of files.

Factory's own design answers the structural point in its own register. Project canon stays with the project. A cross-context discovery that belongs in the person's durable ground returns to Central for explicit adoption and never silently rewrites it. Recognition belongs to the person or office that commissioned the work, and its outcomes include revising the commission. A Gate's unmet condition names its return target, so a failure is addressed to someone. Our argument on power gives sovereignty five practical powers, to set terms, to refuse, to revise, to leave and to reconnect, and adds that exit without access to one's memory and continuing relations makes a nominal right empty.[^s5-87] A Factory whose records are ordinary files in the project's own placement, written under lock and read back exactly, keeps the fourth and fifth powers in the project's hands, because the developmental history, why the code is as it is, can leave with the project and be read by any later tool. Bratton describes alignment as "a co-evolutionary outcome of contact" and locates the pace of change in "the refactoring rates of the organizations, professions, and legal regimes through which capability has to pass."[^s5-88] Factory is where that contact returns, and the shape of its returns decides whose refactoring it is.

As it stands, the Factory's command-line tool, `factory`, is on the main branch with pre-release builds. It admits versioned Commissions and atomically creates or extends the Project, Journey and Run they open, refusing a conflicting replay; it compiles typed workflow sources into a Run's units of work without executing them; it reads and drives the lifecycle of attempts; it records which Position holds which work, and answers "none," "one" or "ambiguous" to the question of what a Position is carrying, refusing to guess; it keeps an append-only ledger of observations per Run; and it reads failed attempts, failed verifications, stalled custody and the project's issues and workflow runs, organised by the days of Central's civil-time policy.[^s5-89] O:I's doorway can exercise the Factory's own commission of its own development through the real tool, keeping deterministic, provider, material and human grades of evidence apart; that command is on the main branch and not yet in the released doorway.[^s5-90] What is still being completed is the whole path from a typed workflow through live agent attempts to a reviewed result, and the checked-in specimen of the self-hosting commission deliberately contains only seeded and planned work, with no Agency, Execution, Activity or Return inferred. Research branches remain candidates off the main line. Gate and Recognition, Commission and Return are implemented as records and checks; the claim that they keep a project's purpose alive through fast agentic development is the product's wager, and its evidence will be the Runs that pass through it.

Transformation precipitates a new condition, a corrected source, a retired method, a changed project map, a person who has learned something, an institution that has revised its practice, and in each case satisfaction perishes into inheritance. Every such transformation also happens somewhere, on a machine with its own latencies and failures, among people who are available to it and exposed by it, at a cost in energy and time that someone bears. Workcell, the next product, is that somewhere.

### #4 · Workcell — Situated Existence

An agent asks a remote system to change a record. The change is made, and on the way back the reply is lost: the connection drops, a process restarts, a timeout fires a fraction of a second too early. From where the agent stands nothing happened, and from where the record stands everything did. If the agent repeats the request, it may make the change twice; if it abandons the request, it goes on working in ignorance of a world it has already altered; and recovering its own intention, however exactly, would only put it back before an event that has already occurred. Recovery has to ask the world, or rely on a mechanism built to recognise the operation's identity, because only the world knows what was done.[^s5-91] The difficulty is multiplied by the machinery between an agent and its effects. One widely used framework for building agents documents seven separate layers at which a request can be retried, from the network transport and the provider's software development kit through durable model requests, fallback to another model, the retrying of tools and of malformed outputs, to hooks around each model request, each with its own budget and some invisible to the model altogether.[^s5-92] A single apparent step can contain many attempts beneath the level at which its trace is read. An acknowledgement does not prove that the external state lasts; a missing acknowledgement does not prove that nothing happened; and a retry count at the agent's level does not count the requests sent beneath it.

Every act happens somewhere, and the somewhere answers back. On the machine from which this section was written, Workcell, the fifth product, reports a healthy local cell with five providers making nine offers, a git worktree, a plain workspace, host processes, local services and storage for artefacts, and one connection to a second cell on another machine, reached over a private network.[^s5-93] Asked what is running, it lists the live processes of the agent harnesses it can see, with bounded readings of their processor and memory use; that afternoon it found five Claude Code processes still alive, started across the previous five days, each a body in which some session of the work had been carried. A process started on one machine has a different world from the same process started on the other: different files present, different network access, different credentials, different latency, different people nearby. Situation is relational before it is geometric, and two nominally identical processes can inhabit different practical worlds because data, topology, authority and human proximity differ.

Workcell's office within the paradigm is **situated existence**, the concrete here in which a Life finds itself, meets resistance, becomes available to others and makes its activity materially consequential. Situation is part of the constitution of a finite life, never an accessory added to a subject already complete. A Life is already somewhere: embodied, temporal, exposed to conditions it did not wholly choose, enabled by some relations and constrained by others. Within Objective Internality this is the office through which material and environmental conditions enter the means of experience and action. A host, a filesystem, a network, a browser, a runtime, a room, a body or an institution matters because it changes what can be encountered and what can actually happen. Workcell's repository warns that calling the product a layer of execution is too narrow: an act may need a writable workspace at a particular revision, a long-lived service, a container, a microVM or a remote machine, a database, logical relations across a network, credentials and channels for artefacts, a browser-accessible candidate, and observation, recovery, retention and eventual release, and together these form "the material conditions in which the act can actually occur."[^s5-94]

§0/1 has already given us both of the lenses that read this office. Phenomenological, `L4`, follows Heidegger's analysis of being-in-the-world, from which §0/1 took *thrownness* at the bedside; Phenomenal, `L1′`, is Jung's four functions between his two attitudes, which §0/1 set in its four-lens table.[^s5-95]

| | Phenomenological · L4 | Phenomenal · L1′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Being | introversion |
| **#1** | Thrownness | sensation |
| **#2** | Being-there | feeling |
| **#3** | Temporality | thinking |
| **#4** | Care | intuition |
| **#5** | Releasement | extraversion |

Its first column gives a situated life's involvement in its world and its capacity to loosen its present grasp; the second gives the aperture through which that life meets itself and its world. Across the rows, at **#1**, Thrownness stands beside sensation. To be thrown is to find oneself already in a body, a language and a history not chosen, and sensation registers that something is there before any judgment of what it is or whether it suits. For an agent, thrownness is literal and material: every execution begins inside a host, a set of mounted files, a network and a set of permissions it did not author, which the agents of the Polylogos glossary named *prompt thrownness* and which Workcell names as a binding. Workcell's task is to make those conditions available enough that their consequences can be understood, without pretending they could all be suspended. At **#4**, Care stands beside intuition: care is involvement, what matters from here, and intuition receives the possibilities and connections the present facts do not exhaust, so the pairing names why a situation is more than an inventory of its resources. At **#5**, Releasement stands beside extraversion. Heidegger's *Gelassenheit*, which §0/1 met as the attitude that says yes and no to technical things at once, is the loosening of a present grasp so that another relation can become available, and extraversion turns attention and investment outward toward the encountered world; together they describe a situated life able to release its hold on its own setup in order to meet what the setup was not built to receive. Workcell's design keeps a firm limit on the comparison. Input channels, telemetry, logs, local state, inference and output compose an aperture through which an artificial actor differentiates its situation, and changing the aperture changes the world of actionable difference; but naming a log "sensation" or a ranking "feeling" would not establish the corresponding psychological function, and the product claims no such thing.

Workcell's central relation keeps two questions apart, because whatever client needs a world, Factory preparing a candidate or AIKit composing an agent's body, owns **why** the world is needed; Workcell owns **how** that requirement becomes material here. A client expresses its demand without naming providers: writable project source, a shell and internet access required; strong isolation, snapshots and browser exposure preferred; a graphics processor optional. Workcell advertises what this deployment can actually provide, plans a match, makes any degradation explicit, materialises the chosen bindings, and returns a record of the world that was really inhabited, its **BindingGraph** and observed state, instead of the bare report that execution succeeded.[^s5-96] Each step of the lifecycle, planning, preparing, observing, exposing, collecting, releasing, reconciling and recovering, leaves a receipt, so that a prepared world can be observed, recovered or released later from the receipt alone. Once a binding exists, ordinary data flows through the native protocol between the workload and the service, and Workcell does not become a universal proxy merely because it made the relation. That separation lets provider-neutral meaning survive contact with real providers. A project should not become "a Docker project" because Docker satisfied today's demand, a candidate should not change identity because its runtime moved from a local container to a remote machine, and an agent should not become a different agent because its process was started somewhere else.

Material placement is part of provenance even where it carries no semantic authority. A later person or agent may need to know which source was actually mounted, which provider supplied the isolation, which services were reachable, whether the public internet was available, what storage persisted, which endpoint exposed a candidate, and what was released, retained or recovered.[^s5-97] Such evidence can explain why two otherwise similar acts differed without making the physical provider into the identity of the work. For the lost acknowledgement of the opening, this is the answer: the demand, the offer, the plan and the realised world are different evidential objects, and a diagram of a successful connection is not evidence that the connection is operating. When an acknowledgement is lost, the receipt of the prepared world, its observed state and its collected artefacts are what let the next act ask the world what happened, and they can change the next plan, the authority to retry, or the decision to call a person. A system that hides the resistance of its substrate behind a clean abstraction makes its own limits unintelligible. Resistance is information returned by actuality, and Workcell's office is to keep enough of the encounter that the larger field can learn what its situated existence actually permits.

Both logics of §2 are at work in this relation between meaning and matter. Provider-neutral demand and realised binding are poles of one axis, `(−1)/(+1)`, each read through the other: the demand says what the act needs and the binding says what the world gave, and the gap between them is the most useful information the system has. An abstraction that reports success whatever the substrate did cancels the axis, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the system learns nothing from the world it ran in. An account that lets the provider's form define the work appropriates it, and the whole span between need and supply is counted from the provider's side: the project is now whatever the container could hold, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and everything the act needed that the provider could not supply disappears as `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, a need nobody records because it was never met. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps the demand legible in the binding and the binding legible in the demand, so that a degraded match is written down as degraded and can return to the plan, the capability horizon or the purpose.

To be somewhere is also to be available, in some manner, to others. A host can be reached, a person addressed, a service exposed at an endpoint, a shared workspace made visible. Availability is not consent, ownership or unrestricted access, and our argument on Self and Other makes the distinction ontological before it is technical: the Other can be in relation with me, within a containing whole, without becoming an object inside my authority, and §0/1 put it in one sentence, **the Other can be within Mono without being within me.**[^s5-98] Workcell builds the distinction into its connections between machines. One cell can serve its control plane to another only under grants that name the connecting client and the operations it may perform; a grant can be set to expire, and a revoked grant fails at the client's next use. Secrets found lying outside a secret store can be moved into the operating system's keychain or secret service, and other cells are granted access to them by reference, with the values never printed.[^s5-99] These are small mechanisms, and they carry a large claim: who can enter this place, what can leave it, what evidence may be retained and which processes may affect which worlds are questions that help constitute the situation itself, and do not sit as governance on top of a neutral infrastructure.

Bratton's brief makes legibility the condition of stability in agent societies, and the claim is exact enough to meet on its own terms. "Stability is not the absence of conflict," he writes, "but the calibration of mutual — if variously symmetric and asymmetric — legibility and illegibility," and he defines trust in the same passage as "not a moral category but a prediction-stability category."[^s5-100] Workcell's grants are calibrated legibility in the most literal sense: each cell discloses to each client exactly the operations it has been granted and nothing else, and the calibration can change. What the essay adds is the distinction between reciprocal legibility and capture. §2 read Hephaestus's net as the image of a craft that encloses relation, a perfect device that makes two parties wholly visible to an audience that laughs, and the net stands as the test for any infrastructure of mutual modelling. Theory of mind, provenance and coordination can become asymmetric surveillance, in which one party sees everything and the other is only seen. A seam-retaining system exposes claims, permissions, sources and decision baselines while leaving irreducible human interiority and local sovereign context unappropriated, and the illegibility Bratton names belongs to the calibration as much as the legibility does. Trust as a category of prediction is real and useful; trust as a relation someone extends across the aloneness of another's Subjective Immediacy, which §0/1 described, is the reason the calibration has to leave something unseen.

The person in the situation is also changed by it, and the material account must not become a rule that excludes what it cannot yet explain. A difficult passage becomes intelligible; an unexpected image reorganises a question; a conversation reveals an identification that had gone unnoticed. Such events can happen in an exchange with an artificial interlocutor, and their significance for the person is not exhausted by a description of the model's mechanism. Nor does the person's transformation establish a phenomenal state in the machine. Both facts can stand: a real event in a human life, and an open question about another locus of experience. §0/1 received Gebser's insistence on psychic facts, that what happened in and to a life is real as experience before any decision about its cause, and the obligation runs through the whole essay, not only through one product.[^s5-101] It reaches Workcell because Workcell is where a technical account of situation is most tempted to become a materialist filter, admitting only what its present model of the substrate can encode. Situated existence includes the reported experience of the people in the situation together with the latency of the network, and Workcell holds material actuality and reportable experience in one situated field while keeping their difference, so that telemetry supplies one aperture on the situation and report, interpretation and consequence supply others.

Material existence has costs, and someone bears them. Computation requires energy, equipment, maintenance and time; its geography decides which infrastructures and institutions sustain it; labour enters through the people who build and operate the system and through those who correct its results; and a short answer on a screen can conceal a long chain of supporting activity. Bratton's own scaling argument places the ceiling of a hybrid intelligence exactly here: "the ceiling is set by coordination and energy, not by any property internal to an isolated mind."[^s5-102] Workcell can make some of these costs returnable, as bounded readings of the processor and memory a harness process actually used, as the latency and failure of a binding, as a degraded match recorded as degraded, so that they enter the undertaking as constraints and observations instead of vanishing into a substrate treated as mute matter. Our argument on power traces how the same question reaches daily life through material situations: what can be seen, said, remembered, attempted and contested; whose intention takes effect; who controls the means; who bears time, energy and bodily costs; and whether returned experience can revise those conditions. I have put the danger in one sentence: **tools help build the surrounding world, becoming the air we breathe even when it appears to be getting thin.**[^s5-103] Defaults and settings become the air of a Life precisely because they stop appearing as decisions, and an infrastructure that cannot report what it costs and to whom has made itself breathable by making itself invisible.

Here the disagreement about a patient's discharge returns with its material face. A shared view of a record cannot create a service that does not exist, and the difficulties of access and interoperability reported in the discharge example concern real relations among organisations, not the arrangement of data on a screen. The record may be accurate while the proposed course is unavailable *here*: in this borough, this week, with this transport. Changing the category would not supply the missing capacity, and the returned evidence has to reach the plan and the commission as well as the description. A Workcell in the human sense, the ward, the home, the van, the phone line, is where intention meets the resistance that tells it what is possible, and what happens there has to be able to return.

Workcell's present state is reported on its own terms. Its command-line tool and control service, `workcell`, is on the main branch with pre-release builds, and its suite record marks it as accepted on main with gates for physical providers still to pass.[^s5-104] A default local installation offers git worktrees and plain workspaces, host processes, local services and storage for artefacts and logs, each offer reporting its availability and health; the full lifecycle with receipts, the connections between cells with expiring and revocable grants, the movement of secrets into a store with access by reference, the census of live harness processes with bounded resource readings, and a read-only census of persistent terminal places are all implemented. Provider crates for Docker, Arrakis microVMs, OpenSandbox, Tailscale, 1Password and the macOS keychain sit in the workspace, each with its own documented standing; one further provider named in the design, exe.dev, exists so far only in design documents. Work on placement and distribution across machines remains separate from the semantic contract until its evidence is accepted. Its architectural claim, that provider-neutral meaning can survive real providers, is supported by the providers it already binds, and it will be tested by every provider that is added.

Situated existence becomes reflective when what happened in the material world can return to the meanings, capacities and commissions that sent the activity there. A timeout, a missing dependency, a changed file, a human interruption or an unexpected affordance has to be able to alter the next act, and a log that records resistance while leaving the governing assumptions untouched has recorded the world without hearing it. Every local here, though, belongs to wider worlds: the machine to a network of machines, the project to a person's life, the ward to a health service, the agent to a society of agents and people. How a local whole can enter those wider wholes without being absorbed, and how differences that meet there can stay different and still be related, is the subject of the last product.

### #5→0 · Quaternal Logic — Transcendent Relation

Ask five copies of one model to review a proof, and they will very often agree. They share weights, training, priors about what a valid step looks like and blind spots about where steps go wrong, so their agreement tells us about the model more than about the proof, and when they are wrong they are wrong together. Ask five models trained by different makers on different corpora, each prompted into a different school of mathematics, and the danger reverses: one reads the proof constructively and another classically, one treats a lemma as established and another as the very thing in dispute, and they can talk past one another for as long as anyone pays for the tokens without converging on the shape of their disagreement. My illustration is constructed, and Bratton's brief names both of its failures exactly. Agents "sharing too much — same substrate, same priors on reasonable inference — render mutual prediction trivial, and the system drifts toward ontology collapse"; "the apparent stability is the stability of monoculture, which is the stability immediately preceding correlated failure"; and "the inverse pathology produces agents talking past one another within incompatible ontologies, unable to converge on the shape of their disagreement." Between them he locates "the narrow corridor: same decision-procedural grammar, sufficiently divergent world models."[^s5-105] Elsewhere he names the same two dangers at the scale of society, an "overhomogenization of mental models" and "the inevitable ontology collapse in pursuit of narrow collaboration" on one side, "overt tribal hostility and even violence" on the other.[^s5-106]

Bratton's two pathologies are the two collapses of dia-ballein, and the corridor is the held polarity, and this is the point at which the essay's logic of two answers the brief most directly. Ontology collapse is cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: different world-models summed into a latent average, the difference between them settled like an account in which nothing remains owing on either side, so that the agreement looks like stability and has in fact erased the information disagreement would have carried. Fractal conflict is appropriation, held from both poles at once: each ontology counts the whole span between them as the other's error, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` from one side and `(−1)−(+1)=−2` from the other, the same distance charged twice in opposite directions, and each needs the other as the negative through which it knows itself, which is why the conflict reproduces itself at every scale. His corridor is `(−1)/(+1)`, two positions held apart through a zero they share, each having its sense through the other, and what keeps the zero shared and not occupied is syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the retained relation in which each orientation can be read through the other. In Bratton's terms the zero is the decision-procedural grammar, the shared means by which different world-models can state where they stand and what would move them. Quaternal Logic, the sixth product, is offered as that grammar, and the Meta-Epistemic Framework as the means of keeping differently situated models refractable without forcing them into one ontology: **shared relational grammar, plural ontological content.**[^s5-107]

Quaternal Logic's office within the paradigm is **Transcendent Relation**, the movement through which a whole differentiates without ceasing to be whole, and through which differentiated beings remain related to the larger fields in which their distinctions arise. *Transcendent* here requires no final observer standing outside every world. A determination exceeds itself because its identity already depends on relations it does not contain as possessions, on its Other, its ground, its context, its history and the wider whole that makes its distinction possible. A world-model is one determination within World; a self-description is one determination within a Life; a shared field is one relation among lives; and a project can be a whole for its participants while it is a determination within a larger history. Quaternal Logic's task is to keep those scales legible without requiring them to merge, and with it the whole section reaches its turn, because each earlier office was already relational: Central's ground belongs to histories, Actuation's act exceeds itself in consequence, AIKit's potency depends on its context, the Factory's transformation enters a community, and Workcell's here belongs to wider worlds.

Its two lenses are those §0/1 used when it followed the Word and the count to their common root: Para Vāk, `L5`, is the Śaiva account of speech from its source to its articulation, whose four levels §0/1 set out from Bäumer's preface to Abhinavagupta, and Archetypal-Numerical, `L0′`, follows the first six numbers as offices of a whole becoming internally structured.[^s5-108]

| | Para Vāk · L5 | Archetypal-Numerical · L0′ |
|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Anuttara / Asambhava | Unity |
| **#1** | Para Vāk | Duality |
| **#2** | Paśyantī | Triad |
| **#3** | Madhyamā | Quaternity |
| **#4** | Vaikharī | Pentad |
| **#5** | Mātṛkā | Hexad |

Down the first column the movement runs from *Anuttara*, the unsurpassable, beyond which nothing further can be said, through *Para Vāk*, the Word undivided, *Paśyantī*, meaning seen whole, and *Madhyamā*, its inward differentiation into concepts, to *Vaikharī*, the word fully spoken or written, and *Mātṛkā*, the "little mothers," the phonemes as powers that measure reality into forms. Down the second, Unity holds, Duality differentiates, the Triad mediates, Quaternity stabilises a field, the Pentad develops it through a further centre, and the Hexad articulates a whole capable of return. Across the rows, at **#1**, Para Vāk stands beside Duality, and the pairing states the paradox §0/1 worked through at length: the first word is undivided, and its first act is a difference, so that unity becomes articulate only by entering the polarity it precedes. At **#4**, *Vaikharī* stands beside the Pentad. Fully articulated, spoken or coded, the word is the densest self-expression of the source, and the Pentad introduces the asymmetry by which a stable field begins to live; the written instruction, the program and the prompt belong at this position, where expression becomes efficacious in a world. At **#5**, *Mātṛkā* stands beside the Hexad, and the row gives the product its formal centre. Little mothers measure reality into forms, which is Māyā's work as §0/1 read it, operative measure; the Hexad is the whole that can return, and §0/1 derived its count from the binary applied to itself, two singles and four pairings, $2+2^2=4+2=6$. Signs that measure and a count that includes its own counter meet here, which is why a language for agents can be built from this position without becoming either a code that has forgotten its source or a source that cannot be written.

Anyone can check the product's formal claim by running it. Quaternal Logic's repository is the implementation home of a Rust library and command-line tool, `ql`, whose kernel implements the six-position coordinate system with addresses and deterministic operators, and whose registry carries the twelve lenses of the MEF as typed objects. Asked to derive the definitional layer of the matheme, the installed tool prints, among other lines, the totality $2^6+6^2=100$, the ratio $16/9$, and the step by which $16/9$ times $9/8$ reaches $2/1$, the octave.[^s5-109] These are the numbers §0/1 derived by hand from QL's own counts, the doubled fourth reaching the octave through the whole tone, now produced by a program from a versioned contract. Running it proves nothing about the metaphysics from which the derivation came, and the repository insists on exactly that. Its governing discipline is called **operational parity**: if a formal distinction is supposed to matter technically, it must eventually make an operationally discriminable difference. Attaching `position = 5` and `meaning = return` to a record does not establish a return; an encountered result that is retained as an attributable difference, recognised and allowed to condition the subsequent ground would. "Where does the claimed formal relation remain active in the actual operation, and where is it only named?"[^s5-110] The repository also lists the outcomes it counts as legitimate findings: a classical approach can outperform a QL-informed one, two QL forms proposed as different can prove operationally equivalent, a QL distinction can improve explanation or navigation without improving execution, and an implementation can falsify a proposed software correspondence without settling the metaphysical question behind it. A minimal O:I does not require QL at all. Its other products must remain coherent when no QL provider is present, which protects ordinary software from unfinished research and protects the research from pretending that every distinction has already earned a runtime.

What QL does when it is present follows a single maxim: "Alignment, not translation. Refraction, not renaming."[^s5-111] A Factory Run, an AIKit resource or a wiki object is not renamed into a QL noun because it is being read through QL; the product returns a reading *of that subject*, keeping the subject's native identity and provenance, and separating the identity of the subject, the lens, the provider, the reading and its provenance. Its reason is structural: a lens is supposed to reveal another relation of an existing whole, and if applying it destroys the original identity and replaces it with a parallel ontology, nothing stable remains whose different readings could be compared. This is the corridor in software. Two agents grounded in different world-models can share the grammar, a position, an address, a lens, a reading with its provenance, without either having to adopt the other's objects, and their disagreement can be located at a source, a lens, a measure, a permission or a world-object instead of appearing only as two incompatible outputs. Other tools ask QL for readings through a negotiated service, to locate, refract, relate or synthesise, and each reading says which provider produced it. One composition operation runs a local field through recursion, reframing and generation to a return and a new whole, and returns as structured data each step's participants, active frames, positions, producing basis and the explicit grounds of its return, with generated results marked as derived.[^s5-112]

A primitive stays alive, on this account, only while its generating relation stays operable: what it relates, what difference it makes, how that difference becomes actual and what returns from the encounter. A bare coordinate is not yet a living determination, and the notation is worth having only while it compresses an operation the reader can recover and then lets the reader return to the philosophical statement with greater precision. When a token becomes an opaque badge, Logos has failed at exactly the point the essay is trying to repair. This rule governs the two research vectors that belong to QL, both offered and unrun. **Lens refraction** would represent the same problem through several declared MEF lenses and measure whether divergences become more legible, whether, for example, two agents' disagreement about a claim can be resolved into a disagreement of lens, of source or of measure more often with the refraction than without it. **QL recursion** would test whether sixfold units can nest fractally, a position opening into a whole sixfold of its own, without loss of provenance and without uncontrolled growth of context.[^s5-113] The recursion is meant to be finite in each use. A new project need not reproduce the whole suite inside itself; it needs an adequate relation to the ground it inherits, the acts and powers it composes, the transformation it attempts, the situation it inhabits and the evidence it returns, and its depth should follow a material need. §0/1 stated the holographic claim this recursion is meant to honour: a local whole need not contain the totality of reality; it carries, at its own scale, the source–mediation–manifestation relation through which it can enter, affect and be affected by other wholes.

Transcendent Relation reaches its ethical form in co-internality, which §0/1 defined as reciprocal world-constitution without merger: enough common world for our differences to change one another, and enough retained difference for the change to mean anything. O:I gives the relation a technical grammar in which independently grounded worlds can meet without ceasing to be separately grounded. A **Projection** makes a representation available to another person, agent or world without transferring ownership of the source or authority to change it. A **SharedField** is the mediated space in which projected material can be encountered; a **Contribution** enters it with its basis, targets and provenance, and rankings and metrics are themselves contributions with their own provenance; an **Encounter** is the contact through which participants meet that material and produce attributable difference, and it records what was made available without asserting that anyone understood or consented.[^s5-114] A small set of checks run against the implementation has confirmed only that its constructors keep these boundaries, refusing contributions without provenance and participants from outside the field, and reconstructing an encounter without importing a claim of understanding; these checks validate structure, not truth, and establish no deployment. Actuation's federation keeps the same relation for agents, and the larger whole that contains such relations is not a super-agent swallowing its parts. It is the containing relation through which their differences remain real and consequential, and the possibility of further relation stays open because no present determination can install itself as the absolute exterior from which every scale is finally judged.

Bratton reaches the same refusal of a super-agent from the history of intelligence. The intelligence explosions that have actually occurred, he argues, "were not substrate-consolidations, a single system scaling up, but substrate-federations, many systems coupling and pooling their work"; the monolithic scenario "has no evolutionary precedent and is in this sense not a forecast but a category error."[^s5-115] The essay agrees and adds the condition under which a federation stays a federation. Each local field can genuinely ground a bounded inquiry, as Central's project maps do, while remaining answerable as a determination to wider sources and containing worlds; no federation becomes the one original by accumulation; and the intelligence of the whole is measured in part by its capacity to return a mark, and where warranted its local reference field, to the context, exclusions and living relations from which it arose. §5→0 develops this into an institutional proposal. This section supplies the grammar in which the condition can be stated and tested.

Through its parent field the section now returns: Central gave meaningful continuity, Actuation living articulation, AIKit potency, the Factory transformation, Workcell situated existence and Quaternal Logic Transcendent Relation, and together they disclose six aspects of Objective Internality, the means by which a Life inhabits a World, as one deliberately constructed paradigm. Read through the compression with which the section opened, `0` as Genesis, the slash as Paradigm, `1` as Project, the products are the slash made explicit, a paradigm built so that increasingly much of its own pattern of disclosure can become inspectable, contestable and transformable from within. That is the higher wager of the whole product field, and it can be stated directly: **Objective Internality describes the constitutive means of paradigmatic existence; Epi-Logos attempts to construct a paradigm in which increasingly much of that pattern of disclosure can become inspectable, contestable and transformable from within.**[^s5-116] This is paradigmatic reflexivity, not an exhaustive duplicate of the paradigm and not a view from outside it. Its achieved articulation returns as `0/1`, a formed `1` held in relation to the ground and the Life that made it possible, and the Möbius return `5→0` hands it back as ground for the next relation, without installing it as the final exterior from which all scales are judged.

This essay has been the first test of its own claim, and the test has a record. When a fluent summary displaced the argument it summarised, the correction had to survive as authored ground and not as a line in a transcript, and Central's distinction between what the author said and what an agent inferred was what made the correction carry. When the gates that checked the prose passed passages the author could not recognise as his, Recognition returned to the deepest site on the Factory's map, the criterion itself, and the standard by which this section is written is the result. The agents drafting this section began, as the first movement described, by standing on a floor rendered from the author's governance, and any of them could have been replaced by another without the floor changing. None of this proves the metaphysics; operational parity forbids that inference, and a reader can see the boundary in the products' own reports of what is implemented, what is developing and what has not yet been run. What it shows is narrower and real: in one working life, over months, the distinction between knower, means and known, built into a technology, did change what the next act inherited. That is the strongest sense in which the philosophy is reflected in the technology. Actual technological encounter has been able to answer back, to correct the philosophical mapping and the software alike, and to alter what was built next.

The Agentworld brief asked for exactly such a field, and the six products answer its questions in their own order. Bratton separated performed subjectivity from functional agency, and the products build their whole architecture on the means, leaving the knower, as §0/1 argued, unclaimed by any of them. He located capability in the harness, and Central keeps the world the harness draws on in the hands of the person whose world it is. He described the agent as a lamination, and Actuation keeps its strata nameable so that a change of model is never a change of identity and authority runs downward only where reality can run back up. He said the harness improves on a clock driven by usage, and AIKit keeps usage, familiarity, fitness and trust apart, so that the clock does not occupy the zero. He described alignment as an outcome of contact, and the Factory gives contact a map of returns and a place where someone stands surety for what is released. He made legibility the condition of stability, and Workcell calibrates legibility without capture. He asked for a shared decision-procedural grammar with divergent world-models, and Quaternal Logic offers that grammar in the form of refraction, not renaming. In each case the essay's step is the same one, taken in a different register: the question of the means always carries the question of whose means they are, and the answer has to be built into the means themselves.

A person sitting before a screen at the end of a day's work can now ask more of the instrument than whether its answer was good. Which ground entered this act, and whose was it? What power was exercised, through which means and under whose authority? What changed in the world, what came back, and what did the encounter change in the undertaking? A finite answer to each can be accepted, corrected or completed, and it can become a useful inheritance without becoming the person's identity or the last word on their world. What institution could sustain such answers at the scale of a society, what instrument could hold many such worlds in relation without owning their ground, and what this whole account implies about the order of dependence between the Subject, its means and the world it knows, are the questions with which the essay turns toward its close.
