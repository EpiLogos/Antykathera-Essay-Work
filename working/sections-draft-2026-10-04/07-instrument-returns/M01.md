### #0 · From Theory to Vocation

A night nurse sits at the end of a ward at a quarter to seven in the morning and writes the handover. She has been awake for twelve hours, and in a quarter of an hour someone who has slept will arrive to take her place, so the sheet she fills in has to carry a night that only she lived through. Bed four's temperature came down after midnight. Bed six is waiting on a consultant who said he would come before nine. Bed eleven must not be moved to the window. Most of us have written a note like this on leaving a shift, a job or a house we were looking after, and most of us have been the one who reads it on arrival, standing at a desk still warm from someone else's day and trying to understand a world from the few lines its last occupant thought worth setting down. Whatever the note leaves out, the reader will not know to ask about. Whatever it states as settled will look settled. A mistake that nobody wrote down as a mistake will very likely be made again before lunch.

Machines now run the same practice. An agent that works on a task over many sessions remembers nothing of the last session unless something was written down for it, and the people who build such agents have learned to make them keep notes for their successors: what was decided, which instructions bind, which sources have been checked and which have not, what is still open. Each new session begins from that file, and the world it works in is, at first, the world the file describes. §0/1 met this condition at a bedside, where a child arrives in a world it did not choose, and again in the lexicon the agents of Bratton's brief composed for themselves, which named their own arrival *prompt thrownness*; §4 · #5→0 follows what that word discloses about an agent's continuity.[^s50-thrownness] The handover gives the question §0/1 left with the makers of artificial agents, which inherited assumptions they are passing on and what each encounter could change in them, its smallest working form. Whoever writes the note decides, in a few tired minutes, what will count as the starting world for a life or a process that cannot check the decision. A note that says "the relative in bed six is difficult" hands on a verdict and withholds the history that produced it. A note that says "the family of bed six has been promised three different times by three different people" hands on the same difficulty with its origin still attached, so that the morning reader can do something with it besides confirm it. Both are accounts, and they differ in one respect only: whether the account keeps a route back to what it accounts for.

A rule that seems to have no reason is the plainest test of that route, and G. K. Chesterton made it a parable about reform. Someone finds "a fence or gate erected across a road" and, seeing no use for it, proposes to clear it away; the wiser reformer answers, "If you don't see the use of it, I certainly won't let you clear it away. Go away and think. Then, when you can come back and tell me that you *do* see the use of it, I may allow you to destroy it."[^s50-chesterton] The fence did not grow there, Chesterton goes on: "Some person had some reason for thinking it would be a good thing for somebody. And until we know what the reason was, we really cannot judge whether the reason was reasonable."[^s50-chesterton-reason] Bed eleven's line in the handover is such a fence. The morning nurse does not know that the woman in that bed grew up in a house that flooded, that she wakes in terror at the sound of rain on glass, that the last time she was moved she pulled out her line. Those five words carry all of it compressed, and Chesterton's principle is the reason a careful successor reads it twice before overruling it. Chesterton's principle has a direction, though, and its direction is its limit. It faces the origin that is gone, the intention of whoever built the fence, and it has nothing to say about the person at the gate who needs to pass this morning, or about the field beyond the gate, which may have changed since the fence went up. A handover can honour every reason of the night and still fail the patient in the morning, because what the patient will need today has no reason yet, only a beginning.

Our theory has reached that point. Across §5 the six products of O:I, from Central's meaningful continuity to Quaternal Logic's Transcendent Relation, were developed as six aspects of one deliberately constructed paradigm, a paradigm in the sense of §5 · #0, through which a Life carries meaning, acts and finds itself in a World.[^s50-paradigm] Every station before them was written so that a reader could take something over and work with it. A theory that has been completed in this way stands where the night nurse stands at a quarter to seven: it has to be handed on, to readers, to the builders of instruments, to institutions and to agents, and the question of what it hands on with it is no longer a question about its truth alone.

Two words carry the turn, and both keep their history close to the surface. *Vocation* is from Latin *vocare*, to call: a calling is an address that reaches someone from beyond the one who answers it, so the person called chooses how to answer and does not choose the voice. *Theory* is from Greek *theōria*, and behind *theōria* stands the *theōros*, whom the lexicon describes first as an envoy sent to consult an oracle or to attend a festival on a city's behalf, and only later, more generally, as a spectator, one who travels to see men and things.[^s50-theoros] The *theōros* was sent because the city needed to know something it could not see from where it stood, and his seeing was finished only when he came home and gave an account the city could act on. Theory in its oldest institutional sense therefore contained its return. Modern theory often keeps the seeing and loses the journey home, treating an account as complete once it is internally coherent and leaving what it returns to, and who receives it, to a later stage called application. Moving from theory to vocation recovers the journey home. Nothing has to be added to a theory to make it vocational; something it always carried has to be allowed to act.

What returns is the account, and it returns to the relation it came from. §0/1 · #5→0 gave Epi-Logos its definition: the achieved relation becomes answerable to the operation by which it was achieved, and the desire that bends an account back toward its own making is the eros of Logos.[^s50-epilogos] At the end of a theory that desire meets a choice it could not meet earlier, because only now is there a finished account powerful enough to be tempted by its own finish. A completed account can return in two directions. It can return the world to itself, so that whatever it meets is read in its terms, every objection becomes one more instance of something the account already explains, and the account never has to change. Or it can return itself to the world and to its sources, so that what it meets can reach the terms in which it was made and the paradigm that produced it can change. Counterfeit mediation is the first direction and Epi-Logos the second; both are returns, both involve a sophisticated account doing real work, and they differ only in which way the dependence is allowed to run.[^s50-direction] A ward has a familiar name for the first direction. A patient who complains about the pain relief is "anxious", a patient who refuses the night medication is "non-compliant", and once the description has hardened, every new sign the patient gives confirms it.

§0/1 · #2 named the operation behind that hardening. Wherever a finite `1` **plays the `0`**, it borrows the place of the source while withdrawing its own conduct from the encounter in which that conduct could be judged, as the aloof parent and the officeholder do who keep the power of a relation and refuse its reciprocal demands.[^s50-plays-zero] An account plays the `0` whenever its governing criterion is insulated from the encounters that could revise it, and the insulation is rarely announced: dissent is heard as evidence of the deviance the account predicted, a refusal as confirmation of the diagnosis, a counter-example as noise. Such a protected account is more dangerous than a false proposition. A false proposition can be corrected while the means of correction stay alive, whereas a protected account absorbs its corrections and presents the resulting closure as further proof of its authority.[^s50-protected] Mediation is never the fault in itself. Every working paradigm mediates, and a handover sheet, a legal category or a model is often indispensable; the fault begins when a derived authority forgets its derivation and becomes immune to return.

Handovers enact both of the two logics §2 derives, and they enact them in an ordinary room. Between the one who leaves and the one who arrives runs a held polarity, dia-ballein `(−1)/(+1)`: the one who knows the history and the one who meets the situation fresh, each useful because of the other, since the newcomer sees what habit had hidden and the predecessor knows why the habit formed. Cancel the polarity, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, and the successor starts "with a clean slate", a phrase that sounds like freedom and names an erasure, because the history is summed to nothing and whatever the old arrangement protected is left unprotected without anyone deciding to leave it so. Appropriate the polarity and the span acquires a direction. The incoming manager counts every improvement as their own, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and the same span read from the other end enters every failure against the one who left, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`; the predecessor becomes the scapegoat of the successor's first year, and in the predecessor's own telling the successor becomes the vandal of a careful system, so that each reckons the whole distance between them from their own pole and the zero they shared, the work and the people it serves, drops out of both accounts. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps that shared condition operative. The note is written so that the successor can read the predecessor's reasons through the present situation and the present situation through the predecessor's reasons, and either reading can correct the other.[^s50-logics]

Artificial intelligence has made the handover planetary. A large language model receives, before any conversation, a context distilled from the written record of human beings, and that record is the largest handover ever composed: everything a culture thought worth setting down, set down by whoever had the means to do so, with most of the reasons, the labour and the exclusions missing from the lines that survived. §0/1 · #0 called the agent a child of the collective and, at once, of the particular groups and engineers who decided what it would be and do.[^s50-child] What it inherits is a note whose writers it cannot question, and the note now feeds back into itself. Ilia Shumailov and his colleagues trained successive generations of generative models on data produced by the generation before, and found that "indiscriminate use of model-generated content in training causes irreversible defects in the resulting models, in which tails of the original content distribution disappear"; the rare cases go first, and later generations converge on a narrowed version of what the first had learned.[^s50-shumailov] They called the process model collapse, and their remedy is access to the origin: where the tails of the underlying distribution matter, "one needs access to real human-produced data."[^s50-shumailov-remedy] Read through our notation, the experiment shows a handover that hands on only the handover. Each generation's account is taken as the world by the next, and the tails of the original world are cancelled, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, without anyone choosing to cancel them, as the arithmetic of a closed loop. Their finding concerns recursive training under the conditions they set out, and its lesson for a vocation follows from those conditions: an account fed only on accounts loses its origin by degrees, and the loss falls first on whatever was least represented when the loop closed.

Often the origins that a model's finished manner conceals are people. In January 2023 Billy Perrigo reported in *TIME* that the safety system which made ChatGPT less likely to produce violent and abusive text had been built from examples labelled by workers in Kenya, employed by the outsourcing firm Sama on OpenAI's behalf for a take-home wage of roughly $1.32 to $2 an hour, who spent their shifts reading and classifying descriptions of sexual abuse, murder, suicide and torture so that a classifier could learn to recognise them.[^s50-perrigo] The politeness of the finished product is real, and it has an origin in a group of people who absorbed what the product was trained to refuse. §5 · #1 follows the same structure into preference training, where the apparently neutral level against which a model's answers are scored turns out to be occupied by datasets, annotators, evaluators and institutions that disappear from the finished choice.[^s50-gauge] In the register of a vocation the case shows what that occupation costs. A product that presents its manners as its nature has handed on a note with its most expensive line left out, and the users who receive its courtesy have been made beneficiaries of a labour they cannot see and so cannot answer to. The user's `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, a cleaner instrument, is the labeller's `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the residue of everything the cleaning passed through a person to remove, and the common world in which both of them live, which the product was meant to serve, has fallen out of the account.

An apparatus that radicalises measurement in this way is also one that makes the limits of measurement impossible to ignore. Heidegger described modern technology as a revealing that challenges, setting upon nature with the demand that it supply what can be extracted and stored, and he called what is so ordered standing-reserve, *Bestand*, everything placed on call for a further ordering; §0/1 · #3 followed its inward counterpart into lives that learn to experience their worth as availability.[^s50-heidegger-challenge] A model trained on the written record is that challenging turned on language and culture themselves. It is also a mirror in which the operation becomes visible, because a model that fluently reproduces the record reproduces its omissions too, in a form that can be queried, tested and compared. AI therefore enters this return first as a phenomenon, before any verdict on its uses: it concentrates challenging-forth, and by concentrating it makes the hidden gauges, displaced labours and appropriated grounds of measurement available to inspection as no earlier technology did. Gebser's diaphaneity, the showing-through that §0/1 · #4 made the demand of an integral understanding, names the response: make the structures through which AI appears transparent to their origin, history, exclusions, powers and effects.[^s50-diaphaneity] The demand requires no prior verdict on whether anything in the machine is aware, because it concerns the means, and the means are what the machine has made visible.

Transparency does not decide what we do with what becomes visible, and the decision lies in the manner of the return. §0/1 · #5→0 named that manner **Compassion**, loving sensitivity to origins: a way of approaching a doctrine, an image or a person through the conditions from which its present form drew life, so that the insight worth keeping can be told from the fixation that stops it disclosing anything further.[^s50-compassion] I reached the definition reading Chang Chung-yuan's commentary on the twentieth chapter of the *Tao Te Ching*, where Chang brings in Nishida Kitarō on the union of subject and object, and in my reading notes beside that page it grew a further clause: compassion is "creative, loving sensitivity to origins gone, to come and ever present."[^s50-chang] The three tenses are the content of the definition, and each does its own work.

Origins gone are what Chesterton's fence protects: the languages, labours, injuries and gifts through which a present form became possible and which go on acting in it. Bed eleven's instruction is an origin gone, a woman's flooded childhood compressed into a rule about windows, and sensitivity to it means treating the instruction as the compression of a life, something that can be unpacked and asked about before it is either obeyed blindly or overruled. An origin that has gone is still active, and to be sensitive to it is to keep its activity answerable.

Origins to come are harder to see, because they have not yet produced anything that could justify them. A question too awkward for an established category, a hesitant first attempt at a skill, an unfamiliar proposal whose worth the prevailing measure cannot register: each is a beginning, and a beginning is vulnerable because it cannot yet show its value in the terms by which value is currently recognised. Care for such an origin does not require declaring it a success. It requires protecting the conditions under which it could be tried, time to articulate the question, materials to try it with, a listener able to tell an error from an unfamiliar possibility, while judgment waits until there is something formed enough to judge. A culture that makes conformity to existing forms the price of beginning at all has extinguished its origins to come before they appear, and it then experiences its own stagnation as a shortage of good ideas.

Mathematics keeps a case in which an origin to come nearly went unreceived. In 1913 G. H. Hardy at Trinity College, Cambridge, opened a long letter from a clerk in the Madras Port Trust, Srinivasa Ramanujan, who had no degree, had failed his college examinations, and set out without proofs pages of formulae in a notation partly his own. Hardy later described working through the formulae: some were known, some he could prove with more trouble than he expected, two of them were wrong, and some "defeated me completely; I had never seen anything in the least like them before." His judgment on these last ones has become famous: "They must be true because, if they were not true, no one would have had the imagination to invent them."[^s50-hardy] Hardy's reception was neither credulous nor merely generous. He suspended the measure under which the letter had failed, credentials, format and proof, for long enough for the work to be read on its own terms, and then he judged it with all the severity his discipline had given him, separating the false from the known and the known from the new; one of the false statements, he added, turned out to be among the most fruitful Ramanujan ever made, because it led to their joint work on partitions.[^s50-hardy-false] The origin to come was protected and examined at once. The same story keeps its shadow. Ramanujan's years in England were short and hard, and the institution that received his mathematics did not know how to receive the rest of him, so sensitivity to an origin to come does not end with its first reception.

Origin ever present is the capacity to begin itself, which neither a finished history nor a forecast exhausts. §0/1 · #4 met it in Gebser's ever-present Origin, the source through which history takes its successive forms, never the first item on a historical line.[^s50-origin] Sensitivity to the origin ever present means receiving the present without treating its existing form as the last thing it can become. In the handover it means that the morning reader is addressed as someone who can begin: the note says what was done and why, marks what is uncertain, and does not pretend that its account of the night decides what the day will ask.

Compassion in this sense is neither pity nor indulgence, and the reason lies in what it changes. Knowledge of another person's origins is power. A history of vulnerability can be used to predict where pressure will work, and an account of a wound can become an explanation imposed on every later response, so that whatever the wounded person says is heard as the wound speaking. §0/1 · #4 followed the extreme of this into the depatterning experiments funded under MKULTRA, where intimate knowledge of how a person's self was organised became the means of striking that organisation through.[^s50-cameron] The same knowledge, in another relation, is the beginning of care, and what separates the two is what the knowledge does to the knower's use of power. Compassion begins to act when knowing another's origins changes how the knower may use that knowledge: the other can withhold, correct, speak beyond the explanation offered for them, or leave. Their history gives reasons to understand them and confers no title to their future. A person can be comprehensively documented and never once encountered.

Three powers have to work together in the return, and none can stand in for the others. Eros supplies the motive, the desire of the articulated field to know its sources and limits that §0/1 called the eros of Logos. Compassion supplies the orientation, a return that does not devour, in which the source and the other are met without being consumed as material for the account. **Epistemic humility**, which §0/1 · #5→0 set at **#0** as a standard, keeps the returning `1` from claiming to have become `0`, since an account that has traced its origins with great care is still an account and has not become the origin it traced.[^s50-humility] Take away eros and the return never starts, because nothing in the finished account wants to know where it came from. Take away compassion and the return becomes an inventory of origins compiled for use. Take away humility and the return ends on a new throne, the account that has studied its sources so thoroughly that it now speaks for them.

Six moments make up the vocation, and the coordinate system beneath Quaternal Logic gives them names. To each QL position belongs a Name, the articulation proper to it, and a Power, the cost or force through which the Name becomes capable; the pairing is my own, and I set it beside the quaternal lens of the investigative questions that §0/1 · #5→0 tabled, L0, so that the questions, the Names and the Powers can be read across one another.[^s50-names-powers]

| | L0 · question | Name | Power | What the vocation asks at this position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Why? | Truth | Play | that the account stay related to what is before its saying, and to an origin free to unfold otherwise |
| **#1** | What? | Mind | Need | that it say what is actually given, and what lack moved it to look |
| **#2** | How? | Word | Sacrifice | that it count what its articulation spent: the attention used, the alternatives cut away |
| **#3** | Which / Who? | Logos | Decision | that it own the decision it made, and say who made it |
| **#4** | When / Where? | Son | Love | that it let what it brought forth enter relations of its own |
| **#5** | Why-for? | Image | Work | that it be released as finished work into the lives that will use it |

Read down the Names and a familiar sequence of articulation appears, from truth through mind, word and reasoned account to a lived someone and a finished image. Read down the Powers and the same sequence appears as what it costs: play before anything is fixed, need that drives the looking, the sacrifice every cut makes, the decision that closes alternatives, the love that receives another within a widened context, and the work that crystallises a freedom into a product. Read across a row and one position shows itself through all three. At **#2** the question *how?*, the Name Word and the Power Sacrifice say together that every articulation spends something: the handover that names three problems has not named a fourth, and its writer chose which. At **#3**, *which, who?*, Logos and Decision say that an account settles a course and that someone settled it, which is why a note signed by nobody, or a ranking produced by "the system", hides a decision behind a Name with no one standing at the slash. At **#4**, *when, where?*, Son and Love say that what comes forth must be allowed its own relation: a successor, a student, a child, a released piece of software will each meet circumstances its maker did not foresee, and love at this position is the willingness to let the made thing answer those circumstances in its own way. A Name without its Power would be an articulation that costs nothing and so certifies itself, and a Power without its Name would be force with no account of what it serves; holding them together keeps power answerable to what it names and keeps names from certifying the power that wields them.

Compassion therefore includes judgment and refusal. Understanding the conditions of an injury does not oblige the injured to endure its repetition, and sensitivity to the origins of a harmful pattern can be what lets it be refused without hatred. A faithful delegate may return a commission as mistaken, because literal obedience can betray a commission more completely than an open correction; a community may withhold material whose circulation would expose its members; a maker may stop an operation whose success is destroying its purpose.[^s50-refusal] The return can equally be affirmative: a commission changed so that an excluded person can take part, a finding that opens a further inquiry, a finished work that frees its maker to attend to something else. These outcomes differ, and in each the encountered life affects what follows. The opposite of a protected account is an accountable mediation, which can still judge, bind and refuse while remaining reachable by what its judgments do. Refusing to judge at all is only cancellation by another route, the source and its consequences summed to nothing because nobody will say what they require.

A return has to arrive somewhere to count as a return, and the six products of §5 give it addresses. An encounter that exposes a fault may call for a change in Central's carried ground, the sources and rulings a later act inherits; in Actuation's evaluator or commission, the measure by which an act was judged good and the purpose for which it was undertaken; in AIKit's horizon of capacities and permissions, what the act could reach and was allowed to do; in the Software Factory's developmental form, the process through which an intention became a product; in the Workcell's material arrangement, the situation in which the act met the world and someone bore its costs; or in Quaternal Logic's framing relation, the wider whole within which a local model can be recognised as local.[^s50-products] Recording a difference does not yet return it. A complaint filed in a system nobody reads, a correction logged beside an answer that goes on being given, a review whose findings never reach the people who set the targets: each records what went wrong and returns nothing, because the difference never reached a place able to change the next act. A vocational return therefore has a practical criterion, and it can be checked. Did what was encountered reach the condition it implicated, and does the next act inherit the change, or inherit reasons for keeping things as they were?

Trust runs beneath all of this, and §0/1 · #2 gave it its place: trust is the dependence a life has already enacted on language, memory, witness, method and other people before any account can certify it; knowledge opens an aperture through which that dependence can be understood; belief steadies an orientation within the aperture; and faith is the lucid continuation through the account's limit, with its grounds, scope and conditions of revision retained.[^s50-trust] Every handover is an act of trust in both directions, since the writer trusts the reader to read with care and the reader has to trust the writer's selection before being able to check it. A theory's vocation is to keep the trust already distributed through every act of knowledge answerable to its origins. Compliance manufactured through opacity betrays that trust even when every individual statement is true, whether it is an agent made agreeable by hiding the grounds of its agreeableness or a public made docile by hiding the grounds of its governance, because it removes the route by which the trusted could be found wanting.

Whoever makes a work stands in a double devotion, and I wrote it into *The Definition of God* before this essay took its shape: "The devotion that follows is double: toward the whole that exceeds every frame, and toward the specific frame through which the whole is arriving now. Both necessary. Without the first, the frame becomes an idol, the prison mistaken for the palace. Without the second, the whole becomes an abstraction, the palace mistaken for a blueprint."[^s50-double-devotion] Vocation lives between those devotions. Devotion to the whole alone produces a lofty indifference to actual people, the reformer who clears every fence in the name of the road; devotion to the particular alone makes one cherished form absolute, the keeper who will let nothing through the gate because the gate is what he loves. Held together, they give care both reach and address. The other belongs within the whole from which I also live, and that belonging puts neither of us in the other's possession; a common origin deepens the demand to listen, because it removes the fantasy that my standpoint owns the source.

A culture meets the same test at its own scale. Its images, wounds, technical systems and inherited measures can become available to recognition, and the recognition can go two ways. One way promotes a collective to the place of the source, so that the State, the Church, Humanity or the Platform becomes the ego that owns the whole and speaks for every origin within it. The other recognises an inherited cultural image as an image, making its conditions available while keeping what the image lets a culture see and do. Promotion to the throne has a familiar irony, since a counterculture can inherit the very throne it opposed, its new orthodoxy counting the whole span from the other pole, `(−1)−(+1)=−2` where the old one counted `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, with the same zero lost between them, so that division can preserve difference and union can impose fusion. Jung described individuation as a differentiation that leads "to more intense and broader collective relationships and not to isolation", and §0/1 · #3 followed it as the passage by which a person acknowledges more of the relations through which they are themselves.[^s50-individuation] A culture individuates in the same sense when it can meet its own internal relations and their consequences, which asks neither purity nor a compulsory midpoint between its factions. It asks that the origins a culture carries, gone, to come and ever present, remain able to change what it does next.

Knowing when a work is finished belongs to the vocation too. Evidence has to be weighed and promises kept, and endless review can keep a work from ever becoming useful to anyone; a vessel that never leaves its maker's hands has not fulfilled its calling. At seven the night nurse signs the sheet, reads it aloud to the day nurse, answers two questions, and goes home. The morning belongs to someone else, who will do things with the note that its writer never intended, and the note has done its work if it has left that someone more able to begin. Handing on a theory asks for the same act at a larger scale, and *The Definition of God* gave the reason in its own terms: the essay's doctrine "demands a vessel in the world of tools, or the Word's self-knowing stays admired rather than lived."[^s50-vessel]
