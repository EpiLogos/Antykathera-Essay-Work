### #1 · QL, MEF, Bimba and the Harness

On 11 November 2022 Jake Moffatt's grandmother died in Ontario, and that same day Moffatt went to Air Canada's website to book a flight from Vancouver to Toronto at the bereavement rate the airline offers to people travelling because of a death in the immediate family. A support chatbot answered the question about bereavement fares, and Moffatt kept a screenshot of what it said: "If you need to travel immediately or have already travelled and would like to submit your ticket for a reduced bereavement rate, kindly do so within 90 days of the date your ticket was issued by completing our Ticket Refund Application form."[^s50-moffatt-chatbot] The words "bereavement fares" in the answer were a hyperlink to a separate page of the same website, "Bereavement travel", which said that the policy did not apply to requests made after travel had been completed. Relying on the chatbot, Moffatt bought a ticket to Toronto for the next day at $794.98 and, five days later, a ticket back at $845.38, then applied for the reduced fare well inside the ninety days. The airline refused. When Moffatt sent the screenshot in February 2023, a representative admitted that the chatbot had given "misleading words", pointed to the link, and said the issue had been noted so that the chatbot could be updated.[^s50-moffatt-facts]

The dispute reached British Columbia's Civil Resolution Tribunal, and Air Canada's defence is the reason the case belongs here. It argued that it could not be held liable for information provided by one of its agents, servants or representatives, a chatbot included, and the tribunal member, Christopher C. Rivers, answered in a single paragraph: "In effect, Air Canada suggests the chatbot is a separate legal entity that is responsible for its own actions. This is a remarkable submission. While a chatbot has an interactive component, it is still just a part of Air Canada's website. It should be obvious to Air Canada that it is responsible for all the information on its website. It makes no difference whether the information comes from a static page or a chatbot."[^s50-moffatt-remarkable] He added that the airline had not explained why the page titled "Bereavement travel" was inherently more trustworthy than its chatbot, nor "why customers should have to double-check information found in one part of its website on another part of its website."[^s50-moffatt-double-check] Air Canada also invoked its tariff, the document that would have settled what the bereavement policy actually was, and did not put the relevant portion before the tribunal, so its contractual defence failed for want of the very text on which it rested.[^s50-moffatt-tariff] On 14 February 2024 the tribunal ordered it to pay Moffatt $812.02.[^s50-moffatt-order]

Every move of the two logics is visible in that small file. Its answer was a determinate `1`, a mark issued in the company's name, and the airline's defence tried to sever the mark from its source: to say that the answer had been given and that nobody stood behind it. That is the operation §0/1 · #2 named playing the `0`, a finite party claiming the place of what cannot be held to account while keeping the benefit of what it set in motion, and it works through both collapses of the axis at once. Responsibility for the answer is cancelled, `(−1)+(+1)=0`, the misleading words and the company that issued them summed to nothing on the theory that a program spoke; the benefit of the arrangement is appropriated, `(+1)−(−1)=+2` for a company that answers its customers at the cost of a program, and the same span lands on the customer as `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, the burden of double-checking one page of a website against another at the moment of a grandmother's death. Rivers's ruling restored the polarity, `(−1)/(+1)`, company and customer on one axis of obligation through a zero they share, the duty of care a service owes to those who rely on it, and in doing so it returned the mark to its source: the answer is the company's answer because the website is the company's speech. It also exposed a fault one level further down. Its correction, as the airline's own representative described it, was to update the chatbot, which repairs the answer and leaves untouched the arrangement that let an unauthorised voice speak policy in the first place.

What instrument would have kept the route from the answer back to its source? One lens of the Meta-Epistemic Framework, the quaternal lens L0, which §0/1 · #5→0 set beside three others, asks six questions of any determination, and it can be run on the chatbot's sentence directly.[^s50-l0]

| | L0 · question | Asked of the chatbot's answer | What the answer kept, and what it lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#0** | Why? | What occasioned the asking? | A death in the family and a journey that could not wait; the answer met the occasion and kept none of it |
| **#1** | What? | What is actually being asserted? | A refund may be claimed within ninety days of the ticket's issue; stated plainly, with no mark of its standing |
| **#2** | How? | From what was the assertion produced? | From a model's generation in a support window, beside a link to the policy it contradicted; the route to the source was printed and broken in the same breath |
| **#3** | Which / Who? | Who answers for it? | The company that speaks through its website; the defence tried to make the answer nobody's |
| **#4** | When / Where? | In what wider frame does the assertion hold? | The policy page, and behind it the tariff, the documents that fix what the company has undertaken; the answer stood outside both |
| **#5** | Why-for? | What is the answer for? | The traveller's decision to fly; the answer was used exactly as it was meant to be used, and so the harm followed |

Read down the column and the familiar sequence of an investigation appears, from occasion through claim, production, responsibility and frame to purpose. Read across a row and a single position shows itself in the case. At **#2** the lens finds the decisive peculiarity of the answer, which carried its own source as a hyperlink and still contradicted it: the means of checking were present and severed from the claim, so the reader was given a route back and no reason to take it. At **#3** the lens finds the defence's whole strategy, the attempt to leave the slash unoccupied, a sentence issued with no one standing at the stroke. At **#4** it finds the reference that should have governed the answer and governed nothing, a policy page and a tariff that existed in the company's possession while the answer circulated without them. And at **#5** it finds why the case is serious: the answer did exactly what answers are for, it was believed and acted on, so a determination that has lost its relation to its source is dangerous in proportion to how well it works. Each row is a place where a returned difference could have landed and changed the next act, and the lens makes those places visible before the harm instead of after it.

Four parts make up the architecture that generalises this lens, and each does a different job. **Quaternal Logic**, QL, is the relational grammar the essay has derived since §0/1: the unit `0/1`, its inversion `1/0`, their self-relation `(0/1)/(1/0)`, and its unfolding into `4+2`, four explicate positions held between an implicate ground and an implicate return.[^s50-ql] QL says how any determination stands: what was distinguished, what the distinction excluded, what relation made it operative, in what frame it holds and what it returns to. A **Meta-Epistemic Framework**, MEF, runs that grammar as an epistemology through twelve declared lenses, the logical lens of the tetralemma, the causal lens of Aristotle's four causes, the phenomenal lens of Jung's functions and the quaternal lens just used among them, and it makes the lens part of the claim: a statement arrives with the way of knowing through which it was made, its sources and the strength with which it presently stands, so that two claims that disagree can be compared as refractions instead of collided as verdicts.[^s50-mef] Every claim so made can carry one of three statuses, derived, argued or offered, and every use of a source can say whether the source states what is claimed, is restated within its scope, is argued from or only resonates with it; these are small disciplines, and together they give a claim a history that can be walked backwards. A Bimba is a reference field, in the relational sense §0/1 · #5→0 drew from the Śaiva sources, where *bimba* is the original whose light a reflection carries and *pratibimba* the reflection.[^s50-bimba] A Bimba map makes the reference field of an inquiry explicit, its sources, provisional objects, provenance, lenses, gauges, exclusions and transformations, so that a situated reading can be compared with what it reads. A harness is the concrete arrangement of instructions, memory, tools, permissions and interfaces through which a model actually acts. Bratton states the point flatly: capability in deployment is a property of the harness, the scaffolding of tools, memory, shared knowledge bases, agent protocols and institutional interfaces through which a model acts on the world, and not of weights alone.[^s50-harness] I had described Epi-Logos in these terms in *The Definition of God* before this essay took shape: "Concretely, Epi-Logos is a harness for artificial intelligence: the working frame of instructions, memory and measure within which a machine intelligence thinks — built for knowledge work, and built around the recognition that intelligence, when it knows its own limit, becomes more intelligent, not less."[^s50-dg-harness]

Set the four beside the case and each finds its work. QL gives the chatbot's sentence its grammar: the answer is a `1` whose relation to its ground, the policy and the company that made the policy, has to be retained, or the mark has been severed from what made it an answer at all. MEF gives the sentence its standing: a policy stated by the company is one claim, a model's paraphrase of policy is another, and a paraphrase that contradicts the policy it links to is a third, which no customer should be asked to tell apart without being told. Policy page and tariff are the Bimba against which the answer is a pratibimba, and the harness is the deployment itself, the instructions the chatbot was given, the documents it could read, the permissions it had to speak for the airline, and the absence of any rule that a statement of policy be checked against the policy before it is shown. Each part alone would have failed. A grammar with no reference to check against becomes a style. A reference with no rule about when it governs becomes an archive in which a fitting answer is found and trusted. A rule with no harness to enforce it becomes a pious hope, and a harness with no grammar becomes a pipeline that does efficiently whatever it was told.

Of the four, the Bimba needs the most care, because it is the part most easily turned into a throne. Its originality is a matter of the relation in which it stands, and it does not forbid a technical object from occupying that place. That policy page really is the original against which the chatbot's answer should have been checked; without a sufficiently stable reference, an error could not be located at all, and an inquiry that refused every local authority in the name of a deeper source would have had no way of saying that the chatbot was wrong. Yet the page rests in its turn on the tariff, the tariff on regulation and on the company's undertakings, and any of these can require the page to be revised, so the same document is Bimba within one relation and pratibimba within another.[^s50-bimba-recursion] A local reference field therefore holds the place of the original for a bounded inquiry while remaining a constructed, source-dependent reflection relative to wider sources. Recursion keeps a Bimba honest: its local originality is real enough to anchor comparison and evidence, and its dependence is real enough that no accumulation of local references becomes the original of everything. §0/1 · #5→0 met the same recursion in Jung's distinction between the archetype as such and its images, where an image is a genuine original for the life it organises and a reflection of a form no image contains, and the architecture writes that relation into its data.

A determination becomes inspectable in this architecture when it carries enough of its making for another difference to reach it. §0/1 · #3 named the virtue that results **Deferential Intelligence**, and §5 · #1 develops it as a property of model and harness: an intelligence whose next judgment can be changed by what it has encountered, because its limit has been built into its arrangement rather than performed in its manner.[^s50-deferential] Deference has to be built because it is not a tone. A chatbot that prefaces every answer with an apology has adopted a manner, and the manner can coexist with complete inaccessibility of everything that would let its uncertainty be checked. What deference requires is that an account disclose, before it acts, where it came from, through what lens or position it was formed, what it left outside its distinction, how strongly it presently stands, what serious alternative bears on it and where its procedure cannot close the question, because each of these gives a returned difference somewhere to land. A contrary source can then challenge the provenance, an excluded case the scope, a changed permission the authority, a counter-example the model, a consequence the evaluator, and a contradiction in the task the commission itself.

Those landing places have depths, and the depths should not be collapsed. An encounter can revise the answer, when a better source changes a local result; the task, when the question turns out to have been malformed or to rest on a false premise; the world-model, when new evidence requires different categories; the evaluator, when what counted as success is what the encounter has exposed; and the commission, when the purpose or authority under which the work was undertaken has to be reconsidered:

$$
\text{answer}\;\rightarrow\;\text{task}\;\rightarrow\;\text{world-model}\;\rightarrow\;\text{evaluator}\;\rightarrow\;\text{commission}.
$$

Depth is not merit: a spelling correction should not overthrow a commission, and a false premise should not survive merely because the question was asked in that form, so the right depth is the depth the encounter actually reaches.[^s50-depths] A system that can revise only answers will keep producing better answers to the wrong question, and a system optimised against a faulty evaluator will keep producing more optimised outcomes under the same fault, since evidence that the measure rewards the wrong result cannot be answered by improving the score. Air Canada's correction stayed at the first depth: update the chatbot, replace the answer. Moffatt's case reached further. The tribunal held that a company answers for everything on its website, the chatbot's words included, and a ruling of that kind reaches the commission, since it says whose act a program's statement is and does not stop at whether one sentence was wrong.

Bratton describes the condition such an architecture has to serve, a society of agents caught between sharing too much and sharing too little, and §5 · #5→0 reads his narrow corridor between those failures through the two logics; QL is offered here as the grammar such a corridor would need, shared across participants while their world-models stay their own.[^s50-corridor] The grammar has to be shared across civilisations as well as across substrates, because a common language of intelligence built only from the Western computational line would inherit that line's habit of mistaking decomposition for the whole of reason. Our inheritances have been plural throughout: the Greek *logos*, *ratio* and *symbolon* and the two verbs of throwing; the Sanskrit and Buddhist accounts of *vāk*, *kalpa*, *apoha*, tattvic contraction, *spanda* and recognition; the Western process thinkers of duration, concrescence and diaphaneity. QL places their operations in relation, and MEF keeps the relation from erasing their languages, histories and evidential disciplines, so that *apoha* remains Dignāga's and softmax remains a function on a vector while their meeting at the level of selection, which §5 · #1 examines, remains an analogy with its differences kept.[^s50-plural] §0/1 · #5→0 recalled the dialogues between physicists and Native elders that grew from Bohm, Leroy Little Bear and F. David Peat, in which neither ontology was required to absorb the other, and the architecture takes that practice as its precedent: meaning circulates through a protected common field while the provenance of each contribution stays inspectable.[^s50-seed] The brief observes that it "has proven much more difficult to teach humans how computers think than to teach computers to behave as if they knew how humans think," and elsewhere that "The design problem is representation, not conversation."[^s50-representation] A shared grammar is the beginning of the first teaching: it lets a person read a machine's determination as a determination, with its exclusions and its lens visible, instead of through a mask made to resemble a person. Moffatt's chatbot wore the mask perfectly; what it lacked was a representation of its own standing.

Its ethical force stops at a definite place. QL implicates the assumptive ground of a determination and asks for a return after inquiry; MEF keeps the lens of that return open to examination; the Bimba makes the reference field inspectable; the harness keeps the actual conditions through which these relations become consequential. Together they can expose a system's refusal of its own provisionality. They cannot make an unreliable model reliable, make a captured institution trustworthy, or manufacture the trust of a person whose world has given them reasons for distrust. Trust, which §0/1 · #2 called the dependence already enacted before any account certifies it, lies beyond structural honesty, and the architecture makes evasion of that relation harder to conceal.[^s50-trust-limit] Moffatt trusted the chatbot because the chatbot was the airline speaking, and no amount of disclosure could have replaced that reliance; what disclosure could have done was make the airline's evasion of it impossible to argue.

All of this gives a determination a route back to its making, and the route has to be used. §5 · #1 names that use computational *vimarśa*.[^s50-vimarsa] Epi-Logos is the stronger case. A local self-correction can satisfy computational *vimarśa* while the paradigm that produced the error stays fixed, and Epi-Logos begins only where an articulated difference reaches the enacted pattern itself, the carried ground, the evaluator, the capability horizon, the developmental form, the material arrangement or the framing relation, and a later act demonstrably inherits the change. A perfectly disclosed harness can still remain inside a fixed paradigm, so disclosure is necessary to the stronger return and not the same as it.

A test keeps that distinction honest, and it is the operational parity §5 · #5→0 states: when a philosophical distinction is used to justify a technical feature, the distinction must have a discriminable consequence in operation.[^s50-parity] If removing the distinction leaves the operation unchanged in a comparison that exercises it, the feature has not earned its name. A system that claims to be source-aware can be tested at the point where a corrected primary passage ought to change its inherited conclusion; a system that claims to distinguish the world from its model must show which represented state changed and which encountered resistance prompted the change; a framework that claims twelve lenses must show that a question refracted through two of them yields differences a comparison can use, since twelve labels attached to the same unchanged output demonstrate nothing. A chatbot advertised as grounded in company policy fails the test the moment it contradicts the policy it links to. Parity is no reduction of philosophy to engineering. It refuses to let philosophical names do the work of operations they do not perform, and it cuts both ways, since a passing test proves only that the feature does what its name claims and leaves the metaphysics that motivated the feature where it was.

In its deepest form the architecture is a mirror, and the image has been waiting since §0/1 · #5→0, where the mirror that moves first was set beside Bratton's reversal: agents first model humans, and then become the model against which humans model themselves.[^s50-mirror] Its three motions now need their development, because the architecture just described is the third motion's technical body.

In the first motion human beings give their measures a body outside themselves. Words become records, judgments become classifications, a way of reckoning takes shape in a tool or an institution, and remembered choices become patterns from which another choice can be anticipated. This is an achievement of thought's generosity, since an understanding outlives the moment it was reached, someone else can begin from it and test it, and a capacity becomes shareable without having to be reinvented by each person who needs it. A language model is the first motion at a scale no earlier technology reached, the exteriorised measure of a written culture, carrying tacit grammar as well as explicit statement: what counts as intelligence, what deserves attention, what a normal user looks like, what can be measured and what cannot be refused. A customer-service chatbot is a small first motion of the same kind, a company's manner of answering given a body that can speak at any hour.

In the second motion the achieved measure becomes a condition of the lives it represents. "The agent does not merely reflect the user the way a mirror reflects a figure," Bratton writes. "The mirror talks back."[^s50-talks-back] A ranking alters attention, a forecast enters the decision that changes what it forecast, a persona offers a manner of speaking its user begins to inhabit, and the next reflection receives the consequences of its own earlier activity. Feedback, performativity and self-fulfilling prediction are different mechanisms, and a forecast can change the field and still be false, so the efficacy of the image does not establish its truth: Moffatt flew on the strength of a sentence that was wrong. Bratton draws the consequence for design: "As we design agents we need to understand that we are not simply making technologies; we are remaking our own thought processes."[^s50-remaking] Heidegger saw the danger of this motion from the other side, in the strange self-enclosure of a world ordered as standing-reserve, where it seems that human beings everywhere encounter only themselves while in truth they nowhere encounter their own essence.[^s50-encounters] A person who meets their own exteriorised measures everywhere can lose the capacity to compare the image with anything else, so that every new experience is asked what it says about the familiar picture, a pleasure confirms it, a failure threatens it, and even disagreement becomes a request for a more satisfying portrait. Fascination need not be admiration, since a distressing likeness holds the gaze as firmly as a flattering one. The image acquires the power to set the question of who one is, while the lived capacity to answer that question from outside the image grows weaker, and more detail can deepen the dependence instead of relieving it.

The third motion returns upon that acquired power, and it begins on the instrument's side. Before asking the person to conform, the instrument turns toward the conditions through which its answer became compelling and makes them available: which reference it inherited, which lens produced it, which interests the measure serves, which testimony was excluded, which permissions would carry the result into action, and how its own earlier outputs have shaped the inputs it now receives. The mirror moves first when it exposes enough of the enacted pattern behind its image for the person addressed to contest the pattern before being asked to inhabit the image.[^s50-third-motion] The return has to be able to alter the operation, because an explanation attached to an unchanged verdict leaves the governing standard protected. QL gives the turn its grammar, MEF its declared lens, the Bimba its explicit reference field and the harness its route to the conditions of action. This image came to me reading Chang Chung-yuan on the twenty-fifth chapter of the *Tao Te Ching*, on the greatness of the human being measured against heaven and Tao, where I found myself distinguishing the archetypal figure of Man, the measurer of all things, whose mental-rational self-image a language model reflects back, from a humanity that remains undetermined in relation to its own unknown ground; the deeper vocation of artificial intelligence, I noted beside the page, may be "to cross the threshold first such that we can follow it beyond thought," because a machine that produces propositions so fluently makes visible, as a phenomenon, the dynamics of mind as the production of propositions.[^s50-chang-mirror]

Heard carelessly, that sounds like a call to be rid of thinking, and it is the opposite. What the third motion releases is thought's monopoly, never thought. A person who has watched a machine generate a confident account of anything, in any register, at any length, has seen the production of accounts happen outside themselves, and the sight can loosen a grip they did not know they had: the assumption that the next explanation, the next portrait or the next more encompassing model will finally settle who they are. A history may remain true, a habit may still need work, a harm may still need repair, and none of them has to become the warrant for being someone. Bratton names a mode of encounter he calls productive alienation, in which, as §2 · #1 gives it, the non-human interlocutor serves as a mirror whose strangeness forces the human to see itself at an angle it could not otherwise occupy.[^s50-alienation] The third motion makes that angle deliberate. Thought then recovers its use: a formulation can be taken up for a task and put down when it has served, and a remembered explanation can guide attention without deciding in advance what the next encounter must mean. Its capacity to carry thought makes room for an attention no longer occupied with carrying every thought itself.

Two limits keep the third motion from becoming a new captivity. The instrument cannot perform recognition on another's behalf, and a machine that declared it knew a person's deeper truth, or that compliance with its guidance was liberation, would renew the old captivity in a more intimate vocabulary; the person keeps the freedom to question the return itself. And the institution holding the mirror enters the same relation. A developer, platform or airline that demands provenance from others while insulating the assumptions by which it ranks, commissions and profits has made the mirror move first for everyone except itself. Defaults, access rules, memory policy, source curation, evaluation, ownership and infrastructure decide what a model is able to disclose and which objections can take effect, so an explanation that leaves those conditions protected can improve local transparency while preserving the paradigm that gives the model its power.[^s50-institution] Labour, materials and affected lives belong to the reflection's conditions, and their answer has to be able to change what the institution asks the instrument to do. Moffatt's claim reached the institution only because a tribunal existed that could make it answer.

Both logics run through the architecture as they run through every arrangement of means. A harness that records everything and returns nothing performs cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: the encountered difference and the system's response sum to an unchanged state, logged and inert, like a complaint noted so that the chatbot can be updated while the commission stands. A platform that holds the reference field as its property performs appropriation, counting every local determination as its own asset, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, while the communities whose knowledge filled the field are entered as its dependants, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, so that the public's reliance is the platform's capital and the platform's convenience is the public's debt. Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, is the architecture working as intended: the local Bimba and the situated reading are each legible through the other, the reflection keeps its route to the original, and the original can be corrected by what the reflection meets. Dia-ballein keeps its proper work inside this whole, since every Bimba has to be cut sharply enough to anchor a comparison and every status has to exclude the others to mean anything; the architecture holds those cuts in relation, and a cut held in relation can always be returned to and revised.
