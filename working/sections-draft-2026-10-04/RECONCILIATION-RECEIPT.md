## A2. Conventions (all seven sections)

1. **Names of sections and movements.** "§0/1" for the threshold section in every section; "the threshold" only as a gloss after "§0/1" has appeared. Other sections and movements are "§N" and "§N · #n" (§0/1's movements are "§0/1 · #n"). No ordinals ("the second section", "our first section", "the previous movement" when it means anything other than the immediately preceding movement of the same section, verified).
2. **Section heading** `## §N — Title`; titles from the plan's station names: §0 — Differentiating Mind; §1 — The Return of Zero; §2 — Two Logics of Two; §3 — Mathematical Substrate; §4 — Psychoid Flowering; §5 — Objective Internality: World and Life in Six Products; §5→0 — Epi-Logos and 4:2 Technē.
3. **Movement heading** `<a id="Mnn"></a>` then `### #n · Title` (§0/1's form). §5's headings keep the form `### #n · Product — Office`. §5→0 · #5→0 takes the plan's title, "Planetary Intelligence Returns to Its Ground".
4. **Notes**: Chicago 18 endnotes in §0/1's clean reader-facing form, IDs `[^<prefix>-<slug>]` (PROSE-STANDARD's form), slug lowercase ASCII with hyphens, unique in the section. Prefixes: §0 `s0`, §1 `s1`, §2 `s2`, §3 `s3`, §4 `s4`, §5 `s5`, §5→0 `s50`. None collides with §0/1's numbered notes. Every marker defined, every definition used.
5. **Movement file shape** (checked by `assemble_section.py`): frontmatter; anchor; `### #n · Title`; body; `## Notes`; `## Sources` (a bibliography list per movement in Chicago bibliography form, as §0/1 has). `SECTION.md` is assembled from the six files by the script, with Notes and Sources grouped by movement.
6. **Status marks**: §0/1's practice. Status is carried in the wording, never as bold or inline badges in body prose: a derivation is shown and then used; an argued reading is stated as the author's ("I argue", "the reading is mine") once, at the audit boundary, and then held at full strength; a genuinely offered conjecture, design or research vector is said to be offered ("I offer this as a conjecture…").
