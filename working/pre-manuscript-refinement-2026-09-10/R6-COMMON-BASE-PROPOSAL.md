## Claim-strength law carried into the base

**The essay is allowed to claim what it argues.**

- A source boundary limits attribution.
- A proof boundary limits formal consequence.
- An implementation boundary limits realised or observed technical achievement.
- None of those boundaries automatically weakens the essay's own Argued metaphysical, mathematical, mythemic, ethical or technological synthesis.
- `Argued` is a positive warrant.
- `Offered` is reserved for genuinely unsettled proposals, designs, research hypotheses or implementation claims.
- Mytheme is truth-bearing in its own register and can prove/disclose a relation through the action of the whole image; that mythemic proof does not substitute for formal mathematical entailment or historical attribution.
- Review must identify underclaim as rigorously as overclaim. Generic caution, imagined developer preference and phantom skeptical readers are not grounds for weakening canon.
