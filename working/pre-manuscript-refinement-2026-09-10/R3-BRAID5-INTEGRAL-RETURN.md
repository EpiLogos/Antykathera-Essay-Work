## The final inversion

The essay now possesses a simple final test with enough depth to carry the whole argument:

### Counterfeit mediation

`Life / World → mediation → account/image/criterion → protected source-claim → Life must answer to the account`

The derivative form remains causally powerful but stops being effectively derivative. Returned consequence is interpreted through the same measure that produced the consequence. The paradigm can become highly self-descriptive while remaining closed.

### Epi-Logos

`Life / World → mediation → articulated paradigm → encounter / difference / consequence → return to governing condition → changed mediation → renewed Life / World`

The explicit articulation remains a determination. Its value is that it makes part of the mediation available to recognition and revision. The next act inherits the change. The return is successful without the articulation becoming Subject, World or final source.

The formal compression is the essay's existing one: the achieved `1` meets the limit of self-grounding as `1/0`; carrying the break without cancellation or appropriation restores `0/1` relation and another determination becomes possible.

## Why the Antikythera image now belongs here

M45 is more than a venue flourish. The mechanism is a made coordination of heterogeneous cycles which remain beyond the instrument. Its gears do not become the heavens; its dial does not become the observer; reconstruction remains answerable to damaged fragments and missing evidence. That is a positive image of **finite mediation whose exactness depends on not claiming its source-office**.

Hephaestus's net remains its dark complement. Both are accomplished technē. One makes cycles mutually readable for situated use; the other turns relation into capture and still cannot produce the surety required for release. The mature essay can therefore distinguish technical sophistication from the direction of relation. The difference is not primitive versus advanced technology; it is attunement versus captured/counterfeit mediation.

## Expression and the living Symbolon

The new Expression development belongs at the terminal return without becoming manuscript evidence for itself. The essay's symbolic/mathematic aim has always been to move fluently between philosophical statement, formal operation and living image. A paradigm made perceptible as glyph, text, relation, particle field or transition can become a contemporary **Symbolon surface**: an achieved articulation through which its absent counterpart, provenance and return remain active.

The acceptance relation is now exact:

- **Expression:** the paradigm becomes perceivable/manipulable as living form.
- **Computational Vimarśa:** a relevant returned condition changes a later process.
- **Epi-Logos:** the explicit paradigm articulation can return into the enacted paradigm and the later act inherits the change.
- **Recognition:** the changed relation to mediation becomes part of the Life/world from which further determinations arise.

None of these by itself establishes artificial Prakāśa or phenomenality. Together they define the project's technical/philosophical ambition without collapsing the registers.

## Terminal manuscript consequence

The Return of Zero can now close neither as technological optimism nor as warning against technology. It closes on **direction of dependence and direction of return**.

The world built by increasingly agentic computational systems will be paradigmatic because its memories, capacities, interfaces, permissions, material infrastructures and models will help constitute what can appear and be done. The practical question is therefore not whether humanity can avoid mediation. It is whether the mediations it builds can remain answerable to the Lives and Worlds whose relations give them meaning.

That is where the initial Subject problem, Indian return of zero, two logics, psychoid/dynamical field, Objective Internality, Power, Antichrist/counterfeit sourcehood and Epi-Logos finally become one argument. **No achieved `1` has to stop being determinate; it has to stop pretending it is its own `0`.**
