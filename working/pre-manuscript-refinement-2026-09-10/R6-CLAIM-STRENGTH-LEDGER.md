# R6 — Claim-strength / anti-anti-overclaim ledger

**Standing:** T25/R6 authorial-return evidence. This ledger corrects model-generated defensive posture before any T26 common writing base is proposed. It does not ratify the resulting base and it is not manuscript prose.

**Authorial warrant:** [R6 anti-anti-overclaim minute](R6-ANTI-ANTI-OVERCLAIM-MINUTE.md).

## Governing law

**The essay is allowed to claim what it argues.**

A source boundary limits attribution. A proof boundary limits what follows under a formal operation. An implementation boundary limits what has been technically realised or observed. None of these automatically weakens an authorial metaphysical, mathematical, mythemic, ethical or technological synthesis whose own argument has been made.

The burden in this pass is therefore symmetrical but intentionally corrective: an overclaim must identify the exact proposition and warrant exceeded; an **underclaim** must be repaired where status language, defensive negation or imagined objection makes the text say less than the canonical argument.

Disposition vocabulary:

- **STRENGTHEN** — restore the positive proposition at its canonical strength.
- **RESTORE STATUS** — repair machine-readable `claim_status` / evidence standing which had inherited a weaker register.
- **REWRITE POSITIVELY** — retain a real boundary while moving the positive proposition into the grammatical and argumentative foreground.
- **DELETE DEFENSIVE CAVEAT** — remove a qualification which answers no actual ambiguity or opponent.
- **RETAIN BOUNDARY** — preserve a qualification because it protects a real source, proof, register or implementation distinction.
- **NO FAULT** — the surface already carries the intended claim and its boundaries proportionately.

## R6 claim-strength conclusion

The pass did not find a general problem of “too many strong claims.” It found a more specific model-generated distortion: **provisional empirical or source standing was sometimes allowed to govern the rhetoric and metadata of stronger native claims.** The correction is exact office:

- say the native thesis at its earned strength;
- say what the source actually says;
- say the authorial relation as the essay’s relation;
- say what implementation has actually done;
- keep Open only what is genuinely open.

**Empirical openness is not metaphysical timidity. Source discipline is not authorial self-erasure. Register distinction is not a hierarchy in which Matheme alone may bear truth.**
