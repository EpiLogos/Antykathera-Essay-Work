## What this braid establishes for manuscript preparation

The essay can now say the relation across registers without changing subjects midstream. A mathematical break, a psychic fact, a second-person interruption, a material consequence and a technical error are not one kind of evidence. They can nevertheless participate in one **returning operation** when the difference they disclose reaches the means by which the next determination will be made.

This is the product field's ethical hinge: **recording is not return**. A system can be perfectly observant and remain extractive if the observed difference cannot alter what commissions, permits, judges, forms or materially realises the next act. Conversely, return does not mean abolishing determination. Dia's discrimination is needed for the difference to become clear enough to matter; Syn is the retention of that difference and its ground so it can become generative rather than cancelled or appropriated.

The Indian/Gebser relation supplies the wider epistemic warrant for this cross-register language. The tradition-specific Śaiva articulation, the mathematical history and the reported psychic fact keep their own warrants; co-presence lets them be held within one Life/World inquiry without any one becoming the reduction of the others. That is the condition under which the essay's Logos can move fluently between philosophical language, matheme, whole image and technē.
