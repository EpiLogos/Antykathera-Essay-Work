## Braid 1 — manuscript consequence

The essay can now say the relation across registers without changing subjects midstream. A mathematical break, a psychic fact, a second-person interruption, a material consequence and a technical error are not one kind of evidence. They can nevertheless participate in one **returning operation** when the difference they disclose reaches the means by which the next determination will be made.

This is the product field's ethical hinge: **recording is not return**. A system can be perfectly observant and remain extractive if the observed difference cannot alter what commissions, permits, judges, forms or materially realises the next act. Conversely, return does not mean abolishing determination. Dia's discrimination is needed for the difference to become clear enough to matter; Syn is the retention of that difference and its ground so it can become generative rather than cancelled or appropriated.

The Indian/Gebser relation supplies the wider epistemic warrant for this cross-register language. The tradition-specific Śaiva articulation, the mathematical history and the reported psychic fact keep their own warrants; co-presence lets them be held within one Life/World inquiry without any one becoming the reduction of the others. That is the condition under which the essay's Logos can move fluently between philosophical language, matheme, whole image and technē.

## Braid 2 — manuscript consequence

The philosophical movement beneath §5 can now be stated without a category jump. A Life's world is not first a neutral exterior into which memory, tools, categories and permissions are later inserted. Finite worldhood is already mediated, apportioned and situated. Articulation gives this lived pattern determinate forms; those forms can become consequential inside the pattern; recognition can reopen their relation to their conditions. Artificial agency matters because its objective internality is increasingly **constructed on purpose**, making parts of paradigmatic mediation unusually inspectable.

That inspectability does not supply a machine with Prakāśa. It supplies a real technical-philosophical question: can the constructed paradigm return upon its own determinations strongly enough that experience changes the pattern from which subsequent action proceeds? Computational Vimarśa names a local affirmative case when a relevant condition changes. Epi-Logos names the wider reflexive office when the **paradigm's own articulated structure** can be compared with enactment, challenged by encounter and refined in response.

This gives “philosophy faithfully reflected in technology” a precise direction in both senses. Philosophy supplies distinctions by which the technology can be built and tested; the technology makes some mediating relations explicit enough that their success, failure and returned consequence can refine the philosophical articulation. Neither side becomes the proof of the other.
