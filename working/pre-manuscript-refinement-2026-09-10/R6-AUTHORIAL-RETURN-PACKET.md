## Claim-strength law

**Do not confuse epistemic hygiene with rhetorical self-erasure.** A source boundary limits attribution; a proof boundary limits formal consequence; an implementation boundary limits observed technical achievement. None licenses weakening a native proposition whose own argument has been made. A reviewer may flag overclaim only by naming the exact proposition and exceeded warrant; a reviewer must equally flag underclaim where caveat, status language or defensive negation makes prose say less than canon.

**Matheme is not the sole truth-bearing register.** Mytheme can prove/disclose a relation mythemically through the action of the whole image; Episteme can establish/historicise/source according to its evidence; Matheme derives/formalises according to its operations; Symbolon holds their differentiated disclosures in relation. Mythemic proof does not substitute for formal mathematical entailment, and mathematical proof does not exhaust the truth carried by a whole image.
