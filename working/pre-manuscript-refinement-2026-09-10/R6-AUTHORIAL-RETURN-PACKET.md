**The Return of Zero argues that determination is real only in relation to the activity and ground through which it becomes determinate.** Subjective Immediacy is the knower; Objective Internality is the mediating means; World is the known; Life / Mind is the whole relation. It then develops the opposite movement: differentiation retained rather than cancelled; the mediating slash becoming visible; paradox exposing the limits of the containing relation; and achieved determination returning to source, Other and consequence without losing its form. That return is **Epi-Logos**, the Return of Zero.

The mature paradigmatic compression says the same thing at the world-forming level:

```text

Genesis  Paradigm   Project
   \      |       /
```

## 7. Dia / syn return

The writing base must carry both logics positively.

**Dia** is differentiation: comparison, proof by exclusion, classification, measurement, decision, refusal. Its centred polar form `(-1)/(+1)` retains zero as axis. Cancellation `(-1)+(+1)=0` and appropriation into `±2` are distinct failures, not the definition of dia itself.

**Syn** is retained differentiated relation under the slash’s AND/OR: `(0/1)/(1/0)`. It does not erase the cut; it lets the terms remain answerable to the relation through which they arose.

The return passage is: break `1/0` → retain `/0` rather than cancel or appropriate → recognise `0/1` → hold the fuller obverse `(0/1)/(1/0)`.

Dia/syn is not diachronic/synchronic. Temporal succession and structural co-presence may each preserve or lose relation.
