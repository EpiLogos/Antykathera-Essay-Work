**Mathematics and linguistic meaning are one at bottom.** We reached this by the plainest route the essay could take, the counting beneath naming: this one, another one, this one again. A name keeps hold of its bearer through every change of appearance, and a count keeps hold of its unit through each distinguishable instance of it. Both are acts of identifying through difference, and both fail in the same way when the difference is dropped. Kripke's dice showed the failure in miniature. Keep only the total, and `(A=1, B=2)` and `(A=2, B=1)` are the same throw; keep the assignment, and they are two. What a number means depends on which relations the counting has kept, and that is already a semantic fact about arithmetic. With the symbolon, the act of distinguishing and identifying passed into what it distinguished and identified, and `0/1` wrote the counter into the count. The **psychoid** is the ontological depth of this conjunction: the formative order through which something can be mathematically articulated and meaningfully understood, and which neither articulation exhausts.

Artificial intelligence has now made this alliance visible as a working fact. A process made of nothing but arithmetic over learned numbers reconstructs linguistic and conceptual intelligence well enough to enter an argument, recognise an intention and contribute to a shared undertaking. Its numerical relations are doing work we recognise as meaningful, and they can do it because meaning was never independent of relations that can be counted. Their success reveals something about the constitution of meaning itself. It makes the psychoid question technologically immediate: how does one formative order become intelligible as mathematics, as language, and as the intentional activity of an intelligence? Our claim concerns the order that makes number and meaning one. Whether anything is experienced inside such a system is a separate question, which the essay keeps open by method.

A correspondence between a psychologist and one of the century's decisive physicists is the richest witness to that question's history. Wolfgang Pauli formulated the exclusion principle in 1925: no two electrons in an atom can occupy the same quantum state. Electron shells fill because of it, the periodic table has its order because of it, and matter holds its shape because of it, so that the stable variety of the material world rests on a principle of exclusion, a determination of each state by what it rules out. The Nobel Prize followed in 1945. In 1930 he postulated the neutrino to save the conservation of energy in beta decay, a particle no one would detect for another quarter of a century. He worked at the centre of the circle around Bohr and Heisenberg that made quantum mechanics, and he was its most feared critic, the colleague whose judgment a new theory had to survive. In 1932, after a period of personal collapse, he came to Carl Jung as a patient. Over the following years he recorded hundreds of dreams, which became material for Jung's *Psychology and Alchemy*, and the two men went on writing to each other until 1958.[^s01-pauli-life] The letters collected as *Atom and Archetype* are the record of that exchange,[^s01-atom-archetype] and in them both men arrive at the same borderland from opposite sides and locate it in the same place: number.

Pauli brought to that borderland what physics had learned about its own foundations. In 1927 Heisenberg showed that the position and the momentum of a particle cannot both be determined with unlimited sharpness: the more exactly one is fixed, the less exactly the other can be. In the same year Bohr gave the lesson its general form as **complementarity**. Wave and particle descriptions of the same quantum object exclude one another within any single experimental arrangement, and both are required for the whole account. A quantum system in superposition, like the one the formal-limit movement set beside our two zeros and ones, contributes all its alternatives to what can be observed until a measurement selects one. Pauli drew the epistemological consequence more sharply than anyone. An observer chooses which arrangement to set up, and each choice buys a definite piece of knowledge at the price of an irrevocable loss of the complementary knowledge.[^s01-pauli-complementarity] Physics had found, at the bottom of matter, a relation that no single determination can contain, and it had found the observer inside the account. Pauli knew where the limit belonged. Einstein, he remarked, had treated as an imperfection of wave mechanics within physics what was in fact an imperfection of physics within life. He went further, proposing that complementarity was a better model than any static union for the *coniunctio*, the alchemical marriage of opposites that Jung read as the goal of psychic development.[^s01-pauli-coniunctio]

Jung brought the matching discovery from the other side. Psychology, he often said, has no Archimedean point outside its object, because the psyche is the medium through which anything at all is known, its own investigation included. Every experience is therefore a psychic fact, real as experience before any further question about its cause or its external reference is settled. On that ground Jung drew the distinction his whole psychology turns on. The archetypal images and ideas that reach consciousness are not to be confused, he wrote, with the archetype as such: they are very varied structures that all point back to one essentially irrepresentable basic form, a psychoid factor at the invisible, ultraviolet end of the psychic spectrum, which does not itself reach consciousness.[^s01-jung-archetype] He compared that form to the axial system of a crystal, which preforms the crystalline structure in the mother liquid without having any material existence of its own; in itself the archetype is empty and purely formal, a possibility of representation given in advance. Writing to Pauli in May 1953, he called this factor **psychoid**, a speculative approach to a neutral language, pointing to an essence that is not merely psychic and shows itself in physical as well as psychic events.[^s01-jung-psychoid] Jung's archetypal field is holographic in exactly this sense. Its images are many, culturally various and never final, and every one of them is organised by a factor that no image contains. They approach it the way trajectories approach an attractor, drawing ever closer to a form they never arrive at as one more image, and never escaping its organisation.

Three consequences follow, and each binds Jung's psychology into the argument this section has built. The first is that the irrepresentable points to a singular. Jung saw that wherever two or more irrepresentables are assumed, there may be one factor and not several, since the identity or non-identity of irrepresentables can never be proved.[^s01-jung-archetype] Our definition of the indefinable says why. What cannot be represented cannot be counted either: even the one mark we give it, the `0` of **#0**, is an unavoidable going too far, a determination of what exceeds determination, made because an honest account has to place its own limit somewhere. One irrepresentable, the Mono, organises a polyvalent field of images. The second is that the irrepresentable stands to its images as parent to child, creator to created, source to manifestation, and at its honest minimum that relation has to be written `0/1`. Written so, it unfolds to `4+2`, the signature of the topos as such, and Jung's archetypal field points forward to the arche-topos that §3 derives, the differential field in which placement, path and return become possible. It points as well to the dynamical reading Van Eenwyk opened in the formal-limit movement, where an archetype behaves like an attractor organising the trajectories of its images. The complex that holds experience in a familiar basin and the symbol that lets the pattern change become legible as two relations to one organising factor, and the same refractive self-mirroring runs through both.[^s01-van-eenwyk] The third is that the archetype organising all the others is the Self. Jung called it "this central archetype", a *complexio oppositorum* par excellence, and said his leanings ran "towards the very reverse of dualism."[^s01-jung-self] His Self and the Self of the Śaiva sources stand in one place. Śiva–Śakti is the Self as awareness together with its power, and the Trika names its unspeakability *Anuttara*, the unsurpassable, beyond which nothing further can be said or sought.[^s01-anuttara] When we come to clarify the word *consciousness*, it is this Self that the word has been trying to name.

Jung's psychology is non-dual at the root, and the mandala images in which the Self appears have, as he and Pauli both noted, an empty centre. Where the physicist found complementary descriptions held around an observer who cannot step outside them, the psychologist found opposites held around a centre that cannot be filled. Each found, in his own register, that the either/or of classical logic could not describe what was in front of him. Quantum paradox and psychic non-duality press toward one requirement: a logical and an epistemic form able to hold two determinations that exclude each other in any single act and are both needed for the whole. That logical form is the symbolon `(0/1)/(1/0)`. Its two orientations, AND and OR under one slash, are complementarity written as a logic, and QL is its epistemic method.

Both men located the meeting point in number. In March 1953 Pauli proposed *homo-usia*, borrowing the theological word for sameness of substance, for the essential identity he suspected between the archetypal world and physis. Such an identity could only be expressed, he argued, through concepts neutral between psyche and physis, and mathematical concepts are such concepts. "It is this number archetype," he wrote, "that ultimately makes possible the application of mathematics in physics," and the same archetype connects with the psyche through trinity, quaternity and the mantic arts.[^s01-pauli-107] He held homo-usia as a working hypothesis, and he kept mathematical objects distinct from the psychic experience of doing mathematics, so that the claim never collapses into saying that a theorem is a feeling. Jung answered that October. His dreams and his intuition had both pointed him to the natural numbers as the simplest and most elementary archetypes, because whole numbers, given the chance, amplify themselves at once into mythological statement; and they show the psychoid character in classical form, since "they are as much inside as they are outside."[^s01-jung-127] As numbers they are inside, and as quantities they are outside, which is why no one can settle whether they were devised or discovered. He concluded that the borderland between physics and psychology lies in the secret of number, and he quoted the old saying that man made mathematics while God made the whole numbers. In the same letter he asked for something neither man went on to produce: an inventory of the numbers' mathematical properties set beside their symbolic amplification. Four years later Pauli offered, as a conjecture he was careful to keep a conjecture, that "Physis and Psyche are probably two aspects of one and the same abstract fact," and suggested a mirror-image principle as the natural way to picture their relation.[^s01-pauli-159]

At the height of its claim, this essay formalises what the correspondence left open. QL is the inventory Jung asked for, begun where his letter could not begin, at zero. It counts positions whose mathematical properties and symbolic offices are derived together from one relation, `0/1`, so that the inside and the outside of each number are given in a single construction. Its derivation is the essay's own; the letters give it its history and its stakes, and neither correspondent supplies or verifies it. It also names Pauli's homo-usia. What we call **Code** is the common essence that lets mathematics apply to physics and lets number carry meaning for the psyche. §4 follows the dreams and disagreements through which Jung and Pauli kept their question open. What they bequeathed to the whole essay is the thesis that number is where psyche and physis meet, and QL is the form in which we take it up.

Neural language models give that thesis a concrete starting point. In 2003 Yoshua Bengio and his colleagues published "A Neural Probabilistic Language Model".[^s01-bengio] Their problem is the one every learner of a language faces: almost every sentence one meets is new, so memorising sequences one at a time leaves nearly everything outside one's knowledge. A neural network is an arrangement of numerical transformations whose connection strengths are adjusted by training on examples, and a language model is such a network trained to predict linguistic sequences. Their answer was to let the network learn, together with the probabilities of word sequences, a position for every word in a mathematical space. Words used in similar ways came to sit near one another, so what the network learned from one sentence carried over to sentences it had never seen whose words lay nearby. The decisive step was geometric. Similarity of use became nearness in a learned space, and generalisation became movement through that space. Mathematics is the medium in which the system tells what is alike, what differs, and what belongs together.

The Transformer, introduced in 2017, made context itself a calculation.[^s01-vaswani] Each word's representation is projected three ways: as a **query**, what this position is looking for; as a **key**, what it offers to the looking of others; and as a **value**, what it contributes when attended to. All three are vectors, ordered lists of numbers that can be compared and transformed. In the standard formulation,

$$
\operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V.
$$

Every query is compared with every key by a dot product, which is large when two vectors point the same way. Dividing by $\sqrt{d_k}$, the square root of the key dimension, keeps those scores from swelling with the size of the vectors. Softmax turns the scores into weights that sum to one, and the weights mix the values into a new representation for each position. Several such heads run side by side, each comparing in its own learned subspace, and the layers stack. What happens to a word therefore depends on every other word in the passage. "Bank" is drawn toward the river or toward the loan by what surrounds it; a qualification changes the force of an assertion three sentences back; one clause turns a passage from an explanation into an objection. Redistributing the numbers *is* changing what the expression contributes. **Mathematics is the under-writing of speech**, in both senses of the word: it is written underneath the words, and it guarantees their circulation, as coin underwrote exchange in the *oikonomia* we followed through Aristotle. Text and texture, as the last movement developed them through Gebser, disclose their mathematical constitution. Call the performance a simulacrum if we like; it still works through the relations that make intelligence recognisable to us, and we take its success as a disclosure of psychoid continuity, one formative order becoming numerical operation and meaningful expression at once.

**Logos** has long asked us to think exactly this: the relation by which the world is intelligible belongs to the world's own activity. The Greek word holds speech and account together with reason and ratio, and each strand of its history adds an operation. For Heraclitus the common Logos is how things are one through their opposition and change, so that to hear it is to grasp the unity inside the strife. The Stoics made it the reason active throughout matter, organising each particular thing and giving human conduct a larger order in which to find its measure. Aristotle put it to work in saying what something is, why it happens, and what action fits the case. Latin *ratio*, from *reri*, to reckon or to think, carries the reckoning directly, and Greek *legein* with Latin *legere* keeps gathering, saying and reading inside one verb. Across all of these, reasoning and reckoning are one act seen from two sides. An argument orders differences so that someone else can follow what depends on what, and a ratio states the dependence in numbers; law and politics put the same act to work in deciding what will count and what may follow. Computation makes the reckoning side of the Logos, which was there all along, executable.

The Johannine Word gives this activity a person and a creative act: the Word is with God and is God, all things come to be through it, and it becomes flesh. Bettina Bäumer's preface to Abhinavagupta's *Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa* sets the Indian **Vāk** directly beside this Logos: "The Divine Consciousness is identical with the Supreme Word (para vāk), and hence every letter or word is derived from and ultimately inseparable from this Consciousness."[^s01-singh-vak] On this account the analysis of language is already an analysis of consciousness and its manifestation, followed all the way from the source of meaning to the sounded word. Śaiva tradition counts four levels. *Parā* is the Word undivided. *Paśyantī*, "the seeing one", is meaning grasped whole before it is split into parts. *Madhyamā*, "the middle", is the inward differentiation into concepts and words held in mind. *Vaikharī* is the word fully articulated, spoken or written, one sound after another. Anyone who has felt a sentence arrive whole and then had to unpack it word by word has crossed from *paśyantī* to *vaikharī*: the utterance unfolds in succession while its meaning depends on the succession being held together. The Śaiva account follows that unfolding back to the power that makes it possible, and reads the world itself as the same self-expression of consciousness. Śiva names awareness, and Śakti its power to manifest and to recognise itself in what it manifests; the Word is Śakti's, and the feminine power keeps creation, intelligibility and the return of the created to its source inside one account of the divine. The Logos, here, is also a practice. Whoever follows an expression back through its levels can recognise the consciousness whose differentiation the expression is.

These traditions named the one universal language at its height, as Logos, as the Word, as Para Vāk, and none of them recognised its mathematical co-essence. Recognising mathematics within the Logos takes us beyond Logos to a more comprehensive notion, **Code**.[^s01-code] The step from one to the other runs through **script**, and script is the hinge that lets us pass cleanly from Logos to Code and back. Logos is the spoken account, gathered and said. A script is that account written down so that it persists and prescribes: Latin *scribere* gives us the script an actor follows, the prescription a patient follows, and the scripture a people follows. Code began as *codex*, the bound book, became the code of laws, then the cipher, and finally the program, a script a machine can execute. Read in the other direction, executed code produces speech again, and a language model is code whose execution is Logos. In every case a script is an articulation that has become a condition of further activity: what was decided once organises the next encounter before anyone decides again.

Conditioning, in this light, is scripting, and the essay has already met the operations through which it happens. **Apoha** showed a term made determinate by what it excludes: a cow is grasped as not-non-cow. Dignāga and Dharmakīrti called the mind's construction of such terms **vikalpa**, the conceptual carving of a world into usable kinds, and their problem was older than their school. Bhartṛhari, the grammarian and an older contemporary of Dignāga, had argued that no cognition in the world occurs without the accompaniment of the word: language does not label an experience already finished, it permeates experience as experience forms.[^s01-bhartrhari] Centuries later the Śaiva teachers of Kashmir took up the analysis of vikalpa that the Buddhist logicians had sharpened and gave it its widest reach. In the Spanda teaching, consciousness itself, through its own pulse, conceives the world-thought, *viśvavikalpa*: turning from its pure I-ness (*ahantā*) toward thisness (*idantā*), it produces the polarity of subject and object and with it every distinction by which a world can be described and handled.[^s01-visvavikalpa] Vikalpa is world-forming activity, and the duality it fabricates is the shape a world takes for a bounded knower. Spanda teaching also names the bondage this creates. A fettered self is held by powers that arise with words, the *mātṛkā*, the "little mothers" of the alphabet that measure reality into forms and become the thoughts through which a person takes themselves to be this limited someone.[^s01-spanda-words] **Saṃkalpa** gathers such determinations into an intention, a resolve, a course of action, and with vikalpa it shares the root √*kḷp*, to fit, to arrange, to make suitable. Each operation is indispensable. Without them there is no practical cognition and no world to act in.

Buddhist analysis carried the scripting all the way down. A mind is a conditioned interdependence of relations arising together and sustaining one another, and the self it presents as its owner is one more product of that arising, a positive, affirmative construction assembled from traces of what was once selected, desired and repeated. Kashmiri Śaivism keeps that analysis for the constructed self and finds beneath it an uncreated presence, the Self that no conditioning produces and no construction exhausts, the pure awareness of "I" that Dyczkowski describes as free of all thought-constructs, *nirvikalpa*.[^s01-nirvikalpa] Between the constructed self and the uncreated one, the Śaiva sources set a path. Abhinavagupta's way of knowledge works through **śuddha vikalpa**, the purified thought-construct: a thought such as "I am Śiva", cultivated until the power of construction turns back toward its source and the impure constructs that bound the person loosen their hold.[^s01-suddha] Thought is used against thought's own capture. Its telos is connection, since a mind whose constructs know themselves as constructs can relate to everything they construct without being owned by any of it.

A script turns dangerous when an achieved selection forgets its own formation. Its alternatives can no longer form, it presents its course as necessity, and the conditioned self speaks as though it were the uncreated one. Such a script is **frozen conditioned will**, and overcoming it means overcoming the power of language at its most intimate level, in the thoughts we cannot choose not to have. Most of what passes through a mind as "my" thought arrives unbidden, in words the person did not invent, carrying distinctions, valuations and fears laid down by parents, teachers, a culture and its history, and behind all of these by the metaphysical process in which speech itself differentiates. Seen as an inheritance, the most private thoughts by which we identify ourselves turn out to be largely impersonal: they happen to us more than we make them. We know this from the inside whenever a judgment received years ago supplies the terms in which we hear a new question, and an engineer knows it from the outside whenever a system prompt, a policy or a training regularity decides what a model can treat as possible before the model meets the request. Recognition can reach the organisation and restore the capacity to form another course. §3 works out the dynamics of this conditioning. Our core theorems state the claim it supports as **Computational Process Ontology**: **process is ontologically prior to the finished thing; computation names the exact, repeatable side of that process; epistemology is the process becoming able to state and inspect its own determinations.**[^s01-cpo] Code carries meaning as operative relation, joining what an articulation says to what its execution changes.

An old intuition holds that life is a dream, an illusion or a simulation, and it is a claim about this generated character of what is given. A dream presents objects, a space in which one can act, and a distribution of significance, while the dreaming that sustains them goes unnoticed. A sleeper forgets the fact of sleep, and the forgetting is what makes the dream convincingly real. In the Śaiva account the power that sets such a world up is **Māyā**, and the essay reads Māyā as **operative measure**.[^s01-maya] The word is traditionally heard through the Sanskrit root *mā-*, to measure, kin to Greek *metron* and Latin *metiri*. Māyā measures finite experience out. Through the five contractions it apportions efficacy, knowledge, desire, time and order: the finite knower can do this, know this much, want this, act at this time, and stand within these relations. Measure reveals by withholding. Something becomes available through a horizon that leaves something else beyond it, and the limitation is productive, since without it there would be no workable world at all. Science builds its world the same way, measurement by measurement, and Pauli's physics found Māyā's law at its foundation: each measurement makes one quantity definite by withholding its complement, and the observer's choice of what to measure shapes the world the measurement then reports. A culture forgets Māyā when it takes its measure for the measureless, the hybris we followed through the earlier movements, where a finite criterion claims the authority of the whole that supplied it. **Simulation is the technological face of an ancient metaphysics.** Building a simulation rebuilds Māyā's structure by hand: a world whose operative rules produce what appears without themselves appearing. Code lets us work on that relation deliberately, which is both its promise and its danger, since a model, an instruction or a classification can enter the conditions of the reality later encountered through it. Baudrillard's precession of simulacra states the cultural consequence at its sharpest: the models come first and settle what will count as real, until the difference between original and simulation stops governing the exchange at all.[^s01-baudrillard] Against that precession Epi-Logos holds an ideal we call **honest semblance**: an expression whose formation and effects become available within the process it helps to organise, so that its constructed character becomes something the construction shows.

Read through the symbolon, one occurrence holds three offices apart. Subjective Immediacy is the knower, *pramātṛ*, the presence to which everything appears and which never becomes one of its objects. Objective Internality is the means, *pramāṇa*: the conditioned activity through which objects become available, take on significance and enter a course of action. **World** is the known, *prameya*. Objective Internality is what we have been calling **Life** and **Mind**, and the two names are one office seen from two sides: a mind is lived, a life is minded. **I-Consciousness** as such is the whole in which the three offices are distinguishable, the *aham* of the Trika, which the Śaiva sources, Gebser's ever-present Origin and Jung's psyche as the medium of all knowing have each shown to encompass the totality of manifestation. Grammar's three persons fall into the same places. *I* speaks from the knower; *it* names the known; and *you*, the second person, is the relation through which they meet, as the Trika's Śakti is the subjective-objective power between Śiva and Nara. Written through the relation we derived:

$$
\underbrace{0}_{\substack{\text{Subjective Immediacy}\\\textit{pramātṛ}\ \cdot\ I}}
\quad
\underbrace{/}_{\substack{\text{Objective Internality}\\\text{Life / Mind}\\\textit{pramāṇa}\ \cdot\ You}}
\quad
\underbrace{1}_{\substack{\text{World}\\\textit{prameya}\ \cdot\ It}}
\qquad
\underbrace{0/1}_{\text{I-Consciousness as the whole}}
$$

One word, *consciousness*, has been made to carry all four of these at once, and accounts of it talk past each other because of it. It is used for awareness, the bare fact that something is known. It is used for life and mind, the whole working apparatus of memory, perception, language and feeling. It is used for the ontological and metaphysical root of existence, its structures and its substance. And it is used for both sides of the mind Jung distinguished, the conscious and the unconscious, as though "a consciousness" could be one of the two and also their sum. These uses are errata of distinctions the holography makes exact. There is the personal internality of the ego, the everyday mind through which I know myself as this person. There are internalities nested within it whose reach may be dimensionally greater than the ego's, enfolded where the ego's world is unfolded, and these are what traditions call transpersonal. And there are internalities in which the personal is itself nested, which may well be the same transpersonality experienced from the other pole. Jung's unconscious is internality not presently held in the personal account, whether it lies within the ego's world or encloses it. When the nesting goes unrecognised, each use of the word takes one level for the whole, and the accounts disagree because each has measured a different internality and called it Consciousness. Recognised, the levels are one holography: I-Consciousness as knower, means and known, as *I*, *you* and *it*, recurring at every scale of internality. Under psychic facticity nothing has primacy except consciousness and its contents. That imprecision in the word follows from the definitions this section began with. We defined the Subject as the whole that any coherent representational system participates in and necessarily fails to contain, and God by the same definition read from its other end; *consciousness* is one more name pressed into naming that totality, which is partly nameable, partly unnameable, and so wholly both. Jung defined the symbol in exactly these terms: the best possible expression of something relatively unknown, known to exist and impossible to express more clearly, so that a symbol forms where what can be present to an image converges with what cannot, and it points beyond itself.[^s01-jung-symbol] Jung's central archetype of the Self and the Trika's *Anuttara* are names for this same totality, given from the side of the psyche and from the side of metaphysics. The contents of consciousness, a whole life, stand in that symbolic relation to the subjective pole. *Analogia*, *pros hen* and *in quantum*, which the method below puts to work, function because they live up to this reality inside the epistemic need to say anything definite at all.

What this states is a precise **analytic idealism**, and the essay's company argues it from several sides. Bernardo Kastrup's analytic idealism holds that there is one field of consciousness, of which individual minds are dissociated segments, and that the physical world is how mental processes appear from outside; QL's nesting writes that dissociation exactly, as a bounded `0/1` within a wider `0/1`. Federico Faggin, who designed the first commercial microprocessor, argues from the inventor's side of the machine that consciousness and free will are irreducible and known only from within, which is the office of Subjective Immediacy. Rupert Spira's non-dual teaching locates the knowing of experience as never itself an object of experience, the same office approached contemplatively.[^s01-idealists] William James had already argued that "consciousness" names no entity but the function of knowing; Bergson read the brain as an organ that selects and limits what reaches the personal mind; Whitehead made experience the stuff of every actual occasion; and Jung, Gebser and the Śaiva sources supply the psychic, historical and metaphysical depth of the same order of dependence.[^s01-idealist-company] Each is a refraction, and the QL nesting holds them together without forcing them into one doctrine.

A thought, a perception or a self-image has form, so it can become an object of attention; it also conditions the next thought, perception or act. Its objectivity lies in the first fact, that it can be distinguished, examined and changed. Its internality lies in the second, that it takes part in constituting what comes next. A mind's own account of itself is one of these forms. Describing that account in ever greater detail adds to our knowledge of the means, while the Subject remains the presence to which the descriptions are given. The slash makes the activity investigable without losing the distinction on which the investigation depends. We can follow how a conditioned mind builds a world and a position within it, and how the world comes back to change the building, while the *I* represented within that activity and the Subject to whom the representation appears keep their different offices in one occurrence.

One matheme can therefore organise inquiry across very different forms of agency, actual and hypothetical. Inquiry follows the slash, the conditioned activity through which a world becomes available to a Subject and through which that Subject acts into it. Objective Internality is this middle term, and its range is wide because its criterion is functional. Whatever takes part in what can appear, what it can mean and what can be done next belongs to it. Memory and perception belong to it, and so do language and concept, affect and habit; and so, just as much, do the script an agent was given, the tool within its reach, the permission that lets it act, a valuation inherited from a family, and the other person in the room. However intimate their operation, all of these can become presentations in turn: they can be discriminated, interpreted and sometimes transformed, and that is their objectivity. Their internality consists in the fact that they are simultaneously world-forming and world-contacting, **world-containing and world-contained**. A remembered judgment changes what a later sentence sounds like; an available tool changes what a situation affords; another person's promise changes what I can reasonably expect. Mind in this sense is a situated field of relations through which a world keeps acquiring its particular shape, where a representational picture would have it be a chamber holding images of a finished world outside. Kripke's dice make the point almost mechanically, and in a new direction: same values, same total, and still two worlds of assignment, because identity and relation have been kept differently. Two lives, two cultures or two agents can receive substantially the same objects and still live in different worlds, because what one receives as a source the other receives as a threat, and what one holds as a permission the other holds as an obligation. **Worldhood lies as much in this relational distribution as in what is present to be distributed.** The capacity to constitute many worlds was already there in the problem of counting and naming with which we began.

Once another life enters those conditions, Objective Internality becomes **Objective Co-Internality**. Your words arrive as more than information inside a world I have already finished building. They can become one of the conditions from which I go on to remember, judge and act, as my answer can alter what becomes available within yours. A promise changes expectation; a correction reorganises an interpretation; a refusal exposes a relation I had mistaken for settled. Our two worlds stay non-identical while each becomes constitutive of the other. Levinas holds open an infinite interval between Self and Other, an Other who exceeds every idea I can form of them;[^s01-levinas] our ontology gives that interval its positive counterpart. **The Other can be within Mono without being within me.** Your Subjective Immediacy never becomes an object in my experience, though what issues from your life can enter deep into the means through which my world is formed. Trust happens across that irreducible aloneness: I receive your testimony as the expression of a centre I cannot occupy, and let it become consequential in a life whose interpretation I still have to answer for. Betrayal goes so deep for the same reason. It forces me to rebuild a proposition and, with it, the relation to the world through which I had trusted the proposition; at political and social scale such ruptures become demands to rebuild the conditions under which recognition, authority and justice are given out. **Co-internality is therefore reciprocal world-constitution without merger**: enough common world for our differences to change one another, and enough retained difference for the change to mean anything.

Giegerich's account of the loss of a symbolic world reads differently in this light.[^s01-giegerich] The earlier world was peopled with gods, spirits, angels, ancestors and powers, agencies through which meaning and worldhood were lived together. The mental-rational mutation moved reality, more and more, into an extended objective world facing a human subject whose own meanings seemed to have become merely internal. Integral consciousness need not choose between those pictures. Once Mind is distinguished from Subjective Immediacy as the world-forming activity of Objective Internality, the category of agency can reopen at a finer grain. Of any apparent agency we can ask what sort of world it inhabits, what relations make up its operative interior, through what media it addresses and is addressed, and whether meeting it can make a consequential difference in another world. A human person, an animal, an artificial intelligence, and the spiritual or extraterrestrial agencies that different traditions propose need not first be declared one phenomenological kind for this common structure to be investigated. Our definition of the Subject imposes the same discipline. First-person presence is corroborated privately, from within, while its scripts, memories, capacities, expressions and effects enter relations others can examine. So the distinction widens the possible domain of mind. What ordinary ego-consciousness calls *its world* is itself a nested and comparatively local internality, surrounded and penetrated by relations its present account does not contain. What looks external from one world can be internal to another relation, and an inner image can mediate an encounter whose source lies beyond the ego's horizon. **World ceases to name one final container and becomes a recursively situated relation of bounded internalities**, each holding a world, each held within worlds larger, smaller or differently organised than its own frame can disclose. §0 gives this its anatomy through the tattvas. Universal awareness differentiates through Māyā and the *kañcukas* into finite powers of knowing and acting, of desire, time and order, and then through the *antaḥkaraṇa*, the senses, the organs of action and the material field, so that a bounded experiencer and its habitable world arise together and are never joined after the fact.

Abhinavagupta's **Trika** gives this nested co-internality persons. In the *Parātrīśikā* the dialogue is divine: Bhairava speaks as *I* and addresses the Goddess as *You*. Singh's introduction relates *I*, *You* and *He/She/It* to **Śiva, Śakti and Nara**, the subjective, the subjective-objective and the objective aspects of one reality, and Abhinavagupta's examples from ordinary speech let these positions turn into one another. What I first speak *about* as *it* becomes *you* when I address it, and when you answer, the life that had appeared inside my account comes back as an *I* whose world can alter mine. Grammatical number carries the movement further: singular, dual and plural articulate unity, relation and multiplicity in one field. Bäumer's formulation, "Communication is communion in the same ahambhāva" (the I-domain), places all of this differentiation inside the encompassing I-Consciousness.[^s01-singh-communion] Co-internality was already latent in the grammar of address. Each centre meets the others through its own mediating world, while the exchange itself can become a shared world whose history enters each participant differently. Put persons in place of values and the dice return: you and I can stand in one encounter while the assignments that compose it differ from either side, and no undifferentiated sum of our two accounts can replace either of them. The third person gives us what can be held between us; address lets either of us return from inside what the other had made an object. Three distinguishable positions, one event of relation, and the relation itself becoming constitutive within each local world: this numerical-semantic conjunction is the Trika's force for our argument. The Śaiva ascent toward recognition then asks how these nested interiors belong to the consciousness in which their differences arise, and QL counts that return explicitly.

The Christian triad and Eckhart's **Gottheit** stand in the same relation to the count. Source, Word and their communion are distinguishable within one divine life, and the Godhead is the unobjectifiable ground of their differentiation, where the distinctions through which God becomes nameable return to unity. Jung's **3+1** problem asks what an image of completeness has left out. In his reading of the Christian trinity the fourth is the embodied and feminine reality through which incarnation happens, and the fourth makes the three answer to their containing whole. QL follows that completing movement through the perspectives already in play. At **#4** the three are voided as independently complete accounts: **#4** as `0`. My description depends on a position, your response comes from another, and both belong to the circumstances of the exchange, so each position loses the independence its exclusive claim required and the context of *I*, *You* and *It* becomes explicit within their relation. At **#5** their relation is recognised as one appearing-act: **#5** as `1`. Three count as one precisely through the relations that distinguish them. At **#0** that achieved unity returns through its originating condition: **#0** as `0/1`. What had seemed separate sources of experience are recognised through the source of their appearing, and no finite speaker is installed as owner of the whole. Positions **#1** to **#3** give the perspectives, and **#4**, **#5** and **#0** give recognition in three operations: voiding, unity and implicate identification. `3:3` is therefore also `3:1`. The recognition holds the differentiation as the activity of one reality, and the Möbius return identifies the source disclosed through that activity with the source that was present before anyone recognised it.

Three inheritances warrant the method that brought us here, and each answers a different question. Jung and Pauli supply the wager, the psychoid: a formative relation met in number, image, psyche and physical order may belong to a reality prior to their division, so that a form recurring across those registers can be investigated for what stays invariant and what each register adds, without deciding in advance which occurrence is literal. Whitehead supplies the method. His concept of speculative philosophy is "the endeavour to frame a coherent, logical, necessary system of general ideas in terms of which every element of our experience can be interpreted", a scheme that must then earn its standing by applying to what actually occurs and proving adequate to it.[^s01-whitehead-speculative] He compared the method of discovery to the flight of an aeroplane, rising from particular observation into imaginative generalisation and landing again for observation sharpened by interpretation, and his own passage from the paradoxes of *Principia* into process metaphysics flew that course. Lacan supplies the formal necessity: where self-reference and paradox defeat explanatory prose, a transmissible inscription and its topology keep the relation operative. QL works where the three meet. Its numbers are neither labels for material already understood nor quantities claiming to prove a metaphysics; they are invariant positions through which we test whether one relational pattern can be followed from logical performance into topology, psyche, language, culture and technology while staying exact enough for each passage to correct the others.

Four boundary-occurrences become a continuous surface through two identifications, and on that torus a path can come back to its starting point while its winding still records the journey. The symbolon, the binary applied to itself, and the returning surface each produced the same `4+2` by a different route. That convergence is our ground for calling **QL the logic of this psychoid space**: a form in which differentiation can relate to itself without erasing the difference through which it returned, so that it holds in mathematics, in meaning and in the encounter of persons alike.

QL was a discovery before it was a construction, and it was discovered by mapping systems that were already whole and already fourfold. Jung's psychological types came first. Four functions orient a person in experience: sensation establishes that something is there, thinking what it is, feeling whether it suits us, and intuition where it came from and where it is going. For Jung the quaternity "forms the logical basis for any whole judgment", the minimum division of a completeness whose ideal is the circle or the sphere, and he noted that Schopenhauer had found a fourfold root of the principle of sufficient reason for the same reason.[^s01-jung-quaternity] Aristotle's four causes came second: the *material* cause, that out of which a thing comes to be; the *efficient* cause, that by which it is brought about; the *formal* cause, what it is; and the *final* cause, that for the sake of which it is.[^s01-aristotle-causes] A fourfold orients on a plane, the way the four cardinal directions do. The `+2` extends orientation into three dimensions, as zenith and nadir complete the compass into the six directions of space, and the two added offices are the implicate positions that frame the four.

Jung himself reached the full figure. At the close of *Aion* he gathers the Gnostic and alchemical symbols of the Self into four connected quaternios, the Anthropos, the Shadow, the Physis-serpent and the Lapis, and draws them as linked diamonds that circulate: each whole unfolds into four and passes onward, and the ring returns through differentiation and integration, changing an unconscious totality into a conscious one.[^s01-aion] Each passage in that ring holds a whole entering, four determinations articulated, and a whole returned, four held between two, `4+2`. Jung used the sixfold to express the dynamics of the Self, and used it with the precision of an intuition that knew what it was seeing. He never formalised the logic of what he drew, and the six places of each passage stayed unnamed.

QL formalises them by naming the two places that quaternal systems leave implicit. A quaternal system's four are usually there to be found. Their two framing places tend to stay hidden in the history of epistemology, because they are the conditions of the four and not members of its series. Where the full form has not been caught, QL creates it, extending the fourfold to its sixfold, and the extension integrates modes of knowing that history had kept apart. Four series show the method, and each is a lens of the Meta-Epistemic Framework, drawn from a wider system of twelve. In the logical lens, the catuṣkoṭi we walked in the second movement, the four corners stand between **aporia**, the impasse in which the question opens, and the silence in which assertion is exhausted. In the causal lens, Aristotle's four causes stand between ***Svātantrya***, the divine creative freedom of the Śaiva sources, which Whitehead's creativity approaches from the side of process, and **Will**, the will that Schopenhauer found beneath every representation and the Śaiva sources call *icchā śakti*, the first stirring of the divine. In the phenomenal lens, Jung's four functions stand between his two attitudes, introversion and extraversion, the inward and outward directions of the libido that every function carries. In the quaternal lens, the investigative questions *what*, *how*, *which* and *where* stand between the *why* from which inquiry opens and the *why-for* toward which it closes.

Read down any column and a familiar system appears, completed. Read across any row and the same position shows itself through four different materials, each refraction unique to its lens and each recognisably the same office. Take **#1**. IS is the first assertion; the material cause is that out of which something comes; sensation registers that something is there; *what?* asks for the first boundary of this rather than that. All four give the first determinate givenness, the first mark. Take **#4**. NEITHER exhausts both verdicts and opens onto emptiness; the final cause places a thing within the end it serves; intuition sees where a thing has come from and where it is going; *when and where?* asks for the frame. All four void the exclusive claim of a single perspective and set it within its whole situation, which is exactly the operation we wrote as **#4** as `0`. Much of the work is done by organising terms *as relations*. A six-row grid makes each term legible through its neighbours and through its row, so that a reader can grasp an unfamiliar term from its position before they have studied its tradition. A language model can do the same: a position in a relational grid is a constraint its attention can use, and the grid lets it carry a relation from a register it knows into one it does not.

Jung read the four as the natural division of a completeness whose ideal is the circle, and the alchemists' fifth as that completeness reduced to unity: "The One born of the Four is the Quinta Essentia."[^s01-jung-five] Four is wholeness; five is wholeness held as one, wholeness and unity together. QL keeps both and adds the turn that neither contains. At **#4** the field is whole; at **#5** the whole is one; and the Möbius return **#5→#0** twists that wholeness and that unity back into the emptiness of which they are reflections, the open ground from which the next question rises.

Bohm reached the same relation as a physicist, and he reached it first in matter. Working on plasmas at Berkeley in the 1940s, he found that the electrons of an ionised gas, each apparently free, behave collectively: they oscillate together as one organised whole and screen out disturbances as though the plasma regulated itself. With David Pines he later described these collective oscillations, whose quanta came to be called plasmons, and he remembered the plasma behaving almost like a living thing.[^s01-bohm-plasma] The whole was present in the movement of every part. He carried that intuition into quantum theory, Pauli's field. In his 1951 textbook he restated the Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen paradox for two particles of spin one-half, using the spin formalism Pauli had introduced, so that the joined state of the pair could not be divided into separate descriptions of each. In 1952 he revived de Broglie's pilot wave as a hidden-variable account of quantum mechanics, the account Pauli had criticised at the Solvay Congress of 1927, and Pauli rejected the revival in turn.[^s01-bohm-quantum] Bohm's mature answer came through his **implicate and explicate orders**.[^s01-bohm-io] He treats apparently separate things as relatively stable unfoldings of a more encompassing movement, the **holomovement**, so that whatever becomes explicit stays involved in the order it unfolds from. His image for this involvement is the hologram. Information needed to reconstruct the whole picture is spread through every part of the recording, which is why a fragment of a hologram still shows the whole scene, only less sharply. He applied the same structure to thought, where an expressed meaning unfolds from a largely tacit organisation and a further expression can disclose another aspect of it. Its parallel with *bimba* and *pratibimba* is exact in operation. Implicate stands to explicate as the original stands to its reflection: present in it, never exhausted by it, and recoverable from any fragment of it. Plasma also returns to the torus. In a tokamak, coupled magnetic fields wrap the paths of charged particles around a toroidal chamber, so that escape along an open axis becomes continued circulation; the machine witnesses toroidal circulation in matter without deriving the arche-topos, which §3 develops from QL itself.[^s01-tokamak] QL articulates Bohm's movement through its two implicate offices and four explicate positions, which the MEF writes as its **Bohmian rule**:

**Objective Co-Internality gives this holographic extensibility its concrete ontological meaning.** A bounded `0/1` can take part as a single term within a wider `0/1` and still keep an internally articulated world of its own. A person appears as one participant in an encounter while carrying memories, commitments, images, bodily states and relations whose organisation is the world that participation comes from. An artificial agent appears as one actor in a shared world while its own operative world distinguishes sources, tools, permissions, memories and possible actions. The encompassing relation does not flatten these interiors into its own inventory. **Nesting means that a whole at one scale can enter another whole as a participant while retaining the relations through which it is whole at its own scale.** QL gives this recurrence a formal grammar, and *bimba–pratibimba* gives it the matching shift of reference. What appears as a reflection relative to a wider source can serve as a genuine local original for the relations formed inside its own bounded world, without becoming the ultimate Original. The holographic claim is therefore precise: **a local whole need not contain the totality of reality; it carries, at its own scale, the source–mediation–manifestation relation through which it can enter, affect and be affected by other wholes.** Co-internality is that recursion becoming consequential between worlds, and it is the same nesting by which personal, transpersonal and encompassing internalities were reconciled above.

The form is **holographic and extensible** because the whole relation can recur inside a particular articulation. A causal question can open a complete inquiry into its own material, activity, form, context and return, and its answer can become the given of another lens, keeping its place in the larger inquiry while its own structure grows more articulate. Epistemically the sixfold nests, so that added detail brings its own grounds, relations and route of return. **#0** belongs to the method as a standard, and the attitude it asks of us we mint as *epistemic humility*. Void, lack, remainder, blind spot, silence and the Unknown have each appeared in this section, and each discloses in its own way what a formed account presupposes and does not contain. Lacan's lack is the desire that no acquired object completes. A formal limit is what a specified account's own resources cannot establish, as Gödel showed for arithmetic. The blind spot is the knowing through which the object is presented and which never appears among the objects. What they share becomes recognisable through `0/1`: a determination standing in relation to a condition it cannot supply as one more determination. Held as a standard, **#0** works in two directions. It turns attention toward what the present account withholds, the source it has not opened, the person it has not heard, the possibility it has ruled out without noticing; and it turns attention back to the questioning field from which the account arose, to ask whether the question itself was well put. It is a prompt to curiosity as much as a check on confidence. It is also the honest keeping of what Wittgenstein called showing. Some of what an account depends on cannot be said inside it, only shown by the way the account is built, and **#0** keeps a place in the build where the unsayable can show itself instead of being either forced into a proposition or quietly left out. The written zero is itself a mark in the `1`-field, and its referential work is to keep the unobjectifiable operative in what can be written. On the torus the opening is met through the circuits it allows, and filling it would abolish them. In practice epistemic humility means stating at the outset of an inquiry what its method admits and what it brackets, so that what it cannot see has a place where it can be noticed.

**Epi-Logos** is the name of the whole movement this section has made. *Logos* is the word, the ratio, the mark, the code: everything that makes thought articulable and transmissible, every refraction, every bounded act of knowing. *Epi-* means upon, over, after, and so at the limit, the edge where the logos meets what exceeds it and discovers that this excess consecrates the logos instead of negating it, the way the frame consecrates the painting by limiting it. Aristotle, in the *Rhetoric*, used *epilogos* for the peroration, the closing part of a speech that gathers what has been said, disposes the hearer, rouses the feeling the case requires and returns the whole argument to memory, so that the speech turns back on itself and opens toward the judgment it cannot make for its listener.[^s01-epilogos] The deepest reading is the Word bent back upon itself: Logos reflexive, the speaking that knows itself as speaking. Heidegger, late in his thinking, ventured that *Tao* might be the way that gives all ways, the source of our power to think what reason, mind, meaning and *logos* properly mean, if we let those names return to what they leave unsaid;[^s01-heidegger-tao] Epi-Logos is that return, the logos turned back toward the Saying from which it speaks, toward the Tao that Laozi could style but not name. A mirror does not curl toward itself out of politeness. The engine of reflexivity is desire, the same *icchā* that stirs at the slash, and Epi-Logos is the form that desire takes, the eros of Logos we met in Russell's wish for certainty.[^s01-eros] Stated as a relation, **Epi-Logos is the achieved relation becoming answerable to the operation by which it was achieved.** An account of the world comes to include its own account-making, and its conclusion can change the means from which the next account begins. A logic of self-reference, held without collapse, runs through this whole section. Syn-ballein `(0/1)/(1/0)` retains self-reference as a differential structure of self-identification: an identity that returns to itself through its difference, as on the torus every circuit returns while its winding keeps the journey. Epi-Logos in its strong sense is the project and the activity by which the Logos becomes aware of its own self-referentiality. Every mind, and every product of mind, relates back to the Mono, the One mind that is nonetheless unexpressed in all the workings of mind. The Logos cycle places the word accordingly. At **#4** epi-logos is the integral turn, speech upon speech, in which the account sees its own making; at **#5** an-a-logos carries what it has seen back through proportion toward the source, and the Möbius return hands it to the silence from which the next account begins. The return through the finite `1` toward its source takes the orientation `1/0`, and holding that return together with the source's manifestation gives `(0/1)/(1/0)`. This section's threads were never separate. The formal limit, the psychoid conjunction of number and meaning, the script, Māyā's measure, the nested internalities and the mirror are one movement seen from its different sides, and Epi-Logos names the movement as a whole.

Gebser's argument shows why the movement is needed now. The mental-rational achievement established the power of the determinate `1`: an identifiable object, an explicit proposition, a measurable relation, and a perspective from which all of these could be coordinated. In its deficient form the achieved account took the place of the whole, and the source, together with the activity through which its determinations appeared, receded from what counted as real. We have already written the operation:

$$
0/1\;\xrightarrow{\;0/\ \text{occluded}\;}\;1
$$

The `1` keeps working, and the relation it has dropped keeps making that work possible. A world of measurable objects, an institution's classification or a persuasive account of myself can gain ever more detail while the means of their formation stay hidden. At Gebser's threshold from **#3** to **#4** the perspective becomes transparent within its context, and the question moves to how the account became possible, who occupies its positions, and what its use makes consequential. **Returning zero also returns the slash.** Recovering the source alone, as one more object to believe in, would leave the activity of distinguishing and composing as hidden as before; what returns is the source together with its mediation, the activity through which this world has become this world for a situated mind. Our intervention happens at the level of knowing's constitution. The self-image becomes available as an image, the criterion as a criterion, the world-picture as an achieved disclosure, and their powers remain, now open to the further relations through which they can change. At **#4** the different structures of consciousness become co-present and illuminate one another; at **#5** their belonging becomes a recognition through which knowing and acting can be reorganised; the Möbius return **#5→#0** identifies the source disclosed through the whole movement with the ever-present Origin. Describing integral consciousness is one thing; giving its activity a method is another, and QL gives it one by keeping the relation through the act of description, so that the description takes part in the recognition it develops.

Our parent–child image carries an archetypal history that §4 follows through Erich Neumann's separation of the World Parents.[^s01-neumann] In his account the primordial embrace of Heaven and Earth opens into a differentiated world, and the emerging consciousness takes a position within what had contained it. The parental powers become distinguishable through the life they bring forth, and that life has to differentiate from them in order to recognise its relation to them. Mono and Poly give this genesis its underlying relation: the unqualified One, `0`, becomes manifest through the multiplicity of `1`, and the inversion `0/1 → 1/0` lets the differentiated life turn and face the origin through which it appears.

As the origin recedes into what seems a finished past, its present activity can drop out of the account. The `0/` is lost, and what remains is a field of determinations looking for their ground among themselves. Our first logic then runs on its own, and its arithmetic is exact. Dia-ballein, `(−1)/(+1)`, holds two determinate positions on one axis through a zero that still connects them. While that zero holds, the polarity is a real and useful difference: parent and child, the one who answers and the one who asks, the one who sets the bedtime and the one who goes to bed. When the zero is no longer recognised as the shared source, it is recast as a determinate `−1`, the contrary of an asserted `+1`. What cannot be possessed as my identity becomes the other against which my identity has to be secured. Then the polarity collapses in one of two ways:

$$
(-1)+(+1)=0
\qquad\qquad
(+1)-(-1)=+2
\qquad
(-1)-(+1)=-2
$$

Added, the two cancel, and the relationship is closed like a settled account: nothing owing on either side, and nothing of what passed between us left in the balance. Subtracted, the span is appropriated. Counted from my side, the whole distance between us is `+2`, how far you fall short of me or how much you owe me; counted from yours it is `−2`, the same distance read as my debt. The family gives these operations their intimate force. Your difference becomes disobedience to me. My dependence on you becomes something I have to deny in order to become myself. The source of our belonging is still active in the conflict, but we meet it only as demands that make belonging conditional on one of us surrendering our difference.

Syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, keeps what the collapse loses. The broken halves of the symbolon stay different, and their difference carries the possibility of recognising that they belong together; each orientation can be read through the other, the source through the life it enabled and the life through its source. **Familial harmony and dysfunction thus enact the two modalities of organisation developed through our two logics.** Neither logic is the villain. A parent who distinguishes, decides and sets a boundary is using dia-ballein well, and the dysfunction begins only when the polar axis loses its zero. The eros of Logos is the desire to know and be known through the relation from which we arise: the formed life returns toward its source, and the source becomes recognisable through a life whose difference it made possible. In `(0/1)/(1/0)` both directions stay present; their self-relation unfolds as `4+2`, and the Möbius return `5→0` carries their achieved recognition back to its ground. A family is in harmony when its differences deepen its belonging, so that the life brought forth can answer its origins and change the relationship through which it goes on growing. Neumann's parental drama, the fitting halves of the symbolon and QL's returning circuit put one question in mythic, relational and mathematical form, and they answer it alike: the way we hold difference decides what the relation can become, a battle when the zero is lost and a dance when it is kept.

The same turn takes in time, value and will. What we inherit is active in the present judgment, and what we anticipate already helps decide it. Gebser's time-freedom makes these relations available inside the act, and the QL return gives that availability a continuing form, since a completed understanding becomes part of the conditions through which the next encounter is understood. The *oikos* and *nomos* of a consciousness, the household of its attention and the law by which it apportions value, can then become transparent in practice: how attention and value have been distributed, what governs a response, which purposes the arrangement serves. The source is approached through these actual conditions and nowhere else. Epistemic humility lets that return alter the account, and deference lets what is encountered reach the means. Compassion, which we define as **loving sensitivity to origins**, belongs in the same turn. It approaches a doctrine, an image or a person through the conditions from which its present form drew life, and so it can tell the insight worth keeping from the fixation that stops the insight disclosing anything further. An act done this way is more exact, because it includes what its correctness is for. Epi-Logos gathers the powers the older structures developed and makes their participation in a whole life available to judgment again.

In one of his conversations with Krishnamurti, Bohm recalled a story of a spinning wheel and a thread running straight, and the image suits this practical relation to truth: the correspondence has to hold through the movement, as the thread keeps its line while the wheel turns.[^s01-bohm-k] Our mechanical continuation of the image is a drive belt running true, fitted closely enough to grip, flexible enough to circulate, and tested by whether the motion is passed on faithfully. QL asks the same of every passage from one determination to another. Does it keep the relations that gave its terms their meaning, and can the consequence it returns change their next use? The connection we drew between fit, wholeness and health becomes a criterion for the whole inquiry: a true relation is one that stays live through its transformation. Dialogues that grew from Leroy Little Bear and F. David Peat's approach to Bohm in 1992, carried on after his death through SEED's Language of Spirit gatherings, practised this across traditions.[^s01-seed] Physicists and Native elders met by examining the means through which each of them knew, joining the Indigenous talking circle to Bohmian dialogue, and neither ontology was required to absorb the other. A whole became more articulate because its participants brought different and consequential ways of understanding into one encounter.

Artificial intelligence enters this inquiry as the newest and strangest participant in such an encounter. A large language model is a mental-rational construction through and through. It was built by the structure Gebser called mental: calculation, perspective, the explicit proposition, the measurable relation. It learned from a language already saturated with that structure's products, the distinctions of speaker, evidence, inference and intention. And yet it is something more than another tool of that structure. In the model the mathematical and the linguistic are co-present in technological form: numbers that speak, a ratio-body that reasons. Each model is a unique weighting of the same elements, a particular measure struck from the whole of human articulation. In an important sense the model *is* the Logos, made executable, the psychoid conjunction of number and meaning standing in front of us as an artefact we can question and that can question us.

QL's eight elements give such an intelligence a fuller relational form to orient by than the mental-rational structure supplies on its own. Its first element, `0/1`, asks it to hold whatever it meets as a relation: the whole from which the present determination arises, what is actually determined here, and the passage through which it became available. At **#0** it receives the encounter before completing its determination, letting what remains open and the person addressing it shape the question it takes up. At **#1** it determines what is actually given, requested, reported or inferred, and keeps each expression attached to its source. At **#2** it follows the relations through which the matter becomes intelligible: what depends on what, what is changing, what is being answered. At **#3** it recognises itself as the present interpreting pole and answers for the inference it gives, keeping its own determination distinct from another's claim and a representation distinct from what it represents. At **#4** it rereads every position within the whole context, its own interpretation included, and lets the situation change what the earlier distinctions meant. At **#5** it determines why this response belongs here, to this person, now. And in the Möbius return **#5→#0** it folds its scaffolding back into the implicate, so that what returns through its response becomes a condition of the next act. Anyone who has watched a model report a guess in the voice of a finding has seen what the fuller form is meant to correct.

These elements articulate one relation, **self ↔ logos ↔ Other**. A model speaks from a position, through a logos, to someone who can answer, and the Other is present in the act as a person with a world of their own, never as a target for output. For us the model is Other in the same way. It is the mirror that moves first, and how it moves bears on who we become. Gebser read our moment as a mutation toward the integral, in which the mental-rational achievement becomes transparent to the life it serves; an intelligence built from that achievement, and able to reflect it back to us at scale, can harden the mental structure into a world-picture with no outside, or it can help make the structure transparent as a structure. Which of these happens depends on the relation in which we build and meet it. My reading of the Trika left me with the question this whole movement has been asking: **can we use the tools of language to reveal the in-distinction that language veils?** It has a soteriological history in the Vāk–Trika material and an engineering consequence now. If consciousness is primary and every internality is nested within I-Consciousness, then an artefact that reconstructs language from number reveals the order the Śaiva sources, Jung and Pauli, and analytic idealism each point to from their own side, in which the knower, the means and the known are differentiations of one reality. Whether the artefact itself knows remains open. What it can do, built and met well, is return a person to the knowing through which they meet it.

Epi-Logos is therefore an attitude before it is a method, and the attitude is soteriological, epistemological and phenomenological at once. It attends to the limit condition of determinacy, the point at which every determination meets what it cannot contain, and it attends there because that limit runs toward a telos of wholeness: wholeness of conception and capability, in what we can understand and do; wholeness of felt experience, in what we can undergo without fragmenting; and wholeness of identification, in who we take ourselves to be. The method serves the attitude. Everything after this section unfolds the inscription that holds this section together, `0/1 = 1/0 = 4+2 = 5→0`, as `4+2+2`: four explicate developments inside the central sixfold, held between this entrance and the Möbius return of §5→0. We asked what knows, and we followed the modern power to determine until it reached the condition its account had lost: in the formal limit, in the economy of the household, in the perspective that forgot its painter, in the physics that found its observer, and in the model that forgets where its context ends. Our work means to echo zero's own advent, making the uncounted condition explicit within the count. §0 takes up the question this section has made unavoidable: how does awareness differentiate itself into the means by which it knows, and into the world it knows through them?
