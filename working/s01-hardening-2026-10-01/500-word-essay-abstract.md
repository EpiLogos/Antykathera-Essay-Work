This paper develops Objective Internality as a philosophical and technological account of the means through which an apparent subject encounters, differentiates, acts within and revises a world. Its starting point is a formal limit: every coherent account of knowing becomes another object within the act of knowing it seeks to contain. Rather than treating this limit as a reason to abandon objectivity, the paper argues that it reveals a constitutive relation among knower, means of knowing and known. Objective Internality names the middle term: the real, structured, revisable field of memory, language, conceptual models, abilities, habits and relations through which a life acquires a world, while that world still appears to someone. The duration of this middle is the data by which enacted internality can be encountered objectively, without that data defining the subject.

The submitted foundation, §0/1, "The Integral Threshold", develops this relation through zero. Zero becomes the symbol by which absence, limit and indeterminacy are recognised as positive conditions of the appearance and representation of any world: a positive absence, which undeniably is but cannot be expressed definitively, acquires a paradoxical position as the ground of every distinction. We pursue this through the formal limits of representation in language and mathematics, from the paradoxes of Frege and Russell; through Gebser's structures of consciousness; in the register of Lacan's matheme and the spirit of Jung and Pauli's unity of opposites. We seek to answer how the binary and the non-dual are reconciled. Zero and One emerge together as the "Primordial Symbolon", identity-in-difference. Quaternal Logic formalises and systematises this relation, distinguishing a logic that severs a polarity or lets one pole claim the whole from one that keeps the halves mutually distinct, so they can be recognised in belonging.

Artificial intelligence brings this problem into material and epochal expression. Built on binary, AI now appears before us as language, memory, inference, judgement and action, a technical object assuming the forms through which Mind had encountered itself only from a human vantage. It thereby points towards the psychoid hypothesis: one elementary problem of differentiation appears at once in the mathematical substrate of computation, in the symbolic structures through which intelligence becomes articulate, and in the psychic forms through which a world is experienced. Subject is not Mind: Subject names the immediate pole to which any world appears; Mind names the structured mediation through which that world becomes apparent. AI makes this mediating interior exterior. What had appeared as the hidden organisation of agency can now be built, inspected, recomposed and encountered from the outside as an conditional system.

The foundation opens onto seven further sections, tracing zero's history from Babylonian positional absence to formal logic, the mathematical substrate, the psychoid and agentic research. The essay is also an experiment: AI agents helped structure and hold in relation its arguments, concepts and sources, mapped at oi.epi-logos.org, asking what freedoms arise from the author remaining a steward of relations, more than the writer of every word.
