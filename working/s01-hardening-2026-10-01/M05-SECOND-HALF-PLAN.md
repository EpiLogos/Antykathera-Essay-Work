*Diabolos* was already defined in M03 as the accuser and slanderer, the one who throws across. So the second half can say outright what it is doing: following one economy that can be gathered (*sym-ballein*) or thrown apart (*dia-ballein*). Your point that the inversion is implicit in the ground becomes the thesis of these paragraphs. Every term of value carries its own fall.

M05's marketplace passage can become that reading. Then M03's sentence has to change, either by pointing forward or by moving. I've added this to the ledger as a choice for you.

### Mono, Poly and monopoly

- **★ The inversion in the ground.** *Monopōlion* is *monos* plus ***pōlein*, to sell**, not *polys*, many.
  - Where our Poly names the many, the *poly* of monopoly names a sale. The pun is exact *because* the roots differ: in a monopoly the many have been replaced by what is sold.
  - Aristotle's first recorded monopoly, Thales cornering the olive presses (*Politics* I.11, 1259a), sits in the same chapter on wealth-getting as the household distinction we already cite.
  - That is the irony of the inversion lying in the ground: the first account of the household already contains its counterfeit.
- ***Theologia* / *oikonomia*.** This is the patristic split, with Tertullian's *dispensatio* ("weighing out") and Rahner's Rule as the modern repair. You read it as the Western Mono/Poly problem, answered by Śiva and Śakti as a co-equal `0/1`. It belongs with Ephesians and the *plērōma*, and it can absorb the current Śiva/Śakti sentence from the Aurobindo paragraph.

## 3. What your Antichrist extracts give us

The theme should not come through; the dynamics should. Read in our notation, the extracts give five operations the second half needs.

1. **The I-Deal.** Your own coinage, and a pun that argues. The ego is an ideal treated as real: "a trade of the whole psyche for one of its fragments", "the making-real-of-the-model of one's self". In our terms this is `Ø`, the crossed subject. The `1` of a self-model is made to stand where the `0/1` relation stands. It answers your instruction to say plainly that **the force is egoic**.
2. **Life on loan.** The counterfeit economy cannot create value. It lends back what it has borrowed, with interest, and it forecloses. It deals in "an erroneous account", "a bloated claim", inflationary worth. This is credit turned into debt-bondage, the trust canon's inversion. It gives the "con of withness substituting for the witness" its economic mechanism: the relation (withness) is charged for, and the witness (the Subject) is effaced.
3. **The un-finite.** It swells toward All, "puffing up and out", and implodes toward a "precise nought", never reaching Being or non-Being. It is "a fraction of the infinite, an infinite fraction". This is *dia-ballein*'s polar axis lived as inflation and collapse, `(+1)/(−1)` with nothing at the slash. It also describes a top-heavy economy: growth mimicking the infinite, then implosion. The present wealth gap reads directly through it.
4. **"Un-dual rather than non-dual."** This is an inverse unity: "the essence of divorce, the break-up of unity that yet remains a cloying bond". It is the cleanest statement we have of the difference between *dia-ballein*'s forced bond and *sym-ballein*'s relation. **One line from it is worth using**, attributed as yours.
5. **The Showman.** "It's not only all for show, it is all show." This is the simulacrum and the PSYOP read as ego-display. It can enrich the simulacrum paragraph without importing the figure.

## 3b. A coherent notion of evil (from the Lucifer conversation, 2025)

Source: `Epi-Logos BimbaDB/Root/5/5-1/Christ-Antichrist and Logos thorugh the lens of lucifer.md`, a ChatGPT conversation. **Your prompts are the authorial material; the replies are quarry, with facts to check.** It is internal corpus: provenance only, never a public citation.

**The thesis we can state boldly.** Everything in this half (Hegel's *Aufhebung*, Jung's shadow, Aurobindo's compassion, the colonial inversion, the re-defined God's "capacity to fall") implies that we need a **coherent notion of evil**. The Antichrist is the right symbol for it, with Jung as the warrant.

Your formulation is: "Lucifer is the whole that becomes a part and struggles to find the whole again… the alignment between wholeness and goodness." QL lets us finish it:

- **The fall itself is not evil.** The whole becoming a part is differentiation, `0/1` itself.
- **Evil begins when the part stops struggling toward the whole and claims to be it.** This is the `1` playing the `0`, the appropriation `+2`, the crossed subject `Ø`.
- **This answers Jung's objection to *privatio boni*.** In *Aion* ¶74–75 Jung rejects evil as a "mere diminution of good" and insists that "one must… take evil rather more substantially". In our account evil has no substance of its own, but it is a real **operation**: it does something, to someone, with measurable consequences. That keeps the old insight that evil is no second principle beside God, and meets Jung's demand that it be real.
- ***Hāl* closes the circle.** Whole, holy, health and heal, with *wealth* and *weal* beside them: goodness aligned with wholeness, and evil as the un-wholing of a whole by one of its parts.

## 4. Nietzsche: the death announced in the marketplace

- **Death in the market.** In *The Gay Science* §125 the madman lights a lantern in the bright morning and runs **into the marketplace** to announce the death of God, among people who already do not believe and who laugh.
- **The birth where the death was announced.** The imago Dei's organising power did not vanish. It flowed into the place where the death was announced. Exchange inherited providence: the "invisible hand", the market that "decides", and growth as a promise of salvation.
- **Early and late at once,** in your sense:
  - **Early:** Roman money was struck in the temple of **Juno Moneta** (whence *money* and *mint*); value has been minted under a god's name from the start. Ancient writers derived *Moneta* from *monere*, to warn; modern etymologists dispute that derivation.
  - **Late:** "In God We Trust" went onto US coins from 1864 and onto paper money from 1957, after becoming the national motto in 1956. The motto literally makes trust in God a denomination.
  - **The theory:** Nietzsche's *Genealogy* II §4 derives guilt (*Schuld*) from debt (*Schulden*). Benjamin's fragment "Capitalism as Religion" (1921) calls capitalism a cult that creates guilt and debt with no redemption. One sentence of Benjamin is enough.
- **The present.** Our era makes this economic foundation visible because the world has dis-organised into an astronomical wealth gap that threatens a top-heavy collapse: the un-finite at civilisational scale.
- **The root of all evil.** 1 Timothy 6:10 reads "**the love of money** is the root of all evil", not money itself. That precision serves you. The evil is the love, the ego's attachment to the empty holder: money as the mathematical God, a pure measure that can stand for anything and therefore occupies the place of the `0`. It effaces the Subject and enforces the crossed subject. "Ye cannot serve God and mammon" (Matt. 6:24) can stand beside it.
