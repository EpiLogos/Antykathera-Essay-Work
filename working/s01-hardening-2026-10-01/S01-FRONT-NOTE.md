This is the foundation section, §0/1, of *Confronting the Limit*, an essay in eight sections whose other seven are in development. Its six movements follow the six positions of Quaternal Logic, from `#0` to the return `5→0`, and each piece of notation is introduced where it first appears. Notes are endnotes in Chicago style, grouped by movement after the text, and the sources of each movement follow them. The essay's arguments, concepts and sources also form a linked field, mapped at [oi.epi-logos.org/essay](https://oi.epi-logos.org/essay/). The map offers depth and routes of return, and everything the argument needs is printed here.

Text and field were made together with AI agents, which drafted, structured, checked and kept in relation material that the author directed, rewrote and accepted. That arrangement is part of the essay's subject: an experiment in what freedoms arise from remaining a steward of relations more than the writer of every word.
