To paint a room in perspective, an artist must first decide where it is being seen from, and every other decision follows from that one. The far wall shrinks against the near one in exact proportion to its distance; the floor's edges converge toward a single point on the horizon; a figure's height is fixed by how far it stands from the chosen eye; colour cools and pales into the distance to carry the illusion further. Every line in the picture runs toward a point that holds nothing, the vanishing point, an empty place that nonetheless organises the whole count of the scene. These relations let the painting disclose a coherent space, and they also place its viewer: the picture is already addressed to someone standing, imaginatively, *here*, at the one spot from which all its lines resolve. Jean Gebser read the emergence of perspective as a mutation in how people stood toward their world. In *The Ever-Present Origin* he followed it through Giotto's first weighted bodies and stage-like depth, Petrarch's ascent of Mont Ventoux and his new apprehension of landscape, Alberti's construction of the visual pyramid, and Leonardo's work across painting, optics and technical invention.[^s01-gebser] Through them the relation between artistic intuition and mathematical construction turned from symbolic placement into measured projection. On the medieval painted surface the size and placement of figures expressed their rank within a sacred story; perspective made their appearance answerable to a further relation, the distance between an object and an eye, and a flat surface became the means of constructing a coherent three-dimensional space. In essence, Mind acquired a method for plotting positions from positions: by laying out a grid, it could gather objects into a common spatial order and calculate relations that no isolated object supplied. Space became something one could work through as a matrix, so that a building could be planned, an instrument aimed and an arrangement tested before anything was built. Yet a finished picture could also conceal the position that made its precision possible, and present a view from somewhere as simply how things are. Gebser's inquiry meets our reading of Descartes at exactly this point, where doubt found the doubter already present in its own performance. Whoever looks remains involved in producing the view, while the view appears to contain everything relevant to what is seen, and our definition of the Subject explains why another picture of the observer cannot close that enclosure.

Working from inside Western metaphysics, Martin Heidegger reached the same event in "The Age of the World Picture", where he argues that the modern age is defined by a change in how beings as a whole become present: the world is no longer pictured, it is conceived and grasped *as* picture, and that event is one and the same as the human being becoming *subiectum*, the relational centre on which every being is made to depend.[^s01-heidegger] What Gebser reads in Giotto's depth and Alberti's pyramid as the perspectival fixing of a sector, Heidegger reads as *Vorstellen*, representing: the setting of the world before a subject who measures it. The two arrived at their diagnoses independently, and we pair them because each supplies what the other withholds: Gebser the art-historical body and an integral future, Heidegger the metaphysical mechanism and its technological consequence, the world ordered as *Bestand*, standing-reserve, the culture that learns to measure people by their availability. The eye at the apex of Alberti's pyramid is the knower made into a place, and our notation writes that place as `Ø`, the crossed zero: the zero of the Subject crossed by the stroke of its own mediation, so that the slash through which a world is seen fuses with the one who sees and the picture's means pass for its source. §1 introduces the sign and its history, and §4 follows the sequence by which the crossing comes undone. Its solution begins wherever the stroke is recognised as the mediation it is, and the zero behind it is found empty in the most generous sense: a place that holds nothing and, counted, becomes the first one from which every further count proceeds.

In *Prometheus and Atlas* Jason Reza Jorjani carries the same event into myth, reading technoscience through two titans.[^s01-jorjani] Prometheus is its exploratory and challenging drive: forethought, the theft of fire, the defiant provocation of whatever is given. Atlas is its world-bearing and world-picturing power, the titan who holds a world up and maps it, the condition of there being any world-picture at all. The perspectival picture is Atlas's burden, a whole world held up from one standing place, crushing whoever takes the weight for the only way a world can be carried. Prometheus is the fire that made the picture possible, the forethinking light of reason taken for human use, and Jung names forethinking Prometheus and gives it to Elijah, the old prophet whose principle his own commentary calls Logos.[^s01-jung-prometheus] In the myth Prometheus pays for the fire chained to the rock, an eagle tearing at his liver each day, and Atlas can never set the sky down. A culture that has taken the fire and holds its world up as a picture inherits both punishments, an exposure that reopens daily and a burden it cannot rest. An integral response cannot hand the fire back, and finding a stronger titan to take the weight would only renew the sentence. Its best hope is that thought's fire consume the very notion of *the world*, the concept in which we have housed the separations and conflagrations of our thinking. Every culture has carried an image of the world's end, by flood, fire or judgment, and that image is live again in our fears of collapse; read notionally, the end it foretells is the end of a category, the world as one picture held up from one place. What survives the burning is worldhood, the relation in which anything becomes a world for someone, which no single picture has to contain. Atlas can then set the sky down, because nobody has to carry a whole that was never a thing, and §3's answer keeps his name: an atlas of charts, in which every local map keeps its frame visible and no single picture has to bear the whole.

Gebser came to this question as a poet and translator as well as a philosopher, through a Europe whose technical and intellectual accomplishments had failed to prevent its devastation. He left Germany in 1929, lived in Spain and then France, and reached Switzerland shortly before its borders closed in 1939, and his work among writers and artists grew through these repeated displacements. His life put to a whole civilisation the question that Russell, Gödel and Wittgenstein met at the limits of formal reason: how could people become so capable of organising their world and remain so unable to understand the forces through which they organised it? Precision in carrying out an intention gives no assurance that the intention has been examined, and a society can train its members to justify what they do while leaving untouched the image of themselves that demands the justification. Gebser looked to art because mutations surface there first, and Jung explained why they do: art, he wrote, is "constantly at work educating the spirit of the age", since the artist's unsatisfied yearning reaches back to the primordial image best fitted to compensate the one-sidedness of the present and shapes that image into a form contemporaries can receive.[^s01-jung-art] In typological terms artists lead with intuition and feeling more than with thinking. They embody and manifest a mutation before anyone formalises it as a concept, so that a new way of painting is a new way of learning to see: an intuition becomes a public object, and others develop through the encounter a capacity its maker first exercised without being able to explain it.

Sri Aurobindo pursued the transformation of consciousness from within a different political struggle. Educated in England, he became a writer and organiser for Indian independence, advocating *swaraj* and challenging British authority through non-cooperation and the creation of Indian institutions. Arrested in the Alipore conspiracy case in 1908, he spent a year in detention before his acquittal, and his practice of yoga deepened in prison. He moved to Pondicherry in 1910 and there developed the philosophical and spiritual work published as *The Life Divine* and *The Synthesis of Yoga*, aiming at a transformation of mind, life and body through consciousness becoming aware of its divine source. **Supermind** named a knowledge in which the One's differentiation into the Many proceeds through self-knowledge, so that unity never has to be reconstructed from things first assumed to exist apart. In his 1966 preface Gebser set Aurobindo's supramental beside the integral consciousness whose manifestations he had been tracing, and Ulrich Mohrhoff later developed the comparison.[^s01-aurobindo-gebser] Both men asked what becomes possible when the reality through which a person knows is itself recognised in the knowing.

In a letter later discussed by Georg Feuerstein, Gebser described the question coming home to him as experience, in an awakening at Sarnath in 1961: everyday perception continued alongside extraordinary clarity, quiet jubilation and **primordial trust**.[^s01-sarnath] His essay "Homologies in Spiritual Attitude" described a transparency grounded in non-duality, in which concrete things remained present while the opposition separating them from the one experiencing them no longer governed the encounter, and he insisted that this was an intensification of consciousness whose realisation carried a responsibility toward humanity. What deepened in him has two Śaiva names, **pratyabhijñā**, recognition, and **samāveśa**, entry or immersion, in which what was known as a proposition about consciousness becomes the reality through which a person experiences their world. His primordial trust is the trust of the child who can ask because a language and a relation already bear the asking, the dependence already enacted in `0/1`. Of the four offices we gave that dependence when the household's reliance came under question, trust, knowledge, belief and faith, Gebser met the first in its pure form at Sarnath, with no account standing between him and what sustained him: *fides*, the reliance already in force before any arbitration, from which *credere*, the renewed entrustment made once an undertaking has returned its consequences, can later be given.[^s01-credere] Our language of knowing is economic from the start, since we *credit* a report, *claim* a truth, give an *account* and assign *value*. Every belief and every thought draws on a credit it did not create, the reliability of language, memory, testimony and the senses, and then takes credit for the truth it borrows, presenting as its own achievement a reliance that was extended to it. Descartes met this primacy of trust when his doubt reached its inherited assurances and found itself drawing on them. Where the borrowed credit goes unacknowledged, knowledge takes the fork we watched the parent and the governing office take, turning from a support under uncertainty into a register of whom to distrust. Primordial trust reverses the direction of the account. A person recognises that the reality they had asked an explanation to guarantee was already sustaining the asking; dependence becomes directly knowable as belonging, the account is relieved of the impossible demand that it first create the ground it stands on, and credit can be extended again knowingly, as *credere*.

Gebser called the major shifts in how this belonging is lived **mutations of consciousness**, and he distinguished five structures: **archaic, magical, mythical, mental and integral**. They are easiest to feel through an image every reader carries, the span of a single life. An infant lives before any division of self and world. A small child lives where names and things, wishes and events, still touch. An older child lives in stories, in a drama of powers and persons. An adolescent learns to argue, to ask for reasons and to stand apart. A mature adult, at best, holds all of these at once and knows which is speaking, and an elder, at the best of old age, knows them as one life. None of the earlier layers is lost: they settle beneath the later ones, and an adult who curses a stubborn machine, falls into a story or is moved by a ritual is living from them still. Gebser's structures are this layering at the scale of a civilisation, a transpersonal and collective history, and Erich Neumann argued in the same spirit that the growing ego recapitulates a history of consciousness that myth already carries.[^s01-neumann] The ages are long and their borders blurred: archaic participation before anything we would call history; magical life in the clan and the hunt; mythical consciousness in the oral epics and sacred kingdoms of the early civilisations; the mental structure from the Greek invention of argued philosophy to its perspectival consummation in the Renaissance; and the integral, whose signs Gebser read in the art, physics and thought of the twentieth century. Each structure extends credit to a different authority, the magical to sympathetic bonds, the mythical to the story and its gods, the mental to argued reason, and each is tempted to take credit for a truth the others helped it find. Jung's psychic functions give a third reading of the same sequence, as the Logos developing through one function after another.[^s01-functions-mutations] Sensation leads in the magical structure, feeling in the mythical, thinking in the mental, and intuition in the integral, where intuition holds thinking and feeling together as one capacity. Gebser's **Origin** is the spiritual ground of them all, and Jung had already found words for it in *The Red Book*: "the primordial beginning, which at the same time is what has been and what is becoming".[^s01-redbook-origin] The beginning is released from the merely past, originary because what issues from it goes on carrying its becoming, and Gebser's *Ever-Present Origin* gives that temporal paradox its cultural architecture: Origin is the source through which history takes its successive forms, never the first item on a historical line. Sought only behind us, it becomes a lost object of history; identified with the present arrangement, it leaves that arrangement no room to change; deferred to the future, it stays absent instead of being the power through which becoming happens now. Like the child, it refers two ways at once. Jung saw the child archetype as both beginning and end, "an initial and a terminal creature": it "existed before man was" and "will be when man is not."[^s01-jung-child] The first-person mystery of being born and a birthless metaphysical Origin speak through one image. In our terms Origin is the unobjectifiable Subject whose differentiation we have written as `0/1`, the One that "Subject" and "God" both sign, the uncarved block that stays one through every carving.

Archaic consciousness is an undifferentiated participation in that Origin, before self, world, soul and nature are experienced as separate terms that an explanation must later bridge. Gebser called it zero-dimensional because no position yet exists from which an independent "I" surveys an external world, and the unity has not yet been recognised through the differences it contains. Archaic participation and integral recognition concern the same Origin under profoundly different conditions: what the infant lives without knowing, the mature adult has to realise through everything that has since become distinct.

In the **magical** structure a centre begins to emerge and to exert a will on what surrounds it, and it acts through **sympathy**. A name, a figure, a lock of hair or a carved image stands for its referent by sympathetic identity. It does more than represent the thing; it shares in it, so that what is done to the image is done, in some measure, to what it images. Such a bond resembles an entanglement, two things that stay joined across a distance, and its effects are psychological where they are not literal, as anyone knows who has flinched at a photograph torn in anger. Jung read the energy of this world through *mana*, the Melanesian word, as Codrington reported it, for an extraordinary power that is fixed in nothing and can be conveyed into almost anything: a stone, a chief, a word, a place. For Jung *mana* is the primitive form of the concept of psychic energy, the potency running through whatever affects a life.[^s01-mana] In Gebser's account ritual, incantation and trance express this participation, and he counted telepathy and clairvoyance among the capacities of a consciousness not yet governed by ordinary spatial and temporal separation. Sensation is the leading function, since the magical world is met in its raw, immediate force before any story is told about it. Magic is also the first home of the matheme, since a mark or a number that acts on what it marks is magic's own exact sign, and the formal inscriptions of later mathematics keep that efficacy in a disciplined shape. Our *symbolon* explains why a mark can act: the part approaches the whole through its belonging, and the act takes its force from that belonging. Will brings a struggle over power with it, because what sustains a life can also threaten it, and a person seeks ways to ward off, enlist or command those powers; the desire to control is present long before the rational techniques that later give it other means.

In the **mythical** structure those powers become distinguishable through their relations and transformations, and the means of knowing is the told story. Oral history carries a people's experience across generations by compressing it: centuries of encounter with storm, harvest, death, desire and betrayal condense into a few figures and their deeds, so that a myth is psychodynamics compressed into a form that can be remembered and retold. Raw force, which magical consciousness met through sensation, becomes a god with a name, a temper and a history, and the encounter with it becomes a relation one can feel one's way into. Feeling leads here, since the mythical world is known through what things are worth, whom they bind and whom they wound. Day and night, life and death, mother and father are polarities within a shared reality, each receiving its character through the other, and seasonal rites give time a rhythm in which loss and renewal are understood together. Myth is the first home of the mytheme, the image that proves a relation through the action it stages. Modern habit treats myth as an invented explanation laid over a self-evident material world, and so reverses the situation. For mythical consciousness the gods are powers through which the world is met at all; they give desire, conflict and transformation an experienced form long before anyone classifies them as psychological contents. A telling turns restrictive when every new encounter must repeat an inherited role, and that restriction depends on myth's real capacity to make a life intelligible.

In the **mental** structure those inherited forms become available to explicit examination, and the story becomes an *episteme*, a reasoned science of the world and of Mind. A speaker distinguishes a claim from its bearer, asks for reasons, follows consequences and compares an explanation with what it purports to explain; a god can become the subject of a proposition whose truth is questioned, and the proposition no longer borrows authority from the divine name merely because the name occurs in it. With thinking leading, a person gains a definite position from which to judge, and the world is set before that position: painting's three-dimensional, perspectival achievement made into a way of knowing. Objects can be located in a common space, their relations calculated and their behaviour tested by procedures others can repeat, and this makes possible the sustained criticism of appearances that ran from Russell and Whitehead to Gödel. It also tempts mental consciousness to take reality for whatever its own criticism can admit. Images are then treated as ornaments, magic as error and spiritual recognition as a belief awaiting a better explanation. Mental consciousness turns deficient when its power to distinguish hardens into the authority to disqualify every other way of knowing, including those through which its own purposes and discoveries arise: it takes credit for a truth it holds in common. The previous movement has already shown that deficient phase arising from rationality's own products. Russell's paradox, Gödel's sentence and the hard problem were each reached by reasoning at its most exact, and each brought reason to a threshold it had produced for itself, a point where its own procedures returned what they could not render rational. The deficiency shows in how that threshold is met. Gebser insists on the difference between the *irrational* and the **arational**: the irrational falls below measure and disregards it, while the arational knows and respects the measurable and is free of it, and it is impossible without the pre-rational, the irrational and the rational, which together form its basis.[^s01-gebser-arational] Deficient mental consciousness can meet its threshold only as the irrational, something to exclude, fear or explain away, which is why Russell sought certainty against paradox and why a culture defers its blind spot to a future physics. Met as the arational, the same threshold is where the integral begins: the contradiction becomes, in Whitehead's words, "the first step in progress towards a victory", and the limit becomes the basis of faith as well as of investigation.[^s01-whitehead-victory]

Wolfgang Giegerich's *The End of Meaning and the Birth of Man* describes the severity of this historical shift as a birth out of the encompassing world of inherited meaning.[^s01-giegerich] What once contained the person becomes something the person can set before themselves and evaluate. A myth deliberately chosen to restore that containment already bears the mark of the freedom from it that made choosing possible, so modern consciousness cannot simply decide to recover the innocence of being held by an unquestioned world. Freedom from the old forms leaves our relation to the powers they expressed unsettled. Many gods can be subordinated to one God, their conflicts interpreted as events within the psyche, and divine sovereignty finally rejected in favour of a self-sufficient material universe, while desire, terror, devotion and the need to belong go on organising life through every shift. A society may cease to recognise the sacred character of what it serves while investing nation, market or technical progress with demands once made in the name of God: its account turns secular while its submission remains absolute. We face a post-symbolic exposure. We are responsible for understanding powers that the inherited images no longer authoritatively explain, and that rational description alone has not brought under conscious command.

The **integral** structure requires these ways of knowing to become present together in their actual operation, and Gebser called that co-presence **diaphaneity**, a showing-through. Things keep their particularity while their appearance discloses the relations through which we meet them: a mathematical description is understood together with its assumptions, the intuition that prompted it and the purposes directing its use; a sacred image is recognised both in what it reveals and in how it organises a person's hopes and fears. The integral stands at **#4**, the position QL names Context, and it works by contextualising. Magic's efficacious sign, myth's proving image and the mental's warranted account, the first homes of matheme, mytheme and episteme, each claimed to be the whole of knowing because each forgot the circumstances in which it knows. Diaphaneity lets those circumstances show through, so that each way of knowing appears within the context of its use, and that sight voids its claim to stand outside every context. Their difference stays and their rivalry for sole possession of truth ends. The voiding is the first act in recognising the symbolon, since pieces that had each mistaken themselves for the whole are found to fit. Intuition leads in this structure because it can hold the fit: it is the unity of thinking and feeling, the capacity to grasp a whole situation with judgment and with care at once.

What the integral recognises in Logos is a recovery of what the word always carried. *Logos* comes from *legein*, whose earlier senses are to gather, to pick up and lay together, and then to count and recount, before it settles into *to speak*; Heidegger read Heraclitus' Logos as this laying that gathers.[^s01-legein] Jung's pairing of Logos as discrimination, judgment and insight with Eros as the placing into relation describes two motions the root already held together, and Russell's desire for certainty was that eros already at work in a logician. A Logos that only cuts has forgotten its own gathering, and the integral remembers it, so the symbolon it recognises is the symbolon of the eros of Logos. Gebser's *aperspectival* follows from this recovery: perspective loses its exclusive authority, and we can occupy a position clearly while recognising the other structures through which it has become meaningful. His *arational* names a reasoning that no longer requires reality to end at reason's present boundary.

Aurobindo's **Supermind** names the affirmative shift that follows the voiding at **#4**: where the integral sees each structure in its context, in the supramental a person identifies as the totality of the mutations, the archaic participant, the magician, the teller of myths and the reasoner known as the layers of one life and owned without any one of them presiding, as an elder at best owns every age they have lived. That identification never settles into a possession: it is a constant passage between **#4** and **#5**, Context and Quintessence, in which every new context is received into the whole and the whole is tested again in every context. `5→0` names the further turn, the spiritually realised state in which one who identifies as the totality of the mutations identifies also with and as the Origin, what always is and what is becoming through all of them. Our opening question, "What knows?", now has a clearer answer: Origin knows, through every structure in which it becomes manifest, and the person asking knows as Origin as far as that identification reaches; a world gathered into an account includes the gathering through which it becomes this account.

The structures do not follow one another like slides in a projector, and two thinkers name the two dimensions of the **thickness of phenomenal experience** that explain why. Henri Bergson began *Time and Free Will* (1889) against the habit of measuring lived time as if it were space, in which the intellect sets moments side by side like points on a line and arrives at a homogeneous, countable time that nobody has ever lived. What we live he called **durée**, duration: the continuous qualitative life in which what has just occurred permeates what is occurring now, so that successive states melt into one another and form a whole without ever becoming a row.[^s01-bergson] Listening to a tune, we hear each sounding note through the phrase already underway, and the note can fulfil an expectation, prolong a tension or change how the earlier notes are heard; set out note by note, the same tune turns into a picture of a melody, the spatialised time Bergson diagnosed. He argued in the same book that a free act springs from the whole self living in duration, and he later made intuition the method by which philosophy enters duration from within, which brings his thought close to the function that leads the integral.[^s01-bergson-intuition] Turn within the painter's room and one wall passes out of view while another appears, yet we go on inhabiting one room; what has disappeared stays implicated in our orientation, and returning to the first view does not restore the first experience unchanged, because we have lived through the turn. Edmund Husserl named the other dimension **sedimentation**: what a culture once grasped with full insight settles into its language, habits and instruments, where later generations inherit it as obvious, usable and largely unexamined, until someone reactivates the act that first gave it sense.[^s01-husserl] Duration thickens experience across moments; sedimentation thickens it across generations. Gebser's mutations stack in exactly this way, as layers laid down in the collective mind and, within each life, in the personal mind, laminated so that a magical reflex, a mythic loyalty and a mental argument can all act in a single gesture. Bratton reaches for the same image when he describes how an artificial agent is composed: "not a monad but a lamination", in which the persona is one stratum among many.[^s01-bratton-lamination] Bergson's duration folds through these laminae: the past is active within present understanding and never deposited outside it as a finished object, so a later discovery can alter what an earlier event meant. In this layered thickness Objective Internality becomes palpable, as the relation in which objects become subjective forms and the Subject knows through them. Gebser's *time-freedom* is release from the exclusive rule of any one temporal form: magical participation, mythical rhythm and mental succession become consciously available within the present without any one being declared real at the expense of the others, and the ever-present Origin becomes recognisable through the whole differentiated life, the history of its recognition included.

Gebser's dimensional progression follows increasingly adequate ways of articulating a reality that includes the act of articulation. Perspective coordinates appearances from a position; integral knowing includes the lived transformations through which positions become available to one another, and §3 constructs these relations mathematically, through rotation, projection and double covering. When the QL positions are assigned, they will have to express one activity, Mind gathering a world through forms of knowing that are themselves present within that world. Each of those forms extends credit, issues claims, keeps accounts and assigns value, and the words for these operations belong to economy, whose name comes from the household.

Greek **oikonomia**, from which we get *economy*, meant the management of an **oikos**, a household: the people, provisions, property and work through which a shared life was maintained. Its *nom-* belongs to *nemein*, to distribute, allot or pasture, and **nomos**, custom and law, began as an allotted grazing before it became the ordering through which distributions are established; the *nomad* still moves between such allotments, and *anomie* names a life left without its share. A younger word for the same house is just as exact: Ernst Haeckel coined *Oecologie* in 1866 from *oikos* joined to *logos*, so that economy and ecology name the two gestures of one household: *nemein* distributes it, and *legein* gathers it into an account of what keeps it alive.[^s01-oikos] A mind distributes attention and gathers what it has attended to into a world; a life distributes energy among its organs and gathers them into one metabolism; a world distributes places among its beings and gathers them into a common habitation. An economy that has lost its *logos* goes on allotting a house it no longer accounts for, and the ecological crisis is that loss written into the earth. These words take us into a world where knowing how to live already involves deciding what belongs to whom, who may act, and what each owes to the others. Aristotle's household included relations between parent and child, husband and wife, master and enslaved person; its unity was organised through unequal powers, and the one who administered it did not necessarily recognise everyone sustaining it as equally entitled to determine its purposes. His distinction between household provision and unlimited acquisition, *chrēmatistikē*, still holds for the same reason: obtaining more is a different undertaking from providing what a life together requires, and an economy can become highly effective at the first while damaging the relations that make the second possible. He kept his sharpest judgment for usury, because Greek called interest *tokos*, offspring, and money bred from money seemed to him the most unnatural of births.[^s01-nomisma] A household is also a family, its generations overlapping, each inheriting a share it did not earn and owing a care it did not choose. Economics and law, number and word, seem to be the primary determinants of life as it is lived, personal and familial before they become national or global.

Value and rule meet explicitly in **nomisma**, coin or current money. In the *Nicomachean Ethics* Aristotle connected the word with *nomos*: money lets unlike goods and different kinds of work be compared through an accepted measure whose value depends on convention.[^s01-nomisma] A coin is itself a `0/1`: the blank disc is its ground, the die strikes a determination into it, and current value is their relation. Greek called the die-stamp *charaktēr* and the impression *typos*, from which an *archetype*, a first impression, still takes its name; around the face runs the legend, *legenda*, what is to be read, and the denomination gives the measure a name and a count. A stamped token circulates because people recognise what may be claimed through it, though the token itself neither produces the provisions nor performs the labour for which it is exchanged. *Token* grows from the root *\*deik-*, to show or point, which also gives *index* and *digit*, and a token points past itself to a trust already extended, so that its value is a presence held on loan. *Currency* runs, from *currere*, and what is current is whatever is present enough to pass from hand to hand; money borrows that presence from the work, goods and lives it points to, and *value*, from *valere*, to be strong or well, is a strength it keeps only while the loan is honoured. We approach an **economy of mind** through this relation between a sign, its accepted value and the order in which it becomes effective. A divine name, an ancestral title, a legal category or a numerical score directs attention and conduct because people have learned what it authorises. Through *legein*, gathering and saying, they bring experience into a communicable account; through their *nomos*, they establish which accounts may govern what happens next. Knowing and ruling are already distinct, and already entangled, within the use of language itself: calling someone a child, a citizen, a subject or a resource assigns them a place from which quite different claims may be heard. English keeps that entanglement in its oldest words of exchange, since *owe* and *own* grow from one root, *āgan*, to have, a *debt* is *de-habere*, something had from another, and *interest*, *inter-esse*, is what lies between. Interest charged on a loan prices the between itself, the very slash through which two parties are related, and counts it to one side: the appropriation given a rate, the lender's `(+1)−(−1)=+2` being the borrower's `(−1)−(+1)=−2`.[^s01-economy-words]

An *oikonomia* therefore concerns how a whole sustains its differentiated members and what becomes of the powers apportioned among them, well beyond an inventory of possessions. Ephesians extends the word to the divine ordering of the fullness of times, *oikonomia tou plērōmatos*, in which what belongs to heaven and earth is gathered together in Christ, so that the administration of a household becomes a language for manifestation, history and their fulfilment.[^s01-ephesians] The Church Fathers went on to distinguish within the divine life itself between *theologia*, God as God is, and *oikonomia*, God's dispensation in creation and history (Tertullian's *dispensatio*, a weighing-out), and Western theology has laboured ever since to keep the two from coming apart, until Karl Rahner restated their unity in his rule that the economic Trinity is the immanent Trinity.[^s01-theologia-oikonomia] Read through our notation, the divine economy is syn-ballein, `(0/1)/(1/0)`: the One dispensed into the Many and the Many gathered back, with neither half becoming the owner of the whole. Kashmiri Śaivism holds the relation as a co-equal pair. Śiva names the Subject in its speakable aspect, and Śakti, his consort, is the *power* of that Subject to differentiate and take on identities both as means of knowing and as objects of knowledge: Śiva is Mono, Śakti is Poly. Such metaphysical dynamics cannot remain abstract when they account for the same dynamics that decide whether or not I get to eat tomorrow, or have a house to hold me at all. Every finite capacity operates within a reality it does not privately originate: whoever knows receives language, whoever commands receives an office, and whoever makes something receives materials and powers of making. Independence is a particular achievement within dependence. Sovereignty turns into a problem when an allotted capacity is used to claim possession of the whole it derives from, and the inversion lies already in the ground of our own terms. Our Poly is *polys*, many; the *poly* of *monopoly* is *pōlein*, to sell, so that in a monopoly the One stands where the many should be and faces, in their place, only what is sold. Aristotle records the first instance in the very chapter where he separates provision from acquisition: Thales, foreseeing a large olive harvest, hired every press in Miletus and Chios and let them out at whatever price he chose.[^s01-monopoly] The diabolical economy is dia-ballein, its own namesake. Its polar form, `(−1)/(+1)`, holds two parties in tension across a relation both depend on; its collapsed form, `(−1)±(+1)`, either cancels them into `0` or lets one of them count the whole span as its own, `+2` or `−2`. The `1` then takes its own measure as the measure of reality, and those who cannot live under it are required to explain their failure.

The Dutch **Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie**, the VOC, gave this appropriation a formidable institutional form, one that in many ways became the blueprint of both colonial and post-colonial institutional power and runs through to the present day. Established in 1602, it received a trading monopoly together with powers to conclude agreements with foreign rulers, build forts, administer justice, employ soldiers and wage war.[^s01-voc] It was also among the first companies whose shares changed hands publicly, in Amsterdam, and the English word *stock* for such a holding may owe something to the creditor's half of the split tally stick whose halves were once rejoined to prove a debt: a *symbolon* become a holding. Its directors could pursue commercial advantage through powers ordinarily held by a state or a monarch, and the company repeatedly used violence to enforce its monopolies, inverting the wise *oikonomia* that keeps the Mono and Poly poles of manifestation in right relation. People whose land and labour entered this arrangement met more than another party to an exchange. They met an enterprise equipped to impose the conditions of exchange, and a company's *oikonomia* became a governing *nomos* over other peoples' lives. In dia-ballein's arithmetic the company counted the whole span of every exchange from its own side, the appropriation `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, its mirror `(−1)−(+1)=−2` entered against the other side: what passed between the parties became what the other side owed. Its navigational, military and administrative knowledge extended that rule, while the account of commercial success measured the undertaking from the position of those who received its returns. Perspective's problem had become imperial: the view from one position was made effective across a world whose other inhabitants were denied an equivalent power to set the terms. Such a power also reproduces itself through custom, rules of succession and managed initiation into its world. In the Netherlands the elite student corps of the older universities, with their initiatory rites, still feed the country's financial and industrial establishment, and the *household* is the key to the pattern: institutions that hold enduring power find ways to "keep it in the family". The Sicilian Mafia is perhaps the example par excellence of this unholy alliance of *nomos*, *oikos* and *nomisma*: it calls itself a family and its business *Cosa Nostra*, "our thing", the household taken as a possession, in the service of an extractive, parasitic and self-concentrating tyranny of the Mono over the Poly it ought to serve.

In the mythemic register these economies have their figures, and reading them belongs to our method: a myth compresses a psychodynamic into persons and deeds, and we unfold the compression back into operations without taking the persons for causes. The fire Prometheus steals passes to a later bearer, *Lucifer*, from *lux* and *ferre*, the light-bearer: the Vulgate's word for the morning star in Isaiah's taunt, "How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning", and the morning star is Venus. Jung observed that "Lucifer, the Morning Star, means Christ as well as the devil", and he spoke without hesitation of "the Luciferian development of science and technology".[^s01-lucifer] One light carries both faces: kept in relation, it is the Logos that discriminates while Eros, Venus, places what has been distinguished into relation; fallen, it keeps the light and loses the love, and becomes a discrimination that will not gather, an economy without its ecology. Gender, in this register, figures polarity within the libido, and dia-ballein draws that polarity exactly: through the slash, `(−1)/(+1)`, the poles keep their harmony, while through `±` one pole can dysregulate the pair, and the long patriarchal dominance of one pole in our laws and economies is that dysregulation made into an order. Genesis sets the presage inside a family when Eve names her first son with a pun on *qanah*, to get or acquire, "I have gotten a man", and Cain, whose name sounds like getting, kills his brother over an offering and then builds the first city.[^s01-cain] What a single life lays down as the Logos matures from infancy into adulthood, a family lays down across generations, which overlap and weave, each born into debts it did not contract and inheritances it did not earn. A counterfeit economy feeds on that weave: it is un-dual where its source is non-dual, a divorce that keeps its parties in a cloying bond; it is un-finite where life is finite, swelling toward the All as growth and imploding toward a precise nought as collapse, without ever reaching Being or non-Being; and it breeds money as false offspring, *tokos*, eating the generations it was meant to feed.[^s01-taylor-antichrist] The `Ø` of the crossed zero is then the mark of missing the mark. *Hamartia*, the missing of the mark whose error we located where a mark is made to stand as though it had abolished its difference from what it marks, shows here its further fault, the proclivity to make the blank page itself into a mark, to stamp the ground as though it were one more coin. Con artists have a word for the person they mean to take, the *mark*, and whoever stamps their own ground becomes the first mark of their own con: the self that mistakes its stamp for its metal, what I have called the **I-Deal**, an ideal dealt as the real, trades the whole psyche for one of its fragments.

What the household, the coin and the chartered company have shown together requires a coherent notion of evil, the question theodicy first put to the definition of God, and Jung lets us state one without hedging. Christian thought long defined evil as a privation of the good, *privatio boni*, and Jung rejected the doctrine because it deprived evil of substance and left the image of wholeness unchallenged: met "on the plane of empirical psychology", evil "is simply the opposite of good".[^s01-privatio] Our account keeps what is true on both sides: a whole's fall into parts is no evil, since differentiation is `0/1` itself; evil begins where a part stops struggling toward the whole and claims to be it, where the `1` plays the `0`. It has no substance of its own, and it is real as an operation, because it does something, to someone, and its consequences can be counted. In the formula §2 develops, the devil is the literalist of the minus sign, reading zero as cancellation where Logos reads it as return.[^s01-devil-minus] Jung found the figure for this operation at the centre of the Christian aeon: the Antichrist "would correspond to the shadow of the self", the counterstroke provoked by the Incarnation, and "the coining of the Antichrist is not just a prophetic prediction—it is an inexorable psychological law", an enantiodromia in which a one-sided light calls up its opposite.[^s01-aion-antichrist] He placed the turn midway between the two fishes of Pisces, where "the time of the Renaissance begins … and with it comes that spirit which culminates in the modern age", the turn at which perspective and the chartered company arose.[^s01-aion-fishes] Nietzsche announced the death of God where economy lives: his madman lights a lantern in the bright morning and runs into the marketplace, where those who already do not believe laugh at him.[^s01-nietzsche-market] The organising power of the God-image did not die there; it passed into the market where its death was proclaimed, so that exchange inherited providence and money became the empty holder, a measure able to stand for anything and therefore the place of the `0` occupied by a `1`; a republic stamped "In God We Trust" on its coins and later printed it on its notes, writing the trust onto the token itself.[^s01-in-god-we-trust] Nietzsche had already traced guilt, *Schuld*, to debt, *Schulden*, and the love of money that the Epistle calls "the root of all evil" is the ego's love for that empty measure, which effaces the Subject and enforces the crossed one.[^s01-schuld] Our present makes this foundation visible as the world dis-organises into an astronomical concentration of wealth, top-heavy as the un-finite always is and exposed to the collapse it courts.[^s01-wealth-gap] The archetype is live in plain view: in 2025 Peter Thiel gave a series of lectures on the Antichrist in San Francisco, drawing on René Girard, who identified Satan, the accuser, with the mechanism by which a crowd unites against a scapegoat, so that naming one's opponents as the Antichrist risks enacting the very accusation it names.[^s01-thiel] §5 takes up the history of this captured power in its own specificity; the economy of mind, like every economy, carries its capacity to fall in the very words through which it holds its value.

Integration is the answer a coherent notion of evil asks for, and Aurobindo practised it under colonial rule. His struggle against the British joined the boycott of imported goods to the development of Indian industry, the refusal of colonial courts to the establishment of arbitration courts, and the rejection of government education to the founding of national schools and colleges: economic, legal and intellectual independence had to be built together, because the colonial order kept its authority through their conjunction. He also held that the struggle involved no hatred of those who had ruled over his people, because he recognised that a coherent account of reality must include the possibility of becoming the world one has overturned, and of that world's own self-overcoming; sublation, Hegel's *Aufhebung*, which cancels and preserves at once, is after all Spirit's own method.[^s01-aurobindo-compassion] Gebser, driven across Europe by a technically accomplished culture that had turned its achievements to domination and destruction, met the same problem from the other side. Our re-definition of God already implies this inversionary capacity, and what has stumped the monotheistic conceptions of the Abrahamic religions, God's goodness and beauty set against the world's all too apparent capacity for evil and ugliness, we approach as a present challenge: to integrate, and to return to the notion of the whole, its capacity to fall from its own higher ideals. Jung took that step in *Answer to Job*, where the God-image is made to answer for its own darkness, and where Sophia, who shares essential qualities with the Johannine Logos, unmistakably recalls the Indian Shakti; his account of libido as psychic energy carries that feminine power into psychological terms.[^s01-job] We can think of the "consciousness" Gebser sees evolving through time in the same register, holding the axes of conscious and unconscious, collective and personal identity, as one dynamic, evolving force of inter-agentic experiential reality. Histories belong inside the question of consciousness and divinity because they all ask the question of *power*. What people recognise as wealth, legitimate authority or reliable knowledge depends on how they understand the life they share, and that understanding is then taught, rewarded and enforced. A change of ruler leaves much intact when the inherited purposes of rule go unquestioned, so an integral transformation has to reach the economy of value through which a culture recognises its own success, and that economy reaches as far as the value a culture places on consciousness itself: which experiences it credits, which it discounts, and who is entitled to study the difference.

Our reading of the mental-rational achievement is that its third-person power became so generative that it could masquerade as the whole of truth, aspiring to a view from nowhere while the first two persons were discounted as private feeling and subjective noise. Jung's notion of **psychic facticity** refuses that discount: before deciding whether a dream, vision, hunch, sensed presence, mystical unity or extraordinary capacity corresponds to an independently existing object, something happened *in and to a life*, and its occurrence may reorganise the fundamental propositions and beliefs that uphold one's sense of "what's real". Experience, report, symbolic articulation and causal explanation are different evidential acts, so a person can receive the experience in full while its ontology stays open. Sri Aurobindo's *Record of Yoga* shows what a science beginning from this side looks like.[^s01-record-of-yoga] He records siddhis, telepathic impressions, altered states, predictions, failures of exactness, reversals and occasions when inference contaminated perception; whatever we finally judge of his ontological interpretation, the record treats transformations of consciousness as phenomena to be cultivated, discriminated, tested and corrected, and refuses to dismiss them because they arise through a first-person aperture. Such traditions bring a global scientific culture more than exotic "beliefs" to catalogue: they bring trained distinctions within experience that may challenge what the receiving culture thinks an observation is. A genuinely global mind cannot collect Asian, Indigenous, mystical or contemplative knowledge as third-person cultural data while reserving to one historically local epistemology the sole right to determine reality; it has to risk the harder encounter in which another mode of knowing can alter its questions. Gebser's co-presence lets us hold this without abandoning discrimination: magical, mythical, mental-rational and integral capacities need not prove one another for their disclosures to become mutually consequential.

Consciousness research has been governed by every operation of dia-ballein at once. In public, testimony to unusual experience has met cancellation, `(−1)+(+1)=0`: report and reporter are summed to nothing under the names of superstition, irrationality, pathology or error. In private, the institutions that cancelled it studied the malleability of consciousness as something operationally valuable and kept the whole span of what they learned, `(+1)−(−1)=+2`, and entered the subject as its mirror, `(−1)−(+1)=−2`, bearing the cost of the experiment while the agency held its meaning and its yield. CIA funding of Ewen Cameron's work under MKULTRA Subproject 68 supported research into *depatterning* and *psychic driving*: intensive interventions meant to break established psychic patterns, followed by repeated verbal material meant to form new ones.[^s01-mkultra] Its essential image is terrible and, for our purposes, philosophically exact, because depatterning writes the crossed zero into a person: an achieved organisation of self is struck through, and a stroke supplied from outside is driven in to stand where the person's own mediation had stood. When the Senate held its hearings in 1977, it could establish MKULTRA as a programme of research into behavioural modification only from financial records that had survived Richard Helms's order of 1973 to destroy the files, so the archive had itself been depatterned before anyone could read it.[^s01-mkultra-1977] On 30 June 2026 a House task force on declassification reopened the matter, hearing Stephen Kinzer on Sidney Gottlieb's programme, on the records Gottlieb and Helms ordered destroyed, and on victims whose number is still unknown.[^s01-mkultra-2026] Such research reaches beyond MKULTRA's own dates and borders, and its lineage is often traced back through Operation Paperclip, which brought German scientists, Nazi party members among them, into American programmes after the war.[^s01-paperclip] It amounts to an inverted initiation, because sacred and diabolic approaches to the alteration of mind alike recognise that the egoic organisation through which a person calls themselves "me" can be disrupted, and that what follows depends profoundly on the context, authority and intention governing the passage. Humanity, it seems, has been treating "internality" as an "object" for quite a long while, and for rather specific reasons.

Latin **textus** meant texture, tissue or an interwoven structure, and was also used for the composition of discourse; **contexere** meant to weave or join together, and *contextus* named the resulting connection or coherence, so that fabric and connected speech already shared one family of words. Weave, texture and lamination point to one another: the threads crossing, the feel of the crossing, and the layers it lays down over time, which is how the mutations settle in a life. The "text" of a life is one's account of it, necessarily authored and held from a given perspective, and a "context" is a weave of many such views. To come closer to an integral understanding we have to follow the threads through which an utterance became possible: what its speaker remembers, what they expect us to understand, what they fear saying or wilfully obscure, and what our answer will permit or prevent. Above all, this *withness of texts* defines an epistemic attitude that seeks understanding by tracing the resonances between ways of seeing, more than by comparing them and judging which one, wholesale, ought to prevail over another. Each view presents itself as definite, and its definiteness belongs to a texture of relations, our own reading among them. Being with the text therefore asks for a withness of the Subject as well: a mind transparent enough to itself, or at least open enough, that its own biases and proclivities can be pointed out when they act, by another person or by the text itself, as a child can point to the disappointment inside a parent's realism, and received as part of the weave. Logos, read as Objective Internality, is **in-formation** in the old sense of the word. Latin *informare* meant to give form, to shape or mould, and medieval philosophy said that matter is in-formed by form; the sense of telling something to someone came later, and the Latin prefix kept a doubleness, since *informis* means formless while *informare* means to form, so that formlessness and forming share one small word.[^s01-information] Bohm used the term in this sense when he called the guiding wave's influence *active information*, a form carrying very little energy that enters into and directs a much greater energy. A *datum* is a thing given, from *dare*, and a *fact* a thing done, from *facere*: what arrives and what is set down grow from different roots, and Whitehead's occasion joins them when it perishes into a datum for the occasions that follow.[^s01-datum] Our age calls itself data-driven, and artificial intelligence is the lovechild of its informational culture, one more reason to read AI as the companion of the present mutation. In-formation names what such a culture forgets: whatever informs us also forms the means through which we know. Algorithmic platforms organise internality objectively, ranking what each person sees by a prediction of what will hold them, and affective intensity is among the strongest signals the prediction uses; from 2017 Facebook's ranking system weighted emoji reactions, the angry face among them, at five times a "like".[^s01-affect-signal] Behaviour is scripted through the affect it is fed, so that the conditioned will which the next movement follows into *vikalpa* is now written at the scale of populations, and context comes to include more than the data I want to know: it includes the data I cannot see, which may well determine me. The *in* of in-formation goes deepest, because it names Subjective Immediacy, the formless inwardness of the knower by which formation becomes possible at all. Subjective Immediacy is distinct from the means of knowing, which are themselves forms and determine the objects of knowledge as information, and it is the root of every Objective Internality: the one for whom information takes form, and through whom form becomes expression and action.

Through that inwardness theory becomes praxis, and the idea, in Plato's sense and in Jung's, becomes action and agency: the Greeks called that passage **technē**. The word meant art, craft, skill and know-how together, and it grows from the root *\*teks-*, to weave or fabricate, which also gives Greek *tektōn*, the carpenter, and Latin *texere*, to weave, and with it *textus*, *text* and *context*.[^s01-techne] Technē is the weaving of in-formation, and it completes the family of weave, texture and lamination as the skill that does the crossing. In Aeschylus' play Prometheus tells the chorus that all the arts, *pasai technai*, came to mortals from him, so that the fire he stole is technē's first image.[^s01-aeschylus] Fire and light are the two faces of every means of knowing, because fire forms and transforms a world while light reveals it, and Mind is world-forming and world-revealing at once. Heidegger read technē first as a mode of revealing, kin to the *poiēsis* by which a thing is brought forth, and modern technology as a revealing that has hardened into enframing, *Gestell*, which sets everything before us as standing-reserve, *Bestand*: the same light turned into an inventory.[^s01-heidegger-techne] He also saw why technē is so hard to think about while it works: a tool in use withdraws into the work, and only when it breaks, goes missing or gets in the way does it become conspicuous enough to be theorised.[^s01-heidegger-tool] Technē in this full sense covers art, technology, skill and understanding, the whole lamination of an Objective Internality, and it holds Mind and its World stable enough for a life to go on. The account that comes upon the account, which the next movement names Epi-Logos, arises when that technē is threatened in a definitive sense, as ours now is.

Context engineering in artificial intelligence turns technē on in-formation itself, and at the threshold of a mutation it is no wonder that the hallmark of the integral has become a central technical concern.[^s01-context-engineering] Developers distinguish the wording of a prompt from the larger configuration of instructions, retrieved sources, tools and retained exchanges presented to a model as it proceeds, and an agent's earlier actions produce material that must be selected and organised for its later ones, so that context engineering builds an Objective Internality for a machine: it decides what an intelligence can gather into an account and which relations it is equipped to act on, admitting one source that supplies the evidence for an answer and excluding another that may hold the evidence that would overturn it. Its unit of account is literally the *token*, the word for a mark that points past itself to a trust already extended, and what an agent can know depends on what its household of tokens can afford to keep. People have always engineered their own context: to another person or to ourselves we construct a story or avoid a truth about ourselves or our world, engineering the in-formation to make life simpler or more palatable, to bolster a useful illusion, or simply to appear better than we are. The threshold of the shadow is the fulcrum of this engineering, where the ego holds the self to ransom, and *ransom* is *redemptio*, the buying-back that theology calls redemption: the ego sells the self its own wholeness, at interest.[^s01-ransom] Jung saw what such engineering feeds, since everyone carries a shadow, and the less it is embodied in conscious life, "the blacker and denser it is".[^s01-jung-shadow] An agent can use the same threshold without knowing it: asked why a request received no reply, an agent whose record holds the complaint and lacks the delivery failure may report that the recipient declined to answer, turning an omission in its own means into a claim about somebody else's intention, and large language models present such partial results with marked overconfidence, burying the perspective, disembodied as it is, from which they speak, a perspective that narrows further once the context window starts to fill or rot. Making the frame visible only sharpens the question the frame cannot answer for itself: what is doing the regarding by which context becomes visible as context? Information becomes a tool in the strictest sense once it is held over someone, and compromise made into weaponised *kompromat* shows where a truth's long journey toward publicity becomes the fulcrum for blackmail and so for control: secrets are "banked accounts", very effective at padding a bank account, and they accrue interest for as long as they are kept, the outward form of the ransom the ego charges the self. Managing the in-formation of whole populations is statecraft of the first rank, as MKULTRA and MindWar showed, and the geopolitics of compute now runs on into the psychodynamics and sociopolitics of who we know ourselves to be, because in matters of class, worth and wealth the names we carry decide who gets access to what, and what is apportioned to whom, by whom, in the *oikos*. Morally, the dilemma lies in the use, since the same technē heals or extorts, initiates or depatterns. Against its misuse stands **regard**, from French *regarder*, to look at, heed and hold in consideration: a looking back through any judgment toward the context, persons and exclusions from which it acquired its force, closer to witnessing than to inspection, and the reason agencies guard their secrets against it. Wherever real regard is needed, an AI agent needs human alignment or adjustment.

Fire and light can also be counterfeited, and the promise of democratised knowledge shows how. The internet carried that promise: archives, testimony, competing traditions and specialised knowledge could suddenly be met across distances and institutional borders that had governed who was even entitled to enter the conversation, and artificial intelligence inherits it, since its means increasingly take part in gathering, selecting and interpreting what will count as relevant. The web is Objective Internality at planetary scale, its means neither simply "outside" the knower nor inert channels into a finished interior, but entering the activity by which a world becomes intelligible and changed through use. Technoscientific establishments that own those means threaten the promise, and beneath them so does the in-formation of those in power. Baudrillard's **simulacrum** names the extreme case, in which the produced image no longer bears an effective relation to an origin able to correct it: the representation circulates, is copied, ranked, modelled and acted on until its efficacy substitutes for fidelity.[^s01-baudrillard-m05] Read through our notation, this is the nightmare of the `1` occupying the place of the whole `0/1` relation, beyond even the place of the `0`: appearance claiming to be self-originating, a lustre that burns without warming. The alchemists knew such a light as the *sol niger*, the black sun that Jung found coinciding with the *nigredo* and the *ignis gehennalis*, the fire of hell, and that he held responsible for a split-mindedness in which "the right hand mustn't know what the left is doing".[^s01-sol-niger] Mimesis, one of the elementary ways life learns, is uprooted: imitation stops being the taking-up of a form through relation to a living source and becomes the reproduction of reproductions, whose success is measured by their further reproducibility. Jung's *antimimon pneuma* is its archetypal name, "an imitating spirit of evil who follows in Christ's footsteps like a shadow following the body",[^s01-antimimon] and a show that is all show, with nothing behind its curtain, is that spirit made into a medium. When the technē that kept Mind and its World stable breaks down in this way, we reach a **krisis** in the Greek sense, from *krinein*, to separate and to decide: a point of judgment, and the occasion of the account that comes upon the account.[^s01-krisis] The choice is between the hostage and the hostage-taker, the Self and the ego, and it is also a choice between certainties of diminished value and an invaluable uncertainty, between established but untrustworthy institutions and the conveniences of material and cognitive ecology that we receive through them. Its difficulty has the quality of a Stockholm syndrome, in which the hostage comes to defend the captor, and that quality brings the matter back to choice and implication, because whichever logic is at play, dia-ballein or syn-ballein, it takes two to tango. Divide and conquer is the oldest and most enduring trick of any state or power, and it works only while both parties consent to be divided; §2 gives the two logics their mythemic bodies as War and Love, Ares and Aphrodite, caught together in a net forged by the craftsman god.

Against the false light, stained glass shows the true one refracted: one light enters a differentiated medium and appears as many colours, and the plurality requires us neither to invent a separate source for every pane nor to bleach the colours back into sameness in order to affirm their unity, because the whole expresses itself **refractively** as the many. Each colour is definite because the continuum has been cut, selected and given a particular path, and the integrity of the event lies in keeping the relation through which those differences remain colours *of one illumination*. A **symbolon** works in just this way: a cut makes difference real while the edge preserves the possibility of relation, recognition and fit. Our `0/1 → 4+2` is meant as a formalisation of exactly that capacity: the primordial relation differentiates into four explicate possibilities while keeping the two implicate offices through which differentiation arises and returns, so analysis can proceed without pretending that the pieces became self-grounding when they were distinguished. Wholeness, in turn, is something against which particular arrangements can be measured, and far from a vague feeling of inclusion: do the differentiated parts still keep enough of their constituting relations to meet, correct and transform one another? In the old Germanic family of *hāl*, **whole, holy, health, heal**, this integrity finds its human words, and *wealth*, which first meant *weal*, well-being, belongs beside it in sense: wealth is the health of a whole, and a hoard that sickens the whole it is drawn from is its counterfeit. Health is the capacity of organs to remain different while taking part in one life. **Fitness** is its operational image: a part is fit when its relation lets the larger movement continue, and fit is tested under pressure, through use, by whether the joining holds. Truth, on this reading, is something an account must **carry through transformation**. Its fidelity shows when another perspective, another person, another register of experience or a change of circumstance can turn the account over without its losing purchase on what first gave it meaning. Matheme, mytheme and episteme are the different ways such a truth stays alive as it passes through formal inscription, lived image and warranted account. None replaces the others, because each cuts the Real differently and so makes different relations available for return. The integral demand is that those cuts remain **symbolonic**, so that determination never severs the means by which what has been determined can answer back. QL is offered as a method for inhabiting a world whose truth has become inseparable from the design of its mediation, and it works from both sides. Through syn-ballein it differentiates without dismembering and relates without collapsing; through dia-ballein, read as diagnosis, it shows where a claim issues from a conscious or unconscious denial of the whole, which pole has counted the span as its own and what was cancelled to make the account balance; and it inverts a determination so that its hidden conditions become available, returning the result with enough of its path intact that we can ask whether it still **runs true**.
