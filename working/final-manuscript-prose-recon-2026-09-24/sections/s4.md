## Full energetic development to preserve

### Pattern, charge and formative capacity

The distinction between state-space pattern and libido charge is necessary but insufficient. The next pass must hold the entire conjunction:

1. A **complex** is a local arbitration regime: a durable organisation of attention, memory, expectation and action.
2. An **image** can gather associations and redirect the receiver without containing the archetypal power it expresses.
3. **Archetypal capacity** names the formative power whose manifestations exceed one image.
4. **Libido/investment** names the charge through which one interpretation protects, attracts, commands or organises a life.
5. Transformation is not a new label on an unchanged investment. It changes the capacity to receive an actual answer.

The delayed reply is the section’s best carrier. A painful story may protect against uncertainty; a favourable story may preserve the same demand for guarantee. Recognition occurs when the other person’s real answer—including refusal—can change the next act. This is not a clinical report, and reassurance is not the criterion.

### Prāṇa, Śakti, Spanda, mantra and breath

These are coordinated but not synonymous energies:

- prāṇa: vital circulation, including breath, rhythm, attention and vitality;
- Śakti: manifestation, maintenance, concealment and recognition;
- Spanda: extension inseparable from a reserve not exhausted by what appears, carried by withdrawal/issue;
- mantra: an actual practice crossing sound, cadence, breath and gathered attention.

The prose should show what each changes. A catalogue of Sanskrit terms would be a regression. No cross-register conservation theorem is established, and no physical quantity is silently equated with libido.
