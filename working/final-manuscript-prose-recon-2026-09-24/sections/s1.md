## 2. What should survive

### Kaplan’s historical current

### Bhāskara and metaphysical provenance

The Bhāskara passage is central because the mathematical and divine images are together in the historical material. Finite increase and decrease are set against an identity they do not measure. This is not a later theology pasted onto neutral arithmetic, nor does theology settle the arithmetic. The exceptional expression and the divine analogy meet around invariance under finite transformation.

The chronology must remain explicit: Abhinavagupta is approximately 975–1025; Bhāskara’s *Līlāvatī* is conventionally dated around 1150. The resonance between Śaiva manifestation and zero/infinity can be argued at the established strength, but no direct transmission from Abhinavagupta to Bhāskara has been recovered. Do not reverse the chronology or convert analogy into influence.

### Gain–sacrifice–return

The historical sequence can sustain the native movement:

- **gain:** absence becomes writable and calculable; procedures become transmissible; new structures and questions become possible;
- **sacrifice:** local cosmology, embodied reckoning or earlier meaning may be stripped away so that the operation can travel; a mark’s abstraction gains independence by relinquishing compulsory shared metaphysics;
- **return:** the abstract operation re-enters questions of criterion, world and use; the omitted bearer, excluded case or changed domain can require the account itself to change.

This should survive, but it must be read from events in the history. Repeating “gain, sacrifice, return” after every episode would turn the braid into a template.

### Mathematical distinctions

Retain the careful boundaries:

- a total reciprocal function can stipulate a value at zero without making that value a multiplicative inverse;
- adjoining infinity changes a domain and its laws; the written mark alone does not do so;
- Robinson’s infinitesimal is nonzero, however small;
- empty product and empty set have exact structural offices;
- a boundary or mediant is not automatically a third substance;
- NOR provides a functionally complete logical operation where warranted.

These are protections against using zero as an all-purpose mystical token.
