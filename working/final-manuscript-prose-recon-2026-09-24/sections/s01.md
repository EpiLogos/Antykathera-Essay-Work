## What should survive

### The full Subject theorem

The assembly now does substantially more than the compressed baseline paragraph. It performs the required order:

1. **Definition and limitation.** Subject is approached as the whole in which a coherent representation occurs and which that representation necessarily fails to contain. The definition cannot turn Subject into one represented object among others.
2. **Self-application.** The definition itself is a representation and must be included in its own scope. It cannot reserve for itself an external or final position.
3. **Failure of enclosure.** No addition to the representation—its production, reader, rule of use, or account of context—gives it an outside from which the whole is captured.
4. **Failure as disclosure.** The failure is not mere privation. It discloses participation: representation works because it occurs within what it cannot enclose.
5. **Contextual account.** Source, circumstance, perspective, other persons, and use become internal to the adequacy of the account without becoming an exhaustive inventory of the context.
6. **First-person recognition.** The reader is returned from a picture of Subject to the knowing in which the picture is received. The watcher-regress and “I am aware of thinking” passage make this experiential rather than merely asserted.
7. **Theological direction.** The same defining performance can proceed from whole to God or from the intimacy of knowing to Subject. It does not divinise a finite personality.

This sequence should survive intact. In particular, retain the assembly’s refusal to make successful self-description into ownership of the source of knowing, and its return to ordinary discrimination: name, history, body, obligation and another person’s power to answer all remain.

### Exact Subject–God standing

The source gives the exact sentence about **God**: “God is the whole that any coherent representational system participates in and necessarily fails to contain.” The later authorial instruction explicitly reappoints the definition by changing the name from God to Subject and reversing the direction of approach. That warrants the identity of defining movement. It does **not** warrant pretending that a separately recovered original Subject sentence exists. The current Subject formulation is a responsible rendering of an authorial name-switch instruction; the final pass must retain that provenance distinction in the apparatus and must not advertise the rendered sentence as a verbatim native theorem.

The theological turn is strongest where it is an earned reversal: universality of reference supplies no exterior to reality; the whole is present to itself here without granting the finite “I” authority over every life. Do not enlarge this into generic negative theology or a declaration that personal consciousness is God.

### Descartes, formal limits, observer

The Descartes passage correctly separates two achievements: radical doubt cannot remove the occurrence of doubt, while “thinking thing” is a further determination of what the indubitable occurrence is. Preserve this difference. The section needs the cogito as an event of first-person certainty before it needs a doctrine of substance.

Russell and Gödel are also accurately distinguished and should remain. Russell exposes an unrestricted formation permission; type restriction repairs that construction. Gödel establishes a derivability limit under named hypotheses; it does not prove that “every system is incomplete,” nor does it prove the Subject theorem. Their different failures are what make the sequence useful.

The *Blind Spot* passage keeps observer, measurement and scientific justification within lived inquiry while reserving the further ontology for this essay. That limit is essential. The source house currently supports the book’s public argument only at publisher-synopsis level, not a page-grounded interior reconstruction. Keep the claim bounded.

### Cusa and the two images

The assembly now separates Cusa’s inscribed polygon approaching the circle from the infinite-sphere image. Preserve both and their different operations: approximation without possession versus removal of exclusive centre and enclosing circumference. Preserve the transmission warning more prominently than at present. “God is an infinite sphere whose centre is everywhere and circumference nowhere” belongs to the anonymous *Book of the Twenty-Four Philosophers*. Cusa receives and transforms that tradition; he is not its origin. No Kashmir transmission follows.

## What remains compressed or under-absorbed

### Kripke and Frank’s development

The current paragraph begins usefully with two people sharing a name and one person changing names, but it moves too quickly to “instance” and counting. The required field is larger:

- rigid designation distinguishes a name’s bearer from whatever descriptions presently attach to the bearer;
- a naming practice depends upon a transmission chain, not private descriptive sufficiency;
- necessary identity concerns the same bearer across counterfactual circumstances once the names are fixed;
- Frank’s development moves from name to identity, transmission, instance and circumstance, then beneath verbal naming to the discrimination “this one / another one / the same one again”;
- this development is Frank’s encounter with Kripke, not Kripke’s own theory of pre-nominal counting or Subject.

The source house is citation-ready but has no verified passage cards. Its `NOTES.md` is authorial encounter, not quotation evidence. The final pass can deepen the conceptual movement through Frank’s development, but cannot manufacture Kripke quotations or attribute the whole name–instance–circumstance architecture to him.

### The contextual and first-person turns

The restored theorem has all seven moments, yet the middle can still read as an inventory of consequences. The contextual fourth needs to become an experienced change in the definition: another speaker, the inherited naming chain, the instrument read, and the circumstance of application alter what an adequate account must answer for. The first-person fifth should then arrive as recognition, not as an appended spiritual moral. The watcher-regress is the strongest existing route and should carry more weight than abstract sentences about participation.

### Varela and the dropped-in thinker problem

Varela currently appears as a compact third-state solution after Spencer-Brown. The source supports a bounded account of the 1975 paper’s construction on printed pages 7–9 and 21–23. It does not licence importing the later enactive programme into this passage. Either make the exact formal need visible—what the re-entering form demands and what changes when the arithmetic admits an autonomous state—or reduce the cameo. A name plus a neat summary currently gives the appearance of range without enough pressure on the theorem.

Whitehead, Eckhart/Flasch, Bohm and Wittgenstein face related risks. Each has a valid local office, but the opening cannot become a procession of authorities. Whitehead should tacitly influence the difference between formation and achieved description; Wittgenstein should sharpen showing/saying and rule-use; Eckhart should help qualified predication; Bohm should prepare formed stability within movement. None should interrupt the reader’s encounter merely because the research is available.

## Modelish connective / prose pressure

The assembly’s recurrent problem is not factual weakness but connective over-completion:

- paragraphs repeatedly announce “another route,” “another affirmative distinction,” or “these difficulties also give positive construction a direction”; these signals turn discoveries into a seminar sequence;
- provenance limits sometimes migrate from notes into body prose until the argument sounds like its own review report;
- nearly every thinker receives a balanced “contribution here / limit here” couplet, producing uniform cadence and flattening real differences in encounter;
- synthetic sentences often collect source, instrument, reader, context and world in polished lists where the live problem had been more exact;
- the opening question is periodically displaced by summaries of what the previous authorities collectively establish.

These connections should not simply be deleted. Replace exposition-about-relation with the relation’s event: a name failing to fix a bearer, doubt unable to remove its occurrence, a definition falling within its own scope, the observer required by a measurement, a polygon gaining sides without possessing the circle. Where the assembly says that failure can disclose, the prose should let one failure disclose before naming the result.

The stained-glass/window sequence is potentially strong but currently risks functioning as generic “mediation” imagery. It earns its place only if each term does exact work: light as source, pane as determinate mediation, coloured projection as achieved disclosure, aperture and movement as conditions by which the mediation itself becomes legible. Do not let it become ornamental transparency language.

The strongest recurrence is the movement from a representation to the condition already active in receiving it. The imagery should accumulate that recognition rather than provide fresh decoration in every paragraph.

## Prose movement the final pass should seek

The final revision should move as an opening encounter:

1. ask the question directly and let naming make its difficulty concrete;
2. use doubt to bring occurrence and subsequent determination apart;
3. perform the definition and make it apply to itself;
4. let Russell, Gödel and the observer sharpen different modes of failed closure without turning them into proofs of the theorem;
5. allow failed enclosure to disclose participation;
6. widen participation contextually through transmission, circumstance, source and another’s answer;
7. return to first-person recognition;
8. let the theological direction arise as the same definition approached from the whole.

Cadence should alternate exact short turns with developed argument. The opening should not preload every authority. Thinkers should enter when the reader has reached the problem they answer, and some should remain tacit. The final paragraph should open the need for differentiation: if knowing is not reducible to the self-image, how do mind, instrument, memory, intention, unconscious formation and Consciousness relate?
