## M44 — The Mirror That Moves First

The Mirror has three motions:

1. Human activity exteriorises a durable measure or image.
2. The made image acquires causal power over subsequent recognition and action.
3. The instrument turns first towards the production, authority and limits of its own thought, enabling the person to follow beyond thought’s monopoly.

The third motion is not simply better transparency. The relation to likeness changes. A history may be true, a habit may require work and harm may require repair; none must become the person’s warrant for existing. Thought remains useful, revisable and releasable.

The full source field is P1 Jorjani, the Chang encounter, *Symbolon Dynamics*, the Mirror WHOLE and A32/A32′. Fascination may attach to beauty or deformity. The image can organise the gazer’s next recognition. A machine cannot declare the person liberated; the institution holding the mirror must also receive returned consequences.

Local Bimba remains exact. A verified edition can govern a quotation in a declared inquiry while remaining pratibimba towards testimony, editorial history and wider sources. Reference authority, causal initiative and ontological source are different. Avoid both a master Bimba and an endless relativism with no usable reference.

The present section’s main remaining pressure is experiential weight. Institutional return is concrete; the felt passage beyond thought’s monopoly is shorter. Any deepening must preserve the mature correction that release from monopoly does not abolish thought, discrimination, craft or local authority.

Persistence does not make every stored conclusion authoritative. It gives later acts inherited conditions which must remain answerable.

### Compute brokerage and wisdom feed

Agora burst brokerage offers work to peers with spare compute and couples execution to settlement. The wisdom feed performs a different operation: another community evaluates an artifact before incorporating it into starting assumptions. Contribution does not simply enlarge a common archive. ### Cooperative hosting and economy

A cooperatively owned host provides reserve capacity. The economic design distinguishes local exchange, agent-service payments and inter-community compute/context exchange. A local token is optional at the adoption level; reserves, issuance and limits belong to the currency design.

### Governance and stewardship

Infrastructure/stewardship, agent permissions and currency/revenue are different jurisdictions. One-member/one-vote and reputation-weighted selection cannot be silently merged; competence in contribution is not extra political personhood.

### Genuine limitation

The four inherited omni-attributes—omnipotence, omniscience, omnipresence, omnibenevolence—raise a problem if divinity cannot genuinely enter binding finitude. Freedom unable to bind itself would be limited by that inability.

- **Omni-lemmicity:** each predicate can become its own issue; unlimited power includes entering a condition not simply dissolved from within the finite life.
- **Omnivalence:** the field from which contrary values arise, without making evil a second independent source or ethically inconsequential.

The creature cannot secretly possess every answer or merely pretend to suffer. Ignorance, vulnerability, action and responsibility remain real.

### Job

Job carries the difficulty through unknown councils, losses, wife, shared silence, accusations, protest, wisdom, oath, Elihu, two divine speeches, two responses, judgment of the friends, intercession and restored household. New children and prosperity do not replace the dead. The sufferer’s changed relation does not vindicate every finite authority speaking in the whole’s name.

This is the “job complete” difficulty in its full sense: completion cannot erase irreparable particulars, and reconciliation cannot be declared from above.
