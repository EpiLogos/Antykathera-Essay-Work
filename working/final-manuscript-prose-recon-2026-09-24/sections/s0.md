Its comparative decision is not “Hinduism over Buddhism.” It is an argued choice about which account can carry the inquiry’s required operations after the Buddhist critique of a fabricated proprietor has been accepted.

## 2. What should survive

### The comparative conclusion in its exact force

The assembly has recovered the required conclusion and should retain it nearly whole in structure:

- Buddhist analysis remains binding where it exposes the personal self as fabricated proprietor, a construction that appropriates memory, intention and experience;
- for **this inquiry**, the Śaiva account is more adequate because memory, comparison, exclusion/absence, intention, action, manifestation, arising and perishing require positive places for an experient and its powers;
- this positive account must not objectify the experient as a second inner witness or reinstall the proprietor under a metaphysical title.

The qualification “for this inquiry” names the criterion of adequacy; it is not a hedge. The criterion is operational: can the account explain how a remembered content is recognised as past, how two presentations are compared, how an absent alternative matters, how an intention passes into action and result, and how manifestation can be self-disclosing? The final pass should keep these operations visible at the point of judgment.

The strongest current sentence distinguishes criticism of the self-thing from erasure of “the one to whom experience is manifest.” Preserve that pressure while avoiding any implication that Buddhism simply denies experience or agency. Watson’s Buddhist and psychotherapeutic encounter and Frank’s response are crucial authorial ground here; her protected notes are rich but remain encounter material and quotation leads, not citable passage cards.

### P2/P3 and the Indian/Kashmiri account

The enriched section now gives real depth to:

- `prakāśa`, the immediate luminosity or appearing of cognition;
- `vimarśa`, self-apprehension/articulating awareness, not an external observer added to bare light;
- the regress exposed by a mirror that would need another mirror to know its reflection;
- the blue/yellow example, where determinate cognition requires differentiation while appearing and knowing remain internally related;
- the cognitive triad and the relation among knower, means and known;
- the levels of Vāk as manifestation from less differentiated power into articulated expression, without reducing them to a psychological stack;
- the inner instrument and tattvic contraction as constituted means and determinate capacities;
- recognition as reorientation of identification, not manufacture of a hidden metaphysical entity.

These materials should survive because they answer the opening section’s unresolved question. Their order, however, should follow the reader’s problem rather than reproduce a dossier.

### Mind, conscious position, unconscious and Consciousness

The directional correction is a major recovery:

- immediate knowing/appearing is not the same as a constituted mental instrument;
- the inner instrument is formed and differentiated;
- “conscious” can name what is available to a psychologically differentiated position;
- “unconscious” is relative to that position and remains dynamically consequential;
- sourceward, Consciousness names the ground and activity of manifestation on which that conscious/unconscious distribution depends;
- the whole Self is not the bounded conscious position enlarged until no unconscious remains.

The crossed-zero movement depends on this distinction. The same word cannot be globally replaced or flattened. Movement rootward changes the direction of inquiry; it does not deny the empirical or therapeutic distinction between conscious and unconscious.

### Objective Internality and practical recognition

The assembly correctly locates Objective Internality in the constituted means—`pramāṇa`—rather than in Subject or the whole Life/Mind. Language, memory, concern, body, other persons, tools and institutions condition what can appear, count as a reason and become actionable. This is not merely “context matters”; it is the objective constitution of an interior through relations that exceed private imagery.

Keep the practical distinctions among:

1. a changed state;
2. an improved account of experience;
3. recognition, in which identification with the means/source relation changes.

Also preserve the warning that thought used as medicine must become releasable. A conceptual cure that must be defended forever has become another possession. `Nirvikalpa` or an undifferentiated state is not automatically recognition; absence of discursive differentiation may leave identification untouched.

## 3. What remains compressed

### Singh/Abhinavagupta as encounter, not bibliography

The section names and explains Kashmir Śaivism more fully than the baseline, but Frank’s encounter through Jaideva Singh and Abhinavagupta still sits behind Dyczkowski’s cleaner doctrinal exposition. The final pass needs to distinguish:

- what belongs to Abhinavagupta’s text;
- what Singh supplies in translation and commentary;
- what Frank recognised or contested in reading them;
- what later scholarship, especially Dyczkowski, helps reconstruct.

The selected Singh/Abhinavagupta edition has not been fully inspected. Encounter notebooks cannot be promoted into verified quotations. Yet the response need not disappear: Frank’s questions, resistances and recognitions can govern the section as authorial development when they are not attributed to the source.

Dyczkowski’s available local transcript ends during page 81 despite its filename; pages 82–91 are unavailable. Claims should remain within the recovered ranges. The final voice must not sound as though Dyczkowski owns `X/x`, crossed zero, Objective Internality or the P2/P3 architecture.

### Crossed-zero and the direction of recognition

The current section states the two directions but does not fully enact the crossed-zero movement:

- outward differentiation produces a determinate conscious position, world and means;
- rootward recognition follows those determinations to their source without cancelling them;
- return restores means to use without letting a means claim to be the whole knower.

This sequence should become the section’s architecture rather than one of its conclusions. It is also the cleanest route into Objective Internality: constituted means are real achievements, neither unreal overlays nor sovereign sources.

### Bohm, vortex and reflexivity

Bohm currently appears mainly near a seam. The appointed use is more exact. The vortex offers a formed identity sustained by movement; enfolding/unfolding lets a determination arise from and return within a wider movement; reflexivity asks what occurs when a formed means turns upon the conditions of its own operation. None proves Śaiva ontology. Used sparingly, these operations can make contraction and constituted instrument intelligible in another register. Left as a short bridge, Bohm becomes a dropped-in analogy.

### Concrete practical recognition

The section has useful tests, but some remain abstractly classified. It needs concrete differences without turning examples into case-study fiction: remembering a mistaken motive is not yet recognition; revising an account of why one acted is not necessarily recognition; seeing that the self-image claiming ownership is itself a formed means changes the act’s source relation. The final pass should use one or two exact situations and let them carry the distinction.

## 4. What modelish connective prose overwrites

The enriched assembly repeatedly converts a difficult encounter into orderly curriculum:

- sequences such as “the first pressure / the second pressure” make the comparative conclusion clear but can make it sound externally scored rather than discovered through use;
- nearly every Sanskrit term receives definition, qualification and application in the same paragraph, producing uniform explanatory blocks;
- transitional sentences frequently tell the reader what “together” the previous materials explain;
- the repeated invented scene of a voice calling the narrator’s name becomes an all-purpose carrier for memory, ownership, response, promise and action; its convenience begins to overwrite the plurality of operations it was meant to disclose;
- source boundaries are often narrated inside the argument, so the section sounds like a responsible report about sources instead of thought taking place through them.

The solution is not to reduce exactness. Let different operations require different scenes. A colour distinction can carry differentiation; a remembered encounter can carry temporal identity; an unchosen affect can carry the unconscious relative to a conscious position; a promise can carry intention and action; a tool or institution can carry Objective Internality. The repeated call can recur, but it should accumulate meaning rather than impersonate every example.

The prose also overuses balanced correction: “not X, but neither Y; rather Z.” Some of those distinctions are load-bearing. Others are protective scaffolding left in the body. Keep a negative only when it names a real doctrine or recurrent mistake and immediately changes the positive account.

## 5. Sources not yet adequately present and tacit influences

- **Singh/Abhinavagupta:** the authorial encounter and textual layering remain under-felt; edition inspection remains a debt.
- **Watson:** Frank’s response to her Buddhist/psychotherapeutic inquiry carries the comparative decision, but protected notes cannot be quoted or silently regularised.
- **Dyczkowski:** doctrinal detail is strong but should not monopolise the section or exceed the actual local-copy range.
- **Bohm:** appointed vortex/enfolding/reflexive function remains compressed.
- **Whitehead:** should tacitly shape formation, satisfaction and inheritance where an intention becomes an achieved result; it should not become another survey paragraph.
- **Gebser:** useful for co-presence and transparency of structures, especially the danger of a standpoint claiming impartiality while deciding whose answer counts. His role should be an influence on contextual transparency, not a civilisational chronology inserted here.
- **Buddhist sources:** the critique must remain philosophically specific. Avoid using “Buddhist” as one homogeneous negative foil for the Śaiva positive account.

The thinker distribution should be asymmetrical. Abhinavagupta/Singh and the Buddhist challenge belong in the foreground. Dyczkowski supports reconstruction. Bohm, Whitehead and Gebser can often act tacitly through movement, formation and transparency.

## 6. Recurring imagery available

- **Light and colour:** appearing has differentiated powers; blue/yellow keeps luminosity and determination together.
- **Mirror and regress:** an image does not know itself by requiring another image behind it.
- **Voice, name and answer:** a formed self responds, but responsiveness does not prove proprietorship.
- **Vortex:** identity maintained through formative movement.
- **Eye, frame and horizon:** a means gives a view; it usurps the horizon only when it denies what can exceed or revise it.
- **Instrument / medicine:** thought and inner means are useful when their use can complete and release.
- **Contraction and aperture:** determination narrows possibility into a workable position without severing it from source.
- **Crossed zero:** the same field read outward into determination and rootward into recognition.

These images should recur with changed office. The mirror belongs to regress, the frame to constituted perspective, the horizon to contextual revisability, and medicine to release. Treating them all as general images of mediation would erase their distinctions.

## 7. Prose movement sought

The final section should proceed through a pressure rather than a catalogue:

1. begin from the first-person recognition gained at the threshold and ask how differentiated mind arises within it;
2. distinguish immediate appearing/knowing from the formed means by which a particular life remembers, compares and acts;
3. work `prakāśa–vimarśa`, cognitive differentiation and the inner instrument deeply enough that the positive Śaiva account is intelligible;
4. let the Buddhist critique test every positive term for covert proprietorship;
5. state the comparative decision through the concrete operations this inquiry must retain;
6. differentiate conscious position, relative unconscious and sourceward Consciousness;
7. make Objective Internality the constituted means of encounter and action;
8. test recognition practically;
9. return through crossed zero with means restored to use, opening the historical question of zero.

The desired voice is an inquiry learning to discriminate, not an expert distributing doctrines. Sanskrit terms should enter only when their exact operation is already needed. Frank’s responses should be present as judgments made in encounter, without being disguised as source attribution.

## 8. Revision priorities and non-negotiable limits

1. Preserve the explicit comparative conclusion and all operations that warrant it.
2. Keep the Buddhist proprietor critique binding after adopting the Śaiva account.
3. Make “more adequate for this inquiry” a criterion-based judgment, never a total religious ranking.
4. Keep immediate knowing, constituted instrument, conscious position, relative unconscious and sourceward Consciousness distinct.
5. Place Objective Internality in `pramāṇa`, the constituted means, not Subject or undifferentiated Life/Mind.
6. Restore the crossed-zero sequence as section architecture.
7. Recover Singh/Abhinavagupta as Frank’s encounter while respecting textual voices and edition limits.
8. Use Bohm only for the appointed vortex/enfolding/reflexive operations.
9. Retain the practical recognition tests and the releasability of thought-as-medicine.
10. Reduce the calling-voice example’s monopoly; let the argument’s distinct operations have distinct concrete carriers.
