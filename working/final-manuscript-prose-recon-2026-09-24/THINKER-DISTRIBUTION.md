additional name-check. It does not mean uncredited quotation.

## Enforcement for the prose pass

1. At a thinker’s first explicit appearance, give the lived/intellectual
   difficulty, then one active distinction, then the authorial relation.
2. At a later appearance, use the distinction or image without a miniature
   biography; name again only if source attribution or a real disagreement is
   required.
3. Preserve the section source returns’ explicit boundaries: house `NOTES.md`
   are authorial encounter records, not quotation licences; a recovered source
   does not endorse Frank’s inference.
4. If a proposed return merely says “as X showed,” omit it. The test is whether
   the reader could identify the changed local consequence.

## Thinkers conceptually present but dispensable as names

Neumann, Watson, Maturana, Kauffman, McGilchrist, Tillich, Levinas, Flasch and several historical carriers may be absent from final prose while their recovered distinctions remain active. Their absence is acceptable when:

1. no distinctive phrase is silently retained;
2. the source-specific warrant is not needed for the sentence;
3. the conceptual work has been fully transformed into Frank’s own derived/argued operation; and
4. the source remains recoverable through the relevant movement, argument and source house.

Conversely, a name is required where a date, text, distinctive technical term, historical claim, reported experiment, or characteristic image bears evidential weight.
