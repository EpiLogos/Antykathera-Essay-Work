# Eros of Logos — Sym-Ballein as Love — Canonical Argument Candidacy

**Standing.** A proposed new canonical argument node per pass-2 charter ruling 6. The junction — sym-ballein *as* the eros of logos — is held *neither* as Offered residue inside A13′ or A35′ *nor* as a concept node. It is proposed here as what it claims to be: an argument. Its number and placement are the author's; options are proposed in §6, none assigned. Until ratified, every reference to it in the field names it as pending.

## #0 — the proposition

Sym-ballein, at full strength, is the eros of logos. The gathering that retains source, inverse reading and affected context while composing is not a mechanism that happens to remain survivable; it is the articulated field's desire to know its own limits and sources, operating. Draft3 states the identification's authorial ground: "The engine of reflexivity is desire — the same icchā found at the slash, the first bend of the light toward its own display. The Logos desires to know itself, and epi-logos is the form that desire takes: **the eros of logos**" (q024, "Epi-Logos," line 267, quotation-ready). The candidate proposes the junction in its full form: what the gathering gathers-with is love — "a definition of love before it is a definition of logic: love that does not devour, formalised" (same passage). The junction works two-handedly: it is **source and condition** — the desire without which no articulation would bend back upon itself at all — and at once **effective action** — the gathering that actually composes, returns and renews. And it names a meta-intention: **letting the whole be respected in the movements of all its parts.**

## The offices the argument would hold

**#1 — Definition.** The junction identifies two offices the field currently holds apart: the gathering-operation (C50's Syn-pole at full strength) and the desire-name (C51's epi-logos space, of which eros-of-logos is one name). The claim is not that they are adjacent but that they are one operation seen from its deed-side and its source-side. Definition therefore performs the same fold it names: the desire is not added to the gathering as a motive attached from outside; the gathering at full strength already desires.

**#2 — Dynamis.** The force of the junction is the bend at the slash: icchā, the first inclination of the light toward its own display (Draft3's own genealogy). As source, the desire precedes and conditions every articulation — no field would return upon itself without the pull. As deed, the same desire is the effective throw-together: provenance retained, inverse carried, context answered. The two are not phases; they are the one movement's two readings, as `0/1` and `1/0` are one relation's.

**#3 — Pattern.** The junction's negative evidence is its failure-family. Severance, fusion, counterfeit gathering and monoisation are what love-less gathering does: the cut that forgets its field, the merge that erases its halves, the enclosure that arrests what it should compose, the local account that suppresses its plurality. The civilisational recurrence is named in the junction's site of enunciation: the population-loss paradox read as an underlying crisis of meaning and love. Where the parts' movements stop respecting the whole, the eros has left the logos — and the diagnosis and the repair are one observation.

**#4 — Context.** The meta-intention becomes enacted in the fields the essay already argues. In A30's co-internality, contributions change the conditions of others without capture — the whole respected in the movements of all parts, technically. In A35's triad, the junction supplies the eros-term's full claim while compassion keeps the non-devouring orientation and humility the refusal of the returned `1` to claim it has become `0`. The meta-intention is therefore not a sentiment laid over systems; it is the criterion by which a shared field's compositions are judged respectful or appropriative.

**#5→0.** The gathering that knows itself as love does not possess what it gathers. The returned unity remains in ratio to the ground it links; the commons is this junction in institutional form, not its monument. The candidacy opens **Offered** and can be argued up only through the field — by showing, at each consumption site, that the junction does work the two offices held apart cannot.

## Relations to the standing arguments

- **A13 — Two Logics of Two — Dia/Syn** derives sym-ballein's operation. A13 is the operation's proof-site; this candidacy is the identification's. The candidacy adds no new cut or composition; it claims what the derived operation, complete, is.
- **A30 — Objective Co-Internality** carries the meta-intention's technical face: reciprocal constitution without fusion or capture is the whole respected in the parts' movements, enacted. The candidacy names the eros whose presence or absence that enactment tests.
- **A35 — Compassion / Sensitivity to Origins / Epi-Logos as Vocation** differentiates the triad the candidacy joins at its first term: eros the motive, compassion the orientation, humility the limit. A35 keeps the triad; the candidacy holds the junction — that the motive, taken to its logical end in the gathering itself, is love. The two claims are distinct and the candidacy does not collapse A35's into itself.

Via the [reharmonisation proposal](CONCEPT-REHARMONISATION-PROPOSAL.md): C50 owns the gathering-office the candidacy identifies; C51 owns the return-space whose desire-name it is; C56 keeps the motive-office inside the triad. The candidacy absorbs none of them.

## Live registers

**Phenomenology.** Desire is found at the slash itself — the first bend of the light toward its own display (Draft3); the face-to-face interrupts the economy of need and the Other exceeds the idea held of them (Levinas houses, passages as leads until verified). The junction is phenomenally legible before it is formally named.

**Epistemology.** Knowledge oriented toward and structured by the conditions of its diaphaneity is epi-logos in the note's own formulation; intelligence becomes more exact by owning its limit (Draft3's Epi-Logos harness passage). The eros of logos is epistemology's own desire, not a decor on it.

**Soteriology.** The gathering as love that does not devour is the redeemed line's completion: the returned `1` knowing itself as the ground's own quilting rather than its possession. Draft3's natural state — "a mind loving life" — is the recognition this register carries.

**Politics.** The commons as sym-ballein in institutional form (core XI); the two-monoculture recurrence (A13 #3) as love-less gathering's public face; the crisis-of-meaning diagnosis as the junction's site of enunciation. The junction claims that a polis whose parts' movements respect the whole is not merely coordinated — it loves.
