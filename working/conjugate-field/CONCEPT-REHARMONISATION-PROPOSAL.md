# Concept Reharmonisation Proposal — Pass 2

**Ruling (author, binding).** Separate spaces are needed for **epi-logos** (of which eros-of-logos is one name), **sym-ballein**, and **compassion as sensitivity to origins**. The concept frame is bounded at **C01–C64**: the C65–C87 "reserved range," C88, and every new C-number invented in pass 1 are **void**. All differentiation happens by **re-scope and re-office of existing nodes**. Office-based reasoning only; the title-mirror argument ("names are the same") is discarded as a reason. Deletion only where a node is genuinely redundant by office, with a succession plan. **PROPOSAL ONLY — no canonical edits are made by this document.**

## 1. The pass-1 failure, named

Pass 1 audited the naming redundancy correctly: [C51](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) §3, [C46](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C46-Epistemic-Cultivation.md) §5→0, and [C56](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C56-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins.md) §1 each gloss one core concept independently, with no space that owns it — and two compound titles ([C51](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md), [C59](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C59-Cultural-Individuation-Epi-Logos-as-Culture.md)) fold the concept into other nodes' names. It then declined the structural act: instead of resolving the redundancy inside the field, it proposed new numbers — a "C88-to-be" junction and a "C89-to-be" epi-logos space — and left the re-scopes as pending routings. Those numbers were uninvented inventions and are void. The correction performed here: **the owner of each space already exists within C01–C64.** Differentiation is an act of office, not an act of numbering, and this pass performs it.

## 2. Method: office-based reasoning only

An **office** is what a node's criterion does that no other node's criterion does — the reusable test or operation its grounding argument performs. Two disciplines follow, both binding:

- **Title-mirror reasoning is discarded in both directions.** A C node whose title mirrors its A argument (C55↔A32, C41↔A26, C42↔A30, C47↔A31, C48↔A23, C55↔A32, C60↔A34, C37↔A22) neither gains nor loses standing from the shared name; only the criterion decides whether the node does distinct reusable work. Pass 1's audit leaned on the pair-list as evidence of soundness; that leaning is withdrawn here and the verdicts restated on office grounds below.
- **A shared gloss is not a shared office, and an unowned office is a real defect.** Three nodes using the word *eros* for three local movements is a routing failure, not duplication of content; the repair is ownership, not deletion.

## 3. The three spaces, by re-office

### 3.1 Epi-logos — the owner is C51

**Re-office.** [C51 — Logos / Epi-Logos](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C51-Logos-Epi-Logos.md) becomes the **owning space of epi-logos**: the authorial return of an achieved articulation upon its own sources, exclusions, effects and renewed possibility. *Eros-of-logos is one of its names* — the plan's naming ("Epi-Logos names the eros of logos—the desire of this field to know its own limits and sources without ceasing to represent," *the-return-of-zero-central-plan.md* ~633) and the quotation-ready Draft3 q024 ("the engine of reflexivity is desire… epi-logos is the form that desire takes: **the eros of logos**"). The desire-name and the return-name belong to this one space.

**What the office does that no other node does.** C51's criterion is the return-upon-the-account as such. Its nearest neighbours each do something else: C46 cultivates the *means* of knowing (the apparatus that makes return possible); C47 lets an *Other* revise the model (the encounter-side of revision); C56 owns the *orientation* of return (non-devouring, humble); C50 owns the conjugate *pair* of operations. None of them performs the account's own folding-back upon its household. That operation exists, is consumed (A06, A14, A35–A36), and now has one owner.

**Registers gathered as determinations, not borrowings.** Knowledge (C51's own #2–#4 body); culture (received from C59's retiring title-component — see §4); vocation (A35's title-component, routed through the reciprocity index at §4); the plan's §5→0 vessel-claim. The space gathers these as determinations of one operation rather than four borrowings of a word.

**Title.** The compound "Logos / Epi-Logos" survives **on office grounds**: the criterion *is* the operational relation between Logos (the articulated field) and Epi-Logos (its return upon itself); the compound names the relation that constitutes the office, not two merged offices. A future plain re-title ("C51 — Epi-Logos") is Frank's call at promotion; nothing here depends on it.

**Claim status: Argued, unchanged.** The operation is settled authorial architecture (C51 #4); the naming is quotation-ready (Draft3 q024; plan ~633). Opening the space is differentiation, not promotion.

### 3.2 Sym-ballein — the owner is C50

**Re-scope.** [C50 — Dia / Syn](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C50-Dia-Syn.md) keeps the conjugate pair as its office and gains an explicit internal differentiation: its **Syn-pole is the sym-ballein space at full strength** — the throw-together that retains source, inverse reading and affected context while composing. The re-scope makes ownership explicit where it was derivable: the gathering operation was already C50 #3's criterion, unowned in name.

**What the office does that no other node does.** C50's criterion tests whether an achieved distinction retains what makes it possible. The positive face of that test — the gathering that composes without fusing, the broken token whose halves and fracture are kept so the refitting can prove belonging — is performed by no other node: C21 keeps the symbol alive or idolised at the *reception* end; C22–C26 name its failure-modes; C61 is locked as disclosure *architecture*, not a second Symbolon theorem. With this re-scope, C23 Severance, C24 Fusion, C25 Counterfeit Gathering and C26 Monoisation read explicitly as **the sym-ballein office's failure-modes** — what the gathering fails at — rather than free-floating pathologies.

**The junction is not a C-node.** The claim that the gathering at full strength *is* the eros-of-logos — sym-ballein as love — is **the pending canonical A-node candidacy** ([EROS-OF-LOGOS-A-CANDIDACY](EROS-OF-LOGOS-A-CANDIDACY.md), ruling 6). C50 references the candidacy as pending; it neither absorbs the junction nor is absorbed by it. The pass-1 "C88-to-be" is void.

**Claim status: Argued (the pair-office); the candidacy it references is Offered on its own file.**

### 3.3 Compassion as sensitivity to origins — the owner is C56

**Confirmed, not created.** [C56 — Compassion / Sensitivity to Origins](../../submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/concepts/C56-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins.md) already owns this space; its criterion — the return to genesis, relations, wounds, exclusions and purposes without consuming them — is performed by no other node. **No re-scope of its office.**

**What changes:** the triad in C56 §1 keeps its operative force (motive / non-devouring orientation / epistemic humility) while the triad's *eros-term* stops being an independent gloss. The motive-sentence routes its name to C51 (the epi-logos space, of which eros-of-logos is one name) and its junction-claim to the pending A-candidacy. C56 keeps the motive-*office* inside the triad — why the return happens — exactly as A35 differentiates it. **Claim status: Argued, unchanged.**

## 5. Audit — office-based, bounded 64

Method: for each numbered node, its stated criterion was compared against its grounding argument's declared dependencies, the `argument_consumers` reverse index, the locked offices, and its nearest neighbours for **criterion** overlap (never title overlap). **Verdict: no merge and no retire.** The near-misses examined hold as distinct offices:

- **C22 / C23 / C24 / C25 / C26** — umbrella failure-family, severance, fusion, counterfeit gathering, monoisation: five distinct operations with distinct sources and distinct repairs; with §3.2 they now read as one office's failure-modes, which is a routing gain, not a redundancy.
- **C45 / C46 / C47** — parity as promotion *test*, cultivation as *process*, deference as the *encounter's* revision power; A33 consumes three distinguishable criteria.
- **C49 / C50 / C52 / C64 (+ C11, C20)** — the two ones as content, the conjugate operations, frame-change mathematics, field-transformation paradox; each explicitly defers to the others.
- **C37 / C55 / C57 / C61 / C62** — atlas transformation, three-motion mirror-field, venue worldhood, disclosure architecture, planetary material conditions: five criteria over adjacent territory, each already routed to the others.
- **C41 / C42** — constitution of one interior vs reciprocal constitution of non-identical interiors.
- **C53 / C54** — delegated-labour answerability vs shared-field non-monopoly.
- **C04 / C05 / C48** — generated limit, qualified promise, commitment under limit.
- **C15 / C16 / C44** — bounded subject-position, inner instrument, arrival into an authored horizon.
- **C30–C36 psychoid family** — distinct registers; C30 alone is **Offered** (Jung–Pauli research intuition) and stays so with its scope note: narrowing it would downgrade a live research question.
- **C58 / C60 / C63** — interface operation, dependence order, predication discipline.
