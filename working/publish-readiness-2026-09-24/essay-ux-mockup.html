The manuscript · written by hand, in progress

This is the long text. It is not finished. Frank is writing it by hand, over time. What you can read linearly today is the current body. What surrounds it is the field the essay stands in: the rooms, and the three parts of the symbolon.

The field itself is largely a demonstration. It shows the method and the intent. It will be refined. It is not a finished doctrine, and it should not speak in slogans.

If you are new, start with the rooms. Each room is one station. Six movements sit inside it. The manuscript is the continuous reading. The symbolon holds the figures, the formal work, and the sources.

The six products of O:I — Central, Actuation, AIKit, Software Factory, Workcell, Quaternal Logic — are described in this field as what they are for. The software is still to come.
