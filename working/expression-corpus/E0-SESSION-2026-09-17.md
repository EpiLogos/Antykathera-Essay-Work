## Production loop (every worker)

```text
read packet + canonical records
→ author <slug>.journey.json in the assigned Point-Cloud collection dir
→ node production/return-of-zero/tools/validate.mjs <file>   (real schema)
→ node production/return-of-zero/tools/capture.mjs <file>    (real engine render → cover)
→ inspect the capture; revise or reject poor treatments
→ write bindings/<slug>.binding.json (exact source refs + revisions)
→ return: statuses, defects (shared engine defects noted for Point-Cloud #6),
  craft observations, asset manifests with provenance
```

## Standing law

Canonical text is never edited to make an Expression easier. Whole Mythemes stay whole.
Family grammar repeats where the source form repeats; individual records stay addressable.
No corpus runtime, no new scene schema, no corpus-specific Ta-Onta roster. Content that
belongs to a register keeps its register.
