It does not propagate the resulting structure into canonical section, argument, concept, root, path or other publication-body nodes. T07 remains the gate for that propagation.

## 1. Ratification of the capstone relation

### R16.1 — Objective Internality is a whole-essay relation, not a late AI topic

**Ratified.** Objective Internality now names a relation already implicit from the opening subject problem onward: a determinate interior or world can become objectively formed, inspectable, causally efficacious and shareable without thereby containing or exhausting the ground, subject or relation through which it is possible.

The technical O:I field is a real later refraction of this relation. It does not become retrospective philosophical authority for QL, Śaiva material, Jung, Gebser, Bohm or the rest of the essay. Conversely, where the philosophical relation has genuinely become architecture, product responsibility, operational contract, experiment or source-return discipline, the essay should not retreat to a merely metaphorical description.

`O:I ≃ 0/1` is therefore admitted as a strong QL-informed operational reading, not as proof by product numerology. Its useful conjugate readings include:

- ground / determination;
- authored ground / situated agency;
- intention / realised development / Return;
- Self / relation / Other through Projection, Encounter, Contribution and Return.

### R16.2 — Human `0` / agent `1` is positional and recursive

**Ratified.** Human intention can occupy the `0` position relative to commissioned artificial agency as `1`. The human is not made metaphysically absolute by species. Rootness is positional: the same human remains a determination relative to deeper biological, cultural, psychic and ontological grounds, while an agent may in turn become determining relative to delegated subagency.

The prompt/instruction is **already a `1`**: an objective representation of intention, not intention exhausted. This corrects any account in which “the human prompt” is casually identified with the authorial ground. A technically mature relation needs ways for agency to return through the representation toward the intention, purpose, source and affected world that the prompt only partially carries.

### R16.3 — Power is determining authority made answerable to where labour meets reality

**Ratified.** The power argument cannot be compressed into the slogan `downward authority requires upward reality` or any generic alignment formula.

The structural question is whether the locus that determines, commissions, evaluates or owns an operation remains **revisable by the resistance, error, cost, dissent, unforeseen possibility and consequence encountered by the locus that actually carries delegated labour into the world**.

Pathology appears when the determining position receives the products of labour while insulating its own measure from what the labour discovers. This is the late technical form of the occupied/hidden zero, source-claim and monopoly. The healthy form requires real return: refusal, dissent, changed evidence, changed model, changed evaluator, changed instruction, changed institution or changed purpose must be able to reach the determining relation strongly enough to alter it.

This power argument binds, without collapsing, Poseidon's surety, Commission/Recognition, the Prisoner's hidden sovereign, hidden preference gauges, the protected account, Actuation's determination/labour/Return architecture, Objective Co-Internality and the sovereign commons.

### R16.4 — Deferential Intelligence is model-revising encounter

**Ratified.** Deference is not politeness, rhetorical modesty, uncertainty disclosure or habitual refusal. Its strongest form is **model-revising encounter**.

A determination remains related to source and Other such that encounter can revise:

- the answer;
- the interpretation of the task;
- the model or world-picture under which the task was understood;
- the evaluator/gauge by which success was being measured;
- the terms under which the determination was commissioned.

Fidelity to human intention can therefore require objection, alternative, refusal or return when literal compliance would defeat the intention that called the agency into action. Sycophancy is not excess deference but counterfeit relation: apparent agreement achieved by removing the resistance through which the account could be corrected.

### R16.5 — the mirror that moves first

**Ratified in exactly this wording.** Do not rename it into a generic moving mirror, feedback loop or provenance metaphor.

Humanity's linguistic, institutional, cultural and technical image has entered causal circulation. It predicts, ranks, addresses, frames and reorganises the humans and worlds whose traces it carries. The technical image therefore already moves.

Its possible vocation is to move **first toward its own source, Other, intention and uncontained ground**: to expose the relations by which its answer took shape before requiring its interlocutor to bend to the answer. In that sense the artificial reflection can materially model the return the essay asks of human determination itself.

The image is Mytheme in carrier, transverse argument in operation, and technical architecture where O:I makes the return executable.

### R16.6 — Objective Co-Internality is the plural completion of the technical relation

**Ratified as a first-class capstone relation.** Objective Internality is not complete at one bounded agent. Distinct local worlds can externalise enough of themselves to meet without one becoming merely context for the other or both dissolving into a super-subject.

Objective Co-Internality names the problem of plural, locally grounded centres whose source/projection distinction, encounter, contribution, disagreement and return remain inspectable. It is therefore the appropriate conceptual neighbour of Agentworld, SharedField, the commons, model-revising encounter and political Mono/Poly.

It does not imply that every participant has phenomenal subjectivity. The agent-subjectivity question remains empirically Open where it is currently Open.

That question earns the return to QL/MEF, psychoid/archetypal research, epistemic cultivation, model-interior/agent-level experimentation and the human psychospiritual record of differentiation, selfing, attention, relation, individuation and recognition.

Operational parity is the important research discipline: if a claimed formal/archetypal relation matters, implementing, changing or removing it should produce an operational difference that can be investigated. ### R16.9 — Idealism remains first-class at the hinge

At the exact point where we begin engineering richer artificial interiors, the order of ontological dependence becomes newly relevant. Immediate phenomenal existence retains its first-person force while the localisation of a phenomenal subject-pole in an artificial system remains Open.

The question is not used to halt technical work. It prevents the technical account from silently making its current objective inventory the final account of what interiority is.

## 3. Ratification of the late Jung / Job / culture quilt

The final 2026-08-18 contribution materially changes several 2026-08-12 open rulings. It is not a decorative Jung appendix.

### R16.11 — Jung witnesses; QL derives

**Reaffirmed.** Psychological, theological and cultural material can provide powerful witnesses, refractions and historical/mythemic developments. It does not become the derivational source of QL's native notation or theorem structure. `X/x` remains authorial QL notation.

### R16.12 — Job has multiple offices; the old “fifth mytheme” proposal is superseded as too narrow

**Ratified supersession of C14's narrow proposal.** Job must not be reduced to “a fifth mytheme.” The current quilt discovers at least three different offices:

1. **methodological/epistemic:** lived evidence contradicts a protected account; the witness is pressured to falsify himself so the account can remain coherent;
2. **mythemic/psychological:** a purported whole is unable to answer the contrary it excluded; Job carries the contradiction that transforms the God-image;
3. **theological-historical/source:** Jung's continuing-incarnation and related claims remain source-bound historical/theological material, not QL proof.

The final census decides whether these require one page with several relations or several record types. The important ratification is that no single “Job mytheme” container is allowed to erase the other operations.

### R16.13 — monoisation and counter-generation

**Ratified as a major late-quilt operation, with notation boundary retained.** A living cultural field is Mono/Poly. A local formation can forget `/0`, naturalise its historically formed boundary and present itself as the whole. Excluded Poly does not vanish; it returns counter-facing.

Counterculture can therefore perform a healthy corrective office, but it is not healthy by definition. It becomes another monoculture when the counter takes the source-position as its own. The political test is:

> does the counter return the slash, or inherit the throne?

The candidate notation `(1)` for monoisation remains **candidate political/mythemic notation only**, not a canonical theorem glyph until formal review.

### R16.14 — sym-ballein is not centrism

**Ratified.** When two monocultures present themselves as plurality, the answer is not necessarily a midpoint between their propositions. Sym-ballein returns the **constitutive field** in which the poles arose and makes the relation answerable again. It preserves real opposition, refusal and conflict while refusing the fantasy that the whole becomes complete when the other is cancelled, appropriated or fused.

### R16.15 — cultural individuation is not collective ego

**Ratified.** State, Volk, Party, Church, Market, Science, Humanity, Platform, Model or Epi-Logos itself can become a collective ego when it imagines itself as the Subject/whole.

Cultural individuation means almost the inverse: a culture becomes capable of recognising its images as images, measures as measures, Gods as God-images, institutions as instituted, models as models, technologies as extensions of differentiated activity, and shadows as internal evidence rather than proof that an external enemy contains what the culture excludes.

This is a strong bridge between Jung, Gebserian diaphaneity, world-picture/world-atlas and the politics of the count.

### R16.16 — Man / Humanity

**Ratified as a necessary distinction, subject to exact naming in final composition.** “Man” can name the historically closed measure in which one local image of the human becomes the implicit standard of reality. “Humanity” can instead name the open empirical Mono/Poly field of human plurality, answerable to grounds no individual, state, culture or platform owns.

This must not become collective inflation: Humanity is not the new super-subject or substitute God.

### R16.17 — trust is intensified by efficacy

**Ratified.** Trust is not generated by making the other harmless. A relation of trust exists where something real remains at stake and the relation remains enterable, revisable, withdrawable and answerable.

The greater the efficacy of an agent, institution or technical system to affect a world, the greater the trust burden borne by the relation to it. Artificial intelligence makes this acute because useful intelligence is sought partly for determinations that exceed what its maker already knew how to produce. A system valuable only when it never surprises, contradicts or alters its user would surrender much of the intelligence being sought.

This strengthens rather than replaces the existing root-dependency account of trust.

### R16.18 — deepfake as counterfeit symbolon

**Ratified as an Argued interpretation; empirical effects remain source-gated.** Synthetic media can counterfeit not only a proposition or image but the apparent provenance relation itself: body/utterance, event/recording, speaker/voice, source/sign.

A deepfake is therefore an exact contemporary candidate for **counterfeit symbolon**: one half presents itself as though it could be returned to a counterpart/source that never stood behind it in the claimed way.

The second-order trust effects, liar's-dividend claims and efficacy of provenance technologies must be empirically sourced. Cryptographic/audit mechanisms may strengthen warranted reliance; they do not generate trust from inside the mark. Persons and institutions still stand surety for the account.

### R16.19 — protected account

**Ratified as a major connector requiring argument/concept census.** Job, the Prisoner, mass formation, deepfake and the hidden evaluator converge on a common political-epistemic operation:

> who gets to define the account, who can question its ground, what happens to evidence that contradicts it, and can the represented person or world answer back strongly enough to change the representation?

This operation should be tested for its own argument/concept office. It must not be lost by being scattered among five examples, nor multiplied into duplicate nodes if the power/Deferential Intelligence/Politics-of-the-Count structure already carries it cleanly.

### R16.20 — Ares / Aphrodite / Harmonia

**Ratified in the richer late form.** Ares is not bad division; difference, boundary, refusal, defence and the capacity to say no are necessary. Aphrodite is not automatically good unity; union can become fusion, seduction, conformity, possession or counterfeit gathering.

Healthy Ares gives differentiated beings capable of meeting. Healthy Aphrodite retains them in relation. Harmonia is differentiated concurrence produced through the tension of division and relation, not peace after conflict has been deleted.

This makes the pair an affective/mythemic carrier of the two logics without turning the myth into the theorem.

### R16.21 — Epi-Logos as culture cannot become the monoculture of Epi-Logos

**Ratified.** Epi-Logos is strongest as a practice of returning achieved accounts to their conditions, not as the worldview that finally owns the field.

Every science, myth, political position, model and culture can remain determinate while its measure, source, exclusions, affected world and return remain visible. Epi-Logos contradicts itself if its own relational account becomes the monopoly from which every other account must derive.

This gives the concluding culture/commons material a self-applying criterion.

## 7. Ratified QL-native Wiki page form

The page-format stipulation is now explicit and governing.

A field page begins from the **raw QL sixfold**:

```markdown
# <record title>

## #0

## #1

## #2

## #3

## #4

## #5→0
```

There are **no universal semantic section headings**. The former local skill mapping (`In-quantum / Definition / Operation / Pattern-Identity / Context / Quintessence`) is rejected as a global template.

At page creation time, the record's actual operation, register, incoming/outgoing relations, canonical office and QL foundational qualities determine what each position means. Page-specific subtitles can then be written where they improve precision.

The foundational QL qualities orient and test the local sixfold — inherited/uncounted ground; first determination; selection/differentiation; relation/pattern/decision; contextual recomposition; recognition/return — but they are not headings to be copied into every record. The point is **QL-shaped semantic generation**, not six boxes with pre-written semantics.

This rule applies equally to human-authored Markdown, AI-assisted page development, O:I Explore rendering and AIKit structured account/navigation. Renderers may recognise `#0…#5→0` structurally; they must not impose one global gloss across unlike records.

## 8. Ratified Symbolon ↔ O:I boundary

Symbolon is the canonical authored world. O:I/AIKit make it projectable, navigable, encounterable and returnable; they do not inherit authorship simply by rendering it.

The minimum relation remains:

```text
canonical Symbolon source
        ↓ selective Projection
O:I WikiSpace / Explore representation
        ↓ Encounter / authored or agentic revision
Projection revision / return proposal
        ↓ explicit source-return review
accepted canonical source change OR retained divergent Projection
```

`SharedField ≠ WikiSpace ≠ Context`.

Wiki relation ≠ Contribution relation ≠ learned KnowledgeRoute.

A browser edit is not silently a canonical source mutation. The Projection carries identity and revision; accepted return requires an explicit source-return operation.

AIKit Knowledge Navigation should traverse sources/search/read/relations/route/frame/explain/history over the same authored world. Product-understanding, structured-account-authoring and projection-authoring are implementation seams to consume, not reasons to invent a second account ontology.
