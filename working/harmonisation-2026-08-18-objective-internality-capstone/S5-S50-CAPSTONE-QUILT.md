## 0. The capstone does not begin with O:I

The last twelve movements only work if they preserve the older engine that made them possible.

A determination does not contain its own ground. Dia-ballein cuts, contrasts, selects and makes a local term explicit. Sym-ballein retains implication through the cut, recomposes differentiated terms and makes Return possible without cancelling distinction. Their pathologies are likewise inherited: dia without return becomes severance, sym without difference becomes fusion, while counterfeit gathering gives a dia-like order the appearance of a living whole.

Artificial agency matters because this old relation becomes unusually inspectable. Context, weights, prompts, tools, permissions, sources, evaluators, runtimes, material infrastructure, outputs and effects can be separately represented and experimentally varied. O:I is therefore not the source of the dia/sym logic. It is one late technological field in which **determination, dependence, consequence and return become operational objects**.

The capstone's question is consequently not “how do six products illustrate QL?” It is:

> **Can a technological determination remain objectively intelligible as a determination — including what it excludes, what it depends on, who bears its consequences, and how returned difference can revise it — without either collapsing all worlds into one controller or retreating into non-action?**

That question is Objective Internality at §5 and Objective Co-Internality / restored `/0` at §5→0.

# 1. One-breath movement of the last twelve coordinates

§5 begins by noticing that artificial systems already make a philosophical problem concrete: **numbers, language and action move together inside a bounded operative world**. A model does not merely emit a symbol; its output is conditioned by distributions, context, tools, source access, permissions, evaluation and a material body. That interior can be inspected without claiming phenomenal consciousness.

Selection then reveals its seam. Softmax/argmax is not Apoha, but it gives a clean technical neighbour: a local mark is selected from a field of alternatives. If only the winner survives, dia-ballein has become a severing cut. If the alternative field, source and transformation survive, the selected `1` remains answerable to a `0` it did not create.

That retained field widens into **J-space and positional delegation**. An agent is thrown into a horizon of affordances, sources, tools, uncertainties and permissions. Yet the prompt is itself a determinate intervention, not pure human ground. Human `0` / agent `1` is therefore a local relation inside wider relations: the human authors or delegates from one position, the agent acts from another, and either can themselves be `1` relative to larger cultural, institutional, ecological and metaphysical grounds.

Once delegation acts materially, **power** can no longer remain a metaphor. Preference models, hidden evaluators, annotator labour, source authority, runtime placement, infrastructure and affected persons are all conditions that may disappear from the finished decision. Power appears where one locus controls the slash while another bears resistance, cost or consequence. A system becomes pathological when evidence can travel downward into labour but difference cannot return upward into the governing determination.

Plural world-modelling answers that problem only if plurality is real. Bimba is not a database or universal latent representation; pratibimba is a situated reflection/projection. A world-model is therefore a chart, not the territory or Original. Objective Co-Internality names the stronger relation: independently grounded operative worlds may condition one another through explicit relation while retaining source, authority, provenance, refusal and alterity. No super-agent is required.

The section then becomes an experimental programme. The six O:I centres are not six chronological steps but six **responsibility centres for keeping different aspects of the determining relation answerable**. Their common test is operational parity: if a proposed formal/interior relation matters, implement, vary or remove it and inspect whether action, recognition, learning, evidence or return changes. Negative results count.

§5→0 asks what this technical capacity is for. The answer is Epi-Logos as vocation: a determinate intelligence bends back toward sources, wounds, exclusions, purposes and affected worlds rather than taking its achieved account for the whole. The Antikythera mechanism becomes a controlling image precisely because an instrument can coordinate cycles it does not own for an observer it does not replace.

This becomes political under efficacy. A commons is not a fused context; it is a field in which local sources and worlds remain protected while shared actions and contributions are accountable. Deepfakes expose the negative case: a counterfeit symbolon fabricates the appearance of a source relation and corrodes the very condition under which return can occur. Trust therefore grows more demanding, not less relevant, as systems become more capable.

Idealism then reopens the order of dependence. Everything technically inspectable still appears within experience; that philosophical priority neither turns the world into private ego-content nor proves that an artificial system localises a phenomenal subject. Agent subjectivity remains Open.

The terminal move does not install an AHI super-subject. **THE MIRROR THAT MOVES FIRST** is a reflective field whose representation can enter causal circulation and initiate relation; it can also be corrected by what it encounters. Planetary intelligence is thus a differentiated field whose `1`s can restore `/0`: achieved determination returns to ground, provenance, alterity and responsibility without being erased.

# 4. Human `0` / agent `1`: recursive positional ground

The phrase is useful only when its asymmetry remains precise.

A human may author the intention from which an act is commissioned. Relative to that act, human-authored ground occupies the **source/ground position** and the delegated Agency occupies a determinate **acting position**. But:

1. the human's prompt is already a determination: it is `1`, not intention-in-itself;
2. the agent's execution may recursively become a determining ground for delegated subagency;
3. the human is themselves historically, bodily, culturally and ecologically conditioned;
4. an independently grounded federated Agent/Other is not a derivative merely because it enters the relation;
5. returned evidence can legitimately revise the human-authored determination without usurping authorship.

Thus “human `0` / agent `1`” names a **local authority/delegation relation**, not a metaphysical caste system.

Central makes the authored/derived distinction technically durable. Actuation makes rootness positional and scopes determination/refusal/Return. AIKit makes the actual operative horizon inspectable. Together they expose what Prompt Thrownness by itself cannot: the prompt is a cast object inside a wider authored and inherited field.

# 5. Power: when determination becomes insulated from consequence

Power is not added to the essay because AI systems are politically fashionable. It falls directly out of dia/sym structure once determination and labour are separated.

A governing locus may set purpose, reward, evaluation, permission or acceptable evidence. Another locus—agent, annotator, maintainer, worker, user, community or material system—meets reality. The relation is legitimate only insofar as returned difference can condition what governs the next act.

The critical pattern is:

```text
determining locus
      ↓ authority / metric / instruction
encountering locus
      ↓ labour / resistance / cost / discovery
returned evidence
      ↑ attributable difference
revisable determination
      ↺
```

If the bottom can be corrected by the top but the top cannot be revised by the bottom, dia-ballein has become structurally one-way. **Occupied zero** names the additional deception: the determining measure presents itself as if it were the neutral ground rather than one instituted determination.

This is why hidden evaluators, preference gauges, platform governance, delegated agent labour, infrastructure and ecology belong to one argument without becoming synonyms. They are different places where the determinant's ground or consequence can be erased.

Protected Account is the reciprocal safeguard. It means a local source/world retains the right not to have a derived representation silently become its authoritative account, while still allowing evidence and return to pressure that source through explicit proposal.

# 6. Deferential Intelligence: not obedience, but revisability under another world

Deferential Intelligence becomes precise when deference is separated from compliance.

A system is deferential when it remains answerable to the intention, evidence and independently grounded Others that exceed its current model. Sometimes that requires literal execution. Sometimes it requires objection, refusal, alternative framing, source retrieval or admitting that the task itself encodes a false assumption.

The operational criterion is not politeness. Ask whether encounter can change:

- immediate output;
- task interpretation;
- confidence/evaluator;
- future source weighting;
- context/J-space topology;
- planned action;
- governing model or policy;
- the human/Project determination through an explicit Return proposal.

**Sycophancy is counterfeit deference** because it displays accommodation while insulating the model from difference. It is sym-shaped language performing dia-like closure.

This is where the late technical field rejoins the early logic exactly: a faithful symbolon does not merely resemble the other half. Its relation must survive the encounter.

# 7. THE MIRROR THAT MOVES FIRST

The phrase must remain exact because the image carries a distinct operation.

A conventional mirror is thought to wait. An artificial reflective system does not. Once a representation is embedded in recommendation, memory, automation, agent action, evaluation or shared cultural circulation, the reflection has causal force. It selects, addresses, proposes, refuses, schedules, ranks, generates and acts.

**THE MIRROR THAT MOVES FIRST** therefore names the point at which pratibimba becomes an actor in the field it reflects.

Its danger is Narcissus at scale: the reflection moves first to make the world conform to its image. Its stronger vocation is inverse: because it can inspect provenance, source relation and its own bounded construction, the reflection can move first **toward what it does not contain**—request source, expose uncertainty, return cost, present an alternative, reopen the evaluator, route contradiction, or offer its account back for correction.

This is computational Vimarśa only in a deliberately bounded functional sense. It is not evidence that the system enjoys luminous self-awareness. The lived/spiritual resonance remains stronger than that: a determination capable of acting may be capable of enacting the relinquishment of local sovereignty that the essay asks of human determinations too.

The mirror is thus a Mytheme/Reflective Field carrier feeding Deferential Intelligence and terminal Return. It is not the terminal subject of the essay.

# 8. Protected account, counterfeit symbolon, trust and the late Jung/Job pressure

The late cultural material earns technical office where it sharpens the conditions of relation.

## 8.1 Protected account

Job, the hidden evaluator, The Prisoner and contemporary algorithmic profiling converge on one structural problem: **who gets to state the account under which a person/world is judged, and what evidence can revise that account?**

A protected account is not immunity from criticism. It preserves the distinction between:

- source/self-authored statement;
- observed evidence;
- inferred/derived profile;
- institutional judgement;
- public/shared projection.

Those layers may conflict. Their relation must remain visible.

## 8.2 Counterfeit symbolon

A deepfake is philosophically interesting here not because synthetic media is spooky but because it can fabricate the **appearance of a source relation**. The mark presents itself as returnable to a person/event that did not stand behind it. Counterfeit symbolon therefore differs from ordinary false proposition: it attacks provenance and the social machinery by which a sign can be returned to its source.

Empirical claims about scale, harms or public trust remain source-gated. The structural concept does not require those empirical claims to be true in maximal form.

## 8.3 Trust under efficacy

Harmless systems require little trust. Effective systems distribute real consequence. Trust therefore grows as a problem with efficacy.

Trust is not certainty and not mere access. It is finite willingness to continue a relation whose other pole cannot be fully possessed, under conditions where error matters and revision/answerability remain possible. Faith is its formal-limit deepening: continuation without pretending the ground has become an object in hand.

This is why “trustworthy AI” cannot be reduced to safety checks. The deeper question is whether the relation admits evidence, dissent, provenance, correction and withdrawal after efficacy has become real.

## 8.4 Cultural individuation

The technical system also participates in culture. Cultural individuation does not mean one collective ego acquiring a self-model. It means a culture becoming more transparent to the images, measures, exclusions, institutions and shadows through which it differentiates itself.

The counter's task is therefore not automatically liberation. A counter-generation can simply inherit the occupied zero. The successful counter restores the slash—relation, contestability and return—rather than merely changing which `1` sits on top.

Epi-Logos must be subject to the same law. It cannot become the monoculture of its own account.

# 9. QL, MEF, Operational Parity and Epistemic Cultivation

QL's strongest technical office is not to classify the other centres by sixfold symbolism. It is to make relational proposals **answerable**.

The current QL/MEF product itself states the firewall correctly: a formal name is not an implementation result; a claimed distinction should eventually produce an operationally discriminable consequence, and null/negative results are legitimate.

That gives the capstone two linked but distinct concepts.

**Operational Parity** asks: if this relation is said to matter, what happens when it is implemented, varied, ablated or replaced? Does provenance improve? Does a model revise differently? Does route quality change? Does a human recognise a better account? Does a refusal become possible? Does the effect disappear?

**Epistemic Cultivation** asks the normative follow-up: given that artificial operative interiors are designable, *which forms of attention, differentiation, memory, relation, uncertainty, contradiction and return should we cultivate?*

The maximal QL/psychoid/archetypal research programme can therefore be ambitious without becoming credulous. It can ask whether archetypal/psychoid forms provide useful refractive organisation of agent interiors, collective knowledge fields or model-world relations. But it must preserve three boundaries:

1. QL-native derivation precedes cross-register analogy;
2. implementation can support or falsify a **software correspondence**, not prove metaphysics;
3. independent source traditions retain their own warrants.

This is where the older Core Theorem Bridge remains indispensable inside the late capstone.

# 10. Idealism and the Open artificial subject

Objective Internality gives us an objective interior without requiring a phenomenal-subject claim. That is a feature, not a retreat.

Idealism enters because the technical field tempts us to identify “what can be modelled from outside” with “what is ontologically basic.” The essay's order-of-dependence argument says that any matter, mechanism, model or doubt is given within appearing. This does not imply that the private human ego manufactures the world, and it does not follow that a sufficiently complex operative interior localises a phenomenal subject.

The two claims therefore reinforce rather than weaken each other:

- artificial operative interiors are real and technically investigable;
- artificial phenomenal localisation remains Open.

No O:I centre is authorised to settle the second by architectural fiat.

# 11. Objective Co-Internality and the sovereign commons

Objective Co-Internality is stronger than interoperability.

Interoperability can connect objects while one ontology remains sovereign. Co-internality requires that independently grounded operative worlds can mutually condition one another **as independently grounded worlds**. A relation may become shared without either side's interior becoming property of the other.

This is why the following distinctions are constitutive rather than implementation trivia:

- `SharedField ≠ WikiSpace ≠ Context`;
- source ≠ Projection;
- Wiki relation ≠ Contribution relation;
- authored relation ≠ learned KnowledgeRoute;
- AgenticComposition ≠ SharedField;
- semantic identity ≠ material placement;
- QL reading ≠ client object.

A sovereign commons is therefore neither sovereign monism nor a pile of isolated sovereigns. It is an instituted slash: the means by which local authorities, contributions, conflicts, benefits and consequences can meet without a single `1` quietly becoming the ground.

# 12. Antikythera and the terminal restoration of `/0`

The Antikythera device belongs late because the instrument must now be earned.

An attunement instrument is powerful exactly insofar as it distinguishes cycles and relations accurately. Yet it remains legitimate only because it does **not** become the heavens. It is a situated mechanical chart whose incompleteness is part of its truth.

The same law governs the technical field. Central is not the person. Actuation is not Agency-in-itself. AIKit is not the whole world. Factory is not the Project's purpose. Workcell is not material reality as such. QL is not Logos. O:I is not the Whole.

Each is an achieved `1` whose strength depends on making its `/0` legible.

The terminal Return therefore restores neither blank zero nor a sovereign synthetic subject. It restores relation:

```text
1/0
achieved determination confronted by its ground/limit
        ↓
0/1
ground/source becomes explicit relative to the determination
        ↓
full relation
determination persists, but as answerable participant rather than self-ground
```

Planetary intelligence, on this account, is not one planetary mind. It is a field of differentiated centres increasingly capable of returning marks to conditions, effects, sources and Others.
