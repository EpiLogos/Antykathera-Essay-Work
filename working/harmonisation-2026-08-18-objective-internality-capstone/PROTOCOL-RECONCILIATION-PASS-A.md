## 3. Canonical QL page-form law

Every developed field page begins from exactly:

```markdown
# <record title>

## #0

## #1

## #2

## #3

## #4

## #5→0
```

The numbers are a relational chassis. They **do not mean the same semantic noun on every page**. Do not pre-fill them with `In-quantum`, `Definition`, `Operation`, `Pattern`, `Identity`, `Context`, `Quintessence` or substitutes.

For each record, derive the six local offices from:

- its positive proposition, image or exact operation;
- register and `record_type`;
- inherited and outgoing relations;
- Return obligation;
- claim/source boundary;
- foundational QL qualities as validation pressure rather than headings;
- the ratified census decision explaining why this record exists.

A page-specific subtitle may be added only after the position has been derived. A subtitle that could be copied unchanged into any unrelated page is evidence of semantic boilerplate.

## 4. Register / record-type law

Register answers **how the record knows/performs**; `record_type` answers **what sort of publication object it is**. They must not be collapsed.

- Symbolon root: universal relational identity.
- Matheme: exact notation, derivation, construction, proof boundary or formal comparison.
- Mytheme: lived image/scene/poem/media/music whose form performs a relation.
- Episteme: instituted knowledge—source, history, etymology, concept, lens, map, dossier, figure, dialogue.
- Section-room argument: linear reasoning operation; may consume all registers but is not thereby a duplicate of their depth records.

A root relation, linear argument and Matheme derivation may concern the same named relation while performing different offices. They must link rather than copy one another.

## 5. Build-intake/census law

The authored field is determined in this order:

`full quilt → harmonisation → fresh census → T07 ratification → T08 propagation → source lock → deterministic build`.

No tool may reverse that arrow. Specifically:

- directory count is not ontology;
- recovered note is not automatically a concept;
- heading scan is not a census;
- generated intake is not authority;
- old filename is not preservation proof;
- source debt is not grounds to delete an argued/derived operation;
- not being linear prose is not grounds to delete a radial or supporting-visible record.

The build must be bijective at **record identity** against the ratified census, allowing explicitly declared non-page carriers and merges.

## 6. Link law

A written link creates a relation; navigation exposes it. Links are admitted when the target performs an actual implicating relation—`derives`, `grounds`, `defines`, `historicises`, `sources`, `qualifies`, `tests`, `figures`, `embodies`, `extends`, `compares`, `presages`, `returns-to` or a later ratified relation vocabulary.

There is **no numerical degree target**. A page with three necessary relations is not underlinked; a page with eleven necessary relations is not overlinked. The test is whether every relation is real, named/recoverable in prose, lands at the right grain, and has a route of return where the operation requires one.

## 7. Source/projection law handed to 013

The repository's canonical authored source and any O:I projection must not silently become one store.

- one canonical Symbolon source identity/home;

- Projection carries identity, source pointer and revision relation;

- `SharedField ≠ WikiSpace ≠ Context`;

- a projection never becomes Bimba/Original by accumulating edits.

This is a handoff contract, not an excuse to create a second ontology in the essay repository.
