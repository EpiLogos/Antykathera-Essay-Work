## R1. P2: what the means serve — M08/M09/M11

**Carrier:** `working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/P2 - Para Trisika Vivarana Notes - Abhinavagupta and Jaideva Singh - Introduction.md`, full file, blob `aee3fd24b75402ed0e8fc2851fc71759f290584a`. The long final substantive discussion is Taylor's account of technique, its obscured end and the project's practical intention.

The notebook begins from divine dialogue and the possibility of rigorous, non-arbitrary plurality of interpretation. Taylor develops these into understanding as renewed participation rather than accumulation of recorded formulations. His distinction between knowing and knowledge does not prohibit preservation: a retained expression must be capable of becoming meaningful in a new act. A stored formulation cannot replace that act. This bears on the difference between retaining an utterance and understanding its present address in M07.

The practical argument has successive steps. A spiritual practice offers means toward an end. When the end remains obscure, possession of more techniques can itself become the measure of progress. The practitioner can then keep seeking an improved object or completed self-image where the purported end concerns the nature of the one already seeking. The means acquire the authority to certify the being they were meant to serve. Taylor does not answer by denying cultivation: he acknowledges preparatory development and proposes intermediate, individualised means. Recognition concerns what those means cannot manufacture; living and becoming continue after it.

**Restoration:** a changed instrument, an improved account of it and recognition of its ground must have different consequences in the prose. M08 will distinguish these through the same misheard call. M09 will let a corrective determination expose another determination's conditions, then relinquish its own claim to finality. ### Memory: relation to the earlier act

Dyczkowski distinguishes the retained trace, its present recollection and imaginative recomposition. The Buddhist causal-imprint account is a real reply: a later cognition need not be causally disconnected from an earlier one. The Śaiva objection then asks a further question. How is what appears now apprehended as a memory of that earlier act, rather than merely as a new image caused by it? Presentness of the image and pastness of its reference have to be known together. The objection concerns the manifest relation across the occurrences, not a denial that traces and causal processes matter. The author's adoption of the positive experient must carry that reason.

**Prose consequence:** retain M07's three operations; make the difference between causal continuation and recognition of a past occurrence explicit in the comparative run. Do not infer infallible memory, an unchanging biography or an enduring mental object.

### Comparison: differences must be apprehended together

The blue/yellow comparison is more than two registrations. A difference can be known only where the terms are available in relation. Combining them by extinguishing their contrast would also destroy what comparison knows. Śaiva reflexivity supplies a positive account of the unity that differentiates without reducing the several appearances to one indistinct content.

**Prose consequence:** the remembered tone and the present tone can differ while being compared. An extra inner spectator would renew the problem rather than solve it. This argument is not a claim that every Buddhist account lacks reflexivity or relation.

### Exclusion: absence has a positive occasion and effect

An absent answer can matter only within a determinate occasion in which an answer was expected; a refusal becomes relevant against an available undertaking. The absent alternative need not become a tiny negative object. Its consequence is borne by the constituted field. This is the native development beyond a mere list of things currently present.

The Pind correction is controlling. The actual source is `submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/SOURCE.md`, blob `de5474af02025eecc714715ae325cfafcebb2852`, read in full. Card q001 is PSV V:11d, dissertation p. 85. Card q002 is annotation 181, p. 183: the positive thing qualified by preclusion, not mere preclusion. Pind provisionally assigns the latter fragment to the Sāmānyaparīkṣāvyāsa. Neither that attribution nor a fictitious absence of positive particulars in Buddhism may be strengthened. The baseline already cites the 2009 dissertation correctly; a 2015 locator considered in working recovery was rejected and does not amend the baseline's established citation.

**Prose consequence:** preserve the cup/positive referent and the difference between semantic formation and propositional double negation. Śaiva preference cannot be purchased by misdescribing apoha. Carry the refusal's capacity to change the promise's authority, not just its wording.

### Action: intention, execution and result

Walking consists of successive changes, but neither one footfall nor an inventory of all the footfalls establishes what was undertaken or whether it was completed. Intention selects a course; execution changes the situation; a result answers, or fails to answer, the purpose. Their unity is enacted through these differences. A result can correct the account of the intention: doing what was first requested can fail to give the help the request concerned.

**Prose consequence:** keep the promise as an alteration of another person's possibilities. The action requires an account of agency, not just a chain of results. Neither an immutable proprietor nor a rule incapable of revision follows. The final judgment remains answerable to the other and the changed situation.

### Arising and perishing: change itself appears

The appearance and disappearance of a sound are distinguishable from a sheer nothing which no act encounters. Beginning, persistence and ending can be known as changes; their appearing cannot be supplied by one isolated phase declared self-sufficient. The Śaiva argument keeps the changing content and the manifesting power distinct within one relation. It does not make every sound endure, or derive a physical cosmology from the fact of hearing a phrase end.

**Prose consequence:** the vanished call can still bear upon a promise. Ended formation and continued efficacy must remain different. This joins the temporal discussion without making Whitehead or Bergson teach Śaiva metaphysics.

### Conclusion, with its scope intact

The essay adopts the Śaiva positive account because its experient and powers address these connected demands in one differentiated relation: remembering, comparing, excluding, intending, acting and apprehending change. This is the author's reasoned choice for the inquiry, not an assertion of parity after all the arguments have been presented. The Buddhist criticism of a fabricated proprietor remains binding. The preference does not require the claim that every Buddhist school reduces experience to unrelated instants, denies positive particulars, or lacks a non-dual account. The revised passage must reach a conclusion without confusing a defended adoption with a completed adjudication of the histories of all these traditions.

## R4. Changed state, improved self-description, recognition — s0-F02

**Carriers:** P2/P3 above; the whole core file `working/sources-texts-references/10-7-2026-core-theorems-pithy.md`, including §X, the tattvic unit, and §XI, kalpa/apoha/Māyā; the original state and recognition developments in §21 of the developmental quilt, read with the independent copula's sourceward return; `working/sources-texts-references/chat-logs-for-quilting/07-08-2026-spirit-nature-man-humanity.md`, the state discussions and subsequent speaker corrections; canonical C19/A07/A26/C41; the complete Phone pieces on two kinds of knowledge, the mind of Man, making the implicit cosmology objective and theory serving praxis.

The distinction is not a ranking of three pleasant experiences. A changed state can reduce agitation or make an action possible. A better self-description can explain why the agitation arose and make its conditions revisable. Both are changes within the means. Recognition concerns the source of their being known and the mistaken identification of that source with a particular state or self-image. The same person can be quieter yet remain invested in being vindicated; can explain the investment while making explanatory sophistication a new badge; and can find that neither badge defines the knower for whom both were present. These are different failures and consequences.

The reason nirvikalpa cannot simply be equated with recognition is operational. Where an absorption depends on excluding differentiated thought, its maintenance requires a condition which ordinary differentiated life interrupts. Returning speech can then be treated as loss, and the pursuit can resume as an attempt to reproduce the state. Recognition knows the ground of both quiet and articulated experience. Its return must therefore survive the resumption of thought, perception and the other's address. This does not deny preparatory practices, the value of concentration, or the possibility of recognitive insight in absorption. It denies the automatic inference from a state's non-conceptual character to recognition of its ground.

Thought-as-medicine supplies a complementary operation. A claim such as 'every such call is an accusation' can be followed through its evidence, remembered circumstances and excluded possibilities. A corrective thought can make its necessity fail. It has succeeded when the encounter can be received again, not when the contrary formula has become compulsory. The correction must be relinquishable as a final identity in its turn. This is the authorial application of the sourceward determination, not a newly invented classical quotation.

**Restoration and location:** a connected M08 run will show the three distinctions in the call/grievance example; M09 will give the medicinal thought a complete action and release; M11 will state the nirvikalpa/recognition consequence at the dream/return junction. No generic practice programme, new autobiographical attainment or claim that recognition guarantees ethical perfection will be inserted. It changes the authority of the instrument; finite mistakes still require correction.
