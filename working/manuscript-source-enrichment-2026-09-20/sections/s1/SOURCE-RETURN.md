### M14 — Arithmetic, a retained denominator, and the Indian/Kashmiri braid

**Sources read.** The Colebrooke SOURCE house was read in full, including the actual selected translated passages: Brahmagupta XVIII.31/34, printed p.339; Bhāskara, Bījagaṇita I.14 and I.16, pp.137–138. **Recovered reasoning.** Addition and multiplication admit zero differently: six plus no further units remains six; six groups of no units have no members. Division asks for a unique return through multiplication. A nonzero denominator ordinarily allows that return; zero does not. Bhāskara nevertheless retains a denominator-zero expression and compares invariance under finite insertion or extraction with divine immutability through creation and destruction. The analogy is in the primary translated text. It is not an embellishment invented by this essay.

Dutta's corrective changes the question from whether an ordinary field admits an inverse for zero to whether historical retained expressions had another regime of operations. Restricted cancellation and cross-multiplication can be part of that regime. Kaplan's ordinary cancellation warning remains correct; it does not by itself settle every historical practice or justify the cultural judgment attached to it.

The Śaiva contribution is positive and specific. Dyczkowski's transcript pp.60–75 develops luminosity, reflexive awareness, knowing/knower/known, unity and differentiated appearance. At p.65 the difficulty is exact: an undifferentiated light which could not distinguish blue from yellow would fail to explain perception. At pp.69–74, vimarśa supplies the power to distinguish, relate and recognise appearances without placing a second independent substance outside awareness. P2/P3's personed language brings that relation into I, you and it, but their copied translator-introduction leads are not authenticated quotations from Abhinavagupta's commentary.

**Mature correction.** The central plan, orienting principles, M14, A10/A36 and the R6 claim-strength ledger retain **Abhinavagupta c.975–1025 / Bījagaṇita 1150**, about 125 years from the conventional terminal date. The raw notebook's looser twelfth-century contemporaneity is superseded. This is not the first invention of zero and not a demonstrated transmission from Kashmir to Bhāskara. Their historically situated co-presence gives the essay two developed accounts of finite differentiation and an exceeding condition, not a vague equation of Indian traditions with emptiness.

**Landing.** M14 will preserve the arithmetic and primary divine analogy, then let the problem of differentiation within unity establish the philosophical junction. The historical qualification follows that positive development rather than replacing it.

### M15 — Generativity consists of different exact operations

**Recovered late Kaplan route.** The raw notebook's Chapter 15 cluster and SOURCE pp.203–215 distinguish mediant insertion, the empty product, finite ordinal construction and the Boolean generator. The baseline developed only part of this route. No operation should be substituted for another merely because each can be described using zero, one or nothing.

The empty product equals one because one preserves multiplication when no further factor is supplied. It does not multiply a zero factor: inserting a zero would annihilate the product. In the von Neumann representation, zero is the empty set; one is its singleton; each successor `S(n)=n∪{n}` retains the predecessors and includes the preceding number itself as a new member. `{∅}` has one member, even though that member has no members. Membership, inclusion and equality are different. Finite iteration does not by itself supply a set of all natural numbers.

NOR is a different generation: `p↓q=¬(p∨q)`. Self-application gives negation; applying NOR twice gives disjunction; NOR of separately negated inputs gives conjunction. Its functional completeness concerns Boolean truth functions. It neither identifies the empty set with awareness nor derives the QL slash. NAND is another complete connective and must not silently replace the NOR actually being encountered here.

**Philosophical consequence.** The full core theorem, section IX, and the complete relevant Mono-Poly development distinguish the formal empty object from the Subject which is not another object. One can hold zero as a member without being zero, just as a mark can refer to its condition without enclosing that condition. The achieved construction gives a precise discipline to the symbolic use; it does not turn a set-theoretic convention into a cosmogony.

**Landing.** M15 will develop the empty product and successor as connected but distinct answers to how an explicit one may arise. NOR will have its own compact operation and a clearly marked later payoff, rather than acting as a substitute First Spanda equation.

### M17 — A limit of the operation discloses the operation's frame

The baseline's ordinary-field proof is retained. Distributivity and additive cancellation give `0q=0`; hence `0q=1` is impossible in a nontrivial field. `0q=0` admits every q and cannot select a unique quotient. Zero products cannot be cancelled to identify distinct multiplicands.

A changed system must state its changed permission. Assigning a total reciprocal a value at zero does not supply an ordinary inverse there. A point at infinity belongs to a changed space. Robinson's infinitesimal route, present in the raw notebook and Kaplan SOURCE pp.173–174, adds another instructive case: a nonzero infinitesimal is not zero, and taking a standard part is not substituting zero before division. This will be independently checked before the new calculation enters prose.
