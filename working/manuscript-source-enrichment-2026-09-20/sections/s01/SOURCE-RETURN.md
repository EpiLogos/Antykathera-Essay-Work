### Six different operations, not six synonyms for non-enclosure

The core's §V and the complete canonical `submission-package/essay/symbolon/episteme/arguments/A01-Subject-God-and-Faithful-Definition.md` give the following connected movement:

1. **Determinate assertion.** The definition distinguishes its intended referent and commits the speaker to consequences. Refusing all determination would not identify anything. Its claim concerns the condition of representation, not an additional object represented alongside other objects.
2. **Failure of final enclosure.** The sentence itself makes a bounded determination. An account of its own determining is still an account, encountered in a further present act. The failure concerns the office of exhaustive capture; it is not a proof that the positive relation just asserted is false.
3. **Productive success.** Once the difference made by the defining act is included in the account's use, the sentence performs the relation it names. Its boundedness becomes a means of disclosure rather than a claim to possession. The cage/window change is a change in what the form does, not a declaration that all boundaries are bad.
4. **Contextual completion.** Assertion, failed enclosure and successful disclosure become available together as one situated activity, with a body, history, concern and someone for whom the answer matters. Success and failure considered in isolation no longer exhaust this activity. This is not an extra pronoun or a spectator outside every circumstance.
5. **First-person identification.** An accurate contextual description can still speak *about* the defining activity. The next turn cannot be supplied by saying the definer “participates.” The source says: “The one who was defining was always the one being defined.” The question changes from obtaining another picture of a supposed source to recognising the presence undertaking the act. “I Am” is the change of position within the inquiry, not a final predicate acquired by a represented personality.
6. **Return without appropriation.** Recognition does not destroy the useful account, erase real differences or turn another person into my possession. It returns the capacity for further determination. The source remains unpossessed by the achieved description; the local life remains situated, answerable and capable of correction.

This distinction between the fourth and fifth turns is the precise force of fidelity finding **s01-F01**. The earlier selected opening already makes participation and non-enclosure intelligible. The commissioned improvement must make the identification happen in the reader's present act, not merely increase the number of statements about participation.

### Prerequisite: identity through difference

The file owns this as an axiom with a performative warrant, not an empirical discovery extracted from a list of philosophers.

From there it distinguishes predication—what is said of this one—from identification—this is that very one. For predications to vary while remaining predications *of someone or something*, an identifying relation must persist through the variation. For that identification to occur, the two presentations must be comparable within a circumstance. The derived triad is **name / instance / circumstance**. Homonymy provides a concrete pressure: two people can carry the same name, so the name's descriptive or audible content cannot by itself individuate its bearer. A changed name can also leave the person identifiable. The source's claim that naming depends on counting means prior individuation, not a requirement that someone recite numerals before speaking a name.
