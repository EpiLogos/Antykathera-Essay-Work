## 2. The direct derivation, in order

### A. The distinction already operates before its terms are named

The opening moves from “I see x” to awareness of the seeing of x. Seeing can become available for examination without the present awareness being manufactured by the examination. The subject, means and known have different offices inside the occurrence. This is the operative prerequisite for the later distinction between the Subject and the inner instrument.

In `§0`, `−/−` gives one mark-kind in two placements. The placements differ, while selection of either returns the same bare mark. `X/x` then preserves letter-identity while letting the selected case matter: determining capacity and determinate instance become distinguishable without being severed. The logical point is that identification requires both the same and a real difference of presentation. The native use of X/x is not a transcription of Lacan's S/s.

### B. The copula must preserve both identification and difference

In `§1`, the writing composes non-equality from equality crossed by the slash and states `/ = ( = =/≠ ≠ )`. The argument does not consist in announcing that identity and difference are the same thing. Identification must reach what is identified; severance would remove that relation. Identification must also have a difference to traverse; fusion would remove its work. The relation therefore carries both offices.

The same section gives the actual Subject/instrument/world distinction: `0`, `Ø`, `X`, `Ø/X` and `(0/Ø)/(1/X)`. The slashed zero is the describable inner instrument mistaken for the final knower. Making that instrument visible does not turn the Subject into another component behind it. This protects the first-person return from a new hidden-observer regress.

### C. Counting and holding the count are different acts

`§2` opens the copula into four appearances while retaining the two operative marks `/` and `=`. The middle `=/≠` is two-faced for the count and one hinge for the relation. `§3` then re-reads the four as three terms held through a contextual act: `4+2 → (3+1)+2`. The bracketed field is returned to the slash as its containing relation.

This matters for the opening's fourth move. Adding the statement “the reader participates” does not by itself perform the contextual turn. The earlier assertions, their limits, the means of asserting and the concern moving the inquiry must become available together. A contextual account is a real gain; it is not yet the first-person recognition.

### D. The crossing tests each isolated determination

In **The Catuṣkoṭi crossing**, encounter precedes the four corners. The lived occurrence is received, not generated by the propositions.

1. **IS:** the phenomenon belongs to appearing in awareness. Taken alone as undifferentiated identity, this removes the difference through which something can be known.
2. **IS-NOT:** the phenomenon and its awareness have distinguishable offices, and awareness is not exhausted by a particular appearance. Taken alone as severance, this removes the relation through which the known is available.
3. **BOTH:** the two true respects are held together. This corrects fusion and severance, but a completed description can still imagine its own holding to be outside the event it describes.
4. **NEITHER:** that imagined final position is itself exposed. Neither isolated predicate, nor the account of their conjunction, possesses the condition under which the whole consideration occurs.
5. **SILENCE:** the source explicitly distinguishes this from muteness and from a fifth alternative alongside the four. It is the lived gathering of their work, followed by return to encounter. The prior discriminations remain available.

The opening must translate this dependence into prose without importing the entire later formal apparatus or pretending that Nāgārjuna, Gödel or a graphic construction independently proves the native ontology. The Subject/God sixfold and the Subject/object crossing are related performances with different local terms; their relation must be argued, not presumed from the count.
