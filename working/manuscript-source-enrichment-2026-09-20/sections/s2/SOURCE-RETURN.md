## 1. The primitive relation must precede both its accountings

**Direct original:** `working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/Mono-Poly — The Two Ones and the Whole Field.md`, complete, blob `2941c62d528d9a683ac323a1bc9b4714a588a72b`; especially “The relation before its refractions,” “The numerical floor and the displacement of the One,” and “The slash: One relation, two orientations.” This is the authorial working synthesis, not an external source quotation. The complete copula/balleins synthesis below supplies its connected formal development.

The terms of `0/1` cannot first be installed as separately complete things and then related: each receives its office through their differentiation. Zero bears the singular, unobjectifiable condition; one is the first determinate mark, already carrying conditions, exclusions and possible further distinctions. Thus Mono/One and Poly/All do not mean one isolated item and the sum of other isolated items. All is not an inventory. A local mark can be exact without being the origin of its own exactness.

The source follows that claim into monopoly through a specific displacement. A local form depends on language, labour, inherited institutions and a world of relations, yet can present those conditions as its own property or achievement. Monopoly is the installation of a determinate `1` in the office of `0`. This does not render the form or its power unreal. It makes its authority answerable to the relations it has concealed.

**Preserve in M19:** the asymmetry between condition and formed result; the reality of differentiation; the difference between whole/many and aggregate/items. **Develop into M20:** the move from a local criterion to the claim that the criterion authorises the entire field. The derivation must precede the political diagnosis, not be inferred from an accumulation of political examples.

**Do not conflate:** Mono/Poly is the primitive relation; dia/syn are accountings of differentiation; trust is the lived reliance beneath knowing. The original explicitly refuses to use them as synonyms. M19 must give the transition among them, not three names for one vague relational attitude.

## 2. Recover the two accountings without reinstating their superseded moralisation

The practical consequence is a bookkeeping failure that can become coercively real: the dependencies denied by the account return through what the account does to other people.

The July synthesis distinguishes slashed polarity from slashless cancellation, but some of its language still lets an arithmetic notation carry the entire moral judgment. ## 3. The native 4+2 is a count of a binary and its self-relation

**Direct originals:** the copula/balleins synthesis §2; the Mono-Poly original's slash section; and `working/sources-texts-references/12-7-2026-poem-set-six-routes-of-two.md`, complete, blob `75f06ddbdb61feb58a60bf70d6405c912969660e`. The core theorem remains the governing native derivation; its directly declared relevant supports are still being traversed.

The source does not begin with six preferred themes. A distinction gives two terms. Once that distinction takes its own binary possibilities as its object, it gives `00`, `01`, `10`, `11`. Retaining the terms and their four ordered possibilities yields `2 + 2² = 4 + 2 = 6`. The pair is not consumed by its self-relation. Conversely, the four are not another name for the two. This is the reason for the count.

The inverse orientations `0/1` and `1/0` make the same relation legible from different ends. Their non-coincidence is indispensable: ordinary arithmetic admits the first quotient and not the second. The native return is not permission to erase that boundary. The nested `(0/1)/(1/0)` must retain both inner relations and the outer relation; it must not be flattened into a typographical slogan.

**M24 consequence:** enumerate the binary terms and all four pairings, then distinguish this six-position body from the eight-determination traversal. The latter brings the relation through its pre-determinate threshold and return; it is not a revised count of the four binary pairings. Full Spanda, ratio, topology and calculus development belongs to s3. Its deferred location does not excuse failure to perform the compact derivation here.

**Poem's actual movement:** P0 is the numerical poem; five verbal poems complete the set. P3's two routes distinguish landing on opposed ones from traversing their difference. ## 4. Symbolic life changes its interpreter and the field of the next encounter

**Direct original:** `working/sources-texts-references/Epi Paper Write-ups/Symbolon Dynamics — Archetype, Attractor, and Objective Internality.md`, complete, blob `9be96778e7e5962671fd8b6a4b1e37d0792f45b6`, including its temporal/logical table, energetic distinctions, objective internality and concluding source relation. It is an authorial synthesis; Van Eenwyk, Jung and Tillich retain their separate source work and must not be credited with deriving QL.

An archetypal image appears, while the ordering capacity it manifests exceeds that image. The living symbol is therefore not merely the visible mark: it is the event in which that mark conducts a power it does not exhaust. Its response can alter the interpreter and the conditions under which the image will next be received. A product of one cycle becomes a condition of another.

The original differentiates three offices rather than calling everything an attractor. A feeling-toned complex gives recurrent association and response an organised stability. Archetypal dynamics concern determining capacity across singular manifestations. Symbols can concentrate energy within an established pattern, expose its boundary, connect previously separated regions or participate in a change of regime. An attractor landscape, an archetype and a lived context-world are not thereby one mathematical object.

The distinctions among libido, prāṇa, Śakti and Spanda also remain substantive. The source assigns psychic valuation, living circulation, manifestation-power and pulsation different work. s2 needs the libidinal and symbolic change of investment, not a catalogue of energetic nouns or the entirety of s4's assigned development.

**M23 consequence:** retain the delayed-reply event and the complex's positive competence. Show how an inherited readiness can construe an uncertain delay, how action based on that construal can help produce apparent confirmation, and what changes when the readiness no longer has sole authority. Distinguish a new answer within the same regime from a change in the possibilities the regime makes available.
