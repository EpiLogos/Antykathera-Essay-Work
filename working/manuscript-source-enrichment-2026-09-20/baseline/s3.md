# §3 — Mathematical Substrate

## A distinction learns to return

The two logics leave us with a positive task. A difference can be made exact, and its terms can be held together without losing that exactness. What must a relation contain if it is to do both? The stroke in `0/1` has so far kept the question open: it distinguishes the condition of appearing from what appears, while preventing either term from becoming intelligible in isolation. We can now follow the operations through which that stroke acquires a mathematical body. Its first achievement is small enough to draw on a page.

Place two dashes beside one another and distinguish their places: `−/−`. Nothing requires us yet to read either dash as a negative number. The marks are the same kind of mark, but one stands here and the other there. Their sameness makes their difference recognisable; their difference gives us something to relate. The slash names this activity before its terms receive more determinate meanings. In the native expression `/ = −/−`, the right-hand side displays what the left-hand side does. This is the parent of the traversal, because every subsequent distinction requires an interval across which its terms can be read together.[^1] ^roz-s03-m25-claim01

The first determination, `0/1`, gives that interval its fundamental offices. Zero names the singular One, the unobjectifiable condition of appearing; one names the polyvalent All, the articulated field in which particular things appear. These are two senses of unity. The One has the office of condition rather than member; the All is the manifest unity within which its members can be distinguished. A particular appearance already arrives through a world of differences. To see a written mark is to distinguish figure and ground, shape and surrounding space, this occurrence and the possibility of recognising another. The appearing is determinate because these relations hold; its condition is operative throughout their disclosure.

Once a determination can be recognised, it can be questioned. `?/!` follows `0/1` because an assertion gives something whose warrant can be asked for, while a question has already determined what it asks. A reader may recognise a shape and still ask what makes it a letter, whether it has been copied correctly, or what the writer intended. The answer can change the reading. Question and assertion thus participate in one act of determination: the assertion brings the act to a local decision; the question keeps its conditions available. An answer that cannot be questioned loses the relation through which it could become better warranted.

That decision has direction and consequence. `−/+` makes explicit the withdrawal and extension, refusal and affirmation, through which a distinction acts. A line is accepted as belonging to this figure and excluded from another; a proposition is affirmed against an alternative; an instruction makes one course of action available and closes another. Here the difference acquires power. Its poles require a shared field in which their opposition matters. Writing them as signed numbers would introduce additional arithmetic rules; the native determination first states the relation that makes directed opposition intelligible.

For a determination to recur, its present instance must be distinguishable from the capacity it instantiates. This is `X/x`. The particular letter is recognisable because its identity can survive another size, another hand, another occasion. Yet no single inscription contains every possible instance or exhausts the act of recognition. `X` names that determining capacity; `x` names the indefinite particular which can receive a definite specification. The relationship is already operative whenever we identify something through changing appearances. Its later psychological reading will acquire its own objects and evidence; the relation has been established here before those objects are introduced.

Recognition also occurs somewhere, for someone, in relation to others. `AM/IS` brings this personed context into the account. What can be said of an experience and the presence for which it is experienced belong together without becoming interchangeable descriptions. The second person makes the difference consequential: a You can answer in terms that the speaker did not supply. First-person presence, second-person address and third-person description form a single encounter, but each has work the others cannot perform on its behalf. Context is the holding of that encounter, including the possibility that an answer will alter what its participants took themselves to be doing.

The sixth determination, `∞/dx`, states the relation between an exact local articulation and its inexhaustible horizon. Calculus gives one precise operation through which to understand this without making infinity a synonym for vagueness. On an interval containing zero, differentiation sends every function in the family

$$
F(x)=x^2+C
$$

to the same derivative, `2x`. Recovering an antiderivative therefore gives a family until another condition is supplied. If `F(0)=3`, the member is `x²+3`. The derivative was exact throughout, while the additive constant was lost under differentiation. Because a determinate operation can preserve one relation while losing another, an exact result need not contain every condition of its production. The native `∞/dx` extends this distinction into the horizon of determination. The mathematical example recovers one finite constant when an initial condition is given; the horizon-reading concerns the wider dependence of a determination on conditions beyond its selected local measure.[^2] ^roz-s03-m25-claim02

The return, `1/0`, gathers what the traversal has made explicit. A determinate appearance turns towards the condition through which it appears. The reader can retain the letter while recognising the activity of reading, and retain a result while recovering its assumptions. What returns is the achieved determination with a changed relation to its source. Ordinary arithmetic gives `0/1` the value zero and leaves `1/0` undefined; QL preserves that asymmetry while reading the two expressions as orientations of one relation. The unobjectifiable condition cannot be acquired by treating it as another denominator with an ordinary inverse.

The eight determinations can now be read in one breath:

$$
-/-\;\longrightarrow\;0/1\;\longrightarrow\;?/!\;\longrightarrow\;-/+
\;\longrightarrow\;X/x\;\longrightarrow\;AM/IS\;\longrightarrow\;\infty/dx
\;\longrightarrow\;1/0.
$$

The parent, the six determinations and the return have different offices. Their order follows the dependencies of an act becoming answerable to itself: appearing, questioning, directed consequence, recurrence, personed context, local articulation and return. Each turn makes explicit something the preceding turn requires but leaves implicit. Their unity lies in that developing dependence. The completed reading can therefore return to the first stroke and find more in it than two empty marks. ^roz-s03-m25-claim03

## The sixfold reads itself

There is an elementary combinatorial construction beneath the sixfold. Begin with two distinguishable terms, `B={0,1}`. Pair each with each. This gives the four ordered pairs `(0,0)`, `(0,1)`, `(1,0)` and `(1,1)`. Retain the original singles as well as their pairs. The resulting collection is the disjoint union

$$
B\sqcup(B\times B),\qquad |B\sqcup(B\times B)|=2+2^2=6.
$$

The disjoint-union sign matters: a single term and an ordered pair are different kinds of object. Four records of explicit pairing stand with the two terms whose possibilities they articulate. This is the exact counting operation expressed by `4+2`. The four pairs belong to `B×B`; a binary relation on `B` is a subset of that set, so counting all such relations would give `2⁴=16` instead.[^3]

The complete inverse parents preserve more than a count. Let

$$
P=\frac{0/1}{1/0},\qquad P'=\frac{1/0}{0/1}.
$$

The outer slash relates two complete orientations. To expose its internal pairings, read each term of the first orientation against each term of the second, keeping their order. For `P` the slash-forms are `0/1, 0/0, 1/1, 1/0`; the retained singles are `0,1`. For `P′` they are `1/0, 1/1, 0/0, 0/1`, with singles `1,0`. These slash-forms record relations rather than evaluations of undefined fractions. Both parents carry the complete four-plus-two body, but its order reverses. The two retained terms remain whole-bearing: each has the parent articulation available within it. A return can therefore unfold the whole from the other side. ^roz-s03-m26-claim01

Two further organisations become available within the six positions, numbered `0` to `5`. Adjacent pairs, `(0,1)`, `(2,3)` and `(4,5)`, articulate the three harmonics of Being, Becoming and Knowing or unKnowing. Complementary folds, `(0,5)`, `(1,4)` and `(2,3)`, articulate Essence and Quintessence, Constitution and Expression, Text and Texture. The two organisations share their middle pair and differ at their ends. An adjacent harmonic follows a developing relation; a complementary fold reads a position through what answers it across the whole. Their difference is useful precisely because one arrangement cannot substitute for the other.

The same requirement becomes personed in the horizontal `3:3`. The first, second and third persons articulate the encounter successively: I, You, what is encountered. Context, recognition and ground hold those standpoints as one act. Their three operations release the isolation of the standpoints, recognise their belonging together, and return that recognised unity as a condition of further encounter. In this respect `3:3` is also `3:1`: three articulated perspectives and one recognition performed through three distinguishable operations. The colon records an organisation of the act, not an arithmetic assertion that three equals one. This subject-level relation gives the later emanation and reversion their sense.[^4]

The First Spanda equation follows those two movements. Its emanative track, `T₀`, reads ground towards expression, from `0/(0/1)` towards `0/1`. Its reversionary track, `T₁`, reads the expression back towards its ground, from `(1/0)/0` towards `1/0`. The indeterminate `0/0` opens into the complete parent; the tracks articulate its two orientations; their recombination recognises one standing relation. Written together, the native chain is

$$
0\;=_{\mathrm{QL}}\;(0/0)
\;\longrightarrow\;\frac{0/1}{1/0}
\;\longrightarrow\;[T_0]
\;\longrightarrow\;[T_1]
\;\longrightarrow\;(1/0\oplus_{\mathrm{QL}}0/1)
\;\longrightarrow\;1/1=1=100\%.
$$

The subscript makes the office of the recombination explicit. The native source writes a plus sign; here it gathers the two readings of the whole rather than adding real-number fractions. The terminal unity is recognition of their standing identity, not an arithmetic value obtained by passing through division by zero. The first three operative stations and the returning three express the `3:3`; the act recognised across them supplies its `3:1`. Spanda names the pulse of manifestation and recognition whose source is not displaced by its articulation.

The Second Spanda takes this achieved whole as something that can account for its internal possibilities. It combines two constructions. A binary assignment at each of six labelled positions has `2⁶=64` possible configurations. A sixfold differentiated through six local positions gives `6²=36` second-order addresses. The first runs the binary through the sixfold; the second runs the sixfold through itself. Keeping the two collections distinct, their joint count is

$$
2^6+6^2=64+36=100.
$$

On this count the components occupy 64 and 36 percentage points of the total. The source's compressed `100%` names the achieved whole; in ordinary numerical notation `100%=1`, whereas the count is `100`. Stating the units prevents the whole's unity and the number of counted possibilities from being silently exchanged.

We now ask a different question: in what proportion do those two components stand? This changes addition into comparison. It gives

$$
\frac{64}{36}
=\frac{2^6}{2^2\,3^2}
=\frac{2^4}{3^2}
=\frac{16}{9}
=\left(\frac43\right)^2.
$$

The common factor four has been removed from numerator and denominator; dividing the sum by four would instead give twenty-five. In the remaining factorisation there are four instances of the binary prime and two of the ternary prime. The native exponent-reading recovers the `4+2` architecture within the whole's proportional account. Its warrant lies in the specified constructions and the change of operation: the counted collections, their proportion and the exponent-reading each remain available for inspection. ^roz-s03-m26-claim02

Holding the vertical `(4,2)` against the horizontal `(3,3)` gives a componentwise comparison:

$$
H_{\mathrm{QL}}\big((4,2),(3,3)\big)
=\left(\frac43,\frac23\right).
$$

The function name spells out what the compressed native display does. Ordinary division of the numerical ratios `4/2` and `3/3` would give two, not this ordered pair. Here the comparison preserves both components. Inversion supplies `3/4` and `3/2`; multiplication and comparison then yield

$$
\frac43\frac32=2,
\qquad
\frac{3/2}{4/3}=\frac98,
\qquad
\frac{2}{16/9}=\frac98.
$$

The same factor relates the fifth-sized span to the fourth-sized span and the doubled-fourth span to the octave. The ratios have been generated before their musical names do explanatory work. Their audition will matter because frequency intervals compose by multiplication: it is the stated operation that lets the derivation pass into sound.[^5]

A sixfold can grow in more than one way. Suppose two genuinely conjugate forms have determinations `Xᵢ` and `X′ᵢ`, one at each of six corresponding positions. Reading corresponding terms through one another can generate six relations, `Rᵢ=Xᵢ-through-X′ᵢ`. In the developed arbitration field, Criterion and Distinction generate Criterion-through-Distinction: a measure becomes determinate by distinguishing, while the distinctions it encounters can require that measure to change. The resulting operation gives the criterion a means of returning through what it distinguishes. Its place in the complete field is retained alongside Continuity-in-Indeterminacy, Delineation-through-Difference, Arbitration-in-Crisis, Con-text-through-Diaphaneity and Resolution-in-Reconciliation. Its compressed `6/6′ → 6+6′` therefore names six generated relations between two complete faces, not an arithmetic addition yielding twelve.[^6]

Distributive recursion asks another question. What does the complete sixfold become within each of its own positions? At the contextual position, for example, a local inquiry can articulate its ground, definition, means, pattern, context and return. Its addresses are `(4,0)` through `(4,5)`, retaining the parent coordinate. If all six parent positions earn this complete local development, there are thirty-six situated determinations. This is `6 → 6×6`. The ordered addresses alone could also be used for pairwise comparisons; distributive recursion gives each row the additional burden of being a complete situated sixfold. Conjugate generation relates two achieved faces, while recursion unfolds a whole within each position. The horizontal and vertical readings orient these different acts. An achieved whole can then enter a further relation as a new participant, carrying its internal differences with it. Recursion has substance because the return retains what was learned.

The native Name and Power articulations keep this substance visible. Truth/Play, Mind/Need, Word/Sacrifice, Logos/Decision, Son/Love and Image/Work give intelligible and effective faces to the six positions. A word can articulate something while its utterance also costs, changes or commits something; an image presents an achieved form while work gives that achievement a public consequence. Both faces remain active at each position. Neither a numerical slot nor a name alone exhausts the relation. The arithmetic supplies a differentiated body in which these determinations can be developed and returned; their semantic content must be carried by the acts and histories that make them real.

## The mark acquires a phase

A retained relation must be able to operate. The calculus of distinctions gives a particularly economical neighbouring construction. In a neutral notation, let `M` indicate a mark and `U` an unmarked state. Spencer-Brown's two elementary operations can be displayed schematically as

$$
MM\longrightarrow M,
\qquad
M(M)\longrightarrow U.
$$

Repeating an indication beside itself retains its value; enclosing a mark within a mark returns to the unmarked state. Juxtaposition and enclosure have different effects. Because the rules specify arrangements of indications, a reader can repeat the operations and determine which rule applies.[^7]

Re-entry asks what happens when the indicating operation enters the field it indicates. One elementary test makes the problem explicit. In the two Boolean values, the instantaneous demand `r=1−r` has no solution: neither zero nor one equals its opposite. Declare instead a delayed operation,

$$
r_{n+1}=1-r_n,\qquad r_n\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Starting at zero produces `0,1,0,1,…`; starting at one produces the shifted sequence. The delay has introduced a temporal organisation which the instantaneous equation did not possess. It is a genuine formal change. Varela's autonomous third state develops another treatment of self-indication by extending the value-system itself. His temporal interpretation leaves frequency characterisation for further work. To develop the native retention claim, the next operation must specify what recurs, how its interval is measured, and what prior state can change the next crossing. A delayed switch establishes periodicity; a memory-bearing return requires this further account.

Kauffman's iterants show how alternation and a shift can yield the square-root-of-minus-one relation. Take two real matrices,

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

`D` distinguishes opposite signs; `η` exchanges the two coordinates. Direct multiplication gives `D²=η²=I` and `ηD=−Dη`. Now compose them in the order `J=ηD`. Then

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
J^2=\eta D\eta D=-I.
$$

A pair `(x,y)` becomes `(-y,x)`: a quarter-turn. With this orientation the operation represents multiplication by `i`; reversing the order gives its negative. The imaginary relation has appeared through two declared real operations, differentiation of signs and exchange of positions. Kauffman's construction is a formal neighbour of the native self-inverting relation because its phase has an operation behind it. His non-uniqueness qualification remains important: the same matrix algebra can admit different iterant representations.[^8] ^roz-s03-m27-claim01

Complex multiplication now gives a visible traversal:

$$
1\;\xrightarrow{\times i}\;i
\;\xrightarrow{\times i}\;-1
\;\xrightarrow{\times i}\;-i
\;\xrightarrow{\times i}\;1.
$$

For a nonzero `z=re^{iθ}`, changing the phase leaves the positive modulus `r` fixed. What persists and what changes are separately expressible. This is why rotation can carry the relation between identity and difference without reducing difference to error. The same modulus can belong to indefinitely many phases, and the same point can be reached after different numbers of turns.

In the dialogue with Krishnamurti, Bohm recalls a spinning wheel and a thread kept straight through its motion. They use the image to discuss a consciousness that remains faithful to what is, including its capacity to acknowledge and correct an error. The comparison with complex rotation preserves a specific action: fidelity can be maintained through changing positions. The fixed modulus supplies the mathematical invariant in this comparison; the dialogue's truth concerns how consciousness relates to actuality. Their relation becomes intelligible through the shared operation, while the modulus remains a magnitude rather than a measure of truth.[^9]

Yet the final complex value does not itself record those turns. The value one is the same at phase zero and phase `2π`; to retain winding we must keep an unwrapped phase, a lifted path, or another state record. A symbol of recurrence therefore becomes an account of memory only when the carrying operation is specified. The mathematical distinction strengthens the philosophical one: a return is enriched when the relation that traversed the difference remains available to the next act.

Even within the complex plane, inversion has different operations. Conjugation sends `re^{iθ}` to `re^{-iθ}`, reflecting the phase and retaining the modulus. For a nonzero number, reciprocal inversion sends it to `r^{-1}e^{-iθ}`, reversing the phase while also reciprocating the modulus. Only on the unit circle do these two operations coincide. Their difference becomes decisive at zero: conjugation remains defined there, while a reciprocal requires a change of containing space. The next construction makes that change explicit.

## The horizon becomes a coordinate

The expression `1/0` can receive a precise geometrical office without acquiring an arithmetic value in a field. On the real projective line, a point is an equivalence class `[x:y]` of nonzero pairs, where `(x,y)` and `(λx,λy)` represent the same point for every nonzero real `λ`. In the chart `y≠0`, the coordinate is `x/y`. This chart contains the ordinary real line, with zero represented by `[0:1]`. The remaining projective point is `[1:0]`.

The construction retains the direction represented by a pair while disregarding its nonzero scale. At `[1:0]` the first chart fails, but the second chart `x≠0` gives the coordinate `y/x=0`. Infinity in one chart is an ordinary coordinate value in another. The pair `[0:0]` remains excluded because it determines no projective direction. We have changed the represented object from a numerical quotient to an equivalence class; no step has made zero invertible. The projective line still has one intrinsic real dimension. A second homogeneous component is not by itself an additional independent dimension of the space. ^roz-s03-m28-claim01

A similar construction makes parallel lines meet in the real projective plane. The affine lines `y=0` and `y=1` become `Y=0` and `Y−Z=0` in homogeneous coordinates. Their common point is `[1:0:0]`, where `Z=0` marks the ideal line. The meeting is exact in the projective plane; in the affine chart the lines remain parallel. The new account preserves their earlier relationship by explaining what the restricted chart leaves outside its domain.

For complex coordinates the corresponding completion is the complex projective line, represented as the Riemann sphere: the complex plane together with one point at infinity. It has one complex dimension and two real dimensions. A nondegenerate bilinear transformation,

$$
w(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad ad-bc\ne0,
$$

acts invertibly on that completed space. At a finite pole it takes the value infinity in the extended-space sense; another chart represents the image. The determinant condition matters because without it the transformation can collapse points rather than preserve their projective relations.[^10]

One relation it preserves can be checked with four points. Using the convention

$$
\operatorname{CR}(z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4)
=\frac{(z_3-z_1)(z_4-z_2)}{(z_3-z_2)(z_4-z_1)},
$$

four distinct finite points have a well-defined cross-ratio. For `(0,1;2,3)` it is `4/3`. Apply `w(z)=(2z+1)/(z+1)` and the images are `1,3/2,5/3,7/4`; their cross-ratio is again `4/3`. The reason is visible in the difference formula

$$
w(u)-w(v)=\frac{(ad-bc)(u-v)}{(cu+d)(cv+d)}.
$$

Substituting four such differences makes the extra factors cancel. Where a point is infinite, the extended definition uses the corresponding limit. NIST's stated invariance is under bilinear transformations, not under arbitrary changes of a picture. The proof names both the change and the relation that survives it. ^roz-s03-m28-claim02

The same complex projective line has an exact quantum use. A pure two-level state is a ray `[α:β]` in `ℂ²`; a normalised representative satisfies `|α|²+|β|²=1`, with an overall phase disregarded. After choosing the basis `|0⟩,|1⟩`, it can be written

$$
|\psi\rangle=\cos(\theta/2)|0\rangle
+e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)|1\rangle,
\qquad 0\le\theta\le\pi.
$$

The corresponding Bloch vector is `(sinθ cosφ, sinθ sinφ, cosθ)`, whose squared length is one. The basis states occupy opposite poles; the relative phase supplies longitude away from those poles. This identifies the pure-state ray space with a sphere. Mixed states require the interior of the Bloch ball. The shared object is the projective ray, and the quotient operation supplies the bridge. QL's ground and manifestation are not quantum basis states merely because both notations use zero and one.[^11]

An atlas develops the same demand across a space that needs several local descriptions. On the unit circle, omit the north pole for one chart and the south pole for another. Their coordinates can be written

$$
u=\frac{x}{1-y},\qquad v=\frac{x}{1+y},\qquad x^2+y^2=1.
$$

On the overlap both coordinates are nonzero, and `uv=1`; hence `v=1/u`. At the point `(3/5,4/5)`, one chart gives three and the other one-third. These unequal numbers describe the same point because their transformation is known. The missing pole of either chart is present in the other. Together they cover a circle that cannot be one global chart into an open subset of the real line: a compact circle cannot be homeomorphic to such a noncompact coordinate domain.[^12]

The transformation also governs motion. If `du/dt=2` when `u=3`, the chain rule gives `dv/dt=−2/9`. Assigning the same numerical velocity in both charts would describe a different motion. Height nevertheless changes by the same amount in either account. Its two formulas,

$$
y=\frac{u^2-1}{1+u^2}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
y=\frac{1-v^2}{1+v^2},
$$

give `dy/dt=6/25` under these transformed velocities. The different coordinate velocities therefore describe one change of height through a stated transition.

A smooth atlas requires these transitions and their inverses to be smooth on the overlaps. With three charts, going through an intermediate chart must agree with changing directly wherever both routes are defined. These conditions let a local calculation participate in one space. Dynamics requires a further choice: a rule of motion determines trajectories on the space whose descriptions the atlas makes compatible. The difference will matter when the formal field is encountered psychologically. A map of possible experience and the organisation drawing a life through those possibilities have distinguishable work.

Logic, too, can change the field in which a result is interpreted. First Degree Entailment uses four information-values, the subsets of `{T,F}`: `{T}`, `{F}`, `{T,F}` and the empty set. A value is designated when it contains `T`; negation exchanges `T` and `F`, leaving both and neither unchanged. Assign a proposition `p` the value `{T,F}` and an unrelated proposition `q` the empty value. Both `p` and its negation are designated, while `q` is not. This countervaluation blocks the inference from a contradiction to an arbitrary conclusion. Non-explosion follows from the declared semantics.

These four values arise from `2²` combinations of two truth-designations. QL's `2+2²` retains a different distinction between original terms and their pairings. Priest's reconstruction of the Buddhist catuṣkoṭi uses the four-valued semantics and then proposes a fifth status for ineffability, raising a question about the adequacy of the value-space itself. Kapsner contests the need for this fifth value and sketches an alternative without it. The disagreement bears directly on the operation: encountering an obstruction does not by itself establish that one more value is the right repair. QL's ground is a generative condition, not a fifth or sixth truth-value; treating it as such would change the native claim's type. The productive comparison therefore requires each extension to disclose what it changes, what it preserves, and why its particular repair is needed.[^13]

## A return can retain its distance

The torus gives local return and retained passage an exact common home. Begin with the plane and identify its integer translates:

$$
\mathbb T^2=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2,
\qquad
(x,y)\sim(x+m,y+n),\quad(m,n)\in\mathbb Z^2.
$$

The unit square's opposite edges are identified in pairs. The result is a compact surface without boundary. Its quotient map sends infinitely many plane points to each surface address. A small neighbourhood is accurately described by a local lift, while the full covering plane preserves the different representatives. The slash now means quotient by a stated equivalence relation. Its operation is neither the native AND/OR nor division of numbers.[^14]

Follow the path

$$
\gamma(t)=[2t,-t],\qquad 0\le t\le1.
$$

It begins and ends at `[0,0]` on the torus. Its lift begins at `(0,0)` and ends at `(2,−1)` in the plane. The surface return is exact, and so is the retained displacement. The two integers record the winding class: two turns in one generating direction and one reversed turn in the other. They do not record every bend, speed or event of the journey. The fundamental group expresses precisely these independent classes,

$$
\pi_1(\mathbb T^2)\cong\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z.
$$

This is the operation which gives return-with-difference its topological force. Local closure no longer requires global passage to vanish. ^roz-s03-m29-claim01

The counts must keep their objects. Four sides belong to the presenting square; two independent loop generators belong to the torus. That `4+2` is an articulated reading of the construction. For the Euler characteristic, however, the gluing leaves one vertex, two edge classes and one face, giving `1−2+1=0`. The zero-characteristic surface and its infinite covering plane furnish the native cross-reading `∞/0`; the actual mathematics is the quotient and covering relation we have just specified. Counting presenting sides is not the same operation as counting quotient cells.

Nor is every cover the same return. The plane is the torus's infinite universal cover. The torus can also cover a Klein bottle in two sheets, relating orientable and non-orientable surfaces. The second map has different fibres and a different target. Hatcher's lattice and glide-reflection example makes this distinction explicit. The image of inverse traversal can draw on both constructions only by retaining which map is being used.

The chosen motion determines whether a surface path closes. For constant linear flow `[αt,βt]`, a positive period `T` requires both `Tα` and `Tβ` to be integers. A nonzero velocity proportional to an integer pair meets that condition; an irrational ratio of its two nonzero components cannot. The resulting absence of a period follows from the stated dynamical rule, while the surface supplies the possible relations of passage. In the familiar embedded torus, the handle belongs to the form's organisation; filling its opening would change the topology. This gives the uroboric image an exact neighbouring thought: the circuit is constituted around what its own surface never occupies.

Music makes another kind of retained difference consequential. For positive frequencies, an interval is a ratio. Moving from `f` to `af` and then to `baf` composes intervals by multiplication. The ratio family derived from the Spanda readings can therefore be performed without changing the arithmetic that produced it. Beginning at `f₀`, the successive frequencies

$$
f_0\;\longrightarrow\;\frac43f_0
\;\longrightarrow\;\frac32f_0
\;\longrightarrow\;2f_0
$$

move through a fourth, a `9/8` whole tone, and another fourth. Their product is

$$
\frac43\cdot\frac98\cdot\frac43=2.
$$

Equivalently, the doubled fourth `16/9` reaches the octave through the factor `9/8`. The interval between its present span and its completion is part of how it completes. The factor is retained in the working: the two unequal spans are related through the interval that completes the passage.[^15] ^roz-s03-m29-claim02

Octave equivalence has a quotient construction of its own. Write pitch height as `p=log₂(f/f₀)`. Doubling frequency adds one. Identifying heights differing by an integer gives `ℝ/ℤ`, the pitch-class circle. Keeping the unreduced height retains register: a tone can return to its pitch class while sounding an octave higher. One independent phase gives a circle; a torus requires two. The spatial and musical constructions thus meet through a specified quotient operation while keeping their distinct dimensions and objects.

A chain of pure fifths exposes a different remainder. Twelve ratios `3/2` do not equal seven octave doublings. Their discrepancy is

$$
\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^7}
=\frac{531441}{524288},
$$

the Pythagorean comma. Six pure `9/8` whole tones likewise exceed an octave by this comma. Equal temperament changes the intervals so that the finite cycle closes: its whole tone is `2^{1/6}`, not exactly `9/8`. Scholtz's account distinguishes these tuning operations. The comma and the octave-completing whole tone cannot exchange names merely because both concern a remainder. One adjusts a chain of fifths; the other completes the already specified doubled-fourth span.
