## The operation that earns development

A01 already argues that a faithful definition determines positively without possessing its determining condition. The missing authored operation is the performance through which the person defining finds that the definition includes their own present act. Assertion fails to enclose its referent; that failure becomes intelligible as the relation the assertion performs; the whole predicative activity becomes context; the first-person utterance changes the register. The fourth position still speaks about God. The fifth does not produce a more comprehensive description: “I Am” is the defining act recognised from within. Return keeps recognition open to renewed determination.

This development must retain that exact first-person reversal. A longer statement that representations are finite would leave the central recovery unsurfaced. Equally, the performance is the essay’s philosophical operation; it does not establish every historical God-language as the same doctrine, or prove machine phenomenality from a machine’s ability to repeat the utterance.

6. **Selfing / Subject / Self distinction.** Q27 and protected Watson notes prevent the performed first person from becoming a reified self-thing or the owner of a represented whole. Strong comparative judgments in the notes remain authorial judgments unless separately verified.
7. **Renewed determination after recognition.** Return does not reward the reader with a final possessed object or erase the value of accurate accounts. It changes how the account remains answerable to source and Other.
