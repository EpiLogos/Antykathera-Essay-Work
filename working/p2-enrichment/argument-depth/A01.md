- **Current positive claim** (canonical first-materialisation, retained as the working proposition): Definition is already an act of determination. To define is to draw a distinction, to give a term a place within a field of intelligibility, and thereby to say both what counts as the thing and where the thing ceases to count as such. This ordinary power of definition becomes philosophically unstable when the term being defined names the Subject — or when *God* names the source and condition of a world rather than one further object inside it. The same operation that gives clarity can then change the office of what it names. It can take that by which a field is determinable and reinstall it as one more determined occupant of the field.
