## #1 — Authored ground becomes effective through a bounded commission

A durable source gives agency more than accumulated information. It can say why a project exists, what the author values, what would count as a faithful development and what must remain available for later judgment. Observation supplies another kind of material: what occurred. An inference proposes an interpretation of that occurrence. A generated summary selects from these materials. Keeping their provenance distinct permits a person to recognise useful evidence without finding their own word silently replaced by a machine's profile of them.

The O:I **Founding Positions**, read at the local committed revision `c1ce2db81eec3b03a9bdff0a1accf8ece4acf903` on2026-09-08, makes this a product commitment. Human authorship belongs at consequential points—purpose, authority, Commission, Recognition, revision, refusal and redirection—while agents can exercise substantial judgment between them. A directory and an acting model can already constitute a technological world; the six products develop its capacities through need. Existing heterogeneous worlds retain their own identities. O:I's architecture is therefore also a research arrangement in which those worlds can expose limitations in the architecture itself.

A commission selects from durable ground for an act. Availability does not mean selection; selection does not grant authority; retrieval does not prove permission. The commission must retain the relation among its source, the actor's permitted scope and the purpose of the selection. A delegated research task supplies a concrete case. The selected source can contradict the expected answer. Fidelity then requires the contradiction to reach the question or its evaluator. Suppressing it can produce a formally compliant report which defeats the purpose of inquiry.

E2's [Arbitration field](../etymologies/arbitration-hybris-regard-anamnesis/WHOLE-FIELD.md) **qualifies** this transition through its complete movement. **Arbitration-in-Crisis → Decision / Hybris** gives an office enough jurisdiction to settle a question and identifies its usurpation when that criterion becomes immune. **Con-text-through-Diaphaneity → Regard** makes the decision's conditions available to what answers it. **Resolution-in-Reconciliation → Anamnesis / Recognition / Return** carries the examined history into another act. The technical responsibility is to let returned difference change the criterion, scope or commission, including a reasoned retention when the evidence warrants it.

E3's [Trust / Place / Logos / Nomos / Natio / Credere field](../etymologies/trust-place-logos-nomos-natio-credere/WHOLE-FIELD.md) **historicises** the distinct lexical and institutional backgrounds, then **compares** the operative circuit at register3. A new participant enters a world already shaped by earlier records, translations and permissions. That inheritance must remain attributable. These six operations are not six product names or one historical descent.
