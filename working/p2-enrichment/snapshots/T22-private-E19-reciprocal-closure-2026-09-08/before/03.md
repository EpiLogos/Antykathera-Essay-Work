# Language, Symbol and Dialogue — The Account Answers Back

## #0 — Exclusion gives a word its reach

<a id="language-exclusion"></a>

A word can reach several things without naming a substance which all of them possess. The problem is how it reaches these things and excludes others. Dignāga's semantic operation enters through [Pind's translation of PSV V:11d](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/pind/pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation/SOURCE.md#pind-2009-dignaga-anyapoha-dissertation-q001), which **sources** the exclusion of other referents. The negative operation gives the word a determinate application. It does not leave only an absence where reference ought to occur.

Pind's second housed passage, annotation 181 on printed p. 183, distinguishes a thing qualified by preclusion from mere preclusion. The hare's-horns example tests what an unreal object can contribute to the account. His wording is verified; the attribution of the underlying prose fragment remains provisional. These are two different source positions: the translated verse-commentary formulation and a scholarly reconstruction of a fragment. Their combination supplies a positive referential problem without making the fragment an unqualified statement by Dignāga.

The later [*Apoha* collection's housed secondary discussion](../../../sources/indian-philosophy/siderits/siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha/SOURCE.md#siderits-tillemans-chakrabarti-2011-apoha-q003) **qualifies** this movement through nominally and verbally bound negation. A term formed by exclusion and a denied predication do different work. Thus the not-non-pot formulation does not become disposable double-negation decoration. The semantic construction has to explain why the positive term applies. Review and reference scholarship transmit the housed discussion; the 2011 volume's own chapter pages remain a separate reading and collation obligation.

[Spinoza's letter to Jelles of 2 June 1674](../../../sources/classical-premodern-philosophy/spinoza/spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles/SOURCE.md#spinoza-1674-letter-50-jelles-q001) **compares** a later, independently situated operation. Figure determines a finite body through its boundary; it does not state the body's being as unlimited matter. Spatial limitation and semantic exclusion each articulate a positive determination through what it is not. The comparison follows their different objects. No passage from Buddhist semantics into Spinoza's letter is established by that relation.

This companion **extends** [the protected history](HISTORY.md) through six positions earned by changes in what language does: reference, use, address, shared inquiry, numerical selection and return. [A08's full exclusion argument](../../../arguments/A08-Apoha-Constitutive-Exclusion.md) **grounds** the author's further claim that the achieved determination retains its constitutive field. Q27's direct comparison makes the positive pressure explicit: recognising exclusion, succession or a retained alternative requires an act in which their relation is available. That is the project's Argued comparison with recognition, not a position borrowed from the dissertation. It preserves the Buddhist critique of a self-thing while pursuing the non-objectifiable continuity of the knower. [C18](../../../concepts/C18-Apoha.md) **defines** the semantic office within this wider movement; [C03](../../../concepts/C03-Determination.md) **extends** it into identification, selection and recurrence.

## #1 — A sentence acquires a practice

<a id="language-practice"></a>

Wittgenstein's 1922 *Tractatus* gives the boundary a different location. Its [4.1212 passage](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1922-tractatus/SOURCE.md#wittgenstein-1922-tractatus-q001) **sources** the distinction between showing and saying; proposition 7 closes the book at a limit of speech. These statements concern the articulation of the work's logical project. The source does not supply Taylor's affirmative return through the subject-condition, nor does its concluding silence supply a general theorem that every formal inquiry has the same limit.

The later *Philosophical Investigations*, published in 1953, changes the examination. In §43 the identification of meaning with use is expressly scoped to a large class of cases. §§197–202 move from an intention supposedly containing its future applications to training, custom and practice: interpretation cannot keep supplying the missing practical connection through another interpretation. §§241–242 distinguish shared linguistic life from agreement in opinions and relate communication to sufficiently stable judgments, including practices of measurement. These [selected passages in the 2009 revised translation](https://danielwharris.com/teaching/38080/readings/WittgensteinPI.pdf) **source** the local development. The edition is Anscombe's translation revised by Hacker and Schulte, not an inspected copy of the first English edition. The [canonical work house](../../../sources/analytic-philosophy/wittgenstein/wittgenstein-1953-philosophical-investigations/SOURCE.md) **sources** its identity; exact admission proposals retain the edition distinction.

[A02's copula argument](../../../arguments/A02-Copula-Self-Identity-through-Difference.md) **grounds** the authorial return. A name alone does not individuate its instances: the circumstance lets this bearer be addressed here. Predication and identification operate together without becoming interchangeable. The word reaches someone or something because a differentiated encounter already permits reference. Count's priority in this argument is constitutional, not a historical assertion that human enumeration preceded speech. The practical comparison makes circumstance harder to omit; it does not make Wittgenstein the historical author of that derivation.

[E1's Name-through-Count](../../../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#name-through-count) **grounds** the operational relation. A usable account must retain what made this name count as this instance. Its historical branches distinguish Latin word histories, German region-language and the Chinese facing-and-response field. Their geography and chronology remain articulated in their own witnesses. Taylor's encounter-before-terms proposition is a separate authorial operation at evidence register 3; a multilingual resemblance cannot establish either its derivation or a common lexical ancestry.

## #2 — Address makes the signifier consequential

<a id="language-address"></a>

Lacan's 1971–72 talks give the problem a room, a speaking position and an audience. [The chapel passage](../../../sources/psychology/lacan/lacan-2017-talking-to-brick-walls/SOURCE.md#lacan-2017-talking-to-brick-walls-q001) **sources** the move from the amphitheatre to the chapel on 6 January 1972. The audience's situation belongs to the address. A lecture delivered to these people in this setting is already more than a string of interchangeable sentences.

The housed wall and echo passages develop that scene through differently supported carriers. Adrian Price's 2017 translation, the French *Je parle aux murs* pagination and the 2021 scholarly reconstruction must be kept apart. The source house marks their matches and uncertainties rather than silently treating a French page as an English locator. [Adleman and Vanderwees](../../../sources/psychology/adleman/adleman-vanderwees-2021-lacan-rhetoric/SOURCE.md#adleman-vanderwees-2021-lacan-rhetoric-q001) **qualify** the scene through their later interpretation of analytic silence and echo; their p. 225 account of the symbol's dividing and connecting belongs to that interpretation. It cannot stand as a quotation from the 1972 talk.

[Darmon's 1992 account of the matheme](../../../sources/psychology/darmon/darmon-1992-matheme-ali/SOURCE.md#darmon-1992-matheme-ali-q001) **defines** another historical office: a formal writing intended to transmit structure, rather than a convenient abbreviation of a verbal lesson. Its adapted Saussurean signifier/signified notation has a specific genealogy. Lacan's locally housed question about whether the matheme will save discourse opens an inquiry; a question is not yet its affirmative resolution. Formal transmission and an address whose effect passes through its hearers remain related demands.

[A13's two logics](../../../arguments/A13-Two-Logics-of-Two-Dia-Syn.md) **grounds** the project's comparison. An effective cut can either retain the relation through which its terms became available or account them as cancelling opposites. The ethical and symbolic difference concerns the accounting of the cut. It is not a historical equation of Lacan's bar, Dignāga's exclusion and Taylor's slash. Nor does a source-specific account of lack exhaust the project's positive recognitive return. Each operation enters the comparison with its own object and force.

The Greek symbolon's broken token enters this history through [E4's developed historical branches](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/HISTORICAL-BRANCHES.md), which **historicise** recognition across a seam. Their lexical witnesses and ancient passage tasks remain distinct. The essay's symbolic operation retains the absent counterpart in the present piece; its validity as an authored operation does not require pretending that a lexicon entry is a newly read ancient scene. Likewise, the protected history's dia-ballein and Homeric comparison remains an authorial application with its own philological obligations.

## #3 — Participants become answerable to a common inquiry

<a id="language-dialogue"></a>

The 18 May 1975 Brockwood Park dialogue lets the account change while it is being spoken. Bohm and Krishnamurti distinguish conditioned reality from truth, retaining thought's real effects even when it distorts. Bohm's straight-thread image concerns consciousness running true, not merely possessing correct information. The conversation then tests whether a person is truth or is of truth, and returns to the possibility of mistake and correction. The [Foundation transcript's selected exchanges](https://kfoundation.org/transcript/dialogue-1-brockwood-park-18-may-1975/) **source** this movement; [the canonical dialogue house](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-krishnamurti-1975-05-18-dialogue/SOURCE.md) retains its identity. The speakers' speculative etymologies remain utterances in the dialogue, not verified lexical ancestry. Their revisions matter: a common question is maintained through distinctions which neither a finished slogan nor a claim of infallibility can replace.

[Bohm's 1980 *Wholeness and the Implicate Order*](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1980-wholeness-implicate-order/SOURCE.md) **compares** a separate book-length inquiry into fragmentation, language and enfolded process. Its physics and rheomode require their own passage-level examination. The 1975 discussion does not discharge those tasks. The distinction keeps a conversation about thought's conditioning from becoming experimental confirmation of an ontological whole.

Rotman's 1987 *Signifying Nothing* shifts the pressure to the production of a semiotic subject. [The canonical work house](../../../sources/mathematics-logic/rotman/rotman-1987-signifying-nothing/SOURCE.md) **sources** the book's assigned zero, vanishing-point and money problem; [reference note 66](../../../concepts/reference-notes/rotman-semiotics-of-zero.md) **qualifies** the encounter through the author's explicit divergence. A sign-system can organise the position from which its signs become readable. Taylor's subject-condition is not thereby explained away as a product of that organisation. Conversely, an irreducible pole does not erase the historical labour by which a numeral or perspective acquires its public office. The book's chapter 2, pp. 27–56, remains a precise collation task. The held relation is already substantive: constituting a readable subject-position and accounting for the condition of that constitution ask different questions.

The canonical [1996 *On Dialogue* house](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/bohm/bohm-1996-on-dialogue/SOURCE.md) **sources** the later edited work's identity. A separately recovered local teaching reproduction, credited to Bohm and bearing Schouten & Nelissen page marks, develops suspension on its pp. 2–8. It asks participants to notice assumptions and the emotional defence of them without immediately enacting or suppressing them. The group can hold meanings together while members retain different opinions; a facilitator's function can recede as participants take responsibility for the process. Frustration and dominance belong to the difficulty of that practice. This is a reading of that local carrier, whose relation to Lee Nichol's 1996 edition still needs collation. Its pagination is not being installed as book pagination.

[Van Eenwyk's source house and protected authorial encounter](../../../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md) **source** a further, psychological relation. Taylor's notes follow a symbol which changes an interpreter's available trajectories. The [direct *Symbolon Dynamics* synthesis](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-symbolon-dynamics/SOURCE.md) **grounds** the authored development: an image's response changes its future efficacy, so the product re-enters the field of interpretation. Attractor and trajectory describe a dynamical organisation; chart and transition describe partial disclosures. [A22](../../../arguments/A22-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) and [C37](../../../concepts/C37-World-Picture-to-World-Atlas.md) **qualify** their conjunction. Neither geometry supplies the other's warrant, and copied book passages in NOTES remain leads for independent verification.

[SEED's 2010 programme house](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/seed/seed-2010-language-spirit/SOURCE.md) **historicises** the institutional continuation, recording the 1992 Little Bear–Peat approach to Bohm and the later Language of Spirit dialogues. The programme's stated form is an institutional witness. It does not license a single Indigenous doctrine, a generic talking-circle origin for dialogue, or a proven transmission into the proposed harness. The newly housed brochure distinguishes the 1992 approach, SEED’s 1996 foundation and its sponsorship from 1999 (PDF pp. 2 and 4). Parry’s PDF p. 5 abstract describes the hybrid form; [Pat McCabe’s own abstract on PDF p. 6](../../../sources/process-systems-theory/seed/seed-2010-language-spirit/SOURCE.md#seed-2010-language-spirit-q003) **qualifies** the gathering through culturally situated tools of perception and a concern with right relations. Her voice is a named participant’s contribution. An advertised inquiry remains distinct from an observed outcome or scientific validation of the programme’s themes. The relation which survives is practical and exact: a gathering must preserve the authority and difference of its participants if shared meaning is to return to them.

## #4 — Weights and selections acquire different offices

<a id="language-selection"></a>

Vaswani and colleagues' 2017 Transformer paper specifies attention through queries, keys and values. Scaled query–key dot products enter softmax; the resulting weights combine values. [The canonical §3.2.1 passage](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/vaswani/vaswani-et-al-2017-attention/SOURCE.md#vaswani-et-al-2017-attention-q002) **sources** that operation. Its architecture and two translation tasks have their own scope. The paper does not claim to have explained Buddhist reference, reproduced psychoanalytic address or established artificial phenomenality.

[PyTorch's versioned 2.9 documentation](../../../sources/computer-science-ml/pytorch/pytorch-2-9-softmax-argmax-api/SOURCE.md#pytorch-2-9-softmax-argmax-api-q001) **defines** normalisation along a stated dimension. The house's separate argmax cards define an index selection and the first-index convention for a tie. A vector of weights and one index retain different information. Attention's weighted combination of values is also different from selecting a final output token. Conflating these stages would erase precisely the field and operation the comparison needs to retain.

The technical return to apoha is therefore an authored comparison between specified acts. A selected output is constituted through an alternatives-field and a selection rule; keeping that context can make a later correction possible. Dignāga's semantic account explains reference within its own epistemology. A distribution over candidates is neither a theory of that reference nor a record of every source, permission and social condition which made a model output possible. [A35's native-to-feature discipline](../../../arguments/A35-Compassion-Sensitivity-to-Origins-Epi-Logos-as-Vocation.md) **tests** the passage: name the native operation, identify its proposed implementation and observe what changes. Neither a failed implementation nor a missing citation alters the standing of the native derivation.

The distinction gives dialogue a consequential technical pressure. An agent can produce an eloquent account of its openness while retaining the same inaccessible source, irreversible decision or unchangeable permission. Retaining a log is one capability; allowing an encountered contradiction to alter the next action is another. The history has reached a new medium whose operations are inspectable under specified conditions, while its normative and experiential questions retain their independent registers.

## #5→0 — The account returns through its conditions

<a id="language-account-return"></a>

[E4's Account does not replace source](../../../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md#account-does-not-replace-source) **grounds** the whole's return. Its three terms—Symbol, Account and Trust—perform four developed operations: Symbol answers to source; Account does not replace source; Trust keeps return possible; Account re-enters the field. The six positions of this history are its own route through changing linguistic practices. They do not add six primitive terms to E4 or turn those practices into one theory of language.

Taylor's [encounter synthesis](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-encounter-region-name-count/SOURCE.md) **grounds** the related E1 operation: an account is an address within the field it tells. The [native theorem spine](../../../sources/internal-corpus/taylor/taylor-2026-core-theorems-pithy/SOURCE.md) retains the full traversal: parent `/ = −/−`, conscious circumstance `0/1`, question/assertion `?/!`, force `−/+`, recurrence `X/x`, personed context `AM/IS`, differential horizon `∞/dx`, and return `1/0`, with their inversions. `X/x` belongs to Taylor; its psychic refraction does not change its authorship. Synchronic and diachronic views cross the two modes of accounting. A co-present gathering can conceal its ground; a chronological telling can preserve it. Time and relation are different axes.

A real local Bimba can serve as the reference for a bounded inquiry while remaining a constructed, source-dependent determination in a wider field. That recursive office lets different situated pratibimbas be compared without treating one map as the world. In Movement 39's Offered J-Space, a change of source, representation, gauge or permission must remain distinguishable. The proposed representation becomes useful when an observed transition can be traced through those conditions. The person's or agent's world is not exhausted by the represented field.

[E1's Count-to-Account](../../../etymologies/encounter-region-name-count/WHOLE-FIELD.md#count-to-account) **grounds** the next act: return the achieved telling with the criterion which made it count, the context which gave it reach and the path by which its addressee can answer. Bohm's group, Wittgenstein's practice, Lacan's address and computational selection remain distinct bodies of that inquiry. A successful return changes a relation which the account depends upon. Trust names the living possibility of receiving that answer; an inventory of historical parallels cannot manufacture it.
