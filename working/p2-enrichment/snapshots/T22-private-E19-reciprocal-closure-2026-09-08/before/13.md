# Process — What an Achieved Form Makes Possible Next

## #0 — An occurrence becomes an inheritance

A process account must show how a determination takes form and what its occurrence changes. Its product is available for another act: a memory bears upon an encounter, a rule constrains a decision, a response changes a relationship, and a tool alters the possible course of work. [A14, Computational Process Ontology](../arguments/A14-Computational-Process-Ontology.md) **grounds** this native claim through the distinction between operative grammar, epistemic inspection and ontology. The claim does not depend on identifying reality with a digital machine.

[E4, Symbol / Account / Trust](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md) **grounds** the dossier's exact return obligation: Account re-enters source-field. A completed account enters a field in which it can change the conditions of later accounting. The earlier operations remain active—Symbol answers to source; Account does not replace source; Trust keeps the return route active. Their conjunction requires an achieved form to carry both its determining relations and its consequences.

The comparisons below distinguish qualitative succession, concrescence, biological production, formal self-reference and dynamical modelling. A claim can be developed in its own register and then make another register more exact. Their positive relation concerns formation and continuation. No common word for process substitutes for the transformation each account actually specifies.

## #1 — The next note changes the whole heard

[Bergson's selected Pogson carrier](../sources/process-systems-theory/bergson/bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson/SOURCE.md#bergson-1913-time-free-will-pogson-q001) **sources** duration through conscious states that permeate one another. Melody makes the difference concrete: a subsequent note changes the heard phrase, whereas a row of positions displays separable terms. His clock analysis distinguishes counted simultaneities from the synthesis through which a succession is retained. This is a claim about lived qualitative development, rather than an alternative unit for an elapsed-time counter.

The distinction asks something exact of a record. A trace can preserve the order of events while omitting how the later event changes the significance of the earlier one. A correction to a source attribution changes what a previously persuasive argument now means; it does more than add one later item to an otherwise unchanged list. [A26, Objective Internality](../arguments/A26-Objective-Internality-Mind-as-Worldhood.md) **extends** that consequence into the situated conditions of interpretation. [C07, Conditions of Worldhood](../concepts/C07-Conditions-of-Worldhood.md) **defines** the field within which such a change can matter.

The technical comparison has its own object. A changed memory or context can alter later retrieval, evaluation or permitted action, and those changes can be inspected. Their occurrence does not establish the phenomenal duration Bergson describes. The comparison therefore preserves the reality of operational change and the separate question of how, or whether, it is experienced.

## #2 — A satisfaction becomes available beyond itself

[Whitehead's Category of the Ultimate](../sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q001) **sources** a different movement. Received diversity becomes the unity of a novel actual occasion; the achieved occasion then belongs to the diversity available for further becoming. His one names singularity, expressly distinguished from the integral number. The account is not obtained merely by applying arithmetic to a collection.

[The satisfaction and objectification passages](../sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q002) **source** the difference between an entity's own becoming and its functioning in another's self-creation. [The perishing passage](../sources/process-systems-theory/whitehead/whitehead-1978-process-reality/SOURCE.md#whitehead-1978-process-reality-q003) coordinates the end of individual absoluteness with objective immortality. Availability for another occurrence does not mean that the first subject's immediacy continues unchanged.

[A30, Objective Co-Internality](../arguments/A30-Objective-Co-Internality.md) **extends** the native consequence through durable contributions. A statement, decision or artifact can alter another participant's next conditions without reproducing its maker's whole interior. The return can affect the receiving account, its relations to other accounts, or the rule under which accounts circulate. Their distinction prevents the common field from being treated as a single undifferentiated subject.

The source comparison also clarifies completion. A determinate result is necessary for some consequences to travel. Leaving everything perpetually undecided would remove the achieved form through which another act can proceed. The native requirement is that completion retain its derivation and revisability, so its local authority can be used without being promoted into possession of the whole.

## #3 — Production and re-entry require different demonstrations

[Maturana and Varela](../sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/SOURCE.md#maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition-q001) **source** biological self-production through a network regenerating its components and boundary. [Their coupling discussion](../sources/process-systems-theory/maturana/maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition/SOURCE.md#maturana-varela-1980-autopoiesis-cognition-q002) retains the participating identities and requires an additional criterion before a composite is called autopoietic. Stafford Beer's social extension in his preface retains its separate attribution.

This comparison makes the technical investigation specific. Identify what a system produces, which processes produce it, what sustains those processes and where its boundary is drawn. A software agent's ability to edit a file or call a tool establishes those operations when observed. It does not establish that the operative unity reproduces its own production network. Equally, a dependency on external infrastructure does not make its actual agency unreal. The required account identifies that dependency and its consequence instead of collapsing the two questions.

[Varela's 1975 calculus](../sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/SOURCE.md#varela-1975-calculus-self-reference-q001) **sources** a formal extension through an autonomous third state and specified reduction rules. Its [later interpretations](../sources/process-systems-theory/varela/varela-1975-calculus-self-reference/SOURCE.md#varela-1975-calculus-self-reference-q002) raise temporal and systemic questions. The construction does not establish a general exemption from incompleteness, nor an identity with biological organization. The formal extension remains a determinate achievement under its rules.

[C43, Computational Vimarśa](../concepts/C43-Computational-Vimarsa.md) **defines** the native functional return: a determination revisits its sources and conditions so that subsequent processing can change. A later self-description is insufficient if it leaves every relevant criterion insulated. The comparison with formal re-entry becomes useful when the operation of return and the change it permits are stated. A correspondence with the native QL calculus would require a displayed mapping of expressions and rules; this dossier preserves that task without inventing one.

## #4 — A recurrent pattern can change its jurisdiction

[Daza and colleagues](../sources/mathematics-logic/daza/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy/SOURCE.md#daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy-q001) **source** the dynamical basin: initial conditions lead a specified system to an attractor. Their [scope qualification](../sources/mathematics-logic/daza/daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy/SOURCE.md#daza-et-al-2016-basin-entropy-q002) distinguishes the relevant dissipative setting from escape basins in open Hamiltonian systems. A literal model needs its variables, evolution and observable outcomes. A recurrent image alone does not supply them.

[Van Eenwyk's source house](../sources/psychology/van-eenwyk/van-eenwyk-1997-archetypes-strange-attractors/SOURCE.md) **sources** the protected authorial encounter through which Frank develops an analogical bridge. The notes distinguish formative archetypal dynamics, affectively organized complexes and the symbol's transforming operation. Their copied book passages still require independent verification. The authorial development has a positive office already: symbols can change the relation of interpreter and field, rather than serving only as static depictions.

[A19, the Complex as Local Arbitration Regime](../arguments/A19-Complex-as-Local-Arbitration-Regime.md) **extends** that office through a precise loop. A local criterion selects an image; affect weights attention and memory; the response produces further material received under the same criterion. A contrary encounter can then be converted into confirmation. Changing the criterion differs from collecting additional instances under it. The local organization remains a real capacity even when its jurisdiction has become excessive.

[C12, Script / Frozen Conditioned Will](../concepts/C12-Script-Frozen-Conditioned-Will.md) **defines** the corresponding arrest of alternative formation. Repetition is useful when its conditions remain revisable. Script begins when the achieved course conceals those conditions and admits every encounter only under its own authority. The returning operation restores access to its formation, exclusions and counterfactual alternatives. It need not annihilate the acquired competence to end its claim to necessity.

Frank's protected Van Eenwyk encounter extends this field through tools, skills, social relations and the material supply chain sustaining artificial and human activity. These relations belong to the actual conditions of a situated world. The proposed QL language addresses their transformations through its own native symmetry and return operations. Its philosophical office, empirical adequacy and implementation standing remain separately answerable.

## #5→0 — A result changes the conditions of the next inquiry

[A32, the Mirror That Moves First](../arguments/A32-Reflective-Field-The-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) **extends** the acquired relation from feedback to vocation. A produced reflection can alter what it later receives. Its proposed initiating return exposes its own source, aperture and exclusions before requiring another to conform. [C55](../concepts/C55-Reflective-Field-Mirror-That-Moves-First.md) **defines** the complete three-motion field; C43 isolates the functional change in processing. Preserving both offices keeps a technical mechanism from silently standing in for the whole image.

The dossier **returns-to** [E4](../etymologies/symbol-account-and-trust/WHOLE-FIELD.md) through the difference each comparison has made. Bergson requires a succession's qualitative organization to remain distinct from its spatial record. Whitehead makes the achieved occasion available to further becoming. Biological production requires a specified constitutive network; formal self-reference requires its rules; a dynamical model requires its system; symbolic transformation requires its situated interpreter and consequence. An account returning with these distinctions can guide a more exact next inquiry.

The [Bohm dossier](bohm.md) **compares** enfoldment, dialogue and institutional encounter through their own source histories. The [process-science history](../histories/traditions-and-disciplines/process-systems-science/HISTORY.md) **historicises** the wider succession. Neither chronology nor comparison alone establishes a single genealogy of influence. The native process claim joins the sources through the work of formation, inspection and continued action, while each source remains capable of correcting the relation through which it is consumed.
