## Movement map (before / as drafting)

### M01 — The Question Before the Mechanism
- **Claim (own words):** Planetary / artificial systems can model and act while displacing the subject for whom distinctions matter; exact inquiry already depends on an uncounted knower, situation, and horizon.
- **Left unresolved by prior:** nothing (section opens).
- **Must make necessary for M02:** that forms through which we know can be asked to contain the knower—so definition of the Subject becomes urgent, not optional.

### M02 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object
- **Claim:** The subject is the non-objectifiable pole of a differentiating event; treating it as a hidden object repeats the error. Full A01 performance through definition → self-application → failed enclosure → disclosure → context → **I am** → God/Subject reappointment.
- **Left unresolved by M01:** what happens when a form is asked to contain the knower.
- **Must make necessary for M03:** definition has retained its cutting power; the failed demand was enclosure, not determination itself.

### M03 — Definition as Cut, Gift, and Danger
- **Claim:** Determination is negational and productive; danger begins when the achieved term forgets the field from which it was cut (apoha / Spinoza / symbolon / protected criterion).
- **Left unresolved by M02:** how the cut that definition still needs can become unfaithful.
- **Must make necessary for M04:** what happens when rules are asked to determine the conditions under which those rules themselves count.

### M04 — Formal-Limit Genealogy
- **Claim:** A sufficiently articulate system cannot convert every condition of its articulation into an ordinary internal term without remainder (distinct scopes: Russell formation, Gödel derivability, Wittgenstein show/say & practice, Whitehead forming/fact, Spencer-Brown/Varela re-entry, Blind Spot experiencer; trust under limit).
- **Left unresolved by M03:** the self-counting of rules at formal scale.
- **Must make necessary for M05:** how disclosed forms can include a working relation to their own conditions (diaphaneity).

### M05 — Gebserian Diaphaneity
- **Claim:** Integral reason retains mental-rational achievement while making positioned seeing transparent to origin, limit, and co-presence; Internet/AI make context operatively constitutive.
- **Left unresolved by M04:** positive method after formal remainder.
- **Must make necessary for M06:** which sign can position the unobjectifiable without installing it as one more object.

### M06 — The Return to Zero
- **Claim (Offered):** Zero enters as promissory meta-sign for the condition a system cannot count as one more object; Bohm’s implicate/vortex bridges into Mind’s differentiating anatomy.
- **Left unresolved by M05:** the uncounted condition after contextual transparency.
- **Must make necessary for §0:** how awareness differentiates into means and known world.
