## Per-movement map

### M13 — A sign migrates between worlds
- **Claim:** Absence already has a place (§0); a sign for it acquires successive offices—separator, astronomical placeholder, lexical/positional mark, arithmetic participant, transmissible procedure—each permission changing what can next be done.
- **Incoming pressure:** §0’s unobjectifiable condition that cannot be counted as one more object; need for a usable mark of absence.
- **Must become necessary next:** Once the sign can be taught and carried without shared metaphysics, arithmetic must state what happens when zero meets other quantities (additive vs multiplicative offices), because transmissibility alone does not yet give every operation.
- **Gain–sacrifice (here):** Gain = reconstructible calculation across disagreement about the divine/space/emptiness. Sacrifice = bracketing the world of meanings so the procedure can travel. Return ≠ putting old meanings back and discarding the calculation.

### M14 — Śūnya becomes operational
- **Claim:** Operative śūnya acts under distinct rules (`a+0=a`, `a·0=0`); division by zero retains an exceptional written form (*khahara*) whose invariance image (Bhāskara) stands beside a separately developed Kashmiri anatomy of manifestation; chronology makes their meeting a conjunction, not a lineage.
- **Incoming pressure:** A sign that can participate in calculation must declare what addition and multiplication do with it; division then forces a retained exceptional expression.
- **Must become necessary next:** Formal constructions that generate one from a sparse beginning (empty product, ordinal successor, NOR) without collapsing them into one miracle—so the essay can compare generative retention without proving Subject from set theory.

### M15 — The empty set generates one
- **Claim:** Three distinct generators—empty product → 1; von Neumann `0=∅`, `S(n)=n∪{n}`; NOR functional completeness—show how a beginning can remain inside what develops; ∅ is an exact object, not Subject; NOR ≠ QL slash.
- **Incoming pressure:** Operative zero invites the question of generation from meagre beginnings.
- **Must become necessary next:** When a determinate presentation succeeds, its mediating stroke can disappear into the presentation—hence the crossed zero as occlusion.

### M16 — The crossed zero
- **Claim:** **Ø** (native) ≠ $\varnothing$ (set-theoretic); the mediating stroke is present but fused into the zero it crosses; brief God-gap: the notion arises at the source that cannot be installed as another determinate item.
- **Incoming pressure:** Successful presentation whose source-relation vanishes behind efficacy.
- **Must become necessary next:** Arithmetic forces the same structure as frame-disclosure: `1/0` under field laws has no inverse; the container must declare itself.

### M17 — The frame disclosed at zero
- **Claim:** Division by zero discloses the containing field; `1/0` is not repaired by being written; altered settings require named changes of law/domain/operation; Robinson’s nonzero infinitesimal is a different procedure.
- **Incoming pressure:** Crossed-zero occlusion needs an exact mathematical neighbour for frame-disclosure.
- **Must become necessary next:** A prohibited form can acquire a new boundary office (mediant) without falsifying the prohibition; only then can Two Ones be written with acquired distinctions.

### M18 — The loan returns
- **Claim:** Mediant retains `0/1` and `1/0` as boundary pairs; after the acquired history, Two Ones (`0` = One/Self-Identity; `1` = All/Self-Difference) name reciprocal dependence in distinct respects; return is through the sign’s operations, not restoration of pre-mathematical atmosphere; brief omitted-receiver scene changes what a correct count is *for*; hands §2 the problem of how differentiation holds its relation.
- **Incoming pressure:** Frame-disclosed zero and retained boundary form make the formal-limit loan repayable with mathematical interest.
- **Must become necessary next (§2):** Differentiation has all its powers; what lets distinctions, decisions and accounts return to the relation they use? (Trust sequence deferred—not rehearsed here.)

## Claim at the gain–sacrifice

**Gain:** a calculation can travel between people who do not share a cosmology; error can be reconstructed from steps.  
**Sacrifice:** a world of meanings (emptiness, divine, space) is bracketed so the procedure remains reconstructible—exactness purchased by leaving that world outside immediate use.  
**Return:** not putting those meanings back and discarding the calculation, but receiving the metaphysical question again through the sign’s acquired operations, prohibitions and history.
