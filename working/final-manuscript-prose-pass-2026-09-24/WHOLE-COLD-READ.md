## Where attention weakens

**§3 — M26 (“The sixfold reads itself”), accounting middle.** After a strong geometric opening, the typed counts (`64+36=100`, `16/9`, `H((4,2),(3,3))`, `1+64+72=137`) stack without enough lived pressure. Short quotation: “Calling the total 100 percent normalises it to one whole; it does not identify the number one with a cardinality of one hundred.” Exact, but the reader is doing ledger work rather than following a thought that needs them.

**§5 — Central → AIKit run (especially M37–M39).** The person-at-the-screen opening earns the inquiry; then the six offices begin to tour themselves. Short quotation: “Show the failure across three openings of the same project.” Pedagogic staging replaces the earlier felt dilemma. Attention recovers at Gate/Recognition and the Hephaestus return, then thins again wherever another “Follow one…” walkthrough restarts.

**§4 — Aion historical fish / quaternio middle (“Wholeness that can change”).** The egg dream and the delayed-reply psychology are gripping; the long Jung itinerary (Pisces, Epiphanius, *Pistis Sophia*, echeneis, connected quaternia) becomes an inventory. Short quotation: “These are the retained windows for the historical fish…” (felt in the prose as successive windows even before the note). The argument’s pressure—can the organ of reception change?—gets buried under exhibits.

**Cross-essay: the delayed reply / readiness example reused as master parable.** After §2 M23 and §4’s person-within-the-pattern, §5’s *ready/not-ready* and three-evening Continuity failures start to feel like the same teaching machine with new labels. Short quotation (§5): “If Central’s continuity carries only the count into the next act, Actuation will articulate events under a false unity…” The chain is clear; the cost is fatigue.

## Where prose sounds machine-generated

**Recurrent discrimination formula.** Across stations: name two things, deny collapse, deny merger, state the positive office. Sample from §2: “Their opposition is a real relation, not its absence.” From §5: “A technically valid operation can still be the wrong act because its relation to the undertaking has been misdetermined.” Useful discipline; when every paragraph does it, the voice flattens into a classifier.

**“What X forces / What remains / Neither… nor…” scaffolding.** §4 refraction: “What the Processual and Alchemical pair force here is a short arc rather than two inventories.” §5: “What the Phenomenological and Phenomenal pair force is the irreducibility of situation…” The pairs speak; the narrator sounds like a product brief.

**Self-applicative process prose in §5 M42.** Short quotation: “This manuscript has undergone a smaller, inspectable version of the problem.” The meta-correction is philosophically apt and stylistically like a status report inserted into the essay.

**Over-qualified provenance sentences inside the body.** Sample pattern (many stations): “This is X’s development, not Y’s claim; no Z is asserted.” Necessary in notes; in continuous prose it reads as defensive machine hygiene.

## Repeated paragraph shapes

2. **Instructional dramatization.** “Watch what happens…”, “Show the failure…”, “Follow one split…”, “Write the disagreement as a sequence…” Dominates §5 offices.
3. **Office catalogue inside a paragraph.** Name / Power lists, six Latin trust offices (*fides…credere*), MEF Day/Night pairs—each item gets one clause; the paragraph becomes a labelled tray.
4. **Return-to-example buttons.** “Return to the call…”, “Return the readiness disagreement…”, “Return briefly to the *delay* label…” Mechanical re-entry.

## Where paragraph development is too chopped

**§5 #### subsectioning** (“The model middle”, “Preference, gauge and commission”, “Failed fitness”, etc.) turns one argument into briefing cards; sentences inside are often fine, but the breath never lengthens.

**§2 claim-anchor density** (many empty `<a id=…>` beats between short blocks) encourages start–stop reading even when prose is continuous.

**§4 MEF six-pair stanzas** (each pair → product name) march rather than develop; chop is structural.

**Places that *feel* chopped because thought turns every two sentences:** parts of §0 M10–M11 (exclusion / tattvic turns) where every sentence resets a distinction.

## Verdict

**PASS FOR COORDINATOR REVISION**

The eight openings do not share one generator template. Machine cadence is real—especially in §5’s office pedagogy and in the universal “neither collapse nor merger” paragraph shape—but it does not dominate so widely that sentence-polishing and targeted run-rewrites cannot repair it. Alive stretches (§0/1, Hephaestus, Pauli egg, Daphne, Job, terminal smile) prove a voice that can carry the whole if the drained middles are brought back under that pressure.
