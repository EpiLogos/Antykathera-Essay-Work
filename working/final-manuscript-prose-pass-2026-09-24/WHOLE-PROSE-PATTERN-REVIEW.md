### Do they fail the opening gate?

**No.** The recon controls diagnosed a manuscript in which strong local objects were quickly forced through one expository template. This assembled candidate has largely left that template. Question, call, positional act, refusal, geometric construction (after a brief inheritance sentence), egg-dream, screen-undertaking, and set-down instrument are different events with different pressures. A reader who hears only the openings does not hear eight applications of one prior sentence.

### Residual opening pressure (coordinator scale, not fail)

- **§3** still begins as architecture (“The preceding section leaves…”). The second paragraph recovers occurrence (centre `O`, stations N/E/W/S, triangle→square). The handoff is audible as briefing; it does not drag the other seven openings into sameness. If anything is rewritten later, rewrite **only the first paragraph of M25** at paragraph scale — not the section’s constructional body.
- **§0** carries a one-paragraph bridge before M07. The bridge is prospectus; the movement opening (“A voice calls my name”) is the real start and should be treated as the section’s audible opening. Do not conflate bridge and beat.
- **§5→0** uses unnamed “someone” consequence. The pressure (completion with continuing reach) is right; the anonymised chain is thinner than §4’s dream or §2’s refusal. Acceptable as a return opening; not the same machine as §3’s typed-sixfold briefing.

## 4. Antithesis patterns

`does not` / `cannot` / `is not` / `rather than` remain frequent. Classic `not X but Y` is largely absent. Judgment:

- **Load-bearing (protect):** enclosure vs disclosure; fusion vs severance; attendance vs standing; 1/0 vs 0/0; positive referent qualified by exclusion; psychic fact vs physical explanation; co-internality without possession.
- **Rhythm (not automatic guilt):** neighbouring soft denials of the same shape still appear (capacity / experience / score reliability; “does not by itself…”). In the present M01 Agentworld paragraph the denials mark real warrant differences. Elsewhere a third neighbouring soft denial of identical syntax should be audited for phantom opposition.
- **`rather than`:** often operational (“rather than as a gap between numbers”); sometimes the model hinge after a positive claim that could lead. Prefer leading with the positive act when the contrast is already earned.

No whole-pass failure from antithesis density alone.

## 5. Abstract-subject patterns

Subjects such as *the relation, the account, the field, the return, the instrument, the inquiry* remain common.

- **Necessary:** Mono–poly, Objective Internality, the Subject theorem, dia/syn as operations, the psychoid common ground — the argument *is* about these objects.
- **Actionless progression (residual):** occasional “The distinction…,” “The return is complete when…,” “Context makes…” where an agent or mechanism the material already supplies could carry the sentence. Repair is local restoration of actors (scribe, dreamer, maker, patient, Hephaestus, writer before the screen), not a purge of technical nouns.

Not a fail of the openings; coordinator revision only where a paragraph advances by abstract noun + soft verb with no new pressure.

## 7. Source-as-agent constructions

Surname + *asks / supplies / approaches / presses / reaches* is **no longer a procession machine** across the whole. Isolated uses do work:

- Kripke “presses”; Descartes “reaches”; Spinoza “approaches” in §0/1 — each enters a distinct difficulty already staged (name/bearer; doubt/occurrence; determination/negation).
- §4 lets Pauli’s dream and the correspondence act before annexation is theorised — source agency here is narrative, not lecture attribution.

**Residual density:** §0/1’s middle third still stacks many named encounters (Kripke → Descartes → Spinoza/Dignāga → Russell → Gödel → Wittgenstein → Cusa → Blind Spot → Gebser/Heidegger). Each has an office and a limit note; the *sequence* can still feel like a reading performed in one continuous movement. That is coordinator-scale pacing (let some returns be tacit; keep problem before name), not a mandate to gut the stations.

## 9. Thinker name density

Highest in §0/1 (formal-limit neighbours) and §4 (Jung/Pauli, then Aion’s historical witnesses). Density tracks genuine comparative burden. The risk is not that names appear; it is that equal syntactic weight makes every surname a station of identical rank. Present prose usually distinguishes operation (what the source *does* to the problem) from endorsement. Keep that discipline; do not add enrichment stacks.
