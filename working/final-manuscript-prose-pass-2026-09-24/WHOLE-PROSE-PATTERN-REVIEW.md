**Manuscript:** `working/final-manuscript-prose-pass-2026-09-24/ASSEMBLED-CANDIDATE.md` (72,512 words including note blocks; ~1,421 lines; 495 prose paragraphs).
**Controls:** `working/final-manuscript-prose-recon-2026-09-24/PROSE-PATTERN-LEDGER.md` and `OPENINGS-AND-RHYTHM.md` (diagnosis of the 22 September enrichment assembly).
**Method:** full cold read of the candidate; counts located by script, judged by reading. Line numbers (L) refer to the candidate file. Counts locate candidates; judgment decides faults.

**Headline.** The two faults the recon named hardest — the uniform 70–100-word paragraph pulse and the eight look-alike section openings — have been substantially repaired. The pulse is gone (whole-manuscript median paragraph is now 137 words, IQR 96–187, against the recon's ~86 / 72–97), and five of the eight sections now open in concrete material. What survived the pass is a different machine: not the paragraph engine but the **movement-closing hand-off** (~36 of 48 movements end by advertising the next burden), the **"X names Y" definitional opener run six times consecutively in §5**, and a local over-merge in **§4**, whose paragraphs are now the longest in the book (median 217 words) while also carrying the highest thinker density. The prose no longer reads as one generator at section level; at movement level, §5 and the closers still do.

## 5. Abstract-subject openings

Paragraph-initial abstract subjects (`The/A/This + relation, account, field, distinction, return, instrument, inquiry, failure, promise, correspondence…`): **29 of 495 paragraphs (~6%)**, distributed: §0/1 ×5, §0 ×5, §1 ×0, §2 ×3, §3 ×1, §4 ×7, §5 ×2, §5→0 ×6. The recon's ~53-strong strong-hit population has collapsed; counts are *not* the judgment, so each flagged candidate was read:

**Earns its form (abstract noun is the argument's own technical object, or the harvest of a scene just performed):**
- "The reliance reaches further than memory, and it is structural." (L18) — the extraction the Thursday scene was performed to earn.
- "The relation between knowing and known can be tested from both directions at once." (L42) — the fusion/severance test is about that relation as such.
- "The promise is a determination, and its determinacy has two faces." (M10, L231) — the promise is the section's lived object.
- "The limits the call arrived inside are not defects awaiting removal." (M11, L251) — opens the tattva movement's actual thesis.
- "The failure has been standing on the page since the section's opening traversal reached `1/0`." (M28, L813) — the failure is the movement's subject, and the sentence converts a retrospective hinge into a staged object.
- "The correspondence around such material shows two thinkers holding one question open…" (L942); "The disagreement sharpens the inquiry…" (L944) — the correspondence is the datum.
- "The instrument is set down." (L1272); "The return begins as `1/0`." (L1403); "The failure is hard to receive…" (L1405) — each is the return-section performing its own office.

**Weakest — opens a movement by assigning an office to an abstraction before any occasion (the recon's mannerism, surviving in exactly two places):**
- **M40, L1193:** "A horizon of powers leaves the undertaking unchanged until intention meets resistance and returns as form." A true thesis, but it is the only §5 product movement whose hook is pure abstraction; M37/M39/M41 hook with a session boundary, a failed method load, and two contrasting processes. M40 grounds within a paragraph (the engineer, the writer, the team), so the cost is one sentence, not one paragraph.
- **M44, L1293:** "The first motion of the mirror is thought's generosity." The mirror is declared as a three-motion machine ("The first motion… The second motion is the reversal." L1295; "The third motion turns the instrument around." L1299) before it has been encountered in anything. The deposited-measure material is concrete; the machine-frame announced over it is the most visibly designed opening in the book. It partially earns itself by delivering all three motions with distinct content.

**Judgment:** the abstract-subject fault is cured at population level. Two movement-openers (M40, M44) retain the office-assigning form; both recover quickly; neither is a repair priority by itself.

## 7. Source-as-agent constructions

Wolfgang Pauli writes to C. G. Jung…" (L920); "Janet"—type event sentences in the Homeric and Ovidian scenes are characters acting in their own scenes, not source-agents, and are exempt.

**Where it survived as a residue:**

- **§1, Kaplan.** The recon complained Kaplan was "the grammatical conveyor of mathematical material." Now down to three mentions, and two are proper history ("The route followed here… is the one Robert Kaplan's natural history of zero lays out," L307; "the construction Kaplan gives at his book's close," L451). Acceptable.
- **§4, Jung.** The spike. In M31–M33 Jung is the subject of acting verbs at least a dozen times: "Jung answers in May…" (L920); "Jung wrote *Aion* out of…" (L996); "Jung assembles these beside older Near Eastern fish worlds and asks why the image grew so active…" (L1001); "Jung distinguishes it from Antichrist" (L1003); "Jung reads the creature as a jellyfish…" (L1005); "Jung inserted the downward balancing movement" (L1010); "Jung corrects his own earlier tendency…" (L1014). - **§3, the dispute list:** "Kapsner rejects the move, Siderits reads the relevant cases as presupposition failure…, Westerhoff presses parsimony" (L831) — a three-surname stack, though each verb is a real, distinct position and the sentence's point is the dispute itself ("The dispute is the discipline."). Borderline-earned.
- **§0/1, Gebser trio** (M05): "Gebser reads the change…" / "What Gebser traces through art history, Heidegger exposes as authority" / "Gebser names the achieved mutation aperspectival" — the twinning is explicitly the essay's own and argued as such (L100), so this is the disciplined form of the construction.

No case found of a source being made to *endorse* the essay's inference — the non-annexation discipline is enforced in the prose itself ("Lacan anticipated nothing of the derivation that follows," L1025; "the correspondence contextualises it, and neither correspondent supplies or verifies it," L946).

## Verdicts

### (1) Would the eight openings still read as written by one reusable generator template?

**NO.** Evidence: the eight first sentences run through eight different machines — 2-word question ("What knows?"), present-tense scene with a bodily act ("A voice calls my name. … I turn."), mathematical fact ("In 205, the middle character contributes no tens."), disputatious question ("Where does counting begin?"), imperative ("Draw a circle."), dated fragment ("Zürich, the spring of 1953."), ordinary scene ("A person sits before a screen…"), passive consequence ("The instrument is set down."). Five of eight open in concrete material; neither of the two questions is built like the other; only §2 still runs thesis → examples → prospectus inside its first movement. The beats that do recur across all eight — a retrospective hinge in seven openings, a forward-naming final sentence in all eight first movements — are the essay's declared architecture (each section receives its predecessor and hands to its successor), and they are worded differently every time. A generator produces shared syntax; what remains is shared office. At movement level, one genuine generator survives: §5's six consecutive "X names Y" definitions and the ~36-movement closer hand-off — that is where the machine still runs, not in the section openings.

### (2) The three highest-value prose-pattern repairs at coordinator scale

1. **Break the movement-closer hand-off monopoly.** ~36 of 48 movements end by naming the next burden, with runs of seven (M25–M31), five (M38–M42) and four (M33–M36); the explicit tour-guide forms — "the next movement follows that disguise…" (L1016), "the next movement is its instrument" (L1068), "that return belongs to the next station" (L1261), "To that life the section now turns…" (L951), "the fidelity the next three movements will need…" (L62) — should be cut or converted into the material itself. Eight to ten movements re-ended inside their own achievement (as M15, M19, M23, M32, M48 already are) would remove the last systemic sameness in the book at the cost of a few sentences per site.
2. **De-stamp §5's six "X names Y" openings.** Keep the definitional naming where the product *is* a definition, but let two or three of the six — M40 ("A horizon of powers leaves the undertaking unchanged…") and M42 ("The concrete here still belongs to wholes…") are the weakest hooks — earn the term through the writer's correction path or the OPTICA/discharge material *before* naming it, so six consecutive movements do not open with the same verb doing the same office. This is a one-day, six-site repair.
3. **Give §4 its air back — surgically, not by re-fragmenting.** Split the four over-merged megaparagraphs at their existing internal seams (L1057 McGilchrist, 407 words; L1005 alchemy, 350; L1036 crossed-zero stations, 344; L983 plasma/Bohm, 340), thin Jung-as-grammar-subject in M33's middle stretch (let the fish, the recipe and the rite act), and let one or two of §4's six movements end without an invoice. Do not touch the egg dream, Daphne, the net or Job — their duration is the pass's chief gain — and do not reopen the paragraph pulse anywhere else; the 137-word median is the repair working.

*No other systemic prose-pattern fault survives the section passes. The classic antithesis is extinct (1 instance, load-bearing), the announcement verbs are reduced and load-bearing, `must now` is gone, abstract-subject openings are down to ~6% of paragraphs with only M40 and M44 not earning their form, and scene-then-harvest is mechanical in exactly one movement (M37). What remains is distributional, and the three repairs above are where the coordinator's budget should go.*
