4. **Abstract-subject drift.** "The return can change the criterion" repeatedly. Countermeasure: name who/what (the engineer selects a source; the trust must answer a patient's correction; the author reopens the theorem).

Countermeasure: lead with the positive operation; keep only sourced or operational negations (genuine boundaries: distribution vs selection; existence vs availability; gate vs recognition; telemetry vs experience; additive shift vs reference policy; synchrony ≠ diachrony ≠ dia/syn).
6. **Reprise bloat.** Egg/Aion reprises could restart §4. Countermeasure: fold to what the technological argument consumes; keep the operations (two divisions; conscious return), not the story.
7. **Self-congratulation in M42.** The self-application must read as evidence about means, not as achievement narration.
