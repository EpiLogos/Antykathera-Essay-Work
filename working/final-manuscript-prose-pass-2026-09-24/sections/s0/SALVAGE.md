## 3. Specific failed rhythms and habits to avoid (diagnosed in the old text)

- **Gloss-on-arrival:** the scene is converted into doctrine within a paragraph or two; Sanskrit terms get definition-qualification-application in one block. (The pass's delay of the gloss by one paragraph is an improvement; go further — let the scene deepen before naming.)
- **The call-scene monopoly:** the same invented walk is asked to carry memory, ownership, response, promise, refusal, field — convenience begins to overwrite the operations. Distinct operations deserve distinct carriers.
- **Scored enumeration:** "First, the agitation may subside… Second, I may understand…" — externally scored rather than discovered through use. Also the five pressures risk the same numbered feel if cut evenly.
- **Balanced-correction cadence:** recurring "does X… not Y… but Z" protective scaffolding around already-established distinctions; count of `not`/`cannot` constructions very high; several phantom-hinge `rather than`s.
- **Report-of-sources voice:** paragraphs that narrate what accounts say about each other instead of thinking through them ("Taylor's notes insist…", "Taylor's encounter with Gebser brings…", "In his study…, Sean MacCracken follows a different question" — the last is a note-shaped paragraph sitting in body prose).
- **Paragraph tiles:** ~80–100-word paragraphs completing themselves; movement breaks at inferential increments; the tattvic descent cut determination-by-determination into lecture slides.
- **Every movement ending on a portable maxim or a "the next act asks" handrail** (M08 close, M09 close, M11 close). Let consequence, not signposting, carry the turn.
- **Abstract subjects running whole paragraphs** ("Openness acquires a discipline here"; "The two words retain different work" is fine once, but the pattern recurs).
- **The double justification of the comparative judgment** (criterion stated, then re-stated defensively). State the criterion in the reasoning, the judgment positively; carry scope once.
