## A. Six-movement chain

1. **M07 — Address makes a world articulate.** A call reorganises notice, memory, place, and possible response. Immediate appearing and intrinsic self-apprehension are one activity (prakāśa–vimarśa), earned after the scene has already done that work. Regress blocked: a second announcer cannot manufacture knowing. Blue/yellow: unity-in-difference. Retention ≠ recollection ≠ imagination. Vāk and grammatical persons: the described can answer as I. Bohm’s vortex recurs only as neighbouring form sustained by movement; apprehension remains the open question. Error becomes distinguishable inside the same means that articulate the street.

2. **M08 — The inner instrument is not the knower.** Misjudgment of intention despite correct recognition of the speaker discloses determinate functions: buddhi settles, ahaṃkāra appropriates, manas coordinates. These are co-active contents/operations; Subjective Immediacy is the pole under which they appear. Practical triad: quieter state; improved account; recognition of ground (neither a third invulnerable portrait nor erasure of finite obligation). Technique that substitutes for its end fails. Bāhiya’s “merely what is seen” keeps Buddhist release while pressing against proprietor-making. “Inner” is not sealed: language, body, instruction participate.

3. **M09 — Alternatives become a course.** Vikalpa differentiates; saṃkalpa gathers into intention. A promise changes another’s possibilities before fulfilment; fidelity answers purpose, not merely retained instruction. Habit carries formation; script freezes conditions out of reach. Thought-as-medicine: follow tone/memory/expectation/speech until a question can be answered; then release the corrective phrase. Bergson’s melody / Whitehead’s achieved occasion / Gebserian co-presence enter as temporal depth of inheritance, not as a thinker parade. Openness is disciplined by another’s refusal that can change what may be done.

4. **M10 — Exclusion within the positive; comparative judgment.** Apoha: a term’s reach is achieved through constitutive exclusion; Pind’s positive particular qualified by preclusion survives. Promise vs command discloses excluded conditions of formation. **Five pressures** (lived, not labelled as curriculum): known recollection of an earlier occasion; comparison holding differences together; absence consequential in a positive field; intention–execution–result as one course; arising/perishing known as change within manifestation. **Comparative conclusion:** for this inquiry the Śaiva experient-and-powers account is more adequate because these operations need positive places without objectifying the experient; Buddhist critique of the fabricated proprietor remains binding. Huayan jewel-net: whole mutual inclusion through a particular jewel, with optical limit and turn to activity—distinct source, shared difficulty.

5. **M11 — Measure, contraction, recognition.** Finite powers (kañcukas) make a world inhabitable. Six-turn compression of the thirty-sixfold field: Anuttara → Śiva–Śakti → Sadāśiva–Īśvara orientations → Sadvidyā–Māyā measure → contracted apparatus → Pratyabhijñā. Eye/frame/horizon: aperture enables; frame usurps when it forbids revision. Dream: experience real, object-status revisable. Nirvikalpa ≠ recognition. Directional consciousness: psychological conscious/unconscious relative to a position; Consciousness rootward names manifestation and ground. Crossed zero: outward differentiation produces determinate position and means; rootward recognition follows them to source without cancelling them; return restores means to use.

6. **M12 — Objective Internality arises.** Constituted means (pramāṇa) within Life/Mind: language, memory, concern, body, others, tools, institutions. Internality + objectivity of those conditions. Permission revoked / obligation recalled: constitution ≠ diagram. Paradigm = lived organisation of means; model useful when revisable. Artificial instance: real inspectable mediation; phenomenality Open. Exit: operative absence → how can a sign of empty position alter present value? (hands §1).
