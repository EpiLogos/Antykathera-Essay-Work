# s0 — PREWRITE (before DRAFT-1; nothing drafted before this exists)

## A. Six-movement dependency chain (incoming pressure → operation → achieved consequence → next necessity)

**M07 — The call; appearing and self-apprehension (prakāśa–vimarśa).**
Incoming: the threshold ended in the defining recognition — the act of knowing cannot be enclosed by what it knows; "I am" stands when every portrait fails; the means can become known without becoming their knower. What is still undetermined: how that undivided knowing becomes a *world with someone in it* — how appearing is heard, addressed, answered.
Operation: the call. Sound reorganises into address; the walker turns; memory, place, obligation arrive together. Through the event: appearing (prakāśa) and self-apprehension (vimarśa) as one dynamism — the mirror/crystal limit (a display nobody knows is a display is not yet experience), the regress (a second knower of the first would need a third), the blue/yellow necessity (a perfectly homogeneous light could not distinguish blue from yellow; appearing is affected by what it shows without dividing), and the retained/recollected/imagined conversation (three different uses of retained trace; a stored sentence surviving is not yet remembering that *this person said it*). Address: the person described can answer as I.
Consequence: differentiation begins inside appearing itself — the cut is activity before it is a separator; the first separation is addressable.
Next necessity: but the turn was made by *this* walker with *these* memories — by what formed means does a particular life know?

**M08 — The inner instrument; three practical changes.**
Incoming: appearing is self-apprehending; the world of the call arrived through attention, memory, expectation — formed functions.
Operation: buddhi, ahaṃkāra, manas as determinate internal functions — judgment, appropriation, coordination — inspectable as contents and operations; the instrument through which, not the musician. The call again, now with a grievance attached: a calmer state can make an unchanged grievance more persuasive; a better explanation of the grievance can become the possession of the one who holds it; recognition concerns the dependence of both positions on the knowing in which they appear. Three different relations changed: conditions of hearing; content of self-description; source-relation of both.
Consequence: the apparatus is real, investigable and not the Subject (antaḥkaraṇa ≠ Subject); functions occupy the objectified side of 0/1.
Next necessity: functions that judge, appropriate and coordinate build — how does difference become a course of action?

**M09 — Vikalpa and saṃkalpa; the promise; thought as medicine.**
Incoming: the instrument can settle, appropriate, coordinate; its acts compose a life.
Operation: the pulse — vikalpa differentiates and qualifies; saṃkalpa gathers selected differences into intention, project, commitment. The promise: a promise changes another person's possibilities before fulfilment; it differs from a command because refusal is possible; intention must remain effective through changed circumstances (the door may need to change). Habit vs script: habit carries useful formation forward; script insulates its governing conditions from the will it directs. Thought as medicine: replacing the compulsory accusation with compulsory reassurance leaves the listening arrested; the corrective thought succeeds when it can relinquish its own compulsory office (śuddha-vikalpa; releasability); a standpoint can decide whose answer counts before the exchange begins (perspective becoming context — tacit Gebser).
Consequence: each act inherits a field, makes a distinction, selects, changes what the next act can encounter; construction proceeds under constraint and remains answerable.
Next necessity: what makes any selected term *determinate at all* — what holds this rather than that?

**M10 — Apoha; the five pressures; the comparative judgment.**
Incoming: determinations and commitments fill the composed world; their determinacy is now the question.
Operation: apoha — determination through exclusion; the achieved term carries the contrast through which it applies. Pind's two passages: the word excludes other referents; the referent is a thing qualified by preclusion — positive particular, not bare negation; nominal term-formation is not propositional double-negation cancellation. Then the five pressures, each already lived in the section: (1) memory — the causal-imprint account gets its due, then: what makes the present image known *as a recollection of an earlier occurrence*; (2) comparison — the remembered and present tone must be held together while different; (3) exclusion/absence — the expected but missing answer matters within a positive field; (4) intention, execution, result — the purpose of helping can require a changed door; a list of steps does not decide whether help was given; (5) arising and perishing — the call's ending is itself apprehended; the manifesting relation differs from the changing content. The comparative judgment: Buddhist analysis of the fabricated proprietor remains binding — a permanent theorem here; but for *this inquiry*, whose tasks are memory, comparison, exclusion, intention-action, manifestation, recognition, the Śaiva account is more adequate because its differentiating unity gives these operations a source without objectifying the experient as a second inner witness. Watson named once as the contemporary carrier of the Buddhist-psychological critique; the critique's strength retained; no global verdict on every Buddhist position. Huayan's jewel-net: its own positive whole (southwest jewel, mutual entry, the two questions, the ink dot), compared at its own office, never evidence that the traditions teach one doctrine.
Consequence: positive places for experient and powers — without reinstalling the proprietor under a metaphysical title.
Next necessity: how does this differentiated field contract into a finite, *effective* world — and how is its source recognised?

**M11 — The sixfold tattvic compression; directional consciousness; state vs recognition.**
Incoming: with determination, gathering, exclusion in hand, the genealogy of finite worldhood can be traversed.
Operation: the six movements — Anuttara (no external measuring object); Śiva–Śakti (appearing and its articulating power); Sadāśiva–Īśvara ("I am this" / "this am I", 0/1 ↔ 1/0); Sadvidyā–Māyā (the workable seam: revealing and withholding as one operation); the kañcukas and contracted apparatus (limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, order → puruṣa and the inner instrument); Pratyabhijñā (recognition reverses amnesia, not differentiation). The dream: an effective world whose objects need not be externally material; waking changes their source relation without cancelling the urgency. Nirvikalpa: a quiet free of differentiated content is a real state; a freedom *identified with keeping contents absent* is threatened by their return; recognition knows the ground through quiet and articulation together. Directional consciousness: from the bounded position, some contents are present against an unconscious depth; sourceward, Consciousness names manifestation and its ground — the whole Self is not the conscious position enlarged until nothing unconscious remains.
Consequence: contraction is a positive apportionment of powers; the same word "conscious" changes scope with the direction of inquiry.
Next necessity: what is the constituted means *as such* — the mediating field every one of these operations inhabits?

**M12 — Objective Internality; the hand to zero.**
Incoming: the anatomy given; the general office can now be named without deducing it from any single genealogy.
Operation: O:I — the constituted means (pramāṇa) through which a Life inhabits, discriminates and acts within a World; notational, nomological, exclusion-constituted; language, memory, body, other persons, tools, permissions, institutions condition what can appear, count as a reason, become actionable. The revoked permission: a correction which merely appears in an account changes nothing; a correction which returns into the means changes the next act. Recognition of means as means: Ø/X — the instrument neither discarded nor promoted to knower. The technical instance: retained context, sources, permissions, tools constitute an inspectable operative interior; phenomenal localisation remains open in both directions. The three changes return concretely at the level of a life.
Consequence: constituted mediation is real, investigable, not the Subject and not the World.
Next necessity: absence has been operative all along — exclusion, the missing answer, the empty hour. What changes when such an absence acquires a sign another person can use? In 205, how can the sign of an empty position alter the value of what is present? — §1.

## B. Missing because-steps the old text compressed or omits (must be carried)

1. Why the call is *address* and not merely sound: a name requires a bearer, a circumstance, and a possible answer; repeating a sound supplies none of these by itself.
2. Why reflective commentary cannot manufacture knowing: the regress — each reflection would need a further reflection; self-apprehension must belong to the appearing.
3. Why blue/yellow requires *affected* unity: a perfectly homogeneous light would collapse blue into yellow (saṅkara); so appearing is determinate without a second substance — this grounds the whole positive account before any tattvas.
4. Why the three practical changes differ: each changes a different relation (conditions of hearing / content of self-description / source-relation of both positions to knowing) — not three depths of the same change.
5. Why a promise differs from a command: refusal must remain possible; intention stays effective through changed circumstances; result answers to what was undertaken.
6. Why exclusion needs a knower holding this/not-that, absent alternatives and traces in one act — and why this cannot be answered by one more image labelled "past": label, image and occasion must still be apprehended together.
7. Why the comparison reaches a judgment at all: the criterion is operational — can the account give positive places to the operations this inquiry must retain? — and the judgment is therefore task-specific, never a global religious ranking.
8. Why contraction is not subtraction: the kañcukas are powers apportioned, not pieces lost; the five contractions make this-here-now-mine actionable.
9. Why nirvikalpa does not automatically mean recognition: a freedom identified with keeping contents absent is hostage to their return; maintenance requires the very interruption it forbids.
10. Why the directional distinction matters: rootward movement changes the *direction of inquiry*, not the truth of the therapeutic conscious/unconscious distribution; the whole Self is not the conscious position inflated.
11. Why O:I lands in pramāṇa: not in the Subject (unobjectifiable), not in the World (the known), but in the constituted means — and why an inspectable model remains a determination within it.
12. Why zero: absence has been doing constitutive work since the exclusion and the missing answer; the outgoing question asks what happens when absence gets a sign — this, not any Śaiva–arithmetic transmission claim.

## E. Opening choice for THIS section and why

Open with the call: a voice calls the name; the walker turns. Rationale: (1) it is this section's own pressure — the called-name/address/response problem — not a template; (2) §0/1 closed on "I am" and the undetermined relation between knowing and its means; the call is the minimal event in which undifferentiated hearing becomes addressed knowing, so it receives the predecessor while opening the new question; (3) the Sanskrit gloss is delayed one breath — the scene must deepen one more step (the turn, the reorganised world, the one who was there before hearing the name) before prakāśa–vimarśa are named to name what has already happened; (4) the scene can then *generate* the doctrine: appearing, self-apprehension, memory, address, answer — the call contains the section in miniature, and its later returns (grievance, promise, missing answer, empty hour) let one event accumulate offices instead of the prose manufacturing a new parable per operation. No prospectus sentence announcing what the section will do; the movement itself is the announcement.

## F. Paragraph-development risks and repairs

1. **The five-pressure run fragmenting into five equal mini-paragraphs** (machine cadence / listing under compression). Repair: one long developing run (or two large paragraphs) where each pressure inherits from material already lived; the run's length is warranted by distinct reasoning; the conclusion names the criterion and the judgment.
2. **Sanskrit terms each receiving definition-qualification-application in one paragraph** (uniform explanatory blocks). Repair: a term enters only when its operation is already needed; taught through the acting scene; later uses tacit.
3. **The call-scene monopoly.** Repair: distinct carriers per operation (colour, tone, promise, jewel, dream, permission); the call returns but accumulates offices.
4. **Uniform medium paragraph pulse.** Repair: paragraph size answers to the local act — the five-pressure run long; the turn short; proofs of distinction medium; no blank-line after every inferential increment where one thought continues.
5. **Negation rhythm / protective scaffolding** ("not X, but neither Y; rather Z"). Repair: every serious comparison takes the four-question burden; the Buddhist disagreement is source-bearing (momentariness, causal imprint, Pind's positive referent named); default to direct positive statement; keep determinate negations only where the limit itself supplies content (regress; unobjectifiable subject; absence).
6. **Register shifts unmarked** (L4). Repair: "conscious" (psychological distribution) vs "Consciousness" (sourceward ground) shown and marked in M11; "mind" as inner instrument vs Life/Mind whole marked in M12; "recognition" never silently = recollection.
7. **Underclaim at the comparative conclusion** (the known s0-F01 failure). Repair: the conclusion states the greater adequacy for this field positively, with the scope carried by the criterion in the reasoning; no defensive disclaimer ending.
8. **The ending drifting into a §1 prospectus.** Repair: end on the concrete question (absence acquiring a sign; 205) — the hand is a question the reader now cannot help, not a chapter announcement.
