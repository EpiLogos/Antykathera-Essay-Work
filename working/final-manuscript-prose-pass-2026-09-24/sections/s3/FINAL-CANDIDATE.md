# FINAL-CANDIDATE — §3 Mathematical Substrate (revision of DRAFT-2 under COLD-REVIEW)

## §3 — Mathematical Substrate

### M25 — The knowing act draws its field

Draw a circle. Its centre, `O`, comes with the drawing: one leg of the compass holds the place while the other sweeps the rim. Set the first radius upward, to `N`; the choice of first station is free, everything afterward determined from it. Set the second radius where it stands at right angles to the first, meeting the rim again at `E` — with two radii an angle exists in the field at all, and here it takes its first determined value. Now carry the same reach straight across the centre, from `E` through `O` to the rim's far side, `W`. The move from E to W crosses the diameter; it is not the next quarter-turn around the circumference. Three stations stand on one circle, and the chords joining them enclose a triangle: three sides, an inside, interior angles summing to 180 degrees. Because `E` and `W` are ends of one diameter and `N` stands on the circle, the angle at `N` faces the diameter and is a right angle; the legs `N–E` and `N–W` are equal, and the angles at `E` and `W` are each 45 degrees. Every station was fixed by one reach and one centre — set outward, turned to a right angle, carried across — yet the figure they make together is already determinate: bounded, angular, complete at its own scale.

Complete the first axis the same way: carry the radius from `N` through `O` to `S`; the two diameters stand at right angles. Join the boundary in the order N–E–S–W–N and the triangle becomes a square; one relation inside the figure changes while surviving: the chord E–W, which bounded the triangle as its far side, now runs through the square's interior as a diagonal, shared by the complementary triangles N–E–W and S–E–W. What previously bounded the figure now articulates its interior. The comparisons this opens stay distinct: four sides where there were three; 360 degrees of interior angle where there were 180; with the radius taken as one, area two where area was one. The route of acquisition — N, E, W, S — is also not the walk that finally bounds the figure; joined in acquisition order the stations give a crossed figure, not this square. An edge became internal, the interior grew, and the earlier bounded truth survives inside the larger figure, still true, no longer exhaustive.

This is where the sixfold of the preceding section comes to live. §2 left a compact typed body: two terms retained, `0` and `1`, together with their four ordered relations, `2+2²=6`, counted as `4+2`. What the counting did not yet show was how that body acts. The constructed square performs the action, because the square does in space what the sixfold does in logic: it keeps a bounded perspective as a true local disclosure while withdrawing that perspective's claim to be the whole — and it does so without installing a fourth spectator who sees everything. There is no view from outside the construction. There is the construction, grown, holding its earlier stage as an interior relation.

Even the simplest judgment about the drawn figure performs more than its words display. To say "this is a triangle" is to predicate a form of something held identifiable through the predication. If the figure changes colour, the question whether it is still the same figure stays answerable; if a second triangle is drawn, its form can be recognised without confusing the two instances. Identification holds an instance across differences, predication makes a difference sayable, and both require a circumstance — the drawn figure, the drawn comparison, someone drawing — in which the comparison can occur. These are distinguishable moments of one determining act, and sorting them into separate boxes would still lean on the relating that lets the boxes be compared. That is the copula's work beneath the written "is," and it is why this section cannot begin from terms already in place: the terms arrive through the act.[^s3-copula]

Before its terms are assigned, QL writes the capacity for relation as `/ = −/−` — two unassigned strokes held in one expression, relation before contrast. The first articulation, `0/1`, gives that relation its conscious circumstance: awareness and what appears. `0` names the unobjectifiable One, present through every determination without becoming one more item in the inventory; `1` names All, the manifest field in which an indefinite particular can be selected. The mark depends on its source for being and coherence; the source becomes articulated and knowable through the mark. Those are different directions of dependence inside one relation, and the construction has already given them their proportion: the inscribed square could not bound itself without the circle that located its stations, while the circle is met only through the figures drawn on it. The act is answerable as it happens. To ask "what is this?" already asserts that something has appeared steadily enough to be questioned; to answer "this is a triangle" keeps open whether the account runs true. In `?/!`, questioning and asserting hold each other in place: without the assertion the question loses its object, without the questionability the assertion loses its accountability.[^s3-native]

Determination also has force. Attention gathers around one chord and withdraws from the rest; the compass leg extends here rather than there. QL writes this differentiating movement as `−/+` — one side functioning as reserve, contraction, withholding; the other as issue, release, extension — a sustained pulse whose poles remain co-present, the way a breath gathers before it gives. The poles can be held as polarity, signed directions on the axis that gives each its meaning; they can also be added, and `−1 + 1 = 0` is a correct arithmetic that becomes a false account of the act when cancellation erases the axis through which the signs were opposed. Under retained polarity the selection stays corrigible: what was unselected remains real, available to the next determination. Recurrence then makes selection recognisable as kind. A second triangle, differently placed and coloured, is identified as another triangle because this instance can express a form no particular instance exhausts. QL writes the relation `X/x`: capital `X` the determining capacity, lower-case `x` its indefinite particular, one letter in two cases so that neither side can replace the other. The capacity is no warehouse of already-drawn triangles. It is what lets a new instance be recognised — and what lets an apparent instance fail the test.

The judgment is also someone's, somewhere. `AM/IS` holds first-person presence and third-person sayability on the slash whose second-person relation lets them meet: I am here, this is what I take the figure to be, and you can answer. Knower, means and known — *pramātṛ*, *pramāṇa*, *prameya* — remain distinct within one appearing-act; the figure, the act of drawing, the available comparisons and what a mistaken judgment would cost all belong to the act's context. Becoming contextual, in the development this essay inherits from Gebser, means caring for the outcome from within the situation rather than annotating an aloof description.[^s3-context]

Exactness then enters the context without claiming to contain it. In `∞/dx`, the inexhaustible horizon meets a local differential, and calculus supplies the exact small case: `F(x)=x²+C` has derivative `F′(x)=2x` for every constant `C`, so integrating `2x` returns a family, and a condition such as `F(0)=3` selects one member.[^s3n1] The derivative was exact throughout; what it lacked was the information needed to select the additive constant. A local operation can be perfectly exact while leaving undetermined the wider state from which it arose — the theorem belongs to the textbook, the provenance reading to this essay, and the two standings stay separate.

The final turn, `1/0`, directs the achieved determination toward its condition and meets the incomputable there: one divided by zero takes no value in the field, and the native notation keeps that limit visible rather than curing it. Recognition — *pratyabhijñā*, recognising what was never absent — changes the mark's relation to what makes it possible; it does not manufacture awareness as its result.

Compressed, the traversal reads

$$
/ = \text{−/−}\;\longrightarrow\;0/1\;\longrightarrow\;?/!\;\longrightarrow\;\text{−/+}\;\longrightarrow\;X/x\;\longrightarrow\;AM/IS\;\longrightarrow\;\infty/dx\;\longrightarrow\;1/0 .
$$

These are eight determinations of one passage: parent relation and return bracket six qualitative determinations, and the traversal does not enlarge the six-position body into an eight-position body. The personed reading belongs to the same stroke. First, second and third person can be taken in sequence; the constructed square shows what it is to hold all three in one act. At `#4` each perspective's claim to exclusivity voids; at `#5` the three are recognised as one appearing-act; at `#0` that achieved one returns as `0/1`, the ground–appearance relation it never escaped. Three perspectives answered by three operations of recognition give `3:3`, which is also `3:1`, since the second triad reckons a single recognition. The angle sums make the recontextualisation visible; they do not prove this account of the Subject. The derivation begins in personed experience, and the later physical and musical refractions inherit that order rather than generating it. Jung's four functions articulated by two attitudes — thinking, feeling, sensation, intuition, each introverted or extraverted — can witness the same `4+2` architecture from psychology's side, after the native derivation and adding nothing to it.

Historically, this completes what the essay's zero–subject braid sought. The mental-rational subject stands within the `#1` line as an apparently self-grounding term and lets its unowned condition fall into the external `−1`; the traversal just drawn keeps the ground–mark relation explicit, `X/x` prevents the particular from severing itself from its determining capacity, and `AM/IS` turns the three-perspective triangle into the square's contextual 360 degrees. The Gebser relation remains a historical comparison, not the source of this native geometry.

The body stands drawn: a distinction that can return to its condition, questioned, forced, recurred, personed, made exact, returned. What it has not yet shown is its own generation — how the sixfold produces itself, accounts for itself, and comes back changed.

### M26 — The body generates and accounts for itself

The First Spanda sets the sixfold in movement before any inventory of its parts. In the native syntax the void reads itself and opens the indeterminate, `0=(0/0)` — every possible ratio held at once, maximal potency; the indeterminate then differentiates into the ratio of ratios, `(0/1)/(1/0)`, the parent unfolding the child it always contained. Within that parent run two nested tracks:

$$
T_0:\quad \frac{0}{(0/1)}\;\rightsquigarrow\;\frac{0}{1},
\qquad\qquad
T_1:\quad \frac{(1/0)}{0}\;\rightsquigarrow\;\frac{1}{0}.
$$

`T0` is the emanative arc: the void takes expression as its denominator and works toward determinate form, ground generating figure. `T1` is the reversionary arc: form takes the void as its denominator and works toward its own condition, figure acknowledging what sustains it. Their bridge recomposes the two orientations,

$$
\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{0}+\tfrac{0}{1}\Bigr)_{\mathrm{QL}}\;\rightsquigarrow\;\frac{1}{1}=1=100\%,
$$

and the plus is the native composition mark, the source notation for QL composition of inverse orientations. The later harmonisation sometimes writes that composition `∨` — a join that affirms a coordinate when either orientation affirms it — precisely so that no reader is tempted to add fractions.[^s3-spanda] Evaluating the terms as ordinary real-number divisions would destroy the expression before its work began: `1/0` and `0/0` are symbolic orientations in this calculus, members of no enlarged number field. The achieved `1/1` is the relation recognised as whole, and it is not the positive pole with the other pole deleted. Emanation and reversion are correlative readings of one operation; printing them in sequence does not require one to finish before the other exists.

The tracks force a further distinction, and everything that follows depends on keeping it: a position in the body is a different object from the content that comes to occupy it. Begin from the four explicate positions between the singles `0` and `1`. Their outer contents, in order, are `(0/0,\;0/1,\;1/0,\;1/1)`: the pure contents `0/0` and `1/1` occupy positions 1 and 4, the mixed contents `0/1` and `1/0` positions 2 and 3. Now read the parent through its internal expansion. For `(a/b)/(c/d)`, relate the first numerator entry to each denominator entry in turn, then the second: `(a/c,\;a/d,\;b/c,\;b/d)`. Retain the two singles beside the four relations, as a different sort. Each parent then carries a complete body:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=(0/1)/(1/0)\;\rightsquigarrow\;(0/1,\;0/0,\;1/1,\;1/0)\;\;+[0,1],\\[2pt]
P'&=(1/0)/(0/1)\;\rightsquigarrow\;(1/0,\;1/1,\;0/0,\;0/1)\;\;+[1,0].
\end{aligned}
$$

Both parents hold a full `4+2`; neither implicate bracket is an empty label around four active items. Compare the positions with their occupants. In the outer body, positions 1 and 4 carried pure contents and positions 2 and 3 mixed; in the expanded parent, the inherited pure places carry the mixed contents and the inherited mixed places carry the pure ones. Structure and Formality — the processual source's names for the pure pair's office and the mixed pair's — exchange across that cross-level reading, with the Essence relation remaining the axis through which the body is held. The reversal from `P` to `P′` belongs to the same account but proves none of it alone: turning the printed row around preserves the pure and mixed sets exactly as they were. The exchange appears only when inherited positions and actual contents are compared at their own levels. Two further readings cross this body without collapsing into it. The harmonic pairing joins neighbours — `(0,1)`, `(2,3)`, `(4,5)`: Being, Becoming, Knowing/unKnowing. The complementary folds join `(0,5)`, `(1,4)`, `(2,3)`: Essence, Constitution, Text-Texture. They share the middle pair and remain different partitions, one following the passage from availability through movement to recognition, the other bringing beginning into relation with return, definition with context, force with pattern.

A minimal categorical carrier makes the difference between unity and erasure exact. Take two objects `A`, `B`, an arrow `f:A→B` and an arrow `g:B→A`, with `g∘f=id_A` and `f∘g=id_B`. Two objects, four arrows — two identities and two crossings — one connected structure. The return recovers the identity appropriate to its starting object, and the structure is whole without `A` and `B` being the same object. The Boolean companion works differently and shows what that difference costs. Let a state alternate, `s(n+1)=1−s(n)`. Its two-step windows are `01` and `10`; their componentwise join is `01∨10=11`, each coordinate affirmed across the complementary windows. A declared change of register then reads the word as the slash-form `1/1`, unit ratio, `100%` — and the declaration has to be made, because as a binary integer `11` is three. What the join cannot carry is order: nothing in `11` says which window came first. There is also a linear carrier that keeps both unity and orientation. Write the windows as `b=(0,1)` and `p=(1,0)`. Their sum `u=(1,1)` is fixed by the coordinate swap; their difference `d=(1,−1)` changes sign under it; and the pair reconstructs exactly,

$$
b=\frac{u-d}{2},\qquad p=\frac{u+d}{2}.
$$

The invariant alone cannot reconstruct what was distinguished. Invariant plus changing difference can. Three exact carriers — invertible crossing, complementary coverage, recovery from invariant and phase — display one result: the achieved whole need not erase its two readings. A completed act can therefore change role rather than vanish: what stood as achieved result takes up the position of inherited condition for a next act. That is a change of recursive role, not the numerical equation `1=0`, and it is where the sixfold begins to retain history instead of repeating a shape.

The Second Spanda asks the completed body to account for itself. Let `F` be its six-position set. A binary assignment to every position is an element of `B^F`: `2⁶=64` such assignments. An ordered pair of positions is an element of `F×F`: `6²=36` such addresses. Retained as tagged collections, the account totals `64+36=100` — and calling the total 100 percent is a declared normalisation, not an identification of the number one with a hundred things. The two sectors can then be compared, which is another operation still:

$$
\frac{64}{36}=\frac{16}{9}=\frac{2^4}{3^2}.
$$

Four binary factors stand against two ternary factors: the vertical reading `4:2`, found by the body's own accounting. Dividing the sum by four would instead have given `25` — the passage from total to proportion must remain visible, because it is a choice, not a consequence of `64+36=100`. The horizontal `3:3` partition has exact effects of its own: the six binary choices split into two triples, `8×8` assignments; the address array splits into four `3×3` blocks. The same sets support different factorisations without becoming different sets, and the cross-reading of the two orientations is a declared componentwise operation,

$$
\mathcal{H}\bigl((4,2),(3,3)\bigr):=\bigl(\tfrac{4}{3},\;\tfrac{2}{3}\bigr),
$$

which ordinary scalar division does not reproduce: `(4/2)/(3/3)=2` is a different comparison entirely. From the declared reading the ratio family follows by ordinary arithmetic, each ratio taking the name the reading gives it: fourth `4/3` — manifestation, the span going forth — and its inverse `3/4` — recognition, the span read home; grounding `2/3`, the fifth as settling-back, and aspirational `3/2`, the fifth as lure drawing the field toward completion; totality `(4/3)²=16/9`, the fourth taken twice, once per conjugate pass; octave `(4/3)(3/2)=2`; and between fifth and fourth, exactly, `(3/2)/(4/3)=9/8`. The names are appointments of the reading, not additional arithmetic; these ratios carry their earlier subject-level meanings as source, and the closing movements will let them sound.

The Third Spanda adds a phase-sensitive sheet. A singled-out actualisation — the achieved whole in its own office — together with the 64 assignments and a two-phase copy of the 36 addresses gives `1+64+72=137`. The tags matter: the actualisation is not a missing uniform state, since both uniform assignments were already inside the 64, and the address-sheet's second copy comes from the phase tag: reversing an already-counted pair does not by itself double that space, because the 36 ordered addresses already include their reverses. A companion compression, `2⁷+3²=137`, partitions the same integer differently and proves nothing new. The harmonic transfer across the sheets is exact — `36·(16/9)=64`, `64·(9/8)=72` — and no identification with any physical constant follows from the count.[^s3n2]

One caution qualifies the narrative use of these counts, and §2 has already staged its figure. The Prisoner whole shows an administered number: a completed assignment whose governing relation stays out of sight — an inquiry into the source of command answered by a classification of the questioner. Neither a television designation nor its narrative success supplies mathematical or musical proof; the sixfold's authority lives in the declared operations above, which keep the native derivation, its harmonic carrier and a story's figure distinct.

Two growth grammars belong to the mature body. Relational form growth pairs a developed field with its conjugate and generates their earned relations: `N/N′ → N+N′`. Criterion and Distinction, for example, generate Criterion-through-Distinction — a criterion selects which differences count, while a consequential difference can require the criterion itself to change; the full exemplar runs from Continuity-in-Indeterminacy through Criterion-through-Distinction, Delineation-through-Difference, Arbitration-in-Crisis and Con-text-through-Diaphaneity to Resolution-in-Reconciliation. Six generated relations, not twelve concatenated labels. Distributive recursion asks a different question: each position may develop a complete local sixfold, `N→N×N`, thirty-six addresses whose contents must still earn their places. The first grammar articulates the relation between conjugate wholes; the second develops the whole within each position. Neither obliges a subject to have six parts. And through both, the body keeps its two faces coequal: Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son, Image — what the relation can name, the fixed faces under which each determination becomes sayable — paired with Play, Need, Sacrifice, Decision, Love, Work — what it costs and does. A determination begins from available possibility, selects under need, gives something up in becoming articulate, decides, binds, produces. Generation alone does not move. The mark has still to acquire a phase.

### M27 — A mark that returns arrives changed

A one-line equation exposes what a phase has to be. `r = 1 − r` has no solution in `{0,1}`: choose either value and the right-hand side becomes the other. Give the operation a next step and the problem changes shape. `r(n+1) = 1 − r(n)` runs — an initial zero yields `0,1,0,1,…`, an initial one the shifted sequence — because argument and result now occupy different times. Delay supplies a domain in which the operation can run; it finds no previously hidden fixed point, because there was none to find. What the running creates is a state that carries its prior crossing with it: the value at `n+1` is intelligible only through the value at `n`. A mark that returns has crossed, and crossing is an operation with an orientation.

Spencer-Brown begins his calculus of indications from the injunction to draw a distinction, and his two elementary reductions name the fork the delay construction has just stepped around. Calling the marked state again leaves it marked — `MM → M`; crossing a crossing returns to the unmarked state — and juxtaposition and enclosure perform different operations. What the calculus does not by itself say is what happens when a form re-enters the space that the distinction established: a static description then demands that the form be identical to what its own operation negates. The essay's argued reading gives the re-entering mark a temporal body. It returns carrying its prior crossing, the way the delayed state carries its predecessor, and cannot return as though no distinction had occurred.

Varela met the same pressure from inside the calculus. His *Calculus for Self-Reference* introduces an autonomous third state that enlarges the arithmetic — new values, new reduction rules — because self-reference cannot be stabilised inside the two initial states. The move changes the admitted state-space rather than adding a comment to the old one, and its formal gain is real. What remains genuinely open is the question any temporal reading must answer: what, exactly, recurs; how is frequency measured; what retains the prior state; how does that retention alter the next crossing. Varela's own temporal and systemic interpretations leave that frequency characterisation for further work, and the task stands open here under its own name. Nothing in the third state is a discovery about consciousness; it is a discovered cost of admitting self-reference formally.

Kauffman's iterants give alternation an explicit algebra, and with it time becomes constructible rather than assumed — the algebraic face of the retention question, though not its complete answer. An ordered view `[a, b]` and its shifted view `[b, a]` are two points of view of one alternating process: "we can think of [b, a] as the same process with a shift of one time step. These two iterant views, seen as points of view of an alternating process, will become the square roots of negative unity, i and −i."[^s3n3] The construction behind the sentence is checkable; Kauffman's own development states its swap law, an iterant view composed with the shift satisfying `[a, b]η = η[b, a]`, at pp. 27–28 of the arXiv v2. Take the shift that exchanges the two entries and the diagonal that gives them opposite signs:

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D^2=\eta^2=I,\quad D\eta=-\eta D .
$$

Each operation is harmless alone; together they refuse to commute, and from the anticommutation it follows that `(ηD)² = −I`. The composite acts as a quarter-turn — applied to a vector `(x,y)` it gives `(−y,x)`, applied twice `(−x,−y)`, four times back to the start: multiplication by `i`, built from alternation plus shift. The orientation was constructed through noncommuting operations, inferred from nothing about circles. Kauffman himself notes that the non-triviality of the kernel behind the construction means there is often a choice of iterant representation for a given algebra, so the building carries its own anti-uniqueness clause: it shows how `i` can arise from declared operations, and it does not show that alternation owns the imaginary unit or that oscillation is time.

Let a continuous nonzero path close at its starting complex value: the final point alone reveals nothing about the journey, which may have gone around the origin once, twice, or not at all. The complex plane states the general law of which the quarter-turn is the discrete case. For `z = re^{iθ}`, multiplication by `e^{iφ}` sends the phase `θ` to `θ+φ` while the modulus `r` stays fixed; four quarter-turns return the orientation, `1 → i → −1 → −i → 1`. Opposition along one axis has become orthogonal relation across two, and a fourfold can be generated by rotating one relation rather than assembling four independent contents — the mathematical bridge to quaternity, crossed here before any psychological register is asked to carry it. The same plane records what return costs and keeps. Choose a continuous lift of the closing path's phase to the real line, and the difference between ending and starting phase records an integer multiple of `2π` — the winding number — which belongs to the path-with-its-lift, to a retained history, never to a property secretly lodged in the endpoint. A system that claims to remember its return must keep the register in which the return is legible.

Bohm's conversation with Krishnamurti gives fidelity through change its lived counterpart. Recalling a wheel turning with a thread running straight through it, Bohm distinguishes thought's running true from the possession of correct information: the thread stays straight while the wheel turns, and the conversation around the image keeps making room for error — a person can notice a mistake and drop it instead of persisting in it. The mathematical modulus makes invariance under rotation exact; the thread makes answerability perceptible in the conduct of thought; neither supplies the other's proof, and the two speakers' disagreement about a common energetic root remains a disagreement rather than a doctrine this essay settles. What the section takes from them is the transition it needed: a determination can return without being unchanged in every respect — identity preserved, orientation changed, local description exact while additional history becomes necessary. The recogniser who corrects a judgment also preserves something through change, and does so by undergoing the inquiry. Whether the frame itself can change while the earlier achievement stays usable is the question the constructions have now earned the right to ask.

### M28 — Where the frame fails, the frame shows

The failure has been standing on the page since the section's opening traversal reached `1/0`. A limit the native notation refuses to cure is informative precisely as a failure: an expression that cannot be evaluated under stated laws is reporting which laws are in force. The projective line is the first construction that takes the report seriously without pretending the arithmetic changed. A point of the real projective line is a nonzero pair `[x:y]`, with nonzero scalar multiples naming the same point; where `y≠0` the ordinary coordinate `u=x/y` works, and the chart fails exactly at `[1:0]`. Add the second chart, valid where `x≠0`, with coordinate `v=y/x`, and the formerly impossible point acquires the perfectly definite coordinate `v=0`. The zero belongs to the new chart. It marks a point that had no finite address in the old one, and it acquires no multiplicative inverse in the real field by moving house. Nothing about arithmetic loosened; the space grew.

Parallel lines give the same report in the plane. In homogeneous coordinates the affine parallels `y=0` and `y=1` become `Y=0` and `Y−Z=0`, and the two equations share the solution `[1:0:0]` — the point at infinity that records their common direction. The all-zero tuple is excluded because it would specify no point at all. The construction has changed what counts as a point and the space in which lines meet; the original affine statement, that these lines have no finite intersection, stands unbroken inside the larger space that explains why. Complex completion repeats the move at a different dimension: the complex plane plus one point at infinity is the Riemann sphere, `CP¹`, and in its reciprocal chart the former infinity is again zero. Maps extend through the change; the extended plane is still no field in which zero has an inverse.

Enlargement earns its keep only if something invariant survives it, and the Möbius transformation supplies the test. Let

$$
w(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad ad-bc\ne0,
$$

and form the cross-ratio of four points in the stated convention,

$$
[z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4]=\frac{(z_3-z_1)(z_4-z_2)}{(z_3-z_2)(z_4-z_1)} .
$$

Every transformed difference picks up the determinant factor and two denominator factors, and in the cross-ratio those added factors cancel between numerator and denominator: the four points keep their cross-ratio through any such transformation, poles and infinity included under the limiting reading. A worked case keeps the claim honest: `(0,1,2,3)` has cross-ratio `4/3`; under `w(z)=(2z+1)/(z+1)` the four images are `(1, 3/2, 5/3, 7/4)`, whose cross-ratio is still `4/3`. The transformation changes intervals while preserving a relation among relations — and QL can meet this construction because the native parent `(0/1)/(1/0)` already asks how two complete orientations belong together. The cross-ratio supplies a specified geometric invariant; the native parent supplies the earlier recognitive question. The two meet without merging, and the quantum instance of the same space keeps the meeting clean: pure states of a two-level system, modulo overall phase, also form `CP¹` and project to the Bloch sphere, with `|0⟩` and `|1⟩` at opposite poles — an exact fact about qubits that makes the essay's `0` and `1` no more quantum than a shared alphabet makes two languages identical.

The frame-changing move has a logical register too, with a live dispute attached. Four-valued semantics distinguishes true, false, both and neither by taking the subsets of `{T,F}` — `{T}`, `{F}`, `{T,F}`, `∅` — with negation exchanging `T` and `F` and designation meaning containment of `T`. The semantics refutes explosion by itself: give `p` the value `{T,F}` and `q` the value `∅`, and both `p` and `¬p` are designated while `q` is not, so contradictory information entails nothing it should not. The four values are a genuine `2²` built from the presence and absence of two designations; QL's `2+2²` is a different construction, retaining the two original terms alongside their four pairings, six where FDE has four. Priest's further proposal — a fifth corner for cases treated as none of the four — matters because the value-space itself becomes part of the problem, and the objection it provokes matters equally: Kapsner rejects the move, Siderits reads the relevant cases as presupposition failure needing no new value, Westerhoff presses parsimony and contextual variation. The dispute is the discipline. Formal enlargement is a response to obstruction with real costs, and the new frame must earn its necessity case by case. One identification stays barred by construction: QL's `#0` is a generative condition, and calling Priest's fifth value an "implicate ground" would erase the difference between a value in a system and the condition through which anyone uses the system.

Algebra states the discipline in one line: division pluralisms. Undefinedness, projective infinity, signed infinities and totalised operations preserve different laws, and the answer a setting gives to a written `1/0` discloses the chosen structure rather than settling arithmetic once for all. The section's own procedure has been exactly this: when a relation fails under a frame, inspect both the relation and the frame before declaring the relation impossible or the frame absolute. Paradox here is no licence to escalate dimensions on demand. It is information about the containing field — and the productive case, in which the obstruction changes what the field can next permit while the local truth that forced the change stays intelligible, is the case the essay carries forward. ### M29 — Two forms of return, and an ending

Played, the ratio family the Second Spanda's cross-reading derived stops being notation. A fourth multiplies a string's frequency by `4/3`, a fifth by `3/2`, and the two compose the octave because `(4/3)(3/2)=2`. Take the fourth twice instead: `(4/3)²=16/9`, and the span falls short of the octave by one exact multiplicative step,

$$
\frac{16}{9}\cdot\frac{9}{8}=2,
\qquad\qquad
\frac{4}{3}\cdot\frac{9}{8}\cdot\frac{4}{3}=2 .
$$

The whole tone `9/8` — the same interval by which the fifth exceeds the fourth, `(3/2)/(4/3)=9/8` — completes the squared fourth to the octave. It does completing work: it is the remainder through which the octave is reached, and erasing it would prevent the very arrival it seems to delay. Music is the register where this can be heard rather than merely computed, because relation is perceptually primary there: a pitch is specified by frequency, an interval by ratio, and a cadence returns to its tonal centre while the ear retains the path by which the return was heard. What the arithmetic establishes is the factor; remainder, cadence and return name its interpretation — an interpretation, not a new theorem. And what is heard is no row of ratios. Bergson's analysis of duration names the difference: the melody is altered by each note it gathers, successive states permeating one another, and it degenerates into a spatial inventory exactly when its moments are set side by side as interchangeable units. The lived interval between fourth and octave is qualitative through and through; the topology about to arrive must be read as a distinct exact witness, never as a diagram of duration.

A second, less forgiving remainder sets the limit of the first. Twelve pure fifths overshoot seven octaves,

$$
\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^7}=\frac{531441}{524288}\ne1,
$$

and the same discrepancy appears as six pure whole tones against one octave. That residual is the Pythagorean comma, and it is not the whole tone `9/8` — the completing factor of one selected span and the drift of an entire cycle are different remainders, however much the one word "remainder" wants to cover both. Twelve-tone equal temperament closes the cycle by declaring its cost: each semitone takes the step `2^{1/12}`, the octave closes exactly, and the fifth approximates rather than equals `3/2`. The tuning history keeps the operations distinct: temperament distributes the comma logarithmically across a cycle of fifths, while the native identity `16/9·9/8=2` retains the factor completing one selected span. An instrument can achieve working cyclic closure by changing the intervals it preserves — a representational choice with audible consequences, not a defeat of return.

Topology now supplies the spatial witness, and its first gift is the distinction between a boundary and a seam. Identify each point of the plane with all its unit translations,

$$
(x,y)\sim(x+m,\,y+n),\qquad m,n\in\mathbb{Z},
$$

and the quotient `T²=ℝ²/ℤ²` is the torus: the unit square presents the whole surface once opposite edges are identified, and crossing an edge continues from the corresponding opposite edge. The edge is a seam in the presentation, not a boundary of the surface. The covering projection keeps the infinite plane in the account — every surface address has infinitely many lattice-related representatives — and Euler's count of the presentation, one vertex, two edge-classes, one face, gives `χ(T²)=0`. Four presentation sides plus two independent generating loops is the count QL's `4+2` recognises as its own shape at genus one, adopted as a structural correspondence whose mathematical office — sides present the square, generators articulate the windings — stays distinct from the native derivation of the sixfold. The correspondence is offered, not proved, and it needs no proof to do its work: the essay's count and the surface's count meet at one architecture.

Return with retained travel is the covering projection's exact gift. Follow the loop `γ(t)=[2t,−t]`, `0≤t≤1`, where brackets name the equivalence class. It begins and ends at the same surface address `[0,0]`, while its lift in the plane runs from `(0,0)` to `(2,−1)`: two turns in one direction, one in the other, retained. A deformation that keeps the loop based cannot erase the pair; the fundamental group `π₁(T²)=ℤ×ℤ` records the two independent winding classes, and the pair records the class — the winding, never the speed, every bend, or the traveller's experience. Local closure and global displacement coexist; return does not mean retracing. Even on one torus the law of motion decides what return means: the linear flow `[αt,βt]` closes exactly only when a single `T` makes both `Tα` and `Tβ` integral, so rational slopes are periodic, irrational slopes wind densely forever, and a bounded space need not produce a periodic trajectory. The sphere supplies the contrast that keeps the image honest: every loop on a sphere contracts, and a sphere still supports circulation — the error the essay's uroboric image names is the presentation of local closure as though nothing exceeded the account, never the mere use of a round figure. And the witness leaves the diagram for the laboratory. A tokamak holds its plasma in a toroidal chamber because toroidal and poloidal magnetic components combine into helical field lines along which charged particles travel, staying off the walls: organised circulation on this geometry can make sustained confinement possible under specified dynamics — a physical articulation of retained circulation with its own laws, not a proof that the world is doughnut-shaped.

Sound also needs a body whose conditions matter. For an ideal string fixed at both ends, a standing mode has the form `y(x,t)=A sin(nπx/L)cos(ωt)`, with `ω=nπv/L`: length, wave speed and boundary conditions determine the admitted modes. Altering the support or moving from a string to a plate changes the problem and its pattern. Cymatic figures can therefore disclose a powerful relation between excitation and form without becoming a universal stencil of QL; their force as examples comes from a specific medium answering to a specific disturbance. Under declared boundary conditions a sounded dynamic and a visible nodal pattern become different measurements of one physical process — a real physical neighbour for later nāda, Vāk and Expression comparisons, whose conscious, symbolic and physical registers keep their distinct operations.[^s3-waves]

Whitehead's grammar then says what the section has been approaching: how an achievement finishes without forfeiting its effect. An actual occasion, in his process account, unifies the many it inherits into one determinate satisfaction and perishes as subject while remaining objectively available — its achieved form enters the data of further becoming. The occasion's end is real; so is its availability. The musical development the essay inherits makes the same allowance acoustically: an accordion's alternating airflow can sustain a phrase through each change of direction, and the phrase can still reach its end — fulfilment includes a genuine telos, and the silence after it. A remainder may invite another movement; it does not invalidate the movement completed. Winding and interval have carried return in two exact forms, each keeping a determinate difference through local closure. The field in which such returns are possible at all now asks to be named — and so does the question of what becomes of the one who has followed them.

### M30 — The Arche-Topos

Two circle charts prepare the naming. On the unit circle, omit the north pole and set `u=x/(1−y)`; omit the south pole and set `v=x/(1+y)`. Each coordinate is valid on its admitted domain, and on the overlap they agree through `v=1/u` — at the point `(3/5,4/5)`, one reports `u=3`, the other `v=1/3`. The reports disagree numerically because they use different coordinates and agree in what they describe; the declared transformation identifies their common referent. Motion tests the agreement: from `du/dt=2` the transition gives `dv/dt=−2/9`, and both inverse coordinates recover the same `dy/dt=6/25`. Charts, domains, overlap rules, declared transitions — that is an atlas, and no single chart covers the whole: a compact circle, sphere or torus cannot be covered by one coordinate system onto an open Euclidean domain. The continuous image of a compact surface is compact; a chart's image is an open subset of a Euclidean domain; and no nonempty open subset of such a domain is compact. A whole picture of the surface remains possible; being a picture does not make it a global chart. The atlas realises unity through crossings — enter a chart, cross the overlap, learn what changed, carry the route — rather than by installing one image every piece must reproduce.

The jigsaw has been waiting for this since the world-picture diagnosis at the essay's threshold, and it returns as the whole action it always was. A puzzle begins with a useful promise: the lid's picture orients every piece, and the work under that promise is exact, patient and productive — edges first, colour-sorts, each successful fit confirming a little of the anticipated whole. The finished picture is an achievement, and the traveller who carries it into the world meets what no piece could repair: a road continuing past the edge, a building with another side, a view whose distortion changes with position. Adding pieces will not help, because the difficulty was never a missing piece; it is the projection criterion itself. The change comes when the maker begins to record how each view was made — from where, over what domain, with which omissions, by what rule another view can meet it. Pieces become local charts. Joins become transition rules, and an honest join may preserve a disagreement or a gap where the translation has not been earned. The lid remains useful as one projection within the atlas; it loses only the authority to decide in advance what the traveller can encounter. Returning to the building does not undo the journey: the façade is still recognisable, and it now belongs with the route around it and the previously unseen side. The traveller can put the pieces down. Mystery, in this image, never meant a permanent failure to finish.

Arche-Topos names the field these operations presuppose. It is the relating through which centre, circumstance, observer, object, path and horizon become mutually locatable — the placing already at work whenever a placed view is taken, not a concealed location where all views would coincide, since such a location would itself be another position. Every exact carrier of this section has been an operation within it: the square whose interior grew around a surviving edge; the parent that carries a complete body in each phase; the mark whose return keeps its crossing; the chart whose transitions hold disagreeing reports to one world; the winding that closes locally while keeping its displacement; the interval that completes a span without erasing the path. No single item carries the field; the coordination is the essay's own derived synthesis, generated by these operations rather than inferred from a resemblance among triangles, circles and doughnuts.

Because its architecture is positioned before the usual division of psyche and matter, the essay names the field psychoid — and the naming is Argued where the field is Derived. The order is one of dependence, not of clocks: before psyche and matter can be compared they must become distinguishable within one field of appearing and relation, and that field conditions both without granting either final priority. Subject conditions appearing; objective internality gives its constituted means; objects become determinate within the relation. The private ego is itself one determination in the field and cannot take the source-position. Mathematics gives the claim an unusually public body — a construction can be repeated by another person, a failed inference identified, an invariant checked across a change of description — yet the construction, its object and its correction remain moments of knowing, not an object supplying the entire condition of its own appearance. The Subject argument bears the ontological claim; the mathematical constructions articulate operations within it; whether a measurable mechanism links particular psychic and particular physical events is a further empirical question that this priority neither settles nor requires. Jung and Pauli supply the question's own modern destination — number as candidate border object between psyche and physis — and §4 will follow it into the life that undergoes such a form.

The refractive image keeps the source-relation visible as the field passes into use. Light arrives through differently composed panes, held apart and together by their joins, casting a changing projection in the room: Bimba names the Original, QL the ordered spectrum immanent to the light, MEF the refractive operation of media and angles, pratibimba the situated reflection. Following a changed image back through its conditions distinguishes a reflection from a picture that claims to have produced its own light. For an instrument the consequence is practical: its output should remain related to the sources, selections and transformations that made it possible, with another participant able to see what can be retraced, what recomputed, where interpretation rather than reversible map crossed the seam. A system that merely keeps producing answers has not thereby performed return; it needs a way for the achieved determination to alter, or cease to command, the next act.

The mathematics of this section has given that thought its positive substance: an invariant together with its transformation, a chart together with its domain, a quotient together with its lift, a completion together with the relation it preserves.
