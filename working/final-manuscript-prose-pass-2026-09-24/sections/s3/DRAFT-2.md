# §3 — Mathematical Substrate

<a id="s3-m25"></a>
## M25 — A distinction learns to return

Begin with a centre `O=(0,0)` and a radius to `N=(0,1)`. A second radius to `E=(1,0)` establishes an angle; the further stations `W=(−1,0)` and `S=(0,−1)` make the opposed directions available. The native acquisition order is **N, E, W, S**. The move from E to W crosses the diameter; it is not the next quarter-turn around the circumference. N, E and W bound a triangle. Adding S and joining the boundary in the order N–E–S–W–N produces a square. The former edge E–W becomes an interior diagonal, shared by the complementary triangles N–E–W and S–E–W. What previously bounded the figure now articulates its interior.

The triangle's interior angles sum to 180 degrees; the square's to 360 degrees. In these coordinates their areas are respectively one and two. Side count, angle sum, area and order of acquisition remain different descriptions of the construction. The gain is particularly clear in the changed office of E–W: the earlier relation survives, but its place in the whole changes. The centre, the cardinal stations and the circumference cannot consequently be treated as six interchangeable points. The centre orients the construction; the circumference carries its completed circuit; the four stations articulate positions within it.

Even the simplest judgment already performs more than its words display. To say that this figure is a triangle is to predicate a form of something held identifiable through the judgment. If the figure changes colour, we can still ask whether it is the same figure; if we draw another triangle, we can recognise the form without confusing the two instances. Identification holds an instance across differences, while predication makes a difference sayable. Both require a circumstance in which the comparison is possible. The statement, the instance and the field of comparison are thus distinguishable moments of one determining act. Putting them in separate boxes would still depend on the relating that lets the boxes be compared. This is the copula's work beneath the written “is.”[^s3-copula] The typed sixfold already counted at the end of the preceding section meets the same construction as work: a difference becomes definite, that determination can be repeated, and its result enters the conditions of another act. Dia-ballein `(+1)/(−1)` and sym-ballein `(0/1)/(1/0)` leave the practical difference already in play—whether the terms pretend to have supplied their own existence, or the relation through which they became distinguishable is retained—and mathematical notation becomes useful here because these are different operations, and because an operation can remain exact when its interpretation changes.

Before the terms have been assigned, QL writes their capacity for relation as `/ = −/−`. The two strokes on either side are not yet the values negative one and negative one. They mark places that can become determinate together. The first articulation, `0/1`, gives the relation its conscious circumstance: awareness and what appears. Here `0` names the unobjectifiable One; `1` names All, the manifest field in which an indefinite particular can be selected. The mark depends on its source for being and coherence. The source becomes articulated and knowable through the mark. These directions of dependence belong to one relation, but they do different work. A particular figure can disclose that relation without becoming the whole field or an image of awareness standing behind it.[^s3-native]

A figure becomes available for judgment before the judgment is settled. “What is this?” acknowledges that something has appeared sufficiently to be questioned; “this is a triangle” answers while remaining answerable to the figure. The next determination, `?/!`, holds questioning and asserting together: without the assertion that there is something to ask about, the question loses its object; without the possibility of being questioned, the assertion loses its accountability. Determination also has force. Attention gathers around a feature and withdraws from others; a line is extended here rather than there. QL writes this differentiating movement as `−/+`. The signs acquire direction through their shared axis; their distinction can be retained as polarity, or their magnitudes can be added and cancelled—the latter a possible calculation that becomes a mistaken account of the whole act when cancellation erases the axis through which the signs were opposed. The retained relation makes selection possible without declaring everything unselected unreal. Selection becomes recognisable through recurrence. We identify another triangle because this instance can express a form that is not exhausted by its particular colour, size or position. In `X/x`, capital `X` is determining capacity and lower-case `x` its indefinite particular — authorial QL notation for that relation, not Jung’s. The same letter keeps their relation visible; the difference of case prevents either from replacing the other. This capacity is not an inventory containing every already-drawn triangle. It is what lets a new instance be recognised and lets an apparent instance fail the test. Recurrence therefore carries both continuity and correction.

The judgment is also someone's judgment. `AM/IS` brings first-person presence into relation with third-person sayability through the second-person encounter: I am here, this is what I take the figure to be, and you can answer. The means of knowing belongs within this act alongside knower and known. Context includes the figure, our positions, the task, the available comparisons and what a mistaken judgment would do. In the original Gebser development, becoming contextual means caring for the outcome from within the situation. Adding more information to an aloof description would leave that participation concealed.[^s3-context]

The personed reading is a further, argued operation. First-, second- and third-person perspectives can be taken in sequence. Context makes all three available in one recognising act without installing a fourth spectator outside them. At `#4`, their claims to exclusive validity are voided; at `#5`, their belonging to one act is recognised; at `#0`, that achieved unity returns to `0/1`, the ground–appearance relation it never escaped. Three perspectives are answered by three operations of recognition: `3:3`. The latter three constitute one recognition: `3:1`. The geometric change makes the recontextualisation visible; its angle sum does not prove this account of the Subject. The derivation begins in personed experience, before its later physical, mental and musical refractions.

Exactness enters the context without claiming to contain it. In `∞/dx`, the indefinite horizon meets a local differential. Calculus provides a precise instance of what locality retains and what it cannot recover by itself. The functions `F(x)=x²+C`, for every constant `C`, have the same derivative `F′(x)=2x`. Integrating `2x` therefore returns a family. A condition such as `F(0)=3` selects one member. The derivative was exact throughout; what it lacked was the information needed to select the additive constant. QL reads this dependence as a demand that local determination remain related to its provenance, rather than as a defect to be cured by less precise calculation.[^s3-calculus]

The final turn, `1/0`, directs the achieved determination toward its condition. Ordinary arithmetic cannot divide one by zero. The native notation keeps this limit visible: the ground is not another measurable object against which the finished mark could be proportioned. Recognition changes the mark's relation to what makes it possible. It can preserve the achieved judgment, correct it or release the inquiry; none of these acts manufactures awareness as its result.

The traversal can now be compressed:

$$
\text{−/−}\;\longrightarrow\;0/1\;\longrightarrow\;?/!\;\longrightarrow\;\text{−/+}\;\longrightarrow\;X/x\;\longrightarrow\;AM/IS\;\longrightarrow\;\infty/dx\;\longrightarrow\;1/0.
$$

These are eight determinations of one passage. The parent relation and return bracket six qualitative determinations; they do not turn the six-position QL body into an eight-position body. That body can itself be constructed. A two-term alphabet `B={0,1}` gives two singles and four ordered pairs. Retaining both sorts gives `B ⊔ (B×B)`, with cardinality `2+2²=6`. The four paired articulations and the two whole-bearing poles yield `4+2`. The arithmetic counts specified objects. Their qualitative articulation has been earned through the act of knowing just followed.

## M26 — The sixfold reads itself

Once the body can hold a distinction together with its self-relations, it can read its own parts from more than one orientation. This is stronger than turning a printed sequence around. We must distinguish a position in the body from the content that comes to occupy it.

Begin with the four explicate positions, whose outer binary contents are `(0/0, 0/1, 1/0, 1/1)`, held between the singles `0` and `1`. The pure contents, `0/0` and `1/1`, occupy positions 1 and 4; the mixed contents, `0/1` and `1/0`, occupy positions 2 and 3. The processual source distinguishes the corresponding Structure and Formality pairings, with the two implicate poles retaining the Essence axis. Now read the parent relation through its internal expansion. For `(a/b)/(c/d)`, relate the first numerator entry to each denominator entry, then do the same for the second: `(a/c, a/d, b/c, b/d)`. Retain the two singles beside these four relations. Each parent thereby carries a complete body:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=(0/1)/(1/0)
  &&\rightsquigarrow (0/1,\;0/0,\;1/1,\;1/0)\quad+[0,1],\\
P'&=(1/0)/(0/1)
  &&\rightsquigarrow (1/0,\;1/1,\;0/0,\;0/1)\quad+[1,0].
\end{aligned}
$$

The square brackets retain the singles as a different sort from the four paired contents. The expansion arrows state the native reading rule; these expressions are not arithmetic fractions awaiting cancellation.[^s3-parents]

Compare the positions with what each now holds. In the outer body, positions 1 and 4 carried pure contents; in the expanded parent they carry mixed contents. Positions 2 and 3, formerly mixed, now carry pure contents. Structure and Formality exchange office across that position-to-content reading. The Essence relation remains the axis through which the body is held. Changing from P to P′ then reverses the orientations of the complete expansion, including its singles. A reversal by itself would preserve the distinction between the pure pair and the mixed pair; it would not explain their exchange of office. The substantive result requires the nested comparison as well as the phase reversal.

Two other readings cross this body. Its adjacent harmonics pair `(0,1)`, `(2,3)` and `(4,5)`: Being, Becoming, and Knowing/unKnowing. Its complementary folds pair `(0,5)`, `(1,4)` and `(2,3)`: Essence, Constitution and Text-Texture in the qualitative account. The shared middle pair does not make the partitions identical. The harmonic reading follows the passage from availability through movement to recognition; the complementary reading brings the beginning into relation with return, definition with context, and force with pattern. A locally used name such as Structure must therefore retain the pairing and register that gives it its work.

The First Spanda gives this self-relation a process. Its starting `0=(0/0)` names the unselected relational capacity in native syntax. Emanation reads the ground through its first distinction; reversion reads the distinction through the ground. Keeping the actual nesting gives

$$
T_0:\quad \frac{0}{(0/1)}\rightsquigarrow\frac01,
\qquad
T_1:\quad \frac{(1/0)}{0}\rightsquigarrow\frac10.
$$

The two tracks make explicit what the parent `(0/1)/(1/0)` holds together. Their bridge is

$$
\left(\frac10+\frac01\right)_{\mathrm{QL}}\rightsquigarrow\frac11=1=100\%.
$$

Printed as one native chain, without dropping a slash:

$$
0 \;\longrightarrow\; 0/0 \;\longrightarrow\; (0/1)/(1/0) \;\longrightarrow\; T_0 \;\longrightarrow\; T_1 \;\longrightarrow\; \bigl(1/0 + 0/1\bigr)_{\mathrm{QL}} \;\longrightarrow\; 1/1 \;\longrightarrow\; 1 = 100\%.
$$

The plus is the native composition mark. The later harmonisation writes `∨` to distinguish this from fraction addition; its Boolean companion below supplies one exact carrier, not a replacement for the native tracks. Evaluating its terms as ordinary real-number fractions would destroy the expression before its work began. The achieved One is the relation recognised as whole; it is not simply the positive pole with the other pole deleted. Emanation and reversion are correlative readings. Their exposition in sequence does not require one process to finish before the other exists.[^s3-spanda]

A small categorical construction makes the retained difference explicit. Take two objects, A and B, an arrow `f:A→B` and its inverse `g:B→A`. Their composites satisfy `g∘f=id_A` and `f∘g=id_B`. There are two objects and four arrows: two identities and two crossings. Both objects belong to one connected structure, and the return recovers the identity appropriate to its starting object. The structure achieves unity without identifying A and B as the same object. This is a precise carrier for the relation's two poles and four articulations.

The later Boolean companion works differently. Let a binary state alternate according to `s(n+1)=1−s(n)`. Its two-step windows are `01` and `10`; their componentwise join is `01∨10=11`. Each coordinate has been affirmed across the complementary windows. Writing this result as the slash-form `1/1` makes a declared change of representation, from a bit word to the native proportional reading. As a binary integer, `11` is three, not one. Nor does the join retain which window came first. It displays completeness of the pair's coordinate coverage, while the ordered process must be retained elsewhere.

There is also a linear construction that preserves both unity and orientation. Represent the two windows by `b=(0,1)` and `p=(1,0)`. Their sum `u=(1,1)` is unchanged by swapping coordinates; their difference `d=(1,−1)` changes sign. Both are needed to recover the original pair:

$$
b=\frac{u-d}{2},\qquad p=\frac{u+d}{2}.
$$

The invariant alone cannot reconstruct what was distinguished. Invariant and changing difference together can. Under the swap, `u` remains fixed and `d` changes sign, so the exchange of the original windows is recoverable from their two channels. These constructions disclose the First Spanda's middle through different exact operations: invertible crossing, complementary coverage, and recovery from invariant plus phase. None requires ordinary division by zero. Each makes visible why the achieved whole need not erase its two readings. A completed act can consequently become a datum for another act: what was an achieved result takes the role of an inherited condition. The change from result to ground is a change of recursive office, not the numerical equation `1=0`. Here the sixfold begins to retain history rather than merely repeat a shape.

The Second Spanda asks the completed sixfold to account for itself. Let `F` be its six-position set. A binary assignment to every position is an element of `B^F`; there are `2⁶=64` such assignments. An ordered pair of positions is an element of `F×F`; there are `6²=36` such addresses. Retaining these as tagged collections yields

$$
|B^F\sqcup(F\times F)|=64+36=100.
$$

The state assignment and the relational address are different kinds of object. Calling the total 100 percent normalises it to one whole; it does not identify the number one with a cardinality of one hundred. The 64 and 36 can be expressed as percentage shares of that declared total.[^s3-accounting]

Comparing the two counts is another operation:

$$
\frac{64}{36}=\frac{16}{9}=\frac{2^4}{3^2}.
$$

The ratio displays four binary factors against two ternary factors. Dividing the sum by four would instead give 25. The passage from total count to proportion must therefore remain visible. Horizontal traversal is `3:3`; vertical determination is `4:2`; the typed body remains `4+2` from `2+2²=6`; personed recognition reads the same field as `3:1`. These are different readings, not numerical identities. The `3:3` partition also has exact effects: the six binary choices split into two triples, giving `8×8` assignments, while the six-by-six address array splits into four three-by-three blocks. The same sets support different factorisations without becoming different sets.

The comparison of `4:2` with `3:3` requires equal care. QL's harmonic cross-reading is componentwise:

$$
H\bigl((4,2),(3,3)\bigr):=(4/3,\;2/3).
$$

It yields the fourth `4/3` and the reciprocal of the fifth `2/3`; inverting the latter gives `3/2`. Ordinary division of the two scalar ratios would yield `(4/2)/(3/3)=2`. From the declared harmonic reading, the relations follow by ordinary arithmetic:

$$
(4/3)^2=16/9,\qquad
(4/3)(3/2)=2,\qquad
\frac{3/2}{4/3}=\frac{2}{16/9}=9/8.
$$

This is how the two orientations enter the musical proportions. Their earlier subject-level meanings remain their source; the ratios give that architecture another exact reading. A numerical result alone would not supply the first-person recognition that `3:3` and `3:1` articulate.

The Third Spanda adds a phase-sensitive accounting. A singled-out actualisation, the 64 assignments, and a two-phase copy of the 36 relational addresses give `1+64+72=137`. These are separately tagged offices: the actualisation is the achieved whole, not an extra binary configuration omitted from the 64. The extra phase tag is an explicit addition to the address space; reversing an already counted pair does not by itself double that space. Its companion compression, `2⁷+3²=137`, is arithmetically equal but partitions the total differently. The fuller derivation and the relation `36·(16/9)=64`, `64·(9/8)=72` remain together in the technical return. No identification with a physical constant follows from the integer count.[^s3-third]

A sixfold can grow in two further ways. A developed field and its conjugate may generate six new relations by pairing corresponding positions. Criterion and Distinction, for example, produce Criterion-through-Distinction: a criterion selects which differences count, while encountering a consequential difference can require the criterion to change. The generated relation does work that neither isolated term performs. The complete exemplar runs from Continuity-in-Indeterminacy through Criterion-through-Distinction, Delineation-through-Difference, Arbitration-in-Crisis and Con-text-through-Diaphaneity to Resolution-in-Reconciliation. This is the native `6/6′→6+6′`: six generated relations, not twelve concatenated labels.[^s3-growth]

Distributive recursion asks a different question. Each of the six positions may itself develop a complete local sixfold, giving `6→6×6`. Thirty-six addresses are available immediately, but the content at each address still has to earn its place. The first growth mode articulates the relation between conjugate wholes; the second develops the whole within each position. Their composition can make a richer field, while neither obliges every subject to have six parts.

The same distinction between naming and doing matters inside the native body. Truth, Mind, Word, Logos, Son and Image are paired with Play, Need, Sacrifice, Decision, Love and Work. A determination begins from available possibility, selects under a need, gives something up in becoming articulate, decides, binds and produces. Both faces carry the complete constellation; one phase does not monopolise Name while the other supplies Power. A form becomes usable when its reader can enact these relations in a judgment or performance. What has been generated must now be able to move.

## M27 — The mark acquires a phase

Spencer-Brown asks what a distinction does when it re-enters the space it establishes. In the calculus of indications, calling the marked state again leaves it marked; crossing a crossing returns to the unmarked state. Writing `M` for the mark and `U` for the unmarked, the elementary reductions can be represented as `MM→M` and `M(M)→U`. Juxtaposition and enclosure perform different operations. Their distinction matters when a form returns into the space it distinguishes: a static description may now demand that the form be what its operation negates.[^s3-mark]

A Boolean example exposes the difficulty. The equation `r=1−r` has no solution in `{0,1}`: choosing either value makes the right-hand side the other. Giving the operation a next step changes the problem. In `r(n+1)=1−r(n)`, an initial zero yields `0,1,0,1,…`; an initial one gives the shifted sequence. Each step is definite because the argument and result occupy different times. Delay has supplied a domain in which the operation can run. It has not found a previously hidden Boolean fixed point.

Varela takes another route in *A Calculus for Self-Reference*: an autonomous third state enlarges the arithmetic itself, with rules governing how its expressions reduce. This is not simply a third instant inserted between zero and one. A temporal alternation, a third value and a new algebra are different responses to the difficulty. Each must show what it retains from the earlier setting and what it adds.[^s3-varela]

Kauffman's iterants give alternation an explicit algebra of observation and shift. An ordered view `[a,b]` and its shifted view `[b,a]` belong to the same repeating process. A shift operator exchanges the entries; a diagonal operator can give them opposite signs. In a real two-dimensional representation, take

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Both square to the identity, but their order matters: `Dη=−ηD`. Combining them in the order `J=ηD` gives

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad J^2=-I,\qquad J^4=I.
$$

On a vector `(x,y)`, J produces `(−y,x)`. Applying it again produces `(−x,−y)`; four applications restore the starting vector. Thus the quarter-turn represents multiplication by `i`, whereas the exchange η alone has period two. The orientation has been constructed through noncommuting operations, not inferred from alternation's visual resemblance to a circle. Kauffman's account also admits choices of representation; this construction does not need a uniqueness claim to do its work.[^s3-iterants]

The complex number `z=re^{iθ}`, with `r>0`, now separates two aspects of change. Multiplication by `e^{iφ}` changes its phase from θ to θ+φ while preserving its modulus r. Conjugation gives `re^{−iθ}`; inversion gives `r^{−1}e^{−iθ}`. They agree on the unit circle, but elsewhere inversion also changes the radius. The native inverse-phase relation can be compared with these operations only by retaining what each one changes. The word “inverse” alone does not make them identical.

An invariant can persist through movement without storing the movement. If a continuous nonzero path closes at its starting complex value, its final point does not reveal whether it went around the origin once, twice or not at all. Choose a continuous lift of its phase to the real line, and the difference between ending and starting phase records an integer multiple of `2π`. The winding number is then recoverable. It belongs to the path with its retained lift, rather than to an extra property secretly lodged in the final point. A system that claims to remember its return must keep the relevant history.[^s3-complex]

Bohm's conversation with Krishnamurti gives fidelity through change a different kind of working. Recalling a spinning wheel and a thread running straight, Bohm distinguishes thought's relation to what is from the mere possession of correct information. The conversation then makes room for error: a person can make a mistake and acknowledge it instead of persisting in it. The image's relevance lies in this active correction. The mathematical modulus makes invariance under a specified rotation exact; the thread makes answerability perceptible in the conduct of thought. Neither supplies the other's proof, and the speakers' later disagreement about a common energetic root remains a disagreement.[^s3-bohm]

A determination can return without being unchanged in every respect. Its identity may be preserved while its orientation changes; its local description may remain exact while additional history becomes necessary. The recogniser who corrects a judgment also preserves something through change, but does so by undergoing the inquiry rather than merely occupying a coordinate. The next formal question is whether the coordinate system itself can change while keeping the earlier achievement usable.

## M28 — The horizon becomes a coordinate

A failed coordinate need not mean a failed object. On the real projective line, a point is represented by a nonzero pair `[x:y]`, with nonzero scalar multiples identified. Where `y≠0`, it has the ordinary coordinate `u=x/y`. That chart cannot give a finite u to `[1:0]`. A second chart, valid where `x≠0`, uses `v=y/x`; the same point then has the perfectly definite coordinate `v=0`. On the overlap the coordinates are related by `v=1/u`. The zero belongs to the new chart. It is not the multiplicative inverse of zero in the real-number field.[^s3-projective]

Projective completion makes this change visible in the plane. The affine parallel lines `y=0` and `y=1` become, in homogeneous coordinates, `Y=0` and `Y−Z=0`. Their common projective point is `[1:0:0]`. It records their shared direction in the enlarged space. The all-zero tuple is excluded because it would specify no projective point. The construction has altered what counts as a point and the space in which lines meet; it has not overturned the original affine statement that these lines have no finite intersection.

Complex projective completion gives a related construction with a different dimension. The complex plane plus its point at infinity is the Riemann sphere, `CP¹`: one complex dimension, two real dimensions. In its reciprocal chart, the former infinity is again zero. Maps can extend through this change of chart even though the extended plane is not a field in which zero has acquired a multiplicative inverse. The containing space makes an earlier limit addressable.

The bilinear, or Möbius, transformation gives the change a testable invariant. Let

$$
w(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad ad-bc\ne0.
$$

On finite points away from its pole,

$$
w(u)-w(v)=\frac{(ad-bc)(u-v)}{(cu+d)(cv+d)}.
$$

Every transformed difference acquires a determinant factor and two denominator factors. Arrange four differences as a cross-ratio, here using the convention

$$
[z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4]
=\frac{(z_3-z_1)(z_4-z_2)}{(z_3-z_2)(z_4-z_1)}.
$$

The added factors cancel between numerator and denominator. Four distinct admissible points therefore keep their cross-ratio even though their individual coordinates change. The limiting formulation includes the pole and infinity on the extended plane. This is the exact relation that survives, not an assertion that everything about a figure remains unchanged.[^s3-bilinear]

For example, `(0,1,2,3)` has cross-ratio `4/3` in this convention. Under `w(z)=(2z+1)/(z+1)`, the four points become `(1,3/2,5/3,7/4)` and their cross-ratio is still `4/3`. The transformation changes intervals while preserving a relation among relations. Native QL can meet this construction because the parent ratio of ratios already asks how two complete orientations belong together. The cross-ratio supplies a specified geometric invariant; the native parent supplies the earlier recognitive question. Neither can be substituted for the other merely because both use nested proportions.

Quantum theory provides a further, bounded instance of why the containing space matters. A pure qubit can be written as `cos(θ/2)|0⟩+e^{iφ}sin(θ/2)|1⟩`, after discarding an overall phase. Its ray is a point of `CP¹`, represented on the Bloch sphere. The relative phase is retained; the physically redundant overall phase is quotiented out. Pure states lie on the sphere and mixed states occupy the ball. This construction concerns quantum states and their equivalence, not a proof that an agent has experience or that a symbol's two terms are a qubit.[^s3-qubit]

A usable atlas requires more than a collection of viewpoints. On the unit circle `x²+y²=1`, omit the north pole to define `u=x/(1−y)` and omit the south pole to define `v=x/(1+y)`. Both coordinates are valid away from the two poles. There

$$
uv=\frac{x^2}{1-y^2}=1,
\qquad v=\frac1u.
$$

At the point `(3/5,4/5)`, the coordinates are `u=3` and `v=1/3`. Their disagreement is a rule-governed translation, not a disagreement about which point is present. The point excluded by either chart remains available through the other.

A moving point tests whether the translation does real work. Suppose `du/dt=2` at this point. Differentiating the transition gives `dv/dt=−2/9`. The two speeds have different values and opposite signs because the charts orient and scale the motion differently. Recover the height through either inverse coordinate:

$$
y=\frac{u^2-1}{u^2+1}
  =\frac{1-v^2}{1+v^2}.
$$

Both descriptions give `dy/dt=6/25`. The transition thus lets differently written motions answer to one moving object. To call a set of charts an atlas, we need their domains and overlap maps, with the required regularity and consistent composition on shared domains. A compact circle, sphere or torus cannot be one global coordinate chart onto an open Euclidean domain: the image of a compact space would have to be compact, whereas a nonempty open Euclidean domain is not. A whole picture remains possible; its being a picture does not give it the office of a global chart. Agreement cannot be supplied by declaring all perspectives valid. Nor does chart compatibility choose a law of motion; we supplied the velocity separately.[^s3-atlas]

There is a logical counterpart to changing the containing setting, but it changes a different object. A four-valued semantics can distinguish truth-supported, falsity-supported, both-supported and neither-supported statements. Represent these by `{T}`, `{F}`, `{T,F}` and `∅`, with negation exchanging T and F and designated values containing T. Give p the value `{T,F}` and q the value `∅`: p and its negation are designated while q is not. Contradictory information need not entail every conclusion in this semantics. Its rules, rather than the mere existence of a fourth label, do the work. The neither-supported truth-value is still a value in the system, not the unobjectifiable condition through which anyone can use it. Priest's further “fifth-corner” proposal and the objections to it concern that additional crossing; they are not settled by counting to five.[^s3-logic]

These constructions preserve a demanding form of continuity. An earlier description can remain true within its domain while ceasing to define the entire field. The new setting must show where it includes that domain, how descriptions translate and what formerly unavailable operation becomes possible. The move from perspective to context has acquired this formal body. Its human consequence is equally exacting: acknowledging another situation means making the passage to it answerable, including where translation fails, rather than adding its name to a collection of views.

## M29 — A return can retain its distance

The circle's overlapping charts preserve an object across descriptions. A quotient surface makes another kind of return exact: a path can arrive at the same surface-address without ending where its lifted journey began. Start with the plane and identify points displaced by whole units in either coordinate:

$$
(x,y)\sim(x+m,y+n),\qquad m,n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The quotient `T²=R²/Z²` is a torus. One unit square presents the entire surface if opposite sides are identified. Crossing an edge continues from the corresponding opposite edge; the edge is a seam in the presentation, not a boundary of the resulting surface.[^s3-torus]

Consider the path `γ(t)=[2t,−t]`, for `0≤t≤1`, where brackets denote the equivalence class. It begins and ends at `[0,0]`. Its lift in the plane begins at `(0,0)` and ends at `(2,−1)`. The two integers record two turns in one direction and a reverse turn in the other. A continuous deformation that keeps the loop based cannot erase that pair. The fundamental group `π₁(T²)=Z×Z` records these independent winding classes. It retains a definite aspect of travel, although not its speed, every bend or the traveller's experience. Bergson’s question presses exactly here: how an earlier phase remains active in a later one without being laid out as a spatial inventory of interchangeable units. The covering plane can host such an inventory; duration cannot be repaired by becoming one. The lift retains winding; it does not convert the journey into a row of simultaneous points.

The relationship between the infinite cover and the compact quotient is exact. Every surface point has infinitely many lattice-related representatives in the plane. The covering plane is not a second surface hidden inside the torus, and the torus is not literally infinite in area. Nor is this universal cover the torus's separate two-sheeted covering of the Klein bottle. Those maps identify different things. Conflating their sheets would lose the very winding that makes the return useful.

The native `4+2` reading has a declared place here. Four sides present the square; two generators articulate the independent winding directions after gluing. Sides and generators do different jobs. They are not the six cells of a torus: the usual quotient cell structure has one vertex, two edges and one face, with Euler characteristic `1−2+1=0`. The native coordination brings presentation and circulation together without renaming their combined count as a topological invariant. The opening in the familiar embedded torus likewise makes the need to keep a place unfilled perceptible; no consciousness is proved by the genus of a surface.

A sphere supplies a real contrast, not an inferior kind of world. Every loop on a sphere can contract to a point, while the torus has noncontractible winding classes. Both surfaces can support circulation. The difference is what a loop may retain under deformation. In the native uroboric image, the mistake is consequently the presentation of a local closure as though nothing exceeded its account, not the mere use of a spherical figure. A faithful sphere and an unfaithful torus-diagram are both possible.

Even on one torus, the law of motion changes what return means. For the linear flow `[αt,βt]`, an exact return requires a positive time T for which both `Tα` and `Tβ` are integers. Two nonzero rates with irrational ratio cannot meet that requirement. A bounded space need not produce a periodic trajectory. Compactness, periodicity and the possibility of winding are distinct properties; the dynamics decide which available paths are realised.

Music gives the retained interval an audible rather than topological operation. In just ratios, a fourth multiplies frequency by `4/3` and a fifth by `3/2`. Their composition reaches the octave because `(4/3)(3/2)=2`. Two fourths reach `16/9`, leaving the exact multiplicative complement `9/8`:

$$
\frac43\;\frac98\;\frac43=2,
\qquad
\frac{16}{9}\;\frac98=2.
$$

The intervening whole tone completes the span; it is not a defect preventing completion. These are the proportions generated by the Second Spanda's cross-reading. The composition gives each interval its place in a larger consonance without making the intervals identical.[^s3-music]

Another return makes a different demand. Twelve pure fifths do not equal seven octaves:

$$
\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^7}=\frac{531441}{524288}\ne1.
$$

That residual is the Pythagorean comma, not the whole tone `9/8`. Twelve-tone equal temperament distributes adjustment through the octave by using steps of `2^{1/12}`. Its twelve steps close exactly, while its fifth approximates rather than equals `3/2`. An instrument can therefore achieve a working cyclic closure by changing the intervals it preserves. The question is which relationship the tuning serves and what adjustment it makes audible, rather than whether closure is possible at all.[^s3-tuning]

Pitch class and pitch height distinguish return from ascent in another way. Map a positive frequency f to `log₂(f/f₀) mod 1`. Octave-related frequencies occupy the same point on the pitch-class circle, while a lift to the real logarithmic coordinate retains their register difference. One pitch-class phase gives a circle. A toroidal model requires two independently specified periodic coordinates; it cannot be obtained merely by calling octave recurrence circular twice.

Sound also needs a body whose conditions matter. For an ideal string fixed at both ends, a standing mode has the form `y(x,t)=A sin(nπx/L)cos(ωt)`, with `ω=nπv/L`. Length, wave speed and boundary conditions determine the admitted modes. Altering the support or moving from a string to a plate changes the problem and its pattern. This is why cymatic figures can disclose a powerful relation between excitation and form without becoming a universal stencil of QL. Their force as examples comes from the specific medium answering to a specific disturbance.[^s3-waves]

The original musical development also permits the player to stop. An accordion's alternating airflow can sustain a phrase through changing direction; the phrase can nevertheless reach its end. Breathing, recurrence and instrumental construction support the performance without sentencing it to perpetual continuation. In the later correction, fulfilment includes a genuine telos and the silence after it. Whitehead’s question meets the same joint from the side of becoming: how a process reaches satisfaction so that what was forming becomes objectively available to subsequent becoming without requiring the phrase to continue forever. A remainder may invite another movement, but it does not invalidate an achieved one. The completed relation becomes available for return, inheritance or release.

The earlier limit now has a positive form. A determinate act can finish without claiming that every condition of its possibility has finished with it. Its result may retain an interval, a winding, an adjustment or a changed capacity in its receiver. The work of an account is to preserve the difference that matters to that act, not to accumulate every conceivable trace. What returns can then be recognised as a completed achievement within a world still capable of being encountered.

## M30 — The Arche-Topos

A jigsaw begins with a useful promise. The picture on the lid gives the pieces a common destination: this patch belongs to that sky, this edge completes that building. Turning a piece, trying a fit and rejecting a false join make increasingly exact sense under the promised whole. The finished picture is an achievement. Without that initial orientation, the later discovery would have little to disturb.[^s3-jigsaw]

Now the picture's maker enters the world it depicts. A road continues beyond the edge; the building looks different from the other side; a feature hidden by one viewpoint appears from another. At first the answer seems to be more pieces. But pieces added under the old criterion keep extending the same projection. What is missing is not simply another patch of scenery. It is an account of how the picture was made: from where, over what domain, with which omissions, and through which rules another view can meet it.

The pieces begin to change office. They become local charts, each exact over an admitted domain, rather than fragments guaranteed to complete one final view. Their joins must now say how one passes into another. A road's continuation, a change of scale and an occluded face make different demands upon that passage. An honest join may preserve a disagreement or a gap where the translation has not been earned. The lid remains useful as one projection within the atlas. Its local success no longer gives it authority to decide in advance what the traveller can encounter.

Returning to the building does not undo the journey. The original façade is still recognisable, but it now belongs with the route around it and the previously unseen side. The torus supplied an exact formal witness to one aspect of such return: the surface-address can recur while the lifted path retains a displacement. The journey in the image carries more—revised expectation, another person's account, perhaps a reason to choose a different route. Its whole action reveals how a criterion is preserved by having its authority relocated. The mathematical example does not exhaust that action; it makes one of its relations sharply legible.

Arche-Topos names the differential field of this placing. It is not a concealed location at which all perspectives would finally coincide, since such a location would already be another position. It is the relating through which centre, circumstance, observer, object, path and horizon become mutually locatable. A determination reorganises what surrounds it; the surrounding field conditions what can be determined next. QL has followed this operation from the first distinction, through its self-relations and inverse phase, into contextual holding and return. The field is generated in the argument by those operations, not inferred from a resemblance among triangles, circles and doughnuts.[^s3-field]

The distinction between atlas and dynamics now becomes decisive. A phase-space represents possible states; a trajectory is one history through it. An attractor is an invariant organisation toward which an appropriate set of trajectories tends, and its basin identifies the starting conditions with that tendency. A bifurcation changes the regime of possible motion. Charts describe such objects locally and translate their descriptions; changing a chart does not by itself change the system. If a symbol changes the interpreter's expectations, attachments or available responses, however, the later trajectory can change. The difference between a better description and a transformed life must remain visible.

Taylor's encounter with Van Eenwyk turns on this active relation. The notes follow perception beyond an incoming stimulus into past experience and expectation, then follow the symbol from familiar consciousness into an unknown implication and back. The resulting *Symbolon Dynamics* distinguishes the ordering capacity, the particular manifestation and the response that alters their next encounter. Amplification can move among images as among local disclosures; the symbol's effect on the person changes what will be available to disclose. The native `X/x` therefore enters psychology as a prior relation with a new burden: to show how recurrent form and a singular life belong together without treating the life as a replaceable example.[^s3-dynamics]

The psychoid relation follows from the place this differentiation occupies. Before psyche and matter can be compared, they must become distinguishable within one field of appearing and relation. That field is prior to their division in the order of dependence, not an earlier event on a physical clock. Subject conditions appearing; objective internality gives its constituted means; objects become determinate within their relation. The private ego is itself such a determination and cannot take the source-position. The Arche-Topos articulates this already-argued dependence as the field through which psychic and material positions become possible together.

Mathematics gives the relation an unusually public body. A construction can be repeated by another person, a failed inference can be identified, and an invariant can survive a change of description. Its form is therefore not exhausted by a private association. Yet the construction, its object and its correction remain moments of knowing, rather than an object supplying the entire condition of its own appearance. The psychoid claim keeps these two facts in one account: psychic and material determinations have a common originating relation, while their particular manifestations require different investigations. The Subject argument bears the ontological claim; the mathematical constructions articulate operations within it. A measured causal mechanism linking a particular psychic event and a particular physical event would be a further result, not something already furnished by this priority.[^s3-dependence]

The refractive image keeps the source relation visible. Light arrives through differently composed panes, held apart and together by their joins, and gives a changing projection within a room. The native appointments are specific: Bimba names the Original; QL names the ordered spectrum immanent to the light; MEF names the refractive operation of media, angles and joins; pratibimba names the situated reflection. Changing the sun's angle, the pane or the viewer changes the image. Following those changes back through their conditions distinguishes a reflection from a picture that claims to have produced its own light. This is the image's argument, not an identification of consciousness with electromagnetic radiation.[^s3-refraction]

For an instrument, the consequence is practical. Its output should remain related to the sources, selections and transformations that made it possible. Another participant needs to know what can be retraced, what can be recomputed and where an interpretation rather than a reversible map has crossed the seam. Some results should be retained; some should be corrected; some inquiries should be released. A system that merely keeps producing answers has not thereby performed return. It needs a way for the achieved determination to alter, or cease to command, the next act. Whether a particular implementation provides that capacity is an operational question, not an accomplishment conferred by the vocabulary.

The native shorthand now carries two related but distinct readings. The defining circuit returns to the relation it articulated:

$$
0/1\;\longrightarrow\;4+2\;\longrightarrow\;(5\!\to\!0)\;\longrightarrow\;0/1.
$$

The processual traversal also unfolds the returned relation from its other phase:

$$
0/1\to4+2\to(5\to0)\to1/0\to4'+2'\to(5'\to0')\to0/1.
$$

The prime marks the conjugate passage, not an extra pair of substances. Each parent contains a whole body; the inverse reading changes the offices its contents occupy. An achieved return can become the condition of a further inquiry, while the actual inquiry that achieved it is allowed to be complete. This is why recognition cannot be replaced by a requirement that everything run again.

In the original note “Maths and the Mystery are One,” exact definition and questionability belong to one task. The mystery is what calls for an account and what keeps the account's conditions from disappearing behind its result. The mathematics has given that thought positive substance: an invariant together with its transformation, a chart together with its domain, a quotient together with its lift, a musical completion together with the relation it preserves. None becomes less exact by admitting how it is situated.[^s3-mystery]

The traveller can put the pieces down. The picture has not become worthless, nor must it be made to contain the journey. It can be carried as something achieved, among other things still to be encountered. The remaining question is what happens when such a form is met in a dream, an image or an event that changes the life by which it is recognised. Mathematics has made the passage distinguishable. The psyche must now be allowed to undergo it.
