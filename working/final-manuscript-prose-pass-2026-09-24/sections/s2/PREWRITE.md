## A. Six-movement dependency chain

**M19 — Difference Cannot Be Escaped.**
Incoming pressure: §1 returned zero with an acquired mathematical life and left a seam anticipated (token, count, renewed entrustment). The recovered zero makes "binary logic" ambiguous: two honest answers to "what comes first" now coexist.
Operation: work the necessity of difference from inside an act (judgment, refusal, perception each require a difference), then the deeper owned premise — comparability precedes opposition; opposed terms need the shared respect in which they oppose. From the numerical floor derive the Two Ones: `0 = One = Self-Identity`, `1 = All = Self-Difference`; reciprocal dependence in distinct respects (constituting vs manifesting — not two causes waiting for each other). The lived case: a person correcting an earlier conviction needs both continuity and change; neither alone is correction. The Other within common reality is not inside my account. Monopoly names the local formation that claims to originate its own conditions. Trust enters as already-enacted dependence beneath every act of knowing.
Achieved consequence: every binary has two provenances — two complete, non-equivalent accountings of two.
Next necessity: the classical accounting governs modern formal and technical systems, so it must be given first and at full strength.

**M20 — Dia-Ballein.**
Incoming pressure: two accountings exist; decision, boundary and classification cannot wait for a reconciliation that has not happened.
Operation: derive dia from the bare stroke `/ = −/−`; signs give `(-1)/(+1)` with zero retained as the axis of tension and connection. The collapse is a change of operation, not a falsification: addition cancels (`= 0`); each subtraction assigns the whole span to one pole (`−2` / `+2`). Three correct operations, three different objects. Severance names the cut that forgets the field and operation that produced its poles; appropriation names the promotion of a result into authority over its source. Projected distrust: knowledge that denies its provisionality exports the distrust it cannot hold onto an enemy, and the export lets the claimed `+1` perform certainty. The name/count/countenance sequence shows designation without answerability. Mass formation carries the sequence into collectives (crowd without assembly; the negligible individual and the State's borrowed face; unbound affect finding a target and a role) with Iakovou's institutional qualification constitutive: division below, pseudo-unity above, combination monopolised. Divide-and-conquer with the combine-phase erased is atomisation. Heidegger's calculative thinking gives the epochal name — a thinking that never collects itself.
Achieved consequence: dia is necessary and exact; its pathology is a specific theft (the combining office made private; the criterion made unanswerable), not the sin of distinguishing.
Next necessity: the other accounting, which retains what the cut discloses and gives the branch somewhere to return.

**M21 — Sym-Ballein.**
Incoming pressure: a branch that carries no provenance is a branch that cannot be recomposed; the cut needs its conjugate.
Operation: derive syn — `(0/1)/(1/0)`; the inner strokes hold indefinite and definite together (AND/OR: conjoined because neither exists as the relation without the other, disjoined because ground is not mark); the outer stroke relates the two orientations themselves. The symbolon gives it a material body: a token broken between parties authenticates across separation and time; the fracture retained is the proof; fusion destroys the test as surely as loss. Sym-mode knowledge offers plural, tested, mutually correcting provisional anchors — the positive answer to projected distrust. "Divine and concur" names the political difference: not a midpoint but the recovery of the third relation that makes both camps possible, with the question of who controls recomposition now exact. Technically: provenance, reversible traversal, visible alternatives, inspectable seams. The gathering has its own counterfeit — the net that gathers to arrest (promised; delivered in M22).
Achieved consequence: gathering is a real operation whose whole is `(0/1)/(1/0)`, defining towards wholeness without possessing it.
Next necessity: the logics need bodies — a whole narrative event can show what capture does and cannot do, and what retained tension generates, in time.

**M22 — Ares, Aphrodite, Harmonia, Eros, and Hephaestus.**
Incoming pressure: syn's counterfeit was named; the difference between generative relation and organised capture needs a whole action, not a gloss.
Operation: tell the whole Homeric scene — Demodocus's song before Odysseus and the Phaeacians; Helios's two reports; the forge in pain; invisible spider's-web bonds and the pretended departure; capture; the summons; asymmetric shame and unquenchable laughter; the anonymous god's gnome (τέχνῃσι; the μοιχάγρια fine); Hermes's incentive arithmetic; Poseidon's first assurance; Hephaestus's warranted objection ("wretched are the sureties taken for the wretched"); the changed pledge (personal contingent liability); release; Thrace and Paphos; payment untold. Read the apparatus's anatomy (detection/fabrication/capture/tribunal/account/incentive economy) with Ge-stell's own gathering grammar as the epochal echo — the counterfeit naming staying the essay's. Give Hesiod's distinct genealogy (Panic and Fear beside Harmonia; Cadmus; primordial Eros elsewhere in the poem). Recall the Huayan contemplation with its own limit: reflection recursively vs capture organised as evidence; the simile that declares its own inadequacy.
Achieved consequence: the apparatus can perfect capture, proof and judgment and still require a personed undertaking to conclude; retained tension can become generative (Harmonia) while the genealogy keeps Fear and Panic beside her; craft is not condemned — the same hands bind and loose.
Next necessity: a life runs the pulse more than once — what alternation, investment and changed criterion look like dynamically.

**M23 — Complex Dynamism.**
Incoming pressure: one completed circuit showed capture failing to conclude; living systems repeat the operation — bifurcation, basin, perturbation.
Operation: read the two logics as one alternating pulse (dia makes a trajectory actual; sym composes a provisional basin; perturbation can open it). ## B. Missing because-steps the old text compressed or omits

1. Why correction needs continuity AND change (same person, changed conviction) — show the two-condition structure, then let monopoly and the Other follow from it.
2. Why the classical accounting comes first: because it is the operative logic of systems that classify, rank, allocate and defend — dominance shown, not declared.
3. Why appropriation is a further act beyond correct subtraction: a zero balance does not erase transactions; a measured span does not make its endpoint the axis's source. The theft is the promotion, not the arithmetic.
4. Why authentication ≠ justice: the token proves a particular fit; it settles neither the obligation's fairness nor future conduct — hence plural anchors and renewed entrustment with altered bearer.
5. Why Poseidon's first assurance fails: the man who can depart can evade the payment; a promise about the debtor's conduct does not bind the debtor. Without Hephaestus's objection the changed pledge is unintelligible — the refusal is what makes the release rational.
6. Why the completed apparatus cannot conclude: the demand for restitution has no mechanism inside the net; a trap can hold a debtor but cannot make a debt good. This because-step must precede Poseidon's entrance.
7. The trust sequence's missing middle: uncertainty impedes action; a total explanation supplies a target AND a role; relief and belonging are invested in the supports; contradiction now threatens the supports, so it is converted into the enemy's confirmation. Make it happen to a person, not as a named list.
8. Why synchrony is virtual and not merely co-presence: a promise's possible breach is consequential before it occurs; an apology and an evasion differ before either happens. And why diachrony can transform the possibility-field: an actual discovery changes what can be entrusted afterwards.
9. Why the sixfold retains the two singles: counting only the pairings would lose what the pairings relate; relabelling six themes would not perform the derivation.
10. Why mono–poly is prior to dia/syn and trust is a distinct thread: order of operations — the two logics account for a two already produced by the prior relation; trust concerns the finite being's reliance within it. Conflating them loses both questions.
11. The A29 grain: the centre need not create every component to control the channels through which components cooperate — horizontal separation coexisting with vertically monopolised combination. Give a particular channel, not a slogan.
