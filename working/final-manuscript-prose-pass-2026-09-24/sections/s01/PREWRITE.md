**M02 — Define the Subject Without Making It an Object (the theorem).**

- Operation: perform the full theorem in order. Scope of "whole" fixed immediately: approached in self-presence, from the Subject pole; not a claim that a personality's contents are all reality. (3) Prerequisites the old text compressed: self-knowledge needs both identity and difference (a self must differ from itself in presentation to have something to recognise, remain itself for recognition to be self-knowledge); naming makes this concrete — Kripke's problem as a living problem (two people, one name; a changed name; the bearer kept across changed descriptions; transmission rather than private descriptive sufficiency), then FRANK'S development marked as his: beneath the name a counting — "this one / another / this one again" — identity proposed relative to circumstance. (11) Theological direction: the same performance from the whole; universality of reference supplies no exterior; no divinised personality. (12) Return to finite discrimination: name, history, body, obligation, the Other who can still answer.
- Achieved consequence: faithful definition — non-exhaustion can be exact; the account is binding without being final.
