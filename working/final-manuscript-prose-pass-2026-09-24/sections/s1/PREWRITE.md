## A. Six-movement dependency chain

**M13 — A Sign Migrates Between Worlds.**
Incoming pressure: §0 closed asking what changes when an absence acquires a sign another person can use. §1 answers by doing it: in 205 the middle character contributes no tens; remove it and 205 becomes 25. The absent quantity is doing work — its place keeps the hundreds from becoming tens.
Operation: perform the act, then run the history as a sequence of changed permissions, each stage changing the reader's problem. Babylonian: positional notation makes a missing power consequential; an unfilled internal column had been left to context; a scribe's internal separator makes the absence legible — while remaining internal, order of magnitude still ambiguous, no terminal digit, no arithmetic. Hellenistic astronomy: a circular mark holds an absent degree/minute/second inside a sexagesimal measure — punctuation within measurement, a free number still lacking. Indian: a word-world for space and openness (kha, ambara, ākāśa, śūnya) meets a positional practice; Gwalior's hollow mark; dust-board, dot, written procedure — mark hypotheses as hypotheses; Bakhshali dating is a historical state of scholarship, not current fact. Transmission: Baghdad, al-Khwārizmī, ṣifr; Latin adaptation; Fibonacci teaching the nine figures and the circle with tables, carrying, checking — a receiver and teacher, not a European origin hero.
Achieved consequence: zero converges distinct inventions; the same visible shape has held different offices; a sign for no counted thing changes the whole field of counting; migration between practices is constitutive, not decorative.
Next necessity: an empty place is legible but has no stated effects; what happens when it meets other quantities is still unsaid → M14.

**M14 — Śūnya Becomes Operational.**
Incoming: the migrated sign needs rules; arithmetic must say what happens when it meets a quantity.
Operation: Brahmagupta places cipher among affirmative and negative quantities and states its effects (q001): addition preserves; multiplication absorbs — of two ciphers, cipher. The two operations differ in kind: addition leaves a determination unchanged; multiplication returns any finite determination to zero (this prepares M17's collapse). Division asks a third question: division is recovery through multiplication; what multiplies zero to give one? Bhāskara (q002) retains the written form — a fraction with cipher for denominator (khahara) — and (q003) reads its invariance theologically: though many be inserted or extracted, no alteration; as no change in the infinite and immutable God at the destruction or creation of worlds, though numerous orders of beings are absorbed or put forth. The analogy is exact in one respect: invariance under finite insertion and extraction. Then the Kashmiri co-presence: in the same civilisational field, Abhinavagupta (conventional c.975–1025) articulated consciousness as self-luminous and self-differentiating — manifestation that neither enlarges nor depletes its source; Śakti as real differentiating power, the light capable of distinction. Roughly 125 years after his conventional terminal date, Bhāskara writes the Bījagaṇita (1150). No line of influence is claimed; the braid of two exact but non-identical articulations of determination and an exceeding condition is itself the philosophical claim — and it prevents the story in which metaphysics arrives only after mathematics finds a formal limit. Dutta's reconstruction: the historical regime can restrict cancellation and cross-multiplication for zero-divided expressions — a changed algebraic regime, judged by its own permissions, judged as a proposal another person may refuse.
Consequence: absence has arithmetic citizenship and an exceptional edge; mathematics and metaphysics speak source-distinct beside each other.
Next necessity: exactness now demands precision about generation — can nothing, exactly, generate one? → M15.

**M15 — The Empty Set Generates One.**
Incoming: zero acts and obstructs; but "nothing generates everything" would be a slogan unless shown under a stated law.
Operation: three distinct generativities, each under its own law. Empty product: one because one is the multiplicative identity — composition with no factor preserves composition (a zero factor does something else: it absorbs). Von Neumann: 0 = ∅; 1 = {∅}; 2 = {∅,{∅}}; successor n+1 = n ∪ {n}; membership, inclusion and identity are three relations; one contains zero as a member and is not identical to it; zero is a subset of every set and a member of each nonzero finite ordinal — not a member of every set. NOR: p ↓ q = ¬(p ∨ q); repetition yields negation, disjunction, conjunction — a functionally complete connective over a finite number of Boolean inputs; NAND is a different connective; the historical naming chain is not settled here.
Consequence: a rigorous formal floor — mathematics has exact cases in which zero participates generatively in one and the naturals; none of them supplies the essay's slash, and none identifies the formally empty object with the unobjectifiable condition of representation.
Next necessity: the essay needs its own sign for the occlusion where mediation fuses with what it mediates → M16.

**M16 — The Crossed Zero.**
Incoming: the formal floor is exact; but the relation the essay carries — the subject-pole, the mediating act, the mark — can be drawn, and can be misdrawn.
Operation: 0 → Ø. The stroke is present but fused into the zero it crosses: determination has occurred, but the act of determining has disappeared into its success; "I see the world" hides the seeing. The misreading: a determinate I that owns or produces the world. The success-criterion working: a criterion that classifies refusal as failure makes the identification consequential and hard to question; recovering the criterion changes the identification without destroying judgment or aspiration. The God-at-gap paragraph (one, compact): the gap is where the notion of God arises and what it fills — the finite account's encounter with a source it cannot install as one more determinate item; empirical gaps can close without threatening that pole, because the subject was never one missing object among others; a capacity migrating from human performance into a machine changes the objective account of mediation, it does not shrink the subject by trimming a list.
Consequence: the occlusion has a sign; the stroke has become a problem in its own right; Ø ≠ ∅.
Next necessity: what happens when a determinate quantity returns toward zero — ordinary arithmetic makes the boundary explicit → M17.

**M17 — Zero Keeps One Foot Outside Mathematics.**
Incoming: the return 1/0 inside arithmetic.
Operation: the ordinary-field proof. Assume 1/0 = x; then 0x = 1; but 0x = 0 for every field element; no such x exists; more, if z were an inverse, a = a·1 = a(0z) = (a0)z = 0z = 1 — every number collapses into one. 0/0 fails differently: 0q = 0 holds for every q; no unique recovery. Cancellation fails: 6·0 = 17·0 does not license 6 = 17. Extensions exist and each names its changed law: the projective line adjoins a point at infinity; the Riemann sphere gives complex infinity a place; wheels, meadows, transreal arithmetics trade distributivity or equality for totality. Robinson: a nonzero infinitesimal — divide it out, take the standard part afterward; that is a changed numerical setting, and it differs from substituting zero before division.
Consequence: division by zero is the point where a formal system must disclose itself; zero is entirely mathematical and at the edge of every current container; "outside" means frame-disclosing, not supernatural. At this register the immutable gap has a mathematical form — the Argued identification is the essay's own, distinct from the field-theoretic result.
Next necessity: the opening borrowed zero as a sign for what cannot be counted as one more object; the loan must now be repaid with what zero has actually acquired → M18.

**M18 — The Loan Returns.**
Incoming: the promissory note from the formal-limit opening.
Operation, in phases: (i) the acquired inventory returns — operations, prohibitions, the exceptional edge; the sign can be returned into a wider account without ceasing to be exact. (ii) Kaplan's mediant: 0/1 and 1/0 as formal boundary markers; insert (a+c)/(b+d) between neighbours; 1/1 appears between them, then 1/2 and 2/1; positive rationals ramify while the endpoints remain; the between-property holds for positive denominators; the result depends on chosen representatives (the same point has other names); 1/0 stays a boundary, a goalpost for the operation, an ordinary quotient still. (iii) The Two Ones, earned after the history: 0 = One = Self-Identity — the uncounted condition present through determination without entering its inventory; 1 = All = Self-Difference — the whole manifest and determinable field, each indefinite particular; dependence reciprocal in distinct respects (All depends on One for being; One becomes manifest through All); ordinary arithmetic's asymmetry intact — 0/1 computes, 1/0 does not. (iv) Reckoning becomes answerable: a count is exact under a criterion and can still omit; the receiver whose reply changes what the reckoning was for; examination first, renewed entrustment after. (v) Release: the root symbolon; 0/1 ⟷ 1/0 as earned notation; return keeps differentiation intact.
Release to §2: the recovered zero reveals why "binary logic" is ambiguous — which cut, and what does the cut retain? Differentiation leaves §1 with all its powers; the question that hands on is the relation those powers maintain with what makes them possible. Token: a brief edge only — M21 owns the fracture, duration, authentication.

## B. Missing because-steps the earlier text compressed

1. M13: why the internal separator was needed at all — a positional system makes every following value depend on position, so an unfilled column menaces the whole reading; and what the separator still could not do (no terminal digit; magnitude ambiguous; nothing to calculate with). The because: an empty internal place must survive reading without changing adjacent magnitudes.
2. M13→M14: a word for emptiness does not create a rule; the receiving practice confers the permission. Sanskrit vocabulary and arithmetic citizenship are different achievements; their conjunction is the Indian development.
3. M14: why multiplication's absorption is not just another rule — it is the operation M17's collapse proof will reuse (absorption is what makes an inverse impossible); teaching it here is planting the proof.
4. M14: the internal logic of Bhāskara's analogy — why invariance under insertion/extraction reaches toward theology: the khahara expression is unchanged by finite addition or subtraction, and Bhāskara names the analogue he has in view: a source neither enlarged by creation nor diminished by destruction. The analogy does not prove the arithmetic; the arithmetic does not establish the doctrine; the match is in the invariance.
5. M14: why co-presence without transmission still matters — two developed inquiries making finite-determination/exceeding-condition thinkable in exact, non-identical ways within one civilisational field; this blocks the myth that metaphysics only arrives after formalism fails, and gives the later return a historical field instead of a generic East/West analogy.
6. M15: why the empty product is 1 (identity preserved under empty composition) and why a zero factor is different (absorption) — the contrast is the teaching.
7. M15: why {∅} ≠ ∅ — the singleton has exactly one member and that member is empty; membership is the operation; the philosophical point is containment-without-identity, not "nothing made everything."
8. M16: why the fused stroke is an occlusion rather than a mere picture — the mediating act vanishes into its success; the criterion example operationalises the fusion (refusal classified as failure by the same measure cannot be questioned from inside that measure).
9. M17: the difference between the two failures — 1/0 has no candidate (contradiction with absorption); 0/0 has too many (non-uniqueness); the container must be declared differently in each case.
10. M18: why the Two Ones must arrive after the history — before the acquisitions they would be assertion; after, they are the relation the acquisitions were always carrying; and why the arithmetic asymmetry (0/1 computes, 1/0 undefined) is retained inside the native reading rather than dissolved by it.

## E. Opening choice and why it belongs here

Open with the mathematical act: 205, remove the middle character, 25. This is s1's own pressure (MUST 5.3): a sign entering human history. §0 has just asked what changes when an absence acquires a sign another person can use; the answer must occur before it is said. The removal shows absence working — the place of nothing holding the hundreds from collapsing into tens — and the whole section is the history of how that working absence acquired each of its powers. No conceptual briefing, no "return of zero" framing sentence; the sign's history does the framing. The second movement of the opening deepens the same act: the trouble an empty place makes for anyone who must preserve a number in writing, which is the historical problem that produced every later stage.
