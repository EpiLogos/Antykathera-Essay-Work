### s0-F01 — The comparative conclusion has been softened into parallel exposition

**Disposition: softened claim; comparative inference incompletely carried.**

**Current location:** M09, paragraph beginning “The traditions differ”; M10, paragraph beginning “The essay’s whole-first inference.”

**Authorial root.** In the Watson `NOTES.md`, in the sequence around the book’s page 96, your response calls for an account that argues:

The early polemical formulation is broader than the later account; its enduring demand is that dismantling the fabricated proprietor must not also dispose of the unobjectifiable experient. fileciteturn55file0L2-L2

**Mature owner.** The Indian philosophy dossier retains five pressures: memory’s relation to the earlier act; comparison’s holding of differences; exclusion’s treatment of absent alternatives; the unity of intention, execution and result; and the manifestation of arising and perishing themselves. **What has changed in the manuscript.** Much of the requisite reasoning remains. M07 already argues that continuity cannot be explained by setting isolated instants beside one another. ## 3. Voice and stance

The manuscript is predominantly spoken from within its inquiry. The call, misheard concern, bodily tension, promise and refused alternative give its abstractions something at stake. The repeated examples develop rather than merely recur: the promise becomes intention, inherited condition, contrastive determination and apportioned capacity. That continuity is an achievement.

Its first person nevertheless requires correct attribution. **“A voice calls my name” is the draft’s constructed first-person example, not a recovered autobiographical passage from your notebooks.** It should not be presented as your remembered incident. Conversely, P3 contains explicitly personal testimony concerning an AI encounter; that testimony must not be silently generalised into what every user experiences or what the artificial participant phenomenally underwent. fileciteturn130file0L2-L2 fileciteturn51file0L2-L2

There is some cooling of the original voice. P2 and P3 repeatedly ask what philosophy does to the person undertaking it: whether knowing has become knowledge without understanding, whether technique has displaced its end, whether language can lead into recognition rather than occupy its place. Their intimacy and urgency are less audible in the current section.

That does not make every calmer sentence a fidelity loss. The manuscript has gained patience, differentiation and teachability. Its ontological commitment remains explicit. The useful intervention is not to imitate every polemical turn of the notebooks, but to restore an occasional authorial passage where it supplies a missing demand or transition.

Likewise, **“the essay’s whole-first inference” is not automatically an outside-narrator fault**. There it distinguishes the author’s synthesis from a claim attributed to a historical source. The problem identified in F01 is that the passage stops short of the comparative consequence—not that it uses the word *essay*. Replacing it with “we” would leave that problem untouched.
