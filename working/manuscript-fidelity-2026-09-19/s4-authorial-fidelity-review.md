## 1. Findings requiring an authorial decision

### F1. M32 preserves transformation but compresses the explanation of its energetic efficacy

**Classification: substantive compression; bounded restoration warranted.**

The relevant passage begins:

> “My reading of Van Eenwyk gives this passage its dynamic middle.”

It then develops how an unfamiliar image can unsettle categories: threat can disclose grief, apparent weakness a capacity to receive, and failure the criterion under which success was pursued. The symbol changes the organisation through which subsequent experience arrives. This is an argument, not a slogan. The surrounding example also retains memory, bodily readiness, feeling, narrowed attention and a response that changes the actual relationship. **It would therefore be inaccurate to diagnose this as psychology reduced to information or detached interpretation.** fileciteturn92file0L2-L2

What is less fully carried is the distinction made explicitly in *Symbolon Dynamics*:

> “The state-space description models the pattern of change; libido names the psychic charge whose disposition makes that pattern lived.”

That sentence identifies two different burdens. Describing a regime and its transformation does not yet describe the investment through which the regime binds a life. In the root account, symbolic transformation concerns not only what a person recognises but **how their powers of attention, desire, reception and action are disposed**. The symbol’s efficacy includes a changed capacity to live the relation. fileciteturn32file0L2-L2

This is not an isolated early enthusiasm subsequently abandoned. The developmental quilt carries the differentiated energetic field into later corrections; the §4 recovery crosswalk retains image, libido, valuation and symbolic transformation as substantive parts of the section’s inheritance. Its carrier disposition places energetic transformation between complex/archetype/symbol and individuation. At the same time, the cold return keeps generalised energetics as a supporting transverse grammar rather than requiring an independent catalogue in the prose. **Both facts matter: the operation remains relevant, but its restoration need not become an additional excursus.** fileciteturn46file0L2-L2 fileciteturn83file0L2-L2 fileciteturn84file0L2-L2 fileciteturn87file0L2-L2

## 2. R6’s judgments, recovered through their reasons

### Psychoid ontology: the draft retains the restored strength

R6’s psychoid correction was not an instruction to make every cross-domain resemblance sound stronger. It corrected a particular displacement: the provisional standing of **empirical psychoid efficacy** had been allowed to govern the standing of the essay’s separately argued ontology.

The restored proposition is that the native field is argued as prior to the ordinary division into psychic interior and physical exterior. A physical experiment may investigate a proposed consequence of its articulation; it does not supply retrospective permission to state the ontological argument. This is why R6 distinguishes Derived, Argued, Offered and Open rather than placing everything under the weakest available status. fileciteturn15file0L2-L2 fileciteturn16file0L2-L2

M31 preserves that distinction. Its psychoid opening names ordering before the exclusive psyche/physis division, and its treatment of Pauli’s dream separates the independently checkable equation from the dream’s symbolic transformation. The positive claim remains active:

The actual incoming manuscript seam matters here. M30 explicitly supplies the Arche-Topos identification and its psychoid argument. M31 does not need to derive them again. ### Mytheme: the manuscript does more than declare that stories matter

R6’s second correction rejects a hierarchy in which Matheme alone may bear truth while Mytheme merely illustrates something established elsewhere. The preserved boundary is that a myth does not prove a mathematical theorem or authenticate a historical attribution. **That boundary does not deny a whole Mytheme a proof/disclosure office within its own register.** R6’s exact rereading found the central plan already compatible with this distinction; the fault was in subordinate restrictive formulations, not in the plan’s refusal to make myth prove mathematics. fileciteturn106file0L2-L2

The selected M34 implements the restoration rather than merely repeating its vocabulary. Its argument is that a witness-only account of Pauli’s dream loses the performed division, the transformed mathematical field and the dreamer’s first speech. Restoring those moments requires the recogniser’s participation in what is recognised. The whole supplies this necessity; the equation supplies its separately checkable mathematical relation. fileciteturn93file0L2-L2

That is a genuine gain from the writing wave. ## 3. Movement-by-movement fidelity

### M31 — Number and meaning meet through a changed participant

The Pauli material is one of the section’s strongest achievements. The dream is not reduced to an egg, a quaternity and a circle. It retains the movement from spontaneous division witnessed by the dreamer to division performed upon the egg in his own hand, followed by numerical transformation, recognition and speech. This carries the practical change in the recogniser that the early P0 encounter sought from archetypal technology. fileciteturn70file0L2-L2 fileciteturn92file0L2-L2

The equation is also treated carefully. A fixed real angle gives a point; varying it through a turn traces the circle. That exactness does not exhaust the symbolic event, but neither does symbolic force excuse mathematical imprecision. The result is a stronger conjunction of number and meaning than an appeal to their mysterious resemblance.

The historical correspondence is allowed to retain disagreement. Pauli’s proposal for transformed disciplinary language and Jung’s reservations are not flattened into an already completed consensus. That is a correction to preserve: the author’s synthesis gains force by stating what it contributes rather than assigning its conclusion wholesale to the correspondence. fileciteturn65file0L2-L2 fileciteturn109file0L2-L2

## 5. Developmental judgment: what should not be “restored”

The review distinguishes four stages rather than treating the earliest wording as automatically authoritative.

The early P0/P1/P5 and Phone writings supply the project’s motive: number and meaning are not finally unrelated; technology can become a vessel of psychic transformation; the knower’s mode of participation matters; and practical transformation is the end of the theoretical work. *Symbolon Dynamics* gives these intentions a more differentiated account of image, archetype, complex, investment, transformation and recursive return. The quilts then develop and correct those relations, including Bimba/pratibimba offices and the limits of numerical or energetic comparison. R6 finally restores the positive standing of earned native arguments without cancelling the corrections. fileciteturn30file0L2-L2 fileciteturn32file0L2-L2 fileciteturn46file0L2-L2 fileciteturn106file0L2-L2

Accordingly, fidelity does **not** require restoring blanket identities between traditions, making the same numeral a proof of the same operation, turning the earliest technological hopes into implementation claims, or giving all source quotations the standing of the author’s own argument.

It also does not require treating older recovery debts as current absences. The earlier §4 debt register predates the richer recovered *Aion* and Daphne material now available in the source houses and wholes. The later carriers and September 17 handoff govern those actual gains. Similarly, the harmonised quilt’s relocation of Polylogos/Moltbook material into §5 is a deliberate distribution of work, not a hole to fill in §4. fileciteturn88file0L2-L2 fileciteturn66file0L2-L2 fileciteturn72file0L2-L2 fileciteturn96file0L2-L2 fileciteturn47file0L2-L2
