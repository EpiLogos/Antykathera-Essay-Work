## What the prose has achieved—and should retain

### Knowing is present, rather than indefinitely missing

M01’s distinction between further discoverable information and the knowing renewed through discovery is soundly established. Its force lies in the ordinary operations: remembering incorrectly, discovering a motive, recognising an answer. The Subject is not introduced as an elusive component whose scientific discovery has merely been postponed.

M02 then gives this distinction positive content. The self-portrait example allows representation to become genuinely more adequate without making the portrait identical with its present apprehension. The definition of God likewise distinguishes an account’s possible universality from its impossible position outside the reality in which it occurs. **That is considerably stronger than saying that every account is partial.** The sentence “The definition succeeds through the failure of its claim to finality” belongs to a performed argument, not a decorative paradox. These passages should survive. fileciteturn111file0L2-L2

### Definition’s danger is demonstrated, not merely denounced

M03’s meeting example is especially successful. An accurate attendance count can become an inadequate measure of affectedness; the deeper danger appears when attendance is made the condition of being entitled to challenge that substitution. The account then protects its own disputed authority.

This retains the positive power of determination. Neither counting nor exclusion is condemned in itself. The reader can identify exactly where a legitimate operation acquires an illegitimate office. The broken *symbolon* also does real work: the particular edge carries an absent relation, and the encounter—not possession of one fragment—tests the fit. **The count, criterion and counterpart passages are among the section’s strongest achieved prose.** fileciteturn111file0L2-L2

### Formal limit remains exact, while affirmative truth survives it

M04 keeps the different operations distinct: formation rules, derivability, showing, rule-following, becoming and inheritance, and the articulation of a new formal state. It does not simply line up authorities behind the proposition that “everything is incomplete.”

Two formulations deserve particular protection:

> “A predicate can be wholly true in the respect it names without becoming the whole truth of its subject.”

> “This is the immutable gap: immutable as a relation renewed through determination, not as an unchanging deficiency in our stock of information.”

The first preserves the affirmative force of qualified predication. The second retains the difference between the Unknown and particular unknowns without disparaging discovery. The receipt records that the affirmative force of qualification was deliberately strengthened during revision; this is a later gain, not wording to undo in pursuit of an earlier, more sweeping formulation. fileciteturn112file0L2-L2 fileciteturn133file0L2-L2

**The semantic difference.** Participation and recognition of identity are not interchangeable propositions. **Confidence:** the difference between the root/canonical formulation and the participation formulation is exact. Whether the existing continuation already carries enough of that identification for this opening is the authorial judgment to ratify.

**The lost reasoning.** The missing connection has three distinguishable steps. A positioned disclosure organises what can appear. Without that connection, M05 can be read principally as a sophisticated discipline of interpretation and care. Those are genuine achievements, but the section’s mature claim also concerns a transformation in the material organisation of intelligence.

**Bounded repair direction.** Recover the operation, not a Heidegger name-check followed by an AI feature list.
