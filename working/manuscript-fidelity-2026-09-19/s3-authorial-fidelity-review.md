## 4. Corrections that should not be reversed in the name of fidelity

### The Second Spanda’s arithmetic is better now

The draft distinguishes:

\[

\[

The latter is an additional comparison, not division of the sum by four. Likewise, the count `100` is not silently exchanged with `100%=1`. The proportional exponent-reading is retained, but its objects and change of operation are exposed.

This is not an outside mathematician’s veto of the native theorem. It is the theorem’s exposition becoming answerable to the operations it actually uses. The reader can now follow where ordinary arithmetic ends and where the native reading begins. fileciteturn152file0L2-L2

## 5. Voice, synthesis and the danger of slogan substitution

### No general outside-narrator regression is established

The mathematical prose repeatedly performs operations rather than merely attributing them to “Taylor’s system.” Its First Spanda attribution—“the native source writes a plus sign”—does necessary notational work. It distinguishes the original inscription from the operation the reader is being asked to follow. That is not automatically alienated narration.

Likewise, M30 does not stop at “the essay interprets these parallels as psychoid.” It argues why the capacity of determination cannot simply be assigned to one already constituted side of the psyche/matter distinction, and then makes the identification. The source-specific boundary follows the positive claim rather than replacing it. fileciteturn152file0L2-L2 fileciteturn121file0L2-L2

The important distinction is between **acknowledging the type and provenance of an operation** and **standing outside the inquiry as a commentator on someone else’s beliefs**. Much of the draft’s apparent caution belongs to the first category.

### “Return through retained difference” is not merely a slogan here

In M29, the phrase has more than one exact body: a lifted displacement, a harmonic factor, and a performance whose recognisable return retains a temporal passage. Their differences remain available. A17 and the current movement expressly make that retained difference the reason the comparison can be argued without asserting a formal identity between a lattice vector and a frequency ratio. fileciteturn145file0L2-L2 fileciteturn146file0L2-L2

The phrase becomes less fully earned at the two local points identified above: contextual holding without its geometrical enactment, and inverse phase without its fuller role exchange. ### The roots contain an urgency worth recovering selectively

The Phone writings and deep copula place the knower inside the operation more forcefully than much of the section’s mathematical exposition. Their claim is not simply that there is a mysterious background beyond formal knowledge. It is that the acts of knowing, defining, equating and distinguishing belong to the relation they seek to understand.

P0 also proposes a transformation of the mathematical sciences, not the addition of a symbolic ornament to an otherwise finished quantitative account. That ambition should remain audible.
