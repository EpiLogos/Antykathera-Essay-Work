> `Derived` and `Argued` are positive statuses. They do not mean "mention cautiously," "avoid sounding committed," or "surround with disclaimers." If the philosophical argument argues a metaphysical, ethical, epistemic or technological thesis, state the thesis with the strength the argument earns.

A reviewer may flag overclaim only by naming the exact proposition and exceeded warrant; a reviewer must equally flag underclaim where caveat, status language or defensive negation makes prose say less than canon. *(law text carried in `working/pre-manuscript-refinement-2026-09-10/R6-AUTHORIAL-RETURN-PACKET.md`, ratified at T26)*
