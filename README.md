# The Return of Zero

This repository is being reorganised in place around the published work's own ontology. The public reading body lives in [`symbolon/`](symbolon/README.md). Its root carries Symbolon records directly; its three internal registers are [`matheme/`](symbolon/matheme/README.md), [`mytheme/`](symbolon/mytheme/README.md), and [`episteme/`](symbolon/episteme/README.md).

The complete writing, review, branch, migration, and publication procedure is [`WRITING-PROTOCOL.md`](WRITING-PROTOCOL.md). That protocol sits beside Symbolon because it governs the making of the work; it is not itself an ontological record within the work.

## Present phase

The repository is in **preliminary reorganisation on `main`**. The ontology-led vault and its governing protocol are now present. The quilt foundation remains under Frank's separate review. Its ratification is a gate for migrating and drafting quilt-dependent content, but it does not block the structural, testing, Git, or publication preparations defined in the protocol.

During this phase:

- `essay-workshop/` remains the development canon under `AGENTS.md`;
- no quilt-dependent canonical file is copied into `symbolon/`;
- publication records will move into their final homes once their content is ratified;
- `submission-package/` remains a sibling support surface for manifests, the reader companion, and Epi-Card work, outside the Symbolon ontology;
- model-specific prose branches will be cut from one ratified `main` commit and used sequentially without worktrees.

## Repository surfaces

- [`symbolon/`](symbolon/README.md) — the natural publication vault: the essay, direct Symbolon records, and the three registers.
- [`essay-workshop/`](essay-workshop/) — the live development canon until each record is ratified and moved.
- [`submission-package/`](submission-package/README.md) — submission manifest, companion systems, and historical design provenance.
- [`writing-guidance-tools/`](writing-guidance-tools/README.md) — the governing writing laws, rubric, and selective calibration corpus.

The public remote is not yet connected. GitHub authentication for the configured `EpiLogos` account is presently invalid, and the existing development history must not be published before the public-file and rights audit. These are release gates, not blockers to the preliminary reorganisation.
