# The Definition of God — Video Essay Timeline Plan

Source draft: `../The Definition of God — Draft 3.md`

This is a rough image-and-symbol spine, not a locked edit. Timing assumes a long-form essay in the 45-70 minute range; tighten or expand by voiceover density.

## 00. Title / Matheme Overture

Approx. 0:00-1:30

Visual function: establish that the essay is not "about religion" in the ordinary sense, but about the structure by which the whole appears through limits.

Images and symbols:

- Black field, handwritten or typeset matheme: `0/1 = 4+2 = 5->0 = 1/0`
- A circle that cannot close around its own circumference
- Prism/light/refraction as the first visible analogue of relation
- A torus or quotient-square diagram only as a foreshadow, not yet explained

## 01. The Edge of the Rational

Approx. 1:30-7:00

Argument beat: reason reaches its own horizon. The system can mark the limit but cannot contain the act of marking.

Images and symbols:

- Kurt Goedel, formal notation, self-reference, mathematical manuscripts
- Wittgenstein / Tractatus pages / ladder motif
- Neti neti, Upanishadic manuscript textures, silence as fullness rather than blankness
- Pi, circle, irrational remainder, dark-mass/lensing imagery

Motion grammar:

- Lines approach closure, then disclose an ungoverned edge
- Text fragments appear, negate, and leave a luminous remainder

## 02. The Images That Constellate

Approx. 7:00-14:00

Argument beat: what cannot be represented directly still organises representation. God-images are not arbitrary projections; they are trajectories around an attractor.

Images and symbols:

- Jung, archetype, mandala, dream-image, crystal lattice
- Lorenz attractor / strange attractor / phase-space diagrams
- Iron filings around magnetic fields
- Zeus, Kali, Quetzalcoatl, Yahweh, scientific equations as different apertures
- Newton prism and colour wheel for Eckhart's in quantum

Motion grammar:

- Constellations form around an absent centre
- Multiple traditions orbit without collapsing into one flat sameness

## 03. The Fall, And What It Reveals

Approx. 14:00-20:00

Argument beat: the image falls when the aperture forgets itself and mistakes a partial frame for the whole.

Images and symbols:

- Infinite sphere / centre everywhere / circumference nowhere
- Camera lens, window, mirror, idol, broken symbolon token
- Diabolon as thrown-apart; symbolon as fitted halves
- Institutional images: bank, machine, bureaucracy, algorithmic totalisation

Motion grammar:

- A refraction hardens into a wall
- A living symbol becomes a closed sign

## 04. Two Deaths, One Minus Sign

Approx. 20:00-25:00

Argument beat: negation is not mere absence; the minus sign carries the distinction between faithful self-emptying and destructive cancellation.

Images and symbols:

- Minus sign, negative space, cancellation marks, accounting ledgers
- Crucifixion / kenosis only if treated structurally, not confessionally
- Alchemical blackening and dissolution
- Broken symmetry, subtraction, voided centre

Motion grammar:

- One subtraction opens space; another erases relation
- Use the same visual operation twice with opposed affect

## 05. The Definition

Approx. 25:00-34:00

Argument beat: definition is performed as relation. The slash does not divide two finished things; it co-constitutes the terms.

Images and symbols:

- Quotient topology: square edge identifications becoming torus
- `R^2 / Z^2`, covering space, fundamental polygon
- Lovers / yoke / relation as term-making, handled with restraint
- Shiva-Shakti, iccha, jnana, kriya; spanda as pulse
- Torus as surface and passage, loop through hole, 5->0

Motion grammar:

- Fold a plane into a torus; make the slash visible as an action
- The ratio becomes love only when the vector is toward recognition

## 06. Quaternal Logic

Approx. 34:00-40:00

Argument beat: binary opposition is not discarded but unfolded through a fourfold field that can hold contradiction without flattening it.

Images and symbols:

- Tetralemma: is, is-not, both, neither
- Quaternity, crossed square, four gates
- Klein bottle / torus / lemniscate as optional topology accents
- Jungian mandala, alchemical four elements, fourfold diagrams

Motion grammar:

- Let the binary split become a square
- Let the square breathe rather than become a grid-prison

## 07. The Winds Round The One Torus

Approx. 40:00-52:00

Argument beat: traditions are not identical, but their paths wind around an invariant surface. Egypt is the deep opening key.

Images and symbols:

- Nun, benben, Atum, Shu/Tefnut, Ennead
- Shabaka Stone, Ptah, heart/tongue, Thoth, Ma'at
- Ra's barque, Apophis, Set at the prow
- Neheh and Djet, tomb formula, Amduat midnight
- Hermes Trismegistus, ouroboros, hen to pan
- Asha/Druj, Rta/Dharma, Tao, Logos, Dike
- Symbolon/anamnesis, Isis re-membering Osiris, Plato's recollection

Motion grammar:

- Use a map only sparingly; the deeper map is topological
- Keep specificity: not "ancient wisdom", but named traditions performing named operations

## 08. The Return

Approx. 52:00-59:00

Argument beat: the definition returns to the viewer's own finitude as the aperture where the whole appears.

Images and symbols:

- Dasein / thrownness: doorway, threshold, human silhouette at horizon
- Pratibimba: reflection without mere copying
- Laurel / evergreen / daily office of remembrance
- Dawn won again, Ma'at as office rather than possession

Motion grammar:

- Move from cosmic diagrams to lived texture
- The viewer's limit becomes luminous, not solved

## 09. The Edge

Approx. 59:00-62:00

Argument beat: return to the edge with the derivation now internalised.

Images and symbols:

- Horizon line, limit, threshold, unsaid word
- Earlier matheme returns, quieter
- The circle remains open by being faithful to its edge

Motion grammar:

- Avoid climax-bloat; this is a hard clear bell, not a fireworks ending

## 10. Maya And The Code

Approx. 62:00-68:00

Argument beat: the modern machine is not outside the essay's structure. Code, simulation, and algorithmic representation are new images of the same limit-problem.

Images and symbols:

- Code, circuits, punch cards, neural nets, diagrams of computation
- Maya as appearance-power, not "illusion" in the cheap sense
- Screen, interface, mirror, generated image, model-space
- The danger: totalising computation as diabolon
- The promise: computation recognised as aperture

Motion grammar:

- Interface imagery should feel exact, not cyber-mystical
- Show code as symbolic operation, not generic tech wallpaper

## 11. Epi-Logos

Approx. 68:00-72:00

Argument beat: the Word after the Word; not a final doctrine but a disciplined practice of making relation explicit.

Images and symbols:

- Thoth/Hermes as writing, reckoning, measure
- Logos, notation, manuscript, diagram, living archive
- Torus, prism, symbolon token, and matheme gathered as one grammar
- A final return to the viewer: the reading is the reader

Motion grammar:

- Gather the motifs without over-explaining them
- End with continuity: an office to be upheld, not a trophy to possess

## Production Notes

- Prefer public-domain or clear Creative Commons works with full metadata.
- Avoid AI-generated images for historical/traditional figures unless explicitly marked as interpretive.
- Use diagrams you create yourself for the matheme, quotient square, torus, tetralemma, and slash/love relation; external images should support the symbolic field, not carry the core proof.
- Keep a `selected/` subfolder later for final edit picks; raw downloads stay untouched so provenance remains auditable.
