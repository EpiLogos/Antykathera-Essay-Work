## the-definition

Visual thesis:

The section should make the viewer feel definition as an enacted relation rather than a terminal sentence. The key visual movement is not "God as a thing" but the slash as operation: plane to quotient, distinction to relation, relation to recognition. The strongest downloaded images should provide historical and symbolic gravity around the proof - manuscripts, figures, traditional iconography, mathematical surfaces - while the central logic of `0/1 = 4+2 = 5->0 = 1/0` should be built as custom motion diagrams. The section wants austere trust: real sources, clear provenance, no devotional haze.

Must-have image motifs:

- Fundamental polygon for the torus: square with identified edges, ideally labelled `a b a^-1 b^-1`.
- Torus as quotient surface: `R^2/Z^2`, lattice translations, covering space.
- Infinite plane / lattice / grid as the 0-field submitted to identification.
- The slash `/` treated as an action, not punctuation.
- Moses and the burning bush / "I Am" as first-person divine naming, handled as textual-iconic source rather than Sunday-school illustration.
- Shaiva material around Shiva-Shakti, spanda, iccha, jnana, kriya, ideally manuscript or temple relief rather than modern fantasy art.
- Jung / Answer to Job / Christ and shadow only as tightly selected supporting material, if the edit keeps that paragraph.
- Descartes / cogito as the Western first-person limit, if the section needs a bridge into the subject.
- Camera lens / aperture / light entering through a finite opening, as a concrete image of partial knowing.
- Bow/string or chord/harmony image for tension-held relation, used sparingly.

Avoid-list for generic imagery:

- Generic "God light in clouds", hands reaching through clouds, cosmic father figures, or AI-looking celestial silhouettes.
- Vague sacred geometry wallpapers, glowing torus stock art, neon chakra spirals, or psychedelic tunnel visuals.
- Modern fantasy Shiva-Shakti art without provenance or license clarity.
- Random "love" imagery: hearts, couples, wedding hands, red roses. The love here is relation that makes terms, not romance stock.
- Stock equations on blackboards where the symbols are unreadable or unrelated.
- Burning bush images that flatten the point into biblical illustration without showing first-person naming.
- Any image implying the definition has successfully captured an object called God.

## quaternal-logic

Visual thesis:

This section exposes the skeleton the definition already walked. The visual task is to move from binary distinction to a fourfold field, then to the six-element surface that can carry self-reference without collapse. It should feel mathematical before it feels mystical: set construction, modulo clocks, binary pairs, Euler characteristic, torus loops, chromatic number, Szilassi polyhedron, and Indra's net as a late symbolic analogue. The fourfold is not decorative symmetry. It is what the binary produces when it is applied to itself.

Must-have image motifs:

- Empty set / von Neumann ordinals: `empty set`, `{empty set}`, `{empty set, {empty set}}`.
- Binary values `{0, 1}` and mod-2 clock.
- Four composite values `{00, 10, 01, 11}` and mod-4 clock.
- `4+2=6` as the self-referential alphabet; perfect number 6.
- Torus fundamental group / two non-contractible loops.
- Sphere versus torus: genus 0 closure versus genus 1 opening.
- Euler characteristic `V - E + F` and `chi = 2 - 2g`.
- Genus as hole and birth: the semantic hinge should be custom text/diagram, with any downloaded historical material used only as supporting atmosphere.
- Visarga as two points, Shiva-bindu / Shakti-bindu, if presented visually with care.
- Szilassi polyhedron and seven-colour theorem for torus map colouring.
- Indra's net / jeweled net as holographic adjacency, after the mathematical seven has been earned.

Avoid-list for generic imagery:

- Generic mandalas used as shorthand for "quaternity" without source, tradition, or argument.
- Random four-elements charts unless they are historically specific alchemical diagrams.
- "Klein bottle" images that distract from the actual torus derivation.
- Pop sacred-geometry grids, glowing flower-of-life textures, or unsourced "tetralemma diagrams."
- Buddhist imagery used merely as exotic atmosphere around Indra's net or tetralemma.
- Any graphic that implies fourfold logic replaces binary logic. The section's claim is that binary, brought into self-relation, unfolds.
- Diagrams that show a torus as a donut object only, without loops, quotient, or genus.
