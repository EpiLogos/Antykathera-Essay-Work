## Discipline statement

Every quote below was read via Read or Grep/Bash tool output in this session — none reconstructed from memory, none paraphrased into a "quote." Verbatim means character-for-character from what the tool returned, including typos and lowercase in chat turns (voice-markers, not cleaned up). Where Frank-vs-AI attribution in a chat log is ambiguous, that is stated explicitly rather than guessed. Anything recognized as already present in `corpus-quote-bank.md` was left out; a few borderline cases are flagged inline as "adjacent to banked material" rather than silently included or silently dropped.

Two important provenance categories that recur heavily below, named once here rather than on every entry:
- **AI-turn synthesis in a live Nara/chat conversation** — the AI is elaborating back to Frank a structure that is *his* (the architecture, the vocabulary, the connections he asked for), but the specific sentences are Claude's, not Frank's. These are flagged "AI turn" throughout. They are usable for the crux (several are exceptionally precise formulations of exactly the logic in question) but must be handled as such in any eventual drafting — not silently absorbed as Frank's voice.
- **Frank's own prompt/chat-turn language quoted back inside an AI turn** — where the AI turn visibly repeats a phrase Frank supplied in his own prior turn (verified by checking Frank's actual turn), this is marked and the underlying Frank turn is cited/quoted directly where possible.
