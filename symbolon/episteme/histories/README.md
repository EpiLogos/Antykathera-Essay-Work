# Histories

Histories develops the transformations through which the essay's problems acquire their present form. The active bodies include ancient philosophy, Indian philosophy, mathematics, myth, psychology, process and systems science, technology and politics, language–symbol–dialogue, and the advent of zero and subject.

A history record has a governing question, dated or ordered movements, changes of term and practice, source anchors, disputes, continuities and breaks. It ends by naming the exact essay movements that inherit the history. A chronology without an account of change belongs in Figures rather than here.
