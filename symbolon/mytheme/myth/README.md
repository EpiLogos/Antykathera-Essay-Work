# Myth

Myth carries inherited scenes whose actions disclose a live relation. The field includes Ares, Aphrodite, Hephaestus and Harmonia; Apollo and Dionysus; Prometheus and Atlas; Indra's net; Christian and Śaiva figures; and other myths admitted by an exact local operation.

Each record identifies the scene, tells only the narrative required to follow it, and states what the figures do for the essay. Variant traditions and scholarly interpretation remain visible through Episteme. Myth figures liveability, enclosure, transformation, return or failure; it does not prove a matheme.
