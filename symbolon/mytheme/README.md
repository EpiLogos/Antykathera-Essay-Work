# Mytheme

Mytheme carries narrative, myth, poetry, media, image, and artistic reference. Its records show how a relation becomes perceptible, inhabitable, memorable, or performable without pretending that an image proves a matheme or that a myth supplies historical entailment.

Each Mytheme record names the exact operation performed by its figure: it may figure, embody, dramatise, presage, invert, hold, or return a relation. It distinguishes Frank's authored poems and writings from external works, preserves exact provenance and rights, and never rewrites sovereign authorial material. An excerpt enters the main essay only when the prose earns its presence and the fuller record gives the reader a real path into and back from it.

Media and artistic references receive the same discipline. The record identifies the relevant scene, form, image, or narrative movement; the claim made from it; the limits of that claim; and any source or rights work required for publication.

Each record points to exact essay and argument locations and closes the circuit back to the passage it deepens.
