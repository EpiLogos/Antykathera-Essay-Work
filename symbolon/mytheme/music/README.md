# Music

Music carries the experienced and composed life of pulse, tension, interval, phase, resolution and return. Its records treat a piece, passage, tuning or performance as heard form: what enters, what changes orientation, what remains, and how the return is altered by the remainder.

Formal ratios and calculations live in [[../../matheme/harmonics/README|Matheme harmonics]]. Music records show what those relations do in time and perception. They include score or recording locators, rights, audible description and exact essay placement.
